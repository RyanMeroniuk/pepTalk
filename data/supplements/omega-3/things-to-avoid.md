# Omega-3 (Fish Oil / Krill Oil)
## Things to Avoid

- **Taking on an empty stomach:** Absorption of omega-3 (especially ethyl ester form) drops dramatically without dietary fat. Always take with a meal containing at least some fat
- **Buying based on total fish oil mg instead of EPA+DHA content:** A "1200mg fish oil" capsule may only contain 360mg EPA+DHA. Always check the supplement facts panel for actual EPA and DHA amounts
- **Ignoring rancidity:** Never consume fish oil that smells strongly fishy, tastes off, or has passed its expiration date. Cut open a capsule and smell it periodically. Store in the refrigerator after opening. Rancid omega-3 causes oxidative damage
- **Storing improperly:** Keep away from heat, light, and air. Refrigerate liquid fish oil and opened capsule bottles. Dark glass bottles are preferred over clear plastic
- **Cheap, unverified brands:** Low-cost fish oils are often ethyl ester form, under-dosed, and may not be tested for heavy metals, PCBs, or oxidation. Look for IFOS 5-star certification, USP verification, or NSF certification
- **High-dose fish oil before surgery:** Stop high-dose omega-3 (3g+/day) 7-14 days before elective surgery due to mild antiplatelet effects. Standard doses (1-2g) are generally not a concern, but inform your surgeon
- **Combining high-dose omega-3 with blood thinners without medical supervision:** Warfarin, heparin, clopidogrel, aspirin, and other anticoagulants/antiplatelets may have additive effects with high-dose omega-3. Does not mean you cannot combine them — many cardiologists recommend it — but dose should be monitored
- **Cod liver oil at high doses:** Contains vitamin A (retinol) which is toxic in excess. If using cod liver oil, respect the vitamin A upper limit (10,000 IU/day). Standard fish oil does not contain vitamin A
- **Expecting plant-based ALA to substitute for EPA/DHA:** Flax, chia, and hemp provide ALA, which converts to EPA at only 5-10% and DHA at 2-5%. Vegans should consider algal oil DHA supplements rather than relying on ALA conversion
- **Smoking while supplementing:** Smoking massively increases oxidative stress and lipid peroxidation, potentially oxidizing omega-3 fatty acids faster than they can exert benefits
