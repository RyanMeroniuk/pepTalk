# Omega-3 (Fish Oil / Krill Oil)
## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Eat fatty fish 2-3x per week:** Whole food omega-3 from fish provides EPA, DHA, protein, selenium, vitamin D, and astaxanthin (in salmon) in a bioavailable matrix that supplements cannot fully replicate. Prioritize SMASH fish (Salmon, Mackerel, Anchovies, Sardines, Herring)
- **Reduce seed oil intake aggressively:** Cutting omega-6 seed oils (soybean, corn, sunflower, cottonseed) from your diet may be as impactful as adding omega-3 supplements. Cook with olive oil, avocado oil, butter, ghee, or coconut oil instead
- **Exercise regularly:** Exercise enhances omega-3 incorporation into cell membranes and upregulates the enzymes that convert omega-3 into anti-inflammatory resolvins and protectins. Resistance training and cardiovascular exercise both show synergy with omega-3 supplementation for body composition and inflammation
- **Test your Omega-3 Index:** A simple blood test (finger prick or venous draw) measures EPA+DHA as a percentage of red blood cell fatty acids. Target 8-12% for optimal cardioprotection. Retest every 6-12 months after starting supplementation to confirm your dose is sufficient
- **Manage stress:** Chronic stress elevates cortisol and promotes systemic inflammation. Omega-3 helps modulate the cortisol response, but combining with stress-reduction practices (meditation, breathwork, nature exposure) amplifies the benefit
- **Prioritize sleep:** Poor sleep increases inflammatory markers (CRP, IL-6) that omega-3 works to reduce. 7-9 hours of quality sleep allows omega-3 anti-inflammatory pathways to function optimally. DHA supplementation may improve sleep architecture
- **Pair with antioxidant-rich diet:** Omega-3 fatty acids are susceptible to oxidation. A diet rich in colorful fruits, vegetables, and green tea provides antioxidants that protect omega-3s in your cell membranes
- **Limit alcohol:** Heavy alcohol consumption increases lipid peroxidation and undermines omega-3 cardiovascular benefits. Moderate intake (1 drink/day or less) is unlikely to be problematic
- **Sun exposure and vitamin D:** Omega-3 and vitamin D have overlapping and complementary anti-inflammatory and immune-modulating effects. Ensure adequate vitamin D status (40-60 ng/mL) alongside omega-3 supplementation
