# Omega-3 (Fish Oil / Krill Oil)
## Classification

- **Category:** Essential Fatty Acid
- **Subcategory:** Polyunsaturated Fatty Acid (PUFA)
- **Type:** Conditionally essential nutrient
- **Primary compounds:** EPA (eicosapentaenoic acid), DHA (docosahexaenoic acid), ALA (alpha-linolenic acid)
- **Regulatory status:** Generally Recognized as Safe (GRAS) by the FDA; prescription omega-3 forms (Lovaza, Vascepa) are FDA-approved drugs
- **Supplement classification:** Dietary supplement (OTC); pharmaceutical grade available by prescription
