# Omega-3 (Fish Oil / Krill Oil)
## Benefits

- **Cardiovascular health:** Reduces triglycerides by 15-45% at therapeutic doses. Lowers blood pressure (systolic ~3-5 mmHg). Reduces resting heart rate. The REDUCE-IT trial showed 25% relative risk reduction in cardiovascular events with 4g/day icosapent ethyl (pure EPA)
- **Anti-inflammatory:** Reduces C-reactive protein (CRP), IL-6, and TNF-alpha. Produces specialized pro-resolving mediators that actively terminate inflammatory responses. Beneficial in rheumatoid arthritis (reduced joint pain and morning stiffness), inflammatory bowel disease, and general systemic inflammation
- **Brain and cognitive function:** DHA comprises ~40% of brain PUFA. Higher omega-3 intake associated with reduced risk of cognitive decline and Alzheimer's disease. Improvements in working memory, processing speed, and executive function in some trials, particularly in those with low baseline omega-3 status
- **Mental health:** EPA at 1-2g/day shows antidepressant effects comparable to fluoxetine in some meta-analyses. Most effective in formulations with EPA:DHA ratio of at least 2:1. Adjunctive benefit with SSRIs. Emerging evidence for anxiety, PTSD, and bipolar depression
- **Eye health:** DHA is concentrated in the retina. Higher intake associated with reduced risk of age-related macular degeneration (AMD). Improves dry eye symptoms by reducing meibomian gland inflammation
- **Joint health:** Reduces joint stiffness and pain scores in osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis. May reduce NSAID requirements. Anti-inflammatory effect on synovial fluid
- **Pregnancy and fetal development:** DHA critical for fetal brain and retinal development, particularly in the third trimester. 200-300mg DHA/day recommended during pregnancy. Associated with reduced preterm birth risk
- **Skin health:** Reduces UV-induced inflammation, supports skin barrier function, may improve acne and psoriasis. EPA particularly protective against photoaging
- **Body composition:** Modest improvements in lean mass preservation during caloric restriction. May enhance fat oxidation during exercise. Reduces cortisol response to stress
- **Sleep quality:** DHA supplementation associated with improved sleep quality in children and adults, potentially through melatonin regulation
