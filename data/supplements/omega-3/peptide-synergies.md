# Omega-3 (Fish Oil / Krill Oil)
## Peptide Synergies

- **BPC-157 (tissue repair):** Omega-3 and BPC-157 share overlapping mechanisms in tissue repair and gut healing. Omega-3 provides the anti-inflammatory substrate (SPMs, resolvins) while BPC-157 upregulates growth factor expression and angiogenesis. Together they create a comprehensive repair environment. Omega-3 also supports gut mucosal integrity, complementing BPC-157's gut-healing properties
- **KPV (anti-inflammatory tripeptide):** KPV acts on alpha-MSH pathways to reduce intestinal and systemic inflammation. Omega-3 provides parallel anti-inflammatory action through eicosanoid modulation and SPM production. Strong synergy for inflammatory bowel conditions and systemic inflammation
- **VIP (Vasoactive Intestinal Peptide):** VIP addresses gut permeability and mucosal immune function, particularly in CIRS/mold illness. Omega-3 complements by reducing systemic inflammatory mediators and supporting mucosal barrier integrity through cell membrane composition
- **Thymosin Alpha-1 (immune modulation):** Omega-3 supports immune cell membrane fluidity and function, enhancing the immune-modulating effects of Thymosin Alpha-1. DHA incorporation into immune cell membranes improves T-cell signaling and resolution of immune responses
- **CJC-1295/Ipamorelin (GH secretagogues):** Growth hormone promotes tissue repair and lean mass. Omega-3 reduces the inflammation that impairs GH signaling and supports the anabolic environment. EPA also helps preserve lean mass during fat loss phases that GH secretagogues promote
- **Semax/Selank (nootropic peptides):** Semax upregulates BDNF and Selank modulates GABA. DHA is a critical structural component of neuronal membranes and supports BDNF expression. The combination enhances neuroplasticity and cognitive function through complementary mechanisms
- **MOTS-c (metabolic peptide):** MOTS-c activates AMPK and improves metabolic function. Omega-3 activates PPARs and reduces metabolic inflammation. Together they address metabolic dysfunction from multiple angles — AMPK signaling, lipid metabolism, insulin sensitivity, and inflammatory reduction
- **Semaglutide/Tirzepatide (GLP-1 agonists):** During the significant weight loss these peptides produce, omega-3 helps preserve lean mass, supports cardiovascular health (important during metabolic changes), and reduces the inflammation associated with adipose tissue loss. Essential supplementation during GLP-1 therapy
- **TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4):** TB-500 promotes wound healing and tissue repair with strong anti-inflammatory properties. Omega-3 SPMs (resolvins, protectins) enhance the resolution phase of inflammation that TB-500 initiates, supporting a complete healing cascade
- **Epitalon (anti-aging):** Epitalon activates telomerase and regulates melatonin. Omega-3 protects cell membranes from oxidative damage that accelerates telomere shortening. DHA in neuronal membranes supports the pineal gland function relevant to Epitalon's melatonin regulation
