# Omega-3 (Fish Oil / Krill Oil)
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

- **Dietary fat (critical):** Omega-3 capsules taken with a fat-containing meal show 3-13x better absorption than fasted. The fat triggers bile release and lipase secretion, which are required to emulsify and digest omega-3 oils. Even a small amount of fat makes a significant difference:
  - **Avocado:** Ideal pairing — healthy monounsaturated fat triggers bile production
  - **Olive oil:** Drizzle on a salad alongside your omega-3 dose
  - **Nuts and nut butters:** Almonds, walnuts, macadamias
  - **Eggs:** Fat in yolk aids absorption; omega-3 enriched eggs add synergy
  - **Cheese or full-fat yogurt:** Dairy fat aids emulsification

- **Phospholipid-rich foods:** Enhance incorporation of omega-3 into cell membranes
  - **Egg yolks:** Rich in phosphatidylcholine
  - **Sunflower lecithin:** Supplemental phospholipid source
  - **Soybeans:** Natural phospholipid content

- **Antioxidant-rich foods:** Protect omega-3s from oxidation in the body, preserving their bioactivity
  - **Berries (blueberries, blackberries):** Anthocyanins protect PUFAs from peroxidation
  - **Dark leafy greens (spinach, kale):** Vitamin E and carotenoids
  - **Turmeric/curcumin:** Anti-inflammatory synergy plus antioxidant protection of omega-3 in tissues
  - **Green tea:** Catechins (EGCG) have antioxidant effects that protect PUFAs
  - **Astaxanthin-rich foods (wild salmon, krill):** Astaxanthin is a potent antioxidant that protects omega-3s from oxidation — this is partly why krill oil is naturally more stable

- **Vitamin E-rich foods:** Vitamin E is the primary fat-soluble antioxidant that prevents omega-3 lipid peroxidation
  - **Almonds and sunflower seeds**
  - **Avocado**
  - **Spinach**
  - **Note:** Many quality fish oil supplements include vitamin E (as mixed tocopherols) for this reason
