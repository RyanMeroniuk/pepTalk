# Omega-3 (Fish Oil / Krill Oil)
## Dosing Protocols

- **General health maintenance:**
  - **Dose:** 1,000-2,000mg combined EPA+DHA per day
  - **Ratio:** Balanced EPA:DHA or slight EPA dominance
  - **Timing:** With a fat-containing meal for optimal absorption (critical for EE form, helpful for TG form)

- **Anti-inflammatory / cardiovascular:**
  - **Dose:** 2,000-4,000mg combined EPA+DHA per day
  - **Ratio:** High EPA (2:1 or 3:1 EPA:DHA preferred)
  - **Timing:** Split into 2 doses with meals containing fat
  - **Note:** The REDUCE-IT trial used 4g/day icosapent ethyl (pure EPA). Prescription Vascepa is pure EPA at this dose

- **Depression / mood support:**
  - **Dose:** 1,000-2,000mg EPA per day (EPA specifically, not total omega-3)
  - **Ratio:** EPA:DHA of at least 2:1 — EPA is the active component for mood
  - **Timing:** With breakfast or lunch (some report activation, so avoid late evening)
  - **Note:** Most meta-analyses show benefit at 1-2g EPA/day. DHA-dominant formulas do not show antidepressant effects

- **Brain health / cognition:**
  - **Dose:** 1,000-2,000mg DHA per day
  - **Ratio:** DHA-dominant or balanced
  - **Timing:** With any fat-containing meal

- **Krill oil dosing:**
  - **Dose:** 500-1,000mg krill oil per day (contains less total EPA+DHA per capsule but phospholipid form has better bioavailability)
  - **Timing:** Can be taken with or without food due to phospholipid form
  - **Note:** mg-for-mg, krill oil delivers more omega-3 to tissues. 500mg krill oil may match 1,000mg fish oil for blood level increases

- **Pregnancy:**
  - **Dose:** Minimum 200-300mg DHA per day; 500-1,000mg combined EPA+DHA is common
  - **Timing:** With meals
  - **Note:** Choose molecularly distilled products tested for heavy metals (mercury, PCBs). Avoid cod liver oil due to vitamin A excess risk

- **Loading phase (optional):** For those with very low omega-3 index, some practitioners recommend 4-6g/day for the first 2-4 weeks to build tissue levels faster, then drop to maintenance
- **With food:** Always take with a meal containing dietary fat. Studies show 3-13x better absorption with a fat-containing meal vs. fasted. Even a small amount of fat (e.g., avocado, nuts, olive oil) makes a significant difference
- **Testing:** The Omega-3 Index blood test measures EPA+DHA as a percentage of red blood cell membranes. Target: 8-12% (cardioprotective range). Most Americans are 4-5%
