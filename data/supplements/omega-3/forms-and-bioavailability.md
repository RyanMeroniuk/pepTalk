# Omega-3 (Fish Oil / Krill Oil)
## Forms and Bioavailability

- **Triglyceride (TG) form:** The natural form found in fish. ~50% better absorption than ethyl ester form. Re-esterified triglyceride (rTG) is the gold standard — molecularly distilled for purity then converted back to TG form. Look for brands specifying "triglyceride form" or "rTG"
- **Ethyl ester (EE) form:** Created during molecular distillation (a purification step). Cheaper to produce but requires pancreatic lipase to break the ethyl bond before absorption. Absorption is significantly lower, especially without food. Most bargain fish oils are EE form. Prescription Lovaza is EE
- **Phospholipid form (krill oil):** EPA and DHA in krill oil are bound to phospholipids (mainly phosphatidylcholine), which are inherently water-miscible. This allows absorption without bile salt emulsification, improving bioavailability even on an empty stomach. Studies show equivalent blood level increases with lower total EPA+DHA doses compared to TG fish oil. Also delivers astaxanthin (potent antioxidant) which protects the oil from oxidation
- **Algal oil:** Plant-based DHA (and increasingly EPA) derived from marine microalgae. TG form. Good option for vegetarians/vegans. Historically DHA-dominant, but newer formulations include meaningful EPA. No risk of ocean contaminants
- **EPA vs DHA ratios by goal:**
  - **Inflammation/cardiovascular/depression:** High EPA — look for 2:1 or 3:1 EPA:DHA
  - **Brain health/cognition/neuroprotection:** High DHA — look for 1:1 or DHA-dominant
  - **General health:** Balanced EPA:DHA or slight EPA dominance
  - **Pregnancy:** DHA-dominant (minimum 200mg DHA/day)

- **Rancidity — a critical concern:** Oxidized fish oil is not just ineffective, it may be harmful (increases LDL oxidation, promotes inflammation). Check for:
  - **TOTOX value:** Should be below 26 (lower is better). TOTOX = 2x peroxide value + anisidine value
  - **Peroxide value:** Should be below 5 meq/kg
  - **Smell/taste test:** Fresh fish oil should have minimal odor. Strong fishy smell or taste = rancid
  - **IFOS certification:** International Fish Oil Standards program. 5-star IFOS rating means independently tested for purity, potency, and freshness. This is the gold standard third-party certification
  - **Storage:** Refrigerate after opening. Dark bottles preferred. Avoid heat and light exposure. Capsules are more shelf-stable than liquid

- **Concentration matters:** A "1000mg fish oil" capsule may contain only 300mg combined EPA+DHA. Always read the supplement facts panel for actual EPA and DHA content, not total fish oil weight
- **Recommended brands/certifications:** Look for IFOS 5-star certified products, USP verified, or NSF certified. Brands like Nordic Naturals, Carlson, Sports Research, and Viva Naturals consistently test well
