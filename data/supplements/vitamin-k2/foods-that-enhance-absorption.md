# Vitamin K2
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

### Dietary Fats (essential — K2 is fat-soluble)
- **Grass-fed butter / ghee:** Provides both dietary fat for K2 absorption and a small amount of natural MK-4. Taking K2 supplements with butter-containing meals is ideal.
- **Extra virgin olive oil:** Monounsaturated fat enhances fat-soluble vitamin absorption. A tablespoon with your K2 supplement significantly improves uptake.
- **Avocado:** Excellent source of monounsaturated fat. Studies on fat-soluble vitamin absorption show avocado increases uptake by 30-50%.
- **Coconut oil / MCT oil:** Medium-chain triglycerides are rapidly absorbed and can enhance K2 uptake. Useful for those with fat malabsorption.
- **Egg yolks (whole eggs):** The fat in the yolk aids absorption of supplemental K2, and pasture-raised yolks also contribute natural MK-4.
- **Fatty fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel):** Provide both fat for K2 absorption and complementary fat-soluble nutrients (D3, omega-3).
- **Nuts and seeds:** Almonds, walnuts, and macadamia nuts provide fat for absorption along with complementary minerals.
- **Cheese (especially aged):** Provides both fat for supplement absorption and natural K2 (MK-8, MK-9). Gouda and Jarlsberg are particularly K2-rich.

### Vitamin D3-Rich Foods and Supplements (synergistic partner)
- **Vitamin D3 supplement (taken with K2):** D3 upregulates osteocalcin and MGP production — the proteins that K2 activates. Taking D3 and K2 together ensures the full pathway is supported: D3 creates the proteins, K2 activates them.
- **Fatty fish (salmon, mackerel):** Provide D3 naturally, supporting the D3-K2 synergy from food.
- **Egg yolks (pasture-raised):** Contain both D3 and K2 (MK-4) naturally, plus fat for absorption.
- **Cod liver oil:** Rich in D3 and vitamin A, paired with K2 creates comprehensive fat-soluble vitamin synergy.

### Calcium-Rich Foods (K2 directs this calcium to bones)
- **Full-fat dairy (yogurt, cheese, milk):** Provides calcium that K2 will direct to bone matrix via activated osteocalcin. The fat in full-fat dairy also enhances K2 absorption.
- **Sardines and canned salmon (with bones):** Calcium-rich plus D3-rich plus fat for absorption — an ideal meal to pair with K2.
- **Dark leafy greens (cooked with fat):** Provide calcium and K1 (which partially converts to MK-4). Cooking spinach, kale, or collards with olive oil or butter maximizes nutrient value.
- **Bone broth:** Provides calcium, magnesium, and collagen — complementary nutrients for the bone-building effects K2 supports.

### Magnesium-Rich Foods (cofactor for D3 and K2 pathways)
- **Pumpkin seeds:** 1 oz = 156 mg magnesium. Magnesium is required for vitamin D activation, which in turn upregulates the K2-dependent proteins.
- **Dark chocolate (85%+):** 1 oz = 65 mg magnesium
- **Almonds:** 1 oz = 80 mg magnesium
- **Spinach (cooked):** 1 cup = 157 mg magnesium

### Bile-Stimulating Foods (enhance fat-soluble vitamin absorption)
- **Bitter greens (arugula, dandelion greens, radicchio):** Stimulate bile production, which is essential for emulsifying and absorbing K2.
- **Artichokes:** Strong bile stimulant, excellent as a meal component when taking K2.
- **Ginger:** Promotes bile secretion and gut motility.
- **Lemon juice / apple cider vinegar:** Support digestive secretions that aid fat-soluble vitamin absorption.

### General Principles
- **Always take K2 with a fat-containing meal.** This is non-negotiable for optimal absorption. Even 10-15g of dietary fat makes a significant difference.
- **The ideal K2 meal includes:** Healthy fats + calcium source + D3 supplement or D3-rich food. Example: salmon with butter sauce, side of sauteed greens in olive oil, with D3/K2 supplements.
- **Those without a gallbladder** may benefit from bile salt supplements or ox bile with K2 to ensure adequate emulsification and absorption.
