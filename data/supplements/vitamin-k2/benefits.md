# Vitamin K2
## Benefits

- **Cardiovascular protection (arterial calcification prevention):** The Rotterdam Study (n=4,807, 10-year follow-up) found that individuals in the highest tertile of dietary K2 intake had a 52% lower risk of severe aortic calcification, 41% lower risk of coronary heart disease, and 26% lower risk of all-cause mortality compared to the lowest tertile. K2 (not K1) was specifically associated with these benefits. MK-7 supplementation (180-360 mcg/day) has been shown to reduce arterial stiffness and slow progression of coronary artery calcification.
- **Bone health and fracture prevention:** K2 activates osteocalcin, which is required for calcium incorporation into bone matrix. MK-4 (45 mg/day) is approved in Japan as a treatment for osteoporosis, where it reduces vertebral fracture risk by 60-80% and hip fracture risk by 77% in clinical trials. MK-7 (180 mcg/day) improves bone mineral density and bone strength indices in postmenopausal women (3-year trial).
- **Dental health:** K2 activates osteocalcin in dentin and supports proper tooth mineralization. Weston A. Price's historical research documented populations with high K2 diets (from organ meats, fermented foods, grass-fed dairy) had dramatically better dental health and facial development. K2 deficiency during development is associated with dental crowding and poor enamel formation.
- **Synergy with vitamin D3:** K2 and D3 together are more effective than either alone for bone health and cardiovascular protection. D3 increases calcium absorption and produces osteocalcin and MGP; K2 activates these proteins. Clinical trials using D3+K2 combination show superior outcomes for bone density and vascular health compared to D3 alone.
- **Anti-inflammatory effects:** MK-7 supplementation (180-360 mcg/day) reduces CRP, IL-6, and TNF-alpha in clinical studies. Relevant for chronic inflammatory conditions and as foundational support for anti-inflammatory protocols.
- **Cancer risk reduction:** Epidemiological studies show higher K2 intake is associated with reduced risk of prostate cancer (35% reduction in the EPIC-Heidelberg cohort), lung cancer, and liver cancer. MK-4 induces apoptosis in cancer cell lines (hepatocellular carcinoma, leukemia, lung cancer) in preclinical studies. MK-4 is used adjunctively in Japan for hepatocellular carcinoma recurrence prevention.
- **Insulin sensitivity and diabetes prevention:** K2 supplementation improves insulin sensitivity and reduces progression to type 2 diabetes. A 36-month supplementation study with MK-7 (720 mcg/day) showed improved insulin sensitivity through osteocalcin-mediated signaling.
- **Brain health:** MK-4 is the dominant K2 form in the brain. It supports sphingolipid synthesis (critical for myelin and neuronal membranes) and protects against oxidative damage. Reduced brain MK-4 levels are associated with Alzheimer's disease and cognitive decline. K2 may prevent neuronal cell death through its anti-inflammatory and antioxidant mechanisms.
- **Kidney health:** K2 activates MGP in renal tissue, potentially reducing kidney calcification and stone formation. Chronic kidney disease patients have extremely high rates of vascular calcification and often have profound K2 deficiency. K2 supplementation in CKD patients is an active area of research.
- **Testosterone support (MK-4):** MK-4 concentrates in the testes and supports steroidogenesis. Animal studies show increased testosterone production with MK-4 supplementation. Human data is limited but promising for male hormonal optimization.
- **Varicose vein and soft tissue calcification prevention:** MGP activation by K2 inhibits calcification in veins, cardiac valves, cartilage, and other soft tissues. Relevant for preventing degenerative calcification with aging.
- **Wrinkle reduction (skin health):** K2 prevents elastin calcification in the skin, which contributes to wrinkle formation. Populations with high K2 intake (Japanese natto consumers) have notably better skin elasticity with aging.
