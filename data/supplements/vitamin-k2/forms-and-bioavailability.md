# Vitamin K2
## Forms and Bioavailability

### MK-7 (Menaquinone-7) — Preferred Supplemental Form
- **Source:** Primarily produced by Bacillus subtilis fermentation of soybeans (natto). Also available as synthetic MK-7. Soy-free MK-7 (from chickpea fermentation or synthetic) is available for those with soy allergies.
- **Half-life:** Approximately 72 hours — the longest of any K2 form. Provides sustained, stable blood levels with once-daily dosing.
- **Bioavailability:** Excellent absorption when taken with fat. Reaches all tissues including bone and vasculature due to its long circulation time. Accumulates to steady-state levels after about 2 weeks of daily dosing.
- **Effective dose:** 100-200 mcg/day for general health; 200-360 mcg/day for cardiovascular and bone therapeutic protocols.
- **Evidence:** Most studied form for cardiovascular and bone health endpoints. 3-year clinical trials demonstrate reduction in age-related bone loss, improved bone strength, and reduced arterial stiffness.
- **Best for:** General supplementation, cardiovascular protection, bone health, D3 co-supplementation. The recommended form for most people.

### MK-4 (Menaquinone-4) — Tissue-Specific Form
- **Source:** Found naturally in animal foods (egg yolks, butter, organ meats, meat). Also produced endogenously from K1 conversion (inefficiently). Available as synthetic supplement.
- **Half-life:** Only 1-2 hours — requires multiple daily doses (typically 3x/day) to maintain blood levels.
- **Bioavailability:** Rapidly absorbed but quickly cleared from blood. However, MK-4 preferentially accumulates in specific tissues: brain, testes, pancreas, kidneys, and arterial walls.
- **Effective dose:** 100-500 mcg/day for general support; 15-45 mg/day (therapeutic, as used in Japanese osteoporosis treatment — split into 3 doses of 5-15 mg).
- **Evidence:** Japanese osteoporosis studies use 45 mg/day, showing 60-80% reduction in vertebral fractures. Unique tissue-specific effects via SXR/PXR receptor activation. Brain-specific benefits.
- **Best for:** Osteoporosis treatment (high-dose), brain health, testosterone support, tissue-specific K2 effects. Often used as a complement to MK-7.

### MK-7 vs. MK-4 Comparison
- **For daily supplementation:** MK-7 is superior due to its long half-life, once-daily dosing, and better steady-state blood levels at lower doses.
- **For osteoporosis treatment:** MK-4 at 45 mg/day is proven in clinical trials (Japanese protocol), but MK-7 at 180-360 mcg/day is also effective and more practical.
- **For brain health:** MK-4 is preferred — it is the dominant K2 form in the brain and has unique neuroprotective mechanisms.
- **For testosterone support:** MK-4 is preferred — it concentrates in the testes and activates SXR/PXR.
- **Comprehensive protocol:** Many practitioners recommend MK-7 (100-200 mcg/day) for sustained systemic levels plus MK-4 (1-5 mg/day) for tissue-specific benefits.

### Other MK Forms
- **MK-8, MK-9 (from fermented cheeses):** Present in aged cheeses like Gouda, Brie, Edam, and Jarlsberg. Contribute to dietary K2 intake. Less studied than MK-4 and MK-7 but likely bioactive.
- **MK-6 (from fermented foods):** Found in some fermented products. Limited research.

### K1 (Phylloquinone) — NOT a Substitute for K2
- **K1 primarily activates clotting factors in the liver.** While the body can convert K1 to MK-4, the conversion rate is extremely low (estimated 5-25%). K1 supplementation does not adequately supply the K2-dependent proteins in bone and vasculature.
- **K1 does not replace K2 supplementation.** They have distinct roles and should be considered separately.

### Absorption Considerations
- **Fat requirement:** K2 is fat-soluble and requires dietary fat for absorption. Taking with a meal containing fat increases absorption significantly. Oil-based softgels provide built-in fat for absorption.
- **Bile salts required:** Like all fat-soluble vitamins, K2 requires bile for micellar solubilization and absorption. Those with bile insufficiency (gallbladder removal, liver disease) may have impaired K2 absorption.
- **No known upper limit:** There is no established tolerable upper intake level (UL) for vitamin K2. Doses up to 45 mg/day of MK-4 and 360 mcg/day of MK-7 have been used in clinical trials without toxicity. K2 has an excellent safety profile.
- **Oil-based softgels or liquid drops** are the preferred delivery forms for maximum absorption.
- **Soy-free options:** For those avoiding soy, look for MK-7 derived from chickpea fermentation (Gnosis MenaQ7 soy-free) or synthetic MK-7.

### Quality and Purity
- **Trans-MK-7 is the bioactive form.** Some synthetic MK-7 products contain a mix of trans and cis isomers — only the trans form is bioactive. Look for products specifying "all-trans MK-7" or using the patented MenaQ7 (Gnosis/Kappa Bioscience) form.
- **Stability:** MK-7 can degrade in the presence of certain minerals (calcium, magnesium) in combination supplements. Standalone K2 supplements or quality combination products with stabilized MK-7 are preferred.
