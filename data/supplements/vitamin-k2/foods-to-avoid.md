# Vitamin K2
## Foods That Block Absorption

- **Hydrogenated and partially hydrogenated oils (trans fats):** Trans fats interfere with vitamin K metabolism. Dihydrophylloquinone (a byproduct of hydrogenation found in margarine and processed foods) competes with natural vitamin K forms for absorption and utilization but has little or no biological activity. High trans fat intake is associated with lower bone density and impaired K2 function.
- **Excessive alcohol:** Chronic alcohol consumption damages the liver and intestinal lining, impairing absorption and metabolism of all fat-soluble vitamins including K2. Alcohol also depletes bile salts needed for K2 absorption and disrupts the gut microbiome that produces some K2 forms.
- **Mineral oil (laxative):** Mineral oil dissolves fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K) in the gut and carries them out in the stool, preventing absorption. Do not take mineral oil laxatives with or near K2 supplementation. Separate by at least 4-6 hours.
- **Olestra (Olean):** A fat substitute that is not absorbed and carries fat-soluble vitamins out of the body. Found in some "fat-free" snack foods. Reduces K2 (and K1) absorption.
- **Very high-fiber meals at supplement time:** Extremely high fiber intake (>30g in a single meal) can bind bile salts and accelerate gut transit, reducing absorption time for fat-soluble vitamins. This is not a reason to avoid fiber in general — simply avoid taking K2 supplements with extremely high-fiber meals like large bran cereal servings.
- **Excessive phytosterol supplements:** Plant sterols/stanols used for cholesterol reduction (Benecol, CholestOff) can reduce absorption of fat-soluble vitamins when taken in excess. Standard doses are unlikely to be problematic, but those taking high-dose phytosterols should separate K2 supplementation by 2-3 hours.
- **Rancid oils and overheated cooking fats:** Oxidized lipids from degraded cooking oils increase oxidative stress and may impair fat-soluble vitamin function. Use fresh, high-quality fats when cooking and consuming K2-rich foods.

### Practical Guidance
- **K2 absorption is primarily limited by fat intake, not food avoidance.** The most important thing is to take K2 with adequate dietary fat.
- **Avoid trans fats and hydrogenated oils** as a general health practice — they interfere with K2 and have no health benefits.
- **If using mineral oil as a laxative** or fat-blocking agents (orlistat), separate K2 supplementation by several hours.
- **Focus on quality fats.** Grass-fed butter, olive oil, avocado, and coconut oil are optimal companions for K2 absorption.
