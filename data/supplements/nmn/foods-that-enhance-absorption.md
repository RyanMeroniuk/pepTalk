# NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide)
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

NMN is water-soluble and absorbs well on its own. The following foods and nutrients enhance NMN's effectiveness by supporting NAD+ metabolism and complementary pathways.

- **Methyl donors (critical for NAD+ metabolism):**
  - **Eggs** — rich in betaine and choline, which support methylation pathways needed to process nicotinamide (NAM), a byproduct of NAD+ consumption
  - **Beets** — high in betaine (trimethylglycine/TMG), which directly supports methylation. David Sinclair specifically recommends TMG alongside NMN
  - **Spinach and dark leafy greens** — folate (B9) supports the methylation cycle that clears nicotinamide
  - **Liver** — rich in B12, folate, and betaine — all methyl donors
- **B-vitamin-rich foods (NAD+ pathway cofactors):**
  - **Nutritional yeast** — B1, B2, B3, B6, B12 all support NAD+ metabolism
  - **Wild-caught salmon** — B vitamins plus omega-3s
  - **Legumes** — B vitamins plus protein
  - **Whole grains** — B1, B2, B3 support the NAD+ pathway
- **Resveratrol-containing foods (SIRT1 activation):**
  - **Red grapes (skin)** — resveratrol activates SIRT1, which requires NAD+ (provided by NMN) as a co-substrate. The combination is synergistic
  - **Blueberries and dark berries** — contain pterostilbene, a resveratrol analog with better bioavailability
  - **Dark chocolate** — contains polyphenols that activate sirtuin pathways
- **Fat-containing foods (for concurrent resveratrol):** If taking NMN with resveratrol, include dietary fat (yogurt, olive oil, nuts, avocado) to enhance resveratrol absorption. The NMN itself does not need fat
- **Tryptophan-rich foods:** Turkey, chicken, eggs, and dairy contain tryptophan, which feeds the de novo NAD+ synthesis pathway via the kynurenine pathway, complementing NMN's salvage pathway contribution
- **Fermented foods:** Kimchi, sauerkraut, yogurt, and kefir support gut health, which may improve NMN absorption in the small intestine
