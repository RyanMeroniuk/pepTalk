# NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide)
## Dosing Protocols

- **Starting dose:** 250mg/day for the first 1-2 weeks to assess tolerance
- **Standard maintenance dose:** 250-500mg/day for general NAD+ support and anti-aging
- **Optimal longevity dose:** 500-1000mg/day, based on extrapolation from animal data and emerging human trials. David Sinclair has publicly stated he takes 1000mg (1g) daily
- **Research-backed doses:** The Washington University insulin sensitivity trial used 250mg/day. Other human trials have used 250-1250mg/day with good safety profiles
- **Timing:** Take in the morning. NAD+ levels naturally peak in the morning and decline throughout the day. Morning dosing aligns with circadian NAD+ rhythms. NMN taken in the evening may disrupt sleep in some individuals by increasing cellular energy
- **Sublingual protocol:** Place NMN powder or tablet under the tongue. Hold for 1-3 minutes to allow absorption through the sublingual mucosa. Then swallow the remainder. This maximizes bioavailability
- **With or without food:** NMN is water-soluble and does not require food for absorption. Some practitioners prefer taking on an empty stomach for faster absorption. Others take with breakfast for convenience and to reduce any GI sensitivity
- **Split dosing (for higher doses):** If taking 500mg+, consider splitting into 250mg morning + 250mg midday. This maintains steadier NAD+ levels throughout the day
- **Cycling (debated):** Some longevity practitioners recommend cycling NMN (5 days on / 2 days off, or 3 weeks on / 1 week off) to prevent potential downregulation of NAMPT (the endogenous NAD+ synthesis enzyme). This is theoretical and not yet supported by clinical evidence. Most researchers take NMN daily without cycling
- **Combination with resveratrol:** Sinclair takes NMN + resveratrol together in the morning with yogurt (the fat in yogurt aids resveratrol absorption). This combination activates SIRT1 (resveratrol) while providing its fuel (NAD+ via NMN)
- **Onset of effects:** Blood NAD+ levels increase within hours of the first dose. Steady-state NAD+ elevation occurs within 1-2 weeks. Subjective benefits (energy, sleep, recovery) often noticed within 2-4 weeks. Deeper anti-aging effects require months to years of consistent use
