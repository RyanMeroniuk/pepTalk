# NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide)
## Natural Food Sources

NMN is found in various foods, though dietary amounts are far below supplemental doses.

- **Vegetables (highest concentrations):**
  - **Edamame (immature soybeans)** — 0.47-1.88mg per 100g
  - **Broccoli** — 0.25-1.12mg per 100g
  - **Cucumber (with skin)** — 0.56mg per 100g
  - **Cabbage** — 0.0-0.90mg per 100g
  - **Avocado** — 0.36-1.60mg per 100g
- **Fruits:**
  - **Tomato** — 0.26-0.30mg per 100g
  - **Mushrooms (raw)** — 0.0-1.01mg per 100g
- **Animal sources:**
  - **Raw beef** — 0.06-0.42mg per 100g
  - **Shrimp** — 0.22mg per 100g
- **Seeds and nuts:**
  - **Peanuts** — trace amounts detected
  - **Almonds** — trace amounts detected

**Important context:** The richest food source (edamame) provides roughly 1-2mg per 100g serving. To get a typical supplement dose of 250mg from edamame alone, you would need to eat approximately 12-25 kg (26-55 lbs) of edamame per day. Dietary sources contribute meaningful but insufficient NMN for therapeutic purposes. Supplementation is necessary to achieve clinically relevant NAD+ elevation.
