# NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide)
## Overview

NMN is a naturally occurring nucleotide and direct precursor to NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide), one of the most critical molecules in human biology. NAD+ is required for over 500 enzymatic reactions, including energy metabolism, DNA repair, gene expression regulation, and cellular stress responses. NAD+ levels decline dramatically with age — by age 50, levels may be half of what they were at age 20.

- **Why it matters:** NAD+ decline is now considered a fundamental hallmark of aging. Restoring NAD+ levels through NMN supplementation has emerged as one of the most promising anti-aging interventions in modern research
- **The David Sinclair connection:** Harvard geneticist David Sinclair popularized NMN through his research and book "Lifespan." His lab demonstrated that NMN supplementation in aged mice restored NAD+ levels, improved energy metabolism, enhanced insulin sensitivity, and reversed several markers of aging
- **NAD+ functions:** NAD+ is required for the Krebs cycle (energy production), serves as a substrate for sirtuins (longevity genes, SIRT1-7), fuels PARP enzymes (DNA repair), and regulates circadian rhythm through NAMPT expression
- **NMN vs NR debate:** NMN (nicotinamide mononucleotide) and NR (nicotinamide riboside) are both NAD+ precursors. NMN is one step closer to NAD+ in the biosynthetic pathway. NMN must be converted to NR to cross some cell membranes (via CD73), though a direct NMN transporter (Slc12a8) has been identified. Both effectively raise NAD+ levels; NMN may have advantages in certain tissues
- **Age-related NAD+ decline:** NAD+ levels drop approximately 50% between ages 20 and 50. This decline accelerates mitochondrial dysfunction, accumulates DNA damage, deactivates sirtuins, and impairs immune function
- **Broad therapeutic potential:** Beyond aging, NMN research shows promise for metabolic disease, neurodegeneration, cardiovascular health, fertility, and immune function
- **Stability concerns:** NMN can degrade in heat and humidity. Proper storage and high-quality sourcing are critical
