# NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide)
## Things to Avoid

- **Poor storage (critical):** NMN degrades in heat and moisture. Never leave NMN in a hot car, bathroom cabinet, or humid environment. Store in a cool, dry, dark place. Refrigeration is strongly recommended. Once opened, use within 2-3 months and keep the container sealed with desiccant
- **Buying low-quality NMN:** The NMN market has significant quality control issues. Some products contain far less NMN than labeled, or contain degradation products. Only buy from manufacturers that provide third-party certificates of analysis (CoA) showing >98% purity. Look for brands that use enzymatic synthesis rather than chemical synthesis
- **Ignoring methylation support:** NMN metabolism produces nicotinamide (NAM), which must be methylated for clearance. High-dose NMN without adequate methyl donors (TMG/betaine, B12, folate) can deplete the methylation pool. Supplement with TMG (500-1000mg) alongside NMN, especially at doses above 500mg/day
- **Evening dosing:** NMN increases NAD+ and cellular energy. Taking it in the evening can disrupt sleep onset. Always take in the morning, aligned with the natural circadian NAD+ peak
- **Expecting NMN to compensate for poor lifestyle:** NMN restores NAD+ but cannot override chronic sleep deprivation, excessive alcohol, sedentary behavior, or poor diet. These behaviors deplete NAD+ faster than NMN can replenish it
- **Taking NMN during active cancer treatment:** NAD+ fuels all cells, including cancer cells. While NMN also fuels DNA repair and tumor suppression, the interaction with active cancer is complex. Consult an oncologist before using NMN if you have active cancer
- **Combining with high-dose niacin (B3):** Excess niacin and NMN both produce nicotinamide, which at high levels inhibits SIRT1 — the opposite of the desired effect. Avoid high-dose niacin supplements (>100mg) alongside NMN
- **Chronic overtraining without recovery:** Intense exercise consumes NAD+ for energy and DNA repair. Without adequate recovery, chronic overtraining depletes NAD+ faster than NMN can restore it. Balance training with rest days
- **Mixing with hot beverages:** Dissolving NMN in hot coffee or tea can degrade it. If mixing with liquid, use cool or room-temperature water
- **Assuming NMN and NR are identical:** While both raise NAD+, their pharmacokinetics, tissue distribution, and transport mechanisms differ. NMN may preferentially elevate NAD+ in certain tissues (liver, gut via Slc12a8). They are complementary options, not interchangeable at every level
