# NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide)
## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Exercise (the most powerful NAD+ booster):** Exercise activates NAMPT, the rate-limiting enzyme in NAD+ synthesis. Regular exercise + NMN creates a powerful positive feedback loop. Include both aerobic exercise (builds mitochondrial NAD+ demand) and resistance training (activates AMPK and sirtuins). Aim for 150-300 min/week moderate cardio + 2-3 resistance sessions
- **Circadian alignment:** NAD+ levels are circadian-regulated. Support this by maintaining consistent sleep/wake times, getting morning sunlight within 30 minutes of waking, eating meals at regular times, and avoiding blue light 1-2 hours before bed. Take NMN in the morning to align with the natural NAD+ peak
- **Caloric restriction or time-restricted eating:** Caloric restriction and fasting activate sirtuins and AMPK — the same pathways NMN supports. A 16:8 intermittent fasting window amplifies NMN's effects. Even a 12:12 eating window is beneficial
- **Prioritize deep sleep:** NAD+ and sirtuin activity are regulated by sleep. Growth hormone secretion during deep sleep depends on adequate NAD+. Optimize sleep hygiene: cool room (65-68F), dark environment, consistent schedule, 7-9 hours total
- **Cold exposure:** Cold activates SIRT3 and AMPK, which are NAD+-dependent. Cold showers or plunges complement NMN by increasing demand for NAD+ in thermogenesis pathways
- **Limit alcohol strictly:** Alcohol is one of the single biggest NAD+ consumers. Even moderate drinking depletes NAD+ significantly. If you invest in NMN, minimizing alcohol maximizes return on that investment
- **Manage inflammation:** Chronic inflammation upregulates CD38, the enzyme that destroys NAD+. Anti-inflammatory practices (omega-3 intake, turmeric/curcumin, stress reduction, adequate sleep, exercise) reduce CD38 activity and preserve the NAD+ that NMN helps build
- **Supplement with TMG (betaine):** 500-1000mg TMG per day supports the methylation pathway needed to clear nicotinamide. This is particularly important at NMN doses above 500mg/day
- **Maintain gut health:** The Slc12a8 NMN transporter is expressed in the gut. A healthy gut lining optimizes NMN absorption. Include fermented foods, fiber, and consider probiotics
- **Sun protection without overdoing it:** UV radiation causes DNA damage that consumes NAD+ via PARP activation. Use reasonable sun protection while still getting adequate morning light for circadian rhythm
- **Cognitive challenges:** The brain consumes ~20% of the body's energy and is highly NAD+-dependent. Mental stimulation — learning, reading, strategic thinking — creates demand that NMN helps meet, supporting long-term cognitive health
