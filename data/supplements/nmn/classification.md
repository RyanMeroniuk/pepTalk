# NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide)
## Classification

- **Category:** NAD+ precursor / longevity compound / metabolic support
- **Supplement class:** Nucleotide (pyridine nucleotide precursor)
- **Also known as:** Nicotinamide mononucleotide, beta-NMN, beta-nicotinamide mononucleotide
- **Endogenous status:** Naturally produced in the body as an intermediate in NAD+ biosynthesis; also obtained from diet
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement in most countries. FDA status in the US became complicated in 2022 when a pharmaceutical company (Metro International Biotech) claimed NMN as a new drug under investigation, leading to FDA enforcement discretion concerns. Still widely sold as a supplement
- **Primary function:** Direct precursor to NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide), a coenzyme essential for energy metabolism, DNA repair, sirtuin activation, and hundreds of enzymatic reactions
- **Solubility:** Water-soluble
- **Key relationship:** NMN is one step away from NAD+ in the salvage pathway (NMN -> NAD+ via NMNAT enzymes)
