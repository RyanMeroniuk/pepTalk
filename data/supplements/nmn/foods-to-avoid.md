# NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide)
## Foods to Avoid

- **Excessive alcohol:** Alcohol metabolism massively consumes NAD+ (alcohol dehydrogenase and aldehyde dehydrogenase both require NAD+). Drinking alcohol while taking NMN directly counteracts the supplement's purpose by consuming the NAD+ you are trying to build. Heavy drinking can negate NMN supplementation entirely
- **High-fructose foods and beverages:** Excess fructose metabolism in the liver depletes NAD+ and generates uric acid. Sodas, fruit juices, candy, and foods with high-fructose corn syrup accelerate NAD+ consumption
- **Highly processed foods:** Processed foods increase inflammation and oxidative stress, which activate PARPs and CD38 — both of which consume NAD+. This creates a "NAD+ drain" that works against NMN supplementation
- **Excess refined carbohydrates:** Blood sugar spikes increase oxidative stress and NAD+ consumption. White bread, white rice, pastries, and sugary cereals all contribute to NAD+ depletion through metabolic stress
- **Charred and overcooked meats:** Heterocyclic amines and advanced glycation end products (AGEs) increase DNA damage, activating PARP enzymes that consume NAD+ for repair
- **Excessive saturated fat (from processed sources):** While healthy fats are fine, excess saturated fat from processed foods promotes inflammation that drives CD38 expression and NAD+ consumption
- **Energy drinks (excessive):** High sugar content plus stimulants increase metabolic stress and NAD+ turnover. If you need energy, NMN itself should help — energy drinks work against the goal
