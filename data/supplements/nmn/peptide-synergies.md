# NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide)
## Peptide Synergies

- **Epitalon (premier anti-aging synergy):** Epitalon activates telomerase to maintain telomere length. NMN fuels SIRT6, which is critical for telomere maintenance and DNA repair. Together they address two of the most important hallmarks of aging — NAD+ decline (NMN) and telomere attrition (Epitalon). This is considered one of the most promising longevity peptide-supplement combinations
- **MOTS-c (mitochondrial + NAD+ synergy):** MOTS-c activates AMPK and improves mitochondrial function. NMN provides the NAD+ that mitochondria require for energy production (as NADH in the ETC). MOTS-c optimizes the machinery, NMN provides the fuel
- **BPC-157 (repair and recovery):** DNA repair and tissue regeneration are intensely NAD+-dependent processes. BPC-157 stimulates healing pathways; NMN ensures the NAD+ pool can support the energy and DNA repair demands of tissue regeneration
- **GH secretagogues (CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin, Sermorelin, Tesamorelin):** Growth hormone drives anabolic processes that require NAD+ for energy metabolism and protein synthesis. NMN ensures NAD+ is not rate-limiting during GH-stimulated growth and repair
- **Thymosin Alpha-1 (immune longevity):** Immune function declines with age (immunosenescence), partly due to NAD+ decline in immune cells. NMN restores immune cell NAD+; Thymosin Alpha-1 activates T-cell function. Together they comprehensively rejuvenate immune capacity
- **Semax (cognitive + NAD+):** Semax upregulates BDNF for neuroplasticity. The brain's synaptic plasticity is NAD+-dependent (SIRT1 regulates synaptic gene expression). NMN ensures neurons have the NAD+ to support BDNF-driven neuroplasticity
- **DSIP / Epitalon (sleep + circadian):** NAD+ levels are circadian-regulated and critical for melatonin production. DSIP improves delta sleep; Epitalon supports melatonin synthesis. NMN provides the NAD+ that fuels the circadian clock machinery (SIRT1/CLOCK/BMAL1). Triple combination for comprehensive sleep and circadian support
- **Pinealon (neuroprotective stack):** Pinealon is a neuroprotective tripeptide targeting memory and circadian rhythm. NMN supports neuronal energy metabolism and circadian NAD+ cycling. Complementary mechanisms for brain health and cognitive preservation
- **Semaglutide / Tirzepatide (metabolic optimization):** GLP-1 agonists drive weight loss and metabolic improvement. NAD+ is required for fatty acid oxidation and metabolic flexibility. NMN ensures NAD+ levels support the increased metabolic demands during active weight loss
- **GHK-Cu (skin + longevity):** GHK-Cu promotes collagen synthesis and skin regeneration. NAD+-dependent sirtuins (particularly SIRT1) regulate fibroblast function and collagen gene expression. NMN + GHK-Cu addresses skin aging from both the energetic and structural perspectives
