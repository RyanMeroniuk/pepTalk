# NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide)
## Benefits

- **NAD+ restoration:** The primary benefit. NMN supplementation (250-1000mg/day) has been shown to increase blood NAD+ levels by 40-100%+ in human clinical trials within 2-4 weeks
- **Energy and physical performance:** Improved aerobic capacity, endurance, and muscle function in both animal and human studies. A 2022 study showed improved running endurance in recreational runners at 600mg/day
- **Insulin sensitivity and metabolic health:** Improved insulin sensitivity in prediabetic women at 250mg/day in a 10-week clinical trial (Washington University, 2021). NMN enhances glucose disposal and may reduce diabetes risk
- **Anti-aging (biological age):** Animal studies consistently show NMN reverses multiple markers of aging: gene expression, epigenetic patterns, mitochondrial function, and physical capacity. Human long-term aging studies are ongoing
- **Cardiovascular health:** Improves vascular endothelial function, reduces arterial stiffness, and lowers blood pressure in animal studies. Protects against ischemia-reperfusion injury. Human cardiovascular trials are underway
- **Neuroprotection:** Protects against age-related cognitive decline, Alzheimer's pathology, and neuronal ischemia in animal models. NAD+ is critical for neuronal energy metabolism and DNA repair in the brain
- **DNA repair enhancement:** By fueling PARP enzymes, NMN supports the body's ability to repair DNA damage from UV radiation, oxidative stress, and environmental toxins. This reduces cancer risk and slows aging
- **Immune function:** NAD+ is essential for immune cell metabolism and function. NMN supplementation restores immune cell NAD+ levels and improves macrophage and T-cell function in aged animal models
- **Fertility:** Restores oocyte quality and fertility in aged female mice. NAD+ is critical for oocyte maturation and embryonic development. Human fertility trials are ongoing
- **Eye health:** Protects retinal cells from degeneration. NAD+ decline in the retina contributes to age-related macular degeneration. NMN supplementation preserves retinal function in animal models
- **Weight management:** Suppresses age-related weight gain in mice without caloric restriction. Enhances fat metabolism through improved mitochondrial function and SIRT1/SIRT3 activation
- **Sleep quality:** Improves sleep quality through circadian rhythm support. NAD+ oscillation is integral to the molecular clock
