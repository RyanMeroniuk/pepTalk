# Boron
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

- **Boron is already extremely well absorbed:** This is the key point. More than ~85-90% of ingested boron is absorbed in the gut and circulated as boric acid regardless of food. Unlike iron, zinc, or fat-soluble vitamins, boron does not have meaningful absorption "enhancers" to optimize — uptake is near-complete on its own

- **Take with food for tolerability (not absorption):** Pairing boron with a meal helps prevent the mild GI upset some people get on an empty stomach. It does not meaningfully increase the already-high absorption

- **Adequate fluid intake:** Boron is cleared renally as boric acid. Normal hydration supports healthy clearance and is the main practical "co-factor" consideration

- **Synergistic nutrients (functional, not absorption-based):**
  - **Vitamin D3:** Boron extends the half-life and bioactivity of vitamin D, so the two are functionally synergistic for bone health. Pairing boron with vitamin-d3 leverages this relationship
  - **Magnesium and calcium:** Boron influences calcium and magnesium metabolism and reduces their urinary loss, supporting bone mineralization. Co-supplementing magnesium and calcium within a bone-health stack is complementary
  - **Vitamin K2:** In bone-health stacks, K2 directs calcium into bone; boron's mineral-sparing effect complements this

- **Boron-rich whole foods:** Eating boron-rich foods (raisins, prunes, avocado, almonds) provides boron in a natural matrix alongside other minerals and polyphenols — a sound dietary baseline before or alongside supplementation

### What Doesn't Matter
- **Specific timing relative to meals:** Because absorption is near-complete, timing is not critical. Consistency of daily intake matters more than precise timing
