# Boron
## Classification

- **Category:** Trace mineral (ultratrace element)
- **Subcategory:** Essential-candidate micronutrient / dietary mineral supplement
- **Chemical symbol:** B (atomic number 5); a metalloid
- **Common supplement forms:** Boron citrate, boron glycinate (bisglycinate/chelate), boron aspartate, sodium tetraborate/borax (food-grade), calcium fructoborate
- **Active species in the body:** Primarily absorbed and circulated as boric acid
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (USA, OTC); not classified as formally "essential" for humans but widely considered a beneficial ultratrace element; a tolerable upper intake level (UL) of 20 mg/day for adults is established
- **Primary systems:** Endocrine (free testosterone, SHBG, estradiol, vitamin D metabolism), skeletal (bone mineralization), mineral metabolism (calcium, magnesium)
- **Typical dietary intake:** Roughly 1-3 mg/day from a plant-rich diet (fruits, vegetables, nuts, legumes)
