# Boron
## Dosing Protocols

- **Testosterone / free-testosterone support:**
  - **Dose:** ~6-10 mg/day elemental boron (the well-known human study used ~10 mg/day)
  - **Timing:** Once daily, with or without food; morning is convenient
  - **Duration:** Hormonal changes (lower SHBG/estradiol, higher free testosterone) were observed within ~1 week in the cited study and accumulate with consistent use
  - **Note:** Stay at or below 10 mg/day for daily use; there is no proven benefit to higher doses for this purpose

- **Bone health / general support:**
  - **Dose:** 3-6 mg/day elemental boron
  - **Timing:** Once daily with a meal
  - **Note:** Often combined with calcium, magnesium, and vitamin D3 in bone-health protocols

- **Joint / anti-inflammatory (e.g., calcium fructoborate research):**
  - **Dose:** Roughly 3-6 mg/day elemental boron equivalent
  - **Duration:** Joint-comfort and inflammatory-marker changes reported over several weeks

- **General "insurance" dose:**
  - **Dose:** 3 mg/day, which simply ensures adequacy beyond typical dietary intake (~1-3 mg/day from a plant-rich diet)

- **Upper limit (important):**
  - **Do NOT exceed 20 mg/day** elemental boron (the established adult tolerable upper intake level). Most protocols stay at 3-10 mg/day, well below this
  - There is no reason to megadose boron; more does not yield proportionally more benefit and approaches the safety ceiling

- **Cycling considerations:**
  - No strict cycling requirement at sensible doses. Some users run boron continuously at 3-6 mg/day; others use 10 mg/day in shorter blocks for hormonal goals. Keeping daily intake modest avoids any need for aggressive cycling

- **With food:** Boron is highly absorbed either way. Taking it with a meal can improve tolerability for sensitive individuals
