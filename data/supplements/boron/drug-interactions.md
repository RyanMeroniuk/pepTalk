# Boron
## Drug Interactions

Boron has relatively few well-documented drug interactions at typical supplemental doses (3-10 mg/day). The main considerations are hormonal overlap, renal clearance, and a few absorption/level effects.

### Contraindications (Avoid / Physician Oversight Required)
- **Pregnancy and breastfeeding:** Contraindicated above normal dietary intake — high boron exposure is linked to reproductive and developmental toxicity in animal studies
- **Hormone-sensitive cancers and prostate conditions:** Contraindicated in active hormone-sensitive cancer without specialist input; use only under physician oversight given boron's free-testosterone-raising, estradiol-lowering effects
- **Hormone therapy / TRT:** Combining boron with testosterone replacement or other hormonal agents adds to androgenic effects and can complicate hormone monitoring. Coordinate with the prescribing clinician

### Caution / Monitor (Moderate)
- **Estrogen-based therapies and hormone therapy:** Boron may raise estradiol levels in some contexts and can interact with estrogen-containing medications/hormone therapy; monitor and discuss with a physician
- **Significant kidney disease / nephrotoxic drugs:** Boron is cleared renally as boric acid. Impaired kidney function (or drugs that stress the kidneys) may slow clearance and raise boron levels. Use caution and monitor
- **Blood pressure medications:** No strong interaction documented, but as with many mineral/hormonal supplements, monitor blood pressure when first combining
- **Antidepressants:** No well-characterized interaction; monitor generally if combining as part of a larger stack

### Timing Considerations
- **Magnesium and other minerals:** Boron influences calcium and magnesium metabolism. When taking multiple mineral supplements, spacing them across the day (rather than all at the same time) can help with tolerability and tracking total mineral load, though boron's own absorption is near-complete regardless
- **Separate large mineral doses** from boron by a few hours if GI tolerability is an issue

### Lab / Monitoring Notes
- **Hormone panels:** If pursuing testosterone goals, baseline and follow-up free testosterone, SHBG, and estradiol confirm the expected modest changes
- **Inform your physician** you take boron before procedures and when interpreting hormone or renal labs

### General Safety Notes
- At 3-10 mg/day, boron is well tolerated with few documented pharmacologic interactions
- The dominant safety levers are respecting the 20 mg/day upper limit, renal clearance in kidney disease, and the hormone-sensitive-condition caution — not classic drug-drug pharmacokinetics
