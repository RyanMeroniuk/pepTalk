# Boron
## Foods to Avoid

- **No specific foods block boron absorption:** Boron is absorbed at >85-90% regardless of diet, so there is no meaningful food that prevents its uptake. There is no "avoid" list driven by absorption interference

- **Excessive alcohol:** While alcoholic beverages contain some boron, heavy alcohol use lowers testosterone, raises cortisol, worsens sleep, and burdens the liver and kidneys — opposing boron's hormonal and bone-health goals. Moderate intake is fine; heavy drinking is counterproductive

- **High-sugar, ultra-processed diets:** These promote inflammation and metabolic dysfunction and tend to displace the fruit-, vegetable-, and nut-rich foods that supply dietary boron. They undermine the hormonal and bone benefits boron supports

- **Very low fruit/vegetable/nut intake (a dietary pattern, not a single food):** Such diets are low in boron. If you are not supplementing, a diet poor in produce and nuts can leave boron intake inadequate

### Behaviors, Not Foods
- **Megadosing toward the 20 mg/day ceiling:** Avoid pushing intake near or above the tolerable upper limit. There is no benefit and a real toxicity risk at high doses
- **Using industrial borax as a boron source:** Industrial/cleaning-grade borax is not for consumption. Use a standardized supplement with stated elemental boron content

### Generally Fine
- Boron-rich foods (raisins, prunes, avocado, almonds, legumes) are encouraged, not avoided
- Normal coffee intake actually contributes to dietary boron and is fine
