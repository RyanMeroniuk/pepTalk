# Boron
## Side Effects

### At Sensible Doses (3-10 mg/day) — Generally Very Well Tolerated
- Boron is one of the better-tolerated trace minerals at typical supplemental doses. Most people experience no noticeable side effects at 3-10 mg/day
- **Mild GI upset:** Occasional nausea or stomach discomfort, more likely on an empty stomach. Taking with food helps

### At High Doses / Toxicity (Well Above the 20 mg/day Upper Limit)
- Boron has a defined tolerable upper intake level of **20 mg/day** for adults. Exceeding this, especially substantially, can cause toxicity (boric acid poisoning) with symptoms such as:
  - Nausea, vomiting, and diarrhea
  - Headache and lethargy
  - Skin flushing or rash
  - Tremors and, at very high acute doses, more serious neurological effects
- **Practical point:** Toxicity is a concern with accidental overdose (e.g., ingesting industrial borax) or chronic megadosing, not with standard 3-10 mg/day supplementation

### Hormonal Considerations
- Because boron raises free testosterone and lowers estradiol/SHBG, the hormone-sensitive-condition caution applies (see contraindications). The hormonal effect is modest but real

### Contraindications
- **Pregnancy and breastfeeding:** Avoid supplemental boron above normal dietary intake. High boron exposure is associated with reproductive and developmental toxicity in animal studies; dietary amounts are fine but supplements should be avoided
- **Hormone-sensitive conditions:** Use caution given the free-testosterone-raising, estradiol-lowering effects; discuss with a physician if you have prostate cancer, prostate conditions, or hormone-sensitive cancers
- **Kidney impairment:** Boron is cleared renally. Those with significant kidney disease may clear it more slowly and should use caution and consult a physician

### Safety Profile Summary
- Very safe at the typical 3-10 mg/day range; toxicity is essentially a high-dose/overdose phenomenon
- The key rule is simply to respect the 20 mg/day upper limit and never use industrial borax as a supplement
