# Saffron Extract
## Dosing Protocols

### Standard Mood Protocol (Most Studied)
- **Dose:** 28-30mg/day of standardized saffron extract (e.g., affron 28mg), often as a single daily dose or split into two 14-15mg doses
- **Timing:** Consistent daily timing; with or without food
- **Duration:** Minimum 2-6 weeks before assessing mood effects; benefits build over the first 4-8 weeks
- **Notes:** This is the dose used in most positive mood trials, including those comparing favorably to low-dose antidepressants

### Sleep Protocol
- **Dose (2025 RCT):** 14mg twice daily (a 2025 randomized controlled trial used this split-dose schedule and found improved sleep quality)
- **Alternative:** ~28mg taken in the evening (1-2 hours before bed), used in earlier sleep studies
- **Duration:** 1-6 weeks; sleep-quality improvements build over time
- **Notes:** Saffron supports sleep via mood/calming pathways rather than heavy sedation; pairs well with melatonin and l-theanine

### Combination Mood-and-Sleep Stack
- **Saffron 14mg 2x/day (or 28mg in evening) + melatonin 0.5-3mg before bed + l-theanine 100-200mg in evening**
- **Notes:** Saffron supports mood and sleep quality, melatonin supports sleep timing/onset, and l-theanine promotes relaxation — a complementary stack for stress-related poor sleep

### PMS Protocol
- **Dose:** 30mg/day of standardized extract, often split into two doses
- **Duration:** Used cyclically or continuously; studies ran across menstrual cycles
- **Notes:** Shown to reduce PMS and premenstrual mood symptoms

### Titration & Tolerance
- **Standardized dose is small and well tolerated** — Less titration is needed than with high-dose supplements
- **With or without food** — Absorption is not strongly food-dependent
- **Do not exceed recommended doses** — Very high doses of saffron (well above supplemental ranges, e.g., multiple grams of the raw spice) can be toxic. Stick to the standardized 28-30mg/day supplemental range. Respect dosing discipline.

### Timing Considerations
- **For mood:** Consistent daily timing matters more than time of day; many take it in the morning
- **For sleep:** Evening dosing (or the 14mg twice-daily schedule with the second dose later in the day)
- **Consistency matters most** — Daily use is needed for cumulative mood and sleep benefits

### Duration & Expectations
- **2-6 weeks minimum** for mood; effects build over 4-8 weeks
- **1-6 weeks** for sleep-quality improvements
- **Ongoing use** is reasonable for sustained mood and sleep support
- Reassess periodically; for clinical depression or chronic insomnia, involve a healthcare professional
