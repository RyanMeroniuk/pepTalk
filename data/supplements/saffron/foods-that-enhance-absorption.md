# Saffron Extract
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

Saffron's active compounds (crocins, which convert to the well-absorbed crocetin, plus safranal) are reasonably absorbed orally. Food pairing is less about boosting absorption and more about supporting the serotonergic and sleep pathways saffron works through, and providing complementary antioxidant and mood-supporting nutrients.

### Dietary Fat (May Aid Carotenoid Absorption)
- Crocins and crocetin are carotenoids, and carotenoid absorption is generally enhanced by dietary fat
- Taking saffron extract with a meal containing some healthy fat (olive oil, avocado, nuts, fatty fish) may support absorption of its carotenoid actives
- **Practical fat sources:** olive oil, avocado, nuts/seeds, eggs

### Tryptophan-Rich Foods (Serotonin Pathway Support)
- Saffron enhances serotonin signaling; dietary tryptophan is the precursor to serotonin
- **Turkey, eggs, dairy, soy, pumpkin seeds** supply tryptophan, supporting the same pathway
- Pairing with complex carbohydrates aids tryptophan uptake into the brain

### Magnesium-Rich Foods (Relaxation & Sleep)
- Magnesium supports relaxation, GABA function, and sleep, complementing saffron's calming and sleep effects
- **Leafy greens, nuts, seeds, legumes, dark chocolate**

### Antioxidant & Anti-Inflammatory Foods
- Saffron's carotenoids are antioxidants; a diet rich in antioxidants and anti-inflammatory foods reinforces this and supports mood (given the inflammation-mood link)
- **Berries, colorful vegetables, olive oil, fatty fish (omega-3), green tea**

### Omega-3 Foods (Mood & Brain Health)
- **Fatty fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel)** provide EPA/DHA that support mood and brain health, complementing saffron's mood effects

### Foods to Pair for Sleep (Evening Dosing)
- A light evening snack with tryptophan and complex carbs, plus magnesium-rich foods, supports the sleep pathway when taking saffron in the evening
- **Tart cherries** (natural melatonin) complement saffron's sleep support and pair with melatonin supplementation

### What Doesn't Matter Much
- **Saffron extract absorbs adequately with or without food** — there is no strict requirement, though some fat may aid its carotenoid actives
- The small standardized dose is potent regardless of elaborate food pairing

### Practical Pairing Strategy
- **Mood:** Take saffron with a balanced meal containing some healthy fat; support the serotonin pathway with tryptophan-rich foods and omega-3
- **Sleep:** Take the evening dose with a light tryptophan/magnesium-containing snack; stack with melatonin and l-theanine
- Consistency of daily dosing matters more than precise food pairing
