# Saffron Extract
## Lifestyle Recommendations

### Prioritize Sleep Hygiene
- For sleep goals, saffron works best alongside good sleep habits: a consistent sleep/wake schedule, a dark and cool bedroom, limiting screens and bright light in the evening, and a wind-down routine. Saffron supports sleep quality, but it cannot override poor sleep hygiene.

### Minimize Alcohol & Late Caffeine
- Alcohol worsens mood and fragments sleep; late-day caffeine impairs sleep onset and can heighten anxiety. Minimizing both substantially amplifies saffron's mood and sleep benefits.

### Get Morning Sunlight & Daytime Light Exposure
- Morning light exposure anchors circadian rhythm and supports both mood and nighttime sleep, complementing saffron's serotonergic and sleep effects. Aim for natural light early in the day.

### Exercise Regularly
- Physical activity is one of the most effective mood interventions and improves sleep quality. Regular aerobic and resistance training complement saffron's mood and sleep benefits.

### Eat a Mood- and Sleep-Supportive Diet
- Emphasize whole foods, tryptophan-rich proteins (turkey, eggs, dairy, seeds), complex carbohydrates, magnesium-rich foods, omega-3 fish, and colorful antioxidant-rich produce. Reduce refined sugar, ultra-processed foods, and alcohol. This supports the serotonergic and antioxidant pathways saffron acts on.

### Build a Mood-and-Sleep Stack Thoughtfully
- Saffron pairs well with melatonin (supports sleep timing/onset) and l-theanine (promotes calm and relaxation) for sleep. Magnesium is a useful addition. Be cautious about combining multiple strong serotonergic agents (5-HTP, l-tryptophan) without guidance.

### Manage Stress
- Chronic stress undermines mood and sleep. Incorporate stress-management practices (breathwork, meditation, time in nature). Saffron's HPA-axis-modulating and calming effects complement active stress management.

### Be Consistent and Patient
- Saffron's mood benefits build over 2-6+ weeks and sleep benefits over 1-6 weeks. Take it consistently at the studied dose. For sleep, the 14mg-twice-daily or evening-dose schedule works; for mood, consistent daily dosing matters most.

### Respect the Dose
- Use the standardized ~28-30mg/day dose. Do not megadose — more is not better and high doses are toxic. Dosing discipline is part of using saffron well.

### Involve a Professional for Significant Conditions
- For clinical depression, chronic insomnia, or if you take psychiatric medication, coordinate saffron use with a healthcare professional. It is a supportive supplement, not a replacement for appropriate care.
