# Saffron Extract
## Classification

- **Category:** Mood Support / Sleep / Nootropic / Botanical Extract
- **Type:** Standardized botanical extract from the stigmas of the saffron crocus flower
- **Botanical source:** Crocus sativus L. (the saffron crocus); the dried stigmas are the spice and source of the extract
- **Key bioactive compounds:** Crocins and crocetin (carotenoids responsible for color and many effects), safranal (the aroma compound), and picrocrocin (taste); these are the standardized actives in supplements
- **Standardized/branded forms:** affron (a leading clinically studied standardized extract), Satiereal, and others standardized to a defined content of Lepticrosalides/crocins and safranal
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (OTC); also a culinary spice (one of the most expensive spices by weight). GRAS as a spice.
- **Primary function:** Supports mood and emotional well-being and improves sleep quality, primarily by enhancing serotonin signaling
- **Primary systems:** Nervous/CNS (serotonergic and dopaminergic signaling, mood, sleep), with antioxidant activity from its carotenoids
- **Key distinction:** Supplement efficacy depends on a standardized extract (e.g., affron) at a clinically studied dose — culinary saffron is not a reliable substitute for the studied effects.
