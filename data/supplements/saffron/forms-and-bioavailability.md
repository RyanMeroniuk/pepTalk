# Saffron Extract
## Forms and Bioavailability

### Standardized Branded Extracts (Recommended)
- **affron** — The leading clinically studied standardized saffron extract, standardized to a defined content of Lepticrosalides (a complex of crocins, safranal, and other actives). Used in many of the positive mood and sleep trials at ~28mg/day (or 14mg twice daily). The most evidence-backed choice.
- **Satiereal** — A standardized extract studied primarily for snacking/appetite and mood.
- **Affron and similar extracts** — Standardization ensures a consistent, clinically relevant dose of the active compounds, which is essential because the effective dose is small (~28-30mg) and active content varies widely in non-standardized products.
- **Bottom line:** Choose a standardized branded extract at the studied dose; this is the single most important quality factor for saffron.

### Generic/Non-Standardized Saffron Extract
- **Variable potency** — Without standardization, the content of crocins and safranal is unknown, making the effective dose unpredictable
- **Verdict:** Less reliable; the clinical evidence is tied to standardized extracts

### Culinary Saffron (The Spice)
- **Not a reliable substitute** — While saffron the spice contains the same bioactives, achieving a consistent ~28-30mg standardized-extract-equivalent dose through cooking is impractical and unreliable. Also, saffron is extremely expensive by weight.
- **Verdict:** Use a standardized extract supplement for the studied mood/sleep effects, not culinary saffron

### Forms Available
- **Capsules/tablets** — The standard form, ideal for the small precise dose
- **Combination products** — Saffron is often combined with other mood/sleep ingredients (e.g., melatonin, l-theanine, magnesium); verify the saffron extract is a standardized one at an effective dose, not a token amount

### Bioavailability
- **Oral absorption of actives** — Crocins are partly hydrolyzed in the gut to crocetin, which is more readily absorbed and crosses the blood-brain barrier; safranal is also absorbed. The body converts and distributes the active carotenoids to relevant tissues including the CNS.
- **Crocetin's role** — Crocetin (the absorbed metabolite of crocins) is thought to mediate much of the central activity, given its better absorption and BBB penetration
- **Small effective dose** — Because the standardized actives are potent, the effective dose is small (~28-30mg of extract), unlike many supplements requiring grams

### Quality Considerations
- Choose products specifying a standardized branded extract (e.g., affron) and the active content
- Beware adulteration — saffron is a frequently adulterated/counterfeited commodity; standardized branded extracts with third-party testing reduce this risk
- Verify the actual extract dose in combination products
