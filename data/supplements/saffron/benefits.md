# Saffron Extract
## Benefits

### Mood & Emotional Well-Being (Strongest Evidence)
- **Improved mild-to-moderate low mood** — Multiple randomized controlled trials and meta-analyses show standardized saffron extract (28-30mg/day) improves depressive symptoms versus placebo.
- **Comparable to low-dose antidepressants in some trials** — Head-to-head studies have found standardized saffron achieving symptom improvement comparable to low doses of conventional antidepressants (e.g., fluoxetine, imipramine) in mild-to-moderate cases, generally with fewer side effects. (Saffron is a supportive supplement, not a replacement for prescribed treatment of clinical depression.)
- **Emotional well-being in non-clinical populations** — Studies in healthy adults with low mood show improved mood, reduced stress, and better emotional balance.
- **Adjunctive use** — Some research explores saffron alongside conventional antidepressants; this requires medical oversight due to serotonergic overlap.

### Sleep Quality
- **Improved sleep quality** — A 2025 randomized controlled trial found saffron extract at 14mg twice daily improved sleep quality. Earlier trials with standardized extracts (e.g., affron ~28mg before bed) showed improved sleep quality and ease of falling asleep.
- **Better sleep without sedation** — Saffron tends to improve sleep via mood and calming pathways rather than heavy sedation, making it useful for those whose poor sleep is linked to stress or low mood.

### Anxiety & Stress (Supportive)
- **Reduced anxiety and stress** — Several trials report reductions in anxiety and perceived stress, consistent with saffron's serotonergic and HPA-axis-modulating effects.

### PMS & Mood-Related Premenstrual Symptoms
- **PMS symptom relief** — Saffron has shown benefit for premenstrual syndrome symptoms, including mood-related symptoms, in controlled studies.

### Appetite & Snacking (Satiereal)
- **Reduced snacking** — The Satiereal saffron extract has shown reductions in snacking and modest support for weight management in some studies, possibly via mood-related eating. Evidence is modest.

### Eye Health (Emerging)
- **Retinal/macular support** — Crocins and crocetin (carotenoid antioxidants) are studied for age-related macular degeneration and retinal protection, with some encouraging early results. Flagged as emerging.

### Cognitive & Other (Preliminary)
- **Cognitive support** — Preliminary research explores saffron in age-related cognitive decline and mild cognitive impairment via its antioxidant and neurotrophic effects. Preliminary.
- **Metabolic markers** — Some studies note modest improvements in metabolic markers; evidence is limited.

### Realistic Expectations
- Saffron's best-supported uses are mood and sleep, with effects building over 2-6+ weeks of consistent use.
- It is a supportive supplement; clinical depression and chronic insomnia warrant professional care. Saffron pairs well with melatonin and l-theanine for sleep and calm.
