# Shilajit
## Peptide Synergies

> Note: All pairings assume a purified, third-party-tested shilajit product. Contaminated shilajit is unsafe in any combination.

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### Kisspeptin + Shilajit (Endogenous Testosterone & Energy)
- **Rationale:** Kisspeptin stimulates the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal (HPG) axis to drive the body's own testosterone production. Shilajit contributes modest peripheral testosterone/DHEA-S support plus mitochondrial energy via fulvic acid and DBPs. Together they support endogenous androgen production while improving the cellular energy backdrop.
- **Protocol:** Purified shilajit 250-500 mg/day in the morning + kisspeptin per protocol
- **Best for:** Men optimizing natural testosterone and energy without exogenous hormone

#### MOTS-c + Shilajit (Mitochondrial Stack)
- **Rationale:** MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-derived peptide acting as an exercise mimetic that activates AMPK and improves metabolic/mitochondrial function. Shilajit's fulvic acid and DBPs support mitochondrial bioenergetics and may help recycle CoQ10. Both target the mitochondria from complementary angles, making this a coherent energy/longevity-oriented pairing.
- **Protocol:** Purified shilajit 250-500 mg/day + MOTS-c at standard dosing
- **Best for:** Mitochondrial/metabolic optimization, anti-fatigue, endurance-focused protocols

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + Shilajit (Recovery & Vitality)
- **Rationale:** GH secretagogues raise GH and IGF-1 for recovery and body composition; shilajit supports the mitochondrial energy and modest androgen tone that underpin recovery and vitality. Better sleep and recovery also support overnight testosterone production.
- **Protocol:** Purified shilajit 250-500 mg morning + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Best for:** Recovery, age-related vitality decline, energy

#### PT-141 (Bremelanotide) + Shilajit (Libido Support)
- **Rationale:** PT-141 drives acute, central sexual arousal via melanocortin receptors. Shilajit contributes a modest hormonal/energy foundation (testosterone support and anti-fatigue). The combination addresses both the acute neurological and the slower hormonal/energy contributors to libido.
- **Protocol:** Purified shilajit 250-500 mg/day ongoing + PT-141 as needed before activity
- **Best for:** Libido with a fatigue or low-energy component

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### MK-677 (Ibutamoren) + Shilajit
- **Rationale:** MK-677 raises GH/IGF-1 for muscle and recovery; shilajit adds mitochondrial energy and modest androgen support. Complementary for vitality and recomposition protocols.
- **Protocol:** Purified shilajit 250-500 mg/day + MK-677 10-25 mg before bed
- **Best for:** Recovery, recomposition, energy

#### Tesamorelin + Shilajit
- **Rationale:** Tesamorelin reduces visceral fat via GH; shilajit supports mitochondrial energy and modest testosterone tone. Reasonable for middle-aged men pursuing fat loss with energy support.
- **Protocol:** Purified shilajit 250-500 mg/day + Tesamorelin at standard dosing
- **Best for:** Visceral fat reduction with energy and vitality goals

#### IGF-1 LR3 + Shilajit
- **Rationale:** IGF-1 LR3 drives hypertrophy directly; shilajit supports the mitochondrial/energy environment for training and recovery. Complementary rather than overlapping.
- **Protocol:** Purified shilajit 250-500 mg/day + IGF-1 LR3 at standard dosing with resistance training
- **Best for:** Advanced muscle-building protocols (with appropriate IGF-1 caution)

### Supplement Stacking (Testosterone & Energy Cluster)
- **Shilajit + CoQ10:** The most mechanistically coherent supplement pairing — shilajit's DBPs are proposed to help recycle/preserve active CoQ10, so combining the two reinforces the mitochondrial-energy rationale.
- **Shilajit + Tongkat Ali:** Tongkat Ali lowers SHBG to raise free testosterone while shilajit offers modest total-testosterone/DHEA-S support and energy — complementary angles within the testosterone cluster.
- **Shilajit + Boron:** Boron lowers SHBG and estradiol and raises free testosterone, complementing shilajit's modest hormonal support.
- **Shilajit + Fadogia Agrestis:** Sometimes combined in "natural T" stacks (fadogia-agrestis targets the LH/testosterone pathway). IMPORTANT: fadogia-agrestis has rodent testicular-toxicity signals and almost no human safety data — review its entry before stacking.
- **Mineral-load caution:** Because shilajit is mineral-dense, be mindful of total mineral/iron intake when stacking with other mineral supplements.
