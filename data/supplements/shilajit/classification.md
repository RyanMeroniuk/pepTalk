# Shilajit
## Classification

- **Category:** Mineral-rich resinous exudate / Ayurvedic rasayana (rejuvenator)
- **Subcategory:** Fulvic-acid and humic-substance supplement; trace-mineral complex
- **Source:** A blackish-brown tar-like exudate that seeps from rocks in high mountain ranges (notably the Himalayas, Altai, Caucasus, and Andes), formed over centuries from the slow decomposition of plant and microbial matter compressed in rock
- **Primary active compounds:** Fulvic acid, humic acid, dibenzo-alpha-pyrones (DBPs/urolithins), and a broad spectrum of trace minerals (e.g., iron, zinc, magnesium, selenium)
- **Standardized form:** PrimaVie is a clinically studied, purified shilajit extract standardized for fulvic acid and DBP content
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (USA, OTC); traditional Ayurvedic medicine ingredient; not FDA-approved as a drug
- **Primary systems:** Mitochondrial energy/bioenergetics, endocrine (modest testosterone support), as a mineral/fulvic-acid delivery vehicle
- **CRITICAL safety note:** Raw or unpurified shilajit carries a real risk of heavy-metal contamination (lead, mercury, arsenic) and mycotoxins. Only purified, standardized, third-party-tested products (e.g., PrimaVie) should be used — see side effects and things-to-avoid
