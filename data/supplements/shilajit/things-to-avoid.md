# Shilajit
## Things to Avoid

- **Raw, "wild-harvested," or unpurified shilajit (the #1 rule):** This is the most dangerous form. Shilajit concentrates whatever is in its rock environment, so raw material frequently contains hazardous levels of lead, mercury, and arsenic, plus possible mycotoxins. "Authentic-looking" is not safe. Only use purified, standardized, third-party-tested products with a certificate of analysis (e.g., PrimaVie)
- **Products without a heavy-metal certificate of analysis:** If a product does not publish third-party testing for lead, mercury, and arsenic, do not consume it. This is non-negotiable for shilajit specifically
- **Trusting vague sourcing claims:** "Himalayan," "pure," "ancient," and "traditional" are marketing terms, not safety guarantees. Demand standardization (fulvic-acid content) and lab testing
- **Megadosing:** Exceeding recommended amounts provides no proven added benefit and increases cumulative mineral and any residual heavy-metal exposure
- **Use during pregnancy or breastfeeding:** Avoid entirely — the heavy-metal risk alone is disqualifying, compounded by hormonal activity and lack of safety data
- **Use with iron-overload conditions (hemochromatosis):** Shilajit's iron content can worsen iron overload. Avoid
- **Use with gout / high uric acid without caution:** Shilajit may raise uric acid
- **Stacking blindly with other mineral supplements:** Account for total mineral/iron load when combining with multivitamins, iron, or other mineral products
- **Using with hormone-sensitive conditions without medical oversight:** Given modest testosterone/DHEA-S effects, anyone with prostate cancer, prostate conditions, or hormone-sensitive cancers should consult a physician
- **Expecting dramatic results:** Shilajit's evidence base is small; benefits (energy, modest testosterone support) are modest and accumulate over weeks. It is a supportive supplement, not a powerful hormonal agent
- **Combining multiple new testosterone-support items at once:** Adding shilajit together with tongkat-ali, boron, and fadogia-agrestis simultaneously makes it impossible to attribute effects or side effects — and fadogia-agrestis in particular carries serious toxicity concerns. Introduce one variable at a time
