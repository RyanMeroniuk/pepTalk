# Shilajit
## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Source quality is a lifestyle decision, not an afterthought:** With shilajit, the most consequential "lifestyle" choice is committing to only purified, third-party-tested products. Build the habit of checking for a heavy-metal certificate of analysis before buying — this single practice eliminates the dominant risk
- **Prioritize sleep:** Quality sleep supports both the mitochondrial recovery and the testosterone production that shilajit is meant to support. Energy and hormonal benefits are amplified by 7-9 hours of good sleep
- **Pair with mitochondrial-supportive habits:** Shilajit's core mechanism is bioenergetic. Aerobic exercise, zone-2 cardio, and adequate recovery improve mitochondrial function and complement shilajit's proposed energy support. CoQ10 supplementation pairs mechanistically (CoQ10-recycling rationale)
- **Resistance training (for testosterone goals):** If using shilajit for its modest testosterone support, combine with resistance training, which is the most reliable lifestyle lever for androgen status and body composition
- **Support the trace-mineral picture with diet, not megadoses:** Eat a varied whole-food diet (meat, shellfish, legumes, nuts, leafy greens) for minerals. Use shilajit as an adjunct, and avoid stacking it with high-dose mineral/iron supplements that could push total intake too high
- **Manage stress:** As a traditional rasayana/tonic, shilajit is used in the context of vitality and resilience. Behavioral stress management (breathwork, time outdoors, sunlight) supports the same goals
- **Moderate alcohol:** Heavy drinking opposes shilajit's energy and hormonal benefits and burdens the liver, which handles clearance
- **Monitor if pursuing hormonal goals:** Consider baseline and follow-up testosterone/free-testosterone labs to confirm whether the modest expected changes are occurring
- **Hydrate well:** Dissolving purified shilajit in warm water and maintaining good overall hydration supports general energy and the mineral-transport functions of fulvic acid
