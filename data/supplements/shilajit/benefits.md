# Shilajit
## Benefits

- **Energy and anti-fatigue (best-characterized claim):** Shilajit's traditional reputation and its proposed mitochondrial/fulvic-acid mechanism center on reducing fatigue and supporting cellular energy. Preliminary human and animal data suggest improved exercise capacity and reduced fatigue, though large rigorous trials are limited
- **Mitochondrial / bioenergetic support:** Via fulvic acid and DBPs, shilajit is proposed to support ATP production and protect mitochondrial function, which is the unifying theme behind its energy, recovery, and longevity claims
- **Modest testosterone support:** A small trial of purified shilajit (PrimaVie) in healthy men reported increases in total and free testosterone and DHEA-S over ~90 days. The effect is modest and the evidence base is thin — shilajit is a supportive, not a primary, testosterone agent
- **Male fertility / sperm parameters:** Some research reports improved sperm count and motility, likely through antioxidant protection of reproductive tissue
- **Trace-mineral and fulvic-acid delivery:** Provides a broad spectrum of trace minerals in a fulvic-acid matrix that may aid mineral transport. (This benefit is inseparable from the contamination risk — only purified products deliver it safely)
- **Antioxidant effects:** Fulvic acid scavenges free radicals, which may contribute to recovery, anti-fatigue, and reproductive benefits
- **Altitude / endurance (preliminary):** Traditionally used in mountain populations for stamina and altitude tolerance; some preclinical support for endurance and oxygen utilization, but human data is limited
- **Cognitive / neuroprotective interest (very preliminary):** In-vitro work on fulvic acid and tau aggregation has generated neuroprotection interest, but this is far from established in humans

### Realistic Expectations
- Shilajit is best framed as a mitochondrial-energy and trace-mineral support supplement with *modest* adjunctive testosterone support
- The evidence base is smaller and weaker than for better-studied supplements; benefits are real but modest, and entirely dependent on using a purified, contaminant-free product
