# Shilajit
## Dosing Protocols

- **General energy / testosterone support (purified extract):**
  - **Dose:** 250-500 mg/day of purified, standardized shilajit (e.g., PrimaVie). The testosterone trial used 250 mg twice daily (500 mg/day) in healthy men
  - **Timing:** Morning, or split morning and early afternoon; with or without food
  - **Duration:** The testosterone trial ran ~90 days; energy/anti-fatigue effects may be noticed sooner but accumulate over weeks

- **Purified resin form:**
  - **Dose:** Typically a rice-grain to pea-sized amount (roughly 300-500 mg), dissolved in warm water or milk
  - **Note:** Resin dosing is harder to standardize than capsules; follow the reputable manufacturer's instructions and confirm third-party heavy-metal testing

- **Mitochondrial / endurance support:**
  - **Dose:** 250-500 mg/day purified extract
  - **Timing:** Morning or pre-activity
  - **Note:** Often paired with CoQ10 given the proposed CoQ10-recycling mechanism

- **Cycling considerations:**
  - No strict cycling requirement, but because shilajit is mineral-dense and carries a (managed) heavy-metal consideration even in purified products, conservative use — staying at recommended doses and taking periodic breaks — is reasonable rather than indefinite megadosing
  - Avoid stacking shilajit with other heavy mineral/iron supplements without attention to total mineral load

- **With food:** Can be taken with or without food. Some people prefer with food to reduce any GI upset; the traditional method is dissolved in warm milk or water

- **CRITICAL dosing caveat:** Any dosing guidance assumes a *purified, third-party-tested* product. Raw or unpurified shilajit should not be dosed at all because the heavy-metal content is unknown and potentially dangerous. Never exceed manufacturer-recommended amounts to "boost" effects — this also increases cumulative heavy-metal exposure
