# Shilajit
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

- **Warm water or warm milk (traditional vehicle):** The classic Ayurvedic method dissolves purified shilajit resin in warm water or warm milk. Warmth aids dissolution of the resin, and milk fat/protein may help with the fat-associated DBP fraction. This is more about palatability and dissolution than dramatic bioavailability enhancement

- **Taken with food to reduce GI upset:** Fulvic acid is water-soluble and well absorbed, but taking shilajit with a meal can reduce the mild nausea some people experience on an empty stomach

- **CoQ10 (functional pairing):** Shilajit's DBPs are proposed to help recycle/preserve CoQ10 in its active form. Co-supplementing CoQ10 leverages this proposed mitochondrial mechanism, making CoQ10 a natural complement rather than strictly an absorption enhancer

- **Vitamin C and antioxidant-rich foods:** A diet rich in antioxidants complements shilajit's own antioxidant/anti-fatigue mechanism and supports the reproductive/Leydig-cell protection associated with its modest testosterone effects

- **Adequate dietary fat and protein:** Supports the overall hormonal environment if using shilajit for testosterone support; cholesterol is the precursor to testosterone

### What to Be Careful About (Not Enhancers — Cautions)
- **Don't pair with additional iron supplements casually:** Shilajit already supplies iron and other minerals. Stacking with iron or many mineral supplements can push total mineral/iron load too high, especially in those with iron-overload risk
- **Fulvic acid is a mineral carrier:** This means it can enhance uptake of minerals taken alongside it — beneficial for desired minerals, but another reason a *contaminated* product is dangerous, since it would also help carry heavy metals into the body

### What Doesn't Matter Much
- **Exact timing relative to meals:** Consistency of daily dosing matters more than precise meal timing. Morning dosing is typical
