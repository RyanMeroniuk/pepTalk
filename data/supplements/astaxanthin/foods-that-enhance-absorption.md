# Astaxanthin
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

Astaxanthin is fat-soluble, so dietary fat is by far the most important factor for absorption.

- **Dietary fat (the key enhancer):** Taking astaxanthin with a meal containing fat dramatically increases its absorption — potentially several-fold versus a fasted state. Healthy fats like olive oil, avocado, nuts, eggs, and fatty fish all serve this role. Never take astaxanthin on an empty stomach if you want maximal absorption
- **Omega-3 fatty acids (fish oil / krill oil):** Pairing astaxanthin with omega-3s provides both a fat carrier for absorption and a complementary lipid-phase nutrient. Krill oil naturally contains both. This is a convenient and synergistic pairing for membrane and cardiovascular health
- **Fatty fish meals:** Salmon, mackerel, and sardines provide fat for absorption plus their own astaxanthin and omega-3 content
- **Avocado and olive oil:** Monounsaturated-fat foods that improve carotenoid absorption broadly — the same principle that boosts lutein, zeaxanthin, and beta-carotene uptake
- **Eggs:** The fat and phospholipids in egg yolk improve carotenoid absorption; eggs are also a vehicle for lutein/zeaxanthin, making them a good co-meal for eye-health stacks
- **Other carotenoid-rich foods (eaten with fat):** Because carotenoids share absorption pathways, a varied carotenoid-rich, fat-containing meal supports overall carotenoid status, though very high doses of one carotenoid can compete with another for uptake

### Practical Pairing Strategy
- **Simplest approach:** Take your astaxanthin softgel with your largest fat-containing meal of the day
- **Eye/skin stack:** Astaxanthin + lutein-zeaxanthin + omega-3, all taken with a fatty meal, covers complementary mechanisms and tissue sites
- **Convenience option:** A krill-oil product delivers astaxanthin, omega-3, and a natural fat carrier in one
- **What to avoid:** Taking it fasted or with a fat-free meal markedly reduces absorption
