# Astaxanthin
## Dosing Protocols

### General Antioxidant / Health Support
- **Dose:** 4-6 mg/day
- **Timing:** With a fat-containing meal (greatly improves absorption)
- **Duration:** Ongoing; benefits accrue over weeks of consistent use

### Eye Fatigue / Screen Use
- **Dose:** 6-12 mg/day (trials commonly used 6 mg or 12 mg)
- **Timing:** With a fatty meal, daily
- **Duration:** 4 weeks or more to assess effect on accommodation and eye strain

### Skin Health / Photoprotection
- **Dose:** 4-6 mg/day (some skin trials used 6 mg; up to 12 mg studied)
- **Timing:** With a fatty meal
- **Duration:** 8-12 weeks for visible changes in elasticity, hydration, and UV resilience
- **Notes:** Often combined with topical astaxanthin for skin-specific goals

### Endurance / Exercise Support
- **Dose:** 4-12 mg/day (commonly 4-6 mg)
- **Timing:** Daily with food; not dependent on workout timing
- **Notes:** Effects on performance are modest and mixed; oxidative-stress reduction is more consistently observed

### Cardiovascular / Lipid Support
- **Dose:** 6-12 mg/day
- **Notes:** Higher end of the range used in lipid and antioxidant cardiovascular studies

### Practical Guidance
- **Always take with dietary fat** — astaxanthin is fat-soluble and absorption is markedly higher with a fatty meal. Pairing with omega-3 (fish oil) or a krill-oil product is convenient
- **Consistency over timing** — a single daily dose with the largest fat-containing meal works well; morning or evening does not meaningfully change outcomes
- **Be patient** — tissue accumulation and benefits (especially eye and skin) emerge over weeks, not days
- **Typical range:** Most supplement use sits at 4-12 mg/day. Doses above 12 mg are sometimes used but offer little added evidence for general goals
- **Combine sensibly:** Astaxanthin, lutein-zeaxanthin, and omega-3 are complementary for eye and skin protection (different mechanisms and tissue locations)
