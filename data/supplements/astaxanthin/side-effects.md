# Astaxanthin
## Side Effects

### Common (Generally Well Tolerated)
- **Excellent tolerability** — astaxanthin is one of the better-tolerated antioxidant supplements; most users report no side effects at typical doses
- **Mild GI effects** — occasional stomach upset, especially if taken without food

### Uncommon
- **Skin reddening / increased pigmentation** — at high doses, the deep red pigment can theoretically contribute to a slight skin tone change; uncommon at normal doses
- **Lower blood pressure** — astaxanthin may modestly lower blood pressure, relevant for those on antihypertensives
- **Hormonal effects (theoretical, high dose)** — some discussion of effects on hormone metabolism (e.g., DHT, estrogen) at high doses, but human evidence is limited and inconclusive

### Rare
- **Allergic reaction** — rare; those with allergies to algae or seafood-derived products should choose sources carefully (krill-derived astaxanthin is shellfish-derived)

### Theoretical / Context-Dependent
- **Antioxidant timing around training** — like other strong antioxidants, very high doses immediately around exercise could theoretically blunt some adaptive oxidative signaling; effect appears smaller than with megadose vitamin C/E and is not well established for astaxanthin
- **Blood-thinning effect** — mild antioxidant/anti-inflammatory action could marginally affect platelet behavior; relevant with anticoagulants

### Safety Profile Summary
- **Strong safety record** — natural astaxanthin has GRAS status and a long history of dietary exposure (it is abundant in salmon and seafood)
- **No serious adverse effects** reported at typical supplemental doses (4-12 mg/day) in human trials
- **Shellfish/algae allergies** — choose source accordingly (Haematococcus algae vs krill)
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding** — insufficient supplemental safety data; dietary amounts from food are fine, but discuss supplemental doses with a physician
- **None of this is medical advice** — consult a healthcare provider if on medication or managing a health condition
