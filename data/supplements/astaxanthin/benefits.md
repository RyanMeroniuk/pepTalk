# Astaxanthin
## Benefits

### Eye Health & Fatigue (Among the Strongest Evidence)
- **Reduces eye strain and accommodation fatigue** — multiple Japanese trials at 6-12 mg/day report less eye fatigue, better accommodation, and reduced visual discomfort in screen and VDT users
- **Supports retinal protection** — crosses the blood-retinal barrier to quench singlet oxygen and protect light-exposed retinal tissue
- **Eases asthenopia symptoms** — reduced blurring, dryness, and tired-eye sensations in some studies

### Skin Health & Photoprotection
- **Reduces UV-induced skin damage** — oral astaxanthin raises the threshold for UV-induced redness and protects against photoaging
- **Improves skin elasticity and hydration** — combined oral + topical use in trials improved moisture, elasticity, and fine-line appearance
- **Reduces age spots and roughness** — antioxidant protection of skin lipids and collagen contributes to a smoother appearance

### Endurance & Exercise (Mixed but Promising)
- **Lowers exercise-induced oxidative stress** — reduces markers of lipid peroxidation after intense exercise
- **May support endurance** — some studies report improved time-to-exhaustion or fat utilization; results are inconsistent and effect sizes modest
- **Supports recovery** — anti-inflammatory effects may aid recovery from training stress

### Cardiovascular Health
- **Protects LDL from oxidation** — a relevant antioxidant effect for vascular health
- **Improves lipid profile in some trials** — modest improvements in HDL and triglycerides at higher doses
- **Supports endothelial function and blood flow** — through antioxidant and anti-inflammatory mechanisms

### Brain & Cognitive (Emerging)
- **Crosses the blood-brain barrier** — delivers antioxidant protection to neural tissue
- **Studied for cognitive support** — small trials suggest possible benefits for cognition in older adults; evidence is early

### General Antioxidant & Anti-Inflammatory
- **Potent singlet-oxygen quencher** — among the most efficient natural antioxidants for this reactive species
- **Lowers systemic inflammation markers** — reductions in CRP and other markers in some studies

### Honest Framing
- The eye-fatigue and skin-photoprotection data are the most developed. Endurance, cardiovascular, and cognitive claims are promising but not definitive. Benefits depend on genuine natural astaxanthin (e.g., from Haematococcus pluvialis) and consistent dosing over weeks. None of this is medical advice.
