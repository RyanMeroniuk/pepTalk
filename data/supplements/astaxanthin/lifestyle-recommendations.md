# Astaxanthin
## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Always take with dietary fat:** Astaxanthin's absorption depends on fat. Pair it with your largest fat-containing meal — fatty fish, eggs, avocado, olive oil, or alongside an omega-3 supplement — for maximal uptake
- **Stack with omega-3 for membranes:** Astaxanthin protects lipid membranes from oxidation while omega-3 fatty acids build them. Taking astaxanthin with fish oil (or a krill-oil product that contains both) is a natural, complementary pairing for cardiovascular, skin, and brain health
- **Pair with lutein and zeaxanthin for eye health:** Astaxanthin reduces eye fatigue and supports the retina broadly, while lutein-zeaxanthin specifically build macular pigment. Together they cover complementary aspects of ocular protection — a sensible combination for heavy screen users
- **Reduce screen strain proactively:** If using astaxanthin for eye fatigue, also apply the 20-20-20 rule (every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds), optimize screen brightness and distance, and ensure good lighting. The supplement supports, but does not replace, good visual habits
- **Continue sun protection:** Oral astaxanthin improves skin UV resilience, but combine it with sunscreen, protective clothing, and sensible sun exposure rather than relying on it alone
- **Be consistent over weeks:** Astaxanthin accumulates in tissues. Daily use for 4-12 weeks is needed for eye and skin benefits to emerge
- **Support the antioxidant network:** Astaxanthin works alongside vitamins C and E, glutathione, and other carotenoids. A nutrient-dense, colorful diet maximizes the benefit
- **Time around training thoughtfully:** Regular exercise improves long-term oxidative resilience; if using astaxanthin for general antioxidant support, you don't need to dose it precisely around workouts
- **Minimize alcohol and pro-oxidant foods:** These deplete the antioxidant systems astaxanthin supports
- **Choose natural, quality sources:** Use Haematococcus-derived natural astaxanthin from reputable, third-party-tested brands
