# Astaxanthin
## Classification

- **Category:** Carotenoid antioxidant / cellular membrane protectant
- **Supplement class:** Xanthophyll carotenoid (oxygenated carotenoid)
- **Also known as:** Astaxanthin, AstaReal (a major branded natural source), AX
- **Molecular formula:** C40H52O4
- **Natural source:** Primarily the microalga Haematococcus pluvialis, which produces astaxanthin as a stress-protective pigment; also found in krill, salmon, shrimp, and other marine life that consume the algae
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (OTC); natural astaxanthin from Haematococcus pluvialis has GRAS status; also used as a feed colorant in aquaculture
- **Primary systems:** Ocular (eye fatigue, macula), dermal (skin photoprotection), muscular/cardiovascular (endurance, oxidative defense), neurological (crosses the blood-brain barrier)
- **Key distinction:** Its molecular structure spans the entire cell membrane, allowing it to quench free radicals on both the inner and outer surfaces simultaneously — and it does not act as a pro-oxidant under physiological conditions, unlike some carotenoids
- **Color:** A deep red-pink pigment; it gives salmon and flamingos their color
