# Quercetin
## Classification

- **Category:** Flavonoid polyphenol / antioxidant / natural antihistamine / candidate senolytic
- **Chemical class:** Flavonol (a subclass of flavonoids); aglycone form is quercetin, but it occurs in plants mostly as glycosides (e.g., rutin, isoquercetin, quercetin-3-glucoside)
- **Molecular formula:** C15H10O7 (aglycone)
- **Also known as:** 3,3',4',5,7-pentahydroxyflavone; sophoretin; meletin
- **Supplement type:** Antioxidant / anti-inflammatory / immune-support / longevity (senolytic) compound
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (OTC) in most countries; not a regulated drug; some forms (e.g., Quercefit/quercetin phytosome) are branded ingredients with clinical data
- **Primary systems:** Immune (mast-cell/histamine modulation), vascular (endothelial), inflammatory signaling, and cellular senescence
- **Endogenous status:** Not produced by the human body; obtained entirely from diet (onions, apples, capers, berries) or supplements
- **Bioavailability note:** Free quercetin aglycone is poorly water-soluble and poorly absorbed; bioavailability is a defining challenge and the reason enhanced forms exist
