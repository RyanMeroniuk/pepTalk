# Quercetin
## Forms and Bioavailability

### The Core Problem: Poor Bioavailability
- **Free quercetin aglycone is poorly water-soluble and poorly absorbed** — oral bioavailability of standard quercetin powder is low (often cited in the single-digit percent range)
- **Extensive first-pass metabolism** — absorbed quercetin is rapidly conjugated (glucuronidated/sulfated) in the gut and liver, lowering circulating free quercetin
- This is the central reason enhanced delivery forms exist and why dose and form matter more for quercetin than for many supplements

### Quercetin Dihydrate (Standard Form)
- **The most common and cheapest supplemental form** — quercetin aglycone with two water molecules
- **Low bioavailability** — fine for general antioxidant intake but requires higher/divided doses to achieve meaningful blood levels
- **Best taken with food and fat** and often combined with absorption enhancers (vitamin C, bromelain)

### Quercetin Phytosome (Quercefit)
- **Quercetin bound to sunflower phospholipids (phytosome technology)** — markedly improves absorption
- **Branded clinical ingredient** — manufacturer and independent data report several-fold higher bioavailability than standard quercetin (commonly cited as up to ~20x in marketing/clinical materials)
- **Lower effective dose** — because more is absorbed, smaller doses can match or exceed the blood levels of much larger standard-quercetin doses
- **Verdict:** The most evidence-backed high-absorption form; preferred when consistent blood levels matter

### Isoquercetin / Enzymatically Modified Isoquercitrin (EMIQ)
- **Quercetin glycosides** (quercetin-3-glucoside / EMIQ) — better absorbed than the aglycone because intestinal enzymes cleave the sugar efficiently for uptake
- **Used in some clinical trials** — including thrombosis-related research
- **Verdict:** A solid, well-absorbed alternative to phytosome

### Quercetin + Bromelain or Vitamin C
- **Bromelain** (a pineapple enzyme) is commonly co-formulated and may aid absorption and add anti-inflammatory effect
- **Vitamin C** is frequently paired for allergy/immune formulas and helps recycle quercetin's antioxidant activity
- **Verdict:** Convenient combination products; useful for allergy/immune goals

### Other Forms
- **Liposomal quercetin** — phospholipid encapsulation for improved uptake; less standardized data than phytosome
- **Rutin (quercetin-3-rutinoside)** — a quercetin glycoside; less efficiently converted to free quercetin

### Practical Summary
| Form | Bioavailability | Best For |
|------|:---:|:---:|
| **Phytosome (Quercefit)** | Highest | Consistent blood levels, lower dose |
| **Isoquercetin/EMIQ** | High | Well-absorbed glycoside |
| Quercetin + bromelain/vitamin C | Moderate | Allergy/immune support |
| Quercetin dihydrate (standard) | Low | General antioxidant, budget |
