# Quercetin
## Lifestyle Recommendations

### Diet (Food-First Foundation)
- **Eat quercetin-rich whole foods** — red and yellow onions, apples with skin, capers, kale, berries, and buckwheat provide a steady dietary base alongside supplementation
- **Pair with healthy fats** — consume quercetin (food or supplement) with olive oil, avocado, nuts, or eggs to improve absorption
- **Build an anti-inflammatory diet** — quercetin works best against a backdrop of low processed-food, low-sugar, polyphenol-rich eating

### Allergy Season Strategy
- **Start early** — begin quercetin (often with vitamin C and bromelain) a few weeks before your allergy season for the best mast-cell-stabilizing effect
- **Be consistent** — allergy benefits build with daily use rather than acting acutely

### Exercise & Recovery
- **Use around training** — quercetin's antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects may support recovery; pair with adequate protein and sleep
- **Don't blunt all training adaptation** — very high antioxidant doses around every workout can theoretically dampen beneficial exercise-induced oxidative signaling; moderate, consistent dosing is sensible

### Sleep & Stress
- **Prioritize 7-9 hours of sleep** — sleep is the strongest driver of inflammation control and cellular repair; quercetin complements but cannot replace it
- **Manage chronic stress** — chronic stress raises inflammatory and oxidative load that quercetin is meant to counter

### Sun and Skin
- **Reasonable sun protection** — UV exposure drives oxidative skin damage; quercetin's antioxidant activity is supportive, not a substitute for sun-smart habits

### Stacking Within a Polyphenol Routine
- **Combine thoughtfully with vitamin C, resveratrol, and fisetin** — these share antioxidant and longevity pathways and are common companions in a polyphenol stack
- **Cycle senolytic-style dosing only under guidance** — if exploring intermittent senolytic protocols, do so informed by current research and medical input

### Long-Term Mindset
- **Quercetin is a supportive antioxidant/immune tool, not a cure-all** — set realistic expectations; effects are generally modest and build over time
- **Choose an absorbable form** — for meaningful blood levels, prioritize phytosome (Quercefit) or isoquercetin over standard powder
