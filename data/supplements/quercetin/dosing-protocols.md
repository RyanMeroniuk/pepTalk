# Quercetin
## Dosing Protocols

### General Antioxidant / Daily Support (Standard Quercetin Dihydrate)
- **Dose:** 500 mg, 1-2 times per day (500-1000 mg/day total)
- **Timing:** With meals containing some fat to aid absorption
- **Notes:** Because standard quercetin is poorly absorbed, divided dosing helps maintain blood levels

### Allergy / Immune Support
- **Dose:** 500 mg twice daily, often combined with vitamin C (500-1000 mg) and bromelain
- **Timing:** Begin a few weeks before allergy season for best results; take with food
- **Notes:** Consistency matters — allergy benefits build over time rather than acting like a fast antihistamine

### Quercetin Phytosome (Quercefit)
- **Dose:** 250-500 mg/day (roughly equivalent to much larger doses of standard quercetin due to higher absorption)
- **Timing:** Once daily with food
- **Notes:** The enhanced absorption means a smaller dose achieves comparable or higher blood levels

### Blood Pressure / Cardiovascular Goals
- **Dose:** ~500 mg/day has shown the clearest blood pressure effects in meta-analyses
- **Duration:** Effects build over weeks of consistent use
- **Notes:** Modest effect; not a replacement for prescribed antihypertensive therapy

### Senolytic "Hit-and-Run" Context (Preliminary — Not Established)
- **In research:** Quercetin appears as part of the dasatinib + quercetin (D+Q) protocol, typically dosed intermittently (e.g., 2-3 consecutive days, then weeks off) rather than continuously
- **Important:** Dasatinib is a prescription chemotherapy drug; the D+Q senolytic protocol is investigational and conducted under medical supervision in trials. This is not a self-administration recommendation.
- **Standalone quercetin** as a senolytic in humans lacks robust evidence

### Timing & Cycling
- **With food/fat** — improves absorption of the standard form
- **Continuous vs cyclic** — daily continuous use is typical for antioxidant/allergy goals; intermittent dosing is the model used in senolytic research
- **Onset:** Antioxidant/allergy effects build over 1-4 weeks of consistent use

### Upper Range
- **Doses up to ~1000 mg/day** are commonly used and generally well tolerated in trials lasting up to several weeks to months
- **Higher chronic doses** lack long-term safety data and are not recommended without guidance
