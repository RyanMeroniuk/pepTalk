# Quercetin
## Mechanisms of Action

### 1. Direct Antioxidant / Free-Radical Scavenging
- Quercetin's multiple hydroxyl groups donate electrons to neutralize reactive oxygen species (ROS) and reactive nitrogen species
- Chelates pro-oxidant transition metals (iron, copper), reducing Fenton-reaction-driven oxidative damage
- Regenerates and spares other antioxidants, including vitamin C and vitamin E

### 2. Anti-Inflammatory Signaling (NF-kB Inhibition)
- Inhibits the NF-kB pathway, a master regulator of pro-inflammatory gene expression
- Reduces production of pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-alpha, IL-1beta, IL-6)
- Inhibits inflammatory enzymes (cyclooxygenase/COX and lipoxygenase/LOX), modulating prostaglandin and leukotriene production

### 3. Mast-Cell Stabilization / Antihistamine Effect
- Stabilizes mast-cell and basophil membranes, reducing degranulation and histamine release
- This is the basis for quercetin's reputation as a natural antihistamine for seasonal allergies
- Also modulates the release of other allergic mediators (leukotrienes, prostaglandin D2)

### 4. Senolytic Activity (Preliminary)
- In the dasatinib + quercetin (D+Q) combination, quercetin helps trigger apoptosis in senescent cells by interfering with pro-survival ("SCAP") networks that senescent cells rely on
- Quercetin's targets include certain BCL-2 family anti-apoptotic proteins and PI3K/AKT survival signaling that senescent cells upregulate to resist death
- Most senolytic evidence is from cell and animal models; human data is early and limited to small pilot studies

### 5. Enzyme and Transporter Inhibition (CYP/Efflux)
- Inhibits several cytochrome P450 (CYP) enzymes and drug transporters (P-glycoprotein), which is the basis for many of quercetin's drug interactions
- This inhibition can raise blood levels of co-administered CYP-metabolized drugs — a key safety consideration

### 6. Vascular and Metabolic Effects
- Supports nitric-oxide-mediated endothelial function and may modestly lower blood pressure in some trials
- Activates AMPK and influences SIRT1 signaling in some models, overlapping with pathways targeted by resveratrol and NAD+ precursors like NMN
- May support insulin sensitivity and modulate lipid metabolism (evidence mixed, effect sizes small)

### 7. Antiviral / Zinc Ionophore Activity (Investigational)
- Quercetin can act as a zinc ionophore, helping shuttle zinc into cells, which is one proposed antiviral mechanism
- Antiviral claims remain investigational and should not be presented as established
