# Quercetin
## Overview

Quercetin is one of the most abundant dietary flavonoids, found in onions, apples, capers, berries, and many other plants. It is best known as a broad-spectrum antioxidant and a natural antihistamine/mast-cell stabilizer, but in longevity research it has gained attention as a candidate senolytic — a compound that helps clear senescent ("zombie") cells, most famously studied alongside the chemotherapy drug dasatinib (the "D+Q" combination). On its own, quercetin's defining practical limitation is poor oral bioavailability: free quercetin aglycone is poorly soluble and heavily metabolized, which is why enhanced forms (phytosome/Quercefit, isoquercetin, quercetin combined with bromelain or vitamin C) were developed.

### Why It Matters

- **Antioxidant and anti-inflammatory:** Quercetin scavenges free radicals and downregulates inflammatory signaling (NF-kB, pro-inflammatory cytokines), supporting recovery and cardiovascular health.
- **Natural antihistamine:** Stabilizes mast cells and reduces histamine release, which is why it is popular for seasonal allergies and as an allergy-support flavonoid.
- **Candidate senolytic:** In the dasatinib + quercetin (D+Q) protocol, quercetin contributes to clearing senescent cells in preclinical models. Human senolytic evidence is early and preliminary — most robust senolytic data comes from animal studies plus small pilot human trials.
- **Vascular and metabolic support:** May modestly support endothelial function and blood pressure; evidence is mixed and effect sizes are generally small.
- **Companion to other polyphenols:** Frequently paired with vitamin C, resveratrol, and the related flavonol fisetin in antioxidant and longevity stacks.

### Who Might Be Interested

- **People focused on allergy/immune support** — for its mast-cell-stabilizing, antihistamine-like properties
- **Longevity-focused users** — interested in senolytic protocols (with the strong caveat that human data is preliminary)
- **Athletes** — for antioxidant and anti-inflammatory support around training
- **Those building a polyphenol stack** — alongside vitamin C, resveratrol, and fisetin
