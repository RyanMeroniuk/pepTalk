# Quercetin
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

Because quercetin aglycone is poorly absorbed, pairing it with the right foods and nutrients meaningfully improves its uptake and effect.

### Dietary Fat (Improves Solubility)
- **Quercetin is fat-soluble-leaning** — taking it with a meal containing fat improves its absorption
- **Practical fat sources:** olive oil, avocado, nuts, eggs, full-fat yogurt, fatty fish
- Taking quercetin on an empty stomach reduces absorption and increases the chance of mild GI upset

### Vitamin C (Synergistic Antioxidant Recycling)
- **Vitamin C regenerates quercetin's antioxidant form**, extending its activity, and the two are classic allergy/immune partners
- **Food sources:** citrus, bell peppers, kiwi, strawberries, broccoli
- This is why quercetin + vitamin C is one of the most common co-formulations

### Bromelain (Enzymatic Aid)
- **Bromelain**, the proteolytic enzyme from pineapple, is frequently combined with quercetin and may aid its absorption while adding anti-inflammatory effect
- **Food source:** fresh pineapple (core is richest)

### Other Polyphenols (Network Effect)
- **Resveratrol** (red grapes, berries) and **fisetin** (strawberries) work through overlapping antioxidant and longevity pathways and are common stack partners
- Eating a varied polyphenol-rich diet supports the broader antioxidant network quercetin participates in

### Healthy Fats + Onions Together
- **Sautéing onions in olive oil** is a practical, food-first way to consume quercetin with fat for better uptake (light cooking preserves much of the content)

### Gut Health (Indirect)
- **Fermented foods and fiber** support a healthy gut microbiome, which influences how quercetin glycosides are cleaved and absorbed in the intestine
- A healthier microbiome may improve conversion of glycoside forms (like rutin and isoquercetin) into absorbable quercetin

### What Doesn't Help
- **Taking standard quercetin without food or fat** — significantly limits an already-low absorption
- **Relying on standard powder alone for high blood levels** — consider phytosome (Quercefit) or isoquercetin instead of trying to out-eat poor bioavailability
