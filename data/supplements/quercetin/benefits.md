# Quercetin
## Benefits

### Allergy & Immune Support (Most Popular Use)
- **Natural antihistamine** — stabilizes mast cells and reduces histamine release, easing seasonal allergy symptoms (sneezing, runny nose, itchy eyes) in some users
- **Anti-inflammatory immune modulation** — dampens excessive inflammatory cytokine production during immune challenges
- **Often paired with vitamin C and bromelain** — these combinations are popular for allergy and respiratory support

### Antioxidant & Anti-Inflammatory
- **Broad free-radical scavenging** — neutralizes ROS and reduces oxidative stress markers
- **Lowers inflammatory markers** — reduces CRP and pro-inflammatory cytokines in some human trials
- **Spares other antioxidants** — regenerates vitamin C and vitamin E, supporting the body's overall antioxidant network

### Cardiovascular & Metabolic
- **Modest blood pressure support** — meta-analyses suggest small reductions in blood pressure, more pronounced at higher doses (around 500 mg/day) and in hypertensive individuals
- **Endothelial function** — supports nitric-oxide-mediated vascular relaxation
- **Lipid and glucose modulation** — preliminary evidence for improved lipid profiles and insulin sensitivity; effect sizes are small and inconsistent

### Exercise & Recovery
- **Anti-inflammatory recovery support** — may reduce exercise-induced inflammation and oxidative stress
- **Endurance** — some studies show small improvements in VO2max and endurance performance; effects are modest and not universally replicated

### Longevity / Senolytic (Preliminary)
- **Senolytic candidate** — as part of the dasatinib + quercetin (D+Q) combination, contributes to clearing senescent cells in preclinical models
- **Human evidence is early** — small pilot trials (e.g., in diabetic kidney disease and idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis) have explored D+Q, but this remains investigational, not established clinical practice
- **Frequently studied alongside fisetin** — another flavonol senolytic with a similar rationale

### Important Caveat
- **Bioavailability limits real-world effect** — much of the published benefit depends on dose and form. Standard quercetin aglycone is poorly absorbed, so enhanced forms (phytosome/Quercefit, isoquercetin) may be needed to achieve meaningful blood levels.
