# Quercetin
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### Epitalon + Quercetin (Longevity & Senescence)
- **Rationale:** Epitalon supports telomere maintenance and circadian/pineal function, while quercetin contributes antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and candidate-senolytic activity (clearing senescent cells). Together they target multiple hallmarks of aging — telomere attrition (Epitalon) and senescent-cell burden plus oxidative inflammation (quercetin).
- **Protocol:** Quercetin 500 mg/day (or 250-500 mg phytosome) with a fat-containing meal + Epitalon per standard longevity cycling
- **Best for:** Longevity-focused protocols emphasizing cellular aging
- **Note:** Quercetin's standalone senolytic evidence in humans is preliminary

#### MOTS-c + Quercetin (Mitochondrial & Anti-Inflammatory)
- **Rationale:** MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-derived peptide that activates AMPK and acts as an exercise mimetic. Quercetin also influences AMPK/SIRT1 signaling and reduces oxidative stress on mitochondria. Together they support metabolic and mitochondrial resilience.
- **Protocol:** Quercetin 500 mg/day with food + MOTS-c at standard dosing
- **Best for:** Metabolic optimization and longevity-focused performance

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### BPC-157 + Quercetin (Recovery + Inflammation Control)
- **Rationale:** BPC-157 accelerates tissue repair and has anti-inflammatory effects; quercetin dampens NF-kB-driven inflammation and oxidative stress at injury sites. Quercetin manages the inflammatory environment while BPC-157 drives repair.
- **Protocol:** Quercetin 500 mg/day with food + BPC-157 250-500 mcg 2x/day
- **Best for:** Injury recovery, post-training inflammation management

#### TB-500 + Quercetin (Systemic Repair + Antioxidant Support)
- **Rationale:** TB-500 promotes systemic tissue repair and flexibility; quercetin provides antioxidant cover and reduces inflammatory signaling during the remodeling process.
- **Protocol:** Quercetin 500 mg/day + TB-500 2.5-5 mg 2x/week
- **Best for:** Recovery phases, return to training

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + Quercetin (Recovery + Antioxidant Cover)
- **Rationale:** GH secretagogues like CJC-1295/Ipamorelin drive recovery and anabolism; quercetin reduces the oxidative and inflammatory load that accompanies intense training, supporting a cleaner recovery environment.
- **Protocol:** Quercetin 500 mg/day with food + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Best for:** Recovery-focused stacks

#### GHK-Cu + Quercetin (Skin & Anti-Aging)
- **Rationale:** GHK-Cu promotes collagen synthesis and skin remodeling; quercetin's antioxidant activity protects skin cells from oxidative damage. A complementary anti-aging skin pairing.
- **Protocol:** Quercetin 500 mg/day + GHK-Cu per standard protocol
- **Best for:** Anti-aging and skin-focused protocols

### Supplement Stack Partners (Non-Peptide)
- **Quercetin + Vitamin C** — vitamin C recycles quercetin's antioxidant form; the classic allergy/immune duo
- **Quercetin + Resveratrol** — overlapping SIRT1/AMPK longevity pathways; common in polyphenol longevity stacks
- **Quercetin + Fisetin** — both are flavonol senolytic candidates with similar rationale; sometimes combined in senescence-focused protocols (human evidence preliminary)
- **Quercetin + NMN** — NMN restores NAD+ to fuel sirtuins while quercetin influences SIRT1/AMPK and reduces oxidative NAD+ drain from inflammation
