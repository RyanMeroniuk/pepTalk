# Cordyceps
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### MOTS-c + Cordyceps (Mitochondrial & Endurance Powerhouse)
- **Rationale:** MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-derived peptide that acts as an exercise mimetic, activates AMPK, and enhances metabolic flexibility and endurance. Cordyceps independently supports cellular energy metabolism, oxygen utilization, and anti-fatigue pathways. Together they target the mitochondrial/aerobic energy system from two angles — peptide-driven metabolic signaling plus mushroom-driven energy and antioxidant support.
- **Protocol:** Cordyceps 1,000-3,000mg/day (morning/pre-workout) + MOTS-c at standard dosing alongside aerobic training
- **Best for:** Endurance athletes, metabolic optimization, longevity-focused aerobic protocols

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + Cordyceps (Recovery + Energy)
- **Rationale:** CJC-1295/Ipamorelin raise growth hormone and IGF-1 to drive recovery and tissue repair after training. Cordyceps supports the energy/endurance side and may reduce exercise-induced oxidative stress. Cordyceps helps you sustain training volume; the GH peptides help you recover from it.
- **Protocol:** Cordyceps 1,000-2,000mg/day (earlier in the day) + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Best for:** Endurance and hybrid athletes wanting better recovery between sessions

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### MK-677 (Ibutamoren) + Cordyceps
- **Rationale:** MK-677 is an oral GH secretagogue that increases GH, IGF-1, recovery capacity, and appetite. Pairing it with Cordyceps' endurance/anti-fatigue support helps offset the fatigue of high training volume during a building phase.
- **Protocol:** Cordyceps 1,000-2,000mg/day + MK-677 10-25mg before bed
- **Caution:** MK-677 can cause water retention and increased appetite — pair with disciplined nutrition
- **Best for:** Recovery during heavy training blocks, hybrid endurance/strength work

#### Tesamorelin + Cordyceps (Metabolic + Endurance)
- **Rationale:** Tesamorelin drives GH-mediated visceral fat reduction and metabolic improvement. Cordyceps adds aerobic-energy and metabolic-adaptogen support. The combination suits a body-composition + cardiovascular-fitness focus.
- **Protocol:** Cordyceps 1,000-2,000mg/day + Tesamorelin at prescribed dosing + regular aerobic training
- **Best for:** Metabolic health and visceral fat reduction with an endurance emphasis

#### Epitalon + Cordyceps (Longevity / Healthy Aging)
- **Rationale:** Epitalon is a telomerase-activating, melatonin-regulating peptide used in longevity protocols. Cordyceps contributes antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and mitochondrial-support effects long associated with traditional longevity tonics. Complementary "healthy aging" pairing.
- **Protocol:** Cordyceps 1,000-2,000mg/day (morning) + Epitalon in standard cyclical courses
- **Best for:** Longevity and healthy-aging stacks

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### BPC-157 + Cordyceps (Recovery + Endurance)
- **Rationale:** BPC-157 accelerates recovery from training-induced tissue damage; Cordyceps supports the energy systems and reduces oxidative stress during endurance work. Cordyceps helps sustain output, BPC-157 helps repair.
- **Protocol:** Cordyceps 1,000-2,000mg/day + BPC-157 250-500mcg 2x/day
- **Best for:** Athletes pushing endurance volume, return-to-training

#### Cordyceps + Lion's Mane + Reishi (The Mushroom Trio)
- **Rationale:** A complementary functional-mushroom protocol from the PepTalk supplement database. Cordyceps provides energy and endurance support, its sibling lion's mane provides cognitive/neurotrophic support (NGF/BDNF), and its sibling reishi provides calming, immune-modulating, and sleep support. Beta-glucans from the three species engage different immune receptors for broader immune modulation.
- **Protocol:** Cordyceps + lion's mane in the morning; reishi in the evening (reishi is mildly sedating)
- **Best for:** A daily all-around energy, focus, immune, and recovery base

#### Cordyceps + Creatine (Energy Systems)
- **Rationale:** A supplement-supplement pairing — creatine supports the phosphocreatine/ATP system for short high-intensity efforts, while Cordyceps supports aerobic/oxidative energy metabolism. Together they cover both anaerobic-power and aerobic-endurance energy pathways, useful for hybrid athletes.
- **Protocol:** Creatine 5g/day + Cordyceps 1,000-3,000mg/day
- **Best for:** Hybrid (strength + endurance) training
