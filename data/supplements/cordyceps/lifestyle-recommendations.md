# Cordyceps
## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Pair with aerobic / endurance training:** Cordyceps' headline benefit is aerobic capacity and anti-fatigue. Without an endurance stimulus there is little for it to amplify. Running, cycling, swimming, rowing, or zone-2 cardio gives Cordyceps a training adaptation to support. The benefit is most likely in those building aerobic fitness from a lower baseline
- **Use consistently for weeks, not acutely:** Treat Cordyceps like a daily base supplement. The aerobic and metabolic adaptations in trials took 3-6 weeks. Build it into a morning routine (e.g., in coffee) so dosing is consistent
- **Prioritize sleep (7-9 hours):** Endurance recovery, mitochondrial repair, and aerobic adaptation are consolidated during sleep. Because Cordyceps is energizing rather than sedating, dose earlier in the day; reserve reishi for the evening if running the mushroom trio
- **Support with adequate carbohydrate and protein:** Endurance performance depends on glycogen availability (carbohydrate) and recovery depends on protein. Cordyceps supports energy metabolism but cannot compensate for under-fueling
- **Manage stress and overtraining:** Cordyceps is positioned as an adaptogen, but chronic overtraining and unmanaged stress elevate cortisol and blunt aerobic adaptation. Include rest days and recovery work
- **Build a functional-mushroom stack:** Many users combine Cordyceps (energy/endurance) with lion's mane (cognition/focus) in the morning and reishi (calm, immune, sleep) in the evening for a complementary daily protocol
- **Time-of-day:** Morning to early afternoon is ideal — Cordyceps can be mildly stimulating/energizing for some, so late-day dosing may interfere with sleep
- **Hydration:** Aerobic training increases fluid and electrolyte needs; maintain good hydration to support the endurance performance Cordyceps is meant to enhance
- **Whole-food, anti-inflammatory diet:** A diet rich in vegetables, fish, olive oil, and antioxidants supports the low-oxidative-stress, well-recovered state in which Cordyceps' benefits are realized
