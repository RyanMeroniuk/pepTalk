# Cordyceps
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

- **Hot water (extraction of beta-glucans):** Cordyceps beta-glucans and water-soluble polysaccharides dissolve in hot water. Adding Cordyceps powder to hot coffee, tea, or broth helps release these bioactives, mirroring the hot-water extraction used in manufacturing. Simmering in soup or broth is the traditional, effective method
- **Coffee and tea:** A popular pairing — Cordyceps in coffee provides complementary energy support (caffeine for acute alertness, Cordyceps for cumulative endurance/anti-fatigue). The hot water aids extraction
- **Vitamin C-rich foods:** Vitamin C may help protect beta-glucans from degradation and supports the immune function beta-glucans enhance:
  - Citrus fruits, bell peppers, kiwi, berries
- **Healthy fats (for fat-soluble compounds):** Cordyceps contains some fat-soluble constituents (e.g., ergosterol/terpenoids). Taking with a meal containing dietary fat may aid their absorption:
  - Olive oil, avocado, nuts, MCT or coconut oil added to a Cordyceps coffee
- **Carbohydrate/pre-workout meal pairing:** For endurance use, taking Cordyceps with a normal pre-workout carbohydrate meal aligns its energy-support role with available fuel; this is practical timing rather than a true absorption enhancer
- **Other medicinal mushrooms (the mushroom trio):** Combining Cordyceps with lion's mane and reishi creates a functional-mushroom stack where beta-glucans from different species engage different immune receptors. This does not increase Cordyceps absorption per se but amplifies the overall mushroom protocol — energy (Cordyceps) + cognition (lion's mane) + calm/immune (reishi)
- **Black pepper / piperine:** Sometimes added to mushroom blends to support general bioavailability; evidence specific to Cordyceps compounds is limited
- **Note on cordycepin:** No food meaningfully protects cordycepin from breakdown by adenosine deaminase in the body. Consistent daily dosing and choosing a high-cordycepin extract matter more than any food pairing for that specific compound
