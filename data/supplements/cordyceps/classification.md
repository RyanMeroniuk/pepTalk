# Cordyceps
## Classification

- **Category:** Medicinal Mushroom / Adaptogen (ergogenic)
- **Subcategory:** Functional fungus, endurance/aerobic-support supplement
- **Botanical names:** Cordyceps militaris (cultivated, commercially dominant) and Cordyceps sinensis / Ophiocordyceps sinensis (wild, traditional "caterpillar fungus")
- **Family:** Cordycipitaceae (C. militaris) / Ophiocordycipitaceae (O. sinensis)
- **Active compounds:** Cordycepin (3'-deoxyadenosine), cordycepic acid (D-mannitol), beta-glucans, polysaccharides, adenosine, ergosterol, nucleosides
- **Common cultivated strain:** CS-4 (a fermented mycelial strain of Cordyceps sinensis used in much of the human research)
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (USA); traditional medicine in China and Tibet; not FDA-approved as a drug
- **Common names:** Cordyceps, caterpillar fungus, dong chong xia cao ("winter worm, summer grass"), yarsagumba
