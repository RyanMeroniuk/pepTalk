# Cordyceps
## Dosing Protocols

- **General endurance / energy (CS-4 or C. militaris extract):**
  - **Dose:** 1,000-3,000mg/day of standardized extract, split into 1-2 doses
  - **Timing:** Morning and/or early afternoon; many take a portion pre-workout
  - **Duration:** Endurance and aerobic adaptations in trials emerged over 3-6 weeks of consistent use. Cordyceps is not an acute stimulant — give it weeks
  - **Note:** Much of the human research used CS-4 at roughly 1-3g/day; matching that range is reasonable

- **Pre-workout use:**
  - **Dose:** 1,000-1,500mg extract about 30-60 minutes before training
  - **Note:** Cordyceps is not a stimulant and will not produce a caffeine-like pre-workout effect; benefit is from cumulative dosing, with the pre-workout timing being optional convenience

- **Higher-cordycepin C. militaris protocols:**
  - **Dose:** 1,000-2,000mg/day of a high-cordycepin extract
  - **Note:** Because cordycepin is rapidly degraded by adenosine deaminase, splitting into 2 daily doses may help maintain exposure

- **Immune / general wellness:**
  - **Dose:** 1,000-2,000mg/day extract standardized to beta-glucans
  - **Timing:** With food, any time of day

- **Whole mushroom powder (culinary/traditional):**
  - **Dose:** 2-5g/day dried powder (higher because bioactives are not concentrated by extraction)
  - **Timing:** Mixed into coffee, broth, or smoothies

- **Mushroom trio stack (functional-mushroom protocol):**
  - Cordyceps for energy/endurance + lion's mane for cognition + reishi for calm/immune and sleep. Typical: Cordyceps + lion's mane in the morning, reishi in the evening (reishi is mildly calming and better suited to nighttime)

- **With food:** Generally well tolerated with or without food; taking with food can reduce any mild GI discomfort
- **Cycling:** No strong evidence that cycling is necessary. Some practitioners cycle (e.g., 5 days on/2 off, or breaks every few months) as a general precaution, but continuous use is common
- **Onset timeline:** Do not expect same-day effects. Endurance/aerobic benefits in studies took weeks of daily dosing to appear
