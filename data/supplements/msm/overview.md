# MSM (Methylsulfonylmethane)
## Overview

MSM is a naturally occurring organosulfur compound that serves as a bioavailable source of dietary sulfur — a mineral that is essential for the structure of connective tissue, the production of the master antioxidant glutathione, and hundreds of enzymatic reactions. It is best known as a joint-support supplement, where it consistently reduces pain and stiffness in osteoarthritis, often used alongside glucosamine and chondroitin. Its mechanisms extend beyond joints to inflammation, oxidative stress, and recovery.

**Why it matters:**

- **Sulfur is a structural nutrient** — Sulfur is the third most abundant mineral in the body and is a critical component of glycosaminoglycans (GAGs), collagen cross-links, keratin, and the sulfur amino acids methionine and cysteine. MSM provides sulfur in a highly absorbable, non-protein form. Modern diets, particularly processed ones, can be relatively low in usable sulfur.
- **Osteoarthritis RCT support** — Several randomized controlled trials (e.g., Kim et al., 2006; Debbi et al., 2011) found that MSM 3-6g/day reduced pain and improved physical function (WOMAC scores) in knee osteoarthritis compared to placebo. The effect is moderate but consistent, and additive when combined with glucosamine and chondroitin.
- **Anti-inflammatory action** — MSM downregulates NF-kB signaling and reduces pro-inflammatory cytokines (IL-6, TNF-alpha) and prostaglandins. This makes it useful not only for chronic joint conditions but also for exercise-induced inflammation and recovery.
- **Antioxidant and glutathione support** — By supplying sulfur, MSM supports the synthesis of glutathione and reduces markers of oxidative stress. Exercise studies show MSM lowers post-exercise oxidative damage and muscle soreness.
- **Skin, hair, and nails** — Sulfur is essential for keratin and collagen. MSM is widely used for skin quality, often paired with hyaluronic acid and collagen peptides for connective-tissue and cosmetic benefits.
- **Excellent safety profile** — MSM is exceptionally well tolerated even at high doses, with a wide margin of safety and minimal side effects in clinical use.
- **A team player, not a standalone cure** — MSM's strongest role is as part of a stack. It pairs naturally with glucosamine-chondroitin (cartilage matrix), collagen peptides (structural protein), and hyaluronic acid (lubrication) to cover the full spectrum of joint and connective-tissue support.
