# MSM (Methylsulfonylmethane)
## Drug Interactions

MSM has a clean interaction profile with few documented clinically significant interactions. Most concerns are theoretical. The following are organized by significance.

### Moderate Interactions (Use with Caution / Monitor)
- **Anticoagulants and antiplatelet drugs (warfarin, heparin, clopidogrel, aspirin)** — There are isolated, low-quality reports of MSM affecting bleeding tendency, and MSM is often combined with chondroitin (which has heparin-like structure). While direct evidence is weak, monitor for bruising or bleeding and inform your prescriber before starting if you are on blood thinners. Caution is warranted, especially in combination joint products that also contain chondroitin.
- **Blood pressure medications** — MSM is not known to meaningfully affect blood pressure, but as with any new supplement, monitor if you have tightly controlled hypertension.

### Mild Interactions (Monitor)
- **Diabetes medications (metformin, insulin, sulfonylureas)** — No established interaction; MSM is not known to affect glucose. Monitor blood sugar as a general precaution when starting any new supplement regimen.
- **Hepatically metabolized drugs** — Theoretical only. MSM is not a known significant inducer or inhibitor of major cytochrome P450 enzymes. No specific timing separation is required.

### Timing Considerations
- **No required separation from medications** — MSM has no established absorption interactions, so it does not need to be taken hours apart from most drugs. Take it with food at a consistent time of day.
- **If using multiple supplements** — MSM can be taken at the same time as glucosamine, chondroitin, collagen peptides, hyaluronic acid, omega-3, and vitamin C without interaction concerns.

### Conditions Requiring Caution
- **Pregnancy and breastfeeding** — Insufficient safety data; avoid supplemental MSM without medical supervision (precautionary).
- **Bleeding disorders** — Given the theoretical bleeding consideration, discuss with a physician before use.
- **Scheduled surgery** — Inform your surgical team of all supplements. If your product also contains chondroitin, consider discontinuing 1-2 weeks before surgery due to chondroitin's theoretical anticoagulant effect.

### Beneficial / Neutral Combinations
- **Glucosamine and chondroitin** — Additive joint benefit; the cornerstone joint-support combination
- **Collagen peptides and hyaluronic acid** — Complementary connective-tissue and lubrication support
- **Omega-3 and vitamin C** — Anti-inflammatory and collagen-synthesis support; no interaction concerns
- **NSAIDs** — Can be used concurrently; MSM may allow gradual NSAID reduction as joint symptoms improve (discuss tapering with a physician)

### Sulfa Allergy Clarification
- MSM is organosulfur and is structurally unrelated to sulfonamide (sulfa) antibiotics. A sulfa drug allergy is NOT a contraindication and does not cause cross-reactivity with MSM.

### General Note
- Always inform healthcare providers of all supplements, including MSM, before procedures or when starting new medications
