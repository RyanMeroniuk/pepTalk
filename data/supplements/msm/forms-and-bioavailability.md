# MSM (Methylsulfonylmethane)
## Forms and Bioavailability

### OptiMSM (Distilled, Pharmaceutical Grade)
- **The gold-standard form** — OptiMSM is produced by multi-stage distillation, the only MSM brand purified this way. Distillation removes contaminants and heavy metals that can accumulate in cheaper crystallized MSM.
- **Third-party tested** — Used in the majority of published clinical trials, giving the strongest evidence base.
- **GRAS status** — Recognized as safe with extensive toxicology data.
- **Bottom line:** When choosing MSM, look for OptiMSM on the label as a marker of purity and clinical relevance.

### Crystallized (Non-Distilled) MSM
- **Cheaper, lower purity** — Produced by crystallization rather than distillation. May contain residual solvents or contaminants depending on manufacturing quality.
- **Verdict:** Functional but lower quality control. Prefer distilled MSM when possible.

### Powder vs Capsules vs Tablets
- **Powder** — Most cost-effective and flexible for high doses (3-6g). Slightly bitter taste; dissolves easily in water or juice. Best for those titrating to higher doses.
- **Capsules** — Convenient and tasteless, but reaching 3-6g requires many capsules. Good for lower maintenance doses.
- **Tablets** — Compact for higher doses, often combined with glucosamine, chondroitin, and other joint nutrients in one tablet.

### Bioavailability
- **Excellent oral absorption** — MSM is a small, water-soluble molecule that is rapidly and nearly completely absorbed from the GI tract.
- **Wide tissue distribution** — Absorbed MSM distributes throughout the body, crosses the blood-brain barrier, and reaches joint and connective tissue.
- **Half-life** — Plasma half-life is roughly 12 hours, supporting once or twice daily dosing.
- **Renal excretion** — Excess MSM is excreted in urine; the body does not accumulate it dangerously.

### Combination Products
- **Most joint formulas combine MSM with glucosamine sulfate, chondroitin sulfate, hyaluronic acid, and/or collagen peptides** — This is the most evidence-supported approach for comprehensive joint support, as each ingredient contributes a different building block (sulfur, GAG substrate, lubrication, structural protein).
- **Check the MSM dose** — Many combination products under-dose MSM (e.g., 500mg) below the 3g+ used in trials. Verify the per-serving MSM amount.

### Quality Considerations
- Choose products with third-party testing (NSF, USP, ConsumerLab) and ideally the OptiMSM designation
- Avoid products with proprietary blends that hide the actual MSM dose
