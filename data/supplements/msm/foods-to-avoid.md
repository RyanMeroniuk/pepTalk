# MSM (Methylsulfonylmethane)
## Foods to Avoid

MSM does not have specific foods that block its absorption. The "avoid" list is about minimizing pro-inflammatory and pro-oxidative dietary patterns that work against MSM's anti-inflammatory and antioxidant benefits, particularly for joint health.

### Pro-Inflammatory Foods (Counteract MSM's Benefits)
- **Refined seed oils** — Soybean, corn, cottonseed, sunflower, and safflower oils are high in omega-6 fatty acids that promote inflammatory eicosanoid production, working against MSM's anti-inflammatory action
- **Trans fats** — Partially hydrogenated oils directly promote inflammation
- **Refined sugar and high-fructose corn syrup** — Drive advanced glycation end products (AGEs), raise inflammatory cytokines, and accelerate joint degradation
- **Ultra-processed foods** — The combination of refined carbs, seed oils, emulsifiers, and additives promotes systemic inflammation

### Excessive Alcohol
- Promotes inflammation and oxidative stress, depletes glutathione (which MSM supports), and dehydrates connective tissue and synovial fluid
- Heavy alcohol use directly opposes MSM's antioxidant and joint-protective effects

### Pro-Oxidant Dietary Patterns
- **Charred/heavily grilled meats** — High in advanced glycation end products and oxidized compounds that increase oxidative burden
- **Rancid or oxidized fats** — Old nuts, reused frying oils, and oxidized PUFAs add to oxidative stress that MSM is trying to counter

### Nightshades (For Sensitive Individuals)
- Tomatoes, peppers, eggplant, and potatoes contain solanine and related alkaloids that some individuals report worsen joint pain
- Not universal and lacks strong clinical evidence, but a 4-week elimination trial may be worthwhile if joint pain persists despite MSM and other joint supplements

### Foods That Are Irrelevant (Don't Overthink)
- **There are no foods that meaningfully block MSM absorption** — it is rapidly and nearly completely absorbed
- **Acidic foods, fiber, and fat do not impair MSM uptake**
- Taking MSM with food is actually preferable, as it reduces mild GI upset

### The Real Priority
- The dietary focus for an MSM user is reducing the overall inflammatory and oxidative load of the diet, not avoiding specific MSM-blocking foods
- An anti-inflammatory, whole-food dietary pattern maximizes the benefit of MSM and its joint-stack partners (glucosamine-chondroitin, collagen-peptides, hyaluronic-acid)
