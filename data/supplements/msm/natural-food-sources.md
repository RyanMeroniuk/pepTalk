# MSM (Methylsulfonylmethane)
## Natural Food Sources

MSM occurs naturally in trace amounts in many foods as part of the sulfur cycle, but the quantities are very small (parts per million) and are easily destroyed by heat, processing, and storage. Reaching the 3-6g doses used in research through food alone is not feasible — this is why supplementation is used. However, sulfur-rich whole foods support the body's broader sulfur economy.

### Foods Containing Trace MSM
- **Cow's milk and dairy** — Among the higher natural sources, but still only a few parts per million; declines with pasteurization and processing
- **Fresh fruits and vegetables** — Tomatoes, leafy greens, and other produce contain trace MSM, mostly lost during cooking and storage
- **Coffee and tea** — Contain small amounts of naturally occurring MSM
- **Whole grains** — Trace amounts present in unrefined grains

### Sulfur-Rich Foods (Support the Broader Sulfur Pool)
These foods don't provide MSM directly but supply the dietary sulfur and sulfur amino acids that MSM also contributes to:
- **Eggs** — Rich in methionine and cysteine (sulfur amino acids), key for keratin and glutathione
- **Cruciferous vegetables** — Broccoli, cabbage, Brussels sprouts, cauliflower, kale provide organosulfur compounds (sulforaphane precursors)
- **Allium vegetables** — Garlic, onions, leeks, shallots are dense in organosulfur compounds
- **Meat, poultry, and fish** — Provide sulfur amino acids (methionine, cysteine)
- **Legumes** — Beans and lentils contribute plant sulfur amino acids

### Why Diet Alone Is Insufficient for MSM Benefits
- **Trace concentrations** — Natural MSM is present at parts-per-million levels; getting 3g would require impractical quantities of food
- **Processing losses** — Heat, pasteurization, dehydration, and prolonged storage destroy most naturally occurring MSM
- **Supplementation closes the gap** — A single 3g dose of supplemental MSM far exceeds total dietary MSM intake

### Practical Takeaway
- Eat sulfur-rich whole foods (eggs, cruciferous and allium vegetables) to support your overall sulfur economy and complement MSM supplementation
- Pair MSM with these foods and with other joint nutrients found in bone broth (glucosamine, chondroitin, collagen, hyaluronic acid) for comprehensive connective-tissue support
