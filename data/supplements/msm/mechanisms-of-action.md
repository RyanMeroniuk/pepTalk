# MSM (Methylsulfonylmethane)
## Mechanisms of Action

### 1. Sulfur Donation for Connective Tissue
- MSM provides bioavailable sulfur that is incorporated into the sulfur amino acids methionine and cysteine, and ultimately into structural proteins
- Sulfur is required for the sulfation of glycosaminoglycans (chondroitin sulfate, keratan sulfate) that form the cartilage extracellular matrix
- Sulfur cross-links (disulfide bonds) stabilize collagen and keratin, contributing to the tensile strength of tendons, ligaments, skin, hair, and nails

### 2. NF-kB Inhibition (Anti-Inflammatory)
- MSM suppresses nuclear factor kappa-B (NF-kB) activation, the master transcription factor for inflammatory genes
- This reduces downstream production of pro-inflammatory cytokines including IL-6, TNF-alpha, and IL-1beta
- The result is reduced joint inflammation and lower systemic inflammatory tone

### 3. Oxidative Stress Reduction
- By supplying sulfur for cysteine synthesis, MSM supports production of glutathione, the body's primary intracellular antioxidant
- MSM scavenges reactive oxygen species (ROS) and reduces markers of oxidative damage (e.g., lipid peroxidation, protein carbonyls)
- This is particularly relevant after intense exercise, where MSM lowers oxidative stress and muscle damage markers

### 4. Prostaglandin and Eicosanoid Modulation
- MSM reduces prostaglandin E2 (PGE2) production, contributing to its analgesic and anti-inflammatory effects in joint tissues
- The mechanism overlaps partly with NSAIDs but without the associated GI and renal toxicity

### 5. Cytokine and Immune Modulation
- MSM modulates the immune response, dampening overactive inflammatory signaling while not broadly immunosuppressing
- Reduces histamine response in some contexts, contributing to anecdotal benefit for allergies

### 6. Cell Membrane Permeability
- As a small, polar molecule structurally related to DMSO, MSM may improve cell membrane permeability and the transport of nutrients and waste across membranes
- This is one proposed reason MSM is often combined with other joint nutrients — it may enhance their utilization

### 7. Cartilage Protection
- By reducing inflammatory cytokines and oxidative stress in the joint, MSM helps protect chondrocytes (cartilage cells) from apoptosis and reduces catabolic enzyme activity
- This complements the substrate-supply role of glucosamine and chondroitin
