# MSM (Methylsulfonylmethane)
## Benefits

### Joint Pain & Osteoarthritis (Strongest Evidence)
- **Reduced knee OA pain** — Randomized controlled trials (Kim et al., 2006; Debbi et al., 2011) found MSM 3-6g/day reduced pain and improved WOMAC physical-function scores in knee osteoarthritis versus placebo. Effect size is moderate but reproducible.
- **Additive with glucosamine and chondroitin** — Combination trials show MSM added to glucosamine/chondroitin produces greater pain relief than either alone, supporting its role in the joint-support cluster.
- **Improved range of motion and stiffness** — Users report reduced morning stiffness and improved joint mobility over 4-12 weeks of consistent use.

### Inflammation Reduction
- **Lower inflammatory markers** — MSM reduces IL-6, TNF-alpha, and CRP in several studies, reflecting systemic anti-inflammatory effects beyond the joint.
- **Useful adjunct in inflammatory conditions** — Applied in arthritis, allergic rhinitis (preliminary), and general inflammatory states.

### Exercise Recovery & Performance
- **Reduced muscle soreness (DOMS)** — Studies in resistance-trained and endurance athletes show MSM (3g/day) reduces post-exercise muscle soreness and perceived fatigue.
- **Lower oxidative stress and muscle damage** — MSM reduces markers of exercise-induced oxidative damage (e.g., lower lipid peroxidation) and may lower CK levels after intense training.
- **Faster recovery** — By dampening inflammation and oxidative stress, MSM supports recovery between training sessions.

### Skin, Hair & Nails
- **Skin quality** — Sulfur is essential for keratin and collagen. Studies (e.g., on facial skin) show MSM improves skin firmness, elasticity, and reduces fine lines, particularly when combined with hyaluronic acid.
- **Hair and nail strength** — Anecdotal and preliminary support for stronger nails and improved hair quality via keratin support.

### Antioxidant & Glutathione Support
- **Glutathione precursor support** — By supplying sulfur for cysteine, MSM supports glutathione synthesis, the body's master antioxidant.
- **Cellular protection** — Reduced oxidative damage has broad relevance for recovery, aging, and tissue protection.

### Allergies (Preliminary)
- **Reduced allergic symptoms** — A small open-label study found MSM (2.6g/day) reduced upper and lower respiratory allergy symptoms. Evidence is preliminary and uncontrolled — flagged as low-confidence.

### Realistic Expectations
- MSM is a moderate-effect, slow-acting supplement. Benefits accrue over 2-8 weeks of consistent use, not acutely.
- It is most powerful as part of a stack (glucosamine-chondroitin, collagen-peptides, hyaluronic-acid) rather than as a standalone intervention.
