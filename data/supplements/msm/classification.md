# MSM (Methylsulfonylmethane)
## Classification

- **Category:** Joint Health / Connective Tissue Support / Anti-Inflammatory / Musculoskeletal
- **Type:** Naturally occurring organosulfur compound; dietary sulfur donor
- **Chemical class:** Organic sulfur-containing compound (sulfone); chemical formula (CH3)2SO2 — the oxidized metabolite of dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO)
- **Source:** Occurs naturally in small amounts in plants, animals, and the environment as part of the global sulfur cycle. Commercial MSM is produced synthetically by oxidizing DMSO. OptiMSM is the leading distilled, third-party-tested pharmaceutical-grade brand.
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (USA, OTC); GRAS status; one of the more researched joint supplements with multiple randomized controlled trials in osteoarthritis
- **Primary function:** Provides bioavailable sulfur for connective tissue, glutathione, and structural protein synthesis; reduces joint pain, inflammation, and oxidative stress
- **Primary systems:** Musculoskeletal (cartilage, tendon, ligament), immune (antioxidant defense), integumentary (skin, hair, nails)
- **Key distinction:** MSM is frequently combined with glucosamine, chondroitin, hyaluronic acid, and collagen peptides as part of a comprehensive joint-support cluster — each member contributes a different building block.
