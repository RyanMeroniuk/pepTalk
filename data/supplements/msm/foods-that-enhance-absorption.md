# MSM (Methylsulfonylmethane)
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

MSM is already nearly completely absorbed orally, so the goal with food pairing is less about boosting absorption and more about supporting how the body uses the sulfur and creating a favorable anti-inflammatory environment for joint and tissue repair.

### Vitamin C-Rich Foods (Collagen Synthesis)
- Vitamin C is a required cofactor for collagen production — MSM supplies sulfur for collagen cross-linking, and vitamin C drives the synthesis itself
- **Bell peppers, citrus fruits, kiwi, strawberries, broccoli** — pairing these with MSM supports the downstream use of sulfur in connective tissue

### Sulfur Amino Acid Foods (Synergistic Sulfur Pool)
- MSM contributes to the body's sulfur amino acid pool; dietary methionine and cysteine reinforce this
- **Eggs, poultry, fish, legumes** — supply sulfur amino acids for glutathione and structural proteins
- **Garlic, onions, cruciferous vegetables** — organosulfur compounds complement MSM's sulfur donation

### Anti-Inflammatory Fats (Omega-3)
- Omega-3 fatty acids create a favorable joint environment that amplifies MSM's anti-inflammatory action
- **Wild salmon, sardines, mackerel** (EPA/DHA); **walnuts, flaxseed** (ALA)
- Pairs naturally with omega-3 supplementation in joint stacks

### Polyphenol-Rich Foods (Antioxidant Synergy)
- MSM reduces oxidative stress; dietary polyphenols add complementary antioxidant capacity
- **Berries, turmeric, ginger, green tea** — anti-inflammatory and antioxidant support for joints

### Bone Broth & Collagen-Rich Foods
- Long-simmered bone broth supplies glucosamine, chondroitin, hyaluronic acid, and collagen — the same building blocks MSM is stacked with
- Consuming bone broth alongside MSM provides a more complete set of connective-tissue substrates

### Adequate Water
- MSM is renally excreted; adequate hydration supports clearance and overall tissue hydration relevant to joint and skin health

### Practical Pairing Strategy
- **Simplest approach:** Take MSM with a balanced meal that includes protein (sulfur amino acids), vegetables (vitamin C, organosulfur), and ideally some omega-3 fat
- **Joint-stack approach:** Take MSM together with glucosamine-chondroitin, collagen peptides, hyaluronic acid, and vitamin C for maximal connective-tissue support
- Food does not impair MSM absorption — taking it with meals also reduces mild GI upset
