# MSM (Methylsulfonylmethane)
## Things to Avoid

### Quitting Before 4-8 Weeks
- The most common mistake. MSM is slow-acting, like glucosamine and chondroitin. Pain and stiffness improvements emerge over 4-8 weeks, not days. People who quit after 1-2 weeks never gave it a fair trial.

### Under-Dosing
- Many combination joint products contain only 500mg of MSM — far below the 3-6g used in positive trials. Check the per-serving dose and supplement to reach an effective amount if needed.

### Buying Low-Quality, Non-Distilled MSM
- Cheap crystallized MSM may contain residual contaminants or heavy metals. Choose distilled, third-party-tested MSM (the OptiMSM designation is the benchmark).
- Avoid proprietary blends that hide the actual MSM dose behind a combined label number.

### Avoiding MSM Due to "Sulfa Allergy" Confusion
- MSM is organosulfur, structurally unrelated to sulfonamide (sulfa) antibiotics. A sulfa drug allergy is NOT a contraindication to MSM and does not cause cross-reaction. Don't avoid MSM for this reason.

### Taking It Too Late in the Day (If Sensitive)
- A minority of users feel mildly energized from MSM. If you are sensitive, avoid taking it close to bedtime to prevent sleep disruption.

### Starting at a High Dose
- Jumping straight to 6g/day can cause GI upset (nausea, loose stools). Titrate up from 1g/day with food.

### Expecting a Standalone Cure
- MSM provides moderate, supportive benefits. It is not a replacement for the broader joint-support stack, weight management, movement, or medical care for significant joint disease. It works best alongside glucosamine-chondroitin, collagen-peptides, and hyaluronic-acid.

### Behaviors That Undermine MSM's Benefits
- **Sedentary behavior** — Cartilage is avascular and needs movement-driven loading for nutrient delivery; MSM cannot compensate for inactivity
- **Pro-inflammatory diet** — A diet high in refined sugar and seed oils works against MSM's anti-inflammatory effects
- **Inadequate hydration** — MSM is renally cleared and connective tissue depends on hydration; drink adequate water

### Medical Cautions
- **Anticoagulant users** — Inform your prescriber before starting MSM (theoretical bleeding consideration; see drug interactions)
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding** — Avoid without medical supervision due to insufficient safety data
- **Inform healthcare providers** of all supplements before surgery or procedures
