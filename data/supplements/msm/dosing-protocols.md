# MSM (Methylsulfonylmethane)
## Dosing Protocols

### Standard Joint-Health Protocol
- **Dose:** 3g/day (the most common research dose)
- **Timing:** Once daily or split into two 1.5g doses, with food to minimize any mild GI upset
- **Duration:** Minimum 4-8 weeks before assessing efficacy; benefits are cumulative
- **Notes:** This is the baseline dose used in most positive osteoarthritis trials

### Higher-Dose Osteoarthritis Protocol
- **Dose:** 4-6g/day, typically split into 2-3 divided doses
- **Used in:** Trials such as Debbi et al. (2011) used 6g/day for knee OA
- **Notes:** Higher doses may produce greater pain relief in moderate-to-severe OA; titrate up gradually to assess tolerance

### Combination Joint-Stack Protocol
- **MSM 1.5-3g/day + glucosamine sulfate 1,500mg/day + chondroitin sulfate 1,200mg/day**
- Optionally add **collagen peptides 10g/day** and **hyaluronic acid 80-200mg/day** for comprehensive connective-tissue support
- **Notes:** The most evidence-supported approach for joint health combines these synergistic building blocks; allow 8+ weeks

### Exercise Recovery Protocol
- **Dose:** 3g/day, with one dose around training
- **Duration:** Can be used continuously during heavy training blocks
- **Notes:** Studies show reduced DOMS and oxidative stress; effect is supportive, not dramatic

### Skin/Cosmetic Protocol
- **Dose:** 1-3g/day, often combined with hyaluronic acid and collagen peptides
- **Duration:** 12+ weeks for skin-quality endpoints
- **Notes:** Sulfur supports keratin and collagen synthesis

### Titration & Tolerance
- **Start low** — Begin at 1g/day for a few days, then increase to target dose, to assess GI tolerance
- **With food** — Taking MSM with meals reduces the occasional mild nausea or loose stools
- **Hydration** — Drink adequate water; MSM is renally excreted

### Timing Considerations
- **Time of day is flexible** — No strong evidence for morning vs evening
- **Some report mild stimulation** — A minority of users feel slightly energized; if so, take earlier in the day
- **Consistency matters most** — Daily dosing maintains tissue sulfur availability

### Duration & Expectations
- **4-8 weeks minimum** before judging effect — like glucosamine/chondroitin, MSM is slow-acting
- **Indefinite maintenance** is common for ongoing joint and connective-tissue support
- Benefits gradually diminish after discontinuation
