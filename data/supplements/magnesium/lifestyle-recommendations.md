# Magnesium
## Lifestyle Recommendations

### Optimize Absorption and Retention

**Manage stress actively:**
- Chronic stress is the single biggest lifestyle depleter of magnesium
- Practices: meditation, deep breathing, yoga, time in nature, adequate sleep
- Magnesium supplementation AND stress reduction work synergistically — each makes the other more effective

**Prioritize sleep:**
- Magnesium taken before bed improves sleep quality; good sleep in turn reduces cortisol and preserves magnesium stores
- Protocol: 300-400mg magnesium glycinate 30-60 minutes before bed
- Combine with sleep hygiene: dark room, cool temperature, consistent bedtime, no screens 1 hour before bed

**Stay hydrated with mineral-rich water:**
- Drink mineral water (Gerolsteiner, San Pellegrino) to add magnesium passively throughout the day
- Add a pinch of high-quality sea salt or trace mineral drops to filtered water
- Aim for at least 2-3 liters of total fluid daily

**Exercise intelligently:**
- Regular exercise improves magnesium utilization and cellular uptake
- However, intense exercise depletes magnesium through sweat and increased metabolic demand
- Supplement an extra 100-200mg magnesium on heavy training days
- Post-workout magnesium (malate or glycinate) supports recovery

**Take magnesium baths:**
- Epsom salt baths: 2 cups magnesium sulfate in warm bath, soak 20-30 minutes
- Magnesium chloride flakes: 1-2 cups in warm bath (better absorbed than Epsom salt)
- Benefits: transdermal magnesium absorption, muscle relaxation, stress reduction
- Frequency: 2-3 times per week, especially after heavy training or stressful days

**Minimize magnesium depleters:**
- Reduce alcohol consumption (potent magnesium waster)
- Limit refined sugar and processed food intake
- Keep caffeine moderate (1-2 cups coffee/day)
- Avoid soft drinks, especially cola (phosphoric acid depletes magnesium)
- Address GI issues (poor gut health = poor mineral absorption)

### Synergistic Lifestyle Practices

**Pair with vitamin D:**
- Vitamin D and magnesium are synergistic — each requires the other for optimal function
- Get 15-20 minutes of sun exposure daily when possible
- Supplement vitamin D3 (3,000-5,000 IU/day) alongside magnesium
- Add vitamin K2 (100-200mcg MK-7/day) to direct calcium properly

**Support gut health:**
- A healthy gut microbiome improves mineral absorption
- Eat fermented foods daily (yogurt, kefir, sauerkraut, kimchi)
- Include prebiotic fiber (garlic, onions, leeks) which enhances colonic magnesium absorption
- Address dysbiosis, SIBO, or leaky gut if present

**Test and monitor:**
- Get RBC magnesium tested (not serum) — optimal: 5.5-6.5 mg/dL
- Test every 6-12 months, especially if supplementing
- Also test vitamin D, zinc, and B6 as common co-deficiencies

### Building a Magnesium-Rich Daily Routine

**Morning:**
- Magnesium malate (200mg) with breakfast for daytime energy
- Mineral water throughout the morning
- Pumpkin seeds or almonds as a snack

**Afternoon:**
- Magnesium-rich lunch: leafy greens, legumes, whole grains
- Dark chocolate square (70%+ cacao) as afternoon treat

**Evening:**
- Magnesium glycinate (300-400mg) 30-60 minutes before bed
- Optional: magnesium bath or foot soak
- Optional: topical magnesium chloride on legs and feet before sleep
