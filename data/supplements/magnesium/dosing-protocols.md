# Magnesium
## Dosing Protocols

### Daily Maintenance
- **Men:** 400-420mg elemental magnesium/day (RDA)
- **Women:** 310-320mg elemental magnesium/day (RDA)
- **Optimal range:** Most functional medicine practitioners recommend 400-600mg/day from diet + supplements combined
- **UL from supplements:** 350mg/day (this refers to supplemental intake only, not dietary)
- **Note:** The UL is based on the laxative threshold of magnesium oxide; chelated forms are tolerated at higher doses without GI effects

### Specific Protocols

**Sleep optimization:**
- Magnesium glycinate: 300-400mg elemental, 30-60 minutes before bed
- Optional add: Magnesium L-threonate: 1,000-2,000mg compound (96-144mg elemental) at bedtime
- Expect noticeable improvement within 1-2 weeks

**Anxiety and stress management:**
- Magnesium glycinate: 200mg morning + 200-300mg evening
- Or magnesium taurate: 200mg twice daily
- Can be combined with calming peptides like Selank

**Migraine prevention:**
- Magnesium citrate: 400-600mg elemental/day, split into 2 doses
- Consistent daily use for at least 3 months for full preventive effect
- Can take up to 12 weeks for migraine frequency to reduce

**Muscle cramps and recovery:**
- Magnesium glycinate: 300-400mg elemental before bed (cramps are worst at night)
- Topical magnesium chloride applied to affected muscles
- Athletes may need 500-600mg total daily during heavy training

**Constipation relief:**
- Magnesium citrate: 200-400mg elemental before bed
- Or magnesium oxide: 400-800mg before bed
- Adjust dose to achieve comfortable, regular bowel movements

**Cardiovascular support:**
- Magnesium taurate: 200-300mg elemental, twice daily
- Or magnesium orotate: 200-300mg elemental daily
- Consistent use; effects on blood pressure take 4-8 weeks

**Cognitive enhancement:**
- Magnesium L-threonate: 1,500-2,000mg compound/day, split morning and evening
- Note: This only provides ~144mg elemental magnesium, so pair with another form for systemic needs

**Blood sugar management:**
- Magnesium taurate or glycinate: 300-400mg elemental/day
- Take with meals for best glycemic effect

### Timing
- **For sleep/relaxation:** Take 30-60 minutes before bed (glycinate, threonate)
- **For energy:** Take in the morning (malate)
- **For general health:** Split doses morning and evening for sustained levels
- **With or without food:** Chelated forms (glycinate, threonate, taurate) can be taken with or without food. Citrate and oxide are better tolerated with food.
- **Separate from calcium supplements** by 2 hours if taking high doses of both (>300mg each)
- **Separate from antibiotics** (tetracyclines, quinolones) by at least 2 hours

### Loading Protocol (for significant deficiency)
- **Week 1-2:** 600-800mg elemental/day (split into 2-3 doses) using a well-absorbed form
- **Week 3-4:** 400-600mg elemental/day
- **Maintenance:** 300-400mg elemental/day
- **Note:** It takes approximately 4-12 weeks to replete magnesium stores in someone who is significantly deficient
- **RBC magnesium test** is the best way to monitor repletion (serum magnesium is unreliable; optimal RBC magnesium: 5.5-6.5 mg/dL)

### Cycling
- Magnesium does not typically require cycling at normal doses
- The body excretes excess magnesium efficiently via the kidneys (assuming normal kidney function)
- Continuous daily supplementation is recommended for best results
