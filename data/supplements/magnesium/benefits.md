# Magnesium
## Benefits

### Sleep Quality
- **Improved sleep onset and duration:** Magnesium supplementation (500mg/day as magnesium oxide) improved subjective sleep quality, sleep time, and melatonin levels in elderly subjects (Abbasi et al., 2012)
- **Insomnia reduction:** Magnesium glycinate or L-threonate taken 30-60 minutes before bed reduces time to fall asleep and nighttime waking
- **GABA enhancement:** The calming effect is mediated through GABA receptor modulation and NMDA receptor antagonism

### Anxiety and Stress
- **Anxiolytic effects:** A systematic review (Boyle et al., 2017) found that magnesium supplementation had a beneficial effect on subjective anxiety, particularly in individuals with low magnesium status
- **Cortisol reduction:** Magnesium attenuates HPA axis activation, reducing cortisol output during stress
- **Mood stabilization:** Multiple studies associate low magnesium with increased risk of depression; supplementation shows antidepressant effects comparable to or enhancing SSRI therapy

### Muscle Recovery and Performance
- **Cramp prevention:** Magnesium supplementation reduces frequency and severity of muscle cramps, particularly nocturnal leg cramps
- **Exercise performance:** Magnesium supplementation (300-400mg/day) improved exercise performance in athletes with suboptimal magnesium status (Zhang et al., 2017)
- **Lactate clearance:** Magnesium supports enzymatic pathways involved in lactate metabolism
- **Muscle protein synthesis:** Required for proper ribosomal function in building new muscle protein

### Cardiovascular Health
- **Blood pressure reduction:** Meta-analysis shows magnesium supplementation (300-500mg/day) reduces systolic BP by 2-4 mmHg and diastolic BP by 1-3 mmHg (Zhang et al., 2016)
- **Arrhythmia prevention:** IV magnesium is used clinically for cardiac arrhythmias; oral supplementation supports heart rhythm stability
- **Heart disease risk:** Higher dietary magnesium intake is associated with 22% lower risk of heart disease (meta-analysis, Fang et al., 2016)

### Migraine Prevention
- **Frequency reduction:** 600mg magnesium citrate/day reduced migraine frequency by 41.6% vs. 15.8% for placebo over 12 weeks (Peikert et al., 1996)
- **American Academy of Neurology:** Lists magnesium as "probably effective" for migraine prevention (Level B evidence)
- **Mechanism:** Magnesium's NMDA receptor antagonism, anti-vasospastic effects, and cortical spreading depression inhibition

### Blood Sugar Control
- **Insulin sensitivity:** Magnesium supplementation improves HOMA-IR (insulin resistance marker) in diabetic and pre-diabetic individuals
- **Diabetes risk reduction:** Every 100mg/day increase in dietary magnesium is associated with 15% lower risk of developing type 2 diabetes (Dong et al., 2011)
- **HbA1c improvement:** Magnesium supplementation modestly reduces HbA1c in type 2 diabetics

### Bone Health
- **Bone mineral density:** Higher magnesium intake is positively associated with bone mineral density in both men and women
- **Fracture risk:** Adequate magnesium intake is associated with reduced fracture risk, particularly hip fractures in older adults
- **Vitamin D synergy:** Magnesium is required to activate vitamin D, which in turn regulates calcium absorption for bone health

### Constipation Relief
- **Osmotic laxative effect:** Magnesium citrate and oxide draw water into the intestines, promoting bowel movements
- **Smooth muscle relaxation:** Magnesium relaxes intestinal smooth muscle, facilitating peristalsis
- **Dose:** 200-400mg magnesium citrate for gentle, regular bowel support
