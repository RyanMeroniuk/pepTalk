# Magnesium
## Natural Food Sources

### Top Magnesium-Rich Foods (per standard serving)

**Nuts and Seeds:**
- **Pumpkin seeds (pepitas):** 156mg per 1oz serving (37% DV) — the single richest common food source
- **Chia seeds:** 111mg per 1oz serving
- **Almonds (dry roasted):** 80mg per 1oz serving (~23 almonds)
- **Cashews (dry roasted):** 74mg per 1oz serving
- **Peanuts (dry roasted):** 49mg per 1oz serving
- **Brazil nuts:** 107mg per 1oz serving
- **Flaxseeds:** 110mg per 1oz serving
- **Hemp seeds:** 197mg per 3 tablespoons
- **Sunflower seeds:** 37mg per 1oz serving

**Dark Leafy Greens:**
- **Spinach (cooked):** 157mg per 1 cup — cooking concentrates magnesium and reduces oxalate interference
- **Swiss chard (cooked):** 150mg per 1 cup
- **Collard greens (cooked):** 40mg per 1 cup
- **Kale (cooked):** 24mg per 1 cup

**Legumes:**
- **Black beans (cooked):** 120mg per 1 cup
- **Lima beans (cooked):** 81mg per 1 cup
- **Edamame (cooked):** 100mg per 1 cup
- **Chickpeas (cooked):** 79mg per 1 cup
- **Lentils (cooked):** 71mg per 1 cup

**Whole Grains:**
- **Quinoa (cooked):** 118mg per 1 cup
- **Brown rice (cooked):** 84mg per 1 cup
- **Oatmeal (cooked):** 63mg per 1 cup
- **Buckwheat (cooked):** 86mg per 1 cup
- **Whole wheat bread:** 46mg per 2 slices

**Fish and Seafood:**
- **Mackerel:** 82mg per 3oz serving
- **Salmon (Atlantic, cooked):** 26mg per 3oz serving
- **Halibut:** 24mg per 3oz serving

**Other:**
- **Dark chocolate (70-85% cacao):** 65mg per 1oz serving
- **Avocado:** 58mg per medium avocado
- **Banana:** 32mg per medium banana
- **Figs (dried):** 50mg per 1/4 cup
- **Tofu (firm):** 53mg per 1/2 cup
- **Potato (baked, with skin):** 48mg per medium potato

**Mineral Water:**
- Some mineral waters contain significant magnesium (50-100mg per liter)
- Gerolsteiner: ~108mg per liter
- San Pellegrino: ~53mg per liter

### Why Dietary Intake Is Often Insufficient
- **Soil depletion:** Industrial farming has reduced soil mineral content by an estimated 20-30% over the past century
- **Food processing:** Refining grains removes 80-97% of magnesium (whole wheat flour has ~160mg per cup; white flour has ~28mg)
- **Water treatment:** Modern water purification removes minerals that historically contributed to daily magnesium intake
- **Cooking losses:** Boiling vegetables in water can leach 30-40% of magnesium into the cooking water (use the water in soups/sauces or steam instead)

### Pro Tips
- **Eat pumpkin seeds daily** — 1-2 tablespoons provides ~80-150mg magnesium
- **Choose dark chocolate** (70%+ cacao) as a dessert — a 1oz square provides 65mg magnesium
- **Cook leafy greens** rather than eating raw — cooking concentrates magnesium per serving and reduces oxalates that inhibit absorption
- **Drink mineral water** as an easy supplemental source
