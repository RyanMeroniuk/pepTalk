# Magnesium
## Peptide Synergies

### DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide)
- **Synergy:** Magnesium and DSIP both target sleep quality through complementary mechanisms — this is one of the strongest supplement-peptide sleep stacks
- **Mechanism:** DSIP promotes delta-wave (deep) sleep via modulation of sleep architecture. Magnesium enhances GABA activity, blocks excitatory NMDA receptors, and supports melatonin synthesis. Together, they address sleep onset, depth, and continuity.
- **Protocol note:** Magnesium glycinate (300-400mg) 30-60 minutes before bed + DSIP at bedtime. Consider adding magnesium L-threonate for additional brain-calming effects.

### Epitalon (Epithalon)
- **Synergy:** Both support circadian rhythm regulation and melatonin production
- **Mechanism:** Epitalon activates telomerase and stimulates pineal gland melatonin production. Magnesium is a cofactor in the tryptophan-to-serotonin-to-melatonin pathway. Together, they optimize endogenous melatonin synthesis through parallel mechanisms.
- **Protocol note:** Useful for anti-aging and sleep optimization stacks

### Selank (Anxiolytic Peptide)
- **Synergy:** Both are anxiolytic through GABAergic mechanisms
- **Mechanism:** Selank modulates GABA receptor expression and enhances GABA signaling. Magnesium directly binds GABA-A receptors and blocks NMDA glutamate receptors. The combination provides multi-target anxiety reduction.
- **Protocol note:** Magnesium glycinate or taurate (300-400mg) daily + Selank for anxiety-dominant presentations. Magnesium provides the foundational mineral support while Selank provides targeted peptide anxiolysis.

### Semax (Nootropic Peptide)
- **Synergy:** Magnesium L-threonate and Semax both enhance cognitive function and neuroplasticity
- **Mechanism:** Semax upregulates BDNF (brain-derived neurotrophic factor) and enhances neuronal survival. Magnesium L-threonate crosses the blood-brain barrier and increases synaptic density and plasticity. Both support long-term potentiation (LTP), the mechanism underlying memory formation.
- **Protocol note:** Magnesium L-threonate (1,500-2,000mg compound/day) + Semax for cognitive optimization

### BPC-157 (Tissue Repair)
- **Synergy:** Magnesium supports the cellular repair and anti-inflammatory processes that BPC-157 initiates
- **Mechanism:** BPC-157 upregulates growth factors and promotes angiogenesis at injury sites. Magnesium is required for protein synthesis, DNA repair, and cell proliferation — all essential for tissue healing. Magnesium also reduces inflammation through NF-kB modulation, complementing BPC-157's anti-inflammatory effects.
- **Protocol note:** 300-400mg magnesium glycinate daily during BPC-157 protocols for healing support

### TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4)
- **Synergy:** Complementary tissue repair and anti-inflammatory effects
- **Mechanism:** TB-500 promotes cell migration, angiogenesis, and wound repair. Magnesium supports the enzymatic processes and energy production (ATP) required for cellular repair activities. Magnesium malate is particularly relevant here for its role in energy production.
- **Protocol note:** Magnesium malate (200-300mg) during TB-500 protocols

### CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin (GH Secretagogues)
- **Synergy:** Magnesium supports GH release and deep sleep (when GH is naturally released)
- **Mechanism:** Growth hormone is primarily released during deep (Stage 3/4) sleep. Magnesium glycinate improves deep sleep quality and duration, potentially enhancing the natural GH pulse as well as the response to GH secretagogues. Magnesium also supports IGF-1 signaling downstream.
- **Protocol note:** Magnesium glycinate (300-400mg) at bedtime synergizes with nighttime CJC-1295/Ipamorelin dosing

### MK-677 (Ibutamoren)
- **Synergy:** Magnesium may help mitigate MK-677's metabolic side effects
- **Mechanism:** MK-677 can elevate blood glucose and reduce insulin sensitivity. Magnesium improves insulin receptor function and glucose metabolism, potentially offsetting these effects. MK-677 also improves sleep quality, which stacks well with magnesium's sleep benefits.
- **Protocol note:** Magnesium taurate (200-300mg) for blood sugar support + magnesium glycinate (200mg) for sleep alongside MK-677

### Semaglutide / Tirzepatide (GLP-1 Agonists)
- **Synergy:** Critical support during weight loss — magnesium deficiency risk is elevated
- **Mechanism:** Reduced caloric intake from GLP-1 agonists can lead to magnesium deficiency. Additionally, GI side effects (nausea, diarrhea) can increase magnesium losses. Magnesium supplementation prevents deficiency and supports the metabolic improvements these peptides provide.
- **Protocol note:** 300-400mg magnesium glycinate daily during GLP-1 therapy. Choose glycinate specifically for GI gentleness.

### Thymosin Alpha-1 (Immune Modulation)
- **Synergy:** Magnesium supports immune cell function and energy production required for immune responses
- **Mechanism:** Immune cells require magnesium for proliferation, activation, and cytokine production. T-cell receptor signaling depends on magnesium (via LFA-1 activation). Thymosin Alpha-1 drives T-cell maturation, and adequate magnesium ensures the immune cells have the metabolic resources to respond.
- **Protocol note:** 300-400mg magnesium daily during immune support protocols

### Pinealon (Neuroprotective Peptide)
- **Synergy:** Both support neuroprotection and circadian rhythm regulation
- **Mechanism:** Pinealon is a neuroprotective tripeptide that supports memory and circadian rhythm. Magnesium L-threonate provides brain-specific magnesium for synaptic function. Together they target neurological health and sleep-wake cycle optimization.
- **Protocol note:** Magnesium L-threonate (1,500-2,000mg compound) + Pinealon for neuroprotective stacks
