# Magnesium
## Classification

- **Category:** Essential Macromineral
- **Type:** Micronutrient (but required in larger amounts than trace minerals)
- **Essentiality:** Cannot be synthesized by the body — must be obtained through diet or supplementation
- **Biological role:** Cofactor for over 600 enzymatic reactions; involved in energy production, DNA synthesis, muscle/nerve function, and bone structure
- **Storage:** ~60% in bone, ~27% in muscle, ~6-7% in other cells, ~1% in extracellular fluid, <1% in serum
- **RDA:** 400-420mg/day (adult males), 310-320mg/day (adult females)
- **Upper Tolerable Intake Level (UL):** 350mg/day from supplements only (does not include dietary intake)
- **Deficiency prevalence:** ~50% of Americans do not meet the RDA; subclinical deficiency is considered one of the most widespread nutrient deficiencies in developed countries
