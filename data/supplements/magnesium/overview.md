# Magnesium
## Overview

Magnesium is the fourth most abundant mineral in the human body and is involved in more enzymatic reactions than any other mineral. It is critical for energy production (ATP), muscle and nerve function, blood sugar control, blood pressure regulation, and bone health.

### Why It Matters
- **Energy production:** Every molecule of ATP (the body's energy currency) must be bound to magnesium to be biologically active. Without magnesium, you literally cannot produce or use energy.
- **Muscle and nerve function:** Magnesium regulates muscle contraction and nerve transmission by modulating calcium and potassium channels
- **Sleep and relaxation:** Magnesium activates the parasympathetic nervous system, modulates GABA receptors, and regulates melatonin production
- **Bone health:** 60% of the body's magnesium is stored in bone; magnesium is required for vitamin D activation and calcium metabolism
- **Cardiovascular health:** Magnesium relaxes blood vessels, supports healthy heart rhythm, and reduces vascular resistance
- **Mental health:** Low magnesium is associated with depression, anxiety, and increased stress reactivity

### Who Needs It Most
- **Nearly everyone** — Soil depletion, food processing, and modern diets have reduced dietary magnesium intake dramatically. Average intake in the US is ~250mg/day vs. the 400-420mg RDA for men.
- **Athletes and heavy exercisers** — Magnesium is lost through sweat and urinary excretion increases with physical activity
- **People under chronic stress** — Stress hormones (cortisol, catecholamines) increase renal magnesium excretion; low magnesium also amplifies the stress response, creating a vicious cycle
- **Diabetics and pre-diabetics** — Insulin resistance increases urinary magnesium loss; magnesium deficiency worsens insulin resistance
- **Older adults** — Intestinal absorption decreases and renal excretion increases with age
- **People taking proton pump inhibitors (PPIs)** — FDA warning: chronic PPI use causes magnesium depletion
- **Heavy alcohol consumers** — Alcohol is a potent magnesium depleter
- **Those with poor sleep** — Magnesium deficiency is a common, correctable cause of insomnia and restless sleep

### Signs of Deficiency
- Muscle cramps, twitches, or spasms (especially calves and eyelids)
- Insomnia or difficulty staying asleep
- Anxiety, irritability, or mood instability
- Fatigue despite adequate sleep
- Heart palpitations or irregular heartbeat
- Headaches or migraines
- Constipation
- Brain fog or difficulty concentrating
- Restless leg syndrome
- Numbness or tingling in extremities
