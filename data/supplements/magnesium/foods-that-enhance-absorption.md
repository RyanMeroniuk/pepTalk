# Magnesium
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

### Vitamin D-Rich Foods
- **Mechanism:** Vitamin D upregulates expression of TRPM6 and TRPM7 magnesium transport channels in the intestine, directly enhancing magnesium absorption. There is also a bidirectional relationship — magnesium is required to activate vitamin D.
- **Foods:** Fatty fish (salmon, mackerel, sardines), egg yolks, fortified foods, cod liver oil
- **Practical tip:** Take vitamin D and magnesium together or ensure adequate vitamin D status for best magnesium absorption

### Vitamin B6-Rich Foods
- **Mechanism:** Vitamin B6 (pyridoxine) enhances intracellular magnesium uptake and retention. B6 facilitates magnesium transport across cell membranes.
- **Foods:** Chicken breast, turkey, salmon, tuna, potatoes, bananas, chickpeas, sunflower seeds
- **Note:** Many magnesium supplements now include B6 for this reason (e.g., ZMA formulas contain zinc, magnesium, and B6)

### Prebiotic and Fermented Foods
- **Mechanism:** Prebiotic fibers are fermented by gut bacteria into short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs), which lower colonic pH and increase magnesium solubility for absorption in the large intestine. Inulin specifically has been shown to increase magnesium absorption by 5-10%.
- **Foods:** Garlic, onions, leeks, asparagus, Jerusalem artichokes, bananas, chicory root, yogurt, kefir, sauerkraut, kimchi
- **Research:** Coudray et al. (2003) demonstrated that inulin supplementation significantly increased magnesium absorption in humans

### Protein-Rich Foods
- **Mechanism:** Amino acids (particularly those with sulfhydryl groups like cysteine) form soluble chelates with magnesium, enhancing absorption. Casein phosphopeptides (from dairy) also improve mineral absorption.
- **Foods:** Eggs, poultry, fish, dairy products
- **Practical tip:** Taking magnesium with a meal containing protein improves absorption

### Healthy Fats
- **Mechanism:** Magnesium absorption is enhanced by the presence of fatty acids, which slow gastric emptying and increase contact time with the intestinal mucosa
- **Foods:** Avocado, olive oil, nuts, seeds, fatty fish
- **Practical tip:** This is why magnesium-rich foods that are also high in fat (pumpkin seeds, almonds, dark chocolate, avocado) are especially efficient magnesium sources

### Medium-Chain Triglycerides (MCTs)
- **Mechanism:** MCTs improve mineral absorption by enhancing intestinal permeability to cations
- **Foods:** Coconut oil, MCT oil, dairy fat
- **Practical tip:** Adding coconut oil or MCT oil to a magnesium-rich meal may modestly improve absorption

### Organic Acids
- **Citric acid, lactic acid, acetic acid** — Found in citrus fruits, fermented foods, and vinegar
- **Mechanism:** Organic acids form soluble magnesium complexes that maintain bioavailability in the alkaline environment of the small intestine
- **Practical tip:** Squeeze lemon on dark leafy greens or drizzle apple cider vinegar on a spinach salad
