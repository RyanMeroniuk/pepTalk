# 5-HTP
## Foods to Avoid

### Alcohol
- Alcohol disrupts sleep architecture (especially REM) and serotonin signaling, working against the mood and sleep benefits 5-HTP is used for. It also adds CNS depressant load on top of 5-HTP's drowsiness. Avoid combining, especially in the evening

### Excessive Caffeine (Evening)
- Late-day caffeine opposes the sleep support 5-HTP provides and can offset its calming effect. Keep caffeine moderate and early if using 5-HTP for sleep

### Tyramine-Rich Foods (Critical If on an MAOI — but Don't Combine at All)
- Aged cheeses, cured/fermented meats, and large amounts of fermented soy are high in tyramine. These are dangerous with MAOIs. Since 5-HTP must NOT be combined with an MAOI in the first place (serotonin-syndrome risk), this is a reinforcing reason to avoid that drug combination entirely (see drug-interactions)

### High-Sugar, Ultra-Processed Diets
- Chronic high-sugar, ultra-processed intake promotes inflammation, which upregulates the kynurenine pathway and reduces the body's overall serotonin-supporting substrate — working against mood benefits

### Foods Are Mostly Irrelevant to Absorption
- Unlike l-tryptophan, 5-HTP does NOT compete with dietary protein for brain transport, so you do not need to time it away from protein. A small amount of food can actually help by reducing nausea
- There are no common foods that block 5-HTP absorption; fiber and acidic foods do not meaningfully impair it

### The Overriding "Avoid" Is a Drug Class, Not a Food
- The single most important thing to avoid with 5-HTP is not a food but **serotonergic drugs (SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, and others) and the precursor l-tryptophan** — combining them risks serotonin syndrome (see drug-interactions). No food choice matters more than this
