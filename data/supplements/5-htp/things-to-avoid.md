# 5-HTP
## Things to Avoid

### Combining With Serotonergic Medications (CRITICAL — The #1 Rule)
- **Never combine 5-HTP with SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, tricyclic antidepressants, triptans, tramadol, dextromethorphan, lithium, or St. John's Wort** without explicit physician supervision. 5-HTP is a potent, direct serotonin precursor, and adding it to these drugs can cause **serotonin syndrome** — a potentially life-threatening medical emergency. This is the single most important thing to avoid (see drug-interactions and side-effects)

### Stacking With Another Serotonin Precursor
- Do NOT take 5-HTP together with **l-tryptophan**. Both raise serotonin; combining two precursors compounds the serotonin-syndrome risk. Use one or the other, not both

### Escalating the Dose to Chase Effects
- More is not better. Higher doses increase nausea and, if any serotonergic exposure is present, the danger of serotonin excess. Start low (50-100 mg), titrate slowly, and use the lowest effective dose

### Daytime Use Before Driving or Demanding Tasks
- 5-HTP can be sedating. Avoid before driving, operating machinery, or tasks needing full alertness

### Low-Quality / Unverified Products
- Given the historical 1989 EMS event linked to a contaminated tryptophan batch, use **pharmaceutical-grade, third-party-tested** 5-HTP from a reputable Griffonia simplicifolia source

### Unmonitored Long-Term High-Dose Use
- Because chronically elevated peripheral serotonin has been associated (in other contexts) with heart-valve concerns, avoid unnecessary long-term high-dose use. Reassess need periodically and consider cycling

### Medical Cautions
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding:** Insufficient safety data — avoid unless directed by a clinician
- **Liver or kidney disease:** Affects metabolism — consult a physician
- **Eosinophilic/scleroderma-spectrum conditions:** Historical EMS concern — avoid or use only under medical supervision
- **Bipolar disorder:** Serotonergic effects could theoretically affect mood states — use only under medical guidance
- **Before surgery:** Disclose use and typically discontinue beforehand; serotonergic effects can interact with anesthetics/analgesics

### Ignoring Warning Symptoms
- Stop immediately and seek medical care if you develop signs of serotonin syndrome (agitation, rapid heart rate, tremor, muscle rigidity, heavy sweating, confusion, fever) or unexplained persistent muscle pain/swelling
