# 5-HTP
## Benefits

> **Safety first:** All benefits below apply to people NOT taking serotonergic medications. 5-HTP must not be combined with SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, or other serotonergic drugs (serotonin-syndrome risk — see drug-interactions).

### Mood
- **Mood support via serotonin:** By directly raising serotonin, 5-HTP has been studied for low mood and mild depression. Some clinical trials suggest benefit, and its mechanism (serotonin elevation) overlaps conceptually with how SSRIs work — though 5-HTP is a precursor, not a reuptake inhibitor
- **Evidence caveat:** Studies are generally older, smaller, and of variable quality; 5-HTP is supportive, not a replacement for evidence-based mental health care

### Sleep
- **Sleep onset and quality:** Through the serotonin → melatonin pathway, 5-HTP may support falling asleep and improve sleep quality. Often used in the evening for this purpose
- **Gentler-than-sedative profile:** Works via the body's own serotonin/melatonin chain rather than direct sedation

### Appetite and Weight
- **Appetite suppression and satiety:** Serotonin promotes satiety. Some studies report reduced appetite, fewer carbohydrate cravings, and modest weight loss with 5-HTP, particularly around meals
- This is one of 5-HTP's more distinctive uses compared with plain melatonin

### Headache / Migraine (Preliminary)
- Older research has examined 5-HTP for headache and migraine prevention, consistent with serotonin's role in headache pathophysiology; evidence is limited

### Fibromyalgia and Related (Preliminary)
- Some small studies explored 5-HTP for fibromyalgia symptoms (pain, sleep, fatigue), again reflecting serotonin's broad role; evidence is preliminary

### Comparison to L-Tryptophan
- **More direct and potent:** 5-HTP bypasses the rate-limiting tryptophan hydroxylase step and crosses the blood-brain barrier without amino-acid competition, so it raises serotonin more directly than l-tryptophan and works at much lower (milligram) doses
- **Same serotonin-syndrome risk** — and arguably higher per-dose serotonergic potency — so the drug-combination caution is even more important
- **Never stack the two precursors together**

### Realistic Expectations
- 5-HTP is a supportive option for mood, sleep, and appetite in people not on serotonergic drugs. Effects vary, evidence is mixed, and the safety profile (GI effects, serotonin-syndrome risk) demands respect
