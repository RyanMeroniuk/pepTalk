# 5-HTP
## Dosing Protocols

> **⚠️ CRITICAL:** Do NOT take 5-HTP if you are using SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, or other serotonergic medications — serotonin-syndrome risk (see drug-interactions). Do NOT stack 5-HTP with l-tryptophan (another serotonin precursor). The dosing below assumes a healthy person not on serotonergic drugs.

### Sleep Support
- **Dose:** 100-300 mg taken ~30-45 minutes before bed
- **Timing:** Evening, often with a small carbohydrate snack; many start at 100 mg
- **Notes:** Higher evening doses increase the chance of vivid dreams and morning grogginess in some people

### Mood Support
- **Dose:** 50-100 mg, 2-3 times per day (total ~150-300 mg/day); some protocols go up to 300 mg/day
- **Titration:** Start low (50 mg) to assess GI tolerance, then increase gradually
- **Notes:** Splitting doses through the day can improve tolerance versus a single large dose

### Appetite / Satiety
- **Dose:** ~300 mg/day, sometimes divided before meals, has been studied for appetite/satiety
- **Notes:** Take before meals to leverage the satiety effect; watch for GI upset

### Start Low — GI Tolerance Is the Limiter
- Nausea is the most common reason people stop 5-HTP, driven by peripheral serotonin. **Start at the low end (50-100 mg), use enteric-coated/time-release forms if needed, and titrate slowly**

### Vitamin B6 Cofactor
- Adequate vitamin B6 supports conversion of 5-HTP to serotonin. Some products include B6

### Food Timing
- Unlike l-tryptophan, 5-HTP does NOT need to be separated from protein (it doesn't compete with amino acids for brain transport). Taking it with a little food may reduce nausea

### ⚠️ Maximum Caution on Combinations
- **Never combine with serotonergic drugs** (SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, tricyclics, triptans, tramadol, dextromethorphan, St. John's Wort, lithium) without physician supervision — serotonin syndrome can be life-threatening
- **Never stack with l-tryptophan** — two precursors compound the risk

### Cycling and Duration
- Use the lowest effective dose. Some practitioners cycle 5-HTP (e.g., a few weeks on, then a break) and pair it with cofactors, partly out of caution about long-term serotonin precursor loading. There is no firmly established protocol; reassess ongoing need and consult a physician for sustained use
