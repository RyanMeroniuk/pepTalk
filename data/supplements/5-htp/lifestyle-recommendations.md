# 5-HTP
## Lifestyle Recommendations

### Confirm You Are Not on Serotonergic Medications First
- Before using 5-HTP at all, the foundational step is confirming you are not taking SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, or other serotonergic drugs (and not stacking l-tryptophan). This is the non-negotiable safety prerequisite — coordinate with your prescriber if there is any doubt (see drug-interactions)

### Start Low and Respect GI Tolerance
- Nausea is the main practical limiter. Start at 50-100 mg, consider enteric-coated/time-release forms, take with a little food, and titrate slowly. Good tolerance keeps the routine sustainable

### Use It Within a Sleep Routine
- For sleep, take 5-HTP in the evening alongside good sleep hygiene — dark, cool room, consistent bedtime, reduced evening screens. 5-HTP feeds the serotonin → melatonin pathway, but darkness and routine are what let melatonin do its job

### Get Morning Light and Evening Dark
- Bright morning light and dim evening light align the circadian system that converts serotonin into melatonin at night, complementing 5-HTP's mechanism

### Ensure Vitamin B6 and Micronutrient Status
- Conversion of 5-HTP to serotonin requires vitamin B6. Adequate B6 (diet or in the product), plus magnesium and general micronutrient sufficiency, supports the pathway and a calm bedtime routine

### Support Serotonin Naturally Too
- Regular exercise, sunlight exposure, and stress management all support serotonin signaling and mood. These foundational habits often matter as much as supplementation and reduce reliance on it

### Manage Inflammation and Stress
- Chronic stress and inflammation divert the upstream tryptophan pool toward the kynurenine pathway, reducing serotonin substrate. An anti-inflammatory diet and stress management help keep the serotonin pathway well-supplied

### Choose Between 5-HTP, Tryptophan, or Melatonin — Don't Combine Precursors
- For direct serotonergic support, 5-HTP is potent; l-tryptophan is the gentler upstream option; melatonin is the direct sleep-timing end-product. Pick the one that fits your goal — and never stack multiple serotonin precursors together

### Keep It Intermittent and Reassess
- Favor the lowest effective dose and intermittent use over indefinite high-dose use. Periodically reassess need and address root causes of low mood or poor sleep alongside any supplement
