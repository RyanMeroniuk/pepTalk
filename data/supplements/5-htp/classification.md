# 5-HTP
## Classification

- **Category:** Amino acid derivative; direct serotonin precursor
- **Chemical class:** Hydroxylated derivative of the amino acid tryptophan (5-hydroxytryptophan)
- **Molecular formula:** C11H12N2O3
- **Common names:** 5-HTP, 5-hydroxytryptophan, oxitriptan (pharmaceutical name)
- **Supplement type:** Amino acid / mood, sleep, and appetite support / serotonin precursor
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (USA, OTC); prescription drug (oxitriptan) in some countries. Commonly derived from the seeds of the African plant **Griffonia simplicifolia**
- **Biochemical role:** The intermediate in the pathway **tryptophan → 5-HTP → serotonin → melatonin**. 5-HTP sits one step downstream of l-tryptophan and one step upstream of serotonin — it bypasses the rate-limiting tryptophan hydroxylase enzyme, making it a more direct serotonin precursor
- **Primary systems:** Neurological (serotonin synthesis — mood, sleep, appetite/satiety)
- **⚠️ Key safety classification:** A potent serotonergic agent. Carries a serious risk of **serotonin syndrome** when combined with SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, or other serotonergic drugs (see drug-interactions). Related precursors: l-tryptophan (upstream), melatonin (downstream end-product)
