# 5-HTP
## Peptide Synergies

> **⚠️ Read first:** 5-HTP is a potent, direct serotonin precursor. Do NOT combine it with serotonergic medications (SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, and others) — serotonin-syndrome risk. Do NOT stack 5-HTP with l-tryptophan (another serotonin precursor). The peptide pairings below are for sleep, recovery, and mood support in people NOT on serotonergic drugs.

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + 5-HTP (Sleep & Recovery)
- **Rationale:** CJC-1295/Ipamorelin are dosed before bed and enhance deep (slow-wave) sleep and overnight recovery. 5-HTP supports the serotonin→melatonin pathway for sleep onset. Together they target both falling asleep (5-HTP) and sleep depth/recovery (GH peptides) — a complementary nighttime stack.
- **Protocol:** 5-HTP 100-200 mg ~30-45 min before bed + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Best for:** Sleep quality, recovery, anti-aging sleep optimization

#### Epitalon + 5-HTP (Melatonin & Circadian Support)
- **Rationale:** Epitalon helps regulate melatonin and circadian rhythm. 5-HTP supplies serotonin, the direct substrate for melatonin synthesis. Pairing the regulator (Epitalon) with the potent precursor (5-HTP) supports the body's melatonin production and sleep timing.
- **Protocol:** 5-HTP in the evening + Epitalon in cyclical anti-aging courses
- **Best for:** Circadian support, age-related sleep decline, longevity sleep protocols

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide) + 5-HTP
- **Rationale:** DSIP supports sleep onset and delta-wave architecture. 5-HTP feeds the serotonin/melatonin pathway. Two complementary routes to deeper, more consolidated sleep.
- **Protocol:** 5-HTP 100-200 mg in the evening + DSIP per standard sleep protocol
- **Best for:** Sleep-onset difficulty, fragmented sleep

#### Selank + 5-HTP (Calm Mood Support)
- **Rationale:** Selank is an anxiolytic nootropic modulating GABA and balancing neurotransmitters without sedation. 5-HTP raises serotonin for mood support. Together they support a calm, stable mood — introduce one at a time, keep doses conservative, and avoid in anyone on serotonergic drugs.
- **Protocol:** 5-HTP 50-100 mg + Selank at standard intranasal dosing
- **Best for:** Stress-related mood support, evening wind-down

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### Semaglutide/Tirzepatide + 5-HTP (Appetite & Satiety Support)
- **Rationale:** GLP-1 agonists (semaglutide, tirzepatide) suppress appetite centrally and peripherally. 5-HTP supports serotonin-mediated satiety and may reduce carbohydrate cravings. Conceptually complementary for appetite control — though appetite suppression can stack, so monitor intake to avoid under-eating, and keep serotonergic exposure in mind.
- **Protocol:** 5-HTP ~50-100 mg before meals + GLP-1 agonist at prescribed dose
- **Best for:** Appetite/craving support during a weight-management protocol (under medical guidance)

#### Tesamorelin + 5-HTP (Body Composition + Sleep)
- **Rationale:** Tesamorelin raises GH and targets visceral fat; quality sleep supports GH's recovery and metabolic benefits. 5-HTP supports sleep onset via the melatonin pathway, helping consolidate recovery during a fat-loss phase.
- **Protocol:** 5-HTP in the evening + Tesamorelin at prescribed dosing
- **Best for:** Body-composition phases where sleep quality supports results

### Supplement Synergies (Non-Peptide)
- **Melatonin:** 5-HTP is the upstream serotonin source; melatonin is the direct end-product. A small melatonin dose for timing plus 5-HTP for substrate can support sleep — keep both conservative to avoid oversedation.
- **GABA:** The calming neurotransmitter gaba complements 5-HTP's serotonergic wind-down for evening relaxation (effect-level synergy).
- **Saffron:** Saffron has mood-supporting, mildly serotonergic-leaning effects and pairs conceptually with 5-HTP for mood — but because both touch serotonin, keep doses modest and avoid layering with serotonergic drugs.
- **L-Tryptophan:** The upstream precursor. **Do NOT combine** l-tryptophan with 5-HTP — two serotonin precursors together compound serotonin-syndrome risk. They are alternatives, not a stack.
- **Vitamin B6 + Magnesium:** B6 is the cofactor converting 5-HTP to serotonin; magnesium supports relaxation/sleep — both reinforce 5-HTP's intended effects.
