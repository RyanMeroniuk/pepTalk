# 5-HTP
## Drug Interactions

> **⚠️ 5-HTP is a potent, direct serotonin precursor. Its serotonin-syndrome risk with serotonergic drugs is the single most important safety issue of this supplement.**

### Contraindicated / Dangerous Combinations (Serotonin Syndrome — CRITICAL)
- **MAOIs (monoamine oxidase inhibitors — phenelzine, tranylcypromine, selegiline, isocarboxazid):** **Contraindicated.** MAOIs block serotonin breakdown; adding 5-HTP can cause severe, potentially fatal serotonin syndrome. Absolutely do not combine.
- **SSRIs (fluoxetine, sertraline, paroxetine, citalopram, escitalopram):** **Contraindicated without physician supervision.** SSRIs block serotonin reuptake; layering a potent precursor on top can precipitate serotonin syndrome. Do not combine.
- **SNRIs (venlafaxine, duloxetine, desvenlafaxine):** **Contraindicated without physician supervision** — same serotonin-syndrome risk as SSRIs.
- **Other serotonergic agents:** Tricyclic antidepressants, triptans (sumatriptan), tramadol, dextromethorphan, linezolid, lithium, and St. John's Wort all add serotonergic load. Combining with 5-HTP risks serotonin syndrome. Avoid or use only under close medical supervision.
- **L-tryptophan (and other serotonin precursors):** Do not stack with 5-htp's upstream precursor — combining two serotonin precursors compounds the risk.

### Moderate Interactions (Use with Caution)
- **Carbidopa (Parkinson's therapy / decarboxylase inhibitor):** Carbidopa is sometimes deliberately combined with 5-HTP clinically to increase brain delivery, but this combination has been associated with scleroderma-like skin reactions and must only be done under medical supervision. Outside that supervised context, use caution.
- **Sedatives, benzodiazepines, sleep medications, and other CNS depressants:** 5-HTP is sedating; additive drowsiness and impairment can occur. Use caution and avoid oversedation.
- **Serotonergic migraine/pain medications:** Triptans and tramadol (also listed above) are common real-world serotonergic co-exposures — monitor closely.

### Mild Interactions (Monitor)
- **Antihypertensives:** Possible additive effects on blood pressure in some individuals; monitor.
- **Weight-loss/appetite drugs:** 5-HTP's appetite-suppressing effect may be additive with serotonergic appetite agents — monitor to avoid excessive appetite suppression.

### Timing Considerations
- 5-HTP does not need to be separated from protein by specific intervals (it does not compete with amino acids for transport — that is a tryptophan concern). For sleep, evening dosing is typical; for daytime sedation, keep it hours apart from tasks needing alertness.
- **Timing separation does NOT make it safe to combine with serotonergic drugs** — the serotonin-syndrome risk is pharmacodynamic, not about absorption timing. Avoid the combination entirely unless a physician directs and monitors it.

### Conditions Requiring Caution
- **Pregnancy:** Insufficient safety data — avoid unless directed by a clinician.
- **Breastfeeding:** Insufficient safety data — avoid unless directed by a clinician.
- **Liver disease / kidney disease:** Affects metabolism — consult a physician before use.
- **Bipolar disorder:** Serotonergic effects could theoretically influence mood-state cycling — use only under medical guidance.
- **Eosinophilic or scleroderma-spectrum conditions:** Historical EMS association with serotonin precursors — avoid or use only under medical supervision.

### General Guidance
- The defining interaction issue for 5-HTP is **serotonin syndrome with serotonergic medications (SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, and others)** — a serious, potentially life-threatening risk and the most important caution for this supplement. Always disclose 5-HTP use to your healthcare providers, never combine serotonin precursors with serotonergic drugs without physician supervision, and discontinue and disclose before surgery (anesthetics/analgesics can be serotonergic).
