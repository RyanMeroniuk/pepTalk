# 5-HTP
## Side Effects

### Common (Mild — Mostly GI)
- **Nausea:** The most common side effect and the leading reason people stop 5-HTP, caused by peripheral serotonin acting on the gut. Often dose-related; reduced by starting low, using enteric-coated/time-release forms, and taking with a little food
- **Other GI effects:** Stomach cramping, diarrhea, reduced appetite (the appetite effect can be a desired benefit or an unwanted side effect)
- **Drowsiness:** Expected via the serotonin/melatonin pathway — useful at night, undesirable in the day
- **Vivid dreams:** Reported with evening dosing, related to serotonergic/melatonergic activity

### Serotonin Syndrome (Serious — Strongly Emphasized)
- **The defining safety risk.** Because 5-HTP is a potent, direct serotonin precursor, combining it with serotonergic drugs — **SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs**, tricyclics, triptans, tramadol, dextromethorphan, lithium, St. John's Wort — can cause **serotonin syndrome**, which can be life-threatening
- **Symptoms:** Agitation, confusion, rapid heart rate, elevated/labile blood pressure, dilated pupils, muscle twitching or rigidity, hyperreflexia, heavy sweating, shivering, tremor, diarrhea, and in severe cases high fever, seizures, and loss of consciousness
- **This is a medical emergency requiring immediate care.** Do not combine 5-HTP with serotonergic medications, and do not stack it with l-tryptophan (see drug-interactions)

### Possible Considerations
- **Eosinophilia-myalgia (EMS) — historical/quality concern:** EMS was historically linked to a contaminated l-tryptophan batch (1989). While the contaminant, not the molecule, was the cause, the episode underlines using pharmaceutical-grade, third-party-tested 5-HTP and watching for unexplained persistent muscle pain
- **Cardiac valve concern (theoretical):** Chronically elevated peripheral serotonin has been associated, in other contexts (e.g., certain serotonergic drugs/tumors), with heart-valve changes. Whether long-term 5-HTP supplementation poses this risk is not established, but it is a reason to avoid unnecessary long-term high-dose use
- **Daytime impairment:** Drowsiness may impair driving or focus-intensive tasks

### When to Be Cautious / Avoid
- **Anyone on serotonergic medication** — the key contraindication
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding:** Insufficient safety data — avoid unless directed by a clinician
- **Liver or kidney disease:** Affects metabolism — consult a physician
- **Scleroderma/eosinophilic conditions:** Historical EMS concern — avoid or use only under medical supervision
- **Before surgery:** Disclose use; serotonergic effects can interact with anesthetics/analgesics — typically discontinue beforehand under medical guidance

### Overall Safety Profile
- In healthy individuals NOT on serotonergic drugs, low-to-moderate 5-HTP is generally tolerated, with GI upset (nausea) the main limiter. The serious, defining risks are **serotonin syndrome** (drug/precursor combinations) and the historical EMS/quality concern
