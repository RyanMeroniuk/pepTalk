# Collagen Peptides
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### GHK-Cu + Collagen Peptides (The Skin Rejuvenation Stack)
- **Rationale:** GHK-Cu is one of the most potent known stimulators of fibroblast collagen production, tissue remodeling, and wound healing. Collagen peptides provide the structural building blocks AND signaling peptides that complement GHK-Cu's fibroblast activation. GHK-Cu tells the cells to build; collagen provides the materials.
- **Protocol:** Collagen 10-15 g/day + vitamin C 500 mg + GHK-Cu (topical cream/serum or subcutaneous injection per standard protocol)
