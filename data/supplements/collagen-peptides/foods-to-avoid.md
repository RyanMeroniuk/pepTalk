# Collagen Peptides
## Foods to Avoid

### Foods That Destroy Existing Collagen
- **Sugar (excessive)** — sugar molecules bind to collagen fibers through a process called glycation, forming advanced glycation end products (AGEs). AGEs make collagen stiff, brittle, and dysfunctional. This is one of the primary drivers of skin aging, wrinkles, and loss of elasticity. High-sugar diets accelerate collagen degradation significantly.
- **Refined carbohydrates** — white bread, pastries, white rice in excess — spike blood sugar and promote glycation, same mechanism as sugar
- **Fried foods and charred meats** — dietary AGEs (exogenous) formed during high-heat cooking (frying, grilling, broiling) contribute to the AGE burden and collagen cross-linking damage
- **Trans fats and highly processed seed oils** — promote oxidative stress and chronic inflammation, both of which accelerate collagen breakdown via MMP (matrix metalloproteinase) activation

### Foods That Impair Collagen Synthesis
- **Alcohol (excessive)** — depletes vitamin C (essential cofactor), increases oxidative stress, promotes inflammation, dehydrates skin, and impairs liver function (where some collagen processing occurs). Even moderate alcohol consumption measurably accelerates skin aging.
- **Caffeine (excessive — more than 3-4 cups/day)** — very high caffeine intake may reduce collagen synthesis in fibroblasts (in vitro studies). Moderate coffee consumption is fine and may even have antioxidant benefits. Don't overdo it.

### Foods That Promote Collagen-Degrading Enzymes
- **High-omega-6 seed oils** — soybean oil, corn oil, sunflower oil, safflower oil, canola oil in excess. High omega-6:omega-3 ratios promote inflammatory signaling, which upregulates MMPs (collagen-degrading enzymes).
- **Processed meats** — nitrates, preservatives, and high sodium promote inflammation and oxidative stress
- **Excessive dairy (for some)** — in individuals with dairy sensitivity, casein and whey can promote inflammatory responses. This is person-specific.

### Timing Considerations
- **Avoid taking UC-II (undenatured Type II collagen) with food** — the oral tolerance mechanism requires UC-II to reach the gut-associated lymphoid tissue (GALT) without being fully digested. Take UC-II on an empty stomach, 30+ minutes before eating.
- **Don't take collagen peptides with very high-dose competing amino acid supplements** at the exact same time — moderate separation helps, but this is a minor concern

### Environmental "Anti-Collagen" Factors (Not Foods, But Critical)
- **UV radiation** — the single biggest external collagen destroyer. UV triggers MMP production, directly degrades collagen, and generates free radicals. Sunscreen is a collagen preservation strategy.
- **Smoking** — damages collagen and elastin fibers, reduces blood flow to skin, depletes vitamin C. Smoking is one of the most potent accelerators of skin aging.
- **Air pollution** — particulate matter triggers oxidative stress and MMP activation in skin
