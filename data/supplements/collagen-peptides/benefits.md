# Collagen Peptides
## Benefits

### Skin Health (Strongest Evidence)
- **Reduced wrinkle depth** — multiple RCTs show 10-15 g/day hydrolyzed collagen for 8-12 weeks reduces facial wrinkle depth by 20-30% (Proksch et al., 2014; Bolke et al., 2019)
- **Improved skin elasticity** — 2.5-10 g/day for 8 weeks significantly improves skin elasticity vs placebo
- **Increased skin hydration** — collagen peptides upregulate hyaluronic acid production in the dermis, increasing moisture retention
- **Improved skin texture and firmness** — visible improvements within 4-8 weeks in most studies
- **Cellulite reduction** — some evidence for improved skin structure in cellulite-affected areas (Schunck et al., 2015)

### Joint Health
- **Osteoarthritis pain reduction** — 10 g/day hydrolyzed collagen for 24 weeks reduced joint pain and improved function in athletes with activity-related joint pain (Clark et al., 2008)
- **UC-II (undenatured Type II collagen)** — 40 mg/day reduced knee joint pain and stiffness more effectively than glucosamine + chondroitin in a head-to-head trial (Lugo et al., 2016)
- **Cartilage protection** — reduces cartilage degradation markers (CTX-II) in clinical studies
- **Improved joint mobility** — particularly in knee and hip joints

### Hair and Nails
- **Nail growth and strength** — 2.5 g/day bioactive collagen peptides for 24 weeks increased nail growth rate by 12% and decreased broken nails by 42% (Hexsel et al., 2017)
- **Hair thickness** — some evidence for improved hair diameter and density with 5-10 g/day collagen supplementation. Provides proline and glycine needed for keratin production.

### Bone Health
- **Increased bone mineral density** — 5 g/day collagen peptides for 12 months significantly increased bone mineral density at the femoral neck and lumbar spine in postmenopausal women (Konig et al., 2018)
- **Reduced bone degradation markers** — collagen peptides decrease CTX (C-terminal telopeptide), a marker of bone resorption

### Tendon and Ligament
- **Enhanced collagen synthesis in tendons** — 15 g gelatin + 50 mg vitamin C taken 30-60 minutes before exercise doubled collagen synthesis rate in engineered ligaments (Shaw et al., 2017 — Keith Baar lab)
- **Injury recovery support** — supports repair of tendon and ligament injuries when combined with appropriate rehabilitation
- **Achilles tendinopathy** — some evidence for improved outcomes with collagen supplementation during rehab

### Gut Health
- **Supports intestinal barrier integrity** — provides glycine and proline for mucosal collagen and tight junction support
- **Traditional use:** Gelatin and bone broth (collagen sources) have been used for centuries for digestive complaints
- **Complementary to gut-healing protocols** with BPC-157, KPV, and L-glutamine

### Muscle Mass (Adjunctive)
- **Collagen + resistance training** in elderly subjects increased muscle mass and strength more than resistance training + whey protein in some studies (Zdzieblik et al., 2015)
- Collagen is NOT a complete protein (low in BCAAs, lacking tryptophan) — it should not replace whey/casein/animal protein for muscle building, but it adds connective tissue support
