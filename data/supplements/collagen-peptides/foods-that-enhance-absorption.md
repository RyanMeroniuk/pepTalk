# Collagen Peptides
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

### Vitamin C (Non-Negotiable)
- **Vitamin C is ESSENTIAL for collagen synthesis** — without it, the enzymes prolyl hydroxylase and lysyl hydroxylase cannot function, and collagen cannot form stable structures
- **This is not optional** — taking collagen without vitamin C is significantly less effective
- **Best food sources to pair with collagen:**
  - Bell peppers (red: 190 mg per pepper — highest per serving)
  - Kiwi (71 mg per fruit)
  - Strawberries (89 mg per cup)
  - Oranges (70 mg per fruit)
  - Broccoli (81 mg per cup cooked)
  - Papaya (88 mg per cup)
  - Guava (377 mg per cup — the absolute richest)
- **Or supplement:** 250-500 mg ascorbic acid with each collagen dose

### Copper (Collagen Cross-Linking)
- **Copper is required by lysyl oxidase**, the enzyme that cross-links collagen fibers, giving them structural strength and durability
- Without adequate copper, collagen is produced but poorly cross-linked (weak and unstable)
- **Food sources:**
  - Oysters (~5 mg per 6 oysters — exceptional source)
  - Beef liver (~12 mg per 100 g)
  - Dark chocolate (70%+ cacao: ~1.8 mg per 100 g)
  - Cashews (~0.6 mg per 30 g)
  - Sesame seeds (~0.4 mg per tablespoon)
  - Shiitake mushrooms (~0.9 mg per cup cooked)

### Zinc (Fibroblast Function)
- **Zinc supports fibroblast proliferation** and is a cofactor for collagen-processing enzymes
- **Food sources:**
  - Oysters (~74 mg per 6 oysters — extraordinary)
  - Beef (~5 mg per 100 g)
  - Pumpkin seeds (~7 mg per 100 g)
  - Lentils (~2.5 mg per cup cooked)
  - Crab (~6.5 mg per 100 g)

### Silica (Connective Tissue Structure)
- **Silica supports collagen cross-linking** and connective tissue integrity
- **Food sources:**
  - Horsetail herb (tea)
  - Cucumbers (with skin)
  - Oats
  - Brown rice
  - Green beans
  - Beer (one of the highest bioavailable silica sources — in moderation)

### Sulfur (Disulfide Bonds)
- **Sulfur amino acids support the connective tissue matrix** surrounding collagen
- **Food sources:**
  - Garlic
  - Onions
  - Eggs
  - Cruciferous vegetables (broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts, cabbage)
  - MSM supplement (methylsulfonylmethane)

### Hyaluronic Acid (Moisture Retention)
- **Works synergistically with collagen** for skin hydration — collagen provides structure, hyaluronic acid provides moisture
- **Food sources:** Bone broth, organ meats, soy products, root vegetables
- **Or supplement:** 120-240 mg/day oral hyaluronic acid

### Optimal Pairing Protocol
- **Morning collagen ritual:** 10-15 g collagen peptides + 250-500 mg vitamin C + warm water with lemon (provides vitamin C + pleasant taste)
- **Pre-workout (Baar protocol):** 15 g collagen + 50-200 mg vitamin C, 30-60 min before exercise
- **Smoothie:** Collagen powder + strawberries + kiwi + spinach + almond butter (provides collagen + vitamin C + zinc + copper)
- **Evening bone broth:** Provides collagen, glycine, proline, glutamine, and minerals naturally — add a squeeze of lemon for vitamin C
