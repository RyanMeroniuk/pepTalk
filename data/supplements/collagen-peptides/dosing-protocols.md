# Collagen Peptides
## Dosing Protocols

### General Skin Health & Anti-Aging
- **Dose:** 10-15 g/day hydrolyzed collagen peptides (Types I and III)
- **Timing:** Any time of day; with a source of vitamin C (250-500 mg)
- **Duration:** 8-12 weeks minimum to see visible skin results; ongoing for sustained benefits
- **Notes:** Mix into coffee, smoothie, water, or food. Results typically noticeable at 4-6 weeks for skin hydration, 8-12 weeks for wrinkle reduction.

### Joint Health (Hydrolyzed Approach)
- **Dose:** 10 g/day hydrolyzed Type II collagen OR 10-15 g/day hydrolyzed bovine collagen
- **Timing:** With vitamin C; before or with meals
- **Duration:** 12-24 weeks for significant joint benefit; ongoing for maintenance
- **Notes:** For osteoarthritis or persistent joint pain

### Joint Health (UC-II Approach)
- **Dose:** 40 mg/day undenatured Type II collagen (UC-II)
- **Timing:** On an EMPTY stomach (oral tolerance requires minimal digestion interference)
- **Duration:** 8-12 weeks to reach full effect; ongoing
- **Notes:** Do NOT take UC-II at the same time as high-dose hydrolyzed collagen. If using both approaches, take UC-II in the morning on an empty stomach and hydrolyzed collagen later with food.

### Tendon & Ligament Support (Keith Baar Protocol)
- **Dose:** 15 g gelatin or hydrolyzed collagen + 50-200 mg vitamin C
- **Timing:** 30-60 minutes BEFORE exercise (specifically before the activity that loads the target tissue)
- **Duration:** Daily on training days; ongoing
- **Notes:** Based on research showing doubled collagen synthesis rate in tendons when collagen + vitamin C is consumed before mechanical loading. This is one of the most actionable findings in sports nutrition.

### Bone Density Support
- **Dose:** 5-10 g/day hydrolyzed collagen peptides (Type I)
- **Timing:** With vitamin C and calcium-rich meal
- **Duration:** 12+ months for measurable bone density changes; ongoing
- **Notes:** Combine with resistance training and adequate calcium/vitamin D intake

### Hair and Nail Growth
- **Dose:** 2.5-10 g/day hydrolyzed collagen
- **Timing:** Any time with vitamin C
- **Duration:** 12-24 weeks for nail improvements; 6+ months for hair changes
- **Notes:** Hair growth is slow — patience required

### Gut Healing Protocol
- **Dose:** 10-15 g/day hydrolyzed collagen OR 10-15 g/day gelatin (gelatin forms a soothing gel in the gut)
- **Timing:** First thing in the morning or between meals
- **Duration:** 8-12 weeks alongside other gut-healing interventions
- **Notes:** Combine with L-glutamine, bone broth, and BPC-157 for comprehensive gut repair

### Post-Surgical / Wound Healing
- **Dose:** 15-20 g/day hydrolyzed collagen + 500 mg vitamin C
- **Timing:** Divided into 2-3 doses throughout the day
- **Duration:** 4-8 weeks post-surgery or until wounds are fully healed
- **Notes:** Provides substrate for fibroblast collagen deposition at wound sites

### The Vitamin C Rule (Non-Negotiable)
- **ALWAYS take collagen with vitamin C** — 250-500 mg at minimum
- Vitamin C is the essential cofactor for prolyl hydroxylase and lysyl hydroxylase — without it, collagen cannot form stable triple-helix structures
- Without vitamin C, collagen supplementation is significantly less effective
- Sources: supplement (ascorbic acid), citrus, bell peppers, kiwi, strawberries

### Important Notes
- **Collagen is not a complete protein** — it lacks tryptophan and is low in BCAAs. Do not count collagen toward your daily protein target for muscle building.
- **Consistency matters more than timing** — daily supplementation for weeks/months produces results
- **Hot or cold liquid** — hydrolyzed collagen dissolves in both. Gelatin only dissolves in hot liquid and gels when cooled.
- **Can be taken with or without food** (except UC-II, which requires empty stomach)
