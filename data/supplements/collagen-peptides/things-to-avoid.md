# Collagen Peptides
## Things to Avoid

### Supplement Mistakes
- **Skipping vitamin C** — the most common and most impactful mistake. Collagen synthesis REQUIRES vitamin C as a cofactor. Taking collagen without vitamin C is like buying building materials but not hiring workers. Always co-supplement 250-500 mg vitamin C.
- **Counting collagen as your protein intake** — collagen is NOT a complete protein. It lacks tryptophan and is low in leucine, isoleucine, and valine (BCAAs). Do not substitute collagen for whey, casein, or animal protein for muscle-building goals. Collagen is for connective tissue, not muscle protein synthesis.
- **Choosing the wrong type for your goal** — Type I/III for skin, tendons, bone, gut. Type II (UC-II) for cartilage and joint pain. Using the wrong type reduces effectiveness.
- **Taking UC-II with food** — UC-II works via oral tolerance, which requires the protein to reach gut immune tissue relatively intact. Food promotes digestion. Take UC-II on an empty stomach.
- **Expecting instant results** — collagen turnover is slow. Skin: 4-8 weeks minimum. Joints: 8-24 weeks. Bone: 12+ months. Hair: 6+ months. Patience is required.
- **Buying low-quality products** — choose third-party tested (NSF, Informed Sport), grass-fed/pasture-raised (bovine), or wild-caught (marine). Avoid products with heavy metal contamination, fillers, or artificial ingredients.

### Lifestyle Factors That Destroy Collagen
- **Sun exposure without protection** — UV radiation is the #1 external collagen destroyer. Wear broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen daily on exposed skin, even on cloudy days. Reapply every 2 hours during direct exposure.
- **Smoking** — directly damages collagen and elastin, reduces vitamin C levels, impairs blood flow to skin. Quitting smoking is one of the most impactful things you can do for collagen health.
- **Chronic stress** — cortisol increases MMP activity (collagen-degrading enzymes) and impairs fibroblast function
- **Sleep deprivation** — growth hormone (released during deep sleep) stimulates fibroblast collagen production. Poor sleep = less collagen repair overnight.
- **Sedentary lifestyle** — mechanical loading (exercise) stimulates collagen synthesis in tendons, ligaments, cartilage, and bone. Without movement, collagen turnover decreases.

### Dietary Behaviors
- **High-sugar diet** — glycation (sugar binding to collagen) is one of the most damaging processes for existing collagen. Reduce added sugar intake.
- **Excessive alcohol** — depletes vitamin C, promotes inflammation, dehydrates tissues, accelerates skin aging
- **Very low-protein diet** — inadequate amino acid intake limits collagen production regardless of supplementation. Ensure total protein intake is adequate (1.2+ g/kg/day minimum).
- **Low-fat diet to extremes** — healthy fats support cell membrane integrity in fibroblasts and reduce inflammatory signaling. Include omega-3s (fatty fish, walnuts, flaxseed).

### Training Mistakes
- **Not exercising before taking collagen for tendon benefit** — the Keith Baar research shows collagen synthesis in tendons is maximized when collagen is taken 30-60 minutes BEFORE mechanical loading. Take your collagen before exercise, not after.
- **Overtraining without recovery** — excessive mechanical stress breaks down collagen faster than it can be rebuilt. Include rest days and deload weeks.
