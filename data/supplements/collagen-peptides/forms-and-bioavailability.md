# Collagen Peptides
## Forms and Bioavailability

### Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides (Most Recommended)
- **Enzymatically broken down** into small peptides (2-5 kDa molecular weight)
- **Dissolves in cold or hot liquid** — no gelling, no clumping
- **~90% absorbed** — di- and tripeptides (especially Pro-Hyp, Hyp-Gly) are absorbed intact via peptide transporters (PepT1) and appear in blood within 1-2 hours
- **Tasteless and odorless** (high-quality brands) — mixes into coffee, smoothies, water
- Best for: daily supplementation, all collagen goals (skin, joints, bone, gut)

### Gelatin
- **Partially hydrolyzed collagen** — larger peptide chains that gel in cold liquid
- **Same amino acid profile** as hydrolyzed collagen
- **Lower bioavailability** than hydrolyzed peptides — larger molecules require more digestion
- **Useful in cooking** — gummies, jello, soups, sauces
- **Cheaper** than hydrolyzed peptides per gram
- Best for: cooking applications, budget-conscious supplementation, gut healing (forms a soothing gel)

### Undenatured Type II Collagen (UC-II)
- **NOT hydrolyzed** — native (intact) Type II collagen, typically from chicken sternum cartilage
- **Works via oral tolerance** — tiny doses (40 mg/day) train the immune system to stop attacking joint cartilage
- **Completely different mechanism** than hydrolyzed collagen — do not confuse them
- **Cannot be combined with high-dose hydrolyzed collagen** at the same time (the hydrolyzed peptides may interfere with the oral tolerance mechanism)
- **Take on an empty stomach** for oral tolerance to work
- Best for: osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, joint-specific protocols

### Collagen Types and Their Sources

#### Type I Collagen (Most Abundant — 90% of Body Collagen)
- **Found in:** Skin, tendons, ligaments, bone, teeth, gut lining, blood vessels
- **Best sources:** Bovine (cow) hide and bone; marine (fish) skin and scales
- **Best for:** Skin anti-aging, tendon/ligament repair, bone density, gut healing

#### Type II Collagen
- **Found in:** Cartilage (hyaline cartilage in joints)
- **Best source:** Chicken sternum cartilage
- **Best for:** Joint health, osteoarthritis, cartilage repair
- **Available as:** UC-II (undenatured, 40 mg/day) or hydrolyzed Type II (higher doses)

#### Type III Collagen
- **Found in:** Blood vessels, organs, skin (alongside Type I), gut lining
- **Usually co-extracted with Type I** from bovine sources
- **Best for:** Vascular health, organ support, skin (works synergistically with Type I)

### Marine Collagen vs Bovine Collagen

#### Marine (Fish) Collagen
- **Primarily Type I collagen** — excellent for skin
- **Smaller peptide size** — some evidence for faster absorption
- **Sourced from fish skin and scales** — sustainable if responsibly sourced
- **Higher bioavailability** claimed (some studies support this, effect size is small)
- **Better for:** Pescatarians, those prioritizing skin benefits, people with beef sensitivity
- **Downside:** More expensive; fish allergen risk; exclusively Type I (no Type III)

#### Bovine Collagen
- **Types I and III** — broader coverage (skin, gut, tendons, blood vessels)
- **Sourced from cow hide and bone** — grass-fed/pasture-raised preferred
- **Well-studied** — most clinical trials use bovine-derived hydrolyzed collagen
- **Better for:** Comprehensive support (skin + gut + tendons + bone), cost-effectiveness
- **Downside:** Not suitable for pescatarians; potential BSE concerns (mitigated by sourcing from BSE-free countries)

### Bioavailability Summary
| Form | Absorption | Best For | Dose Range |
|------|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Hydrolyzed peptides (bovine) | ~90% | Comprehensive | 10-20 g/day |
| Hydrolyzed peptides (marine) | ~90%+ | Skin-focused | 5-15 g/day |
| Gelatin | ~80% | Cooking, gut, budget | 10-20 g/day |
| UC-II (undenatured) | Oral tolerance | Joints/OA | 40 mg/day |

### Quality Markers to Look For
- **Third-party tested** (NSF, Informed Sport, USP)
- **Grass-fed, pasture-raised** (bovine) or **wild-caught** (marine)
- **No added sugars, fillers, or artificial flavors**
- **Hydrolyzed to <5 kDa** molecular weight for optimal absorption
- **Vitamin C included** (or co-supplement separately — non-negotiable)
