# Collagen Peptides
## Lifestyle Recommendations

### Skin Collagen Optimization
- **Wear sunscreen daily (SPF 30+)** — UV protection is the single most effective collagen preservation strategy. Apply to face, neck, and hands every morning. Reapply during prolonged sun exposure.
- **Don't smoke** — smoking accelerates collagen breakdown and impairs synthesis. If you smoke, quitting will do more for your skin than any supplement.
- **Sleep 7-9 hours** — growth hormone released during deep sleep drives fibroblast collagen production. "Beauty sleep" is literally real.
- **Stay hydrated** — skin hydration supports collagen structure and elasticity. 2-3 liters of water daily.
- **Consider topical retinoids** — prescription retinoids (tretinoin) or OTC retinol stimulate collagen production in the dermis. Oral collagen + topical retinoid = inside-out + outside-in approach.
- **Antioxidant-rich diet** — colorful fruits and vegetables provide polyphenols that reduce oxidative collagen damage. Berries, dark leafy greens, sweet potatoes, tomatoes (lycopene).

### Exercise for Collagen
- **Resistance training 3-4x/week** — mechanical loading stimulates collagen synthesis in tendons, ligaments, and bone. This is not optional for connective tissue health.
- **Pre-exercise collagen protocol (Baar method):** 15 g collagen + 50-200 mg vitamin C, 30-60 minutes before exercise. This has been shown to double collagen synthesis rate in tendons.
- **Include eccentric loading** — slow eccentric movements (lowering phase) stimulate tendon collagen remodeling. Eccentric heel drops for Achilles tendinopathy, eccentric squats for patellar tendinopathy.
- **Stretching and mobility** — maintains collagen flexibility and range of motion in connective tissue. Yoga, dynamic stretching, foam rolling.
- **Walking/running** — compressive loading stimulates cartilage collagen turnover and bone collagen deposition. Weight-bearing exercise is essential for bone health.

### Dietary Strategy
- **Nose-to-tail eating** — incorporate bone broth, skin-on poultry, collagen-rich cuts (oxtail, short ribs, shanks), gelatin, and organ meats
- **Vitamin C with every collagen dose** — non-negotiable. 250-500 mg at minimum.
- **Minimize sugar** — glycation is collagen's enemy. Reduce added sugars, refined carbs, and high-glycemic foods.
- **Omega-3 fatty acids** — anti-inflammatory support. Fatty fish 2-3x/week, or fish oil supplement. Reduces MMP activity that degrades collagen.
- **Adequate protein** — 1.2-1.6 g/kg/day minimum to support overall protein synthesis including collagen

### Complementary Practices
- **Red light therapy (photobiomodulation)** — 630-660 nm red light and 810-850 nm near-infrared light stimulate fibroblast collagen production, increase blood flow, and reduce inflammation. 10-20 minutes, 3-5x/week on target areas.
- **Facial massage / gua sha** — promotes blood flow and lymphatic drainage in facial tissue, supporting nutrient delivery to collagen-producing cells
- **Microneedling** — controlled micro-injury stimulates wound healing response and new collagen deposition. Professional or at-home (0.25-0.5 mm derma roller). Combine with oral collagen supplementation for substrate support.
- **Sauna** — heat shock proteins support protein folding and may support collagen quality. Also promotes detoxification, reducing oxidative burden.

### Recovery Priorities
- **After injury:** Increase collagen to 15-20 g/day + 500 mg vitamin C, combine with appropriate rehabilitation
- **Post-surgery:** Collagen 15-20 g/day + vitamin C 500 mg for 4-8 weeks post-op
- **During caloric deficit:** Maintain collagen supplementation to prevent connective tissue loss during weight loss (especially important with GLP-1 agonists)
