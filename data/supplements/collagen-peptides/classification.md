# Collagen Peptides
## Classification

- **Category:** Structural protein supplement (hydrolyzed protein)
- **Chemical class:** Hydrolyzed collagen (enzymatically broken down collagen protein into bioactive peptides, typically 2-5 kDa molecular weight)
- **Supplement type:** Functional protein / beauty-from-within / connective tissue support
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (OTC); GRAS status; widely available as powder, capsules, and functional food ingredient
- **Primary systems:** Integumentary (skin, hair, nails), musculoskeletal (joints, tendons, ligaments, bone), gastrointestinal (gut lining)
- **Key distinction:** "Collagen peptides" (hydrolyzed collagen) are NOT the same as "collagen protein" (gelatin). Hydrolyzed collagen is enzymatically broken into smaller peptides that dissolve in cold liquid and are more bioavailable. Gelatin gels in cold liquid and is less bioavailable but cheaper.
- **Collagen types matter** — the human body contains at least 28 types of collagen. Types I, II, and III account for ~90% of all collagen and are the most supplemented.
