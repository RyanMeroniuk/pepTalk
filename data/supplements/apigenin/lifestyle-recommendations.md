# Apigenin
## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Incorporate chamomile tea ritual** — Brewing 2-3 cups of strong chamomile tea (steeped 10-15 minutes with a lid) provides a meaningful dose of apigenin alongside the calming ritual. The ritual itself activates the parasympathetic nervous system. Use loose-leaf chamomile flowers for highest apigenin content.
- **Dim lights after sunset** — Apigenin's GABAergic calming effects work synergistically with natural melatonin production. Reduce overhead lighting, use warm-tone bulbs (2700K or lower), and avoid blue light from screens 1-2 hours before bed. Blue-light blocking glasses are a reasonable option if screen use is unavoidable.
- **Eat apigenin-rich foods regularly** — Parsley (dried and fresh), celery, chamomile, and artichokes are the richest dietary sources. Incorporating these into meals provides a baseline of apigenin, flavonoids, and anti-inflammatory compounds.
- **Pair with a consistent sleep schedule** — Apigenin supports but does not force sleep. Its calming effects are most impactful when combined with a fixed wake time, consistent bedtime, and a structured wind-down routine.
- **Add fat to enhance absorption** — Apigenin is a lipophilic flavonoid with poor water solubility. Consuming it with a fat-containing snack (nuts, olive oil, avocado, fish oil capsule) significantly improves absorption.
- **Practice stress-reduction techniques** — Apigenin's anxiolytic effects compound with meditation, breathwork, journaling, or gentle yoga. The combination of pharmacological and behavioral calming is more effective than either alone.
- **Support NAD+ from multiple angles** — If using apigenin for CD38 inhibition (NAD+ preservation), complement with NAD+ precursors (NMN or NR), regular exercise (the strongest natural NAD+ booster), and time-restricted eating, which upregulates sirtuin activity.
- **Maintain an anti-inflammatory diet** — Apigenin's NF-kB suppression works best in the context of an overall anti-inflammatory dietary pattern: high in vegetables, fruits, fatty fish, olive oil, nuts, and herbs. Reduce ultra-processed foods, seed oils, and added sugars.
- **Grow fresh herbs** — Parsley and celery are easy to grow at home. Fresh herbs have higher flavonoid content than dried, and the gardening itself supports mental health and circadian rhythm through outdoor light exposure.
