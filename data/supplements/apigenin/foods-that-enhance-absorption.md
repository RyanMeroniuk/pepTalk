# Apigenin
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

- **Healthy fats and oils** — Apigenin is a lipophilic (fat-soluble) flavonoid with poor water solubility. Taking apigenin with fat-containing foods dramatically improves absorption by promoting bile secretion and micelle formation in the small intestine. **Best options:** extra virgin olive oil, avocado, nuts (almonds, walnuts), coconut oil, MCT oil, or fish oil capsules. A simple approach: take apigenin supplements with a small handful of nuts or a spoonful of olive oil.
- **Black pepper (piperine)** — Piperine inhibits glucuronidation enzymes (UGT) in the intestinal wall and liver. Since apigenin undergoes extensive glucuronidation during first-pass metabolism, piperine may reduce this inactivation and increase bioavailable apigenin. A pinch of black pepper or a 5-10mg BioPerine capsule taken alongside apigenin is a simple strategy.
- **Quercetin-containing foods** — Quercetin and apigenin share some metabolic pathways and may compete for glucuronidation enzymes, potentially sparing each other from extensive metabolism. Quercetin sources: **onions (especially red), apples, berries, capers, green tea**. Including these in the same meal as apigenin may modestly improve its bioavailability.
- **Fermented foods** — Fermentation can convert apigenin glycosides (bound forms) into free apigenin aglycone (the active form). Gut bacteria also perform this conversion. Supporting gut microbial health with **yogurt, kefir, sauerkraut, kimchi, and miso** may improve apigenin liberation from food-bound forms.
- **Citrus flavonoids (naringenin, hesperidin)** — Found in **grapefruit, oranges, and lemons**. These flavonoids may modestly inhibit CYP enzymes involved in apigenin metabolism. However, grapefruit juice interactions should be considered if taking other medications (see drug interactions).
- **Curcumin (turmeric)** — Like piperine, curcumin modulates Phase II metabolism and may reduce apigenin glucuronidation. Turmeric in evening meals or golden milk before bed combines well with apigenin supplementation.

**Practical evening snack for apigenin absorption:**

A small serving of nuts (almonds or walnuts) with a cup of chamomile tea, or apigenin capsule taken alongside a fish oil capsule, provides both the fat needed for absorption and the relaxation benefits for sleep preparation.
