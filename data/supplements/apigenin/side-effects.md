# Apigenin
## Side Effects

**Apigenin is generally very well-tolerated with few reported side effects at standard doses.**

**Uncommon:**

- **Mild sedation** — The intended effect for sleep use, but may be unwanted if taking during the day. At 50mg, sedation is mild. Avoid driving or operating heavy machinery until you know your individual response.
- **Contact dermatitis (topical)** — People with ragweed or Asteraceae/Compositae family allergies may react to chamomile-derived apigenin. Cross-reactivity with ragweed, chrysanthemums, marigolds, and daisies is possible. Oral supplementation is less likely to trigger this than topical application, but be aware if you have these allergies.
- **GI discomfort** — Rare reports of mild nausea or stomach upset, particularly at higher doses on an empty stomach.
- **Headache** — Infrequently reported, usually mild and transient.

**Theoretical concerns (based on preclinical data):**

- **Hormonal effects in women** — Apigenin has shown both estrogenic and anti-estrogenic activity in cell culture depending on concentration and tissue type. The clinical significance in women at 50mg supplemental doses is unclear, but women with estrogen-receptor-positive breast cancer or other estrogen-sensitive conditions should exercise caution and consult their oncologist.
- **Muscle relaxation** — At very high doses in animal studies, apigenin produces significant muscle relaxation (myorelaxant effect). Unlikely at human supplemental doses but worth noting.
- **Drug metabolism interaction** — Apigenin inhibits CYP2C9 and to a lesser extent CYP1A2 and CYP3A4 in vitro. At standard supplemental doses, the clinical significance of these interactions is likely small, but caution is warranted with narrow therapeutic index drugs metabolized by these enzymes (see drug interactions).
- **Iron chelation** — Like many flavonoids, apigenin can chelate metal ions including iron. In individuals with iron deficiency, high-dose chronic flavonoid intake could theoretically worsen iron status. Take apigenin separately from iron supplements or iron-rich meals by 2 hours.

**Not observed:**

- **No dependency or withdrawal** — Apigenin does not produce physical dependence, and discontinuation does not cause rebound insomnia or anxiety.
- **No tolerance** — Efficacy is maintained with chronic daily use.
- **No cognitive impairment** — Unlike full benzodiazepine agonists, apigenin does not impair memory, learning, or executive function at standard doses.
- **No respiratory depression** — As a partial GABA-A agonist, apigenin does not suppress respiration, unlike benzodiazepines.
