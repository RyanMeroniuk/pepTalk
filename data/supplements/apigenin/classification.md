# Apigenin
## Classification

- **Category:** Flavonoid / Natural Anxiolytic
- **Subcategory:** Sleep Support & NAD+ Preservation
- **Type:** Flavone (a subclass of flavonoids)
- **Chemical Name:** 4',5,7-trihydroxyflavone
- **Primary System:** Central nervous system (GABAergic modulation), NAD+ metabolism (CD38 inhibition)
- **Regulatory Status:** Dietary supplement (USA). Generally Recognized as Safe as a food component.
- **Naturally Occurring:** Yes — abundant in chamomile (Matricaria chamomilla), parsley, celery, and other plants
- **Notable Endorsement:** Popularized for sleep by neuroscientist Andrew Huberman as part of his sleep stack (magnesium threonate + L-theanine + apigenin)
