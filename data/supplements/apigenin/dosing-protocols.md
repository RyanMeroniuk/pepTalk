# Apigenin
## Dosing Protocols

- **Sleep support (Huberman stack)** — 50mg apigenin + 200mg magnesium threonate + 100-400mg L-theanine, taken 30-60 minutes before bed. This is the popularized "sleep cocktail" from the Huberman Lab podcast. The apigenin provides mild GABAergic sedation, magnesium threonate crosses the blood-brain barrier for neural calming, and L-theanine promotes alpha waves and reduces mental chatter.
- **Sleep support (standalone)** — 50mg apigenin, 30-60 minutes before bed. Start here if new to apigenin. Can increase to 100mg if 50mg is insufficient, but most people find 50mg effective.
- **Anxiety reduction** — 50-100mg, taken as needed or as a daily regimen. Can be taken 1-2 times daily. For chronic anxiety, consistent daily use provides more stable GABA modulation.
- **NAD+ preservation** — 50-100mg daily. Timing is less critical for CD38 inhibition — apigenin can be taken morning or evening. For combined sleep + NAD+ goals, evening dosing covers both.
- **Anti-inflammatory support** — 50-100mg daily. Consistent daily use is important for sustained NF-kB suppression.
- **Chamomile tea equivalent** — 2-3 strong cups of chamomile tea (steeped 10-15 minutes with a lid to prevent volatile compound loss) provides approximately 10-30mg of apigenin. While below supplemental doses, the combination of apigenin, bisabolol, and the tea ritual itself provides meaningful relaxation.

**Timing:**

- **For sleep:** 30-60 minutes before bed
- **For anxiety:** As needed, or on a consistent daily schedule
- **For NAD+ preservation:** Any time of day; evening is convenient if also using for sleep
- **With or without food:** Take with a small fat-containing snack or fish oil capsule for best absorption

**Dose range in research:**

- Clinical studies on chamomile extract use 220-1,500mg of chamomile standardized to 1.2% apigenin (approximately 3-18mg apigenin per dose)
- The 50mg isolated apigenin dose is based on extrapolation from chamomile research and practical experience rather than large-scale dose-finding trials
- Doses up to 100mg daily are generally well-tolerated
- Higher doses (200mg+) have limited safety data in humans

**Important note for women:** Some sources suggest high-dose apigenin may have estrogenic or anti-estrogenic effects depending on concentration and context. Women who are pregnant, breastfeeding, or have estrogen-sensitive conditions should consult a physician before using supplemental apigenin beyond amounts found in food and tea.
