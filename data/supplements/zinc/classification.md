# Zinc
## Classification

- **Category:** Essential Trace Mineral
- **Type:** Micronutrient
- **Essentiality:** Cannot be synthesized by the body — must be obtained through diet or supplementation
- **Biological role:** Cofactor for over 300 enzymes; structural component of proteins and cell membranes
- **Storage:** No dedicated zinc storage system in the body; requires consistent daily intake
- **RDA:** 11mg/day (adult males), 8mg/day (adult females), 11-12mg/day (pregnant/lactating women)
- **Upper Tolerable Intake Level (UL):** 40mg/day for adults
- **Deficiency prevalence:** Estimated 2 billion people worldwide are zinc-deficient; ~12% of the US population is at risk of inadequate intake
