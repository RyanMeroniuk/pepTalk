# Zinc
## Natural Food Sources

### Top Zinc-Rich Foods (per standard serving)

**Animal Sources (highest bioavailability):**
- **Oysters (Eastern, cooked):** 74mg per 3oz serving — the single richest food source of zinc by a wide margin
- **Beef chuck roast (braised):** 7mg per 3oz serving
- **Crab (Alaska king, cooked):** 6.5mg per 3oz serving
- **Beef patty (ground, 85% lean):** 5.3mg per 3oz serving
- **Lobster (cooked):** 3.4mg per 3oz serving
- **Pork chop (loin, cooked):** 2.9mg per 3oz serving
- **Dark meat chicken (cooked):** 2.4mg per 3oz serving
- **Lamb (shoulder, braised):** 4.5mg per 3oz serving
- **Turkey (dark meat, cooked):** 2.0mg per 3oz serving
- **Sardines (canned in oil):** 1.5mg per 3oz serving

**Dairy and Eggs:**
- **Swiss cheese:** 1.2mg per 1oz serving
- **Cheddar cheese:** 1.0mg per 1oz serving
- **Yogurt (plain, whole milk):** 1.4mg per 8oz serving
- **Whole egg:** 0.6mg per large egg
- **Milk (whole):** 1.0mg per 8oz serving

**Plant Sources (lower bioavailability due to phytates):**
- **Pumpkin seeds (pepitas):** 2.2mg per 1oz serving
- **Hemp seeds:** 3.0mg per 3 tablespoons
- **Cashews (dry roasted):** 1.6mg per 1oz serving
- **Chickpeas (cooked):** 1.3mg per 1/2 cup
- **Lentils (cooked):** 1.3mg per 1/2 cup
- **Black beans (cooked):** 1.0mg per 1/2 cup
- **Oatmeal (instant, plain):** 1.1mg per packet
- **Quinoa (cooked):** 1.0mg per 1/2 cup
- **Shiitake mushrooms (cooked):** 1.0mg per 1/2 cup
- **Tofu (firm):** 0.9mg per 1/2 cup
- **Dark chocolate (70-85% cacao):** 0.9mg per 1oz

**Fortified Foods:**
- **Fortified breakfast cereals:** 2.8-15mg per serving (varies widely by brand)

### Important Notes
- **Animal-source zinc** is approximately 2-3x more bioavailable than plant-source zinc because plant foods contain phytates that inhibit absorption
- **Vegetarians and vegans** may need to consume up to 50% more zinc than the RDA to compensate for lower bioavailability
- **Soaking, sprouting, and fermenting** grains and legumes can reduce phytate content and improve zinc absorption by 20-50%
