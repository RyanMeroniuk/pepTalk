# Zinc
## Dosing Protocols

### Daily Maintenance
- **Men:** 15-30mg elemental zinc/day
- **Women:** 8-15mg elemental zinc/day
- **Upper Tolerable Intake Level (UL):** 40mg/day for adults (chronic use)
- **Form:** Zinc picolinate or bisglycinate for best absorption

### Specific Protocols

**Immune support (daily prevention):**
- 15-25mg elemental zinc/day (picolinate or citrate)
- Take with food to reduce nausea

**Acute cold/flu protocol:**
- Zinc gluconate or acetate lozenges: 13-23mg per lozenge, every 2-3 hours while awake
- Total daily dose: 75-100mg/day for up to 2 weeks
- Must start within 24 hours of symptom onset for maximum benefit
- Do NOT exceed 2 weeks at this dose

**Testosterone optimization:**
- 25-30mg elemental zinc/day (picolinate)
- Pair with 2-3mg copper to prevent depletion
- Allow 8-12 weeks for measurable hormonal changes

**Gut healing (zinc carnosine):**
- 75mg zinc carnosine (~16mg elemental zinc) twice daily
- Take on an empty stomach, 30 minutes before meals
- Protocol duration: 8-12 weeks

**Acne protocol:**
- 30mg elemental zinc/day (gluconate or picolinate)
- Expect results at 8-12 weeks
- Reduce to 15mg/day maintenance after clearing

### Timing
- **With food** is generally recommended to minimize nausea — especially for zinc sulfate
- **Away from grains, legumes, and dairy** by at least 2 hours for optimal absorption (phytates and calcium compete)
- **Morning or early afternoon** preferred — some people report vivid dreams or sleep disruption when taking zinc at night
- **Separate from iron and calcium supplements** by at least 2 hours (competitive absorption)
- **Separate from copper supplements** by at least 2 hours for independent absorption, though taking a combined zinc+copper formula is fine

### Cycling
- At doses under 25mg/day, no cycling is necessary
- At 30-40mg/day, consider 5 days on / 2 days off, or cycle 8 weeks on / 2 weeks off
- Always include copper at any dose above 15mg/day for more than 4 weeks
- Test serum zinc and copper levels every 6 months if supplementing long-term

### Populations Requiring Higher Doses
- **Vegetarians/vegans:** May need 50% more zinc than omnivores due to phytate-related absorption inhibition (12-20mg/day)
- **Pregnant women:** 11-12mg/day (RDA); consult provider before exceeding
- **Athletes:** 25-30mg/day to compensate for sweat losses
