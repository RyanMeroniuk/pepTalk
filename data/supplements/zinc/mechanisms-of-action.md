# Zinc
## Mechanisms of Action

### Enzymatic Cofactor
- **300+ enzymes** depend on zinc for catalytic activity, including carbonic anhydrase, alcohol dehydrogenase, and RNA polymerase
- Zinc participates in all six classes of enzymes (oxidoreductases, transferases, hydrolases, lyases, isomerases, ligases)

### Zinc Finger Proteins
- **Zinc fingers** are structural motifs in proteins that bind DNA and regulate gene transcription
- Over 2,500 transcription factors in the human genome are zinc finger proteins
- These regulate cell proliferation, differentiation, apoptosis, and immune response

### Immune Modulation
- **Thymulin activation:** Zinc is required for the biological activity of thymulin, the thymic hormone that drives T-cell maturation
- **NK cell function:** Zinc supports natural killer cell cytotoxic activity
- **Cytokine regulation:** Modulates production of IL-2, IL-6, TNF-alpha, and interferon-gamma
- **NF-kB pathway:** Zinc inhibits NF-kB signaling, reducing inflammatory gene expression
- **Antiviral activity:** Zinc ions directly inhibit viral RNA-dependent RNA polymerase (shown with rhinoviruses and coronaviruses in vitro)

### Antioxidant Defense
- **Cu/Zn-SOD (SOD1):** Zinc is a structural component of copper-zinc superoxide dismutase, which neutralizes superoxide radicals in the cytoplasm
- **Metallothionein induction:** Zinc stimulates production of metallothioneins, which scavenge free radicals and sequester toxic metals
- **Nrf2 activation:** Zinc activates the Nrf2 antioxidant response pathway, upregulating glutathione and other protective enzymes

### Hormonal Regulation
- **Testosterone synthesis:** Zinc is required for Leydig cell function and the conversion of androstenedione to testosterone via 17-beta-hydroxysteroid dehydrogenase
- **Aromatase inhibition:** Zinc inhibits aromatase, the enzyme that converts testosterone to estrogen
- **5-alpha-reductase modulation:** Zinc modulates the enzyme that converts testosterone to DHT
- **Insulin signaling:** Zinc is involved in insulin synthesis, storage, and secretion from pancreatic beta cells

### DNA and Cell Division
- **DNA synthesis and repair:** Zinc is required for DNA polymerase activity and the function of DNA repair enzymes like poly(ADP-ribose) polymerase (PARP)
- **Cell division:** Essential for normal cell cycle progression; deficiency leads to cell cycle arrest
- **Apoptosis regulation:** Zinc influences caspase activity and programmed cell death pathways

### Gut Barrier Integrity
- **Tight junction regulation:** Zinc maintains epithelial barrier function by supporting tight junction protein expression (occludin, ZO-1, claudins)
- **Intestinal cell turnover:** Supports rapid renewal of intestinal epithelial cells
