# Zinc
## Benefits

### Immune Defense
- **Reduced cold duration:** Zinc lozenges (75mg+ elemental zinc/day) taken within 24 hours of symptom onset reduce cold duration by an average of 33% (Cochrane review, 2013)
- **Infection resistance:** Zinc supplementation reduces incidence of pneumonia, diarrhea, and malaria in deficient populations
- **T-cell function:** Restores thymulin activity and T-cell mediated immunity in deficient individuals

### Testosterone and Male Health
- **Testosterone levels:** Men with marginal zinc deficiency who supplemented with 30mg/day for 6 months showed significant increases in serum testosterone (Prasad et al., 1996)
- **Sperm quality:** Zinc supplementation (66mg/day) combined with folic acid improved sperm count by 74% in subfertile men (Wong et al., 2002)
- **Prostate health:** The prostate contains the highest zinc concentration of any soft tissue; adequate zinc supports normal prostate function

### Skin Health and Wound Healing
- **Acne reduction:** 30mg elemental zinc/day (as gluconate) reduced acne lesion count by ~33% over 3 months (Dreno et al., 2001)
- **Wound repair:** Zinc supplementation accelerates wound healing in deficient individuals by supporting collagen synthesis and immune function at the wound site
- **Eczema and dermatitis:** Zinc supports skin barrier function and reduces inflammatory skin conditions

### Cognitive Function
- **Neuroprotection:** Zinc is concentrated in synaptic vesicles and plays a role in neurotransmitter release and synaptic plasticity
- **Memory and learning:** Zinc modulates NMDA receptor function, which is critical for long-term potentiation (memory formation)
- **BDNF support:** Zinc potentiates brain-derived neurotrophic factor signaling

### Metabolic Health
- **Insulin sensitivity:** Zinc supplementation improves fasting blood glucose and HbA1c in diabetic patients (meta-analysis: Jayawardena et al., 2012)
- **Glycemic control:** Zinc is a structural component of insulin and is required for its crystallization and storage

### Eye Health
- **AMD prevention:** The AREDS2 study showed that 80mg zinc oxide combined with antioxidants reduced progression of age-related macular degeneration by 25%

### Gut Health
- **Intestinal barrier:** Zinc carnosine (marketed as PepZin GI) has been shown to repair intestinal permeability and reduce gut inflammation
- **H. pylori:** Zinc carnosine inhibits H. pylori adherence and reduces gastric mucosal damage
