# Zinc
## Peptide Synergies

### BPC-157 (Tissue Repair)
- **Synergy:** Zinc supports wound healing and collagen synthesis, directly complementing BPC-157's tissue repair mechanisms
- **Mechanism:** BPC-157 upregulates growth factor expression (VEGF, FGF2) at injury sites, while zinc provides the structural mineral cofactor needed for collagen cross-linking and matrix metalloproteinase activity
- **Zinc form:** Zinc carnosine (PepZin GI) for gut-related healing alongside BPC-157; zinc picolinate for general tissue repair
- **Protocol note:** Zinc carnosine and BPC-157 together are a potent gut-healing stack for leaky gut, gastritis, or IBD

### TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4)
- **Synergy:** Both zinc and TB-500 promote wound healing and reduce inflammation, but through complementary pathways
- **Mechanism:** TB-500 promotes cell migration and angiogenesis at wound sites; zinc supports the immune cells and enzymatic processes required to rebuild tissue at those sites
- **Protocol note:** Useful combination for recovery from injuries, surgeries, or chronic tendon/ligament issues

### GHK-Cu (Copper Peptide)
- **Synergy:** Critical interaction — zinc and copper must be balanced
- **Mechanism:** GHK-Cu delivers copper for skin regeneration, collagen synthesis, and anti-aging. Since zinc supplementation depletes copper, users of GHK-Cu should be especially mindful of zinc intake.
- **Protocol note:** If using GHK-Cu topically, the interaction is minimal. If using GHK-Cu subcutaneously, monitor copper levels and consider reducing supplemental zinc or ensuring adequate copper co-supplementation. The copper in GHK-Cu is a small amount and should not replace dietary/supplemental copper.

### Thymosin Alpha-1 (Immune Modulation)
- **Synergy:** Zinc is required for thymulin activation, and Thymosin Alpha-1 enhances T-cell function — together they provide comprehensive immune support
- **Mechanism:** Zinc activates thymulin (a thymic hormone), while Thymosin Alpha-1 promotes T-cell maturation and differentiation. Both pathways converge on adaptive immune competence.
- **Protocol note:** Excellent stack for immune compromised individuals, chronic infections, or pre/post illness recovery

### Thymulin
- **Synergy:** Direct biochemical dependency — thymulin is biologically inactive without zinc
- **Mechanism:** Zinc is the required cofactor that converts inactive thymulin (FTS) to its active form (ZnFTS). Supplementing thymulin without adequate zinc status is essentially ineffective.
- **Protocol note:** Ensure zinc status is optimized before and during thymulin peptide use

### LL-37 (Antimicrobial Peptide)
- **Synergy:** Both zinc and LL-37 support innate immune defense against pathogens
- **Mechanism:** Zinc supports neutrophil function, NK cell activity, and antimicrobial peptide expression. LL-37 directly kills bacteria, disrupts biofilms, and modulates the immune response.
- **Protocol note:** Consider this combination for chronic infections, biofilm-related conditions, or immune recovery

### CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin (GH Secretagogues)
- **Synergy:** Zinc supports growth hormone production and IGF-1 signaling
- **Mechanism:** Zinc is involved in GH synthesis and release from the pituitary. Zinc deficiency blunts GH response. Adequate zinc status ensures maximum responsiveness to GH secretagogue peptides.
- **Protocol note:** Ensure zinc is optimized before starting GH peptides to get full benefit

### MK-677 (Ibutamoren)
- **Synergy:** Similar to CJC-1295/Ipamorelin — zinc supports the GH/IGF-1 axis that MK-677 stimulates
- **Additional note:** MK-677 increases appetite and can elevate blood glucose; zinc's role in insulin signaling may help mitigate the metabolic effects
- **Protocol note:** Take zinc separately from MK-677 (which is typically taken at bedtime); zinc is better taken with a meal earlier in the day

### Semax and Selank (Nootropics)
- **Synergy:** Zinc modulates NMDA receptor function and BDNF signaling, complementing the cognitive-enhancing effects of Semax (which upregulates BDNF) and Selank (which modulates GABA)
- **Mechanism:** Zinc is concentrated in glutamatergic synaptic vesicles and plays a role in synaptic plasticity and memory formation
- **Protocol note:** A reasonable combination for cognitive optimization stacks

### DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide)
- **Synergy:** Zinc supports sleep quality and duration; DSIP promotes delta wave sleep
- **Mechanism:** Zinc is involved in melatonin synthesis (via tryptophan metabolism) and GABA receptor modulation, both of which influence sleep architecture
- **Protocol note:** Note that some individuals report zinc disrupts sleep — if so, take zinc in the morning and DSIP at bedtime

### Semaglutide / Tirzepatide (GLP-1 Agonists)
- **Synergy:** Users of GLP-1 agonists for weight loss are at increased risk of micronutrient deficiencies due to reduced food intake
- **Mechanism:** Caloric restriction and reduced appetite from these peptides can lead to inadequate zinc intake from food alone
- **Protocol note:** Zinc supplementation (15-25mg/day) is recommended for anyone on a GLP-1 agonist to prevent deficiency during the weight loss phase
