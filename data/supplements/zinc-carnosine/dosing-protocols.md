# Zinc Carnosine
## Dosing Protocols

- **Gastric ulcer healing** — 75mg zinc carnosine twice daily (150mg total/day), taken between meals on an empty stomach. This is the Japanese clinical protocol used in prescription practice. Duration: 8-12 weeks minimum, with endoscopic follow-up.
- **Leaky gut / intestinal permeability repair** — 37.5mg zinc carnosine twice daily (75mg total/day) was the dose used in the Mahmood et al. intestinal permeability study. Many practitioners use the higher 75mg twice daily dose for therapeutic gut repair.
- **NSAID gastroprotection** — 75mg zinc carnosine taken 30 minutes before or with each NSAID dose. If taking NSAIDs twice daily, take zinc carnosine twice daily. This protocol was shown to prevent indomethacin-induced permeability increases.
- **General gut health maintenance** — 75mg zinc carnosine once daily, between meals. A lower maintenance dose for individuals with history of gut issues who have completed a therapeutic course.
- **H. pylori support (adjunctive)** — 75mg zinc carnosine twice daily alongside standard triple or quadruple therapy. Continue for the duration of eradication therapy (typically 10-14 days) and for 2-4 weeks after.
- **Chemotherapy-induced oral mucositis** — 75mg zinc carnosine dissolved in mouth or swallowed, three times daily. Begin before chemotherapy cycle and continue throughout treatment. Under oncologist supervision.
- **Exercise-induced gut permeability** — 75mg zinc carnosine 1-2 hours before intense training or competition. Particularly relevant for endurance athletes and those with "runner's gut."

**Timing guidelines:**

- **Between meals (empty stomach)** — Preferred for ulcer healing and mucosal repair. Maximizes direct mucosal contact.
- **With meals** — Preferred when used for NSAID protection or if stomach upset occurs on empty stomach.
- **Twice daily** — Most clinical protocols use a divided dose (morning and evening) rather than a single large dose.

**Duration of use:**

- **Therapeutic gut repair:** 8-12 weeks, then reassess
- **NSAID protection:** Ongoing, as long as NSAID use continues
- **Maintenance:** Can be used long-term at lower doses, but monitor total zinc intake

**Zinc intake monitoring:**

- 150mg zinc carnosine provides approximately 34mg elemental zinc
- The upper tolerable intake for elemental zinc is 40mg/day for adults
- Account for zinc from diet (oysters, red meat, pumpkin seeds) and other supplements (multivitamins, standalone zinc)
- Long-term zinc supplementation above 40mg/day can deplete copper. Consider adding 1-2mg copper if using zinc carnosine long-term
