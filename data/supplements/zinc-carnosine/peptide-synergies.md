# Zinc Carnosine
## Peptide Synergies

- **BPC-157** — This is perhaps the most powerful gut-healing combination available. BPC-157 promotes angiogenesis (new blood vessel formation) in damaged tissue, accelerates mucosal healing, protects against NSAID-induced gut damage, and has documented anti-ulcer effects. Zinc carnosine provides localized mucosal protection, tight junction stabilization, and growth factor stimulation. Together, they address gut repair from complementary mechanisms: BPC-157 drives tissue regeneration while zinc carnosine provides the protective and structural support for that regeneration. Used together in protocols for leaky gut, IBD, NSAID damage, and ulcer healing.
- **KPV** — KPV is an anti-inflammatory tripeptide that specifically targets intestinal inflammation through NF-kB suppression in gut epithelial cells. Zinc carnosine also suppresses NF-kB locally. The combination provides potent, localized anti-inflammatory action in the GI tract, making it highly relevant for IBD, IBS, and chronic gut inflammation. KPV addresses the inflammatory component while zinc carnosine provides structural repair.
- **TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4)** — TB-500 promotes tissue repair and reduces inflammation systemically. While its primary applications are musculoskeletal, its wound-healing and anti-inflammatory properties extend to GI tissue. Combined with zinc carnosine's targeted mucosal repair, this pairing supports comprehensive tissue healing for individuals with both gut and musculoskeletal repair needs.
- **VIP (Vasoactive Intestinal Peptide)** — VIP supports gut motility, mucosal immune function, and has anti-inflammatory effects throughout the GI tract. It is also used in CIRS/mold illness protocols where gut dysfunction is common. Zinc carnosine provides the structural mucosal repair to complement VIP's functional restoration.
- **Larazotide** — Larazotide is a tight junction regulator that modulates zonulin signaling to reduce intestinal permeability. Zinc carnosine independently stabilizes tight junction proteins. The combination provides dual-mechanism tight junction support — Larazotide modulates the signaling pathway while zinc carnosine provides the structural proteins. Highly relevant for celiac disease and non-celiac gluten sensitivity.
- **GHK-Cu** — GHK-Cu promotes tissue remodeling and collagen synthesis. In the gut context, it supports mucosal tissue architecture alongside zinc carnosine's direct mucosal repair actions.
- **Thymosin Alpha-1** — Supports immune modulation and T-cell function. Since a significant portion of immune activity occurs in the GALT, zinc carnosine's gut-healing effects create a healthier environment for immune regulation by Thymosin Alpha-1.
