# Zinc Carnosine
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

- **Animal protein** — Amino acids from meat, poultry, and fish (particularly cysteine and histidine) enhance zinc absorption by forming soluble zinc-amino acid chelates in the gut. Consuming zinc carnosine alongside a meal containing animal protein may improve overall zinc utilization, though the primary therapeutic action is local mucosal contact.
- **Fermented foods** — Fermentation reduces phytate content in grains and legumes, improving zinc bioavailability from the overall diet. Sourdough bread, tempeh, miso, and fermented vegetables all have reduced phytate loads.
- **Bone broth** — Rich in glutamine and glycine that independently support gut mucosal health, complementing zinc carnosine's repair mechanisms. The collagen-derived amino acids provide building blocks for mucosal tissue regeneration.
- **Cabbage and cabbage juice** — Contains S-methylmethionine (vitamin U), a traditional ulcer-healing compound. Cabbage juice combined with zinc carnosine provides synergistic mucosal support.
- **Ginger** — Supports gastric motility and has anti-inflammatory effects in the GI tract. Ginger tea or fresh ginger consumed alongside zinc carnosine provides complementary gastroprotection.
- **Vitamin A-rich foods** — Vitamin A is essential for intestinal epithelial cell turnover and mucosal immune function. Sweet potatoes, carrots, liver, and leafy greens support the mucosal environment that zinc carnosine is repairing.
- **Omega-3 fatty acids** — Anti-inflammatory fats from salmon, sardines, mackerel, and fish oil reduce GI inflammation, creating a more favorable environment for zinc carnosine's repair actions.
- **Manuka honey** — Has documented gastroprotective and antimicrobial properties, including activity against H. pylori. Combining manuka honey with zinc carnosine provides complementary mucosal protection and antimicrobial support.
- **Turmeric/curcumin** — Anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties that complement zinc carnosine's NF-kB suppression in the gut. Take with black pepper (piperine) and fat for curcumin absorption.
