# Zinc Carnosine
## Classification

- **Category:** Gut Health / Mucosal Repair
- **Type:** Chelated zinc-amino acid complex (zinc bound to L-carnosine in a 1:1 molar ratio)
- **Chemical name:** Zinc L-carnosine (also known as polaprezinc, Z-103)
- **Chemical class:** Metal-dipeptide chelate
- **Regulatory status:** Prescription medication in Japan (since 1994) for gastric ulcers; dietary supplement in USA, EU, and most other countries
- **Primary function:** Targeted repair and protection of the gastrointestinal mucosal lining — distinct from regular zinc supplements
- **Key distinction:** Zinc carnosine is NOT interchangeable with zinc picolinate, zinc citrate, or other zinc forms. The carnosine chelation fundamentally changes its mechanism, delivery, and therapeutic application.
