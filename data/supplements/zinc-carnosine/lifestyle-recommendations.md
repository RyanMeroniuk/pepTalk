# Zinc Carnosine
## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Implement a comprehensive gut-healing protocol** — Zinc carnosine is most effective as part of a structured gut-healing approach that addresses multiple factors: remove triggers (food sensitivities, excessive NSAIDs, alcohol), restore digestive capacity (enzymes, HCl if appropriate), reinoculate with probiotics, and repair the lining (zinc carnosine, glutamine, bone broth). The "4R" framework (Remove, Replace, Reinoculate, Repair) provides a useful structure.
- **Prioritize anti-inflammatory nutrition** — Support zinc carnosine's mucosal repair with an anti-inflammatory diet: fatty fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel) 2-3 times weekly, abundant vegetables, olive oil, bone broth, turmeric, ginger, and fermented foods. Reduce or eliminate ultra-processed foods, seed oils, refined sugar, and alcohol during the healing phase.
- **Manage stress actively** — Stress-induced cortisol directly increases intestinal permeability and reduces mucosal repair capacity. Daily stress management (meditation, breathwork, walking in nature, gentle yoga) is not optional during a gut-healing protocol — it is foundational.
- **Eat mindfully and chew thoroughly** — Mechanical digestion begins in the mouth. Chewing food thoroughly reduces the digestive burden on the stomach and small intestine, decreasing irritation to damaged mucosa. Eat slowly, without screens, and stop before feeling overly full.
- **Drink bone broth regularly** — 1-2 cups of bone broth daily provides glutamine, glycine, proline, and collagen that directly support intestinal mucosal regeneration. Bone broth complements zinc carnosine's repair mechanisms.
- **Avoid late-night eating** — Allow 2-3 hours between your last meal and bedtime. Lying down with a full stomach increases gastric acid reflux and mucosal exposure to acid, opposing zinc carnosine's protective effects.
- **Stay hydrated between meals** — Adequate hydration supports mucosal health and digestive function. Drink most water between meals rather than with meals to avoid diluting digestive secretions.
- **Exercise moderately** — Moderate exercise supports gut motility and reduces systemic inflammation. Avoid intense exercise during active gut healing (intense training temporarily increases gut permeability). Walking, yoga, swimming, and light resistance training are ideal during repair phases.
- **Track symptoms in a journal** — Document daily GI symptoms (pain, bloating, stool quality, food triggers) during your zinc carnosine protocol. This helps identify progress, remaining triggers, and optimal timing for dose adjustments.
- **Sleep 7-9 hours consistently** — Tissue repair occurs primarily during sleep. Growth hormone secretion during deep sleep supports mucosal regeneration. Prioritize sleep quality and duration during any healing protocol.
