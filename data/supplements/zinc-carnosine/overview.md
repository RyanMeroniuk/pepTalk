# Zinc Carnosine
## Overview

Zinc carnosine (also called polaprezinc or Z-103) is a specific chelated compound in which one zinc ion is bound to one L-carnosine molecule. This is not simply "zinc + carnosine taken together" — the chelation creates a distinct molecule with unique pharmacological properties, particularly its affinity for damaged gastrointestinal tissue.

**Why it matters:**

- **Targeted gut mucosal repair** — Unlike other zinc forms that are absorbed systemically, zinc carnosine adheres preferentially to damaged and ulcerated GI tissue. It concentrates at the site of injury, providing localized zinc delivery for tissue repair. This targeted delivery is the reason it was approved as an anti-ulcer drug in Japan in 1994.
- **Gastric ulcer healing** — Clinical trials in Japan demonstrate that zinc carnosine heals gastric ulcers with efficacy comparable to standard anti-ulcer drugs. It stimulates mucus secretion, promotes epithelial cell migration, enhances growth factor expression, and reduces gastric acid-induced damage.
- **Intestinal permeability reduction** — Zinc carnosine reduces intestinal permeability ("leaky gut") by stabilizing tight junction proteins and supporting mucosal integrity. A clinical study showed it prevented the 3-fold increase in intestinal permeability caused by indomethacin (an NSAID).
- **Anti-inflammatory action in the gut** — Inhibits NF-kB activation in GI epithelial cells, reduces pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-alpha, IL-8, IL-1beta), and suppresses oxidative stress at the mucosal surface.
- **H. pylori support** — Zinc carnosine has bacteriostatic effects against Helicobacter pylori in vitro and enhances the efficacy of standard H. pylori eradication therapy (triple/quadruple therapy).
- **NSAID gastroprotection** — Zinc carnosine protects the gastric and intestinal mucosa from NSAID-induced damage, making it a valuable co-supplement for anyone requiring chronic NSAID use.
- **Different from regular zinc** — Standard zinc supplements (zinc picolinate, zinc citrate, zinc gluconate) are absorbed into the bloodstream for systemic zinc functions (immune support, testosterone, enzyme cofactor). Zinc carnosine's value is specifically in its GI-targeted delivery. They serve different purposes and are not interchangeable.
- **Synergy with gut-healing peptides** — Zinc carnosine pairs exceptionally well with BPC-157 and KPV for comprehensive gut healing protocols, addressing repair from multiple complementary mechanisms.
