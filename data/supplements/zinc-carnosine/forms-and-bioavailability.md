# Zinc Carnosine
## Forms and Bioavailability

- **Zinc carnosine capsules (standard)** — The most common supplement form. Each capsule typically provides 75mg of zinc carnosine complex, yielding approximately 16mg of elemental zinc and 59mg of L-carnosine. Standard dosing is one capsule twice daily (150mg total zinc carnosine).
- **Zinc carnosine powder** — Available as bulk powder for those who prefer custom dosing or adding to beverages. Can be mixed with water or taken with food. Useful for individuals who cannot swallow capsules.
- **PepZin GI (branded form)** — A patented, clinically researched form of zinc carnosine used in many commercial supplements. PepZin GI is the form used in several key clinical trials, including the intestinal permeability study. If available, this is the preferred form due to research validation.
- **Zinc carnosine chewable tablets** — Some formulations are designed to dissolve in the mouth and coat the upper GI tract, providing direct mucosal contact before reaching the stomach.

**Bioavailability considerations:**

- **Slow dissociation is the feature** — Unlike regular zinc forms designed for rapid systemic absorption, zinc carnosine's therapeutic value lies in its SLOW dissociation in the GI tract. This slow release maintains a sustained presence of zinc at the mucosal surface. Do not evaluate it by systemic zinc absorption metrics.
- **GI-targeted delivery** — Approximately 60% of zinc carnosine remains intact in the stomach after 30 minutes, allowing prolonged contact with gastric and intestinal mucosa. Regular zinc supplements would be absorbed and cleared much faster.
- **Take between meals for ulcer/mucosal healing** — When using zinc carnosine for gut repair, taking it between meals or on an empty stomach maximizes mucosal contact time. The empty stomach provides less buffer and allows direct mucosal adhesion.
- **Take with meals for NSAID protection** — If using zinc carnosine to protect against NSAID damage, take it with or just before the NSAID-containing meal.
- **Not interchangeable with zinc + carnosine taken separately** — Taking zinc picolinate and L-carnosine as separate supplements does NOT replicate zinc carnosine's effects. The chelated molecule has distinct pharmacokinetics and tissue-targeting properties that the individual components do not possess.
- **Elemental zinc content** — 150mg of zinc carnosine contains approximately 34mg of elemental zinc. This contributes to total daily zinc intake and should be considered when calculating zinc from all sources (diet + supplements) to avoid exceeding the upper tolerable limit (40mg elemental zinc/day for adults).
