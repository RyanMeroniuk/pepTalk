# Zinc Carnosine
## Benefits

- **Gastric ulcer healing** — Clinical trials demonstrate zinc carnosine (150mg/day) heals gastric ulcers with efficacy comparable to standard anti-ulcer medications. In Japan, it has been prescribed for this purpose since 1994. Endoscopic studies confirm accelerated ulcer closure and mucosal regeneration.
- **Intestinal permeability reduction** — A clinical study by Mahmood et al. showed zinc carnosine (37.5mg twice daily) prevented the 3-fold increase in intestinal permeability caused by the NSAID indomethacin. This is among the strongest evidence for any supplement's ability to protect the gut barrier.
- **NSAID-induced gut damage protection** — Zinc carnosine significantly reduces the gastric and small intestinal damage caused by NSAIDs (ibuprofen, naproxen, aspirin, indomethacin). Relevant for athletes, chronic pain patients, or anyone requiring regular NSAID use.
- **H. pylori eradication support** — When added to standard H. pylori triple therapy, zinc carnosine improves eradication rates and reduces treatment-related GI side effects. It addresses H. pylori through both direct bacteriostatic action and mucosal protection.
- **Gastritis symptom relief** — Reduces epigastric pain, nausea, heartburn, and bloating associated with chronic gastritis. Improvements are typically noted within 2-4 weeks.
- **Leaky gut repair** — By stabilizing tight junction proteins and enhancing mucosal integrity, zinc carnosine addresses the structural basis of increased intestinal permeability. This has implications for food sensitivities, autoimmune conditions, and systemic inflammation originating from the gut.
- **Oral mucositis prevention** — Clinical studies in cancer patients show zinc carnosine reduces the severity of chemotherapy and radiation-induced oral mucositis. The mucosal-protective mechanism extends to the oral cavity.
- **Taste disorder improvement** — Zinc carnosine improves or resolves taste disturbances (dysgeusia), which are often zinc-deficiency-related. Used in Japanese clinical practice for this indication.
- **Small intestinal bacterial overgrowth (SIBO) support** — By strengthening the intestinal barrier and reducing inflammation, zinc carnosine creates a healthier environment for microbial balance. Often included in comprehensive SIBO management protocols.
- **Exercise-induced GI distress** — Athletes frequently experience gut permeability increases during intense exercise ("runner's gut"). Zinc carnosine taken before training may reduce exercise-induced GI symptoms and permeability changes.
