# CoQ10 (Coenzyme Q10)
## Classification

- **Category:** Mitochondrial support / antioxidant
- **Supplement class:** Fat-soluble quinone (benzoquinone)
- **Also known as:** Ubiquinone (oxidized form), Ubiquinol (reduced/active form), Coenzyme Q10, CoQ10, Q10
- **Endogenous status:** Naturally produced by the body; production declines with age (significant drop after age 40)
- **Regulatory status:** Sold as a dietary supplement (not FDA-approved as a drug); widely available OTC
- **Primary function:** Electron carrier in the mitochondrial electron transport chain (Complex I to Complex III); essential for ATP production
- **Solubility:** Fat-soluble (lipophilic) — requires dietary fat for absorption
