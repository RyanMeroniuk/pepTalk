# CoQ10 (Coenzyme Q10)
## Foods to Avoid

- **Taking on an empty stomach:** CoQ10 absorption drops dramatically without dietary fat. Never take CoQ10 with just water or on an empty stomach
- **High-fiber meals (timing):** Very high-fiber meals (large salads, bran cereals, psyllium husk) can bind fat-soluble nutrients and reduce absorption. Avoid taking CoQ10 with an exclusively high-fiber, low-fat meal
- **Excessive alcohol:** Chronic alcohol consumption depletes CoQ10 levels and damages mitochondria. Alcohol also impairs liver function, which is where CoQ10 conversion (ubiquinone to ubiquinol) occurs
- **Excessive sugar and refined carbohydrates:** High glycemic foods increase oxidative stress, consuming more CoQ10 as an antioxidant and leaving less available for mitochondrial energy production
- **Trans fats and heavily processed oils:** Hydrogenated oils increase oxidative stress and mitochondrial dysfunction, working against CoQ10's benefits
- **Grapefruit (moderate concern):** Grapefruit inhibits CYP3A4 enzymes. While CoQ10 itself is not primarily metabolized by CYP3A4, if you are taking CoQ10 alongside medications that are (statins, calcium channel blockers), grapefruit can alter drug levels and complicate dosing
- **Excessive caffeine (timing):** Large amounts of coffee taken simultaneously with CoQ10 may speed gastric emptying and reduce absorption time. Space coffee and CoQ10 by 30-60 minutes if consuming large amounts of caffeine
