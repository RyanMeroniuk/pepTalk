# CoQ10 (Coenzyme Q10)
## Forms and Bioavailability

- **Ubiquinone (oxidized form):** The conventional, less expensive form. Must be converted to ubiquinol in the body before it can function as an antioxidant. Conversion efficiency declines with age. Suitable for younger individuals (under 40) with healthy metabolic function
- **Ubiquinol (reduced/active form):** The bioactive, pre-converted form. Does not require enzymatic reduction. Significantly better absorbed — studies show 3-8x higher plasma levels compared to equivalent doses of ubiquinone. Recommended for anyone over 40, those on statins, or anyone with compromised health
- **Age cutoff guideline (~40 years):** Under 40 with good health — ubiquinone is generally adequate and more cost-effective. Over 40, on statins, or with chronic illness — ubiquinol is strongly preferred because the body's ability to convert ubiquinone to ubiquinol diminishes
- **Softgel vs powder capsule:** Softgels (oil-based) are significantly better absorbed than dry powder capsules. CoQ10 is fat-soluble and dissolves poorly in water. Oil-based delivery (soybean oil, MCT oil) dramatically improves bioavailability
- **Nano/microemulsion formulations:** Some brands use nano-emulsification or solubilized forms (e.g., Q-Gel, Qunol) that improve water dispersibility and absorption by 2-3x over standard powder
- **Crystal-free formulations:** Standard CoQ10 can form crystals that resist absorption. Crystal-free processing (used by premium brands) keeps CoQ10 in an amorphous state for better dissolution
- **Liposomal CoQ10:** Encapsulated in phospholipid vesicles for enhanced cellular uptake. Emerging but limited comparative data vs ubiquinol softgels
- **Absorption rate:** Only about 2-5% of oral CoQ10 (powder form) is absorbed. Ubiquinol softgels achieve roughly 6-10% absorption. Taking with dietary fat increases absorption by 3-6x
- **Key brands to note:** Kaneka is the primary manufacturer of pharmaceutical-grade ubiquinol (Kaneka QH) and ubiquinone (Kaneka Q10). Most reputable supplement brands source from Kaneka
