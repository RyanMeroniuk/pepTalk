# CoQ10 (Coenzyme Q10)
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

CoQ10 is fat-soluble — absorption increases 3-6x when taken with dietary fat. Always take CoQ10 with a meal containing fat.

- **Healthy fats (critical for absorption):**
  - **Extra virgin olive oil** — monounsaturated fat enhances absorption and provides complementary antioxidants
  - **Avocado** — contains its own CoQ10 plus healthy fats for absorption of supplemental CoQ10
  - **Coconut oil / MCT oil** — medium-chain triglycerides may enhance absorption speed
  - **Nuts (almonds, walnuts, macadamias)** — fat content supports absorption; walnuts add omega-3s
  - **Fatty fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel)** — omega-3 fats plus endogenous CoQ10 in the fish itself
  - **Eggs (whole, with yolk)** — the fat and phospholipids in yolk aid CoQ10 absorption
  - **Grass-fed butter or ghee** — saturated fat effectively solubilizes CoQ10
- **Black pepper (piperine):** Piperine inhibits glucuronidation and intestinal P-glycoprotein efflux, potentially improving CoQ10 bioavailability. Add black pepper to meals taken with CoQ10
- **Lecithin-rich foods:** Egg yolks, soybeans, and sunflower seeds contain phosphatidylcholine, which may help emulsify and absorb fat-soluble nutrients
- **Warm or cooked meals:** CoQ10 dissolves better in warm fats than cold. A warm meal with olive oil or butter is ideal
- **Medium-chain triglycerides (MCT):** MCT oil in coffee or smoothies taken alongside CoQ10 may speed absorption since MCTs are rapidly absorbed via the portal vein
