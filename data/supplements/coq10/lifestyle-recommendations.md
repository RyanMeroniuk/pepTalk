# CoQ10 (Coenzyme Q10)
## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Exercise regularly (moderate intensity):** Regular moderate exercise increases mitochondrial density and CoQ10 utilization efficiency. Aim for 150-300 minutes/week of moderate aerobic activity. CoQ10 supplementation enhances exercise capacity and recovery
- **Prioritize sleep:** Mitochondrial repair and CoQ10 recycling (ubiquinone back to ubiquinol) occur during sleep. Aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep. Poor sleep increases oxidative stress and CoQ10 consumption
- **Manage stress:** Chronic stress elevates cortisol and mitochondrial ROS production, depleting CoQ10 stores. Incorporate stress management: meditation, breathwork, nature exposure, or yoga
- **Eat mitochondria-friendly foods:** Build meals around organ meats, fatty fish, colorful vegetables, and healthy fats. These provide both CoQ10 and cofactors needed for mitochondrial function
- **Limit processed food intake:** Processed foods increase systemic inflammation and oxidative burden, consuming antioxidant reserves including CoQ10
- **Stay hydrated:** Dehydration impairs cellular function and nutrient transport. Adequate hydration supports CoQ10 delivery to tissues
- **Reduce toxin exposure:** Environmental toxins (pesticides, heavy metals, air pollution, household chemicals) damage mitochondria and deplete CoQ10. Choose organic produce when possible, use air filtration, and minimize chemical exposure
- **Cold exposure (emerging):** Brief cold exposure (cold showers, cold plunges) may upregulate mitochondrial biogenesis and improve CoQ10 utilization through activation of PGC-1alpha
- **Sauna / heat therapy:** Regular sauna use improves cardiovascular function and may enhance CoQ10's cardiovascular benefits through complementary mechanisms (heat shock proteins, improved endothelial function)
- **Maintain healthy body composition:** Excess adipose tissue increases systemic inflammation and oxidative stress. Weight management reduces CoQ10 depletion and improves mitochondrial efficiency
- **Combine with complementary supplements:** CoQ10 works synergistically with PQQ (mitochondrial biogenesis), magnesium (ATP requires Mg), omega-3 fatty acids (membrane health), and B vitamins (ETC cofactors)
