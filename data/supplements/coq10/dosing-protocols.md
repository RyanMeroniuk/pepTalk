# CoQ10 (Coenzyme Q10)
## Dosing Protocols

- **General maintenance (healthy adults):** 100-200mg/day ubiquinone or 100mg/day ubiquinol
- **Statin users (critical):** 200-300mg/day ubiquinol. Begin supplementation when starting statin therapy. Do not wait for muscle symptoms to develop
- **Heart failure / cardiovascular support:** 200-400mg/day ubiquinol. The Q-SYMBIO trial used 300mg/day (100mg three times daily)
- **Migraine prevention:** 300mg/day, typically split into 100mg three times daily. Allow 4-12 weeks for full effect
- **Male fertility:** 200-300mg/day for a minimum of 3-6 months (full spermatogenesis cycle is ~74 days)
- **Blood pressure support:** 100-200mg/day
- **Exercise performance:** 100-300mg/day, taken 2-3 hours before training
- **Anti-aging / longevity:** 100-200mg/day ubiquinol
- **Parkinson's disease (high-dose):** 600-1200mg/day (under medical supervision only)
- **Timing:** Take with the largest meal of the day that contains dietary fat. Fat is essential for absorption. Breakfast or lunch preferred; some people report increased energy that may interfere with sleep if taken late
- **Split dosing:** Doses above 200mg should be split into 2-3 doses throughout the day (taken with meals containing fat) to maximize absorption. Single large doses have diminishing returns
- **Loading phase (optional):** For acute deficiency or statin-related depletion, some practitioners recommend 300-600mg/day for the first 2-4 weeks, then reduce to maintenance
- **Onset of effects:** Blood levels reach steady state in 2-3 weeks. Clinical benefits for heart failure and migraines may take 4-12 weeks
- **No known toxicity:** CoQ10 has an excellent safety profile. Doses up to 1200mg/day have been used in clinical trials without serious adverse effects
