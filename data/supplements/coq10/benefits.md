# CoQ10 (Coenzyme Q10)
## Benefits

- **Cardiovascular health:** Improves heart function in congestive heart failure (CHF). The Q-SYMBIO trial showed 43% reduction in cardiovascular death with 300mg/day CoQ10 over 2 years. Reduces hospitalization rates in heart failure patients
- **Blood pressure reduction:** Meta-analyses show systolic BP reduction of 11-17 mmHg and diastolic reduction of 7-10 mmHg at doses of 100-200mg/day
- **Statin side effect mitigation:** Reduces statin-induced myopathy (muscle pain, weakness, cramping) by replenishing depleted CoQ10 stores. Multiple studies show significant reduction in muscle symptoms at 100-200mg/day
- **Exercise performance:** Improves physical performance, reduces fatigue during exercise, and enhances recovery. Particularly beneficial in older adults and those with mitochondrial dysfunction
- **Migraine prevention:** 300mg/day reduced migraine frequency by ~50% in clinical trials. Recognized by the American Academy of Neurology and American Headache Society as "probably effective" for migraine prevention
- **Male fertility:** Improves sperm count, motility, and morphology. Doses of 200-300mg/day for 3-6 months showed significant improvements in semen parameters
- **Neuroprotection:** Slows progression in early Parkinson's disease at high doses (1200mg/day) in some studies. Reduces oxidative damage in neuronal mitochondria
- **Blood sugar support:** Improves glycemic control in type 2 diabetes. Reduces HbA1c and fasting blood glucose. Improves insulin sensitivity
- **Skin aging:** Topical and oral CoQ10 reduces wrinkle depth, improves skin smoothness, and protects against UV-induced oxidative damage
- **Gum health:** Improves periodontal disease outcomes when applied topically or taken orally. Reduces gingival inflammation and bleeding
- **Kidney protection:** Protects against oxidative damage in chronic kidney disease. May slow progression of kidney dysfunction
- **Fatigue reduction:** Significant improvement in fatigue scores across multiple conditions including fibromyalgia, chronic fatigue, and general age-related fatigue
