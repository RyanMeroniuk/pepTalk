# L-Tyrosine
## Side Effects

### Common (Mild, Rare)
- **Generally well tolerated** — tyrosine has a good safety profile at typical doses (500 mg - 2 g)
- **Mild GI discomfort** — nausea or stomach upset, more likely at high doses (7+ g) or on a very empty stomach in sensitive people
- **Headache** — occasionally reported, usually at higher doses

### Uncommon
- **Overstimulation / jitteriness** — because tyrosine feeds catecholamine synthesis, sensitive individuals or those taking large doses may feel keyed-up, anxious, or restless (more likely when combined with stimulants)
- **Difficulty sleeping** — if taken too late in the day, the noradrenergic/dopaminergic substrate effect can interfere with sleep onset in some people
- **Increased heart rate / blood pressure** — rare and dose-dependent, via catecholamine pathways

### Population-Specific Cautions
- **Hyperthyroidism / Graves' disease** — tyrosine is a thyroid hormone precursor; supplementation could theoretically increase thyroid hormone production. Use caution or avoid; consult a physician.
- **On thyroid medication (levothyroxine)** — see drug-interactions; theoretical interaction and timing considerations
- **Melanoma history** — tyrosine is a melanin precursor; a theoretical caution exists for those with melanoma, though evidence is limited. Discuss with an oncologist.
- **Bipolar disorder / psychiatric conditions** — catecholamine substrate could theoretically affect mood states; consult a prescriber
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding** — insufficient safety data for supplemental doses; consult a healthcare provider

### Drug-Related Cautions (See drug-interactions)
- **MAOIs** — potentially serious interaction (hypertensive risk via catecholamine pathways); avoid
- **Thyroid medications, levodopa** — interactions possible

### Safety Profile Summary
- **No established toxic dose** in healthy individuals at supplemental ranges; excess tyrosine is metabolized or excreted
- **No liver or kidney toxicity** at supplemental doses in healthy people
- **Not banned** by WADA or athletic organizations
- **Acute, non-cumulative** — effects don't build up dangerously with situational use

### When to Seek Guidance
- If you have **thyroid disease, melanoma history, bipolar disorder**, take **MAOIs, thyroid medication, or levodopa**, or are **pregnant/breastfeeding**, consult a healthcare provider before use
