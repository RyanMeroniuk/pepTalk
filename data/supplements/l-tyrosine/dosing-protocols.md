# L-Tyrosine
## Dosing Protocols

### Acute Cognitive Stress (Primary Use Case)
- **Dose:** 500 mg - 2 g, taken ~30-60 minutes before the stressor
- **Timing:** Before exams, demanding deadlines, high-pressure multitasking, or cognitively loaded work
- **Notes:** This pre-stressor, on-demand approach matches how the evidence is structured — tyrosine is a substrate buffer for an upcoming demand, not a daily maintenance dose

### Research-Style High Dose
- **Dose:** 100-150 mg/kg body weight (~7-13 g for a typical adult)
- **Timing:** ~60 minutes before testing
- **Notes:** This is the dose used in many laboratory/military studies; most people get benefit from far lower practical doses (500 mg - 2 g) and reserve high doses for severe stressors

### Sleep Deprivation / Shift Work
- **Dose:** 2 g, repeated as needed for prolonged wakefulness (military studies used ~150 mg/kg, sometimes split)
- **Timing:** At the onset of the demanding sleep-deprived period, with possible re-dosing
- **Notes:** A non-stimulant aid to preserve alertness and cognition during sleep loss; not a substitute for sleep

### Cold / Physical Stress
- **Dose:** ~100-150 mg/kg before cold or combined-stressor exposure
- **Notes:** Based on environmental-stress research (e.g., Banderet & Lieberman, 1989)

### NALT (N-Acetyl-L-Tyrosine) Adjustment
- **Dose:** Often dosed similarly by label, but because NALT converts to tyrosine less efficiently, a somewhat higher dose may be needed to match free L-tyrosine's effect
- **Notes:** Plain L-tyrosine is preferred for reliability

### Critical Timing Rule
- **Take away from high-protein meals** — competing large neutral amino acids reduce tyrosine's entry into the brain. An empty stomach or a carbohydrate-only pairing is ideal.
- **~30-60 minutes before the stressor** aligns with peak plasma levels

### Frequency & Tolerance
- **Best used situationally, not daily** — tyrosine's effects are acute and tied to stress/depletion; there is little rationale for continuous daily dosing in healthy people
- **No strong tolerance issue**, but daily baseline use shows minimal benefit, so reserve it for demands
- **No loading phase** — effects are felt the same session

### Safety Ceiling
- **Doses up to ~12 g** have been used in research with good tolerability; GI upset and (rarely) headache or overstimulation are the main limiters at very high doses
