# L-Tyrosine
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### Semax + L-Tyrosine (Catecholamine + Neurotrophic Cognition)
- **Rationale:** Semax is a nootropic peptide that upregulates BDNF and modulates the dopaminergic and serotonergic systems, enhancing focus and cognitive resilience. L-Tyrosine supplies the substrate for dopamine and norepinephrine synthesis under cognitive load. Semax enhances the signaling and neuroprotection while tyrosine ensures the raw material for catecholamine production — a tightly complementary cognitive-under-stress pairing.
- **Protocol:** L-Tyrosine 500 mg-2 g ~45 min before demanding cognitive work (away from protein) + Semax at standard intranasal nootropic dosing
- **Best for:** High cognitive load, demanding intellectual work, sleep-deprived focus

#### Selank + L-Tyrosine (Calm Focus Under Stress)
- **Rationale:** Selank is an anxiolytic peptide that modulates GABAergic and monoamine systems and reduces stress-related cognitive impairment. Tyrosine maintains catecholamine availability under acute stress. Selank takes the edge off stress/anxiety while tyrosine preserves the dopamine/norepinephrine needed for performance — calm plus capable.
- **Protocol:** L-Tyrosine 1-2 g before a stressor + Selank intranasally
- **Best for:** High-pressure performance, anxiety-prone individuals needing focus under stress

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + L-Tyrosine (Recovery + Drive)
- **Rationale:** GH secretagogues (CJC-1295, Ipamorelin) support recovery and body composition; tyrosine supports dopaminergic drive and cognitive performance, useful during demanding training or sleep-restricted phases. Tyrosine can help preserve motivation and focus when GH protocols are run alongside heavy schedules.
- **Protocol:** L-Tyrosine 1-2 g in the morning as needed + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Best for:** Athletes balancing training demands with cognitive performance

#### MOTS-c + L-Tyrosine (Metabolic Performance + Cognition)
- **Rationale:** MOTS-c acts as an exercise mimetic improving metabolic flexibility and energy; tyrosine supports catecholamine output during physically and cognitively demanding sessions. Together they support performance under combined physical and mental stress.
- **Protocol:** L-Tyrosine 1-2 g pre-session + MOTS-c at standard dosing
- **Best for:** Demanding training under fatigue or sleep restriction

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### Selank + L-Tyrosine + L-Theanine (Layered Calm Focus)
- **Rationale:** Selank reduces stress, L-theanine promotes calm alpha-wave focus, and tyrosine supplies catecholamine substrate. A layered approach to focused, non-jittery cognition under pressure.
- **Protocol:** L-Tyrosine 1-2 g + L-Theanine 100-200 mg + Selank intranasally before demanding work
- **Best for:** Deep work, high-stakes cognitive performance

### Supplement Synergies

#### L-Tyrosine + L-Theanine (Caffeine-Friendly Focus Stack)
- **Rationale:** L-theanine promotes calm, alpha-wave focus and smooths the edge of stimulants; L-tyrosine supplies dopamine/norepinephrine substrate under cognitive load. With caffeine, the trio delivers alert, calm, well-fueled focus — caffeine for alertness, theanine for calm, tyrosine for catecholamine substrate.
- **Protocol:** L-Tyrosine 500 mg-2 g + L-Theanine 100-200 mg (+ optional caffeine 100 mg) before demanding cognitive work
- **Best for:** Focus, productivity, exam/deadline performance

#### L-Tyrosine + Alpha-GPC (Dual-System Cognitive Stack)
- **Rationale:** Alpha-GPC raises acetylcholine (memory, learning, focus) while L-tyrosine supports the catecholamine system (drive, alertness, working memory under stress). Hitting two distinct neurotransmitter systems gives broader cognitive support than either alone.
- **Protocol:** L-Tyrosine 1-2 g + Alpha-GPC 300-600 mg before demanding cognitive or physical-cognitive tasks
- **Best for:** Comprehensive cognitive enhancement, demanding multitasking
