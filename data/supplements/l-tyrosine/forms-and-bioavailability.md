# L-Tyrosine
## Forms and Bioavailability

### L-Tyrosine (Free Form — Most Studied)
- **The standard, most-researched form** — virtually all positive cognitive/stress studies used plain L-tyrosine
- **Effective dose:** typically 100-150 mg/kg body weight in studies (often ~2 g per dose for a typical adult, up to ~7 g in research)
- **Poor water solubility** — tyrosine doesn't dissolve well in water (gritty); capsules or mixing into a slurry are common
- **Well absorbed orally** when not competing with a high-protein meal
- **Cheap and widely available**

### N-Acetyl-L-Tyrosine (NALT)
- **An acetylated derivative** marketed as more water-soluble and "more bioavailable"
- **Reality check:** NALT IS more water-soluble, BUT a significant fraction is excreted in the urine unchanged rather than converted to tyrosine. Studies suggest NALT raises plasma tyrosine LESS efficiently than equivalent free L-tyrosine.
- **Verdict:** despite the marketing, plain L-tyrosine appears to deliver tyrosine to the body and brain more reliably. NALT's main advantage is solubility/convenience, not superior efficacy.
- **If using NALT**, recognize it may require a higher dose to match free L-tyrosine's effect

### Bioavailability Notes
- **The biggest factor is competition at the blood-brain barrier** — tyrosine shares the LAT1 transporter with other large neutral amino acids (phenylalanine, tryptophan, branched-chain aa). Taking tyrosine away from protein-heavy meals dramatically improves brain uptake.
- **Peak plasma levels** occur ~1-2 hours after an oral dose, supporting dosing ~30-60 minutes before a stressor
- **Carbohydrate pairing** can lower competing amino acid levels (insulin drives BCAAs into muscle), modestly favoring tyrosine uptake

### Powder vs Capsules
- **Capsules** — most convenient given tyrosine's poor solubility and slightly bitter taste
- **Powder** — economical but gritty; mix into juice or a small amount of liquid and drink quickly
- **Both are equally effective** when matched for dose of free L-tyrosine

### Practical Guidance
- **Choose plain L-tyrosine** as the default — best evidence, most reliable plasma elevation, cheapest
- **Take on an empty stomach or away from high-protein meals** for best brain uptake
- **Don't overpay for NALT** expecting superiority — its main benefit is solubility, not efficacy
