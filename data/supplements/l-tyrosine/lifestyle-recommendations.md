# L-Tyrosine
## Lifestyle Recommendations

### Use It Situationally (The Core Principle)
- **Reserve tyrosine for acute stressors** — exams, high-pressure deadlines, demanding multitasking, cold exposure, or sleep-deprived shifts. This is where the evidence is strongest.
- **Dose ~30-60 minutes before the stressor**, away from protein, for best brain uptake
- **Don't take it daily expecting a baseline boost** — its value is as an on-demand substrate buffer, not a maintenance nootropic

### Cognitive Work
- **Pair with a non-protein pre-work routine** — empty stomach or a little fruit/juice, then your demanding cognitive session
- **Stack with caffeine + L-theanine** for a balanced focus stack: caffeine for alertness, theanine for calm focus, tyrosine for catecholamine substrate under load
- **Consider Alpha-GPC** for a cholinergic + catecholaminergic cognitive combination (see peptide-synergies)

### Sleep Deprivation / Shift Work
- **Use tyrosine to buffer cognition during occasional sleep loss** — at the onset of the demanding period, with possible re-dosing
- **Take it early in the wake period, not late** — to avoid interfering with eventual recovery sleep
- **Prioritize actual sleep** — tyrosine is a stopgap, not a substitute; chronic sleep debt needs sleep, not supplements

### Stress Management
- **Tyrosine complements stress-management fundamentals** — it preserves performance under acute stress but does not address chronic stress; pair with sleep, exercise, and stress-reduction practices
- **Useful before high-stakes performances** (presentations, competitions) where cognitive sharpness under pressure matters

### Nutrition
- **Ensure adequate protein overall** — supplies tyrosine (and its precursor phenylalanine) for general needs
- **Keep cofactors adequate** — vitamin B6, vitamin C, copper, folate, and iron support the conversion of tyrosine to dopamine and norepinephrine
- **Time supplemental tyrosine away from protein meals** for the cognitive effect

### Timing & Sleep Hygiene
- **Avoid late-day dosing** — the catecholamine-substrate effect can disrupt sleep onset in sensitive people
- **Morning or early-afternoon use** suits most cognitive and stress applications

### Long-Term Mindset
- **Tyrosine is a tool, not a daily staple** — its effects are acute and stress-dependent. Use it strategically before demands rather than continuously.
- **Inexpensive and low-risk** for occasional, well-timed use in healthy people without the contraindicated conditions
