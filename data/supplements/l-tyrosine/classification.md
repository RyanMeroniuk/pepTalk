# L-Tyrosine
## Classification

- **Category:** Conditionally essential amino acid; catecholamine (dopamine/norepinephrine) precursor
- **Chemical class:** Aromatic alpha-amino acid (phenolic side chain)
- **Molecular formula:** C9H11NO3
- **Supplement type:** Nootropic / cognitive support / stress-resilience supplement
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (OTC); GRAS status; not banned by athletic organizations
- **Primary systems:** Nervous system (catecholamine synthesis — dopamine, norepinephrine, epinephrine), endocrine (thyroid hormone and melanin precursor)
- **Common forms:** Free-form L-tyrosine and N-Acetyl-L-Tyrosine (NALT, an acetylated, more water-soluble derivative)
- **Endogenous synthesis:** Synthesized from the essential amino acid phenylalanine via phenylalanine hydroxylase; becomes "conditionally essential" when phenylalanine is limited or demand is high
- **Biological role:** Rate-influencing substrate for tyrosine hydroxylase, the enzyme that initiates dopamine and norepinephrine production; also a precursor to thyroid hormones (T3/T4) and melanin
