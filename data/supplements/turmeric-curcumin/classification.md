# Turmeric/Curcumin
## Classification

- **Category:** Anti-Inflammatory / Antioxidant
- **Subcategory:** Polyphenol (curcuminoid)
- **Chemical class:** Diarylheptanoid — curcumin (diferuloylmethane) is the principal curcuminoid
- **Source:** Curcuma longa (turmeric plant), a member of the ginger family (Zingiberaceae). The rhizome (root) is used
- **Active compounds:** Curcumin (77% of curcuminoids), demethoxycurcumin (17%), bisdemethoxycurcumin (6%). Turmeric root powder contains only 2-5% curcuminoids by weight
- **Regulatory status:** Generally Recognized as Safe (GRAS) by the FDA as a food ingredient (turmeric spice) and dietary supplement. Not FDA-approved as a drug despite extensive clinical research. EU-approved food additive (E100)
- **Common names:** Turmeric, curcumin, curcuminoids, Haldi (Hindi), Jiang Huang (TCM)
