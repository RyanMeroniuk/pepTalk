# Turmeric/Curcumin
## Peptide Synergies

- **BPC-157 (tissue repair — strongest synergy):** BPC-157 promotes tissue repair, angiogenesis, and gut healing through growth factor modulation. Curcumin provides potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant support that complements BPC-157's healing mechanisms. The combination addresses both the repair process (BPC-157) and the inflammatory environment that impairs healing (curcumin). Particularly powerful for GI healing (BPC-157's gut-protective effects + curcumin's local GI anti-inflammatory action), joint recovery, and post-injury rehabilitation. Curcumin's NF-kB inhibition creates a less inflammatory tissue environment for BPC-157's repair signals to work optimally
- **TB-500 / Thymosin Beta-4 (wound healing):** TB-500 promotes wound healing, tissue repair, and flexibility through actin regulation and anti-inflammatory mechanisms. Curcumin's anti-inflammatory effects reduce the inflammatory phase of healing, allowing the regenerative phase (where TB-500 excels) to proceed more efficiently. The combination supports recovery from injuries, surgery, and chronic inflammatory tissue damage
- **KPV (anti-inflammatory tripeptide):** KPV reduces inflammation through alpha-MSH receptor activation, primarily in the gut and skin. Curcumin's broad NF-kB inhibition complements KPV's targeted anti-inflammatory mechanism. Together they provide multi-pathway inflammation resolution — KPV for acute inflammatory signaling, curcumin for the broader inflammatory transcriptional program. Particularly synergistic for inflammatory gut conditions and skin inflammation
- **GHK-Cu (copper peptide):** GHK-Cu promotes skin regeneration, collagen production, wound healing, and has broad anti-aging gene expression effects. Curcumin's antioxidant protection (Nrf2 activation) and anti-inflammatory effects complement GHK-Cu's regenerative signals. The combination supports skin health, wound healing, and anti-aging from both the regenerative (GHK-Cu) and protective (curcumin) sides
- **Semax (nootropic):** Semax upregulates BDNF through melanocortin pathways. Curcumin also increases BDNF levels through different mechanisms (neuroinflammation reduction, epigenetic modulation). The dual BDNF upregulation provides enhanced cognitive support, neuroplasticity, and neuroprotection. Curcumin's reduction of neuroinflammation creates a healthier brain environment for Semax's neurotrophic effects
- **Selank (anxiolytic):** Selank modulates GABA and reduces anxiety while enhancing cognition. Curcumin's effects on serotonin, dopamine, and BDNF complement Selank's anxiolytic properties. The combination addresses anxiety and cognitive function from complementary angles — Selank for GABAergic calm and curcumin for neuroinflammation-driven mood disturbance
- **Pinealon (neuroprotective):** Pinealon promotes neuroprotection and memory. Curcumin's BDNF upregulation, beta-amyloid inhibition, and neuroinflammation reduction provide a comprehensive neuroprotective foundation. The combination is relevant for age-related cognitive decline and neurodegenerative disease prevention
- **MOTS-c (mitochondrial peptide):** MOTS-c activates AMPK for metabolic optimization. Curcumin also activates AMPK and provides anti-inflammatory effects that address the inflammatory component of metabolic syndrome. The combination supports metabolic health through AMPK activation (both compounds) plus inflammation resolution (curcumin). Relevant for individuals with metabolic syndrome seeking a comprehensive supplement-peptide approach
- **Thymosin Alpha-1 (immune modulation):** Thymosin Alpha-1 enhances T-cell function and adaptive immunity. Curcumin modulates innate immune inflammation (NF-kB, cytokines) while supporting appropriate immune function. The combination balances immune enhancement (Thymosin Alpha-1) with immune regulation (curcumin's anti-inflammatory effects), relevant for autoimmune conditions, chronic infections, and immune optimization
- **VIP (Vasoactive Intestinal Peptide):** VIP supports gut health, reduces inflammation, and improves vasodilation, particularly relevant for CIRS/mold illness. Curcumin's broad anti-inflammatory effects complement VIP's targeted GI and vascular support. The combination addresses systemic inflammation and gut dysfunction from multiple mechanisms
- **Semaglutide / Tirzepatide / Retatrutide (GLP-1 agonists):** Curcumin's anti-inflammatory and metabolic effects complement the weight loss and metabolic benefits of GLP-1 agonist peptides. Curcumin may reduce the inflammatory consequences of rapid weight loss and support liver health during metabolic transition. The anti-inflammatory effects also address the chronic low-grade inflammation that often accompanies obesity
- **Epitalon (anti-aging):** Epitalon activates telomerase for cellular longevity. Curcumin's broad anti-aging effects (NF-kB inhibition, Nrf2 antioxidant activation, AMPK activation, epigenetic modulation) complement telomerase activation with cellular protection and inflammation reduction. A comprehensive anti-aging stack addressing aging from multiple biological angles
