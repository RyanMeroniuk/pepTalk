# Turmeric/Curcumin
## Dosing Protocols

- **General anti-inflammatory and health maintenance:**
  - **Meriva (phytosome):** 500-1,000mg twice daily (1,000-2,000mg Meriva total/day, providing ~200-400mg curcumin with 29x absorption)
  - **Curcumin + piperine (BioPerine):** 500mg curcumin + 5-10mg piperine, twice daily (1,000mg total/day)
  - **NovaSOL / micellar:** 500mg once daily (high absorption makes once-daily feasible)
  - **BCM-95:** 500mg twice daily (1,000mg total/day)
  - **Timing:** Take with meals containing fat for best absorption (even enhanced forms benefit from dietary fat)

- **Joint pain and osteoarthritis:**
  - **Meriva:** 500mg twice daily — this is the most studied dose for joint pain, with multiple trials showing benefit within 2-4 weeks and continuing improvement over 3 months
  - **Curcumin + piperine:** 500mg curcumin + piperine, 2-3 times daily (1,000-1,500mg/day). Higher end for more severe pain
  - **Duration:** Initial effects in 2-4 weeks. Full benefit at 8-12 weeks. Can be used long-term
  - **Loading strategy:** For acute joint flares, some practitioners use 1,500-2,000mg enhanced curcumin daily for 1-2 weeks, then reduce to maintenance dose

- **Depression and mood support:**
  - **Dose:** 500mg enhanced curcumin twice daily (1,000mg/day). Clinical trials used BCM-95 1,000mg/day or standard curcumin 1,000mg/day with piperine
  - **Timing:** Split morning and evening doses. Can be taken alongside antidepressants (additive benefit shown in trials with fluoxetine)
  - **Duration:** Mood improvements typically begin at 4-6 weeks. Full antidepressant effects at 8 weeks
  - **Note:** Curcumin is evidence-based for mild-to-moderate depression. Not a substitute for professional mental health treatment in severe depression

- **Exercise recovery and sports performance:**
  - **Dose:** 500mg enhanced curcumin twice daily — one dose 1-2 hours before training, one dose within 2 hours post-training
  - **Acute loading:** For major events or intense training blocks, some athletes use 1,500-2,000mg enhanced curcumin for 2-3 days before and after
  - **Duration:** Benefits for DOMS reduction are acute (within 24-72 hours). Ongoing supplementation reduces chronic training-related inflammation

- **Cognitive support and neuroprotection:**
  - **Dose:** 500-1,000mg enhanced curcumin daily. Theracurmin 90-180mg/day was used in the UCLA Alzheimer's trial showing brain PET scan improvements
  - **Timing:** Morning dosing preferred (some users report mild alertness/mood enhancement)
  - **Duration:** Cognitive benefits develop over 8-12 weeks. Neuroprotective effects are long-term

- **Inflammatory bowel conditions (IBS, ulcerative colitis):**
  - **Dose:** 500-1,000mg curcumin twice daily as adjunctive therapy (alongside conventional treatment)
  - **Note:** For GI conditions, standard curcumin (even with low systemic absorption) may be preferable because unabsorbed curcumin acts locally in the GI tract. Enhanced absorption forms may actually deliver less curcumin to the lower GI tract
  - **Duration:** 4-8 weeks for symptom improvement. Can be used long-term for maintenance

- **Metabolic health / blood sugar support:**
  - **Dose:** 500mg enhanced curcumin twice daily
  - **Timing:** With meals, particularly higher-carbohydrate meals
  - **Duration:** Metabolic improvements at 8-12 weeks

- **Important dosing notes:**
  - **Always take with food:** Fat-containing meals improve absorption of all curcumin forms. Even enhanced forms benefit from dietary fat
  - **Split doses:** Twice daily dosing maintains more consistent blood levels than once daily (except for very high-absorption forms like NovaSOL)
  - **Do not compare mg across forms:** 500mg Meriva is NOT equivalent to 500mg curcumin + piperine. Always consider the bioavailability multiplier of the specific form
  - **Maximum recommended dose:** Most clinical trials use 1,000-2,000mg of enhanced curcumin daily. Doses up to 8,000mg standard curcumin (with piperine) have been used in short-term trials without significant adverse effects, but there is no benefit to exceeding 2,000mg of enhanced forms
  - **Onset of action:** Curcumin is not an acute pain reliever like ibuprofen. Anti-inflammatory benefits build over 2-4 weeks. Do not expect immediate relief
