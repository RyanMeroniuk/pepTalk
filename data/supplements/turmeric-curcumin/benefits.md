# Turmeric/Curcumin
## Benefits

- **Joint pain and inflammation (strongest clinical evidence):** Curcumin (500-1,000mg/day of enhanced-bioavailability form) reduces pain and improves function in osteoarthritis comparable to ibuprofen 800mg/day in head-to-head trials, with fewer GI side effects. Reduces inflammatory markers (CRP, ESR) in rheumatoid arthritis as adjunctive therapy. Effective for general joint stiffness and exercise-related inflammation
- **Systemic inflammation reduction:** Consistently reduces CRP (C-reactive protein) by 1-3 mg/L, IL-6, TNF-alpha, and other inflammatory markers in meta-analyses. Relevant for chronic low-grade inflammation underlying metabolic syndrome, cardiovascular disease, and accelerated aging
- **Depression and mood (notable evidence):** Multiple clinical trials show curcumin (500-1,000mg/day enhanced form) comparable to fluoxetine (Prozac) 20mg for mild-to-moderate depression, with some trials showing additive benefit when combined with antidepressants. Mechanism involves BDNF upregulation, monoamine modulation (serotonin, dopamine, norepinephrine), and neuroinflammation reduction
- **Cardiovascular protection:** Improves endothelial function (the endothelium lines blood vessels and regulates blood pressure and clotting). Reduces LDL oxidation (oxidized LDL is the form that drives atherosclerosis). Modest lipid improvements. Anti-inflammatory effects reduce cardiovascular risk broadly
- **Cognitive function and neuroprotection:** Improves working memory and attention in clinical trials, particularly in older adults. BDNF upregulation supports neuroplasticity. Beta-amyloid binding and tau inhibition provide theoretical Alzheimer's prevention, though clinical trial results in established dementia have been limited by bioavailability issues
- **Metabolic syndrome and blood sugar:** Improves insulin sensitivity, reduces fasting blood glucose, and improves lipid profiles in metabolic syndrome. AMPK activation contributes to these effects. Not as potent as berberine or metformin for blood sugar, but provides meaningful benefit as part of a comprehensive protocol
- **Digestive health:** Reduces symptoms of IBS (irritable bowel syndrome), ulcerative colitis (as adjunctive therapy), and general digestive inflammation. Local anti-inflammatory effects in the GI tract occur even with poorly absorbed formulations. Stimulates bile secretion, improving fat digestion
- **Exercise recovery:** Reduces delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS), exercise-induced inflammation, and oxidative stress. Taking curcumin before and after intense exercise accelerates recovery. Effective dose: 500mg enhanced curcumin twice daily around training
- **Skin health:** Anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects improve inflammatory skin conditions (psoriasis, eczema, acne). Reduces UV-induced skin damage and supports wound healing. Topical and oral forms both studied
- **Liver protection (hepatoprotection):** Reduces liver inflammation, improves liver enzymes in NAFLD, and protects against hepatotoxicity from medications and alcohol. Nrf2 activation enhances the liver's own antioxidant defenses
- **Cancer prevention (epidemiological and preclinical):** Population studies consistently show lower cancer rates in populations with high turmeric consumption. Curcumin modulates every stage of cancer development: inhibits initiation (antioxidant, DNA repair), prevents promotion (NF-kB inhibition, anti-proliferative), and blocks progression (anti-angiogenic, pro-apoptotic). Clinical applications primarily in colorectal cancer (local GI contact) and as adjunctive therapy. Not a stand-alone cancer treatment
- **Pain management:** Effective for multiple pain types beyond joint pain: headaches, post-operative pain, menstrual pain, neuropathic pain. Mechanism combines COX-2 inhibition with broader anti-inflammatory effects. Does not have the GI, renal, or cardiovascular risks of chronic NSAID use
- **Antioxidant protection:** Beyond direct free radical scavenging, Nrf2-mediated upregulation of endogenous antioxidant enzymes provides sustained cellular protection. Relevant for anti-aging, protection from environmental toxins, and reducing oxidative damage from exercise
