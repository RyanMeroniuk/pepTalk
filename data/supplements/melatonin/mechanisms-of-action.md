# Melatonin
## Mechanisms of Action

- **MT1 and MT2 receptor activation** — Melatonin binds to G-protein-coupled receptors MT1 and MT2 in the suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN). MT1 activation promotes sleepiness by inhibiting SCN neuronal firing. MT2 activation phase-shifts the circadian clock, resetting sleep-wake timing.
- **Circadian clock entrainment** — Acts on the SCN to synchronize peripheral clocks throughout the body (liver, gut, muscle, fat tissue). This coordinates metabolic processes, hormone release, and body temperature rhythms with the light-dark cycle.
- **Core body temperature reduction** — Melatonin promotes vasodilation in distal skin (hands, feet), redirecting blood flow to the periphery and cooling the core. A 1-2 degree Fahrenheit drop in core temperature is a prerequisite for sleep onset.
- **Direct free radical scavenging** — Melatonin neutralizes hydroxyl radicals, peroxyl radicals, singlet oxygen, nitric oxide, and peroxynitrite. Unlike most antioxidants, melatonin's metabolites (cyclic 3-hydroxymelatonin, AFMK, AMK) are also potent antioxidants, creating an "antioxidant cascade."
- **Antioxidant enzyme upregulation** — Stimulates gene expression and activity of superoxide dismutase (SOD), glutathione peroxidase (GPx), glutathione reductase (GRd), and catalase via ROR/RZR nuclear receptors.
- **Mitochondrial protection** — Accumulates in mitochondria at concentrations 100x higher than in plasma. Stabilizes the inner mitochondrial membrane, inhibits the mitochondrial permeability transition pore (mPTP), and optimizes electron transport chain efficiency, reducing electron leakage and ROS production.
- **Anti-inflammatory signaling** — Inhibits NF-kB activation and suppresses pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-alpha, IL-1beta, IL-6). Activates the Nrf2 pathway, enhancing phase II detoxification enzymes.
- **GABAergic modulation** — Melatonin potentiates GABA-A receptor activity indirectly, contributing to its calming (but not sedating) effects. This is a modulatory effect, not a direct agonist action.
- **Hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal (HPG) regulation** — Melatonin modulates GnRH secretion and influences reproductive hormone timing, particularly relevant in seasonal reproduction research.
- **Immune cell regulation** — Enhances natural killer cell activity, promotes T-helper cell differentiation, and modulates cytokine profiles. Melatonin receptors are present on virtually all immune cells.
