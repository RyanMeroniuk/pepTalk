# Melatonin
## Foods to Avoid

- **Caffeine (coffee, tea, chocolate, energy drinks)** — Caffeine blocks adenosine receptors, directly opposing the sleep drive that melatonin complements. Caffeine also competes with melatonin for CYP1A2 metabolism, potentially altering melatonin clearance. **Avoid caffeine within 8-10 hours of bedtime** (caffeine half-life is 5-6 hours, but quarter-life extends to 10-12 hours).
- **Alcohol** — Despite making people feel drowsy, alcohol profoundly disrupts sleep architecture. It suppresses REM sleep, fragments sleep in the second half of the night, and suppresses endogenous melatonin production by 15-20%. Even moderate alcohol intake (1-2 drinks) 3 hours before bed measurably impairs sleep quality. **Avoid alcohol within 3-4 hours of bedtime.**
- **High-sugar foods and refined carbohydrates before bed** — Blood sugar spikes followed by reactive hypoglycemia cause cortisol and adrenaline release, which directly antagonize melatonin's sleep-promoting effects. **Avoid candy, ice cream, sugary cereals, white bread, and pastries within 2 hours of bed.**
- **Spicy foods** — Capsaicin raises core body temperature and can cause gastric discomfort, directly opposing melatonin's core temperature-lowering mechanism. **Avoid highly spiced meals within 3 hours of bedtime.**
- **High-fat meals** — Delay gastric emptying and slow melatonin supplement absorption, reducing peak levels by 20-40%. Also associated with lighter, more fragmented sleep. **Avoid heavy, greasy meals within 3 hours of bedtime.**
- **Tyramine-rich foods close to bedtime** — Tyramine stimulates norepinephrine release, which is alerting and opposes melatonin's calming cascade. Sources: **aged cheeses (Parmesan, blue cheese, Gouda), cured meats (salami, pepperoni), fermented foods (sauerkraut, kimchi in large amounts), soy sauce**. Small amounts are fine; avoid large servings near bedtime.
- **Excessive protein-only meals at night** — Large protein loads without carbohydrates increase tyrosine (precursor to dopamine and norepinephrine) competition with tryptophan at the blood-brain barrier, potentially reducing melatonin synthesis. Balance protein with some complex carbs at dinner.
- **Grapefruit and grapefruit juice** — While grapefruit inhibits CYP3A4 (not melatonin's primary metabolism pathway), it can alter the metabolism of co-administered medications and may unpredictably affect melatonin levels in combination with certain drugs.
