# Melatonin
## Peptide Synergies

- **DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide)** — Highly complementary pairing. DSIP promotes delta wave (deep/slow-wave) sleep through modulation of GABAergic and glutamatergic transmission, while melatonin gates sleep onset and maintains circadian timing. Together, they address both sleep initiation and sleep depth. DSIP also reduces cortisol, which can further support melatonin's function. **Protocol:** Low-dose melatonin (0.3-0.5mg) 30-60 minutes before bed + DSIP administered per protocol. This combination is particularly useful for stress-related insomnia where both cortisol elevation and circadian disruption are present.

- **Pinealon** — A tripeptide (Glu-Asp-Arg) that supports pineal gland function and endogenous melatonin production. Pinealon has neuroprotective properties and may help restore age-related decline in pineal gland activity. Combining exogenous low-dose melatonin with Pinealon may support both immediate circadian signaling and long-term pineal gland health. **Rationale:** In older adults where pineal calcification reduces natural melatonin output, Pinealon may help preserve remaining pineal function while low-dose melatonin provides the circadian signal directly.

- **Epitalon (Epithalon)** — A tetrapeptide (Ala-Glu-Asp-Gly) that activates telomerase and stimulates endogenous melatonin production from the pineal gland. Epitalon has been shown in research to restore nocturnal melatonin secretion patterns in aging animal models. **Synergy:** Epitalon supports the body's ability to produce its own melatonin, potentially reducing the need for exogenous supplementation over time. During an Epitalon cycle, low-dose melatonin provides immediate circadian support while Epitalon works to restore endogenous production. **Protocol consideration:** Some practitioners use Epitalon cycles (10-20 day courses) and maintain low-dose melatonin as a bridge and ongoing circadian anchor.

- **BPC-157** — Indirect synergy through gut healing. Since the gut produces 400x more melatonin than the pineal gland, restoring gut health with BPC-157 may enhance enteric melatonin production and overall GI-mediated sleep signaling.

- **Selank** — Anxiolytic peptide that modulates GABA and reduces anxiety. Pairs with melatonin for individuals whose sleep is disrupted primarily by evening anxiety or racing thoughts. Selank addresses the cognitive-emotional arousal component while melatonin provides the circadian signal.

- **Thymosin Alpha-1** — Both melatonin and TA-1 are immunomodulatory. In contexts where immune support is a goal alongside sleep optimization, combining melatonin's nighttime immune enhancement with TA-1's T-cell support provides comprehensive immune coverage.

**Note:** Melatonin is one of the safest and best-studied supplements to combine with peptides. Its low side-effect profile and wide therapeutic index make it an excellent foundational component of any sleep-focused peptide stack.
