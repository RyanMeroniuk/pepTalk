# Melatonin
## Benefits

- **Sleep onset improvement** — Low-dose melatonin (0.3-1mg) reduces sleep onset latency by 7-12 minutes on average. Most effective for delayed sleep phase disorder and jet lag rather than primary insomnia.
- **Circadian rhythm resetting** — Gold standard for jet lag management. Taking melatonin at the target bedtime of the destination time zone accelerates circadian adaptation by 1-2 days. Also effective for shift workers transitioning between schedules.
- **Antioxidant protection** — Clinical studies demonstrate reduced markers of oxidative stress (8-OHdG, MDA, isoprostanes) with melatonin supplementation. Uniquely effective in mitochondria where oxidative damage is most consequential.
- **Neuroprotection** — Preclinical and emerging clinical evidence supports melatonin's role in reducing neuroinflammation, supporting blood-brain barrier integrity, and protecting against neurodegenerative processes. Reduced melatonin levels are consistently observed in Alzheimer's and Parkinson's disease.
- **Immune support** — Enhances vaccine responses in elderly populations. Modulates inflammatory responses — anti-inflammatory in acute inflammation, immunostimulatory when immune function is suppressed.
- **Gut protection** — Reduces gastric acid secretion, strengthens esophageal sphincter tone (relevant for GERD), and supports intestinal mucosal integrity. Clinical trials show benefit comparable to omeprazole for GERD symptoms at 6mg doses.
- **Blood pressure regulation** — Nocturnal melatonin (controlled-release) reduces nighttime blood pressure by 3-6 mmHg in hypertensive patients ("non-dipping" pattern correction).
- **Metabolic health** — Emerging evidence for improved insulin sensitivity, reduced fasting glucose, and metabolic syndrome markers, mediated through circadian clock synchronization of metabolic organs.
- **Eye health** — Protects retinal cells from oxidative damage. MT1/MT2 receptors in the retina regulate intraocular pressure; melatonin supplementation may reduce IOP in glaucoma patients.
- **Headache and migraine reduction** — Multiple trials show 3mg melatonin reduces migraine frequency by 50% in responders, potentially through serotonin pathway modulation and anti-inflammatory effects.
- **Perioperative anxiety and pain** — Preoperative melatonin (3-5mg) reduces anxiety comparable to midazolam without the cognitive impairment, and reduces postoperative pain scores and analgesic requirements.
