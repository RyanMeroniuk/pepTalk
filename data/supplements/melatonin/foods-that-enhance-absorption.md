# Melatonin
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

- **Vitamin B6-rich foods** — B6 (pyridoxine) is a critical cofactor for the enzyme aromatic L-amino acid decarboxylase, which converts 5-HTP to serotonin (the direct precursor to melatonin). Consuming B6-rich foods in the evening supports endogenous melatonin synthesis. Sources: **chickpeas, bananas, potatoes, poultry, sunflower seeds, pistachios**.
- **Magnesium-rich foods** — Magnesium is a cofactor for the enzyme N-acetyltransferase (NAT), which catalyzes the rate-limiting step of melatonin synthesis (serotonin to N-acetylserotonin). Magnesium deficiency impairs melatonin production. Sources: **pumpkin seeds, dark chocolate, spinach, almonds, avocado, black beans**.
- **Folate-rich foods** — Folate supports methylation pathways involved in the final step of melatonin synthesis (N-acetylserotonin to melatonin via hydroxyindole-O-methyltransferase, which requires SAMe). Sources: **dark leafy greens, lentils, asparagus, broccoli, beets**.
- **Tryptophan-rich foods consumed with carbohydrates** — Insulin released in response to carbohydrates drives branched-chain amino acids into muscle cells, reducing competition for tryptophan transport across the blood-brain barrier. A small carb-containing snack with a tryptophan source in the evening can boost brain tryptophan availability. Example: **turkey on whole grain bread, banana with oatmeal, yogurt with honey**.
- **Calcium-rich foods** — Calcium assists in the conversion of tryptophan to melatonin. Sources: **dairy products, sardines, kale, fortified plant milks**.
- **Foods containing melatonin itself** — Consuming melatonin-containing foods in the evening adds directly to circulating levels. **Tart cherry juice (1 oz concentrate), a small handful of pistachios (2-3), or walnuts** before bed can complement supplementation.
- **Light evening meals** — Melatonin supplements absorb most consistently on a relatively empty stomach or with a small, low-fat meal. Heavy or high-fat meals delay gastric emptying and can reduce peak melatonin concentration by 20-40%.

**Optimal evening pattern:**

A light dinner containing tryptophan (poultry, fish, eggs) with complex carbohydrates (sweet potato, brown rice) and magnesium-rich sides (spinach, avocado), consumed 2-3 hours before bed, supports both endogenous production and supplement absorption.
