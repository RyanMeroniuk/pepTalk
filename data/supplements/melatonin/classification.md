# Melatonin
## Classification

- **Category:** Hormone / Chronobiotic
- **Subcategory:** Sleep & Circadian Rhythm Regulator
- **Type:** Endogenous neurohormone (also available as exogenous supplement)
- **Primary System:** Pineal gland / Suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN)
- **Regulatory Status:** Over-the-counter dietary supplement (USA); prescription-only in parts of Europe and Australia
- **Chemical Class:** Indoleamine (N-acetyl-5-methoxytryptamine)
- **Naturally Occurring:** Yes — produced endogenously from serotonin via the pineal gland in response to darkness
