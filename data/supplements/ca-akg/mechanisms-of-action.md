# Calcium Alpha-Ketoglutarate (Ca-AKG)
## Mechanisms of Action

### 1. TCA-Cycle (Krebs Cycle) Intermediate (Core Role)
- AKG is a central intermediate in the TCA cycle, where it is converted to succinyl-CoA by alpha-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase, generating NADH and feeding the electron transport chain
- It sits at the crossroads of carbohydrate, fat, and amino-acid metabolism, making it a hub for cellular energy production and metabolic flexibility
- Supplementing AKG provides additional substrate for mitochondrial energy metabolism

### 2. Cofactor for Dioxygenase Enzymes (2-Oxoglutarate-Dependent)
- AKG is the required co-substrate for a large family of 2-oxoglutarate-dependent dioxygenases — enzymes that carry out hydroxylation and demethylation reactions
- This single role connects AKG to collagen synthesis, oxygen sensing (HIF), and epigenetic regulation simultaneously

### 3. Collagen Synthesis (Prolyl/Lysyl Hydroxylases)
- Prolyl-4-hydroxylase and lysyl hydroxylase — the enzymes that hydroxylate proline and lysine residues to stabilize the collagen triple helix — require AKG (and vitamin C) as co-substrates
- Adequate AKG supports proper collagen maturation, relevant to skin, bone, tendons, and connective tissue — the basis for pairing Ca-AKG with collagen-peptides and vitamin C

### 4. Epigenetic Regulation (TET and JmjC Enzymes)
- TET enzymes (DNA demethylation) and JmjC-domain histone demethylases use AKG as a co-substrate
- AKG availability influences DNA and histone methylation patterns, linking it to epigenetic-clock biology and the longevity hypothesis (the basis for the biological-age findings in the preliminary human study)

### 5. mTOR Inhibition / Caloric-Restriction-Like Signaling
- In model organisms (notably C. elegans), AKG extends lifespan in part by inhibiting ATP synthase and reducing mTOR signaling, mimicking aspects of caloric restriction
- This connects AKG to the broader caloric-restriction-mimetic longevity theme shared with compounds like resveratrol and NMN

### 6. Nitrogen / Amino-Acid Metabolism
- AKG is a key acceptor in transamination reactions (becoming glutamate), central to amino-acid metabolism and ammonia handling
- This supports protein metabolism and may contribute to its use in some clinical nutrition contexts

### 7. Immune & Stem-Cell Effects (Preclinical)
- AKG influences immune cell metabolism and has been reported to support stem-cell function and reduce inflammatory signaling in animal models

### Evidence Note
- The metabolic and enzymatic roles of AKG are well established biochemistry. The *longevity* application rests on animal data plus one small, uncontrolled human biological-age study — this requires replication in controlled trials before being considered established.
