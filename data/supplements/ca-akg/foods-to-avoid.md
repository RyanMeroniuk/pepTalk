# Calcium Alpha-Ketoglutarate (Ca-AKG)
## Foods to Avoid

### Foods/Substances That Work Against Ca-AKG's Goals
- **Excessive alcohol** — impairs mitochondrial function, disrupts the TCA cycle AKG feeds, and increases oxidative stress, working against AKG's metabolic and longevity purpose
- **Highly processed, high-sugar foods** — promote metabolic dysfunction and inflammation; chronic overnutrition keeps mTOR active, counter to AKG's caloric-restriction-mimetic signaling
- **Chronic caloric excess** — undercuts the fasting-like, mTOR-inhibiting pathway through which AKG's longevity effects partly operate

### Excessive Calcium Intake (Form-Specific Caution)
- **Ca-AKG already supplies calcium** — pairing it with high-dose calcium supplements or very high-calcium diets can push total calcium too high. Account for the calcium from Ca-AKG to avoid excess (relevant for kidney-stone-prone individuals).

### No Specific Food Blocks AKG
- **There are no common foods that destroy or block AKG** in a meaningful way
- The relevant factor is the overall metabolic pattern: a diet that drives metabolic dysfunction works against AKG's intended metabolic and longevity benefits

### Practical Guidance
- **Support, don't sabotage, mitochondrial health** — minimize alcohol and ultra-processed foods so the TCA cycle AKG feeds runs cleanly
- **Pair AKG with vitamin C and adequate protein** (and collagen-peptides for connective-tissue goals) rather than worrying about foods to avoid
- **Watch total calcium** if combining Ca-AKG with other calcium sources
