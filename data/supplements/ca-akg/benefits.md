# Calcium Alpha-Ketoglutarate (Ca-AKG)
## Benefits

### Longevity / Biological Age (Preliminary — Headline Use)
- **Healthspan extension in mice** — a 2020 study (Shahmirzadi et al.) found Ca-AKG extended healthspan and modestly extended lifespan in mice, compressing late-life morbidity (animals stayed healthier later and had a shorter period of decline)
- **Biological-age reduction in one human study** — a small, uncontrolled 2021 study (Demidenko et al., "Rejuvant") using a Ca-AKG-based formulation reported an average reduction in biological age by DNA-methylation clock (~8 years on average after ~7 months). **This is a single, uncontrolled study and needs replication before being treated as established.**
- **Featured in longevity protocols** — notably an ingredient in Bryan Johnson's "Blueprint" regimen

### Energy Metabolism
- **Supports the TCA cycle** — as a central Krebs-cycle intermediate, AKG supports mitochondrial energy production and metabolic flexibility
- **Metabolic hub** — connects carbohydrate, fat, and amino-acid metabolism

### Collagen & Connective Tissue
- **Supports collagen synthesis** — AKG is a required co-substrate for the prolyl/lysyl hydroxylase enzymes that build stable collagen, supporting skin, bone, tendons, and connective tissue
- **Natural pairing with collagen-peptides and vitamin C** — together they supply both the building blocks and the cofactors for collagen formation

### Bone Health
- **Bone support** — animal and some human data suggest AKG may support bone density (its role in collagen and the calcium contribution of the salt form are relevant); evidence is preliminary
- **Calcium contribution** — the Ca-AKG salt provides a modest amount of calcium

### Epigenetic / Cellular Aging
- **Influences the epigenome** — by fueling TET and JmjC demethylase enzymes, AKG can affect DNA/histone methylation patterns tied to epigenetic aging clocks

### Exercise / Recovery (Limited)
- **Some interest in muscle and recovery** — AKG's role in energy and amino-acid metabolism draws interest, but human performance evidence is limited

### Important Caveat
- **Ca-AKG's metabolic roles are well-established biochemistry, but its longevity/anti-aging benefits in humans rest on one small uncontrolled study plus animal data.** Benefits should be framed as biologically plausible and promising, but not yet proven in controlled human trials.
