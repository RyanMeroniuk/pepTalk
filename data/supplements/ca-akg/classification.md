# Calcium Alpha-Ketoglutarate (Ca-AKG)
## Classification

- **Category:** TCA-cycle (Krebs cycle) intermediate / energy metabolism support / longevity compound
- **Chemical class:** Calcium salt of alpha-ketoglutaric acid (a keto acid / dicarboxylic acid)
- **Molecular formula:** Alpha-ketoglutarate (2-oxoglutarate) is C5H6O5; supplied as its calcium salt (Ca-AKG)
- **Also known as:** Ca-AKG, calcium 2-oxoglutarate, calcium alpha-ketoglutarate
- **Supplement type:** Metabolic / longevity / collagen-synthesis-support compound
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (OTC); calcium salt form is favored for stability over the free acid; notably featured as an ingredient in Bryan Johnson's "Blueprint" longevity protocol
- **Primary systems:** Cellular energy metabolism (mitochondria), connective tissue (collagen synthesis), epigenetic regulation, and amino-acid/nitrogen metabolism
- **Endogenous status:** Produced by the body as a central intermediate in the TCA cycle and in amino-acid metabolism; tissue/serum levels tend to decline with age
- **Key relationship:** AKG is a substrate/cofactor for many dioxygenase enzymes (including prolyl hydroxylases for collagen and TET/JmjC enzymes for epigenetic regulation), linking energy metabolism to connective tissue and gene expression
