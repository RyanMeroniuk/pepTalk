# Calcium Alpha-Ketoglutarate (Ca-AKG)
## Dosing Protocols

### Important Framing
- Human dosing for longevity is based largely on **one small uncontrolled study plus extrapolation from animal data**. There is no firmly established, controlled-trial-validated human dose. The ranges below reflect common longevity-community practice.

### Standard Longevity Dose (Most Common)
- **Dose:** ~1,000-2,000 mg (1-2 g) of Ca-AKG per day
- **Timing:** Often taken in the morning or split with meals; consistency matters more than precise timing
- **Notes:** The human "Rejuvant" biological-age study used a Ca-AKG-based formulation in roughly this range (around 1,000 mg/day of Ca-AKG)

### Split Dosing (Higher Intake)
- **For 2 g/day:** consider splitting into 1 g morning + 1 g later in the day
- **Rationale:** Because AKG is a rapidly metabolized intermediate, divided dosing may help maintain more consistent availability

### Collagen-Support Protocol
- **Dose:** 1-2 g/day Ca-AKG combined with collagen-peptides and vitamin C
- **Rationale:** AKG and vitamin C are cofactors for the prolyl/lysyl hydroxylase enzymes that stabilize collagen; collagen-peptides supply the amino-acid building blocks
- **Best for:** Skin, bone, and connective-tissue support

### Timing & Food
- **With or without food** — generally well tolerated either way; taking with meals can reduce any GI sensitivity
- **Morning dosing** is common in longevity routines, often alongside other compounds (e.g., NMN)

### Cycling
- **Continuous daily use is typical** — there is no established need to cycle Ca-AKG
- Some users align it with broader longevity-stack routines rather than cycling on/off

### Onset of Effects
- **No acute, directly felt effect** — Ca-AKG works through metabolism and enzyme cofactor roles
- **Longevity/epigenetic effects (if real) accrue over months** — the human study measured biological age after ~7 months of use
- **Collagen/connective-tissue benefits** would also build over months

### Practical Guidance
- **Use the calcium AKG form specifically** (the studied form), not arginine/ornithine AKG
- **Account for the calcium content** if you already take high-dose calcium supplements
- **Pair with vitamin C** (and collagen-peptides) when targeting connective-tissue goals
