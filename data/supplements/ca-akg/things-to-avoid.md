# Calcium Alpha-Ketoglutarate (Ca-AKG)
## Things to Avoid

### Overstating the Evidence (Most Important)
- **Treating the biological-age reduction as proven** — the standout human finding comes from a single, small, **uncontrolled** study (Demidenko et al., 2021, "Rejuvant") with no placebo group. It is intriguing but needs replication in controlled trials. Do not present it as established fact.
- **Assuming mouse lifespan results transfer directly to humans** — the 2020 mouse study showed healthspan extension and modest lifespan gains, but animal-to-human extrapolation is uncertain

### Using the Wrong Form
- **Buying arginine-AKG (AAKG) or ornithine-AKG (OKG) for longevity** — these are different products marketed for pre-workout/clinical-nutrition uses and are not the form studied for longevity. Use **calcium AKG (Ca-AKG)** specifically.

### Calcium Overload
- **Ignoring the calcium content** — Ca-AKG contributes calcium; stacking it with high-dose calcium supplements can push total calcium too high. Account for it, especially if kidney-stone-prone.

### Dosing & Quality Mistakes
- **Buying unstandardized products** — choose third-party-tested Ca-AKG stating elemental AKG (and calcium) per serving
- **Chasing very high doses** — long-term human safety above the researched 1-2 g/day range is not established; "more" is not proven better
- **Expecting acute effects** — AKG works metabolically; any longevity/epigenetic or collagen benefits accrue over months, not days

### Medical Cautions
- **Kidney disease / kidney stones** — caution due to the calcium load; consult a clinician
- **Active cancer** — AKG influences metabolism and epigenetic enzymes; consult an oncologist before use
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding** — avoid supplemental doses (insufficient data)
- **High-dose calcium users / hypercalcemia risk** — account for the added calcium

### Realistic Expectations
- **Ca-AKG is a biologically plausible, well-tolerated metabolic/longevity compound — but its anti-aging benefits in humans rest on one uncontrolled study plus animal data.** Use it with measured expectations and prioritize the lifestyle fundamentals (diet, exercise, sleep, fasting) that have far stronger evidence.
