# Calcium Alpha-Ketoglutarate (Ca-AKG)
## Side Effects

### Generally Well Tolerated
- **Ca-AKG is a salt of a normal metabolic intermediate** and is generally well tolerated at typical doses (1-2 g/day)
- The human "Rejuvant" study and animal studies reported good tolerability with no serious adverse events attributed to Ca-AKG

### Common (Mild, Infrequent)
- **Mild GI discomfort** — occasional stomach upset, nausea, or loose stools, more likely on an empty stomach or at higher doses. Taking with food usually resolves this.
- **No characteristic significant side effects** at researched doses

### Calcium-Related Consideration
- **The calcium salt contributes some calcium** — usually minor, but those taking high-dose calcium supplements should account for the added intake to avoid excessive total calcium (relevant for kidney-stone-prone individuals and overall calcium balance)

### Theoretical / Limited-Data Concerns
- **Long-term human safety is not well characterized** — the longest human data is the ~7-month uncontrolled study; multi-year safety at supplemental doses is unknown
- **Metabolic/epigenetic influence** — because AKG affects epigenetic enzymes and metabolism, the long-term consequences of chronic supplementation are not fully understood; this is a reason for measured expectations rather than a documented harm

### Special Populations
- **Pregnancy and breastfeeding** — insufficient safety data for supplemental doses; avoid or discuss with a clinician
- **Kidney disease / kidney stones** — caution due to the calcium load; consult a clinician
- **Active cancer** — given AKG's role in metabolism and epigenetic regulation, those with active cancer should consult their oncologist before use
- **Hypercalcemia or high-dose calcium users** — account for the additional calcium

### Overall Safety Profile
- **A benign profile at typical doses** for healthy adults, reflecting its status as a natural metabolic intermediate
- **Main practical considerations are the calcium contribution and the limited long-term human safety data**
- No established organ toxicity at researched supplemental doses
