# Calcium Alpha-Ketoglutarate (Ca-AKG)
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

Ca-AKG does not face a severe solubility/absorption barrier like polyphenols (quercetin, fisetin). The emphasis here is on supporting the enzymes AKG fuels and the metabolic pathways it participates in.

### Vitamin C (Critical Collagen Cofactor)
- **Vitamin C and AKG work together** as co-substrates for the prolyl/lysyl hydroxylase enzymes that build stable collagen
- Pairing Ca-AKG with vitamin C-rich foods (citrus, peppers, kiwi, strawberries, broccoli) or a vitamin C supplement directly supports its collagen-synthesis benefit
- This is the single most logical companion nutrient for Ca-AKG's connective-tissue role

### Adequate Protein / Amino Acids
- **Glutamine, glutamate, and overall dietary protein** feed the metabolic pool that AKG participates in (transamination) and supply collagen building blocks
- **Collagen-peptides** specifically provide the glycine/proline/hydroxyproline used to build collagen — a natural stack with AKG plus vitamin C

### Iron-Containing Foods (Enzyme Cofactor)
- **AKG-dependent dioxygenases are iron-dependent** — adequate (not excessive) iron status supports their function
- Sources: lean red meat, legumes, leafy greens; vitamin C also improves non-heme iron absorption

### Balanced Meals (Metabolic Context)
- **Taking Ca-AKG with a balanced meal** supports tolerability and provides the broader metabolic substrates (carbs, fats, amino acids) the TCA cycle uses
- No special fat requirement (unlike fat-soluble polyphenols)

### Fasting / Caloric-Restriction Context (Pathway Synergy)
- AKG's lifespan effects in model organisms partly involve mTOR inhibition / caloric-restriction-like signaling; aligning with time-restricted eating may complement this pathway (a synergy, not an absorption effect)

### What Doesn't Help
- **Looking for a special "absorption enhancer"** — AKG absorbs readily; the limiting factors are dose, consistency, and supplying its enzyme cofactors (vitamin C, iron)
- **Megadosing calcium alongside Ca-AKG** — the salt already contributes calcium; excessive total calcium is counterproductive
