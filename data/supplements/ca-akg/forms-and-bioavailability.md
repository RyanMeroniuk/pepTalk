# Calcium Alpha-Ketoglutarate (Ca-AKG)
## Forms and Bioavailability

### Calcium Alpha-Ketoglutarate (Ca-AKG) — Preferred Supplemental Form
- **The calcium salt of alpha-ketoglutaric acid** — the most common and preferred supplement form
- **Chosen for stability** — free alpha-ketoglutaric acid is hygroscopic and less stable; binding it to calcium produces a more shelf-stable, neutral-tasting powder/capsule
- **Used in the key studies** — the mouse healthspan study and the human "Rejuvant" biological-age study used calcium AKG
- **Delivers some calcium** — a minor nutritional consideration (relevant for those already taking high-dose calcium)
- **Verdict:** The standard, best-studied form for longevity use

### Other AKG Salts and Forms
- **Arginine alpha-ketoglutarate (AAKG)** — historically marketed as a pre-workout/nitric-oxide aid (arginine donor); a different use case than longevity-oriented Ca-AKG, and not the form used in the longevity studies
- **Ornithine alpha-ketoglutarate (OKG)** — used in clinical nutrition (e.g., wound healing, critical care); again a different context
- **Free alpha-ketoglutaric acid** — less stable and more acidic; generally not preferred for supplementation

### Bioavailability Notes
- **AKG is absorbed orally**, entering metabolic pathways as a TCA-cycle intermediate
- **Rapid metabolism** — because it is a central metabolic intermediate, supplemental AKG is used/turned over relatively quickly; this is part of why dosing is in the gram range and why timing and consistency matter
- **No severe solubility problem** — unlike polyphenols (quercetin, fisetin), AKG does not require special delivery technology; the focus is on adequate dose and consistent intake

### Quality & Stability
- **Choose third-party-tested Ca-AKG** stating the elemental AKG (and calcium) content per serving
- **Stability** — keep cool, dry, and sealed; the calcium salt is more stable than the free acid but still benefits from good storage
- **Form consistency** — for longevity goals, use Ca-AKG specifically (the studied form), not arginine/ornithine AKG marketed for other purposes
