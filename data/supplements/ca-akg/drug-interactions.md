# Calcium Alpha-Ketoglutarate (Ca-AKG)
## Drug Interactions

Ca-AKG is a salt of a normal metabolic intermediate and has few well-documented pharmacological drug interactions. The main considerations stem from its calcium content and from precautionary, theoretical concerns rather than established conflicts.

### Calcium-Related Interactions (Most Relevant)
- **Drugs whose absorption is reduced by calcium:** Calcium can bind certain medications in the gut and reduce their absorption. Notable examples include **tetracycline and fluoroquinolone antibiotics, levothyroxine (thyroid medication), and bisphosphonates (osteoporosis drugs)**.
- **Recommendation:** **Separate** Ca-AKG from these medications by **several hours** (take them at different times of day) to avoid the calcium reducing their absorption. This is a **timing** issue, not a contraindication.
- **Other calcium supplements / high-calcium intake:** Account for the calcium in Ca-AKG to avoid excessive total calcium

### Diuretics (Monitor)
- **Thiazide diuretics** can raise serum calcium; combined with the calcium from Ca-AKG, there is a theoretical additive effect on calcium levels. **Monitor** calcium status if on thiazides long-term.

### Chemotherapy / Active Cancer (Caution)
- **Interaction:** AKG influences cellular metabolism and epigenetic-regulating enzymes (TET/JmjC). The interaction with cancer biology and specific chemotherapy agents is not well characterized.
- **Recommendation:** Those undergoing active cancer treatment should consult their oncologist before using Ca-AKG.

### Diabetes / Metabolic Medications (Minimal/Monitor)
- **Drugs:** Metformin, insulin. No strong documented interaction, but as a metabolic-pathway intermediate, those on tightly managed regimens can reasonably **monitor** glucose when starting any new metabolic supplement.

### Low CYP-Interaction Profile
- **Unlike the flavonols (quercetin, fisetin), Ca-AKG is not characterized by significant cytochrome P450 enzyme inhibition** — broad CYP-driven interactions are not a primary concern

### Contraindications / Avoid
- **Pregnancy and breastfeeding** — avoid supplemental doses (insufficient safety data)
- **Hypercalcemia** — avoid additional calcium load
- **Active cancer treatment** — consult oncologist before use (caution pending medical approval)

### General Guidance
- **Take Ca-AKG separated by several hours from calcium-sensitive medications** (thyroid medication, certain antibiotics, bisphosphonates)
- **Inform healthcare providers** of supplement use, especially regarding total calcium intake and any active medical treatment
- **Overall interaction risk is low**, with the calcium content being the most practically relevant factor
