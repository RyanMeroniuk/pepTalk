# Calcium Alpha-Ketoglutarate (Ca-AKG)
## Lifestyle Recommendations

### Diet (Support the Metabolic Engine)
- **Eat adequate protein** — supplies the glutamine/glutamate and amino acids that feed AKG's metabolic pool and collagen synthesis
- **Pair with vitamin C** — vitamin C and AKG are joint cofactors for collagen-building enzymes; include citrus, peppers, kiwi, or a supplement
- **Add collagen-peptides for connective-tissue goals** — collagen-peptides + Ca-AKG + vitamin C is a logical skin/bone/tendon stack
- **Minimize alcohol and ultra-processed foods** — these impair mitochondrial function and the TCA cycle AKG feeds

### Fasting & Time-Restricted Eating (Pathway Synergy)
- **Consider time-restricted eating** — AKG's lifespan effects partly involve mTOR inhibition / caloric-restriction-like signaling; a consistent eating window complements this pathway
- **Avoid chronic caloric excess** — keeps mTOR active and works against the fasting-like benefits

### Exercise (Metabolic & Connective-Tissue Support)
- **Regular exercise improves mitochondrial function and metabolic flexibility** — complementary to AKG's role as an energy-metabolism intermediate
- **Resistance training plus collagen/vitamin C/AKG** supports tendon, bone, and connective-tissue health

### Sleep & Recovery
- **Prioritize 7-9 hours of sleep** — sleep drives cellular repair and metabolic regulation; AKG complements but cannot replace it
- **Consistent circadian rhythm** — supports the metabolic cycling that underpins healthy aging

### Stress Management
- **Manage chronic stress** — chronic stress and inflammation impair mitochondrial and metabolic health that AKG supports

### Mineral Balance
- **Account for total calcium intake** — Ca-AKG contributes calcium; keep overall calcium in a healthy range, especially if taking other calcium supplements
- **Maintain adequate (not excessive) iron and vitamin C** — cofactors for the AKG-dependent dioxygenase enzymes

### Stacking Within a Longevity Routine
- **Combine thoughtfully with NMN and other caloric-restriction-mimetic compounds** — NMN restores NAD+ (electron carrier for the TCA cycle AKG feeds), making the two metabolically complementary
- **Ca-AKG + collagen-peptides + vitamin C** — for connective-tissue and skin-aging support

### Long-Term Mindset
- **Ca-AKG is a steady, daily metabolic/longevity habit with promising-but-preliminary human evidence** — set realistic expectations and let lifestyle fundamentals do the heavy lifting
