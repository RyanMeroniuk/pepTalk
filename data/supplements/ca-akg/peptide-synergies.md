# Calcium Alpha-Ketoglutarate (Ca-AKG)
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### MOTS-c + Ca-AKG (Mitochondrial & Metabolic)
- **Rationale:** MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-derived peptide that activates AMPK and acts as an exercise mimetic. Ca-AKG is a central TCA-cycle intermediate that fuels mitochondrial energy production. Together they target mitochondrial function from complementary angles — MOTS-c optimizes the signaling/machinery, Ca-AKG supplies metabolic substrate.
- **Protocol:** Ca-AKG 1-2 g/day + MOTS-c at standard dosing, ideally with time-restricted eating
- **Best for:** Metabolic and mitochondrial longevity optimization

#### Epitalon + Ca-AKG (Longevity & Epigenetic Aging)
- **Rationale:** Epitalon supports telomere maintenance and circadian function, while Ca-AKG influences epigenetic regulation (as a cofactor for TET/JmjC demethylases) and was associated with reduced epigenetic-clock age in a preliminary human study. Together they address telomere and epigenetic hallmarks of aging.
- **Protocol:** Ca-AKG 1-2 g/day + Epitalon per standard longevity cycling
- **Best for:** Longevity protocols focused on biological-age markers
- **Note:** Ca-AKG's biological-age data is from a single uncontrolled study

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### GHK-Cu + Ca-AKG (Collagen & Skin Powerhouse)
- **Rationale:** GHK-Cu strongly promotes collagen synthesis and skin remodeling; Ca-AKG is a required cofactor (with vitamin C) for the prolyl/lysyl hydroxylase enzymes that build stable collagen. Ca-AKG supplies the metabolic cofactor while GHK-Cu drives the collagen-synthesis signal — a natural skin/connective-tissue pairing.
- **Protocol:** Ca-AKG 1-2 g/day + vitamin C + GHK-Cu per standard protocol (often with collagen-peptides)
- **Best for:** Skin aging, wound healing, connective-tissue support

#### BPC-157/TB-500 + Ca-AKG (Tissue Repair + Collagen Cofactor)
- **Rationale:** BPC-157 and TB-500 accelerate tissue and tendon repair, which depends heavily on collagen synthesis. Ca-AKG supports the collagen-building hydroxylase enzymes and supplies metabolic energy substrate for the repair process.
- **Protocol:** Ca-AKG 1-2 g/day + vitamin C + BPC-157 250-500 mcg 2x/day (and/or TB-500 2.5-5 mg 2x/week)
- **Best for:** Tendon/ligament recovery, connective-tissue healing

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + Ca-AKG (Recovery + Energy Substrate)
- **Rationale:** GH secretagogues like CJC-1295/Ipamorelin drive recovery, anabolism, and collagen turnover; Ca-AKG provides TCA-cycle energy substrate and collagen-synthesis cofactor support for the increased metabolic and connective-tissue demand.
- **Protocol:** Ca-AKG 1-2 g/day + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Best for:** Recovery and connective-tissue-focused stacks

### Supplement Stack Partners (Non-Peptide)
- **Ca-AKG + Collagen-peptides + Vitamin C** — the premier connective-tissue trio: collagen-peptides supply the amino-acid building blocks, while Ca-AKG and vitamin C are the cofactors for the enzymes that build stable collagen
- **Ca-AKG + NMN** — NMN restores NAD+/NADH, the electron carrier for the TCA cycle that Ca-AKG feeds; metabolically complementary longevity compounds
- **Ca-AKG + Spermidine/Resveratrol** — overlapping caloric-restriction-mimetic and metabolic longevity themes
