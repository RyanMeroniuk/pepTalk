# L-Glutamine
## Classification

- **Category:** Conditionally essential amino acid
- **Chemical class:** L-alpha-amino acid (proteinogenic)
- **Molecular formula:** C5H10N2O3
- **Supplement type:** Single amino acid supplement
- **Regulatory status:** Available as a dietary supplement (OTC); also used in clinical nutrition (IV glutamine in hospital settings)
- **Primary systems:** Gastrointestinal, immune, muscular, neurological
- **Most abundant amino acid** in human blood plasma and skeletal muscle, comprising ~60% of the free amino acid pool in muscle tissue
- **"Conditionally essential"** means the body produces it endogenously, but demand can exceed production during physiological stress — surgery, burns, intense exercise, critical illness, or chronic gut disorders
