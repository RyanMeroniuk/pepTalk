# L-Glutamine
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### BPC-157 + L-Glutamine (The Gut Healing Powerhouse)
- **Rationale:** BPC-157 accelerates angiogenesis (new blood vessel formation) and tissue repair in the GI tract. Glutamine provides the primary fuel for enterocyte proliferation. Together, BPC-157 builds the infrastructure while glutamine fuels the workers.
- **Mechanisms:** BPC-157 upregulates growth factor receptors (VEGF, FGF) in the gut lining; glutamine activates mTOR and HSP70 in enterocytes. Complementary, non-overlapping pathways.
- **Protocol:** L-Glutamine 10-15 g/day (divided doses) + BPC-157 250-500 mcg 2x/day (oral or subcutaneous)
- **Timing:** Take glutamine 30 min before oral BPC-157 to prime the gut environment
- **Best for:** Leaky gut, IBS, IBD, NSAID-induced gut damage, post-antibiotic gut repair

#### KPV + L-Glutamine (Anti-Inflammatory Gut Repair)
- **Rationale:** KPV is a potent anti-inflammatory tripeptide that reduces NF-kB activation and inflammatory cytokines in the gut. Glutamine fuels repair once inflammation is controlled. KPV calms the fire; glutamine rebuilds.
- **Mechanisms:** KPV suppresses TNF-alpha, IL-6, and NF-kB; glutamine restores tight junction proteins and feeds enterocytes
- **Protocol:** L-Glutamine 10-15 g/day + KPV 200-500 mcg 2x/day (oral)
- **Best for:** Inflammatory bowel conditions, ulcerative colitis, Crohn's (adjunctive), gut inflammation

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4) + L-Glutamine
- **Rationale:** TB-500 promotes tissue repair and reduces inflammation systemically. Combined with glutamine, it supports both systemic and gut-specific healing.
- **Protocol:** L-Glutamine 10 g/day + TB-500 at standard dosing (2.5-5 mg 2x/week)
- **Best for:** Post-surgical recovery, systemic tissue repair with gut healing component

#### GHK-Cu + L-Glutamine
- **Rationale:** GHK-Cu promotes collagen synthesis, tissue remodeling, and wound healing. Glutamine provides the building blocks and energy for fibroblast activity.
- **Protocol:** L-Glutamine 10 g/day + GHK-Cu (topical or subcutaneous per standard protocols)
- **Best for:** Skin healing, wound repair, anti-aging protocols with gut health component

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + L-Glutamine
- **Rationale:** Growth hormone secretagogues increase GH and IGF-1, which directly support gut mucosal regeneration, muscle protein synthesis, and tissue repair. Glutamine is the fuel for these GH-driven processes.
- **Protocol:** L-Glutamine 10 g/day (5 g morning, 5 g before bed) + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Timing:** Take glutamine 30 min before the peptide injection at night
- **Best for:** Recovery optimization, body composition, anti-aging with gut support

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### Semaglutide/Tirzepatide + L-Glutamine
- **Rationale:** GLP-1 agonists reduce appetite and caloric intake significantly. Glutamine supplementation helps maintain gut barrier integrity during caloric deficit, prevents muscle catabolism, and may reduce GI side effects (nausea, constipation) common with these peptides.
- **Protocol:** L-Glutamine 10-15 g/day + Semaglutide or Tirzepatide at prescribed dose
- **Best for:** Weight loss protocols where gut health maintenance and muscle preservation are priorities

#### Thymosin Alpha-1 + L-Glutamine
- **Rationale:** Both are potent immune modulators. Thymosin Alpha-1 enhances T-cell function; glutamine fuels lymphocyte proliferation. Together they create a comprehensive immune support stack.
- **Protocol:** L-Glutamine 10 g/day + Thymosin Alpha-1 at standard dosing
- **Best for:** Immune optimization, chronic infection support, post-illness recovery

#### VIP (Vasoactive Intestinal Peptide) + L-Glutamine
- **Rationale:** VIP regulates gut motility, reduces inflammation, and supports the gut-brain axis. Glutamine provides the structural repair substrate. Particularly relevant for CIRS/mold illness protocols.
- **Protocol:** L-Glutamine 15 g/day + VIP (nasal spray per standard protocol)
- **Best for:** CIRS, mold illness, complex gut-brain dysfunction
