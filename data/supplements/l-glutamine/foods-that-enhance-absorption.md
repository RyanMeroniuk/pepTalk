# L-Glutamine
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

### Direct Absorption Enhancers
- **Electrolytes (sodium, potassium)** — glutamine is co-transported with sodium in the intestinal lumen via the ASCT2 and B0AT1 transporters. A pinch of sea salt in your glutamine water genuinely improves uptake.
- **Glucose / simple carbohydrates** — co-ingestion with small amounts of glucose enhances intestinal glutamine absorption via coupled transport mechanisms. A teaspoon of honey or a few sips of juice works.
- **Coconut water** — natural source of electrolytes and simple sugars; excellent mixing vehicle for glutamine

### Synergistic Nutrients (Enhance Glutamine's Effects)
- **Vitamin C-rich foods** — supports collagen synthesis and immune function alongside glutamine. Examples: bell peppers, citrus, kiwi, strawberries
- **Zinc-rich foods** — zinc and glutamine together support gut barrier repair and immune function. Examples: oysters, pumpkin seeds, beef
- **N-Acetyl Cysteine (NAC) or cysteine-rich foods** — glutamine provides glutamate for glutathione; cysteine is the rate-limiting precursor. Together they maximize glutathione production. Examples: eggs, garlic, onions, cruciferous vegetables
- **Glycine-rich foods** — glycine + glutamate + cysteine = glutathione. Bone broth, collagen, gelatin are ideal.
- **Omega-3 fatty acids** — reduce intestinal inflammation, allowing better glutamine utilization for repair rather than defense. Examples: wild salmon, sardines, mackerel

### Optimal Pairing Meals
- **Post-workout:** Glutamine powder + whey protein shake + banana (provides leucine, glutamine, glucose, and potassium)
- **Gut healing:** Glutamine powder + bone broth + pinch of sea salt (provides glutamine, glycine, collagen, sodium)
- **Morning protocol:** Glutamine in cool water with a squeeze of lemon and pinch of Himalayan salt (enhances transport, adds vitamin C)
- **Evening:** Glutamine + chamomile tea (cooled) + honey (1 tsp) for gut repair and sleep support
