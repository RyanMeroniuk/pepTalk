# L-Glutamine
## Benefits

### Gut Health & Repair
- **Reduces intestinal permeability** — clinical trials show glutamine supplementation (0.5 g/kg/day) significantly reduces lactulose/mannitol ratio, a direct measure of leaky gut
- **IBS symptom reduction** — a 2019 RCT in *Gut* (Zhou et al.) showed 5 g 3x/day reduced IBS-D severity scores by 50+ points vs placebo over 8 weeks
- **Post-infectious IBS** — particularly effective when gut permeability is elevated after GI infections
- **Supports mucosal healing** in IBD (adjunctive, not standalone therapy)
- **Prevents NSAID-induced gut damage** — protects against aspirin and ibuprofen-related intestinal permeability increases

### Immune Function
- **Reduces infection rates** in endurance athletes — marathon runners supplementing with glutamine (5 g post-race) showed significantly fewer upper respiratory infections in the 7 days post-race
- **Critical care outcomes** — IV glutamine supplementation in ICU patients reduces infectious complications and hospital stay duration (multiple meta-analyses)
- **Supports immune recovery** during and after intense training blocks

### Exercise & Recovery
- **Reduces muscle soreness (DOMS)** — some evidence for faster recovery when dosed at 0.3 g/kg/day
- **Glycogen replenishment** — glutamine enhances muscle glycogen resynthesis post-exercise when combined with carbohydrates
- **Anti-catabolic** — helps preserve lean mass during caloric deficit or overtraining
- **Reduces exercise-induced ammonia** — buffers ammonia accumulation during prolonged exercise

### Cravings & Blood Sugar
- **Reduces sugar and alcohol cravings** — 1-2 g dissolved under the tongue can acutely reduce cravings by providing alternative brain fuel
- **Supports blood glucose stability** via gluconeogenesis during fasting states

### Wound Healing
- **Accelerates wound repair** — glutamine is the preferred fuel for fibroblasts and is consumed at very high rates during tissue repair
- **Post-surgical recovery** — clinical protocols often include 20-30 g/day glutamine for surgical patients

### Antioxidant Support
- **Increases glutathione levels** — by providing the glutamate precursor, glutamine indirectly supports glutathione synthesis, the body's master antioxidant
