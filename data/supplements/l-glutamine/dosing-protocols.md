# L-Glutamine
## Dosing Protocols

### General Maintenance
- **Dose:** 5 g/day
- **Timing:** Morning on an empty stomach or before bed
- **Duration:** Ongoing; safe for long-term use

### Gut Healing Protocol (Leaky Gut, IBS, IBD)
- **Dose:** 10-20 g/day, divided into 2-3 doses
- **Timing:**
  - 5 g first thing in the morning on an empty stomach (30 min before food)
  - 5 g mid-afternoon between meals
  - 5 g before bed on an empty stomach
- **Duration:** 8-12 weeks for a focused gut repair protocol, then reduce to maintenance (5 g/day)
- **Notes:** Mix in room-temperature or cool water. Drink immediately after mixing.

### Athletic Performance & Recovery
- **Dose:** 10-15 g/day
- **Timing:**
  - 5 g pre-workout (30 min before)
  - 5 g post-workout (within 30 min)
  - Optional: 5 g before bed for overnight recovery
- **Loading phase:** Not required, but some athletes front-load with 20 g/day for the first 5-7 days

### Immune Support (Heavy Training Blocks)
- **Dose:** 10 g/day
- **Timing:** 5 g post-training + 5 g before bed
- **Duration:** Throughout intense training periods; reduce to 5 g/day during deload weeks

### Craving Reduction (Sugar/Alcohol)
- **Dose:** 1-2 g as needed
- **Timing:** Dissolve powder under the tongue when cravings hit (sublingual absorption is rapid)
- **Frequency:** Up to 3-4 times daily
- **Notes:** This is an acute intervention, not a long-term protocol

### Post-Surgical / Critical Illness
- **Dose:** 20-30 g/day (under medical supervision)
- **Timing:** Divided into 3-4 doses throughout the day
- **Duration:** Duration of recovery period (typically 2-6 weeks)

### With BPC-157 Gut Healing Stack
- **Dose:** 10-15 g/day L-Glutamine + BPC-157 at standard peptide dose
- **Timing:** Take glutamine 30 min before BPC-157 oral dose to prime the gut lining
- **Duration:** 4-8 week cycles

### Important Notes
- **No loading phase required** for most use cases
- **Dissolve in cool or room-temperature water only** — heat destroys glutamine
- **Drink immediately after mixing** — glutamine degrades in solution
- **Can be taken with or without food**, but empty stomach maximizes gut-targeted delivery
- **Upper limit:** 40 g/day has been used safely in clinical settings, but most people don't need more than 20 g/day
