# Taurine
## Things to Avoid

### Misconceptions to Avoid
- **Don't treat taurine as a proven anti-aging drug** — the 2023 *Cell* longevity findings were in animals, and the 2025 NIH human study (*Science*) found taurine does NOT reliably decline with human age. Take taurine for its well-supported cardiometabolic benefits, not for an unproven human lifespan effect.
- **Don't expect creatine-level performance gains** — taurine's ergogenic effects are modest and supportive, not primary
- **Don't get taurine from energy drinks** for health purposes — the caffeine and sugar undermine the benefits

### Dosing Mistakes
- **Megadosing without reason** — there is no evidence that exceeding ~3 g/day adds cardiometabolic benefit for most people; excess is excreted
- **Taking giant single doses** — very large single doses can saturate the transporter and may cause GI upset; split 3+ g into two doses
- **Inconsistency** — tissue taurine accumulates with regular intake; sporadic dosing limits benefit

### Population-Specific Cautions
- **Low blood pressure / on antihypertensives** — taurine can further lower BP; monitor and consult your physician
- **Bipolar disorder** — caution due to energy-drink case reports (confounded by caffeine); discuss with your prescriber
- **Kidney disease** — the kidneys regulate taurine; impaired renal function warrants medical guidance
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding** — taurine is critical in fetal/infant development and present in breast milk, but supplemental dosing during pregnancy lacks robust safety data; consult an OB/GYN

### Medical Cautions
- **Inform your physician** if you have cardiovascular, renal, hepatic, or psychiatric conditions before supplementing
- **Do not use taurine to self-treat hypertension or diabetes** in place of prescribed care — it is an adjunct, framed for research/education, not medical advice
- **Discontinue and seek care** if you experience unusual symptoms, though serious adverse effects are very rare

### Behaviors That Undermine Benefits
- **Pairing taurine with a high-sugar, high-caffeine lifestyle** that opposes its metabolic and cardiovascular benefits
- **Heavy alcohol use** — depletes taurine and stresses the same systems taurine supports
- **Expecting taurine alone to fix cardiometabolic health** — diet, exercise, sleep, and stress management remain primary
