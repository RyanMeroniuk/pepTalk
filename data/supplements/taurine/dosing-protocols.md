# Taurine
## Dosing Protocols

### General Health / Cardiometabolic (Most Common)
- **Dose:** 1-3 g/day
- **Timing:** With or without food; can be split (e.g., 1.5 g twice daily) or taken once daily
- **Duration:** Safe for long-term daily use; cardiometabolic benefits build over weeks
- **Notes:** Most human trials showing blood pressure, glucose, and triglyceride benefits used 1-3 g/day

### Blood Pressure Support
- **Dose:** 1.5-3 g/day
- **Timing:** Split dosing (morning and evening) is common in trials
- **Duration:** Benefits typically observed over 4-12 weeks of consistent use
- **Notes:** Larger effects seen in hypertensive individuals; not a replacement for prescribed antihypertensives

### Glucose / Metabolic Syndrome
- **Dose:** 1.5-3 g/day
- **Timing:** With meals
- **Duration:** Several weeks to months for measurable changes in glucose and triglycerides

### Exercise / Performance
- **Dose:** 1-3 g, taken ~1-2 hours before training (single pre-workout dose) or 1-2 g/day chronically
- **Timing:** Pre-exercise for acute endurance/fatigue support
- **Notes:** Effects are modest; taurine is a supportive rather than primary ergogenic

### Longevity-Oriented Use (Speculative)
- **Dose:** Often 1.5-3 g/day, extrapolated from the 2023 *Cell* mouse data (which used doses far higher relative to body weight)
- **Honest caveat:** No validated human longevity dose exists. The 2025 NIH study undercut the rationale that low taurine drives human aging. Use the cardiometabolic dose range and do not expect a proven anti-aging effect.

### Upper Range
- **Doses up to 6 g/day** have been used in studies (e.g., heart failure trials) with good tolerability
- **No established toxic dose** in humans; the body excretes excess via the kidneys
- **No official Tolerable Upper Intake Level** has been set, but staying at or below 3 g/day covers nearly all evidence-based benefits

### Timing & Practical Notes
- **Consistency matters more than precise timing** — tissue taurine accumulates with regular intake
- **Can be combined freely with most supplements** — taurine has no significant absorption conflicts
- **Split large doses** — if taking 3+ g, dividing into two doses may improve retention by avoiding transporter saturation
- **No loading phase needed**
