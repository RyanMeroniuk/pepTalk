# Taurine
## Classification

- **Category:** Conditionally essential amino acid (sulfur-containing beta-amino acid)
- **Chemical class:** Beta-amino sulfonic acid (2-aminoethanesulfonic acid) — not a true alpha-amino acid and not incorporated into proteins
- **Molecular formula:** C2H7NO3S
- **Supplement type:** Cardiovascular / metabolic / cellular health supplement; also widely used as a performance and energy-drink ingredient
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (OTC); GRAS status; one of the most abundant free amino acids in the body
- **Primary systems:** Cardiovascular (blood pressure, contractility), metabolic (glucose, triglycerides), nervous system (osmoregulation, GABAergic activity), skeletal muscle, retina, bile metabolism
- **Endogenous synthesis:** Synthesized from cysteine (via cysteine dioxygenase and cysteinesulfinic acid decarboxylase, CSAD) primarily in the liver; synthesis is limited in humans, making dietary intake meaningful
- **Storage:** Highly concentrated intracellularly in the heart, skeletal muscle, retina, brain, and white blood cells; one of the most abundant free amino acids in excitable tissues
