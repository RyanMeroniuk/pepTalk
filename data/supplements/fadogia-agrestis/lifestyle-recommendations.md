# Fadogia Agrestis
## Lifestyle Recommendations

> Given fadogia agrestis's rodent testicular-toxicity findings and near-total absence of human safety data, the most important lifestyle recommendation is to prioritize the proven, low-risk levers for testosterone and to treat fadogia as experimental (and likely skippable).

- **Exhaust the safe fundamentals first:** Sleep (7-9 hours), resistance training, controlling body fat, adequate protein and healthy fat, limiting alcohol, and managing stress are the most reliable, lowest-risk ways to support testosterone. These should be fully in place before considering any experimental herb
- **Prefer better-characterized supplements:** If supplementing for testosterone support, tongkat-ali (lowers SHBG, raises free-T) and boron (lowers SHBG/estradiol) have far better evidence-to-risk profiles than fadogia. Address the nutrient base with zinc, magnesium, and vitamin D3
- **If you choose to experiment anyway, do so conservatively and monitored:**
  - Involve a physician; get baseline and follow-up labs (total/free testosterone, LH, and reproductive/organ-health markers given the testicular-toxicity concern)
  - Use only the commonly cited dose at most (~600 mg/day), strictly cycled rather than continuous, and stop at the first sign of testicular pain, swelling, or other adverse effects
  - Do not stack it simultaneously with other hormonal agents — isolate the variable
- **Protect the liver and kidneys:** Because rodent studies hinted at broader organ stress, avoid heavy alcohol and other hepatotoxic/nephrotoxic exposures while using it, and do not combine with other experimental compounds
- **Reassess constantly:** This is genuine self-experimentation without a safety net. Periodically ask whether perceived benefits justify the unquantified risk — and recognize that placebo and concurrent lifestyle changes often explain anecdotal "results"
- **Fertility-conscious individuals should not use it:** The clearest lifestyle guidance is that anyone who values current or future fertility should avoid fadogia entirely
