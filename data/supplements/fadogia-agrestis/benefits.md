# Fadogia Agrestis
## Benefits

> CRITICAL CONTEXT: The "benefits" below are derived almost exclusively from rodent studies and traditional use. There are essentially NO human clinical trials demonstrating these benefits in people, and the same research base includes testicular-toxicity signals. These should be read as *purported/hypothetical* benefits, not established outcomes.

- **Purported testosterone increase (rodent data only):** Rat studies report dose-dependent increases in serum testosterone with fadogia stem extract. This is the headline reason for its popularity, but it has not been demonstrated in controlled human trials. Anecdotal human reports exist but are unverified and prone to placebo and confounding (people often add it alongside other lifestyle changes and supplements)
- **Purported libido / aphrodisiac effect (traditional use + rodent data):** Traditionally used as an aphrodisiac in West Africa, and rat studies show increased sexual behavior. Human efficacy for libido is not established in trials
- **Proposed LH-pathway support:** The appeal is that it might raise testosterone via increased LH signaling (endogenous pathway) rather than supplying a hormone — but this mechanism and its human relevance remain unconfirmed
- **Fertility/sexual interest (claimed):** Sometimes promoted for sexual performance; the only supporting data is animal behavior, and the toxicity findings raise concern about reproductive tissue rather than reassurance

### Honest Assessment
- **The benefit case is weak and unproven in humans.** The popularity of fadogia is driven by a plausible mechanism and high-profile mentions, not by clinical evidence
- **The benefit-to-risk balance is unfavorable on current evidence:** the purported benefits are unverified in humans, while the most concrete findings in the literature are rodent **testicular toxicity** at higher doses
- For most people seeking testosterone support, far better-characterized options exist (tongkat-ali, boron, addressing sleep/training/body fat). Fadogia should be regarded as experimental, and the burden of caution is high
