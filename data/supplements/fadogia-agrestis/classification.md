# Fadogia Agrestis
## Classification

- **Category:** Herbal supplement / purported testosterone (LH) stimulant — with a notably weak safety profile
- **Subcategory:** West African traditional botanical; "experimental" testosterone-support herb
- **Botanical name:** Fadogia agrestis
- **Family:** Rubiaceae (the coffee/madder family)
- **Plant part used:** Stem (traditionally a stem decoction)
- **Active compounds:** Believed to include alkaloids and saponins; the specific bioactive constituents responsible for hormonal effects are not well characterized
- **Regulatory status:** Sold as a dietary supplement (USA, OTC) but with essentially NO clinical safety/efficacy data in humans; not an approved drug; not formally evaluated by FDA for safety
- **Primary purported system:** Endocrine — proposed to increase luteinizing hormone (LH) and thereby testosterone
- **Popularization:** Brought to attention by Andrew Huberman and others as a cycled "natural testosterone" agent (commonly ~600 mg/day, cycled); this popularity vastly outpaces the underlying evidence
- **CRITICAL safety flag:** The available evidence is almost entirely from rodent studies, some of which show **testicular toxicity at higher doses**. Human safety data is essentially absent. This item should be framed as experimental and high-uncertainty — see side effects and things-to-avoid
