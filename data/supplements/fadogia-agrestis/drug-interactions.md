# Fadogia Agrestis
## Drug Interactions

> There is essentially NO human drug-interaction data for fadogia agrestis. The guidance below is precautionary, extrapolated from its purported hormonal activity and the rodent toxicity (testicular and possible liver/kidney) signals. Absence of documented interactions reflects lack of study, not safety.

### Contraindications (Strongly Advised — Avoid)
- **Hormone-sensitive cancers and prostate conditions:** Avoid given fadogia's purported testosterone-pathway activity (prostate cancer, other hormone-sensitive cancers, prostate conditions)
- **Active cancer (general):** Do not use without oncologist input
- **Pregnancy and breastfeeding:** Contraindicated — experimental hormonal agent with reproductive-toxicity signals
- **Fertility concerns / trying to conceive:** Avoid given rodent testicular-toxicity findings
- **Existing liver or kidney disease:** Avoid given rodent organ-toxicity signals and hepatic processing of botanicals

### Caution / Monitor (Moderate — Theoretical)
- **Hormone therapy / TRT and other testosterone agents:** Combining fadogia with testosterone replacement or other testosterone-raising compounds stacks hormonal load and risk, and complicates monitoring. Avoid or use only under specialist oversight
- **Hepatotoxic drugs (e.g., certain statins, acetaminophen in excess, some antifungals):** Given rodent liver-stress signals, combining with drugs that burden the liver is a theoretical additive concern. Use caution and monitor liver enzymes
- **Nephrotoxic drugs (e.g., chronic high-dose NSAIDs, some antibiotics):** Theoretical additive kidney burden given rodent organ findings; monitor
- **Blood pressure medications:** No data, but as with many botanicals, monitor blood pressure if combining
- **Antidepressants:** No characterized interaction; monitor mood and for any adverse effects given the unstudied profile
- **Anticoagulants / blood thinners:** Unknown; use caution as botanical effects on clotting are unstudied here

### Timing Considerations
- **Separate from medications generally:** Because fadogia's pharmacokinetics and enzyme interactions are unknown, it is prudent to keep it hours apart from prescription medications rather than co-administering, to limit unstudied interaction potential
- **Take other supplements at a different time** to better isolate any effects or adverse reactions

### Monitoring (Essential If Used)
- Baseline and follow-up labs: total/free testosterone, LH, liver enzymes, kidney function, and reproductive-health markers given the testicular-toxicity concern
- **Inform your physician** before use and before any procedures or lab interpretation

### General Safety Notes
- The dominant concern is not a known pharmacologic interaction but the **inherent toxicity uncertainty** of an unstudied hormonal botanical with rodent testicular-damage findings
- The most defensible position for most people is non-use, favoring better-characterized testosterone-support options
