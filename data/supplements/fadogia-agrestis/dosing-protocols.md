# Fadogia Agrestis
## Dosing Protocols

> CRITICAL DISCLAIMER: There is NO clinically established safe or effective human dose of fadogia agrestis. The figures below reflect popular/anecdotal practice (largely extrapolated from rodent studies and public figures like Andrew Huberman), NOT validated human protocols. Rodent studies show testicular toxicity at higher doses, so dosing guidance here is descriptive of common practice and its cautions — not an endorsement.

- **Commonly cited popular protocol:**
  - **Dose:** ~600 mg/day of fadogia stem extract
  - **Pattern:** Typically **cycled** — a common anecdotal approach is several weeks on followed by time off (e.g., 8 weeks on / period off, or "cycle and don't use continuously"). The cycling itself reflects safety concern, not a validated schedule
  - **Timing:** Usually morning; no evidence-based timing exists
  - **Reality check:** This dose is derived from anecdote and rodent extrapolation. It is NOT supported by human safety or efficacy trials

- **Why cycling is emphasized (and still not protective by evidence):**
  - The animal toxicity findings (testicular damage at higher/continuous dosing) are the reason proponents recommend cycling and avoiding continuous use
  - However, cycling is a precaution born of *uncertainty*, not a demonstrated safe pattern. There is no human data confirming that any on/off schedule prevents harm

- **Monitoring (essential if used at all):**
  - Anyone choosing to experiment should consider baseline and follow-up labs: total/free testosterone, LH, and — given the testicular-toxicity concern — markers of reproductive and organ health, ideally with physician involvement
  - Discontinue at any sign of testicular pain, swelling, libido collapse, or other adverse effects

- **What to avoid:**
  - **Continuous, uncycled use** and **higher-than-typical doses** — these most closely mirror the conditions associated with toxicity in rodents
  - **Stacking it with other hormonal agents** (DHEA, TRT) or multiple testosterone herbs at once, which obscures cause of any effect or harm

- **Bottom line:** The most defensible "protocol" for most people is not to use fadogia at all given the risk/evidence imbalance, and to favor better-characterized options (tongkat-ali, boron) plus lifestyle fundamentals
