# B-Complex
## Classification

- **Category:** Water-soluble vitamin complex / essential coenzymes
- **Also known as:** B-vitamin complex, vitamin B complex
- **Vitamin group:** Water-soluble (8 distinct B vitamins that work synergistically)
- **Individual B vitamins in the complex:**
  - **B1 (Thiamine)** — energy metabolism, nerve function
  - **B2 (Riboflavin)** — energy production, antioxidant recycling, FAD/FMN coenzyme
  - **B3 (Niacin/Niacinamide)** — NAD+/NADH production, energy metabolism, DNA repair
  - **B5 (Pantothenic acid)** — coenzyme A synthesis, adrenal function, fatty acid metabolism
  - **B6 (Pyridoxine/P-5-P)** — neurotransmitter synthesis, amino acid metabolism, homocysteine regulation
  - **B7 (Biotin)** — carboxylase cofactor, hair/skin/nail health, glucose metabolism
  - **B9 (Folate/Folic acid)** — DNA synthesis, methylation, neural tube development
  - **B12 (Cobalamin)** — methylation, myelin synthesis, red blood cell formation, nerve function
- **Essential nutrients:** Yes — all 8 B vitamins are essential and must be obtained from diet or supplementation
- **Endogenous production:** Minimal — gut bacteria produce small amounts of some B vitamins (biotin, B12, folate) but not enough to meet requirements
