# B-Complex
## Benefits

- **Energy production:** B vitamins are cofactors in every step of cellular energy production — glycolysis, citric acid cycle, electron transport chain, and fatty acid oxidation. Deficiency causes profound fatigue. Supplementation improves energy levels particularly in those with subclinical deficiency, chronic fatigue, or high metabolic demands.
- **Homocysteine reduction:** B6 + B9 + B12 supplementation reduces homocysteine levels by 20-40%. Elevated homocysteine is an independent risk factor for cardiovascular disease, stroke, cognitive decline, and depression. Methylated forms are most effective, especially in MTHFR carriers.
- **Mood and mental health:** B vitamins are required for serotonin, dopamine, GABA, and norepinephrine synthesis. Meta-analyses show B-complex supplementation reduces stress perception by 20-30%, reduces anxiety symptoms, and improves depressive symptoms. B6, B9, and B12 deficiency are independently associated with depression.
- **Cognitive function and neuroprotection:** B12, B9, and B6 supplementation slows brain atrophy by 30-50% in elderly individuals with elevated homocysteine (VITACOG trial). Higher B vitamin status is associated with reduced risk of Alzheimer's disease and cognitive decline. B12 is critical for myelin maintenance.
- **Cardiovascular health:** Beyond homocysteine reduction, B vitamins support endothelial function, nitric oxide production (B9), and healthy lipid metabolism (B3/niacin raises HDL by 15-35% and lowers triglycerides by 20-50%).
- **Red blood cell formation:** B12 and folate are essential for erythropoiesis. Deficiency in either causes megaloblastic anemia with symptoms of fatigue, weakness, shortness of breath, and pallor.
- **Nerve function and peripheral neuropathy:** B12 and B1 are critical for nerve health. B12 deficiency causes peripheral neuropathy (numbness, tingling, burning in hands and feet). B1 deficiency causes beriberi neuropathy. High-dose B1 (benfotiamine, 300-600 mg/day) reduces diabetic neuropathy symptoms.
- **Skin, hair, and nail health:** B7 (biotin) is well-known for supporting keratin production. B2 (riboflavin) deficiency causes dermatitis and angular cheilitis. B3 (niacinamide) reduces acne, rosacea, and skin aging. B5 supports skin barrier function and wound healing.
- **Pregnancy and fertility:** Folate (methylfolate) prevents neural tube defects (70-80% reduction). B12 is essential for fetal brain development. B6 reduces nausea/morning sickness (10-25 mg three times daily is first-line treatment for hyperemesis gravidarum).
- **Adrenal and stress support:** B5 (pantothenic acid) is required for cortisol synthesis and adrenal function. B-complex supplementation during periods of stress supports HPA axis function and resilience.
- **Methylation support:** B9 (methylfolate), B12 (methylcobalamin), and B6 (P-5-P) directly fuel the methylation cycle — the body's master regulatory system controlling gene expression, detoxification, neurotransmitter metabolism, hormone metabolism, and immune function. Inadequate methylation is linked to a wide range of chronic diseases.
- **Immune function:** B6, B9, and B12 are required for lymphocyte proliferation, antibody production, and natural killer cell activity. B6 deficiency significantly impairs immune response. B12 deficiency reduces CD8+ T-cell counts.
