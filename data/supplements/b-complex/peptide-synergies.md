# B-Complex
## Peptide Synergies

### Cognitive and Neuroprotective Peptides
- **Semax:** Semax upregulates BDNF and enhances cognitive function. B vitamins are critical cofactors for the neurotransmitter synthesis that Semax amplifies — B6 (P-5-P) is required for serotonin, dopamine, GABA, and norepinephrine production; B9 (methylfolate) and B12 (methylcobalamin) support the methylation cycle that produces SAMe, the methyl donor for neurotransmitter metabolism. Inadequate B vitamin status blunts the cognitive benefits of Semax. Ensure B6 (25-50 mg P-5-P), methylfolate (400-800 mcg), and methylcobalamin (500-1,000 mcg) are optimized.
- **Selank:** Selank modulates GABA and has anxiolytic effects. B6 is the essential cofactor for glutamic acid decarboxylase (GAD), the enzyme that converts glutamate to GABA. Without adequate B6, GABA synthesis is impaired and Selank's anxiolytic effects may be diminished. B-complex provides the neurochemical foundation for Selank's calming mechanism.
- **Dihexa:** Dihexa promotes synapse formation and cognitive enhancement. B12 is essential for myelin maintenance (the insulation surrounding neurons), and folate supports DNA synthesis in rapidly dividing neural cells. Adequate B vitamin status ensures the neurological infrastructure is intact for Dihexa's synaptogenic effects.
- **Pinealon:** Pinealon supports memory and circadian rhythm regulation. B6 is required for melatonin synthesis (serotonin → melatonin pathway). B-complex supports the neurotransmitter and hormonal systems that Pinealon modulates.

### Growth Hormone Peptides
- **CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin:** GH secretagogues promote protein synthesis, tissue repair, and metabolic function. B vitamins are cofactors in every step of protein synthesis and energy metabolism that GH stimulates. B6 supports amino acid metabolism (transamination reactions), B1/B2/B3/B5 fuel the energy production pathways that anabolic processes require, and B9/B12 support the DNA synthesis needed for cell proliferation. Optimizing B vitamin status maximizes the anabolic response to GH secretagogue protocols.
- **GHRP-2 / GHRP-6:** Both stimulate GH release and increase appetite (especially GHRP-6). B1 (thiamine) is essential for carbohydrate metabolism from increased food intake, and B-complex supports the metabolic processing of the additional nutrients consumed due to appetite stimulation.

### Healing and Tissue Repair Peptides
- **BPC-157:** BPC-157 accelerates wound healing, tendon repair, and gut mucosal regeneration. B vitamins support multiple aspects of this healing process: B5 (pantothenic acid) is required for coenzyme A synthesis critical to tissue repair pathways; B6 supports collagen cross-linking (lysyl oxidase is a B6-dependent process); B9 and B12 fuel the rapid cell division required for tissue regeneration. Particularly synergistic for gut healing protocols where BPC-157's mucosal repair benefits require adequate cellular building blocks.
- **TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4):** TB-500 promotes cell migration and tissue remodeling. B9 and B12 are essential for the DNA synthesis driving new cell production during repair. B5 supports the energy metabolism of actively remodeling tissues. The combination accelerates recovery from soft tissue injuries.
- **GHK-Cu:** Both B-complex and GHK-Cu support collagen production and tissue regeneration. B vitamins provide systemic metabolic support while GHK-Cu acts locally through copper-dependent enzyme activation. B2 (riboflavin) supports glutathione recycling, which protects healing tissues from oxidative damage.

### Immune Peptides
- **Thymosin Alpha-1:** Thymosin Alpha-1 promotes T-cell maturation and immune surveillance. B6, B9, and B12 are required for lymphocyte proliferation, antibody production, and NK cell activity. B6 deficiency significantly impairs immune response. Optimizing B vitamin status ensures the immune cells that Thymosin Alpha-1 activates are functionally competent.
- **LL-37:** LL-37 provides direct antimicrobial activity. B vitamins support the immune cells (neutrophils, macrophages) that work alongside LL-37 for pathogen defense. B12 supports CD8+ T-cell counts and B6 enhances neutrophil function.
- **Thymulin:** Both thymulin and B vitamins support T-cell differentiation. Thymulin requires zinc for activation — B6 supports zinc absorption and metabolism, creating an indirect synergy. B9 and B12 fuel the rapid cell division of maturing immune cells.

### Sleep and Recovery Peptides
- **DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide):** B6 is required for the serotonin → melatonin conversion pathway. Adequate B6 (taken in the morning) ensures melatonin precursor availability for DSIP's sleep-inducing effects at night. B5 supports adrenal function and cortisol regulation, complementing DSIP's cortisol-modulating properties.
- **Epitalon:** Epitalon activates telomerase and regulates pineal melatonin production. B9 and B12 support DNA integrity and methylation — critical for the telomere biology that Epitalon targets. B6 supports the melatonin synthesis pathway that Epitalon enhances. B3 (as NAD+) serves as a substrate for sirtuins involved in the longevity pathways that Epitalon also influences.

### Metabolic Peptides
- **Semaglutide:** Semaglutide reduces appetite and promotes weight loss via GLP-1 receptor agonism. During caloric restriction and weight loss, B vitamin requirements may increase as stored nutrients are mobilized and metabolic pathways are upregulated for fat catabolism. B5 is required for coenzyme A (central to fatty acid oxidation), B1/B2/B3 fuel energy metabolism, and B12/folate support the cellular turnover that increases during metabolic changes. Metformin users (often on semaglutide concurrently for diabetes) have increased B12 depletion risk.
- **MOTS-c:** MOTS-c is a mitochondrial peptide that acts as an exercise mimetic. B vitamins are integral to mitochondrial energy production — B1, B2, B3, and B5 are all directly involved in the electron transport chain and citric acid cycle that MOTS-c enhances. NAD+ (from B3) is particularly critical as a substrate for mitochondrial function.

### Anti-Aging Peptides
- **Epitalon:** (See Sleep and Recovery above) B vitamins support telomere integrity through methylation (B9/B12/B6), DNA repair (B3/NAD+ for PARPs), and oxidative stress protection (B2 for glutathione recycling).
- **GHK-Cu:** B vitamins and GHK-Cu both support anti-aging mechanisms. B-complex provides systemic metabolic support for the cellular processes that GHK-Cu stimulates locally. B2 supports glutathione, which protects against the oxidative aging that GHK-Cu helps reverse.

### General Protocol Notes
- **B vitamins are foundational coenzymes** that support virtually every metabolic process peptides target. Deficiency in any B vitamin can limit peptide efficacy.
- **Optimize B vitamin status before starting peptide protocols.** Check homocysteine, MMA, and serum B12 at minimum.
- **Use methylated forms** (methylfolate, methylcobalamin, P-5-P) to ensure optimal utilization regardless of MTHFR status.
- **Take B-complex in the morning** with breakfast — peptides are typically dosed at different times, and B vitamins provide the metabolic infrastructure peptides need throughout the day.
- **During any peptide protocol:** A high-quality methylated B-complex (25-100 mg potency range) daily is considered essential foundational support.
