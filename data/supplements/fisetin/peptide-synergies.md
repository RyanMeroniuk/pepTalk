# Fisetin
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### Epitalon + Fisetin (Premier Longevity Pairing)
- **Rationale:** Epitalon supports telomere maintenance and circadian/pineal function, while fisetin clears senescent cells and lowers the SASP inflammation that drives tissue aging. Together they target two distinct hallmarks of aging — telomere attrition (Epitalon) and senescent-cell burden plus inflammaging (fisetin).
- **Protocol:** Fisetin in periodic "hit-and-run" senolytic windows (with fat) + Epitalon per standard longevity cycling
- **Best for:** Longevity-focused protocols centered on cellular aging
- **Note:** Fisetin's senolytic evidence in humans is preliminary

#### MOTS-c + Fisetin (Mitochondrial + Senescence)
- **Rationale:** MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-derived peptide that activates AMPK and acts as an exercise mimetic. Fisetin overlaps via SIRT1/AMPK signaling and reduces inflammatory and oxidative stress on mitochondria. Together they support mitochondrial and metabolic resilience while reducing senescent-cell load.
- **Protocol:** Fisetin senolytic dosing (with fat) + MOTS-c at standard dosing
- **Best for:** Metabolic and longevity-focused optimization

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### BPC-157 + Fisetin (Recovery + Inflammation Control)
- **Rationale:** BPC-157 accelerates tissue repair while fisetin reduces NF-kB-driven inflammation and may clear senescent cells that impair healing in aged tissue. Fisetin lowers the inflammatory/senescent background; BPC-157 drives active repair.
- **Protocol:** Fisetin daily low-dose (or periodic senolytic windows) + BPC-157 250-500 mcg 2x/day
- **Best for:** Recovery, especially in older individuals with higher senescence burden

#### TB-500 + Fisetin (Systemic Repair + Antioxidant Support)
- **Rationale:** TB-500 promotes systemic tissue repair and flexibility; fisetin provides antioxidant cover and reduces inflammatory signaling during remodeling.
- **Protocol:** Fisetin (with fat) + TB-500 2.5-5 mg 2x/week
- **Best for:** Recovery phases, return to training

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + Fisetin (Recovery + Antioxidant Cover)
- **Rationale:** GH secretagogues like CJC-1295/Ipamorelin drive recovery and anabolism; fisetin reduces the oxidative and inflammatory load of intense training and may help clear senescent cells that blunt regeneration.
- **Protocol:** Fisetin low-dose daily or periodic + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Best for:** Recovery-focused, anti-aging stacks

#### GHK-Cu + Fisetin (Skin & Anti-Aging)
- **Rationale:** GHK-Cu promotes collagen synthesis and skin remodeling; fisetin clears senescent skin fibroblasts and protects cells from oxidative damage — senescent fibroblasts are a known driver of skin aging.
- **Protocol:** Fisetin periodic senolytic dosing + GHK-Cu per standard protocol
- **Best for:** Anti-aging skin protocols

### Supplement Stack Partners (Non-Peptide)
- **Fisetin + Quercetin** — both are flavonol senolytic candidates with shared rationale; commonly combined in senescence-focused protocols (human evidence preliminary)
- **Fisetin + Resveratrol** — overlapping SIRT1/AMPK longevity pathways; common in polyphenol longevity stacks
- **Fisetin + NMN** — NMN restores NAD+ to fuel sirtuins and DNA repair while fisetin reduces senescent-cell burden and inflammaging; complementary longevity mechanisms
