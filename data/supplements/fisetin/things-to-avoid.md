# Fisetin
## Things to Avoid

### Overstating the Evidence
- **Treating fisetin's senolytic benefits as proven in humans** — the dramatic results are from mouse studies (notably Yousefzadeh et al., 2018); human trials are ongoing and unfinished. Set expectations accordingly.
- **Assuming "hit-and-run" dosing is clinically validated** — the protocol is extrapolated from animal data and longevity-community practice, not from confirmed human dosing guidelines

### Ignoring Bioavailability
- **Taking standard fisetin powder dry or without fat** — fisetin absorbs poorly and is cleared quickly. Always take with a fatty meal, and consider liposomal/phospholipid forms for better uptake.

### Drug-Interaction Blind Spots (Important)
- **Combining fisetin with CYP-metabolized medications without checking** — fisetin can inhibit certain CYP enzymes and transporters, potentially raising drug levels (see drug-interactions). Review your medication list with a pharmacist.
- **Stacking fisetin with grapefruit** while on CYP3A4-metabolized drugs — additive enzyme inhibition

### Medical Cautions
- **Anticoagulants/blood thinners** — possible mild antiplatelet effect; use caution and inform your physician, especially before surgery
- **Active cancer / chemotherapy** — consult an oncologist before high-dose use; antioxidant/enzyme effects could interact with treatment
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding** — avoid concentrated supplemental doses (insufficient safety data)
- **Chronic disease or polypharmacy** — discuss high-dose protocols with a clinician

### High-Dose & Quality Mistakes
- **Assuming high doses are automatically safe because it's "natural"** — long-term safety of repeated high-dose fisetin in humans is not established
- **Buying low-quality or unstandardized products** — choose third-party-tested fisetin (98%+ purity); the market quality varies
- **Daily mega-dosing as a senolytic** — the senolytic rationale is intermittent clearance ("hit-and-run"), not continuous high exposure; chronic high daily dosing is neither validated nor clearly beneficial

### Realistic Expectations
- **Fisetin is a promising, well-tolerated longevity candidate — not a proven anti-aging therapy.** Use it with an understanding of where the evidence currently stands.
