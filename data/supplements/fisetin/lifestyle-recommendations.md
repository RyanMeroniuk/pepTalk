# Fisetin
## Lifestyle Recommendations

### Diet (Food-First Foundation)
- **Eat fisetin-rich foods** — strawberries are the standout (and conveniently rich in vitamin C); apples, persimmons, grapes, and onions add to dietary intake
- **Pair fisetin (food or supplement) with healthy fats** — olive oil, avocado, nuts, eggs — to improve its poor absorption
- **Build an anti-inflammatory, low-processed diet** — fisetin works best against a low background of inflammation and oxidative stress; a poor diet generates the senescence and inflammaging it aims to reduce

### Exercise (Reduces Senescence Burden)
- **Regular exercise itself reduces senescent-cell accumulation and inflammaging** — combining training with fisetin's senolytic rationale is complementary
- **Include both cardio and resistance training** — for metabolic, vascular, and muscle health that supports healthy aging

### Sleep & Stress
- **Prioritize 7-9 hours of sleep** — sleep drives cellular repair and inflammation control; fisetin complements but cannot replace it
- **Manage chronic stress** — chronic stress and elevated cortisol promote inflammation and accelerate cellular aging

### Senolytic Protocol Practicalities
- **If using "hit-and-run" dosing, time it with fatty meals** to maximize absorption during the short dosing window
- **Keep a simple log** of dosing windows (which days, what dose) given the intermittent schedule
- **Stay conservative and informed** — track how you feel and adjust; consult a clinician if on medications or with chronic conditions

### Sun and Skin
- **Reasonable sun protection** — UV exposure drives oxidative damage and skin-cell senescence; fisetin's antioxidant activity is supportive, not a substitute for sun-smart habits

### Stacking Within a Longevity Routine
- **Combine thoughtfully with quercetin, resveratrol, and NAD+ precursors (NMN)** — these share antioxidant, SIRT1/AMPK, and longevity pathways
- **Quercetin + fisetin** is a common senescence-focused flavonol pairing (human evidence preliminary)

### Long-Term Mindset
- **Fisetin is a promising longevity tool, not a proven therapy** — pair it with the lifestyle fundamentals (diet, exercise, sleep) that have far stronger evidence for healthy aging
