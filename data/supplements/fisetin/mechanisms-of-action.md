# Fisetin
## Mechanisms of Action

### 1. Senolytic Activity (Defining Mechanism)
- **Selective clearance of senescent cells** — fisetin tips senescent cells toward apoptosis (programmed cell death) while largely sparing healthy cells
- Senescent cells survive by upregulating pro-survival ("senescent cell anti-apoptotic pathway," SCAP) networks; fisetin interferes with these, including PI3K/AKT and certain BCL-2 family anti-apoptotic signals
- By reducing senescent-cell burden, fisetin lowers the senescence-associated secretory phenotype (SASP) — the inflammatory cytokines and proteases these cells secrete that drive tissue aging
- This is the mechanism behind the 2018 mouse data showing fisetin as the most potent senolytic flavonoid tested

### 2. Anti-Inflammatory Signaling (NF-kB Inhibition)
- Inhibits NF-kB, reducing transcription of pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-alpha, IL-6, IL-1beta)
- Lowers the chronic low-grade inflammation ("inflammaging") associated with aging
- Inhibits inflammatory enzymes (COX-2, lipoxygenase)

### 3. Direct Antioxidant & Glutathione Support
- Scavenges reactive oxygen species directly via its hydroxyl groups
- Notably, fisetin helps maintain intracellular glutathione (the master antioxidant) levels under oxidative stress, a property emphasized in neuroprotection research
- Activates the Nrf2 pathway, upregulating the body's endogenous antioxidant defense genes

### 4. Neurotrophic / Neuroprotective Pathways
- Promotes pathways involved in neuronal differentiation and memory (ERK/CREB signaling) in preclinical models
- Supports neuronal survival under oxidative and inflammatory stress
- Drives interest in fisetin for age-related cognitive decline (preclinical stage)

### 5. SIRT1 / AMPK and Longevity Overlap
- Influences SIRT1 and AMPK signaling, overlapping with pathways targeted by resveratrol and fueled by NAD+ precursors like NMN
- These pathways regulate metabolic stress resistance and autophagy

### 6. mTOR Modulation / Autophagy
- May modestly inhibit mTOR signaling and support autophagy, complementing the cellular "cleanup" theme shared with compounds like spermidine

### Important Evidence Caveat
- Most of these mechanisms are best established in cell and animal models. Human senolytic confirmation is the goal of ongoing clinical trials, and current human data remains preliminary.
