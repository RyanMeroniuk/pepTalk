# Fisetin
## Forms and Bioavailability

### The Core Problem: Poor Bioavailability
- **Fisetin is poorly water-soluble and has low, short-lived oral bioavailability** — it is rapidly metabolized and cleared, so a large oral dose yields only modest, transient blood levels
- This is a central reason high "hit-and-run" doses are used and why enhanced delivery forms were developed

### Standard Fisetin (Powder/Capsule)
- **The most common and cheapest form** — typically derived from plant sources (e.g., the wax tree, Rhus/Toxicodendron, or smoke tree)
- **Look for high-purity (98%+) standardized extract** — quality varies
- **Low bioavailability** — much of an oral dose is poorly absorbed; taking with fat helps
- **Used in most self-administered "hit-and-run" senolytic protocols** because it is inexpensive

### Liposomal Fisetin
- **Fisetin encapsulated in phospholipid vesicles** — protects it through the GI tract and improves cellular uptake
- **Reported higher bioavailability** than standard powder, allowing lower effective doses
- **Verdict:** A reasonable choice for those prioritizing absorption, though standardized clinical data is more limited than for some other phytosome ingredients

### Fisetin + Phospholipid / Phytosome
- **Phospholipid-complexed forms** aim to improve solubility and absorption, similar in concept to quercetin's Quercefit phytosome
- **Verdict:** Promising for absorption; check for third-party testing

### Fisetin with Absorption Enhancers
- **Co-formulated with fats/oils, piperine (black pepper extract), or other bioavailability enhancers** in some products
- **Taking with a high-fat meal or with oils** (e.g., olive oil) is the simplest practical enhancer

### Sourcing & Quality Notes
- **Third-party testing matters** — confirm purity and identity, as the supplement market for fisetin has variable quality
- **Stability** — store cool, dry, and dark; flavonols can degrade with heat, light, and moisture

### Practical Summary
| Form | Bioavailability | Best For |
|------|:---:|:---:|
| **Liposomal** | Higher | Better absorption, lower dose |
| Phospholipid/phytosome | Higher | Improved solubility |
| Standard powder + fat | Low-moderate | Budget, high-dose protocols |
| Standard powder alone | Low | Least efficient |
