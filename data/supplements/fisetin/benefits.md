# Fisetin
## Benefits

### Senolytic / Longevity (Headline Use — Preliminary in Humans)
- **Senescent-cell clearance** — in aged mice, fisetin reduced senescent-cell markers across multiple tissues, lowered SASP inflammation, improved tissue function, and extended both healthspan and lifespan (Yousefzadeh et al., 2018)
- **Most potent natural flavonoid senolytic tested** in that 2018 screen
- **Human trials are ongoing** — Mayo Clinic and others are studying fisetin for senescence-related outcomes (e.g., in frailty, osteoarthritis, and inflammatory conditions); robust human efficacy is not yet established
- **Generally well tolerated** as a senolytic compared to drug-based senolytics (e.g., dasatinib), making it attractive for research and cautious self-experimentation

### Anti-Inflammatory
- **Reduces inflammaging** — lowers chronic low-grade inflammatory signaling (NF-kB, TNF-alpha, IL-6) in preclinical models
- **Supports a lower inflammatory burden** that contributes to many age-related conditions

### Antioxidant & Cellular Protection
- **Maintains glutathione** — preserves the master intracellular antioxidant under oxidative stress
- **Activates Nrf2** — upregulates the body's own antioxidant defense systems
- **Direct ROS scavenging** — neutralizes free radicals

### Neuroprotection & Cognition (Preclinical)
- **Supports memory pathways** — promotes ERK/CREB neurotrophic signaling in animal models
- **Protects neurons** under oxidative and inflammatory stress
- **Interest in age-related cognitive decline** — promising preclinical data; human cognitive evidence is not yet established

### Metabolic & Vascular (Early)
- **May support metabolic and vascular health** through anti-inflammatory and SIRT1/AMPK pathways; evidence is early and largely preclinical

### Important Caveat
- **Fisetin's most exciting benefits are senolytic and longevity-related, and these are predominantly demonstrated in animals.** Human confirmation is the subject of active, unfinished research. Claims should be framed as promising but preliminary — not established.
