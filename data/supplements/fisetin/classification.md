# Fisetin
## Classification

- **Category:** Flavonoid polyphenol / antioxidant / leading candidate senolytic / longevity compound
- **Chemical class:** Flavonol (a flavonoid subclass, structurally related to quercetin)
- **Molecular formula:** C15H10O6
- **Also known as:** 7,3',4'-flavon-3-ol; 3,7,3',4'-tetrahydroxyflavone
- **Supplement type:** Senolytic / anti-aging / antioxidant / neuroprotective flavonoid
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (OTC); not an approved drug; the subject of ongoing clinical research into senolytic effects
- **Primary systems:** Cellular senescence clearance, inflammatory signaling, neurological, and vascular
- **Endogenous status:** Not produced by the human body; obtained from diet (strawberries are the richest common source) or supplements
- **Distinguishing feature:** In a 2018 mouse screen of flavonoids, fisetin was identified as the most potent senolytic of those tested — the basis for intense interest in periodic high-dose "hit-and-run" protocols
- **Bioavailability note:** Poorly water-soluble with low and short-lived oral bioavailability; enhanced (liposomal) forms exist to address this
