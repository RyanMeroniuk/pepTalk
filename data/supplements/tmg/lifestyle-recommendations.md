# TMG (Trimethylglycine)
## Lifestyle Recommendations

### Methylation & Homocysteine Support (Primary Use)
- **Pair TMG with B-vitamins** — folate, B12, and B6 cover the other homocysteine pathways; TMG handles betaine remethylation. Together they manage homocysteine comprehensively.
- **Monitor homocysteine and lipids** — track homocysteine to confirm benefit, and lipids (LDL/triglycerides) if using higher doses, since high-dose TMG can raise them
- **Whole-food methylation support** — a diet rich in leafy greens, whole grains, beets, and eggs supports the methyl-donor network alongside TMG

### NAD+ / Longevity Protocols (Key Pairing)
- **Co-supplement TMG with NMN or NR** — NAD+ precursor metabolism methylates nicotinamide for excretion, consuming methyl groups; TMG replenishes them and helps keep homocysteine in check
- **Typical methyl-replenishment dose:** 500 mg-1 g TMG alongside the NAD+ precursor
- **This is one of the most mechanistically grounded supplement pairings** in longevity stacks

### Training (Ergogenic Use)
- **Use 2.5 g/day for power/strength goals** — the studied ergogenic dose; benefits build over 1-2 weeks
- **Combine with creatine** — creatine for ATP/power, TMG for cell hydration, methylation support, and modest power benefit; TMG also supports endogenous creatine synthesis via SAMe (see peptide-synergies)
- **Progressive resistance training** is needed to realize the ergogenic and body-composition benefits

### Liver Health
- **Support hepatic fat metabolism** — TMG aids fat export from the liver; pair with adequate choline, a whole-food diet, and limited alcohol
- **Limit alcohol** — heavy drinking depletes methyl donors and stresses the liver, opposing TMG's benefits

### Nutrition
- **Adequate protein** supports the methylation cycle (methionine context)
- **Choline-rich foods (eggs, liver)** complement TMG in the methyl-donor network
- **Take with meals** for better tolerance

### Methylation Balance
- **Start low if stacking multiple methyl donors** — balance TMG with methylfolate, SAMe, and choline to avoid over-methylation symptoms in sensitive people
- **Individualize** — methylation needs vary by genetics (e.g., MTHFR context), diet, and other supplements

### Long-Term Mindset
- **TMG is best as part of a methylation-aware protocol** — most valuable alongside NAD+ precursors or for homocysteine support, with B-vitamins as partners
- **Inexpensive and well-tolerated** at sensible doses; monitor lipids if going high
