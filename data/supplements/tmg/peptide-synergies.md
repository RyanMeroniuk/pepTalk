# TMG (Trimethylglycine)
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### Epitalon + TMG (Longevity & Methylation Support)
- **Rationale:** Epitalon is a pineal/telomerase-associated peptide used in longevity protocols, often alongside NAD+ precursors. TMG replenishes the methyl groups consumed during NAD+ metabolism and supports healthy homocysteine and methylation — keeping the methylation cycle robust during a longevity stack. TMG is the methylation backbone of NAD+-focused anti-aging protocols.
- **Protocol:** TMG 500 mg-1 g/day (matched to NAD+ precursor intake) + Epitalon in cyclical longevity protocols
- **Best for:** Longevity protocols combining NAD+ precursors, epitalon, and methylation support

#### MOTS-c + TMG (Mitochondrial & Methylation Metabolism)
- **Rationale:** MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-derived peptide acting as an exercise mimetic and metabolic regulator, frequently used in NAD+/longevity contexts. TMG supports the methylation demands of NAD+ metabolism and homocysteine management, complementing MOTS-c's metabolic effects.
- **Protocol:** TMG 1-2 g/day + MOTS-c at standard dosing
- **Best for:** Metabolic and longevity optimization protocols

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + TMG (Power, Recovery & Methylation)
- **Rationale:** GH secretagogues (CJC-1295, Ipamorelin) drive recovery and muscle. TMG adds a modest ergogenic (power/strength) effect, supports cell hydration, and underpins endogenous creatine synthesis (via SAMe). It also manages the methylation load of an active, supplement-heavy protocol.
- **Protocol:** TMG 2.5 g/day + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Best for:** Strength/power phases with methylation support

#### IGF-1 LR3 + TMG (Anabolism + Methyl Support)
- **Rationale:** IGF-1 LR3 strongly drives muscle protein synthesis; TMG supports the methylation cycle (creatine synthesis, protein methylation) and cell hydration that underpin an anabolic environment.
- **Protocol:** TMG 2.5 g/day + IGF-1 LR3 per protocol
- **Best for:** Muscle-building phases with methylation/homocysteine awareness

#### Tesamorelin + TMG (Body Composition + Liver/Lipid Awareness)
- **Rationale:** Tesamorelin reduces visceral fat and supports body composition; TMG supports liver fat metabolism via methylation. Note the lipid caveat — monitor LDL/triglycerides at higher TMG doses, since both liver health and lipids are in play.
- **Protocol:** TMG 1-2 g/day + Tesamorelin at prescribed dosing; monitor lipids
- **Best for:** Body recomposition with liver and methylation support

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### MK-677 (Ibutamoren) + TMG (Volume + Methylation)
- **Rationale:** MK-677 raises GH/IGF-1 and supports recovery; TMG provides modest ergogenic support and manages methylation load during a high-output protocol.
- **Protocol:** TMG 2.5 g/day + MK-677 10-25 mg before bed
- **Best for:** Bulking/recovery phases

### Supplement Synergies

#### TMG + NMN (The Core Methylation Pairing)
- **Rationale:** NMN (a NAD+ precursor) is ultimately broken down via nicotinamide, which is methylated by NNMT for excretion — consuming SAMe-derived methyl groups and risking homocysteine elevation. TMG remethylates homocysteine to methionine → SAMe, directly replenishing the methyl groups NMN metabolism drains. This is the strongest, most mechanistically grounded reason to pair them.
- **Protocol:** NMN at chosen dose + TMG 500 mg-1 g/day to replenish methyl groups; pair with folate/B12 for full homocysteine support
- **Best for:** NAD+/longevity protocols — TMG is considered near-essential methylation insurance for regular NMN users

#### TMG + Creatine (Methylation-Sparing Power Stack)
- **Rationale:** Endogenous creatine synthesis is one of the body's largest consumers of SAMe-derived methyl groups. Supplementing creatine spares methyl groups, while TMG replenishes the methyl pool and adds its own modest power/strength benefit and cell hydration. Together they support power output and methylation balance.
- **Protocol:** Creatine 5 g/day + TMG 2.5 g/day
- **Best for:** Strength/power athletes who also care about methylation and homocysteine
