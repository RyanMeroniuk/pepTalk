# TMG (Trimethylglycine)
## Forms and Bioavailability

### Betaine Anhydrous (Standard Supplement Form)
- **"TMG," "trimethylglycine," and "betaine anhydrous" are the same compound** — anhydrous simply means without water of crystallization
- **The form used in nearly all research** — both the ergogenic (~2.5 g) and homocysteine studies
- **Highly bioavailable** — rapidly and almost completely absorbed orally; peak plasma levels within ~1 hour
- **Cheap and widely available** as powder or capsules
- **Note on "betaine":** in supplements, "betaine" usually means betaine anhydrous (TMG). Do NOT confuse it with **betaine HCl**, which is a different product used as a stomach-acid supplement — it provides hydrochloric acid for digestion, not primarily methyl-donor benefits.

### Betaine HCl (Different Product — Don't Confuse)
- **Betaine hydrochloride** is sold to increase stomach acidity for digestion
- It does contain the betaine (TMG) molecule, but the dosing and purpose differ; for methylation/ergogenic benefits, use **betaine anhydrous / TMG**, not betaine HCl

### Powder vs Capsules
- **Powder** — most economical for the gram-scale doses needed (1.5-6 g); TMG has a slightly sweet taste and dissolves well in water
- **Capsules** — convenient but require several pills to reach effective doses; more expensive per gram
- **Both are equally effective** when matched for dose

### Bioavailability Notes
- **Excellent oral absorption** — TMG is well absorbed and distributed; the liver and kidney are major sites of use
- **No significant absorption competition** at typical doses
- **Hydrophilic and stable** — dissolves readily, stable in water and at normal temperatures

### Prescription Form (Distinct)
- **Cystadane (betaine anhydrous)** is an FDA-approved prescription for homocystinuria, using much higher doses under medical supervision — separate from the OTC supplement context

### Practical Guidance
- **Use betaine anhydrous / TMG** (not betaine HCl) for methylation, homocysteine, and ergogenic goals
- **Powder is the practical, economical choice** for the gram-scale doses
- **No exotic or "enhanced" form is needed** — plain betaine anhydrous is what the research used
