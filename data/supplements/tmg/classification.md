# TMG (Trimethylglycine)
## Classification

- **Category:** Methyl donor / osmolyte; conditionally beneficial nutrient (also known as betaine)
- **Chemical class:** Trimethylated amino acid derivative of glycine (a quaternary ammonium compound); the "betaine" referred to in supplements is betaine anhydrous
- **Molecular formula:** C5H11NO2
- **Supplement type:** Methylation support / cardiovascular (homocysteine) / ergogenic (power & strength) supplement; also a cellular osmolyte
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (OTC); GRAS status. Note: a prescription form, betaine anhydrous (Cystadane), is FDA-approved for homocystinuria — distinct from the OTC supplement.
- **Primary systems:** Methylation cycle (homocysteine remethylation), cardiovascular, skeletal muscle (power/strength, cell hydration), liver
- **Common names:** TMG, trimethylglycine, betaine anhydrous, glycine betaine
- **Endogenous source:** Derived from choline oxidation in the liver and kidney, and obtained from diet (beets, grains, spinach)
- **Biological role:** Donates a methyl group to convert homocysteine back to methionine (via betaine-homocysteine methyltransferase, BHMT); acts as an organic osmolyte protecting cells from osmotic and thermal stress
