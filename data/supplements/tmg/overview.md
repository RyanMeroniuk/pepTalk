# TMG (Trimethylglycine)
## Overview

TMG (trimethylglycine), also called betaine anhydrous, is a methyl-donor compound and cellular osmolyte with two main evidence-based uses. First, it is one of the body's primary methyl donors: it converts homocysteine back into methionine, lowering homocysteine levels (a cardiovascular and methylation-cycle marker) and regenerating the methyl groups needed for SAMe production. Second, it is a well-supported ergogenic aid for power and strength, with multiple trials showing improvements in body composition, power output, and strength when dosed at ~2.5 g/day. TMG has gained particular relevance in longevity and NAD+ circles: NAD+ precursors like NMN and NR are metabolized and ultimately methylated for excretion (the methylated metabolite MeNAM), which consumes methyl groups. TMG is frequently co-supplemented to replenish those methyl groups and keep homocysteine in check during NAD+-boosting protocols — a strong, mechanistically grounded methylation pairing.

### Why It Matters

- **Methyl donor:** Converts homocysteine to methionine via BHMT, lowering homocysteine and supporting the methylation cycle (SAMe regeneration)
- **Power & strength:** Consistent ergogenic benefits at ~2.5 g/day — improved power output, strength, and body composition in resistance-trained individuals
- **Cellular osmolyte:** Protects cells against osmotic and thermal stress and supports cell hydration, contributing to its performance effects
- **NAD+ methylation support:** Replenishes methyl groups consumed when NAD+ precursors (NMN, NR) are methylated for clearance — the rationale for pairing TMG with NMN
- **Liver support:** Supports healthy liver fat metabolism (relevant to fatty liver in some studies)

### Who Benefits Most

- **People supplementing NAD+ precursors (NMN, NR)** — to replenish methyl groups and prevent homocysteine elevation
- **People with elevated homocysteine** — TMG is an effective remethylation route, especially alongside folate and B12
- **Strength and power athletes** — power output, strength, and body composition benefits
- **Those on methylation-heavy protocols** — high methylation demand from various supplements or genetics (e.g., certain MTHFR contexts)
- **People supporting liver health** — TMG aids hepatic fat metabolism
