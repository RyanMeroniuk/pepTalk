# TMG (Trimethylglycine)
## Dosing Protocols

### Ergogenic / Power & Strength (Most Studied)
- **Dose:** 2.5 g/day
- **Timing:** Often split (1.25 g twice daily) or taken once daily; some studies dose pre-workout
- **Duration:** Benefits build over 1-2 weeks of consistent use; used continuously during training blocks
- **Notes:** 2.5 g/day is the dose used in most positive resistance-training studies

### Homocysteine Lowering
- **Dose:** 3-6 g/day (higher doses produce larger homocysteine reductions)
- **Timing:** Split into 2 doses with meals
- **Duration:** Ongoing; homocysteine responds within weeks
- **Notes:** Pair with adequate folate, B12, and B6 for comprehensive homocysteine management. Higher doses (>3 g/day) can modestly raise LDL cholesterol in some people — monitor lipids (see things-to-avoid).

### NAD+ Protocol Methylation Support (Common Pairing)
- **Dose:** 500 mg - 1 g per day, often matched to NAD+ precursor (NMN/NR) intake
- **Timing:** Taken alongside NMN/NR
- **Rationale:** Replenishes methyl groups consumed when NAD+ precursors are methylated for excretion, helping keep homocysteine in check
- **Notes:** Lower doses (500 mg-1 g) are typical for this "methyl insurance" role; higher methylation demand may warrant up to 2 g

### General Methylation Support
- **Dose:** 1-2 g/day
- **Timing:** With meals; can split
- **Notes:** A general dose for those supporting methylation (e.g., high methylation demand, certain MTHFR contexts), ideally alongside B-vitamins

### Liver Support
- **Dose:** 1-3 g/day
- **Notes:** Supports hepatic fat metabolism; studied in fatty liver contexts

### Timing Considerations
- **With meals** improves tolerance and aligns with metabolic use
- **Pre-workout dosing** is reasonable for the ergogenic effect, though consistency matters more than precise timing for the methylation benefits
- **Split larger doses (3+ g)** to improve GI tolerance

### Cofactor Pairing (Important)
- **Pair with B-vitamins (folate, B12, B6)** for homocysteine management — TMG handles one remethylation pathway; B-vitamins handle the other and the transsulfuration route
- **Pair with NAD+ precursors** when used for methyl-group replenishment

### Safety Ceiling
- **Doses up to 6 g/day** are commonly used and well-tolerated; very high doses can cause GI upset and raise LDL/triglycerides in some individuals
- **No loading phase needed**
