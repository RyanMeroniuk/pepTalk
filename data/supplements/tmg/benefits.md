# TMG (Trimethylglycine)
## Benefits

### Homocysteine & Methylation (Strong Evidence)
- **Lowers homocysteine** — TMG is an effective folate-independent route to remethylate homocysteine to methionine; doses of 3-6 g/day produce meaningful reductions, even ~1.5-3 g/day helps
- **Supports the methylation cycle** — regenerates methionine and keeps the SAMe pool full for downstream methylation (DNA, neurotransmitters, creatine, phospholipids)
- **Cardiovascular relevance** — elevated homocysteine is associated with cardiovascular risk; TMG addresses it via the betaine pathway
- **Especially useful when the folate/B12 pathway is limited** — provides an alternative remethylation route

### Power & Strength (Good Ergogenic Evidence)
- **Improves power output** — multiple trials show increased power in resistance-trained men at ~2.5 g/day (e.g., Hoffman et al., 2009; Lee et al., 2010)
- **Increases strength** — improvements in bench press and squat performance metrics in several studies
- **Improves body composition** — some trials report increased lean mass and reduced fat mass with training at 2.5 g/day (Cholewa et al., 2013)
- **Note:** results are somewhat inconsistent across studies, but the weight of evidence supports a modest ergogenic benefit; effect size is smaller and less universal than creatine

### NAD+ Protocol Support (Mechanistically Strong)
- **Replenishes methyl groups consumed by NAD+ metabolism** — when NMN/NR are broken down, nicotinamide is methylated for excretion, draining methyl groups; TMG offsets this
- **Helps prevent homocysteine elevation** during high-dose NAD+ precursor use
- **A standard pairing in longevity stacks** for this reason (see peptide-synergies)

### Liver Health
- **Supports healthy liver fat metabolism** — aids phosphatidylcholine synthesis and fat export from the liver; studied in non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD) with mixed but promising results
- **Hepatoprotective** in the context of methylation support

### Cellular Protection
- **Osmoprotection** — stabilizes cells and proteins against osmotic and thermal stress as a compatible osmolyte
- **Cell hydration** — supports muscle cell volume, part of its ergogenic profile

### Realistic Expectations
- **TMG's two best-supported roles are methylation/homocysteine support and as a power/strength ergogenic** — these are the reasons to take it
- **It is not a stimulant or a primary muscle-builder** like creatine; think of it as a methylation insurance + modest ergogenic adjunct
- **Most valuable when paired with NAD+ precursors** or when homocysteine/methylation support is the goal
