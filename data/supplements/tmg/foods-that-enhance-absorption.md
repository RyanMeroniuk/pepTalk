# TMG (Trimethylglycine)
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

### The Reality: TMG Is Already Well Absorbed
- **TMG (betaine anhydrous) has excellent oral bioavailability** — rapidly and nearly completely absorbed with peak plasma levels within ~1 hour. There is no need to pair it with anything to absorb it.
- **The meaningful "enhancement" is supplying the cofactors and partners that let TMG do its job** — methylation and homocysteine management

### B-Vitamin Cofactors (Support the Methylation Cycle)
- **Folate (B9), B12, and B6 are essential partners** for homocysteine management — TMG handles the betaine remethylation pathway, while folate/B12 handle the other remethylation route and B6 supports transsulfuration
- **Without adequate B-vitamins, homocysteine management is incomplete** — pair TMG with a B-complex or folate/B12-rich foods
- **B-vitamin food sources:** leafy greens (folate), eggs and animal products (B12), poultry/fish/bananas (B6)

### Choline (Related Methyl-Donor Network)
- **Choline and betaine are metabolically linked** — choline is oxidized to betaine in the body; adequate choline supports the overall methyl-donor pool
- **Choline-rich foods:** eggs, liver, soy

### Methionine / Protein Context
- **TMG remethylates homocysteine to methionine** — adequate dietary protein supplies the broader amino acid context for the methylation cycle
- Taking TMG with meals improves tolerance and aligns with metabolic use

### NAD+ Precursors (Partner, Not Absorption Enhancer)
- **When the goal is methyl-group replenishment**, TMG is paired with NMN or NR — not to enhance absorption, but because NAD+ metabolism consumes the methyl groups TMG replenishes (see peptide-synergies)

### Practical Pairings
- **With meals** — improves GI tolerance and aligns with metabolic demand
- **With a B-complex (folate, B12, B6)** — for comprehensive homocysteine and methylation support
- **Alongside NMN/NR** — for methyl-group replenishment in longevity protocols

### What Doesn't Matter
- **Hot vs cold liquid** — TMG is stable and water-soluble
- **Fed vs fasted for absorption** — TMG absorbs well either way; fed is gentler on the stomach

### Bottom Line
- **TMG absorbs effortlessly on its own.** The real optimization is pairing it with B-vitamins (folate, B12, B6) for methylation/homocysteine work, and with NMN/NR when the goal is methyl-group replenishment.
