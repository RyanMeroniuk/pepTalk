# Spermidine
## Classification

- **Category:** Polyamine / autophagy inducer / longevity compound / cellular renewal support
- **Chemical class:** Aliphatic polyamine (a small, positively charged organic molecule)
- **Molecular formula:** C7H19N3
- **Also known as:** N-(3-aminopropyl)-1,4-diaminobutane
- **Supplement type:** Longevity / cardiovascular / cognitive / autophagy-support compound
- **Common source:** Wheat-germ extract is the most common commercial supplement source; also produced via fermentation
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (OTC); wheat-germ-derived spermidine is sold as a food/supplement; not an approved drug
- **Primary systems:** Cellular (autophagy/proteostasis), cardiovascular, neurological, and immune
- **Endogenous status:** Produced by the body (from the amino acid ornithine via the polyamine pathway), obtained from diet, and synthesized by gut bacteria; tissue levels tend to decline with age
- **Mechanistic theme:** Induces autophagy — the cell's recycling/cleanup process — overlapping with caloric-restriction-mimetic pathways (AMPK/SIRT1), placing it in the same conceptual family as compounds like resveratrol and NAD+ precursors (NMN)
