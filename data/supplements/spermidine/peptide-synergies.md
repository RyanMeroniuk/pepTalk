# Spermidine
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### Epitalon + Spermidine (Premier Longevity Pairing)
- **Rationale:** Epitalon supports telomere maintenance and circadian/pineal function, while spermidine induces autophagy and improves proteostasis and mitochondrial quality. Together they address distinct hallmarks of aging — telomere attrition (Epitalon) and loss of proteostasis/autophagy (spermidine).
- **Protocol:** Spermidine 1-6 mg/day with food + Epitalon per standard longevity cycling
- **Best for:** Longevity-focused protocols emphasizing cellular renewal

#### MOTS-c + Spermidine (Mitochondrial + Autophagy)
- **Rationale:** MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-derived peptide that activates AMPK and acts as an exercise mimetic. Spermidine also engages AMPK and drives mitophagy (clearance of damaged mitochondria). Together they optimize mitochondrial quantity and quality — MOTS-c stimulates mitochondrial biogenesis/function, spermidine clears the damaged ones.
- **Protocol:** Spermidine 1-6 mg/day + MOTS-c at standard dosing, ideally with time-restricted eating
- **Best for:** Mitochondrial and metabolic longevity optimization

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### BPC-157 + Spermidine (Repair + Cellular Renewal)
- **Rationale:** BPC-157 accelerates tissue repair; autophagy (driven by spermidine) is essential for clearing damaged components during regeneration. Spermidine supports the cellular cleanup that healthy repair depends on.
- **Protocol:** Spermidine 1-6 mg/day + BPC-157 250-500 mcg 2x/day
- **Best for:** Recovery and tissue repair, especially with age

#### TB-500 + Spermidine (Systemic Repair + Autophagy)
- **Rationale:** TB-500 promotes systemic tissue repair; spermidine supports the autophagic clearance and proteostasis that underpin healthy remodeling.
- **Protocol:** Spermidine 1-6 mg/day + TB-500 2.5-5 mg 2x/week
- **Best for:** Recovery phases, return to training

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + Spermidine (Anabolism + Cleanup Balance)
- **Rationale:** GH secretagogues like CJC-1295/Ipamorelin drive anabolic growth and recovery; spermidine balances this with autophagy, helping clear damaged proteins generated during high turnover. Growth plus quality control.
- **Protocol:** Spermidine 1-6 mg/day + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Best for:** Recovery and healthy-aging stacks

#### GHK-Cu + Spermidine (Skin & Anti-Aging)
- **Rationale:** GHK-Cu promotes collagen synthesis and skin remodeling; spermidine supports autophagy-mediated skin-cell renewal and has preliminary interest for hair growth. A complementary anti-aging pairing.
- **Protocol:** Spermidine 1-6 mg/day + GHK-Cu per standard protocol
- **Best for:** Anti-aging skin and hair protocols

### Supplement Stack Partners (Non-Peptide)
- **Spermidine + NMN** — NMN restores NAD+ to fuel sirtuins and DNA repair while spermidine drives autophagy; both are core caloric-restriction-mimetic longevity tools with complementary mechanisms
- **Spermidine + Resveratrol** — overlapping SIRT1/AMPK pathways; resveratrol activates sirtuins, spermidine induces autophagy — a classic longevity combination
- **Spermidine + Quercetin/Fisetin** — pairs autophagy induction (spermidine) with senolytic and antioxidant support (the flavonols), covering complementary aging mechanisms
