# Spermidine
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

Spermidine does not face the severe solubility problems of polyphenols like quercetin or fisetin. The emphasis here is less on a single absorption hack and more on supporting overall polyamine status, gut health, and the autophagy pathways spermidine works through.

### Polyamine-Rich Foods (Additive Intake)
- **Eating spermidine-rich foods alongside supplementation raises total polyamine load** — wheat germ, aged cheese, natto/soy, mushrooms, and legumes all contribute
- This stacking of dietary plus supplemental spermidine reflects how the protective observational data was generated (high dietary intake)

### Gut-Health Foods (Endogenous Polyamine Production)
- **Gut bacteria produce polyamines**, an important source of body spermidine
- **Fermented foods** (natto, yogurt, kefir, kimchi, sauerkraut) and **fiber/prebiotics** support a microbiome that generates polyamines and aids gut absorption
- A healthy gut lining optimizes uptake of dietary and supplemental spermidine

### Fasting & Caloric-Restriction Support (Pathway Synergy)
- **Time-restricted eating and fasting independently induce autophagy** via AMPK/mTOR — the same pathway spermidine engages
- Aligning spermidine intake with a fasting window can reinforce its autophagy effect (a pathway synergy rather than an absorption effect)

### Polyphenol Companions
- **Resveratrol** and **NAD+ precursors (NMN)** engage overlapping caloric-restriction-mimetic pathways (SIRT1/AMPK); a polyphenol- and polyamine-rich diet supports the broader longevity network
- **Green tea (EGCG)** also supports autophagy-related signaling

### Healthy Fats & Balanced Meals
- **A balanced meal with some fat and protein** supports general nutrient absorption and tolerability; spermidine is commonly taken with food

### What Doesn't Help
- **Expecting a single "absorption enhancer" to transform spermidine** — its limitation is total intake and consistency, not poor solubility. Focus on steady dietary plus supplemental polyamine load and gut health.
- **Poor gut health** — disrupted microbiome reduces both endogenous polyamine production and absorption
