# Spermidine
## Benefits

### Autophagy & Cellular Renewal (Core Benefit)
- **Induces autophagy** — spermidine is one of the few well-characterized nutritional triggers of autophagy, the cellular cleanup/recycling process that declines with age
- **Improved proteostasis** — better clearance of damaged proteins and aggregates, relevant to many age-related diseases
- **Mitophagy** — recycling of damaged mitochondria supports cellular energy quality

### Cardiovascular Health
- **Cardioprotection in animal models** — improves diastolic cardiac function, reduces hypertrophy and fibrosis, lowers blood pressure, and improves arterial elasticity
- **Lifespan extension in mice** — dietary spermidine extended lifespan with cardiovascular benefits as a major contributor (Eisenberg et al., 2016)
- **Human observational data** — higher dietary spermidine intake is associated with lower cardiovascular and all-cause mortality in population studies (e.g., the Bruneck cohort)

### Cognitive Support (Preliminary)
- **Memory support in older adults** — preliminary human pilot work (e.g., the SmartAge trial) explored spermidine for memory in older adults at risk of cognitive decline; results have been mixed and modest, and larger trials are needed
- **Neuroprotection in animal models** — supports memory and reduces neurodegenerative markers, attributed to enhanced neuronal autophagy

### Longevity / Healthspan
- **Lifespan extension across species** — yeast, flies, worms, and mice show extended lifespan with spermidine, consistently linked to autophagy
- **Caloric-restriction-mimetic effects** — engages fasting-like pathways (AMPK) without food restriction

### Immune Function
- **Supports immune aging** — improves aspects of T-cell function and immune regulation in aged models, partly via eIF5A hypusination and autophagy in immune cells

### Hair & Skin (Limited)
- **Hair growth** — small studies suggest spermidine may prolong the hair growth (anagen) phase; evidence is limited
- **Skin** — interest in autophagy-mediated skin renewal; preliminary

### Important Caveat
- **Spermidine's strongest evidence is mechanistic (autophagy) and observational (dietary intake linked to better outcomes).** Randomized human trials are limited and preliminary, so cardiovascular and cognitive benefits in humans should be framed as promising but not yet definitively proven.
