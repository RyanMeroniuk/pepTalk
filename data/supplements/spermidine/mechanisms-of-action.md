# Spermidine
## Mechanisms of Action

### 1. Autophagy Induction (Defining Mechanism)
- **Spermidine is a potent natural inducer of autophagy** — the process by which cells degrade and recycle damaged proteins, aggregates, and organelles
- It promotes autophagy partly by **inhibiting acetyltransferases (notably EP300)**, leading to hypoacetylation of key autophagy-related proteins, which switches the autophagy machinery on
- Enhanced autophagy improves proteostasis (protein quality control), clears dysfunctional mitochondria (mitophagy), and is strongly linked to longevity across species
- This autophagy induction is considered the central explanation for spermidine's lifespan and healthspan effects in model organisms

### 2. AMPK Activation / Caloric-Restriction Mimetic
- Engages AMPK signaling, the cellular energy sensor activated by fasting and caloric restriction
- This places spermidine among "caloric-restriction mimetics" — compounds that reproduce some fasting benefits without food restriction, conceptually alongside resveratrol and NAD+-boosting NMN

### 3. mTOR Modulation
- Autophagy is normally suppressed by mTOR; spermidine's pro-autophagy effect aligns with reduced mTOR-driven suppression of cellular cleanup
- This complements the fasting/caloric-restriction pathway theme

### 4. Mitochondrial Function & Mitophagy
- By promoting mitophagy (autophagy of damaged mitochondria), spermidine helps maintain a healthier mitochondrial pool
- Improved mitochondrial quality supports energy metabolism and reduces oxidative stress with age

### 5. Anti-Inflammatory Effects
- Reduces chronic low-grade inflammation ("inflammaging") in part through enhanced clearance of damaged cellular components and modulation of immune cell function
- Supports regulatory immune function and may improve aspects of immune aging

### 6. Cardiovascular Protection
- In animal models, spermidine improves cardiac diastolic function, reduces cardiac hypertrophy and fibrosis, lowers blood pressure, and enhances arterial elasticity — effects largely attributed to cardiomyocyte autophagy and improved mitochondrial function

### 7. Essential Polyamine Roles
- As a fundamental polyamine, spermidine stabilizes nucleic acids and membranes, supports cell growth and proliferation, and is a precursor to hypusination of the translation factor eIF5A — a process required for efficient protein synthesis and immune cell function

### Evidence Note
- Autophagy induction and the downstream cardiovascular/cognitive effects are well demonstrated in cell and animal models and supported by human observational data. Randomized human trials are still limited and preliminary.
