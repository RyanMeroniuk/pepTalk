# Spermidine
## Lifestyle Recommendations

### Fasting & Time-Restricted Eating (Primary Synergy)
- **Pair spermidine with fasting or time-restricted eating** — fasting independently induces autophagy via AMPK/mTOR, the same pathway spermidine engages. A 16:8 (or even 12:12) eating window reinforces spermidine's central benefit.
- **Avoid constant grazing** — frequent eating keeps mTOR active and autophagy suppressed, working against spermidine

### Diet (Food-First Foundation)
- **Build a polyamine-rich diet** — wheat germ, aged cheese, natto/soy, mushrooms, and legumes provide substantial dietary spermidine on top of supplementation
- **Minimize ultra-processed foods, refined sugar, and alcohol** — these promote the inflammation and metabolic stress spermidine works against
- **Support gut health** — fermented foods and fiber feed the microbiome that produces polyamines and aids absorption

### Exercise (Autophagy Amplifier)
- **Regular exercise independently induces autophagy and mitochondrial renewal** — combining training with spermidine is complementary
- **Include both aerobic and resistance training** for cardiovascular and metabolic health that aligns with spermidine's benefits

### Sleep & Recovery
- **Prioritize 7-9 hours of sleep** — sleep is a key time for cellular repair and autophagy; spermidine complements but cannot replace it
- **Consistent circadian rhythm** — supports the metabolic cycling (fed/fasted) that autophagy depends on

### Stress Management
- **Manage chronic stress** — chronic stress and elevated cortisol promote inflammation and can blunt autophagy

### Stacking Within a Longevity Routine
- **Combine thoughtfully with NMN, resveratrol, and other caloric-restriction-mimetic compounds** — these engage overlapping SIRT1/AMPK longevity pathways
- **Spermidine + NMN** is a logical longevity pairing (autophagy support plus NAD+ restoration)

### Long-Term Mindset
- **Spermidine is a steady, daily longevity habit** — its benefits accrue over months and years, consistent with the dietary observational data
- **Lifestyle does the heavy lifting** — spermidine amplifies the fundamentals (fasting, diet, exercise, sleep) rather than replacing them
