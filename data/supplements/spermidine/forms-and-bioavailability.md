# Spermidine
## Forms and Bioavailability

### Wheat-Germ Extract (Most Common Commercial Form)
- **The standard supplemental source** — spermidine-rich wheat-germ extract, standardized to a specific spermidine content per dose
- **Used in human clinical pilot trials** (e.g., cognitive and tolerability studies often use wheat-germ-derived spermidine)
- **Naturally accompanied by other polyamines** (spermine, putrescine) and wheat-germ nutrients
- **Gluten note:** Wheat-germ extracts can contain trace gluten; people with celiac disease or gluten sensitivity should seek certified gluten-free or non-wheat sources
- **Verdict:** The best-studied and most widely used form

### Fermentation-Derived / Synthetic Spermidine
- **Spermidine produced via microbial fermentation or chemical synthesis** — offers a gluten-free, defined-purity alternative
- **Often used as spermidine trihydrochloride** (a stable salt) in some products
- **Verdict:** A clean alternative, especially for those avoiding wheat

### Food-Based Polyamine Sources
- **Whole foods (wheat germ, aged cheese, soy/natto, mushrooms, legumes)** provide spermidine plus the gut-bacterial polyamine contribution
- Many longevity-minded users combine a polyamine-rich diet with supplementation

### Bioavailability Notes
- **Spermidine is absorbed in the gut**, and dietary polyamines contribute meaningfully to body polyamine pools
- **Gut microbiota produce polyamines**, an important endogenous source — gut health influences overall polyamine status
- Unlike highly insoluble polyphenols (quercetin, fisetin), spermidine does not have the same severe solubility/absorption problem; the focus is more on consistent intake and total polyamine load than on special delivery technology

### Stability & Quality
- **Standardization matters** — choose products that state the spermidine content per serving (often a few milligrams per dose)
- **Third-party testing** confirms the labeled spermidine amount and screens for contaminants
- **Storage** — keep cool, dry, and sealed

### Practical Summary
| Form | Notes | Best For |
|------|:---:|:---:|
| **Wheat-germ extract** | Best-studied, standardized | Most users; clinical-style dosing |
| Fermentation/synthetic | Gluten-free, defined purity | Gluten avoidance |
| Whole foods | Plus gut polyamine support | Food-first foundation |
