# Spermidine
## Things to Avoid

### Undermining Autophagy with Lifestyle
- **Chronic overnutrition and constant snacking** — keeping mTOR perpetually active suppresses autophagy, the very process spermidine promotes. Pair spermidine with time-restricted eating or periodic fasting for best effect.
- **Excessive alcohol** — impairs autophagy and adds oxidative/inflammatory burden
- **Chronic sleep deprivation** — sleep supports cellular repair and autophagy; spermidine cannot compensate for poor sleep

### Gluten Mistake (Form Choice)
- **Using wheat-germ-derived spermidine if you have celiac disease or gluten sensitivity** — choose fermentation-derived or synthetic spermidine to avoid trace gluten

### Dosing & Quality Mistakes
- **Buying unstandardized products** — choose spermidine that states its content per serving (often a few mg) and is third-party tested
- **Chasing very high doses** — long-term human safety above the researched range (~6-10 mg/day) is not established; "more" is not proven better
- **Expecting to feel acute effects** — autophagy is biochemical and not directly sensed; benefits accrue over months to years and are inferred from mechanism and outcome data

### Overstating the Evidence
- **Treating human cardiovascular/cognitive benefits as proven** — the strongest human data is observational (dietary intake linked to lower mortality) plus small, mixed pilot trials. Frame benefits as promising but not definitively established.

### Medical Cautions
- **Active cancer** — consult an oncologist before supplementing, given the unresolved theoretical question about polyamines and cell proliferation (note that dietary intake is associated with *lower* mortality, but caution is warranted in active malignancy)
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding** — avoid concentrated supplemental doses (insufficient data); dietary amounts are fine
- **Existing GI conditions** — start low if prone to GI sensitivity and take with food

### Realistic Expectations
- **Spermidine is a low-risk, mechanistically compelling longevity tool best used alongside fasting/dietary discipline, exercise, and sleep** — the lifestyle fundamentals it complements have far stronger evidence
