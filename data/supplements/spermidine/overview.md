# Spermidine
## Overview

Spermidine is a naturally occurring polyamine — a small molecule found in all living cells and essential for growth, proliferation, and cellular renewal. It is best known in longevity research as a potent natural inducer of autophagy, the cellular "self-eating" process that recycles damaged proteins and organelles. Because autophagy declines with age and is central to cellular health, spermidine is studied as a caloric-restriction mimetic and longevity compound. Tissue and dietary polyamine levels tend to fall with age, and observational data (notably from European cohort studies) have linked higher dietary spermidine intake with lower mortality and better cardiovascular outcomes. Most commercial supplements use wheat-germ extract as the spermidine source.

### Why It Matters

- **Autophagy induction:** Spermidine is one of the few well-characterized nutritional triggers of autophagy, the process that clears cellular debris and supports proteostasis. This is its defining mechanism.
- **Cardiovascular health:** Animal studies show spermidine improves cardiac function, reduces hypertension, and extends lifespan; human observational data associate higher intake with reduced cardiovascular mortality.
- **Cognitive support:** Preliminary human work (e.g., the SmartAge pilot trial) and animal studies suggest spermidine may support memory and brain health in older adults, though results are mixed and early.
- **Caloric-restriction-mimetic pathways:** Spermidine engages AMPK and influences SIRT1-related signaling and acetylation, overlapping conceptually with resveratrol and NAD+ precursors like NMN.
- **Food-derived and familiar:** Spermidine is consumed daily in foods like wheat germ, aged cheese, soy, and mushrooms, giving it a strong real-world safety record at dietary levels.

### Who Might Be Interested

- **Longevity-focused users** — interested in autophagy and caloric-restriction-mimetic effects
- **People focused on cardiovascular and cognitive aging** — based on observational and preliminary trial data
- **Those building a longevity stack** — alongside NMN, resveratrol, and other healthy-aging compounds
