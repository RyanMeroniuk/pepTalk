# Spermidine
## Foods to Avoid

### Foods/Substances That Work Against Spermidine's Goals
- **Excessive alcohol** — impairs autophagy, increases oxidative stress and inflammation, and burdens the liver, working against spermidine's cellular-cleanup purpose
- **Highly processed and high-sugar foods** — promote inflammation and metabolic stress, and chronic overnutrition suppresses autophagy via mTOR activation (the opposite of what spermidine aims to do)
- **Chronic caloric excess / constant snacking** — frequent eating keeps mTOR active and autophagy suppressed; spermidine works best against a backdrop of metabolic discipline (e.g., time-restricted eating)

### Gluten (Form-Specific, Not Universal)
- **For celiac disease or gluten sensitivity:** avoid wheat-germ-derived spermidine, which can contain trace gluten. Choose fermentation-derived/synthetic spermidine instead. This is a product-choice issue, not a food that blocks spermidine.

### No Specific Food Blocks Spermidine
- **There are no common foods that destroy or block spermidine absorption** in a meaningful way
- The relevant factor is the overall metabolic pattern: chronic overnutrition and constant high-insulin states suppress the autophagy that spermidine promotes

### Practical Guidance
- **Avoid keeping mTOR chronically activated** — constant grazing and caloric excess undercut autophagy; pairing spermidine with time-restricted eating amplifies its effect
- **Build a polyamine-rich, whole-food diet** (wheat germ, aged cheese, natto, mushrooms, legumes) and minimize ultra-processed foods and alcohol to let spermidine's autophagy benefits take hold
