# Spermidine
## Dosing Protocols

### Standard Daily Dose (Most Common)
- **Dose:** ~1-6 mg of spermidine per day (commonly 1-5 mg/day from wheat-germ extract)
- **Timing:** Once daily, often in the morning with food; consistency matters more than precise timing
- **Duration:** Continuous daily use — autophagy support is an ongoing process, not a one-time event
- **Notes:** Many human pilot trials used wheat-germ-derived spermidine in the low-single-digit mg/day range

### Clinical-Trial-Style Dosing
- **Cognitive pilot trials** (e.g., SmartAge) used roughly 0.9-3.3 mg/day of spermidine from wheat-germ extract over months
- **Tolerability studies** have used up to ~6 mg/day with good safety
- **Notes:** These are the doses with the most direct human data, modest as the data is

### Higher Longevity Doses
- **Some longevity practitioners use higher amounts (up to ~10 mg/day or more)** based on extrapolation, but human safety/efficacy data above ~6 mg/day is limited
- **Caution:** More is not established as better; stay within researched ranges unless guided otherwise

### Timing & Fasting Synergy
- **Pairs naturally with fasting / time-restricted eating** — fasting independently induces autophagy via AMPK/mTOR, and spermidine reinforces the same pathway. Taking spermidine during or alongside a fasting window is a common longevity strategy.
- **With or without food** — generally taken with food for convenience and tolerability; no strict requirement

### Cycling
- **Continuous daily use is typical** — there is no established need to cycle spermidine
- Some users align dosing with fasting protocols rather than cycling on/off

### Onset of Effects
- **Autophagy effects are biochemical and not directly felt** — benefits are inferred from mechanism and long-term outcomes rather than acute sensations
- **Cardiovascular and cognitive benefits (if achieved) accrue over months to years** of consistent intake, consistent with the observational dietary data

### Practical Guidance
- **Combine supplementation with a polyamine-rich diet** (wheat germ, aged cheese, soy/natto, mushrooms) for higher total intake
- **Choose standardized products** stating spermidine content per serving
- **Gluten-sensitive individuals** should use fermentation-derived/synthetic spermidine instead of wheat-germ extract
