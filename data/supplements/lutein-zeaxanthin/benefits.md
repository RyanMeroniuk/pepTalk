# Lutein & Zeaxanthin
## Benefits

### Age-Related Macular Degeneration (Strongest Evidence)
- **Slows progression to advanced AMD** — the AREDS2 trial (National Eye Institute) showed lutein 10 mg + zeaxanthin 2 mg (with other nutrients) reduced progression to advanced AMD in at-risk individuals, and was a safer substitute for beta-carotene
- **Raises macular pigment optical density (MPOD)** — supplementation reliably increases macular pigment, the protective filter depleted in AMD
- **Most beneficial in those with low dietary intake** — people with low baseline macular pigment see the largest gains

### Blue-Light & Screen Protection
- **Reduces eye strain and visual fatigue** — trials in screen/computer users report less eye fatigue, headache, and visual discomfort
- **Improves contrast sensitivity** — higher macular pigment improves the ability to distinguish objects from their background
- **Speeds glare recovery (photostress recovery)** — faster recovery of vision after exposure to bright light, useful for night driving and bright environments

### Cataract Support
- **May reduce cataract risk** — higher dietary and supplemental lutein/zeaxanthin associate with lower risk of cataract development and cataract surgery in observational studies; antioxidant protection of the lens is the proposed mechanism

### Cognitive Function (Emerging)
- **Deposits in the brain** — macular pigment correlates with brain carotenoid levels
- **Associated with better cognition** — observational and some interventional data link higher lutein/zeaxanthin status with better cognitive performance, particularly in older adults; evidence is emerging, not definitive

### Skin Health (Emerging)
- **Photoprotection** — oral (and topical) lutein/zeaxanthin support skin resilience to UV-induced oxidative damage and may improve skin hydration and elasticity in some trials

### General Antioxidant
- **Protects light- and oxygen-exposed tissues** — quench singlet oxygen and reactive species, especially in the retina, lens, skin, and brain

### Honest Framing
- The AMD-progression and macular-pigment benefits are well established. Cataract, cognitive, and skin benefits are supported by emerging and observational evidence. Benefits require consistent intake over months and absorption with dietary fat. None of this is medical advice.
