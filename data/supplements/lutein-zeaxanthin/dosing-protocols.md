# Lutein & Zeaxanthin
## Dosing Protocols

### General Eye Health / Prevention (Most Common)
- **Dose:** Lutein 10 mg + zeaxanthin 2 mg daily (the AREDS2 ratio)
- **Timing:** With a fat-containing meal (greatly improves absorption)
- **Duration:** Ongoing; macular pigment builds over weeks to months

### AMD Risk (AREDS2-Based)
- **Dose:** Lutein 10 mg + zeaxanthin 2 mg daily, typically as part of the full AREDS2 formula (with vitamin C, vitamin E, zinc, and copper)
- **Notes:** AREDS2 is intended for those with intermediate or advanced AMD in one eye, under guidance of an eye doctor. It slows progression; it is not a cure or a preventive for those without AMD risk

### Blue-Light / Screen Use
- **Dose:** Lutein 10-20 mg + zeaxanthin 2-4 mg daily (some screen-strain trials used higher lutein)
- **Timing:** With a fatty meal
- **Duration:** 8-12 weeks to assess effects on eye fatigue, contrast, and glare recovery

### Macular Triad (with Meso-Zeaxanthin)
- **Dose:** Common ratios are around lutein 10 mg + zeaxanthin 2 mg + meso-zeaxanthin 10 mg, or balanced macular-carotenoid blends
- **Notes:** Adding meso-zeaxanthin targets the central macula directly rather than relying solely on retinal conversion

### Cognitive / General Antioxidant (Emerging)
- **Dose:** Lutein 10-12 mg + zeaxanthin 2 mg daily
- **Notes:** Cognitive research used similar eye-health doses; evidence is emerging

### Practical Guidance
- **Always take with dietary fat** — these are fat-soluble carotenoids; a fatty meal can multiply absorption. Egg, avocado, olive oil, nuts, or fish all work
- **Be patient** — macular pigment accumulates slowly; allow 2-3 months for measurable changes in MPOD and visual performance
- **Consistency over timing** — once daily with the largest fatty meal is ideal; morning vs evening does not matter
- **Don't megadose** — there is little benefit to far exceeding studied doses; the AREDS2 amounts are well validated
- **Pair sensibly** — lutein-zeaxanthin (macular pigment), astaxanthin (eye fatigue/retina), and omega-3 (retinal structure, dry eye) are complementary for comprehensive eye support
