# Lutein & Zeaxanthin
## Classification

- **Category:** Macular carotenoid antioxidants / eye-health nutrients
- **Supplement class:** Xanthophyll carotenoids (oxygenated carotenoids)
- **Also known as:** Lutein, zeaxanthin, meso-zeaxanthin, macular pigment carotenoids, the "macular carotenoids"
- **Molecular formula:** Lutein C40H56O2; zeaxanthin C40H56O2 (structural isomers)
- **Natural source:** Dark leafy greens, egg yolks, corn, and marigold flowers (the main commercial source for supplements); meso-zeaxanthin is largely formed in the retina from lutein
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (OTC); key components of the AREDS2 formula studied by the National Eye Institute for age-related macular degeneration (AMD)
- **Primary systems:** Ocular (macula/retina), dermal (skin photoprotection), and to a lesser extent cognitive (they cross into brain tissue)
- **Key distinction:** These are the only dietary carotenoids that selectively accumulate in the macula of the retina, where they form the macular pigment that filters high-energy blue light and quenches free radicals
- **The three macular carotenoids:** Lutein, zeaxanthin, and meso-zeaxanthin together constitute the macular pigment, with each concentrating in slightly different zones of the retina
