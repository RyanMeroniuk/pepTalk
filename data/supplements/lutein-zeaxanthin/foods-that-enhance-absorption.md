# Lutein & Zeaxanthin
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

Lutein and zeaxanthin are fat-soluble carotenoids, so dietary fat is by far the most important factor for their absorption.

- **Dietary fat (the key enhancer):** Co-ingesting lutein/zeaxanthin with fat dramatically increases absorption. Studies show adding fat (eggs, oil, avocado) to a carotenoid source can multiply uptake. Never take these fasted or with a fat-free meal if maximal absorption is the goal
- **Egg yolk:** A standout — egg yolk provides lutein/zeaxanthin in a highly bioavailable form, and adding eggs to a salad measurably increases carotenoid absorption from the greens. The yolk's fat and phospholipids drive this effect
- **Olive oil and avocado:** Monounsaturated-fat foods that markedly enhance carotenoid absorption from vegetables. Dress your greens with olive oil or add avocado
- **Omega-3 fats (fish, fish oil):** Provide a fat carrier and complement retinal health (omega-3 builds the retina; lutein/zeaxanthin protect it). A natural pairing for eye support
- **Nuts and seeds:** Their fat content supports carotenoid uptake; pistachios also add lutein directly
- **Whole eggs in mixed meals:** The classic research example — adding eggs to a green salad increases lutein and zeaxanthin absorption from the whole meal
- **Cooking greens lightly with fat:** Gentle cooking softens plant cell walls (improving release), and cooking with oil provides the fat needed for absorption

### Practical Pairing Strategy
- **Simplest approach:** Take the supplement with your largest fat-containing meal, or eat leafy greens dressed in olive oil with eggs
- **Eye stack:** Lutein-zeaxanthin + astaxanthin + omega-3, all taken with a fatty meal, covers macular pigment, eye fatigue, and retinal structure
- **What to avoid:** Fat-free meals at dosing time, and simultaneous megadoses of competing carotenoids
