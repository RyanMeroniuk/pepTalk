# Inositol (Myo-Inositol)
## Forms and Bioavailability

### Myo-Inositol (MI) — The Primary Form
- **The most abundant and most-studied isomer** — Predominates in body tissues and is the backbone of most inositol supplements
- **Excellent oral absorption** — Well absorbed from the GI tract; bioavailability is high
- **Used alone for mood/anxiety** — High-dose mood studies typically used pure myo-inositol (12-18g/day)
- **Powder form is practical** — Tasteless to slightly sweet, dissolves readily in water; ideal for the larger doses inositol requires

### Myo-Inositol + D-Chiro-Inositol (40:1 Ratio) — The Women's Health Standard
- **The clinically studied PCOS ratio** — Combines ~2,000mg myo-inositol with ~50mg D-chiro-inositol per dose, mirroring the physiological plasma ratio
- **Why the ratio matters** — Myo-inositol supports ovarian/FSH function and egg quality; D-chiro-inositol supports insulin-mediated glycogen and androgen pathways. Excess D-chiro-inositol can impair egg quality (the "DCI paradox"), so the 40:1 ratio is preferred over high-DCI products.
- **Branded forms** — Some products use standardized 40:1 blends specifically formulated for PCOS and fertility

### D-Chiro-Inositol (DCI) Alone
- **Less commonly recommended alone** — High-dose DCI monotherapy has fallen out of favor for ovarian health due to the egg-quality concern
- **Niche metabolic use** — Sometimes used for specific insulin-resistance contexts, but the combined 40:1 ratio is generally preferred

### Inositol Hexaphosphate (IP6 / Phytic Acid)
- **A distinct compound** — IP6 is inositol with six phosphate groups, studied separately for antioxidant and other effects. It is NOT interchangeable with free myo-inositol for metabolic or mood purposes.

### Inositol-Stabilized Forms & Combinations
- Often combined with folate, B-vitamins, chromium, or berberine in metabolic and fertility formulations
- Verify the actual myo-inositol dose per serving, as some blends under-dose it

### Bioavailability & Dosing Practicalities
- **High oral bioavailability** — Inositol is efficiently absorbed; endogenous production is several grams per day, and supplemental doses are well tolerated
- **Large doses needed** — Effective doses (2-4g metabolic, 12-18g mood) are large, making powder more practical than capsules
- **Split dosing** — Larger daily amounts are usually split into two doses (e.g., morning and evening) to improve tolerance and maintain steady levels
- **No special timing required for absorption** — Can be taken with or without food

### Quality Considerations
- Choose products that clearly state the myo-inositol (and D-chiro-inositol) amounts and ratio
- Third-party testing (NSF, USP) preferred
- For PCOS/fertility, choose the 40:1 myo:D-chiro ratio specifically
