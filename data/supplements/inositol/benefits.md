# Inositol (Myo-Inositol)
## Benefits

### PCOS & Women's Metabolic Health (Strongest Evidence)
- **Restored ovulation and menstrual regularity** — Multiple RCTs show myo-inositol (often with D-chiro-inositol 40:1) restores ovulation and regular cycles in women with PCOS.
- **Reduced insulin resistance** — Improves fasting insulin, HOMA-IR, and glucose handling, addressing the core metabolic driver of PCOS.
- **Lowered androgens** — Reduces testosterone and free androgen index, improving hirsutism and acne over months of use.
- **Improved egg quality and fertility** — Myo-inositol supports oocyte quality; used adjunctively in fertility treatment and IVF protocols.
- **Comparable to metformin with better tolerability** — Head-to-head studies find inositol achieves similar metabolic and ovulatory benefits to metformin with far less GI distress.

### Metabolic & Blood Sugar Support (Beyond PCOS)
- **Improved insulin sensitivity** — Benefits extend to prediabetes, metabolic syndrome, and insulin-resistant states in both men and women.
- **Gestational diabetes prevention** — Studies suggest myo-inositol may reduce the incidence of gestational diabetes in at-risk pregnant women (under medical supervision).
- **Improved lipid profile** — Reductions in triglycerides and improvements in HDL cholesterol accompany improved insulin sensitivity.
- **Modest blood pressure support** — Some studies show small reductions in blood pressure alongside metabolic improvement.

### Mood, Anxiety & Mental Health (High-Dose Application)
- **Anxiety and panic disorder** — High-dose inositol (12-18g/day) reduced panic attack frequency and anxiety in controlled trials, with efficacy approaching some pharmaceutical comparators.
- **OCD** — Preliminary trials at high doses showed benefit in obsessive-compulsive symptoms.
- **Depression (preliminary)** — Mixed but some positive signals at high doses; evidence is less robust than for anxiety. Flagged as preliminary.
- **Note:** Mood benefits require much higher doses (12-18g/day) than the metabolic dose (2-4g/day).

### Sleep & Other
- **Sleep support (anecdotal/preliminary)** — Some users report improved sleep, possibly via anxiolytic and serotonergic effects; evidence is limited.
- **Respiratory distress in newborns** — Inositol has a research history in neonatal respiratory distress (clinical, not a consumer use).

### Realistic Expectations
- Metabolic and PCOS benefits emerge over 8-12+ weeks of consistent use; this is not an acute intervention.
- Mood/anxiety benefits require high doses and should be approached thoughtfully, ideally with professional guidance.
- Inositol pairs well with other insulin-sensitivity supplements (berberine, chromium) for metabolic goals.
