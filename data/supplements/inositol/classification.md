# Inositol (Myo-Inositol)
## Classification

- **Category:** Metabolic Health / Insulin Sensitivity / Women's Health / Mood Support
- **Type:** Sugar alcohol (polyol); vitamin-like compound (formerly called "vitamin B8")
- **Chemical class:** Cyclohexane-1,2,3,4,5,6-hexol; a six-carbon cyclitol with nine possible stereoisomers, of which myo-inositol is the most biologically abundant
- **Key isomers:** Myo-inositol (MI, predominant form) and D-chiro-inositol (DCI); the physiological plasma ratio in many tissues is approximately 40:1 (MI:DCI), which is the ratio used in many women's-health formulations
- **Source:** Synthesized endogenously (~4g/day, primarily in the kidneys from glucose), obtained from the diet (fruits, beans, grains, nuts), and recycled within cells
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (OTC); GRAS status; one of the most-studied supplements for PCOS and metabolic/insulin function
- **Primary function:** Acts as a secondary messenger in insulin and FSH signaling; precursor to phosphatidylinositol membrane phospholipids and inositol phosphates
- **Primary systems:** Endocrine/metabolic (insulin signaling), reproductive (ovarian function, FSH signaling), nervous (neurotransmitter signal transduction, mood)
- **Key distinction:** The myo-inositol + D-chiro-inositol 40:1 ratio is the most-studied formulation for PCOS and women's metabolic health. The two isomers have distinct roles and the ratio matters.
