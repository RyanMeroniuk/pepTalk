# Inositol (Myo-Inositol)
## Overview

Inositol is a sugar alcohol that functions as a secondary messenger in insulin and hormone signaling. Though the body makes several grams per day, supplemental myo-inositol — usually combined with D-chiro-inositol in a 40:1 ratio — has become arguably the most evidence-backed supplement for polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) and women's metabolic health. It improves insulin sensitivity, supports ovulatory function, and at higher doses has documented anxiolytic and mood-supporting effects.

**Why it matters:**

- **Insulin signaling secondary messenger** — Inositol-containing molecules (inositol phosphoglycans) act as second messengers downstream of the insulin receptor. Supplementing inositol improves insulin sensitivity, which is central to its benefits in PCOS, prediabetes, and metabolic syndrome.
- **The #1 PCOS supplement** — Numerous randomized controlled trials and meta-analyses show myo-inositol (often with D-chiro-inositol) improves insulin resistance, restores ovulation and menstrual regularity, reduces androgen levels, and improves egg quality in PCOS. It is frequently compared favorably to metformin with better tolerability.
- **The 40:1 ratio** — Myo-inositol predominates in most tissues and supports FSH signaling and egg quality; D-chiro-inositol supports insulin-mediated androgen synthesis and glycogen storage. The ~40:1 plasma ratio is replicated in the most-studied formulations, as excess D-chiro-inositol can impair egg quality.
- **Metabolic benefits beyond PCOS** — Improves markers of insulin resistance, supports healthy blood lipids and blood pressure, and has been studied for gestational diabetes prevention.
- **Anxiolytic and mood effects at higher doses** — At doses of 12-18g/day, inositol has shown benefit in anxiety, panic disorder, and OCD in clinical trials, likely via modulation of serotonin and other neurotransmitter signaling. These doses are much higher than the metabolic dose.
- **Excellent tolerability** — Inositol is very well tolerated; the main side effect at high doses is mild GI upset. It is generally better tolerated than metformin.
- **Works well in metabolic stacks** — Inositol pairs naturally with berberine and chromium, two other insulin-sensitivity supplements, and complements GLP-1-based metabolic interventions.
