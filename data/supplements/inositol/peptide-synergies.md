# Inositol (Myo-Inositol)
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### Semaglutide/Tirzepatide + Inositol (Metabolic & PCOS Powerhouse)
- **Rationale:** GLP-1 receptor agonists (semaglutide) and dual GLP-1/GIP agonists (tirzepatide) drive weight loss and improve glycemic control by enhancing insulin secretion and reducing appetite. Inositol improves insulin sensitivity at the cellular signaling level and directly supports ovarian function in PCOS. Together they attack insulin resistance from complementary angles — incretin-driven glycemic control plus second-messenger insulin sensitization — making this a strong pairing for PCOS, metabolic syndrome, and weight management.
- **Protocol:** Myo-inositol 4,000mg/day (40:1 with D-chiro for PCOS) + semaglutide or tirzepatide at prescribed dose. Monitor blood glucose to avoid additive hypoglycemia.
- **Best for:** PCOS with weight/metabolic component, insulin resistance, metabolic syndrome

#### Retatrutide + Inositol (Advanced Metabolic Support)
- **Rationale:** Retatrutide is a triple agonist (GLP-1/GIP/glucagon) with potent effects on weight and metabolic health. Inositol complements it by improving cellular insulin signaling and supporting ovarian/hormonal balance in women with metabolic dysfunction. The combination targets both systemic incretin pathways and downstream insulin second-messenger signaling.
- **Protocol:** Myo-inositol 2,000-4,000mg/day + retatrutide at prescribed dose. Monitor glucose closely given the strong metabolic effects of both.
- **Best for:** Significant insulin resistance, metabolic syndrome, obesity with PCOS features

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### Tesamorelin + Inositol (Metabolic & Body Composition)
- **Rationale:** Tesamorelin reduces visceral fat via GH release; visceral fat is a key driver of insulin resistance. Inositol improves insulin sensitivity at the signaling level. Reducing visceral adiposity (tesamorelin) while sensitizing tissues to insulin (inositol) is a complementary metabolic strategy.
- **Protocol:** Myo-inositol 2,000-4,000mg/day + tesamorelin at prescribed dose
- **Best for:** Visceral fat reduction with insulin-resistance management

#### MOTS-c + Inositol (Mitochondrial + Insulin Signaling)
- **Rationale:** MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-derived peptide that acts as an exercise mimetic, improving metabolic flexibility and insulin sensitivity via AMPK activation. Inositol improves insulin second-messenger signaling. Both improve glucose handling through distinct mechanisms, supporting metabolic health and athletic metabolic adaptation.
- **Protocol:** Myo-inositol 2,000-4,000mg/day + MOTS-c at standard dosing
- **Best for:** Metabolic optimization, insulin sensitivity, longevity-focused protocols

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### Kisspeptin + Inositol (Reproductive/Hormonal Support)
- **Rationale:** Kisspeptin stimulates GnRH and downstream LH/FSH release, central to reproductive hormone signaling. Inositol supports ovarian FSH signaling and egg quality. In a fertility/reproductive context, they address hormonal signaling from upstream (kisspeptin) and at the ovarian level (inositol).
- **Protocol:** Myo-inositol 4,000mg/day (40:1) + kisspeptin per protocol, under medical guidance
- **Best for:** Reproductive/hormonal support, fertility-oriented protocols (specialist-guided)

#### Ipamorelin/CJC-1295 + Inositol (Body Composition + Metabolic)
- **Rationale:** GH secretagogues support lean mass and fat loss; inositol improves insulin sensitivity. In a body-recomposition context, better insulin sensitivity complements the GH-driven improvements in body composition.
- **Protocol:** Myo-inositol 2,000-4,000mg/day + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Best for:** Body recomposition with metabolic support

### Metabolic Supplement Cluster
- Inositol is a core member of the insulin-sensitivity cluster alongside **berberine** (potent AMPK-activating glucose-lowering agent) and **chromium** (supports insulin receptor action).
- **Stack idea:** Myo-inositol 4,000mg + berberine 500mg 2-3x/day + chromium 200-400mcg/day, alongside a low-glycemic diet, for comprehensive insulin-sensitivity support. Monitor blood glucose, especially if also on glucose-lowering medication or a GLP-1 peptide.
