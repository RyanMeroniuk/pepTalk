# Inositol (Myo-Inositol)
## Foods to Avoid

Inositol has no specific foods that block its absorption. Because its primary value is improving insulin sensitivity and metabolic health, the "avoid" list focuses on dietary patterns that drive insulin resistance and undermine its benefits.

### Refined Sugar & High-Glycemic Foods (Directly Oppose the Goal)
- **Refined sugar and high-fructose corn syrup** — Repeated glucose/insulin spikes drive insulin resistance, working directly against inositol's insulin-sensitizing effect. This is the single most important category to limit.
- **Sugar-sweetened beverages** — Soda, sweetened juices, energy drinks cause rapid glucose surges
- **Refined carbohydrates** — White bread, pastries, white rice, and other processed carbs spike blood sugar
- **For PCOS specifically** — High-glycemic diets worsen the hyperinsulinemia central to PCOS; reducing them amplifies inositol's benefit

### Pro-Inflammatory Foods (Worsen Insulin Resistance)
- **Refined seed oils** — Soybean, corn, cottonseed, sunflower, safflower oils promote inflammation that contributes to insulin resistance
- **Trans fats** — Directly impair metabolic health
- **Ultra-processed foods** — The combination of refined carbs, seed oils, and additives promotes inflammation and metabolic dysfunction

### Excessive Alcohol
- Disrupts blood sugar regulation, adds empty calories, stresses the liver's metabolic role, and can worsen insulin resistance over time

### Pro-Inflammatory Dietary Patterns
- **Excessive processed/red meat and charred foods** — High in advanced glycation end products (AGEs) that promote inflammation and insulin resistance
- **Late-night heavy meals** — Can worsen glucose regulation and counter metabolic goals

### Foods That Are Irrelevant (Don't Overthink)
- **There are no foods that meaningfully block inositol absorption** — it is well absorbed
- **Phytate-heavy foods** provide bound inositol but do not interfere with supplemental free inositol
- Inositol can be taken with or without food

### The Real Priority
- For an inositol user pursuing metabolic or PCOS goals, the dietary focus is reducing the glycemic and inflammatory load of the diet — especially refined sugar and refined carbohydrates — because these drive the insulin resistance inositol is working to reverse
- A low-glycemic, whole-food, anti-inflammatory diet dramatically amplifies inositol's benefit and works synergistically with berberine and chromium
