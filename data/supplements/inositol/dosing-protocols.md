# Inositol (Myo-Inositol)
## Dosing Protocols

### PCOS / Women's Metabolic Protocol (Most Studied)
- **Dose:** Myo-inositol 4,000mg/day + D-chiro-inositol 100mg/day (the 40:1 ratio), typically split into two 2,000mg myo-inositol doses
- **Timing:** Morning and evening, with or without food
- **Duration:** Minimum 8-12 weeks before assessing; ovulatory and metabolic benefits build over 3-6 months
- **Notes:** This 40:1 protocol is the standard in most positive PCOS and fertility trials

### General Insulin Sensitivity / Metabolic Protocol
- **Dose:** Myo-inositol 2,000-4,000mg/day, split into two doses
- **Duration:** 8-12+ weeks for measurable metabolic improvement
- **Notes:** Can be combined with berberine and/or chromium for additive insulin-sensitizing effects

### Anxiety / Panic / OCD Protocol (High-Dose)
- **Dose:** Myo-inositol 12,000-18,000mg/day (12-18g), split into 2-3 doses
- **Timing:** Divided through the day to manage GI tolerance
- **Duration:** Several weeks; mood studies typically ran 4-6 weeks
- **Notes:** This is substantially higher than the metabolic dose. Titrate up gradually. Best approached with professional guidance, especially if also on psychiatric medication.

### Fertility / IVF Adjunct Protocol
- **Dose:** Myo-inositol 4,000mg/day + D-chiro-inositol 100mg/day (40:1), often with folate
- **Duration:** Typically started 1-3 months before and continued during fertility treatment, under medical supervision
- **Notes:** Used adjunctively to support oocyte quality; coordinate with the fertility specialist

### Titration & Tolerance
- **Start lower and build** — Begin at 2g/day and increase toward the target over a week or two to assess GI tolerance, especially for high-dose mood protocols
- **Split doses** — Dividing the daily amount improves tolerance and maintains steadier levels
- **Powder is practical** — Given the large doses, powder mixed in water is easier than many capsules

### Timing Considerations
- **With or without food** — Absorption is not strongly food-dependent
- **Morning + evening split** is the common pattern for the 4g metabolic dose
- **Consistency matters most** — Daily, divided dosing maintains steady signaling support

### Duration & Expectations
- **8-12 weeks minimum** for metabolic/PCOS endpoints — this is slow-acting, not acute
- **3-6 months** for full ovulatory, fertility, and androgen improvements
- **Indefinite maintenance** is common for ongoing metabolic and PCOS support
