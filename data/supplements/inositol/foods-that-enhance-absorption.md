# Inositol (Myo-Inositol)
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

Inositol is already efficiently absorbed orally, so food pairing is less about boosting absorption and more about reinforcing its metabolic effects, supporting the broader signaling pathways it participates in, and managing the GI tolerance of larger doses.

### Foods That Support Insulin Sensitivity (Reinforce the Metabolic Effect)
- **High-fiber foods** — Vegetables, legumes, and whole grains slow glucose absorption and improve insulin sensitivity, complementing inositol's metabolic action
- **Low-glycemic carbohydrates** — Whole, minimally processed carbs reduce glucose/insulin spikes, working with inositol toward better glycemic control
- **Protein with meals** — Stabilizes blood sugar response, supporting the insulin-sensitizing goal

### Magnesium-Rich Foods (Insulin & Glucose Metabolism)
- Magnesium is a cofactor in insulin signaling and glucose metabolism, complementing inositol's metabolic role
- **Leafy greens, nuts, seeds, legumes, dark chocolate**

### Chromium-Containing Foods (Glucose Metabolism Synergy)
- Chromium supports insulin action; pairs conceptually with inositol and the chromium supplement in metabolic stacks
- **Broccoli, whole grains, green beans, nuts**

### Omega-3 & Anti-Inflammatory Foods
- Reducing inflammation supports insulin sensitivity, reinforcing inositol's metabolic effects
- **Fatty fish (salmon, sardines), olive oil, vegetables, berries**

### Foods to Pair for GI Tolerance (High-Dose Mood Protocols)
- Taking large inositol doses with or after food, and splitting doses, reduces the GI upset that can occur at 12-18g/day
- A small meal or snack with the dose softens the osmotic GI effect

### Vitamin & Cofactor Support
- **Folate-rich foods** (leafy greens, legumes) — folate is often co-supplemented with inositol in fertility/PCOS protocols
- **B-vitamin-rich foods** — support the broader metabolic and neurological pathways inositol participates in

### What Doesn't Matter Much
- **Inositol absorbs well with or without food** — there is no strict requirement to take it with a meal for absorption
- **Timing relative to specific nutrients is not critical** — consistency and splitting larger doses matter more

### Practical Pairing Strategy
- **Metabolic/PCOS:** Take inositol with balanced, fiber-rich, lower-glycemic meals; pair with magnesium, chromium, and an anti-inflammatory diet
- **High-dose mood:** Split the dose and take with food to manage GI tolerance
- Combine with berberine and chromium for additive insulin-sensitizing effects in metabolic stacks
