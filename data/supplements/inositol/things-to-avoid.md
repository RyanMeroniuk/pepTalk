# Inositol (Myo-Inositol)
## Things to Avoid

### Quitting Before 8-12 Weeks
- The most common mistake. Inositol's metabolic and PCOS benefits (ovulation, insulin sensitivity, androgen reduction) build over 8-12 weeks and beyond, not days. Premature discontinuation means an unfair trial.

### Using High-Dose D-Chiro-Inositol Alone
- For ovarian and fertility goals, high-dose D-chiro-inositol monotherapy can impair egg quality (the "DCI paradox"). Use the myo-inositol-dominant 40:1 ratio (myo:D-chiro) for PCOS and fertility rather than DCI-heavy products.

### Under-Dosing
- The metabolic dose is 2-4g/day and the mood dose is 12-18g/day. Products providing only a few hundred milligrams are insufficient. Check the actual myo-inositol amount per serving.

### Confusing Inositol Forms
- Free myo-inositol (used for metabolic and mood effects) is distinct from inositol hexaphosphate (IP6/phytic acid), which is studied for different purposes. They are not interchangeable.

### Jumping Straight to High Doses
- Going directly to 12-18g/day causes GI upset (gas, loose stools). Titrate up gradually and split doses, taking them with food.

### High-Dose Use in Bipolar Disorder Without Supervision
- High-dose inositol affects the same phosphatidylinositol signaling system targeted by lithium and influences serotonergic signaling. In bipolar disorder there is a theoretical risk of mood destabilization. Use only under psychiatric supervision.

### Combining with Glucose-Lowering Drugs Without Monitoring
- Inositol improves insulin sensitivity. Combined with insulin, sulfonylureas, metformin, or other insulin-sensitizers (berberine), it can additively lower blood sugar. Monitor glucose and watch for hypoglycemia. See drug interactions.

### Expecting It to Replace Lifestyle Change
- Inositol works best alongside a low-glycemic, anti-inflammatory diet, exercise, and weight management for metabolic and PCOS goals. It is not a substitute for these foundations.

### Behaviors That Undermine Inositol's Benefits
- **High-sugar, high-refined-carb diet** — Drives the insulin resistance inositol is trying to reverse
- **Sedentary lifestyle** — Exercise is a powerful insulin sensitizer; inactivity blunts inositol's metabolic benefit
- **Chronic poor sleep and high stress** — Both worsen insulin resistance and androgen balance

### Medical Cautions
- **Pregnancy** — Use only under medical supervision (though myo-inositol has been studied favorably in pregnancy for gestational diabetes prevention)
- **Psychiatric medication users** — Coordinate high-dose use with a prescriber due to serotonergic effects
- **Inform healthcare providers** of all supplements, especially if managing diabetes or a mood disorder
