# Inositol (Myo-Inositol)
## Lifestyle Recommendations

### Eat a Low-Glycemic, Anti-Inflammatory Diet
- This is the single most impactful lifestyle factor for an inositol user. Reducing refined sugar and refined carbohydrates directly amplifies inositol's insulin-sensitizing effect. Emphasize vegetables, legumes, whole grains, lean protein, fatty fish, olive oil, and fruit. For PCOS especially, a lower-glycemic diet works synergistically with inositol.

### Exercise Regularly — Especially Resistance & Zone 2
- Exercise is one of the most powerful insulin sensitizers available. Resistance training builds glucose-disposing muscle mass; zone-2 cardio improves metabolic flexibility. Combined with inositol, exercise substantially improves insulin sensitivity and PCOS metabolic markers. Aim for a mix of resistance training and aerobic work most days.

### Prioritize Sleep
- Poor sleep worsens insulin resistance, raises cortisol, and disrupts hormonal balance — all counterproductive for metabolic and PCOS goals. Aim for 7-9 hours nightly. For those using inositol for anxiety, good sleep also supports mood.

### Manage Stress
- Chronic stress raises cortisol and worsens insulin resistance and androgen balance. Stress management (breathwork, meditation, time outdoors) complements inositol's metabolic and anxiolytic effects.

### Support Metabolic Goals with a Stack
- Inositol pairs well with other insulin-sensitivity supplements: berberine (potent glucose-lowering) and chromium (supports insulin action). Magnesium and omega-3 further support metabolic health. Build a coherent metabolic stack rather than relying on inositol alone.

### Maintain a Healthy Body Weight
- For PCOS and metabolic syndrome, even modest weight loss markedly improves insulin sensitivity, ovulation, and androgen levels. Weight management amplifies inositol's benefits more than almost any other intervention.

### Be Consistent and Patient (Metabolic Goals)
- Inositol is slow-acting. Take it daily in split doses and allow 8-12 weeks (and 3-6 months for full ovulatory/fertility effects). Set reminders for the twice-daily dosing pattern.

### For Mood/Anxiety Goals
- Use the higher dose (12-18g/day) split through the day, titrating up to manage GI tolerance. Combine with the same anxiety-supporting lifestyle foundations: sleep, stress management, limiting caffeine and alcohol. Coordinate with a clinician if on psychiatric medication.

### Monitor Your Markers
- For metabolic goals, track fasting glucose/insulin, HOMA-IR, lipids, and (for PCOS) menstrual regularity and androgen symptoms over time to gauge response. Periodic blood work shows whether the protocol is working.
