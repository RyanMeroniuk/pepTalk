# Rhodiola Rosea
## Things to Avoid

### Dosing Late in the Day
- Rhodiola is a stimulating adaptogen. Taking it in the afternoon or evening commonly causes insomnia or disrupted sleep. Keep dosing to the morning or early afternoon

### Starting Too High
- Rhodiola is dose-sensitive with a relatively narrow effective window for many people. Too high a dose causes jitteriness, irritability, anxiety, and overstimulation. Start low (100-200 mg standardized extract) and titrate up

### Buying Adulterated or Wrong-Species Products
- Adulteration with cheaper Rhodiola species (e.g., Rhodiola crenulata) that lack the rosavin complex is a documented problem. Insist on products specifying **Rhodiola rosea**, standardized to **3% rosavins / 1% salidroside**, ideally third-party tested
- Products standardized only to salidroside (or with no rosavin testing) may not match the research

### Stacking Excessive Stimulants
- Combining rhodiola with heavy caffeine or other stimulants risks overstimulation and anxiety. If you want layered energy, pair with l-theanine for calm energy rather than piling on stimulants

### Using It Like a Calming Herb
- Rhodiola is energizing, not sedating. If you need an evening, calming adaptogen, ashwagandha is the counterpart. Don't use rhodiola to wind down — it is for daytime fatigue and stress-performance

### Medical Cautions
- **Bipolar disorder:** Stimulating, antidepressant-like effects could theoretically provoke agitation or mania — use only under medical guidance
- **Anxiety/panic disorders:** May be overstimulating; start low or favor calming alternatives
- **Serotonergic / MAOI medications:** Possible mild MAO inhibition and monoamine effects create a theoretical interaction risk (see drug-interactions) — coordinate with a physician
- **Diabetes medications:** May affect blood sugar; monitor (see drug-interactions)
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding:** Insufficient safety data — avoid unless directed by a clinician
- **Before surgery:** Discontinue and disclose use to your surgical team

### Ignoring the Foundations
- Rhodiola buffers stress-related fatigue but cannot replace adequate sleep, recovery, and stress management. Chronic sleep deprivation and unrelenting stress will outpace what any adaptogen can offset
