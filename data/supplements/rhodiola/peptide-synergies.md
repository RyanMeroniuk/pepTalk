# Rhodiola Rosea
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### Selank + Rhodiola (Calm, Anti-Fatigue Resilience)
- **Rationale:** Selank is an anxiolytic nootropic that reduces anxiety and balances neurotransmitters without sedation. Rhodiola is a stimulating, anti-fatigue adaptogen that can sometimes feel overstimulating. Selank takes the anxious edge off rhodiola's energizing effect, producing focused, calm resilience under stress — ideal for high-pressure work without jitteriness.
- **Protocol:** Rhodiola 200-400 mg standardized extract in the morning + Selank at standard intranasal dosing
- **Best for:** Anxious high-performers, burnout recovery, stress-heavy schedules

#### Semax + Rhodiola (Stress-Resilient Cognition)
- **Rationale:** Semax upregulates BDNF and supports attention, memory, and stress adaptation through melanocortin pathways. Rhodiola buffers the HPA-axis/cortisol stress response and combats mental fatigue. Together they support cognitive performance specifically under stress and fatigue — one of rhodiola's strongest use cases.
- **Protocol:** Rhodiola 200-400 mg in the morning + Semax at standard intranasal nootropic dosing
- **Best for:** Sustained mental performance during high-stress or high-fatigue periods

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + Rhodiola (Recovery + Daytime Energy)
- **Rationale:** CJC-1295/Ipamorelin raise GH and IGF-1, improving sleep quality and overnight recovery. Rhodiola supports daytime energy and stress resilience. The combination covers the full cycle — overnight recovery from GH peptides and daytime anti-fatigue support from rhodiola — useful for demanding training or work loads.
- **Protocol:** Rhodiola 200-400 mg in the morning + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Best for:** Athletes and high-output professionals balancing recovery and daytime demand

#### MOTS-c + Rhodiola (Metabolic Energy & Endurance)
- **Rationale:** MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-derived exercise-mimetic peptide that enhances cellular energy metabolism and exercise capacity. Rhodiola (via salidroside) supports mitochondrial ATP production and reduces perceived exertion. Together they target cellular energy and endurance from complementary angles.
- **Protocol:** Rhodiola 200-300 mg pre-activity + MOTS-c at standard dosing
- **Best for:** Endurance performance, anti-fatigue, metabolic optimization

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### Tesamorelin + Rhodiola (Body Composition + Stress Resilience)
- **Rationale:** Tesamorelin promotes GH release and targets visceral fat. Rhodiola supports energy, stress resilience, and exercise capacity during a fat-loss phase, helping maintain training output and mood when in a deficit (a high-stress state for the body).
- **Protocol:** Rhodiola 200-400 mg morning + Tesamorelin at prescribed dosing
- **Best for:** Body recomposition phases where stress and fatigue management matter

#### Epitalon + Rhodiola (Stress-Recovery Longevity)
- **Rationale:** Epitalon regulates melatonin/circadian rhythm and supports anti-aging processes, improving sleep. Better sleep from Epitalon complements rhodiola's daytime anti-fatigue effect, while both contribute antioxidant/neuroprotective support — a stress-resilience plus recovery longevity stack.
- **Protocol:** Rhodiola 200-400 mg morning + Epitalon in cyclical anti-aging courses
- **Best for:** Longevity-focused stress and fatigue management

### Supplement Synergies (Non-Peptide)
- **Ashwagandha:** The calming counterpart adaptogen. Rhodiola energizes and combats daytime fatigue; ashwagandha calms and lowers cortisol/arousal in the evening. Used together (rhodiola AM, ashwagandha PM) they provide balanced day/night stress coverage.
- **L-Theanine:** L-theanine smooths rhodiola's stimulating edge, producing calm, focused energy. A widely used pairing for anti-fatigue without jitteriness.
- **Bacopa:** As adaptogenic and cognitive support, rhodiola buffers acute stress/fatigue while bacopa slowly builds memory — short-term resilience plus long-term cognitive support.
