# Rhodiola Rosea
## Forms and Bioavailability

### Standardized Extract (3% Rosavins / 1% Salidroside) — Recommended
- **The clinical standard:** Most positive research uses extracts standardized to ~**3% rosavins and 1% salidroside** (the natural ~3:1 ratio found in the root). The branded **SHR-5** extract is the form used in many landmark fatigue and stress trials
- **Why it matters:** Both the rosavin complex and salidroside contribute to effects. Products standardized to only one marker (or to unusually high salidroside) may behave differently from the studied extracts
- **What to look for:** Label stating "Rhodiola rosea" (the species matters — see below), standardized to 3% rosavins and 1% salidroside

### Species Authenticity (A Real Quality Issue)
- **Rhodiola rosea specifically:** Only Rhodiola rosea reliably contains the rosavin complex. Adulteration with cheaper Rhodiola species (e.g., Rhodiola crenulata) that contain salidroside but little or no rosavin is a documented marketplace problem
- Insist on products that specify **Rhodiola rosea** and ideally provide testing for both rosavins and salidroside

### Whole-Root Powder
- **What it is:** Dried, ground rhodiola root. Rosavin/salidroside content is variable and generally lower per gram than a standardized extract
- **Use:** Traditional teas and preparations; less precise for targeted dosing

### Bioavailability and Absorption
- Rhodiola's actives are reasonably bioavailable orally. It is typically taken on an empty stomach (see below), as food is not required for absorption
- **Tinctures/liquid extracts** are also available and absorb readily; check the standardization and extract ratio

### Capsules vs Powder vs Tincture
- **Capsules/tablets:** Convenient, standardized dosing, mask the somewhat astringent taste
- **Powder:** Cost-effective and flexible but bitter/astringent
- **Tincture:** Fast and easy to dose; verify standardization

### Timing Note (Empty Stomach)
- Rhodiola is commonly taken on an empty stomach in the morning or before activity for best effect. Taking it with a large meal is fine for tolerance but may slightly slow onset

### Storage
- Store sealed, cool, and dry, away from light, to preserve rosavin and salidroside content
