# Rhodiola Rosea
## Classification

- **Category:** Adaptogenic herb; anti-fatigue stimulating adaptogen
- **Subcategory:** Stress-resilience botanical, cognitive/physical performance support
- **Botanical name:** Rhodiola rosea (genus Rhodiola, family Crassulaceae)
- **Common names:** Golden root, rose root, arctic root, king's crown
- **Active compounds:** Rosavins (rosavin, rosin, rosarin — the rosavin complex) and salidroside (also called rhodioloside); also tyrosol, flavonoids, and other phenolics
- **Standardization marker:** Quality extracts are standardized to **3% rosavins and 1% salidroside** (the traditional 3:1 ratio seen in most clinical research, e.g., the branded SHR-5 extract)
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (USA, OTC); long history of traditional use in Russia, Scandinavia, and Central Asia for fatigue and stamina
- **Primary systems:** Neurological (mental fatigue, mood), endocrine (cortisol/HPA-axis modulation), physical performance (endurance, fatigue resistance)
- **Adaptogen profile:** A **stimulating, activating adaptogen** — it tends to energize and reduce fatigue, in contrast to ashwagandha, which is the calming, sedating counterpart. The two are often described as opposite ends of the adaptogen spectrum
