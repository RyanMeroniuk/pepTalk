# Rhodiola Rosea
## Side Effects

### Common (Mild, Often Dose-Related)
- **Overstimulation / jitteriness:** As a stimulating adaptogen, rhodiola can cause restlessness, agitation, or a "wired" feeling, especially at higher doses or in sensitive individuals. Lower the dose to resolve
- **Insomnia:** Taking rhodiola too late in the day commonly disrupts sleep. Dose in the morning/early afternoon
- **Irritability:** Some users report increased irritability, particularly at higher doses

### Uncommon
- **Dizziness or lightheadedness:** Occasionally reported
- **Dry mouth:** Reported by some users
- **Headache:** Infrequent and usually mild
- **Vivid dreams:** Some users report unusually vivid dreams, possibly related to monoamine effects

### Specific Considerations
- **Activating in anxiety-prone individuals:** While rhodiola can help stress-related fatigue, its stimulating nature may worsen anxiety or panic in some people. Those with anxiety disorders should start low and monitor; the calming counterpart (ashwagandha) or l-theanine may suit them better or pair well
- **Blood pressure / heart rate:** Stimulating effects could modestly affect heart rate or blood pressure in sensitive individuals; monitor if you have cardiovascular conditions
- **Possible MAO inhibition:** Rhodiola may have mild monoamine oxidase inhibitory activity, which is the basis for caution with serotonergic/stimulant drugs (see drug-interactions)

### Overall Safety Profile
- **Generally well tolerated** in clinical trials, typically lasting a few weeks to a few months. Side effects are usually mild and dose-related (overstimulation being the most common)
- **Long traditional use** supports a reasonable safety margin at standard doses
- **Long-term (multi-year) human safety data is limited** — most trials are short to medium term

### When to Be Cautious
- **Bipolar disorder:** Stimulating, antidepressant-like effects could theoretically trigger agitation or mania in susceptible individuals — use only under medical guidance
- **Anxiety/panic disorders:** May be overstimulating — start low
- **Autoimmune conditions:** As with some adaptogens, theoretical immune modulation warrants caution
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding:** Insufficient safety data — avoid unless directed by a clinician
- **Before surgery:** Disclose use to your surgical team
