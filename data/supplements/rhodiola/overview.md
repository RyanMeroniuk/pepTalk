# Rhodiola Rosea
## Overview

Rhodiola rosea (golden root) is an adaptogenic herb native to cold, high-altitude regions of Europe, Asia, and the Arctic, with a long history of traditional use for combating fatigue, enhancing stamina, and improving resilience to physical and mental stress. Its primary active compounds — the rosavins and salidroside — are believed to modulate the stress-response system (the HPA axis and cortisol) and key neurotransmitters, producing an effect best described as **anti-fatigue and energizing rather than sedating**. This makes rhodiola the stimulating counterpart to ashwagandha: where ashwagandha tends to calm and reduce arousal, rhodiola tends to lift fatigue, sharpen mental performance under stress, and support endurance. Notably, rhodiola often has a relatively fast onset compared with slow-build nootropics — many people notice reduced fatigue within days. It is among the better-studied adaptogens for stress-related fatigue and burnout, and pairs naturally with l-theanine for calm energy and with ashwagandha for balanced day/night stress coverage.
