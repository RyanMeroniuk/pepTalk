# Rhodiola Rosea
## Mechanisms of Action

### 1. HPA-Axis and Cortisol Modulation (Core Adaptogen Mechanism)
- Rhodiola modulates the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis, helping normalize the cortisol response to stress
- Unlike ashwagandha, which tends to lower cortisol and arousal broadly, rhodiola modulates the stress response while preserving alertness — it buffers excessive cortisol spikes without sedating
- This blunting of an overactive stress response is central to its anti-fatigue and stress-resilience effects

### 2. Neurotransmitter Modulation (Monoamines)
- Rhodiola influences serotonin, dopamine, and norepinephrine, partly by affecting the enzymes that synthesize and degrade them (including possible monoamine oxidase inhibition)
- Increased availability/activity of these monoamines supports mood, motivation, and the activating, anti-fatigue character of the herb
- The dopaminergic/noradrenergic support helps explain the "energizing" rather than "calming" profile

### 3. Anti-Fatigue / Cellular Energy
- Salidroside in particular is associated with improved cellular energy metabolism — supporting mitochondrial ATP production and reducing markers of fatigue
- Rhodiola may improve oxygen utilization and reduce the perception of effort during physical and mental work, underlying its endurance and anti-fatigue benefits

### 4. Antioxidant and Anti-Inflammatory Effects
- Rosavins and salidroside have antioxidant activity, reducing oxidative stress that accumulates during physical exertion and chronic stress
- Anti-inflammatory effects contribute to recovery and resilience

### 5. Neuroprotection and BDNF
- Salidroside has shown neuroprotective effects in preclinical models, including support of BDNF and protection of neurons from oxidative and excitotoxic damage
- This supports cognitive performance under stress and potential long-term neural resilience

### 6. AMPK and Metabolic Effects
- Salidroside may activate AMPK, a cellular energy sensor, contributing to metabolic and anti-fatigue effects in preclinical work

### 7. Cardioprotective / Stress-Buffering
- By modulating stress hormones and supporting antioxidant defenses, rhodiola may buffer some of the cardiovascular strain of chronic stress in preclinical models
