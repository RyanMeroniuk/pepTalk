# DHEA
## Peptide Synergies

> IMPORTANT: DHEA is a hormone precursor, not an inert supplement. "Synergy" here describes mechanistic complementarity, but combining DHEA with other hormonally active agents increases the total androgen/estrogen load and the need for monitoring and physician oversight. None of these pairings should be undertaken casually, and all are contraindicated in hormone-sensitive conditions.

### Tier 1: Mechanistically Coherent (Use With Oversight)

#### Kisspeptin + DHEA (Endogenous Hormone Axis)
- **Rationale:** Kisspeptin stimulates the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal (HPG) axis to drive the body's own gonadal hormone production, while DHEA supplies an adrenal precursor that peripheral tissues convert to androgens and estrogens. Both raise sex-hormone tone, but via different sources (gonadal signaling vs adrenal precursor). The overlap means careful monitoring of testosterone and estradiol is essential to avoid excess.
- **Protocol:** Physician-guided; conservative DHEA dose with labs + kisspeptin per protocol
- **Best for:** Hormone optimization in deficiency states under medical supervision; caution in men due to estrogen conversion

#### PT-141 (Bremelanotide) + DHEA (Sexual Function)
- **Rationale:** PT-141 acts centrally on melanocortin receptors for acute sexual arousal; DHEA provides a hormonal substrate that supports libido and sexual function, particularly in women and those with low baseline DHEA. The combination pairs an acute neurological trigger with a slower hormonal foundation.
- **Protocol:** Conservative DHEA dose (with labs) ongoing + PT-141 as needed before activity
- **Best for:** Sexual function with a hormonal-deficiency component, especially in postmenopausal women under guidance

### Tier 2: Complementary (Recovery / Vitality)

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + DHEA
- **Rationale:** GH secretagogues raise GH and IGF-1 for recovery and body composition; DHEA supports the sex-hormone side of the anabolic/vitality picture. In aging adults replacing declining hormones, the two address different age-related declines (GH/IGF-1 axis and adrenal androgens).
- **Protocol:** Conservative DHEA dose with labs + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed, physician-guided
- **Best for:** Age-related vitality and recovery within a supervised hormone-optimization framework

#### MK-677 (Ibutamoren) + DHEA
- **Rationale:** MK-677 raises GH/IGF-1; DHEA contributes adrenal-androgen precursor support. Complementary for recomposition and vitality in older adults, again with monitoring of both the GH/IGF-1 and sex-hormone axes.
- **Protocol:** Conservative DHEA dose + MK-677 10-25 mg before bed, under oversight
- **Best for:** Supervised recomposition/vitality protocols in aging adults

### Supplement Stacking Notes (Caution)
- **DHEA + Tongkat Ali / Boron / Fadogia Agrestis:** These testosterone-cluster supplements (tongkat-ali, boron, fadogia-agrestis) each push free testosterone or LH/testosterone pathways. Adding DHEA, a hormone precursor, on top of them stacks androgen/estrogen load substantially. This is NOT a casual combination — it requires labs and physician oversight, and is contraindicated in hormone-sensitive conditions. Fadogia-agrestis additionally carries serious toxicity concerns and almost no human safety data.
- **DHEA + Vitamin D3 (+ calcium/K2):** A more benign, bone-health-oriented pairing; DHEA's modest bone benefit (mainly in older women) is supported by adequate vitamin D3 and bone nutrients.
- **General rule:** Because DHEA is itself a hormone, stacking it with other hormonal agents is the area requiring the most caution of any item in the testosterone cluster.
