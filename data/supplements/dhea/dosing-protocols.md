# DHEA
## Dosing Protocols

> IMPORTANT: DHEA is a hormone precursor, not an inert supplement. Dosing should ideally be guided by bloodwork (DHEA-S, testosterone, estradiol) and a physician, especially for women and anyone with hormone-sensitive conditions. The doses below are educational/research context, not a recommendation to self-prescribe.

- **General principle — start low, replace don't overshoot:**
  - The goal in most legitimate use is to *restore* an age-related deficiency to youthful-normal range, not to push levels supraphysiologically. Lower doses are usually safer and often sufficient
  - "More is better" does NOT apply — excess simply increases androgenic/estrogenic side effects

- **Older men (deficiency-replacement context):**
  - **Dose:** Often 25-50 mg/day, though many do well starting at 25 mg or lower
  - **Timing:** Morning, mirroring the natural diurnal adrenal rhythm
  - **Monitoring:** Check DHEA-S, total/free testosterone, and estradiol at baseline and ~6-8 weeks; men can aromatize a meaningful fraction to estrogen

- **Older / postmenopausal women:**
  - **Dose:** Typically much lower — often 5-25 mg/day. Women are more sensitive to androgenic effects (acne, hair changes, voice deepening) and need conservative dosing
  - **Monitoring:** DHEA-S, testosterone, and estradiol at baseline and follow-up

- **Adrenal insufficiency (physician-directed):**
  - **Dose:** Usually 25-50 mg/day in men and lower (5-25 mg) in women, titrated to restore normal DHEA-S
  - **Note:** This is a medical replacement scenario managed by an endocrinologist

- **Intravaginal DHEA (prasterone):**
  - **Dose/form:** Prescription insert dosed per the product (e.g., daily). Managed by a clinician for vulvovaginal atrophy

- **Fertility (IVF / diminished ovarian reserve):**
  - **Dose:** Commonly ~75 mg/day in studies, but strictly under a reproductive endocrinologist — not a general protocol

- **What to avoid:**
  - **High "bodybuilding" doses** (well above replacement) to chase muscle/testosterone — this disproportionately raises estrogen in men and androgenic side effects in women, with poor evidence of benefit
  - **Indefinite use without labs** — DHEA should be monitored, not taken blindly

- **Sport caution:** DHEA is banned by WADA and most athletic organizations. Competitive athletes should NOT use it
