# DHEA
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

> Because DHEA is a hormone metabolized heavily on first pass through the liver, "food enhancement" matters less than dose accuracy, individual enzyme activity, and monitoring. There is no food that meaningfully boosts its hormonal effect.

- **Take with a meal containing some fat:** DHEA is a fat-soluble steroid. Taking micronized DHEA with a meal that includes dietary fat may modestly support absorption and reduce GI upset. This is a minor optimization, not a major lever

- **Morning dosing with breakfast:** Aligning DHEA with the natural morning peak of adrenal hormone output (and taking it with breakfast) is a reasonable practical approach for absorption and circadian alignment

- **Consistency over enhancement:** Far more important than any absorption trick is taking the same dose at the same time consistently and then verifying serum levels (DHEA-S, testosterone, estradiol) by bloodwork. Individual conversion to androgens vs estrogens varies widely and cannot be "enhanced" predictably by food

### What Does NOT Help
- **Wild yam / diosgenin foods:** These do NOT convert to DHEA in the human body and do not enhance DHEA levels or absorption (see natural food sources)
- **High-dose individual nutrients:** No vitamin, mineral, or food has been shown to reliably increase DHEA's hormonal effect

### Practical Note
- Given the wide variability in how individuals metabolize oral DHEA, the meaningful "optimization" is medical: appropriate dose selection, third-party-tested product, and follow-up labs — not food pairing
