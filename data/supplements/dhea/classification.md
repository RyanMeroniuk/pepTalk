# DHEA
## Classification

- **Category:** Endogenous steroid hormone / hormone (prohormone) precursor — NOT a typical vitamin/mineral or herbal supplement
- **Subcategory:** Adrenal androgen precursor; "parent" steroid hormone
- **Full name:** Dehydroepiandrosterone (and its sulfated form, DHEA-S)
- **Chemical class:** C19 steroid hormone
- **Endogenous source:** Produced primarily by the adrenal cortex (and gonads/brain in smaller amounts); the most abundant circulating steroid hormone in the human body
- **Downstream conversion:** Serves as an upstream precursor that the body converts into androgens (testosterone, DHT) and estrogens (estradiol, estrone)
- **Regulatory status (important nuance):** In the USA, DHEA is sold over the counter as a dietary supplement (it was specifically exempted from scheduling under DSHEA). However, it is a **hormone**, not an inert nutrient. It is **banned in sport by WADA** and most athletic organizations as an anabolic agent / prohormone. In several other countries (e.g., Canada, UK, Australia) it is a prescription-only or controlled substance. Its OTC status in the US is a regulatory artifact, not an indication that it is risk-free
- **Primary systems:** Endocrine (sex hormones, adrenal axis), with age-related decline being the central clinical context
