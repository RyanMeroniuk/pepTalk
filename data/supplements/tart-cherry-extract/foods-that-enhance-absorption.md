# Tart Cherry Extract
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

- **Healthy fats** — Anthocyanins show improved absorption when consumed with fat. Pair tart cherry with:
  - **Nuts and seeds** — Walnuts, almonds, or pumpkin seeds provide fat for anthocyanin absorption plus complementary anti-inflammatory omega-3s (walnuts) or magnesium (pumpkin seeds)
  - **Avocado** — Monounsaturated fats enhance polyphenol absorption
  - **Olive oil** — Extra virgin olive oil provides oleocanthal (anti-inflammatory) alongside fat for absorption
  - **Full-fat yogurt** — Combines fat for absorption with probiotics that may further metabolize polyphenols into bioactive metabolites
  - **Coconut cream** — Blends well with tart cherry juice or powder in smoothies
- **Vitamin C-rich foods** — Vitamin C stabilizes anthocyanins in the GI tract and may enhance their antioxidant effects:
  - **Citrus fruits** — Orange or lemon juice added to tart cherry juice
  - **Kiwi** — High vitamin C plus its own sleep-supporting properties
  - **Bell peppers** — If combining in savory dishes
  - **Strawberries** — Their own anthocyanins complement tart cherry polyphenols
- **Piperine (black pepper)** — Piperine inhibits glucuronidation, a liver detoxification pathway that clears polyphenols from the bloodstream. A small amount of black pepper may extend anthocyanin bioavailability.
- **Quercetin-rich foods** — Quercetin and anthocyanins may share transport mechanisms and have synergistic antioxidant effects:
  - **Onions (especially red onions)** — Highest common quercetin source
  - **Apples** — Quercetin concentrated in the skin
  - **Capers** — Exceptionally quercetin-dense
- **Fermented foods** — Gut microbiome composition affects polyphenol metabolism. Beneficial bacteria convert anthocyanins into bioactive phenolic acid metabolites. Kimchi, sauerkraut, kefir, and kombucha support this conversion.
- **Acidic environments** — Anthocyanins are more stable and better absorbed in acidic conditions. Consuming tart cherry with citrus juice or apple cider vinegar may preserve anthocyanin integrity through the GI tract.
