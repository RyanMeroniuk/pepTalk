# Tart Cherry Extract
## Classification

- **Category:** Sleep Support / Anti-Inflammatory / Recovery
- **Type:** Botanical extract (whole-food concentrate)
- **Source plant:** Prunus cerasus (sour cherry), specifically the Montmorency variety
- **Active compounds:** Anthocyanins (cyanidin glycosides), melatonin, proanthocyanidins, phenolic acids, flavonoids
- **Chemical class:** Polyphenol-rich fruit extract with endogenous melatonin content
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (USA), food product
- **Primary function:** Natural melatonin source combined with potent anti-inflammatory anthocyanins for sleep, recovery, and inflammation management
