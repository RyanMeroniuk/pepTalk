# Tart Cherry Extract
## Foods to Avoid

- **High-sugar beverages and snacks** — Consuming tart cherry juice alongside additional high-sugar foods amplifies the glycemic load. The natural sugars in tart cherry juice concentrate (12-17g per 30ml) are already significant. Avoid pairing with fruit juice, soda, candy, or sweetened yogurt.
- **Dairy calcium at the same time (for anthocyanin absorption)** — High-calcium dairy products may bind to and reduce absorption of some polyphenols. If using tart cherry for its anthocyanin benefits, separate from large dairy servings by 1-2 hours. Small amounts of yogurt are acceptable given the probiotic trade-off.
- **Highly processed foods** — Ultra-processed foods promote systemic inflammation and oxidative stress, counteracting the anti-inflammatory benefits of tart cherry. Seed oils, trans fats, and refined carbohydrates are particularly counterproductive.
- **Alcohol** — Alcohol disrupts sleep architecture (suppresses REM, causes rebound waking), directly opposing the sleep benefits of tart cherry. Alcohol also increases oxidative stress, partially negating the antioxidant effects. If using tart cherry for sleep, avoid alcohol within 3-4 hours of bedtime.
- **Excessive caffeine (if using for sleep)** — Caffeine blocks adenosine receptors and suppresses melatonin production. If using tart cherry for sleep support, limit caffeine to the morning hours (before noon). Even moderate afternoon caffeine can reduce sleep quality.
- **High-oxalate foods in the same meal (if kidney stone-prone)** — Tart cherries contain moderate oxalates. Individuals prone to calcium-oxalate kidney stones should avoid pairing with other high-oxalate foods (spinach, rhubarb, beet greens, Swiss chard, almonds) in the same sitting.
- **Foods high in iron (timing consideration)** — Polyphenols can reduce non-heme iron absorption by 50-90%. If managing iron deficiency, separate tart cherry from iron-rich meals or iron supplements by at least 2 hours.
