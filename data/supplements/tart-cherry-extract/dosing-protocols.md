# Tart Cherry Extract
## Dosing Protocols

- **Sleep support (juice concentrate)** — 30ml (1 oz) Montmorency tart cherry juice concentrate mixed with water, taken 30-60 minutes before bed. Can also split into two doses: 30ml in the morning and 30ml in the evening. The split-dose protocol was used in the Louisiana State University insomnia study.
- **Sleep support (capsules)** — 480-1,000mg tart cherry extract (CherryPURE or equivalent), taken 30-60 minutes before bed. Two capsules of 480mg is a common protocol.
- **Exercise recovery (pre-event)** — Begin tart cherry supplementation 4-5 days before a demanding event (marathon, competition, high-volume training block). Take 30ml concentrate twice daily (morning and evening) or 480mg capsules twice daily. Continue for 2-3 days after the event.
- **Exercise recovery (daily training)** — 30ml concentrate or 480mg capsules once daily, preferably post-workout or in the evening to support both recovery and sleep.
- **Gout management** — 30ml tart cherry concentrate daily, or 480mg capsules twice daily. Consistent daily use is important for sustained uric acid reduction. Some protocols use 2 tablespoons (30ml) twice daily during active flares.
- **General anti-inflammatory support** — 30ml concentrate once daily or 480-1,000mg capsules daily. Minimum 4-week trial to assess anti-inflammatory benefits.
- **Cardiovascular support** — 60ml (2 oz) concentrate daily, based on studies showing blood pressure reduction at this dose.

**Timing guidelines:**

- **For sleep:** 30-60 minutes before bed (or split morning/evening)
- **For exercise recovery:** Within 1 hour post-workout and/or before bed
- **For gout:** Consistent daily timing, with or without food
- **Duration:** Benefits for inflammation and uric acid accrue over 2-4 weeks of consistent use. Sleep benefits may be noticed within the first few days.

**Practical notes:**

- Tart cherry juice concentrate has approximately 50-70 calories and 12-17g sugar per 30ml serving. Factor this into daily macros.
- The tart flavor mixes well with sparkling water for an evening beverage ritual.
- Capsules are preferable for those managing blood sugar, on low-carb diets, or traveling.
- Do not confuse tart cherry products with sweet cherry products — they are nutritionally distinct.
