# Tart Cherry Extract
## Mechanisms of Action

- **Melatonin-mediated circadian signaling** — Tart cherry provides exogenous melatonin that binds MT1 and MT2 receptors in the suprachiasmatic nucleus, signaling the biological night and facilitating sleep onset. The melatonin content in Montmorency cherries has been confirmed through HPLC analysis at concentrations sufficient to raise serum melatonin levels in human studies.
- **COX-1 and COX-2 inhibition** — Anthocyanins in tart cherry, particularly cyanidin-3-glucoside, directly inhibit cyclooxygenase enzymes responsible for prostaglandin synthesis. This reduces pain signaling and inflammation through the same mechanism as NSAIDs, but with a gentler side-effect profile.
- **NF-kB pathway suppression** — Tart cherry polyphenols suppress nuclear factor kappa-B activation, reducing transcription of pro-inflammatory cytokines including TNF-alpha, IL-1beta, and IL-6. This addresses upstream inflammatory signaling rather than just downstream symptoms.
- **Xanthine oxidase inhibition** — Tart cherry compounds inhibit xanthine oxidase, the enzyme responsible for converting hypoxanthine to xanthine and xanthine to uric acid. This reduces uric acid production and associated oxidative stress, relevant for gout and cardiovascular health.
- **Reactive oxygen species scavenging** — Anthocyanins and proanthocyanidins directly neutralize superoxide, hydroxyl, and peroxyl radicals. Tart cherry also upregulates endogenous antioxidant enzymes (superoxide dismutase, catalase, glutathione peroxidase).
- **Tryptophan metabolism support** — Some evidence suggests tart cherry components influence tryptophan availability and metabolism, supporting serotonin and downstream melatonin synthesis beyond the direct melatonin content.
- **Muscle damage mitigation** — Reduces exercise-induced creatine kinase (CK) and lactate dehydrogenase (LDH) elevations, indicating less sarcolemma disruption and muscle cell damage during recovery from intense exercise.
- **PPARgamma modulation** — Anthocyanins activate peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor gamma, influencing glucose metabolism, fat storage, and inflammatory gene expression.
