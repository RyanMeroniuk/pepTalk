# Tart Cherry Extract
## Benefits

- **Improved sleep duration and quality** — A randomized controlled trial in older adults with insomnia found that tart cherry juice increased sleep time by 84 minutes and improved sleep efficiency. Participants showed elevated urinary melatonin metabolites (6-sulfatoxymelatonin), confirming the bioavailability of cherry-derived melatonin.
- **Reduced muscle soreness (DOMS)** — Multiple studies in marathon runners, cyclists, and resistance-trained athletes demonstrate significant reductions in delayed-onset muscle soreness when tart cherry is consumed before and after intense exercise. Strength recovery is accelerated by 24-48 hours.
- **Decreased inflammatory markers** — CRP, IL-6, and TNF-alpha are consistently reduced in tart cherry supplementation studies. A study in obese adults showed significant CRP reduction after 4 weeks of tart cherry juice.
- **Gout attack prevention** — A study of 633 gout patients found that cherry intake over a 2-day period was associated with a 35% lower risk of gout attacks. Cherry extract intake was associated with a 45% lower risk. Uric acid levels decrease measurably within days of consistent use.
- **Exercise performance recovery** — Reduced isometric strength loss, faster force recovery, and lower oxidative stress markers following eccentric exercise. Benefits are most pronounced for endurance events and high-volume resistance training.
- **Cardiovascular support** — Anthocyanins improve endothelial function, reduce LDL oxidation, and support healthy blood pressure. Montmorency tart cherry juice has been shown to reduce systolic blood pressure by 7mmHg in early hypertension.
- **Reduced post-exercise oxidative stress** — Significantly lower markers of oxidative damage (F2-isoprostanes, protein carbonyls) in athletes supplementing with tart cherry around training sessions.
- **Cognitive support** — Preliminary research in older adults suggests anthocyanin-rich tart cherry improves memory, processing speed, and satisfaction with cognitive performance.
