# Mucuna Pruriens
## Things to Avoid

> Mucuna delivers active L-DOPA. The cautions below are not boilerplate — they reflect that this is a pharmacologically active dopaminergic agent.

- **NEVER combine with MAOIs (most serious caution):** Combining Mucuna's dopaminergic L-DOPA with a monoamine oxidase inhibitor (phenelzine, tranylcypromine, isocarboxazid, selegiline, rasagiline, or the antibiotic linezolid and the herb high-dose St. John's Wort which have MAOI-like activity) can cause a **hypertensive crisis** (dangerous blood-pressure spike) and serotonin/catecholamine excess. This combination can be life-threatening. Do not do it
- **Do NOT combine with prescription Parkinson's medications without a neurologist:** Mucuna IS a levodopa source. Stacking it on top of levodopa/carbidopa, dopamine agonists, COMT inhibitors, or MAO-B inhibitors risks additive dopaminergic toxicity — dyskinesia, hypotension, hallucinations, and psychiatric effects. Any combined use must be managed and monitored by the prescribing neurologist
- **Avoid with antipsychotics / dopamine antagonists:** Antipsychotics (haloperidol, risperidone, olanzapine, etc.) and the anti-nausea drug metoclopramide block dopamine receptors — they directly oppose Mucuna's action, and Mucuna can undermine the treatment. The combination is counterproductive and inappropriate without medical oversight
- **Caution with psychiatric history:** Raising dopamine can worsen or trigger psychosis or mania in susceptible people. Those with schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, or a psychotic history should avoid Mucuna unless a psychiatrist approves
- **Do not use daily indefinitely / avoid chronic high doses:** Chronic dopamine elevation downregulates dopamine receptors, causing tolerance, reduced effect, and a low-dopamine crash between doses. Use intermittently or in cycles, not as an everyday forever supplement
- **Avoid late-day dosing:** It is activating; evening doses can cause insomnia, anxiety, and racing thoughts
- **Avoid stacking with other strong dopaminergics/stimulants carelessly:** Combining Mucuna with stimulants, high-dose tyrosine, or other dopaminergic agents can over-elevate dopamine (anxiety, palpitations, agitation)
- **Be mindful of high-dose vitamin B6:** Without carbidopa, supplemental B6 can increase peripheral L-DOPA breakdown, reducing brain delivery and increasing side effects
- **Do not handle raw plant pods bare-handed:** The pod hairs (cowhage) contain mucunain, causing intense itching/irritation. (Finished supplements are not a contact hazard)
- **Avoid in pregnancy/breastfeeding:** Insufficient safety data plus dopaminergic and prolactin-lowering effects make it inappropriate
- **Disclose to all healthcare providers and before surgery:** Its L-DOPA content and cardiovascular/CNS effects are relevant to anesthesia and to any medication review
