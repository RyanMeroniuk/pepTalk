# Mucuna Pruriens
## Dosing Protocols

> **Dose by L-DOPA content, not by Mucuna weight.** A gram of 15% extract delivers ~150mg L-DOPA; a gram of 40% extract delivers ~400mg. Always know the actual L-DOPA dose. This is a pharmacologically active compound — start low.

- **General mood / motivation / focus (healthy adults):**
  - **L-DOPA dose:** Roughly 100-250mg L-DOPA per dose to start (e.g., ~1 capsule of a standardized extract). Begin at the low end and assess
  - **Timing:** Morning or early afternoon (it is dopaminergic and can be activating; late dosing may disrupt sleep)
  - **Frequency:** Intermittent use (a few times per week, or as-needed) is preferable to daily continuous use to limit tolerance and dopamine downregulation
  - **Note:** Effects come on within ~30-60 minutes and last a few hours given L-DOPA's short half-life

- **Cycling (strongly recommended for healthy users):**
  - Because chronic dopamine elevation can downregulate dopamine receptors and blunt effect (and risk a low-dopamine "crash" between doses), use Mucuna in cycles or intermittently rather than daily indefinitely. A common approach is 2-5 days/week, or short on/off cycles. This is a meaningful difference from gentle daily supplements

- **Libido / male reproductive support (traditional):**
  - **Dose:** Standardized extract providing a modest L-DOPA dose, taken over weeks; small clinical studies used several grams/day of seed powder over 12 weeks
  - **Note:** Sustained use here should still respect tolerance and interaction cautions

- **Parkinson's / levodopa-source use — MEDICAL SUPERVISION ONLY:**
  - **Do NOT self-dose for Parkinson's.** Mucuna has been used in research as a natural levodopa source, but it interacts directly with prescription levodopa/carbidopa and other Parkinson's drugs and can cause additive dopaminergic toxicity (dyskinesia, hypotension, psychiatric effects). Any such use must be dosed and monitored by a neurologist
  - The absence of carbidopa means Mucuna is not interchangeable milligram-for-milligram with prescription levodopa

- **With or without food (a real trade-off):**
  - Taking on an empty stomach maximizes absorption (L-DOPA competes with dietary protein for transport) but increases nausea
  - Taking with a small low-protein snack reduces nausea but may slightly reduce/delay absorption. Separate from high-protein meals (see foods-to-avoid)

- **Onset/duration:** Fast onset (~30-60 min), short duration (a few hours) — unlike the slow, cumulative effects of mushroom or nutrient supplements
- **Start low, go slow, watch for nausea, lightheadedness, racing thoughts, or anxiety** — these signal too high a dose
