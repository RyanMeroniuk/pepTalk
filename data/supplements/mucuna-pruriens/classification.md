# Mucuna Pruriens
## Classification

- **Category:** Botanical Supplement / Dopaminergic Adaptogen (natural L-DOPA source)
- **Subcategory:** Legume-derived nootropic / mood and motivation supplement
- **Botanical name:** Mucuna pruriens
- **Family:** Fabaceae (legume family)
- **Active compounds:** L-DOPA (levodopa) — the signature compound, typically standardized to 15-40% in extracts; also serotonin, 5-HTP, nicotine, bufotenine, beta-carbolines, and antioxidants
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (USA); long history in Ayurvedic medicine (where it is called "kapikacchu" and used for nervous-system and reproductive support); the isolated drug levodopa is a prescription Parkinson's medication, but Mucuna is sold OTC as a botanical
- **Common names:** Velvet bean, cowhage, cowitch, kapikacchu (Ayurveda), atmagupta, Bengal velvet bean
- **Safety note:** Because it contains pharmacologically active L-DOPA, Mucuna carries serious interaction potential with MAOIs, antipsychotics, and Parkinson's (levodopa) medications — see drug-interactions and things-to-avoid
