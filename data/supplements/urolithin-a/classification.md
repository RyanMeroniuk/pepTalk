# Urolithin A
## Classification

- **Category:** Gut-microbiome metabolite / mitophagy inducer / mitochondrial-health and longevity compound
- **Chemical class:** Urolithin (a dibenzo-pyranone metabolite derived from ellagitannins and ellagic acid)
- **Molecular formula:** C13H8O4
- **Also known as:** UA; 3,8-dihydroxy-urolithin; sold as the branded ingredient Mitopure
- **Supplement type:** Mitochondrial-health / muscle endurance and strength / healthy-aging compound
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement; the branded form (Mitopure) received FDA GRAS (Generally Recognized As Safe) status — relatively strong regulatory footing for a longevity-oriented compound
- **Primary systems:** Skeletal muscle (mitochondrial function/endurance), cellular (mitophagy), and broadly mitochondrial health
- **Endogenous status:** NOT produced by the human body directly; it is generated by gut bacteria that metabolize dietary ellagitannins (from pomegranate, walnuts, berries). Critically, **only an estimated ~30-40% of people harbor the gut bacteria needed to produce meaningful urolithin A from food** — the rest are effectively "non-producers"
- **Why supplementation matters:** Because most people cannot reliably produce urolithin A endogenously even with an ellagitannin-rich diet, direct supplementation (rather than eating pomegranate) is the dependable way to achieve consistent blood levels
