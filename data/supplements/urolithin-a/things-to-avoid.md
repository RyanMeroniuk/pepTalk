# Urolithin A
## Things to Avoid

### Relying on Pomegranate Instead of Supplementing
- **Assuming you can get meaningful urolithin A from eating pomegranate or walnuts** — only ~30-40% of people ("producers") carry the gut bacteria to make it, and even they produce variable, generally sub-trial amounts. The non-producer majority gets little to none. Use direct urolithin A (e.g., Mitopure) for reliable dosing.

### Using the Wrong Product
- **Buying pomegranate/ellagitannin extracts expecting a urolithin A effect** — these depend on gut conversion you may not have. For a dependable dose, use direct urolithin A.
- **Buying unverified generic powders** — choose third-party-tested products; the human trial evidence specifically used Mitopure

### Dosing Mistakes
- **Taking it without fat** — reduces absorption of this lipophilic compound; take with a fat-containing meal
- **Expecting acute effects** — muscle/mitochondrial benefits build over ~2-4 months of consistent use, not from a single dose
- **Underdosing** — trial benefits used 500-1000 mg/day; much smaller doses may not replicate the results
- **Megadosing beyond 1000 mg/day** — not well studied and offers no established additional benefit

### Overstating Broader Claims
- **Treating brain/joint/immune benefits as proven** — the strong human evidence is in muscle endurance/strength and mitochondrial function; broader anti-aging claims are largely preclinical. Frame them accordingly.

### Medical Cautions
- **Active cancer** — given effects on mitophagy and mitochondrial metabolism, consult an oncologist before use as a precaution
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding** — avoid supplemental doses (insufficient data); dietary ellagitannin foods are fine
- **Children** — not studied; not recommended

### Realistic Expectations
- **Urolithin A is among the better-evidenced longevity-adjacent supplements thanks to human RCTs in muscle/mitochondrial function** — but it works best alongside training and a mitochondria-supportive lifestyle, and its broader anti-aging benefits remain preliminary
