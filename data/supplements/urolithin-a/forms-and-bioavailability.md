# Urolithin A
## Forms and Bioavailability

### Direct Urolithin A Supplement (Mitopure — Preferred)
- **The branded ingredient Mitopure** is a synthetically produced, highly pure urolithin A — the form used in the published human randomized trials
- **FDA GRAS status** — relatively strong regulatory and safety footing
- **Bypasses the gut-bacteria problem** — delivers urolithin A directly, so it works regardless of whether you carry the bacteria needed to produce it from food
- **Comes as powder or softgels/capsules** — softgels are common for the standardized 500 mg and 1000 mg doses used in trials
- **Verdict:** The reliable, evidence-backed way to achieve consistent urolithin A blood levels

### Generic / Non-Branded Urolithin A
- **Standalone urolithin A powder** is also sold; quality and purity vary
- **Verdict:** Can work, but choose third-party-tested products with verified purity; the trial evidence specifically used Mitopure

### Pomegranate / Ellagitannin Extracts (Unreliable)
- **Pomegranate extract, ellagic acid, or ellagitannin supplements** rely on your gut bacteria to convert them into urolithin A
- **The conversion problem:** Only ~30-40% of people ("producers") harbor the bacteria to make meaningful urolithin A; the rest get little to none
- **Verdict:** Unreliable as a urolithin A source — you cannot know if you are a producer without testing. Direct urolithin A is the dependable route.

### Bioavailability Notes
- **Urolithin A is lipophilic (fat-soluble-leaning)** — absorption improves when taken with a meal containing fat
- **Conjugation** — absorbed urolithin A is partly conjugated (glucuronidated) in the body; the trials achieved meaningful plasma levels of urolithin A and its conjugates with standardized dosing
- **Standardized dosing is why supplements outperform food** — even "producers" generate variable amounts from diet, whereas a 500-1000 mg dose delivers a consistent amount

### Quality & Stability
- **Choose third-party-tested products** (Mitopure for trial-matched dosing) stating urolithin A content per serving
- **Store cool, dry, and sealed**

### Practical Summary
| Form | Reliability | Best For |
|------|:---:|:---:|
| **Mitopure (direct UA)** | Highest; trial-backed | Consistent, evidence-based dosing |
| Generic direct UA | Variable | Budget; verify purity |
| Pomegranate/ellagitannin extract | Unreliable (gut-dependent) | Not recommended as a UA source |
