# Urolithin A
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### MOTS-c + Urolithin A (Mitochondrial Powerhouse)
- **Rationale:** MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-derived peptide that activates AMPK, promotes mitochondrial biogenesis, and acts as an exercise mimetic. Urolithin A clears damaged mitochondria via mitophagy. Together they form a complete mitochondrial-renewal loop — urolithin A removes dysfunctional mitochondria while MOTS-c drives the building of new, healthy ones.
- **Protocol:** Urolithin A 500-1000 mg/day with a fatty meal + MOTS-c at standard dosing
- **Best for:** Mitochondrial health, endurance, and metabolic longevity

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + Urolithin A (Muscle & Recovery)
- **Rationale:** GH secretagogues like CJC-1295/Ipamorelin increase GH and IGF-1, driving muscle protein synthesis and recovery. Urolithin A improves muscle mitochondrial quality and endurance (with human trial support). Together you get enhanced anabolism/recovery (GH peptides) plus improved muscle energy machinery (urolithin A).
- **Protocol:** Urolithin A 500 mg/day + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed + resistance training
- **Best for:** Muscle building, endurance, recovery, healthy-aging performance

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### MK-677 (Ibutamoren) + Urolithin A (Muscle Growth + Mitochondria)
- **Rationale:** MK-677 is an oral GH secretagogue that raises GH/IGF-1 and supports muscle gain; urolithin A improves the mitochondrial quality of that muscle, supporting both size and functional endurance.
- **Protocol:** Urolithin A 500-1000 mg/day + MK-677 10-25 mg before bed
- **Caution:** MK-677 can increase appetite and water retention — pair with disciplined nutrition
- **Best for:** Muscle-building phases with attention to muscle quality

#### IGF-1 LR3 + Urolithin A (Hypertrophy + Muscle Energy)
- **Rationale:** IGF-1 LR3 strongly drives muscle hypertrophy and satellite-cell activity; urolithin A improves the mitochondrial function of the growing muscle, supporting endurance alongside size.
- **Protocol:** Urolithin A 500 mg/day + IGF-1 LR3 per protocol + structured training
- **Best for:** Aggressive hypertrophy phases where muscle quality and endurance also matter

#### BPC-157/TB-500 + Urolithin A (Recovery + Mitochondrial Support)
- **Rationale:** BPC-157 and TB-500 accelerate tissue and muscle repair; urolithin A supports the mitochondrial energy and quality that recovering tissue depends on.
- **Protocol:** Urolithin A 500 mg/day + BPC-157 250-500 mcg 2x/day (and/or TB-500 2.5-5 mg 2x/week)
- **Best for:** Injury recovery, return to training

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### Epitalon + Urolithin A (Longevity Stack)
- **Rationale:** Epitalon supports telomere maintenance and circadian function; urolithin A targets mitochondrial aging via mitophagy. Two complementary hallmarks of aging addressed together.
- **Protocol:** Urolithin A 500-1000 mg/day + Epitalon per standard longevity cycling
- **Best for:** Longevity-focused protocols

### Supplement Stack Partners (Non-Peptide)
- **Urolithin A + CoQ10** — CoQ10 supports the electron transport chain while urolithin A clears damaged mitochondria; a "fuel the survivors, clear the broken" mitochondrial pairing
- **Urolithin A + PQQ** — PQQ promotes mitochondrial biogenesis; urolithin A handles mitophagy — together a clear-and-rebuild mitochondrial combination
- **Urolithin A + NMN** — NMN restores NAD+/NADH (the electron carrier mitochondria need) while urolithin A renews the mitochondrial pool; complementary mitochondrial-longevity mechanisms
