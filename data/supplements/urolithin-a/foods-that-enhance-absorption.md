# Urolithin A
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

### Dietary Fat (Most Important)
- **Urolithin A is lipophilic (fat-soluble-leaning)** — taking it with a meal containing fat improves absorption
- **Practical fat sources:** olive oil, avocado, nuts (walnuts conveniently also supply ellagitannins), eggs, full-fat yogurt, fatty fish
- Taking urolithin A with breakfast or another fat-containing meal is the simplest absorption strategy

### The Gut-Bacteria Point (Why Food Conversion Is Unreliable)
- **For dietary precursors (pomegranate, walnuts, berries), "absorption" depends on having the right gut bacteria** to convert ellagitannins into urolithin A — and most people (non-producers) lack them
- **Direct urolithin A supplementation sidesteps this entirely** — it does not require the conversion bacteria, so a fat-containing meal is the only real "enhancer" needed

### Gut Health (For Precursor Conversion Only)
- **For producers**, a healthy, diverse microbiome supports the conversion of dietary ellagitannins into urolithin A
- **Fermented foods and fiber/prebiotics** support microbiome diversity, which may help producers — but this does not turn a non-producer into a producer
- This is relevant only if relying on food precursors; with direct supplementation it is moot

### Companion Mitochondrial Nutrients (Network Effect)
- **CoQ10, PQQ, and NMN** support mitochondrial energy and biogenesis, complementing urolithin A's mitophagy effect (cleanup plus rebuild)
- A nutrient-dense, antioxidant-rich diet supports the broader mitochondrial environment

### What Doesn't Help
- **Taking urolithin A fasted with no fat** — reduces absorption of a lipophilic compound
- **Relying on pomegranate alone to deliver urolithin A** — unreliable for the non-producer majority; use direct urolithin A with a fatty meal instead
