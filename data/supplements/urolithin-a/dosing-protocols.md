# Urolithin A
## Dosing Protocols

### Standard Daily Dose (Trial-Backed)
- **Dose:** 500-1000 mg/day of urolithin A (Mitopure)
- **500 mg/day** is the most common maintenance dose and was used in human trials showing muscle/mitochondrial benefits
- **1000 mg/day** was also studied and showed favorable mitochondrial gene-expression effects; sometimes used for a stronger effect
- **Timing:** Once daily, ideally with a meal containing some fat to aid absorption
- **Duration:** Continuous daily use — mitochondrial and muscle benefits in trials built over ~2-4 months of consistent dosing

### Muscle Endurance / Strength Protocol
- **Dose:** 500 mg/day (the dose with the clearest endurance data) for at least 2-4 months
- **Pair with resistance/endurance training** — improved mitochondrial quality complements a training stimulus
- **Notes:** Benefits accrue over months; this is not an acute pre-workout effect

### Older Adults / Healthy Aging
- **Dose:** 500-1000 mg/day with food
- **Rationale:** Targets age-related mitochondrial decline in muscle; relevant for maintaining muscle function with age
- **Duration:** Ongoing — age-related benefits require sustained use

### Timing & Absorption
- **Take with a fat-containing meal** — urolithin A is lipophilic and absorbs better with dietary fat
- **No strict time of day** — consistency matters more; morning with breakfast is convenient

### Cycling
- **Continuous daily use is typical** — there is no established need to cycle urolithin A
- The trial protocols used continuous daily dosing over months

### Why Not Just Eat Pomegranate?
- **Most people are "non-producers"** (~60-70%) who cannot convert dietary ellagitannins into meaningful urolithin A
- Even "producers" generate variable, generally small amounts from food
- **Direct urolithin A supplementation** delivers a consistent, trial-relevant dose regardless of gut-bacteria status — this is the practical reason to supplement rather than rely on diet

### Onset of Effects
- **Plasma urolithin A rises within hours** of dosing
- **Functional muscle/mitochondrial benefits build over ~2-4 months** of consistent daily use, per the trial timelines
