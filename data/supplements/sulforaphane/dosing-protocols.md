# Sulforaphane
## Dosing Protocols

Doses are best expressed as actual sulforaphane (or sulforaphane yield), not just glucoraphanin, because conversion varies enormously with formulation.

### General Antioxidant / Nrf2 Support
- **Dose:** ~10-30 mg sulforaphane equivalent per day (or a broccoli-sprout extract delivering this), often standardized products labeled in mg sulforaphane
- **Timing:** With or without food; consistency matters more than timing
- **Duration:** Ongoing; Nrf2-induced enzyme effects accumulate with regular use

### Detoxification Support
- **Dose:** Trials supporting pollutant clearance used concentrated broccoli-sprout beverages delivering substantial glucoraphanin + sulforaphane; supplement equivalents typically aim for ~20-40 mg sulforaphane/day
- **Notes:** Effect depends on actual sulforaphane delivered (active myrosinase or pre-formed sulforaphane)

### Metabolic / Blood Sugar Support
- **Dose:** A notable type 2 diabetes trial used concentrated broccoli-sprout extract providing a high glucoraphanin dose daily; supplement equivalents target the higher end of sulforaphane delivery
- **Notes:** Emerging evidence; coordinate with a physician if managing blood sugar

### Whole-Food Approach (Broccoli Sprouts)
- **Amount:** Roughly 1-2 ounces (a small handful, ~30-60 g) of fresh broccoli sprouts daily provides a meaningful glucoraphanin dose with native myrosinase
- **How:** Eat raw or very lightly steamed; chew thoroughly to release myrosinase
- **Notes:** Sprout glucoraphanin content varies; home-grown 3-4 day sprouts are richest

### Practical Guidance
- **Formulation is everything:** A "high glucoraphanin" product without active myrosinase may deliver little actual sulforaphane. Choose stabilized pre-formed sulforaphane, or glucoraphanin explicitly paired with active myrosinase
- **Restore conversion in cooked food:** If eating cooked cruciferous vegetables, add a myrosinase source (raw mustard seed powder, or some raw sprouts) to enable conversion
- **Start moderate:** Begin at a lower dose to assess GI tolerance (cruciferous compounds can cause gas/bloating), then increase
- **Consistency:** Because the benefit is enzyme induction, regular daily intake sustains the protective effect better than sporadic large doses
