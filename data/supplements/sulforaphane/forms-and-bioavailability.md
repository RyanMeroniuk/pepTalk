# Sulforaphane
## Forms and Bioavailability

Bioavailability is the single most important and most variable aspect of sulforaphane supplementation. The active compound is created only when glucoraphanin meets active myrosinase.

- **Glucoraphanin + active myrosinase (preferred supplement form):** The precursor glucoraphanin combined with the active myrosinase enzyme. When taken together, myrosinase converts glucoraphanin to sulforaphane in the gut, yielding meaningful absorption. This co-delivery is what separates effective products from ineffective ones
- **Glucoraphanin alone (often poorly converted):** Many broccoli extracts standardized to glucoraphanin lack active myrosinase (it is heat-sensitive and often destroyed in processing). Without it, conversion to sulforaphane relies on gut bacteria, which is inefficient and highly variable between individuals — so the labeled "glucoraphanin" content can overstate the actual sulforaphane delivered
- **Stabilized / pre-formed sulforaphane:** Some newer products deliver stabilized sulforaphane directly (e.g., as a complex), bypassing the conversion step. This can give the most reliable, consistent sulforaphane levels
- **Fresh broccoli sprouts (the natural source):** Young broccoli sprouts (3-4 days old) are the richest dietary source of glucoraphanin and contain native myrosinase. Eating raw or lightly steamed sprouts (chewing thoroughly to release myrosinase) is an effective whole-food route
- **The cooking problem:** Heat destroys myrosinase. Cooked broccoli/sprouts lose most conversion capacity. Lightly steaming broccoli (around 60-70 C / brief steaming) preserves more myrosinase than boiling; adding a myrosinase source (e.g., raw mustard seed powder or raw sprouts) to cooked cruciferous food can restore conversion
- **Bioavailability profile:** Sulforaphane (once formed) is reasonably well absorbed, appears in plasma within 1-3 hours, and is conjugated and excreted over ~24 hours. The induced enzyme effects persist longer than the compound itself
- **Practical choice:** Choose a product that either delivers stabilized pre-formed sulforaphane or explicitly pairs glucoraphanin with active myrosinase. Standardize to actual sulforaphane yield, not just glucoraphanin content. Fresh broccoli sprouts are an excellent whole-food alternative
