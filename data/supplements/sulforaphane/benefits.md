# Sulforaphane
## Benefits

### Antioxidant Enzyme & Glutathione Upregulation (Core Mechanism, Well Supported)
- **Activates endogenous antioxidant defenses** — the most potent dietary Nrf2 activator, raising SOD, catalase, glutathione peroxidase, and glutathione synthesis enzymes
- **Sustained protection** — because it induces gene expression, a single dose elevates protective enzymes for hours to days
- **Raises endogenous glutathione** — complementing direct glutathione support (e.g., NAC, ALA)

### Detoxification (Strong Human Mechanistic Evidence)
- **Enhances clearance of environmental toxins** — human trials (notably in highly air-polluted regions) showed increased urinary excretion of airborne pollutants such as benzene and acrolein
- **Induces Phase II detox enzymes** — upregulates glutathione S-transferases and other conjugating enzymes that process xenobiotics and carcinogens

### Metabolic Health (Emerging)
- **Blood sugar support** — a notable trial reported reduced fasting glucose and improved markers in type 2 diabetes with concentrated broccoli sprout extract; evidence is emerging
- **Liver support** — studied for reducing liver enzymes and supporting hepatic function

### Anti-Inflammatory
- **Lowers inflammatory markers** — suppresses NF-kB signaling, reducing pro-inflammatory cytokines; relevant across many chronic conditions

### Neurological & Cognitive (Largely Early/Preclinical)
- **Crosses the blood-brain barrier** — provides Nrf2-mediated neural antioxidant support
- **Studied in autism-spectrum and neuroprotection research** — some small trials are promising, but this remains an early research area

### Cancer-Prevention Research (Preclinical to Early Clinical)
- **Extensively studied for chemoprevention** — strong preclinical evidence via detox-enzyme induction, HDAC inhibition, and effects on cancer cell signaling; human evidence is largely observational/early. Sulforaphane is not a cancer treatment

### Skin & Other
- **Skin photoprotection** — Nrf2 activation supports skin antioxidant defenses against UV damage in some studies
- **Cardiovascular and respiratory** — preliminary work on vascular and airway inflammation

### Honest Framing
- The Nrf2/antioxidant-enzyme and detoxification mechanisms are well supported in humans. Metabolic, neurological, and cancer-prevention claims are emerging or preclinical. Crucially, benefits require bioavailable sulforaphane — products lacking active myrosinase may deliver little. None of this is medical advice.
