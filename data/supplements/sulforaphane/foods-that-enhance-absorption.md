# Sulforaphane
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

For sulforaphane, "enhancing absorption" is really about enabling conversion — ensuring active myrosinase is present to turn glucoraphanin into sulforaphane, and supporting downstream utilization.

- **Myrosinase-containing foods (the key enhancer):** This is the most important factor. If eating cooked broccoli or taking a glucoraphanin-only supplement, add a raw myrosinase source to restore conversion:
  - **Raw mustard seed powder** — a small sprinkle (about 1/4-1/2 tsp) added to cooked broccoli markedly increases sulforaphane formation
  - **Raw broccoli sprouts** — added to a cooked cruciferous dish supply both glucoraphanin and myrosinase
  - **Daikon radish, arugula, watercress, horseradish, wasabi** — raw, myrosinase-rich crucifers
- **Thorough chewing:** Mechanically ruptures plant cells, bringing glucoraphanin and myrosinase together. Chew raw crucifers well
- **Eating raw or lightly steamed crucifers:** Preserves the native myrosinase that cooking destroys. Brief steaming (not boiling) retains far more conversion capacity
- **A healthy gut microbiome:** Certain gut bacteria can convert glucoraphanin to sulforaphane. Supporting gut health (fiber, fermented foods, probiotics) provides a partial backup conversion route, though it is less reliable than active myrosinase
- **Adequate protein / sulfur amino acids:** Sulforaphane upregulates glutathione synthesis (via Nrf2). Adequate dietary cysteine, glycine, and glutamate (from protein) ensures the body can build the glutathione that sulforaphane's enzyme induction calls for
- **Taking with some food:** May reduce the GI discomfort that concentrated cruciferous compounds can cause, improving consistency

### Practical Pairing Strategy
- **Whole-food approach:** Eat raw or lightly steamed broccoli sprouts, chewing thoroughly
- **Cooked-food fix:** Add raw mustard seed powder or a few raw sprouts to cooked broccoli to restore sulforaphane formation
- **Supplement approach:** Choose pre-formed stabilized sulforaphane, or glucoraphanin explicitly paired with active myrosinase — this removes reliance on conversion
- **Glutathione support:** Pair with adequate protein (and optionally NAC/glycine) so the body can act on the enzyme induction
