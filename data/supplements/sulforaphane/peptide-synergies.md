# Sulforaphane
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### Epitalon + Sulforaphane (Longevity & Endogenous Defense)
- **Rationale:** Epitalon is studied for telomere/longevity signaling; sulforaphane activates Nrf2 to durably upregulate the body's own antioxidant and detoxification enzymes. Pairing a longevity-signaling peptide with the most potent dietary inducer of endogenous cellular defenses targets aging through complementary, sustained mechanisms.
- **Protocol:** Sulforaphane ~10-30 mg/day (bioavailable form) + Epitalon per cycle protocol
- **Best for:** Longevity protocols, sustained antioxidant/detox capacity

#### MOTS-c + Sulforaphane (Mitochondrial + Nrf2 Defense)
- **Rationale:** MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-derived exercise-mimetic peptide enhancing metabolic function; sulforaphane's Nrf2 activation boosts mitochondrial antioxidant enzymes (SOD, glutathione peroxidase), protecting mitochondria from the ROS generated during heightened metabolism. Energy/metabolic support plus durable mitochondrial protection.
- **Protocol:** Sulforaphane ~10-30 mg/day + MOTS-c at standard dosing
- **Best for:** Metabolic optimization, mitochondrial health, longevity performance

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### BPC-157 + Sulforaphane (Healing + Detox/Antioxidant)
- **Rationale:** BPC-157 promotes tissue repair, angiogenesis, and gut healing; sulforaphane reduces inflammation (NF-kB suppression) and raises endogenous glutathione, supporting a cleaner healing environment. Repair signaling plus Nrf2-driven antioxidant and anti-inflammatory cover.
- **Protocol:** Sulforaphane ~10-30 mg/day + BPC-157 250-500 mcg 2x/day
- **Best for:** Recovery, gut healing, inflammation reduction

#### KPV + Sulforaphane (Gut Anti-Inflammatory Stack)
- **Rationale:** KPV is an anti-inflammatory tripeptide used for gut healing; sulforaphane suppresses NF-kB and induces protective enzymes in the gut lining. Two anti-inflammatory mechanisms converging on intestinal health.
- **Protocol:** Sulforaphane ~10-30 mg/day + KPV per protocol
- **Best for:** Gut inflammation, inflammatory bowel support

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + Sulforaphane (Recovery + Antioxidant Defense)
- **Rationale:** CJC-1295/Ipamorelin raise GH and IGF-1 for recovery; sulforaphane's Nrf2-driven antioxidant induction supports cellular health under the metabolic demands of intensified training and growth. Consider taking sulforaphane away from workouts if maximizing training adaptation.
- **Protocol:** Sulforaphane ~10-30 mg/day + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Best for:** Recovery, anti-aging performance protocols

#### TB-500 + Sulforaphane
- **Rationale:** TB-500 supports systemic tissue repair and reduces inflammation; sulforaphane adds Nrf2-mediated antioxidant and anti-inflammatory support to the recovery environment.
- **Protocol:** Sulforaphane ~10-30 mg/day + TB-500 2.5-5 mg 2x/week
- **Best for:** Injury recovery, inflammation reduction

#### GHK-Cu + Sulforaphane (Skin Antioxidant Defense)
- **Rationale:** GHK-Cu drives collagen synthesis and skin remodeling; sulforaphane activates skin Nrf2 defenses against UV-induced oxidative damage. Structural repair plus endogenous skin antioxidant induction.
- **Protocol:** Sulforaphane ~10-30 mg/day + GHK-Cu per standard protocol
- **Best for:** Skin photoprotection, anti-aging aesthetics

### Supplement Co-Stacks (Non-Peptide)
- **Sulforaphane + NAC + glycine:** The ideal "induce + supply" glutathione strategy. Sulforaphane upregulates glutathione synthesis enzymes via Nrf2, while NAC supplies cysteine and glycine supplies the other rate-limiting amino acid — together maximizing endogenous glutathione. Reference: pair with **nac** and **glycine**.
- **Sulforaphane + alpha-lipoic-acid:** Both activate Nrf2 and support glutathione; **alpha-lipoic-acid** also recycles other antioxidants. Layered, sustained antioxidant defense.
- **Sulforaphane + turmeric-curcumin:** Curcumin is another Nrf2 activator and NF-kB inhibitor; combined with sulforaphane they provide robust anti-inflammatory and antioxidant-pathway activation. Reference: **turmeric-curcumin**.
