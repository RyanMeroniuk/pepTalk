# Sulforaphane
## Classification

- **Category:** Nrf2 activator / phytochemical antioxidant inducer / detoxification support
- **Supplement class:** Isothiocyanate (organosulfur phytochemical)
- **Also known as:** Sulforaphane, SFN; supplied as broccoli sprout extract, or as glucoraphanin (the precursor) plus myrosinase (the activating enzyme)
- **Molecular formula:** C6H11NOS2
- **Natural source:** Cruciferous vegetables, especially broccoli sprouts; formed when the precursor glucoraphanin is acted upon by the enzyme myrosinase (released by chopping/chewing or supplied in the supplement)
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (OTC); broccoli sprout extracts are widely sold; the active compound is the subject of extensive research, including human trials
- **Primary systems:** Cellular antioxidant defense (Nrf2/ARE pathway), hepatic/Phase II detoxification, anti-inflammatory signaling; studied in metabolic, neurological, and other contexts
- **Key distinction:** Considered the most potent naturally occurring dietary Nrf2 activator — rather than acting as a direct antioxidant itself, it switches on the body's own endogenous antioxidant and detoxification enzyme systems
- **Critical formulation note:** Sulforaphane bioavailability hinges on active myrosinase. Glucoraphanin without active myrosinase converts to sulforaphane poorly, so formulation determines effectiveness
