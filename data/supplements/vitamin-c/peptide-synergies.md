# Vitamin C
## Peptide Synergies

### Healing and Tissue Repair Peptides
- **BPC-157:** Vitamin C is essential for collagen synthesis — the primary structural outcome of BPC-157's tissue repair activity. BPC-157 accelerates wound healing, tendon repair, and gut mucosal regeneration, all of which depend on adequate collagen production. Vitamin C (500-1,000 mg/day) provides the biochemical substrate (hydroxylated proline and lysine) that BPC-157's healing signals require. Particularly powerful for tendon, ligament, and gut healing protocols.
- **TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4):** TB-500 promotes cell migration, angiogenesis, and tissue remodeling. Vitamin C supports these processes by enabling collagen deposition in the remodeling matrix and providing antioxidant protection during the inflammatory phase of healing. The combination accelerates recovery from soft tissue injuries.
- **GHK-Cu:** Both vitamin C and GHK-Cu stimulate collagen synthesis through distinct pathways — vitamin C as a cofactor for prolyl/lysyl hydroxylase and GHK-Cu through copper-dependent enzyme activation and TGF-beta signaling. Together they provide comprehensive collagen support for skin anti-aging, wound healing, and tissue regeneration.

### Immune Peptides
- **Thymosin Alpha-1:** Vitamin C accumulates in immune cells and enhances T-cell function, NK cell activity, and neutrophil killing capacity. Thymosin Alpha-1 promotes T-cell maturation and immune surveillance. The combination provides layered immune support — vitamin C fuels the immune cells that Thymosin Alpha-1 activates. Essential for immunocompromised patients or infection-fighting protocols.
- **LL-37:** Vitamin C supports endogenous LL-37 production (via vitamin D/VDR pathway) and directly enhances neutrophil antimicrobial capacity. Exogenous LL-37 adds direct antimicrobial and anti-biofilm activity. Vitamin C's antioxidant protection also helps preserve LL-37 from oxidative degradation.
- **Thymulin:** Both support immune cell differentiation and function. Vitamin C enhances the lymphocyte response that thymulin's T-cell maturation promotes. Useful for comprehensive immune restoration protocols.
- **KPV:** Vitamin C and KPV both reduce inflammation — vitamin C through antioxidant mechanisms and NF-kB modulation, KPV through alpha-MSH-mediated anti-inflammatory signaling. Synergistic for gut inflammation, skin inflammation, and systemic inflammatory conditions.

### Growth Hormone Peptides
- **CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin:** Vitamin C supports connective tissue repair and collagen synthesis that growth hormone promotes. GH increases IGF-1, which stimulates collagen and cartilage production — processes that require vitamin C as a cofactor. Ensuring adequate vitamin C maximizes the anabolic, tissue-building effects of GH secretagogue protocols.

### Cognitive Peptides
- **Semax:** Vitamin C is concentrated in the brain at 10x plasma levels and supports neurotransmitter synthesis (dopamine, norepinephrine, serotonin). Semax enhances BDNF and cognitive function. Vitamin C provides the neurochemical substrate and antioxidant protection for the enhanced neural activity that Semax promotes.

### Sleep and Recovery Peptides
- **DSIP:** Vitamin C's cortisol-reducing properties (1,000-1,500 mg/day lowers cortisol by 25-30%) complement DSIP's sleep-inducing and cortisol-modulating effects. The combination may improve sleep quality in stress-driven insomnia by addressing both biochemical (cortisol) and neurological (sleep architecture) components.

### Anti-Aging Peptides
- **Epitalon:** Vitamin C's antioxidant protection reduces telomere-shortening oxidative damage. Epitalon activates telomerase to extend telomeres. The combination addresses aging from both protective (C) and restorative (Epitalon) angles. Vitamin C also supports the collagen integrity that declines with age.

### Metabolic Peptides
- **Semaglutide:** Vitamin C supports metabolic health through improved insulin sensitivity and reduced oxidative stress. During weight loss with semaglutide, vitamin C supports skin elasticity (collagen maintenance), immune function, and the metabolic demands of fat catabolism (carnitine synthesis requires vitamin C for fat transport into mitochondria).

### General Protocol Notes
- **Vitamin C is one of the most universally synergistic supplements with peptide protocols.** Its roles in collagen synthesis, immune function, antioxidant defense, and neurotransmitter production support virtually every category of peptide therapy.
- **Minimum recommended during any peptide protocol:** 500-1,000 mg/day in divided doses
- **During healing/repair peptide protocols (BPC-157, TB-500):** Consider 1,000-2,000 mg/day to ensure collagen substrate availability
