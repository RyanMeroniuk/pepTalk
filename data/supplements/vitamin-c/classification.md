# Vitamin C
## Classification

- **Category:** Water-soluble vitamin / essential antioxidant
- **Also known as:** Ascorbic acid, L-ascorbic acid, ascorbate
- **Vitamin group:** Water-soluble (B vitamins and C)
- **Chemical class:** L-ascorbic acid (a six-carbon lactone structurally related to glucose)
- **Essential nutrient:** Yes — humans cannot synthesize vitamin C due to a mutation in the GULO (L-gulonolactone oxidase) gene, making dietary intake essential
- **Endogenous production:** None — unlike most mammals, humans, primates, and guinea pigs lack the enzyme for biosynthesis
- **Primary roles:** Antioxidant defense, collagen synthesis cofactor, immune function, iron absorption, neurotransmitter production
