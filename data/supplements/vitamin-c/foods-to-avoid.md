# Vitamin C
## Foods That Block Absorption

- **Excessive cooking water:** Vitamin C leaches into cooking water when foods are boiled. Steaming, microwaving, or stir-frying retains significantly more vitamin C than boiling. If you boil vegetables, use the cooking water in soups or sauces to recapture the vitamin C.
- **Alcohol:** Chronic alcohol consumption depletes vitamin C stores. Alcohol increases urinary excretion of vitamin C and increases oxidative stress, consuming available ascorbate. Avoid consuming alcohol at the same time as vitamin C supplements for optimal absorption.
- **Excessive sugar/high-glycemic foods:** Glucose and vitamin C compete for the same SVCT (sodium-dependent vitamin C transporter) and GLUT transporters on cell membranes. High blood sugar can reduce cellular uptake of vitamin C. Avoid taking vitamin C supplements with high-sugar meals or sugary drinks.
- **Copper-rich foods (in excess at supplement time):** Very high copper intake may compete with vitamin C at the absorption level and catalyze vitamin C oxidation. This is not a concern at normal dietary copper levels — only relevant in supplemental copper excess.
- **Highly processed foods:** Contain minimal vitamin C and high levels of pro-oxidant compounds (oxidized seed oils, advanced glycation end products) that consume available vitamin C for antioxidant defense, leaving less for physiological functions.
- **Rancid fats / overheated oils:** Lipid peroxidation products (MDA, HNE) generated from overheated or rancid cooking oils create oxidative stress that depletes vitamin C reserves.

### Practical Guidance
- **Vitamin C is water-soluble and generally well-absorbed** regardless of food context. The items above represent factors that reduce efficacy or deplete stores rather than truly "blocking" absorption.
- **The biggest enemies of vitamin C are heat, light, air, and water** — these destroy it in food before you even eat it. Store vitamin C-rich produce properly and eat it fresh.
- **Simply avoid taking vitamin C supplements with sugary beverages** and you will capture most of the absorption benefit.
