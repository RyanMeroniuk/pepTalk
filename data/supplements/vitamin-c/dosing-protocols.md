# Vitamin C
## Dosing Protocols

### General Maintenance
- **RDA (minimum to prevent scurvy):** 90 mg/day for men, 75 mg/day for women
- **Optimal daily dose (most experts):** 500-1,000 mg/day in divided doses
- **Smokers:** Add 35 mg/day to baseline needs (minimum); most practitioners recommend 1,000-2,000 mg/day for smokers due to increased oxidative stress
- **Pregnancy:** 85 mg/day (RDA); many practitioners recommend 500-1,000 mg/day
- **Breastfeeding:** 120 mg/day (RDA); 500-1,000 mg/day commonly recommended

### Therapeutic Dosing
- **Immune support (maintenance):** 500-1,000 mg/day in 2-3 divided doses
- **Acute illness protocol:** 1,000 mg every 1-2 hours at the first sign of illness until bowel tolerance (loose stools), then reduce to maintenance. Most people tolerate 4-10g/day during acute illness (bowel tolerance increases when the body is under stress).
- **Collagen support / skin health:** 500-1,000 mg/day, ideally with 10-15 mg hyaluronic acid and bone broth
- **Iron absorption enhancement:** 100-200 mg taken with iron-containing meals or iron supplements
- **Cardiovascular support:** 500-1,000 mg/day
- **Cortisol/stress management:** 1,000-1,500 mg/day in divided doses
- **Histamine reduction / allergy support:** 1,000-2,000 mg/day in divided doses
- **Uric acid reduction (gout):** 500-1,000 mg/day

### Bowel Tolerance Method
- **Concept:** The amount of vitamin C tolerable before causing loose stools increases when the body is under oxidative stress or fighting infection. This is sometimes used as a guide for acute dosing.
- **Protocol:** Start with 1,000 mg every hour. When loose stools occur, reduce to 75% of that dose. A healthy person may reach bowel tolerance at 4-8g; a sick person may tolerate 20-50g+ orally.
- **Limitation:** This is not a precise dosing method and is not universally accepted. Liposomal vitamin C achieves higher plasma levels without GI effects and is preferred by many practitioners.

### Upper Limits
- **Tolerable Upper Intake Level (UL):** 2,000 mg/day (Institute of Medicine) — based on GI tolerance, not toxicity
- **Practical safe range:** Up to 4,000 mg/day for most healthy adults without issues
- **Maximum single oral dose absorption:** ~200 mg. Doses above this are progressively less absorbed. Always divide daily doses into 2-4 servings.

### Timing and Administration
- **Divide doses:** Take 2-4 times daily rather than all at once. Vitamin C is water-soluble with a half-life of ~2 hours. Divided dosing maintains higher, more stable plasma levels.
- **With or without food:** Standard ascorbic acid is absorbed well with or without food. Buffered forms can be taken on an empty stomach. Taking with meals reduces potential GI irritation.
- **Morning and midday preferred:** Vitamin C may have mild stimulating effects in some individuals. Avoid late evening doses if this occurs.
- **With iron:** Always take vitamin C alongside iron supplements or iron-rich plant foods to maximize non-heme iron absorption.
- **Separate from B12 (debated):** Older data suggested high-dose vitamin C degrades B12. Modern research disputes this, but some practitioners recommend a 2-hour separation as a precaution.
