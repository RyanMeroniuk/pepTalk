# GABA
## Forms and Bioavailability

### PharmaGABA (Fermentation-Derived — Most Researched)
- **Produced by natural fermentation using *Lactobacillus hilgardii*** — the form used in most positive human relaxation and sleep studies
- **The branded research base is on PharmaGABA**, so it is the evidence-backed choice if trying GABA
- **Typical study doses:** 100-300 mg
- Whether it functionally differs from synthetic GABA is not firmly established, but the human data favor it

### Synthetic GABA
- **Chemically identical GABA produced synthetically** — cheaper and widely available
- **Less directly studied for the relaxation/sleep effects** than PharmaGABA, though the molecule is the same
- A reasonable, economical option, but the research support is weaker than for PharmaGABA

### The Bioavailability Question (Central Caveat)
- **The key bioavailability issue is NOT gut absorption — it is whether GABA reaches the brain**
- **GABA is absorbed from the gut**, but as a small, charged, hydrophilic molecule it is widely thought to cross the blood-brain barrier (BBB) poorly
- **This is the defining uncertainty:** oral GABA reaches the bloodstream, but its access to the central nervous system is debated. Observed effects may be mediated peripherally (enteric nervous system, vagus nerve / gut-brain axis) rather than by direct brain entry.
- **Practical implication:** don't assume oral GABA floods your brain with the neurotransmitter; its effects, where present, are likely indirect

### Forms That Bypass the BBB Question (Related Compounds — Not GABA Itself)
- **Some related compounds were designed to cross the BBB** (e.g., phenibut, a phenyl-GABA derivative) — but phenibut is a different, far riskier substance with dependence and withdrawal potential and is NOT the same as a GABA supplement. Do not conflate them.
- **L-theanine and glycine** are alternative calming amino acids that influence the GABAergic/inhibitory system through their own mechanisms and cross the BBB readily — often used instead of or alongside GABA

### Powder vs Capsules
- **Capsules** — convenient and standard for the 100-300 mg doses; mask GABA's slightly sour/savory taste
- **Powder** — economical; GABA is water-soluble
- **Both deliver the same molecule**

### Practical Guidance
- **Choose PharmaGABA** for the best-supported option, typically 100-300 mg
- **Keep expectations modest** given the BBB uncertainty
- **Do not substitute phenibut** for GABA — it is a different, dependence-forming compound
