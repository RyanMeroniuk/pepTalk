# GABA
## Classification

- **Category:** Inhibitory neurotransmitter; non-proteinogenic amino acid
- **Chemical class:** Gamma-amino acid (gamma-aminobutyric acid); a non-protein amino acid
- **Molecular formula:** C4H9NO2
- **Supplement type:** Calm / sleep / stress-relief supplement
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (OTC) in many regions; GRAS status; PharmaGABA (a fermentation-derived form) is a recognized branded ingredient. (Note: regulatory status as a supplement varies by country.)
- **Primary systems:** Central nervous system (primary inhibitory neurotransmitter), enteric nervous system, and peripheral tissues
- **Common forms:** PharmaGABA (naturally fermented via *Lactobacillus hilgardii*) and synthetic GABA
- **Endogenous role:** GABA is the brain's main inhibitory neurotransmitter, synthesized from glutamate by the enzyme glutamate decarboxylase (GAD) with vitamin B6 as a cofactor
- **Key scientific debate:** Whether orally supplemented GABA meaningfully crosses the blood-brain barrier (BBB) is contested — see overview and mechanisms-of-action for an honest treatment
