# GABA
## Dosing Protocols

### Relaxation / Stress (Most Common)
- **Dose:** 100-300 mg (PharmaGABA preferred)
- **Timing:** As needed during stressful periods, or daytime when calm focus is wanted
- **Notes:** The relaxation/alpha-wave studies used doses in this range; effects, if noticed, appear within ~30-60 minutes

### Sleep
- **Dose:** 100-300 mg, ~30-60 minutes before bed
- **Timing:** Pre-bed
- **Notes:** A study using ~300 mg reported reduced sleep latency; best for mild sleep-onset difficulty. Often combined with L-theanine, magnesium, glycine, or 5-HTP in sleep formulas.

### Higher Doses (Used in Some Studies)
- **Dose:** up to 500-750 mg has been used
- **Notes:** Higher doses occasionally produce a noticeable physical "flush" or tingling (see side-effects); more is not clearly better, and the BBB uncertainty applies at any dose

### Timing Considerations
- **Effects are acute, not cumulative** — GABA is used situationally (before stress or sleep), not as a daily maintenance supplement that builds up
- **On an empty stomach or away from large protein meals** — like other amino acids, GABA may be modestly outcompeted for transport when taken with lots of other amino acids; this is a minor consideration
- **Take with vitamin B6 context in mind** — B6 is the cofactor for endogenous GABA synthesis; ensuring adequate B6 supports the body's own GABA production (a complementary strategy)

### Combination Dosing (Common in Practice)
- **GABA + L-theanine** — a popular calm-focus or sleep pairing (see peptide-synergies)
- **GABA + magnesium + glycine** — a layered relaxation/sleep stack
- **Start with the lowest effective dose** and adjust based on subjective response

### Tolerance & Dependence
- **Non-habit-forming** — unlike GABAergic drugs (benzodiazepines), GABA supplements don't cause dependence or withdrawal
- **No need to cycle**, though situational use is the norm given the acute effects

### Honest Note on Response
- **Individual response varies widely** — because of the BBB uncertainty, some people feel a clear calming effect and others notice nothing. Trial at a low dose and judge by your own response rather than expecting a guaranteed effect.
