# GABA
## Side Effects

### Common (Mild, Generally Rare)
- **Generally well tolerated** — GABA supplements have a good safety profile at typical doses (100-300 mg)
- **Tingling / flushing sensation** — some people experience a brief flushing, tingling, or warm sensation (sometimes in the face or extremities), particularly at higher doses (500+ mg). This is harmless and transient.
- **Mild drowsiness** — expected given the calming intent; can be used to advantage at bedtime but be cautious if driving or operating machinery

### Uncommon
- **Shortness of breath sensation** — rarely reported at higher doses; transient and benign
- **Mild GI upset** — occasional, usually minor
- **Headache** — rarely reported

### Important Safety Context
- **Non-habit-forming** — unlike GABAergic drugs (benzodiazepines, alcohol), GABA supplements do not cause dependence, tolerance, or withdrawal. This is a key safety advantage.
- **No significant next-day grogginess** at typical doses, unlike many sleep medications

### Do NOT Confuse With Phenibut
- **Phenibut is a different, far riskier compound** — a phenyl-GABA derivative that crosses the BBB and carries real dependence, tolerance, and dangerous withdrawal potential. It is NOT a GABA supplement. The benign safety profile described here applies to GABA, not phenibut.

### Population-Specific Cautions
- **On sedatives / CNS depressants** — additive drowsiness possible; use caution (see drug-interactions)
- **On blood pressure medication** — GABA may modestly lower blood pressure; monitor
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding** — insufficient safety data; consult a healthcare provider
- **Driving / operating machinery** — due to potential drowsiness, assess your individual response first

### Safety Profile Summary
- **No established toxic dose** at supplemental ranges in healthy people
- **No liver or kidney toxicity** documented at supplemental doses
- **Not a controlled substance** (in contrast to many prescription GABAergic drugs and to phenibut in some jurisdictions)
- **The main "downside" is uncertain efficacy** (the BBB debate), not safety

### When to Seek Guidance
- If you take **sedatives, anti-anxiety medication, or blood pressure medication**, or are **pregnant/breastfeeding**, consult a healthcare provider before use
