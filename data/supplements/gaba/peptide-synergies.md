# GABA
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### Selank + GABA (Anxiolytic Stack)
- **Rationale:** Selank is an anxiolytic peptide that modulates GABAergic and monoamine systems and reduces anxiety without sedation or dependence. GABA contributes inhibitory, calming tone (via the gut-brain axis and peripheral routes, given the BBB caveat). Selank provides the centrally-acting anxiolysis that GABA's uncertain brain access cannot guarantee, while GABA adds peripheral/enteric calming — a complementary calm stack.
- **Protocol:** GABA 100-300 mg (PharmaGABA) + Selank intranasally during anxious or high-stress periods
- **Best for:** Anxiety-dominant presentations, stress relief

#### DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide) + GABA (Sleep Stack)
- **Rationale:** DSIP promotes deep, delta-wave sleep through central sleep-architecture modulation. GABA supports sleep-onset calm (via its inhibitory/gut-brain effects). DSIP provides the reliable central sleep-promoting action while GABA contributes pre-sleep relaxation — addressing both falling asleep and sleep depth.
- **Protocol:** GABA 100-300 mg ~30-60 min before bed + DSIP at bedtime
- **Best for:** Sleep onset and depth, wind-down protocols

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### Semax + GABA (Calm Focus)
- **Rationale:** Semax is a nootropic peptide enhancing focus, BDNF, and cognitive resilience but can feel stimulating. GABA provides a calming counterbalance, smoothing any over-stimulation for a calm-but-focused state.
- **Protocol:** GABA 100-200 mg + Semax intranasally during demanding cognitive work
- **Best for:** Focused work without jitteriness, calm productivity

#### Epitalon + GABA (Circadian / Sleep Support)
- **Rationale:** Epitalon supports pineal melatonin production and circadian rhythm; GABA supports sleep-onset calm. Together they support the wind-down and circadian side of sleep within a longevity-oriented protocol.
- **Protocol:** GABA 100-300 mg before bed + Epitalon in cyclical protocols
- **Best for:** Sleep and circadian support in longevity stacks

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + GABA (Recovery & Deep Sleep)
- **Rationale:** GH secretagogues (CJC-1295, Ipamorelin) are dosed before bed and benefit from deep sleep, when most GH is released. GABA supports pre-sleep calm and sleep onset, helping create the restful state that maximizes nocturnal GH release and recovery.
- **Protocol:** GABA 100-300 mg + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Best for:** Recovery protocols leveraging deep sleep for GH release

### Supplement Synergies

#### GABA + L-Theanine (Calm Stack)
- **Rationale:** L-theanine promotes alpha-wave relaxation and calm focus and crosses the blood-brain barrier readily — compensating for GABA's uncertain central access. The two are frequently combined for relaxation and sleep, with theanine providing reliable central calm and GABA adding inhibitory/enteric tone.
- **Protocol:** GABA 100-300 mg + L-Theanine 100-200 mg for daytime calm or pre-sleep relaxation
- **Best for:** Stress relief, calm focus, sleep onset

#### GABA + Magnesium (Relaxation & Sleep)
- **Rationale:** Magnesium enhances GABA-A receptor activity and blocks excitatory NMDA receptors, directly supporting the inhibitory system GABA targets. Magnesium provides the central GABAergic modulation that crosses the BBB, while GABA adds its own inhibitory tone — a complementary calm/sleep pairing.
- **Protocol:** GABA 100-300 mg + Magnesium 300-400 mg (glycinate) before bed
- **Best for:** Sleep quality, relaxation, stress reduction

#### GABA + Glycine (Inhibitory Sleep Stack)
- **Rationale:** Glycine is itself an inhibitory neurotransmitter (acting at glycine and modulating NMDA receptors) that improves sleep quality and lowers core body temperature for sleep onset; it crosses the BBB. Combined with GABA, the two inhibitory amino acids layer their calming effects for sleep.
- **Protocol:** GABA 100-300 mg + Glycine 3 g before bed
- **Best for:** Sleep onset and quality, evening relaxation
