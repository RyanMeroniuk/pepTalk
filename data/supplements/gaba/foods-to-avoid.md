# GABA
## Foods to Avoid

### There Are No Foods That Block GABA Absorption
- **No common food meaningfully impairs GABA's gut absorption** — it is water-soluble and absorbed normally
- **The limiting factor is brain access (the BBB), not any dietary blocker**

### Alcohol (Important Interaction-Adjacent Concern)
- **Alcohol acts on GABA-A receptors** — the same inhibitory system GABA targets. Combining alcohol with GABA (and especially with other calming supplements) can produce additive sedation and impair coordination and judgment.
- **Chronic alcohol also dysregulates the GABA system** — counterproductive for anyone using GABA for calm or sleep
- **Practical advice:** avoid combining GABA with alcohol, particularly before driving or at bedtime

### Excess Caffeine / Stimulants
- **Caffeine is excitatory and works against GABA's calming intent** — heavy stimulant intake near GABA dosing blunts the desired relaxation effect
- Not a chemical "food to avoid," but a self-defeating pairing if calm or sleep is the goal

### Large Protein Meals (Minor Timing Consideration)
- **Taking GABA with a large protein meal** may modestly increase amino acid competition for transport — a minor consideration; take GABA away from big protein meals if convenient

### Foods That Are Irrelevant (Don't Overthink It)
- **Acidic foods, fiber, and fat** do not block GABA
- **There is no food that destroys GABA** in any meaningful way

### Timing With Other Supplements
- **GABA pairs well with calming agents** — L-theanine, magnesium, glycine (see peptide-synergies)
- **Be cautious stacking with other sedating supplements** (e.g., valerian, high-dose melatonin) due to additive drowsiness — a tolerability rather than absorption issue
- **Avoid combining with phenibut** — a different, dependence-forming GABAergic compound; do not treat them as interchangeable

### Bottom Line
- No food chemically blocks GABA. The real "avoid" list is **alcohol** (additive sedation and GABA-system dysregulation) and **excess stimulants** (which oppose GABA's calming purpose).
