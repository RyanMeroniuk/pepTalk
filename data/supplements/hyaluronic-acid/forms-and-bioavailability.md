# Hyaluronic Acid (oral)
## Forms and Bioavailability

### Sodium Hyaluronate (Most Common Oral Form)
- **The standard supplemental form** — Sodium hyaluronate is the sodium salt of hyaluronic acid, more stable and slightly smaller than free HA, with better solubility.
- **Used in most oral HA trials** — The form behind much of the joint and skin research.
- **Fermentation-derived** — Most modern HA is produced by bacterial fermentation (vegan-friendly), avoiding the rooster-comb-derived sourcing of older products.

### Molecular Weight: The Key Variable
- **High-molecular-weight (HMW) HA** — Too large to be absorbed intact; broken down by gut bacteria into smaller fragments and building blocks before absorption. May have more anti-inflammatory signaling once in tissue.
- **Low-molecular-weight (LMW) HA** — Absorbed more readily in oral form; some research suggests better bioavailability. However, very low-molecular-weight fragments can be pro-inflammatory in certain contexts.
- **Practical note:** Both HMW and LMW oral HA have shown benefits in studies. Molecular weight is an active research area; there is no single agreed-upon "best" weight for oral use.

### Branded/Standardized Forms
- **Mobilee** — A patented HA-rich extract (also containing collagen and polysaccharides) studied for joint health.
- **Injuv / Hyabest** — Standardized low-molecular-weight HA ingredients used in skin and joint studies.
- Choosing a studied branded ingredient gives more confidence in the dose and effect.

### Bioavailability
- **Partial intact absorption + building-block absorption** — Tracer studies show that orally consumed HA is partly absorbed intact (especially LMW) and partly broken down into fragments and sugars that are absorbed and distributed to skin and joint tissue.
- **Tissue distribution** — Labeled HA and its fragments have been detected reaching skin and joint tissue after oral dosing.
- **Gut microbiome involvement** — Gut bacteria participate in breaking down HMW HA; an individual's microbiome may influence how HA is processed.

### Oral vs Injectable vs Topical
- **Oral HA** — Systemic, gentle, supportive effect for joints and skin; the subject of this entry.
- **Intra-articular injection (viscosupplementation)** — A medical procedure delivering HA directly into the joint; stronger localized effect but invasive and temporary.
- **Topical HA** — Cosmetic hydration of the skin surface; does not penetrate deeply.
- **Dermal fillers** — Injectable cosmetic procedure; distinct from oral supplementation.

### Quality Considerations
- Choose products listing the specific HA form, molecular weight (or a studied branded ingredient), and dose
- Third-party testing (NSF, USP) is preferable
- Often formulated with collagen peptides, vitamin C, glucosamine, chondroitin, and MSM in joint/skin blends — verify the HA dose isn't token
