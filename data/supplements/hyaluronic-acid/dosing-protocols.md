# Hyaluronic Acid (oral)
## Dosing Protocols

### Standard Joint-Health Protocol
- **Dose:** 80-200mg/day of oral HA (sodium hyaluronate)
- **Timing:** Once daily, with or without food
- **Duration:** Minimum 8-12 weeks before assessing efficacy; benefits are cumulative
- **Notes:** This is the dose range used in most positive knee osteoarthritis trials. 120-200mg/day is a common effective target.

### Skin Hydration Protocol
- **Dose:** 120-240mg/day of oral HA
- **Duration:** 8-12 weeks for measurable improvements in skin moisture, elasticity, and fine wrinkles
- **Notes:** Frequently combined with collagen peptides (10g) and vitamin C for comprehensive skin support

### Combination Joint-Stack Protocol
- **HA 80-200mg/day + glucosamine sulfate 1,500mg/day + chondroitin sulfate 1,200mg/day + MSM 1.5-3g/day**
- Optionally add **collagen peptides 10g/day**
- **Notes:** The most comprehensive joint approach — HA addresses lubrication, glucosamine/chondroitin address the cartilage matrix, MSM provides sulfur, and collagen provides structural protein

### Low-Molecular-Weight Protocol
- **Dose:** Some LMW HA ingredients are studied at lower doses (e.g., 80mg/day) due to better absorption
- **Notes:** Follow the dose for the specific branded ingredient used in research (e.g., Hyabest, Injuv)

### Timing Considerations
- **Time of day is flexible** — No strong evidence for morning vs evening
- **With or without food** — HA absorption is not significantly food-dependent; take it consistently
- **Consistency matters most** — Daily dosing maintains supply for tissue HA support

### Duration & Expectations
- **8-12 weeks minimum** before judging effect — like other connective-tissue supplements, oral HA is slow-acting
- **Indefinite maintenance** is common for ongoing joint and skin support
- Benefits gradually diminish after discontinuation

### Distinguishing from Injections
- Oral dosing is entirely separate from intra-articular HA injections (a medical viscosupplementation procedure performed by a clinician, typically a series of weekly injections). Do not conflate the two; oral HA is a gentler, systemic, daily supplement.

### Practical Notes
- Choose a product with a clearly stated HA dose and ideally a studied branded ingredient
- If using a combination product, confirm the HA amount falls within the 80-200mg/day effective range and isn't a token inclusion
