# Hyaluronic Acid (oral)
## Foods to Avoid

Oral HA has no specific foods that block its absorption. The "avoid" list focuses on dietary patterns that degrade connective tissue, promote inflammation, or impair the body's own HA production and the skin/joint environment HA supports.

### Pro-Inflammatory Foods (Counteract Joint & Skin Benefits)
- **Refined seed oils** — Soybean, corn, cottonseed, sunflower, and safflower oils are high in omega-6 fatty acids that promote inflammation, working against HA's joint-supporting role
- **Trans fats** — Partially hydrogenated oils directly promote inflammation
- **Ultra-processed foods** — Refined carbs, seed oils, and additives promote systemic inflammation

### Excess Sugar & High-Glycemic Foods (Glycation Damages HA and Collagen)
- **Refined sugar and high-fructose corn syrup** — Excess sugar drives advanced glycation end products (AGEs) that cross-link and degrade collagen and hyaluronic acid in the skin and joints, accelerating skin aging and joint degradation
- **High-glycemic refined carbohydrates** — Spike blood sugar, promote glycation and inflammation
- This is especially relevant for HA's skin-quality benefits — glycation is a primary driver of skin aging

### Excessive Alcohol
- Dehydrates tissues (directly opposing HA's water-binding function in skin and joints), promotes inflammation, and depletes nutrients needed for connective-tissue synthesis

### Pro-Oxidant Patterns
- **Charred/heavily processed foods** — High in AGEs and oxidized compounds that damage connective tissue
- **Rancid/oxidized fats** — Add oxidative burden to skin and joint tissue

### Excessive Sodium (For Skin Hydration Goals)
- Very high sodium intake can promote tissue dehydration and counteract the skin-hydration goal HA supports; moderate intake is fine

### Foods That Are Irrelevant (Don't Overthink)
- **There are no foods that meaningfully block oral HA absorption**
- **Acidic foods, fiber, and fat do not impair HA uptake**
- HA can be taken with or without food

### The Real Priority
- For HA users, the dietary focus is reducing glycation (sugar), inflammation (seed oils, processed food), and dehydration (alcohol) — all of which damage the very tissues HA supports
- Pair an anti-inflammatory, low-glycemic, well-hydrated diet with HA and its stack partners (collagen-peptides, glucosamine-chondroitin, MSM) for best results
