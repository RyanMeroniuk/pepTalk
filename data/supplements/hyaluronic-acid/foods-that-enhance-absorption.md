# Hyaluronic Acid (oral)
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

Oral HA is partly absorbed intact and partly broken into building blocks the body uses to make its own HA. So food pairing serves two goals: supporting the gut processing/absorption of HA and supplying nutrients the body needs to synthesize and utilize HA in skin and joint tissue.

### Vitamin C-Rich Foods (Collagen & Matrix Synthesis)
- Vitamin C is essential for collagen synthesis; HA and collagen work together in the dermal and cartilage matrix
- **Bell peppers, citrus fruits, kiwi, strawberries, broccoli** — pairing with HA supports the connective-tissue matrix in which HA functions
- Particularly relevant when stacking HA with collagen peptides for skin

### Magnesium-Rich Foods (HA Synthesis Cofactor)
- Magnesium is involved in hyaluronic acid synthesis
- **Leafy greens, nuts, seeds, legumes, dark chocolate** — support the body's own HA production

### Zinc-Rich Foods (Skin & Connective Tissue)
- Zinc supports skin integrity and connective-tissue maintenance
- **Oysters, beef, pumpkin seeds, lentils**

### Sulfur-Rich Foods (GAG Matrix Support)
- HA is a glycosaminoglycan; dietary sulfur supports the broader GAG and connective-tissue matrix
- **Garlic, onions, cruciferous vegetables, eggs** — also complement MSM in joint stacks

### Polyphenol & Anti-Inflammatory Foods
- A favorable, low-inflammation tissue environment supports HA's joint and skin functions
- **Berries, turmeric, ginger, green tea, olive oil** — antioxidant and anti-inflammatory support

### Omega-3 Fatty Acids
- Anti-inflammatory fats create a favorable joint environment alongside HA's lubrication role
- **Wild salmon, sardines, mackerel** (EPA/DHA); pairs naturally with omega-3 supplementation in joint stacks

### Bone Broth & Collagen-Rich Foods
- Bone broth supplies additional HA plus collagen, glucosamine, and chondroitin — the same connective-tissue cluster
- Collagen peptides taken alongside HA provide structural protein to complement HA's hydration role

### Adequate Water
- HA's defining function is water binding. Adequate hydration is essential for HA to perform its role in skin moisture and joint lubrication. Drink plenty of water.

### Practical Pairing Strategy
- **Skin focus:** HA + collagen peptides + vitamin C, with a hydrating, vegetable-rich diet
- **Joint focus:** HA + glucosamine-chondroitin + MSM + omega-3, with an anti-inflammatory diet
- HA does not require food for absorption — take it consistently; pairing is about supporting utilization and hydration
