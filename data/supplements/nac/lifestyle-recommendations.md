# NAC (N-Acetylcysteine)
## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Pair with glycine for the GlyNAC effect:** Glutathione requires both cysteine (from NAC) and glycine. Combining NAC with a glycine source (supplemental glycine, collagen, or bone broth) addresses both rate-limiting substrates — the basis of the GlyNAC longevity approach being studied in older adults
- **Support the whole antioxidant network:** NAC raises glutathione, which works in concert with vitamins C and E, selenium, and CoQ10. A nutrient-dense diet rich in colorful produce ensures NAC's glutathione boost is fully utilized rather than working in isolation
- **Minimize alcohol:** Alcohol depletes glutathione and burdens the liver. Reducing intake lets NAC's liver and antioxidant support actually accumulate rather than constantly compensate
- **Prioritize sleep:** Oxidative stress rises with sleep deprivation. Adequate sleep lowers the baseline oxidative burden so NAC's glutathione support goes further
- **Time around exercise thoughtfully:** Regular exercise improves long-term antioxidant capacity. If using NAC mainly as an antioxidant, take it away from workouts so it doesn't blunt the beneficial oxidative signaling that drives training adaptations
- **Support detoxification pathways:** NAC aids hepatic detoxification. Stay well hydrated, eat adequate fiber (25-35 g/day), include cruciferous vegetables (which add Nrf2 activation via sulforaphane), and sweat regularly via exercise or sauna
- **Eat adequate protein:** Glutathione synthesis needs glutamate and glycine in addition to cysteine. A protein-sufficient diet provides these building blocks
- **Don't smoke; reduce toxin exposure:** NAC supports the lungs and detox systems, but it works best when the oxidative and toxic load is also being reduced at the source
- **Be consistent:** Glutathione support compounds with steady daily intake rather than sporadic use
- **Coordinate with medications:** If on nitrates, blood thinners, or blood-pressure medication, discuss NAC with your physician given the relevant interactions
