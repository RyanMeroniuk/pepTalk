# NAC (N-Acetylcysteine)
## Dosing Protocols

### General Antioxidant / Glutathione Support
- **Dose:** 600-1200 mg/day, often as a single 600 mg capsule once or twice daily
- **Timing:** Any time; can be taken with or without food (with food reduces nausea)
- **Duration:** Can be used continuously or in cycles depending on goals

### Liver / Detoxification Support
- **Dose:** 600-1800 mg/day in divided doses
- **Notes:** Studied for NAFLD support at these ranges; clinical acetaminophen-overdose dosing is far higher and is hospital-administered only — never self-administer for an overdose

### Respiratory / Mucolytic Support
- **Dose:** 600 mg 1-3 times daily (1200-1800 mg/day commonly studied in chronic bronchitis/COPD)
- **Notes:** Sustained use is typical for the mucolytic and exacerbation-reduction benefits

### GlyNAC Longevity Combination (with Glycine)
- **NAC:** Commonly studied around 600-1200 mg/day (trials have scaled NAC to body weight)
- **Glycine:** Paired at a substantially higher gram dose (trials used glycine dosed by body weight, often several grams per day)
- **Rationale:** Supplies both rate-limiting glutathione substrates (cysteine from NAC + glycine) simultaneously
- **Notes:** GlyNAC trial doses were weight-based and physician-supervised; the higher experimental doses should not be assumed safe without guidance. See peptide-synergies for the glycine pairing rationale.

### Psychiatric / Behavioral Research Doses
- **Dose:** Trials in OCD-spectrum behaviors and addiction often used 1200-2400 mg/day (sometimes up to 3000 mg/day)
- **Notes:** These are research doses used under supervision; evidence is mixed and this is not established treatment

### PCOS / Fertility Research
- **Dose:** 1200-3000 mg/day used in some trials
- **Notes:** Investigational; coordinate with a physician

### Practical Guidance
- **Start low:** Begin at 600 mg/day to assess GI tolerance, then increase if needed
- **Split higher doses:** Doses above 1200 mg/day are best divided to reduce GI upset
- **Around training:** If using NAC primarily as an antioxidant, consider taking it away from workouts — high antioxidant doses immediately around exercise may blunt some training adaptations
- **Upper range:** Most supplement use stays at or below ~1800 mg/day; higher doses are research/clinical contexts
