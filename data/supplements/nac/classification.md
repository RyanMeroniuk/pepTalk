# NAC (N-Acetylcysteine)
## Classification

- **Category:** Glutathione precursor / antioxidant / mucolytic agent
- **Supplement class:** Acetylated derivative of the amino acid L-cysteine (N-acetyl-L-cysteine)
- **Also known as:** NAC, N-acetylcysteine, N-acetyl-L-cysteine, acetylcysteine
- **Molecular formula:** C5H9NO3S
- **Endogenous status:** Not produced by the body directly; it is the supplemental, stabilized delivery form of cysteine, which the body uses to synthesize glutathione
- **Regulatory status:** Used as an FDA-approved prescription drug (acetylcysteine) for acetaminophen overdose and as a mucolytic; also widely sold as a dietary supplement (its supplement status has been the subject of FDA regulatory debate in the US)
- **Primary systems:** Hepatic (liver glutathione/detoxification), respiratory (mucus thinning), antioxidant defense network, psychiatric (glutamate modulation)
- **Key distinction:** The rate-limiting nutrient for glutathione synthesis is cysteine — NAC delivers cysteine in a stable, well-absorbed form, making it the most practical way to raise intracellular glutathione
