# NAC (N-Acetylcysteine)
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### Glutathione (peptide) + NAC (Direct Glutathione Stack)
- **Rationale:** Glutathione is the tripeptide NAC helps the body synthesize. Supplemental glutathione provides the finished antioxidant directly, while NAC supplies the rate-limiting cysteine substrate for ongoing endogenous production. Together they raise and sustain glutathione from both ends — supply and synthesis.
- **Protocol:** NAC 600-1200 mg/day + glutathione (oral liposomal or per protocol). NAC taken away from hard workouts if fitness adaptation is a goal
- **Best for:** Maximizing total glutathione status, detoxification support, antioxidant defense

#### Epitalon + NAC (Longevity & Oxidative Defense)
- **Rationale:** Epitalon is studied for telomere/longevity effects and circadian regulation. NAC restores glutathione and lowers oxidative stress — a hallmark of aging. Combining a longevity-signaling peptide with foundational antioxidant substrate support targets aging from complementary angles.
- **Protocol:** NAC 600-1200 mg/day (ideally with glycine for GlyNAC) + Epitalon per cycle protocol
- **Best for:** Longevity-focused protocols, oxidative-stress reduction in aging

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### MOTS-c + NAC (Mitochondrial + Redox Support)
- **Rationale:** MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-derived peptide acting as an exercise mimetic and improving metabolic function. NAC supports mitochondrial glutathione, protecting mitochondria from the oxidative byproducts of energy metabolism. Energy production plus redox protection.
- **Protocol:** NAC 600-1200 mg/day (timed away from workouts) + MOTS-c at standard dosing
- **Best for:** Metabolic optimization, mitochondrial health, longevity performance

#### SS-31 (Elamipretide) + NAC (Mitochondrial Antioxidant Pairing)
- **Rationale:** SS-31 targets the inner mitochondrial membrane (cardiolipin) to improve mitochondrial efficiency and reduce ROS at the source. NAC supplies cysteine for mitochondrial glutathione, reinforcing the cell's downstream antioxidant capacity. Two layers of mitochondrial protection.
- **Protocol:** NAC 600-1200 mg/day + SS-31 per protocol
- **Best for:** Mitochondrial dysfunction support, longevity, oxidative-stress-heavy contexts

#### BPC-157 + NAC (Healing + Detox Support)
- **Rationale:** BPC-157 promotes tissue repair, angiogenesis, and gut healing. NAC supports the glutathione needed by regenerating tissues and reduces oxidative load that can impair repair. Healing signal plus antioxidant substrate.
- **Protocol:** NAC 600 mg 1-2x/day + BPC-157 250-500 mcg 2x/day
- **Best for:** Recovery, gut healing, tissue repair

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### TB-500 + NAC
- **Rationale:** TB-500 supports systemic tissue repair and flexibility; NAC reduces oxidative stress in recovering tissue and supports glutathione for cellular repair processes.
- **Protocol:** NAC 600-1200 mg/day + TB-500 2.5-5 mg 2x/week
- **Best for:** Injury recovery, return to training

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + NAC (Recovery Support)
- **Rationale:** CJC-1295/Ipamorelin raise GH and IGF-1 to drive recovery and anabolism; NAC provides antioxidant support so the heightened metabolic and repair activity proceeds with adequate glutathione. Take NAC away from training to preserve workout adaptations.
- **Protocol:** NAC 600 mg/day (morning) + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Best for:** Recovery, anti-aging performance protocols

#### GHK-Cu + NAC (Skin & Tissue Antioxidant Support)
- **Rationale:** GHK-Cu promotes collagen synthesis and skin remodeling; NAC's glutathione support reduces oxidative damage to skin and connective tissue. Note copper balance — NAC's mild metal-binding is minor, but pairing supports tissue with antioxidant cover.
- **Protocol:** NAC 600 mg/day + GHK-Cu per standard protocol
- **Best for:** Skin health, anti-aging aesthetics with antioxidant support

### Supplement Co-Stacks (Non-Peptide)
- **NAC + Glycine = GlyNAC:** The headline pairing. Glycine is the second rate-limiting amino acid for glutathione (NAC supplies cysteine). GlyNAC trials in older adults report restored glutathione, lower oxidative stress, and improved mitochondrial markers — early but compelling. Reference: pair NAC with **glycine** daily.
- **NAC + NMN:** NMN restores NAD+ for mitochondrial/cellular energy; NAC restores glutathione for redox defense. A common longevity foundation pairing **nmn** with NAC.
- **NAC + alpha-lipoic-acid:** Both raise glutathione (ALA upregulates synthesis and recycles oxidized glutathione; NAC supplies cysteine). Complementary antioxidant network support.
- **NAC + selenium + vitamin C:** Selenium powers glutathione peroxidase; vitamin C keeps thiols reduced — a complete glutathione-support trio with NAC.
