# NAC (N-Acetylcysteine)
## Benefits

### Glutathione & Antioxidant Support (Core Benefit)
- **Raises intracellular glutathione** — reliably increases the body's master antioxidant by supplying the rate-limiting cysteine substrate
- **Replenishes depleted stores** — particularly valuable when oxidative demand is high (illness, intense training, toxin exposure, aging)
- **Reduces oxidative stress markers** — lowers lipid peroxidation and other oxidative damage indicators in multiple studies

### Liver Support (Strong Clinical Evidence)
- **Acetaminophen overdose antidote** — the established, FDA-approved hospital use; restores liver glutathione and binds the toxic NAPQI metabolite. This is a medical emergency treatment, not a self-administered protocol
- **Hepatoprotection** — supports glutathione-dependent detoxification; studied for non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD) with reductions in liver enzymes in some trials

### Respiratory Health (Strong Clinical Evidence)
- **Mucolytic** — thins thick mucus by breaking disulfide bonds, improving clearance in chronic bronchitis and COPD
- **COPD exacerbation reduction** — some trials show fewer exacerbations with sustained NAC use, attributed to combined mucolytic and antioxidant effects

### Brain & Mental Health (Emerging — Mixed Evidence)
- **Glutamate modulation** — studied as an adjunct in obsessive-compulsive spectrum behaviors, trichotillomania, and skin-picking, with some positive trials
- **Addiction research** — investigated for reducing cravings (nicotine, cocaine, cannabis) via glutamate normalization; evidence is preliminary and mixed
- **Mood** — researched as an adjunct in bipolar depression; results are inconsistent and it is not an established treatment

### Other Areas of Study
- **Fertility (PCOS):** Some trials report improved ovulation and insulin sensitivity in PCOS, occasionally compared favorably with metformin in small studies
- **Immune function:** By supporting lymphocyte glutathione, NAC may support immune resilience, though clinical benefit varies by context
- **Exercise recovery:** Antioxidant support may aid recovery, but high doses around training may blunt some beneficial exercise adaptations (see things-to-avoid)

### Honest Framing
- NAC's liver and respiratory uses are well-established clinically; its psychiatric, fertility, and longevity uses are areas of active research with promising but not definitive human data. None of this is medical advice.
