# Bacopa Monnieri
## Side Effects

### Common (Mild, Often Dose-Related)
- **GI upset:** The most common side effect — nausea, stomach cramping, increased bowel movements, or diarrhea, especially on an empty stomach or at higher doses. Taking bacopa with food (ideally containing fat) substantially reduces this
- **Dry mouth and increased thirst:** Reported by some users
- **Fatigue or sedation:** Bacopa is calming rather than stimulating; some people feel mildly tired, particularly when starting

### Uncommon
- **Mental fog or dullness:** A minority of users report feeling flat or mentally dulled — sometimes attributed to its serotonergic/calming profile. Lowering the dose may help
- **Reduced libido:** Reported anecdotally by some users
- **Headache:** Occasionally reported

### Specific Considerations
- **Thyroid effects:** Animal studies suggest bacopa may increase thyroid hormone (T4) levels. People with thyroid conditions or on thyroid medication (e.g., levothyroxine) should be cautious and monitor — see drug-interactions
- **Slows gastric motility / cholinergic effects:** Bacopa's cholinergic activity can increase secretions in the GI and respiratory tract. Those with conditions worsened by increased secretions (e.g., asthma, COPD, peptic ulcers, urinary obstruction, bradycardia) should use caution
- **Blood sugar:** May modestly affect blood glucose; monitor if diabetic

### Overall Safety Profile
- **Generally well tolerated** in clinical trials lasting up to 12 weeks, with GI effects being the main complaint
- **Long history of traditional use** in Ayurveda supports a reasonable safety margin at typical doses
- **Long-term human safety data beyond ~12 weeks is limited** — most trials run for 12 weeks, so very long-term effects are less characterized

### When to Be Cautious
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding:** Insufficient safety data — avoid unless directed by a clinician
- **Thyroid disorders:** Monitor due to potential T4-raising effect
- **Slow heart rate (bradycardia), GI/urinary obstruction, asthma:** Cholinergic effects could theoretically worsen these — use caution
- **Before surgery:** Discontinue and disclose use to your surgical team
