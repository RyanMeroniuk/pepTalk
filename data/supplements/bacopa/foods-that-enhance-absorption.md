# Bacopa Monnieri
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

### Dietary Fat (Most Important)
- **Bacosides are fat-soluble** — taking bacopa with a meal or snack containing fat meaningfully improves their absorption
- **Best fat pairings:**
  - **Ghee (clarified butter):** The traditional Ayurvedic vehicle — bacopa is classically prepared as "brahmi ghee." Both masks bitterness and provides fat for absorption
  - **Coconut oil / MCT oil:** Easy to add to a smoothie or coffee alongside bacopa
  - **Avocado, olive oil, nuts:** Whole-food fat sources that support absorption
  - **Whole eggs or fatty fish:** Provide fat plus complementary brain nutrients (choline, DHA)

### Take With a Meal
- Beyond absorption, taking bacopa with food is the single best way to reduce its most common side effect — GI upset. Dose it with breakfast or your largest meal

### Warm Milk (Traditional)
- Brahmi is traditionally taken with warm milk, which supplies fat (if whole milk) and is gentle on the stomach. This is a long-standing Ayurvedic delivery method

### Black Pepper / Piperine (Possible Enhancer)
- Piperine (from black pepper) is a general bioavailability enhancer for many botanicals by inhibiting certain metabolic enzymes. Some bacopa formulas include it. Evidence specific to bacosides is limited, but it is a plausible, low-cost addition

### Complementary Brain Nutrients (Not Absorption, but Synergy)
- Pairing bacopa with a diet rich in **omega-3 DHA** (fatty fish), **choline** (eggs, supports the cholinergic system bacopa also engages), and antioxidants (berries, leafy greens) supports the same memory and neuroprotective pathways bacopa works through

### Practical Pairing
- **Simplest approach:** Take your standardized bacopa extract with breakfast containing some fat (eggs, avocado, or a coffee with MCT/coconut oil). This optimizes absorption and minimizes GI upset
- Consistency at this same daily time builds the habit needed for the 8-12 week protocol
