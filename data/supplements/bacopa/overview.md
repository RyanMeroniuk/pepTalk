# Bacopa Monnieri
## Overview

Bacopa monnieri (brahmi) is a creeping marsh herb used for centuries in Ayurvedic medicine as a "rasayana" — a rejuvenating tonic for memory, learning, and the nervous system. Its primary active compounds, the bacosides, are believed to support memory consolidation, modulate key neurotransmitter systems, and provide antioxidant protection in the brain. What distinguishes bacopa from fast-acting nootropics is its timeline: the well-documented benefits to memory and cognition build gradually over roughly **8-12 weeks of consistent daily use**, not hours or days. Several human randomized controlled trials have shown improvements in memory acquisition and retention, delayed recall, and information processing after 12 weeks of supplementation. This makes bacopa a "slow build" supplement that rewards patience and consistency. It is one of the better-evidenced herbal nootropics and pairs naturally with other cognitive supplements such as lions-mane and the cholinergic alpha-gpc.
