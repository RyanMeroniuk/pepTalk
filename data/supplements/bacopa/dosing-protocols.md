# Bacopa Monnieri
## Dosing Protocols

### Standard Cognitive Dose (Standardized Extract)
- **Dose:** 300 mg/day of extract standardized to ~50% bacosides (delivering ~150 mg bacosides) — the most common dose in clinical memory trials
- **Range:** 300-600 mg/day of standardized extract depending on standardization percentage
- **Timing:** With a meal containing fat (bacosides are fat-soluble); morning or with the largest meal of the day
- **Duration:** Benefits build over **8-12 weeks**. This is not an acute supplement — plan for at least 12 weeks of consistent use before judging effectiveness

### Branded Extract Dosing
- **Bacognize:** Commonly 300-450 mg/day per label and study protocols
- **BacoMind:** Commonly ~300 mg/day in cognitive research
- Follow the standardized dosing on the specific product, as bacoside content differs between brands

### Whole-Herb Powder
- **Dose:** 750-1,500 mg/day (higher than extract because bacoside content is lower and variable)
- **Traditional preparation:** Mixed with ghee or warm milk to provide fat for absorption and mask bitterness

### Timing and Food
- **Take with food, ideally containing fat** — bacosides absorb better with dietary fat
- **GI tolerance:** Bacopa can cause stomach upset on an empty stomach. Taking with food reduces this — a key practical reason to dose with meals

### Splitting Doses
- Higher doses (450-600 mg) can be split (morning + evening) with meals to improve tolerance and maintain steady levels

### Onset Timeline (Critical)
- **Weeks 1-4:** Often little to no perceptible effect. Some people notice mild calming
- **Weeks 4-8:** Subtle improvements in recall and learning may begin
- **Weeks 8-12:** The memory and cognitive benefits documented in trials typically become apparent
- **Do not abandon bacopa after a few days or weeks of "nothing"** — the slow build is inherent to its mechanism

### Cycling
- Many users take bacopa continuously. Some cycle (e.g., 12 weeks on, then a break) as a general practice, but there is no strong evidence that cycling is required. Tolerance is not a recognized issue
