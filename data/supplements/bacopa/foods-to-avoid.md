# Bacopa Monnieri
## Foods to Avoid

### Taking Bacopa on an Empty Stomach
- The most practical "avoid" is dosing bacopa without food. On an empty stomach it frequently causes nausea, cramping, and loose stools. Always pair with a meal (ideally containing fat)

### Excessive Alcohol
- Alcohol is neurotoxic, lowers BDNF, and impairs hippocampal memory consolidation — directly opposing the memory-supporting changes bacopa promotes over its 8-12 week course. Chronic or heavy drinking undermines the cumulative neural benefits bacopa works to build

### High-Sugar, High-Glycemic Diets
- Chronic hyperglycemia and insulin resistance drive neuroinflammation and impair hippocampal function and memory consolidation — working against bacopa's purpose. Refined sugar and high-glycemic refined carbs create the inflammatory conditions bacopa is trying to counter

### Ultra-Processed and Pro-Inflammatory Foods
- Trans fats, excessive omega-6 seed oils, and ultra-processed foods promote systemic and neuroinflammation. Since bacopa's benefits depend partly on reducing neuroinflammation and oxidative stress, a pro-inflammatory diet blunts its net effect

### Goitrogenic Caution (Thyroid)
- Because bacopa may raise thyroid hormone (T4), individuals with thyroid conditions should be mindful of large, sudden changes in goitrogen-heavy diets (very high raw cruciferous intake) alongside bacopa and monitor thyroid status. This is a monitoring point, not a hard restriction for healthy individuals

### Foods That Are Irrelevant (Don't Overthink It)
- There are no common foods that "block" bacoside absorption beyond the need for some dietary fat. Fiber, normal coffee, and acidic foods do not meaningfully impair bacopa
- Note: bacopa's benefits are slow and cumulative — no single food choice will make or break a given day. Diet matters over the full 8-12 week course, not meal-to-meal
