# Bacopa Monnieri
## Classification

- **Category:** Adaptogenic nootropic herb; Ayurvedic medicinal plant
- **Subcategory:** Memory-supporting botanical, neuroprotective adaptogen
- **Botanical name:** Bacopa monnieri (also classified as Bacopa monniera)
- **Family:** Plantaginaceae (formerly Scrophulariaceae)
- **Common names:** Brahmi, water hyssop, herb of grace, thyme-leaved gratiola
- **Active compounds:** Bacosides (bacoside A and bacoside B are the primary actives), bacopasides, bacopasaponins, alkaloids (brahmine, herpestine), flavonoids
- **Standardization marker:** Products are typically standardized to bacoside content (commonly 20-55%); the branded extract **Bacognize** and **BacoMind** are standardized forms used in research
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (USA, OTC); classified as a medicinal herb (rasayana) in traditional Ayurvedic medicine for centuries
- **Primary systems:** Neurological (memory consolidation, learning), antioxidant defense in neural tissue
- **Key characteristic:** Effects build slowly over weeks — bacopa is a cumulative, tonic-style nootropic, not an acute stimulant
