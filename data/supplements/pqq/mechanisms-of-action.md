# PQQ (Pyrroloquinoline Quinone)
## Mechanisms of Action

- **PGC-1alpha activation (mitochondrial biogenesis):** PQQ activates cAMP response element-binding protein (CREB) and PGC-1alpha, the master transcriptional coactivator that drives the expression of genes required to build new mitochondria. This includes nuclear respiratory factors (NRF-1, NRF-2) and mitochondrial transcription factor A (TFAM)
- **AMPK activation:** PQQ activates AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK), the cellular energy sensor. AMPK activation promotes mitochondrial biogenesis, fatty acid oxidation, glucose uptake, and autophagy of damaged mitochondria (mitophagy)
- **Redox cycling antioxidant:** PQQ functions as a catalytic antioxidant, cycling between its oxidized (quinone) and reduced (quinol) forms. A single PQQ molecule can neutralize over 20,000 free radicals through continuous redox cycling — orders of magnitude more efficient than vitamin C or vitamin E
- **Nerve growth factor (NGF) stimulation:** PQQ upregulates NGF synthesis in astrocytes and Schwann cells. NGF is critical for neuronal survival, growth, and differentiation. This underlies PQQ's nootropic and neuroprotective effects
- **Reactive oxygen species (ROS) scavenging:** Directly neutralizes superoxide, hydroxyl radicals, and peroxynitrite. Particularly effective at protecting mitochondrial membranes and mitochondrial DNA from oxidative damage
- **SIRT1 and SIRT3 modulation:** PQQ may activate sirtuins (NAD+-dependent deacetylases) involved in mitochondrial function, stress resistance, and longevity pathways
- **Anti-inflammatory signaling:** Reduces NF-kB activation and downstream pro-inflammatory cytokines (IL-6, TNF-alpha, CRP). This is partly secondary to reduced mitochondrial ROS leakage
- **Mitochondrial membrane protection:** Stabilizes cardiolipin in the inner mitochondrial membrane, preserving electron transport chain efficiency and preventing cytochrome c release (which triggers apoptosis)
- **Epigenetic effects:** PQQ influences DNA methylation patterns and histone modifications related to mitochondrial gene expression, potentially reprogramming cells toward more youthful mitochondrial function
