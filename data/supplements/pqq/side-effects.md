# PQQ (Pyrroloquinoline Quinone)
## Side Effects

- **Generally very well tolerated:** PQQ has an excellent safety profile at recommended doses (10-20mg/day). Side effects are uncommon and typically mild
- **Headache:** The most commonly reported side effect, usually mild and transient. More likely at higher doses (>20mg) or when starting supplementation. Typically resolves within a few days
- **Insomnia / overstimulation:** Some users report difficulty sleeping or feeling "wired" if PQQ is taken too late in the day. Solution: take in the morning only
- **Mild GI discomfort:** Occasional nausea or stomach upset, especially when taken on an empty stomach. Taking with food resolves this in most cases
- **Fatigue (paradoxical, rare):** A small number of users report initial fatigue, possibly as the body upregulates mitochondrial biogenesis. Usually resolves within 1-2 weeks
- **Vivid dreams:** Some users report more vivid or intense dreams, possibly related to changes in sleep architecture
- **Hypoglycemia (theoretical):** PQQ activates AMPK, which can lower blood glucose. Diabetics on medication should monitor blood sugar when starting PQQ
- **No serious adverse events:** Clinical trials up to 60mg/day have not reported serious adverse effects. Animal studies show PQQ is non-mutagenic and non-carcinogenic with a large safety margin
- **Allergic reaction (very rare):** As with any supplement, allergic reactions are theoretically possible. Discontinue if rash, swelling, or difficulty breathing occurs
