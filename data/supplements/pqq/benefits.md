# PQQ (Pyrroloquinoline Quinone)
## Benefits

- **Mitochondrial biogenesis:** The defining benefit. PQQ stimulates the creation of new mitochondria, increasing total mitochondrial mass per cell. This is especially valuable in aging, where mitochondrial number and quality decline
- **Cognitive enhancement:** Improves memory, attention, and processing speed. A 12-week study in older adults showed significant improvement in cognitive function tests at 20mg/day. PQQ enhances cerebral blood flow and neuronal energy metabolism
- **Neuroprotection:** Protects neurons against oxidative stress, glutamate excitotoxicity, and ischemia-reperfusion injury. Promotes nerve growth factor (NGF) synthesis for neuronal maintenance and repair
- **Sleep quality improvement:** Improves sleep duration, sleep onset latency, and subjective sleep quality. Users report feeling more refreshed upon waking. Likely mediated through improved cortisol regulation and reduced oxidative stress
- **Energy and fatigue reduction:** Reduces fatigue and improves vigor scores in clinical trials. The mechanism is fundamental — more mitochondria means more cellular energy capacity
- **Cardiovascular support:** Reduces CRP and oxidized LDL (ox-LDL), both markers of cardiovascular risk. Improves endothelial function and reduces systemic inflammation
- **Immune modulation:** Enhances innate immune function. PQQ-supplemented individuals show improved T-cell and B-cell responses. Reduces inflammatory markers without immune suppression
- **Anti-aging (cellular level):** By increasing mitochondrial number and reducing oxidative damage, PQQ addresses one of the primary hallmarks of aging — mitochondrial dysfunction
- **Stress resilience:** Reduces cortisol response to stress. Improves subjective measures of tension, anxiety, and mental fatigue in clinical studies
- **Skin health:** Antioxidant protection reduces UV-induced skin damage. Improved mitochondrial function supports collagen synthesis in dermal fibroblasts
- **Liver protection:** Protects hepatocytes from oxidative damage. May support liver function in metabolic stress conditions
