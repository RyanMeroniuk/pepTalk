# PQQ (Pyrroloquinoline Quinone)
## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Exercise (the essential partner):** Exercise activates PGC-1alpha through the same AMPK pathway as PQQ. Combining PQQ supplementation with regular exercise amplifies mitochondrial biogenesis beyond what either achieves alone. Include both aerobic (Zone 2 cardio, 150+ min/week) and resistance training (2-3x/week)
- **Prioritize Zone 2 cardio:** Low-intensity, steady-state aerobic exercise (60-70% max heart rate) specifically targets mitochondrial biogenesis in slow-twitch muscle fibers. This directly complements PQQ's mechanism. Walking, easy cycling, or swimming for 30-60 minutes are ideal
- **Optimize sleep:** Mitochondrial biogenesis peaks during deep sleep. Prioritize 7-9 hours of quality sleep. Maintain consistent sleep/wake times, limit blue light exposure 1-2 hours before bed, and keep the bedroom cool (65-68F)
- **Cold exposure:** Cold showers (2-5 minutes) or cold plunges activate PGC-1alpha and AMPK — the same pathways PQQ targets. Cold exposure combined with PQQ may provide additive mitochondrial biogenesis stimulation
- **Intermittent fasting (optional):** Fasting activates AMPK and promotes mitophagy (clearing damaged mitochondria), which complements PQQ's creation of new mitochondria. A 16:8 or 18:6 eating window is commonly used
- **Heat exposure (sauna):** Regular sauna use (4-7x/week, 20 min at 174F per the Finnish cardiovascular studies) activates heat shock proteins and may support mitochondrial quality control
- **Minimize environmental toxin exposure:** Use air filtration, choose organic produce (at minimum the "Dirty Dozen"), filter drinking water, and avoid plastic food containers heated in microwaves
- **Stress management:** Chronic stress impairs mitochondrial function through excess cortisol and catecholamines. Regular meditation, breathwork, or nature immersion reduces the oxidative burden on mitochondria
- **Sunlight exposure:** Morning sunlight supports circadian rhythm, which regulates mitochondrial function and turnover. 10-20 minutes of morning sun exposure without sunglasses helps set the circadian clock
- **Stay mentally active:** Cognitive challenges increase neuronal mitochondrial demand, supporting PQQ's neurotrophin (NGF) production. Reading, learning new skills, puzzles, and strategic games maintain neuronal health
