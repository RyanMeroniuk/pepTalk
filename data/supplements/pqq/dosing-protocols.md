# PQQ (Pyrroloquinoline Quinone)
## Dosing Protocols

- **Standard dose:** 10-20mg/day. This is the dose range used in most clinical trials and is effective for mitochondrial biogenesis, cognitive enhancement, and antioxidant protection
- **Starting dose:** 10mg/day for the first 1-2 weeks to assess tolerance
- **Optimal maintenance:** 20mg/day for most adults seeking mitochondrial and cognitive benefits
- **Upper range:** Some practitioners use up to 40mg/day for specific therapeutic goals, though evidence beyond 20mg/day is limited. Higher doses have not shown proportionally greater benefits
- **Timing:** Morning is preferred. PQQ can increase cellular energy and may interfere with sleep if taken late in the day. Take with breakfast or early in the day
- **With or without food:** PQQ is water-soluble and absorbs well regardless. Taking with food may reduce mild GI sensitivity in sensitive individuals
- **CoQ10 combination dosing:** The optimal pairing is 20mg PQQ + 100-200mg ubiquinol (CoQ10), taken together with a meal containing fat (the fat is for CoQ10 absorption)
- **Cycling (optional):** PQQ does not appear to require cycling. Mitochondrial biogenesis is an ongoing process, and consistent supplementation maintains the stimulus. Some practitioners suggest 5 days on / 2 days off, but this is not evidence-based
- **Onset of effects:** Subjective energy and cognitive improvements may be noticed within 1-2 weeks. Measurable changes in mitochondrial markers and inflammation take 4-8 weeks. Full mitochondrial biogenesis effects likely require 8-12 weeks of consistent use
- **No known toxicity at supplement doses:** PQQ has been tested at doses up to 60mg/day in humans without adverse effects. Animal toxicology studies show a wide safety margin
