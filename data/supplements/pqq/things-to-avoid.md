# PQQ (Pyrroloquinoline Quinone)
## Things to Avoid

- **Taking PQQ without CoQ10:** PQQ creates new mitochondria, but those new mitochondria need CoQ10 to function. Taking PQQ alone is like building new power plants without fuel. Always pair PQQ with CoQ10 (ubiquinol) for maximum benefit
- **Late-day dosing:** PQQ increases cellular energy production and can cause insomnia or overstimulation if taken in the afternoon or evening. Take in the morning
- **Mega-dosing:** PQQ works at very small doses (10-20mg). Higher doses (>40mg) have not shown additional benefits and may cause headaches. More is not better with PQQ
- **Expecting immediate results:** Mitochondrial biogenesis is a slow process. Building new mitochondria takes weeks to months. Allow 4-12 weeks for full effects
- **Sedentary lifestyle:** Exercise is the most powerful natural stimulus for mitochondrial biogenesis (via PGC-1alpha). PQQ supplements this signal but does not replace it. Physical inactivity limits PQQ's potential
- **Chronic sleep deprivation:** Mitochondrial biogenesis and repair are upregulated during sleep. Sleep deprivation impairs these processes and limits the benefit of PQQ supplementation
- **Environmental toxin exposure:** Pesticides (especially rotenone), heavy metals (mercury, lead), and certain chemicals (BPA, phthalates) are mitochondrial toxins. Minimize exposure to support the new mitochondria PQQ helps create
- **Smoking:** Cigarette smoke contains thousands of mitochondrial toxins. Smoking destroys mitochondria and generates massive oxidative stress, overwhelming PQQ's protective capacity
- **Chronic overtraining:** While moderate exercise enhances PQQ's effects, chronic overtraining increases mitochondrial damage beyond the body's repair capacity and may cause a net mitochondrial deficit despite supplementation
- **Ignoring foundational nutrition:** PQQ cannot compensate for severe nutritional deficiencies. Ensure adequate intake of B vitamins, magnesium, iron, and protein — all required for building and fueling new mitochondria
