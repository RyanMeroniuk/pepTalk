# PQQ (Pyrroloquinoline Quinone)
## Drug Interactions

PQQ has relatively few known drug interactions due to its unique mechanism and low dosage. However, caution is warranted in certain situations.

- **Diabetes medications (moderate concern):**
  - **Drugs:** Metformin, insulin, sulfonylureas (glipizide, glyburide), SGLT2 inhibitors
  - **Interaction:** PQQ activates AMPK (the same pathway as metformin) and may lower blood glucose. Additive hypoglycemic effect is possible, especially with metformin where the mechanism overlaps
  - **Recommendation:** Monitor blood glucose more frequently when starting PQQ. The combination with metformin may be beneficial but requires dose awareness
- **Anticoagulants and antiplatelet agents (theoretical):**
  - **Drugs:** Warfarin, aspirin, clopidogrel (Plavix), apixaban (Eliquis), rivaroxaban (Xarelto)
  - **Interaction:** PQQ has antioxidant and mild anti-inflammatory properties that could theoretically affect platelet function. No clinical reports of significant bleeding interactions, but caution is reasonable
  - **Recommendation:** Inform physician of PQQ use if on anticoagulant therapy
- **Chemotherapy agents (theoretical):**
  - **Interaction:** PQQ's potent antioxidant properties could theoretically interfere with chemotherapy agents that rely on oxidative mechanisms to kill cancer cells (e.g., doxorubicin, cisplatin)
  - **Recommendation:** Do not take PQQ during active chemotherapy without oncologist approval
- **Immunosuppressants (theoretical):**
  - **Drugs:** Cyclosporine, tacrolimus, mycophenolate, corticosteroids
  - **Interaction:** PQQ has immune-modulating effects. Theoretical concern that it could interfere with immunosuppressive regimens
  - **Recommendation:** Consult transplant team or rheumatologist before starting PQQ if on immunosuppressants
- **Sedatives and sleep medications (mild):**
  - **Drugs:** Benzodiazepines, zolpidem (Ambien), trazodone
  - **Interaction:** PQQ can improve sleep quality, which may alter the perceived need for sleep medications. Not a direct pharmacological interaction but may affect dosing requirements
  - **Recommendation:** If sleep improves on PQQ, discuss potential dose reduction of sleep medications with physician
- **Levodopa / carbidopa (Parkinson's):**
  - **Interaction:** PQQ's neuroprotective and NGF-promoting effects are theoretically beneficial alongside dopaminergic therapy. No known negative interactions
  - **Recommendation:** PQQ may be a beneficial adjunct; discuss with neurologist
- **Overall interaction profile:** PQQ is considered to have a very low drug interaction risk. Its primary mechanism (PGC-1alpha/AMPK activation) is a signaling pathway rather than a metabolic enzyme pathway, so it does not significantly affect CYP450 drug metabolism
