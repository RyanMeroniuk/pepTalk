# PQQ (Pyrroloquinoline Quinone)
## Foods to Avoid

- **Excessive alcohol:** Alcohol is directly toxic to mitochondria, causing mitochondrial DNA damage, membrane disruption, and increased ROS production. It directly counteracts PQQ's mitochondrial biogenesis benefits. Chronic alcohol use destroys mitochondria faster than PQQ can create them
- **Highly processed foods and refined sugars:** Excess sugar drives mitochondrial dysfunction through glycation, oxidative stress, and insulin resistance. This undermines PQQ's biogenesis signaling
- **Trans fats and hydrogenated oils:** Damage mitochondrial membranes and increase lipid peroxidation. New mitochondria built under PQQ's influence need healthy membranes to function properly
- **Excessive fructose (from added sugars, not whole fruit):** High-fructose corn syrup and excess added fructose promote hepatic mitochondrial dysfunction and fatty liver, counteracting PQQ's benefits
- **Excessive iron-rich foods taken simultaneously:** While adequate iron supports mitochondria, excess free iron catalyzes Fenton reactions producing hydroxyl radicals. Avoid taking high-dose iron supplements at the same time as PQQ. Space them apart
- **Charred/burnt meats:** Polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) and heterocyclic amines (HCAs) from charred meat are mitochondrial toxins that increase oxidative burden
- **Excessive caffeine (timing concern):** While moderate caffeine is fine and may even support mitochondrial function, excessive caffeine late in the day can compound PQQ's energizing effects and disrupt sleep. Sleep is when mitochondrial repair and biogenesis are most active
