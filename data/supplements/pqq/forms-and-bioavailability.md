# PQQ (Pyrroloquinoline Quinone)
## Forms and Bioavailability

- **PQQ disodium salt (standard):** The most common supplemental form. PQQ is bound to two sodium ions for stability. This is the form used in most clinical trials and is well-absorbed orally
- **BioPQQ (Mitsubishi Gas Chemical):** The primary branded, patented form of PQQ disodium salt produced through bacterial fermentation. Most quality supplements use BioPQQ as the raw material. Verified purity and potency
- **Free-form PQQ:** Less stable than the disodium salt. Not commonly used in supplements due to stability concerns
- **Oral bioavailability:** PQQ is water-soluble and reasonably well absorbed through the gastrointestinal tract. Estimated oral bioavailability is approximately 60-80% — much higher than fat-soluble compounds like CoQ10
- **No fat requirement:** Unlike CoQ10, PQQ does not require dietary fat for absorption. It can be taken with or without food, though taking with food may reduce potential GI sensitivity
- **Capsules vs lozenges:** Standard capsules are the most common delivery. Some brands offer sublingual lozenges, but there is limited evidence that sublingual delivery offers significant advantage for PQQ given its already good oral bioavailability
- **Combination products:** Many supplements combine PQQ with CoQ10 (ubiquinol) in a single product. This is a rational combination — PQQ for biogenesis, CoQ10 for function. Look for products containing 10-20mg PQQ plus 100-200mg ubiquinol
- **Stability:** PQQ disodium salt is relatively stable at room temperature. Protect from excessive heat and moisture. Does not require refrigeration
- **Dose matters more than form:** Because PQQ is water-soluble and well-absorbed, the form matters less than with fat-soluble supplements. Focus on verified dosage (10-20mg) and reputable sourcing (BioPQQ)
