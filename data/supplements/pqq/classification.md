# PQQ (Pyrroloquinoline Quinone)
## Classification

- **Category:** Mitochondrial biogenesis activator / antioxidant / neuroprotectant
- **Supplement class:** Redox cofactor (quinone derivative)
- **Also known as:** Pyrroloquinoline quinone, methoxatin, BioPQQ
- **Endogenous status:** Not synthesized by the human body; obtained exclusively through diet. Classified as a conditionally essential micronutrient
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (GRAS status granted by FDA in 2009); sold OTC worldwide
- **Primary function:** Stimulates mitochondrial biogenesis (creation of NEW mitochondria) via PGC-1alpha signaling; powerful redox cycling antioxidant
- **Solubility:** Water-soluble
- **Key distinction from CoQ10:** CoQ10 supports existing mitochondria; PQQ creates new ones. They are complementary, not redundant
