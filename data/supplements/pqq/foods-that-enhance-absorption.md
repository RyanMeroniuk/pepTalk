# PQQ (Pyrroloquinoline Quinone)
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

PQQ is water-soluble and absorbs well without special dietary considerations. However, certain foods and nutrients enhance its effectiveness.

- **CoQ10-rich foods (functional synergy):** Since PQQ creates new mitochondria and CoQ10 fuels them, eating CoQ10-rich foods alongside PQQ supplementation maximizes the benefit. Sardines, organ meats, and peanuts provide dietary CoQ10
- **Antioxidant-rich foods (supportive):**
  - **Green tea** — contains PQQ naturally plus catechins that complement PQQ's antioxidant activity
  - **Berries (blueberries, blackberries, strawberries)** — anthocyanins support mitochondrial function and complement PQQ's oxidative stress protection
  - **Dark chocolate / cacao** — flavanols enhance mitochondrial biogenesis pathways and amplify PQQ's PGC-1alpha activation
- **B-vitamin-rich foods:** B vitamins (especially B2/riboflavin and B3/niacin) are essential cofactors in the electron transport chain. New mitochondria created by PQQ need B vitamins to function. Include eggs, leafy greens, legumes, and whole grains
- **Magnesium-rich foods:** Magnesium is required for ATP production. New mitochondria need Mg to make ATP. Dark leafy greens, nuts, seeds, and dark chocolate provide magnesium
- **Iron-containing foods (moderate):** Iron is a cofactor in several mitochondrial enzymes. Adequate iron supports the function of new mitochondria. Red meat, spinach, lentils, and pumpkin seeds are good sources. Avoid excess iron, which increases oxidative stress
- **Omega-3 rich foods:** Fatty fish (salmon, mackerel, sardines), walnuts, and flaxseeds. Omega-3s support mitochondrial membrane composition, helping new mitochondria function optimally
- **Fermented foods:** Natto, kimchi, sauerkraut, and miso contain trace PQQ and support gut health, which may improve overall nutrient absorption
