# Probiotics
## Benefits

**Gut Health:**

- **Antibiotic-associated diarrhea prevention** — Saccharomyces boulardii and Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG reduce AAD incidence by 42-52% in meta-analyses. S. boulardii is particularly effective because, as a yeast, it is unaffected by antibacterial antibiotics.
- **C. difficile infection prevention** — S. boulardii reduces C. difficile recurrence by approximately 50%. Lactobacillus strains also show benefit in prevention, though less robustly.
- **IBS symptom relief** — Bifidobacterium infantis 35624 (Alflorex/Align) significantly reduces bloating, abdominal pain, and bowel irregularity in multiple RCTs. Lactobacillus plantarum 299v reduces IBS pain and bloating.
- **Inflammatory bowel disease support** — VSL#3 (multi-strain high-dose formulation) maintains remission in ulcerative colitis. E. coli Nissle 1917 shows efficacy comparable to mesalazine for UC maintenance.
- **H. pylori eradication support** — Lactobacillus reuteri and S. boulardii improve H. pylori eradication rates when added to standard triple therapy and reduce treatment side effects.

**Immune Function:**

- **Reduced respiratory infections** — Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG and L. paracasei reduce the duration and severity of upper respiratory infections by 30-40% in meta-analyses.
- **Enhanced vaccine response** — Specific strains improve antibody responses to influenza, oral polio, and other vaccines.
- **Allergy and eczema reduction** — L. rhamnosus GG given to pregnant mothers and infants reduces eczema incidence by approximately 50% in at-risk children.

**Mental Health (Psychobiotics):**

- **Anxiety reduction** — Lactobacillus helveticus R0052 + Bifidobacterium longum R0175 (Cerebiome/Probio'Stick) significantly reduce cortisol and self-reported anxiety in RCTs.
- **Depression improvement** — The same L. helveticus + B. longum combination improves depression scores. Bifidobacterium longum 1714 reduces stress and improves cognitive performance.
- **Stress resilience** — L. plantarum PS128 reduces neuroinflammation markers and improves stress resilience in clinical studies.

**Metabolic Health:**

- **Cholesterol reduction** — Lactobacillus reuteri NCIMB 30242 reduces LDL cholesterol by 11.6% in a randomized trial.
- **Blood sugar management** — Multi-strain probiotics improve fasting glucose and HbA1c in type 2 diabetes meta-analyses.
- **Weight management support** — Lactobacillus gasseri SBT2055 reduces visceral fat by 4.6% in an RCT. Effects are modest but significant.

**Women's Health:**

- **Vaginal health** — Lactobacillus crispatus and L. rhamnosus GR-1 + L. reuteri RC-14 reduce bacterial vaginosis recurrence and support vaginal microbiome health.
- **Urinary tract health** — L. rhamnosus GR-1 + L. reuteri RC-14 reduce UTI recurrence in women.
