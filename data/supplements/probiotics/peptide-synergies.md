# Probiotics
## Peptide Synergies

- **BPC-157** — BPC-157 is one of the most synergistic peptides with probiotics. BPC-157 accelerates gut mucosal healing, promotes angiogenesis in damaged tissue, and protects against NSAID-induced gut damage. Combined with probiotics that reinforce the intestinal barrier (L. rhamnosus GG, S. boulardii), this pairing addresses gut repair from both structural (BPC-157) and microbial (probiotics) angles. Particularly valuable for leaky gut, IBD, NSAID damage recovery, and post-antibiotic gut restoration.
- **KPV** — KPV is an anti-inflammatory tripeptide derived from alpha-MSH that specifically targets gut inflammation. It suppresses NF-kB in intestinal epithelial cells and reduces inflammatory cytokines. Paired with anti-inflammatory probiotic strains (B. infantis 35624, L. plantarum 299v), this combination addresses gut inflammation from both peptide signaling and microbial modulation pathways. Excellent for IBS, IBD, and general gut inflammation.
- **Larazotide** — Larazotide is a tight junction regulator that reduces intestinal permeability by modulating zonulin signaling. Probiotics that also reinforce tight junctions (L. rhamnosus GG, L. plantarum) provide complementary barrier support. This combination is particularly relevant for celiac disease, leaky gut, and conditions where intestinal permeability is a primary concern.
- **VIP (Vasoactive Intestinal Peptide)** — VIP supports gut motility, mucosal immune function, and has potent anti-inflammatory effects in the GI tract. It is also used in CIRS/mold illness protocols. Probiotics complement VIP by populating the gut with beneficial organisms that support the mucosal environment VIP is optimizing.
- **Thymosin Alpha-1** — Thymosin Alpha-1 enhances T-cell function and overall immune competence. Since 70% of the immune system is gut-associated, probiotics that modulate GALT immune function (L. rhamnosus GG, B. infantis) complement systemic immune enhancement from Thymosin Alpha-1.
- **LL-37** — LL-37 is an antimicrobial peptide that targets pathogenic bacteria and disrupts biofilms. Probiotics provide beneficial competitive exclusion alongside LL-37's antimicrobial activity. S. boulardii is particularly relevant as it neutralizes bacterial toxins while LL-37 addresses the pathogens directly.
- **Selank** — Selank modulates GABA, reduces anxiety, and supports immune function. Combined with psychobiotic strains (L. helveticus R0052 + B. longum R0175), this pairing addresses the gut-brain axis from both the neural (Selank) and microbial (psychobiotics) sides. Relevant for anxiety and stress-related gut dysfunction.
- **Semax** — Semax upregulates BDNF and enhances cognitive function. Psychobiotic probiotics also influence BDNF production and cognitive performance via the gut-brain axis. The combination may provide enhanced nootropic benefits through complementary pathways.
