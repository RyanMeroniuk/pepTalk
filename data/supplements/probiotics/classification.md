# Probiotics
## Classification

- **Category:** Gut Health / Immune Support / Microbiome Modulation
- **Type:** Live microorganisms (bacteria and yeast strains)
- **Definition:** Live microorganisms that, when administered in adequate amounts, confer a health benefit on the host (WHO/FAO definition)
- **Major genera:** Lactobacillus, Bifidobacterium, Bacillus (spore-forming), Saccharomyces (yeast)
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (USA); some strains have drug status in specific countries for specific indications
- **Critical principle:** Probiotics are STRAIN-SPECIFIC — benefits documented for one strain do NOT automatically apply to another strain within the same species. Always look for the full strain designation (genus + species + strain, e.g., Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG).
