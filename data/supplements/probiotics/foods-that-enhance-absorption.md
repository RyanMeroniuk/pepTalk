# Probiotics
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

- **Prebiotic fibers (critical pairing)** — Prebiotics are non-digestible fibers that selectively feed beneficial gut bacteria, including supplemented probiotics. Think of probiotics as planting seeds and prebiotics as fertilizer:
  - **Inulin and FOS (fructo-oligosaccharides)** — Found in chicory root, Jerusalem artichokes, garlic, onions, leeks, asparagus, and bananas (especially slightly green). Feed Bifidobacterium and Lactobacillus species specifically.
  - **GOS (galacto-oligosaccharides)** — Found in legumes (lentils, chickpeas, beans). Strong bifidogenic effect.
  - **Resistant starch** — Found in cooked-then-cooled potatoes, rice, green bananas, and plantains. Feeds butyrate-producing bacteria in the colon.
  - **Pectin** — Found in apples, citrus peel, berries. Feeds beneficial Bacteroidetes and Bifidobacterium species.
  - **Beta-glucans** — Found in oats, barley, and mushrooms. Prebiotic and immunomodulatory.
- **Polyphenol-rich foods** — Polyphenols from berries, green tea, dark chocolate, and red wine are metabolized by gut bacteria into bioactive compounds. They also selectively promote beneficial bacteria growth:
  - **Blueberries and other berries** — Anthocyanins and ellagic acid support Bifidobacterium growth
  - **Green tea** — Catechins promote Lactobacillus and inhibit pathogenic bacteria
  - **Dark chocolate (70%+ cacao)** — Cocoa polyphenols increase Bifidobacterium and Lactobacillus populations
- **Fermented foods alongside supplements** — Consuming fermented foods (kefir, kimchi, sauerkraut) alongside probiotic supplements introduces additional diverse microbial species and creates a more hospitable gut environment for the supplemented strains.
- **Bone broth or collagen** — Provides glutamine and glycine that support intestinal epithelial integrity, creating a healthier mucosal surface for probiotic colonization.
- **Omega-3 fatty acids** — Fish oil and fatty fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel) reduce gut inflammation and support a microbiome composition that favors beneficial bacteria.
