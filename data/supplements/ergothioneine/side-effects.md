# Ergothioneine
## Side Effects

### Common (Very Well Tolerated)
- **Excellent tolerability** — ergothioneine has a strong safety profile in studies to date; most users report no side effects at dietary or typical supplemental doses
- **No significant adverse effects** reported in human safety evaluations at doses well above typical dietary intake

### Uncommon
- **Mild GI effects** — occasional, mild digestive discomfort; uncommon
- **Headache** — rarely reported

### Theoretical / Context-Dependent
- **Long-term high-dose data limited:** Because ergothioneine is retained in tissues for a long time, the long-term effects of sustained high-dose supplementation in humans are not yet fully characterized. Typical doses appear safe, but very high chronic intake is less studied
- **Antioxidant timing around training:** As with other antioxidants, a theoretical concern exists that very high doses immediately around exercise could blunt some adaptive oxidative signaling; this has not been demonstrated for ergothioneine and is likely minor given its physiological, transporter-regulated nature

### Rare
- **Allergic reaction** — rare; those with mushroom allergies should be mindful if using mushroom-derived products

### Safety Profile Summary
- **Strong safety record** — long dietary history (humans have always consumed it via food), GRAS/novel-food approvals for synthetic L-ergothioneine, and clean safety studies at supplemental doses
- **Self-limiting uptake:** Transporter-mediated, saturable absorption provides a degree of natural regulation
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding** — ergothioneine is naturally present in the body and crosses to the fetus (it concentrates in fetal tissues), and dietary intake is normal; however, concentrated supplemental safety data in pregnancy are limited, so discuss supplements with a physician
- **Mushroom allergy:** Choose purified synthetic L-ergothioneine if allergic to mushrooms
- **None of this is medical advice** — consult a healthcare provider if pregnant, breastfeeding, or managing a health condition
