# Ergothioneine
## Dosing Protocols

Human dosing is still being established; the ranges below reflect amounts used in research and commercial supplements. Because ergothioneine accumulates in tissues, consistency matters more than precise timing.

### General Antioxidant / Longevity Support
- **Dose:** ~5-15 mg/day is common in commercial supplements and reflects amounts achievable through a mushroom-rich diet plus supplementation
- **Timing:** Any time, with or without food (transporter-mediated uptake)
- **Duration:** Ongoing; tissue levels build over weeks of consistent intake

### Higher Research Doses
- **Dose:** Some studies and products use higher amounts (e.g., 25-30 mg/day); safety studies have evaluated doses substantially higher than typical dietary intake
- **Notes:** The 2025 RCT and other human work used defined daily doses; higher doses have been tolerated in safety evaluations, but optimal dosing for specific outcomes is not yet established

### Sleep / Cognitive Support (Per Emerging RCT Evidence)
- **Dose:** Within the supplemental range used in human trials (commonly single-digit to ~25 mg/day depending on the product/study)
- **Notes:** Based on early, single-trial RCT evidence; allow several weeks of consistent use given tissue accumulation

### Whole-Food Approach (Mushrooms)
- **Amount:** A regular serving of mushrooms (especially higher-ergothioneine species like oyster, shiitake, maitake, king trumpet) provides a meaningful dietary dose
- **Notes:** Ergothioneine is heat-stable, so cooked mushrooms retain it well; dietary intake varies widely between mushroom species

### Practical Guidance
- **Consistency over timing:** Tissue accumulation via the OCTN1 transporter means daily intake builds a reserve; you do not need to dose at a particular time
- **Combine diet and supplement:** A mushroom-rich diet plus a modest supplement reliably raises blood and tissue ergothioneine
- **Be patient:** Because it accumulates and is retained, benefits (and rising blood levels) develop over weeks of steady intake
- **Start at typical doses:** Begin in the ~5-15 mg/day range; higher doses can be considered but optimal targets are still being researched
