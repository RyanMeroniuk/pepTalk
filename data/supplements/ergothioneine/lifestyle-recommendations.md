# Ergothioneine
## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Eat mushrooms regularly (the foundation):** The simplest, most reliable way to raise ergothioneine is a mushroom-rich diet. Favor higher-content species — oyster, king trumpet, shiitake, maitake — several times per week. Cooking is fine, since ergothioneine is heat-stable
- **Be consistent over weeks:** Ergothioneine accumulates in tissues via its dedicated transporter and is retained long-term. Steady daily intake (food and/or supplement) builds a protective reserve; sporadic use does not
- **Support the broader antioxidant network:** Ergothioneine is one player in a team. Pair it with a colorful, antioxidant-rich diet and, if desired, complementary supplements — NAC and glycine for glutathione, CoQ10 for mitochondria — so cellular defenses are robust across compartments
- **Pair with mitochondrial support for longevity goals:** Ergothioneine concentrates in and protects mitochondria. Combining it with mitochondrial-supportive nutrients (CoQ10) and NAD+ precursors (NMN) fits a longevity-oriented approach
- **Prioritize sleep:** Oxidative stress rises with poor sleep, and the 2025 RCT specifically noted sleep improvements with ergothioneine. Good sleep hygiene complements its effects and lowers the baseline oxidative burden it must counter
- **Protect high-stress tissues:** Ergothioneine targets the eyes, skin, liver, blood cells, and brain. Support these with UV protection (sunglasses, sunscreen), limited alcohol (liver), and cognitive engagement (brain)
- **Minimize alcohol and pro-oxidant foods:** These deplete the antioxidant systems ergothioneine supports, especially in the liver
- **Stay active:** Regular exercise improves long-term oxidative resilience and overall cellular health, complementing ergothioneine's protective role
- **Choose quality sources:** Use purified L-ergothioneine from a reputable, third-party-tested brand, or build intake through specialty mushrooms — or both
