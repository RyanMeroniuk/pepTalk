# Ergothioneine
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### Epitalon + Ergothioneine (Longevity & Cellular Protection)
- **Rationale:** Epitalon is studied for telomere/longevity signaling and circadian regulation; ergothioneine is a candidate "longevity vitamin" that concentrates in high-stress tissues and protects them from oxidative damage over the long term. Pairing a longevity-signaling peptide with a stable, tissue-targeted antioxidant reserve addresses cellular aging from complementary directions — and both touch on sleep/circadian themes (the 2025 RCT noted sleep benefits with ergothioneine).
- **Protocol:** Ergothioneine ~5-15 mg/day + Epitalon per cycle protocol
- **Best for:** Longevity protocols, cellular stress resilience, sleep/circadian support

#### MOTS-c + Ergothioneine (Mitochondrial Longevity Stack)
- **Rationale:** MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-derived exercise-mimetic peptide that improves metabolic function; ergothioneine accumulates in mitochondria and protects mitochondrial DNA, lipids, and proteins from oxidative damage. Mitochondrial function support plus targeted mitochondrial antioxidant protection.
- **Protocol:** Ergothioneine ~5-15 mg/day + MOTS-c at standard dosing
- **Best for:** Mitochondrial health, metabolic optimization, longevity performance

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### SS-31 (Elamipretide) + Ergothioneine (Layered Mitochondrial Defense)
- **Rationale:** SS-31 targets cardiolipin in the inner mitochondrial membrane to improve efficiency and reduce ROS at the source; ergothioneine provides a stable, retained antioxidant reserve within mitochondria. Two complementary mitochondrial-protection strategies.
- **Protocol:** Ergothioneine ~5-15 mg/day + SS-31 per protocol
- **Best for:** Mitochondrial dysfunction support, longevity, oxidative-stress-heavy contexts

#### BPC-157 + Ergothioneine (Recovery + Tissue Protection)
- **Rationale:** BPC-157 promotes tissue repair and angiogenesis; ergothioneine concentrates in and protects stressed tissues from oxidative damage, supporting a cleaner recovery environment.
- **Protocol:** Ergothioneine ~5-15 mg/day + BPC-157 250-500 mcg 2x/day
- **Best for:** Recovery, tissue repair

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + Ergothioneine (Recovery + Longevity Antioxidant)
- **Rationale:** CJC-1295/Ipamorelin raise GH and IGF-1 for recovery and body composition; ergothioneine adds long-lasting antioxidant protection to tissues under the metabolic demands of training and growth.
- **Protocol:** Ergothioneine ~5-15 mg/day + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Best for:** Recovery, anti-aging performance protocols

#### GHK-Cu + Ergothioneine (Skin Antioxidant Support)
- **Rationale:** GHK-Cu drives collagen synthesis and skin remodeling; ergothioneine concentrates in skin and protects against UV-induced oxidative damage. Note that ergothioneine chelates copper, so for copper-dependent GHK-Cu effects, separate timing is prudent — but the antioxidant cover for skin tissue is complementary.
- **Protocol:** Ergothioneine ~5-15 mg/day + GHK-Cu per standard protocol (consider separating dosing times)
- **Best for:** Skin photoprotection, anti-aging aesthetics

#### TB-500 + Ergothioneine
- **Rationale:** TB-500 supports systemic tissue repair and flexibility; ergothioneine provides retained antioxidant protection to recovering tissues.
- **Protocol:** Ergothioneine ~5-15 mg/day + TB-500 2.5-5 mg 2x/week
- **Best for:** Injury recovery, return to training

### Supplement Co-Stacks (Non-Peptide)
- **Ergothioneine + NAC:** Complementary antioxidant strategies — ergothioneine is a stable, tissue-targeted scavenger and reserve, while **nac** supplies cysteine to build glutathione for active redox cycling. Together they cover both a persistent reserve and a renewable working antioxidant.
- **Ergothioneine + CoQ10:** Both protect mitochondria; **coq10** supports the electron transport chain while ergothioneine guards mitochondrial components from oxidative damage. A strong mitochondrial-longevity pairing.
- **Ergothioneine + NMN:** **nmn** restores NAD+ for cellular energy and repair; ergothioneine protects against oxidative stress. A common longevity foundation combination.
