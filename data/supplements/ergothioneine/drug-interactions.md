# Ergothioneine
## Drug Interactions

Ergothioneine has a favorable interaction profile and a clean safety record, but human interaction data are limited, and a few transporter- and antioxidant-related considerations are worth noting.

### Theoretical / Monitor

- **OCTN1 (SLC22A4) transporter substrates and inhibitors:**
  - **Interaction:** Ergothioneine is taken up by the OCTN1 transporter, which also transports certain drugs (for example, some forms of metformin and certain other cations have been studied in relation to OCTN1/OCTN family transporters). Competition at this transporter is theoretically possible, potentially affecting uptake of ergothioneine or co-transported drugs
  - **Recommendation:** Generally low practical concern at typical doses, but those on medications known to interact with organic cation transporters can mention ergothioneine use to their physician

- **Chemotherapy agents:**
  - **Interaction:** As an antioxidant, ergothioneine could theoretically influence treatments that rely on oxidative mechanisms; the net effect in humans is not established
  - **Recommendation:** Do not use during active cancer treatment without oncologist approval

- **Anticoagulants (theoretical, minor):**
  - **Interaction:** No established clinically significant interaction; as a general antioxidant, only a theoretical influence on oxidative balance
  - **Recommendation:** Inform your physician of all supplements; no specific action needed at typical doses

### Timing-Sensitive Notes

- **Copper-dependent therapies (e.g., GHK-Cu):** Ergothioneine chelates divalent metals including copper. For protocols that depend on copper availability, consider separating ergothioneine dosing in time
- **No fat or special timing required for absorption** — the transporter handles uptake; this is not an interaction issue

### Population & Condition Cautions

- **Pregnancy:** Ergothioneine is naturally present and concentrates in fetal tissues; dietary intake is normal, but concentrated supplemental safety data in pregnancy are limited — discuss supplements with a physician
- **Breastfeeding:** Dietary intake is normal; consult a provider before concentrated supplements
- **Active cancer:** Coordinate any antioxidant use with the oncology team
- **Mushroom allergy:** Choose purified synthetic L-ergothioneine rather than mushroom-derived products
- **On medication generally:** Interaction data are limited; inform your physician of supplement use

### Beneficial / Neutral Combinations
- **NAC, glycine, CoQ10, NMN:** Complementary antioxidant and mitochondrial-support nutrients; no adverse interaction expected
- **Generally compatible** with most supplements
- **No known major drug contraindications** at dietary or typical supplemental doses

*None of this is medical advice. Inform your healthcare provider of all supplements before starting medications or procedures.*
