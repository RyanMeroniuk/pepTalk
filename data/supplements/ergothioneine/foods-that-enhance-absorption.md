# Ergothioneine
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

Ergothioneine absorption is handled by the dedicated OCTN1 (SLC22A4) transporter, so it is efficiently absorbed regardless of food. "Enhancement" is mostly about supplying it consistently and supporting the broader antioxidant network it works within.

- **Eat it consistently (the main lever):** Because uptake is transporter-mediated and ergothioneine is retained in tissues, regular intake — from mushrooms and/or a supplement — steadily builds tissue levels. Consistency matters far more than any absorption "trick"
- **Mushrooms as the food vehicle:** Eating a variety of mushrooms (oyster, king trumpet, shiitake, maitake) is the natural delivery method. Cooking them is fine — ergothioneine is heat-stable, and cooking improves digestibility
- **Pair with a broad antioxidant-rich diet:** Ergothioneine works alongside other antioxidants (vitamins C and E, glutathione, carotenoids). A colorful, nutrient-dense diet ensures the whole antioxidant network is supported, so ergothioneine's protection is complemented rather than working alone
- **Adequate overall nutrition:** A well-nourished body with healthy cellular function expresses and uses the OCTN1 transporter effectively. There is no specific nutrient required to "unlock" ergothioneine absorption, but general nutritional adequacy supports transporter function
- **No fat requirement:** Unlike fat-soluble carotenoids, ergothioneine is water-soluble and does not need dietary fat for absorption — it can be taken with or without food
- **Gut health:** A healthy gut lining supports intestinal transporter expression; supporting gut health (fiber, fermented foods) is generally beneficial

### Practical Pairing Strategy
- **Whole-food approach:** Include cooked specialty mushrooms in meals several times per week
- **Supplement approach:** Take purified L-ergothioneine daily, any time, with or without food
- **Network approach:** Combine with a generally antioxidant-rich diet and, if desired, complementary antioxidants (e.g., NAC for glutathione, CoQ10 for mitochondria) so ergothioneine is one part of a robust defense
