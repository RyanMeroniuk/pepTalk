# Ergothioneine
## Mechanisms of Action

- **Dedicated transporter and tissue targeting (defining mechanism):** Ergothioneine is actively transported into cells by OCTN1 (SLC22A4), a highly specific carrier. This transporter concentrates ergothioneine in tissues with high oxidative or metabolic stress — liver, kidneys, red and white blood cells, bone marrow, lens/cornea, skin, and brain — positioning the antioxidant precisely where damage is most likely
- **Direct free-radical scavenging:** Ergothioneine scavenges reactive oxygen and nitrogen species, including hydroxyl radicals, singlet oxygen, hypochlorous acid, and peroxynitrite. Its thiourea/thiol structure provides the reactive site
- **Exceptional stability (slow-burning reserve):** Unlike glutathione and many thiol antioxidants, ergothioneine exists predominantly in its stable thione form and does not auto-oxidize readily at physiological pH. This lets it persist in tissues for long periods, acting as a stored antioxidant reserve drawn upon under stress rather than rapidly consumed
- **Metal ion chelation:** Ergothioneine binds divalent transition metals (e.g., copper, iron) that catalyze damaging Fenton-type free-radical reactions, reducing metal-driven oxidative damage
- **Mitochondrial protection:** It accumulates in mitochondria and protects mitochondrial DNA, lipids, and proteins from oxidative damage, helping preserve mitochondrial function
- **Anti-inflammatory and cytoprotective signaling:** Ergothioneine reduces pro-inflammatory cytokine production and oxidative-stress-driven inflammatory signaling, and supports cell survival under oxidative and metabolic stress
- **Potential Nrf2 interaction:** Emerging work suggests ergothioneine may support the broader cytoprotective response, complementing endogenous antioxidant defenses
- **Neuroprotection:** It crosses into the brain and concentrates in neural tissue; lower levels are associated with cognitive decline, and it protects neurons from oxidative and inflammatory injury in preclinical models — a basis for ongoing cognitive research
- **Important caveat:** Much of the detailed mechanistic work is preclinical (cell and animal studies). The precise physiological role in humans is still being defined, which is part of why ergothioneine is described as a candidate "longevity vitamin" rather than an established essential nutrient
