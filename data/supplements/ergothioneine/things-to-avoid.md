# Ergothioneine
## Things to Avoid

- **Expecting rapid effects:** Ergothioneine accumulates slowly in tissues via the OCTN1 transporter and is retained for a long time. Benefits and rising blood levels develop over weeks of consistent intake, not days. Be patient and consistent
- **Overhyping early human evidence:** The mechanistic case and observational associations are compelling, and the 2025 RCT on sleep and memory is encouraging — but human interventional data are still early and largely single-trial or preclinical. Avoid treating ergothioneine as a proven therapy for any specific condition
- **Megadosing without rationale:** Optimal human doses are not yet established. Typical supplemental doses (around 5-15 mg/day, up to ~25-30 mg in some studies) are sensible; very high chronic doses are not well studied and offer no proven added benefit
- **Ignoring the easy whole-food route:** A mushroom-rich diet is an inexpensive, well-tolerated way to raise ergothioneine. Don't overlook food in favor of supplements alone
- **High antioxidant dosing right around hard training (if adaptation is the goal):** As a general antioxidant caution, very high doses peri-workout could theoretically blunt some exercise adaptations; this is not demonstrated for ergothioneine and is likely minor, but separating from workouts is a cautious option
- **Assuming all mushrooms are equal:** White button mushrooms contain far less ergothioneine than specialty species (oyster, king trumpet, shiitake, maitake). For dietary intake, favor the higher-content species
- **Mushroom allergy oversight:** If allergic to mushrooms, choose purified synthetic L-ergothioneine rather than mushroom-derived products

### Population Cautions
- **Pregnancy and breastfeeding:** Ergothioneine is naturally present in the body and concentrates in fetal tissues, and dietary intake is normal; however, concentrated supplemental safety data during pregnancy/breastfeeding are limited — discuss supplements with a physician
- **On medication:** Interactions are not well characterized; inform your physician and see drug-interactions
- **General:** As with any supplement, consult a healthcare provider if managing a health condition
