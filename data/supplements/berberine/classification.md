# Berberine
## Classification

- **Category:** Metabolic / Blood Sugar Support
- **Subcategory:** Plant alkaloid, AMPK activator
- **Chemical class:** Isoquinoline alkaloid (quaternary ammonium salt)
- **Source plants:** Berberis vulgaris (barberry), Berberis aristata (Indian barberry/tree turmeric), Coptis chinensis (goldthread/Huang Lian), Hydrastis canadensis (goldenseal), Phellodendron amurense (Amur cork tree)
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (USA); traditional medicine ingredient in Chinese (TCM) and Ayurvedic medicine; not FDA-approved as a drug despite clinical evidence comparable to some pharmaceuticals
- **Common names:** Berberine HCl, berberine hydrochloride, "nature's metformin"
