# Berberine
## Dosing Protocols

- **Blood sugar and metabolic health (standard berberine HCl):**
  - **Dose:** 500mg three times daily (1,500mg total/day)
  - **Timing:** Take with meals or immediately before meals. Each dose coincides with a meal to reduce postprandial glucose spikes and improve GI tolerance
  - **Ramp-up:** Start with 500mg once daily for the first week to assess GI tolerance. Increase to 500mg twice daily in week 2, then 500mg three times daily in week 3. This gradual approach significantly reduces GI side effects
  - **Duration:** Metabolic benefits begin within 1-2 weeks. Full effect on HbA1c requires 8-12 weeks

- **Blood sugar and metabolic health (dihydroberberine/GlucoVantage):**
  - **Dose:** 100-200mg two to three times daily (200-600mg total/day)
  - **Timing:** With meals
  - **Advantage:** Equivalent metabolic effects with lower dose and far fewer GI side effects
  - **Ramp-up:** Usually not needed due to better GI tolerance, but start with one dose daily if concerned

- **Lipid management:**
  - **Dose:** 500mg berberine HCl twice daily (1,000mg total) or 500mg three times daily (1,500mg total)
  - **Timing:** With meals
  - **Duration:** Lipid improvements typically appear within 4-8 weeks. 12 weeks for full effect

- **Gut health / SIBO / dysbiosis:**
  - **Dose:** 500mg berberine HCl two to three times daily
  - **Timing:** With meals
  - **Duration:** Antimicrobial gut protocols typically run 4-8 weeks. Microbiome remodeling effects continue to develop over 8-12 weeks
  - **Note:** The GI effects (loose stools, cramping) are partially therapeutic in this context — berberine is directly killing pathogenic bacteria. However, if symptoms are severe, reduce dose or switch to dihydroberberine

- **PCOS:**
  - **Dose:** 500mg three times daily (1,500mg total/day) — same as the dose used in PCOS clinical trials
  - **Timing:** With meals
  - **Duration:** Hormonal and metabolic improvements in PCOS typically require 12+ weeks

- **Weight management support:**
  - **Dose:** 500mg two to three times daily
  - **Timing:** With meals — particularly with higher-carbohydrate meals to blunt glucose/insulin spikes
  - **Note:** Berberine is not a weight loss drug. It supports metabolic health and may produce modest weight reduction (2-5 lbs) through improved fat metabolism

- **Important dosing notes:**
  - **Always take with food:** Reduces GI side effects and times the glucose-lowering effect to the postprandial period
  - **Split doses throughout the day:** Berberine has a short half-life (~5 hours). Single large doses are less effective and worse tolerated than split doses
  - **Do not exceed 1,500mg/day (standard berberine):** Higher doses do not show additional benefit and increase side effects and drug interaction risk
  - **Monitor blood sugar:** If diabetic or on diabetes medications, monitor blood glucose closely when starting berberine. Hypoglycemia is possible
  - **Cycling consideration:** Some practitioners recommend cycling berberine (8 weeks on, 2-4 weeks off) to prevent gut microbiome adaptation and maintain effectiveness, though evidence for this is anecdotal
