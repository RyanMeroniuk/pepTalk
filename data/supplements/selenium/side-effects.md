# Selenium
## Side Effects

### CRITICAL: Narrow Therapeutic Window
Selenium is the trace mineral most likely to cause toxicity from supplementation because the margin between beneficial and toxic doses is relatively small. The UL (400mcg/day) is only ~7x the RDA (55mcg/day) — compare this to zinc where the UL is ~5x the RDA, or vitamin C where it is ~27x.

### Common Side Effects (at recommended doses, 100-200mcg/day)
- Generally well-tolerated at recommended doses
- **Mild GI upset** — Occasionally reported, especially with inorganic forms (selenite, selenate). Taking with food resolves this.
- **Garlic-like breath odor** — Indicates selenium is being metabolized; more noticeable at higher doses (>200mcg/day). Caused by volatile dimethyl selenide exhalation.

### Selenosis (Chronic Selenium Toxicity)
Occurs with chronic intake above 400mcg/day. Symptoms develop gradually:

**Early signs (400-700mcg/day chronic):**
- **Garlic breath** — The hallmark early warning sign; caused by excretion of dimethyl selenide through the lungs
- **Metallic taste** in the mouth
- **Hair loss** — Diffuse hair thinning, often the first noticeable symptom
- **Nail changes** — Brittleness, white spots, horizontal streaks (Mees' lines), nail loss
- **Fatigue and irritability**
- **Nausea and diarrhea**
- **Skin rash or dermatitis**

**Advanced signs (>700mcg/day chronic):**
- **Peripheral neuropathy** — Numbness, tingling, burning sensation in extremities
- **Motor dysfunction** — Difficulty walking, tremors
- **Tooth decay and discoloration**
- **Liver and kidney damage**

**Acute selenium poisoning (single massive dose >1000mcg):**
- Severe GI symptoms (vomiting, diarrhea)
- Cardiac arrhythmias
- Myocardial infarction
- Respiratory failure
- Acute kidney failure
- Death (rare; documented from accidental supplement manufacturing errors — a 2008 incident involving 200x labeled dose)

### The U-Shaped Risk Curve
- **Deficiency (<55mcg/day):** Increased risk of cancer, thyroid disease, immune dysfunction, cardiomyopathy
- **Optimal (100-200mcg/day):** Reduced risk of cancer, improved thyroid function, strong immune defense
- **Excess (>300-400mcg/day):** Increased risk of type 2 diabetes, increased cancer risk (some studies), neurological symptoms, selenosis
- **The SELECT trial** (Lippman et al., 2009) found that 200mcg selenium/day in men with ALREADY adequate selenium status was associated with a non-significant trend toward increased prostate cancer and a significant increase in type 2 diabetes risk

### Who Is at Higher Risk of Toxicity
- **People in high-selenium soil regions** (parts of the Dakotas, Nebraska, Wyoming in the US; parts of Venezuela, China)
- **Brazil nut enthusiasts** — A single Brazil nut can contain up to 160mcg selenium; eating 5-6 high-selenium nuts daily can exceed the UL
- **People taking multiple supplements containing selenium** — Check multivitamins, thyroid support formulas, and antioxidant blends for selenium content. Doses can add up.
- **People with kidney disease** — Reduced selenium excretion increases toxicity risk

### GI Tolerance by Form
1. **Selenomethionine** — Best tolerated
2. **Selenium yeast** — Well tolerated
3. **Methylselenocysteine** — Well tolerated
4. **Sodium selenite** — More likely to cause GI upset and nausea
5. **Sodium selenate** — Similar to selenite; less commonly used
