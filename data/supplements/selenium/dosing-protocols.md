# Selenium
## Dosing Protocols

### Daily Maintenance
- **Adults (both sexes):** 55mcg/day (RDA) — minimum to prevent deficiency
- **Optimal intake:** 100-200mcg/day from food + supplements combined
- **Upper Tolerable Intake Level (UL):** 400mcg/day — DO NOT exceed chronically
- **Preferred form:** Selenomethionine or selenium yeast for daily use

### Specific Protocols

**General health and antioxidant support:**
- 100-200mcg selenomethionine/day
- Take with food (any meal)
- No cycling required at this dose
- Expect benefit within 4-8 weeks

**Thyroid support (Hashimoto's thyroiditis):**
- 200mcg selenomethionine/day
- This is the dose used in most clinical trials showing TPO antibody reduction
- Take consistently for at least 3-6 months before assessing antibody levels
- Can be taken long-term at this dose
- **Important:** Do not exceed 200mcg/day for thyroid support — higher doses do not provide additional benefit and increase toxicity risk

**Thyroid support (Graves' disease):**
- 200mcg selenomethionine/day
- Combined with standard medical therapy
- Used in EUGOGO trial for Graves' orbitopathy with positive results

**Immune support (general):**
- 100-200mcg/day selenomethionine or selenium yeast
- Particularly important during cold/flu season or periods of immune challenge
- Combine with zinc, vitamin C, and vitamin D for comprehensive immune support

**Male fertility:**
- 200mcg selenomethionine/day
- Often combined with N-acetylcysteine (600mg/day), zinc (30mg/day), and vitamin E (400 IU/day)
- Allow 3-6 months for sperm parameter improvements (spermatogenesis cycle is ~74 days)

**Cancer prevention (in deficient/suboptimal individuals):**
- 100-200mcg/day selenium yeast or methylselenocysteine
- **Only for those with baseline selenium below optimal** (serum selenium <120mcg/L)
- Do NOT supplement above 200mcg/day for cancer prevention — the SELECT trial showed potential harm in selenium-replete men

### Timing
- **With food:** Recommended to improve absorption and reduce any GI irritation (especially with inorganic forms)
- **Morning or afternoon:** No specific timing advantage; consistency matters more
- **Can be taken with most other supplements** — selenium does not have significant competitive absorption issues with common minerals
- **Separate from vitamin C** if using sodium selenite — high-dose vitamin C can reduce selenite to elemental selenium (poorly absorbed). This does NOT apply to selenomethionine.

### Brazil Nut Protocol (Food-Based Alternative)
- **1-2 Brazil nuts per day** can provide 68-180mcg selenium
- **Advantage:** Whole-food source with additional minerals and healthy fats
- **Disadvantage:** Selenium content per nut is highly variable (8-160mcg depending on soil)
- **Caution:** Do NOT eat more than 3-4 Brazil nuts daily — risk of selenosis from high-selenium batches
- **Practical tip:** If using Brazil nuts as your selenium source, buy a consistent brand and limit to 2 nuts/day

### Monitoring
- **Serum selenium:** Reflects recent intake; optimal range 100-150mcg/L
- **Plasma selenoprotein P:** Best functional marker of selenium status; saturates at ~120mcg/L
- **Red blood cell selenium:** Reflects longer-term status
- **Test frequency:** Every 6-12 months if supplementing at 200mcg/day
- **Monitor for toxicity signs** if taking >200mcg/day: garlic breath odor, metallic taste, brittle nails/hair

### Cycling
- At 100-200mcg/day, no cycling is necessary
- Selenium is not stored in toxic amounts at physiological doses (organic forms are safely stored in body proteins)
- If taking >200mcg/day for any reason, limit to 8-12 week cycles with breaks
