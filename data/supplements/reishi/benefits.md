# Reishi
## Benefits

- **Immune modulation (strongest evidence):** Reishi's beta-glucans balance innate immune activity — supporting macrophage and NK-cell function. This is its most researched benefit, used traditionally and studied for immune resilience and recovery from illness/fatigue
- **Adjunct in cancer care (supportive, not curative):** Systematic reviews (e.g., Cochrane analyses of Ganoderma lucidum) suggest reishi, used alongside conventional cancer treatment, may improve immune markers and quality of life and may enhance tumor response when combined with chemo/radiotherapy. Critically, it is NOT a standalone cancer treatment and should only be used under oncologist supervision. Evidence quality is limited and reishi is positioned strictly as a supportive adjunct
- **Calm and stress reduction:** Traditionally the premier "calming" tonic. Reported benefits include reduced anxiety, a sense of calm, and stress buffering. Human clinical evidence is preliminary; the effect is supported mainly by tradition and animal data
- **Sleep support:** Used in the evening to support sleep onset and quality. Rodent studies show increased sleep time; human sleep-specific trials are sparse but it is widely used anecdotally as a non-sedative-drug sleep aid
- **Anti-inflammatory and antioxidant:** Reduces inflammatory and oxidative markers in preclinical work, supporting general recovery and "healthy aging" use
- **Liver support:** Triterpenoids show hepatoprotective effects in animal models; a small human study suggested benefit in some liver conditions. Part of reishi's traditional tonic/detox reputation
- **Cardiovascular and metabolic markers:** Preliminary evidence for modest blood-pressure lowering, improved lipid profiles, and lower blood glucose. Human data is limited and mixed
- **Fatigue and quality of life:** A trial in patients with neurasthenia (fatigue/well-being) reported improvements in fatigue and well-being scores with a reishi polysaccharide extract — consistent with its tonic, restorative positioning
- **Allergy/histamine modulation (preliminary):** Triterpenoids show antihistamine-like activity in lab models, a basis for traditional use in allergic and inflammatory conditions
- **Stacks well with the mushroom trio:** Combined with cordyceps (energy/endurance) and lion's mane (cognition) for a complementary daily protocol — reishi contributes the calm, immune, and evening/sleep angle
