# Reishi
## Foods to Avoid

- **Excessive alcohol (especially in the evening):** Reishi is taken largely for calm and sleep. Alcohol fragments sleep architecture, suppresses deep and REM sleep, and burdens the liver — directly opposing reishi's restorative, hepatoprotective, and sleep-supporting goals. Heavy drinking undermines the very benefits reishi is taken for
- **Caffeine late in the day:** Reishi's calming, sleep-supporting role is sabotaged by late-afternoon or evening caffeine. If you run a mushroom trio, keep energizing cordyceps and caffeine to the morning and reserve reishi for the evening
- **High-sugar, high-glycemic foods:** Chronic hyperglycemia promotes systemic inflammation and works against reishi's anti-inflammatory and metabolic benefits. Evening sugar spikes also disrupt sleep quality
- **Ultra-processed, pro-inflammatory foods:** Refined seed oils, trans fats, and additive-heavy foods drive oxidative stress and inflammation — the opposite of the calm, low-inflammation state reishi supports
- **No specific food blocks reishi:** There is no well-documented food that directly blocks reishi's active compounds. Guidance is mostly about diet quality and timing (avoiding stimulants/alcohol near bedtime) that supports vs. undermines its calming and immune goals
- **Foods you are intolerant to:** Undiagnosed sensitivities drive low-grade inflammation and can disturb sleep, counteracting reishi's restorative aims
