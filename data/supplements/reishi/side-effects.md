# Reishi
## Side Effects

- **GI discomfort:** Mild nausea, upset stomach, or loose stools, especially at higher doses or when starting. Taking with food helps. Reishi's intense bitterness can also be off-putting
- **Dry mouth, dry throat, dry nasal passages:** Reported with continuous high-dose use over weeks/months in some traditional and clinical sources
- **Dizziness or drowsiness:** Consistent with reishi's calming character; usually mild. Because it can be mildly sedating, evening dosing is preferred and caution with daytime alertness-dependent tasks is reasonable until you know your response
- **Liver enzyme elevation (rare):** Although reishi is traditionally hepatoprotective, there are rare case reports of liver injury associated with reishi powder (particularly raw powder, possibly contamination-related). Discontinue and seek care if signs of liver problems appear; use extracted, third-party-tested products
- **Bleeding/antiplatelet potential:** Reishi triterpenoids may inhibit platelet aggregation. Theoretical increased bleeding risk with anticoagulant/antiplatelet drugs; discontinue before surgery as a precaution
- **Blood pressure / blood sugar lowering:** May modestly lower blood pressure and blood glucose — usually beneficial, but those on antihypertensives or diabetes medication should monitor for additive effects
- **Immune activation in autoimmune disease:** Beta-glucan immune modulation is theoretically undesirable in some autoimmune conditions; consult a physician before use
- **Allergic reactions:** Rare. Those with mold/mushroom allergies should be cautious. Discontinue if rash, swelling, or breathing difficulty occur
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding:** Insufficient safety data; avoid unless directed by a physician
- **Overall safety profile:** Reishi has a long traditional-use safety record and is generally well tolerated at standard extract doses for several months. Main cautions are bleeding risk, blood-pressure/sugar lowering, autoimmune contexts, rare liver reports (favor tested extracts over raw powder), and pregnancy
