# Reishi
## Dosing Protocols

- **General calm / immune support (dual-extract fruiting body):**
  - **Dose:** 1,000-2,000mg/day of standardized dual extract
  - **Timing:** Evening is most common (reishi is mildly calming and traditionally used for sleep), though it can be split morning/evening for steady immune support
  - **Duration:** Calming and immune effects build with consistent use over 2-4+ weeks

- **Sleep support:**
  - **Dose:** 1,000-1,500mg dual extract
  - **Timing:** 30-60 minutes before bed
  - **Note:** Reishi is not a fast-acting sedative; it supports sleep gradually and gently rather than knocking you out

- **Stress / anxiety (adaptogenic use):**
  - **Dose:** 1,000-2,000mg/day, often split or taken in the evening
  - **Note:** Pairs with other calming supplements (e.g., L-theanine, magnesium, glycine); effects are subtle and cumulative

- **Immune-focused / higher-dose traditional use:**
  - **Dose:** Up to 3,000-6,000mg/day of extract, or higher of crude fruiting body, has been used short-term in traditional and clinical contexts. Higher doses raise GI and (theoretical) bleeding cautions
  - **Note:** Stay at the lower end for routine daily use; reserve higher doses for short courses and ideally under guidance

- **Supportive use alongside cancer therapy:**
  - **Only under oncologist supervision.** Trials of reishi polysaccharide extracts as adjuncts to chemo/radiotherapy have used variable doses. Reishi is supportive, not a treatment, and must not replace conventional care or be started without the oncology team's knowledge

- **Whole dried fruiting body (traditional decoction):**
  - **Dose:** Simmer several grams of sliced dried reishi in water for 1-2 hours to make a bitter tea/broth
  - **Note:** This is a hot-water extraction; it captures beta-glucans but under-extracts triterpenoids

- **Mushroom trio stack:**
  - Cordyceps + lion's mane in the morning (energy + cognition); reishi in the evening (calm + immune + sleep). This places reishi where its mildly sedating character is an asset
- **With food:** Well tolerated with or without food; with food can reduce mild GI upset
- **Cycling:** Not strictly necessary. Some cycle (e.g., breaks every few months) as general practice; continuous use is common
- **Onset timeline:** Subtle and cumulative — do not expect a dramatic same-day effect
