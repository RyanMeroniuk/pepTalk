# Reishi
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### DSIP + Reishi (Sleep & Calm Powerhouse)
- **Rationale:** DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide) improves sleep quality and delta-wave architecture. Reishi is the calming member of the mushroom family, traditionally used in the evening to support sleep onset and relaxation through CNS and stress-axis modulation. Together they target sleep from two complementary angles — peptide-driven sleep architecture plus mushroom-driven calm.
- **Protocol:** Reishi 1,000-1,500mg dual extract 30-60 min before bed + DSIP per standard evening protocol
- **Best for:** Sleep quality, evening wind-down, stress-related insomnia

#### Epitalon + Reishi (Longevity / Healthy Aging)
- **Rationale:** Epitalon is a telomerase-activating, melatonin-regulating peptide central to longevity protocols and itself improves sleep via melatonin pathways. Reishi adds immune modulation, anti-inflammatory and antioxidant support, and its own sleep/calm benefit. Both improve the sleep that consolidates recovery, and both fit a "healthy aging" framing.
- **Protocol:** Reishi 1,000-2,000mg/day (evening) + Epitalon in standard cyclical courses
- **Best for:** Longevity stacks, immune-and-sleep-focused aging protocols

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### Selank + Reishi (Calm Focus / Anti-Anxiety)
- **Rationale:** Selank is an anxiolytic nootropic that modulates GABA and reduces anxiety. Reishi adds a calming adaptogenic effect and HPA-axis modulation. The combination supports a relaxed, non-sedated calm — Selank for daytime anxiety relief, reishi for evening calm and immune support.
- **Protocol:** Selank at standard dosing during the day + Reishi 1,000-2,000mg in the evening
- **Best for:** Anxiety, stress resilience, calm without heavy sedation

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + Reishi (Recovery via Sleep + Immune)
- **Rationale:** CJC-1295/Ipamorelin raise GH/IGF-1 with the largest natural GH pulse occurring during deep sleep — so improving sleep amplifies their benefit. Reishi supports evening calm and sleep while adding immune modulation and anti-inflammatory support during recovery.
- **Protocol:** Reishi 1,000-1,500mg before bed + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Best for:** Recovery-focused protocols where better sleep enhances GH output

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### Thymosin Alpha-1 / Thymulin + Reishi (Immune Modulation)
- **Rationale:** Thymic peptides like Thymosin Alpha-1 modulate and support adaptive immune function. Reishi beta-glucans modulate innate immunity (macrophages, NK cells). Together they touch both arms of immune modulation. Use immune-modulating combinations cautiously and avoid in autoimmune disease without medical guidance.
- **Protocol:** Reishi 1,000-2,000mg/day + thymic peptide per standard protocol
- **Best for:** Immune resilience protocols (with medical oversight)

#### Semax + Reishi (Calm Cognition)
- **Rationale:** Semax upregulates BDNF and enhances focus and cognitive processing. Reishi reduces neuroinflammation and provides a calming baseline. The pairing supports clear-but-calm cognition — Semax driving the cognitive upside while reishi tempers stress and inflammation.
- **Protocol:** Semax at standard nootropic dosing (daytime) + Reishi 1,000-2,000mg (evening)
- **Best for:** High-stress cognitive work, calm focus

#### Reishi + Lion's Mane + Cordyceps (The Mushroom Trio)
- **Rationale:** A complementary functional-mushroom protocol from the PepTalk supplement database. Reishi provides calm, immune modulation, and evening/sleep support; its sibling lion's mane provides cognitive/neurotrophic support (NGF/BDNF); its sibling cordyceps provides energy and endurance support. Beta-glucans from the three species engage different immune receptors for broader immune modulation.
- **Protocol:** Cordyceps + lion's mane in the morning; reishi in the evening (reishi is the calming, sleep-supporting member)
- **Best for:** A daily all-around calm, focus, energy, immune, and recovery base
