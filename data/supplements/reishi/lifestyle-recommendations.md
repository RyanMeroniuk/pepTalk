# Reishi
## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Use it in the evening as a wind-down ritual:** Reishi's mildly calming, sleep-supporting character makes it ideal as part of an evening routine — a reishi tea, coffee-alternative, or cacao 30-60 minutes before bed signals the body to downshift. This places it where its sedating tendency is a benefit rather than a liability
- **Build good sleep hygiene around it:** Reishi supports sleep but cannot override a poor sleep environment. Pair it with consistent bed/wake times, a dark cool room, and reduced evening screen/blue-light exposure. The supplement and the behavior reinforce each other
- **Pair with active stress management:** Reishi is a calming adaptogen, but it works best alongside genuine stress-reduction practices — breathwork, meditation, nature exposure, and limiting late-day stimulation. Chronic unmanaged stress (high cortisol) works against reishi's calming aim
- **Keep stimulants to the morning:** If you run the mushroom trio, take energizing cordyceps and lion's mane (and caffeine) in the morning, and reserve reishi for the evening so the energizing and calming roles do not collide
- **Support immune health holistically:** Reishi modulates immunity, but sleep, nutrition, sunlight/vitamin D, and stress management remain the foundation. Reishi is an adjunct to, not a replacement for, immune-supportive lifestyle basics
- **Anti-inflammatory, whole-food diet:** A diet rich in vegetables, fish, olive oil, and antioxidants complements reishi's anti-inflammatory and liver-supportive effects
- **Moderate alcohol and protect the liver:** Reishi is traditionally hepatoprotective; heavy drinking burdens the liver and contradicts that benefit. Keeping alcohol low supports both liver health and the sleep reishi is meant to improve
- **Be patient and consistent:** Reishi's calming and immune effects are cumulative over weeks. Daily, consistent evening dosing produces better results than sporadic use
- **Combine with calming co-supplements:** For sleep/relaxation goals, reishi works well in an evening stack with L-theanine, magnesium, and glycine
