# Reishi
## Classification

- **Category:** Medicinal Mushroom / Adaptogen
- **Subcategory:** Functional fungus, calming immune-modulating supplement
- **Botanical name:** Ganoderma lucidum (and related species including G. sinense, G. lingzhi)
- **Family:** Ganodermataceae
- **Active compounds:** Beta-glucans and other polysaccharides (immune-modulating), triterpenoids/ganoderic acids (calming, hepatoprotective, anti-inflammatory), peptidoglycans, sterols
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (USA); traditional medicine in China, Japan, and Korea for over 2,000 years; not FDA-approved as a drug
- **Common names:** Reishi, lingzhi (Chinese, "spirit/divine mushroom"), mannentake (Japanese, "10,000-year mushroom"), yeongji (Korean)
