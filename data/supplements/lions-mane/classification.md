# Lion's Mane
## Classification

- **Category:** Medicinal Mushroom / Nootropic
- **Subcategory:** Functional fungus, neurotrophic supplement
- **Botanical name:** Hericium erinaceus
- **Family:** Hericiaceae
- **Active compounds:** Hericenones (found in fruiting body), erinacines (found in mycelium), beta-glucans, polysaccharides, terpenoids
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (USA); traditional food and medicine in East Asia; not FDA-approved as a drug
- **Common names:** Lion's mane mushroom, yamabushitake (Japanese), hou tou gu (Chinese), bearded tooth mushroom, pom pom mushroom
