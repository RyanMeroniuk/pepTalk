# Lion's Mane
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

- **Healthy fats (enhance hericenone absorption):** Hericenones are fat-soluble compounds. Taking Lion's Mane with dietary fat improves their absorption:
  - **Butter or ghee:** Traditional and effective — sauteing fresh Lion's Mane in butter both enhances flavor and creates a fat matrix for hericenone absorption
  - **MCT oil or coconut oil:** Medium-chain triglycerides are rapidly absorbed and can carry fat-soluble compounds. Adding MCT oil to a Lion's Mane coffee or smoothie is an efficient delivery method
  - **Avocado:** Rich in monounsaturated fats that support fat-soluble compound absorption
  - **Olive oil:** High-quality extra virgin olive oil provides healthy fats plus its own polyphenol antioxidants
  - **Nuts (walnuts, almonds, macadamias):** Provide fat for absorption plus complementary brain-supporting nutrients (walnuts especially, rich in ALA omega-3)

- **Hot water:** Beta-glucans and water-soluble polysaccharides dissolve in hot water. Adding Lion's Mane powder to hot coffee, tea, or broth helps extract water-soluble bioactives. This mirrors the hot water extraction process used in supplement manufacturing

- **Coffee:** A popular combination — Lion's Mane in coffee provides complementary cognitive benefits. Caffeine enhances alertness while Lion's Mane supports sustained neurotrophin production. Many report smoother, less jittery focus with the combination. The hot water and milk fat (if added) both aid extraction and absorption

- **Vitamin C-rich foods:** Vitamin C may help protect beta-glucans from degradation in the gut and supports immune function that beta-glucans enhance:
  - **Citrus fruits (oranges, lemons)**
  - **Bell peppers**
  - **Kiwi**
  - **Strawberries**

- **Prebiotic foods (enhance gut-brain axis benefits):** Lion's Mane has prebiotic properties that are amplified when combined with other prebiotic foods:
  - **Garlic**
  - **Onions**
  - **Asparagus**
  - **Jerusalem artichoke**
  - **Leeks**

- **Other medicinal mushrooms:** Combining Lion's Mane with other mushrooms (reishi, cordyceps, turkey tail, chaga) creates a "mushroom stack" where beta-glucans from different species activate different immune receptors. This does not enhance Lion's Mane absorption per se but amplifies the overall mushroom supplement protocol
