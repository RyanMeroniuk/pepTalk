# Lion's Mane
## Dosing Protocols

- **Cognitive enhancement and neuroprotection:**
  - **Dose:** 500-1,000mg fruiting body extract (dual extraction, 30%+ beta-glucans) twice daily, or 1,000-2,000mg once daily
  - **Timing:** Morning and early afternoon (Lion's Mane is not sedating — it may actually increase alertness)
  - **Duration:** Cognitive benefits typically emerge after 2-4 weeks of consistent use. The landmark Japanese cognitive impairment trial used 3g/day for 16 weeks
  - **Note:** Effects are cumulative and related to actual neurotrophin-mediated changes, not acute neurotransmitter modulation. Patience is required

- **Nerve repair and peripheral neuropathy:**
  - **Dose:** 1,000-3,000mg fruiting body extract per day
  - **Timing:** Split into 2-3 doses throughout the day for sustained NGF stimulation
  - **Duration:** Nerve regeneration is slow — 3-6+ months may be needed for noticeable improvement in neuropathy symptoms
  - **Note:** Combine with adequate B12, B6, and alpha-lipoic acid for comprehensive nerve support

- **Depression and anxiety:**
  - **Dose:** 500-1,000mg fruiting body extract, 2-3 times daily
  - **Timing:** With meals throughout the day
  - **Duration:** Mood improvements noted within 4-8 weeks in clinical observations

- **Gut health:**
  - **Dose:** 500-1,000mg fruiting body extract, 1-2 times daily
  - **Timing:** With meals
  - **Note:** Hot water extract (beta-glucan-rich) is preferred for gut health benefits

- **General nootropic stack (daily maintenance):**
  - **Dose:** 500-1,000mg fruiting body extract once daily
  - **Timing:** Morning with breakfast or in coffee/tea
  - **Note:** Can be combined with coffee — some find it reduces coffee jitteriness while enhancing focus

- **Whole mushroom powder (culinary/traditional):**
  - **Dose:** 3-5g dried mushroom powder per day (higher dose needed because bioactives are not extracted from chitin)
  - **Timing:** Mixed into food, coffee, soups, or smoothies
  - **Note:** Less potent than extracted forms but suitable for general wellness

- **With food:** Taking with food is recommended to reduce any GI discomfort. Fat in the meal may enhance absorption of fat-soluble hericenones
- **Cycling:** Unlike ashwagandha, Lion's Mane does not appear to cause tolerance or emotional blunting. Many users take it continuously without cycling. However, some practitioners recommend periodic breaks (e.g., 5 days on/2 days off, or 1 month off every 3 months) based on the general principle of supplement cycling
- **Onset timeline:** Do not expect noticeable effects in the first few days. NGF/BDNF-mediated neuroplasticity changes take weeks to manifest. Most users report noticeable cognitive improvement between weeks 2-6
