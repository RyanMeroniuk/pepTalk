# Lion's Mane
## Peptide Synergies

- **Semax (nootropic — strongest synergy):** Semax upregulates BDNF through melanocortin pathways and enhances cognitive function, memory, and attention. Lion's Mane stimulates both NGF and BDNF through completely different mechanisms (hericenone/erinacine-mediated). The combination creates a powerful dual-pathway neurotrophic stimulus — Semax for rapid BDNF upregulation and Lion's Mane for sustained NGF/BDNF support. This is arguably the strongest nootropic pairing in the PepTalk database for cognitive enhancement and neuroprotection
- **Selank (anxiolytic nootropic):** Selank modulates GABA, reduces anxiety, and has mild BDNF-enhancing effects. Lion's Mane adds NGF stimulation, neuroinflammation reduction, and complementary BDNF support. The combination addresses both cognitive enhancement and anxiety relief — Selank provides the calm focus while Lion's Mane builds the neural infrastructure
- **Dihexa (potent nootropic):** Dihexa promotes synaptogenesis through HGF/c-Met receptor activation — a completely different mechanism from Lion's Mane's NGF/BDNF pathway. The combination stimulates new synapse formation from multiple angles. Both cross the blood-brain barrier. Extremely potent cognitive enhancement stack for those already using peptides
- **Pinealon (neuroprotective):** Pinealon is a tripeptide that promotes neuroprotection, memory, and circadian rhythm regulation. Combined with Lion's Mane's NGF stimulation and neuroinflammation reduction, it provides a comprehensive neuroprotective stack. Particularly relevant for age-related cognitive decline
- **BPC-157 (tissue repair):** BPC-157 promotes healing through growth factor modulation and has emerging evidence for gut-brain axis benefits. Lion's Mane supports both gut health (prebiotic effects, gastric mucosal protection) and nerve repair. The combination addresses gut-brain axis dysfunction from both ends
- **GHK-Cu (copper peptide):** GHK-Cu has broad regenerative effects and upregulates genes involved in nerve regeneration. Combined with Lion's Mane's NGF/BDNF stimulation, it supports comprehensive tissue and nerve repair. Relevant for anti-aging protocols focused on neural longevity
- **Epitalon (anti-aging):** Epitalon activates telomerase and regulates melatonin. Lion's Mane's neuroprotective and neurogenerative properties complement the anti-aging approach — Epitalon protects telomere length while Lion's Mane maintains neural health and promotes neurogenesis. Sleep improvement from both (Epitalon via melatonin, Lion's Mane via neuroplasticity consolidation) adds synergy
- **CJC-1295/Ipamorelin (GH secretagogues):** Growth hormone supports overall tissue repair and cognitive function. GH enhances BDNF effects, and Lion's Mane provides additional neurotrophic support. The combination is relevant for age-related cognitive decline where GH levels are naturally declining
- **DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide):** DSIP improves sleep quality and delta wave architecture. Since Lion's Mane's neurotrophic effects are consolidated during sleep, improving sleep quality with DSIP amplifies the neuroplasticity benefits. The combination optimizes the "build during the day, consolidate at night" cycle of cognitive enhancement
