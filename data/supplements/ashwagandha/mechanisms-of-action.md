# Ashwagandha
## Mechanisms of Action

- **HPA axis modulation:** Ashwagandha's primary adaptogenic mechanism is modulating the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis. It attenuates the cortisol response to stress by acting on the hypothalamus and adrenal glands, reducing excessive cortisol secretion without suppressing baseline cortisol needed for normal function. Studies show 14-28% cortisol reductions with standardized extracts
- **GABAergic activity:** Withanolides and their metabolites act as positive allosteric modulators of GABA-A receptors, similar (but milder) to benzodiazepines. This contributes to anxiolytic and sleep-promoting effects without the sedation or dependence risk of pharmaceutical GABA modulators
- **Serotonin modulation:** Ashwagandha may modulate serotonin receptor activity (particularly 5-HT1A and 5-HT2) and inhibit serotonin reuptake, contributing to mood stabilization and antidepressant effects
- **Thyroid hormone stimulation:** Ashwagandha stimulates thyroid gland activity, increasing both T4 (thyroxine) and T3 (triiodothyronine) production and potentially enhancing T4-to-T3 conversion. This is beneficial for subclinical hypothyroidism but potentially dangerous for hyperthyroid conditions or those on thyroid medication
- **Testosterone and reproductive hormones:** In males, ashwagandha increases testosterone through multiple mechanisms: reducing cortisol (cortisol and testosterone are inversely related), improving Leydig cell function, increasing LH (luteinizing hormone), and reducing oxidative stress in reproductive tissues. Increases of 10-22% testosterone have been reported in clinical trials
- **Antioxidant and anti-inflammatory:** Withanolides inhibit NF-kB signaling, reduce COX-2 expression, and scavenge free radicals. Withaferin A is a potent inhibitor of NF-kB and has shown anti-tumor activity in preclinical research
- **Neuroplasticity and neuroprotection:** Ashwagandha promotes dendrite formation, supports BDNF levels, inhibits acetylcholinesterase (preserving acetylcholine), and protects against beta-amyloid toxicity in cell and animal models. Withanolide A specifically promotes neurite outgrowth
- **Muscle and strength:** Beyond testosterone, ashwagandha may enhance muscle recovery by reducing exercise-induced muscle damage (lower creatine kinase levels), improving VO2 max, and enhancing creatine resynthesis in muscle tissue
- **Immunomodulation:** Stimulates natural killer (NK) cell activity, promotes Th1 immune response, and increases immunoglobulin production. The effects are immunomodulatory rather than purely immunostimulant — it can both upregulate and downregulate immune pathways depending on context
