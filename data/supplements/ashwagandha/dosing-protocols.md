# Ashwagandha
## Dosing Protocols

- **Stress and anxiety (KSM-66):**
  - **Dose:** 300mg twice daily or 600mg once daily
  - **Timing:** Morning and evening with meals, or 600mg in the morning
  - **Duration:** Benefits typically begin within 2-4 weeks. Most clinical trials run 8-12 weeks
  - **Note:** This is the most well-studied protocol with robust clinical data

- **Stress and anxiety (Sensoril):**
  - **Dose:** 125-250mg once daily
  - **Timing:** Evening or before bed (more sedating than KSM-66)
  - **Note:** Lower dose needed due to higher withanolide concentration (10% vs 5%)

- **Sleep improvement:**
  - **Dose:** 300-600mg KSM-66 or 125-250mg Sensoril
  - **Timing:** 30-60 minutes before bed
  - **Note:** Sensoril may be more effective for sleep due to stronger GABAergic properties. Can be combined with magnesium glycinate for enhanced effect

- **Testosterone and male fertility:**
  - **Dose:** 600mg KSM-66 per day
  - **Timing:** Split 300mg morning and 300mg evening, with meals
  - **Duration:** Minimum 8 weeks for testosterone effects. Fertility parameters (sperm count, motility) may take 12 weeks to improve significantly

- **Physical performance and muscle:**
  - **Dose:** 300-600mg KSM-66 per day
  - **Timing:** 300mg morning, 300mg 30-60 minutes pre-workout (or with post-workout meal)
  - **Duration:** Strength and body composition changes appear over 8-12 weeks

- **Cognitive function:**
  - **Dose:** 300mg KSM-66 twice daily
  - **Timing:** Morning and afternoon with meals
  - **Duration:** Cognitive improvements noted within 2-8 weeks in clinical trials

- **Thyroid support (subclinical hypothyroidism):**
  - **Dose:** 600mg root extract per day
  - **Timing:** Morning with food
  - **Duration:** Thyroid hormone changes observed within 8 weeks
  - **CRITICAL:** Monitor thyroid labs (TSH, free T3, free T4) before starting and at 4-8 week intervals. Do NOT use without physician supervision if on thyroid medication

- **Cycling recommendations:**
  - **Cycle pattern:** 8-12 weeks on, 2-4 weeks off
  - **Why cycle:** Prevents potential receptor desensitization, addresses the anhedonia/emotional blunting concern reported with prolonged continuous use, and allows assessment of whether the adaptogenic effects have become self-sustaining
  - **Alternative:** Some practitioners recommend 5 days on, 2 days off as a lighter cycling approach

- **With food:** Take with meals containing some fat for optimal absorption. Traditionally taken with warm milk (the fat and casein may enhance withanolide absorption)
