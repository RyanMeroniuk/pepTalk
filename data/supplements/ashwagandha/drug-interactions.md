# Ashwagandha
## Drug Interactions

- **Thyroid medications (levothyroxine/Synthroid, liothyronine/Cytomel, Armour Thyroid):** Ashwagandha stimulates thyroid hormone production (T3 and T4). This can alter thyroid medication requirements — potentially causing hyperthyroid symptoms in patients whose dose was previously optimized. Monitor thyroid labs closely if combining. May require medication dose reduction
- **Benzodiazepines (alprazolam/Xanax, diazepam/Valium, clonazepam/Klonopin, lorazepam/Ativan):** Additive GABAergic effects. Ashwagandha potentiates sedation, drowsiness, and psychomotor impairment. Start with lower ashwagandha doses. Do not drive or operate machinery until you understand the combined effect
- **Sleep medications (zolpidem/Ambien, eszopiclone/Lunesta, suvorexant/Belsomra):** Additive sedation through overlapping GABAergic mechanisms. Excessive drowsiness and next-day impairment possible. Use lower doses if combining
- **Antidepressants (SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs):** Ashwagandha modulates serotonin pathways. Theoretical risk of serotonin syndrome with MAOIs (unlikely but be aware). With SSRIs/SNRIs, the combination is generally well-tolerated and may enhance antidepressant efficacy, but monitor for excessive sedation or serotonergic symptoms
- **Immunosuppressants (cyclosporine, tacrolimus, mycophenolate, azathioprine):** Ashwagandha stimulates immune function, potentially counteracting immunosuppressive therapy. Contraindicated in organ transplant recipients and those on immunosuppressants for autoimmune disease
- **Diabetes medications (metformin, sulfonylureas, insulin):** Ashwagandha lowers blood glucose. Additive hypoglycemic effect when combined with diabetes medications. Monitor blood sugar more frequently. Dose adjustments may be needed
- **Blood pressure medications:** Ashwagandha may modestly lower blood pressure. Additive hypotensive effect with antihypertensives. Monitor blood pressure, especially during initial combination
- **Barbiturates and CNS depressants:** Enhanced sedation and CNS depression. Ashwagandha may increase the duration and intensity of barbiturate effects
- **Anticonvulsants (phenobarbital, phenytoin, valproate):** Potential additive CNS depression. Some anticonvulsants affect thyroid levels — combining with ashwagandha's thyroid-stimulating effects adds complexity. Monitor drug levels and thyroid function
- **Corticosteroids (prednisone, dexamethasone):** Complex interaction — ashwagandha lowers cortisol while corticosteroids provide exogenous cortisol. The clinical significance is unclear, but the opposing mechanisms may reduce effectiveness of one or both
- **Digoxin:** Ashwagandha may affect thyroid hormone levels, which influence digoxin sensitivity. Monitor digoxin levels if combining

- **CYP enzyme interactions:** Ashwagandha may inhibit CYP3A4 and CYP2D6 to a modest degree, potentially increasing blood levels of drugs metabolized by these enzymes. Clinically significant interactions are not well-documented at standard doses, but exercise caution with narrow therapeutic index drugs
