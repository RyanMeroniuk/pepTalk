# Ashwagandha
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

- **Healthy fats (enhance withanolide absorption):** Withanolides are fat-soluble steroidal lactones. Taking ashwagandha with dietary fat improves their absorption:
  - **Warm milk (traditional method):** The Ayurvedic tradition of taking ashwagandha with warm milk is backed by science — milk fat aids withanolide absorption, and the casein protein may form complexes that improve bioavailability. The KSM-66 extract is actually produced using a milk-based extraction process for this reason
  - **Ghee (clarified butter):** Traditional Ayurvedic vehicle for fat-soluble herbs. Excellent absorption enhancer
  - **Coconut oil or MCT oil:** Medium-chain triglycerides are rapidly absorbed and can carry withanolides effectively
  - **Avocado:** Monounsaturated fat content aids fat-soluble compound absorption
  - **Nut butters (almond, cashew):** Fat content enhances absorption; also provide magnesium which synergizes with ashwagandha's calming effects

- **Black pepper (piperine):** Piperine inhibits hepatic and intestinal glucuronidation, which may slow the metabolism and clearance of withanolides, effectively increasing their bioavailability. A pinch of black pepper with ashwagandha is a traditional Ayurvedic combination
- **Turmeric/curcumin:** Often combined with ashwagandha in Ayurvedic formulations. Curcumin's anti-inflammatory effects complement ashwagandha, and the combination may have synergistic absorption effects
- **Honey:** Traditional Ayurvedic carrier (anupana) for ashwagandha. May enhance palatability and provide quick energy that aids absorption, though direct bioavailability enhancement is not well-studied
- **Warm water or warm milk vs. cold:** Traditionally, warm liquids are preferred for ashwagandha delivery as warmth may improve dissolution and absorption of withanolides
- **Cardamom and cinnamon:** Traditional Ayurvedic spices paired with ashwagandha. Cinnamon may have complementary blood sugar benefits; cardamom aids digestion
