# Ashwagandha
## Benefits

- **Stress and anxiety reduction:** The strongest evidence base. Multiple RCTs show significant reductions in perceived stress (PSS scores), anxiety (HAM-A, DASS-21), and cortisol levels. KSM-66 at 600mg/day reduced cortisol by 27.9% and stress scores by 44% in a landmark 60-day trial. Effective for both generalized anxiety and stress-related conditions
- **Sleep improvement:** Improves sleep onset latency, sleep quality, and total sleep time. The "somnifera" in its botanical name means "sleep-inducing." KSM-66 at 600mg/day improved sleep quality scores by 72% in one trial. Particularly effective for stress-induced insomnia rather than primary insomnia
- **Testosterone and male fertility:** Multiple trials show 10-22% increases in testosterone in stressed and healthy men. Also increases sperm count (167% increase in one study), sperm motility, and semen volume. Effects are most pronounced in men with stress-related hormonal suppression
- **Physical performance and body composition:** Increases in muscle strength (bench press +20kg, leg extension +9kg over placebo in one 8-week trial), VO2 max, and lean muscle mass. Reduces body fat percentage and exercise-induced muscle damage. Enhances recovery between training sessions
- **Cognitive function:** Improvements in reaction time, attention, executive function, and working memory in several trials. Enhanced information processing speed and task performance under stress. KSM-66 and Sensoril both show cognitive benefits through different mechanisms
- **Thyroid support:** Normalizes thyroid hormones in subclinical hypothyroidism. An 8-week trial showed significant increases in T3 (by 41.5%) and T4 (by 19.6%) with 600mg/day root extract. Beneficial for those with sluggish thyroid function, but requires caution (see side effects)
- **Blood sugar management:** Reduces fasting blood glucose and HbA1c in diabetic and pre-diabetic populations. AMPK activation and improved insulin sensitivity contribute to these effects
- **Cardiorespiratory endurance:** Improves VO2 max by 4.9-12% in trained and untrained individuals. Enhances oxygen utilization efficiency during exercise
- **Immune function:** Increases NK cell activity, immunoglobulin levels, and white blood cell counts. May improve response to vaccination and reduce frequency of upper respiratory infections
- **Anti-aging potential:** Withanolides show telomerase-activating properties in cell studies. Antioxidant effects reduce markers of cellular aging. Animal studies show lifespan extension, though human longevity data is lacking
