# Ashwagandha
## Classification

- **Category:** Adaptogen
- **Subcategory:** Herbal supplement / Ayurvedic botanical
- **Botanical name:** Withania somnifera
- **Family:** Solanaceae (nightshade family)
- **Active compounds:** Withanolides (primarily withaferin A, withanolide D, withanoside IV/V), glycowithanolides, alkaloids
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (USA); traditional medicine ingredient in India (Ayurvedic pharmacopeia); not FDA-approved as a drug
- **Common names:** Indian ginseng, winter cherry, Ashwagandha root
