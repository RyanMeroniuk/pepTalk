# L-Citrulline
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + L-Citrulline (Pump, Performance & Recovery)
- **Rationale:** GH secretagogues (CJC-1295, Ipamorelin) drive recovery, body composition, and tissue repair. L-citrulline maximizes blood flow and nutrient/oxygen delivery to muscle and reduces training fatigue and soreness. Better perfusion enhances delivery of the substrates and growth factors that GH-driven recovery depends on.
- **Protocol:** Citrulline malate 6-8 g pre-workout + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Best for:** Hypertrophy phases, training volume, recovery

#### IGF-1 LR3 + L-Citrulline (Nutrient Delivery for Anabolism)
- **Rationale:** IGF-1 LR3 strongly drives muscle protein synthesis and hyperplasia. Anabolism depends on nutrient and amino acid delivery to muscle — exactly what citrulline's enhanced blood flow provides. Citrulline supports the perfusion needed to feed IGF-1-driven growth.
- **Protocol:** Citrulline malate 6-8 g pre-workout + IGF-1 LR3 per protocol
- **Best for:** Aggressive muscle-building phases, nutrient partitioning

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### Tesamorelin + L-Citrulline (Vascular & Body Composition)
- **Rationale:** Tesamorelin reduces visceral fat and improves cardiovascular risk markers via GH release. Citrulline independently improves endothelial function and lowers blood pressure. The pairing reinforces vascular health alongside body recomposition.
- **Protocol:** Citrulline 3-6 g/day + Tesamorelin at prescribed dosing
- **Best for:** Cardiometabolic and body composition goals

#### MK-677 (Ibutamoren) + L-Citrulline (Volume & Pump Support)
- **Rationale:** MK-677 raises GH/IGF-1 and supports recovery and fullness; citrulline adds blood flow and reduces training fatigue, enhancing the ability to handle higher training volume.
- **Protocol:** Citrulline malate 6-8 g pre-workout + MK-677 10-25 mg before bed
- **Best for:** Bulking phases, high-volume training

#### BPC-157 + L-Citrulline (Perfusion-Assisted Repair)
- **Rationale:** BPC-157 promotes angiogenesis and tissue repair. Citrulline's enhanced blood flow complements angiogenesis by improving perfusion to recovering tissue, supporting nutrient delivery to injury sites.
- **Protocol:** Citrulline 3-6 g/day + BPC-157 250-500 mcg 2x/day
- **Best for:** Injury recovery while maintaining training

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### MOTS-c + L-Citrulline (Metabolic & Vascular Endurance)
- **Rationale:** MOTS-c acts as an exercise mimetic improving metabolic flexibility and endurance; citrulline reduces fatigue via ammonia clearance and improves oxygen delivery. Together they support aerobic capacity and endurance performance.
- **Protocol:** Citrulline malate 6-8 g pre-endurance + MOTS-c at standard dosing
- **Best for:** Endurance and metabolic performance

### Supplement Synergies

#### L-Citrulline + Creatine (Delivery + Power)
- **Rationale:** Creatine fuels the phosphocreatine/ATP system for power and strength but depends on delivery to muscle; citrulline increases blood flow and nutrient/creatine delivery while reducing fatigue. Creatine builds the engine, citrulline improves the fuel line. A foundational non-stimulant performance pairing.
- **Protocol:** Creatine 5 g/day (any time) + Citrulline malate 6-8 g pre-workout
- **Best for:** Strength, hypertrophy, pump, and training volume — one of the most popular evidence-based stacks
