# L-Citrulline
## Forms and Bioavailability

### L-Citrulline (Pure / Free Form)
- **Just citrulline, no malate** — the most direct way to raise plasma arginine and nitric oxide
- **Highly bioavailable** — efficiently absorbed and converted to arginine in the kidneys, bypassing the first-pass breakdown that limits oral arginine
- **Effective dose:** 3-6 g (lower gram-for-gram than citrulline malate because there's no malate weight)
- **Best when the goal is the NO/blood-flow/blood-pressure effect specifically**

### Citrulline Malate (Most Studied for Performance)
- **Citrulline bound to malic acid**, commonly in a **2:1 ratio** (2 parts citrulline to 1 part malate)
- **The form used in most performance research** (e.g., the 8 g protocols showing increased reps and reduced soreness)
- **Effective dose:** 6-8 g of citrulline malate (which delivers ~4.5-5.5 g of actual citrulline at 2:1)
- **Malate may add aerobic energy support** via the TCA cycle, though its independent contribution is debated
- **Watch the ratio:** some products use 1:1 — for the same total grams, 1:1 delivers more citrulline but less malate. Check the label; the 2:1 ratio matches most studies.

### Bioavailability Notes
- **Superior to L-arginine** — citrulline raises plasma arginine more effectively than equivalent arginine doses (Schwedhelm et al., 2008), the central reason to choose citrulline
- **Peak plasma arginine** occurs roughly 1-2 hours after ingestion — supporting pre-workout timing ~60 minutes before training
- **No transporter saturation concerns** at typical doses

### Powder vs Capsules
- **Powder** — most economical and easiest to dose at the gram quantities needed (capsules would require many pills); slightly sour/tart taste, especially the malate form
- **Capsules** — convenient but impractical for full 6-8 g doses
- **Powder is the practical choice** for effective dosing

### Practical Guidance
- **For performance/pump:** citrulline malate 6-8 g (2:1) pre-workout
- **For blood pressure / NO health:** pure L-citrulline 3-6 g/day
- **Avoid underdosed "NO booster" blends** — many proprietary pre-workouts contain far less citrulline than the effective dose; buy bulk citrulline or citrulline malate and dose it properly
- **Don't substitute arginine** expecting the same effect — citrulline is the more effective NO precursor orally
