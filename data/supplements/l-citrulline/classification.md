# L-Citrulline
## Classification

- **Category:** Non-essential (non-proteinogenic) amino acid; nitric oxide precursor
- **Chemical class:** Alpha-amino acid (a non-protein amino acid involved in the urea cycle)
- **Molecular formula:** C6H13N3O3
- **Supplement type:** Ergogenic aid / performance supplement / vascular and blood flow supplement
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (OTC); GRAS status; not banned by athletic organizations
- **Primary systems:** Cardiovascular (nitric oxide, blood flow, blood pressure), muscular (exercise performance, "pump"), urea cycle (ammonia clearance)
- **Common forms:** L-citrulline (free form) and citrulline malate (citrulline bound to malic acid in a ~2:1 ratio)
- **Endogenous role:** Produced in the body as an intermediate of the urea cycle and from the conversion of glutamine in the intestine; efficiently converted to L-arginine in the kidneys
- **Distinctive feature:** Raises plasma arginine more effectively than supplemental arginine itself, because it bypasses first-pass intestinal and hepatic breakdown
