# L-Citrulline
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

### The Reality: Citrulline Absorbs Well On Its Own
- **Citrulline has high oral bioavailability** and is efficiently converted to arginine in the kidneys without needing food cofactors
- **The main practical lever is timing** (pre-workout for acute effects) rather than food pairing

### Antioxidants / Polyphenols (Support NO Pathway)
- **Nitric oxide is fragile** — it is degraded by oxidative stress. Dietary antioxidants help preserve NO bioavailability, supporting citrulline's downstream effect.
- **Vitamin C** — supports endothelial NO and recycles the NOS cofactor BH4 (tetrahydrobiopterin); citrus, berries, peppers
- **Polyphenol-rich foods** — dark berries, beets, pomegranate, dark chocolate (flavanols) complement citrulline's vascular effects

### Dietary Nitrates (Complementary NO Pathway)
- **Beets, leafy greens (spinach, arugula), and other nitrate-rich vegetables** raise NO through the separate nitrate→nitrite→NO pathway
- **Combining citrulline (arginine→NO pathway) with dietary nitrates** hits NO production from two independent routes — a popular and synergistic pre-workout strategy

### B Vitamins (Urea Cycle / NOS Support)
- **Folate and B-vitamins** support endothelial function and the methylation environment around NO metabolism
- Adequate B-vitamin status supports the enzymatic context citrulline operates in

### Practical Pairings
- **Pre-workout with beet juice or a nitrate source** — dual NO pathway support
- **With vitamin C or polyphenol-rich foods** — protect the NO that citrulline helps produce
- **With creatine** — citrulline improves blood flow/delivery; creatine fuels ATP (complementary, see peptide-synergies)

### What Doesn't Matter
- **Fed vs fasted** — citrulline absorbs well either way; empty stomach is fine pre-workout
- **Hot vs cold liquid** — citrulline is stable and water-soluble
- **Fat content** — irrelevant to citrulline absorption

### Bottom Line
- **Citrulline doesn't need help being absorbed.** The smart moves are timing it pre-workout, protecting NO with antioxidants, and stacking it with dietary nitrates for additive blood-flow effects.
