# L-Citrulline
## Drug Interactions

### Contraindicated / Avoid Without Medical Supervision
- **Nitrates (nitroglycerin, isosorbide mononitrate/dinitrate)** — these drugs work through the nitric oxide pathway. Citrulline amplifies NO, and combining the two can cause dangerous, excessive drops in blood pressure. This combination should be avoided unless explicitly cleared by a physician.
- **PDE5 inhibitors (sildenafil/Viagra, tadalafil/Cialis, vardenafil)** — these potentiate NO-mediated vasodilation. Adding citrulline can cause excessive hypotension. Avoid combining without medical guidance.

### Moderate Interactions (Use with Caution / Monitor)
- **Antihypertensives (ACE inhibitors, ARBs, calcium channel blockers, beta-blockers, diuretics)** — citrulline independently lowers blood pressure via nitric oxide. Combined use can be additive, risking hypotension. Monitor blood pressure and consult your physician.
- **Other vasodilators** — additive blood-pressure-lowering effect; monitor

### Mild Interactions (Monitor)
- **Anticoagulants / blood thinners (warfarin)** — no well-established direct interaction, but improved blood flow and any supplement addition warrants informing your physician; monitor
- **Stimulant pre-workouts** — no harmful chemical interaction; citrulline is commonly combined with caffeine, but be mindful of the opposing cardiovascular effects (caffeine can raise BP, citrulline lowers it)

### Timing-Sensitive Notes
- **Separate from medications with narrow therapeutic windows when uncertain** — taking citrulline a few hours apart from sensitive medications avoids theoretical concerns, though no strong citrulline-specific absorption timing conflict is documented
- **Levothyroxine / thyroid meds** — no documented direct interaction; standard practice is to take thyroid medication on an empty stomach separate from other supplements, so keep citrulline to a different time of day

### Positive / Neutral Interactions
- **Creatine** — no interaction; complementary performance support (delivery + ATP)
- **Beta-alanine** — no interaction; common complementary pre-workout combo
- **Dietary nitrates (beet juice)** — additive NO support via a separate pathway (beneficial, but compounds the BP-lowering effect — relevant for those on antihypertensives)

### Population Cautions
- **Low blood pressure** — citrulline can worsen hypotension; use caution
- **Cardiovascular disease** — consult a cardiologist, especially if on nitrates or BP medications
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding** — insufficient safety data for supplemental doses; consult an OB/GYN
- **Surgery** — inform your surgeon; consider pausing before procedures due to BP/NO effects

### Safety Notes
- **Always inform healthcare providers** of citrulline use, especially if taking nitrates, PDE5 inhibitors, or blood-pressure medications — the nitrate and PDE5 combinations are the most important to flag
- **This is educational information, not medical advice** — review interactions with a pharmacist or physician
