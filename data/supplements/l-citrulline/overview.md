# L-Citrulline
## Overview

L-Citrulline is a non-essential amino acid best known as a nitric oxide (NO) precursor and one of the most effective "blood flow" and "pump" supplements available. Its key trick is counterintuitive: although the body uses arginine to make nitric oxide, supplementing arginine directly is inefficient because much of it is broken down in the gut and liver before reaching circulation. L-citrulline bypasses this first-pass metabolism and is converted to arginine in the kidneys, raising plasma arginine and NO more effectively than arginine itself. The result is enhanced vasodilation, blood flow, and nutrient delivery to muscle — which translates into better exercise performance, a stronger muscle "pump," reduced fatigue, and a mild reduction in blood pressure. It is commonly sold as plain L-citrulline or as citrulline malate (citrulline bound to malic acid, often in a 2:1 ratio), the form used in much of the performance research.

### Why It Matters

- **Nitric oxide production:** Citrulline → arginine → nitric oxide via nitric oxide synthase, driving vasodilation and improved blood flow
- **Superior to arginine:** Raises plasma arginine more effectively than supplemental arginine because it avoids first-pass breakdown
- **Exercise performance:** Reduces fatigue, increases reps to failure, and improves endurance and recovery
- **The pump:** Enhanced blood flow produces the visible, sustained muscle fullness lifters value
- **Cardiovascular:** Modest blood pressure reduction and improved endothelial/vascular function
- **Ammonia clearance:** As a urea cycle intermediate, citrulline supports clearance of exercise-generated ammonia, contributing to reduced fatigue

### Who Benefits Most

- **Resistance-training athletes** — improved training volume, reps to failure, and pump
- **Endurance athletes** — reduced fatigue and improved aerobic performance
- **People with mildly elevated blood pressure** — modest BP-lowering and vascular support
- **Older adults** — endothelial function and blood flow tend to decline with age
- **Anyone seeking a non-stimulant pre-workout component** — citrulline is not a stimulant and stacks well with caffeine
