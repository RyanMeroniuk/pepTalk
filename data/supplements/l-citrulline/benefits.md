# L-Citrulline
## Benefits

### Exercise Performance (Strong Evidence)
- **Increases reps to failure** — citrulline malate (8 g) increased repetitions in resistance training and reduced post-exercise soreness (Pérez-Guisado & Jakeman, 2010)
- **Reduces fatigue** — both peripheral (muscle) and central (CNS) fatigue, partly via ammonia clearance
- **Improves endurance performance** — better time-to-exhaustion and aerobic output in several trials
- **Enhances training volume** — more total work per session, which compounds into better long-term adaptation

### Blood Flow & The "Pump"
- **Increases muscle blood flow** — via elevated nitric oxide, producing visible and sustained muscle fullness
- **Improves nutrient and oxygen delivery** to working muscle
- **Enhances vascularity** during and after training — a sought-after effect among lifters

### Cardiovascular (Good Evidence)
- **Lowers blood pressure** — modest reductions in systolic and diastolic BP, more pronounced in those with elevated baseline pressure or endothelial dysfunction
- **Improves endothelial function** — supports the health and responsiveness of blood vessel linings
- **More effective than arginine** for raising plasma arginine and supporting NO — the key practical advantage
- **Erectile function support** — preliminary evidence that citrulline improves mild erectile dysfunction via the NO pathway (Cormio et al., 2011)

### Recovery
- **Reduces muscle soreness** — citrulline malate reduced delayed-onset muscle soreness (DOMS) at 24-48 hours in several studies
- **Supports ammonia clearance** post-exercise, aiding recovery

### Other
- **Ammonia detoxification** — relevant in urea cycle support; citrulline is used clinically in certain urea cycle disorders (under medical supervision)
- **Potential growth hormone support** — modest, less consistent than vascular effects

### Realistic Expectations
- **Citrulline is a vascular/endurance/pump supplement, not a strength powerhouse** — for raw strength and power, creatine remains the gold standard; citrulline complements it
- **Best results require adequate dosing** — many products are underdosed; the effective range is 6-8 g citrulline malate or 3-6 g pure L-citrulline
- **Effects build with consistent use** for blood pressure; performance/pump effects are more acute (felt the same session)
