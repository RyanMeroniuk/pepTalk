# L-Citrulline
## Dosing Protocols

### Performance / Pump (Citrulline Malate)
- **Dose:** 6-8 g citrulline malate (2:1 ratio), delivering ~4.5-5.5 g citrulline
- **Timing:** ~60 minutes before training (peak plasma arginine occurs ~1-2 hours post-ingestion)
- **Notes:** This is the protocol used in most performance studies showing increased reps and reduced soreness

### Performance / Pump (Pure L-Citrulline)
- **Dose:** 3-6 g pure L-citrulline
- **Timing:** ~60 minutes pre-workout
- **Notes:** Gram-for-gram more concentrated in citrulline than the malate form (no malate weight); choose this if you only want the NO effect

### Blood Pressure / Vascular Health
- **Dose:** 3-6 g pure L-citrulline per day
- **Timing:** Once daily or split; consistency matters for vascular adaptation
- **Duration:** Benefits on blood pressure and endothelial function build over days to weeks of consistent use
- **Notes:** Watermelon-derived citrulline studies used similar ranges

### Endurance
- **Dose:** 6-8 g citrulline malate, ~60 minutes before endurance sessions
- **Notes:** Supports time-to-exhaustion and ammonia clearance

### Older Adults / General Vascular Support
- **Dose:** 3-6 g L-citrulline daily
- **Notes:** Endothelial function declines with age; daily citrulline supports NO availability

### Timing Considerations
- **Pre-workout (~60 min before)** is optimal for acute performance and pump effects, aligning with peak plasma arginine
- **For blood pressure**, time of day is less important than daily consistency
- **Empty stomach is fine** — citrulline absorbs well with or without food
- **Can be combined freely** with caffeine, creatine, beta-alanine, and most pre-workout ingredients

### Stacking Doses
- **With creatine:** citrulline improves blood flow/delivery while creatine fuels ATP — a complementary performance pairing (see peptide-synergies)
- **With beta-alanine:** common pre-workout combo — citrulline for pump, beta-alanine for muscular endurance buffering

### Tolerance & Cycling
- **No tolerance buildup or need to cycle** — citrulline can be used continuously
- **No loading phase required** — performance/pump effects are felt acutely from the first effective dose

### Safety Ceiling
- **Doses up to ~10 g** have been used with good tolerability; GI upset is the main limiter at very high single doses
