# L-Citrulline
## Things to Avoid

### Dosing Mistakes
- **Underdosing** — the single most common error. Many "NO booster" pre-workouts contain only 1-2 g citrulline, far below the effective 6-8 g citrulline malate (or 3-6 g pure L-citrulline). Buy bulk citrulline and dose it properly.
- **Confusing the malate ratio** — 6-8 g of 2:1 citrulline malate delivers ~4.5-5.5 g citrulline; a 1:1 product delivers more citrulline but less malate per gram. Know which you have.
- **Expecting strength gains like creatine** — citrulline improves blood flow, pump, endurance, and reduces fatigue/soreness; it is not a primary strength/power agent. Use creatine for that and citrulline alongside it.

### Choosing the Wrong NO Precursor
- **Don't use L-arginine expecting better results** — oral arginine is largely degraded before reaching circulation and causes more GI upset; citrulline raises plasma arginine more effectively. Citrulline is the smarter oral NO precursor.

### Population-Specific Cautions
- **Low blood pressure / on antihypertensives** — citrulline lowers BP; monitor and consult a physician
- **PDE5 inhibitors (Viagra/sildenafil, Cialis/tadalafil) and nitrates** — citrulline amplifies the same NO pathway; combining risks excessive hypotension. Avoid or use only with medical guidance.
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding** — insufficient safety data for supplemental doses; consult a healthcare provider
- **Surgery** — because citrulline affects blood pressure and NO, inform your surgeon and consider pausing before procedures

### Medical Cautions
- **Inform your physician** if you have cardiovascular disease, low blood pressure, or take blood-pressure or erectile-dysfunction medications
- **This is educational information, not medical advice** — review interactions with a pharmacist or physician

### Behaviors That Undermine Benefits
- **Pairing citrulline with an oxidative, ultra-processed, high-alcohol lifestyle** that degrades nitric oxide
- **Frequent antibacterial mouthwash** if relying on the complementary dietary-nitrate pathway (it kills nitrate-reducing oral bacteria)
- **Expecting acute pump effects from a single tiny dose** — effective dosing and pre-workout timing (~60 min before) matter
