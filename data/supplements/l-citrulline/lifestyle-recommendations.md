# L-Citrulline
## Lifestyle Recommendations

### Training (Maximize the Pump & Performance)
- **Take 6-8 g citrulline malate (or 3-6 g L-citrulline) ~60 minutes before training** — aligns with peak plasma arginine for the best pump and performance window
- **Use the extra capacity** — citrulline increases reps to failure and reduces fatigue; capitalize with progressive overload rather than just enjoying the pump
- **Best for high-volume, hypertrophy-style training** — the blood flow and fatigue-resistance effects shine in moderate-rep, high-volume work
- **Stack with creatine** — citrulline improves delivery, creatine fuels ATP; a complementary performance base

### Cardiovascular Health
- **For blood pressure, dose daily (3-6 g L-citrulline) consistently** — vascular adaptation builds over days to weeks
- **Pair with aerobic exercise** — both improve endothelial function and NO availability
- **Combine with a whole-food, lower-sodium diet** and nitrate-rich vegetables (beets, leafy greens) for additive blood-flow benefits

### Nutrition
- **Protect nitric oxide with antioxidants** — vitamin C, polyphenol-rich foods (berries, beets, pomegranate) preserve the NO citrulline helps produce
- **Add dietary nitrates** — beet juice and leafy greens hit NO via a second, independent pathway
- **Minimize oxidative, ultra-processed foods** that degrade NO

### Recovery
- **Citrulline reduces DOMS** — useful during high-volume training blocks
- **Supports ammonia clearance** post-exercise, aiding recovery between sessions

### Stacking Strategy
- **Citrulline + creatine + beta-alanine** is a well-rounded non-stimulant performance trio (pump + power + endurance buffering)
- **Add caffeine** if a stimulant effect is desired — citrulline is non-stimulant and combines cleanly

### Sleep & Timing Flexibility
- **Citrulline is not a stimulant** — it won't disrupt sleep, so evening training sessions are fine
- **For BP goals**, time of day is unimportant; consistency is what matters

### Long-Term Mindset
- **Acute effects (pump, performance) are felt the same session** with proper dosing; **vascular/BP benefits accumulate** with daily use
- **Inexpensive and well-tolerated** — citrulline is a low-risk, high-value addition to a performance or cardiovascular protocol
