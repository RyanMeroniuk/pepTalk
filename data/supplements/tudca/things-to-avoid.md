# TUDCA (Tauroursodeoxycholic Acid)
## Things to Avoid

### Treating TUDCA as a License to Use Hepatotoxic Compounds
- The most important caution. Using TUDCA does NOT make oral anabolics, prohormones, or other hepatotoxic compounds "safe." It mitigates liver stress but does not eliminate risk. This is risk reduction, not a free pass. Monitor liver enzymes with blood work and respect cycle limits.

### Skipping Blood Work During Stressful Cycles
- If using TUDCA for on-cycle liver protection, you still need baseline and periodic liver panels (ALT, AST, GGT, bilirubin). TUDCA support does not replace monitoring.

### Taking It at the Same Time as Bile-Acid Sequestrants
- Cholestyramine, colestipol, and colesevelam bind bile acids and will reduce TUDCA absorption. Separate dosing by several hours (see drug interactions).

### Using It with Complete Biliary Obstruction
- Bile-acid (choleretic) therapy is contraindicated in complete biliary obstruction and could worsen the situation. Anyone with significant gallbladder/biliary disease needs medical evaluation before use.

### Under-Dosing in Combination Products
- Some liver-support blends contain only a small amount of TUDCA below the 250-500mg+ used for meaningful effect. Verify the per-serving dose; single-ingredient TUDCA gives the most control.

### Buying Low-Purity or Bile-Derived Products
- Choose high-purity (98%+), third-party-tested, synthetic TUDCA. Avoid products implying animal-bile sourcing or with vague purity claims.

### Starting at a High Dose
- Jumping to 1,000mg+ can cause GI upset and loose stools. Start at 250-500mg, take with food, and titrate up if needed.

### Self-Prescribing in Pregnancy or Serious Liver Disease
- Although the parent compound UDCA is used clinically (e.g., for intrahepatic cholestasis of pregnancy) under supervision, OTC TUDCA should not be self-prescribed in pregnancy or in serious liver/biliary disease. These require a physician's guidance.

### Behaviors That Undermine TUDCA's Benefits
- **Continued alcohol use** — Directly hepatotoxic; works against the liver support TUDCA provides
- **High-fructose, ultra-processed diet** — Drives fatty liver, counteracting TUDCA's metabolic benefit
- **Ignoring the underlying stressor** — TUDCA supports the liver but cannot offset ongoing high hepatotoxic exposure; reduce the burden

### Medical Cautions
- **Biliary obstruction, active gallbladder disease** — Medical evaluation required
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding** — Only under physician guidance
- **Inform healthcare providers** of all supplements, especially before procedures or when on bile-acid-binding or other medications
