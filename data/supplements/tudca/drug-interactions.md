# TUDCA (Tauroursodeoxycholic Acid)
## Drug Interactions

As a bile acid, TUDCA's main interactions involve other agents that affect bile acids or are absorbed via bile-dependent processes. Organized by significance.

### Timing-Sensitive Interactions (Separate Dosing)
- **Bile-acid sequestrants (cholestyramine, colestipol, colesevelam)** — These cholesterol-lowering drugs bind bile acids in the gut, which will bind and reduce the absorption of TUDCA. Take TUDCA several hours apart (typically TUDCA at least 1 hour before or 4-6 hours after the sequestrant) to avoid this. This is the most clinically relevant timing interaction.
- **Aluminum-containing antacids** — May also bind bile acids; separate dosing by a few hours to be safe.

### Moderate Interactions (Use with Caution / Monitor)
- **Drugs causing or worsening cholestasis** — Some medications impair bile flow. While TUDCA may be used to support bile flow, anyone on such medications with a liver/biliary condition should coordinate with their physician rather than self-manage.
- **Hepatotoxic medications and oral anabolic/PED compounds** — TUDCA is often used alongside hepatically stressful compounds for liver support. It mitigates but does not neutralize hepatotoxicity. Monitor liver enzymes; do not assume TUDCA makes a hepatotoxic regimen safe.

### Mild Interactions (Monitor)
- **Fat-soluble drug absorption** — Because bile acids participate in fat and fat-soluble-compound absorption, TUDCA could theoretically influence the absorption of some fat-soluble drugs or nutrients. Clinical significance is generally low; maintain consistent timing.
- **Diabetes medications** — TUDCA may improve insulin sensitivity (emerging evidence). When combined with glucose-lowering drugs, monitor blood sugar as a general precaution, though the effect is mild.
- **Hepatically metabolized drugs** — By influencing liver function and bile, TUDCA could theoretically affect the handling of some drugs. No major specific interactions are well established; inform your prescriber.

### Conditions Requiring Caution
- **Complete biliary obstruction** — Contraindicated; choleretic (bile-flow-stimulating) agents could worsen obstruction. Requires medical evaluation.
- **Active gallbladder disease / gallstones** — Effects on bile and gallstones depend on the specific condition; use only under medical guidance.
- **Pregnancy and breastfeeding** — The parent compound UDCA is used clinically in pregnancy for intrahepatic cholestasis under supervision, but OTC TUDCA should be used in pregnancy only under a physician's guidance.

### Beneficial / Neutral Combinations
- **NAC (N-acetylcysteine)** — Complementary liver support via glutathione; no interaction concern
- **Omega-3** — Anti-inflammatory, fatty-liver support; no interaction concern
- **Milk thistle, B-complex** — Common liver-support stack partners

### General Note
- Separate TUDCA from bile-acid sequestrants and aluminum antacids by several hours
- Always inform healthcare providers of all supplements, especially during hepatically stressful protocols, when on bile-acid-binding drugs, or if you have any liver/biliary condition
