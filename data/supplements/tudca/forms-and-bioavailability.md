# TUDCA (Tauroursodeoxycholic Acid)
## Forms and Bioavailability

### TUDCA (Tauroursodeoxycholic Acid) — The Supplement Form
- **Taurine-conjugated UDCA** — TUDCA is the taurine conjugate of ursodeoxycholic acid, more water-soluble and absorbed somewhat more readily than unconjugated UDCA
- **Synthetic/semi-synthetic production** — Modern commercial TUDCA is produced synthetically (historically derived from bear bile in traditional medicine; reputable supplements are not bile-derived). Vegan/synthetic forms are standard.
- **Capsule form is standard** — Usually sold in 250mg or 500mg capsules due to its very bitter taste, which makes powder unpleasant

### TUDCA vs UDCA (Ursodeoxycholic Acid)
- **UDCA** — The unconjugated parent compound; an established prescription drug (Ursodiol/Actigall) for cholestatic liver disease and gallstones, with the strongest clinical evidence
- **TUDCA** — The taurine conjugate; more hydrophilic, with strong cytoprotective/ER-stress and mitochondrial mechanisms in research. Available OTC as a supplement.
- **Relationship:** In the body, the two are interconvertible to a degree; both modify the bile acid pool favorably. TUDCA is the form sold as a supplement; UDCA is the form with the most prescription evidence.

### Purity & Quality
- **Look for high-purity (98%+) TUDCA** — Quality varies; choose products specifying purity percentage
- **Third-party testing** — NSF, USP, or independent lab verification preferred, given variable supplement quality in this category
- **Avoid bile-derived products** — Reputable TUDCA is synthetic; avoid any product implying animal-bile sourcing

### Bioavailability
- **Oral absorption** — TUDCA is absorbed in the small intestine and enters enterohepatic circulation, concentrating in the liver and bile (first-pass uptake by the liver is high, which is favorable for liver-targeted effects)
- **Enterohepatic recycling** — Like other bile acids, TUDCA is recycled between gut and liver, supporting its hepatic concentration
- **Systemic distribution** — A portion reaches systemic circulation and can cross into the CNS, relevant to its neuroprotective research
- **Taking with food** — Bile acids are physiologically released with meals; some users take TUDCA with food (especially fat-containing meals) to align with natural bile flow, though it can be taken fasted

### Combination Products
- Sometimes combined with NAC, milk thistle, or other liver-support ingredients in hepatic-support blends
- Verify the actual TUDCA dose per serving, as some blends under-dose it below the 250-500mg+ used for meaningful effect

### Practical Notes
- Choose a single-ingredient, high-purity TUDCA for precise dosing
- Capsules are preferred over powder due to the intensely bitter taste of bile acids
