# TUDCA (Tauroursodeoxycholic Acid)
## Lifestyle Recommendations

### Minimize Alcohol
- This is the single most impactful lifestyle factor for a TUDCA user. Alcohol is directly hepatotoxic and depletes glutathione, working against the liver support TUDCA provides. Minimizing or eliminating alcohol dramatically amplifies TUDCA's hepatoprotective benefit.

### Eat a Liver-Supportive, Low-Fructose Diet
- Reduce refined sugar and high-fructose foods (major drivers of fatty liver). Emphasize vegetables (especially cruciferous and bitter greens), berries, lean protein, healthy fats (olive oil, fatty fish), and fiber. This supports the liver TUDCA is protecting and reinforces its metabolic benefits.

### Monitor Liver Markers with Blood Work
- Especially during hepatically stressful cycles, get baseline and periodic liver panels (ALT, AST, GGT, bilirubin). TUDCA supports the liver, but objective monitoring tells you whether your protocol is safe. This pairs directly with the app's blood-work lifestyle topic.

### Respect Cycle Limits & Reduce the Underlying Stressor
- For on-cycle protection users: TUDCA mitigates but does not eliminate hepatotoxic risk. Respect dose and duration limits of the stressful compound, and prioritize reducing the liver burden over relying on TUDCA to offset it.

### Exercise Regularly
- Physical activity improves insulin sensitivity, reduces liver fat, and supports overall metabolic health — complementing TUDCA's metabolic and hepatic benefits. A mix of resistance and aerobic training is ideal.

### Maintain a Healthy Body Weight
- Excess visceral fat drives fatty liver and metabolic dysfunction. Weight management is one of the most effective interventions for liver health, amplifying TUDCA's benefits.

### Build a Liver-Support Stack
- TUDCA pairs well with NAC (supports glutathione, the liver's main antioxidant) and omega-3 (anti-inflammatory, supports fatty-liver health). Milk thistle and a B-complex are common additions. A coherent stack covers bile flow, antioxidant defense, and anti-inflammatory support.

### Stay Hydrated
- Adequate hydration supports overall metabolic and excretory function. Drink sufficient water daily.

### Prioritize Sleep & Manage Stress
- Poor sleep and chronic stress worsen metabolic health and insulin resistance, indirectly burdening the liver. Aim for 7-9 hours of sleep and incorporate stress management.

### Take It Sensibly with Meals
- Take TUDCA with a meal containing some healthy fat to align with natural bile flow and reduce GI upset. Separate it from any bile-acid-binding medications by several hours.

### Be Honest About Hepatotoxic Exposures
- Track and minimize other liver stressors (excessive acetaminophen, unverified herbal products, recreational hepatotoxins). TUDCA cannot offset an ongoing high burden.
