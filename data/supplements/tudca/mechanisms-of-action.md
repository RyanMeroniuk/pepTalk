# TUDCA (Tauroursodeoxycholic Acid)
## Mechanisms of Action

### 1. ER Stress Reduction (Chemical Chaperone — Key Mechanism)
- TUDCA acts as a chemical chaperone, assisting proper protein folding and reducing the accumulation of misfolded proteins in the endoplasmic reticulum
- It dampens the unfolded protein response (UPR), reducing ER-stress signaling through pathways such as PERK, IRE1, and ATF6
- Because ER stress drives cell dysfunction in liver disease, insulin resistance, and neurodegeneration, this mechanism underlies much of TUDCA's broad cytoprotective potential

### 2. Mitochondrial Stabilization & Anti-Apoptosis
- TUDCA stabilizes the mitochondrial membrane and inhibits the intrinsic (mitochondrial) apoptosis pathway
- It reduces cytochrome c release, Bax translocation, and caspase activation, protecting cells from programmed death under stress
- This anti-apoptotic, mitochondria-protective action is central to its neuroprotective and hepatoprotective effects

### 3. Bile Flow Enhancement (Choleresis) & Bile Pool Modification
- TUDCA stimulates bile secretion and flow, helping clear bile and reduce cholestasis
- It shifts the composition of the bile acid pool toward more hydrophilic, less cytotoxic bile acids, replacing harsher hydrophobic bile acids that damage hepatocytes and bile duct cells
- This protects the liver and biliary system and underlies its use in cholestatic conditions and gallstone management (as with its parent UDCA)

### 4. Hepatocyte Protection
- By reducing bile-acid toxicity, ER stress, and apoptosis, TUDCA protects liver cells from injury
- It reduces markers of liver stress and supports hepatocyte survival under toxic, cholestatic, or metabolic stress
- This is the basis for "liver support" and on-cycle protection use

### 5. Anti-Inflammatory & Antioxidant Effects
- TUDCA reduces inflammatory signaling (including NF-kB-mediated pathways) and oxidative stress in stressed tissues
- It supports cellular antioxidant defenses, complementing glutathione-based antioxidant systems

### 6. Insulin Sensitivity & Metabolic Effects
- By reducing ER stress in metabolic tissues (liver, adipose), TUDCA may improve insulin signaling
- Early clinical work shows improved hepatic and muscle insulin sensitivity in some contexts, though evidence is still emerging

### 7. Neuroprotection
- TUDCA crosses into the central nervous system and protects neurons via its anti-apoptotic, ER-stress-reducing, and mitochondrial-stabilizing actions
- This drives research interest in ALS, Huntington's, Alzheimer's, and Parkinson's models — preliminary and not yet established clinical uses
