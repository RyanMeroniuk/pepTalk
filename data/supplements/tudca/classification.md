# TUDCA (Tauroursodeoxycholic Acid)
## Classification

- **Category:** Liver Support / Bile Acid / Cytoprotective / Mitochondrial & ER Stress
- **Type:** Taurine-conjugated bile acid; hydrophilic (water-soluble) secondary bile acid
- **Chemical class:** Bile acid — the taurine conjugate of ursodeoxycholic acid (UDCA); a hydrophilic bile acid that is gentler on cells than hydrophobic bile acids
- **Source:** Present in small amounts in human bile (and historically concentrated in bear bile in traditional medicine); commercial TUDCA is produced synthetically/semi-synthetically. Vegan/synthetic versions are widely available.
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (USA, OTC); ursodeoxycholic acid (UDCA, the parent compound) is an approved prescription drug for cholestatic liver disease and gallstones in many countries
- **Primary function:** Reduces endoplasmic reticulum (ER) stress, stabilizes mitochondria, protects cells from apoptosis, enhances bile flow, and supports liver health
- **Primary systems:** Hepatobiliary (liver, bile flow, gallbladder), cellular (ER and mitochondrial stress response), and broadly cytoprotective (neurons, kidney, eye in research)
- **Key relevance to this app:** TUDCA is widely used for liver support and "on-cycle" organ protection by peptide and performance/PED users, making it highly relevant to optimization protocols involving hepatic stress.
