# TUDCA (Tauroursodeoxycholic Acid)
## Benefits

### Liver Support & Bile Flow (Strongest Rationale)
- **Improved bile flow** — TUDCA stimulates choleresis and helps relieve cholestasis (impaired bile flow), supporting healthy bile clearance.
- **Hepatocyte protection** — By reducing bile-acid toxicity, ER stress, and apoptosis, TUDCA protects liver cells from injury. Its parent compound (UDCA) is an established prescription liver drug for cholestatic conditions.
- **Improved liver enzymes** — In cholestatic and some metabolic liver conditions, bile-acid therapy can improve markers like ALT, AST, GGT, and bilirubin. (UDCA has the strongest clinical evidence; TUDCA shares the mechanism.)
- **On-cycle organ protection** — Widely used by performance/PED and peptide users to support liver health during hepatically stressful cycles (oral anabolics, prohormones). This is the most common real-world application in optimization communities — supportive, not a license to use hepatotoxic compounds safely.

### Gallbladder & Cholestasis Support
- **Gallstone management** — As with UDCA, bile-acid therapy can help dissolve certain cholesterol gallstones and reduce biliary cholesterol saturation.
- **Cholestasis of various causes** — Used to support bile flow in cholestatic states (under medical guidance for clinical conditions).

### Metabolic Health (Emerging)
- **Improved insulin sensitivity** — By reducing ER stress in liver and muscle, early clinical work shows TUDCA may improve insulin sensitivity. Evidence is preliminary.
- **Fatty liver (NAFLD/MASLD) support** — Mechanistically promising for non-alcoholic/metabolic fatty liver disease via ER-stress and anti-inflammatory effects; clinical evidence is still developing.

### Neuroprotection (Preliminary/Research)
- **Neurodegenerative disease models** — TUDCA's anti-apoptotic and ER-stress-reducing actions show neuroprotective effects in preclinical models of ALS, Huntington's, Alzheimer's, and Parkinson's. Some early human trials (e.g., in ALS) are encouraging but not conclusive. Flagged as preliminary — not an established treatment.

### Eye Health (Preclinical)
- **Retinal protection** — Preclinical research suggests TUDCA protects retinal cells in models of retinal degeneration via its anti-apoptotic effects. Human evidence is lacking.

### Cellular Protection (Broad)
- **Cytoprotection under stress** — TUDCA's chemical-chaperone and mitochondrial-stabilizing actions provide broad protection to cells under toxic, metabolic, or oxidative stress.

### Realistic Expectations
- TUDCA's strongest evidence is in bile flow and liver protection (sharing mechanism with prescription UDCA). Metabolic and neuroprotective uses are promising but preliminary.
- It is best used as targeted liver/cellular support, often stacked with NAC and omega-3, and is not a substitute for avoiding hepatotoxic exposures.
