# TUDCA (Tauroursodeoxycholic Acid)
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

As a bile acid, TUDCA's absorption and physiological action are tied to the digestive process. Pairing it with the right foods aligns it with natural bile flow and supports its liver-protective goals.

### Dietary Fat (Aligns with Natural Bile Release)
- Bile acids are physiologically released in response to dietary fat to emulsify it
- Taking TUDCA with a meal containing some healthy fat (olive oil, avocado, nuts, fatty fish) aligns it with the body's natural bile flow and gallbladder contraction
- **Practical fat sources to pair:** olive oil, avocado, nuts/seeds, eggs, fatty fish
- This is the most logical pairing for a bile acid supplement

### Taurine-Rich Foods (Bile Acid Conjugation Substrate)
- Taurine is the amino acid conjugated to UDCA to form TUDCA; adequate taurine supports the body's bile acid conjugation
- **Shellfish, fish, meat, poultry** provide taurine
- Some users co-supplement taurine for its own benefits and bile support

### Bitter & Cholagogue Foods (Support Bile Flow)
- **Dandelion greens, arugula, radicchio, beets** stimulate bile flow, complementing TUDCA's choleretic action
- Including bitters with the meal supports overall biliary function

### Antioxidant & Liver-Supportive Foods
- A liver under less oxidative stress responds better to support
- **Cruciferous vegetables, berries, leafy greens, sulfur-rich foods (garlic, onions, eggs)** support glutathione and liver detoxification, complementing TUDCA's cytoprotective action

### Omega-3 Foods (Anti-Inflammatory, Fatty-Liver Support)
- **Fatty fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel)** provide EPA/DHA that support fatty-liver health and reduce hepatic inflammation, complementing TUDCA — pairs with omega-3 supplementation in liver stacks

### Important: Separate from Bile-Acid Sequestrants
- If you take a bile-acid sequestrant (cholestyramine, colestipol, colesevelam) for cholesterol, it will bind TUDCA in the gut and reduce its absorption
- Take TUDCA several hours apart from these medications (see drug interactions)

### Practical Pairing Strategy
- **Simplest approach:** Take TUDCA with a balanced meal that includes some healthy fat (to align with bile release) and liver-supportive vegetables
- **Liver-stack approach:** Take TUDCA with a meal alongside NAC and omega-3 for comprehensive hepatic support
- Avoid taking it at the same time as bile-acid sequestrants
