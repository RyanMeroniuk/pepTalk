# TUDCA (Tauroursodeoxycholic Acid)
## Dosing Protocols

### General Liver Support Protocol
- **Dose:** 250-500mg/day
- **Timing:** Once daily, commonly with a meal containing some fat (aligns with natural bile flow); can be taken fasted
- **Duration:** Often used continuously during periods of liver stress, or in cycles aligned with the stressor
- **Notes:** This is the common maintenance/support dose for general liver health

### On-Cycle Organ Protection Protocol (Performance/PED & Peptide Users)
- **Dose:** 500-1,000mg/day, sometimes split into two doses
- **Timing:** With meals, throughout the cycle of the hepatically stressful compound
- **Duration:** For the duration of the cycle (e.g., oral anabolics, prohormones), often continued into post-cycle recovery
- **Notes:** This is the most common real-world use in optimization communities. TUDCA supports the liver but does NOT make hepatotoxic compounds safe — it is risk mitigation, not a license. Monitor liver enzymes with blood work.

### Higher-Dose / Therapeutic-Range Protocol
- **Dose:** 1,000-1,750mg/day (research and clinical contexts have used doses in this range and higher)
- **Notes:** Higher doses are used in research for metabolic and neuroprotective endpoints. These should be approached cautiously and ideally with medical oversight; not necessary for routine support.

### Combination Liver-Support Stack
- **TUDCA 500mg/day + NAC 600-1,200mg/day + omega-3 (EPA/DHA)**
- Optionally add milk thistle and a B-complex
- **Notes:** NAC supports glutathione (the liver's main antioxidant), omega-3 supports fatty-liver health, and TUDCA addresses bile flow and ER/mitochondrial stress — a comprehensive liver-support approach

### Titration & Tolerance
- **Start at 250-500mg** to assess GI tolerance, then increase if needed
- **Split higher doses** (e.g., 500mg twice daily) to improve tolerance
- **With food** reduces the occasional GI upset and aligns with physiological bile release

### Timing Considerations
- **With meals** — Common, as bile acids are naturally released with food, especially fatty meals
- **Time of day is flexible** — No strong circadian requirement; consistency matters
- **Separate from bile-acid sequestrants** — If taking cholestyramine, colestipol, or colesevelam, take TUDCA several hours apart, as sequestrants bind bile acids and reduce TUDCA absorption (see drug interactions)

### Duration & Expectations
- **For on-cycle protection** — Use throughout the cycle; benefit is protective rather than producing a felt effect
- **For liver markers** — Allow weeks and reassess with blood work (liver enzymes)
- **Cycling vs continuous** — Many use it intermittently aligned with stressors; continuous use is also reasonable for ongoing support
