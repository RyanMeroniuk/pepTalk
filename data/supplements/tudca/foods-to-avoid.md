# TUDCA (Tauroursodeoxycholic Acid)
## Foods to Avoid

TUDCA has no specific foods that block its absorption (aside from the timing issue with bile-acid sequestrant medications). The "avoid" list focuses on dietary patterns that stress the liver and counteract TUDCA's hepatoprotective and metabolic goals.

### Alcohol (The Most Important to Limit)
- Alcohol is directly hepatotoxic, depletes glutathione, promotes fatty liver and inflammation, and stresses the very organ TUDCA is supporting
- Heavy alcohol use directly undermines TUDCA's liver-protective purpose; minimizing alcohol is the single most important dietary step for liver health

### Refined Sugar & High-Fructose Foods (Drive Fatty Liver)
- **Excess fructose and refined sugar** are major drivers of non-alcoholic/metabolic fatty liver disease (NAFLD/MASLD), as fructose is metabolized in the liver and promotes hepatic fat accumulation
- **Sugar-sweetened beverages and high-fructose corn syrup** are particularly implicated in liver fat
- Limiting these supports TUDCA's metabolic and fatty-liver benefits

### Pro-Inflammatory & Processed Foods
- **Refined seed oils** — Soybean, corn, cottonseed, sunflower, safflower oils promote inflammation and oxidative stress on the liver
- **Trans fats** — Directly harmful to liver and metabolic health
- **Ultra-processed foods** — Contribute to fatty liver, inflammation, and metabolic dysfunction

### Excessive Saturated Fat from Poor Sources (Context-Dependent)
- While some healthy fat aids bile flow and TUDCA pairing, excessive intake of low-quality fried and processed fats burdens the liver. Quality matters — olive oil and fatty fish are supportive; deep-fried and oxidized fats are not.

### Charred / High-AGE Foods & Oxidized Fats
- **Charred meats and reused frying oils** are high in oxidized compounds and advanced glycation end products that add oxidative burden to the liver

### Hepatotoxic Exposures (Not Foods, but Critical)
- **Excessive acetaminophen, certain herbal hepatotoxins, and recreational hepatotoxins** stress the liver. TUDCA cannot neutralize ongoing hepatotoxic exposure. Reduce the burden rather than relying on TUDCA to offset it.

### Foods That Are Irrelevant (Don't Overthink)
- **There are no common foods that meaningfully block TUDCA absorption** — only bile-acid sequestrant medications do (a timing, not a food, issue)
- Fiber and fat do not impair TUDCA; in fact fat aligns with bile release

### The Real Priority
- For a TUDCA user, the dietary focus is reducing the liver's burden: minimize alcohol, refined sugar/fructose, seed oils, and ultra-processed foods, while emphasizing a liver-supportive, anti-inflammatory diet
- This maximizes TUDCA's hepatoprotective benefit and works alongside NAC and omega-3 in a liver-support stack
