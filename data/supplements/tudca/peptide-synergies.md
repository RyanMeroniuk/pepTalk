# TUDCA (Tauroursodeoxycholic Acid)
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### MOTS-c + TUDCA (Mitochondrial & Cellular Protection)
- **Rationale:** MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-derived peptide that improves mitochondrial function, metabolic flexibility, and insulin sensitivity via AMPK activation. TUDCA stabilizes mitochondria, reduces ER stress, and protects cells from apoptosis. Together they support mitochondrial health from complementary angles — MOTS-c enhancing function and metabolic signaling, TUDCA protecting mitochondrial integrity and reducing cellular stress. A strong pairing for metabolic and longevity-focused protocols.
- **Protocol:** TUDCA 250-500mg/day + MOTS-c at standard dosing
- **Best for:** Mitochondrial support, metabolic optimization, longevity protocols

#### Oral Peptide / On-Cycle Protocols + TUDCA (Liver Protection)
- **Rationale:** Users running hepatically stressful protocols — including certain orally dosed compounds and broader performance/PED cycles often run alongside peptides — use TUDCA to protect the liver. TUDCA improves bile flow and reduces hepatocyte ER stress and apoptosis during periods of elevated liver load. This is the most common real-world pairing in optimization communities.
- **Protocol:** TUDCA 500-1,000mg/day throughout the cycle, with blood-work monitoring of liver enzymes. Often stacked with NAC and omega-3.
- **Best for:** On-cycle liver/organ protection during hepatically stressful protocols

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### Tesamorelin + TUDCA (Metabolic / Fatty Liver Support)
- **Rationale:** Tesamorelin reduces visceral fat via GH release and has been studied for reducing liver fat in some populations. TUDCA supports liver health by reducing ER stress and improving bile flow, with emerging fatty-liver relevance. Together they support the liver from both a fat-reduction (tesamorelin) and cytoprotective (TUDCA) standpoint.
- **Protocol:** TUDCA 250-500mg/day + tesamorelin at prescribed dose
- **Best for:** Visceral fat and fatty-liver-oriented metabolic protocols

#### Semaglutide/Tirzepatide + TUDCA (Metabolic & Hepatic)
- **Rationale:** GLP-1 and dual agonists drive weight loss and improve metabolic markers, often improving liver fat as a downstream effect. TUDCA adds direct hepatic ER-stress reduction and bile-flow support. For metabolic-syndrome and fatty-liver-prone individuals, the combination supports systemic metabolic improvement plus targeted liver protection. (Note: rapid weight loss can transiently affect bile/gallstone risk; TUDCA's bile effects may be relevant here.)
- **Protocol:** TUDCA 250-500mg/day + semaglutide or tirzepatide at prescribed dose
- **Best for:** Metabolic syndrome, fatty-liver-prone weight loss, with liver support

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### BPC-157 + TUDCA (Gut-Liver Axis & Recovery)
- **Rationale:** BPC-157 supports gut integrity and tissue repair and has some research interest in the gut-liver axis and organ protection. TUDCA protects hepatocytes and supports bile flow. In recovery and gut-liver-focused protocols, the two provide complementary cytoprotective support.
- **Protocol:** TUDCA 250-500mg/day + BPC-157 250-500 mcg 2x/day
- **Best for:** Gut-liver axis support, recovery protocols

#### Cerebrolysin/Selank + TUDCA (Neuroprotection — Preliminary)
- **Rationale:** TUDCA has neuroprotective research interest via its anti-apoptotic and ER-stress-reducing actions in CNS models. Neuropeptides oriented toward cognitive/neuroprotective support could conceptually complement this. This is speculative and based on mechanism, not established clinical synergy — flagged as preliminary.
- **Protocol:** TUDCA 250-500mg/day + neuropeptide per protocol
- **Best for:** Experimental neuroprotective-oriented protocols (low-confidence)

### Liver-Support Supplement Cluster
- TUDCA is a core member of the liver-support cluster alongside **NAC** (N-acetylcysteine, a glutathione precursor and the primary antioxidant support for the liver) and **omega-3** (anti-inflammatory, fatty-liver support).
- **Stack idea:** TUDCA 500mg + NAC 600-1,200mg + omega-3 (EPA/DHA) daily, with a liver-supportive diet, for comprehensive bile-flow, antioxidant, and anti-inflammatory liver support. Monitor liver enzymes with blood work during any stressful protocol.
