# TUDCA (Tauroursodeoxycholic Acid)
## Overview

TUDCA is a hydrophilic, taurine-conjugated bile acid with a dual identity: it improves bile flow and liver health, and at the cellular level it is a potent chemical chaperone that reduces endoplasmic reticulum (ER) stress and stabilizes mitochondria. It is best known among health-optimization and performance communities as a liver-support and "on-cycle" organ-protection supplement, but its cytoprotective mechanisms have attracted research across liver disease, neuroprotection, and metabolic health.

**Why it matters:**

- **Liver support and bile flow** — TUDCA increases bile flow (choleresis) and shifts the bile acid pool toward more hydrophilic, less cytotoxic species. This protects liver cells (hepatocytes) and bile ducts, and is the basis for its use in cholestatic conditions. Its parent compound, ursodeoxycholic acid (UDCA), is an established prescription liver medication.
- **Reduces ER stress (chemical chaperone)** — TUDCA helps misfolded proteins fold correctly and dampens the unfolded protein response, reducing ER-stress-induced cell death. ER stress is implicated in liver disease, insulin resistance, and neurodegeneration.
- **Stabilizes mitochondria & reduces apoptosis** — TUDCA inhibits the mitochondrial apoptosis pathway (reducing cytochrome c release and caspase activation), protecting cells from programmed death under stress. This underlies much of its cytoprotective and neuroprotective research interest.
- **"On-cycle" protection for peptide/PED users** — Many users of oral anabolics, prohormones, or hepatically stressful compounds use TUDCA to support liver health during a cycle. This is the most common real-world use in the optimization community and the reason it is highly relevant to this app's audience.
- **Metabolic and neuroprotective research** — Preclinical and early clinical work explores TUDCA in insulin resistance, fatty liver (NAFLD/MASLD), ALS, and other neurodegenerative conditions, largely via its ER-stress and mitochondrial mechanisms. These uses are promising but still emerging — flagged as preliminary.
- **Pairs with other liver/antioxidant supports** — TUDCA is commonly stacked with NAC (glutathione precursor) and omega-3 (anti-inflammatory, supports fatty-liver health) for comprehensive liver support.
- **Generally well tolerated** — Side effects are usually limited to mild GI symptoms at higher doses.
