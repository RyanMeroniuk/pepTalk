# Citicoline (CDP-Choline)
## Lifestyle Recommendations

### Prioritize Sleep (7-9 Hours)
- Acetylcholine and cholinergic signaling are central to REM sleep, memory consolidation, and attention. Citicoline supports the cholinergic system, but sleep is when memory is consolidated. Chronic sleep loss undermines the cognitive benefits citicoline can provide

### Use Citicoline for Demanding Cognitive Windows
- Citicoline shines when paired with actual cognitive effort. Take it before deep-work sessions, study blocks, or high-focus tasks
- Because it can be mildly activating, dose in the morning or early afternoon to support focus without disrupting sleep

### Exercise Regularly
- Aerobic exercise raises BDNF and cerebral blood flow, complementing citicoline's membrane and cholinergic support. The combination supports neuroplasticity better than either alone

### Eat a Brain-Supporting Diet
- A whole-food, anti-inflammatory diet rich in choline (eggs, fish, lean meat, or soy lecithin for plant-based eaters), omega-3 DHA (fatty fish), and B-vitamins supplies the cofactors and structural fats that citicoline's pathways depend on
- Mediterranean-style eating is a strong foundation

### Manage Total Choline Load
- If you stack citicoline with alpha-gpc or other cholinergics, track total intake and watch for cholinergic excess (headache, jaw tension). Adjust to find your optimal range

### Engage in Cognitive Challenge
- Citicoline provides substrate for acetylcholine and membrane synthesis, but those resources are best used when challenged. Learn new skills, read demanding material, and practice focused attention to capitalize on the support

### Manage Stress
- Chronic stress and elevated cortisol impair memory and attention systems. Pair citicoline with stress management (breathwork, meditation, adaptogens like rhodiola or ashwagandha) for a more complete cognitive approach

### Stay Hydrated
- Adequate hydration helps with the headaches some people experience when starting citicoline and supports overall cognitive performance

### Consistency Over Time
- While some focus effects are acute, the memory and neuroprotective benefits build over weeks. Make citicoline a consistent daily habit (e.g., with morning coffee or breakfast) rather than an occasional add-on
