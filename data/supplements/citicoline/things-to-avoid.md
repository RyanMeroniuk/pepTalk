# Citicoline (CDP-Choline)
## Things to Avoid

### Stacking Too Many Cholinergics at Once
- Combining citicoline with alpha-gpc, high-dose lecithin, and racetams simultaneously can push total cholinergic tone too high, causing headaches, jaw tension, brain fog, irritability, and depression-like feelings. Track your total choline load and add one source at a time
- More choline is not always better — there is an optimal range, and overshooting it backfires

### Dosing Late in the Day
- Citicoline can be mildly activating. Taking it in the evening may interfere with sleep in sensitive individuals. Keep dosing to morning/early afternoon

### Expecting It to Replace Sleep or Foundations
- Citicoline supports cognition but cannot compensate for chronic sleep deprivation, poor diet, or unmanaged stress, all of which suppress the cholinergic and neurotrophic systems it works through

### Buying Unverified Generic Products
- Generic citicoline quality varies. For research-matched results, the patented Cognizin form is the studied standard. If buying generic, choose third-party-tested products

### Assuming Citicoline and Alpha-GPC Are Interchangeable
- They overlap but differ: alpha-GPC delivers more choline by weight; citicoline adds cytidine/uridine for membrane support. Don't assume swapping one for the other gives identical effects

### Medical Cautions
- **Bipolar disorder:** Cholinergic/dopaminergic modulation could theoretically affect mood states — use only under medical guidance
- **Low blood pressure:** Citicoline may mildly lower blood pressure; monitor if prone to hypotension
- **Levodopa/Parkinson's medication:** Citicoline can interact with dopaminergic therapy (see drug-interactions) — coordinate with the prescribing physician
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding:** Insufficient safety data — avoid unless a clinician directs otherwise
- **Before surgery:** Disclose all supplements to your anesthesiologist and surgical team

### Ignoring TMAO/Cardiovascular Monitoring
- If you track cardiovascular risk markers, account for the choline contribution from citicoline plus dietary choline when interpreting TMAO
