# Citicoline (CDP-Choline)
## Classification

- **Category:** Cholinergic nootropic; brain-supporting nutrient
- **Chemical class:** Mononucleotide composed of ribose, cytosine, pyrophosphate, and choline (cytidine 5'-diphosphocholine)
- **Molecular formula:** C14H26N4O11P2
- **Common names:** CDP-choline, cytidine diphosphate-choline, citicoline; sold as the branded, patented form **Cognizin**
- **Supplement type:** Choline donor / cognitive enhancer / neuroprotective agent
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (USA, OTC); prescription drug in some European and Asian countries (e.g., used clinically after stroke). Cognizin holds GRAS status in the US
- **Active components delivered:** Choline (precursor to acetylcholine and phosphatidylcholine) and cytidine (converted to uridine in humans, supporting membrane phospholipid synthesis)
- **Primary systems:** Neurological (acetylcholine synthesis, neuronal membrane integrity), focus/attention circuits
- **Related compounds:** Alpha-GPC (another choline donor with a different delivery profile); CDP-choline is the focus/attention-leaning counterpart
