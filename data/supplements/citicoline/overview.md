# Citicoline (CDP-Choline)
## Overview

Citicoline (CDP-choline) is a naturally occurring compound and an intermediate in the synthesis of phosphatidylcholine, the major phospholipid of neuronal cell membranes. When taken as a supplement, it is rapidly broken down into choline and cytidine, both of which are absorbed and then re-synthesized into citicoline inside cells — providing raw materials for two distinct jobs: making the neurotransmitter acetylcholine (critical for attention, learning, and memory) and building/repairing the phospholipid membranes of neurons. This dual role is why citicoline is valued both as a focus-and-attention nootropic and as a neuroprotective agent. Unlike alpha-GPC, which delivers a higher proportion of choline by weight, citicoline also supplies cytidine/uridine for membrane synthesis, giving it a slightly more "structural" profile alongside its acetylcholine support. The patented form Cognizin has been the subject of small human trials for attention and adolescent and adult cognitive performance. It pairs naturally with other cholinergics like alpha-GPC and with neurotrophic supplements like lions-mane, and is framed here for educational and research purposes only — not as medical advice.
