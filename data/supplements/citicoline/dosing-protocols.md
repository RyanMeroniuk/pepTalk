# Citicoline (CDP-Choline)
## Dosing Protocols

### Standard Cognitive / Focus Dose
- **Dose:** 250-500 mg/day (Cognizin trials commonly used 250 mg and 500 mg)
- **Timing:** Morning or early afternoon — citicoline can be mildly activating in some people
- **Duration:** Effects on focus may be noticeable acutely; cumulative cognitive benefits build over 2-4 weeks of consistent use
- **Notes:** 500 mg/day is a common, well-tolerated maintenance dose for attention support

### Higher / Clinical Range
- **Dose:** 500-2,000 mg/day, often split into two doses
- **Use case:** Higher ranges appear in clinical research on cognitive impairment, age-related memory decline, and neuroprotection (under medical supervision abroad)
- **Notes:** For general nootropic use, more is not necessarily better — most people do well at 250-500 mg/day

### Splitting Doses
- Doses above 500 mg/day are commonly split (e.g., 250-500 mg morning + 250-500 mg midday) for steadier effects and to avoid late-day stimulation
- Avoid dosing late in the evening if you find it activating

### With or Without Food
- Can be taken with or without food; taking with a meal can reduce the rare GI upset
- No special pairing is required for absorption (it is highly bioavailable)

### Stacking with Other Cholinergics
- **With alpha-gpc:** Some users layer a lower dose of each (e.g., citicoline 250 mg + alpha-GPC 150-300 mg) for combined attention + raw acetylcholine support. Watch total choline load to avoid cholinergic side effects (headache, jaw tension)
- **With racetams (if used):** Citicoline is a classic choline source to pair with racetam nootropics to reduce "choline-depletion" headaches

### Onset and Expectations
- **Acute:** Some people notice sharper focus within 1-3 hours
- **Cumulative:** Memory and sustained-attention benefits typically build over weeks. Be patient and consistent

### Cycling
- No strong evidence that citicoline requires cycling. It can be used continuously. Some practitioners take periodic breaks as a general supplement principle, but tolerance/dependence is not established
