# Citicoline (CDP-Choline)
## Benefits

### Focus and Attention
- **Sustained attention:** Small human trials (including studies using the Cognizin form) report improvements in attention and reduced impulsivity, particularly in adolescents and middle-aged adults
- **Mental energy and motivation:** Users commonly report cleaner, less jittery focus than stimulants, attributed to cholinergic and dopaminergic support
- **Reduced cognitive fatigue:** Supports sustained mental effort during demanding tasks

### Memory and Learning
- **Working memory support:** By supplying choline for acetylcholine synthesis, citicoline supports the neurotransmitter system most associated with working memory and encoding
- **Age-related memory:** Studies in older adults with age-associated memory impairment have reported modest improvements in memory performance

### Neuroprotection and Recovery
- **Stroke and ischemia:** Citicoline is used clinically in some countries as an adjunct in acute ischemic stroke and vascular cognitive impairment. Evidence is mixed across large trials, but the membrane-stabilizing mechanism is well characterized
- **Traumatic brain injury:** Investigated for cognitive recovery support, with mixed results in large trials but a strong mechanistic rationale
- **Membrane repair:** The phosphatidylcholine pathway supports neuronal membrane maintenance, relevant to aging and recovery

### Eye and Visual Health
- **Glaucoma and optic nerve support:** Some clinical research suggests citicoline may support retinal ganglion cell function and the optic nerve, reflecting its general neuroprotective profile

### Mood
- **Dopaminergic support:** The modulation of dopamine pathways may contribute to improvements in motivation and mood in some users; preliminary research has examined citicoline as an adjunct in certain mood and substance-use contexts

### Complementary Role
- **Pairs with other cholinergics:** Often stacked with alpha-gpc (a higher-choline donor) for layered cholinergic support, and with lions-mane for combined neurotrophic-plus-cholinergic effects
- **Stimulant balance:** Helps replenish choline that heavy mental work and certain nootropics can deplete, reducing "choline headaches" some people experience with racetams
