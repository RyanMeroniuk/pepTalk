# Citicoline (CDP-Choline)
## Mechanisms of Action

### 1. Acetylcholine Precursor (Cholinergic Support)
- **Choline release:** Citicoline is hydrolyzed in the gut to choline and cytidine. The liberated choline crosses the blood-brain barrier and feeds the synthesis of acetylcholine via choline acetyltransferase
- Acetylcholine is the primary neurotransmitter of attention, working memory, learning, and arousal — explaining citicoline's reputation as a focus/attention nootropic
- By increasing substrate availability, citicoline supports cholinergic tone without directly acting as a receptor agonist

### 2. Phosphatidylcholine Synthesis (Membrane Integrity)
- Citicoline is a rate-limiting intermediate in the **Kennedy pathway** (CDP-choline pathway) that produces phosphatidylcholine, the dominant phospholipid in neuronal membranes
- Supports the construction, maintenance, and repair of neuronal cell membranes, dendrites, and synapses
- This structural role distinguishes citicoline from pure choline salts — it supplies both the head group (choline) and supports the broader phospholipid machinery

### 3. Cytidine / Uridine Contribution
- The cytidine portion is converted to uridine in humans, which is incorporated into membrane phospholipid synthesis (via UTP and CDP-choline cycling)
- Uridine independently supports synaptic membrane production and may contribute to neuroplasticity
- This is a key mechanistic difference from alpha-GPC, which does not supply cytidine/uridine

### 4. Dopaminergic Modulation
- Citicoline has been reported to increase dopamine receptor density and modulate dopamine release in some preclinical models
- This may contribute to its effects on motivation, attention, and mood

### 5. Neuroprotection
- Stabilizes neuronal membranes and reduces the breakdown of phospholipids into free fatty acids during ischemic or oxidative stress
- Reduces glutamate excitotoxicity and supports mitochondrial function in preclinical models of ischemia — the basis for its clinical use after stroke in some countries

### 6. Antioxidant and Anti-Inflammatory Support
- Supports endogenous antioxidant systems (e.g., glutathione) and reduces lipid peroxidation in neural tissue in preclinical work
- Lower neuroinflammation supports the broader cognitive and neuroprotective profile
