# Citicoline (CDP-Choline)
## Forms and Bioavailability

### Cognizin (Branded Citicoline)
- **The most-studied supplement form** — a patented, stabilized, plant-derived (fermentation-produced) citicoline used in most published human trials
- Standardized purity and dosing make it the benchmark for research-backed citicoline
- **Best for:** Anyone who wants to match the form used in clinical studies of attention and cognition

### Generic Citicoline (CDP-Choline)
- Chemically identical to Cognizin but without the branded standardization/testing
- Often more affordable; quality and purity vary by manufacturer — third-party testing is worth seeking out

### Bioavailability and Absorption
- **High oral bioavailability** — citicoline is rapidly hydrolyzed in the gut to choline and cytidine, both of which are well absorbed
- These components then cross the blood-brain barrier independently and are re-synthesized into citicoline inside cells (the body reassembles what it broke down)
- **Peak plasma choline** rises within hours; cytidine is converted to uridine in humans
- Bioavailability is high enough that intranasal or injectable forms (used clinically abroad) offer no meaningful advantage for routine supplementation

### Citicoline vs Alpha-GPC (Choline Donor Comparison)
- **Choline content by weight:** Alpha-GPC delivers a higher percentage of choline (~40%) than citicoline (~18%), so alpha-gpc is the more concentrated pure-choline donor
- **Unique to citicoline:** The cytidine/uridine component supports membrane phospholipid synthesis — alpha-GPC does not provide this
- **Practical takeaway:** Citicoline is often preferred for attention/focus and membrane support; alpha-GPC for raw acetylcholine and power-output/cognitive-stimulant uses. Some stacks use both
- Both raise plasma choline; neither is universally "better" — they have overlapping but distinct profiles

### Capsules vs Powder
- **Capsules:** Convenient, standardized, no taste issues (citicoline powder is mildly bitter)
- **Powder:** Cost-effective and flexible for dose titration; dissolves in water

### Storage
- Keep sealed, cool, and dry. Citicoline is reasonably stable but, like most cholinergics, should be protected from moisture and heat
