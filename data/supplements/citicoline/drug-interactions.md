# Citicoline (CDP-Choline)
## Drug Interactions

### Moderate Interactions (Use with Caution)
- **Levodopa / Parkinson's medications:** Citicoline modulates dopamine pathways and has been studied alongside levodopa, where it may enhance or alter levodopa's effects and potentially allow dose adjustments under medical supervision. Patients on levodopa should not combine without coordinating with their neurologist — monitor for changes in motor symptoms.
- **Centrally acting anticholinergic drugs (e.g., some antihistamines, oxybutynin, scopolamine, tricyclic antidepressants):** Citicoline supports cholinergic tone and may oppose the intended action of anticholinergic medications. Conversely, anticholinergics may blunt citicoline's cognitive effects. Use caution and monitor.
- **Cholinesterase inhibitors (donepezil/Aricept, rivastigmine, galantamine):** These raise acetylcholine; citicoline supplies choline substrate. The combination could be additive on cholinergic tone — potentially beneficial in dementia care but may increase cholinergic side effects (GI upset, bradycardia). Use under physician supervision.

### Mild Interactions (Monitor)
- **Antihypertensive drugs:** Citicoline may modestly lower blood pressure in some people. Monitor for additive effects (lightheadedness) when combined with blood-pressure medications.
- **Stimulants and other nootropics:** Combining citicoline with stimulants or racetams can stack cognitive/activating effects. Generally well tolerated and citicoline is often used to offset racetam-induced choline depletion, but monitor for overstimulation or headache.

### Timing Considerations
- Citicoline does not need to be separated from most medications by specific intervals. If you find it activating, take it hours apart from any evening sedatives or sleep medications to avoid working against them.
- When stacking with other cholinergics (alpha-gpc), spreading doses across the day rather than taking them at the same time can reduce cholinergic-excess symptoms.

### Conditions Requiring Caution
- **Bipolar disorder:** Cholinergic/dopaminergic modulation could theoretically influence mood-state cycling. Use only under medical guidance.
- **Pregnancy:** Insufficient safety data for supplemental citicoline. Avoid unless directed by a clinician.
- **Breastfeeding:** Insufficient safety data. Avoid unless directed by a clinician.
- **Liver disease:** The liver is central to choline and uridine metabolism. Discuss with a hepatologist before use in significant liver disease.

### Positive / Neutral Combinations
- **B-vitamins (B5, B12, folate):** Cofactors that support converting citicoline's choline into acetylcholine — complementary, not conflicting.
- **Omega-3 (DHA):** Complementary membrane support; no adverse interaction.

### General Guidance
- Citicoline has a relatively mild drug-interaction profile, with the most important cautions around dopaminergic (levodopa) and anticholinergic/cholinergic medications. Always disclose citicoline use to your healthcare providers, especially before surgery or when starting new neurological or psychiatric medications.
