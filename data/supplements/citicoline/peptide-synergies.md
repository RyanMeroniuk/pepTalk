# Citicoline (CDP-Choline)
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### Semax + Citicoline (Focus & Neurotrophic Stack)
- **Rationale:** Semax upregulates BDNF and enhances attention, memory, and stress resilience through melanocortin pathways. Citicoline supplies choline for acetylcholine (the attention neurotransmitter) and supports neuronal membranes. Together you get neurotrophic signaling (Semax) plus the cholinergic substrate and membrane support (citicoline) — a powerful focus and cognitive-enhancement pairing.
- **Protocol:** Citicoline 250-500 mg in the morning + Semax at standard intranasal nootropic dosing
- **Best for:** Sustained attention, demanding intellectual work, students, professionals

#### Dihexa + Citicoline (Synaptogenesis + Cholinergic Support)
- **Rationale:** Dihexa is an extremely potent promoter of synaptogenesis via the HGF/c-Met pathway. Citicoline provides the choline and membrane phospholipid substrate needed to build and maintain those new synapses. Dihexa creates the demand for new connections; citicoline helps supply the raw materials.
- **Protocol:** Citicoline 500 mg/day + Dihexa at standard low-dose protocol
- **Best for:** Aggressive cognitive enhancement, memory formation, neuroplasticity-focused stacks

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### Selank + Citicoline (Calm Focus)
- **Rationale:** Selank is an anxiolytic nootropic that modulates GABA and balances neurotransmitters without sedation. Citicoline adds cholinergic and attentional support. Together they offer calm, anxiety-reduced focus — Selank takes the edge off while citicoline sharpens attention.
- **Protocol:** Citicoline 250-500 mg morning + Selank at standard intranasal dosing
- **Best for:** Anxious high-performers, focus under pressure, test/exam preparation

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + Citicoline (Recovery + Cognition)
- **Rationale:** CJC-1295/Ipamorelin raise GH and IGF-1, supporting overall recovery, sleep quality, and tissue repair. Citicoline supports cholinergic function and neuronal membranes. The combination pairs nightly recovery (GH peptides) with daytime cognitive support (citicoline), relevant to age-related cognitive maintenance.
- **Protocol:** Citicoline 500 mg in the morning + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Best for:** Anti-aging cognitive protocols, recovery-focused users

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### Epitalon + Citicoline (Longevity + Neuroprotection)
- **Rationale:** Epitalon supports circadian/melatonin regulation and has anti-aging properties. Citicoline supports neuronal membrane integrity and cholinergic function. Together they form a neuroprotective, longevity-leaning stack, with Epitalon improving sleep architecture and citicoline maintaining membrane and acetylcholine systems.
- **Protocol:** Citicoline 500 mg/day + Epitalon in cyclical anti-aging courses
- **Best for:** Longevity protocols, age-related cognitive maintenance

#### MOTS-c + Citicoline (Mitochondrial + Membrane Support)
- **Rationale:** MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-derived peptide that acts as an exercise mimetic and supports cellular energy metabolism. Citicoline supports neuronal membrane synthesis and may aid mitochondrial function in neural tissue. Together they support both the energy and the structural sides of neural health.
- **Protocol:** Citicoline 500 mg/day + MOTS-c at standard dosing
- **Best for:** Metabolic and longevity-focused cognitive performance

### Supplement Synergies (Non-Peptide)
- **Alpha-GPC:** The natural companion cholinergic. Alpha-gpc delivers more choline by weight for raw acetylcholine; citicoline adds cytidine/uridine for membrane support. Layered at moderate doses, they provide complementary cholinergic coverage — watch total choline load.
- **Lion's Mane:** Lions-mane stimulates NGF and BDNF (neurotrophic growth), while citicoline supplies acetylcholine substrate and membrane phospholipids. Growth signals plus building materials — a strong cognitive-longevity pairing.
- **Omega-3 (DHA):** DHA and citicoline both support neuronal membrane integrity through complementary routes; a classic brain-membrane combination.
