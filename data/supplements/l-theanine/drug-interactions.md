# L-Theanine
## Drug Interactions

**L-Theanine has very few clinically significant drug interactions. Its safety profile is one of its greatest strengths.**

**Mild interactions (monitor, generally safe):**

- **Blood pressure medications (antihypertensives)** — L-Theanine may modestly reduce blood pressure, particularly during stress. Combined with antihypertensives, this could theoretically cause excessive blood pressure lowering. In practice, the effect is small, but monitor blood pressure if combining, especially with ACE inhibitors, ARBs, calcium channel blockers, or diuretics.
- **Sedative medications (benzodiazepines, Z-drugs, barbiturates)** — Additive relaxation effects. L-Theanine's GABAergic modulation is mild, but combined with prescription sedatives, increased drowsiness is possible. This is not dangerous at standard L-Theanine doses but may affect tasks requiring alertness. Adjust L-Theanine dose downward if excessive drowsiness occurs.
- **Stimulant medications (amphetamines, methylphenidate)** — L-Theanine may partially counteract the anxiogenic and jittery side effects of stimulants, similar to its interaction with caffeine. This is generally a beneficial interaction and is being studied as an adjunct in ADHD treatment. No dangerous interaction expected.
- **Antidepressants (SSRIs, SNRIs)** — L-Theanine modulates serotonin and dopamine. Theoretical potential for additive serotonergic effects, but no clinical reports of serotonin syndrome with L-Theanine. Generally considered safe to combine. Some practitioners use L-Theanine as an adjunct to antidepressants for anxiety reduction.
- **Thyroid medications (levothyroxine)** — No known direct interaction, but as with all supplements, take thyroid medication on an empty stomach and separate from L-Theanine by at least 30-60 minutes as a precaution.
- **Chemotherapy agents** — L-Theanine has been studied as an adjunct to chemotherapy (particularly doxorubicin and cisplatin) and appears to enhance anti-tumor effects while reducing side effects. However, always discuss with an oncologist before adding any supplement during cancer treatment.

**Contraindications:**

- **No absolute contraindications** — L-Theanine has no documented absolute contraindications in otherwise healthy adults.
- **Caution in severe hypotension** — Individuals with already-low blood pressure should be aware of L-Theanine's mild hypotensive effects during stress.

**Safe combinations:**

- **Caffeine** — The most studied and beneficial combination. Safe and synergistic.
- **Melatonin** — Safe for sleep support stacks.
- **Magnesium** — Complementary GABAergic mechanisms. Safe.
- **Omega-3 fatty acids** — Complementary neuroprotective effects. Safe.
- **Ashwagandha** — Both reduce cortisol through different mechanisms. Safe.
- **Apigenin** — Both promote relaxation through distinct pathways. Safe.
- **B vitamins** — Support neurotransmitter synthesis. Safe.
