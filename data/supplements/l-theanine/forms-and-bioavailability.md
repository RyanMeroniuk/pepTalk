# L-Theanine
## Forms and Bioavailability

- **Suntheanine (patented, enzymatically produced L-isomer)** — The gold standard form. Produced through an enzymatic process using Pseudomonas nitroreducens that yields pure L-isomer theanine (the biologically active form). Most clinical studies use Suntheanine. Look for the Suntheanine logo or "L-isomer" specification on labels. **Recommended as the preferred form.**
- **Generic L-Theanine (synthetic)** — Chemically synthesized L-Theanine. Quality varies by manufacturer. May contain a mix of L- and D-isomers if not carefully produced. D-Theanine is not biologically active for the desired effects. Choose products that specify "L-Theanine" (not just "theanine") and are third-party tested.
- **Green tea-derived (natural extract)** — Extracted from Camellia sinensis leaves. Contains pure L-isomer naturally. May include trace amounts of other tea compounds (catechins, caffeine) depending on extraction process. Generally well-regarded but more expensive.
- **Capsules** — Most common supplement form. 100-200mg per capsule. Absorbed in the small intestine with good bioavailability. Onset approximately 30-45 minutes.
- **Powder** — Allows flexible dosing. Can be mixed into water, smoothies, or beverages. Slightly sweet, umami taste. Dissolves well in warm or hot water. Faster absorption than capsules when dissolved in liquid.
- **Chewable tablets** — Convenient for those who dislike swallowing pills. Absorption may be slightly faster due to partial sublingual absorption.
- **Liquid drops/tinctures** — Allow precise dosing and potentially faster absorption. Less common than capsules or powder.
- **Combined formulas** — Often sold pre-combined with caffeine (for focus), magnesium (for relaxation), or melatonin (for sleep). Convenient but limits dosing flexibility.

**Bioavailability notes:**

- **Oral bioavailability is high** — L-Theanine is well-absorbed from the GI tract with minimal first-pass metabolism. Bioavailability is estimated at approximately 75-80%.
- **Peak plasma levels** — Reached within 30-60 minutes of oral ingestion. Peak brain levels within 1-2 hours.
- **Half-life** — Approximately 1-2 hours in plasma, but subjective effects last 3-5 hours due to sustained brain levels and downstream neurotransmitter effects.
- **Food interactions** — Can be taken with or without food. No significant food-related absorption issues. Taking with a protein-rich meal may slightly slow absorption (competition for amino acid transporters) but does not reduce total absorption.
- **Caffeine co-administration** — When taken with caffeine, both compounds are absorbed on similar timelines, allowing synergistic effects. A cup of green tea provides approximately 20-30mg L-Theanine and 25-50mg caffeine naturally.
