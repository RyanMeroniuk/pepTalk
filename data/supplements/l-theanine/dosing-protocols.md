# L-Theanine
## Dosing Protocols

- **General relaxation and anxiety reduction** — 100-200mg, 1-2 times daily. Can be taken as needed or on a regular schedule. Effects noticeable within 30-60 minutes and last 3-5 hours.
- **Focus and cognitive enhancement (with caffeine)** — 100-200mg L-Theanine + 50-100mg caffeine. The commonly recommended ratio is approximately 2:1 (L-Theanine:caffeine). One cup of green tea provides a natural approximation (~25mg L-Theanine + ~30mg caffeine), but supplementation achieves more reliable and effective doses. Can be taken 1-3 times daily during work hours.
- **Pre-sleep anxiety and sleep quality** — 200-400mg, 30-60 minutes before bed. This is not a sedative dose — it works by quieting mental hyperarousal. Start at 200mg and increase to 400mg if needed. Safe to combine with melatonin, magnesium, or apigenin.
- **Acute stress or performance anxiety** — 200-400mg, 30-60 minutes before a stressful event (public speaking, exam, difficult conversation, competition). Provides noticeable calming without cognitive impairment or drowsiness.
- **ADHD support (adjunctive, under supervision)** — 200-400mg daily, divided into 2 doses. Studied in children at 2.5mg/kg twice daily (approximately 100-200mg twice daily for children). Should be supervised by a physician and used alongside, not instead of, standard ADHD management.
- **Daily wellness** — 100-200mg once or twice daily as a general stress-resilience support. Can be maintained indefinitely without tolerance or dependency concerns.

**Timing guidelines:**

- **For focus:** Take in the morning or early afternoon, ideally with caffeine
- **For anxiety:** Take as needed, 30-60 minutes before anticipated stressor
- **For sleep:** Take 30-60 minutes before bed
- **With meals or without:** Either is fine; empty stomach may provide slightly faster onset
- **Consistent daily use vs. as-needed:** Both approaches are valid. Unlike many supplements, L-Theanine works acutely (single dose) and does not require loading or buildup

**Dose ceiling:**

- Doses up to 900mg/day have been used in clinical studies without adverse effects. However, most people find 200-400mg/day sufficient. Higher doses do not proportionally increase benefits and may cause mild headache or GI discomfort in sensitive individuals.
- There is no established upper limit, but 400-600mg/day is a reasonable practical ceiling for daily use.
