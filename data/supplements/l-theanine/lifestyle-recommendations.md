# L-Theanine
## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Morning focus ritual** — Take 100-200mg L-Theanine with your morning coffee or tea for clean, jitter-free energy. This is the most well-documented nootropic combination. The caffeine provides drive and alertness; the L-Theanine provides calm and focus. Ideal for deep work sessions, creative projects, or demanding cognitive tasks.
- **Pre-meditation or mindfulness practice** — 100-200mg L-Theanine 30 minutes before meditation enhances alpha wave production, which is the same brain state that skilled meditators cultivate. This can deepen meditation quality, especially for beginners who struggle with mental chatter.
- **Stress inoculation** — Take 200mg before known stressful situations (meetings, confrontations, medical appointments, public speaking). L-Theanine reduces physiological stress markers without dulling cognitive sharpness.
- **Evening wind-down** — 200-400mg in the evening as part of a relaxation routine. Combine with dim lighting, light reading, or gentle stretching. L-Theanine quiets the "racing mind" that many people experience when trying to transition from work mode to rest mode.
- **Regular physical exercise** — Exercise increases BDNF and supports the same neurotransmitter systems that L-Theanine modulates. The combination of regular exercise + L-Theanine creates a robust stress-resilience foundation. Exercise timing: morning or afternoon for energy; avoid intense exercise close to bedtime.
- **Breathwork pairing** — Combine L-Theanine with structured breathing practices (box breathing, 4-7-8 breathing, coherent breathing at 5-6 breaths per minute). Both independently promote parasympathetic activation and alpha wave states. Together, they are more potent than either alone.
- **Screen breaks and nature exposure** — L-Theanine supports attention, but chronic screen use depletes focus regardless. Take regular breaks (Pomodoro technique: 25 minutes work, 5 minutes rest) and spend time in natural environments when possible. Nature exposure independently increases alpha wave activity.
- **Journaling practice** — The calm clarity L-Theanine provides can enhance reflective journaling. Take 100-200mg before an evening journaling session to promote clear, non-anxious self-reflection.
- **Sleep hygiene synergy** — When using L-Theanine for sleep, combine with standard sleep hygiene: consistent bedtime, cool room, dark environment, no screens 30-60 minutes before bed. L-Theanine is most effective for sleep when the environment supports the transition.
- **Caffeine cycling** — If you take L-Theanine + caffeine daily, consider periodic caffeine breaks (1 week off every 4-6 weeks) to maintain caffeine sensitivity. L-Theanine can be continued during caffeine breaks — it provides calming benefits independently.
