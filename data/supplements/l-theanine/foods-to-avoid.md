# L-Theanine
## Foods to Avoid

**L-Theanine has very few dietary conflicts. The following are contextual considerations rather than strict contraindications.**

- **Excessive caffeine (when using L-Theanine for sleep)** — If taking L-Theanine in the evening for sleep support, avoid caffeine within 8-10 hours of bedtime. While L-Theanine counteracts some of caffeine's anxiogenic effects, it cannot fully override caffeine's adenosine-blocking stimulant action. Caffeine's half-life of 5-6 hours means a 3 PM coffee still has significant stimulant activity at 11 PM.
- **Alcohol** — L-Theanine and alcohol both modulate GABA, though through different mechanisms. While there is no dangerous interaction, combining them may increase sedation unpredictably. If using L-Theanine for focus and cognitive performance, alcohol directly undermines those goals. If using L-Theanine for sleep, alcohol disrupts sleep architecture regardless of L-Theanine's presence.
- **High-sugar meals when seeking focus** — If taking L-Theanine + caffeine for cognitive performance, a high-sugar meal can cause blood glucose spikes and crashes that undermine sustained attention. Pair the stack with protein and complex carbs instead.
- **Very large protein meals (timing consideration)** — L-Theanine is an amino acid that uses the large neutral amino acid transporter to cross the blood-brain barrier. Theoretically, a very large protein meal consumed simultaneously could create transporter competition, slightly delaying brain uptake. In practice, this is a minor effect, but for fastest onset, take L-Theanine 15-30 minutes before or after a large protein-heavy meal.

**Bottom line:**

L-Theanine is remarkably free of food interactions. There are no foods that significantly block its absorption or create dangerous interactions. The above considerations are optimization tips rather than safety warnings.
