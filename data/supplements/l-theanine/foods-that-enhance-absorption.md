# L-Theanine
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

- **Caffeine-containing beverages** — Caffeine does not enhance L-Theanine absorption per se, but it creates the most well-documented synergistic nootropic effect. Coffee (80-100mg caffeine per cup), green tea (25-50mg), or yerba mate (40-60mg) consumed alongside L-Theanine supplementation produces superior cognitive outcomes. The caffeine activates dopaminergic and noradrenergic pathways while L-Theanine smooths the response. **Practical approach:** Take 100-200mg L-Theanine with your morning coffee or tea.
- **Green tea itself** — Drinking green tea provides L-Theanine in its natural matrix alongside catechins (EGCG), caffeine, and other polyphenols. The natural co-occurrence of these compounds suggests optimized bioavailability in the tea matrix. Consider green tea as a supplemental source alongside capsules.
- **Light meals with complex carbohydrates** — A light meal containing whole grains, sweet potato, or oats does not impair L-Theanine absorption and can provide a steady glucose supply to the brain, supporting the cognitive benefits of L-Theanine.
- **Water** — Simple but important. L-Theanine dissolves readily in water and is well-absorbed from the GI tract. Taking capsules or powder with a full glass of water ensures consistent dissolution and absorption.
- **Magnesium-rich foods** — Magnesium supports GABAergic function, complementing L-Theanine's GABA-enhancing effects. Combining L-Theanine with magnesium-rich foods (pumpkin seeds, dark chocolate, almonds, spinach) in the evening amplifies the relaxation effect for sleep.
- **Omega-3 rich foods** — Fatty fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel), walnuts, and flaxseed support neuronal membrane fluidity, which may facilitate L-Theanine's neurotransmitter effects at the receptor level.

**Absorption is naturally high:**

L-Theanine is one of the more bioavailable supplements, with approximately 75-80% oral bioavailability and no significant food-related absorption barriers. Unlike many supplements, there is no need to take it on an empty stomach, avoid certain foods, or pair it with specific absorption enhancers. It works well in almost any dietary context.
