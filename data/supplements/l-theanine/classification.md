# L-Theanine
## Classification

- **Category:** Amino Acid / Nootropic
- **Subcategory:** Anxiolytic & Cognitive Enhancer
- **Type:** Non-proteinogenic amino acid (not used to build proteins)
- **Chemical Name:** L-gamma-glutamylethylamide (also gamma-N-ethyl-L-glutamine)
- **Primary System:** Central nervous system (GABAergic, glutamatergic, serotonergic, dopaminergic modulation)
- **Regulatory Status:** Generally Recognized as Safe (GRAS) by the FDA. Over-the-counter dietary supplement.
- **Naturally Occurring:** Yes — found almost exclusively in the tea plant (Camellia sinensis), particularly in green tea, and in small amounts in certain mushrooms (Boletus badius)
- **Structural Note:** Analog of glutamate and glutamine; crosses the blood-brain barrier within 30-45 minutes of ingestion
