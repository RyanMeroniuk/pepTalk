# L-Theanine
## Side Effects

**L-Theanine has an exceptionally clean safety profile. Side effects are rare and mild.**

**Uncommon (reported at higher doses or in sensitive individuals):**

- **Headache** — Occasionally reported, typically at doses above 400mg. Mild and transient. May be related to changes in cerebral blood flow or neurotransmitter shifts.
- **Mild GI discomfort** — Nausea or stomach upset, rare, usually associated with taking high doses on an empty stomach.
- **Dizziness** — Very rarely reported, typically when combined with other calming agents or blood pressure medications.
- **Slight drowsiness** — Uncommon at standard doses (100-200mg). More likely at 400mg+ or when combined with other relaxants. Not true sedation — rather a deep relaxation that may feel sleepy in already-tired individuals.

**Not observed:**

- **No dependency or addiction potential** — L-Theanine does not activate reward pathways, does not produce euphoria, and does not create cravings. It can be stopped abruptly without withdrawal symptoms.
- **No tolerance development** — Efficacy is maintained with chronic use. No dose escalation needed over time.
- **No cognitive impairment** — Unlike GABAergic drugs (benzodiazepines, alcohol), L-Theanine does not impair memory, reaction time, or executive function. It actually improves them.
- **No motor impairment** — Safe to use before driving, operating machinery, or engaging in activities requiring coordination.
- **No rebound anxiety** — Stopping L-Theanine does not cause a resurgence of anxiety above baseline levels.
- **No hangover effect** — No morning grogginess or next-day cognitive fog, even at sleep-supportive doses.

**Special populations:**

- **Pregnancy and breastfeeding** — Insufficient clinical data for supplemental doses. Green tea consumption (which contains L-Theanine) is generally considered safe in moderation during pregnancy, but concentrated supplements have not been specifically studied. Consult a physician.
- **Children** — Studied in children aged 8-12 for ADHD and sleep at 200-400mg/day with good tolerability. Should be supervised by a pediatrician.
- **Liver or kidney disease** — No known hepatotoxic or nephrotoxic effects. L-Theanine undergoes minimal hepatic metabolism. However, caution is warranted in severe organ impairment as with any supplement.

**Overall safety assessment:**

L-Theanine is one of the safest supplements available. It has FDA GRAS status, decades of safe use in tea consumption, and a clean record in clinical trials. It is an excellent first-line option for individuals seeking anxiety relief or cognitive support without the risks of pharmaceutical interventions.
