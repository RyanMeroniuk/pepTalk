# L-Theanine
## Peptide Synergies

- **Selank** — The most natural pairing for L-Theanine in the peptide world. Selank is an anxiolytic heptapeptide that modulates GABA-A receptors, increases BDNF, and regulates serotonin metabolism. Both L-Theanine and Selank reduce anxiety without sedation or cognitive impairment. **Synergy:** L-Theanine promotes alpha brain waves and provides immediate, accessible anxiolysis, while Selank offers deeper GABA modulation and immune support. Together, they create a comprehensive anti-anxiety stack that enhances focus, mood, and stress resilience without any dependency risk. **Use case:** Ideal for individuals managing chronic stress, performance anxiety, or seeking nootropic benefits with anxiolytic properties. L-Theanine provides daily oral support while Selank (intranasal) can be used for more targeted intervention.

- **Semax** — Semax is a nootropic peptide that upregulates BDNF and enhances dopaminergic and serotonergic transmission. L-Theanine's calming influence complements Semax's cognitive-enhancing effects. **Synergy:** Semax drives cognitive enhancement and neuroplasticity while L-Theanine prevents the overstimulation or anxiety that some users report with potent nootropics. The combination produces focused, calm cognitive enhancement. **Use case:** Deep work, learning-intensive periods, cognitive rehabilitation.

- **Dihexa** — A potent nootropic peptide that promotes synaptogenesis. L-Theanine can provide a calming baseline that allows Dihexa's cognitive enhancement to be experienced as clean, productive focus rather than restless mental activation.

- **DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide)** — For sleep optimization. L-Theanine (200-400mg) before bed reduces pre-sleep anxiety and mental chatter, while DSIP promotes deep delta wave sleep. L-Theanine addresses the cognitive-emotional barrier to sleep onset; DSIP enhances sleep depth and quality.

- **Pinealon** — Both support neuroprotection and cognitive function. L-Theanine provides daily anxiolytic and alpha-wave support while Pinealon supports pineal function and memory. Complementary mechanisms for brain health optimization.

- **BPC-157** — Indirect synergy. BPC-157 supports the dopaminergic and serotonergic systems through gut-brain axis healing. L-Theanine modulates the same neurotransmitter systems centrally. In individuals with gut-brain dysfunction, combining BPC-157's restorative effects with L-Theanine's direct CNS modulation addresses the problem from both ends.

**General note:** L-Theanine's exceptional safety profile makes it one of the easiest supplements to combine with peptides. It does not interfere with peptide pharmacokinetics and provides a supportive neurological baseline for virtually any peptide protocol.
