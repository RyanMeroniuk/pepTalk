# L-Tryptophan
## Classification

- **Category:** Essential amino acid; serotonin and melatonin precursor
- **Chemical class:** Aromatic, essential amino acid (indole-containing)
- **Molecular formula:** C11H12N2O2
- **Common names:** L-tryptophan, tryptophan, Trp, W (single-letter amino acid code)
- **Supplement type:** Amino acid / sleep and mood support / neurotransmitter precursor
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (USA, OTC); also a normal dietary component of protein. Has a notable historical safety episode (see overview/side-effects — the 1989 EMS contamination event)
- **Biochemical role:** The dietary precursor in the pathway **tryptophan → 5-HTP → serotonin → melatonin**, and also a substrate for the kynurenine pathway and for niacin (vitamin B3) synthesis
- **Primary systems:** Neurological (serotonin/melatonin synthesis — mood, sleep), and broader metabolism (kynurenine, niacin)
- **Related compounds:** 5-htp (the direct downstream metabolite/precursor to serotonin) and melatonin (the end-product relevant to sleep). Tryptophan is "upstream" and more regulated than 5-HTP
