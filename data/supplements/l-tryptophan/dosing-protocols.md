# L-Tryptophan
## Dosing Protocols

### Sleep Support
- **Dose:** 500-2,000 mg taken ~30-60 minutes before bed
- **Timing:** Evening, away from a high-protein meal (protein's competing amino acids reduce brain uptake). A small carbohydrate snack may improve brain entry
- **Notes:** Larger doses (1-3 g) have been studied for sleep; start at the lower end and titrate

### Mood Support
- **Dose:** 500-1,500 mg/day, often split, taken away from high-protein meals
- **Timing:** Can be taken during the day for mood, though it may be mildly relaxing; many prefer evening dosing
- **Notes:** Tryptophan needs larger doses than 5-htp for comparable serotonergic effect because much is diverted to other pathways

### Why Doses Are Higher Than 5-HTP
- Because tryptophan is rate-limited at tryptophan hydroxylase and substantially diverted into the kynurenine and niacin pathways, only a fraction becomes serotonin. Effective doses are therefore higher (gram-level) than 5-HTP (milligram-level)

### Food Timing (Critical for Effectiveness)
- **Take away from protein:** A high-protein meal supplies competing large neutral amino acids that block tryptophan's transport into the brain. Take tryptophan on a relatively empty stomach
- **Small carbohydrate context can help:** A modest carbohydrate snack raises insulin, which clears competing amino acids from the blood, improving the ratio of tryptophan reaching the brain

### Cofactor Support
- Adequate **vitamin B6** supports conversion along the pathway. Some formulas pair tryptophan (or 5-HTP) with B6

### CRITICAL SAFETY — Do Not Combine With Serotonergic Drugs
- **Do NOT combine L-tryptophan with SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, or other serotonergic medications** without explicit physician supervision. Adding a serotonin precursor on top of these drugs can cause **serotonin syndrome**, a potentially life-threatening condition (see drug-interactions and side-effects). This is the single most important dosing caution

### Cycling and Duration
- No firmly established cycling requirement. Use the lowest effective dose. If using for sleep, intermittent use is reasonable. Reassess ongoing need periodically
