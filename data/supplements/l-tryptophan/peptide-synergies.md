# L-Tryptophan
## Peptide Synergies

> **Safety note first:** L-tryptophan is a serotonin precursor. Do NOT combine it with serotonergic medications (SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs) due to serotonin-syndrome risk. The peptide pairings below are for sleep/recovery and mood support in people NOT on serotonergic drugs. Avoid stacking with 5-htp (another serotonin precursor).

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + L-Tryptophan (Sleep & Recovery)
- **Rationale:** GH secretagogues like CJC-1295/Ipamorelin are dosed before bed and improve deep (slow-wave) sleep and recovery. L-tryptophan supports the serotonin→melatonin pathway that governs sleep onset. Together they support both falling asleep (tryptophan) and sleep depth/recovery (GH peptides) — a complementary nighttime stack.
- **Protocol:** L-tryptophan 500-1,500 mg ~30-60 min before bed (away from protein, with a small carb) + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Best for:** Sleep quality, overnight recovery, anti-aging sleep optimization

#### Epitalon + L-Tryptophan (Melatonin & Circadian Support)
- **Rationale:** Epitalon helps regulate melatonin production and circadian rhythm. L-tryptophan supplies the upstream substrate (serotonin → melatonin). Pairing the regulator (Epitalon) with the precursor (tryptophan) supports the body's own melatonin synthesis and sleep timing.
- **Protocol:** L-tryptophan in the evening + Epitalon in cyclical anti-aging courses
- **Best for:** Circadian rhythm support, age-related sleep decline, longevity-focused sleep protocols

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide) + L-Tryptophan
- **Rationale:** DSIP supports sleep onset and delta-wave (deep) sleep architecture. L-tryptophan feeds the serotonin/melatonin pathway for sleep timing. Two complementary routes to better sleep — one via delta-wave modulation, one via the melatonin precursor pathway.
- **Protocol:** L-tryptophan in the evening + DSIP per standard sleep protocol
- **Best for:** Sleep-onset difficulty, fragmented sleep, recovery

#### Selank + L-Tryptophan (Calm Mood Support)
- **Rationale:** Selank is an anxiolytic nootropic that modulates GABA and balances neurotransmitters without sedation. L-tryptophan supports serotonin for mood and calm. Together they support a relaxed, stable mood state — note both touch neurotransmitter systems, so introduce one at a time and avoid in those on serotonergic drugs.
- **Protocol:** L-tryptophan in the evening + Selank at standard intranasal dosing
- **Best for:** Stress-related mood support, evening wind-down

### Tier 3: Complementary Synergy

#### Tesamorelin + L-Tryptophan (Body Composition + Sleep)
- **Rationale:** Tesamorelin raises GH and targets visceral fat; quality sleep supports the recovery and metabolic benefits of GH therapy. L-tryptophan supports sleep onset via the melatonin pathway, helping consolidate the recovery window during a fat-loss phase.
- **Protocol:** L-tryptophan in the evening + Tesamorelin at prescribed dosing
- **Best for:** Body-composition phases where sleep quality supports results

### Supplement Synergies (Non-Peptide)
- **Melatonin:** Tryptophan is the upstream precursor; melatonin is the end-product. Some protocols use a small melatonin dose for timing plus tryptophan for substrate support — keep melatonin doses low and avoid overlap that oversedates.
- **5-HTP:** The direct downstream serotonin precursor. **Do NOT stack tryptophan and 5-htp together** — both raise serotonin and combining them compounds serotonin-syndrome risk. They are alternatives, not a stack.
- **Magnesium + Vitamin B6:** Magnesium supports relaxation/sleep; B6 is a cofactor for converting 5-HTP to serotonin — both support tryptophan's pathway and a calming bedtime routine.
- **GABA:** Calming neurotransmitter support that complements tryptophan's role in evening wind-down (effect-level synergy).
