# Tongkat Ali
## Foods to Avoid

- **Excessive caffeine:** Tongkat Ali can be mildly alerting on its own. Stacking it with high caffeine intake (especially the popular Tongkat Ali coffee preparations) can compound restlessness, jitteriness, and sleep disruption. Keep caffeine moderate and avoid late-day combinations
- **Excessive alcohol:** Heavy alcohol use suppresses testosterone, raises cortisol, and stresses the liver — directly opposing Tongkat Ali's free-testosterone and stress-resilience goals. Alcohol also worsens sleep, which is when much of natural testosterone production occurs
- **High-sugar, highly processed diets:** Chronic high sugar intake promotes insulin resistance and inflammation and is associated with lower testosterone. A diet built on processed food undermines the hormonal environment Tongkat Ali is trying to optimize
- **Soy in very large amounts (modest concern):** Large habitual intakes of soy isoflavones have a weak, inconsistent association with estrogenic effects in some individuals. Moderate soy is fine for most people, but those specifically chasing free-testosterone optimization may choose to keep it moderate
- **Trans fats and excessive seed-oil-heavy fried foods:** Associated with poorer metabolic and hormonal health, working against the goal

### Behaviors, Not Foods
- **Late-day dosing:** Because Tongkat Ali can be alerting, taking it in the evening can impair sleep — and poor sleep lowers testosterone, defeating the purpose
- **Chronic under-sleeping:** No supplement compensates for the testosterone suppression caused by chronic sleep deprivation

### Generally Fine (Don't Overthink)
- There are no foods that meaningfully block Tongkat Ali absorption
- Normal coffee intake in the morning is fine for most people; it's *excessive* or *late* caffeine that causes issues
