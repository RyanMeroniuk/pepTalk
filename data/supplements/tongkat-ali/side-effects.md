# Tongkat Ali
## Side Effects

- **Restlessness, irritability, or insomnia:** The most commonly reported issue, especially at higher doses or with evening dosing. Tongkat Ali can feel mildly stimulating/alerting for some users. Taking it in the morning and keeping the dose at 200-400 mg/day usually prevents this
- **GI discomfort:** Mild nausea or stomach upset, more likely on an empty stomach. Taking with food helps
- **Increased aggression or "edginess":** A small subset of users report feeling more irritable or aggressive, plausibly linked to the rise in free testosterone. Reducing the dose typically resolves it
- **Headache:** Occasionally reported, usually transient
- **Sleep disruption:** Linked to the alerting effect and to higher free-testosterone tone in sensitive individuals. Avoid late-day dosing
- **Heavy-metal exposure (product-quality issue, not the herb itself):** Cheap, unstandardized, or poorly sourced Tongkat Ali has been found contaminated with mercury and other heavy metals. This is a manufacturing/sourcing problem — choose standardized, third-party-tested extracts (e.g., LJ100). (For an exudate-based sibling, shilajit, this contamination risk is even more pronounced — see the shilajit entry.)

### Theoretical / Less-Established Concerns
- **Hormone-sensitive conditions:** Because it raises free testosterone and may modestly inhibit aromatase, Tongkat Ali should be approached cautiously by anyone with a hormone-sensitive condition (e.g., prostate cancer, certain hormone-driven cancers). Discuss with a physician
- **Sleep and mood in already-optimized men:** Men who are not stressed and already have healthy free testosterone may notice fewer benefits and more of the alerting/restlessness side effects

### Contraindications
- **Pregnancy and breastfeeding:** Avoid — insufficient safety data and hormonal activity make it inappropriate
- **Hormone-sensitive cancers / prostate conditions:** Use only with physician oversight given the free-testosterone-raising mechanism
- **Children and adolescents:** Not appropriate — endocrine system is still developing

### Safety Profile Summary
- Generally well-tolerated in healthy adult men at standardized doses of 200-400 mg/day across trials of up to several months
- Most adverse effects are dose-related (restlessness, irritability, sleep) and resolve with dose reduction or morning dosing
- The biggest practical safety variable is product quality (heavy-metal contamination in low-grade products), not the herb's inherent toxicity
