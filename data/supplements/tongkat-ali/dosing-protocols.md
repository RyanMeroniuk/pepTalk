# Tongkat Ali
## Dosing Protocols

- **General testosterone/libido support (standardized extract):**
  - **Dose:** 200-400 mg/day of standardized water extract (e.g., LJ100). The commonly referenced "Huberman" daily dose is ~400 mg/day
  - **Timing:** Morning, with or without food. Some users report mild stimulation/alertness, so morning dosing avoids sleep disruption
  - **Duration:** Effects accumulate over 2-4+ weeks; most trials run 4-12 weeks
  - **Note:** Start at the lower end (200 mg) to assess tolerance, then titrate up

- **Stress/cortisol reduction:**
  - **Dose:** ~200 mg/day standardized extract (the dose used in the well-known moderately-stressed-adults trial)
  - **Timing:** Morning
  - **Duration:** Cortisol/testosterone and mood changes reported within ~4 weeks

- **Male fertility (eurycomanone-focused):**
  - **Dose:** 200-300 mg/day of eurycomanone-standardized extract
  - **Duration:** Sperm parameters (count, motility, morphology) typically take 8-12 weeks to improve, reflecting the ~74-day spermatogenesis cycle

- **Body composition / performance adjunct:**
  - **Dose:** 200-400 mg/day standardized extract alongside resistance training
  - **Timing:** Morning or pre-workout
  - **Duration:** 8-12 weeks; effects are modest and training-dependent

- **Cycling considerations:**
  - **No strict requirement to cycle**, but many users run 5 days on / 2 days off, or 8-12 weeks on / 1-2 weeks off, to reassess baseline and avoid any tolerance to the alerting effect
  - Because it works largely by freeing endogenous testosterone (not by supplying a hormone), it does not suppress the HPG axis the way exogenous hormones (e.g., TRT, DHEA) can — but conservative cycling remains a reasonable practice

- **With food:** Can be taken fasted or fed. Some users find an empty stomach increases the alerting effect; taking with food can smooth this out

- **What to avoid:** Megadosing well beyond 400-600 mg/day of standardized extract provides no proven additional benefit and increases the chance of restlessness, irritability, or sleep disruption
