# Tongkat Ali
## Drug Interactions

### Contraindications (Avoid / Physician Oversight Required)
- **Hormone-sensitive cancers (prostate cancer, certain breast cancers) and prostate conditions:** Tongkat Ali raises free testosterone and may modestly inhibit aromatase. This is contraindicated in androgen-sensitive cancers and warrants medical oversight in benign prostate conditions (BPH). Do not use without specialist guidance
- **Hormone therapy / TRT:** Combining Tongkat Ali with testosterone replacement therapy or other hormonal agents stacks androgenic effects and complicates lab monitoring (free testosterone, estradiol, hematocrit). Coordinate with the prescribing clinician
- **Pregnancy and breastfeeding:** Contraindicated — hormonally active with insufficient safety data
- **Active cancer (general):** Avoid initiating without oncologist input given the hormonal activity

### Caution / Monitor (Moderate)
- **Blood pressure medications (antihypertensives):** Some reports suggest Tongkat Ali may modestly influence blood pressure. Monitor blood pressure when combining, especially during initiation
- **Blood thinners / anticoagulants (warfarin, apixaban, antiplatelet agents):** Limited data, but botanical extracts can theoretically affect platelet function or interact with clotting pathways. Use caution and monitor if combined, particularly around procedures
- **Antidepressants (SSRIs, SNRIs):** No well-characterized interaction, but because Tongkat Ali affects cortisol and can be alerting, monitor for changes in mood, agitation, or sleep when combining
- **Diabetes medications:** Some adaptogenic herbs can modestly influence blood glucose. Monitor blood sugar if combining, though the evidence for Tongkat Ali specifically is limited
- **Immunosuppressants:** Theoretical caution, as some Eurycoma compounds have immunomodulatory activity; monitor if combined

### Timing Considerations
- **Stimulating supplements/medications:** Because Tongkat Ali can be alerting, take it at the same time as morning routines and keep it hours apart from any evening sedatives or sleep aids to avoid opposing effects on sleep
- **Separate from CNS depressants at night:** If using sleep medications, dose Tongkat Ali in the morning and keep the two well separated across the day

### Lab / Monitoring Notes
- **Hormone panels:** If pursuing testosterone goals, baseline and follow-up total testosterone, free testosterone, SHBG, and estradiol let you confirm the expected SHBG drop and free-T rise
- **Inform your physician:** Always disclose Tongkat Ali use before procedures and when interpreting hormone or metabolic labs

### General Safety Notes
- Most documented issues are dose-related (restlessness, irritability, sleep disruption) rather than hard drug interactions
- The strongest interaction concern is conceptual overlap with other hormonal agents (TRT, DHEA) and contraindication in hormone-sensitive disease, rather than classic pharmacokinetic interactions
