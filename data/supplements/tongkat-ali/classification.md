# Tongkat Ali
## Classification

- **Category:** Adaptogen / pro-fertility botanical / testosterone-support herb
- **Subcategory:** Herbal supplement / Southeast Asian traditional botanical
- **Botanical name:** Eurycoma longifolia
- **Family:** Simaroubaceae
- **Common names:** Longjack, Malaysian ginseng, Tongkat Ali, Pasak Bumi, Ali's walking stick
- **Active compounds:** Quassinoids (notably eurycomanone), eurypeptides, glycosaponins, alkaloids; the standardized extract LJ100 is characterized by ~22% eurypeptides, 40% glycosaponins, and 30% polysaccharides
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (USA, OTC); long-standing traditional medicine ingredient in Malaysia, Indonesia, and Vietnam; not FDA-approved as a drug; not banned by WADA (unlike DHEA)
- **Primary systems:** Endocrine (free testosterone, SHBG, HPG axis), reproductive (sperm parameters, libido), neuroendocrine (cortisol/stress resilience)
- **Popularization:** Brought to mainstream attention by Andrew Huberman and other longevity/performance figures as a daily testosterone-support and libido herb (commonly ~400 mg/day of standardized extract)
