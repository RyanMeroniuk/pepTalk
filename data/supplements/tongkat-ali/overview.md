# Tongkat Ali
## Overview

Tongkat Ali (Eurycoma longifolia) is a flowering shrub native to the rainforests of Malaysia, Indonesia, and Vietnam, where its root has been used for centuries as a traditional remedy for low energy, declining libido, and male fertility. In Western supplementation it has become one of the most popular "natural testosterone support" herbs, valued less because it forcefully spikes hormones and more because it appears to gently optimize the body's own endocrine signaling. Its best-characterized mechanism is lowering sex hormone-binding globulin (SHBG), the protein that binds testosterone in the blood — by freeing bound testosterone, Tongkat Ali raises the *bioavailable (free)* testosterone fraction without necessarily changing total testosterone dramatically. Modern human trials, most using standardized extracts like LJ100, report modest improvements in free testosterone, libido, stress resilience (lower cortisol-to-testosterone ratio), and well-being, particularly in stressed, aging, or hypogonadal men. It is best understood as a slow, accumulating adaptogen for the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis rather than a hormone or a quick fix, and is frequently stacked with sibling testosterone-cluster supplements like boron, shilajit, zinc, and fadogia-agrestis.
