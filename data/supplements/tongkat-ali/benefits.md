# Tongkat Ali
## Benefits

- **Increased free testosterone:** The most reproducible benefit. By lowering SHBG, Tongkat Ali raises the bioavailable testosterone fraction. Effects are most pronounced in men with stress-related or age-related low testosterone, and more modest in young men with already-optimal hormone levels.
- **Improved libido and sexual function:** Multiple human trials report increased sexual desire, satisfaction, and erectile quality, in both men and (less studied) women. This is among the most consistently reported subjective benefits.
- **Stress resilience and lower cortisol:** A frequently cited trial in moderately stressed adults found reduced cortisol (~16%), increased testosterone (~37%), and improved tension/anger/confusion scores after ~4 weeks of standardized extract. This adaptogenic, anti-stress profile is a major reason for its popularity.
- **Male fertility support:** Standardized eurycomanone extracts have improved sperm concentration, motility, and morphology in subfertile men over 1-3 months, sometimes with measurable increases in successful conception in case series.
- **Mood and well-being:** Reductions in subjective stress, fatigue, and irritability, plus improved sense of well-being, are reported alongside the hormonal changes.
- **Body composition and performance (modest, less robust):** Some studies in older or active adults suggest improvements in muscle strength, lean mass, and fat loss when combined with training, though the effect sizes are smaller and less consistent than the hormonal/libido outcomes.
- **Bone and muscle support in aging men:** As a free-testosterone-raising adaptogen, it is studied in the context of age-related decline (andropause) where free-T tends to fall faster than total-T due to rising SHBG with age.

### Realistic Expectations
- Tongkat Ali is a gentle optimizer, not a hormone. Expect modest, cumulative changes over weeks, not dramatic overnight effects.
- The strongest, most replicated outcomes are libido and stress/well-being; the hormonal numbers are real but modest and most meaningful in stressed or older men.
