# Alpha-GPC
## Lifestyle Recommendations

### Cognitive Optimization
- **Pair Alpha-GPC with cognitive challenges** — cholinergic enhancement is most noticeable during demanding cognitive tasks. Study sessions, complex work, language learning, and skill acquisition all benefit from increased acetylcholine.
- **Practice active learning** — Alpha-GPC supports memory encoding; use it during periods of active study, not passive reading. Flashcards, active recall, spaced repetition, and problem-solving maximize the benefit.
- **Meditation and mindfulness** — acetylcholine supports attention and awareness. Meditation combined with cholinergic support can enhance the depth of practice.
- **Learn new skills** — Alpha-GPC supports synaptic plasticity. Challenge your brain with new instruments, languages, sports, or complex games (chess, Go).

### Exercise Integration
- **Take 600 mg pre-workout** for the GH potentiation effect — specifically 30-60 minutes before resistance training
- **Combine with progressive resistance training** — the GH increase from Alpha-GPC is most pronounced during intense resistance exercise
- **High-intensity intervals and sprints** also benefit from enhanced neuromuscular transmission
- **Track performance metrics** — log weights, reps, and power output to observe Alpha-GPC's impact on training capacity

### Sleep Hygiene (Protect Cholinergic Sleep Benefits)
- **Avoid Alpha-GPC after 3 PM** — acetylcholine is involved in wakefulness and REM sleep. Late dosing may delay sleep onset.
- **Prioritize 7-9 hours of sleep** — sleep is when the brain consolidates memories that Alpha-GPC helps encode during the day. Memory benefits of cholinergic enhancement depend on subsequent sleep for consolidation.
- **REM sleep supports learning** — Alpha-GPC's cholinergic effects support REM sleep quality, but only if you sleep adequately. Cutting sleep short negates this.

### Diet for Cholinergic Support
- **Eat eggs regularly** — 2-3 eggs/day provides ~300-450 mg dietary choline, supporting baseline cholinergic function alongside supplemental Alpha-GPC
- **Include organ meats weekly** — liver is the richest whole-food choline source (356 mg per 100 g)
- **Omega-3 fatty acids** — DHA supports neuronal membranes alongside Alpha-GPC's phospholipid donation. Fatty fish 2-3x/week or fish oil supplement.
- **Antioxidant-rich diet** — berries, dark leafy greens, dark chocolate — protects cholinergic neurons from oxidative damage
- **B-vitamin-rich diet** — eggs, meat, leafy greens, legumes — supports choline metabolism and neurotransmitter synthesis pathways

### Nootropic Stack Synergies
- **Foundation:** Alpha-GPC 300 mg (choline source)
- **Focus:** + Caffeine 100-200 mg + L-Theanine 100-200 mg (alert calm focus)
- **Memory:** + Bacopa monnieri 300 mg (standardized to 50% bacosides; long-term memory support)
- **Neurogenesis:** + Lion's Mane 500-1000 mg (NGF stimulation for neuronal growth)
- **Neuroprotection:** + Omega-3 DHA 1-2 g (membrane support)
- **Take these in the morning** for peak cognitive performance during the day

### Stress Management
- **Address chronic stress** — cortisol impairs hippocampal function and cholinergic signaling. Alpha-GPC supports the system but cannot override chronically elevated cortisol.
- **Techniques:** Regular exercise (endorphins, BDNF), meditation (10+ minutes daily), nature exposure (reduced cortisol), social connection, adequate sleep
- **Adaptogens** — ashwagandha (reduces cortisol), rhodiola (stress resilience) can complement Alpha-GPC's cognitive support

### Monitoring and Optimization
- **Journal cognitive performance** — track focus, memory, mood, and productivity to assess Alpha-GPC's impact
- **Cycle dosing if needed** — while tolerance is uncommon, some people benefit from taking weekends off (5 days on / 2 days off)
- **Adjust dose based on response** — 300 mg may be sufficient; not everyone needs 600 mg for cognitive effects
