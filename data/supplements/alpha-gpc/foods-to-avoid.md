# Alpha-GPC
## Foods to Avoid

### Foods That May Increase TMAO Production
- **If TMAO is a concern** (see Side Effects for full context), certain foods combined with high choline intake may increase TMAO production:
  - **Red meat (large quantities)** — L-carnitine in red meat is also converted to TMA by gut bacteria, adding to the TMAO load alongside choline
  - **Processed meats** — combine L-carnitine, choline, and preservatives that may affect gut bacteria composition
  - **Energy drinks containing L-carnitine** — additional TMA substrate
- **Context:** These foods are not "bad" and do not need to be eliminated. The TMAO concern is based on observational data and is not a proven causal risk factor. Normal dietary choline + standard Alpha-GPC doses are unlikely to cause problematic TMAO levels.

### Foods/Substances That May Reduce Alpha-GPC's Cognitive Effects
- **Alcohol** — acetylcholine signaling is impaired by alcohol. Alcohol reduces cognitive function through multiple pathways, including cholinergic suppression, GABA potentiation, and glutamate inhibition. Taking Alpha-GPC with alcohol is counterproductive.
- **Anticholinergic foods/substances** — some OTC medications (diphenhydramine/Benadryl, doxylamine) are anticholinergic and directly oppose Alpha-GPC's mechanism. If using Alpha-GPC for cognitive benefits, minimize anticholinergic medication use.
- **Excessive sugar/refined carbs** — blood sugar spikes and crashes impair cognitive function, reducing the benefit of Alpha-GPC's cholinergic enhancement

### Timing-Related Avoidance
- **Caffeine late in the day** — if taking Alpha-GPC in the morning/afternoon (as recommended), combining it with late-afternoon caffeine may cause overstimulation and sleep disruption
- **Heavy meals with Alpha-GPC** — while a small fat-containing snack enhances absorption, a very large, heavy meal may slow gastric emptying and delay Alpha-GPC's effects

### What NOT to Worry About
- **Coffee with Alpha-GPC** — despite some claims, moderate coffee consumption is synergistic with Alpha-GPC for attention and focus. Don't avoid coffee because of Alpha-GPC.
- **Eggs** — eating eggs with Alpha-GPC provides additional choline. This is beneficial, not problematic.
- **Other supplements** — Alpha-GPC has no significant negative interactions with common supplements (protein, creatine, vitamins, fish oil, etc.)
