# Alpha-GPC
## Dosing Protocols

### Cognitive Enhancement (Daily Nootropic Use)
- **Dose:** 300-600 mg/day Alpha-GPC (active compound; if using 50% product, double the weight)
- **Timing:** Morning or early afternoon. Avoid evening dosing as cholinergic stimulation may interfere with sleep in some individuals.
- **Duration:** Ongoing; safe for long-term daily use
- **Notes:** Start at 300 mg and assess. Some people respond well to 300 mg; others benefit from 600 mg. Rarely is more than 600 mg/day needed for cognitive effects.

### Pre-Workout (Growth Hormone + Power Output)
- **Dose:** 600 mg Alpha-GPC (active compound)
- **Timing:** 30-60 minutes before exercise
- **Duration:** Training days only, or daily if also using for cognitive benefits
- **Notes:** Based on the Kawamura (2012) and Bellar (2015) studies. The GH increase is exercise-dependent.

### Nootropic Stack Foundation
- **Dose:** 300 mg Alpha-GPC (active)
- **Timing:** Morning, taken with other nootropics (racetams, Semax, Selank, Lion's Mane, etc.)
- **Duration:** Ongoing with the stack
- **Notes:** Alpha-GPC prevents the "choline depletion headache" caused by racetams and other acetylcholine-demanding nootropics. Always include a choline source in nootropic stacks.

### Cognitive Decline / Clinical Use (European Protocols)
- **Dose:** 1200 mg/day Alpha-GPC (active compound), divided into 3 doses of 400 mg
- **Timing:** Morning, midday, and early afternoon
- **Duration:** 3-6 months in clinical trials; often ongoing in clinical practice
- **Notes:** This is the prescription dose used in European clinical trials for Alzheimer's, vascular dementia, and post-stroke recovery. Requires medical supervision.

### Age-Related Cognitive Support
- **Dose:** 300-600 mg/day
- **Timing:** Morning
- **Duration:** Ongoing
- **Notes:** Supports declining cholinergic function. Combine with other neuroprotective strategies (exercise, sleep optimization, omega-3s).

### With CJC-1295/Ipamorelin (GH Enhancement Stack)
- **Dose:** 600 mg Alpha-GPC pre-workout + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Rationale:** Alpha-GPC acutely enhances exercise-induced GH release; peptide secretagogues amplify the nocturnal GH pulse. Together, they maximize GH output across the day.

### Important Notes
- **50% vs 99% matters:** If your product says "Alpha-GPC 50%," you need to take TWICE the weight to get the stated active dose. Example: to get 600 mg active Alpha-GPC from a 50% product, take 1200 mg total.
- **Avoid evening dosing** — some people experience sleep disruption from cholinergic stimulation. Take all doses before 3 PM.
- **Start low, assess response** — 300 mg is effective for many people. Not everyone needs 600 mg.
- **Tolerance is not typically an issue** — Alpha-GPC can be taken daily without loss of effectiveness
- **Take with or without food** — Alpha-GPC is well-absorbed either way. Some people prefer taking it with a small fat-containing meal for slightly enhanced absorption.
