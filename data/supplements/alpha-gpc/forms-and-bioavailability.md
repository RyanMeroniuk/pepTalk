# Alpha-GPC
## Forms and Bioavailability

### Alpha-GPC 50% Powder
- **Standard supplement form** — Alpha-GPC is hygroscopic (absorbs moisture aggressively), so it is typically sold as a 50% concentration stabilized with silica or other flow agents
- **600 mg "Alpha-GPC 50%" = 300 mg actual Alpha-GPC = ~120 mg choline**
- **Read labels carefully** — some products list the total weight (including carrier), others list active Alpha-GPC. A product labeled "600 mg Alpha-GPC 50%" provides 300 mg of the active compound.
- Best for: those who prefer powder form for stacking or precise dosing

### Alpha-GPC Capsules (Standard)
- **Most common consumer form** — 300 mg or 600 mg capsules (usually 50% concentration)
- Convenient and precisely dosed
- **Check if the label states 50% or 99%** — this dramatically affects the actual dose
- Best for: daily cognitive support, convenience

### Alpha-GPC 99% (Concentrated)
- **Pharmaceutical-grade, near-pure Alpha-GPC**
- Much more potent per milligram — 300 mg of 99% provides nearly 300 mg active Alpha-GPC (~120 mg choline)
- **Very hygroscopic** — must be stored in airtight containers; clumps easily when exposed to air
- **More expensive** per unit but more cost-effective per actual mg of Alpha-GPC
- Best for: those who want precise dosing without filler

### Comparison to Other Choline Sources

#### CDP-Choline (Citicoline)
- **~18% choline by weight** (vs Alpha-GPC's ~40%)
- Also crosses the blood-brain barrier effectively
- Provides cytidine (converted to uridine) which supports RNA and membrane synthesis
- **CDP-choline may be better for membrane repair and overall neuroprotection**
- **Alpha-GPC may be better for raw acetylcholine production and acute cognitive/physical effects**
- Both are excellent; they can even be combined

#### Choline Bitartrate
- **~40% choline by weight** but **poorly crosses the blood-brain barrier**
- Raises plasma choline but does not efficiently increase brain acetylcholine
- Much cheaper than Alpha-GPC
- Best for: meeting dietary choline requirements, liver support — NOT for nootropic effects

#### Phosphatidylcholine (from lecithin)
- **~13% choline by weight**
- Poorly crosses the blood-brain barrier
- Requires very high doses (grams) for meaningful choline delivery
- Best for: general dietary choline, emulsification — NOT for nootropic effects

#### Choline Citrate
- Similar to choline bitartrate — peripheral choline source, poor brain penetration
- Not recommended for cognitive purposes

### Bioavailability Summary
| Source | Choline Content | Brain Penetration | Best For |
|--------|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Alpha-GPC** | ~40% | Excellent | Cognition, performance |
| **CDP-Choline** | ~18% | Excellent | Cognition, neuroprotection |
| Choline bitartrate | ~40% | Poor | Dietary choline, liver |
| Phosphatidylcholine | ~13% | Poor | Dietary choline |

### Storage Notes
- **Alpha-GPC is hygroscopic** — keep containers tightly sealed, store in a cool, dry place
- **Powder form clumps easily** — silica gel packets in the container help
- **Capsules are more stable** than loose powder for long-term storage
- **Do not expose to excessive heat or humidity**
