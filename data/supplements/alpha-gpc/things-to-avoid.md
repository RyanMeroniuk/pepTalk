# Alpha-GPC
## Things to Avoid

### Dosing Mistakes
- **Evening dosing** — Alpha-GPC's cholinergic stimulation can interfere with sleep onset. Take all doses before 3 PM. If you find it stimulating, restrict to morning-only dosing.
- **Mega-dosing without need** — 300-600 mg/day is effective for most cognitive and performance goals. Higher doses (1200 mg/day) are used clinically for dementia but are not necessary for healthy individuals seeking nootropic effects.
- **Confusing 50% and 99% products** — a "600 mg" capsule of 50% Alpha-GPC only contains 300 mg active compound. Read labels carefully and do the math.
- **Not storing properly** — Alpha-GPC is extremely hygroscopic. Exposed powder absorbs moisture and becomes a sticky, clumpy mess. Keep containers tightly sealed. Silica gel packets help.

### Substance Interactions
- **Anticholinergic medications** — diphenhydramine (Benadryl), scopolamine, atropine, and tricyclic antidepressants have anticholinergic effects that directly oppose Alpha-GPC. Taking both is counterproductive.
- **Excessive nicotine** — nicotine acts on nicotinic acetylcholine receptors. While moderate nicotine and Alpha-GPC are not directly conflicting, excessive nicotine use can dysregulate cholinergic signaling.
- **Alcohol (cognitive goals)** — alcohol impairs the cholinergic cognitive enhancement Alpha-GPC provides. Avoid alcohol during periods where cognitive performance matters.

### Lifestyle Factors
- **Sleep deprivation** — while Alpha-GPC provides some cognitive resilience during sleep loss, it cannot replace adequate sleep. Chronic sleep deprivation degrades cholinergic neuron function over time.
- **Sedentary lifestyle (for GH benefits)** — Alpha-GPC's growth hormone increase is exercise-dependent. Without training, you miss the GH potentiation benefit.
- **Chronic stress without management** — cortisol impairs hippocampal function (memory) and cholinergic signaling. Alpha-GPC supports the system, but chronic stress overwhelms it.

### Supplement Stacking Cautions
- **Multiple high-dose cholinergics simultaneously** — taking Alpha-GPC + CDP-Choline + phosphatidylcholine + choline bitartrate all at once is excessive. Choose one primary cholinergic source (Alpha-GPC for nootropic purposes) and don't stack redundantly.
- **Acetylcholinesterase inhibitors (huperzine A) + high-dose Alpha-GPC** — huperzine A prevents acetylcholine breakdown; Alpha-GPC increases production. Together at high doses, they could produce excessive acetylcholine (SLUDGE symptoms). If combining, use conservative doses of each.

### Common Misconceptions
- **"Alpha-GPC is just for bodybuilders"** — while the GH increase gets attention, the cognitive benefits are the primary reason most people should take it
- **"Choline bitartrate is the same thing"** — it is not. Choline bitartrate does not cross the blood-brain barrier efficiently. For cognitive effects, Alpha-GPC or CDP-Choline are necessary.
- **"TMAO means I shouldn't take choline"** — choline is an essential nutrient. Avoiding it to minimize TMAO is nutritionally counterproductive. See Side Effects for the full TMAO context.
