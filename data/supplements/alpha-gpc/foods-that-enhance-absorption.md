# Alpha-GPC
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

### Fat-Containing Foods (Phospholipid Absorption)
- **Alpha-GPC is a phospholipid** — absorption is enhanced by co-ingestion with dietary fats, which stimulate bile secretion and form mixed micelles that facilitate phospholipid uptake
- **Healthy fat sources to pair with Alpha-GPC:**
  - Eggs (also provide additional choline)
  - Avocado
  - Olive oil
  - Nuts (almonds, walnuts, macadamias)
  - Fatty fish (salmon, sardines)
  - MCT oil or coconut oil
  - Full-fat yogurt
- **A small fat-containing meal or snack** (10-15 g fat) is sufficient to enhance absorption. A handful of nuts or half an avocado works well.

### Synergistic Nutrients for Acetylcholine Production

#### Acetyl Groups (Acetyl-CoA Donors)
- **Pantothenic acid (vitamin B5)** — essential cofactor for acetyl-CoA production (which donates the acetyl group to choline to form acetylcholine). Found in: eggs, avocado, mushrooms, sunflower seeds, sweet potatoes.
- **Acetyl-L-Carnitine (ALCAR)** — supplement that provides acetyl groups directly. ALCAR + Alpha-GPC is a well-known nootropic stack for maximizing acetylcholine synthesis.

#### B-Vitamins (Choline Metabolism)
- **Folate (B9)** — supports choline metabolism via one-carbon pathways. Found in: dark leafy greens, legumes, asparagus, citrus.
- **Vitamin B12** — works with folate in methylation pathways that intersect with choline metabolism. Found in: meat, fish, eggs, dairy.
- **Vitamin B6** — cofactor for various neurotransmitter synthesis pathways. Found in: chicken, bananas, potatoes, chickpeas.

#### DHA (Omega-3 Fatty Acid)
- **DHA is concentrated in neuronal membranes** alongside phosphatidylcholine (which Alpha-GPC supports)
- **DHA + Alpha-GPC** supports both the lipid and phospholipid components of neuronal membranes
- **Sources:** Fatty fish (salmon, mackerel, sardines), fish oil, algae oil (vegan source)

### Optimal Pairing Meals
- **Morning brain stack:** 2-3 eggs (choline + fat + B vitamins) + Alpha-GPC 300 mg + coffee (caffeine enhances cholinergic attention effects)
- **Pre-workout:** Alpha-GPC 600 mg + banana + handful of almonds (fat for absorption + carbs for energy)
- **Nootropic smoothie:** Alpha-GPC + MCT oil + blueberries (anthocyanins support cognitive function) + spinach (folate)

### Compounds That Enhance Alpha-GPC's Nootropic Effects (Not Foods)
- **Racetams (piracetam, aniracetam, oxiracetam)** — increase acetylcholine demand and utilization, making Alpha-GPC more impactful
- **Caffeine** — enhances attention and alertness synergistically with cholinergic signaling
- **L-Theanine** — provides calm focus that complements Alpha-GPC's sharpening effect
- **Lion's Mane mushroom** — stimulates NGF (nerve growth factor), supporting the neurons that Alpha-GPC fuels
- **Bacopa monnieri** — enhances memory consolidation alongside Alpha-GPC's encoding support
