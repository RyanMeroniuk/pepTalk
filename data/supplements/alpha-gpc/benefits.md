# Alpha-GPC
## Benefits

### Cognitive Enhancement
- **Improved memory formation and recall** — acetylcholine is essential for encoding new memories (hippocampal function). Alpha-GPC supports this by increasing ACh availability.
- **Enhanced attention and focus** — cholinergic signaling in the prefrontal cortex drives sustained attention. Users commonly report sharper focus within 30-60 minutes of dosing.
- **Faster learning** — acetylcholine facilitates synaptic plasticity (LTP — long-term potentiation), the cellular mechanism underlying learning
- **Improved verbal fluency** — some users report enhanced word recall and verbal processing speed

### Clinical Cognitive Support
- **Alzheimer's disease** — multiple European clinical trials using 1200 mg/day Alpha-GPC (as prescription Gliatilin) showed significant improvement in cognitive scores (ADAS-cog) vs placebo in mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's
- **Vascular dementia** — improvements in cognitive function, mood, and daily living activities with 1200 mg/day
- **Post-stroke recovery** — 1000-1200 mg/day improved cognitive recovery in stroke patients (Italian multicenter study, ~2,000 patients)
- **Age-related cognitive decline** — supports cholinergic function that naturally declines with aging

### Physical Performance
- **Increased growth hormone output** — 600 mg before exercise increased GH secretion by ~44% vs placebo during resistance training (Kawamura et al., 2012)
- **Enhanced power output** — 600 mg Alpha-GPC increased peak force by 14% in a bench press throw test in college-aged men (Bellar et al., 2015)
- **Improved rate of force development** — faster muscle contraction initiation
- **Potential for sprint performance** — enhanced neuromuscular transmission may benefit explosive activities

### Neuroprotection
- **Supports recovery from brain injury** — clinical use in TBI and post-stroke recovery in European medicine
- **Membrane repair** — phosphatidylcholine donation supports neuronal membrane integrity after damage
- **Anti-ischemic** — preserves cholinergic function during reduced blood flow to the brain

### Choline Sufficiency
- **Prevents choline deficiency** — choline is an essential nutrient (adequate intake: 550 mg/day men, 425 mg/day women) that many people under-consume
- **Supports liver function** — choline is required for VLDL synthesis and fat export from the liver. Deficiency can cause fatty liver disease.
- **Supports methylation** — choline is a methyl donor (via betaine conversion), supporting homocysteine metabolism

### Synergy with Other Nootropics
- **Prevents "racetam headache"** — racetam nootropics (piracetam, aniracetam, etc.) increase acetylcholine demand. Without sufficient choline, users experience headaches and brain fog. Alpha-GPC eliminates this.
- **Enhances nootropic stack effectiveness** — provides the cholinergic foundation that most nootropic compounds build upon
