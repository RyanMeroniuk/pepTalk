# Alpha-GPC
## Classification

- **Category:** Cholinergic nootropic compound
- **Chemical name:** L-Alpha-glycerylphosphorylcholine (also: choline alfoscerate, alpha-glycerophosphocholine)
- **Molecular formula:** C8H20NO6P
- **Supplement type:** Choline donor / nootropic / cognitive enhancer
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (OTC) in the United States; prescription drug in Europe (Gliatilin, Delecit) for cognitive decline and dementia
- **Primary systems:** Neurological (acetylcholine synthesis), muscular (motor neuron function), endocrine (growth hormone modulation)
- **Choline content:** Alpha-GPC is ~40% choline by weight — meaning 600 mg Alpha-GPC provides ~240 mg of choline
- **Key distinction:** Alpha-GPC is the most bioavailable oral choline source for raising brain acetylcholine levels, superior to choline bitartrate, choline citrate, and lecithin
