# Alpha-GPC
## Mechanisms of Action

### 1. Acetylcholine Synthesis (Primary Mechanism)
- Alpha-GPC is hydrolyzed in the gut and liver to release free choline
- Choline crosses the blood-brain barrier via the choline transporter (CHT1)
- In cholinergic neurons, choline + acetyl-CoA → acetylcholine (via choline acetyltransferase, ChAT)
- Alpha-GPC raises brain acetylcholine levels more effectively than other choline sources because:
  - Higher bioavailability (~40% choline by weight, efficiently absorbed)
  - Better blood-brain barrier penetration
  - Also donates a glycerophosphate group that supports membrane synthesis

### 2. Phospholipid Membrane Support
- Alpha-GPC provides glycerophosphocholine, which is incorporated into phosphatidylcholine — the primary phospholipid in neuronal cell membranes
- Supports membrane fluidity, receptor function, and signal transduction
- Neuroprotective effect: maintains membrane integrity under oxidative stress and ischemic conditions
- This dual role (neurotransmitter precursor + membrane building block) makes Alpha-GPC uniquely valuable among choline sources

### 3. Growth Hormone Potentiation
- **600 mg Alpha-GPC before exercise** increased GH secretion by ~44% compared to placebo during resistance exercise (Kawamura et al., 2012)
- Mechanism: likely involves cholinergic stimulation of hypothalamic GHRH (growth hormone-releasing hormone) neurons, and/or suppression of somatostatin (GH-inhibiting hormone)
- The GH increase is acute and exercise-dependent — Alpha-GPC alone at rest does not significantly increase GH

### 4. Dopaminergic Modulation
- Cholinergic and dopaminergic systems are interconnected
- Alpha-GPC may increase dopamine release in the frontal cortex via cholinergic-dopaminergic pathway interactions
- This contributes to motivation, focus, and reward-related cognitive functions

### 5. Neuroprotection & Cognitive Recovery
- In clinical studies (European prescription use), Alpha-GPC improved cognitive function in patients with:
  - Vascular dementia
  - Alzheimer's disease (mild to moderate)
  - Post-stroke cognitive impairment
  - Traumatic brain injury
- Mechanisms include: membrane repair, cholinergic restoration, anti-inflammatory effects in neural tissue, and nerve growth factor (NGF) potentiation

### 6. Power Output Enhancement
- Acetylcholine is the neurotransmitter at the neuromuscular junction — it initiates muscle contraction
- By increasing acetylcholine availability, Alpha-GPC may enhance motor neuron firing rate and force production
- Some studies show acute increases in peak force and rate of force development (Bellar et al., 2015)
- Effect size is moderate and may depend on training status

### 7. Cholinergic Anti-Inflammatory Pathway
- The vagus nerve uses acetylcholine to suppress systemic inflammation (the "cholinergic anti-inflammatory pathway")
- By increasing acetylcholine availability, Alpha-GPC may indirectly support anti-inflammatory signaling via the vagus nerve
