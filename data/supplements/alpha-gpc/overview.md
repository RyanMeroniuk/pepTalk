# Alpha-GPC
## Overview

Alpha-GPC (L-alpha-glycerylphosphorylcholine) is the most efficient oral choline compound for increasing brain acetylcholine levels. Acetylcholine is the neurotransmitter most associated with learning, memory, attention, and muscle contraction. Alpha-GPC crosses the blood-brain barrier more effectively than other choline sources and serves as both a cognitive enhancer and a physical performance aid.

### Why It Matters

- **Acetylcholine precursor:** Acetylcholine is critical for memory formation, learning, attention, and the cholinergic system that declines with aging. Alpha-GPC directly increases acetylcholine synthesis in the brain.
- **Phosphatidylcholine donor:** Alpha-GPC provides the choline backbone for phosphatidylcholine, a key component of neuronal cell membranes. This supports membrane integrity and fluidity.
- **Growth hormone release:** 600 mg Alpha-GPC has been shown to acutely increase growth hormone output during exercise (Kawamura et al., 2012).
- **Power output:** Emerging evidence for increased peak force production and power output in trained individuals.
- **Neuroprotection:** Used clinically in Europe for cognitive decline, vascular dementia, and Alzheimer's disease (as a prescription drug).

### Who Benefits Most

- **Students and knowledge workers** — enhanced memory, learning, and attention
- **Older adults** — supports declining cholinergic function and may slow cognitive decline
- **Athletes** — acute growth hormone increase, potential power output enhancement
- **Anyone using racetams or other nootropics** — racetams increase acetylcholine demand; Alpha-GPC ensures adequate supply (prevents "racetam headache")
- **People using Semax or Selank** — complementary nootropic stacking; Alpha-GPC provides the cholinergic foundation
- **Individuals with low dietary choline intake** — eggs and liver are the main food sources; many people under-consume choline
- **Post-stroke or TBI patients** — clinical evidence for cognitive recovery support (used medically in Europe)
