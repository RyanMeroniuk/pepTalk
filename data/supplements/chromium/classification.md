# Chromium
## Classification

- **Category:** Essential Trace Mineral
- **Type:** Micronutrient
- **Essentiality:** Required in very small amounts; considered essential for normal carbohydrate and lipid metabolism
- **Chemical Symbol:** Cr
- **Atomic Number:** 24
- **Biologically active form:** Trivalent chromium (Cr3+); hexavalent chromium (Cr6+) is toxic and is NOT the supplemental form
- **Biological Role:** Potentiates insulin signaling; involved in macronutrient metabolism via chromodulin (low-molecular-weight chromium-binding substance)
- **Supplement Class:** Mineral supplement
- **Regulation:** Available over-the-counter as a dietary supplement; regulated by the FDA under DSHEA
- **Adequate Intake (AI):** 35mcg/day (adult males), 25mcg/day (adult females) — no RDA has been established due to insufficient data
- **Upper Tolerable Intake Level (UL):** Not established by the IOM; however, doses above 1,000mcg/day are generally not recommended without medical supervision
