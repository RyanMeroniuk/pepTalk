# Chromium
## Dosing Protocols

### General Maintenance
- **Dose:** 200mcg/day chromium picolinate
- **Purpose:** Ensure adequate chromium status for general metabolic health
- **Timing:** Take with a meal (enhances absorption and mirrors insulin-mediated chromium uptake)
- **Duration:** Can be taken long-term

### Blood Sugar Management (Prediabetes / Type 2 Diabetes)
- **Starting dose:** 200mcg/day chromium picolinate for 2-4 weeks
- **Therapeutic dose:** 400-1,000mcg/day, divided into 2 doses (200-500mcg with breakfast and dinner)
- **Duration:** 3-6 months minimum to assess response; can be continued long-term if beneficial
- **Monitoring:** Track fasting glucose, HbA1c, and fasting insulin at baseline and every 3 months
- **Important:** If taking diabetes medications (metformin, sulfonylureas, insulin), monitor blood sugar closely — chromium may enhance their effects and increase hypoglycemia risk. Work with your physician to adjust medication doses as needed.

### Weight Management / Appetite Control
- **Dose:** 400-600mcg/day chromium picolinate
- **Timing:** 200mcg with breakfast and 200-400mcg with lunch (to reduce afternoon/evening cravings)
- **Duration:** 8-12 weeks for initial assessment; continue if effective
- **Best combined with:** High-protein diet, regular exercise, and adequate sleep

### PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome)
- **Dose:** 200-1,000mcg/day chromium picolinate
- **Starting dose:** 200mcg/day, titrate up based on response
- **Timing:** Split doses with meals
- **Duration:** Minimum 3-6 months to assess hormonal and metabolic improvements
- **Monitoring:** Track fasting insulin, HOMA-IR, free testosterone, DHEA-S

### Atypical Depression
- **Dose:** 400-600mcg/day chromium picolinate
- **Timing:** Split doses — 200mcg with breakfast, 200-400mcg with lunch
- **Duration:** 8-12 weeks for initial assessment
- **Note:** Most evidence is for atypical depression with metabolic features (increased appetite, carb cravings, weight gain)

### Athletic Support
- **Dose:** 200-400mcg/day chromium picolinate
- **Timing:** Take with a post-workout meal (to support glycogen resynthesis)
- **Rationale:** Exercise increases urinary chromium excretion by 100-300%

### Timing Guidelines
- **With food:** Always take chromium with a meal — absorption is enhanced when taken with food, and the insulin response to meals mobilizes chromium uptake
- **Vitamin C:** Taking chromium with vitamin C (or foods rich in vitamin C) enhances absorption
- **Split doses:** For doses above 200mcg, split into 2 doses with meals to improve absorption and maintain steady blood levels
- **Morning vs. evening:** No strong preference; splitting between breakfast and dinner is most common

### Upper Limits and Safety
- **No established UL:** The IOM has not set a Tolerable Upper Intake Level for chromium due to lack of adverse effect data at typical doses
- **Practical ceiling:** Most clinicians recommend not exceeding 1,000mcg/day without medical supervision
- **Long-term safety:** Chromium picolinate at 200-1,000mcg/day has been used safely in studies lasting up to 5 years
- **Kidney considerations:** Chromium is renally excreted; individuals with kidney disease should use lower doses (200mcg/day maximum) and consult a nephrologist

### Cycling
- **Not typically required:** Unlike some supplements, chromium does not require cycling
- **Optional approach:** Some practitioners recommend 5 days on, 2 days off (weekdays only) for long-term use above 400mcg/day
- **Reassessment:** After 6 months of supplementation, reassess whether the metabolic benefits warrant continued use at the current dose

### Dose Titration for Diabetics
- **Week 1-2:** 200mcg/day with dinner
- **Week 3-4:** 200mcg with breakfast + 200mcg with dinner (400mcg/day total)
- **Week 5+:** If needed and tolerated, increase to 200mcg breakfast + 200mcg lunch + 200mcg dinner (600mcg/day)
- **Maximum:** 1,000mcg/day in divided doses, only with physician oversight
- **Monitor:** Blood glucose closely during titration, especially if on diabetes medications
