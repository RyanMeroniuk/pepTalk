# Vitamin D3
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

### Healthy Fats (critical — D3 is fat-soluble)
- **Avocado:** Excellent source of monounsaturated fat. One study showed eating avocado with D3 increased absorption by ~32%.
- **Extra virgin olive oil:** Monounsaturated fat improves fat-soluble vitamin absorption
- **Coconut oil / MCT oil:** Medium-chain triglycerides are rapidly absorbed and enhance D3 uptake
- **Grass-fed butter / ghee:** Rich in saturated fat and also contains small amounts of natural D3 and K2
- **Nuts (almonds, walnuts, macadamia):** Provide fat matrix for D3 absorption
- **Nut butters (almond butter, tahini):** Convenient fat source to pair with D3 supplementation
- **Eggs (whole, with yolk):** The fat in the yolk aids absorption; yolks also contain natural D3
- **Fatty fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel):** Provide both D3 and the fat needed for absorption in one food
- **Cheese:** Contains fat for absorption plus small amounts of vitamin K2 (especially gouda and brie)

### Magnesium-Rich Foods (cofactor for D3 metabolism)
- **Pumpkin seeds:** 1 oz = 156 mg magnesium
- **Dark chocolate (85%+):** 1 oz = 65 mg magnesium
- **Spinach (cooked):** 1 cup = 157 mg magnesium
- **Swiss chard:** 1 cup cooked = 150 mg magnesium
- **Black beans:** 1 cup cooked = 120 mg magnesium
- **Almonds:** 1 oz = 80 mg magnesium

### Vitamin K2-Rich Foods (synergistic partner)
- **Natto (fermented soybeans):** Richest food source of K2 (MK-7), ~1,000 mcg per 3.5 oz
- **Gouda cheese:** ~75 mcg K2 per 3.5 oz
- **Brie cheese:** ~50 mcg K2 per 3.5 oz
- **Egg yolks (pasture-raised):** ~30-40 mcg K2 per yolk
- **Grass-fed butter:** ~15 mcg K2 per tablespoon
- **Chicken liver:** ~14 mcg K2 per 3.5 oz

### Bile-Stimulating Foods (improve fat-soluble vitamin absorption)
- **Bitter greens (arugula, dandelion greens, endive):** Stimulate bile production
- **Artichokes:** Strong bile stimulant
- **Ginger:** Enhances bile secretion and gut motility
- **Lemon juice:** Stimulates bile flow when consumed with meals
- **Apple cider vinegar:** Supports digestive acid and bile production
