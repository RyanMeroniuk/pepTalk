# Vitamin D3
## Forms and Bioavailability

- **Vitamin D3 (cholecalciferol) vs. D2 (ergocalciferol):** D3 is the preferred form. It is 56-87% more effective at raising and maintaining serum 25(OH)D levels than D2. D3 has a longer half-life (approximately 2-3 weeks vs. 2 weeks for D2) and stronger binding affinity to vitamin D-binding protein. D2 is plant-derived (mushrooms, yeast) while D3 is animal-derived or synthesized from lichen.
- **Standard D3 (cholecalciferol):** The most common and cost-effective form. Well absorbed when taken with fat. Available in capsules, softgels, tablets, drops, and sprays. Requires dietary fat for optimal absorption.
- **Microencapsulated D3:** D3 enclosed in a starch or cellulose matrix. Enhanced stability and water-dispersible. Useful for those with fat malabsorption conditions (celiac, Crohn's, cystic fibrosis, post-bariatric surgery).
- **Liposomal D3:** D3 encapsulated in phospholipid liposomes. May enhance absorption by 2-3x compared to standard forms, especially in those with compromised gut function or bile insufficiency. Does not require co-ingestion with dietary fat.
- **D3 in oil-based softgels:** Dissolved in MCT oil, olive oil, or coconut oil within a gelatin or vegetarian capsule. Pre-dissolved in fat — may improve absorption compared to dry tablets.
- **Emulsified D3 drops:** Nano-emulsified liquid drops. Good absorption regardless of fat intake. Convenient for dose titration and for those who cannot swallow pills.
- **Calcifediol (25-hydroxyvitamin D3):** The pre-converted liver metabolite. Approximately 2-3x more potent at raising serum levels than standard D3. Bypasses the hepatic hydroxylation step. Useful for liver disease, obesity (less sequestration in adipose tissue), or non-responders to standard D3. Prescription form: Rayaldee.
- **Vegan D3 (lichen-derived):** Chemically identical to animal-derived D3 (cholecalciferol) but sourced from lichens. Equally effective. Suitable for vegans and vegetarians.

### Absorption Considerations
- **Fat requirement:** D3 is fat-soluble and requires dietary fat for absorption. Taking with a meal containing 11-15g of fat increases absorption by ~32% compared to taking on an empty stomach.
- **Bile salts:** Required for micellar solubilization and absorption. Conditions reducing bile flow (gallbladder removal, liver disease) may impair absorption.
- **Obesity impact:** D3 is sequestered in adipose tissue, reducing bioavailability. Obese individuals (BMI >30) typically need 2-3x higher doses to achieve the same serum levels.
- **Best form for most people:** Oil-based softgels or liquid drops taken with the largest fat-containing meal of the day.
- **Best form for malabsorption:** Liposomal or emulsified D3, or calcifediol (25-OH D3).
