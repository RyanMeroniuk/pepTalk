# Vitamin D3
## Lifestyle Recommendations

### Sun Exposure Optimization
- **Target 10-30 minutes of midday sun** (10am-2pm) with arms and legs exposed, 2-4 times per week. Fair-skinned individuals need less time; darker-skinned individuals need 2-3x longer.
- **Do not shower immediately after sun exposure:** Vitamin D3 synthesized on the skin surface takes time to be absorbed. Wait at least 30-60 minutes before showering or applying soap to sun-exposed areas (controversial but commonly recommended).
- **Gradual sun exposure:** Build a base tan gradually to avoid burning while maximizing D3 production. Never burn — sunburn increases skin cancer risk without additional D3 benefit.
- **Winter strategy:** At latitudes above 35°N (most of the continental US), UV-B radiation is insufficient for D3 synthesis from October through March. Supplementation is essential during these months.
- **Know your latitude:** Use the dminder app or similar tools to track when UV-B is sufficient for D3 synthesis at your location.

### Testing and Monitoring
- **Get a baseline 25(OH)D test** before starting supplementation. Many doctors include this in routine blood work upon request.
- **Retest every 3-6 months** until you find your maintenance dose, then annually.
- **Test in late winter** (February-March) to catch your annual low point.
- **Target 40-60 ng/mL** — this is the functional medicine optimal range associated with the best health outcomes.

### Cofactor Stacking
- **Always pair D3 with K2 (MK-7):** 100-200 mcg MK-7 per 5,000 IU D3
- **Ensure adequate magnesium:** 300-400 mg/day of a well-absorbed form (glycinate, threonate, malate)
- **Maintain zinc status:** 15-30 mg/day of zinc picolinate or zinc bisglycinate
- **Boron:** 3-6 mg/day may enhance vitamin D metabolism and hormonal balance

### Absorption Optimization
- **Take D3 with your fattiest meal of the day** — this simple change can increase absorption by 30-50%
- **Use oil-based softgels or liquid drops** rather than dry tablets
- **If you have gut issues** (IBD, celiac, SIBO), consider liposomal or emulsified D3 forms
- **If you are obese (BMI >30),** expect to need 2-3x the standard dose to achieve the same serum levels

### Exercise
- **Weight-bearing exercise** synergizes with vitamin D for bone health. Resistance training + adequate D3/K2/calcium is the strongest bone-building combination.
- **Outdoor exercise** provides both the exercise benefit and sun exposure for D3 synthesis.
- **Vitamin D supports muscle recovery** — ensure levels are optimized before starting new training programs.

### Sleep Hygiene
- **Take D3 in the morning or with lunch,** not at night. D3 may suppress melatonin production and disrupt sleep when taken in the evening.
- **Optimize D3 levels to improve sleep quality** — deficiency is independently associated with poor sleep, but take the supplement earlier in the day.

### Body Composition
- **Losing excess body fat** can increase serum D3 levels by 10-20% due to release of D3 stored in adipose tissue.
- **Maintaining healthy body composition** reduces the dose needed to maintain optimal levels.
