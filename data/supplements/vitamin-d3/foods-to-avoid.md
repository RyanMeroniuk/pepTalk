# Vitamin D3
## Foods That Block Absorption

- **High-phytate foods (when consumed at the same time as D3):** Phytic acid in unsoaked grains, legumes, and seeds can bind minerals (calcium, magnesium, zinc) needed for vitamin D metabolism. Soaking, sprouting, or fermenting reduces phytate content significantly.
  - Unsoaked oats, wheat bran, raw legumes, unsprouted seeds
- **High-oxalate foods (in excess):** Oxalates bind calcium, potentially interfering with the calcium-D3 partnership. Not necessary to eliminate, but avoid consuming large amounts with D3 supplements.
  - Spinach (raw), rhubarb, beet greens, Swiss chard (raw)
- **Excessive fiber at supplement time:** Very high fiber intake (>30g in a single meal) can reduce absorption of fat-soluble vitamins by binding bile salts and increasing gut transit time. Space D3 supplementation from extremely high-fiber meals.
- **Excessive caffeine:** High caffeine intake (>400 mg/day) may reduce calcium absorption and increase urinary calcium excretion, indirectly opposing vitamin D's calcium-retaining effects.
  - More than 4 cups of coffee per day is associated with reduced calcium balance
- **Excessive alcohol:** Chronic alcohol intake impairs hepatic hydroxylation of D3 to 25(OH)D, reducing conversion to the active form. It also damages the intestinal lining, reducing absorption.
- **Soft drinks (phosphoric acid):** Cola beverages contain phosphoric acid, which disrupts calcium-phosphorus balance and may oppose vitamin D's bone-building effects.
- **Trans fats and highly processed seed oils:** May impair fat-soluble vitamin absorption and increase systemic inflammation, opposing vitamin D's anti-inflammatory effects.
- **Grapefruit (in excess):** Can inhibit CYP3A4 enzyme, which plays a minor role in vitamin D metabolism. Unlikely to be clinically significant at normal dietary intake but worth noting for those on very high D3 doses.

### Practical Guidance
- **You do not need to eliminate these foods.** Simply avoid consuming them at the exact same time as your D3 supplement. A 1-2 hour separation is sufficient.
- **Focus on taking D3 with a fat-containing meal** that includes some of the absorption-enhancing foods listed in the companion guide.
