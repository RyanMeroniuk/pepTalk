# Vitamin D3
## Classification

- **Category:** Fat-soluble vitamin / secosteroid hormone precursor
- **Also known as:** Cholecalciferol
- **Vitamin group:** Fat-soluble (A, D, E, K)
- **Hormone classification:** Vitamin D3 is technically a prohormone — the body converts it into the active hormone calcitriol (1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D)
- **Chemical class:** Secosteroid (derived from cholesterol via UV-B exposure)
- **Essential nutrient:** Yes — required for calcium metabolism, immune function, and gene expression
- **Endogenous production:** Yes — synthesized in the skin upon UV-B radiation exposure (310-320 nm wavelength)
