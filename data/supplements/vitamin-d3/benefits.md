# Vitamin D3
## Benefits

- **Bone health:** Essential for calcium absorption and bone mineralization. Deficiency causes rickets in children and osteomalacia/osteoporosis in adults. Supplementation reduces fracture risk by 20-30% when combined with calcium.
- **Immune defense:** Reduces risk of respiratory infections by 12-75% depending on baseline status (BMJ 2017 meta-analysis of 25 RCTs, n=11,321). Greatest benefit seen in those with baseline levels <25 nmol/L taking daily or weekly doses.
- **Autoimmune disease prevention:** The VITAL study (2022, n=25,871) found that vitamin D3 supplementation (2,000 IU/day) reduced autoimmune disease incidence by 22% over 5 years.
- **Cancer risk reduction:** Meta-analyses show 13-17% reduction in cancer mortality with vitamin D supplementation. Strongest evidence for colorectal cancer (serum levels >40 ng/mL associated with 40-50% lower risk).
- **Cardiovascular health:** Low vitamin D is associated with hypertension, endothelial dysfunction, and increased cardiovascular mortality. Supplementation may reduce blood pressure by 2-6 mmHg in deficient individuals.
- **Mood and depression:** Meta-analyses show vitamin D supplementation significantly reduces depressive symptoms, particularly in those with clinical depression and low baseline levels. Effect size comparable to mild antidepressant benefit.
- **Cognitive function:** Higher vitamin D levels associated with better cognitive performance and reduced risk of dementia. The VDR is widely expressed in brain regions including the hippocampus and prefrontal cortex.
- **Insulin sensitivity:** Vitamin D improves insulin receptor expression and beta-cell function. The D2d trial (2019) showed 4,000 IU/day reduced progression to type 2 diabetes by 12% in prediabetic individuals, with greater effects in those achieving levels >40 ng/mL.
- **Muscle strength and fall prevention:** Supplementation improves lower extremity strength and reduces fall risk by 19-26% in elderly populations, particularly those with deficiency.
- **Testosterone support:** Men with vitamin D levels >30 ng/mL have significantly higher total and free testosterone. Supplementation (3,332 IU/day for 1 year) increased total testosterone by ~25% in deficient men.
- **Sleep quality:** Vitamin D deficiency is associated with poor sleep quality, short sleep duration, and increased daytime sleepiness. Levels >40 ng/mL are associated with better sleep outcomes.
- **Pregnancy outcomes:** Adequate vitamin D reduces risk of preeclampsia, gestational diabetes, preterm birth, and low birth weight. Levels >40 ng/mL during pregnancy associated with best outcomes.
