# Resveratrol
## Side Effects

- **Gastrointestinal discomfort:** The most common side effect. Nausea, diarrhea, abdominal pain, and flatulence occur in some users, especially at doses above 1000mg/day. Taking with food (which is recommended for absorption anyway) usually mitigates GI issues
- **Headache:** Occasionally reported, usually mild. May relate to vasodilation effects or histamine modulation
- **Estrogenic effects (important at high doses):** Resveratrol has phytoestrogenic activity — it binds to estrogen receptors (primarily ER-beta). At high doses (>500mg/day), this may cause breast tenderness, menstrual changes, or exacerbate estrogen-sensitive conditions. Individuals with estrogen receptor-positive breast cancer, endometriosis, uterine fibroids, or estrogen dominance should use caution or avoid resveratrol
- **Blood thinning:** Resveratrol inhibits platelet aggregation and COX-1. This contributes to cardiovascular benefit but increases bleeding risk when combined with anticoagulants or before surgery. Discontinue 2 weeks before elective surgery
- **Joint pain (rare):** Some users report joint discomfort, possibly related to resveratrol's effect on COX enzymes or detoxification pathways
- **Achilles tendon discomfort (rare, anecdotal):** Reported anecdotally in longevity communities at high doses. Mechanism unclear. Discontinue if tendon pain occurs
- **Insomnia (uncommon):** Some users report difficulty sleeping, potentially related to SIRT1-mediated metabolic activation. Taking in the morning rather than evening resolves this
- **Skin flushing:** Mild facial flushing reported occasionally, likely related to nitric oxide-mediated vasodilation
- **Drug interaction effects:** See drug interactions section. Resveratrol affects CYP450 enzymes and can alter the metabolism of several medications
- **Iron chelation (high doses):** Resveratrol has mild iron-chelating properties. At very high doses, could theoretically affect iron status in deficient individuals
- **Overall safety profile:** Good at standard doses (250-1000mg/day). Side effects increase above 2500mg/day. Long-term safety data beyond 1-2 years is limited but no serious adverse events have been consistently reported
