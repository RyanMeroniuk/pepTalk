# Resveratrol
## Benefits

- **Cardiovascular protection:** Reduces oxidized LDL, inhibits platelet aggregation, improves endothelial function, increases nitric oxide production, and reduces arterial stiffness. Meta-analyses show reduction in systolic blood pressure and CRP levels
- **Anti-aging / longevity:** SIRT1 activation mimics caloric restriction, the most validated life-extension intervention. Extends lifespan in yeast, worms, flies, and fish. Improves healthspan markers in mice on high-fat diets. Human longevity trials are ongoing
- **Metabolic health:** Improves insulin sensitivity, reduces fasting blood glucose, and lowers HbA1c in type 2 diabetes. Activates AMPK to promote fat oxidation and metabolic flexibility. Meta-analyses confirm improvements in glucose homeostasis
- **Anti-inflammatory:** Potent NF-kB inhibition reduces systemic inflammation. Reduces CRP, IL-6, TNF-alpha in clinical trials. Beneficial in inflammatory conditions including metabolic syndrome, cardiovascular disease, and neuroinflammation
- **Neuroprotection:** Crosses the blood-brain barrier. Protects against Alzheimer's (reduces amyloid-beta aggregation and tau phosphorylation), Parkinson's (protects dopaminergic neurons), and general cognitive decline. Improves cerebral blood flow
- **Cancer prevention (in vitro and animal data):** Inhibits cancer cell proliferation, induces apoptosis, suppresses tumor angiogenesis, and reduces metastasis in numerous cancer cell lines and animal models. Chemopreventive rather than chemotherapeutic at typical supplement doses
- **Skin health and photoprotection:** Reduces UV-induced skin damage, inhibits melanogenesis, promotes collagen synthesis, and reduces wrinkle formation. Topical and oral resveratrol both show skin benefits
- **Exercise performance enhancement:** Improves mitochondrial function (PGC-1alpha activation), increases endurance capacity, enhances muscle recovery, and reduces exercise-induced oxidative damage
- **Weight management:** Promotes fat oxidation, reduces adipogenesis, and improves metabolic rate through SIRT1/AMPK activation. Modest weight loss effects in some clinical trials (1-2 kg over 3 months)
- **Bone health:** Promotes osteoblast differentiation and inhibits osteoclast formation. May reduce osteoporosis risk, particularly in postmenopausal women (partly through estrogenic effects)
- **Liver protection:** Reduces hepatic steatosis (fatty liver), lowers liver enzymes (ALT, AST), and protects against alcohol-induced liver damage through antioxidant and anti-inflammatory mechanisms
- **Gut health:** Modulates gut microbiome composition favorably. Reduces intestinal inflammation and improves gut barrier integrity
