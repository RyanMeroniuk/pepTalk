# Resveratrol
## Dosing Protocols

- **General health / anti-aging:** 250-500mg/day trans-resveratrol
- **Cardiovascular support:** 250-500mg/day trans-resveratrol
- **Metabolic health / blood sugar:** 500-1000mg/day trans-resveratrol. Most diabetes studies used 500-1500mg/day
- **Longevity protocol (Sinclair-inspired):** 1000mg (1g) trans-resveratrol daily, taken with NMN (500-1000mg) and mixed into full-fat yogurt or taken with a fat-containing meal
- **Cognitive support / neuroprotection:** 250-500mg/day trans-resveratrol
- **Skin health (oral):** 250mg/day trans-resveratrol. Topical resveratrol products complement oral supplementation
- **Timing:** Take in the morning with a meal containing fat. Fat is essential for absorption. Breakfast with eggs, yogurt, or avocado is ideal
- **With fat (non-negotiable):** Resveratrol is fat-soluble. Taking without fat dramatically reduces absorption. Always combine with dietary fat — olive oil, butter, yogurt, nuts, eggs, avocado, or fatty fish
- **Piperine co-administration:** Take with 5-20mg piperine (BioPerine) to inhibit glucuronidation and increase bioavailability by up to 229%. Many quality products include piperine in the formulation
- **NMN combination dosing:** The optimal longevity pairing is resveratrol (activates SIRT1) + NMN (provides the NAD+ SIRT1 requires). Take both in the morning with fat-containing food
- **Split dosing:** For doses above 500mg, consider splitting into 250-500mg with breakfast + 250-500mg with lunch/dinner (both with fat). Resveratrol has a short half-life (1-3 hours for free resveratrol), so split dosing maintains more consistent plasma levels
- **Starting dose:** Begin with 250mg/day for 1-2 weeks to assess tolerance. Increase gradually
- **Onset of effects:** Antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects begin within days. Metabolic improvements are measurable within 2-4 weeks. Cardiovascular and anti-aging effects develop over months of consistent use
- **Cycling (optional):** Not strictly necessary, but some practitioners cycle 5 days on / 2 days off. There is no strong evidence for or against cycling resveratrol
- **Upper limit:** Doses up to 5000mg/day have been used in clinical trials (cancer studies). Doses above 2500mg/day showed increased GI side effects. Most benefits are achieved at 250-1000mg/day
