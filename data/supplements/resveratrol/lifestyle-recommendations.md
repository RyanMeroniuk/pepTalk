# Resveratrol
## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Take with a fat-containing meal:** Resveratrol is lipophilic and requires dietary fat for optimal absorption. Pair each dose with a meal containing healthy fats (olive oil, avocado, nuts, fatty fish, eggs). This can improve bioavailability by 2-5x compared to taking on an empty stomach. A simple approach: take with breakfast if that meal includes eggs, avocado toast, or nuts
- **Morning dosing for SIRT1 / circadian alignment:** SIRT1 activity is highest in the morning and plays a key role in circadian clock regulation. Taking resveratrol in the morning aligns its peak SIRT1 activation with the body's natural circadian upregulation of this enzyme, potentially maximizing metabolic benefits. SIRT1 also regulates CLOCK and BMAL1 gene expression, core components of the circadian machinery. Morning dosing supports this natural rhythm rather than disrupting it
- **Exercise synergy:** Resveratrol activates PGC-1alpha and AMPK, the same pathways activated by endurance exercise. When combined with regular exercise (particularly aerobic training), resveratrol and exercise produce additive or synergistic mitochondrial biogenesis and improved oxidative capacity. Take resveratrol 1-2 hours before exercise to have circulating levels available during the training stimulus. Note: some research suggests very high-dose resveratrol (>1,000 mg) may blunt certain exercise adaptations in young, healthy athletes, so moderate doses (250-500 mg) are preferred for exercise optimization
- **Caloric restriction mimetic strategy:** Resveratrol is the most well-characterized caloric restriction (CR) mimetic through its SIRT1 activation. For those who cannot sustain chronic caloric restriction, resveratrol supplementation activates many of the same longevity pathways (SIRT1, AMPK, reduced mTOR signaling, improved autophagy). This makes it particularly valuable for individuals eating at maintenance calories who still want CR-associated metabolic benefits
- **Intermittent fasting synergy:** Resveratrol and intermittent fasting (IF) activate overlapping metabolic pathways: both upregulate SIRT1, AMPK, and autophagy while downregulating mTOR. Combining resveratrol with an IF protocol (16:8 or 18:6) may amplify the metabolic and longevity benefits of both interventions. Take resveratrol with the first meal of the eating window to break the fast with a SIRT1-activating stimulus paired with dietary fat for absorption
- **Stress management and sleep optimization:** Resveratrol's anti-inflammatory benefits (NF-kB inhibition, reduced CRP and IL-6) are amplified in a low-stress, well-rested state. Chronic stress elevates cortisol, which drives NF-kB activation and systemic inflammation, partially counteracting resveratrol's mechanisms. Prioritize 7-9 hours of sleep and incorporate stress management practices (meditation, breathwork, nature exposure) for maximum synergy
- **Avoid smoking and minimize environmental toxin exposure:** Resveratrol's antioxidant capacity is finite. Smoking and high environmental toxin exposure (air pollution, pesticides, heavy metals) generate excessive oxidative stress that consumes resveratrol's radical-scavenging capacity before it can provide systemic benefits. In a cleaner internal environment, resveratrol can direct its activity toward longevity and anti-aging pathways rather than damage control
- **Consistent daily supplementation:** Resveratrol has a short half-life (1-3 hours for free resveratrol, 6-8 hours for metabolites). Benefits accumulate with consistent daily dosing over weeks to months. Most clinical trials showing significant outcomes ran for 4-12 weeks minimum. Do not expect immediate results; treat it as a long-term longevity investment
- **Pair with complementary polyphenols:** A "polyphenol stack" of resveratrol + quercetin + curcumin + green tea extract (EGCG) targets overlapping and complementary anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and senolytic pathways. This multi-polyphenol approach mirrors the diversity of a Mediterranean diet in concentrated supplement form
