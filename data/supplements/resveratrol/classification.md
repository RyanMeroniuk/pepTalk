# Resveratrol
## Classification

- **Category:** Polyphenol / sirtuin activator / anti-aging compound
- **Supplement class:** Stilbenoid (polyphenolic phytoalexin)
- **Also known as:** Trans-resveratrol (active form), 3,5,4'-trihydroxystilbene
- **Endogenous status:** Not produced by the human body; obtained exclusively from diet or supplementation
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement; not FDA-approved as a drug. Widely sold OTC
- **Primary function:** SIRT1 activator, antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, cardioprotective polyphenol
- **Solubility:** Fat-soluble (lipophilic). Poorly water-soluble. Requires dietary fat for optimal absorption
- **Key distinction:** Resveratrol activates SIRT1 (the longevity gene); NMN provides the NAD+ fuel SIRT1 requires. They are complementary — the "gas pedal" (resveratrol) and the "fuel" (NMN/NAD+)
