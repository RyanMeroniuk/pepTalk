# Resveratrol
## Peptide Synergies

- **Epitalon (Epithalon) — anti-aging and telomere synergy:** Epitalon activates telomerase to maintain telomere length, the primary mechanism of cellular aging. Resveratrol activates SIRT1, which independently protects telomeres by deacetylating shelterin complex proteins and reducing telomeric DNA damage from oxidative stress. Together, they address telomere aging from two complementary angles: Epitalon directly lengthens telomeres while resveratrol protects existing telomere integrity. Both also modulate melatonin and circadian rhythm, creating a comprehensive anti-aging protocol
- **MOTS-c — mitochondrial function and longevity:** MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-derived peptide that activates AMPK, improves insulin sensitivity, and enhances metabolic flexibility. Resveratrol activates both SIRT1 and AMPK through complementary mechanisms (SIRT1 deacetylates LKB1, which activates AMPK). The combination creates a powerful dual-pathway AMPK activation strategy, amplifying mitochondrial biogenesis (via PGC-1alpha), fat oxidation, and cellular energy production. Both compounds are exercise mimetics, making this pairing ideal for metabolic optimization and longevity
- **GHK-Cu (Copper Peptide) — skin anti-aging and tissue repair:** GHK-Cu stimulates collagen synthesis, elastin production, and glycosaminoglycan formation while resveratrol provides potent antioxidant protection against UV-induced photoaging, reduces MMP (matrix metalloproteinase) activity that degrades collagen, and promotes SIRT1-mediated skin cell longevity. GHK-Cu rebuilds the structural matrix while resveratrol protects it from degradation. Together, they address both the construction and preservation sides of skin anti-aging
- **CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin — anti-aging GH optimization:** This GH secretagogue stack stimulates natural growth hormone release, which declines with age (somatopause). Resveratrol complements GH optimization by activating SIRT1 longevity pathways that GH does not directly engage, while also improving insulin sensitivity (GH can transiently reduce insulin sensitivity). Resveratrol's anti-inflammatory effects (NF-kB inhibition) create a lower-inflammation environment where GH-mediated tissue repair and recovery operate more efficiently. The combination addresses aging through both hormonal restoration and metabolic/longevity pathway activation
- **Semax — neuroprotection and cognitive enhancement:** Semax upregulates BDNF (brain-derived neurotrophic factor) and enhances neuroplasticity. Resveratrol crosses the blood-brain barrier and provides complementary neuroprotection through SIRT1-mediated reduction of amyloid-beta aggregation, improved cerebral blood flow (via eNOS activation and nitric oxide production), and reduced neuroinflammation (NF-kB inhibition in microglia). Semax promotes new neural connections while resveratrol protects existing neurons from oxidative and inflammatory damage. Together, they offer a growth-plus-protection model for cognitive longevity
- **BPC-157 — anti-inflammatory and healing synergy:** BPC-157 accelerates tissue repair through nitric oxide modulation, angiogenesis, and growth factor upregulation. Resveratrol provides systemic anti-inflammatory support (NF-kB inhibition, reduced IL-6, TNF-alpha, and CRP) that creates an optimal low-inflammation environment for BPC-157's healing mechanisms. Resveratrol also promotes autophagy, clearing damaged cellular components that BPC-157 can then replace with healthy tissue. Particularly effective for gut healing protocols where BPC-157's direct gut repair combines with resveratrol's intestinal anti-inflammatory and microbiome-modulating effects
- **NAD+ precursors (NMN/NR) — SIRT1 fuel and activation:** SIRT1 requires NAD+ as a cofactor to function. Resveratrol activates SIRT1 expression and activity, but without adequate NAD+ levels, SIRT1 cannot perform its enzymatic function. NMN or NR supplementation replenishes the NAD+ pool that declines with age, effectively providing the fuel that resveratrol-activated SIRT1 needs to operate. This is arguably the most mechanistically direct supplement-peptide pairing: resveratrol presses the accelerator (SIRT1 activation) while NAD+ precursors fill the gas tank (NAD+ substrate). Note: NMN/NR are not peptides but are commonly included in peptide-adjacent longevity protocols
