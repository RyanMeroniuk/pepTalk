# Glucosamine/Chondroitin
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

- **Vitamin C-rich foods** — Vitamin C is essential for collagen synthesis and supports the utilization of glucosamine in cartilage production. Taking glucosamine/chondroitin with vitamin C-rich foods enhances the downstream conversion of supplemented building blocks into functional cartilage:
  - **Bell peppers (red, yellow)** — Highest common vegetable source of vitamin C
  - **Citrus fruits** — Oranges, grapefruit, lemons
  - **Kiwi fruit** — Exceptionally vitamin C-dense
  - **Strawberries and other berries** — Vitamin C plus anti-inflammatory anthocyanins
  - **Broccoli** — Vitamin C plus sulforaphane (anti-inflammatory)
- **Sulfur-containing foods** — Sulfate is critical for GAG synthesis and cartilage function. Dietary sulfur supports glucosamine sulfate utilization:
  - **Garlic and onions** — Rich in organosulfur compounds
  - **Cruciferous vegetables** — Broccoli, cabbage, Brussels sprouts, cauliflower
  - **Eggs** — Contain methionine and cysteine (sulfur amino acids)
  - **MSM-rich foods** — Small amounts of MSM occur naturally in cruciferous vegetables and some fruits
- **Omega-3 fatty acids** — Anti-inflammatory fats that create a favorable joint environment for the structural repair glucosamine/chondroitin supports:
  - **Wild salmon** — EPA and DHA reduce joint inflammation
  - **Sardines and mackerel** — High omega-3 content per serving
  - **Walnuts and flaxseed** — Plant-based ALA omega-3 (less potent than EPA/DHA but still beneficial)
- **Manganese-containing foods** — Manganese is a cofactor for enzymes involved in GAG and proteoglycan synthesis. Many glucosamine formulations include manganese:
  - **Pineapple** — Highest common fruit source of manganese (also contains bromelain, an anti-inflammatory enzyme)
  - **Pecans and almonds** — Good nut sources of manganese
  - **Oats and brown rice** — Whole grain manganese sources
  - **Spinach and kale** — Leafy green manganese sources
- **Bone broth** — Provides complementary collagen, hyaluronic acid, and additional glucosamine/chondroitin from the broth itself. Consuming bone broth alongside supplementation creates a synergistic effect.
- **Gelatin and collagen peptides** — Provide glycine and proline, amino acids essential for collagen and cartilage synthesis. Taking collagen peptides alongside glucosamine/chondroitin provides a more complete set of cartilage building blocks.
