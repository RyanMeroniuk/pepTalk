# Glucosamine/Chondroitin
## Dosing Protocols

- **Standard osteoarthritis protocol** — Glucosamine sulfate 1,500mg/day + chondroitin sulfate 1,200mg/day. Can be taken as a single daily dose or split into 2-3 divided doses. The once-daily 1,500mg glucosamine sulfate protocol was used in the pivotal European long-term trials. Minimum trial period: 8 weeks before assessing efficacy. Full structural benefits may take 3-6 months.
- **Divided dosing protocol** — Glucosamine sulfate 500mg + chondroitin sulfate 400mg, three times daily with meals. Some practitioners prefer divided doses for more consistent plasma levels throughout the day.
- **Loading phase (optional)** — Some protocols recommend a higher initial dose for the first 2-4 weeks: glucosamine sulfate 2,000mg/day + chondroitin sulfate 1,600mg/day, then reduce to maintenance. Evidence for loading is limited but used in clinical practice.
- **Preventive/athletic maintenance** — Glucosamine sulfate 1,500mg/day + chondroitin sulfate 800-1,200mg/day. For active individuals seeking to protect joints from repetitive loading stress, even without current OA diagnosis.
- **With MSM (methylsulfonylmethane)** — Many combination products add MSM 500-1,500mg/day. MSM provides sulfur for connective tissue, has anti-inflammatory properties, and some trials show additive benefit when combined with glucosamine/chondroitin.
- **With hyaluronic acid** — Some formulations add oral hyaluronic acid (80-200mg/day). Evidence for oral HA is growing, particularly for joint lubrication and skin hydration. Complements the cartilage-building effects of glucosamine/chondroitin.
- **With collagen peptides** — Adding collagen peptides (10g/day, type II collagen or hydrolyzed collagen) provides additional building blocks for cartilage and connective tissue. Particularly relevant for comprehensive joint support.

**Timing:**

- **With meals** — Reduces GI side effects (nausea, heartburn). Food does not significantly impair absorption.
- **Consistent daily timing** — Take at the same time(s) each day for steady tissue levels.
- **No specific time-of-day advantage** — Morning, afternoon, or evening is fine. Some prefer evening dosing as cartilage repair may be more active during sleep.

**Duration and expectations:**

- **4-8 weeks minimum** — Do not expect or assess results before this point. This is the most common reason for perceived "failure" — people quit after 1-2 weeks.
- **3-6 months for structural benefits** — Joint space narrowing reduction and structural modification require months of consistent use.
- **Indefinite maintenance** — Unlike a medication course, glucosamine/chondroitin is typically continued indefinitely for ongoing joint support. Benefits reverse upon discontinuation.
- **Periodic reassessment** — Every 6-12 months, assess whether the supplement is providing ongoing benefit. If pain and function have not improved after 6 months, the supplement may not be effective for your specific situation.
