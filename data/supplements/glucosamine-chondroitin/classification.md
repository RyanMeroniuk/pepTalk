# Glucosamine/Chondroitin
## Classification

- **Category:** Joint Health / Cartilage Support / Musculoskeletal
- **Type:** Naturally occurring amino sugar (glucosamine) and glycosaminoglycan (chondroitin)
- **Chemical class:** Glucosamine is an amino monosaccharide; chondroitin sulfate is a sulfated glycosaminoglycan (GAG) polymer
- **Source:** Glucosamine is primarily derived from shellfish exoskeletons (shrimp, crab, lobster shells) or produced synthetically. Chondroitin is derived from bovine, porcine, or shark cartilage.
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (USA); prescription medication for osteoarthritis in parts of Europe (SADOA — Symptomatic Slow-Acting Drug for Osteoarthritis)
- **Primary function:** Structural support and repair of articular cartilage, synovial fluid maintenance, and modulation of joint inflammation
- **Key distinction:** The sulfate form of glucosamine is the clinically studied form. Glucosamine HCl has weaker evidence. This matters.
