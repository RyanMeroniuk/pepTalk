# Creatine
## Dosing Protocols

### Standard Daily Maintenance (Recommended for Most People)
- **Dose:** 3-5 g/day (5 g is the most studied dose)
- **Timing:** Any time of day; consistency matters more than timing
- **Duration:** Indefinite — creatine is safe and beneficial for long-term daily use
- **Saturation time:** Muscle creatine stores reach full saturation in ~3-4 weeks without loading
- **Notes:** This is the simplest and most practical approach. No loading phase needed.

### Loading Protocol (Optional — Faster Saturation)
- **Loading phase:** 20 g/day divided into 4 doses of 5 g, for 5-7 days
- **Maintenance:** 3-5 g/day after loading
- **Advantage:** Saturates muscle stores in 5-7 days vs 3-4 weeks
- **Disadvantage:** GI discomfort is more common during loading (5 g at a time is fine; 20 g at once is not). Temporary water weight gain is more abrupt.
- **Who should load:** Athletes who need immediate performance benefit (upcoming competition). Most people should just start with 5 g/day.

### Timing Considerations
- **Post-workout** — some evidence suggests slightly better uptake when taken post-workout with carbs and protein (Cribb & Hayes, 2006; Antonio & Ciccone, 2013). Effect size is small.
- **With meals** — taking creatine with a meal containing carbohydrates and protein may enhance uptake via insulin-mediated creatine transporter activity. Not mandatory.
- **Morning vs evening** — no significant difference. Take it when you'll remember.
- **Pre-workout** — fine, but not necessarily advantageous over other times
- **The most important thing:** Take it daily. Timing is secondary.

### Body Weight Adjustment
- **Under 120 lbs (55 kg):** 3 g/day is sufficient
- **120-200 lbs (55-90 kg):** 5 g/day (standard dose)
- **Over 200 lbs (90+ kg):** 5-10 g/day may be appropriate for very large or highly muscular individuals
- **Precise dosing:** 0.03-0.05 g/kg/day for maintenance; 0.3 g/kg/day for loading

### For Cognitive Benefits
- **Dose:** 5 g/day (same as muscle dosing)
- **Duration:** Brain creatine takes longer to saturate than muscle — allow 4-8 weeks for full cognitive effects
- **Notes:** Higher doses (up to 20 g/day) have been used in TBI and depression studies, but 5 g/day is the standard starting point

### For Older Adults
- **Dose:** 5 g/day combined with resistance training
- **Duration:** Ongoing — age-related benefits require sustained use
- **Notes:** Creatine + resistance training is one of the most effective interventions for sarcopenia (age-related muscle loss)

### For Vegetarians/Vegans
- **Dose:** 5 g/day
- **Notes:** Vegetarians and vegans have lower baseline creatine stores (no dietary creatine from meat) and typically experience larger absolute improvements in both muscle and cognitive performance

### Cycling: Necessary?
- **No.** There is no evidence that the body downregulates creatine transporters or becomes "resistant" to creatine with continuous use. The ISSN position stand recommends continuous daily use.
- Some people cycle off for 4 weeks every 12 weeks out of abundance of caution, but there is no scientific basis for this.

### Mixing Instructions
- **Mix in any liquid** — water, juice, protein shake, coffee (creatine is heat-stable up to ~250°F)
- **Creatine does not need to be fully dissolved** to be effective — undissolved particles are still absorbed in the GI tract
- **Do not pre-mix and store for days** — while creatine is more stable than glutamine, fresh is best
- **Pair with liquid** — drink a full glass of water with creatine to aid dissolution and absorption

### Water Intake
- **Increase water intake by 16-24 oz (500-750 ml) daily** when supplementing creatine — creatine draws water into muscle cells, so overall hydration needs increase slightly
