# Creatine
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + Creatine (Muscle & Recovery Powerhouse)
- **Rationale:** GH secretagogues increase growth hormone and IGF-1, driving muscle protein synthesis and recovery. Creatine provides the immediate energy (ATP) for high-intensity training and enhances cell volumization. Together, you get enhanced training capacity (creatine) + enhanced recovery and anabolism (GH peptides).
- **Protocol:** Creatine 5 g/day (any time) + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Best for:** Muscle building, body composition, recovery, anti-aging performance

#### BPC-157 + Creatine (Performance + Tissue Repair)
- **Rationale:** Creatine supports high-intensity training performance; BPC-157 accelerates recovery from training-induced tissue damage and injury. Creatine helps you push harder; BPC-157 helps you recover faster.
- **Protocol:** Creatine 5 g/day + BPC-157 250-500 mcg 2x/day
- **Best for:** Athletes pushing training intensity, injury rehabilitation while maintaining training

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### TB-500 + Creatine
- **Rationale:** TB-500 promotes systemic tissue repair and flexibility. Creatine supports the energy systems and cell hydration needed for tissue recovery. Particularly useful during return-to-training after injury.
- **Protocol:** Creatine 5 g/day + TB-500 at standard dosing (2.5-5 mg 2x/week)
- **Best for:** Injury recovery, return to training, flexibility improvement

#### Tesamorelin + Creatine (Body Composition)
- **Rationale:** Tesamorelin specifically targets visceral fat reduction via GH release. Creatine supports lean mass maintenance and performance. Together, they optimize the fat loss + muscle preservation equation.
- **Protocol:** Creatine 5 g/day + Tesamorelin at prescribed dosing
- **Best for:** Body recomposition, visceral fat reduction while maintaining muscle

#### MK-677 (Ibutamoren) + Creatine
- **Rationale:** MK-677 is an oral GH secretagogue that increases GH, IGF-1, and appetite. Combined with creatine's training enhancement, this stack supports aggressive muscle building phases.
- **Protocol:** Creatine 5 g/day + MK-677 10-25 mg before bed
- **Caution:** MK-677 increases appetite and can cause water retention — combine with disciplined nutrition
- **Best for:** Bulking phases, muscle gain, recovery

### Sparing Effect: Creatine + Glycine
- **Creatine supplementation spares glycine** — endogenous creatine synthesis (from glycine + arginine + methionine) is the largest consumer of methyl groups and a significant consumer of glycine in the body
- By supplementing creatine directly, you free up glycine for collagen synthesis, glutathione production, and sleep support
- **Stack idea:** Creatine 5 g/day + Glycine 3-5 g/day = more glycine available for repair and recovery
