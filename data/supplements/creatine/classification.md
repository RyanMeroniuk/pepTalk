# Creatine
## Classification

- **Category:** Naturally occurring organic compound; conditionally essential nutrient
- **Chemical class:** Nitrogenous organic acid (guanidino compound)
- **Molecular formula:** C4H9N3O2
- **Supplement type:** Ergogenic aid / performance supplement / cognitive supplement
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (OTC); one of the most studied supplements in history with over 500 peer-reviewed studies; GRAS status
- **Primary systems:** Muscular (ATP regeneration), neurological (brain energy), skeletal (bone density support)
- **Endogenous synthesis:** The body produces ~1 g/day (liver, kidneys, pancreas) from glycine, arginine, and methionine. An omnivorous diet provides ~1 g/day additional. Total daily turnover is ~2 g/day.
- **Storage:** ~95% of creatine is stored in skeletal muscle as phosphocreatine; the remaining 5% is in the brain, heart, and testes
