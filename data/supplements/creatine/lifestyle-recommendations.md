# Creatine
## Lifestyle Recommendations

### Training (Maximize Creatine's Benefits)
- **Resistance training is essential** — creatine amplifies the results of progressive resistance training. Without training, you miss the primary benefit.
- **Train 3-5 days per week** with progressive overload — creatine helps you do more reps, more sets, and recover faster between sets. Use that extra capacity.
- **Include high-intensity work** — creatine primarily enhances the phosphocreatine system (efforts lasting 0-30 seconds). Sprints, heavy lifts, explosive movements, HIIT — these are where creatine shines.
- **Track your workouts** — creatine's benefits accumulate through progressive overload. Log weights, reps, and sets to ensure you're capitalizing on the increased capacity.

### Nutrition
- **Eat adequate protein** — 1.6-2.2 g/kg/day to support the muscle protein synthesis creatine enhances
- **Don't fear carbohydrates** — insulin from carbs enhances creatine uptake and supports the glycogen replenishment creatine aids. Carbs are particularly important peri-workout.
- **Eat enough total calories** — creatine cannot build muscle in a severe deficit. Eat at maintenance or a slight surplus for muscle gain.
- **Post-workout nutrition** — a meal with protein (30-50 g) and carbs (50-100 g) within 1-2 hours post-workout optimizes recovery and creatine uptake

### Hydration
- **Increase water intake by 16-24 oz (500-750 ml) daily** — creatine increases intracellular water. Support this with adequate fluid intake.
- **Monitor urine color** — aim for pale yellow. Dark urine suggests inadequate hydration.
- **Electrolytes matter** — especially sodium and potassium. Creatine's osmotic effects work best when electrolyte balance is maintained.

### Sleep & Recovery
- **Prioritize 7-9 hours of sleep** — muscle repair and growth happen during sleep. Creatine supports the energy systems for training, but recovery requires sleep.
- **Don't overtrain** — creatine lets you do more, but more is not always better. Include rest days and deload weeks.

### Cognitive Optimization
- **Use creatine as cognitive insurance** — even without exercise, 5 g/day supports brain energy and cognitive performance
- **Especially valuable during:** exam periods, high-stress work deadlines, travel across time zones, periods of sleep restriction
- **Allow 4-8 weeks** for brain creatine to reach maximum levels

### Body Composition Monitoring
- **Expect 1-3 lbs of water weight** in the first 1-2 weeks — this is intracellular water in muscle, not fat. Don't adjust your diet based on initial scale increase.
- **Track body composition, not just weight** — use measurements, progress photos, or DEXA scans to assess real changes
- **Creatine + resistance training + adequate protein** is one of the most effective legal stacks for body composition improvement

### Long-Term Mindset
- **Creatine is a daily supplement, like a vitamin** — take it consistently, indefinitely, and don't overthink timing or cycling
- **Benefits compound over time** — the performance, cognitive, and health benefits grow with consistent use over months and years
- **Cost-effectiveness** — at $0.03-0.05 per day, creatine is the best value supplement available
