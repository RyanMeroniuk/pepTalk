# Creatine
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

### Carbohydrates (Insulin-Mediated Uptake)
- **Creatine uptake into muscle cells is enhanced by insulin** — the creatine transporter (SLC6A8/CRT1) activity increases in the presence of insulin
- **~50-100 g of carbohydrates** taken with creatine can significantly increase muscle creatine retention (Green et al., 1996)
- **Practical carb sources to pair with creatine:**
  - Fruit juice (grape juice is classic — ~35 g carbs per cup)
  - Banana (~27 g carbs)
  - Rice (~45 g carbs per cup cooked)
  - Oatmeal (~27 g carbs per cup cooked)
  - Post-workout shake with dextrose or maltodextrin
- **You do NOT need carbs for creatine to work** — it absorbs fine without them, just slightly less efficiently. Over days/weeks of daily dosing, the difference is negligible.

### Protein (Additional Insulin Stimulus)
- **Protein + carbohydrate together** maximizes the insulin response, further enhancing creatine uptake
- **50 g protein + 50 g carbs** is as effective as 100 g carbs alone for enhancing creatine retention (Steenge et al., 2000)
- **Practical pairings:**
  - Whey protein shake with banana
  - Chicken breast with rice
  - Post-workout meal with protein and starchy carbs

### Electrolytes (Sodium)
- **Sodium-dependent creatine transport** — the SLC6A8 transporter is sodium-dependent. Adequate sodium intake supports creatine uptake.
- A normal dietary sodium intake is sufficient — no need to megadose salt
- Post-workout electrolyte drinks naturally provide this

### Alpha-Lipoic Acid
- **ALA (600-1000 mg) may enhance creatine uptake** into muscle via GLUT-4 transporter and improved insulin sensitivity (Burke et al., 2003)
- Not essential but potentially useful during a loading phase

### Optimal Pairing Strategy
- **Simplest approach:** Take 5 g creatine with your post-workout meal (which should contain protein and carbs anyway). No special preparation needed.
- **Loading phase optimization:** Take each 5 g dose with a meal containing ~50 g carbs + 30 g protein
- **Daily convenience:** Mix creatine into your morning protein shake or coffee. Consistency > timing optimization.

### What Doesn't Matter
- **Hot vs cold liquid** — creatine is heat-stable. Coffee, warm water, cold water — all fine.
- **Dissolved vs undissolved** — creatine that doesn't fully dissolve is still absorbed in the GI tract. Gritty texture is fine.
- **Fasted vs fed** — creatine is absorbed either way. Fed state is slightly better but the difference is trivial with daily dosing.
