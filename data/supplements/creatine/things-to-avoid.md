# Creatine
## Things to Avoid

### Wasting Money on "Superior" Forms
- **Creatine HCl, Kre-Alkalyn, ethyl ester, liquid creatine** — all marketed as superior to monohydrate, none proven to be. You are paying 3-10x more per effective gram for no additional benefit. Stick with creatine monohydrate.
- **"Creatine blends" with proprietary formulas** — often underdosed creatine mixed with cheap fillers at premium prices. Buy pure creatine monohydrate separately.
- **"Buffered" or "pH-corrected" creatine** — solutions looking for problems that don't exist. Monohydrate is stable at stomach pH and nearly 100% absorbed.

### Common Mistakes
- **Loading when you don't need to** — loading (20 g/day for 5-7 days) saturates stores faster but causes more GI discomfort and water weight fluctuation. Unless you have a competition in the next week, just start with 5 g/day and let it saturate over 3-4 weeks.
- **Cycling on and off** — there is no scientific basis for cycling creatine. You lose your saturated stores during "off" periods and gain nothing. Take it daily, indefinitely.
- **Panicking about creatinine on blood tests** — creatine supplementation raises serum creatinine. This is a normal metabolic byproduct, NOT kidney damage. Tell your doctor you take creatine. Request cystatin C-based eGFR if kidney function is a concern.
- **Under-hydrating** — increase daily water intake by 16-24 oz when supplementing. Not because creatine is dangerous without it, but for optimal muscle hydration and digestion.
- **Taking mega-doses** — more than 5 g/day (maintenance) provides no additional benefit for most people. Excess creatine is simply excreted. Exception: very large individuals (200+ lbs) may benefit from 10 g/day.

### Behaviors That Undermine Creatine's Benefits
- **Not training** — creatine amplifies training adaptations. Without resistance training or high-intensity exercise, you're getting cognitive benefits but missing the primary performance benefits.
- **Chronic sleep deprivation** — creatine can partially compensate for cognitive decline during sleep loss, but it cannot replace sleep for muscle recovery and growth
- **Excessive alcohol consumption** — alcohol suppresses muscle protein synthesis and mTOR signaling, reducing the anabolic environment creatine helps create
- **Severe caloric restriction** — creatine cannot build muscle without adequate calories and protein. Ensure protein intake is at least 1.6 g/kg/day.

### Medical Cautions
- **Do not take creatine if you have diagnosed kidney disease** without consulting your nephrologist
- **Inform all healthcare providers** that you take creatine — it affects blood markers (creatinine, eGFR) used to assess kidney function
- **Before surgery** — mention creatine supplementation to your anesthesiologist, as creatinine levels may affect pre-surgical kidney function assessment
