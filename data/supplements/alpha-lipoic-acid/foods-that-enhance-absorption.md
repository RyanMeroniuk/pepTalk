# Alpha-Lipoic Acid
## Foods That Enhance Absorption

ALA is best absorbed on an EMPTY stomach. However, certain nutrients and foods — taken at different times — support ALA's effectiveness.

- **Empty stomach is the best "enhancer":** ALA absorption decreases by 40-60% when taken with food. The single most important thing for ALA absorption is taking it 30-60 minutes before a meal on an empty stomach. Plain water is the ideal vehicle
- **B-vitamin-rich foods (take with meals, separate from ALA):**
  - **Thiamine (B1) sources** — nutritional yeast, pork, sunflower seeds, legumes. ALA may increase thiamine utilization; adequate B1 intake supports the metabolic pathways ALA participates in
  - **Biotin-rich foods** — eggs, liver, nuts, sweet potatoes. ALA at high doses may compete with biotin for transport. Ensuring adequate dietary biotin prevents potential depletion
- **Sulfur-rich foods (support glutathione production):**
  - **Cruciferous vegetables** — broccoli, Brussels sprouts, cabbage, kale, cauliflower. These provide sulforaphane and sulfur amino acids that support glutathione synthesis, amplifying ALA's glutathione-boosting effects
  - **Garlic and onions** — rich in organosulfur compounds that support Phase II detoxification alongside ALA
  - **Eggs** — contain cysteine, a rate-limiting amino acid for glutathione synthesis
- **Selenium-rich foods:** Brazil nuts (1-2 per day), sardines, eggs. Selenium is required for glutathione peroxidase, which works alongside ALA-regenerated glutathione
- **Protein-rich meals (after ALA absorption):** Whey protein, eggs, meat, and legumes provide the amino acids (cysteine, glycine, glutamate) needed for glutathione synthesis that ALA stimulates. Eat protein-rich meals after ALA has been absorbed
- **Vitamin C-rich foods:** Citrus, bell peppers, strawberries, kiwi. ALA regenerates vitamin C, and having adequate vitamin C stores maximizes this synergy
- **Magnesium-rich foods:** Dark leafy greens, nuts, dark chocolate. Magnesium supports hundreds of enzymatic reactions including those in the antioxidant defense system ALA enhances
