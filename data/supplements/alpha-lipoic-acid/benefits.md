# Alpha-Lipoic Acid
## Benefits

- **Diabetic neuropathy (strongest evidence):** The ALADIN, SYDNEY, and NATHAN trials demonstrated significant improvement in neuropathic symptoms (pain, burning, numbness, tingling) with 600mg/day IV or oral ALA. Prescribed in Germany for this indication
- **Blood sugar regulation:** Reduces fasting blood glucose by 10-30 mg/dL and HbA1c by 0.3-0.5% in clinical trials. Improves insulin sensitivity in type 2 diabetes and prediabetes. Effective at 300-600mg/day
- **Weight management:** Meta-analyses show modest but consistent weight loss (1-3 lbs over 8-12 weeks) at 300-1800mg/day. ALA reduces appetite through hypothalamic AMPK modulation and improves metabolic rate
- **Antioxidant protection:** Provides comprehensive antioxidant defense in every cellular compartment. Reduces markers of oxidative stress including malondialdehyde (MDA), 8-hydroxy-2-deoxyguanosine (8-OHdG), and F2-isoprostanes
- **Glutathione boost:** Increases intracellular glutathione by 30-70%. This is particularly important for the liver, immune function, and detoxification capacity
- **Heavy metal detoxification:** Chelates mercury, lead, arsenic, and cadmium. One of the few agents that crosses the blood-brain barrier for CNS metal chelation. Used in integrative medicine protocols for mercury toxicity
- **Cardiovascular health:** Reduces oxidized LDL, improves endothelial function, lowers blood pressure modestly, and reduces inflammatory markers (CRP, IL-6). Protects against atherosclerosis progression
- **Neuroprotection:** Slows cognitive decline in Alzheimer's disease at 600mg/day in some studies. Protects against neuronal oxidative damage. Crosses the blood-brain barrier to provide direct CNS antioxidant protection
- **Liver protection:** Hepatoprotective effects in non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD). Reduces liver enzymes (ALT, AST) and hepatic steatosis. Supports glutathione-dependent liver detoxification
- **Skin health and anti-aging:** Topical and oral ALA reduce wrinkles, improve skin texture, and protect against photoaging. ALA reduces AGE formation, which contributes to skin aging and loss of elasticity
- **Eye health:** Protects against cataracts and glaucoma through antioxidant protection of the lens and retinal cells. Improves visual function in diabetic retinopathy
- **Anti-inflammatory:** Reduces systemic inflammation markers. Beneficial in inflammatory conditions including rheumatoid arthritis, metabolic syndrome, and chronic fatigue
