# Alpha-Lipoic Acid
## Forms and Bioavailability

- **Racemic ALA (R/S-ALA):** The most common and least expensive form. Contains a 50/50 mixture of R-ALA (bioactive) and S-ALA (synthetic, largely inactive). Most clinical trials used racemic ALA. Effective but you are paying for 50% inactive material
- **R-ALA (R-alpha-lipoic acid — preferred):** The naturally occurring, biologically active enantiomer. R-ALA is the form produced by the body and found in food. It is 10-40x more potent than S-ALA at the cellular level. R-ALA is absorbed faster, reaches higher peak blood levels, and has greater bioactivity per milligram. The preferred form for supplementation
- **Stabilized R-ALA (Na-R-ALA / Bio-Enhanced R-ALA):** Pure R-ALA is unstable and tends to polymerize (clump together) at room temperature, reducing absorption. Stabilized forms include sodium R-lipoate (Na-R-ALA) and the branded form Bio-Enhanced R-Lipoic Acid (GeroNova Research). These forms resist polymerization and provide superior bioavailability
- **S-ALA:** The synthetic enantiomer not found in nature. Largely biologically inactive or may even antagonize some R-ALA effects. Present in racemic supplements as 50% of the content. No benefit to isolated S-ALA supplementation
- **Oral bioavailability:** ALA oral bioavailability is approximately 20-38% (racemic) due to hepatic first-pass metabolism. R-ALA achieves 40-50% higher peak plasma levels than racemic ALA at equivalent doses. Taking on an empty stomach improves absorption by 40-60% (food significantly reduces absorption)
- **IV administration:** Used clinically for diabetic neuropathy (especially in Germany) at 600mg/day. Provides 100% bioavailability. Achieves much higher plasma levels than oral dosing
- **Sustained-release formulations:** Some brands offer controlled-release ALA to extend plasma levels. Standard ALA has a very short half-life (~30 minutes for R-ALA), so sustained-release may maintain therapeutic levels longer. However, peak plasma levels are lower with sustained-release
- **Capsule vs tablet:** Capsules are generally preferred over tablets. Some tablet binders may slow dissolution. Quick-dissolving capsules maximize absorption
- **Take on an empty stomach:** This is critical for ALA. Food reduces ALA absorption by 40-60%. Take 30-60 minutes before meals or 2 hours after meals for optimal absorption
- **Key brands:** GeroNova (Bio-Enhanced R-Lipoic Acid), Doctor's Best (stabilized R-ALA), NOW Foods (R-ALA), and Jarrow Formulas (R-ALA) are commonly used
