# Alpha-Lipoic Acid
## Classification

- **Category:** Universal antioxidant / metabolic support / neuroprotectant
- **Supplement class:** Organosulfur compound (dithiol)
- **Also known as:** ALA, alpha-lipoic acid, thioctic acid, lipoic acid
- **Endogenous status:** Naturally synthesized in mitochondria in small amounts; also obtained from diet
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement in the US. Prescription medication in Germany and several European countries for diabetic neuropathy (as alpha-lipoic acid or thioctic acid)
- **Primary function:** Universal antioxidant active in both water-soluble and fat-soluble environments; cofactor for mitochondrial energy enzymes; regenerates other antioxidants; chelates heavy metals
- **Solubility:** Uniquely amphipathic — soluble in both water AND fat. This is rare among antioxidants and allows ALA to work in every compartment of the cell
- **Key distinction:** The only antioxidant that works in both aqueous (cytoplasm, blood) and lipid (cell membranes, brain) environments
