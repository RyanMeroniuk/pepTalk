# Alpha-Lipoic Acid
## Peptide Synergies

- **BPC-157 (healing + detox synergy):** BPC-157 promotes tissue repair, angiogenesis, and gut healing. ALA provides antioxidant protection to healing tissues and supports glutathione levels needed for tissue regeneration. ALA's heavy metal chelation also reduces toxic burden that can impair healing
- **Semaglutide / Tirzepatide / Retatrutide (metabolic stack):** GLP-1 agonists drive weight loss and improve metabolic function. ALA enhances insulin sensitivity through AMPK activation, supports fat metabolism, and provides antioxidant protection during the metabolic stress of weight loss. The combination addresses blood sugar from multiple angles
- **MOTS-c (mitochondrial + metabolic synergy):** MOTS-c activates AMPK and improves glucose metabolism (exercise mimetic). ALA also activates AMPK and serves as a mitochondrial cofactor (pyruvate dehydrogenase, alpha-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase). Together they provide comprehensive AMPK activation and mitochondrial support
- **AOD-9604 / 5-Amino-1MQ (fat metabolism):** ALA improves mitochondrial fat oxidation and insulin sensitivity. Combined with fat-targeting peptides, ALA ensures the mitochondrial machinery can efficiently burn mobilized fatty acids
- **GHK-Cu (skin and anti-aging):** GHK-Cu promotes collagen synthesis and skin regeneration. ALA reduces AGE formation (a major driver of skin aging) and provides antioxidant protection. The combination addresses skin aging through both structural repair (GHK-Cu) and oxidative damage prevention (ALA)
- **VIP (Vasoactive Intestinal Peptide — detox synergy):** VIP addresses mold illness/CIRS and gut inflammation. ALA supports glutathione levels critical for mycotoxin detoxification and chelates heavy metals that often co-occur with mold exposure. Complementary detoxification support
- **KPV (anti-inflammatory gut synergy):** KPV is an anti-inflammatory tripeptide for gut healing. ALA boosts glutathione in the gut lining, reduces intestinal inflammation via NF-kB inhibition, and provides additional mucosal antioxidant protection
- **Thymosin Alpha-1 (immune + detox):** Thymosin Alpha-1 enhances immune function. ALA supports immune cells by maintaining glutathione levels (critical for lymphocyte function) and reducing the oxidative burden that suppresses immunity
- **Semax / Selank (neuroprotective stack):** ALA crosses the blood-brain barrier and provides neuronal antioxidant protection. Combined with Semax (BDNF) or Selank (GABA/anxiolytic), ALA protects the neurons these peptides are stimulating and supporting
- **Epitalon / NMN (longevity stack):** ALA's antioxidant recycling, glutathione boosting, and mitochondrial cofactor roles complement NMN (NAD+ restoration) and Epitalon (telomerase activation). Comprehensive anti-aging through multiple pathways
