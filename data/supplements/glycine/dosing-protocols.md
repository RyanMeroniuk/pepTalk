# Glycine
## Dosing Protocols

### Sleep Optimization
- **Dose:** 3 g (3,000 mg)
- **Timing:** 30-60 minutes before bed
- **Method:** Dissolve powder in warm (not hot) water or herbal tea; pleasant sweet taste
- **Duration:** Ongoing; no tolerance development; safe for indefinite use
- **Notes:** This is the most studied dose for sleep. Some people benefit from 5 g. Start at 3 g and adjust.

### Collagen Synthesis Support
- **Dose:** 5-10 g/day
- **Timing:** Divided — 5 g morning, 5 g evening. Or take with collagen supplement.
- **Duration:** Ongoing for connective tissue support
- **Notes:** Best combined with vitamin C (500 mg) and a collagen supplement for maximum collagen synthesis

### GlyNAC Anti-Aging Protocol
- **Dose:** Glycine 100 mg/kg/day + NAC 100 mg/kg/day (approximately 7 g glycine + 7 g NAC for a 70 kg person)
- **Timing:** Divided into 2 doses — morning and evening
- **Duration:** Studied for 16-24 weeks in clinical trials; benefits reverse upon stopping, suggesting ongoing use
- **Notes:** Based on Sekhar et al. research at Baylor College of Medicine. The combination restores glutathione levels in older adults.

### Liver Detoxification Support
- **Dose:** 3-5 g/day
- **Timing:** Morning on an empty stomach or divided doses
- **Duration:** Ongoing, especially during periods of high toxic exposure
- **Notes:** Increase to 5-10 g/day if on medications that burden Phase II liver pathways

### Anti-Inflammatory Support
- **Dose:** 3-5 g/day
- **Timing:** With meals to reduce postprandial inflammation
- **Duration:** Ongoing for chronic inflammatory conditions

### Schizophrenia (Adjunctive — Under Medical Supervision Only)
- **Dose:** 0.4-0.8 g/kg/day (28-56 g/day for a 70 kg person)
- **Timing:** Divided into 3 doses throughout the day
- **Duration:** As directed by psychiatrist
- **Notes:** These are very high doses used in clinical research; not for self-directed use

### Combined Sleep + Collagen Protocol
- **Dose:** 5 g before bed + 5 g with morning collagen
- **Timing:** 5 g with collagen supplement + vitamin C in the morning; 5 g 30 min before bed
- **Duration:** Ongoing
- **Notes:** This covers both sleep and connective tissue needs cost-effectively

### Important Notes
- **No loading phase needed** — glycine works acutely for sleep (first night) and builds benefits over time for collagen/glutathione
- **Very safe at high doses** — no significant toxicity reported even at 30+ g/day in clinical trials
- **Natural sweetness** — can be used to sweeten beverages as a sugar replacement bonus
- **Pairs exceptionally with magnesium glycinate** — if taking mag glycinate for sleep, you get ~2.4 g glycine from a standard magnesium dose; add 1-3 g free glycine to reach 3-5 g total
