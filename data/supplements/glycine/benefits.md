# Glycine
## Benefits

### Sleep Quality (Most Robust Evidence)
- **3 g before bed** reduces time to sleep onset, improves subjective sleep quality, and reduces daytime sleepiness — demonstrated in multiple RCTs (Yamadera et al., 2007; Bannai et al., 2012; Inagawa et al., 2006)
- **Improves slow-wave sleep (deep sleep)** without altering total sleep architecture — no REM suppression, no hangover effect
- **Mechanism is unique** — works via peripheral vasodilation and core temperature drop, plus direct action on the suprachiasmatic nucleus, NOT via sedation
- **No tolerance development** — glycine does not lose effectiveness with continued use (unlike many sleep aids)
- **Next-day benefits** — improved reaction time, cognitive clarity, and reduced fatigue the morning after, even with shortened sleep

### Collagen & Connective Tissue
- **Supports skin elasticity and hydration** — glycine is rate-limiting for collagen synthesis; supplementation supports dermal collagen production
- **Joint health** — supports cartilage (Type II collagen) maintenance and repair
- **Tendon and ligament strength** — critical for Type I collagen in connective tissue
- **Bone health** — collagen provides the organic matrix of bone; glycine supports this structure
- **Wound healing acceleration** — provides substrate for fibroblast collagen production at wound sites

### Anti-Aging (GlyNAC Research)
- **GlyNAC (Glycine + N-Acetyl Cysteine) supplementation** in older adults (2021-2023 studies by Sekhar et al.) showed:
  - Restored glutathione levels to those of young adults
  - Improved mitochondrial function
  - Reduced oxidative stress markers
  - Improved insulin resistance
  - Reduced waist circumference
  - Improved muscle strength, gait speed, and exercise capacity
  - Improved cognitive function
- These effects reversed within weeks of stopping supplementation, suggesting ongoing need

### Metabolic Health
- **Improves insulin sensitivity** — glycine supplementation reduces fasting glucose and HbA1c in some studies
- **Reduces metabolic syndrome markers** — lower fasting glucose, improved lipid profiles
- **Low plasma glycine is a biomarker** for Type 2 diabetes risk and metabolic syndrome

### Neuroprotection
- **Protects against excitotoxicity** — inhibitory neurotransmitter activity balances glutamate excitation
- **Supports cognitive function** — NMDA receptor co-agonism facilitates learning, memory, and synaptic plasticity
- **May benefit schizophrenia** — high-dose glycine (0.4-0.8 g/kg/day) has shown benefit as adjunctive therapy in schizophrenia trials (reduces negative symptoms)

### Liver Protection & Detoxification
- **Supports alcohol metabolism** — glycine protects hepatocytes from alcohol-induced damage in animal models
- **Enhances Phase II detoxification** — conjugates toxins for excretion
- **Protects against acetaminophen (Tylenol) liver injury** in animal studies (by supporting glutathione)

### Anti-Inflammatory
- **Systemic inflammation reduction** — glycine supplementation reduces CRP, IL-6, and TNF-alpha in inflammatory conditions
- **Cytoprotective** — protects cells from ischemia-reperfusion injury (relevant for surgical and cardiac contexts)
