# Glycine
## Peptide Synergies

### Tier 1: Exceptional Synergy

#### DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide) + Glycine (The Sleep Stack)
- **Rationale:** DSIP promotes delta wave sleep and modulates cortisol rhythm. Glycine lowers core body temperature and acts as an inhibitory neurotransmitter. These are complementary, non-overlapping sleep pathways — DSIP works centrally on sleep architecture while glycine works peripherally on thermoregulation and inhibitory signaling.
- **Protocol:** Glycine 3-5 g 30-60 min before bed + DSIP at standard peptide dosing (100-200 mcg before bed)
- **Expected outcome:** Faster sleep onset, deeper sleep, improved sleep architecture, better morning alertness
- **Best for:** Insomnia, poor sleep quality, shift workers, jet lag recovery

#### Epitalon + Glycine (Sleep + Anti-Aging)
- **Rationale:** Epitalon activates telomerase and regulates melatonin production. Glycine supports collagen synthesis, glutathione production, and sleep quality. Together, they address aging from multiple angles — telomere maintenance, antioxidant defense, connective tissue integrity, and restorative sleep.
- **Protocol:** Glycine 5-10 g/day (divided) + Epitalon at standard cycling protocol
- **Best for:** Comprehensive anti-aging protocols, longevity optimization

### Tier 2: Strong Synergy

#### BPC-157 + Glycine
- **Rationale:** BPC-157 stimulates tissue repair and angiogenesis. Glycine provides the structural amino acid for collagen deposition at repair sites. BPC-157 sends the repair signal; glycine provides the building material.
- **Protocol:** Glycine 5-10 g/day + BPC-157 250-500 mcg 2x/day
- **Best for:** Tendon/ligament repair, wound healing, gut healing (glycine supports mucosal collagen)

#### TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4) + Glycine
- **Rationale:** TB-500 promotes tissue repair, reduces inflammation, and increases flexibility. Glycine supports collagen synthesis in the tissues TB-500 is repairing. TB-500 + glycine = repair signal + raw material.
- **Protocol:** Glycine 5-10 g/day + TB-500 at standard dosing (2.5-5 mg 2x/week)
- **Best for:** Injury recovery, athletic tissue repair, flexibility improvement

#### GHK-Cu + Glycine (Skin & Collagen)
- **Rationale:** GHK-Cu is a potent collagen-stimulating peptide that activates fibroblasts and remodels tissue. Glycine ensures adequate substrate for the collagen production GHK-Cu initiates. Without sufficient glycine, GHK-Cu's collagen-building signal goes unanswered.
- **Protocol:** Glycine 5-10 g/day + GHK-Cu (topical or subcutaneous per standard protocol) + Vitamin C 500 mg
- **Best for:** Skin rejuvenation, anti-aging skin protocols, wound healing

#### CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + Glycine (Recovery + Sleep)
- **Rationale:** GH secretagogues are typically taken before bed to amplify the natural nocturnal GH pulse. Glycine enhances sleep quality (more deep sleep = more GH release) and provides substrate for the tissue repair that GH drives.
- **Protocol:** Glycine 3-5 g before bed + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin before bed
- **Timing:** Take glycine 30 min before bed, then peptide 15-20 min before bed on an empty stomach
- **Best for:** Recovery optimization, body composition, anti-aging
