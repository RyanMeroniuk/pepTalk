# Glycine
## Classification

- **Category:** Conditionally essential amino acid
- **Chemical class:** Simplest amino acid (non-essential under normal conditions, conditionally essential under metabolic stress)
- **Molecular formula:** C2H5NO2
- **Supplement type:** Single amino acid supplement
- **Regulatory status:** Dietary supplement (OTC); used in clinical nutrition; GRAS status
- **Primary systems:** Neurological (inhibitory neurotransmitter), connective tissue (collagen synthesis), hepatic (detoxification), metabolic (glutathione synthesis)
- **Unique properties:** Glycine is the smallest amino acid with no chiral center. It serves simultaneously as a neurotransmitter, a structural amino acid (one-third of all collagen is glycine), and a metabolic cofactor.
- **Endogenous synthesis:** The body produces ~3 g/day, but metabolic demand may be 10-15 g/day — creating a chronic "glycine gap" in most people
