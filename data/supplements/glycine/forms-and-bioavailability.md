# Glycine
## Forms and Bioavailability

### Glycine Powder (Free Form)
- **The gold standard** — simple, pure, inexpensive, and highly bioavailable
- **Naturally sweet taste** — glycine literally means "sweet" (Greek: glykys); pleasant to consume dissolved in water
- Absorbs rapidly via intestinal amino acid transporters
- **Nearly 100% bioavailable** orally — glycine is the simplest amino acid and is efficiently absorbed
- Best for: sleep protocols, high-dose supplementation, mixing into beverages

### Glycine Capsules
- Convenient for travel and precise dosing
- Typical capsule: 500 mg - 1000 mg per cap
- **Impractical at therapeutic doses** — 3 g (sleep) requires 3-6 capsules; higher doses need many more
- Best for: moderate supplementation, people who dislike powders

### Trimethylglycine (TMG / Betaine)
- **Not the same as glycine** — TMG is a methyl donor that contains a glycine backbone but functions differently
- TMG supports methylation (donates methyl groups) and is used alongside creatine to support homocysteine metabolism
- Does NOT substitute for glycine in sleep, collagen, or glutathione protocols
- Best for: methylation support, paired with creatine supplementation

### Glycine from Collagen/Gelatin
- **Collagen protein is ~33% glycine** — a 10 g collagen serving provides ~3.3 g glycine
- **Gelatin** is cooked collagen; same amino acid profile
- **Bone broth** provides glycine naturally (1-3 g per cup depending on preparation)
- This is the most "food-like" way to get glycine, but standalone glycine supplementation allows precise dosing

### Magnesium Glycinate (Bisglycinate)
- **Magnesium chelated with two glycine molecules** — provides both magnesium and glycine
- A typical dose of 400 mg elemental magnesium from magnesium glycinate provides ~2.4 g of glycine
- **Excellent for sleep** — the combination of magnesium (muscle relaxation, GABA support) and glycine (core temperature reduction, inhibitory neurotransmission) is synergistic
- Best for: sleep optimization, people who need both magnesium and glycine

### Bioavailability Notes
- **Glycine is one of the best-absorbed amino acids** — small molecular size, efficient transport
- **No special absorption requirements** — works with or without food
- **Timing matters more than form** — for sleep, take 30-60 minutes before bed regardless of form
- **Sweetness is a feature** — glycine's natural sweetness makes it easy to add to water, tea (cooled), or other beverages
