# Glycine
## Lifestyle Recommendations

### Optimize Sleep Benefits
- **Create a cool sleep environment** — 65-68°F (18-20°C) amplifies glycine's core temperature-lowering mechanism. Use breathable bedding, consider a cooling mattress pad.
- **Establish a consistent pre-bed routine** — take glycine 30-60 minutes before bed as part of a wind-down ritual. Combine with dim lighting, reading, or gentle stretching.
- **Avoid screens 1 hour before bed** — or use blue light blocking glasses. Glycine works best when your melatonin rhythm is intact.
- **Morning sunlight exposure** — 10-15 minutes of bright light within 30 minutes of waking sets the circadian clock, making glycine's evening effects more potent
- **Layer sleep supports** — glycine (temperature) + magnesium glycinate (GABA/muscle relaxation) + dim lighting (melatonin) covers all three major sleep pathways

### Maximize Collagen Synthesis
- **Take glycine with vitamin C** — 250-500 mg vitamin C alongside glycine or collagen supplements. Non-negotiable for collagen production.
- **Exercise regularly** — mechanical loading stimulates fibroblasts and collagen deposition. Resistance training, running, and even walking provide mechanical signals to connective tissues.
- **Eat nose-to-tail** — include bone broth, collagen-rich cuts, skin-on poultry, and gelatin-based foods regularly. Ancestral diets provided far more glycine than modern diets.
- **Protect existing collagen** — use sunscreen (UV degrades collagen), minimize sugar (glycation), don't smoke (collagen destruction), and manage inflammation

### Support Detoxification
- **Stay hydrated** — water supports kidney excretion of glycine-conjugated metabolites. 2-3 liters daily.
- **Minimize unnecessary medications** — every drug metabolized by the liver may use glycine for conjugation. Use medications as needed, but don't overburden the system.
- **Reduce environmental toxin exposure** — eat organic when possible (reduce pesticide load), filter drinking water, avoid unnecessary chemicals in personal care products
- **Sweat regularly** — sauna, exercise, or hot baths help excrete toxins through skin, reducing the liver's glycine burden

### Exercise Recommendations
- **Resistance training** — 3-4x/week to stimulate collagen synthesis in tendons, ligaments, and fascia. Glycine ensures the raw material is available.
- **Gelatin/glycine before exercise** — some research (Keith Baar, UC Davis) suggests taking 15 g gelatin + vitamin C 30-60 minutes before exercise enhances collagen synthesis in response to the mechanical stimulus. Glycine can substitute for or supplement gelatin.
- **Stretching and mobility work** — maintains collagen flexibility and range of motion. Yoga, foam rolling, dynamic stretching.
- **Avoid overtraining** — excessive training without recovery breaks down collagen faster than it can be rebuilt

### Stress Management
- **Glycine is calming but not a substitute for stress management** — practice daily stress reduction (meditation, breathwork, journaling, time in nature)
- **Consider taking 1-2 g glycine during daytime stress** — the inhibitory neurotransmitter effect provides gentle calming without sedation at low doses
- **Pair with adaptogens** — ashwagandha, rhodiola, or L-theanine can complement glycine's calming properties during the day

### Dietary Integration
- **Make bone broth a staple** — 1-2 cups daily provides glycine, glutamine, collagen, and minerals in a whole-food form
- **Use gelatin in cooking** — add to soups, stews, smoothies, homemade gummies. Each tablespoon provides ~1.5 g glycine.
- **Collagen in morning coffee or smoothie** — 10 g collagen provides ~3.3 g glycine plus proline and hydroxyproline
