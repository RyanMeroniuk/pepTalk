# Sleep & Circadian Rhythm

## Why Sleep Is the #1 Foundation

- **Non-negotiable baseline**: Sleep is the single most important health behavior. No supplement, peptide, or training program can compensate for consistently poor sleep. The recommendation of 7-9 hours per night is backed by decades of research and applies to virtually every adult.
- **Peptide efficacy depends on sleep**: Growth hormone release peaks during deep sleep. If you are taking GH secretagogues (CJC-1295, Ipamorelin, MK-677) and sleeping poorly, you are wasting your money. Sleep is the delivery mechanism for their benefits.
- **Muscle recovery and growth**: Muscle protein synthesis, tissue repair, and inflammation resolution all occur primarily during sleep. Training hard without sleeping well leads to overtraining, injury, and stalled progress.
- **Fat loss**: Sleep deprivation increases ghrelin (hunger hormone), decreases leptin (satiety hormone), raises cortisol, and shifts the body toward storing fat and burning muscle. Even a calorie deficit becomes less effective with poor sleep.
- **Cognitive function**: Memory consolidation, learning, creativity, and emotional regulation all depend on adequate sleep. Nootropic peptides like Semax and Selank work best when the brain has time to consolidate during REM sleep.
- **Immune health**: T-cell function, cytokine production, and infection defense are all sleep-dependent. One night of poor sleep can reduce natural killer cell activity by up to 70%.
- **Circadian rhythm**: Your body runs on a roughly 24-hour internal clock that governs hormone release, body temperature, digestion, and alertness. When this clock is disrupted (irregular sleep times, late-night light exposure, jet lag), virtually every system in the body suffers. Aligning your behavior with your circadian rhythm is the master lever for health optimization.
