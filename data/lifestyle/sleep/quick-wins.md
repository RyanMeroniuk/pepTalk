# Sleep & Circadian Rhythm

## Quick Wins

Five things you can do tonight to immediately improve your sleep:

1. **Set your bedroom to 65-68°F (18-20°C)**: Turn down the thermostat or turn on a fan. Your body needs to cool down to fall asleep and stay asleep. A cool room is the single easiest environmental change you can make. If you do not have climate control, use lighter bedding and keep a window cracked.

2. **Put your phone in another room**: Charge your phone outside the bedroom. This eliminates the temptation to scroll before sleep, removes blue light exposure, prevents middle-of-the-night screen checking, and removes the anxiety-inducing glow of notifications. Use a simple alarm clock instead.

3. **Take 400mg magnesium glycinate 30 minutes before bed**: Magnesium is the most well-supported sleep supplement and is safe for nightly use. It calms the nervous system, relaxes muscles, and supports GABA activity. Most adults are deficient. Available at any pharmacy or supplement store.

4. **No caffeine after noon tomorrow**: Start this rule tomorrow and maintain it. Caffeine has a 5-7 hour half-life, meaning a 2pm coffee still has significant caffeine in your system at bedtime. This single change often produces noticeable improvements in sleep quality within 2-3 days.

5. **Set a consistent alarm (even weekends) starting tomorrow**: Pick a wake time you can maintain 7 days a week and set it now. Consistency is the foundation of circadian rhythm health. Keep your wake time within 30 minutes of your target every single day. Your body will begin to naturally feel sleepy at the right time in the evening once your wake time is locked in.
