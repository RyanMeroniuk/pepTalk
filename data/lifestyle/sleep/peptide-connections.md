# Sleep & Circadian Rhythm

## Peptide Connections

- **DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide)**: Directly promotes delta wave (N3/deep) sleep by modulating GABAergic and glutamatergic signaling. DSIP increases the proportion of time spent in slow-wave sleep, which is the stage most critical for growth hormone release and tissue repair. It also helps normalize disrupted sleep patterns and reduces cortisol levels. Typically dosed subcutaneously before bed.
- **Pinealon**: A bioregulatory tripeptide that acts on the pineal gland to normalize melatonin synthesis. Pinealon helps restore the natural circadian melatonin rhythm rather than flooding receptors with exogenous melatonin. Particularly useful for age-related decline in melatonin production and for those with disrupted circadian rhythms. Can be used intranasally or sublingually.
- **Epitalon (Epithalon)**: Activates telomerase in the pineal gland and restores endogenous melatonin production to youthful levels. Epitalon addresses the root cause of age-related melatonin decline rather than supplementing melatonin directly. Also supports circadian rhythm regulation through its effect on the pineal gland. Typically administered in cycles.
- **CJC-1295/Ipamorelin**: Growth hormone release naturally peaks during the first bout of deep sleep. Bedtime dosing of GH secretagogues leverages this natural rhythm, amplifying the GH pulse that occurs during N3 sleep. Poor sleep quality directly reduces the effectiveness of these peptides because there is less deep sleep during which GH can be released. Optimizing sleep is arguably the most important lifestyle factor for anyone using GH secretagogues.
- **MK-677 (Ibutamoren)**: Improves sleep quality through the GH/IGF-1 pathway. Users commonly report deeper, more restorative sleep with vivid dreams (indicating more REM). MK-677 increases the duration of both REM and N3 sleep stages. This is one of the reasons it is popular despite being an oral secretagogue with a broader side effect profile.
- **Semax**: A nootropic peptide that increases BDNF and stimulates the central nervous system. Avoid evening dosing as it can cause alertness, mental stimulation, and difficulty falling asleep. Best dosed in the morning or early afternoon. Sleep quality remains important for consolidating the cognitive gains Semax provides.
- **Selank**: An anxiolytic peptide that modulates GABA and serotonin systems. By reducing anxiety and promoting calm, Selank can indirectly support sleep quality for those whose sleep is disrupted by stress or anxious rumination. Can be dosed in the evening without the stimulatory effects of Semax.
- **Semaglutide/Tirzepatide**: Poor sleep increases ghrelin and decreases leptin, amplifying hunger and cravings. This directly undermines the appetite-suppressing effects of GLP-1 agonists and can stall weight loss progress. Additionally, sleep deprivation shifts the body toward losing lean mass rather than fat during a caloric deficit. Prioritizing sleep is essential for maximizing the body composition benefits of these peptides.
