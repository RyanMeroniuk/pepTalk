# Sleep & Circadian Rhythm

## Resources

### Sleep Trackers

- **Oura Ring**: Gold standard for consumer sleep tracking. Tracks sleep stages, HRV, respiratory rate, body temperature, and blood oxygen. Worn on the finger, comfortable for sleep. Provides a daily Readiness score. Subscription required for full features.
- **Whoop**: Wrist-worn strap focused on recovery and strain. Excellent sleep stage detection and HRV tracking. Provides a Recovery score each morning. Subscription model with no upfront hardware cost.
- **Apple Watch**: Convenient if already owned. Sleep tracking improved significantly with watchOS 9+. Tracks sleep stages, HRV, respiratory rate, and blood oxygen. Less comfortable for sleep than Oura due to size.
- **Fitbit**: Affordable entry point for sleep tracking. Tracks sleep stages and provides a Sleep Score. Premium subscription unlocks detailed insights.

### Apps

- **Sleep Cycle**: Uses phone microphone or accelerometer to detect sleep stages and wake you during light sleep within a set window. Simple and effective alarm alternative.
- **AutoSleep**: Apple Watch companion app with detailed sleep analysis. Automatic tracking without manually starting a session.
- **Pillow**: Sleep tracking and analysis app for Apple Watch and iPhone. Records sleep sounds and provides detailed stage breakdowns.

### Blue Light Management

- **f.lux**: Free software for Mac and Windows that shifts screen color temperature after sunset. Set to its warmest setting for evening use.
- **Night Shift (iOS)**: Built-in blue light filter for iPhone and iPad. Set to activate automatically at sunset. Use the warmest setting.
- **Blue light glasses**: Amber or orange-tinted lenses that block blue wavelengths. Wear after sunset for all indoor activities, not just screen time. Look for lenses that block wavelengths below 500nm.

### Environment

- **Blackout curtains**: Essential for anyone in an urban area or who needs to sleep during daylight. Look for curtains that block 99%+ of light. Alternatives include blackout shades or a high-quality sleep mask.
- **White noise machines**: Mask environmental noise with consistent sound. LectroFan and Hatch are popular options. A simple fan also works. Consistent background noise prevents noise-related awakenings.
- **Cooling mattress pads**: Eight Sleep Pod and ChiliPad/OOLER allow precise temperature control of the sleep surface. Can be programmed to cool at bedtime and warm before the alarm. High-end but highly effective for those with temperature regulation issues.

### Metrics to Track

- **Total sleep time**: Aim for 7-9 hours. Track your average over weeks, not individual nights.
- **Deep sleep percentage**: Aim for 15-25% of total sleep time, or roughly 1.5-2 hours. This is where GH release and physical restoration occur.
- **REM sleep percentage**: Aim for 20-25% of total sleep time, or roughly 1.5-2 hours. Critical for memory and emotional processing.
- **Sleep latency**: Time to fall asleep. Ideal is 10-20 minutes. Falling asleep instantly suggests sleep deprivation. Taking longer than 30 minutes suggests hyperarousal or circadian misalignment.
- **Heart rate variability (HRV)**: Higher HRV during sleep indicates better autonomic nervous system balance and recovery. Track your personal trend over time rather than comparing to others.

### Books

- **"Why We Sleep" by Matthew Walker**: The definitive popular science book on sleep. Covers sleep stages, circadian rhythm, and the consequences of sleep deprivation. Essential reading for anyone optimizing health.
- **"The Circadian Code" by Satchin Panda**: Focuses on circadian rhythm and time-restricted eating. Practical advice for aligning daily behaviors with your internal clock.
