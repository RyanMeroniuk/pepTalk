# Sleep & Circadian Rhythm

## Supplement Connections

- **Magnesium Glycinate (400mg)**: The number one sleep supplement. Magnesium supports GABA receptor activity, the primary inhibitory neurotransmitter that calms neural activity. The glycinate form is preferred for sleep because glycine itself has calming properties and the chelated form is well-absorbed without GI distress. Magnesium also relaxes skeletal muscle, reducing restless legs and nighttime cramping. Take 30-60 minutes before bed.
- **Magnesium L-Threonate (2g)**: The only form of magnesium clinically shown to cross the blood-brain barrier and increase brain magnesium levels. Supports relaxation at the neural level rather than just peripheral muscle relaxation. Particularly useful for racing thoughts and cognitive overactivation at night. Can be combined with glycinate (use threonate for the brain, glycinate for the body).
- **L-Theanine (200mg)**: An amino acid found in green tea that promotes alpha brain wave activity, the same brain state associated with calm, relaxed alertness and the transition to sleep. L-theanine increases GABA, serotonin, and dopamine without causing sedation. It smooths the transition to sleep and improves sleep quality without morning grogginess.
- **Apigenin (50mg)**: A flavonoid found in chamomile that acts as a mild sedative through modulation of GABA receptors. Apigenin also inhibits CD38, an enzyme that degrades NAD+. This dual action supports both sleep and cellular energy/longevity pathways. Popularized by Andrew Huberman as part of a nightly sleep stack.
- **Glycine (3g)**: An amino acid that lowers core body temperature by dilating peripheral blood vessels, mimicking the natural thermoregulatory drop needed for sleep onset. Glycine also acts as an inhibitory neurotransmitter in the brainstem. Studies show 3g before bed improves subjective sleep quality and reduces next-day fatigue. Take 30-60 minutes before bed.
- **Melatonin (0.3-1mg)**: A chronobiotic hormone that signals sleep timing to the body. The effective dose is much lower than what most products contain. Use 0.3-0.5mg for circadian timing adjustment, up to 1mg maximum. Higher doses do not improve sleep and may cause grogginess, vivid nightmares, or receptor downregulation. Best used for jet lag or circadian rhythm correction, not as a nightly sedative.
- **Tart Cherry Extract**: A natural source of melatonin plus anti-inflammatory anthocyanins. Provides a gentle, food-based melatonin source alongside compounds that reduce inflammation and oxidative stress. Studies show tart cherry juice increases sleep time and efficiency. A good option for those who prefer food-derived sleep support.
- **Ashwagandha (300-600mg KSM-66 or Sensoril)**: An adaptogen that reduces cortisol levels by modulating the HPA axis. Most useful for individuals whose sleep disruption is driven by stress, anxiety, or elevated evening cortisol. Ashwagandha does not directly induce sleep but removes the cortisol barrier that prevents sleep. Take in the evening. Not recommended for those with thyroid conditions without medical guidance.
