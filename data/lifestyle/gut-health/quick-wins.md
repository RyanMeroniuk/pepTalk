# Gut & Digestive Health

## Quick Wins

- **Add one fermented food to your diet this week** — plain yogurt with live cultures, raw sauerkraut (refrigerated section, not shelf-stable), or kimchi are the easiest entry points; start with a small serving and increase gradually
- **Eat one meal today in silence, without screens, chewing thoroughly** — sit down, put your phone away, and focus on your food; chew each bite 20-30 times; this single practice activates the parasympathetic nervous system and dramatically improves digestion
- **Count how many different plant foods you eat in a week** — track vegetables, fruits, herbs, spices, nuts, seeds, legumes, and whole grains; most people eat fewer than 15; aim for 30 to build microbiome diversity
- **Stop snacking between meals** — give your gut 4+ hours of rest between meals to allow the migrating motor complex to activate and sweep bacteria and debris from the small intestine; drink water or plain tea between meals instead
- **If you take NSAIDs regularly, talk to your doctor about gut protection** — chronic use of ibuprofen, naproxen, or aspirin damages the gut lining; discuss alternatives, protective strategies, or whether BPC-157 may be appropriate for gut repair
