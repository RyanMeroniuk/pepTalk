# Gut & Digestive Health

## Resources

## Gut Testing

- **GI-MAP** — the most comprehensive stool test available; uses qPCR technology to detect pathogens (bacteria, parasites, viruses, fungi), assess microbiome composition, measure inflammation markers (calprotectin, secretory IgA), and evaluate digestive function (elastase, steatocrit)
- **Genova GI Effects** — another comprehensive stool analysis covering microbiome diversity, digestion, inflammation, and metabolic markers
- **Viome** — at-home gut microbiome test using metatranscriptomic analysis to assess microbial activity (what organisms are doing, not just what is present); provides personalized food recommendations

## Tracking Apps

- **Cara Care** — symptom tracking app for digestive issues; log meals, symptoms, stress, and bowel movements to identify patterns and triggers
- **Monash University FODMAP App** — the definitive guide for low-FODMAP diets; developed by the research team that created the FODMAP framework; essential if SIBO or IBS is suspected

## Fermented Foods

- **Plain yogurt** — choose brands with "live and active cultures" on the label and no added sugar; Greek yogurt or skyr are high-protein options
- **Sauerkraut** — must be refrigerated (shelf-stable versions are pasteurized and contain no live organisms); look for ingredients that are only cabbage and salt
- **Kimchi** — available at most grocery stores in the refrigerated section; contains Lactobacillus and other beneficial organisms plus anti-inflammatory compounds from garlic and ginger
- **Kefir** — a fermented milk drink with a broader range of organisms than yogurt; also available in dairy-free versions

## Bone Broth

- **Kettle & Fire** — widely available store-bought bone broth; convenient and reasonably high quality
- **Homemade** — simmer beef or chicken bones with apple cider vinegar (helps extract minerals) for 12-24 hours; the most cost-effective and nutrient-dense option

## Tracking Metrics

- **Bristol Stool Chart** — a visual scale from Type 1 (hard lumps) to Type 7 (liquid); Types 3-4 are ideal
- **Bowel movement frequency** — 1-2 well-formed movements daily is the target
- **Bloating patterns** — track when bloating occurs relative to meals to identify trigger foods
- **Transit time** — swallow 5-6 sesame seeds or charcoal capsules and note when they appear; 12-24 hours is optimal

## Books

- **"Gut" by Giulia Enders** — accessible and engaging overview of how the digestive system works; good starting point for understanding gut health
- **"The Good Gut" by Justin and Erica Sonnenburg** — research-backed guide to the microbiome, fiber, and how diet shapes gut health; written by Stanford microbiome researchers
