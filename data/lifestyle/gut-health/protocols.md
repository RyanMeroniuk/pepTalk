# Gut & Digestive Health

## Protocols

## Foundation

- **30+ different plants per week** — diversity in plant foods feeds diversity in the microbiome; count vegetables, fruits, herbs, spices, nuts, seeds, legumes, and whole grains as individual plants
- **6+ servings of fermented foods weekly** — yogurt, kefir, sauerkraut, kimchi, kombucha, miso, and tempeh introduce beneficial organisms and their metabolites into the gut
- **25-40g fiber daily** — ramp up slowly (increase by 5g per week) to avoid bloating and gas; sudden large increases overwhelm the microbiome's capacity to ferment
- **Eat in a calm, parasympathetic state** — digestion requires the "rest and digest" branch of the nervous system; eating while stressed, rushing, or distracted impairs enzyme production and motility
- **Chew thoroughly** — digestion starts in the mouth with salivary amylase and mechanical breakdown; inadequate chewing puts extra burden on the stomach and small intestine

## Gut Healing Protocol (for Leaky Gut, IBS, IBD)

- **Peptides** — BPC-157 oral (500-750mcg daily) for gut lining repair combined with KPV (200-500mcg daily) for intestinal inflammation reduction
- **L-Glutamine** — 5-10g daily, the primary fuel for enterocytes (gut lining cells); supports barrier repair
- **Zinc Carnosine** — 75mg twice daily; specifically repairs gastric and intestinal lining
- **Probiotics** — strain-specific selection based on condition; Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG for general gut health, Saccharomyces boulardii for antibiotic recovery and diarrhea
- **Collagen** — 10-15g daily; provides glycine and proline for gut lining repair
- **Eliminate known triggers** — gluten (if sensitive), dairy (if sensitive), alcohol, NSAIDs, and processed foods; these are the most common gut barrier disruptors
- **Bone broth daily** — 2 cups; rich in glycine, proline, glutamine, and gelatin that support intestinal lining repair
- **Aloe vera juice** — 2-4oz before meals; soothes the gut lining and supports mucosal healing
- **Low-FODMAP if SIBO suspected** — follow for 2-6 weeks only as a diagnostic and symptom-reduction tool; this is not a long-term diet and restricts beneficial prebiotic fibers
- **Meal spacing** — 4-5 hours between meals to allow the migrating motor complex (MMC) to activate and sweep the small intestine

## Maintenance

- **Diverse whole food diet** — prioritize variety over perfection; a wide range of minimally processed foods supports a resilient microbiome
- **Probiotics or fermented foods daily** — maintain microbial diversity with consistent exposure to beneficial organisms
- **Manage stress** — chronic stress elevates cortisol, which increases gut permeability, reduces beneficial bacteria, and impairs digestion; daily stress management is a gut health practice
- **Regular bowel movements** — 1-2 well-formed bowel movements daily is the target; transit time of 12-24 hours is optimal
- **Hydration** — adequate water intake supports mucosal lining integrity and healthy transit time
