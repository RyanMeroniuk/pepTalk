# Gut & Digestive Health

## Why Gut Health Matters

- **The gut is the center of health** — it is not just a digestive organ but a complex system that influences immunity, mood, cognition, and systemic inflammation
- **70% of the immune system lives in the gut** — the gut-associated lymphoid tissue (GALT) is the largest immune organ in the body, making gut health and immune health inseparable
- **Serotonin is primarily produced in the gut** — approximately 90% of the body's serotonin is made by enterochromaffin cells in the intestinal lining, directly linking gut health to mood and mental health
- **Nutrient absorption depends on gut integrity** — a damaged or inflamed gut lining cannot properly absorb vitamins, minerals, amino acids, or fatty acids, undermining every other health intervention

## Gut Barrier Integrity

- **"Leaky gut" is a real physiological phenomenon** — increased intestinal permeability allows partially digested food particles, bacteria, and toxins to cross the gut lining into the bloodstream
- **Systemic inflammation begins in the gut** — when the barrier is compromised, the immune system reacts to these foreign particles, driving chronic low-grade inflammation throughout the body
- **Tight junctions are the gatekeepers** — these protein structures between intestinal cells regulate what passes through the gut wall; when they loosen, permeability increases

## Peptides That Target the Gut

- **BPC-157** — originates from gastric juice; the most studied peptide for gut healing, ulcer repair, and barrier restoration
- **KPV** — an anti-inflammatory tripeptide that reduces intestinal inflammation via NF-kB inhibition
- **Larazotide** — directly regulates tight junctions by blocking the zonulin pathway, addressing the mechanism of leaky gut
- **VIP (Vasoactive Intestinal Peptide)** — supports gut health, reduces inflammation, and is critical in CIRS/mold illness gut recovery

## The Absorption Connection

- **Gut health determines supplement effectiveness** — if your gut is compromised, you are not absorbing the supplements you are taking, regardless of quality or dose
- **Gut health affects peptide response** — oral peptides like BPC-157 depend on gut function for delivery; overall health and inflammation levels influence how well any intervention works
