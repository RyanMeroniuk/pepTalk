# Gut & Digestive Health

## Common Mistakes

- **Taking antibiotics without probiotic support** — antibiotics do not discriminate between pathogenic and beneficial bacteria; always take strain-specific probiotics (Saccharomyces boulardii is antibiotic-resistant) during and for 4-8 weeks after antibiotic courses to support recovery
- **Chronic NSAID use without gut protection** — ibuprofen, naproxen, and aspirin damage the gut lining with regular use, increasing intestinal permeability; if chronic NSAID use is necessary, discuss gut protection strategies with your doctor
- **Eating when stressed** — the sympathetic ("fight or flight") nervous system shuts down digestive processes; eating in a stressed state leads to poor enzyme production, reduced motility, bloating, and incomplete digestion
- **Not enough fiber diversity** — eating the same foods every day feeds the same bacterial species while others starve; aim for 30+ different plant foods per week to cultivate microbial diversity
- **Staying on restrictive diets too long** — low-FODMAP, elimination diets, and carnivore diets are therapeutic tools meant to be temporary; long-term restriction reduces microbiome diversity, which creates new problems
- **Ignoring food sensitivities** — continuing to eat foods that trigger immune reactions (even low-grade ones) perpetuates gut inflammation and prevents healing; an elimination diet followed by systematic reintroduction is the gold standard for identifying triggers
- **Snacking constantly** — the migrating motor complex (MMC) only activates during fasting; constant eating prevents the gut's natural cleansing wave, contributing to bacterial overgrowth and sluggish motility
- **Over-supplementing probiotics without dietary fiber** — probiotics need prebiotic fiber as food to colonize and thrive; taking probiotics without adequate fiber is like planting seeds in soil with no nutrients
- **Ignoring the stress-gut connection** — chronic psychological stress is one of the most potent disruptors of gut health, increasing permeability, reducing microbial diversity, and impairing motility; no supplement protocol can overcome unmanaged chronic stress
