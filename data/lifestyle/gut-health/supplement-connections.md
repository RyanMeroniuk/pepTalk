# Gut & Digestive Health

## Supplement Connections

- **L-Glutamine** (5-10g daily) — the primary fuel source for enterocytes, the cells that line the intestinal wall; supplementation supports gut barrier repair and reduces intestinal permeability; take on an empty stomach for best absorption; commonly used alongside BPC-157 in gut healing protocols
- **Zinc Carnosine** (75mg twice daily) — a compound that specifically binds to damaged areas of the gastric and intestinal lining, promoting localized repair; shown in research to stabilize the gut mucosa and reduce inflammation; particularly useful for those with gastritis or NSAID-induced damage
- **Probiotics** — strain selection matters; Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG is the most studied general-purpose strain for gut health; Saccharomyces boulardii is a beneficial yeast that survives antibiotic use and is effective for antibiotic-associated diarrhea and C. difficile prevention; Bifidobacterium infantis is effective for IBS symptoms
- **Collagen** (10-15g daily) — provides the amino acids glycine, proline, and hydroxyproline that are building blocks for gut lining repair; hydrolyzed collagen peptides are the most bioavailable form; pairs well with vitamin C which is required for collagen synthesis
- **Omega-3 Fatty Acids** (2-4g EPA+DHA daily) — anti-inflammatory effects that support gut barrier integrity; reduce the production of pro-inflammatory cytokines in the intestinal mucosa; choose a high-quality triglyceride-form fish oil or algae-based DHA
- **Berberine** (500mg 2-3 times daily) — a plant alkaloid with antimicrobial properties that can help rebalance gut flora and address pathogenic overgrowth; also has metabolic benefits (blood sugar regulation); start with a low dose as it can cause GI upset during the initial adjustment period
- **Turmeric / Curcumin** (500-1000mg daily with piperine or in liposomal form) — reduces gut inflammation through multiple pathways including NF-kB inhibition; poor bioavailability requires enhanced formulations (liposomal, phytosomal, or combined with black pepper extract)
- **Vitamin D3** (2,000-5,000 IU daily) — supports gut immune function and barrier integrity; vitamin D receptors are abundant in intestinal epithelial cells; deficiency is associated with increased intestinal permeability and higher rates of inflammatory bowel disease
