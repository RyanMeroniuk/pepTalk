# Hydration & Electrolytes

## Resources

### Water Bottles

- **Hydro Flask** — Double-wall vacuum insulated stainless steel, keeps water cold 24 hours
- **Nalgene** — Durable BPA-free Tritan plastic, wide mouth, measurement markings
- **Glass bottles** — Ello, Lifefactory, or any silicone-sleeved glass bottle for zero chemical leaching
- **Recommendation** — Avoid single-use plastic bottles entirely; invest in one quality reusable bottle

### Electrolyte Products

- **LMNT** — 1000mg sodium, 200mg potassium, 60mg magnesium; no sugar, clean ingredients
- **Liquid IV** — Cellular Transport Technology (CTT) for rapid absorption; contains some sugar
- **Drip Drop** — Medical-grade oral rehydration solution, used in clinical settings
- **Re-Lyte** — Real Salt brand, balanced electrolytes with trace minerals, no artificial ingredients
- **Nuun** — Effervescent tablets, low calorie, convenient for travel and on-the-go

### Tracking Tools

- **WaterMinder app** — Visual tracking with reminders and Apple Health integration
- **Plant Nanny** — Gamified hydration tracker where you grow a virtual plant by drinking water
- **Simple tally marks** — Low-tech but effective: mark each 16 oz serving on a sticky note or whiteboard

### Water Filters

- **Berkey** — Gravity-fed countertop filter, removes bacteria, viruses, heavy metals, and chemicals
- **AquaTru** — Countertop reverse osmosis system, no installation required, NSF-certified
- **Under-sink RO systems** — iSpring, APEC, or Home Master for whole-kitchen filtered water
- **Pitcher filters** — Clearly Filtered or Brita (basic) for entry-level filtration

### Testing

- **TDS meter** — Measures total dissolved solids in water; inexpensive way to verify filter performance
- **Home water quality test kits** — Tap Score, SimpleLab, or hardware store test strips for basic screening
- **Municipal water report** — Free from your water provider; legally required to publish annually (search "[your city] water quality report")

### Metrics to Track

- **Daily intake** — Total ounces consumed per day
- **Urine color** — Pale straw is the target
- **Body weight consistency** — Sudden drops often indicate dehydration, not fat loss
- **Energy and headache patterns** — Often the first signs of chronic mild dehydration
