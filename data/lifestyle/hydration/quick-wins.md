# Hydration & Electrolytes

## Quick Wins

1. **Drink 16 oz of water right when you wake up tomorrow** — You lose 1-2 lbs of water overnight through breathing and perspiration. Rehydrating first thing kickstarts your metabolism and cognitive function
2. **Get a 32 oz water bottle and aim to fill it 2-3x per day** — Having a dedicated, measured bottle removes guesswork and makes tracking simple
3. **Add a pinch of sea salt to your morning water** — This single addition improves water absorption at the cellular level and supports adrenal function after overnight fasting
4. **Set 3 hydration reminders on your phone** — Place them at mid-morning, mid-afternoon, and early evening. Especially important if you take GLP-1 agonists or any appetite-suppressing peptide
5. **Check your urine color today** — Aim for pale straw. If it is clear, you may be flushing electrolytes. If it is dark yellow or amber, you need more fluids immediately
