# Nutrition & Eating Patterns

## Resources

- **Tracking Apps** — MyFitnessPal (largest food database, easy barcode scanning), Cronometer (superior micronutrient tracking, shows vitamin and mineral intake), MacroFactor (algorithm-adjusted calorie targets based on real weight trends)
- **Protein Calculator** — multiply your body weight in kilograms by 1.6 for the minimum target and 2.2 for the upper target. Example: 80kg person needs 128-176g protein per day. For pounds, multiply by 0.7-1.0. Aim for the higher end when in a caloric deficit or using GH peptides.
- **Meal Prep Strategy** — batch cook proteins on Sunday for the entire week. Grill 2-3 lbs of chicken breast, hard boil a dozen eggs, portion Greek yogurt into containers, cook a large batch of ground turkey. Having protein ready to eat eliminates the friction that leads to poor choices.
- **GLP-1 Specific Resources** — bariatric-style cookbooks and meal plans are designed for exactly the constraints GLP-1 users face: small portions, high protein density, easy digestibility, and maximum nutrients per bite. Search for "bariatric sleeve meal plans" for practical templates.
- **Books** — "The Renaissance Diet 2.0" by Mike Israetel (evidence-based nutrition for body composition, protein science), "Outlive" by Peter Attia (longevity-focused nutrition and metabolic health), "The Obesity Code" by Jason Fung (insulin, fasting, and metabolic dysfunction)
