# Nutrition & Eating Patterns

## Why Nutrition Is Foundational

- **Protein is the universal recommendation** across virtually every peptide protocol — whether you're using growth hormone secretagogues, healing peptides, or weight loss drugs, adequate protein intake is non-negotiable
- **Eating patterns change dramatically on GLP-1 drugs** like semaglutide, tirzepatide, and retatrutide — appetite drops significantly, gastric emptying slows, and food tolerance shifts, requiring a completely different approach to meals
- **Anti-inflammatory eating is the default baseline** for anyone using peptides — chronic inflammation undermines healing peptides, immune peptides, gut peptides, and cognitive peptides alike
- **Nutrient density matters more than calorie counting** — when appetite is reduced or you're optimizing for recovery and performance, every bite needs to deliver maximum micronutrients
- **Food timing interacts directly with peptide protocols** — growth hormone peptides require fasted administration, and what you eat around injection windows affects efficacy
- **The gut is a pharmacy** — your microbiome influences inflammation, neurotransmitter production, immune function, and nutrient absorption, all of which determine how well your peptides work
- **Undereating is as dangerous as overeating** — especially on GLP-1 drugs, inadequate nutrition leads to muscle loss, micronutrient deficiency, hair loss, and metabolic adaptation that stalls progress
