# Nutrition & Eating Patterns

## Quick Wins

- **Eat protein first at your next meal** — before touching carbs, vegetables, or fats, eat the protein portion of your plate first. This ensures you hit your protein target even if you get full, and it blunts the glycemic response of the rest of the meal.
- **Buy Greek yogurt, eggs, and chicken breast on your next grocery run** — these three foods cover the highest-leverage protein sources for cost, convenience, and versatility. Greek yogurt (plain, full-fat or 2%) has 15-20g protein per serving. Eggs have 6g each. Chicken breast has 30g per serving.
- **Track protein for one day** — use MyFitnessPal or Cronometer to log everything you eat for a single day. Most people are shocked to find they are eating 60-80g of protein when they need 120-160g. This one day of awareness changes behavior permanently.
- **Replace one processed snack with a whole food this week** — swap chips, crackers, or candy for a handful of almonds, a hard-boiled egg, beef jerky, or an apple with almond butter. One swap, sustained consistently, compounds over months.
- **If on a GLP-1, sip fluids between meals, not during** — this single habit change dramatically reduces nausea and allows you to eat more nutrient-dense food at meals. Keep a water bottle with you and drink steadily between eating windows.
