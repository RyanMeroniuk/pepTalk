# Nutrition & Eating Patterns

## Common Mistakes

- **Not eating enough protein** — this is the number one mistake across all peptide users. Most people dramatically overestimate their protein intake. Track it for even one day and the shortfall becomes obvious. Without adequate protein, GH peptides cannot build muscle, healing peptides lack raw materials, and GLP-1 users lose muscle instead of fat.
- **Eating too little on GLP-1 drugs** — appetite suppression feels like a superpower at first, but dropping below 1200 calories per day leads to muscle wasting, hair loss, micronutrient deficiency, metabolic adaptation, and eventual weight loss stalls. The goal is fat loss, not starvation.
- **Skipping meals entirely instead of eating smaller meals** — on GLP-1 drugs especially, going long periods without eating and then attempting a normal-sized meal guarantees nausea. Four to six small protein-rich meals is the correct approach.
- **Drinking lots of liquid with meals on GLP-1s** — water fills already-limited stomach capacity and worsens nausea. Drink fluids between meals instead of during them.
- **Eating high-fat and greasy food on GLP-1s** — fried foods, heavy cream sauces, and greasy takeout cause severe nausea and gastrointestinal distress because gastric emptying is already slowed by 30-50%.
- **Thinking "clean eating" means low protein** — salads, fruit bowls, and grain bowls look healthy but often contain only 10-15g of protein. Clean eating without protein targets is just expensive calorie restriction.
- **Fear of carbohydrates** — carbs support training performance, recovery, sleep quality (via serotonin production), and thyroid function. Cutting them entirely while on peptide protocols that demand physical activity is counterproductive.
- **Ignoring protein timing and distribution** — eating 150g of protein in one meal and nothing the rest of the day is far less effective than spreading 40g across four meals. Each meal is a separate muscle protein synthesis event.
- **Not supplementing when appetite is suppressed** — when you are eating 1200-1500 calories on a GLP-1 drug, it is nearly impossible to get adequate micronutrients from food alone. A multivitamin, omega-3, magnesium, and electrolytes are essential gap-fillers.
