# Nutrition & Eating Patterns

## Supplement Connections

- **Collagen Peptides (10-15g daily)** — must be paired with Vitamin C (at least 50mg) to enable proper collagen synthesis. Take 30-60 minutes before exercise for targeted tendon, ligament, and joint support. Type I and III collagen for skin and tendons, Type II for cartilage. Pairs well with BPC-157 and GHK-Cu for comprehensive tissue repair.
- **L-Glutamine (5-10g daily)** — the primary fuel source for intestinal epithelial cells. Supports gut barrier integrity and repair, especially important on anti-inflammatory protocols and when using gut-healing peptides like BPC-157 and KPV. Take on an empty stomach or mixed into a protein shake.
- **Omega-3 Fish Oil (2-4g combined EPA/DHA daily)** — the foundational anti-inflammatory supplement. Corrects the omega-6/omega-3 imbalance that drives chronic inflammation. Supports brain health for nootropic peptide users, reduces neuroinflammation, and provides the substrate for anti-inflammatory resolvins and protectins. Choose a high-quality triglyceride-form product and store refrigerated.
- **Probiotics (multi-strain, 10+ billion CFU)** — supports microbiome diversity and the gut-brain axis. Different strains serve different purposes: Lactobacillus rhamnosus for gut barrier, Bifidobacterium longum for mood, Saccharomyces boulardii for antibiotic recovery. Rotate strains periodically. Pairs with fermented foods for maximum diversity.
- **Zinc Carnosine (75mg twice daily)** — specifically studied for gut lining repair and mucosal defense. Reduces H. pylori colonization and supports gastric integrity. Particularly relevant for users on BPC-157 or KPV gut-healing protocols. Take with meals.
- **Berberine (500mg, 2-3x daily with meals)** — activates AMPK similarly to metformin, supporting blood sugar regulation and insulin sensitivity. Relevant for metabolic peptide users (semaglutide, tirzepatide, MOTS-c). Do not combine with metformin without medical guidance due to additive blood sugar lowering effects. Take with meals to reduce GI side effects.
- **Chromium Picolinate (200-1000mcg daily)** — supports glucose metabolism and insulin receptor sensitivity. A useful adjunct for metabolic and weight loss peptide protocols. Modest but consistent evidence for improving glycemic control.
- **Protein Powder (whey isolate or casein, 25-50g per serving)** — insurance for hitting daily protein targets, especially critical for GLP-1 drug users whose appetite is severely suppressed. Whey isolate for fast absorption post-workout, casein for slow release before bed. Greek yogurt and cottage cheese serve as whole-food alternatives with similar protein profiles.
