# Nutrition & Eating Patterns

## Peptide Connections

- **Semaglutide / Tirzepatide / Retatrutide** — these GLP-1 receptor agonists slow gastric emptying by 30-50% and dramatically suppress appetite. This demands a protein-first approach at every meal, smaller and more frequent meals (4-6 per day), easy-to-digest food choices, and separation of liquids from solid food. Without deliberate protein prioritization, muscle loss accounts for up to 40% of total weight lost.
- **CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin / Sermorelin / GHRP-2 / GHRP-6 / Hexarelin** — all growth hormone secretagogues must be injected in a fasted state. Carbohydrates and fats cause insulin release, which directly blunts growth hormone secretion. No food for 2 hours before injection and 30 minutes after. Protein intake throughout the rest of the day fuels the muscle growth and recovery that elevated GH enables.
- **MK-677 (Ibutamoren)** — significantly increases appetite, especially in the first few weeks. This is an opportunity to hit high protein targets and support muscle growth, not a license to eat junk food. Channel the hunger toward protein-rich whole foods. Take at night if daytime appetite becomes unmanageable.
- **BPC-157 / KPV** — both are gut-healing peptides whose effectiveness depends heavily on dietary environment. An anti-inflammatory diet (no seed oils, no processed food, high omega-3, fermented foods) creates the conditions for these peptides to repair the gut lining. Eating inflammatory foods while taking gut-healing peptides is like mopping the floor while the faucet is still running.
- **MOTS-c** — this mitochondrial peptide activates AMPK, the same pathway activated by fasting and exercise. Pairing MOTS-c with intermittent fasting (16:8) and an anti-inflammatory diet creates synergistic metabolic effects. MOTS-c plus fasting is a powerful combination for metabolic optimization.
- **Semax / Selank** — nootropic and anxiolytic peptides work better when the brain has adequate raw materials. Omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA) support neuronal membrane fluidity and reduce neuroinflammation. Choline (from eggs, liver, or supplementation) supports acetylcholine production. B vitamins support methylation and neurotransmitter synthesis. Brain-supporting nutrition amplifies the cognitive effects of these peptides.
- **Tesamorelin** — FDA-approved for visceral fat reduction, but dietary context matters. A moderate caloric deficit with high protein preserves lean mass while tesamorelin targets visceral adipose tissue specifically. Excess sugar and refined carbohydrates drive visceral fat accumulation and work against the peptide.
- **AOD-9604** — a modified growth hormone fragment that targets fat metabolism. Unlike full GH, it does not require fasted administration, but an anti-inflammatory, protein-adequate diet supports the metabolic environment in which AOD-9604 operates most effectively.
