# Sunlight & Light Exposure

## Resources

### Light Therapy Lamps

- **Carex Day-Light Classic** — 10,000 lux at therapeutic distance, large surface area, adjustable height and angle. One of the most clinically validated light therapy devices
- **HappyLight by Verilux** — Compact 10,000 lux option, good for desk use, more affordable entry point
- **Usage** — Position 16-24 inches from your face for 20-30 minutes each morning during winter months or low-light seasons

### Blue Light Blocking Glasses

- **Ra Optics** — Premium amber/orange lenses that block 100% of blue and green light below 550 nm. Day and night lens options
- **BLUblox** — Australian brand with sleep (amber), summer (yellow), and screen (clear) lens options
- **Swanwick** — Night Swannies with orange lenses for evening use
- **Important note** — Look for amber or orange lenses specifically. Clear "blue light" glasses marketed by most eyewear brands block only 10-20% of blue light, which is insufficient to protect melatonin production

### Apps and Software

- **f.lux** — Free desktop app (Mac/Windows/Linux) that automatically adjusts screen color temperature based on time of day
- **Night Shift** — Built into iOS and macOS; activates warm screen tones on a schedule or at sunset
- **Android night mode** — Built-in on most Android devices; reduces blue light emission after a set time
- **Screen brightness** — Manually reduce to the lowest comfortable level in the evening, regardless of app filters

### Vitamin D Testing

- **Quest Diagnostics** — Standard lab draw for 25-hydroxyvitamin D, typically covered by insurance with a doctor's order
- **Everlywell home test** — At-home finger-prick test kit for vitamin D levels, results in days
- **Ask your doctor** — Request 25-OH vitamin D on your next routine blood panel. Most doctors will add it without issue

### Light Measurement

- **Lux meter app** — Free apps available for iOS and Android that use the phone's light sensor to measure approximate lux. Compare indoor vs outdoor readings to understand the magnitude difference
- **Dedicated lux meter** — Dr. Meter or UNI-T handheld lux meters for precise measurements (under $25)

### Books and Further Reading

- **"The Circadian Code" by Satchin Panda** — Comprehensive guide to circadian biology and practical protocols for light, eating, and sleep timing
- **"Why We Sleep" by Matthew Walker** — Covers light's role in sleep architecture and the consequences of circadian disruption
- **Huberman Lab podcast** — Episodes on light exposure, circadian rhythm, and dopamine/cortisol timing are freely available and highly detailed
