# Sunlight & Light Exposure

## Supplement Connections

- **Vitamin D3 (4,000-5,000 IU daily)** — The sun-replacement supplement for when adequate exposure is not possible. Essential during winter months at northern latitudes, for people with darker skin tones, and for anyone with an indoor-dominant lifestyle. Take with a fat-containing meal for optimal absorption
- **Vitamin K2 (MK-7 form, 100-200 mcg daily)** — Must be paired with vitamin D3 supplementation. Vitamin D increases calcium absorption from the gut; vitamin K2 directs that calcium into bones and teeth rather than depositing it in arteries and soft tissues. Without K2, high-dose D3 can contribute to arterial calcification
- **Magnesium** — A required cofactor for vitamin D metabolism. The enzymes that convert vitamin D into its active form (calcitriol) are magnesium-dependent. Supplementing vitamin D without adequate magnesium can deplete magnesium stores and leave vitamin D inactive. Magnesium glycinate or L-threonate are well-absorbed forms
- **Omega-3 fatty acids** — Support skin health and UV resilience by reducing inflammation and supporting cell membrane integrity. EPA and DHA from fish oil help protect skin from UV-induced oxidative damage while maintaining the skin's barrier function
- **Melatonin (0.3-1 mg, low dose)** — A backup for evenings when light exposure management has not been optimal. Use the lowest effective dose 30-60 minutes before bed. This is not a replacement for proper light hygiene but can help on travel days, late-screen nights, or during circadian disruption
