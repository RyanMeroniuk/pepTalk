# Environmental & Toxin Avoidance

## Overview

The body can't heal and optimize while being constantly poisoned. Alcohol is the #1 substance that undermines peptide protocols (mentioned in nearly every peptide's "things to avoid"). Beyond alcohol: endocrine disruptors, mold, pesticides, household chemicals all create inflammatory burden. Reducing toxic load amplifies everything else you're doing.

- **Alcohol** — The single biggest saboteur of peptide protocols, disrupting sleep, gut health, hormones, and liver function
- **Endocrine disruptors** — BPA, phthalates, parabens found in plastics, personal care products, and food packaging
- **Mold and mycotoxins** — Hidden environmental toxins that trigger chronic inflammatory responses
- **Pesticides and herbicides** — Chemical residues on food and in the environment that disrupt gut and neurological health
- **Household chemicals** — Cleaning products, air fresheners, and VOCs from furniture and building materials
- **Key principle** — You can't out-supplement or out-peptide a toxic environment; reducing exposure comes first
