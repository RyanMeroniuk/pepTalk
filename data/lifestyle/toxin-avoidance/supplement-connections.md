# Environmental & Toxin Avoidance

## Supplement Connections

- **NAC (N-Acetyl Cysteine)** — Glutathione precursor, supports liver detoxification pathways, mold detox support. Dose: 600-1200mg daily.
- **Vitamin C (1000mg+)** — Antioxidant that supports Phase I and Phase II detoxification pathways. Helps neutralize free radicals generated by toxin exposure.
- **Glutathione** — Master antioxidant and primary detoxification molecule. Liposomal form for oral supplementation provides better bioavailability than standard oral glutathione.
- **Activated Charcoal** — Binder for mycotoxins and other environmental toxins in the GI tract. Take away from other supplements and medications (2 hours apart) as it binds indiscriminately.
- **Chlorella/Spirulina** — Heavy metal chelation support. Chlorella binds mercury, lead, and cadmium in the gut. Spirulina provides additional antioxidant and immune support.
- **Milk Thistle** — Liver support via its active compound silymarin. Protects hepatocytes from toxic damage and supports liver regeneration.
- **Omega-3** — Reduces systemic inflammation caused by toxic exposure. EPA and DHA help resolve inflammatory cascades triggered by environmental toxins.
- **Probiotics** — Gut microbiome resilience against environmental toxins. Diverse strains help maintain gut barrier integrity compromised by alcohol, pesticides, and other toxins.
