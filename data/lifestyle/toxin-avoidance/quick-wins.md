# Environmental & Toxin Avoidance

## Quick Wins

1. **Skip alcohol for the next 7 days** and notice how your sleep improves
2. **Replace one plastic food container with glass** — start with the ones you use most often
3. **Check under your sinks for water damage or mold** — look for discoloration, warping, or musty smell
4. **Switch to a stainless steel or glass water bottle** — stop drinking from plastic throughout the day
5. **Download the EWG Skin Deep app** and scan your personal care products to see what you're putting on your skin daily
