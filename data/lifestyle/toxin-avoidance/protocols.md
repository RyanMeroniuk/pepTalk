# Environmental & Toxin Avoidance

## Protocols

### Alcohol

- **Ideal** — Eliminate completely during any peptide protocol
- **Minimum** — Limit to 1-2 drinks/week maximum, never on injection days
- **Why it matters** — Alcohol destroys sleep, elevates cortisol, damages gut lining, suppresses GH release, impairs liver function
- **If you drink** — Avoid for 24 hours around GH peptide injections, avoid entirely during gut healing protocols

### Endocrine Disruptors

- **Food storage** — Switch to glass/stainless steel food containers (no plastic)
- **Microwaving** — Never microwave food in plastic
- **Water** — Filter drinking water (RO or carbon block)
- **Personal care** — Switch to clean personal care products (EWG Skin Deep database)
- **Receipts** — Avoid receipts (thermal paper contains BPA)
- **Produce** — Choose organic for the "Dirty Dozen" produce at minimum

### Mold & Mycotoxins

- **Water damage** — Fix water damage immediately
- **Humidity** — Keep humidity below 50%
- **Air quality** — HEPA air purifier in bedroom
- **Testing** — If symptoms of CIRS: get tested (C4a, TGF-beta-1, MMP-9, VIP, MSH)
- **Treatment** — VIP peptide is a key treatment in Shoemaker protocol for mold illness

### Household

- **Cleaning products** — Switch to natural cleaning products (vinegar, baking soda, or brands like Branch Basics)
- **Ventilation** — Open windows regularly for ventilation
- **Shoes** — Remove shoes at the door (tracks in pesticides, heavy metals)
- **Vacuuming** — HEPA vacuum instead of regular vacuum

### Tobacco/Nicotine

- **Complete avoidance** — Non-negotiable. Damages every system these peptides are trying to optimize.
