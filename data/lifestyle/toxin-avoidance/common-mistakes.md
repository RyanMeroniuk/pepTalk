# Environmental & Toxin Avoidance

## Common Mistakes

- **"I only drink on weekends"** — Still disrupts sleep 2-3 nights/week and elevates cortisol. Weekend drinking undermines the entire week's recovery and peptide benefits.
- **Thinking "organic" means pesticide-free** — It doesn't, but it is significantly lower. Organic certification allows certain approved pesticides; the key benefit is avoiding synthetic pesticides and herbicides like glyphosate.
- **Ignoring mold because you "can't see it"** — Mold grows behind walls, in HVAC systems, under sinks, and in crawl spaces. Musty smell or unexplained chronic symptoms warrant professional testing.
- **Using "natural" cleaning products that still contain harmful chemicals** — Read labels carefully. Marketing terms like "natural," "green," and "eco-friendly" are not regulated. Check the EWG Guide to Healthy Cleaning for verified products.
- **Storing food in plastic and microwaving in plastic** — Heat dramatically increases leaching of BPA, phthalates, and other chemicals from plastic into food. Even "microwave-safe" plastic still leaches chemicals.
- **Not filtering water** — "City water is safe" is misleading. Municipal water contains chlorine, chloramines, fluoride, microplastics, and pharmaceutical residues that pass through treatment. A quality filter removes the majority of these contaminants.
- **Using scented candles and air fresheners** — These release VOCs and synthetic fragrances that contain phthalates and other endocrine disruptors. They add to indoor air pollution rather than improving air quality.
- **Thinking moderate alcohol is healthy** — The "red wine is good for you" myth has been debunked. Recent large-scale studies show no safe level of alcohol consumption. Any resveratrol benefits are negligible compared to the harm from ethanol.
