# Environmental & Toxin Avoidance

## Peptide Connections

- **BPC-157** — Counteracts alcohol-induced gastric damage, but that doesn't mean you should drink on it. Alcohol still undermines the broader healing process BPC-157 is supporting.
- **VIP** — Primary treatment peptide for CIRS/mold illness in the Shoemaker protocol. VIP levels are often suppressed in mold-exposed individuals, and exogenous VIP helps restore regulatory function.
- **LL-37** — Antimicrobial peptide that supports innate immune defense against environmental pathogens. Reducing toxic burden lets LL-37 focus on actual threats rather than fighting constant inflammation.
- **CJC-1295/Ipamorelin/All GH peptides** — Alcohol suppresses GH release for up to 24 hours. Drinking on GH peptide protocols is essentially wasting your investment.
- **DSIP/Sleep peptides** — Alcohol destroys sleep architecture, completely negating the benefits of sleep optimization peptides. Even one drink can suppress REM sleep.
- **Thymosin Alpha-1/Immune peptides** — Toxic burden suppresses immune function. Reducing exposure allows immune-modulating peptides to work more effectively.
- **Semaglutide** — Alcohol combined with GLP-1 agonists increases the risk of pancreatitis and liver damage. This is a particularly dangerous combination.
- **Semax/Selank** — Alcohol is neurotoxic and directly counteracts neuroprotective peptides. You cannot protect the brain while simultaneously poisoning it.
- **Kisspeptin** — Endocrine disruptors interfere with reproductive hormone signaling, working against kisspeptin's role in gonadotropin release and fertility support.
