# Temperature Therapy

## Supplement Connections

- **Electrolytes** — Critical for sauna use. Sweating depletes sodium, potassium, and magnesium. Replenish before and after every session with a quality electrolyte mix (not sugary sports drinks).
- **Magnesium (400mg)** — Replenish after sauna sessions. Magnesium is lost through sweat and is essential for muscle relaxation, sleep, and over 300 enzymatic reactions.
- **Omega-3** — Supports the anti-inflammatory benefits of cold exposure. EPA and DHA help resolve inflammation through specialized pro-resolving mediators.
- **CoQ10** — Mitochondrial support alongside cold-induced mitochondrial biogenesis via PGC-1alpha. Ensures mitochondria have the cofactors they need to function optimally.
- **NMN** — NAD+ support for the cellular stress response pathways activated by both cold and heat. Helps fuel sirtuins and PARPs involved in the hormetic response.
- **Vitamin C** — Antioxidant support for the hormetic stress response. Helps manage the transient oxidative stress generated by temperature extremes.
- **Creatine** — Heat exposure may increase creatine kinase levels. Ensure adequate creatine levels to support cellular energy demands during temperature therapy.
