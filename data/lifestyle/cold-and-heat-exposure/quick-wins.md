# Temperature Therapy

## Quick Wins

1. **End your next shower with 30 seconds of cold water** — just try it once and notice how you feel afterward
2. **If you have gym access, try the sauna for 15 minutes** after your next workout
3. **Practice controlled breathing during the cold** — in through nose, slow out through mouth. The discomfort is the point.
4. **Aim for 2 cold exposure sessions this week**, even if they are just cold showers
5. **Hydrate with electrolytes before and after** any temperature therapy session
