# Temperature Therapy

## Protocols

### Cold Exposure — Beginner

- **Method** — End your regular shower with 30 seconds of cold water
- **Progression** — Gradually increase to 1-2 minutes over weeks
- **Breathing** — Focus on controlled breathing through the discomfort (in through nose, slow out through mouth)

### Cold Exposure — Intermediate

- **Duration** — 2-5 minutes of cold shower (as cold as it goes)
- **Cold plunge/ice bath** — 50-59F (10-15C) for 2-5 minutes
- **Frequency** — 2-4 sessions per week
- **Timing** — Morning cold exposure for alertness and dopamine

### Cold Exposure — Advanced

- **Weekly target** — 11 minutes total per week of deliberate cold exposure (Huberman protocol)
- **Temperature** — Ice bath at 38-50F (3-10C) for 1-3 minutes
- **Post-workout** — End workouts with cold for inflammation reduction (controversial for hypertrophy — may blunt muscle growth signal if done immediately after resistance training)

### Sauna — Beginner

- **Duration** — 15-20 minutes at 150-175F (traditional) or 130-150F (infrared)
- **Frequency** — 2x per week
- **Hydration** — Hydrate before and after (electrolytes)

### Sauna — Intermediate/Advanced

- **Duration** — 20-30 minutes per session
- **Frequency** — 3-7x per week (Finnish studies showed 4-7x optimal)
- **Temperature** — 175-210F traditional, 130-170F infrared
- **Cool down** — Finish with cool shower (not ice cold if you want to preserve heat benefits)
- **GH protocol** — 3-4 sauna sessions in one day with cooling breaks between sessions produces a massive GH spike (research protocol, not for beginners)

### Contrast Therapy

- **Method** — Alternate hot (sauna or hot tub) to cold (cold plunge) for 3-4 rounds
- **Hot phase** — 3-5 minutes
- **Cold phase** — 1-2 minutes
- **End on cold** — For metabolic benefits
- **End on hot** — For relaxation
