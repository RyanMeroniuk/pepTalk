# Temperature Therapy

## Overview

Deliberate cold and heat exposure trigger powerful adaptive stress responses. Cold activates brown fat, boosts norepinephrine (2-3x), reduces inflammation, and builds mental resilience. Heat (sauna) increases heat shock proteins, improves cardiovascular health, enhances detoxification, and may extend lifespan (Finnish sauna studies). Both are free, accessible, and synergize with peptide protocols.

- **Cold exposure** — Activates brown adipose tissue, dramatically increases norepinephrine and dopamine, reduces systemic inflammation, and builds stress tolerance
- **Heat exposure (sauna)** — Triggers heat shock protein production, mimics cardiovascular exercise, increases growth hormone acutely, and supports detoxification through sweat
- **Hormesis** — Both cold and heat work through hormetic stress: a short, controlled stressor that triggers adaptive responses making the body stronger
- **Accessibility** — A cold shower and gym sauna are all you need to start; no expensive equipment required
- **Synergy with peptides** — Temperature therapy activates many of the same pathways targeted by peptides, creating compounding effects
