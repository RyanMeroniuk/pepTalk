# Temperature Therapy

## Peptide Connections

- **MOTS-c** — Cold exposure activates mitochondrial biogenesis and AMPK, the same pathways as MOTS-c. Powerful synergy for metabolic optimization and fat loss.
- **Epitalon** — Heat shock proteins support cellular repair alongside Epitalon's telomerase activation. Both target longevity at the cellular level.
- **BPC-157/TB-500** — Cold reduces inflammation at injury sites; heat increases blood flow for healing. Use strategically based on the phase of recovery (cold for acute inflammation, heat for later-stage healing and tissue remodeling).
- **CJC-1295/Ipamorelin** — Sauna acutely increases GH release, which can amplify the effects of GH secretagogue peptides. Sauna sessions timed on the same day as peptide dosing may compound GH output.
- **MK-677** — Sauna combined with MK-677 creates an amplified GH response. Both independently raise GH, and together the effect may be synergistic.
- **Thymosin Alpha-1** — Cold exposure boosts immune cell count and activity, complementing Thymosin Alpha-1's immune-modulating effects.
- **Semax** — Cold-induced norepinephrine boost pairs with Semax's dopaminergic effects for enhanced alertness and focus. Morning cold exposure plus Semax is a potent cognitive stack.
- **DSIP** — Evening sauna promotes deep sleep by triggering a core temperature drop afterward, which is a primary sleep signal. This complements DSIP's sleep-regulating effects.
