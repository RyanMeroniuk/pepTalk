# Blood Work & Health Monitoring

## Testing Protocols

## Essential Panel for All Peptide Users

- **Complete Metabolic Panel (CMP)** — glucose, kidney function (BUN, creatinine), liver enzymes (AST, ALT, ALP), electrolytes (sodium, potassium, calcium, chloride); provides a broad snapshot of organ function
- **Complete Blood Count (CBC)** — red blood cells, white blood cells, platelets, hemoglobin, hematocrit; reveals anemia, infection, immune status, and clotting capacity
- **Lipid Panel** — total cholesterol, LDL, HDL, triglycerides; cardiovascular risk assessment
- **Fasting Insulin + Fasting Glucose + HbA1c** — the metabolic health trifecta; catches insulin resistance years before diabetes diagnosis
- **IGF-1** — critical for anyone using GH secretagogues; reflects growth hormone activity
- **Thyroid Panel** — TSH, Free T3, Free T4; thyroid dysfunction affects energy, metabolism, and recovery
- **Vitamin D (25-OH)** — target 40-60 ng/mL; most people are deficient
- **Inflammatory Markers** — hs-CRP and homocysteine; systemic inflammation and methylation status

## Peptide-Specific Add-Ons

- **GH Peptide Users** (CJC-1295, Ipamorelin, MK-677, Sermorelin, Tesamorelin, GHRP-2, GHRP-6, Hexarelin) — add IGF-1 as the primary tracking marker, plus fasting glucose and fasting insulin to monitor for GH-induced insulin resistance
- **GLP-1 Users** (Semaglutide, Tirzepatide, Retatrutide) — add HbA1c, full lipid panel, liver enzymes (ALT, AST), and pancreatic enzymes (lipase, amylase) to monitor for pancreatitis risk
- **Immune Peptide Users** (Thymosin Alpha-1, Thymulin, LL-37) — add CBC with differential and lymphocyte subsets (CD4, CD8, NK cells) to track immune modulation
- **Nootropic Peptide Users** (Semax, Selank, Dihexa, Pinealon) — add B12, folate, and homocysteine to assess methylation and neurological nutrient status

## Optimal Ranges (Functional Medicine)

- **Fasting glucose** — optimal 72-85 mg/dL (lab "normal" goes up to 100 mg/dL)
- **Fasting insulin** — optimal 2-5 uIU/mL (lab "normal" goes up to 25 uIU/mL)
- **HbA1c** — optimal 4.8-5.2% (lab "normal" up to 5.7%)
- **Vitamin D (25-OH)** — optimal 40-60 ng/mL (lab "normal" starts at 30 ng/mL)
- **hs-CRP** — optimal <1.0 mg/L (lab "normal" up to 3.0 mg/L)
- **TSH** — optimal 1.0-2.0 mIU/L (lab "normal" 0.4-4.0 mIU/L)
- **Homocysteine** — optimal <8 umol/L (lab "normal" up to 15 umol/L)
- **Triglycerides** — optimal <100 mg/dL (lab "normal" up to 150 mg/dL)
- **HDL** — optimal >60 mg/dL for men, >70 mg/dL for women

## Testing Frequency

- **Baseline** — get a full panel before starting any peptide protocol; this is your reference point for all future comparisons
- **Initial follow-up** — recheck at 8-12 weeks after starting a new peptide or changing a protocol
- **Maintenance** — every 3-6 months once protocols are stable and markers are trending well
- **Acute changes** — retest 4-6 weeks after any significant protocol change (new peptide, dose adjustment, new supplement)
