# Blood Work & Health Monitoring

## Peptide Connections

- **CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin / MK-677 / Sermorelin / GHRP-2 / GHRP-6 / Hexarelin** — track IGF-1 as the primary marker (should rise into the upper-normal range); monitor fasting glucose and fasting insulin closely because growth hormone can cause insulin resistance; MK-677 in particular is known to elevate fasting glucose
- **Tesamorelin** — track IGF-1, liver enzymes (ALT, AST), and visceral fat via DEXA scan; Tesamorelin is FDA-approved specifically for visceral fat reduction, so body composition testing complements blood work
- **Semaglutide / Tirzepatide** — track HbA1c (should decrease), fasting glucose, full lipid panel (triglycerides should drop significantly), liver enzymes (ALT, AST), and pancreatic enzymes (lipase, amylase) to monitor for pancreatitis risk
- **Retatrutide** — same markers as other GLP-1 agonists plus a liver panel to track hepatic fat clearance; Retatrutide's glucagon receptor activity drives liver fat reduction that shows up in liver enzyme improvements
- **Thymosin Alpha-1** — track CBC with differential and lymphocyte subsets (CD4, CD8, NK cells); the goal is to see improved T-cell populations and balanced immune function
- **BPC-157** — no specific blood markers are required, but inflammatory markers (hs-CRP, ESR) can show indirect evidence of reduced systemic inflammation and improved healing
- **KPV** — similar to BPC-157; hs-CRP and other inflammatory markers may reflect reduced intestinal and systemic inflammation
- **Epitalon** — no established blood tracking markers; telomere length testing is available but expensive, variable between labs, and not yet clinically standardized
- **Semax / Selank / Dihexa / Pinealon** — track B12, folate, and homocysteine to ensure methylation and neurological nutrient support; no direct blood markers for cognitive peptide efficacy
- **PT-141 / Melanotan II** — no specific blood markers required; standard hormone panels (testosterone, estrogen, LH, FSH) can provide context for sexual health peptide use
- **MOTS-c** — track fasting glucose, fasting insulin, and HbA1c as MOTS-c has metabolic and exercise-mimetic effects
- **All peptides** — CMP and CBC serve as the universal baseline panel; every peptide user should have these regardless of which specific peptides they are using
