# Blood Work & Health Monitoring

## Quick Wins

- **Schedule a basic blood panel with your doctor this week** — call your primary care provider or walk into a direct-to-consumer lab and order a panel; the hardest part is making the appointment
- **Ask for the right tests** — request a CMP, CBC, lipid panel, fasting glucose, fasting insulin, HbA1c, vitamin D (25-OH), and TSH; if your doctor will not order fasting insulin, use a direct-to-consumer lab
- **Get the actual numbers** — do not accept "everything looks normal" as a complete answer; request a copy of your results with the numerical values for every marker
- **Write down your results and date** — start a simple spreadsheet or note with each marker, the value, the date, and the reference range; this is the beginning of tracking your trends over time
- **If using GH peptides, add IGF-1 to the order** — this is the single most important marker for evaluating whether your GH secretagogue protocol is working; make sure it is included in your panel
