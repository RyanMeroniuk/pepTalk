# Blood Work & Health Monitoring

## Resources

## Direct-to-Consumer Labs

- **Marek Health** — comprehensive panels designed for optimization, not just disease screening; popular in the peptide and hormone optimization community
- **Quest Diagnostics** — walk-in locations nationwide; can order panels through third-party services like Quest Direct
- **LabCorp** — another major lab network with walk-in availability; partners with many direct-to-consumer ordering services
- **Ulta Lab Tests** — order labs online at discounted prices, draw at Quest locations; no doctor visit required
- **InsideTracker** — integrates blood work with AI-driven recommendations and longitudinal tracking

## At-Home Testing

- **Everlywell** — limited panels available via finger prick at home; convenient but not as comprehensive as a venous draw
- **imaware** — at-home testing kits for specific panels including metabolic health and inflammation

## Tracking Tools

- **Google Sheets** — free and simple; create columns for date, marker name, value, and reference range
- **Heads Up Health** — dedicated health data aggregator that imports lab results and tracks trends over time
- **InsideTracker Dashboard** — automatically tracks and visualizes your results over time with optimal zone highlighting

## Body Composition

- **DEXA Scan** — gold standard for body composition (fat mass, lean mass, bone density, visceral fat); available at BodySpec and DexaFit locations
- **InBody** — bioelectrical impedance devices found at many gyms; less accurate than DEXA but useful for tracking trends between scans
- **BodySpec** — DEXA scan service with locations in major cities; approximately $45-50 per scan
- **DexaFit** — DEXA scans plus metabolic testing (RMR, VO2 max) at locations across the US

## Optimal Range References

- **"Outlive" by Peter Attia** — the most comprehensive mainstream book on proactive health testing and optimal biomarker ranges
- **Marek Health Reference Ranges** — publicly available optimal range charts used by their clinical team

## Cost

- **Basic panels** — $50-200 through direct-to-consumer labs without insurance
- **Comprehensive panels** (including hormones, IGF-1, advanced markers) — $200-500
- **DEXA scans** — $40-100 per scan depending on location
- **Insurance** — many basic panels are covered by insurance with a doctor's order; ask your provider what is included in annual wellness labs
