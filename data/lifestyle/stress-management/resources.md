# Stress & Cortisol Management

## Resources

- **Meditation Apps** — Headspace (structured courses for beginners, good habit-building design), Waking Up by Sam Harris (deeper philosophical approach, daily meditations, theory lessons), Insight Timer (largest free library, community features, timer for unguided practice), Calm (sleep stories, daily calm sessions, beginner-friendly)
- **Breathing Apps** — Breathwrk (guided breathwork sessions for different goals: calm, energy, sleep, focus), Othership (breathwork classes with community, structured programs)
- **HRV Monitoring Devices** — Oura Ring (best for overnight HRV and sleep tracking, passive and effortless), Whoop (continuous HRV, strain tracking, recovery scores, best for athletes), EliteHRV app (free morning HRV readings using phone camera, good starting point before investing in hardware)
- **Key Metrics to Track** — resting heart rate trend over weeks (lower is better, sudden increases indicate stress or illness), HRV trend over weeks (higher is better, reflects parasympathetic capacity), sleep quality correlation with stress interventions (track what actually moves the needle for you personally)
- **Books** — "Breath" by James Nestor (the science of breathing, nasal breathing, CO2 tolerance, and how modern humans breathe wrong), "The Body Keeps the Score" by Bessel van der Kolk (how trauma and chronic stress live in the body and evidence-based approaches to healing, essential reading if stress has a trauma component)
- **Biofeedback Devices** — Muse headband (real-time EEG feedback during meditation, gamifies the practice with soundscapes that respond to brain activity), HeartMath Inner Balance (real-time HRV biofeedback through ear sensor, trains coherent breathing and parasympathetic activation with visual feedback)
