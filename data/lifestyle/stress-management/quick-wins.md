# Stress & Cortisol Management

## Quick Wins

- **Do 5 physiological sighs right now** — double inhale through the nose (one full breath plus a short "sip"), then a long slow exhale through the mouth. This is the fastest evidence-based technique for reducing cortisol and heart rate in real time. Five repetitions take less than one minute and produce a measurable shift in nervous system state.
- **Put your phone in another room for the next hour** — notifications, social media, and news are chronic low-grade stressors that activate the sympathetic nervous system through constant novelty and threat detection. One hour of phone-free time allows your nervous system to downshift into a parasympathetic state. Notice how different you feel.
- **Take a 10-minute walk outside without headphones** — combine natural light exposure, gentle movement, and sensory engagement with the environment. No podcasts, no music, no phone. Just walking and noticing. This activates the parasympathetic nervous system and reduces cortisol more effectively than indoor exercise.
- **Set a "no screens" alarm for 30 minutes before bed tonight** — blue light and stimulating content in the final 30 minutes before sleep suppress melatonin and elevate cortisol at the exact time your body needs both to shift. Use the 30 minutes for reading, stretching, or conversation instead. This single change improves sleep onset and quality measurably.
- **Take 400mg magnesium glycinate before bed** — most people are magnesium deficient, and chronic stress accelerates depletion. Magnesium glycinate promotes GABA receptor activation, muscle relaxation, and calm. Take it 30-60 minutes before bed. You will likely notice a difference in sleep quality on the first night.
