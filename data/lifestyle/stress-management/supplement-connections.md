# Stress & Cortisol Management

## Supplement Connections

- **Ashwagandha (KSM-66, 600mg daily)** — the most clinically validated supplement for cortisol reduction. Multiple randomized controlled trials show KSM-66 ashwagandha reduces serum cortisol by 30% or more over 8 weeks. Also improves sleep quality, reduces anxiety scores, and supports thyroid function. Take in the evening or split into morning and evening doses.
- **Magnesium Glycinate (400mg daily)** — magnesium is a natural GABA receptor agonist, promoting calm and muscle relaxation. The glycinate form has the best absorption and the least GI distress. Chronic stress depletes magnesium, and most people are already deficient. Take before bed for combined sleep and stress support.
- **L-Theanine (200mg as needed)** — an amino acid from green tea that promotes alpha brain wave production, creating a state of calm alertness without sedation. Pairs exceptionally well with caffeine to smooth out the jittery, cortisol-spiking effects of coffee. Can be taken 1-3 times daily.
- **Omega-3 Fish Oil (2-4g combined EPA/DHA daily)** — reduces neuroinflammation driven by chronic stress. EPA in particular has antidepressant and anti-anxiety effects at higher doses (2g+ EPA). Chronic stress increases brain inflammation, and omega-3s provide the raw materials for anti-inflammatory resolvins that counteract this damage.
- **B-Complex (methylated forms)** — B vitamins are rapidly depleted under chronic stress because they are cofactors in cortisol production, neurotransmitter synthesis, and energy metabolism. Methylated forms (methylfolate, methylcobalamin) ensure absorption even in those with MTHFR variants. Look for a B-complex that includes B1, B2, B3, B5 (pantothenic acid, the "anti-stress vitamin"), B6 (P5P form), folate, and B12.
- **Vitamin C (1000mg daily)** — the adrenal glands contain the highest concentration of vitamin C in the body, and they burn through it during cortisol production. Supplementing vitamin C supports adrenal function under stress and also serves as a foundational antioxidant. Pair with collagen supplementation for dual benefit.
- **Rhodiola Rosea (200-400mg daily, standardized to 3% rosavins)** — an adaptogen with strong evidence for acute stress resilience, fatigue reduction, and cognitive performance under stress. Works differently from ashwagandha: rhodiola is more stimulating and better for daytime energy, while ashwagandha is more calming and better for evening use. They can be stacked (rhodiola morning, ashwagandha evening).
