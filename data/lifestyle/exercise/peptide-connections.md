# Exercise & Movement

## Peptide Connections

- **CJC-1295/Ipamorelin**: Exercise amplifies the GH response to secretagogue peptides. Resistance training triggers a natural GH pulse, and when combined with peptide-stimulated GH release, the total GH exposure is greater than either stimulus alone. Training in a fasted state may further enhance this effect, as insulin suppresses GH release. For maximum synergy, dose GH secretagogues at bedtime after an earlier training session, allowing both exercise-induced and sleep-induced GH pulses.
- **Semaglutide/Tirzepatide/Retatrutide**: Resistance training is the single most critical lifestyle intervention for GLP-1 agonist users. Without it, 25-40% of weight lost is lean mass. With consistent resistance training (3x/week minimum) and adequate protein intake, weight loss can be shifted to 85-95% fat loss. This is not optional; it is essential for a good outcome. Cardio alone does not prevent muscle loss on these drugs.
- **MOTS-c**: An exercise mimetic peptide that activates AMPK, the same pathway activated by exercise. When combined with actual exercise, the metabolic benefits are synergistic. MOTS-c improves insulin sensitivity, glucose uptake, and fat oxidation through pathways that overlap with and amplify exercise-induced adaptations. Exercise remains necessary even when using MOTS-c.
- **BPC-157/TB-500**: These healing peptides support exercise consistency by accelerating recovery from injuries, tendinopathies, and muscle strains. BPC-157 promotes angiogenesis and tendon healing. TB-500 supports tissue repair and reduces inflammation. Together, they allow athletes and recreational exercisers to maintain training frequency by shortening recovery timelines from injuries that would otherwise require extended rest.
- **Semax**: Exercise increases BDNF production, and Semax also upregulates BDNF through a separate mechanism. The combination produces a greater total BDNF response than either alone, amplifying the cognitive and neuroplasticity benefits. For maximum cognitive enhancement, dose Semax in the morning and exercise in the morning or early afternoon.
- **MK-677 (Ibutamoren)**: Elevates GH and IGF-1 around the clock, which enhances the muscle-building response to resistance training. The increased IGF-1 supports satellite cell activation and muscle protein synthesis, making training sessions more productive. The improved sleep quality from MK-677 also enhances recovery between sessions.
- **AOD-9604**: A modified fragment of growth hormone that targets fat metabolism without the anabolic effects of full GH. Fasted morning cardio may enhance the fat-burning effects of AOD-9604, as both fasting and aerobic exercise promote lipolysis. Resistance training remains important alongside AOD-9604 to prevent any lean mass loss during fat reduction.
- **Creatine**: While technically a supplement rather than a peptide, creatine (5g daily) is the most well-studied and effective ergogenic aid in existence. It increases phosphocreatine stores for explosive strength and power, supports muscle hydration and volumization, and may have cognitive benefits. Every person who resistance trains should take creatine unless medically contraindicated.
