# Exercise & Movement

## Quick Wins

Five things you can do today to start building an exercise habit:

1. **Take a 10-minute walk after your next meal**: Walk at a comfortable pace immediately after eating. This blunts the postprandial glucose spike by 30-50%, aids digestion, and adds steps to your daily total. No equipment, no gym membership, no preparation required. If you only change one thing, make it this.

2. **Do 3 sets of bodyweight squats and push-ups today**: Right now, wherever you are. Perform 10-15 bodyweight squats, rest 60 seconds, repeat for 3 sets. Then do 3 sets of push-ups to failure (even if that is 3 reps, or against a wall). This takes less than 10 minutes and begins training your body to handle resistance. Consistency matters more than intensity at the start.

3. **Set a step count goal on your phone**: Open Apple Health or Google Fit and set a daily step goal of 8,000 steps. Check it throughout the day. Being aware of your step count naturally increases movement. Park farther away, take stairs, pace during phone calls. Small behavioral changes compound.

4. **Take 5g creatine monohydrate daily**: Purchase creatine monohydrate powder (it is inexpensive and available everywhere). Mix 5g (about one teaspoon) into water and drink it at any time of day. No loading phase needed. Within 3-4 weeks, your muscles will be saturated and you will notice improved strength, endurance, and recovery during workouts.

5. **Schedule 3 resistance training sessions this week**: Put them in your calendar right now. Choose a gym, a home workout space, or a park. If you are a complete beginner, start with a simple full-body routine: goblet squats, push-ups, dumbbell rows, and Romanian deadlifts. Three sets of 10 reps each. The most important thing is showing up consistently.
