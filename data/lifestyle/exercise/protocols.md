# Exercise & Movement

## Protocols

### Beginner Protocol

For those just starting, returning from a long break, or on GLP-1 drugs with low energy:

- **3x/week full-body resistance training**: Focus on compound movements that work multiple muscle groups. Each session should include one movement from each pattern: squat (goblet squat, bodyweight squat), hinge (Romanian deadlift, kettlebell swing), push (push-up, dumbbell press), pull (dumbbell row, lat pulldown). Perform 3 sets of 8-12 reps per exercise. Rest 60-90 seconds between sets.
- **8,000+ steps daily**: Track with a phone or wearable. This is the minimum threshold associated with significant health benefits. Build up gradually if currently sedentary. Steps from any source count: walking the dog, parking farther away, taking stairs.
- **10-minute walks after meals**: Walk at a comfortable pace after breakfast, lunch, or dinner (or all three). This blunts postprandial glucose spikes by 30-50% and aids digestion. Especially valuable for those on GLP-1 agonists or anyone managing blood sugar.

### Intermediate Protocol

- **4x/week upper/lower split**: Alternate between upper body and lower body days (e.g., Mon upper, Tue lower, Thu upper, Fri lower). This allows for more volume per muscle group while providing adequate recovery. Include 4-5 exercises per session, 3-4 sets of 6-12 reps.
- **2x/week Zone 2 cardio**: 30-45 minutes at conversational pace (you can hold a conversation but prefer not to). Use cycling, jogging, swimming, rowing, or brisk walking. Heart rate should be 60-70% of max (roughly 180 minus age as a starting estimate). This builds the aerobic base and mitochondrial density.
- **10,000+ steps daily**: Increase from beginner level. Use a standing desk, take walking meetings, and add a dedicated daily walk.
- **Post-workout protein within 1 hour**: Consume 30-50g of protein within 60 minutes of finishing resistance training to maximize muscle protein synthesis. A protein shake, chicken breast, or Greek yogurt all work.

### Advanced Protocol

- **4-5x/week PPL or specialized split**: Push/Pull/Legs or a body-part split that allows for high volume and targeted training. Include both compound and isolation exercises. Periodize rep ranges (strength: 3-6 reps, hypertrophy: 8-12 reps, endurance: 15-20 reps) across training blocks.
- **2-3x Zone 2 + 1x HIIT weekly**: Maintain the aerobic base with 2-3 Zone 2 sessions while adding one high-intensity interval session (e.g., 4-6 rounds of 30 seconds all-out effort with 2-4 minutes recovery). HIIT maximizes BDNF release and VO2 max improvement.
- **12,000+ steps daily**: Higher step counts correlate with better body composition and metabolic health. Integrate movement throughout the day rather than relying solely on structured exercise.
- **Periodization and deload weeks**: Every 4-6 weeks, reduce training volume and intensity by 40-50% for one week. This allows for accumulated fatigue to dissipate and adaptation to consolidate. Deload weeks prevent overtraining and support long-term progress.
- **Cold plunge post-training**: Cold water immersion (50-59°F for 2-5 minutes) may enhance recovery and reduce inflammation. However, evidence suggests cold exposure immediately after resistance training may blunt the hypertrophy signal (mTOR activation). Consider reserving cold plunges for non-training days or after cardio sessions only.

### GLP-1 Specific Protocol

- **Start resistance training before starting GLP-1 drugs if possible**: Establishing a training habit and building a foundation of muscle before the caloric deficit created by GLP-1s gives the body more lean mass to preserve.
- **Minimum 3x/week resistance training**: This is the floor, not the ceiling. Resistance training is the single most important exercise modality for GLP-1 users. Missing sessions directly increases the proportion of muscle lost.
- **Prioritize compound lifts**: Squats, deadlifts, bench press, overhead press, and rows recruit the most muscle mass and produce the strongest hormonal response. These should form the core of every session.
- **Never skip legs**: Lower body muscles are the largest in the body. Training them drives the greatest metabolic and hormonal response and preserves the most functional muscle mass.
- **Protein within 1 hour post-workout**: Non-negotiable for GLP-1 users. Even if appetite is suppressed, prioritize a protein shake or high-protein meal after training. Aim for 30-50g of protein in this window.
