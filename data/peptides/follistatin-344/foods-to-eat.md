# Follistatin-344

## Foods to Eat

- **Lean protein (chicken breast, turkey, lean beef)**: Supplies amino acids for the elevated muscle protein synthesis a low-myostatin state enables
- **Wild-caught salmon and mackerel**: Omega-3 fatty acids support muscle protein synthesis and reduce inflammation during heavy training
- **Eggs (pasture-raised)**: Complete protein plus choline and leucine to drive anabolism
- **Greek yogurt and cottage cheese**: Slow-digesting casein protein and calcium that supports bone health
- **Bone broth and collagen-rich cuts**: Provide glycine and proline to support tendon and ligament adaptation as muscle grows
- **Leafy greens (spinach, kale, collards)**: Vitamin K and minerals that support bone mineralization
- **Sardines and canned salmon with bones**: Calcium and vitamin D in a whole-food form, relevant given the bone-density concern
- **Sweet potatoes and oats**: Quality complex carbohydrates to fuel training volume
- **Nuts and seeds (almonds, pumpkin seeds)**: Magnesium, zinc, and healthy fats supporting recovery and bone health
- **Citrus and berries**: Vitamin C is a cofactor for collagen synthesis, supporting connective tissue
