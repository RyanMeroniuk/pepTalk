# Follistatin-344

## Drug Interactions

- **Active cancer / chemotherapy**: Contraindicated — TGF-β signaling has tumor-suppressive functions; broad antagonism is theoretically pro-tumorigenic and may interfere with cancer treatment
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding**: Contraindicated — a potent developmental growth modulator with no safety data
- **Corticosteroids (prednisone, dexamethasone)**: Caution — corticosteroids are catabolic to muscle and bone, opposing the anabolic intent and potentially compounding bone-density risk; monitor
- **Fertility/hormone medications**: Caution — activin inhibition perturbs FSH and the reproductive axis, which may interact unpredictably with hormonal therapies
- **Anticoagulants/blood thinners**: Caution — injectable use carries bleeding/bruising risk at injection sites; monitor
- **Other anabolic agents (IGF-1 LR3, GH, androgens)**: Caution — additive musculoskeletal stress and compounded unknown risk; not a documented pharmacological interaction but a practical one
- **Bisphosphonates / osteoporosis drugs**: Monitor — given the bone-remodeling concern, anyone on bone-targeted therapy should be especially cautious
- **General caveat**: No formal human drug-interaction studies exist; all listed interactions are mechanism-based precautions, not documented clinical findings
