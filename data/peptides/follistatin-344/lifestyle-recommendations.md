# Follistatin-344

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Prioritize connective-tissue conditioning**: Because muscle can grow faster than tendons and ligaments adapt, emphasize controlled tempo, progressive overload, and adequate warm-up to reduce injury risk
- **Resistance training is the lever**: Myostatin suppression creates a permissive environment, but mechanical loading is what directs growth — train compound lifts 3–5x/week
- **Protein intake**: Aim for 1.6–2.2g/kg bodyweight daily to supply substrate for elevated protein synthesis
- **Protect bone health**: Given the theoretical bone-density concern, include weight-bearing exercise, adequate vitamin D and calcium, and avoid prolonged inactivity
- **Sleep 7–9 hours**: Recovery and tissue remodeling are sleep-dependent
- **Conservative cycle length**: The research community uses short cycles rather than continuous use, reflecting unknown long-term safety
- **Track objectively**: If using at all, monitor strength, circumference, and any joint/tendon discomfort closely
- **Understand the evidence gap**: Treat any use as experimental; there is no human safety or efficacy validation for injectable FST-344
