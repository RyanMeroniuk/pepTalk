# Follistatin-344

## Side Effects

- **Bone density concern**: Myostatin and the broader TGF-β/activin axis help regulate bone remodeling; chronic broad antagonism raises a theoretical risk to bone mineral density that is not well studied in humans
- **Reproductive/endocrine disruption**: Activin inhibition can perturb the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis and FSH signaling, with poorly characterized effects on fertility and hormones
- **Tendon-to-muscle imbalance**: Rapid muscle growth outpacing connective-tissue adaptation is a theoretical injury risk, as tendons and ligaments do not strengthen as quickly as myostatin-inhibited muscle
- **Unknown long-term safety**: Because broad TGF-β signaling governs tissue homeostasis and tumor suppression, chronic systemic inhibition carries unquantified risk
- **Injection-site reactions**: Redness, swelling, irritation typical of subcutaneous peptide injection
- **Product-related risk**: Gray-market sourcing introduces contamination, endotoxin, and mislabeling risks independent of the molecule itself
- **Evidence caveat**: Human side-effect data is essentially absent; the above are extrapolated from biology and animal work, not documented in trials
