# Follistatin-344

## Dosing Protocols

- **No established or approved dosing**: There is no validated human dosing protocol — all figures below are anecdotal gray-market practice, not clinically supported
- **Common anecdotal range**: 100mcg subcutaneous per day is the most frequently cited research-community dose
- **Typical reported cycle length**: 10–30 days, reflecting the unproven belief that myostatin suppression is most useful in short bursts
- **Reconstitution**: Lyophilized powder reconstituted with bacteriostatic water; potency and actual peptide content of gray-market vials is highly variable and frequently misrepresented
- **Administration**: Subcutaneous injection; some users rotate localized injection sites hoping for regional effect, though systemic distribution is expected
- **Storage**: Refrigerate after reconstitution; the protein is fragile and prone to degradation
- **Major caveat**: Product purity, sterility, and even identity cannot be assumed from research-chemical suppliers; dosing precision is illusory when the source material is unverified
