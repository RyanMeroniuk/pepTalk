# Follistatin-344

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT FDA-approved** for any indication — there is no approved follistatin peptide drug
- **No completed human efficacy trials** for injectable FST-344; the human-relevant data comes from early gene-therapy and preclinical animal research
- **Sold only as a "research chemical"** through gray-market suppliers, explicitly not for human use
- **Not a dietary supplement** and not legally marketable for human consumption
- **Quality is unregulated**: purity, sterility, dosing accuracy, and even molecular identity are not verified by any authority
- **Banned in sport**: Myostatin inhibitors are prohibited by WADA at all times
- **Legal status varies by jurisdiction**; possession/import of unapproved injectables can carry legal risk
- **Bottom line**: Experimental, unapproved, and unverified — any use is outside regulated medicine
