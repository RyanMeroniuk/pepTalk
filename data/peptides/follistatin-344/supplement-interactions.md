# Follistatin-344

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Synergistic — Creatine Monohydrate**: Supports the increased muscle protein synthesis a low-myostatin environment enables; well-tolerated and evidence-based (5g/day)
- **Synergistic — Omega-3 Fatty Acids**: Anti-inflammatory support and improved muscle protein synthesis complement the anabolic context (2–4g EPA/DHA daily)
- **Critical — Vitamin D3**: Important for both muscle function and bone mineralization; given the theoretical bone-density concern with myostatin inhibition, maintaining adequate vitamin D status is especially relevant (2,000–5,000 IU/day)
- **Critical — Collagen Peptides**: Connective tissue may lag behind rapid muscle growth; collagen plus vitamin C may support tendon/ligament adaptation (10–15g/day)
- **Enhanced by — Magnesium**: Supports muscle contraction, recovery, and bone health (300–400mg/day)
- **Enhanced by — Zinc**: Supports protein synthesis and recovery (15–30mg/day with copper balance)
- **Bone support — Vitamin K2 + Calcium**: Reasonable given the bone-density question, to support mineralization alongside vitamin D3
- **Caution — anything masking joint/tendon pain**: Avoid blunting connective-tissue warning signs while muscle is growing faster than tendon
