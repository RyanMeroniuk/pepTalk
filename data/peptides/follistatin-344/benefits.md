# Follistatin-344

## Benefits

- **Muscle mass gains (preclinical)**: Animal and gene-therapy models report substantial increases in lean muscle mass via combined hypertrophy and hyperplasia — human magnitude is unknown
- **Muscle hyperplasia potential**: Unlike most anabolics, myostatin inhibition is associated with new muscle fiber formation in models, not just enlargement of existing fibers
- **Strength alongside size**: Increased fiber number and size translate to greater strength in rodent studies
- **Potential muscle preservation**: Theoretically useful against muscle wasting because it removes the catabolic myostatin signal — but this remains a research hypothesis, not a demonstrated human outcome
- **Possible recovery support**: A lower-myostatin environment may favor repair when paired with growth-factor signaling (anecdotal in human users, unproven)
- **Important framing**: These benefits are extrapolated from animal/early research; no controlled human efficacy data exists for injectable FST-344, and reported user results are anecdotal
