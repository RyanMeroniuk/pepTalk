# Follistatin-344

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Myostatin (GDF-8) inhibition**: Binds and neutralizes myostatin, the dominant negative regulator of skeletal muscle mass — removing this brake permits both fiber enlargement and new fiber formation in animal models
- **Activin antagonism**: Also binds activin A and activin B with high affinity, contributing to its anabolic signal but also explaining its effects on reproductive and adrenal axes
- **Broad TGF-β superfamily binding**: Sequesters multiple TGF-β ligands (including some GDF/BMP family members), making its action less selective than receptor-specific inhibitors
- **Muscle hyperplasia + hypertrophy**: Preclinical studies report increases in both fiber size and fiber number, distinguishing it from agents that only enlarge existing fibers
- **Isoform conversion (FST-344 → FST-315)**: FST-344 is the longer precursor form that is processed in tissue toward the heparin-binding FST-315 isoform, which has shorter systemic half-life and stays more localized to tissue
- **Satellite cell context**: By lowering myostatin tone, it creates a permissive environment for satellite-cell-driven repair — often paired conceptually with IGF-1 pathway activators
- **Evidence caveat**: Mechanistic claims rest on rodent, gene-therapy, and in vitro data — human pharmacology of injected FST-344 peptide is essentially uncharacterized
