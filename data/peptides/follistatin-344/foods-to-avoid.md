# Follistatin-344

## Foods to Avoid

- **Excess alcohol**: Impairs muscle protein synthesis, recovery, and bone density — directly counterproductive to the anabolic goal and the bone-density concern
- **Cola and phosphoric-acid soft drinks**: High phosphoric acid intake is associated with poorer bone mineral density, relevant given the theoretical bone risk
- **Added sugars and high-fructose corn syrup**: Promote inflammation and fat gain that undermine recomposition
- **Ultra-processed foods**: Low in the protein, minerals, and micronutrients needed for muscle and connective-tissue building
- **Trans fats and fried foods**: Pro-inflammatory and impair recovery
- **Very low-calorie or crash diets**: Insufficient energy and protein blunt anabolism and can worsen bone loss
- **Excess caffeine in large doses**: Very high intakes can modestly impair calcium retention; keep within moderate limits given the bone consideration
