# Follistatin-344

## Things to Avoid

- **Assuming gray-market product is what the label says**: Research-chemical follistatin is frequently underdosed, misidentified, or contaminated — purity and even identity cannot be assumed
- **Ignoring the bone-density concern**: Broad TGF-β/activin antagonism may affect bone remodeling; do not neglect weight-bearing exercise, vitamin D, and calcium
- **Overtraining muscle faster than tendons adapt**: Rapid hypertrophy without connective-tissue conditioning is an injury risk — avoid ego lifting and abrupt load jumps
- **Long continuous cycles**: Long-term systemic TGF-β inhibition has unknown safety; avoid open-ended use
- **Use during pregnancy or breastfeeding**: A potent growth/developmental modulator with no safety data — absolutely avoid
- **Use with active or prior cancer**: TGF-β signaling has tumor-suppressive roles; broad inhibition is theoretically risky — avoid
- **Stacking blind with other unproven anabolics**: Compounding unknown risks from multiple gray-market agents
- **Skipping bloodwork and objective tracking**: Without monitoring, endocrine or other disruptions go undetected
- **Treating animal results as human-proven**: The efficacy data is preclinical; expectations should be calibrated accordingly
