# Ac-SDKP (Thymosin Beta-4 Fragment)

## Side Effects

- Human safety profile is essentially uncharacterized — no formal human safety studies exist
- Generally well-tolerated in animal studies at researched doses
- Injection site irritation or redness (injectable route)
- Possible mild GI upset (oral route, anecdotal)
- Theoretical concern: pro-angiogenic and anti-apoptotic properties (shared with parent TB-500) may theoretically support growth of existing tumors — not established
- Potential interaction with blood pressure regulation due to its relationship with the ACE/renin-angiotensin system
- No established LD50 or organ toxicity data in humans
- Long-term safety is unknown
