# Ac-SDKP (Thymosin Beta-4 Fragment)

## Foods to Eat

- **Wild-Caught Fatty Fish**: Salmon, sardines, mackerel, and anchovies supply omega-3s (EPA/DHA) that suppress fibrotic and inflammatory signaling, complementing Ac-SDKP's anti-fibrotic action
- **Cruciferous Vegetables**: Broccoli, cauliflower, and Brussels sprouts provide sulforaphane, which supports detoxification pathways and reduces oxidative stress in tissue
- **Berries**: Blueberries, blackberries, and pomegranate provide anthocyanins and polyphenols that reduce oxidative damage in heart, liver, and kidney tissue
- **Dark Leafy Greens**: Spinach, kale, and Swiss chard deliver vitamin C, magnesium, and folate that support collagen quality and tissue repair
- **Turmeric & Ginger**: Curcumin and gingerols downregulate TGF-beta and NF-kB fibrotic pathways. Pair turmeric with black pepper (piperine) for absorption
- **Green Tea**: EGCG has demonstrated anti-fibrotic effects in liver and kidney models — a useful daily addition
- **Bone Broth & Collagen-Rich Foods**: Provide glycine, proline, and collagen precursors for organized connective tissue repair
- **Olive Oil (Extra Virgin)**: Oleocanthal and polyphenols offer anti-inflammatory support and are a healthy fat source for cardiovascular health
- **Garlic & Onions**: Allicin and quercetin provide anti-inflammatory and cardioprotective support
- **Potassium-Rich Foods**: Avocado, banana, and sweet potato support healthy blood pressure given Ac-SDKP's ties to the renin-angiotensin system
