# Ac-SDKP (Thymosin Beta-4 Fragment)

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT FDA-approved** for any indication
- Sold strictly as a research chemical — not a dietary supplement or approved drug
- Evidence base is primarily animal and mechanistic; human clinical data are very limited
- As a fragment/relative of Thymosin Beta-4, it falls under the same scrutiny as TB-500
- **Likely prohibited by WADA** for competitive athletes given its relationship to Thymosin Beta-4 (which is banned)
- Oral bioavailability marketing claims are not backed by published human pharmacokinetic studies
- Legal status varies by country; generally legal to purchase for research purposes where TB-500 is
