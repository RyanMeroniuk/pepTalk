# Ac-SDKP (Thymosin Beta-4 Fragment)

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Anti-Inflammatory Diet**: Since Ac-SDKP's primary value is anti-fibrotic and anti-inflammatory, support it with a whole-food, low-processed diet rich in omega-3s, polyphenols, and colorful vegetables. Minimize chronic inflammatory inputs that drive fibrosis
- **Blood Pressure Awareness**: Ac-SDKP is tied to the ACE/renin-angiotensin system. Maintain healthy blood pressure through sodium moderation, potassium-rich foods, regular cardio, and stress management — and monitor BP if combining with any cardiovascular medications
- **Protein for Tissue Repair**: Consume 1.6-2.2g protein per kg body weight daily to supply amino acids for organized tissue repair rather than fibrotic scarring
- **Sleep Optimization**: Target 7-9 hours nightly. Deep sleep supports growth hormone release and tissue repair that complement Ac-SDKP's mechanisms. Keep a cool, dark room and a consistent schedule
- **Avoid Fibrogenic Exposures**: Minimize alcohol, smoking, and environmental toxins (the chief drivers of organ fibrosis). These directly oppose Ac-SDKP's anti-fibrotic action
- **Regular Aerobic Exercise**: Moderate cardiovascular exercise improves endothelial function and tissue perfusion, supporting angiogenic repair pathways
- **Hydration**: Drink at least half your body weight in ounces of water daily to support tissue perfusion and kidney function
- **Morning Sunlight**: 15-20 minutes of morning light supports circadian rhythm and vitamin D status, both of which underpin healthy repair cascades
