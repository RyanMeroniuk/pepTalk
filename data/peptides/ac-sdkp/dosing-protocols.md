# Ac-SDKP (Thymosin Beta-4 Fragment)

## Dosing Protocols

- **No established human dosing**: There are no published human pharmacokinetic or dose-finding studies — all protocols are extrapolated from animal research and vendor suggestions
- **Animal Research Reference**: Most rodent studies use continuous infusion (e.g., ~800 mcg/kg/day via osmotic minipump), which does not translate cleanly to human bolus dosing
- **Vendor-Suggested Oral**: Commonly marketed as an oral fragment at roughly 200-500 mcg/day, claimed to be more bioavailable than full TB-500 — this claim is not supported by published data
- **Subcutaneous (Community Use)**: Some users inject 250-500 mcg 1-2x daily, mirroring small-peptide protocols, but no safety or efficacy basis exists for this
- **Short Half-Life**: Ac-SDKP is rapidly degraded by ACE in circulation, which is why animal studies favor continuous infusion — bolus dosing may have limited duration of action
- **Reconstitution (Injectable)**: Bacteriostatic water; refrigerate after reconstitution
- **Cycling**: No evidence-based cycling guidance exists; community protocols often mirror TB-500 (several weeks on, then off)
- **Caution**: Because human data are minimal, all dosing is experimental and should be framed as research only, not therapeutic guidance
