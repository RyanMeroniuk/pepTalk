# Ac-SDKP (Thymosin Beta-4 Fragment)

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **ACE inhibitors (lisinopril, enalapril, ramipril)**: ACE is the primary enzyme that degrades Ac-SDKP, so ACE inhibitors substantially raise endogenous Ac-SDKP levels. Combining supplemental Ac-SDKP with ACE inhibitors may amplify its effects unpredictably — use caution and monitor blood pressure
- **Anticoagulants / blood thinners (warfarin, heparin, aspirin, clopidogrel)**: Ac-SDKP's pro-angiogenic activity may theoretically affect bleeding risk — caution and monitoring advised
- **Immunosuppressants (tacrolimus, cyclosporine, mycophenolate)**: Theoretical interaction with immune and tissue-repair modulation — use caution
- **Corticosteroids**: Corticosteroids impair tissue repair and may oppose Ac-SDKP's regenerative action — caution with concurrent use
- **NSAIDs**: May blunt the inflammatory phase of healing; not strictly contraindicated but monitor
- **Anti-angiogenic / chemotherapy agents (bevacizumab)**: Ac-SDKP's pro-angiogenic properties may theoretically counteract anti-angiogenic cancer therapy — contraindicated during such treatment
- **Contraindicated: Active cancer or history of cancer** — pro-angiogenic and anti-apoptotic properties may theoretically support tumor growth
- **Contraindicated: Pregnancy and breastfeeding** — insufficient safety data
- **Timing note**: If used alongside other injectable peptides, separate injections by a few hours is a common community practice, though no specific timing conflict is established
