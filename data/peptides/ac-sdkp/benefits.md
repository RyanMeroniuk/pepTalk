# Ac-SDKP (Thymosin Beta-4 Fragment)

## Benefits

- **Anti-Fibrotic (Cardiac)**: Reduced cardiac fibrosis and improved cardiac remodeling after hypertension and ischemic injury in rodent models
- **Anti-Fibrotic (Renal)**: Attenuated kidney fibrosis and preserved renal function in models of diabetic and hypertensive nephropathy
- **Anti-Fibrotic (Hepatic)**: Reduced liver fibrosis and collagen accumulation in animal models of chronic liver injury
- **Anti-Fibrotic (Pulmonary)**: Limited lung fibrosis progression in preclinical bleomycin-induced fibrosis studies
- **Anti-Inflammatory**: Lowered inflammatory cytokine load and immune cell infiltration in injured tissue
- **Angiogenesis Support**: Promotes blood vessel formation to support tissue repair, inherited from the parent TB-500 molecule
- **Tissue Protection**: Broadly protective against fibrotic remodeling across multiple organ systems in animal research
- **Note on Evidence**: Benefits are derived almost exclusively from animal and in-vitro studies. Human clinical efficacy has not been established, and oral bioavailability claims by vendors are unverified
