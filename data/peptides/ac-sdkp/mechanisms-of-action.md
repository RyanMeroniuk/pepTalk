# Ac-SDKP (Thymosin Beta-4 Fragment)

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Anti-Fibrotic Signaling**: Inhibits TGF-beta1/Smad signaling, the central driver of fibrosis, reducing collagen deposition and extracellular matrix accumulation in heart, kidney, and liver tissue (animal models)
- **Anti-Inflammatory**: Suppresses NF-kB activation and downstream pro-inflammatory cytokines, reducing macrophage infiltration into damaged tissue
- **Pro-Angiogenic**: Stimulates endothelial cell proliferation and migration, promoting new blood vessel formation similar to its parent peptide TB-500
- **Inhibits Fibroblast-to-Myofibroblast Transition**: Limits the conversion of fibroblasts into the collagen-secreting myofibroblasts responsible for scar tissue
- **ACE Substrate Relationship**: Ac-SDKP is hydrolyzed by angiotensin-converting enzyme; ACE inhibitors increase its endogenous levels, which partly explains some anti-fibrotic effects attributed to ACE inhibitor therapy
- **Hematopoietic Modulation**: Acts as a natural regulator of hematopoietic stem cell proliferation, helping keep stem cells in a quiescent state
- **Reduced Oxidative Stress**: Attenuates reactive oxygen species production in injured tissue in preclinical studies
