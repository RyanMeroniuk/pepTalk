# Ac-SDKP (Thymosin Beta-4 Fragment)

## Things to Avoid

- **Treating Vendor Claims as Fact**: Marketing claims that oral Ac-SDKP is "more bioavailable than TB-500" are not supported by published human data. Do not assume reliable oral absorption
- **Smoking & Nicotine**: Potent drivers of vascular damage and organ fibrosis (especially pulmonary), directly opposing Ac-SDKP's anti-fibrotic and angiogenic mechanisms
- **Excessive Alcohol**: Promotes hepatic fibrosis and oxidative stress, undermining the peptide's primary anti-fibrotic benefit
- **Unmonitored Use with Blood Pressure Medication**: Because Ac-SDKP interacts with the ACE/renin-angiotensin system, combining it with ACE inhibitors or other antihypertensives without monitoring could affect blood pressure unpredictably
- **High-Sodium, Pro-Inflammatory Diet**: Continually feeding fibrotic and inflammatory pathways negates the peptide's intended action
- **Chronic Stress & Sleep Deprivation**: Elevated cortisol promotes inflammation and impairs tissue repair
- **Assuming Long-Term Safety**: Long-term human safety is unknown; avoid treating this as a proven therapeutic compound
- **Use During Pregnancy/Breastfeeding**: Insufficient safety data — avoid entirely
