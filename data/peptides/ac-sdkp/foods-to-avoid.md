# Ac-SDKP (Thymosin Beta-4 Fragment)

## Foods to Avoid

- **Alcohol**: A major driver of hepatic fibrosis and oxidative stress — directly opposes Ac-SDKP's anti-fibrotic action in the liver. Eliminate during protocols
- **Refined Sugar & High-Fructose Corn Syrup**: Drive inflammatory cytokine production, fatty liver, and oxidative stress that promote tissue fibrosis
- **Processed Seed Oils**: Soybean, corn, canola, and sunflower oils are high in omega-6 fats that fuel inflammatory signaling counter to Ac-SDKP's effects
- **Excess Sodium**: High-sodium processed foods elevate blood pressure and promote cardiac and renal fibrosis — particularly relevant given Ac-SDKP's renin-angiotensin connection
- **Trans Fats & Hydrogenated Oils**: Found in fried and packaged foods; increase systemic inflammation and cardiovascular fibrotic risk
- **Charred & Grilled Meats**: Advanced glycation end products (AGEs) and heterocyclic amines promote oxidative stress and tissue damage
- **Ultra-Processed Foods**: Emulsifiers, preservatives, and additives drive low-grade inflammation that opposes anti-fibrotic goals
- **Excess Alcohol-Adjacent Stimulants**: Heavy caffeine combined with high sodium can elevate blood pressure; moderate intake
