# Ipamorelin

## Side Effects

- Very mild side effect profile — the key advantage over other GHRPs
- Injection site irritation
- Head rush or lightheadedness (transient, after injection)
- Water retention (mild, dose-dependent)
- Tingling or numbness in extremities (rare)
- **No significant cortisol increase**
- **No significant appetite increase**
- **No significant prolactin increase**
- Overall considered the safest GH secretagogue
