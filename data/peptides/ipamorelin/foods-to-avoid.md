# Ipamorelin

## Foods to Avoid

- **Sugary foods and drinks near injection time** — glucose triggers insulin release, which directly suppresses the GH pulse. This includes soda, candy, fruit juice, and desserts
- **Refined carbs within 2 hours of injection** — white bread, white rice, pasta, and cereals cause rapid blood sugar and insulin elevation
- **High-fat meals within 2 hours of injection** — free fatty acids independently suppress GH secretion at the pituitary
- **Alcohol** — suppresses GH release by up to 75% and destroys deep sleep architecture. Since Ipamorelin's primary benefit window is during sleep, alcohol directly undermines it
- **Excessive dairy near injection** — milk and sweetened yogurt trigger significant insulin responses
- **Heavily processed foods** — promote inflammation and insulin resistance, impairing long-term GH signaling
- **Large meals before bed** — a full stomach near your bedtime injection blunts the GH pulse and can cause acid reflux that disrupts sleep
