# Ipamorelin

## Overview

Ipamorelin is a synthetic pentapeptide (5 amino acids) classified as a growth hormone secretagogue receptor (GHSR) agonist. Developed by Novo Nordisk in the late 1990s. Mimics the hormone ghrelin to stimulate GH release. Distinguished from other GHRPs by its exceptional selectivity — triggers robust GH release with minimal impact on cortisol, prolactin, or appetite. This selectivity makes it the "cleanest" growth hormone secretagogue available, and the most popular choice for GH optimization protocols.
