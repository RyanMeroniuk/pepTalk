# Ipamorelin

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Diabetes medications**: May require monitoring
- **Insulin**: May affect blood sugar management
- **Other GH secretagogues**: Careful stacking to avoid excessive GH
- **Contraindicated**: Active cancer or cancer history
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy/breastfeeding
- **Contraindicated**: Pituitary tumors
