# Ipamorelin

## Foods to Eat

- **Wild-caught salmon** — omega-3 fatty acids reduce inflammation and support GH receptor sensitivity, plus high-quality complete protein
- **Pasture-raised eggs** — amino acids, cholesterol for hormone synthesis, and choline for cognitive function
- **Chicken breast and turkey** — lean, high-protein sources that support muscle protein synthesis without excessive fat that could blunt GH if eaten near injection time
- **Greek yogurt (plain, full-fat)** — rich in glutamine, which independently stimulates GH release. Eat at meals well-separated from injection times
- **Cottage cheese before bed** — casein protein provides a slow, steady release of amino acids overnight, complementing Ipamorelin's nighttime GH pulse
- **Sweet potatoes** — complex carbohydrates that replenish muscle glycogen without a sharp insulin spike (eat at meals away from injection)
- **Steel-cut oats** — slow-digesting carbs with fiber; ideal for breakfast if your injection is at bedtime
- **Spinach and leafy greens** — magnesium-rich, supporting sleep quality and GH axis function
- **Berries (blueberries, raspberries, strawberries)** — antioxidants that reduce exercise-induced oxidative stress and support recovery
- **Avocado** — healthy monounsaturated fats that support hormone production (eat at meals, not near injection)
- **Bone broth** — glycine, proline, and collagen precursors that synergize with GH-mediated tissue repair
- **Pumpkin seeds** — excellent source of zinc and magnesium, both critical for GH axis function
- **Watermelon** — rich in L-citrulline, which converts to arginine, a known GH-stimulating amino acid
