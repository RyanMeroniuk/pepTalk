# Ipamorelin

## Things to Avoid

- **Eating within 2 hours of injection** — the most common mistake that undermines Ipamorelin's effectiveness. Even a small snack can elevate insulin enough to significantly blunt the GH pulse
- **Alcohol, especially on injection days** — destroys deep sleep and suppresses GH. Since Ipamorelin's primary mechanism involves amplifying sleep-related GH release, alcohol directly opposes it
- **Chronic sleep restriction** — sleeping under 6 hours leaves insufficient time for deep sleep. Ipamorelin amplifies the natural GH pulse; without adequate sleep opportunity, the amplification has nothing to work with
- **Exceeding 300mcg per injection** — Ipamorelin has a bell-shaped dose-response curve. Going above ~300mcg provides no additional GH benefit and may desensitize the ghrelin receptor
- **High caffeine intake after noon** — caffeine's 6-hour half-life means afternoon doses disrupt sleep architecture. Since bedtime is the optimal injection window, protecting sleep quality is paramount
- **Chronic high-intensity training without recovery days** — overtraining elevates cortisol chronically, which opposes GH signaling. Ipamorelin improves recovery, but you must still allow adequate rest between sessions
- **Skipping training entirely** — Ipamorelin creates an ideal hormonal environment for muscle growth and repair. Without resistance training, you miss the primary body composition benefits
- **Inconsistent dosing schedule** — Ipamorelin works best with regular, timed dosing that maintains consistent GH pulsatility. Irregular use reduces cumulative benefit
- **Chronic unmanaged stress** — sustained cortisol elevation from psychological or physiological stress opposes the GH axis. Implement daily stress management practices
