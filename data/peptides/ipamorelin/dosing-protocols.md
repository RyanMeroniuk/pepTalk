# Ipamorelin

## Dosing Protocols

- **Standard**: 200-300 mcg/day subcutaneous injection
- **Split Dosing**: 100-150 mcg morning + 100-150 mcg before bed (optimal for GH pulsatility)
- **Most Popular Combo**: 100mcg Ipamorelin + 100mcg CJC-1295 no DAC, 2-3x daily
- **Timing**: Fasted state required — minimum 2 hours post-meal; before bed is ideal
- **Cycle**: 8-12 weeks on, 4 weeks off
- **Dose Ceiling**: Beyond ~300mcg per injection, no additional benefit — may reduce receptor sensitivity
- **Reconstitution**: Bacteriostatic water; refrigerate after reconstitution
- **Results Timeline**: Sleep improvement within 1-2 weeks; body composition changes 4-8 weeks
