# Ipamorelin

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT FDA-approved** for any indication
- Limited clinical trial data (small-scale, short-term studies)
- Preclinical research compound
- FDA regulatory status uncertain — compounding pharmacy access varies by jurisdiction
- Available from research suppliers and some compounding pharmacies
- **Banned by WADA** for competitive athletes
- Widely regarded as the safest and most selective GH secretagogue available
