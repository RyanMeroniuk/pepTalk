# Ipamorelin

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Ghrelin Receptor Agonist**: Binds to GHS-R1a receptors in the hypothalamus and pituitary
- **Selective GH Release**: Stimulates rapid GH pulse without activating the ACTH-cortisol axis
- **No Prolactin Stimulation**: Unlike GHRP-2 and GHRP-6, does not increase prolactin even at high doses
- **Somatostatin Suppression**: Reduces inhibitory somatostatin signaling, allowing greater GH release
- **IGF-1 Elevation**: Raises IGF-1 via GH-mediated hepatic production
- **Minimal Appetite Effect**: Does not significantly increase hunger (unlike MK-677 and GHRP-6)
- **Bell-Shaped Dose Response**: Beyond ~300mcg, no additional GH benefit and possible receptor desensitization
