# Ipamorelin

## Lifestyle Recommendations


### Injection Timing & Fasting
- **Always inject in a fasted state** — insulin and free fatty acids from food directly suppress pituitary GH release, reducing Ipamorelin's effectiveness by up to 50-80%
- **Bedtime injection is the optimal timing** — Ipamorelin's GH pulse stacks with the natural nocturnal GH surge during deep sleep. This is why improved sleep is often the first benefit users notice
- **Morning fasted injection** is the second-best option — inject immediately upon waking, wait 20-30 minutes before eating
- **Post-workout (fasted or 2+ hours post-meal)** is a third effective window
- **Wait 20-30 minutes after injection before eating** — allow the GH pulse to reach its peak before introducing insulin

### Exercise
- **Resistance training 4-5x/week** — compound lifts (squats, deadlifts, bench press, pull-ups, overhead press) generate the strongest synergy with Ipamorelin's GH elevation
- **Moderate rest periods (60-90 seconds)** — lactic acid buildup from shorter rest periods independently stimulates GH release, amplifying the peptide's effect
- **HIIT 2-3x/week** — 15-20 minute sessions of sprints, cycling intervals, or kettlebell complexes drive additional GH pulses
- **Prioritize progressive overload** — Ipamorelin creates a favorable anabolic environment; steadily increasing training volume ensures you capitalize on enhanced recovery
- **Yoga or mobility work 1-2x/week** — Ipamorelin supports joint health and collagen synthesis; flexibility work complements this by maintaining joint range of motion
- **Avoid excessive endurance training** — sessions longer than 60 minutes at moderate intensity can elevate cortisol, which opposes GH signaling

### Sleep Optimization
- **Aim for 7-9 hours nightly** — Ipamorelin enhances deep sleep, but you must give it enough sleep opportunity to work. This is where the peptide truly shines
- **Keep your bedroom dark, cool (65-68°F), and quiet** — optimal conditions for both melatonin and GH secretion
- **Maintain a consistent sleep schedule** — go to bed and wake up at the same time daily, even on weekends. Circadian regularity maximizes the nocturnal GH pulse
- **Take your injection 15-20 minutes before lying down** — the onset of deep sleep shortly after injection creates the ideal hormonal cascade
- **Consider a magnesium glycinate supplement (300-400mg) before bed** — supports GABA activity, sleep quality, and GH release synergistically

### Daily Habits
- **Stay well hydrated** — aim for at least half your body weight in ounces of water daily. Dehydration impairs recovery and GH signaling
- **Manage stress proactively** — cortisol directly antagonizes GH. Although Ipamorelin does not raise cortisol itself, external stress can still blunt its benefits. Use meditation, walking, or breathwork
- **Get 15-20 minutes of morning sunlight** — anchors your circadian rhythm, optimizing the timing of your natural GH pulses
- **Track your sleep quality** — use a wearable or sleep app to monitor deep sleep duration. Ipamorelin should noticeably increase deep sleep within the first 1-2 weeks
- **Blood work every 8-12 weeks**: IGF-1, fasting glucose, fasting insulin, and a comprehensive metabolic panel
