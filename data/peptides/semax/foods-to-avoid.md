# Semax

## Foods to Avoid

- **Excess Refined Sugar**: Causes rapid blood glucose spikes followed by crashes that impair focus and memory. Chronic high sugar intake reduces BDNF expression — directly opposing Semax's mechanism
- **Processed Seed Oils (soybean, canola, corn, sunflower)**: High omega-6 content promotes neuroinflammation and disrupts the omega-3/omega-6 ratio critical for brain cell membrane health
- **Ultra-Processed Foods**: Additives, emulsifiers, and artificial ingredients damage the gut lining, disrupting the gut-brain axis and reducing the absorption of nutrients needed for neurotransmitter synthesis
- **Excessive Caffeine (>300mg/day)**: While moderate caffeine can complement Semax, excess caffeine overstimulates the dopaminergic system already modulated by Semax, causing anxiety, jitters, and sleep disruption
- **Alcohol**: Directly suppresses BDNF, kills neurons, impairs memory consolidation, and disrupts sleep architecture — counteracts every primary benefit of Semax
- **Trans Fats & Fried Foods**: Promote systemic inflammation that crosses into the brain and impairs synaptic plasticity
- **Artificial Sweeteners (aspartame, sucralose)**: May disrupt gut microbiome and neurotransmitter signaling pathways
- **High-Sodium Processed Foods**: Can impair cerebrovascular function and blood-brain barrier integrity
