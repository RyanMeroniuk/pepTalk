# Semax

## Regulatory Status

- **Approved pharmaceutical** in Russia and Ukraine (prescription drug for stroke, TBI, cognitive disorders, anxiety)
- **NOT FDA-approved** in US or EU
- Classified as a "research chemical" in the United States
- Legal to purchase for research purposes in US
- No WADA classification specifically for nasal administration
- Available from specialized peptide clinics and research suppliers
- Decades of clinical data in Russia with strong safety record
- Pre-mixed nasal sprays available from reputable vendors
