# Semax

## Foods to Eat

- **Wild-Caught Fatty Fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel)**: Rich in EPA/DHA omega-3s that are essential building blocks for neuronal membranes and amplify BDNF signaling — directly synergistic with Semax
- **Blueberries & Dark Berries**: Anthocyanins cross the blood-brain barrier and reduce neuroinflammation while improving memory consolidation
- **Eggs (whole, pastured)**: Excellent source of choline, the precursor to acetylcholine — Semax enhances cholinergic function, so adequate choline intake is essential
- **Walnuts**: High in ALA omega-3s and polyphenols that support cerebrovascular health and neurotransmitter function
- **Dark Chocolate (85%+ cacao)**: Flavonoids improve cerebral blood flow and BDNF expression; theobromine provides gentle stimulation complementary to Semax
- **Leafy Greens (spinach, kale, arugula)**: Rich in folate, lutein, and nitrates that support brain blood flow and methylation pathways critical for neurotransmitter synthesis
- **Avocado**: Monounsaturated fats support myelin sheath integrity and brain cell membrane fluidity
- **Turmeric/Curcumin**: Potent anti-neuroinflammatory; crosses BBB and upregulates BDNF independently
- **Green Tea**: L-theanine promotes alpha brain waves (calm focus) while EGCG provides neuroprotective antioxidant effects
- **Fermented Foods (kimchi, sauerkraut, kefir)**: Gut microbiome health directly influences brain function via the vagus nerve — the gut-brain axis is critical for optimizing nootropic peptide effects
- **Liver & Organ Meats**: Highest bioavailable source of B vitamins, choline, and minerals essential for neurotransmitter production
