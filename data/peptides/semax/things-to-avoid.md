# Semax

## Things to Avoid

- **Chronic Sleep Deprivation**: Without adequate sleep, BDNF-mediated neuroplasticity cannot consolidate. Sleep debt accumulates and cannot be "caught up" — it directly nullifies Semax's cognitive benefits
- **Chronic Unmanaged Stress**: Sustained elevated cortisol damages the hippocampus, reduces BDNF expression, and impairs the very neural pathways Semax is designed to support. Implement a stress management practice
- **Stacking Too Many Stimulants**: Combining Semax with high-dose caffeine, modafinil, and other stimulants simultaneously risks overstimulation, anxiety, and adrenal fatigue. Choose one complementary stimulant at moderate doses
- **Late-Day Dosing (after 3 PM)**: Semax's dopaminergic effects will disrupt sleep onset and reduce sleep quality, negating the neuroplasticity benefits that require deep sleep
- **Excessive Alcohol Use**: Even 2-3 drinks suppress BDNF for 24-48 hours and impair sleep architecture — directly counteracting Semax
- **Sedentary Lifestyle**: Physical inactivity drastically reduces baseline BDNF levels. Without exercise, you are leaving the most powerful Semax synergy on the table
- **Constant Digital Multitasking**: Fragmenting attention with notifications, social media, and task-switching trains the brain toward shallow processing, reducing the benefit of enhanced synaptic plasticity
- **Ignoring Gut Health**: The gut-brain axis is a major pathway for neurotransmitter precursors. Gut dysbiosis from poor diet or antibiotics can reduce serotonin and dopamine availability, limiting Semax's mood and cognitive effects
- **Skipping Hydration**: Dehydration impairs cognitive performance by up to 25% and is the primary cause of Semax-related headaches
