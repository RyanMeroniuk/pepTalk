# Semax

## Overview

Semax is a synthetic heptapeptide derived from the ACTH fragment (4-10), modified with a Pro-Gly-Pro C-terminal sequence for enhanced stability and brain penetration. Developed by Russian scientists at the Institute of Molecular Genetics in the 1980s. Approved as a pharmaceutical drug in Russia and Ukraine for stroke recovery, cognitive disorders, and neuroprotection. Unlike full ACTH, Semax delivers neurotrophic effects without hormonal/endocrine side effects — no cortisol stimulation. Primary administration is intranasal (nasal spray), which partially bypasses the blood-brain barrier via olfactory nerve pathways.
