# Semax

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Stack**: Selank — Russian anxiolytic peptide, pairs for calm focus without sedation
- **Stack**: DSIP — sleep optimization alongside daytime Semax focus
- **Stack**: BPC-157 — neuroprotection and inflammation during recovery
- **Supplement**: Krill oil / Omega-3 (EPA/DHA) — supports BDNF, reduces neuroinflammation
- **Supplement**: Ashwagandha (300-600mg KSM-66) — cortisol regulation, amplifies focus
- **Supplement**: Lion's Mane Mushroom (500-1000mg) — NGF support, neuroprotection
- **Supplement**: Magnesium L-Threonate (2g) — brain-specific magnesium for cognition
- **Supplement**: Alpha-GPC (300mg) — acetylcholine precursor for memory/focus
- **Supplement**: B-Complex — methylation and neurotransmitter support
- **Caution**: SSRIs/dopamine-targeting medications — theoretical interaction
- **Caution**: Stimulants (caffeine, modafinil) — may amplify overstimulation
- **Contraindicated**: Diabetes (may elevate blood glucose)
- **Contraindicated**: Uncontrolled hypertension
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy/breastfeeding
