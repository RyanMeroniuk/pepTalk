# Semax

## Mechanisms of Action

- **BDNF Upregulation**: Significantly increases brain-derived neurotrophic factor expression, supporting neuronal growth, synaptic plasticity, and learning
- **Dopamine/Serotonin Modulation**: Modulates dopaminergic and serotonergic signaling for motivation, focus, and emotional stability without overstimulation
- **Acetylcholine Enhancement**: Potentially increases acetylcholine availability in the brain, supporting memory and cognition
- **Neuroprotection**: Reduces oxidative stress and neuroinflammation, particularly beneficial during mental fatigue, overtraining, or sleep deprivation
- **Melanocortin Receptors**: May interact with MC3/MC4 receptors
- **Enkephalinase Inhibition**: May inhibit enkephalin-degrading enzymes, contributing to mood effects
- **No Cortisol Effect**: Unlike full ACTH, does not stimulate adrenal cortisol release
- **Immune Modulation**: Enhances antigen presentation, antibody production, and interferon pathways
