# Semax

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Consistent Sleep Schedule (7-9 hours)**: Sleep is when the brain consolidates memories and prunes synapses. Semax upregulates BDNF, but neuroplasticity requires sleep to solidify new neural connections. Go to bed and wake at the same time daily, including weekends
- **Aerobic Exercise (30-45 min, 4-5x/week)**: Running, cycling, swimming, or brisk walking independently raises BDNF levels by 200-300%. Combined with Semax's BDNF upregulation, exercise creates a powerful synergistic effect for neuroplasticity, learning, and mood
- **Resistance Training (2-3x/week)**: Strength training supports growth hormone release and reduces neuroinflammation, complementing Semax's neuroprotective mechanisms
- **Active Learning Practices**: Semax enhances synaptic plasticity — leverage this by engaging in deliberate learning during peak effect windows (15-90 minutes post-dose). Study new skills, read challenging material, or practice a musical instrument
- **Meditation & Mindfulness (10-20 min daily)**: Mindfulness practice increases gray matter density in the prefrontal cortex and hippocampus, amplifying Semax's cognitive benefits. Even brief sessions reduce cortisol, which otherwise impairs BDNF signaling
- **Cold Exposure (cold showers, 2-3 min)**: Brief cold exposure increases norepinephrine by 200-300%, synergizing with Semax's catecholamine modulation for alertness and focus
- **Limit Screen Time & Multitasking**: Constant task-switching fragments attention and reduces the deep focus states where Semax provides the most benefit. Use focused work blocks (25-50 min) with single-task commitment
- **Hydrate Aggressively (3+ liters daily)**: Dehydration is one of the most common causes of Semax-related headaches and impairs cognitive function by up to 25% even at mild levels
- **Limit Alcohol**: Alcohol is directly neurotoxic and suppresses BDNF expression — directly counteracting Semax's primary mechanism. Even moderate drinking impairs neuroplasticity for 24-48 hours
- **Morning Sunlight (10-15 min)**: Early light exposure sets circadian rhythm, optimizes cortisol awakening response, and supports the dopamine and serotonin systems that Semax modulates
- **Dose Timing**: Always dose in the morning or early afternoon. Semax's dopaminergic activity can disrupt sleep if taken after 3 PM
