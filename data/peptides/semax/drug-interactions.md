# Semax

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **SSRIs/SNRIs**: Theoretical serotonin interaction — use with caution
- **Dopaminergic drugs**: May amplify or interfere with dopamine modulation
- **Stimulants**: Risk of overstimulation when combined
- **Diabetes medications**: May affect blood glucose
- **Contraindicated**: Anxiety disorders (may paradoxically worsen in some individuals)
- **Contraindicated**: Neurological hypersensitivity history
