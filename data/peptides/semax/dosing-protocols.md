# Semax

## Dosing Protocols

- **Intranasal (Primary Route)**:
  - Standard: 300-600 mcg/day
  - Advanced: Up to 900 mcg/day (split into 2-3 doses)
  - Microdosing: 100-200 mcg, 1-3x daily
- **Concentration**: 0.1% solution for cognitive support; 1% for neurological conditions
- **Cycle**: 10-14 days on, minimum 7 days off
- **Timing**: Morning upon waking + midday during mental work. Optionally pre-workout. AVOID evening dosing (disrupts sleep)
- **Onset**: Effects manifest within 15-30 minutes of intranasal administration
- **Beginner**: Start with 100-200 mcg/day, titrate up gradually
- **Injectable**: Up to 1mg/kg (clinical settings only, provides additional analgesic benefits)
- **Preparation**: Reconstitute powder with bacteriostatic water (0.9% NaCl preferred)
- **Storage**: 36-46°F refrigerated; use within 20-30 days after reconstitution
