# Semax

## Side Effects

- **Common**: Mild nasal irritation (burning, dryness, congestion — ~10% of intranasal users)
- **Common**: Headache (usually dose-related or dehydration-linked; resolves with hydration)
- **Uncommon**: Restlessness or overstimulation (high doses or late-day dosing)
- **Uncommon**: Mood fluctuations from dopamine/serotonin modulation
- **Uncommon**: Nasal mucosa discoloration (~10% of users)
- **Rare**: Temporary blood pressure elevation (very high doses or stacked with stimulants)
- **Rare**: Sleep disruption in dopamine-sensitive individuals
- **Elevated blood glucose**: Reported in diabetic populations
- **Low toxicity profile**: No habit-forming potential
- **No negative effects** on major bodily systems or drug metabolism
- **Decades of clinical use in Russia** with minimal adverse events, even in stroke patients
