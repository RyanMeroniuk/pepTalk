# Peptide Stacking, Supplement & Lifestyle Master Guide

## Universal Lifestyle Guidelines

### For ALL Peptide Users
- Sleep 7-9 hours consistently
- Stay hydrated 2-3L water daily
- Manage stress (cortisol antagonizes many peptide benefits)
- Regular blood work: IGF-1, glucose, insulin, CBC, metabolic panel
- Work with a knowledgeable healthcare provider
- Source pharmaceutical-grade peptides only (research-grade has purity concerns)
- Store reconstituted peptides refrigerated (36-46°F)
- Use bacteriostatic water for reconstitution
- Rotate injection sites to prevent lipodystrophy
