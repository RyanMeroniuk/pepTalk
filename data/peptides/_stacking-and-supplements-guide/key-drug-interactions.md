# Peptide Stacking, Supplement & Lifestyle Master Guide

## Key Drug Interactions to Watch
- Diabetes medications + GH secretagogues = monitor glucose closely
- Blood thinners + BPC-157 = theoretical angiogenesis risk
- SSRIs/antidepressants + Semax = serotonin modulation overlap
- Immunosuppressants + immune peptides = opposing mechanisms
- Metformin + MOTS-c = dual AMPK activation, hypoglycemia risk
- GLP-1 agonists + oral medications = delayed absorption from slowed gastric emptying
