# Retatrutide

## Benefits

- **Dramatic Weight Loss**: Up to 24.2% body weight reduction in 48-week Phase II trial (highest of any weight loss drug in development)
- **Visceral Fat Reduction**: Significant reduction in abdominal and liver fat
- **Metabolic Improvement**: Improved glycemic control, insulin sensitivity, lipid profiles
- **Liver Health**: Substantial reduction in hepatic steatosis (fatty liver)
- **Blood Pressure**: Improvements in cardiovascular risk markers
- **Type 2 Diabetes**: Strong glucose-lowering effects
- **NASH/MASLD**: Potential treatment for non-alcoholic fatty liver disease
