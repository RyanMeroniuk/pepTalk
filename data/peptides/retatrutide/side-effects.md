# Retatrutide

## Side Effects

- **GI Effects** (most common): Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation — typical of GLP-1 class
- **Decreased Appetite**: Profound (therapeutic effect but can be excessive)
- **Injection Site Reactions**: Standard
- **Heart Rate Increase**: Small, similar to other GLP-1 agonists
- **Pancreatitis Risk**: Theoretical class risk (GLP-1 agonists)
- **Gallbladder Issues**: Increased risk with rapid weight loss
- **Muscle Loss**: Significant lean mass loss alongside fat loss — a concern shared with all GLP-1 drugs
- **Thyroid**: Theoretical medullary thyroid carcinoma risk (GLP-1 class warning from animal studies)
