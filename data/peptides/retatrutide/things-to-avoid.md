# Retatrutide

## Things to Avoid

- **Skipping Meals Entirely**: Even though appetite is dramatically suppressed, skipping meals accelerates muscle loss and nutrient deficiency. Force yourself to eat adequate protein even when not hungry
- **Lying Down After Eating**: Wait at least 30-60 minutes after meals before lying down. Slowed gastric emptying plus lying flat equals acid reflux and nausea. Take a gentle walk instead
- **Neglecting Resistance Training**: Cardio alone will not prevent the significant muscle loss that occurs with retatrutide. You MUST lift weights or do resistance exercises
- **Drinking Large Amounts of Fluid With Meals**: Drink water between meals, not during. Liquid fills the already slow-emptying stomach and causes bloating and early fullness before adequate protein is consumed
- **Crash Dieting On Top of the Drug**: Retatrutide already causes dramatic weight loss. Adding extreme caloric restriction leads to severe muscle wasting, nutrient deficiency, hair loss, gallstones, and metabolic adaptation. Eat at least your basal metabolic rate in calories
- **Ignoring GI Warning Signs**: Persistent vomiting, severe abdominal pain, or inability to eat for days warrants immediate medical attention — these can signal pancreatitis or bowel obstruction
- **Rapid Dose Escalation**: Increasing doses too quickly to chase faster results dramatically worsens GI side effects and does not meaningfully speed up fat loss. Follow the titration schedule
- **Sedentary Behavior**: Even outside of structured exercise, avoid prolonged sitting. Stand, walk, stretch — daily movement supports metabolic rate and GI motility
- **Ignoring Protein Targets**: This bears repeating — with up to 24% body weight loss possible, inadequate protein intake leads to significant muscle, bone, and metabolic damage
