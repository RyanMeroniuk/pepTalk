# Retatrutide

## Mechanisms of Action

- **GLP-1 Receptor Agonist**: Reduces appetite, slows gastric emptying, improves insulin secretion
- **GIP Receptor Agonist**: Enhances insulin sensitivity, complements GLP-1 effects on satiety and metabolism
- **Glucagon Receptor Agonist**: Increases energy expenditure, promotes hepatic fat oxidation, thermogenesis — the unique differentiator
- **Triple Mechanism**: The glucagon component drives additional fat burning and energy expenditure beyond what GLP-1/GIP alone achieves
- **Appetite Suppression**: Profound reduction in hunger and food intake
- **Hepatic Fat Reduction**: Glucagon receptor activation drives liver fat clearance
