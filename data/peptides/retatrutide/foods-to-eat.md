# Retatrutide

## Foods to Eat

- **Lean Proteins (prioritize at every meal)**: Chicken breast, turkey breast, wild-caught salmon, tilapia, cod, shrimp, eggs, egg whites, Greek yogurt (plain, non-fat), cottage cheese, lean ground turkey, bison, tofu, tempeh, edamame
- **Protein Supplements**: Whey protein isolate, casein protein (before bed), collagen peptides (for skin/joint support during rapid weight loss), bone broth
- **Complex Carbohydrates (small portions)**: Sweet potatoes, quinoa, oats, brown rice, lentils, chickpeas, black beans
- **Healthy Fats (moderate portions)**: Avocado, extra virgin olive oil, nuts (almonds, walnuts), seeds (chia, flaxseed, pumpkin), fatty fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel)
- **Vegetables (easy on digestion)**: Steamed or roasted broccoli, zucchini, spinach, kale, bell peppers, cucumber, carrots. Cooked vegetables are gentler on slowed digestion than raw
- **Fruits (low-sugar, easy to digest)**: Berries (blueberries, strawberries, raspberries), melon, kiwi, banana (ripe)
- **Gut-Soothing Foods**: Bone broth, ginger, fermented foods (kimchi, sauerkraut, kefir — small amounts), papaya
- **Hydration**: Water, herbal teas (ginger, peppermint, chamomile), electrolyte drinks (sugar-free)
