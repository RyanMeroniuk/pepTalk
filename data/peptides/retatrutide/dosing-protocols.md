# Retatrutide

## Dosing Protocols

- **Phase II Trial Doses**: Escalated from 0.5mg to up to 12mg weekly
- **Route**: Subcutaneous injection, once weekly
- **Dose Escalation**: Gradual increase over weeks to manage GI side effects
- **Highest Tested**: 12mg/week (produced 24.2% weight loss)
- **Not yet commercially available** — still in Phase III trials
- **Expected to follow** similar escalation protocols as tirzepatide/semaglutide
