# Retatrutide

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Protein-First Eating**: Eat protein first at every meal before carbs or fats. Aim for 1.6-2.2g protein per kg body weight daily — this is NON-NEGOTIABLE. Retatrutide causes the most dramatic weight loss of any GLP-1 drug, and up to 40% of weight lost can be lean muscle mass if protein intake is inadequate
- **Resistance Training 3-5x/Week**: Lift heavy to preserve muscle. Compound movements (squats, deadlifts, bench press, rows, overhead press) are most effective. Progressive overload is essential — do not just do cardio. Muscle loss accelerates without resistance training on triple agonists
- **Daily Step Count**: Aim for 8,000-10,000+ steps per day. Walking is gentle on the GI system and supports fat loss without the nausea that intense cardio can trigger
- **Meal Timing & Size**: Eat 4-6 small, protein-rich meals throughout the day rather than 2-3 large meals. Slowed gastric emptying means large meals sit in the stomach and cause severe nausea. Stop eating when you feel 80% full
- **Hydration**: Drink at least 80-100oz of water daily, but drink BETWEEN meals, not during. Liquid with food further fills the stomach and worsens nausea. Sip water throughout the day
- **Minimize "Retatrutide Face" (Facial Volume Loss)**: Rapid fat loss causes facial hollowing similar to "Ozempic face." Maintain adequate protein and calorie intake (do not crash diet on top of the drug), include healthy fats (avocado, olive oil, nuts), consider collagen peptides (10-15g/day), and use SPF daily. Facial exercises and hyaluronic acid skincare can help
- **Sleep 7-9 Hours**: Sleep deprivation increases cortisol, promotes muscle breakdown, increases hunger hormones, and impairs fat oxidation. Prioritize sleep hygiene
- **Manage GI Side Effects Proactively**: Ginger tea or ginger chews for nausea, peppermint tea for bloating, psyllium husk or magnesium citrate for constipation. Walk after meals to aid gastric motility
- **Track Body Composition**: Use a DEXA scan or bioimpedance scale, not just body weight. You need to know your lean mass is being preserved
- **Gallbladder Monitoring**: Rapid weight loss significantly increases gallstone risk. Report any right upper quadrant pain, especially after fatty meals, to your provider immediately
- **Stress Management**: Chronic stress elevates cortisol, which promotes visceral fat storage and muscle catabolism — directly counteracting the drug. Include meditation, breathwork, or yoga
