# Retatrutide

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Critical**: Adequate protein intake (1.6-2.2g/kg) to minimize muscle loss during rapid weight loss
- **Critical**: Resistance training to preserve lean mass
- **Enhanced by**: Creatine monohydrate for muscle preservation
- **Monitor**: Blood glucose, lipids, liver enzymes, pancreatic enzymes
- **Caution**: Diabetes medications (risk of hypoglycemia with combination)
- **Caution**: Oral medications (delayed gastric emptying affects absorption)
- **Contraindicated**: Personal/family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma, MEN2 syndrome
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy/breastfeeding
- **Contraindicated**: History of pancreatitis
