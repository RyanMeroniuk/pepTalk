# Retatrutide

## Overview

Retatrutide (LY3437943) is a novel triple agonist peptide developed by Eli Lilly that simultaneously activates three receptors: GLP-1 (glucagon-like peptide-1), GIP (glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide), and glucagon receptors. This triple mechanism makes it potentially the most powerful weight loss peptide in development — Phase II trials showed up to 24.2% body weight loss at 48 weeks, exceeding results from semaglutide (GLP-1 only) and tirzepatide (GLP-1/GIP dual). Currently in Phase III clinical trials.
