# Retatrutide

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT YET FDA-approved** — currently in Phase III clinical trials (Eli Lilly)
- Phase II results (2023) were the most impressive weight loss data ever published for a drug
- Expected FDA submission estimated 2025-2026
- Will likely be prescription-only, similar to tirzepatide (Mounjaro/Zepbound)
- Not available from compounding pharmacies (proprietary Eli Lilly molecule)
