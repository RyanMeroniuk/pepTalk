# Retatrutide

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Insulin and Sulfonylureas**: Significantly increased risk of hypoglycemia when combined with retatrutide's potent insulin-sensitizing effects across three receptor pathways. Dose reduction of diabetes medications is typically required; close blood glucose monitoring is essential
- **Oral Medications (general)**: Retatrutide profoundly slows gastric emptying, which can delay absorption and reduce peak concentrations of oral medications. Particularly relevant for drugs with narrow therapeutic windows (warfarin, levothyroxine, anticonvulsants, oral contraceptives)
- **Thyroid Medications (Levothyroxine)**: Delayed gastric emptying may reduce absorption. Take levothyroxine at least 60 minutes before retatrutide injection day meals; monitor TSH more frequently during dose escalation
- **Pancreatitis History**: Contraindicated in patients with a history of pancreatitis. GLP-1 receptor agonism carries a class-associated risk of acute pancreatitis. The triple-agonist mechanism may compound this risk
- **Medullary Thyroid Carcinoma / MEN2**: Absolute contraindication. GLP-1 agonists caused thyroid C-cell tumors in rodent studies. Personal or family history of MTC or Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia type 2 is a hard contraindication
- **GI Motility Drugs (Metoclopramide, Erythromycin)**: Conflicting mechanisms — prokinetic agents attempt to speed gastric emptying while retatrutide slows it. Combination may cause unpredictable GI effects
- **Other GLP-1 / GIP Agonists**: Do not combine with semaglutide, tirzepatide, liraglutide, or other incretin-based therapies. Overlapping receptor agonism increases adverse effects without established benefit
- **Pregnancy / Breastfeeding**: Absolutely contraindicated. Discontinue at least 2 months before planned conception due to long half-life. Effects on fetal development are unknown. Weight loss during pregnancy is harmful
- **Eating Disorders**: Use with extreme caution or avoid in patients with history of anorexia nervosa or restrictive eating disorders. Profound appetite suppression may trigger or worsen disordered eating patterns
