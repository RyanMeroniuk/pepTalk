# Retatrutide

## Foods to Avoid

- **High-Fat Greasy Foods**: Fried foods (fries, fried chicken, onion rings), fast food, heavy cream sauces, pizza, fatty cuts of red meat. Slowed gastric emptying means greasy food sits in your stomach for hours, causing severe nausea, bloating, and potential "dumping syndrome"
- **Carbonated Beverages**: Soda, sparkling water, beer. Carbonation creates gas in a stomach that is already emptying slowly, causing painful bloating, belching, and nausea
- **High-Sugar Foods**: Candy, pastries, donuts, ice cream, sugary cereals, fruit juice, regular soda. Blood sugar spikes and crashes worsen appetite dysregulation, and sugar provides no nutritional value when you can only eat small amounts
- **Large Portions of Anything**: Even healthy food in large quantities will cause nausea and discomfort. Retatrutide profoundly slows digestion
- **Alcohol**: Especially on an empty stomach or during dose escalation. Alcohol absorption is unpredictable with slowed gastric emptying, and it provides empty calories when nutrient density matters most
- **Very Spicy Foods**: Can worsen GI symptoms, especially nausea and acid reflux, which are already common
- **Tough, Fibrous Raw Vegetables in Large Amounts**: Raw broccoli, cauliflower, cabbage — these are hard to break down with slowed motility. Steam or cook them instead
- **Processed/Ultra-Processed Foods**: Chips, packaged snacks, deli meats, hot dogs. Low nutritional value; you need every calorie to count when appetite is suppressed
