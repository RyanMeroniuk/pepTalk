# Retatrutide

## Stacking Protocols

- **Triple vs Dual vs Single Comparison**: Retatrutide (4-12mg/week) is generally used as monotherapy — it already activates GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors. Stacking with semaglutide or tirzepatide is NOT recommended, as overlapping GLP-1/GIP agonism increases GI side effects and hypoglycemia risk without proportional benefit. Choose one: retatrutide for maximum weight loss, tirzepatide for strong loss with better tolerability, semaglutide for proven cardiovascular data
- **Metabolic Recomposition Stack**: Retatrutide (escalating dose per protocol) + Creatine Monohydrate (5g/day) + Whey Protein Isolate (30-50g/day) — maximizes fat loss from triple agonism while creatine and protein aggressively defend lean mass during rapid weight loss
- **Visceral Fat & Liver Stack**: Retatrutide (escalating dose) + NAD+ precursor (NMN 500mg/day or NR 300mg/day) — retatrutide's glucagon receptor activation drives hepatic fat oxidation while NAD+ supports mitochondrial function and liver cell energy metabolism
- **Metabolic Syndrome Stack**: Retatrutide (escalating dose) + Berberine (500mg 2x/day) + Omega-3 (2-4g EPA/DHA daily) — comprehensive metabolic support targeting insulin resistance, lipids, inflammation, and weight simultaneously. Monitor blood glucose closely as berberine also lowers glucose
- **Body Composition Preservation Stack**: Retatrutide (escalating dose) + HMB (3g/day) + Vitamin D3 (5000 IU/day) + Magnesium Glycinate (400mg/day) — HMB reduces muscle protein breakdown during caloric deficit, vitamin D supports muscle function, magnesium supports over 300 enzymatic processes including protein synthesis
