# Survodutide

## Benefits

- **MASH/NASH Improvement**: Leading candidate for metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis — Phase 2 showed high rates of MASH resolution without worsening of fibrosis, the key regulatory endpoint
- **Liver Fat Reduction**: Glucagon arm directly mobilizes and burns hepatic fat, often producing large reductions in liver fat content
- **Weight Loss**: Substantial body weight reduction in earlier-phase trials, driven by combined appetite suppression and increased energy expenditure
- **Increased Energy Expenditure**: Glucagon agonism raises metabolic rate, a mechanism GLP-1-only drugs lack
- **Glycemic Support**: GLP-1 arm supports glucose control, offsetting glucagon's glucose-raising tendency
- **Once-Weekly Dosing**: Long half-life allows weekly subcutaneous injection
