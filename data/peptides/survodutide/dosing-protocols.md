# Survodutide

## Dosing Protocols

- **Investigational — No Approved Dosing**: Survodutide is not approved; the following reflects clinical trial regimens, not medical guidance
- **Trial Doses**: Once-weekly subcutaneous dosing studied at target maintenance doses in the range of roughly 2.4mg to 6mg weekly, reached via gradual escalation
- **Route**: Subcutaneous injection, once weekly
- **Dose Escalation**: Stepwise monthly titration is essential to manage nausea and GI tolerability, as with all incretin-class agents
- **MASH vs Obesity Programs**: Phase 3 programs are run separately for MASH and for obesity; target doses and titration may differ between indications
- **Continuous Use**: As with other incretin agents, weight regain is expected upon discontinuation
