# Survodutide

## Overview

Survodutide (development code BI 456906) is an investigational dual agonist of the GLP-1 and glucagon receptors, developed by Boehringer Ingelheim in partnership with Zealand Pharma. The GLP-1 arm drives appetite suppression and glycemic control while the glucagon arm increases energy expenditure and directly targets liver fat — making it one of the leading clinical candidates for MASH (metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis, formerly NASH). It is administered as a once-weekly subcutaneous injection. Survodutide has advanced to Phase 3 trials for both MASH and obesity, with earlier-phase data showing substantial weight loss and high rates of MASH improvement without worsening of fibrosis. It is investigational and not approved by any regulatory agency.
