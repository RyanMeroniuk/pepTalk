# Survodutide

## Side Effects

- **GI Effects**: Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, and decreased appetite — most common, dose-dependent, and worst during escalation
- **Heart Rate Increase**: Glucagon agonism and the incretin class can modestly raise resting heart rate — monitored in trials
- **Decreased Appetite**: Therapeutic but can be excessive, risking inadequate intake
- **Muscle Loss Risk**: Rapid weight loss risks lean mass loss without adequate protein and resistance training
- **Glucose Fluctuations**: Glucagon arm can transiently raise glucose; net effect is usually controlled by the GLP-1 arm, but monitoring matters in diabetes
- **Injection Site Reactions**: Generally mild
- **Long-Term Safety Unknown**: Investigational agent — long-term safety and the glucagon-receptor risk profile are still being characterized
