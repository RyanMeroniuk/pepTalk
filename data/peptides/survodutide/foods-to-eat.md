# Survodutide

## Foods to Eat

- **Lean Proteins (eat FIRST)**: Chicken breast, turkey, wild-caught salmon, cod, shrimp, eggs and egg whites, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, lean ground turkey, tofu, tempeh, edamame
- **Liver-Supportive Foods**: Fatty fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel) for omega-3s, olive oil, cruciferous vegetables (broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts), leafy greens, coffee (associated with lower liver fat)
- **Protein Supplements**: Whey isolate shakes when solid food causes nausea, casein before bed, bone broth for soothing protein
- **Complex Carbohydrates (small portions after protein)**: Sweet potatoes, quinoa, oats, brown rice, lentils, chickpeas, black beans
- **Healthy Fats**: Avocado, extra virgin olive oil, almonds, walnuts, chia, flaxseed
- **Cooked Vegetables (easier to digest than raw)**: Steamed broccoli, roasted zucchini, sauteed spinach, cooked carrots, steamed green beans, mashed cauliflower
- **Low-Sugar Fruits**: Blueberries, strawberries, raspberries, cantaloupe, kiwi
- **Gut-Soothing Foods**: Bone broth, ginger, cooked oatmeal, plain rice, small amounts of fermented foods
