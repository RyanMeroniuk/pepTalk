# Survodutide

## Foods to Avoid

- **Alcohol**: Critically important with MASH/liver disease — alcohol directly drives hepatic fat and inflammation, undermines the drug's primary benefit, and absorption is unpredictable with slowed gastric emptying
- **High-Fructose and Added-Sugar Foods**: Soda, fruit juice, candy, baked goods, sweetened drinks. Fructose is preferentially converted to liver fat — directly counterproductive in MASH
- **High-Fat Greasy/Fried Foods**: Fries, fried chicken, fast food, cream-based sauces. The top trigger of nausea and vomiting on incretin agents and a contributor to liver fat
- **Refined Carbohydrates**: White bread, white rice, pastries, sugary cereals — promote liver fat and blood sugar swings
- **Carbonated Beverages**: Soda, seltzer, beer — trapped gas in a slow-emptying stomach causes bloating
- **Large Portion Sizes**: Even healthy food in large amounts causes nausea and bloating with delayed emptying
- **Very Spicy Foods**: Worsen reflux and nausea
- **Ultra-Processed Foods**: Low nutritional density and high in additives, sugar, and refined fat — counterproductive for both weight and liver
