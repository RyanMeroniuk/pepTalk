# Survodutide

## Regulatory Status

- **INVESTIGATIONAL**: Not approved by the FDA, EMA, or any regulatory agency
- Developed by Boehringer Ingelheim in partnership with Zealand Pharma (development code BI 456906)
- In Phase 3 development for MASH (metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis) and obesity
- Received regulatory breakthrough/fast-track-type designations for MASH in some jurisdictions, reflecting high unmet need
- Not legally available by prescription as an approved product; research-use-only sourcing carries quality and legal risk
- Long-term safety, including the glucagon-receptor profile, still being characterized
