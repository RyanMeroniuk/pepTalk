# Survodutide

## Mechanisms of Action

- **GLP-1 Receptor Agonist**: Mimics GLP-1 to suppress appetite, slow gastric emptying, enhance glucose-dependent insulin secretion, and suppress glucagon-driven hyperglycemia
- **Glucagon Receptor Agonist**: Activates hepatic glucagon receptors to increase energy expenditure and stimulate lipolysis and fat oxidation in the liver
- **Direct Liver Fat Reduction**: The glucagon arm directly mobilizes and burns hepatic fat, the mechanism behind its MASH/NASH efficacy
- **Increased Energy Expenditure**: Glucagon agonism raises metabolic rate, complementing the GLP-1 appetite-reduction effect for greater weight loss
- **Balanced Dual Agonism**: Engineered to balance glucagon's fat-burning benefits against its tendency to raise blood glucose, so net glycemic control is maintained or improved
- **Long Half-Life**: Acylated for albumin binding, enabling once-weekly subcutaneous dosing
