# Survodutide

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Liver-Supportive Nutrition**: Given the MASH/liver focus, prioritize a Mediterranean-style pattern — lean protein, olive oil, fatty fish, vegetables, and minimal added sugar and refined carbs, which independently reduce liver fat
- **Protein-First Eating**: Eat protein first and target 1.6-2.2g/kg/day to defend against muscle loss during rapid weight reduction
- **Resistance Training 3-5x/Week**: Progressive-overload compound lifts preserve lean mass far better than cardio alone and improve insulin sensitivity
- **Daily Walking**: 8,000-10,000+ steps per day supports fat loss, liver health, and GI motility without triggering nausea
- **Eliminate Alcohol**: Especially important with liver disease — alcohol directly worsens hepatic fat and inflammation, undermining the drug's MASH benefit
- **Small Frequent Meals**: Slowed gastric emptying means large meals cause nausea and bloating — eat slowly, stop at ~80% fullness
- **Hydration Between Meals**: 80-100oz water daily between meals so liquid does not fill the slow-emptying stomach
- **Sleep 7-9 Hours**: Poor sleep worsens insulin resistance and metabolic dysfunction
- **GI Symptom Management**: Ginger for nausea, peppermint for bloating, psyllium or magnesium citrate for constipation, gentle walks after meals
- **Track Liver and Metabolic Markers**: With provider oversight, monitor liver enzymes and metabolic panels to confirm benefit
