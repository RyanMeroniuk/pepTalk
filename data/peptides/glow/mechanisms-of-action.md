# GLOW Stack

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Angiogenesis (BPC-157 + TB-500)**: Both components promote VEGF-driven new blood vessel formation, increasing oxygen and nutrient delivery to damaged tissue
- **Growth Factor Upregulation (BPC-157)**: Increases EGF, FGF, and HGF expression to drive tissue regeneration
- **Actin Regulation & Cell Migration (TB-500)**: Sequesters actin monomers to enable migration of endothelial, progenitor, and myocardial cells to injury sites
- **Anti-Apoptotic Signaling (TB-500)**: Activates the Akt survival pathway, reducing cell death in stressed tissue
- **Collagen Synthesis & Skin Remodeling (GHK-Cu)**: Stimulates fibroblast collagen and elastin production, activates matrix metalloproteinases for tissue remodeling, and improves skin firmness
- **Copper Delivery (GHK-Cu)**: Delivers bioavailable copper, a cofactor for lysyl oxidase (collagen cross-linking) and antioxidant enzymes
- **Anti-Inflammatory (All Three)**: Collectively reduce pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-alpha, IL-6) and modulate inflammation without fully suppressing the healing cascade
- **GI Mucosal Repair (BPC-157)**: Maintains and repairs gut barrier integrity, useful when recovery involves GI stress
- **Hair Follicle Stimulation (TB-500 + GHK-Cu)**: Both support follicle stem cells and follicle regeneration, contributing to the blend's skin/hair reputation
- **Antioxidant Action (GHK-Cu + BPC-157)**: Reduce oxidative stress at injury sites
