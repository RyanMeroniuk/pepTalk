# GLOW Stack

## Overview

GLOW is a pre-mixed peptide blend, not a single peptide — it combines three well-known regenerative peptides into one vial: BPC-157 (10mg), TB-500/Thymosin Beta-4 (10mg), and GHK-Cu/copper peptide (50mg), for roughly 70mg of total peptide content. It is marketed as the "regenerative triad" for tissue repair, accelerated recovery, and skin and hair quality, combining BPC-157's localized healing, TB-500's systemic cell-migration and angiogenesis, and GHK-Cu's collagen-stimulating and skin-remodeling effects. The rationale is that the three peptides act on complementary repair pathways, producing broader recovery than any one alone. Evidence for GLOW is anecdotal and community-driven — no blend-specific clinical trials exist, and all support comes from studies of the individual components.
