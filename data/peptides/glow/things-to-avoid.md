# GLOW Stack

## Things to Avoid

- **Returning to Full Training Too Early**: The blend accelerates repair but new tissue needs time to mature. Avoid maximal loads, plyometrics, and sport-specific drills for the first 4-6 weeks
- **NSAIDs During Healing**: Ibuprofen, naproxen, and aspirin block the inflammatory phase needed for proper healing. The blend modulates inflammation without fully suppressing it — avoid NSAIDs unless medically necessary
- **Corticosteroid Injections**: Cortisone weakens tendons and suppresses the tissue repair the blend is designed to enhance — avoid at the same site
- **Smoking & Nicotine**: Nicotine's vasoconstriction reduces blood flow to healing tissue, directly opposing the angiogenic action of the BPC-157 and TB-500 components
- **Excess Copper Load**: Because the GHK-Cu component delivers copper, avoid stacking with additional high-dose copper supplements; those with Wilson's disease or copper metabolism disorders should avoid the blend
- **High-Dose Zinc Stacking**: Large zinc doses antagonize copper and may blunt the GHK-Cu component — keep zinc moderate and separate timing
- **Chronic Stress & Sleep Deprivation**: Both elevate cortisol, which breaks down connective tissue and impairs healing
- **Prolonged Ice/Cryotherapy**: Extended icing reduces blood flow and may impair the blend's cell-migration and angiogenic effects — limit to 10-15 minutes
- **Treating the Blend as Clinically Proven**: No trials have tested GLOW itself; treat dosing and benefits as experimental and community-derived
- **Use During Pregnancy/Breastfeeding**: Insufficient safety data — avoid entirely
