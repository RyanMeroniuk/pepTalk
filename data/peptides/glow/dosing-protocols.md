# GLOW Stack

## Dosing Protocols

- **Standard Blend Composition**: BPC-157 10mg + TB-500 10mg + GHK-Cu 50mg per vial (~70mg total peptide). Ratio is approximately 1:1:5 (BPC:TB:GHK)
- **Reconstitution**: Add 2-3mL bacteriostatic water to the 70mg vial. At 3mL, each 0.1mL (10 units on an insulin syringe) delivers roughly BPC-157 ~333mcg + TB-500 ~333mcg + GHK-Cu ~1.67mg
- **Common Protocol**: 15-25 units (0.15-0.25mL) subcutaneously once daily, often in the morning or near the area being targeted — delivers roughly 500-833mcg BPC-157, 500-833mcg TB-500, and 2.5-4.2mg GHK-Cu per dose
- **Daily Dosing Rationale**: BPC-157 favors daily dosing, while TB-500 is normally dosed 2-3x/week; the blend compromises toward daily low-dose administration to keep BPC-157 in range
- **Cycle**: Commonly run 4-8 weeks on, then 4-6 weeks off — limited by TB-500's recommendation not to exceed ~3 months continuous use
- **Injection Site**: Subcutaneous; can rotate sites or inject near the target area for BPC-157's localized benefit (TB-500 and GHK-Cu act more systemically)
- **Storage**: Refrigerate after reconstitution; GHK-Cu gives the solution a characteristic blue tint, which is normal
- **Caution**: Dosing is community-derived, not clinically validated. The fixed ratio means you cannot independently adjust one component without changing the others
