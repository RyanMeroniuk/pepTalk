# GLOW Stack

## Foods to Eat

- **Bone Broth**: Rich in collagen, glycine, proline, and glutamine — the building blocks the GHK-Cu and BPC-157 components use to repair tissue. Consume 1-2 cups daily
- **Collagen-Rich Foods**: Chicken skin, pork skin, oxtail, and fish with skin provide direct collagen precursors that pair with GHK-Cu's collagen-stimulating action
- **Wild-Caught Fatty Fish**: Salmon, sardines, and mackerel supply omega-3s (EPA/DHA) that reduce inflammation and support the repair process
- **Citrus & Bell Peppers**: High vitamin C content is critical for collagen cross-linking driven by the GHK-Cu component. Consume 2-3 servings daily
- **Organ Meats**: Liver and kidney are dense in copper, zinc, vitamin A, and B-vitamins for wound healing and collagen synthesis
- **Eggs (Whole)**: Complete protein with all essential amino acids plus choline for cell membrane repair
- **Dark Leafy Greens**: Spinach, kale, and Swiss chard provide vitamin C, vitamin K, folate, and magnesium for tissue repair pathways
- **Berries**: Blueberries, blackberries, and strawberries supply antioxidants that reduce oxidative stress at injury and remodeling sites
- **Shellfish & Oysters**: Provide bioavailable copper and zinc — but balance intake so zinc does not overwhelm copper status given GHK-Cu's copper delivery
- **Turmeric & Ginger**: Natural anti-inflammatories that complement the blend's cytokine modulation; pair turmeric with black pepper for absorption
- **Sweet Potatoes & Squash**: Beta-carotene (vitamin A) supports epithelial and connective tissue repair
