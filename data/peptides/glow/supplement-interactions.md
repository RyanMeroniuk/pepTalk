# GLOW Stack

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Synergistic**: KPV — adding a gut/skin anti-inflammatory arm turns GLOW into the KLOW blend
- **Synergistic**: Collagen peptides (10-15g/day) — critical substrate for the GHK-Cu-driven collagen synthesis
- **Synergistic**: Vitamin C (1000mg) — essential cofactor for collagen cross-linking, enhanced effect with GHK-Cu and BPC-157
- **Enhanced by**: Omega-3 fatty acids (2-3g EPA/DHA) for anti-inflammatory support
- **Enhanced by**: Adequate protein intake (1.6-2.2g/kg) to supply repair substrates
- **Enhanced by**: Glucosamine-chondroitin for joint and connective tissue protocols
- **Caution**: Zinc — high-dose zinc competes with copper absorption and may offset the GHK-Cu component; keep zinc moderate (under 30mg/day) and separate timing
- **Caution**: Blood thinners — theoretical bleeding risk from the angiogenesis-promoting components
- **Caution**: Immunosuppressants — theoretical interaction with TB-500's immune modulation
