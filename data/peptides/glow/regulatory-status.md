# GLOW Stack

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT FDA-approved** — none of the three components are FDA-approved, and the blend is not a recognized drug product
- Sold strictly as a research chemical blend — not a dietary supplement or approved medication
- No blend-specific clinical trials exist; all evidence comes from studies of the individual peptides (BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu)
- **Banned by WADA** for competitive athletes — BPC-157 and TB-500 are both prohibited
- BPC-157 has been placed on the FDA Category 2 bulk drug list (under evaluation for compounding)
- Available from research chemical suppliers; quality and exact ratios vary by vendor
- Legal to purchase for research purposes in most countries
