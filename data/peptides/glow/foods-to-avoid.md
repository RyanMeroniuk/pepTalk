# GLOW Stack

## Foods to Avoid

- **Alcohol**: Impairs protein synthesis, disrupts sleep-related growth hormone release, damages gut mucosa, and increases inflammation — counteracting all three components. Eliminate during protocols
- **Refined Sugar & High-Fructose Corn Syrup**: Drive inflammatory cytokine production and compete with vitamin C for cellular uptake, impairing the collagen synthesis the blend promotes
- **Processed Seed Oils**: Soybean, corn, canola, and sunflower oils promote omega-6-driven inflammation, opposing the blend's anti-inflammatory action
- **Trans Fats & Hydrogenated Oils**: Found in fried and packaged foods; increase systemic inflammation and impair cell membrane integrity needed for repair
- **Excess Sodium**: Promotes water retention and swelling that can worsen inflammation around healing tissue
- **Fried & Charred Foods**: Advanced glycation end products (AGEs) promote oxidative stress and impair healing and skin quality
- **High-Dose Zinc Supplements with Meals**: Excess zinc competes with the copper delivered by GHK-Cu; avoid pairing large zinc doses directly with the blend
- **Excess Caffeine**: More than 200mg/day can elevate cortisol and impair sleep, both of which slow tissue repair
- **Processed & Ultra-Processed Foods**: Emulsifiers and preservatives damage the gut barrier the BPC-157 component is helping repair
