# GLOW Stack

## Benefits

- **Comprehensive Tissue Repair**: Combines localized (BPC-157) and systemic (TB-500) healing for muscles, tendons, ligaments, and skin
- **Accelerated Recovery**: Community reports of faster recovery from training, injury, and surgery — driven by the combined angiogenic and cell-migration effects
- **Skin Quality**: GHK-Cu's collagen and elastin stimulation supports firmer skin, reduced fine lines, and improved wound appearance
- **Hair Support**: TB-500 and GHK-Cu both support hair follicle health and regeneration
- **Reduced Scarring**: TB-500 and GHK-Cu promote organized tissue remodeling rather than fibrotic scar formation
- **Joint & Connective Tissue**: Supports tendon-to-bone healing and connective tissue integrity
- **Gut Support**: BPC-157 component aids GI mucosal healing alongside systemic repair
- **Anti-Inflammatory**: Collective cytokine modulation supports recovery and reduced soreness
- **Note on Evidence**: All benefits are extrapolated from studies of the individual peptides. No clinical trials have tested the GLOW blend itself — evidence is anecdotal and community-based
