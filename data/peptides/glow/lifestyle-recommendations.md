# GLOW Stack

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Protein Prioritization**: Consume 1.6-2.2g protein per kg body weight daily to supply amino acids for the tissue and collagen synthesis the blend drives. Space across 4-5 meals for steady substrate availability
- **Progressive Rehabilitation**: With BPC-157 and TB-500 both promoting repair, support them with gentle controlled movement of recovering areas — start at 30-50% intensity and increase ~10% per week. Avoid maximal loads for the first 4-6 weeks
- **Sleep Optimization**: Target 7-9 hours. Deep-sleep growth hormone release compounds with the blend's repair mechanisms. Keep a cool, dark room and a consistent schedule
- **Skin Care for the GHK-Cu Component**: Support the skin/collagen benefits with daily sun protection, adequate hydration, and a vitamin C-rich diet. Avoid excessive sun exposure on areas you are trying to remodel
- **Stress Management**: Chronic cortisol impairs healing and breaks down connective tissue. Practice 10-15 minutes of daily breathwork or meditation; magnesium glycinate (300-400mg) at night supports sleep and recovery
- **Hydration**: Drink at least half your body weight in ounces of water daily — hydrated connective tissue heals and remodels more efficiently
- **Avoid Alcohol & Nicotine**: Both impair healing; nicotine's vasoconstriction directly opposes the blend's angiogenic action
- **Morning Sunlight**: 15-20 minutes of morning light supports circadian rhythm and vitamin D, both of which underpin healing cascades
