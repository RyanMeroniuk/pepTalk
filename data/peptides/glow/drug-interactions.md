# GLOW Stack

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Anticoagulants / blood thinners (warfarin, heparin, aspirin, clopidogrel, rivaroxaban)**: The BPC-157 and TB-500 components promote angiogenesis, which may increase bleeding risk or complicate anticoagulant therapy — monitor closely, caution advised
- **Immunosuppressants (tacrolimus, cyclosporine, mycophenolate)**: TB-500 modulates immune function and tissue repair; combining may cause unpredictable immune responses — caution
- **Corticosteroids (cortisone injections, oral steroids)**: Suppress tissue repair and weaken tendons, directly opposing the blend's healing mechanisms — avoid concurrent use, especially at the same site
- **NSAIDs (ibuprofen, naproxen)**: Suppress the inflammatory phase needed for healing and may reduce the blend's effectiveness — monitor
- **Anti-angiogenic / chemotherapy agents (bevacizumab)**: The blend's pro-angiogenic properties may theoretically counteract anti-cancer therapy — contraindicated during such treatment
- **Contraindicated: Active cancer or history of cancer** — the TB-500 component promotes cell migration, proliferation, angiogenesis, and anti-apoptotic signaling that may theoretically support tumor growth
- **Contraindicated: Pregnancy and breastfeeding** — insufficient safety data
- **Caution: Copper metabolism disorders (Wilson's disease)** — the GHK-Cu component delivers copper and is contraindicated in copper overload conditions
- **Timing note**: If stacking with other injectable peptides, keep injections separate (a few hours apart) per common community practice
