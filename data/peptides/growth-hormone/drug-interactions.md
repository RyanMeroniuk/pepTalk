# Growth Hormone (Somatropin)

## Drug Interactions

- **Insulin and sulfonylureas**: Caution — GH raises blood glucose and reduces insulin sensitivity, so doses of these glucose-lowering drugs often need upward adjustment; monitor glucose closely
- **Metformin and other oral antidiabetics**: Monitor — GH opposes their glucose-lowering effect, frequently requiring dose changes
- **Corticosteroids (prednisone, dexamethasone)**: Caution — glucocorticoids can blunt GH's growth-promoting effect, and GH can alter cortisol metabolism (may unmask central adrenal insufficiency); doses may need adjustment
- **Thyroid medications (levothyroxine, T3/T4)**: Monitor — GH can unmask central hypothyroidism; thyroid status should be assessed and treatment adjusted as needed
- **Oral estrogen / contraceptives**: Caution — oral estrogen reduces IGF-1 response to GH, so women on oral estrogen often need higher GH doses
- **Active cancer / chemotherapy**: Contraindicated — GH/IGF-1 elevation may promote tumor growth
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding**: Caution/contraindicated — safety not established; use only if clearly directed by a physician
- **Critical illness (acute respiratory failure, post-surgery, trauma)**: Contraindicated — high-dose GH increased mortality in critically ill patients
- **CYP450-metabolized drugs**: Monitor — GH may alter clearance of some drugs metabolized by cytochrome P450 enzymes
