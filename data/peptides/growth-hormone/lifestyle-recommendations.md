# Growth Hormone (Somatropin)

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Use only under medical supervision**: Somatropin is prescription-only; dosing should be titrated to IGF-1 levels by a physician, with regular monitoring
- **Monitor glucose and IGF-1 regularly**: GH is diabetogenic and raises IGF-1 — periodic fasting glucose, HbA1c, and IGF-1 testing are essential to stay in a safe range
- **Evening injection**: Often timed in the evening to loosely mimic the natural nocturnal GH rise, per product instructions
- **Resistance training**: Mechanical loading channels GH/IGF-1 toward lean tissue and recovery
- **Prioritize sleep (7–9 hours)**: Supports the GH/IGF-1 axis and overall recovery
- **Maintain insulin sensitivity**: Regular aerobic exercise, a low-glycemic whole-food diet, and adequate sleep counter GH's tendency to raise glucose
- **Watch for warning signs**: Carpal tunnel symptoms, swelling, joint pain, or rising glucose warrant a dose review with the prescriber
- **Avoid supraphysiologic dosing**: Pushing GH/IGF-1 above age-appropriate ranges for performance or anti-aging sharply increases acromegaly and metabolic risk
