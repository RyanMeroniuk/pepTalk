# Growth Hormone (Somatropin)

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Critical — glucose-support supplements (Berberine, Alpha Lipoic Acid, Magnesium)**: GH raises blood glucose and reduces insulin sensitivity; these may help support glucose control, but require physician oversight since they can also alter medication needs (magnesium 300–400mg/day)
- **Synergistic — Vitamin D3**: Supports the GH/IGF-1 axis, bone health, and metabolic function (2,000–5,000 IU/day)
- **Synergistic — Omega-3 Fatty Acids**: Improve insulin sensitivity and reduce inflammation, helping offset GH's metabolic effects (2–4g EPA/DHA daily)
- **Synergistic — Creatine Monohydrate**: Complements GH-supported lean mass and recovery (5g/day)
- **Enhanced by — Zinc**: Supports IGF-1 receptor function and production (15–30mg/day with copper balance)
- **Synergistic — Collagen Peptides**: Supports connective tissue alongside GH-driven collagen turnover (10–15g/day)
- **Monitor — anything affecting blood glucose**: Because GH is diabetogenic, track glucose when adding or removing glucose-active supplements
- **Caution — high-dose IGF-axis stimulants**: Layering additional growth-factor supplements/peptides amplifies IGF-1 and its associated risks
