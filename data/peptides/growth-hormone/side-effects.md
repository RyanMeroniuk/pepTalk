# Growth Hormone (Somatropin)

## Side Effects

- **Insulin resistance / elevated glucose**: GH is counter-regulatory to insulin; can raise blood glucose, reduce insulin sensitivity, and unmask or worsen diabetes — a primary risk
- **Acromegaly-like changes (chronic/high dose)**: Enlargement of hands, feet, facial features, jaw, and soft tissues, plus organ enlargement, with sustained supraphysiologic GH/IGF-1
- **Fluid retention and edema**: Common early on — peripheral swelling, often dose-related
- **Carpal tunnel syndrome**: Fluid retention can compress the median nerve, causing wrist/hand numbness and pain
- **Joint and muscle pain (arthralgia/myalgia)**: Frequently reported, especially during titration
- **Headache and intracranial hypertension (rare)**: Including rare benign intracranial hypertension, particularly in children
- **Theoretical cancer/proliferation risk**: Elevated IGF-1 is a growth signal; GH is contraindicated in active malignancy
- **Gynecomastia, paresthesia**: Less common effects reported with therapy
- **Note**: Side effects scale with dose; supraphysiologic non-medical use markedly increases acromegaly and metabolic risks compared with monitored replacement dosing
