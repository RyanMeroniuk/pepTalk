# Growth Hormone (Somatropin)

## Regulatory Status

- **FDA-APPROVED** recombinant human growth hormone — the actual hormone, not a secretagogue
- **Approved indications include**: pediatric and adult growth hormone deficiency, Turner syndrome, Prader-Willi syndrome, chronic kidney disease in children, short stature/small-for-gestational-age, idiopathic short stature, and AIDS wasting (varies by brand)
- **Brands**: Genotropin, Norditropin, Humatrope, Omnitrope, and others (Omnitrope was an early biosimilar)
- **Prescription-only**: Dispensed through pharmacies under physician supervision with IGF-1 and glucose monitoring
- **Legally restricted for non-medical use**: In the U.S., distribution/possession of GH for non-approved uses (e.g., anti-aging, athletic performance) is specifically prohibited by law beyond ordinary prescription rules
- **Banned in sport**: Prohibited by WADA at all times; detectable via specialized testing
- **Counterfeit risk**: Black-market "HGH" is commonly faked, underdosed, or contaminated
- **Bottom line**: A legitimate, regulated medicine for defined conditions — but illegal and risky when used off-label without a prescription
