# Growth Hormone (Somatropin)

## Foods to Avoid

- **Added sugars and high-fructose corn syrup**: GH already raises blood glucose and reduces insulin sensitivity — sugary foods compound the diabetogenic risk
- **Refined carbohydrates (white bread, white rice, pastries)**: Spike blood glucose and insulin, worsening GH-induced insulin resistance
- **Sugary beverages including fruit juice and soda**: Rapidly absorbed liquid sugar that strains glucose control
- **Alcohol**: Disrupts sleep and glucose regulation and adds empty calories, working against metabolic goals
- **Trans fats and fried foods**: Pro-inflammatory and worsen insulin sensitivity
- **Ultra-processed and fast foods**: Drive inflammation and glucose dysregulation
- **Excess sodium from processed foods**: GH can cause fluid retention; high sodium may worsen edema
- **Very large high-glycemic meals**: Large glucose loads are harder to manage given GH's counter-insulin effect
