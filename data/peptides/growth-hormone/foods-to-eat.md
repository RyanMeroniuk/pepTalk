# Growth Hormone (Somatropin)

## Foods to Eat

- **Lean protein (chicken, turkey, fish, lean beef)**: Supplies amino acids for the protein synthesis GH/IGF-1 supports, without excess saturated fat
- **Wild-caught salmon and mackerel**: Omega-3 fatty acids improve insulin sensitivity, helping counter GH's diabetogenic effect
- **Leafy greens and cruciferous vegetables**: Low-glycemic, fiber-rich, and nutrient-dense — support stable blood sugar
- **Berries**: Low-glycemic fruit with polyphenols that support metabolic and vascular health
- **Avocado and extra virgin olive oil**: Monounsaturated fats that support insulin sensitivity and satiety
- **Legumes (lentils, chickpeas)**: Fiber and protein that blunt glucose spikes — useful given GH's glucose effect
- **Nuts and seeds (almonds, walnuts, pumpkin seeds)**: Magnesium, zinc, and healthy fats supporting metabolic health
- **Whole intact grains (oats, quinoa)**: Lower-glycemic complex carbohydrates to fuel training while limiting glucose spikes
- **Greek yogurt and cottage cheese**: Protein and calcium supporting lean tissue and bone
- **Cinnamon, vinegar-based dressings**: May modestly support post-meal glucose control
