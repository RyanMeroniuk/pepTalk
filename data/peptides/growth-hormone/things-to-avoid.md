# Growth Hormone (Somatropin)

## Things to Avoid

- **Non-prescription/illegal use**: Somatropin is prescription-only; obtaining or using it without a prescription is illegal and bypasses essential medical monitoring
- **Supraphysiologic dosing**: Pushing GH/IGF-1 above age-appropriate ranges sharply increases the risk of acromegaly-like changes, insulin resistance, and organ enlargement
- **Use with active cancer**: GH/IGF-1 is a growth signal and is contraindicated in active malignancy
- **Use with uncontrolled diabetes**: GH worsens insulin resistance and can destabilize glucose control — requires careful medical management
- **Ignoring glucose monitoring**: Skipping glucose/HbA1c checks risks missing GH-induced hyperglycemia
- **Use during pregnancy/breastfeeding**: Not established as safe — avoid unless directed by a physician
- **Use in critical illness/acute respiratory failure**: High-dose GH in critically ill patients has been linked to increased mortality — a recognized contraindication
- **Gray-market/counterfeit "HGH"**: Black-market vials are frequently underdosed, contaminated, or fake, adding danger on top of the legal risk
- **Ignoring warning signs**: Carpal tunnel symptoms, persistent swelling, joint pain, or rising glucose warrant immediate prescriber review
