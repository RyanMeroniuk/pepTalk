# Growth Hormone (Somatropin)

## Benefits

- **Corrects GH deficiency**: In diagnosed pediatric and adult GH deficiency, restores normal growth, body composition, energy, and quality of life — the core evidence-based benefit
- **Increased linear growth (children)**: Promotes height gain in children with GH deficiency, Turner syndrome, Prader-Willi, and other approved pediatric indications
- **Improved body composition**: Reduces fat mass (especially visceral) and increases lean mass in deficient adults
- **Bone density**: Supports bone mineral density in adults with documented deficiency
- **AIDS wasting**: FDA-approved to counter wasting/cachexia, preserving lean body mass
- **Recovery and connective tissue**: Supports collagen turnover and tissue repair via elevated GH/IGF-1
- **Important framing**: These benefits are established in deficiency and specific approved conditions. In GH-replete healthy adults using it for anti-aging or performance, the benefit-to-risk balance is unfavorable and such use is off-label, illegal without prescription, and banned in sport
