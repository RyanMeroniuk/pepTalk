# Cagrilintide

## Things to Avoid

- **Skipping Meals**: Even with suppressed appetite, you must eat. Skipping accelerates muscle loss, nutrient deficiency, and fatigue. Protein shakes count if solid food feels impossible
- **Lying Down After Eating**: Wait 30-60 minutes before reclining — slowed emptying plus gravity worsens reflux and nausea. Take a gentle walk instead
- **Relying Only on Cardio**: Cardio burns calories but does not prevent muscle loss. Resistance training is required for muscle preservation
- **Drinking Large Volumes With Meals**: Fills the slow-emptying stomach, causing bloating and crowding out protein. Sip small amounts, hydrate between meals
- **Crash Dieting on Top of the Drug**: The agent already creates a strong caloric deficit; extreme restriction risks muscle wasting, deficiencies, and gallstones
- **Rapid Dose Escalation**: Jumping doses to speed weight loss sharply worsens GI side effects without better long-term outcomes — follow gradual titration
- **Ignoring Persistent GI Symptoms**: Severe vomiting, inability to keep down food/water for 24+ hours, or severe abdominal pain require medical attention
- **Combining With GLP-1 Agonists Casually**: Cagrilintide is studied with semaglutide as the fixed-dose CagriSema, not as ad-hoc self-combination — uncontrolled stacking compounds GI and hypoglycemia risk
- **Treating It as Approved Therapy**: Cagrilintide is investigational; long-term safety is not established
