# Cagrilintide

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Amylin Receptor Agonist**: Mimics the pancreatic hormone amylin, acting on amylin and calcitonin receptor complexes in the hindbrain (area postrema) to promote satiety
- **Appetite Suppression**: Reduces food intake via central satiety signaling, complementary to and distinct from GLP-1 pathways
- **Slowed Gastric Emptying**: Delays stomach emptying, prolonging fullness after meals
- **Glucagon Suppression**: Reduces post-meal glucagon secretion, supporting glucose control
- **Long Half-Life**: Engineered with fatty-acid acylation for albumin binding, enabling once-weekly subcutaneous dosing
- **Complementary to GLP-1**: When combined with semaglutide (CagriSema), the amylin and GLP-1 mechanisms appear additive for appetite and weight reduction
