# Cagrilintide

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Protein-First Eating**: Eat protein first at every meal and target 1.6-2.2g/kg/day. Amylin analogs suppress appetite strongly, so the limited food you do eat must prioritize protein to defend against muscle loss
- **Resistance Training 3-5x/Week**: Non-negotiable on any potent weight-loss agent. Progressive-overload compound lifts preserve lean mass far better than cardio alone
- **Daily Walking**: 8,000-10,000+ steps per day supports fat loss, insulin sensitivity, and GI motility (helps with constipation) without triggering the nausea high-intensity cardio can cause
- **Small Frequent Meals**: Slowed gastric emptying means large meals cause nausea and bloating. Eat slowly, stop at ~80% fullness
- **Hydration Between Meals**: 80-100oz water daily, but between meals rather than with food, so liquid does not fill the slow-emptying stomach
- **Sleep 7-9 Hours**: Poor sleep raises ghrelin and cortisol, undermining appetite control and metabolic benefits
- **GI Symptom Management**: Ginger for nausea, peppermint for bloating, psyllium or magnesium citrate for constipation, gentle walks after meals
- **Body Composition Tracking**: Track with DEXA, bioimpedance, or tape measurements rather than scale weight alone to confirm fat loss, not muscle loss
- **Plan for Discontinuation**: Build durable habits while on the drug — weight regain is expected after stopping
