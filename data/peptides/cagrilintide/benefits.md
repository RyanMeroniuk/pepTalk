# Cagrilintide

## Benefits

- **Weight Loss (Combination)**: CagriSema (cagrilintide + semaglutide) produced roughly 20-23% body weight reduction in the REDEFINE Phase 3 program — among the highest reported for an injectable regimen
- **Weight Loss (Monotherapy)**: Standalone cagrilintide produced meaningful weight reduction in earlier-phase trials, less than the combination but mechanistically independent
- **Appetite Control**: Strong satiety effect via amylin signaling, complementary to GLP-1
- **Glycemic Support**: Glucagon suppression and slowed gastric emptying support post-meal glucose
- **Once-Weekly Dosing**: Long half-life allows weekly subcutaneous injection
- **Complementary Mechanism**: Adds a non-GLP-1 lever, potentially helpful for those who plateau on GLP-1 alone (investigational, not established clinical practice)
