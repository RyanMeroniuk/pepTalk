# Cagrilintide

## Side Effects

- **GI Effects**: Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, and constipation — most common, typically dose-dependent and worst during escalation (similar profile to GLP-1 agonists)
- **Decreased Appetite**: Therapeutic but can be excessive, risking inadequate intake
- **Injection Site Reactions**: Mild redness or irritation
- **Muscle Loss Risk**: As with any rapid weight-loss agent, lean mass loss is a concern without adequate protein and resistance training
- **Hypoglycemia Risk**: Low alone, but increased when combined with insulin or sulfonylureas
- **Long-Term Safety Unknown**: As an investigational agent, long-term safety data are limited compared to approved GLP-1 drugs
- **Gallbladder**: Rapid weight loss generally increases gallstone risk
