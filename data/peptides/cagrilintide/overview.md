# Cagrilintide

## Overview

Cagrilintide (development code AM833) is a long-acting amylin analog developed by Novo Nordisk for weight management. Amylin is a pancreatic hormone co-secreted with insulin that promotes satiety, slows gastric emptying, and suppresses glucagon. Cagrilintide is engineered for once-weekly subcutaneous dosing and is most prominent as the amylin component of CagriSema, a fixed-dose co-formulation with semaglutide that has produced roughly 20% body weight reduction in the Phase 3 REDEFINE trial program. As a monotherapy it has shown meaningful standalone weight loss in earlier-phase studies. It is investigational and not approved by any regulatory agency; it matters because it represents a mechanistically distinct (amylin) lever that complements the GLP-1 class.
