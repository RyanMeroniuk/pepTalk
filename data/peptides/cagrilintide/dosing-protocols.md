# Cagrilintide

## Dosing Protocols

- **Investigational — No Approved Dosing**: Cagrilintide is not approved; all dosing below reflects clinical trial regimens, not medical guidance
- **Monotherapy (trials)**: Escalated weekly subcutaneous dosing studied up to 4.5mg once weekly, with gradual titration to manage GI tolerability
- **CagriSema (trials)**: Fixed-dose combination targeting cagrilintide 2.4mg + semaglutide 2.4mg once weekly, reached via monthly dose escalation
- **Route**: Subcutaneous injection, once weekly
- **Dose Escalation**: Gradual monthly titration is essential to manage nausea and GI effects, mirroring the GLP-1 class
- **Continuous Use**: As with GLP-1 agonists, weight regain is expected upon discontinuation
