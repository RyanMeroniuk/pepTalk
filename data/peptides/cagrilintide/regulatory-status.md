# Cagrilintide

## Regulatory Status

- **INVESTIGATIONAL**: Not approved by the FDA, EMA, or any regulatory agency
- Developed by Novo Nordisk (development code AM833)
- Most advanced as the amylin component of CagriSema (with semaglutide) in the Phase 3 REDEFINE program
- Also studied as monotherapy in earlier-phase trials
- Not legally available by prescription as an approved product; research-use-only sourcing carries quality and legal risk
- Long-term safety data limited compared to approved GLP-1 agonists
