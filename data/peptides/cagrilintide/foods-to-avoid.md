# Cagrilintide

## Foods to Avoid

- **High-Fat Greasy/Fried Foods**: Fries, fried chicken, fast food burgers, pizza, cream-based sauces. The leading trigger of nausea and vomiting on appetite-suppressing weight-loss agents — slowed gastric emptying means greasy food sits for hours
- **Carbonated Beverages**: Soda, sparkling water, seltzer, beer. Carbonation creates trapped gas in a slow-emptying stomach, causing bloating and distension
- **High-Sugar Foods and Drinks**: Candy, cookies, cake, ice cream, sugary cereals, fruit juice, sweetened coffee drinks. Empty calories when appetite and intake are limited
- **Alcohol**: Absorption is unpredictable with slowed gastric emptying, adds empty calories, worsens nausea and dehydration
- **Large Portion Sizes**: Even healthy food in large amounts causes nausea and bloating with delayed emptying
- **Very Spicy Foods**: Worsen reflux and nausea, which are already common
- **Raw Fibrous Vegetables in Large Amounts**: Hard to digest with slowed motility — cook vegetables instead
- **Tough Fatty Red Meats**: Slow to digest and often trigger nausea — choose lean, tender cuts
