# Cagrilintide

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Critical**: High protein intake (1.6-2.2g/kg/day) to minimize muscle loss during rapid weight reduction
- **Synergistic**: Creatine monohydrate (5g/day) for lean mass preservation during a caloric deficit
- **Enhanced by**: Collagen peptides (10-15g/day) to support skin elasticity during rapid fat loss
- **Synergistic**: Omega-3 (2-4g EPA/DHA) for cardiovascular and anti-inflammatory support
- **Recommended**: Magnesium and B-complex to support energy and electrolyte balance when intake is reduced
- **Caution**: Berberine or other glucose-lowering supplements may compound glycemic effects — monitor blood glucose
- **Caution**: Oral supplements and medications may have delayed absorption due to slowed gastric emptying — separate timing where possible
