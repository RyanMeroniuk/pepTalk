# Hexarelin

## Foods to Avoid

- **Sugar and refined carbs within 2 hours of injection** — insulin directly suppresses GH release. Do not blunt hexarelin's unmatched GH pulse with poorly timed carbohydrates
- **High-fat meals near injection** — free fatty acids independently suppress pituitary GH secretion
- **Alcohol** — suppresses GH release (up to 75%), disrupts deep sleep (where hexarelin's value is greatest), elevates cortisol (compounding hexarelin's existing cortisol effect), and impairs recovery
- **Caffeine in excess** — more than 2 cups of coffee or after noon. Caffeine elevates cortisol; hexarelin already does this. The combination can create an unfavorable cortisol environment and disrupt sleep
- **Highly processed foods** — inflammatory, high-sodium (worsens water retention), and devoid of nutrients needed for hormone optimization
- **Excess sodium** — hexarelin causes more water retention than other GHRPs at equivalent doses. High sodium from processed foods, canned goods, and restaurant meals exacerbates this significantly
- **Soy products in excess** — phytoestrogens may compound prolactin-related concerns in sensitive individuals
- **Sugary beverages** — insulin spike plus zero satiety. The worst food category for any GH secretagogue user
