# Hexarelin

## Dosing Protocols

- **Start**: 100 mcg once daily (bedtime) during Week 1 to assess tolerance
- **Standard**: 100 mcg 2-3x daily, weeks 2-16
- **Conservative dosing recommended**: Higher doses don't proportionally increase GH but accelerate desensitization
- **Cycle**: 4-8 weeks with extended off-periods — OR — 12-16 weeks followed by mandatory 4-6 week break
- **Desensitization**: Hexarelin causes receptor desensitization faster than other GHRPs due to intense activation
- **Timing**: Fasted state, before bed or post-workout
