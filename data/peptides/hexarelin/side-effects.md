# Hexarelin

## Side Effects

- **Cortisol elevation**: More pronounced than other GHRPs
- **Prolactin elevation**: More pronounced than other GHRPs
- **Appetite increase**: Moderate (less than GHRP-6)
- **Water retention**: Significant at higher doses
- **Receptor desensitization**: Faster than other GHRPs — requires careful cycling
- **Carpal tunnel symptoms**: From extreme GH/IGF-1 elevation
- **Injection site reactions**: Standard
