# Hexarelin

## Things to Avoid

- **Using hexarelin as a first GHRP** — hexarelin is the most potent but also the least forgiving GH secretagogue. Start with Ipamorelin (cleanest) or GHRP-2 (moderate) to learn how your body responds to GH elevation before using hexarelin
- **Increasing dose to overcome desensitization** — when hexarelin's effects seem to diminish (typically 4-8 weeks), the correct response is to cycle off, not increase the dose. Higher doses only accelerate desensitization and amplify cortisol/prolactin
- **Running hexarelin continuously without breaks** — unlike Ipamorelin, hexarelin requires strict cycling. Continuous use leads to receptor desensitization that can take months to recover from
- **Ignoring cortisol and prolactin labs** — hexarelin's cortisol and prolactin elevation is its primary limitation. Without monitoring, chronic elevation manifests as fatigue, irritability, decreased libido, water retention, and impaired recovery — the opposite of what users seek
- **Eating within 2 hours of injection** — hexarelin produces the strongest GH pulse available. Blunting it with food is the most wasteful mistake possible with this peptide
- **Alcohol** — triply problematic with hexarelin: (1) suppresses GH, (2) elevates cortisol on top of hexarelin's existing cortisol effect, (3) disrupts the deep sleep that makes bedtime dosing so effective
- **Chronic high-intensity training without rest** — overtraining elevates cortisol. Combined with hexarelin's cortisol stimulation, this creates a strongly catabolic hormonal environment despite high GH levels
- **Stacking with GHRP-6** — both significantly elevate cortisol and prolactin. Combined use creates excessive hormonal burden without proportional GH benefit
- **Combining with other cortisol-elevating compounds or practices** — chronic stress, excessive caffeine, sleep deprivation, and overtraining all raise cortisol. With hexarelin's existing cortisol effect, any additional sources tip the balance unfavorably
- **Skipping the cycle-off period** — during the mandatory break (4-6 weeks minimum after a cycle), switch to Ipamorelin for continued GH support while allowing ghrelin receptors to re-sensitize
- **Exceeding 100mcg per injection for most users** — hexarelin's potency means 100mcg is sufficient for near-maximal GH release. Going higher primarily increases cortisol, prolactin, and desensitization rate
