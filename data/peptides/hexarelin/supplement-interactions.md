# Hexarelin

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Stack**: CJC-1295 no DAC for even greater GH release (use with caution)
- **Monitor**: Cortisol, prolactin, IGF-1, glucose regularly
- **Cycle strictly**: More important with Hexarelin than any other GHRP
- **Caution**: Diabetes medications
- **Contraindicated**: Active cancer, pregnancy/breastfeeding
- **Not recommended for beginners**: Start with Ipamorelin or GHRP-2 first
