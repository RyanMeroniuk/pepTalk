# Hexarelin

## Lifestyle Recommendations


### Injection Timing & Fasting
- **Inject strictly fasted** — minimum 2 hours after last meal. Hexarelin produces the strongest GH pulse of any secretagogue; do not waste it with food-induced insulin suppression
- **Bedtime injection is optimal** — amplifies the natural nocturnal GH surge. The combination of hexarelin's extreme potency with deep sleep GH release creates the highest GH peaks achievable with peptides
- **Start with 100mcg once daily (bedtime only) for the first week** — hexarelin's extreme potency means side effects (cortisol, prolactin, water retention) must be assessed before escalating
- **Wait 20-30 minutes after injection before consuming anything** — allow the GH pulse to reach its peak
- **Conservative dosing is critical** — higher doses do not proportionally increase GH but dramatically accelerate receptor desensitization, which is hexarelin's primary limitation

### Exercise
- **Resistance training 4-5x/week** — hexarelin's extreme GH elevation creates the most powerful anabolic environment of any GHRP. Compound movements (squats, deadlifts, bench, rows, overhead press) are essential to capitalize on this
- **Moderate rest periods (60-90 seconds)** — lactic acid from shorter rest independently stimulates additional GH, stacking with hexarelin's already-massive pulse
- **HIIT 1-2x/week** — brief, intense sessions (15 minutes of sprints or kettlebell complexes). Limit frequency because hexarelin already elevates cortisol; excessive intense exercise compounds this
- **Recovery days are mandatory** — hexarelin enhances recovery capacity, but the cortisol elevation means overtraining risk is higher than with cleaner GHRPs like Ipamorelin. Take 2-3 rest days per week
- **Avoid extended endurance training** — 60+ minutes of moderate cardio significantly elevates cortisol. Combined with hexarelin's existing cortisol stimulation, this creates an unfavorable catabolic environment
- **Focus on strength and hypertrophy** — hexarelin's extreme GH/IGF-1 elevation is best utilized for maximal muscle growth and strength, not endurance activities

### Managing Cortisol and Prolactin (Critical for Hexarelin)
- **Hexarelin elevates cortisol more than any other GHRP** — this is the primary trade-off for its unmatched GH potency. Active cortisol management is essential
- **Adaptogenic herbs**: Ashwagandha (300-600mg KSM-66 extract daily) has demonstrated cortisol-lowering effects in clinical trials. Phosphatidylserine (400-800mg) also blunts cortisol response
- **Vitamin C (1-2g daily)** — research shows high-dose vitamin C reduces cortisol output, particularly around exercise
- **Prolactin management**: Vitamin B6 in the P-5-P (pyridoxal-5-phosphate) form, 50-100mg daily, helps modulate prolactin levels. This is important for hexarelin users because prolactin elevation can cause fatigue, low libido, and mood changes
- **Zinc (30-50mg daily)** — supports testosterone production and helps counteract prolactin elevation
- **If cortisol or prolactin become problematic despite supplementation**, reduce hexarelin dose or cycle off. These elevations are dose-dependent and reversible

### Cycling Protocol (Critical for Hexarelin)
- **Hexarelin causes receptor desensitization faster than any other GHRP** — this is its biggest limitation. The ghrelin receptor downregulates in response to hexarelin's intense activation
- **Conservative cycle**: 4-8 weeks on, 4-6 weeks off. During the off-period, switch to Ipamorelin to maintain GH support without further desensitizing the receptor
- **Extended cycle**: 12-16 weeks maximum, followed by mandatory 6-8 week break
- **Watch for diminishing returns** — if the "rush" or noticeable effects decrease after 4-6 weeks, the receptor is desensitizing. This is the signal to cycle off rather than increase the dose
- **Do NOT increase dose to overcome desensitization** — this accelerates the problem and amplifies cortisol/prolactin side effects

### Sleep Optimization
- **7-9 hours of sleep nightly** — hexarelin's GH pulse during sleep is the most powerful of any GHRP. Insufficient sleep wastes this advantage
- **Consistent sleep schedule** — same bedtime and wake time daily. Circadian regularity maximizes the nocturnal GH surge that hexarelin amplifies
- **Cool, dark, quiet bedroom (65-68°F)** — standard sleep optimization that supports GH secretion
- **Magnesium glycinate (400mg) before bed** — supports sleep quality and may help modulate cortisol for a calmer transition to sleep

### Daily Habits
- **Blood work every 6-8 weeks** (more frequent than other GHRPs): IGF-1, fasting glucose, morning cortisol, prolactin, and a comprehensive metabolic panel. Hexarelin's potency demands closer monitoring
- **Track your response** — log energy levels, mood, libido, sleep quality, and training performance. Cortisol and prolactin elevation often manifest as subtle fatigue, irritability, or decreased libido before they show up clearly on labs
- **Morning sunlight (15-20 minutes)** — supports circadian rhythm and helps regulate cortisol's natural diurnal pattern
- **Daily stress management is non-negotiable** — hexarelin already elevates cortisol. Unmanaged psychological stress compounds this, potentially negating the GH benefits. Meditation (10+ minutes), nature walks, breathwork, or yoga should be daily practices
- **Hydrate aggressively** — hexarelin causes significant water retention at higher doses. Aim for 100+ oz of water daily
