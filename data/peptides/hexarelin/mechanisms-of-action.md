# Hexarelin

## Mechanisms of Action

- **GHS-R1a Agonist**: Binds ghrelin receptors with high affinity
- **Triple GH Mechanism**: (1) Stimulates hypothalamic GHRH release, (2) Directly acts on pituitary, (3) Suppresses somatostatin — explains extreme potency
- **CD36 Receptor Binding**: Unique among GHRPs — binds cardiac CD36 receptors for cardioprotection
- **Cortisol/ACTH/Prolactin**: Stimulates all three more than other GHRPs (dose-dependent)
- **Most Potent GH Pulse**: Strongest GH release of any secretagogue
