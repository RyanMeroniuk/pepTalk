# Hexarelin

## Overview

Hexarelin (His-D-2-methylTrp-Ala-Trp-D-Phe-Lys-NH2) is a synthetic hexapeptide and the most potent growth hormone releasing peptide available. A super-analog of GHRP-6 — the only structural difference is the inclusion of two methyl groups. Produces GH release amplitude that dwarfs Ipamorelin, GHRP-6, and even GHRP-2. However, this extreme potency comes with faster receptor desensitization and more pronounced cortisol/prolactin effects. Notable for unique cardioprotective properties via CD36 receptor binding on cardiac tissue.
