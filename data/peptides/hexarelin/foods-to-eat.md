# Hexarelin

## Foods to Eat

- **Wild-caught salmon and sardines** — omega-3 fatty acids are anti-inflammatory and help counteract hexarelin's cortisol-driven inflammation. High-quality protein supports the extreme anabolic environment
- **Chicken breast and turkey** — lean, high-protein, low-inflammatory. The foundation of meals for any GHRP user
- **Pasture-raised eggs** — complete amino acids, cholesterol for hormone synthesis, and choline for neurological function
- **Grass-fed beef** — complete protein with zinc, iron, and B vitamins. Zinc is particularly important for hexarelin users as it helps counteract prolactin elevation
- **Ashwagandha-infused foods or supplements** — KSM-66 ashwagandha (300-600mg) is clinically shown to reduce cortisol. Consider adding to a smoothie or taking in capsule form
- **Sweet potatoes** — complex carbs with anti-inflammatory properties and potassium for water balance
- **Cruciferous vegetables (broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts)** — fiber, low calorie, and DIM (diindolylmethane) supports healthy hormone metabolism
- **Berries (blueberries, tart cherries, strawberries)** — antioxidants reduce cortisol-driven oxidative stress. Tart cherries specifically contain natural melatonin for sleep support
- **Spinach and dark leafy greens** — magnesium for sleep, folate for methylation, and virtually zero calories
- **Pumpkin seeds** — one of the best food sources of zinc, which is critical for hexarelin users managing prolactin and supporting testosterone
- **Turmeric (with black pepper for absorption)** — curcumin is a potent anti-inflammatory that helps offset cortisol-driven inflammation
- **Bone broth** — glycine supports sleep quality and the collagen precursors complement hexarelin's GH-driven tissue repair
- **Dark chocolate (85%+ cacao)** — magnesium, antioxidants, and theobromine. A controlled treat that also supports cortisol management (small amounts)
- **Cottage cheese before bed** — casein protein provides slow amino acid release during hexarelin's powerful overnight GH window
