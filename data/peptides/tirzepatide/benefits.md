# Tirzepatide

## Benefits

- **Superior Weight Loss**: Up to 22.5% body weight reduction (SURMOUNT-1 trial, 15mg dose)
- **Diabetes Remission**: Some patients achieved normal HbA1c off all diabetes medications
- **Cardiovascular**: Reduced cardiovascular events
- **Sleep Apnea**: Significant improvement in obstructive sleep apnea (SURMOUNT-OSA trial)
- **Fatty Liver**: Liver fat reduction
- **Blood Pressure/Lipids**: Improvements in cardiovascular risk markers
- **Better Tolerability**: GIP component may reduce GI side effects compared to pure GLP-1 agonists
