# Tirzepatide

## Mechanisms of Action

- **GLP-1 Receptor Agonist**: Appetite suppression, slowed gastric emptying, enhanced insulin secretion
- **GIP Receptor Agonist**: Complements GLP-1 effects; improves insulin sensitivity, enhances fat metabolism, may protect against nausea
- **Dual Mechanism**: GIP activation may explain why tirzepatide produces greater weight loss and better GI tolerability than GLP-1-only drugs
- **Glucose-Dependent**: Both insulin secretion and glucagon suppression are glucose-dependent (lower hypoglycemia risk)
