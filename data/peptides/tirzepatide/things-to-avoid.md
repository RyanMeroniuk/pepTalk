# Tirzepatide

## Things to Avoid

- **Skipping Meals**: Suppressed appetite does NOT mean you should stop eating. Skipping meals accelerates muscle loss, causes nutrient deficiency, hair loss, and fatigue. Set meal reminders. Use protein shakes if solid food is difficult
- **Lying Down After Eating**: Wait 30-60 minutes after meals before lying down. Slowed stomach emptying plus lying flat causes acid reflux and worsens nausea. Walk gently after eating instead
- **Cardio-Only Exercise**: Running and cycling alone will NOT preserve muscle. You MUST include resistance training (weights, bands, or bodyweight) to prevent lean mass loss on tirzepatide
- **Drinking Large Volumes With Meals**: Drink fluids BETWEEN meals. Large amounts of liquid during meals fill the slow-emptying stomach, prevent adequate protein intake, and cause bloating
- **Crash Dieting**: Tirzepatide already produces dramatic weight loss. Extreme caloric restriction on top of the drug causes dangerous muscle wasting, hair loss, gallstones, severe facial volume loss, nutrient deficiency, and metabolic damage. Eat at least your basal metabolic rate
- **Ignoring Protein Goals**: Track protein intake daily. At least 100-150g of protein per day for most people. Without adequate protein, you lose muscle — and muscle loss is the primary long-term health risk of GLP-1 therapy
- **Sedentary Lifestyle**: Prolonged sitting worsens constipation, accelerates muscle loss, and reduces metabolic rate. Move frequently throughout the day — stand, walk, stretch
- **Rapid Dose Increases**: Follow the every-4-weeks escalation schedule. Jumping doses too quickly dramatically worsens GI side effects without meaningfully improving outcomes
- **Ignoring Warning Signs**: Persistent vomiting, severe abdominal pain, inability to eat/drink for 24+ hours, or signs of severe dehydration require immediate medical evaluation for pancreatitis or bowel obstruction
- **Neglecting Oral Contraceptive Backup**: If you take birth control pills, use a backup method during dose escalation — slowed gastric emptying can reduce pill absorption
- **Assuming the Drug Replaces Lifestyle Changes**: Tirzepatide is a tool. Without resistance training, adequate protein, and sustainable habits, you will lose muscle, regain weight after stopping, and develop deficiencies
