# Tirzepatide

## Side Effects

- Similar GI profile to semaglutide: nausea, diarrhea, vomiting, constipation
- Possibly better tolerated than semaglutide at equivalent efficacy (GIP may be protective)
- Muscle loss (shared concern with all GLP-1 class)
- Gallbladder disease with rapid weight loss
- Pancreatitis (rare)
- Injection site reactions
- Thyroid: Medullary thyroid carcinoma warning (rodent studies)
