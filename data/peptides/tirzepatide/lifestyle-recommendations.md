# Tirzepatide

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Protein-First Eating at Every Meal**: Eat protein FIRST before carbs or fats. Target 1.6-2.2g protein per kg body weight daily. Tirzepatide produces up to 22.5% body weight loss — a significant portion can be lean muscle if protein intake and exercise are inadequate
- **Resistance Training 3-5x/Week**: NON-NEGOTIABLE. Compound lifts (squats, deadlifts, bench press, rows, overhead press) with progressive overload. Tirzepatide may be slightly better tolerated than semaglutide (the GIP component may reduce nausea), making it easier to train — take advantage of this
- **Daily Step Count**: Walk 8,000-10,000+ steps per day. Walking aids fat loss, improves insulin sensitivity, promotes gastric motility (helps with constipation and bloating from slowed digestion), and is gentle enough to do even on nauseous days
- **Meal Size and Frequency**: Eat 4-6 small meals throughout the day instead of 2-3 large ones. Tirzepatide slows gastric emptying — large meals cause nausea, bloating, and discomfort. Eat slowly and mindfully, stopping at 80% fullness
- **Hydration Strategy**: Drink 80-100oz of water daily, but BETWEEN meals, not during. Drinking large amounts of fluid with food fills the slow-emptying stomach, causing bloating and preventing adequate protein intake. Keep a water bottle and sip throughout the day
- **Preventing Facial Volume Loss ("Ozempic Face")**: Rapid fat loss causes facial hollowing. Prevent with: adequate total calorie intake (no crash dieting), healthy fats daily (avocado, olive oil, nuts, salmon), collagen peptides (10-15g/day), SPF daily, hyaluronic acid skincare, and staying well-hydrated
- **Sleep 7-9 Hours**: Sleep deprivation raises cortisol and ghrelin, lowers leptin and insulin sensitivity — directly undermining tirzepatide's metabolic benefits. Consistent sleep schedule is important
- **GI Side Effect Management**: While tirzepatide may cause less nausea than semaglutide, GI effects are still common. Ginger tea for nausea, peppermint tea for bloating, psyllium husk or magnesium citrate for constipation. Walk 10-15 minutes after meals to support digestion
- **Oral Contraceptive Users**: Tirzepatide may reduce absorption of oral birth control pills due to slowed gastric emptying. Use a backup contraceptive method during dose escalation periods — discuss with your provider
- **Body Composition Monitoring**: Track body composition with DEXA scans, bioimpedance, or waist measurements — not just the scale. Confirm you are losing fat and preserving muscle
- **Gallbladder Monitoring**: Rapid weight loss increases gallstone risk. Report any right upper quadrant pain (especially after fatty meals), fever, or jaundice immediately
- **Stress Reduction**: Chronic stress raises cortisol, which promotes visceral fat storage and accelerates muscle breakdown. Practice meditation, deep breathing, yoga, or nature walks
- **Plan for Long-Term Maintenance**: Weight regain is common after discontinuation. Use the time on tirzepatide to build sustainable exercise and nutrition habits that persist after stopping
