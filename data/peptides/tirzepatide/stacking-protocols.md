# Tirzepatide

## Stacking Protocols

- **Tirzepatide vs Semaglutide (Choose One)**: Do NOT combine. Tirzepatide (GLP-1/GIP dual) produces ~22.5% weight loss vs semaglutide's ~15-17%. Tirzepatide may be better tolerated (GIP component appears to reduce nausea). Semaglutide has more long-term CV outcome data (SELECT trial). Choose tirzepatide for greater weight loss and potentially better GI tolerability; choose semaglutide for the strongest cardiovascular evidence base
- **Metabolic Recomposition Stack**: Tirzepatide (Mounjaro/Zepbound titration) + Creatine Monohydrate (5g/day) + Whey Protein Isolate (30-50g/day) — tirzepatide drives aggressive fat loss while creatine and protein defend against the significant lean mass loss that occurs with all GLP-1 class drugs
- **Sleep Apnea & Metabolic Stack**: Tirzepatide (therapeutic dose) + Magnesium Glycinate (400mg/day) + Omega-3 (2-4g EPA/DHA daily) — leverages tirzepatide's proven sleep apnea improvement (SURMOUNT-OSA trial) with magnesium for sleep quality and omega-3s for inflammation and lipid support
- **Body Composition & Skin Stack**: Tirzepatide (therapeutic dose) + Collagen Peptides (15g/day) + HMB (3g/day) + Vitamin C (1000mg/day) — collagen and vitamin C support skin elasticity and minimize "Ozempic face" during rapid weight loss; HMB reduces muscle protein breakdown in caloric deficit
- **Comprehensive Metabolic Stack**: Tirzepatide (therapeutic dose) + Berberine (500mg 2x/day) + Vitamin D3 (5000 IU/day) + Probiotics (multi-strain) — broad metabolic support for insulin sensitivity, gut health, and nutrient status during the dramatic dietary changes that accompany GLP-1 therapy
