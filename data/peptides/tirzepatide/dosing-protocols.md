# Tirzepatide

## Dosing Protocols

- **Mounjaro/Zepbound**: 2.5mg → 5mg → 7.5mg → 10mg → 12.5mg → 15mg weekly (escalated every 4 weeks)
- **Route**: Subcutaneous injection, once weekly
- **Dose Escalation**: Gradual increase is critical
- **Maintenance**: Most patients settle at 10mg or 15mg
- **Continuous Use**: Like semaglutide, weight regain occurs upon discontinuation
