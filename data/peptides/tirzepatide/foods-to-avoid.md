# Tirzepatide

## Foods to Avoid

- **High-Fat Greasy/Fried Foods**: French fries, fried chicken, onion rings, fast food, pizza, cream-heavy sauces, greasy takeout. The #1 cause of severe nausea on tirzepatide. Slowed gastric emptying means greasy food sits in the stomach for hours, causing nausea, cramping, and potential dumping syndrome
- **Carbonated Beverages**: Soda, sparkling water, beer, seltzer. Trapped gas in a slow-emptying stomach causes painful bloating and distension
- **High-Sugar Foods**: Candy, pastries, ice cream, sugary cereals, fruit juice, regular soda, sweetened coffee drinks. When appetite is suppressed, every calorie must be nutrient-dense — sugar wastes precious appetite capacity
- **Alcohol**: Absorption is unpredictable with slowed gastric emptying (can hit harder than expected), provides empty calories, worsens nausea, and impairs liver fat metabolism
- **Very Large Portions**: Even healthy food in large amounts overwhelms the slow-emptying stomach. Smaller, frequent meals are essential
- **Very Spicy Foods**: Hot sauces, spicy dishes. Worsen acid reflux and nausea, which are already elevated
- **Raw Fibrous Vegetables in Bulk**: Large raw salads, raw broccoli, raw cauliflower, raw cabbage. Difficult to break down with slowed motility — cook or steam vegetables instead
- **Tough/Fatty Red Meats**: Fatty steak, ribs, brisket. Very slow to digest. Choose lean cuts cooked until tender
- **Ultra-Processed Foods**: Chips, packaged snacks, deli meats, frozen dinners. Low nutritional value per calorie when you can only eat small amounts
