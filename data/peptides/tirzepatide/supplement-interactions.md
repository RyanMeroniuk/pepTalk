# Tirzepatide

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- Same as semaglutide: high protein, resistance training, creatine
- **Caution**: Diabetes medications (hypoglycemia with sulfonylureas/insulin)
- **Caution**: Oral contraceptives (absorption may be affected — use backup method during dose escalation)
- **Contraindicated**: MTC/MEN2 history, pancreatitis, pregnancy/breastfeeding
