# Tirzepatide

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Sulfonylureas and Insulin**: Significantly increased hypoglycemia risk. Dose reduction of sulfonylureas or insulin is typically required when starting tirzepatide. Monitor blood glucose closely, especially during dose escalation
- **Oral Contraceptives**: FDA-acknowledged interaction — tirzepatide slows gastric emptying and can reduce absorption of oral birth control pills. Use a non-oral backup contraceptive method for 4 weeks after initiating tirzepatide and for 4 weeks after each dose increase. Consider switching to non-oral contraception (IUD, patch, ring, implant)
- **Thyroid Medications (Levothyroxine)**: Delayed gastric emptying may alter absorption. Take levothyroxine on empty stomach, 60+ minutes before food. Monitor TSH every 6-8 weeks during tirzepatide dose escalation
- **Warfarin and Narrow Therapeutic Index Drugs**: Delayed gastric emptying can alter pharmacokinetics of drugs requiring precise absorption timing. Monitor INR frequently with warfarin; exercise caution with anticonvulsants, digoxin, and similar drugs
- **Medullary Thyroid Carcinoma / MEN2**: Absolute contraindication (black box warning). GLP-1 agonists caused thyroid C-cell tumors in rodent models. Do not use with personal or family history of MTC or MEN2 syndrome
- **Pancreatitis History**: Contraindicated. GLP-1 class carries risk of acute pancreatitis. Discontinue immediately if persistent severe abdominal pain develops
- **Other GLP-1 / Incretin-Based Therapies**: Do not combine with semaglutide, liraglutide, retatrutide, or other GLP-1/GIP agonists. Overlapping mechanisms increase adverse effects
- **Gastroparesis / Severe GI Motility Disorders**: Use with caution — tirzepatide further delays gastric emptying and may worsen pre-existing motility disorders
- **Pregnancy / Breastfeeding**: Contraindicated. Discontinue at least 1 month before planned conception. Weight loss during pregnancy poses risks to fetal development. Effects on breast milk unknown
- **Diabetic Retinopathy**: Rapid glycemic improvement may temporarily worsen pre-existing diabetic retinopathy. Ophthalmologic monitoring recommended in patients with retinopathy history
- **Eating Disorders**: Use with caution in patients with history of anorexia nervosa or restrictive eating — profound appetite suppression may exacerbate disordered eating
