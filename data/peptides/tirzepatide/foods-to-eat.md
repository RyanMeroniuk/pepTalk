# Tirzepatide

## Foods to Eat

- **Lean Proteins (eat FIRST at every meal)**: Chicken breast, turkey breast, wild-caught salmon, cod, tilapia, shrimp, tuna (canned in water), eggs and egg whites, Greek yogurt (plain, 0% or 2%), cottage cheese (low-fat), lean ground turkey, bison, tofu, tempeh, edamame, lentils
- **Protein Supplements**: Whey protein isolate (when solid food is hard to eat), casein protein (slow-digesting, great before bed), collagen peptides (skin support during rapid weight loss), bone broth (gentle on the stomach and protein-rich)
- **Complex Carbohydrates (small portions after protein)**: Sweet potatoes, quinoa, oats, brown rice, lentils, chickpeas, black beans, whole grain bread (limited)
- **Healthy Fats (moderate amounts — important for skin and hormones)**: Avocado, extra virgin olive oil, almonds, walnuts, chia seeds, flaxseed, fatty fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel)
- **Cooked Vegetables (better tolerated than raw)**: Steamed broccoli, roasted zucchini, sauteed spinach, steamed green beans, roasted bell peppers, cooked carrots, mashed cauliflower, roasted asparagus
- **Fruits (low-sugar, easy to digest)**: Blueberries, strawberries, raspberries, cantaloupe, watermelon, kiwi, ripe banana
- **Gut-Soothing Foods**: Bone broth, ginger (fresh or tea), papaya, plain oatmeal, fermented foods in small amounts (kefir, yogurt, sauerkraut)
- **Hydrating Options**: Cucumber, watermelon, soup broths, herbal teas (ginger, peppermint, chamomile), electrolyte drinks (sugar-free)
