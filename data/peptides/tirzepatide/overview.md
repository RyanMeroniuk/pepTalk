# Tirzepatide

## Overview

Tirzepatide is a dual GLP-1 and GIP receptor agonist developed by Eli Lilly. FDA-approved as Mounjaro (diabetes) and Zepbound (weight management). The first dual-agonist in its class — combining GLP-1 and GIP receptor activation for greater efficacy than GLP-1 alone. Produced even more dramatic weight loss results than semaglutide in head-to-head trials. Weekly subcutaneous injection.
