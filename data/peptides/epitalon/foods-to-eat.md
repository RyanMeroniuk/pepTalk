# Epitalon (Epithalon)

## Foods to Eat

- **Antioxidant-Rich Berries (blueberries, acai, goji, blackberries)**: Rich in anthocyanins and polyphenols that neutralize reactive oxygen species (ROS). Epitalon enhances mitochondrial health, and antioxidant-rich foods protect cells from the oxidative damage that shortens telomeres
- **Fatty Fish (wild-caught salmon, sardines, mackerel)**: Omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA) reduce systemic inflammation and have been directly associated with slower telomere shortening in clinical studies. Essential for Epitalon's anti-aging synergy
- **Cruciferous Vegetables (broccoli, cauliflower, kale, Brussels sprouts)**: Contain sulforaphane, which activates the Nrf2 antioxidant pathway — one of the body's most powerful cellular defense systems. Supports Epitalon's oxidative stress resilience mechanism
- **Nuts & Seeds (walnuts, almonds, flaxseed, chia)**: Rich in vitamin E, selenium, and omega-3s — all potent antioxidants that protect cell membranes and telomeres from oxidative damage
- **Green Tea (and matcha)**: EGCG is a powerful polyphenol with demonstrated telomere-protective properties in studies. Also supports mitochondrial function
- **Turmeric/Curcumin**: Potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant that activates sirtuin pathways and reduces the chronic low-grade inflammation (inflammaging) that accelerates aging
- **Dark Chocolate (85%+ cacao)**: Flavonoids improve vascular function and reduce oxidative stress. Theobromine supports mitochondrial health
- **Pomegranate**: Contains urolithin A precursors, which activate mitophagy (selective recycling of damaged mitochondria) — complementing Epitalon's mitochondrial support
- **Tart Cherries**: Natural melatonin source that supports Epitalon's pineal gland and sleep benefits
- **Olive Oil (extra virgin)**: Rich in oleocanthal and polyphenols with anti-inflammatory effects comparable to ibuprofen. A cornerstone of the Mediterranean diet, which is associated with longer telomeres
- **Fermented Foods (kimchi, sauerkraut, kefir, miso)**: Support gut microbiome diversity, which influences systemic inflammation levels. Chronic inflammation accelerates telomere shortening
- **Organ Meats (liver)**: Highest bioavailable source of CoQ10, B vitamins, and minerals essential for mitochondrial energy production
