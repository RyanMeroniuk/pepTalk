# Epitalon (Epithalon)

## Foods to Avoid

- **Refined Sugar & High-Glycemic Foods**: Chronic high sugar intake increases advanced glycation end products (AGEs), which damage proteins and DNA, accelerate cellular aging, and shorten telomeres — directly opposing Epitalon's mechanism
- **Processed Seed Oils (soybean, canola, corn, sunflower)**: High omega-6 content promotes chronic inflammation (inflammaging), which is one of the primary drivers of telomere shortening and cellular aging
- **Ultra-Processed Foods**: Contain inflammatory additives, excess sugar, and oxidized fats. Studies directly link ultra-processed food consumption to shorter telomere length
- **Charred & Smoked Meats**: Contain heterocyclic amines (HCAs) and polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) that increase oxidative DNA damage and cancer risk — particularly concerning alongside telomerase activation
- **Trans Fats**: Directly damage cell membranes, promote inflammation, and accelerate cellular aging
- **Excess Alcohol**: Increases oxidative stress, damages mitochondria, disrupts melatonin secretion (opposing Epitalon's pineal support), and is associated with accelerated telomere shortening
- **Artificial Sweeteners**: May disrupt gut microbiome and promote metabolic dysfunction, contributing to the inflammatory environment that accelerates aging
- **Excess Processed Red Meat**: High heme iron content can generate free radicals via the Fenton reaction, increasing oxidative stress. Moderate intake is fine; daily processed meat is not
