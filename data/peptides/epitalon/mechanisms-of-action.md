# Epitalon (Epithalon)

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Telomerase Activation**: Activates telomerase enzyme, extending telomere length and cellular replicative lifespan beyond the Hayflick limit
- **Melatonin Restoration**: Restores melatonin secretion by the aging pineal gland (demonstrated in both aged monkeys and humans)
- **Epigenetic Regulation**: Modulates gene expression patterns associated with aging
- **Oxidative Stress Resilience**: Enhances mitochondrial health, reduces intracellular reactive oxygen species
- **Immune Recalibration**: Supports immune function that declines with age
- **Circadian Rhythm Restoration**: Normalizes sleep-wake cycles via melatonin regulation
- **Low-Dose Efficacy**: Clinical studies show low, cyclical dosing is sufficient — increasing dose does not increase benefits
