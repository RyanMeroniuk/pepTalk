# Epitalon (Epithalon)

## Overview

Epitalon (also spelled Epithalon) is a synthetic tetrapeptide (Ala-Glu-Asp-Gly) based on the amino acid composition of Epithalamin, a bovine pineal gland extract. The peptide was confirmed to naturally occur in human pineal tissue in 2017. Primarily known for its ability to activate telomerase — the enzyme that extends telomeres (protective caps on chromosomes that shorten with age). Also restores melatonin secretion by the aging pineal gland. Developed by Russian gerontologist Professor Vladimir Khavinson, who has studied pineal peptides for decades.
