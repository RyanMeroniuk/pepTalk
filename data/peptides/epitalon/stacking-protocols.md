# Epitalon (Epithalon)

Each targets a different hallmark of aging
- **Longevity Sleep Stack**: Epitalon (1mg/day, evening) + DSIP (100-300mcg before bed) + Magnesium Glycinate (400mg, evening) — Epitalon restores pineal melatonin production while DSIP promotes delta-wave deep sleep. Combined with magnesium for GABA support, this stack optimizes the sleep quality that is essential for cellular repair and longevity processes
- **Cellular Resilience Stack**: Epitalon (1mg/day, 10-20 day cycle) + MOTS-c (5mg 3x/week) + Omega-3 (2-4g EPA/DHA daily) — Epitalon for telomere extension, MOTS-c as a mitochondrial-derived peptide for metabolic optimization and exercise mimetic effects, omega-3s for anti-inflammatory support. Targets telomeres, mitochondria, and inflammation — three of the nine hallmarks of aging
- **Skin & Systemic Anti-Aging Stack**: Epitalon (1mg/day, 10-20 day cycle) + GHK-Cu (topical and/or subcutaneous) + Collagen Peptides (15g/day oral) + Vitamin C (1000mg/day) — Epitalon provides cellular-level anti-aging through telomerase activation, GHK-Cu stimulates collagen synthesis and skin regeneration, oral collagen provides building blocks, vitamin C is essential cofactor for collagen production
- **Immune & Longevity Stack**: Epitalon (1mg/day, 10-20 day cycle) + Thymosin Alpha-1 (1.6mg 2x/week) + Vitamin D3 (5000 IU/day) — Epitalon supports age-related immune decline mitigation alongside telomere extension, Thymosin Alpha-1 directly enhances T-cell function and immune competence, vitamin D supports innate and adaptive immunity. Addresses immunosenescence as a key aging driver
