# Epitalon (Epithalon)

## Dosing Protocols

- **Standard**: 1mg subcutaneous injection daily
- **Cycle**: 10-20 day courses, repeated every 6-12 months
- **Frequency**: Daily injections during active cycle
- **Dose Ceiling**: Higher doses do not proportionally increase benefits — cyclical low dosing is optimal
- **Timing**: Evening preferred (aligns with pineal/melatonin function)
- **Reconstitution**: Bacteriostatic water; refrigerate
- **Long-term Protocol**: Short annual or biannual cycles rather than continuous use
