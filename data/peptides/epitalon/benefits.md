# Epitalon (Epithalon)

## Benefits

- **Telomere Extension**: Significantly increased telomere lengths in blood cells of patients aged 60-65 and 75-80 in human clinical studies
- **Cellular Longevity**: Extended replicative lifespan of human cells while preserving youthful morphology and function
- **Melatonin Restoration**: Restores age-related decline in melatonin production
- **Sleep Quality**: Improved via restored melatonin secretion
- **Anti-Oxidant**: Enhanced mitochondrial health and reduced oxidative damage
- **Immune Support**: Age-related immune decline mitigation
- **Potential Anti-Cancer**: Some research suggests anti-carcinogenic properties (though telomerase activation is a theoretical concern for existing cancers)
- **Favorable Safety Profile**: Alzheimer's Drug Discovery Foundation concluded "no severe adverse events"
