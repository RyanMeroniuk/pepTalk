# Epitalon (Epithalon)

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Active Cancer**: CRITICAL CONCERN. Epitalon activates telomerase — the same enzyme cancer cells hijack for unlimited replication. While some research suggests anti-carcinogenic properties of Epitalon, telomerase activation in the presence of existing malignant cells could theoretically promote tumor growth. Absolutely contraindicated in patients with active cancer
- **Cancer History**: Use with extreme caution. Patients with a history of cancer (especially within 5 years of remission) should consult their oncologist before considering Epitalon. Regular cancer screening is essential for anyone using telomerase-activating compounds
- **Immunosuppressants (Tacrolimus, Cyclosporine, Mycophenolate)**: Epitalon has immune-modulating properties. Combining with immunosuppressant drugs (transplant patients, autoimmune conditions) may create unpredictable immune effects — either blunting immunosuppression or causing immune dysregulation. Consult transplant team or rheumatologist
- **Chemotherapy / Radiation**: Telomerase activation during cancer treatment directly opposes the therapeutic goal of limiting cancer cell replication. Absolutely contraindicated during active cancer treatment
- **Exogenous Melatonin (High-Dose)**: Epitalon restores endogenous melatonin production. High-dose exogenous melatonin (5-10mg+) on top of restored natural production may cause excessive drowsiness, vivid dreams, or circadian disruption. If combining, use only low-dose melatonin (0.5-1mg) and reassess as Epitalon restores natural production
- **Autoimmune Conditions**: Epitalon's immune-recalibrating effects could theoretically worsen autoimmune conditions by stimulating an already overactive immune system. Use with caution in patients with lupus, rheumatoid arthritis, multiple sclerosis, or other autoimmune disorders
- **Blood Thinners (Warfarin, Heparin)**: No established direct interaction, but standard injection-site caution applies. Monitor for bruising at injection sites
- **Pregnancy / Breastfeeding**: Contraindicated. Telomerase activation and epigenetic modulation during pregnancy could have unpredictable effects on fetal development. No safety data exists
- **Children / Adolescents**: Not recommended. Young, healthy cells already have adequate telomerase activity. The risk-benefit profile of exogenous telomerase activation in developing individuals is unfavorable
