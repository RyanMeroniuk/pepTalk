# PT-141 (Bremelanotide)

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Naltrexone (FDA black box warning)**: PT-141 is contraindicated with naltrexone. The FDA specifically warns against this combination due to risk of significantly increased adverse effects. Naltrexone blocks opioid receptors and may alter melanocortin signaling in unpredictable ways
- **Blood pressure medications**: PT-141 causes transient blood pressure increases (~2-3 mmHg systolic). Patients on antihypertensives need BP monitoring on dosing days; dose adjustments of BP medication may be needed
- **Other melanocortin agonists (Melanotan II, setmelanotide)**: Overlapping MC4R activation dramatically increases nausea, blood pressure effects, and hyperpigmentation risk. Never combine
- **PDE5 inhibitors (sildenafil, tadalafil)**: Different mechanisms (central vs. peripheral) but additive effects on sexual arousal and erectile function. Combining may increase priapism risk in men. Use with caution and medical supervision
- **Nitrates (nitroglycerin, isosorbide)**: PT-141's blood pressure effects combined with nitrate-induced hypotension could cause dangerous cardiovascular events
- **Antidepressants (SSRIs, SNRIs)**: SSRIs commonly cause sexual dysfunction; PT-141 may partially counteract this, but serotonergic effects may also blunt PT-141's dopaminergic arousal mechanism. Discuss with prescriber
- **Alpha-blockers (tamsulosin, doxazosin)**: Additive blood pressure effects may cause orthostatic hypotension
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy and breastfeeding — hormonal and CNS effects pose unknown risks to fetal development
- **Contraindicated**: Uncontrolled hypertension or cardiovascular disease — transient BP increase may be dangerous
- **Contraindicated**: Concurrent naltrexone use — per FDA labeling
