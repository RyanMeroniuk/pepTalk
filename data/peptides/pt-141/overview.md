# PT-141 (Bremelanotide)

## Overview

PT-141 (bremelanotide, brand name Vyleesi) is a synthetic peptide melanocortin receptor agonist derived from Melanotan II. FDA-approved in 2019 for hypoactive sexual desire disorder (HSDD) in premenopausal women. Unlike Viagra/Cialis which work on vascular pathways, PT-141 acts on the central nervous system — it stimulates the brain's sexual arousal pathways directly through melanocortin-4 (MC4) receptor activation. This makes it effective for both desire and arousal, not just blood flow.
