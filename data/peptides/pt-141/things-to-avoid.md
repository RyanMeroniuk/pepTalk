# PT-141 (Bremelanotide)

## Things to Avoid

- **Chronic Stress** — sustained cortisol elevation is the primary lifestyle killer of sexual desire. Cortisol directly suppresses gonadotropin-releasing hormone, reducing testosterone and estrogen. PT-141 works through the hypothalamus, which is highly stress-sensitive
- **Excessive Alcohol Use** — impairs sexual desire, arousal, and performance through multiple mechanisms: reduced testosterone, impaired dopamine signaling, and peripheral nerve damage with chronic use
- **Sleep Deprivation** — testosterone production peaks during sleep. Chronic sleep loss reduces testosterone by 10-15% and impairs dopamine receptor sensitivity, reducing PT-141 effectiveness
- **Sedentary Lifestyle** — poor cardiovascular fitness impairs blood flow and hormonal production. Regular exercise is essential for sexual health and enhances PT-141's effects
- **Endocrine Disruptors in Plastics** — BPA and phthalates in plastic containers, especially heated plastics, mimic estrogen and disrupt hormonal balance. Use glass or stainless steel for food/beverage storage. Never microwave food in plastic
- **Excess Pornography** — can reduce dopamine receptor sensitivity over time, potentially blunting PT-141's dopaminergic mechanism of action. PT-141 works by increasing dopamine in the hypothalamus — desensitized dopamine receptors reduce response
- **Combining with Other Melanocortin Agonists** — do not use PT-141 with Melanotan II. Overlapping melanocortin receptor activation increases side effect risk (nausea, blood pressure changes, hyperpigmentation)
- **Exceeding Dosing Frequency** — using PT-141 more than 8 times per month increases risk of permanent skin hyperpigmentation. Follow FDA-approved frequency limits
- **Ignoring Cardiovascular Health** — PT-141 transiently raises blood pressure. Uncontrolled hypertension is a contraindication. Monitor BP and maintain cardiovascular health through diet and exercise
