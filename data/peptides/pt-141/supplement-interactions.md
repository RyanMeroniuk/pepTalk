# PT-141 (Bremelanotide)

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Caution**: Blood pressure medications — PT-141 transiently elevates BP
- **Caution**: Other melanocortin agonists (Melanotan II) — overlapping mechanisms
- **Contraindicated**: Uncontrolled hypertension or cardiovascular disease
- **Contraindicated**: Concurrent use with naltrexone (FDA warning)
- **Manage nausea**: Take with anti-nausea measures for first few doses
