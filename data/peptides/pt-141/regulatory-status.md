# PT-141 (Bremelanotide)

## Regulatory Status

- **FDA-APPROVED** (2019) as Vyleesi for HSDD in premenopausal women
- Off-label use in men for ED/low libido is growing
- Available by prescription
- Also available from compounding pharmacies at lower cost
- **Banned by WADA** (melanocortin agonist)
