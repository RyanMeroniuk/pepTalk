# PT-141 (Bremelanotide)

## Side Effects

- **Nausea**: 40% — the most common side effect, particularly with first injection. May last 2+ hours
- **Flushing**: 20% of users
- **Injection Site Reactions**: 13%
- **Headache**: 11%
- **Blood Pressure**: Small transient increase (~2-3 mmHg systolic, ~1-2 mmHg diastolic)
- **Heart Rate**: Slight decrease (~2 bpm)
- **Skin Hyperpigmentation**: May occur if used more than 8 times/month — may not resolve after stopping
- Nausea typically decreases with subsequent doses
