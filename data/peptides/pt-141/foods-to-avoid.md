# PT-141 (Bremelanotide)

## Foods to Avoid

- **Alcohol** — directly suppresses sexual desire, impairs arousal, reduces testosterone, and worsens PT-141 nausea. The single most counterproductive substance for sexual health
- **Excess Sugar** — causes blood sugar spikes and crashes that impair energy, mood, and hormonal balance. Chronic high sugar intake reduces testosterone and increases insulin resistance
- **Soy in Excess** — contains phytoestrogens that may disrupt hormonal balance when consumed in large quantities. Moderate fermented soy (miso, tempeh) is fine; avoid excessive soy protein isolate
- **Processed Foods and Seed Oils** — promote inflammation and oxidative stress that impair vascular function and hormonal health
- **Excess Caffeine** — while moderate coffee can be beneficial, excessive caffeine (>400mg) elevates cortisol, which suppresses sex hormones and can worsen PT-141 side effects
- **Heavy Meals Before Dosing** — fatty, large meals before PT-141 injection significantly increase nausea risk. Keep pre-dose meals light
- **Licorice Root** — contains glycyrrhizin, which has been shown to reduce testosterone levels
- **Trans Fats and Fried Foods** — impair vascular function (blood flow is critical for sexual arousal) and promote inflammation
