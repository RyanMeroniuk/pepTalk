# PT-141 (Bremelanotide)

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Manage Nausea with Food Timing**: Nausea affects 40% of users and is the primary barrier to PT-141 use. Eat a light meal 1-2 hours before injection — not too full, not empty stomach. Avoid fatty or heavy foods immediately before dosing. Ginger tea or ginger chews 30 minutes before injection can significantly reduce nausea
- **Prioritize Sleep for Hormonal Health**: Sleep deprivation directly reduces testosterone, dopamine sensitivity, and sexual desire — the exact pathways PT-141 targets. Aim for 7-9 hours. Poor sleep blunts the dopaminergic response that makes PT-141 effective
- **Manage Stress for Libido**: Chronic stress elevates cortisol, which directly suppresses sex hormones (testosterone, estrogen) and dopamine production. Since PT-141 works through dopamine pathways in the hypothalamus, chronic stress reduces its effectiveness. Practice daily stress management: meditation, exercise, nature exposure
- **Cardiovascular Exercise**: Regular cardio (30-45 min, 4-5x/week) improves blood flow, hormonal health, and dopamine receptor sensitivity — all of which complement PT-141's mechanism. Strong cardiovascular health enhances the arousal response
- **Strength Training**: Resistance training naturally boosts testosterone and growth hormone, supporting the hormonal environment that PT-141 enhances. Focus on compound movements (squats, deadlifts, presses)
- **Limit Alcohol Around Dosing**: Alcohol suppresses sexual function, reduces dopamine sensitivity, and may worsen PT-141 nausea. Avoid alcohol on the day you plan to use PT-141
- **Address Underlying Hormonal Issues**: PT-141 works best when baseline hormonal health is optimized. Test testosterone, estrogen, thyroid, and prolactin levels. Address deficiencies alongside PT-141 use
- **Stay Hydrated**: Dehydration worsens nausea and can impair the arousal response. Drink adequate water, especially on dosing days
- **Monitor Blood Pressure**: PT-141 causes a small transient BP increase. If you have borderline hypertension, monitor BP on dosing days and discuss with your provider
