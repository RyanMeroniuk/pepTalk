# PT-141 (Bremelanotide)

## Dosing Protocols

- **FDA-Approved Dose**: 1.75mg subcutaneous injection
- **Timing**: At least 45 minutes before anticipated sexual activity
- **Frequency Limit**: Maximum 1 dose per 24 hours; no more than 8 doses per month
- **Route**: Subcutaneous self-injection (autoinjector available as Vyleesi)
- **Onset**: 30-60 minutes
- **Duration**: Several hours
