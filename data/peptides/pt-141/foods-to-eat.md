# PT-141 (Bremelanotide)

## Foods to Eat

- **Nitric Oxide-Boosting Foods** (beets, arugula, spinach, dark leafy greens) — nitric oxide supports vascular blood flow to sexual organs, complementing PT-141's central nervous system effects with peripheral arousal support
- **Zinc-Rich Foods** (oysters, pumpkin seeds, grass-fed beef) — zinc is essential for testosterone production and sexual function in both men and women
- **Dark Chocolate** (70%+ cacao) — contains phenylethylamine and flavonoids that support dopamine production and vascular health
- **Ginger** — proven anti-nausea food; consume as tea or chews before PT-141 dosing to reduce the most common side effect
- **Fatty Fish** (wild-caught salmon, sardines, mackerel) — omega-3s support hormonal production, reduce inflammation, and improve cardiovascular health
- **Avocado** — healthy fats support hormone synthesis; also contains vitamin B6 for dopamine production
- **Pomegranate** — shown to increase testosterone levels and improve blood flow; potent antioxidant
- **Eggs** — provide cholesterol (precursor to all sex hormones), choline, and B vitamins for neurotransmitter production
- **Watermelon** — contains citrulline, which converts to arginine and boosts nitric oxide production
- **Maca Root** — adaptogenic root with evidence for improved libido and sexual function; can be added to smoothies
- **Walnuts and Brazil Nuts** — selenium (Brazil nuts) supports testosterone production; walnuts provide omega-3s and arginine
