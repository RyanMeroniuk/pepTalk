# PT-141 (Bremelanotide)

## Stacking Protocols

- **Standalone Use Preferred**: PT-141 is best used as a standalone sexual health peptide. Its melanocortin receptor activation produces potent central nervous system effects that increase unpredictably when combined with other peptides affecting similar pathways
- **Hormonal Optimization Stack (with caution)**: PT-141 (1.75 mg as needed) + Kisspeptin (1-10 mcg/kg) — PT-141 addresses acute arousal/desire while kisspeptin supports upstream hormonal production (LH/FSH/testosterone). Different mechanisms but complementary; use on separate days or with careful timing
- **Cardiovascular Support Stack**: PT-141 (1.75 mg as needed) + Omega-3 supplementation (2-4g daily) + CoQ10 (200 mg daily) — supports vascular health and hormonal environment alongside PT-141's CNS-mediated arousal; important for users with borderline cardiovascular profiles
- **Do NOT Stack with Melanotan II**: Overlapping melanocortin receptor activation causes severe additive side effects including extreme nausea, dangerous blood pressure changes, priapism, and permanent hyperpigmentation
- **Do NOT Stack with Other Melanocortin Agonists**: Any additional MC receptor stimulation increases the risk of all side effects. PT-141 should be the sole melanocortin agent in any protocol
