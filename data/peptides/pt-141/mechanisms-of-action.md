# PT-141 (Bremelanotide)

## Mechanisms of Action

- **MC4 Receptor Agonist**: Activates melanocortin-4 receptors in the hypothalamus — a key brain region for sexual function
- **Dopamine Release**: Increases dopamine in the medial preoptic area of the hypothalamus, governing sexual desire and arousal
- **Central Nervous System**: Works on the brain, not peripheral vasculature (fundamentally different from PDE5 inhibitors like Viagra)
- **Affects Desire**: Targets libido/desire at the neurological level, not just erectile function
