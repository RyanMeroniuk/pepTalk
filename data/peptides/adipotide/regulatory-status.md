# Adipotide

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT APPROVED — PRECLINICAL ONLY**: Adipotide (FTPP) is not approved by the FDA, EMA, or any agency for any use
- Evidence is limited to animal and cell studies, including a published study in obese rhesus monkeys; there are no completed controlled human efficacy trials
- A documented nephrotoxicity (kidney damage) signal in animal work has been a major barrier to human development
- Not in routine clinical development as a marketed-drug candidate
- Sold only as a gray-market research chemical; quality, purity, and legality are not assured, and human use is unsafe and unvalidated
- Should be regarded strictly as an experimental research compound, not a weight-loss therapy
