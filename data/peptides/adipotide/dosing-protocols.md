# Adipotide

## Dosing Protocols

- **NO ESTABLISHED HUMAN DOSING**: There is no validated human dosing protocol. Any figure circulating online is extrapolated from animal studies and is not safe guidance
- **Animal Studies**: Dosing in the published monkey work was weight-based and administered as a short daily subcutaneous course under controlled laboratory conditions
- **No Titration Schedule Exists**: Unlike approved peptides, there is no human-validated escalation or maintenance schedule
- **Route (Animal)**: Subcutaneous injection in the preclinical studies
- **Why This Section Is Sparse**: Robust dosing data simply do not exist for humans — presenting specific human doses would be inventing information. Treat adipotide as research-only
- **Renal Risk Limits Dosing**: Nephrotoxicity observed in animals constrained dosing and duration, a major reason it has not advanced to routine human protocols
