# Adipotide

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Prohibitin Targeting**: A targeting peptide binds prohibitin, a protein expressed on the endothelium of blood vessels that supply white adipose tissue
- **Vascular Disruption**: By homing to the fat-tissue vasculature, the molecule concentrates its effect on the blood supply feeding fat depots rather than fat cells directly
- **Pro-Apoptotic Payload**: The targeting peptide is fused to a pro-apoptotic (cell-death-inducing) sequence that triggers apoptosis in the targeted endothelial cells
- **Adipocyte Death via Ischemia**: Destroying the vascular supply starves fat tissue, causing adipocytes to die and fat depots to shrink — a fundamentally different approach than appetite suppression
- **Not Appetite-Based**: Unlike GLP-1 agonists, it does not act on satiety or gastric emptying; weight loss is from direct fat-tissue destruction
- **Mechanism Evidence Is Preclinical**: All mechanistic data derive from animal and cell studies, not validated human pharmacology
