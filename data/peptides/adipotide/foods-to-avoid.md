# Adipotide

## Foods to Avoid

- **Excessive Protein Loads**: Very high protein intake increases renal filtration burden — a real concern given adipotide's documented nephrotoxicity
- **High-Sodium Processed Foods**: Chips, deli meats, fast food, canned soups — excess sodium stresses the kidneys and raises blood pressure
- **Alcohol**: Burdens the kidneys and liver and worsens dehydration — particularly inadvisable given renal risk
- **High-Oxalate Foods in Excess (if kidney concerns exist)**: Large amounts of spinach, beets, and nuts can stress kidneys prone to stones
- **Sugary Drinks and Excess Added Sugar**: Soda, juice, sweets — metabolically counterproductive and offer no benefit
- **Dehydrating Beverages in Excess**: Heavy caffeine without adequate water, given the importance of renal hydration
- **Honest Note**: These are general kidney-protective cautions, not adipotide-specific findings — there are no dietary studies with this experimental compound
