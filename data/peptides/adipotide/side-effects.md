# Adipotide

## Side Effects

- **Nephrotoxicity (Kidney Damage)**: The signature serious risk — animal studies documented dose-dependent kidney injury, including changes in renal tissue. This is the primary reason adipotide has not advanced in human development
- **Unknown Human Safety Profile**: With no controlled human trials, the full human side-effect profile is genuinely unknown — a major hazard
- **Vascular Disruption Off-Target Effects**: A mechanism that kills blood vessels carries theoretical risk to non-target tissues that also express prohibitin
- **Dehydration / Renal Stress**: Rapid fat tissue breakdown and the renal mechanism raise concerns about fluid and electrolyte handling
- **Injection Site Reactions**: Expected with any subcutaneous peptide
- **No Reversibility Data in Humans**: Whether adverse effects are reversible in humans is unknown
- **Bottom Line**: The risk profile is poorly characterized and includes a documented serious organ toxicity — this is an experimental compound, not a safe weight-loss option
