# Adipotide

## Benefits

- **Rapid Fat Loss (Animal Data Only)**: Obese rhesus monkeys lost a meaningful percentage of body weight and showed reduced abdominal fat over a short dosing period in the primary published study
- **Targets Fat Directly**: Mechanism destroys fat-tissue vasculature rather than relying on appetite suppression — of theoretical interest for resistant fat depots
- **Improved Insulin Sensitivity (Animal Data)**: Treated obese monkeys showed improvements in insulin sensitivity alongside fat loss
- **Non-Incretin Mechanism**: Distinct pathway from GLP-1/glucagon drugs, of academic interest for understanding adipose biology
- **IMPORTANT — No Human Efficacy Data**: None of these benefits have been demonstrated in controlled human trials; all benefit claims are preclinical and must not be treated as established
