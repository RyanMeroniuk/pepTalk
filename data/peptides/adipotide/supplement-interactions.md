# Adipotide

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **No Validated Interaction Data**: Because there are no human trials, no supplement interactions have been formally studied — the items below are general cautions, not evidence-based pairings
- **Caution**: Any supplement with renal clearance or kidney burden (high-dose creatine, high-dose vitamin-d3, excessive protein loads) could theoretically compound the documented nephrotoxicity risk — avoid stacking renal stressors
- **Caution**: Nephrotoxic or kidney-processed supplements should be avoided given the kidney risk
- **General Support**: Adequate hydration and electrolytes (magnesium, sodium balance) would be basic supportive measures if the renal mechanism is active, but this is theoretical
- **Avoid**: Combining with other experimental fat-loss agents — uncharacterized compound risk
- **Honest Limitation**: Evidence here is too thin to give specific synergistic recommendations; the responsible position is that adipotide is not ready for human supplement co-use
