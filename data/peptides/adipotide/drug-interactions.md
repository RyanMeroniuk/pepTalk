# Adipotide

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **No Formal Human Interaction Studies**: Because adipotide has no controlled human trials, interactions are inferred from its nephrotoxicity mechanism, not measured
- **Nephrotoxic Drugs (NSAIDs, certain antibiotics)**: Caution — combining with other kidney-stressing drugs likely compounds the documented renal toxicity risk
- **Diuretics**: Caution — additive effects on fluid balance and renal stress
- **Kidney Disease**: Contraindicated — the documented nephrotoxicity makes pre-existing renal impairment a clear contraindication
- **Insulin and Diabetes Medications**: Caution and monitor — rapid fat loss and metabolic shifts could alter glucose control
- **Anticoagulants / Blood Thinners**: Caution — a vascular-disrupting mechanism could theoretically interact with clotting/bleeding risk
- **Pregnancy / Breastfeeding**: Contraindicated — a pro-apoptotic, vascular-disrupting agent is wholly inappropriate
- **Active Cancer**: Caution — pro-apoptotic, vasculature-targeting biology is uncharacterized in this context and should not be self-administered
- **Liver Disease**: Caution — overall organ stress from rapid fat tissue breakdown is uncharacterized
- **Honest Limitation**: The lack of human data is itself the central safety problem; treat adipotide as contraindicated for self-use pending real clinical evidence
