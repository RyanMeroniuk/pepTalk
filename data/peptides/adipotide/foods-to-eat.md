# Adipotide

## Foods to Eat

- **Kidney-Supportive Hydration**: Plenty of water and hydrating foods (cucumber, watermelon, broth) given the documented renal risk — supporting kidney function is the priority concern with this compound
- **Lean, Moderate Protein**: Adequate but not excessive protein (chicken, fish, eggs, Greek yogurt) — very high protein loads increase renal filtration burden, a concern given nephrotoxicity
- **Antioxidant-Rich Vegetables**: Leafy greens, broccoli, bell peppers, berries — general support for tissue under oxidative stress
- **Healthy Fats**: Olive oil, avocado, nuts, fatty fish for overall metabolic health
- **Complex Carbohydrates**: Sweet potatoes, quinoa, oats, brown rice for steady energy
- **Anti-Inflammatory Foods**: Fatty fish (omega-3s), turmeric, ginger, green tea
- **Honest Note**: These are generic healthy-eating and kidney-supportive suggestions, not adipotide-specific data — no diet has been studied with this experimental compound
