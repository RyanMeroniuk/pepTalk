# Adipotide

## Stacking Protocols

- **No Validated Stacks Exist**: Adipotide has no human trials, so there are no evidence-based stacking protocols — the items below are framed as research cautions, not endorsements
- **Do Not Stack With GLP-1 Agonists**: Combining adipotide with semaglutide or tirzepatide is uncharacterized and compounds rapid fat-loss stress on the body — not advisable
- **Avoid Combining With Other Experimental Agents**: Stacking with aod-9604, 5-amino-1mq, or other fat-loss compounds has no safety data and multiplies risk
- **Theoretical Renal Support (Not a Performance Stack)**: If studied, basic kidney support such as adequate hydration, magnesium, and electrolyte balance would be supportive measures rather than a synergy stack
- **Honest Limitation**: Evidence is far too thin to recommend any combination — the responsible position is that adipotide should not be stacked with anything for human use
