# Adipotide

## Things to Avoid

- **Human Self-Experimentation**: The most important thing to avoid — adipotide has no controlled human trials and a documented kidney-toxicity signal. Using it on yourself is genuinely dangerous
- **Trusting Online Dosing Charts**: Any human dose circulating online is extrapolated from animal data and is not safe or validated
- **Ignoring Kidney Symptoms**: Reduced urination, swelling, flank pain, or fatigue could signal renal injury — the signature toxicity seen in animals
- **Combining With Other Nephrotoxic Agents**: NSAIDs, other experimental compounds, heavy alcohol, or dehydration compound the renal risk
- **Treating It Like an Approved Drug**: It is not approved anywhere and is not in routine clinical development
- **Assuming Animal Results Transfer to Humans**: Monkey efficacy and safety do not guarantee human outcomes; the human profile is unknown
- **Sourcing from Gray-Market Vendors**: Research-only powders carry purity, dosing, and contamination risks on top of the inherent danger
- **Skipping Renal Monitoring**: In any research context, kidney function monitoring would be essential — but the responsible stance is to avoid human use altogether
