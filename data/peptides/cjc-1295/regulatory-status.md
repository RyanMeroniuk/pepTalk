# CJC-1295

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT FDA-approved** for any indication
- Clinical trials conducted for GH deficiency
- One death reported during clinical trials (likely unrelated, but led to program pause)
- Placed on FDA Category 2 bulk drug list (regulatory uncertainty)
- Available from compounding pharmacies and research chemical suppliers
- **Banned by WADA** for competitive athletes
- Legal challenges have temporarily suspended FDA enforcement against compounding
