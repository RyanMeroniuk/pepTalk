# CJC-1295

## Lifestyle Recommendations


### Injection Timing & Fasting
- **Inject in a fasted state** — this is non-negotiable for efficacy. Insulin and free fatty acids from food directly suppress GH release at the pituitary level
- **Evening injection 20-30 minutes before bed** is the gold standard timing — it amplifies the natural nocturnal GH pulse that occurs during Stage 3/4 deep sleep
- **Morning fasted injection** (upon waking, before eating) is the second-best window, especially if training in the morning
- **Post-workout injection** (if fasted or 2+ hours post-meal) capitalizes on the exercise-induced GH pulse
- **Wait at least 20-30 minutes after injection** before consuming any food — allow the GH pulse to peak

### Exercise
- **Resistance training 4-5x/week** — compound movements (squats, deadlifts, bench press, rows, overhead press) produce the strongest endogenous GH response and synergize with CJC-1295
- **High-intensity interval training (HIIT) 2-3x/week** — sprint intervals, rowing intervals, or battle ropes for 15-20 minutes drive additional GH pulses
- **Keep rest periods moderate** (60-90 seconds between sets) — shorter rest periods elevate lactic acid, which further stimulates GH release
- **Train in the evening before your bedtime injection** if possible — the exercise-induced GH pulse stacks with the peptide-induced pulse
- **Avoid excessive steady-state cardio** (60+ minutes) — prolonged moderate cardio elevates cortisol, which antagonizes GH signaling

### Sleep Optimization
- **Prioritize 7-9 hours of quality sleep** — approximately 70% of daily GH secretion occurs during deep sleep
- **Keep bedroom dark, cool (65-68°F), and quiet** — melatonin production supports GH release
- **Consistent sleep/wake schedule** — irregular sleep disrupts the circadian GH pulse pattern
- **Avoid screens 30-60 minutes before bed** — blue light suppresses melatonin, which is synergistic with GH secretion
- **Consider magnesium glycinate (400mg) before bed** — supports both sleep quality and GH release

### Daily Habits
- **Stay well hydrated** (half your body weight in ounces of water daily) — dehydration impairs GH signaling and recovery
- **Manage stress actively** — chronic cortisol elevation directly suppresses GH release. Practice deep breathing, meditation, or walks in nature
- **Get morning sunlight within 30 minutes of waking** — sets circadian rhythm, which optimizes the nocturnal GH pulse
- **Monitor blood work every 8-12 weeks**: IGF-1, fasting glucose, fasting insulin, HbA1c, and a comprehensive metabolic panel
- **Track body composition** rather than just scale weight — GH optimization often increases lean mass while reducing fat, so the scale may not move dramatically
