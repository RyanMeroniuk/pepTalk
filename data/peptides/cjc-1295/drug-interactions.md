# CJC-1295

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Diabetes medications**: May need adjustment (GH affects glucose metabolism)
- **Insulin**: Altered blood sugar management
- **Glucocorticoids**: May reduce effectiveness of CJC-1295
- **Contraindicated**: Active cancer or cancer history (elevated GH/IGF-1)
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy/breastfeeding
- **Contraindicated**: Pituitary tumors or disorders
