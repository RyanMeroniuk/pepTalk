# CJC-1295

## Dosing Protocols

- **With DAC**: 2mg subcutaneous injection, 1-2x per week
- **Without DAC (Mod GRF)**: 100-300 mcg per injection, 2-3x daily
- **Most Common Combo**: 100mcg Mod GRF + 100mcg Ipamorelin, 2-3x daily
- **Timing**: Morning fasted, post-workout, and/or before bed — fasted state strongly preferred
- **Must fast 2+ hours before** and 30 minutes after injection for optimal GH release (carbs and fats blunt GH secretion)
- **Cycle**: 8-12 weeks on, 4 weeks off (with DAC); continuous cycling common (without DAC)
- **Reconstitute** with bacteriostatic water; store refrigerated at 36-46°F
- **Results Timeline**: 4-6 weeks for initial effects; 8-12 weeks for pronounced changes
