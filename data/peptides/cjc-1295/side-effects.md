# CJC-1295

## Side Effects

- **Injection Site Reactions**: Redness, swelling, mild irritation
- **Flushing**: Warmth sensation, facial flushing shortly after injection
- **Headache**: Usually transient, resolves within first week
- **Water Retention**: Dose-dependent, typically mild
- **Dizziness**: Occasional, related to vasodilation
- **Transient Low Blood Pressure**: From vasodilation effects
- **Potential Insulin Resistance**: With prolonged high-dose use
- **Immunogenicity**: Body may develop antibodies (especially with DAC form)
- **Carpal Tunnel Symptoms**: From elevated GH/IGF-1 levels (dose-dependent)
