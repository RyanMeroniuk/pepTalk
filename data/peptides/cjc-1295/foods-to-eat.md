# CJC-1295

## Foods to Eat

- **Wild-caught salmon** — rich in omega-3 fatty acids that reduce inflammation and support GH signaling, plus high-quality protein
- **Grass-fed beef** — complete amino acid profile with high arginine and ornithine content, both GH precursors
- **Pasture-raised eggs** — excellent protein source with cholesterol needed for hormone synthesis
- **Turkey breast** — high in tryptophan, which supports serotonin and melatonin production for better sleep and GH release
- **Greek yogurt (plain, full-fat)** — combine protein with glutamine, an amino acid that stimulates GH release (eat at least 2 hours before injection)
- **Pineapple** — contains serotonin precursors; eating a small amount before bed may support melatonin production
- **Sweet potatoes** — slow-digesting complex carb that replenishes glycogen without spiking insulin excessively (eat at meals away from injection time)
- **Oats (steel-cut)** — complex carbs with beta-glucan fiber; eat at breakfast if injecting in the evening
- **Spinach and kale** — high in magnesium, which supports GH release and sleep quality
- **Brazil nuts** — selenium supports thyroid function, which interacts with the GH/IGF-1 axis (2-3 nuts daily)
- **Watermelon** — one of the highest food sources of L-citrulline, which converts to arginine and supports GH
- **Raw cacao or dark chocolate (85%+)** — contains magnesium and tryptophan; can support sleep and GH indirectly
- **Bone broth** — rich in glycine and collagen precursors that complement GH-mediated tissue repair
- **Cottage cheese (before bed)** — casein protein provides slow-release amino acids overnight, supporting the GH-driven anabolic window during sleep
