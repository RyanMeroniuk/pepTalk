# CJC-1295

## Overview

CJC-1295 is a synthetic 30-amino acid analog of growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH). Developed by ConjuChem Biotechnologies. Available in two distinct forms: CJC-1295 with DAC (Drug Affinity Complex) for extended release, and CJC-1295 without DAC (also called Modified GRF 1-29 or Mod GRF) for shorter, more pulsatile action. The DAC modification binds to serum albumin in the bloodstream, dramatically extending the peptide's half-life from minutes to approximately 6-8 days.
