# CJC-1295

## Foods to Avoid

- **High-sugar foods near injection time** — glucose and the resulting insulin spike directly suppress GH release from the pituitary. This includes candy, soda, fruit juice, and desserts
- **Refined carbohydrates within 2 hours of injection** — white bread, pasta, rice, and cereals cause rapid insulin elevation that blunts the GH pulse
- **Fatty meals within 2 hours of injection** — free fatty acids in the bloodstream inhibit GH secretion independently of insulin
- **Alcohol** — even moderate alcohol consumption suppresses GH secretion by up to 75% for several hours. Avoid entirely on injection days, especially in the evening
- **Processed foods and seed oils** — promote systemic inflammation that impairs GH signaling and recovery
- **Excessive dairy close to injection** — the insulin response from dairy (especially milk) can blunt GH release
- **Large meals before bed** — eating a heavy meal before your bedtime injection defeats the fasting requirement and suppresses the nocturnal GH pulse
- **Diet soda and artificial sweeteners** — some evidence suggests they can still trigger an insulin response, potentially blunting GH release
