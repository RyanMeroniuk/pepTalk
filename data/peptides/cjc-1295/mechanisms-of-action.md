# CJC-1295

## Mechanisms of Action

- **GHRH Analog**: Directly stimulates pituitary somatotroph cells to synthesize and release growth hormone
- **IGF-1 Elevation**: Increases circulating IGF-1 via GH-mediated hepatic production
- **Pulsatile GH**: Amplifies the body's natural GH pulsatility (especially the without-DAC form)
- **Does not desensitize** the GH axis when used at appropriate doses
- **Synergy with GHRPs**: When combined with Ipamorelin or GHRP-6, produces amplified GH release that is synergistic (greater than additive) — GHRH releases GH while GHRP reduces somatostatin inhibition
