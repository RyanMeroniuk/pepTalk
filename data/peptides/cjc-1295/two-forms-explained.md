# CJC-1295

## Two Forms Explained

- **With DAC**: Binds serum albumin, half-life ~6-8 days. Produces sustained, continuous GH elevation. Dosed 1-2x per week. More convenient but less physiological (constant GH vs pulses).
- **Without DAC (Mod GRF 1-29)**: Half-life ~30 minutes. Produces acute GH pulses that better mimic natural physiology. Dosed 2-3x daily. More commonly combined with Ipamorelin. Generally preferred by experienced users.
