# CJC-1295

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Best Stack**: Ipamorelin — the most popular combination ("CJC/Ipa stack")
- **Synergistic**: Resistance training maximizes muscle and body composition changes
- **Synergistic**: Adequate protein intake (1.6-2.2g/kg body weight)
- **Critical**: Must inject in fasted state — carbs and fats significantly blunt GH release
- **Avoid**: High-glycemic meals near injection time
- **Enhanced by**: Quality sleep, stress management, consistent training
- **Supplement support**: Zinc (30-50mg), Magnesium (400mg), Vitamin D3 (4000-5000 IU)
- **Caution**: Diabetes medications — monitor blood glucose
- **Caution**: Other GH-elevating compounds — risk of excess GH/IGF-1
