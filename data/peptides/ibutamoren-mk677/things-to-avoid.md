# Ibutamoren (MK-677)

## Things to Avoid

- **Giving in to every hunger signal** — MK-677's appetite increase is pharmacological, not a sign of genuine caloric need. Eating everything in sight leads to significant fat gain. Pre-plan your meals and stick to portions
- **Skipping exercise** — without training, MK-677 primarily produces water retention, appetite increase, and potential fat gain. The muscle-building and body composition benefits require a training stimulus
- **Ignoring blood glucose** — MK-677's impact on insulin sensitivity is its most significant health concern. Failure to monitor can lead to prediabetic glucose levels, especially with prolonged use or genetic predisposition
- **Alcohol** — compounds the metabolic burden (blood sugar, sleep disruption, empty calories, GH suppression). Particularly problematic with MK-677's existing glucose effects
- **Taking MK-677 with uncontrolled diabetes or prediabetes** — the insulin resistance effect can push borderline glucose levels into diabetic range
- **Weighing yourself daily and panicking** — water retention of 3-8 pounds in the first few weeks is normal and does not represent fat gain. Track weekly trends and body measurements instead
- **Doses above 25mg** — diminishing returns on GH release with proportionally more side effects (appetite, water retention, glucose impairment)
- **Combining with other compounds that impair glucose tolerance** — stacking MK-677 with other insulin-sensitizing disruptors increases metabolic risk
- **Eating close to bedtime when dosing at night** — while MK-677 does not strictly require fasting, a large meal before bed plus the appetite surge can lead to excessive nighttime eating
- **Chronic use without breaks** — while the 2-year study showed no tachyphylaxis, periodic breaks (4-8 weeks off every 12-16 weeks) allow insulin sensitivity to recover
