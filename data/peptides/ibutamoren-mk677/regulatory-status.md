# Ibutamoren (MK-677)

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT FDA-approved** for any indication
- Multiple clinical trials completed (obesity, sarcopenia, osteoporosis, GH deficiency, frailty in elderly)
- 2-year study demonstrated sustained IGF-1 elevation without tolerance (no tachyphylaxis)
- **Banned by WADA** for competitive athletes
- Available from research chemical suppliers
- Classified as an investigational drug
- Some compounding pharmacies carry it with prescription
