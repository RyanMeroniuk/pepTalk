# Ibutamoren (MK-677)

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Synergistic**: Resistance training amplifies muscle-building effects dramatically
- **Synergistic**: High protein intake (2g/kg body weight) maximizes muscle protein synthesis
- **Manage Appetite**: High-fiber foods, protein-rich meals, and evening dosing help control hunger
- **Caution**: Diabetes medications — MK-677 affects blood glucose, may require dose adjustments
- **Caution**: Stacking with other GH secretagogues may overstimulate the GH axis
- **Monitor**: Blood glucose, fasting insulin, and IGF-1 levels with prolonged use
- **Enhanced by**: Quality sleep hygiene (synergizes with MK-677's sleep improvements)
- **Supplement support**: Berberine or chromium may help offset glucose effects
- **Magnesium**: 400mg for sleep quality and GH support
