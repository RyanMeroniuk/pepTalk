# Ibutamoren (MK-677)

## Benefits

- **Muscle Growth**: Increased lean muscle mass and strength, enhanced protein synthesis
- **Fat Loss**: Reduced body fat, especially when combined with caloric control
- **Sleep Quality**: Significantly improved sleep depth and duration — one of the most reliable benefits
- **Bone Density**: Enhanced bone mineral density with long-term use (2+ year studies)
- **Skin & Hair**: Improved skin quality, collagen production, and hair health via GH/IGF-1
- **Recovery**: Faster recovery from exercise and injury
- **Nitrogen Retention**: Reversal of diet-induced nitrogen wasting (muscle-sparing during caloric deficit)
- **Neuroprotection**: Potential neuroprotective effects via IGF-1 elevation
- **Anti-Aging**: Restoration of youthful GH/IGF-1 levels in aging populations
- **Convenience**: Oral dosing — no injections required
