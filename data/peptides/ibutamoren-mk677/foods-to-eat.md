# Ibutamoren (MK-677)

## Foods to Eat

- **Chicken breast and turkey** — lean, high-protein sources that satisfy hunger without excess calories. Essential for channeling MK-677's appetite increase into muscle-building nutrition
- **Wild-caught salmon and sardines** — omega-3s improve insulin sensitivity (counteracting MK-677's glucose effects) and reduce inflammation
- **Eggs (whole, pasture-raised)** — complete protein, healthy fats, and cholesterol for hormone synthesis. A satiating breakfast option when hunger is high
- **Lentils and black beans** — high in fiber and protein, these keep you full far longer than refined carbs. Ideal for managing the appetite increase
- **Steel-cut oats** — slow-digesting complex carbs with beta-glucan fiber that stabilizes blood sugar
- **Sweet potatoes** — complex carbs with a lower glycemic index than white potatoes, plus potassium to help with water retention
- **Broccoli, cauliflower, and Brussels sprouts** — cruciferous vegetables provide fiber and volume with minimal calories, helping manage appetite
- **Avocado** — healthy fats and potassium; satiating and helps counteract water retention via potassium content
- **Spinach and kale** — magnesium for sleep and GH support, potassium for water balance
- **Berries (blueberries, raspberries)** — low-glycemic fruit with antioxidants; a smart choice when sugar cravings hit
- **Greek yogurt (plain)** — high protein, contains glutamine, and probiotics for gut health. Add berries instead of sugar
- **Bone broth** — supports tissue repair, provides glycine for sleep, and the warm liquid is satiating
- **Cinnamon** — add to oats or yogurt; research shows it may improve insulin sensitivity and help with MK-677's glucose effects
- **Apple cider vinegar** — use as salad dressing base; may improve post-meal glucose response
