# Ibutamoren (MK-677)

## Dosing Protocols

- **Beginner**: 10 mg/day orally
- **Standard**: 15-20 mg/day
- **Advanced**: 25 mg/day (higher doses yield diminishing returns with more side effects)
- **Timing**: Evening/before bed preferred — leverages GH pulse during sleep and manages appetite increase (you sleep through the hunger)
- **Cycle**: 8-12 weeks on, 4-8 weeks off OR continuous low-dose (10mg) — no tachyphylaxis observed in 2-year study
- **Can take with or without food**
- **No reconstitution needed** — oral tablet or liquid
