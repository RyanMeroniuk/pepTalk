# Ibutamoren (MK-677)

## Foods to Avoid

- **High-sugar foods and beverages** — MK-677 already impairs insulin sensitivity. Adding sugar creates a compounding metabolic burden. Avoid soda, candy, pastries, and fruit juice
- **Refined carbohydrates** — white bread, white rice, pasta, and cereals spike blood sugar rapidly. MK-677 users are more susceptible to glucose dysregulation
- **Processed and fast food** — high in sodium (worsens water retention), sugar, and inflammatory seed oils. The appetite increase from MK-677 makes junk food cravings particularly dangerous
- **Excessive sodium** — canned soups, chips, deli meats, and restaurant food exacerbate MK-677's water retention significantly
- **Alcohol** — impairs GH release, disrupts the sleep improvements MK-677 provides, and adds empty calories when appetite is already elevated
- **Ice cream and sugary desserts** — the combination of sugar and fat is the worst choice when both appetite and insulin resistance are elevated
- **Sugary cereals and granola bars** — marketed as healthy but spike blood sugar; particularly problematic with MK-677's glucose effects
- **Fruit juice** — concentrated sugar without fiber. Eat whole fruit instead
