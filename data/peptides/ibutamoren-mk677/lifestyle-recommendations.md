# Ibutamoren (MK-677)

## Lifestyle Recommendations


### Dosing Timing & Strategy
- **Take before bed (30-60 minutes)** — this is the optimal timing for three reasons: (1) leverages the natural nocturnal GH pulse, (2) you sleep through the peak appetite increase, and (3) MK-677 dramatically improves sleep quality
- **Take with a small amount of food if needed** — unlike injectable GH secretagogues, MK-677 does not require strict fasting. A small protein-rich snack is acceptable and may reduce initial nausea
- **Consistent daily timing** — MK-677's 24-hour half-life means once-daily dosing is sufficient. Same time each day maintains stable blood levels
- **Start at 10mg for the first 2 weeks** — allows you to assess appetite increase and water retention before moving to the standard 15-25mg dose

### Exercise
- **Resistance training 4-5x/week is essential** — MK-677 without exercise yields primarily water weight and appetite increase rather than meaningful body composition changes. Compound movements (squats, deadlifts, bench, rows) maximize the anabolic environment
- **HIIT 2-3x/week** — 15-20 minute sessions improve insulin sensitivity, which directly counteracts MK-677's tendency to reduce glucose tolerance
- **Morning fasted cardio (20-30 minutes, low to moderate intensity)** — helps manage the water retention and body fat that can accumulate if appetite is not controlled
- **Train in the evening before your dose** — the post-workout window plus the bedtime GH surge creates a powerful recovery and growth stimulus
- **Do not skip training days** — MK-677 increases appetite significantly. Without the caloric demand of training, excess calories easily become fat storage

### Managing Appetite (Critical for MK-677)
- **Expect intense hunger**, especially in the first 2-4 weeks — this is ghrelin receptor activation and is the most common complaint
- **Evening dosing is the primary strategy** — you sleep through the worst of the appetite spike
- **Pre-prepare meals and snacks** — having portioned, high-protein meals ready prevents impulsive overeating when hunger hits
- **High-fiber, high-protein meals** keep you satiated longer — a meal of chicken breast, roasted vegetables, and sweet potato will hold you far better than processed foods
- **Drink 16oz of water when hunger strikes** — thirst is often mistaken for hunger, and MK-677's water retention means you need extra fluids anyway
- **The appetite typically moderates after 3-4 weeks** as ghrelin receptors partially downregulate

### Managing Water Retention
- **Water retention peaks in weeks 2-4** and typically subsides — this is not fat gain, though it can be discouraging on the scale
- **Reduce sodium intake** — excess sodium exacerbates MK-677 water retention. Avoid processed foods, canned soups, and restaurant meals
- **Increase potassium intake** — bananas, avocados, sweet potatoes, and spinach help balance sodium and reduce bloating
- **Stay well hydrated** — paradoxically, drinking more water helps flush excess water retention. Aim for 100+ oz daily
- **Dandelion root tea** — a natural mild diuretic that can help manage bloating without electrolyte disruption

### Blood Sugar Management (Critical for MK-677)
- **Monitor fasting blood glucose monthly** — MK-677 can reduce insulin sensitivity, especially at doses above 15mg or with prolonged use
- **Exercise is the best countermeasure** — both resistance training and HIIT significantly improve insulin sensitivity
- **Consider berberine (500mg 2-3x daily with meals)** — research supports its ability to improve insulin sensitivity, and it may offset MK-677's glucose effects
- **Chromium picolinate (200-400mcg daily)** — supports glucose metabolism
- **Apple cider vinegar (1-2 tbsp before meals)** — may blunt post-meal glucose spikes
- **If fasting glucose rises above 100 mg/dL**, reduce dose or discontinue

### Sleep Optimization
- **MK-677 is one of the best sleep-enhancing compounds available** — studies show increased REM and Stage 4 deep sleep
- **Maintain a consistent sleep schedule** — go to bed within 30 minutes of the same time each night
- **Keep bedroom cool (65-68°F), dark, and quiet** — maximize the sleep quality enhancement
- **Expect vivid dreams** — this is a common and generally benign effect of enhanced REM sleep
- **Aim for 8-9 hours of sleep opportunity** — MK-677 makes sleep deeper and more restorative; give it sufficient time to work

### Daily Habits
- **Weigh yourself weekly, not daily** — water retention causes daily fluctuations that are misleading. Track weekly averages and body measurements instead
- **Take progress photos monthly** — MK-677's water retention can mask visual fat loss on the scale, but photos reveal body composition changes
- **Blood work every 8-12 weeks**: fasting glucose, fasting insulin, HbA1c, IGF-1, and a comprehensive metabolic panel
- **Morning sunlight exposure (15-20 minutes)** — supports circadian rhythm and helps regulate the GH axis
