# Ibutamoren (MK-677)

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Diabetes medications**: May require adjustment due to glucose effects
- **Insulin**: Risk of altered blood sugar management
- **Other GH-elevating compounds**: Risk of excess GH/IGF-1
- **Corticosteroids**: May compound metabolic side effects
- **Contraindicated**: Active cancer (elevated IGF-1 may promote tumor growth)
- **Contraindicated**: Uncontrolled diabetes
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy/breastfeeding
