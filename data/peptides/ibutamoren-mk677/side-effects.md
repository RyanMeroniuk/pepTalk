# Ibutamoren (MK-677)

## Side Effects

- **Increased Appetite** (most common and most significant — the primary complaint)
- **Water Retention/Bloating** (dose-dependent, typically subsides after 2-3 weeks)
- **Numbness/Tingling** in extremities (transient, usually resolves)
- **Lethargy/Fatigue** initially (first 1-2 weeks)
- **Elevated Blood Glucose**: Reduced insulin sensitivity, especially with prolonged high-dose use
- **Joint Pain**: Related to water retention, usually temporary
- **Vivid Dreams**: Due to enhanced deep sleep (many consider this a positive)
- **Potential Insulin Resistance**: With prolonged use at higher doses — monitor fasting glucose
- **No significant cortisol or prolactin changes**
