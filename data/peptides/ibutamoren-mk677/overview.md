# Ibutamoren (MK-677)

## Overview

Ibutamoren (MK-677) is a non-peptide, orally active growth hormone secretagogue that mimics the hormone ghrelin. Developed by Merck. Unlike most GH-releasing peptides which require injection, MK-677 is taken orally as a pill or liquid. It stimulates the pituitary gland to release growth hormone (GH) and raises IGF-1 levels without affecting cortisol. Half-life of approximately 24 hours, allowing convenient once-daily dosing. Does not suppress the body's natural GH production, unlike exogenous growth hormone.
