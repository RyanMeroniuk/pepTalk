# Ibutamoren (MK-677)

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Ghrelin Mimetic**: Binds to ghrelin/GHS-R1a receptors in the hypothalamus and pituitary gland
- **Pulsatile GH Release**: Stimulates growth hormone secretion in a pattern that mimics the body's natural pulsatile release
- **IGF-1 Elevation**: Raises insulin-like growth factor 1 levels by 40-90% with sustained use
- **No GH Suppression**: Does not suppress natural GH production (unlike exogenous GH injection)
- **Appetite Stimulation**: Activates ghrelin pathways, significantly increasing hunger signals
- **Sleep Architecture**: Increases REM sleep and Stage 4 deep sleep duration and quality
- **Nitrogen Retention**: Promotes positive nitrogen balance, supporting muscle protein synthesis
