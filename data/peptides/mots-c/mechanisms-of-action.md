# MOTS-c (Mitochondrial ORF of the 12S rRNA type-C)

## Mechanisms of Action

- **AMPK Activation**: Activates AMP-activated protein kinase — the master regulator of cellular energy metabolism
- **Folate-Methionine Cycle Inhibition**: Inhibits the folate cycle, which drives AMPK activation as a downstream effect
- **Glucose Uptake**: Enhances glucose uptake and utilization in skeletal muscle
- **Fat Oxidation**: Stimulates fatty acid oxidation in mitochondria
- **Mitochondrial Biogenesis**: Promotes creation of new mitochondria in cells
- **NAD+ Regulation**: Supports NAD+ levels critical for cellular energy metabolism
- **Nuclear Translocation**: Under cellular stress, physically moves from mitochondria to the nucleus to regulate gene expression
- **Mito-Nuclear Communication**: Facilitates critical signaling between mitochondria and nucleus
- **Anti-Senescence**: Reduces cellular senescence markers in aging tissues
- **Insulin Sensitization**: Improves insulin sensitivity via mechanisms similar to metformin
