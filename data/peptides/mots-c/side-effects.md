# MOTS-c (Mitochondrial ORF of the 12S rRNA type-C)

## Side Effects

- Injection site irritation and redness
- Heart palpitations (uncommon)
- Insomnia (if dosed too late in the day)
- Fever (rare)
- Headache and flushing
- Fatigue (paradoxically, during initial 1-2 week adjustment period)
- Mild GI symptoms (nausea, stomach discomfort)
- **Limited long-term human safety data** — most evidence is preclinical
