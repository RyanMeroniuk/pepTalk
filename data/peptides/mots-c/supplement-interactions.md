# MOTS-c (Mitochondrial ORF of the 12S rRNA type-C)

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Synergistic**: Exercise — MOTS-c is naturally released during exercise; combining amplifies all benefits
- **Synergistic**: Resistance training + MOTS-c for muscle and metabolic benefits
- **Synergistic**: Intermittent fasting (both activate AMPK pathway)
- **Mitochondrial Support**: CoQ10 (200mg), PQQ (20mg) — support electron transport chain
- **NAD+ Precursors**: NMN (500mg-1g) or NR — support mitochondrial NAD+ levels
- **Caution**: METFORMIN — both activate AMPK, risk of over-activation and hypoglycemia
- **Caution**: Thiazolidinediones (TZDs) — AMPK interaction
- **Caution**: Aspirin — AMPK pathway overlap at high doses
- **Enhanced by**: Anti-inflammatory diet, adequate sleep, regular exercise
- **Contraindicated**: Active cancer (uncertain effects on tumor metabolism)
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy/breastfeeding
