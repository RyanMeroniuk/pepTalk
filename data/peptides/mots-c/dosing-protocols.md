# MOTS-c (Mitochondrial ORF of the 12S rRNA type-C)

## Dosing Protocols

- **Standard**: 5mg subcutaneous injection
- **Frequency**: 2x per week (e.g., Monday/Thursday)
- **Dose Range**: 5-10mg per injection
- **Half-Life**: ~1-2 hours in circulation (hence the higher mg-level dosing)
- **Cycle**: 8-12 weeks on, 4 weeks off
- **Timing**: Morning preferred; some protocols dose pre-exercise for synergistic effects
- **Results Timeline**: Energy improvements 1-2 weeks; physical performance gains 4-6 weeks
- **Reconstitution**: Bacteriostatic water; refrigerate after reconstitution
- **Storage**: Use within approximately 1 month after reconstitution
