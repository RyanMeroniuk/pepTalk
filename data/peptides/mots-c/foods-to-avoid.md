# MOTS-c (Mitochondrial ORF of the 12S rRNA type-C)

## Foods to Avoid

- **Refined Sugars**: Candy, pastries, ice cream, sugary cereals, soda, fruit juice. Cause insulin spikes that promote fat storage and impair the insulin sensitization that MOTS-c provides
- **Refined Carbohydrates**: White bread, white pasta, white rice, bagels, crackers. Rapid glucose spikes counteract MOTS-c's glucose regulation benefits
- **Processed and Ultra-Processed Foods**: Fast food, packaged snacks, frozen dinners, deli meats. Contain inflammatory ingredients that damage mitochondria and impair metabolic function
- **Seed Oils and Trans Fats**: Soybean oil, corn oil, canola oil (in excess), margarine, deep-fried foods. Promote inflammation and damage mitochondrial membranes
- **Excessive Alcohol**: Directly toxic to mitochondria, depletes NAD+ (which MOTS-c works to preserve), disrupts sleep, and promotes insulin resistance
- **High-Fructose Corn Syrup**: Found in many processed foods and drinks. Promotes liver fat accumulation and insulin resistance
- **Late-Night Heavy Meals**: Eating large meals close to bedtime impairs insulin sensitivity (the opposite of what MOTS-c supports) and disrupts sleep quality
