# MOTS-c (Mitochondrial ORF of the 12S rRNA type-C)

## Foods to Eat

- **Lean Proteins**: Chicken breast, turkey, salmon, cod, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, lean beef, bison, tofu, tempeh, lentils, chickpeas — protein supports muscle maintenance and AMPK-driven body recomposition
- **Mitochondria-Supporting Foods**: Wild-caught fatty fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel — omega-3s support mitochondrial membranes), organ meats (liver — rich in CoQ10, B vitamins), dark leafy greens (spinach, kale — magnesium for mitochondrial ATP production)
- **NAD+-Supporting Foods**: Foods rich in niacin (vitamin B3) — chicken breast, tuna, turkey, mushrooms, green peas, avocado. Supports NAD+ levels that MOTS-c helps regulate
- **Polyphenol-Rich Foods**: Blueberries, dark chocolate (85%+), green tea, pomegranate, red grapes, extra virgin olive oil — polyphenols support mitochondrial function and reduce oxidative stress
- **Complex Carbohydrates (insulin-sensitive choices)**: Sweet potatoes, quinoa, oats, brown rice, lentils, chickpeas, black beans — steady energy without insulin spikes
- **Healthy Fats**: Avocado, extra virgin olive oil, almonds, walnuts, chia seeds, flaxseed, MCT oil (rapidly used by mitochondria for energy)
- **Anti-Inflammatory Foods**: Turmeric (with black pepper for absorption), ginger, fatty fish, berries, leafy greens, extra virgin olive oil
- **Fermented Foods**: Kefir, yogurt, sauerkraut, kimchi, kombucha — gut health supports systemic metabolic function
