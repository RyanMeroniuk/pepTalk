# MOTS-c (Mitochondrial ORF of the 12S rRNA type-C)

## Things to Avoid

- **Sedentary Lifestyle**: MOTS-c provides "exercise mimetic" benefits, but this does NOT mean you can skip exercise. Physical activity multiplies MOTS-c's effects many-fold. Without exercise, you are leaving the majority of the benefit on the table
- **Chronic Sleep Deprivation**: Less than 6 hours of sleep consistently impairs mitochondrial repair, raises cortisol, promotes insulin resistance, and reduces MOTS-c's metabolic benefits. Sleep is when cellular repair happens
- **Chronic Unmanaged Stress**: Prolonged cortisol elevation damages mitochondria, promotes visceral fat storage, and impairs glucose metabolism — directly counteracting MOTS-c's mechanisms
- **Late-Night Eating**: Eating within 2-3 hours of bedtime reduces insulin sensitivity and impairs metabolic function overnight. Time-restricted eating windows work synergistically with MOTS-c
- **Excessive AMPK Activation**: If taking metformin alongside MOTS-c, monitor for over-activation of the AMPK pathway. Symptoms include excessive fatigue, hypoglycemia, or GI distress. Do not stack multiple AMPK activators without medical supervision
- **Dosing Late in the Day**: MOTS-c can cause insomnia if injected too late. Morning dosing is preferred
- **Ignoring Metabolic Markers**: Regular monitoring of fasting glucose, HbA1c, and insulin levels ensures the peptide is working as expected and catches any issues early
- **Alcohol Consumption on Injection Days**: Alcohol impairs the mitochondrial pathways that MOTS-c activates. Avoid alcohol within 24 hours of injection for optimal results
- **Expecting Instant Results**: Energy improvements appear in 1-2 weeks, but body composition and performance changes take 4-6 weeks. Stay consistent with exercise and diet
