# MOTS-c (Mitochondrial ORF of the 12S rRNA type-C)

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Exercise is the #1 Force Multiplier**: MOTS-c is an "exercise mimetic," but when combined with actual exercise, the effects are dramatically amplified — far greater than either alone. Your body naturally releases MOTS-c during exercise; supplementing it on top of regular training creates a powerful synergy for metabolism, endurance, and body composition
- **Resistance Training 3-4x/Week**: Compound movements (squats, deadlifts, presses, rows) stimulate the AMPK pathway that MOTS-c also activates. Together they drive mitochondrial biogenesis, glucose uptake, and lean mass preservation. Progressive overload is important
- **Cardio/Endurance Training 3-5x/Week**: MOTS-c enhances endurance capacity. Combine with zone 2 cardio (brisk walking, cycling, jogging at conversational pace) for 30-45 minutes to maximize mitochondrial function. High-intensity interval training (HIIT) 1-2x/week for additional metabolic stimulus
- **Pre-Exercise Dosing**: Some protocols call for injecting MOTS-c 30-60 minutes before exercise for maximal synergy. Morning exercise after morning injection aligns well
- **Daily Step Count**: Aim for 8,000-10,000+ steps. Consistent low-level movement keeps AMPK pathway active throughout the day
- **Sleep 7-9 Hours**: Sleep is when mitochondrial repair and metabolic recovery occur. Poor sleep impairs mitochondrial function, raises cortisol, increases insulin resistance, and reduces the metabolic benefits MOTS-c provides. Maintain consistent sleep/wake times
- **Meal Timing for Insulin Sensitivity**: Eat meals earlier in the day when insulin sensitivity is naturally highest. Avoid large meals within 3 hours of bedtime. Consider time-restricted eating (eating within an 8-10 hour window) — this activates AMPK similarly to MOTS-c and creates complementary metabolic effects
- **Intermittent Fasting Synergy**: Both intermittent fasting and MOTS-c activate the AMPK pathway. Combining them (e.g., 16:8 fasting protocol) may amplify metabolic benefits. Ensure adequate total calorie and protein intake within your eating window
- **Stress Management**: Chronic stress raises cortisol, which promotes visceral fat storage, impairs insulin sensitivity, and damages mitochondria — directly opposing MOTS-c's benefits. Incorporate meditation, deep breathing, cold exposure, or yoga
- **Cold Exposure**: Cold showers or ice baths activate mitochondrial biogenesis and brown fat metabolism via similar pathways as MOTS-c. Consider 2-3 minutes of cold exposure post-workout
- **Track Metabolic Markers**: Monitor fasting glucose, HbA1c, fasting insulin, and lipid panels quarterly. Track body composition (waist circumference, body fat percentage) — not just scale weight
- **Anti-Inflammatory Lifestyle**: MOTS-c has anti-inflammatory properties. Support this by minimizing processed food, managing stress, sleeping well, and exercising regularly
- **Limit Alcohol**: Alcohol damages mitochondria, impairs NAD+ metabolism, disrupts sleep, and promotes insulin resistance — all of which undermine MOTS-c's core mechanisms
