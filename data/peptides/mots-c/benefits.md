# MOTS-c (Mitochondrial ORF of the 12S rRNA type-C)

## Benefits

- **Metabolic Function**: Enhanced glucose metabolism and insulin sensitivity. Exhibits "physiological similarities to metformin"
- **Obesity Prevention**: Prevented obesity onset in high-fat diet animal models. Higher circulating levels negatively correlate with obesity
- **Physical Performance**: Improved muscle strength, endurance, and motor coordination across age groups in both animal and human research
- **Body Composition**: Visceral fat reduction potential, improved lean mass to fat ratio
- **Anti-Inflammatory**: Antiallodynic effects (2024 study), pain reduction from inflammatory stimuli, improved inflammatory markers
- **Bone Health**: Promotes osteoblast proliferation and mineralization, strengthens bone density — particularly beneficial for postmenopausal women
- **Longevity/Aging**: Counteracts age-related metabolic decline, potential healthspan extension
- **Pancreatic Health**: Reduces senescence in pancreatic islet cells, may delay diabetes onset
- **Cardiac Function**: Restores mitochondrial respiration in type 2 diabetic heart tissue (2025 Frontiers study)
- **Exercise Mimetic**: Provides metabolic benefits even without exercise (but exercise amplifies effects dramatically)
