# MOTS-c (Mitochondrial ORF of the 12S rRNA type-C)

## Overview

MOTS-c is a naturally occurring 16-amino acid peptide encoded by mitochondrial DNA, specifically within the 12S rRNA gene. Discovered in 2015 by Dr. Changhan David Lee at USC. Functions as a "mitokine" — a mitochondrial-derived signaling molecule that communicates between mitochondria and the cell nucleus. Often called an "exercise mimetic" because it replicates many metabolic benefits of physical exercise at the cellular level. Circulating MOTS-c levels naturally decline with age and are lower in people with type 2 diabetes and obesity. Notable for requiring milligram-level doses (not micrograms like most peptides).
