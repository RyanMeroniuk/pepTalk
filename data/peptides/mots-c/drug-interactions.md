# MOTS-c (Mitochondrial ORF of the 12S rRNA type-C)

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Metformin**: Both activate AMPK — potential hypoglycemia risk, use with medical supervision
- **Thiazolidinediones**: AMPK pathway interaction
- **Aspirin (high-dose)**: AMPK pathway overlap
- **Insulin**: May enhance insulin sensitivity — monitor for hypoglycemia
- **Contraindicated**: Active cancer
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy/breastfeeding
- **Use with caution**: Any AMPK-activating medications
