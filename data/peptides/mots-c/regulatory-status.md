# MOTS-c (Mitochondrial ORF of the 12S rRNA type-C)

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT FDA-approved** for any indication
- No completed formal human therapeutic trials for native MOTS-c
- CB4211 (MOTS-c analog by CohBar/Morphic Therapeutic) completed Phase 1a/1b trials
- Extensive preclinical data (mice, rats, cell studies)
- Human correlational studies confirm lower MOTS-c = higher diabetes/obesity risk
- Available from research peptide suppliers
- **Banned by WADA** (prohibited substance)
- Active area of longevity and metabolic research (2025-2026)
