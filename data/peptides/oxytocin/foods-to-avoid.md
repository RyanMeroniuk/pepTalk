# Oxytocin

## Foods to Avoid

- **Excess Alcohol**: Blunts oxytocin signaling and impairs the emotional regulation and bonding oxytocin supports; it also disrupts sleep.
- **Excess Refined Sugar**: Blood glucose swings and inflammation undermine mood stability and stress regulation.
- **Ultra-Processed Foods**: Disrupt the gut-brain axis that supports mood and social neuropeptide function.
- **Excessive Caffeine (>300mg/day)**: While moderate caffeine may help, excess can heighten anxiety and counteract oxytocin's calming effect.
- **Excess Sodium With High Dosing**: Given oxytocin's mild antidiuretic activity, very high sodium plus fluid shifts is best avoided; keep intake moderate.
- **Trans Fats & Fried Foods**: Promote inflammation that impairs mood and neuronal function.
- **Processed Seed Oils (soybean, canola, corn)**: High omega-6 load promotes neuroinflammation.
