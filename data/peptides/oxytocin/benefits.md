# Oxytocin

## Benefits

- **Social Connection (anecdotal/mixed)**: Reported to enhance feelings of trust, empathy, and bonding; human intranasal research is mixed and effects are often subtle and context-dependent.
- **Anxiety Reduction**: May reduce social anxiety and stress reactivity in some individuals, though results vary across studies.
- **Sexual & Intimacy Effects**: Anecdotally enhances arousal, intimacy, and the intensity of orgasm; controlled human data is limited.
- **Mood & Wellbeing**: Associated with feelings of calm and connection, particularly around physical closeness.
- **Labor & Lactation (approved clinical)**: Established medical use for labor induction/augmentation, postpartum hemorrhage control, and supporting milk let-down.
- **Pair-Bonding Support**: Often used anecdotally by couples to enhance closeness.
- **IMPORTANT — Off-Label Evidence Is Anecdotal**: Outside its approved obstetric uses, the social/sexual/anxiety benefits of intranasal oxytocin are largely anecdotal or inconsistent in trials; manage expectations accordingly.
