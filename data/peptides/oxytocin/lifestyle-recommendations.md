# Oxytocin

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Physical Affection & Connection**: Hugging, touch, and quality time naturally release endogenous oxytocin; pairing supplemental use with genuine social connection amplifies the experience.
- **Stress Management**: Oxytocin and cortisol are mutually antagonistic — lowering chronic stress through meditation and breathwork supports oxytocin's calming effects.
- **Prioritize Sleep (7-9 hours)**: Good sleep supports emotional regulation and the social-cognitive systems oxytocin acts on.
- **Aerobic Exercise**: Exercise supports mood and endogenous oxytocin/endorphin release, complementing the bonding and anxiolytic effects.
- **Social Engagement**: Effects are context-dependent; positive social settings tend to enhance oxytocin's pro-bonding benefits, while negative contexts can reverse them.
- **Use Intermittently**: Acute, situational use avoids habituation and the diminished response seen with frequent dosing.
- **Hydration Without Excess**: Stay hydrated, but avoid combining high oxytocin doses with very large fluid intake given its mild antidiuretic activity.
- **Avoid Late High Doses if Stimulating**: Most users find it relaxing, but tailor timing to your individual response.
