# Oxytocin

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Supplement**: Magnesium (glycinate, 300-400mg) — supports calm, relaxation, and healthy oxytocin signaling; synergistic for stress reduction.
- **Supplement**: Vitamin D3 — involved in oxytocin synthesis pathways; foundational support for mood and social function.
- **Supplement**: L-Theanine (100-200mg) — promotes calm focus, complementary to oxytocin's anxiolytic, pro-social effects.
- **Supplement**: Omega-3 (EPA/DHA) — supports mood, emotional regulation, and overall neuronal health.
- **Supplement**: Vitamin C — a cofactor in oxytocin biosynthesis; supports endogenous production.
- **Supplement**: Ashwagandha (300-600mg) — lowers cortisol, complementary to oxytocin's stress-reducing, bonding-supportive effects.
- **Caution**: Sedative supplements (high-dose valerian, kava) — may add to any sedation/fatigue; use conservatively.
- **Note**: Human supplement-interaction data is limited; pairings are mechanism-based and best discussed with a clinician.
