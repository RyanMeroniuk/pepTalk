# Oxytocin

## Regulatory Status

- **FDA-approved (injectable)**: Oxytocin injection (e.g., Pitocin) is an approved, long-established drug for labor induction/augmentation and control of postpartum bleeding.
- **Off-label intranasal/sublingual use**: Social, sexual, and anxiety applications via nasal sprays or troches are off-label and typically obtained through compounding pharmacies.
- **Prescription / compounded**: A regulated hormone, not a pure "research chemical"; legitimate access is via prescription or compounding pharmacy.
- **Historical nasal product**: A nasal oxytocin product (Syntocinon) was previously marketed for lactation in some regions but is not a current FDA-approved nasal product in the US.
- **Research interest**: Studied extensively for autism, social anxiety, and PTSD, with mixed results and no resulting general approval for those uses.
- **WADA**: Not specifically prohibited, but athletes should verify current status.
- **Use under supervision**: Given cardiovascular, fluid-balance, and uterine effects, clinician oversight is advised for any use.
