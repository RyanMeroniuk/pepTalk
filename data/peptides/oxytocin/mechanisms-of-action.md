# Oxytocin

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Oxytocin Receptor Agonism**: Binds oxytocin receptors in the brain and peripheral tissues (uterus, mammary glands, cardiovascular system) to produce its effects.
- **Social Bonding & Trust**: Modulates limbic regions (amygdala, nucleus accumbens) involved in social recognition, pair-bonding, trust, and empathy.
- **Anxiolysis**: Dampens amygdala reactivity to social stress, contributing to reduced anxiety and a sense of calm connection.
- **Sexual Arousal & Orgasm**: Released during arousal and surges at orgasm, reinforcing intimacy and the post-orgasm bonding state.
- **Reproductive Actions**: Stimulates uterine contraction (labor) and the milk let-down reflex — the basis of its approved clinical uses.
- **Dopamine Interaction**: Interacts with reward circuitry, linking social and sexual cues to positive reinforcement.
- **Cardiovascular/Fluid Effects**: At higher doses, mild vasodilatory and antidiuretic (vasopressin-like cross-reactivity) effects can occur, underlying high-dose caution.
- **Complementary to PT-141**: PT-141 drives arousal via central melanocortin (MC4) receptors, a distinct pathway, so the two address different layers of sexual response.
