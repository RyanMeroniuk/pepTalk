# Oxytocin

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Vasoconstrictors / Sympathomimetics**: Caution — combined with oxytocin, severe hypertension has been reported clinically; monitor blood pressure.
- **PDE5 Inhibitors (sildenafil, tadalafil)**: Caution — when stacked for sexual use, additive vasoactive effects on blood pressure are possible; monitor, especially with PT-141.
- **Beta-Blockers / Antihypertensives**: Caution — oxytocin's dose-dependent cardiovascular effects may interact; monitor blood pressure and heart rate.
- **SSRIs/SNRIs**: Caution — both modulate mood and social cognition; effects may be additive or unpredictable.
- **Prostaglandins / Uterotonics**: Caution — additive uterine stimulation; relevant in obstetric settings.
- **Contraindicated in Pregnancy**: Outside supervised obstetric use — stimulates uterine contractions and can be dangerous if unmonitored.
- **Caution in Breastfeeding**: Affects let-down; use only with clinical guidance.
- **Caution / Contraindicated in Cardiovascular Disease**: Dose-dependent CV and fluid effects make significant heart or blood-pressure conditions a reason for medical clearance or avoidance.
