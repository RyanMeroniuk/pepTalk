# Oxytocin

## Side Effects

- **Common (intranasal)**: Nasal irritation, mild headache, or transient flushing.
- **Common**: Mild nausea or a feeling of fatigue/sedation in some users.
- **Emotional Effects**: Heightened emotional sensitivity; in some individuals oxytocin can increase, not decrease, anxiety or negative social feelings depending on context.
- **Cardiovascular (dose-dependent)**: At higher doses, changes in blood pressure and heart rate are possible due to vasoactive effects — the basis for high-dose caution.
- **Fluid/Electrolyte Risk (high dose)**: Because oxytocin has mild vasopressin-like activity, excessive dosing with large fluid intake risks water retention and hyponatremia; relevant mainly to high clinical IV doses but a theoretical concern.
- **Uterine Effects**: Can stimulate uterine contractions — a key reason it is contraindicated in pregnancy outside supervised obstetric use.
- **Habituation**: Effects may diminish with frequent use; intermittent use is preferred.
- **Generally Mild at Low Nasal Doses**: At typical low intranasal doses, reported side effects are usually mild and short-lived.
