# Oxytocin

## Dosing Protocols

- **Intranasal (off-label, primary self-use route)**: Commonly used in the range of roughly 10-40 IU per session, taken acutely before a social or intimate situation; this is anecdotal off-label dosing, not an approved protocol.
- **Short Duration of Action**: Effects are brief (often under an hour) due to a short half-life of just a few minutes in circulation; nasal central effects, where present, are also short-lived.
- **Acute, As-Needed Use**: Typically taken situationally rather than on a fixed daily schedule.
- **Sublingual Troches/Lozenges**: Compounded oral/sublingual forms exist; bioavailability differs from nasal and is less predictable.
- **Clinical IV Use**: Labor/postpartum dosing is carefully titrated IV in a hospital setting — entirely separate from off-label nasal use and not for self-administration.
- **Nasal Delivery Uncertainty**: How much intranasal oxytocin actually reaches the brain is debated, which contributes to inconsistent reported effects.
- **Storage**: Refrigerate compounded nasal/sublingual products; follow product-specific stability windows.
- **Caution**: Avoid high or frequent dosing due to cardiovascular and fluid-balance effects; off-label use should ideally involve a knowledgeable clinician.
