# Oxytocin

## Things to Avoid

- **High or Frequent Dosing**: Excessive doses risk cardiovascular and fluid-balance effects, and frequent use causes habituation with diminished response.
- **High Doses With Large Fluid Intake**: Oxytocin's mild vasopressin-like activity can promote water retention; avoid combining high doses with excessive fluids (hyponatremia risk).
- **Use During Pregnancy**: Oxytocin stimulates uterine contractions and is contraindicated outside supervised obstetric care — a critical avoidance.
- **Assuming Strong, Reliable Effects**: Off-label social/sexual effects are anecdotal and context-dependent; in negative settings oxytocin can heighten anxiety rather than reduce it.
- **Cardiovascular Conditions Without Clearance**: Given dose-dependent CV effects, those with heart or blood-pressure conditions should get medical clearance first.
- **Unverified Compounded Sources**: Sterility and concentration vary; use pharmaceutical or reputable compounded product only.
- **Stacking Multiple Sedating Agents**: Combining with alcohol or strong sedatives can compound fatigue and impair judgment.
- **Relying on It for Relationship Problems**: It is not a substitute for genuine connection or for addressing underlying relationship or mental-health issues.
