# Oxytocin

## Foods to Eat

- **Vitamin C-Rich Foods (citrus, bell peppers, kiwi)**: Vitamin C is a cofactor in oxytocin biosynthesis, supporting endogenous production.
- **Magnesium-Rich Foods (leafy greens, pumpkin seeds, dark chocolate)**: Magnesium supports oxytocin receptor function and overall relaxation.
- **Fatty Fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel)**: Omega-3s support mood, emotional regulation, and neuronal signaling.
- **Eggs (whole, pastured)**: Provide choline and vitamin D supporting neurotransmitter and hormonal function.
- **Dark Chocolate (85%+ cacao)**: Associated with mood elevation and contains compounds linked to feelings of wellbeing and connection.
- **Fermented Foods (kimchi, kefir, yogurt)**: Gut-brain axis health supports mood and social neuropeptide function.
- **Avocado & Olive Oil**: Healthy fats support hormone synthesis and brain cell membranes.
- **Coffee (moderate)**: Some evidence links moderate caffeine to enhanced oxytocin signaling in social contexts; keep intake moderate.
