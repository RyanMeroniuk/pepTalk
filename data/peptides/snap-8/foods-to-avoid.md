# SNAP-8 (Acetyl Octapeptide-3)

## Foods to Avoid

- **Refined Sugar & High-Glycemic Foods**: Drive glycation of collagen (AGEs), stiffening skin and deepening lines.
- **Excess Alcohol**: Dehydrates skin, disrupts sleep-driven repair, and depletes collagen-supporting nutrients.
- **Processed Seed Oils**: Soybean, corn, and canola oils promote omega-6-driven inflammation that accelerates skin aging.
- **Fried and Charred Foods**: Generate AGEs and oxidative byproducts that damage skin proteins.
- **Excess Salt**: High sodium contributes to facial puffiness and skin-cell dehydration.
- **Highly Processed Snack Foods**: Combine refined carbohydrates, inflammatory oils, and additives that collectively worsen skin quality.
