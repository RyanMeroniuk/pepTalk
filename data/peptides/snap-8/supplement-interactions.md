# SNAP-8 (Acetyl Octapeptide-3)

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Synergistic**: Argireline — the two SNARE-inhibiting peptides are commonly combined for additive dynamic-wrinkle softening.
- **Synergistic**: GHK-Cu — copper-peptide collagen and barrier support complements SNAP-8's line-softening.
- **Synergistic**: Collagen-peptides (oral) — systemic dermal-matrix support alongside topical neuromodulation.
- **Enhanced by**: Vitamin-C (topical, applied at a separate time) — supports collagen synthesis and brightening.
- **Enhanced by**: Omega-3 and vitamin-d3 for general skin-barrier and anti-inflammatory support.
- **Caution**: Applying with strong exfoliating acids or high-strength retinoids simultaneously can increase irritation; separate or alternate them.
