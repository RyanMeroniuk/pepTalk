# SNAP-8 (Acetyl Octapeptide-3)

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Extended SNAP-25 Mimicry**: An eight-amino-acid sequence modeled on the N-terminal domain of SNAP-25, two residues longer than Argireline.
- **Stronger SNARE Complex Inhibition**: The longer sequence is marketed as competing more effectively with native SNAP-25, further destabilizing SNARE complex formation.
- **Reduced Acetylcholine Release**: Impaired SNARE assembly lowers neurotransmitter release at the neuromuscular junction, weakening facial mimic-muscle contraction.
- **Softened Dynamic Wrinkles**: Reduced contraction of expression muscles diminishes the dynamic lines those muscles produce.
- **Topical, Reversible Effect**: Acts at the surface, is mild, and reverses on discontinuation; it does not permanently disable SNAP-25.
- **Limited Skin Penetration**: As a large, hydrophilic peptide, only a fraction reaches the target muscle, capping potency despite the longer sequence.
