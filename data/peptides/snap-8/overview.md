# SNAP-8 (Acetyl Octapeptide-3)

## Overview

SNAP-8 (acetyl octapeptide-3) is a synthetic eight-amino-acid peptide developed as an extended, elongated version of Argireline (acetyl hexapeptide-8). Like Argireline, it mimics the N-terminal region of SNAP-25 to interfere with SNARE-complex assembly and reduce neurotransmitter release at the neuromuscular junction, softening the appearance of dynamic expression lines. The two extra amino acids are marketed as giving SNAP-8 stronger competitive inhibition of the SNARE complex than Argireline, though it shares the same topical-only, modest-efficacy, limited-penetration profile. It is used exclusively in leave-on cosmetic serums and creams for forehead lines, frown lines, and crow's feet, and is very commonly co-formulated with Argireline and GHK-Cu. It is well-tolerated topically and is not injected.
