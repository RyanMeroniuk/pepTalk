# SNAP-8 (Acetyl Octapeptide-3)

## Dosing Protocols

- **Topical Only**: Used as a leave-on serum or cream; not injected or taken orally.
- **Typical Concentration**: Cosmetic products commonly use around 5-10% SNAP-8 solution (supplied as a dilute aqueous raw material).
- **Application Frequency**: Apply 1-2x daily to clean, dry skin over target expression-line areas.
- **Results Timeline**: Visible smoothing generally appears after 4-8 weeks of consistent daily use; effects are cumulative.
- **Layering**: Apply to clean skin, allow brief absorption, then follow with moisturizer and (in the morning) sunscreen.
- **Maintenance**: Effects reverse once discontinued, so ongoing daily use is required to maintain results.
