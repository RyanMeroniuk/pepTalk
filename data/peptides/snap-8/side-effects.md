# SNAP-8 (Acetyl Octapeptide-3)

## Side Effects

- **Generally Well-Tolerated**: Topical cosmetic use carries a low side-effect profile.
- **Mild Irritation**: Occasional redness, stinging, or dryness, more likely at higher concentrations or on sensitive skin.
- **Contact Sensitivity**: Rare irritant or allergic contact reactions; patch testing is prudent for reactive skin.
- **Eye-Area Caution**: When used near the eyes for crow's feet, avoid direct eye contact to prevent transient irritation.
- **Modest, Reversible Effect**: Results are subtle and reverse on discontinuation; manage expectations to avoid overuse.
- **Limited Long-Term Data**: Long-term topical safety is considered favorable but not exhaustively studied.
