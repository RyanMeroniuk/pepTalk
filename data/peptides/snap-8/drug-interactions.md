# SNAP-8 (Acetyl Octapeptide-3)

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Topical retinoids and prescription acids**: Not a true drug interaction, but co-application with tretinoin or strong acids can increase irritation; separate by hours or alternate days.
- **Injectable botulinum toxin (in-office)**: No known systemic interaction; the topical should not be assumed to add meaningfully on top of professional treatment. Discuss with the treating clinician.
- **Negligible Systemic Absorption**: At cosmetic concentrations, systemic drug interactions are not expected because little peptide reaches circulation.
- **Caution: Sensitive or compromised skin / active dermatitis**: Use caution and monitor; apply only to intact skin.
- **Contraindicated: Known allergy** to the peptide or formulation components.
- **Caution: Pregnancy and breastfeeding**: Topical cosmetic safety data are limited; discuss with a clinician before use during pregnancy or breastfeeding.
