# SNAP-8 (Acetyl Octapeptide-3)

- **Expression-Line Anti-Aging**: SNAP-8 + argireline (combined ~5-10% topical) — two complementary SNARE-inhibiting peptides for stronger dynamic-wrinkle softening than either alone.
- **Topical Rejuvenation Stack**: SNAP-8 + ghk-cu (1-2 mg/mL topical) — neuromodulating line-softening plus copper-peptide collagen and barrier support.
- **Triple Cosmetic Serum**: SNAP-8 + argireline + ghk-cu — a common multi-peptide serum targeting both dynamic lines and overall skin quality.
- **Collagen Support Pairing**: SNAP-8 (topical) + collagen-peptides (oral) — surface line-softening alongside systemic dermal-matrix support.
- **Note**: All combinations here are topical/cosmetic; SNAP-8 is not stacked with injectable systemic peptides for internal effects.
