# SNAP-8 (Acetyl Octapeptide-3)

## Foods to Eat

- **Vitamin C-Rich Foods**: Bell peppers, citrus, kiwi, strawberries, and broccoli supply the cofactor for collagen synthesis, supporting skin firmness alongside topical line-softening.
- **Wild-Caught Fatty Fish**: Salmon, sardines, and mackerel provide omega-3s that reduce skin inflammation and support the moisture barrier.
- **Collagen Sources**: Bone broth and lean protein provide glycine, proline, and amino acids used in skin-matrix repair.
- **Colorful Antioxidant Produce**: Berries, tomatoes, and leafy greens deliver polyphenols and carotenoids that protect skin from UV-driven free-radical damage.
- **Healthy Fats**: Avocados, olive oil, and nuts provide vitamin E and monounsaturated fats supporting skin-barrier integrity.
- **Adequate Water and Electrolytes**: Well-hydrated skin shows fewer fine lines; pair water with mineral-rich whole foods.
