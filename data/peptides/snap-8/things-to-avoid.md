# SNAP-8 (Acetyl Octapeptide-3)

## Things to Avoid

- **UV Exposure Without Protection**: Unprotected sun deepens expression lines faster than any topical can soften them; daily sunscreen is essential.
- **Over-Layering Irritants**: Applying high-strength retinoids or strong AHA/BHA acids simultaneously can compromise the barrier and increase irritation. Separate or alternate.
- **Expecting Injectable-Level Results**: SNAP-8 is modest and surface-acting; it is not a Botox replacement, and treating it as one leads to overuse.
- **Skipping Days**: The effect is reversible, so inconsistent use prevents results from building.
- **Applying to Broken or Irritated Skin**: Apply only to intact skin to avoid stinging and irritation.
- **Getting Product in the Eyes**: When treating crow's feet, keep the serum off the lash line and out of the eye.
- **Smoking and Heavy Sun-Bed Use**: Both dramatically accelerate the wrinkling the peptide is meant to address.
