# SNAP-8 (Acetyl Octapeptide-3)

## Benefits

- **Expression Line Softening**: May reduce the appearance of dynamic forehead lines, frown lines, and crow's feet with consistent topical use.
- **Potentially Stronger Than Argireline**: The extended octapeptide sequence is marketed as offering greater SNARE inhibition, though real-world differences are modest.
- **Non-Invasive**: A needle-free, low-commitment cosmetic option for softening dynamic wrinkles.
- **Well-Tolerated**: Low irritation profile suitable for daily leave-on use, including for many sensitive-skin users.
- **Stacks Well**: Compatible with Argireline and GHK-Cu, enabling layered multi-peptide anti-aging serums.
- **Cumulative and Reversible**: Benefits build with continued use and fade on discontinuation; best treated as ongoing maintenance.
