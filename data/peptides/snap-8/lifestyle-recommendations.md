# SNAP-8 (Acetyl Octapeptide-3)

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Consistent Daily Application**: SNAP-8 works through cumulative, reversible effects. Apply 1-2x daily at consistent times and expect visible smoothing only after 4-8 weeks of uninterrupted use.
- **Sun Protection**: UV exposure is the leading cause of expression-line deepening. Apply broad-spectrum SPF 30-50 every morning to protect any smoothing benefit.
- **Hydration and Moisture Barrier**: Keep skin well-moisturized and drink adequate water; a healthy barrier improves the look of fine lines and tolerance to the peptide.
- **Sleep Quality**: Skin repair peaks during deep sleep. Target 7-9 hours nightly and consider sleeping on your back to reduce facial creasing.
- **Stress Management**: Habitual frowning and elevated cortisol reinforce dynamic lines and degrade collagen; daily relaxation practice complements the peptide's action.
- **Avoid Smoking**: Smoking accelerates wrinkling, depletes vitamin C, and reduces skin blood flow — working directly against SNAP-8's goal.
- **Patch Test First**: Test on a small area for 24-48 hours before full-face use, especially with sensitive skin.
- **Realistic Expectations**: This is maintenance skincare with modest effect, not a replacement for injectable neuromodulators.
