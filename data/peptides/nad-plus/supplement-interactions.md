# NAD+

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Synergistic**: NMN — the direct NAD+ precursor in this library; supports the same NAD+ pool via the salvage pathway and is the preferred oral route.
- **Critical Cofactor Pairing**: B-complex — niacin/nicotinamide (B3) is the dietary precursor backbone of NAD+; supports synthesis.
- **Synergistic**: Resveratrol — sirtuin activator that works downstream of NAD+; classic NAD+-sirtuin pairing.
- **Synergistic**: PQQ (20mg) and CoQ10 (100–200mg) — support mitochondrial function that NAD+ powers.
- **Enhanced by**: Alpha-lipoic-acid — mitochondrial antioxidant complementing NAD+-driven energetics.
- **Enhanced by**: Apigenin and quercetin — inhibit CD38, the enzyme that degrades NAD+, helping preserve levels.
- **Synergistic**: MOTS-c — mitochondrial peptide that itself helps regulate NAD+ levels.
- **Critical**: Magnesium — cofactor for ATP-dependent reactions downstream of NAD+ metabolism.
- **Caution**: Stacking with multiple stimulatory or metabolic agents may amplify energy/insomnia effects — dose earlier in the day.
- **Contraindicated**: Active cancer (sirtuin/metabolic effects on tumor biology uncertain).
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy/breastfeeding.
