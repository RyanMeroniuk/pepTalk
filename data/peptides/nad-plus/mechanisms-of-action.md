# NAD+

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Redox Electron Carrier**: Cycles between NAD+ and NADH to transfer electrons through glycolysis, the citric acid cycle, and oxidative phosphorylation, driving ATP synthesis.
- **Sirtuin Substrate**: Required cofactor for sirtuins (SIRT1–7), the NAD+-dependent enzymes that regulate longevity, metabolism, and mitochondrial biogenesis.
- **PARP Activation**: Serves as substrate for PARP enzymes that carry out DNA damage repair, consuming NAD+ in the process.
- **CD38 Consumption**: CD38 and related enzymes degrade NAD+, and their activity rises with age and inflammation, contributing to the age-related decline.
- **Mitochondrial Function**: Adequate NAD+ is essential for efficient electron transport chain operation and cellular energy output.
- **Salvage Pathway**: Cells recycle nicotinamide back into NAD+ via the salvage pathway; precursors like NMN and NR feed this route.
- **Calcium Signaling Precursor**: Source of secondary messengers (cADPR) involved in cellular calcium signaling.
- **Metabolic Regulation**: NAD+/NADH ratio acts as a key sensor of cellular energy status influencing fuel selection.
