# NAD+

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Exercise Raises NAD+ Naturally**: Regular aerobic and resistance training increases NAD+ biosynthesis and sirtuin activity — the cheapest, most proven way to boost NAD+. Aim for 3–5 sessions per week.
- **Time-Restricted Eating / Fasting**: Caloric restriction and fasting raise the NAD+/NADH ratio and activate sirtuins, complementing supplementation. A 10–12 hour eating window is a practical start.
- **Prioritize Sleep**: NAD+ follows a circadian rhythm and supports cellular repair during sleep. Target 7–9 hours with consistent timing.
- **Limit Alcohol**: Alcohol metabolism consumes NAD+ and depletes the pool you are trying to restore. Minimize, especially around dosing.
- **Dose Earlier in the Day**: NAD+ can be activating; dosing in the morning avoids interfering with sleep.
- **Sun and Cold Exposure**: Mild hormetic stressors support mitochondrial biogenesis and NAD+-linked pathways.
- **Manage Inflammation**: Chronic inflammation raises CD38 activity that degrades NAD+; an anti-inflammatory lifestyle helps preserve levels.
- **Stay Hydrated for IV Sessions**: Adequate hydration improves comfort and tolerance during IV infusions.
- **Track Energy and Recovery**: Monitor energy, sleep, and exercise recovery to gauge response, since clinical biomarkers for non-medical use are limited.
