# NAD+

## Things to Avoid

- **Rapid IV Infusion**: Pushing NAD+ too fast causes the classic flushing, chest tightness, and nausea. Always slow the drip — this is the main tolerability lever.
- **Relying on Oral NAD+**: Oral NAD+ is largely degraded in digestion; if going oral, precursors like NMN or NR are far more sensible.
- **Excessive Alcohol**: Directly depletes NAD+ and undermines the entire rationale for supplementation.
- **Use With Active Cancer**: NAD+ fuels metabolism and DNA repair that could theoretically support tumor cells; avoid with active cancer.
- **Dosing Late in the Day**: NAD+ can be activating and disrupt sleep if taken in the evening.
- **Unregulated Sourcing**: Injectable NAD+ purity and sterility vary; impure or non-sterile product risks infection and contamination.
- **Assuming It Is a Peptide**: NAD+ is a coenzyme, not a peptide — do not apply peptide-specific dosing logic to it.
- **Overstating Anti-Aging Claims**: Human longevity benefits are unproven; most rigorous data involves precursors, not direct NAD+.
