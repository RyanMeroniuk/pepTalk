# NAD+

## Stacking Protocols

- **NAD+ Optimization Stack**: NAD+ (IV 250–500mg or SubQ 50–100mg) + NMN (500mg/day) + resveratrol (250mg/day) — pairs direct repletion with oral precursor and sirtuin activation.
- **Mitochondrial Energy Stack**: NAD+ (50–100mg SubQ) + CoQ10 (200mg) + PQQ (20mg) — supports the electron transport chain that NAD+ powers.
- **Longevity Stack**: NAD+ (50–100mg SubQ) + epitalon (1mg/day, 10–20 day cycle) + omega-3 (3g/day EPA/DHA) — targets DNA repair, telomeres, and inflammation.
- **Metabolic Stack**: NAD+ (50–100mg SubQ) + MOTS-c (5mg 2x/week) + alpha-lipoic-acid (600mg/day) — combines NAD+ repletion with mitochondrial signaling and antioxidant support.
- **NAD+ Preservation Stack**: NAD+ (50–100mg SubQ) + b-complex (1 serving/day) + magnesium (400mg/day) — supplies synthesis precursors and downstream cofactors.
