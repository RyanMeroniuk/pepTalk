# NAD+

## Foods to Avoid

- **Excessive Alcohol**: Alcohol metabolism directly consumes NAD+, depleting the pool supplementation aims to restore — the single most counterproductive item.
- **Refined Sugars**: Candy, soda, pastries — drive metabolic stress and excess NADH, skewing the NAD+/NADH ratio unfavorably.
- **Ultra-Processed Foods**: Fast food, packaged snacks, deli meats — inflammatory additives raise CD38 activity that degrades NAD+.
- **Trans Fats and Oxidized Seed Oils**: Promote inflammation and mitochondrial dysfunction that strain NAD+ metabolism.
- **High-Fructose Corn Syrup**: Drives liver fat and metabolic dysfunction that impair NAD+-dependent pathways.
- **Chronic Overnutrition**: Persistent caloric excess lowers the NAD+/NADH ratio and blunts sirtuin activity.
- **Heavy Late-Night Meals**: Eating close to bedtime disrupts circadian NAD+ rhythms and sleep-time repair.
