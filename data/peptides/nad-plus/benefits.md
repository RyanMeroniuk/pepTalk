# NAD+

## Benefits

- **Cellular Energy**: Supports ATP production as the central redox cofactor of metabolism; the most fundamental, well-established role.
- **Longevity Signaling**: Fuels sirtuin activity linked to metabolic health and healthspan in preclinical research; human longevity benefits remain unproven.
- **DNA Repair Support**: Provides substrate for PARP-mediated DNA repair, theoretically supporting genomic stability.
- **Subjective Energy and Clarity**: IV users frequently report improved energy, mental clarity, and mood, though these reports are largely anecdotal.
- **Metabolic Health**: Restoring age-depleted NAD+ improved metabolic markers in animal models; human data is mixed.
- **Mitochondrial Support**: Enables efficient mitochondrial energy production, complementing mitochondrial peptides and supplements.
- **Recovery and Addiction Protocols**: Used anecdotally in IV "recovery" and addiction clinics, but controlled evidence is limited and inconsistent.
- **Evidence Caveat**: Much human benefit data comes from precursor studies (NMN/NR) rather than direct NAD+ administration; direct-NAD+ efficacy is less rigorously established.
