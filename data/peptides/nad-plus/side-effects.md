# NAD+

## Side Effects

- Flushing and warmth during IV infusion (very common, dose- and rate-dependent).
- Chest tightness or pressure if infused too quickly.
- Nausea during or after rapid infusion.
- Lightheadedness or dizziness.
- Cramping or abdominal discomfort during infusion.
- Injection site stinging (subcutaneous/intramuscular).
- Headache.
- Most acute effects are tied to infusion rate and resolve when the drip is slowed.
- Long-term safety of high-dose injectable NAD+ is not well characterized in controlled trials.
