# NAD+

## Regulatory Status

- **Not a peptide** — NAD+ is a coenzyme, included here because it is widely used in injectable/IV biohacker protocols alongside peptides.
- **NOT FDA-approved** as a drug for anti-aging or any therapeutic indication.
- Oral NAD+ and its precursors (NMN, NR, niacin) are sold as dietary supplements; NR has GRAS status, while NMN's supplement status has faced FDA scrutiny.
- Injectable and IV NAD+ are administered through clinics and compounding pharmacies; not an FDA-approved injectable drug product.
- Endogenous NAD+ is essential and present in all cells; supplementation aims to counter age-related decline.
- Human clinical evidence is strongest for precursors (NMN/NR) rather than direct NAD+ administration.
- Long-term safety of high-dose injectable NAD+ is not established in controlled trials.
- Active area of longevity and metabolic research (2025–2026).
