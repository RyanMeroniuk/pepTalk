# NAD+

## Foods to Eat

- **Niacin (B3) Rich Foods**: Chicken breast, tuna, turkey, salmon, mushrooms, green peas — provide the NAD+ precursor nicotinamide/niacin.
- **NMN/NR-Containing Foods**: Edamame, broccoli, avocado, cabbage, cucumber — contain trace NAD+ precursors that feed the salvage pathway.
- **Tryptophan Sources**: Eggs, turkey, dairy, seeds — tryptophan can be converted to NAD+ via the de novo pathway.
- **CD38-Inhibiting Polyphenols**: Apples and red onions (quercetin), parsley and chamomile (apigenin) — help slow NAD+ degradation.
- **Dark Leafy Greens**: Spinach, kale — magnesium and folate supporting NAD+-dependent metabolism.
- **Whole Grains and Legumes**: Brown rice, lentils, peanuts — modest niacin and steady energy substrate.
- **Polyphenol-Rich Foods**: Berries, green tea, dark chocolate, extra virgin olive oil — support sirtuin pathways downstream of NAD+.
- **Quality Protein**: Fish, poultry, eggs, legumes — supply tryptophan and amino acids for metabolic function.
