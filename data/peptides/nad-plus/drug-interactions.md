# NAD+

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Chemotherapy**: Use with caution under oncology supervision — NAD+ supports DNA repair (via PARP) and metabolism that could theoretically aid tumor survival; contraindicated during active cancer treatment.
- **Metformin**: Both influence cellular metabolism and NAD+-linked pathways; monitor energy and glucose if combined.
- **Statins**: Statin-related mitochondrial effects may interact with NAD+ energetics; no established interaction, monitor for muscle symptoms.
- **Antidiabetics / Insulin**: Monitor blood glucose, as improved metabolic efficiency could modestly shift response.
- **Anticoagulants / Blood Thinners**: No established interaction; standard injection-site caution applies, monitor for bruising.
- **PARP Inhibitors**: Theoretical interaction given NAD+ is a PARP substrate; use caution and discuss with a clinician.
- **Contraindicated**: Active cancer (DNA-repair and metabolic support to tumor cells is a theoretical concern).
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy and breastfeeding (insufficient safety data for high-dose injectable use).
- **Caution**: Liver disease or kidney disease — high-dose injectable use is not well characterized in organ impairment.
