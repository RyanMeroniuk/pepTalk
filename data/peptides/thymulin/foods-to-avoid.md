# Thymulin

## Foods to Avoid

- **Excess Sugar** — high sugar intake impairs neutrophil function for hours after consumption and promotes chronic inflammation that suppresses T-cell activity. Directly undermines thymulin's immune-enhancing effects
- **Alcohol** — depletes zinc stores, which is particularly damaging since thymulin requires zinc for activation. Even moderate alcohol reduces T-cell counts and function
- **Phytate-Heavy Unsoaked Grains and Legumes** — phytic acid binds zinc and prevents absorption. If eating grains/legumes, soak, sprout, or ferment them first to reduce phytate content
- **Excess Calcium Supplements with Meals** — high-dose calcium competes with zinc for absorption. Separate calcium and zinc supplementation by at least 2 hours
- **Highly Processed Foods** — preservatives and emulsifiers disrupt gut microbiome balance, impairing the gut immune tissue that works with thymulin-matured T-cells
- **Excess Coffee with Zinc-Rich Meals** — tannins and polyphenols in coffee can reduce mineral absorption. Drink coffee 1 hour before or after zinc-rich meals
- **Trans Fats and Fried Foods** — promote oxidative stress and inflammation, impairing immune cell membrane integrity
