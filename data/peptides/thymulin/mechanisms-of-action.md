# Thymulin

## Mechanisms of Action

- **T-Cell Differentiation**: Essential for proper T-lymphocyte maturation in the thymus
- **Zinc-Dependent**: Requires zinc binding for biological activity — the zinc-thymulin complex is the active form
- **Anti-Inflammatory**: Reduces inflammation of various etiologies
- **Anti-Nociceptive**: Pain-reducing effects in hyperalgesia and neurogenic pain models
- **Immune Balance**: Modulates both innate and adaptive immune responses
- **Cytokine Regulation**: Influences pro- and anti-inflammatory cytokine production
