# Thymulin

## Overview

Thymulin (also known as Facteur Thymique Serique or FTS) is a naturally occurring nonapeptide (9 amino acids) produced by thymic epithelial cells. Requires zinc for biological activity — the active form is a zinc-thymulin complex. Essential for T-lymphocyte differentiation and maturation. Thymulin levels decline significantly with age as the thymus involutes, contributing to immunosenescence (age-related immune decline). Related to but distinct from Thymosin Alpha-1.
