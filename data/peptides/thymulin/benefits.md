# Thymulin

## Benefits

- **Immune Restoration**: Restores age-related decline in thymic function
- **T-Cell Support**: Enhances T-cell maturation and function
- **Anti-Inflammatory**: Reduces chronic inflammation
- **Pain Management**: Anti-nociceptive effects for hyperalgesia and neurogenic pain
- **Autoimmune Support**: May help rebalance dysregulated immune responses
- **Zinc Homeostasis**: Supports zinc-dependent immune processes
- **Immunosenescence**: Counteracts age-related immune decline
