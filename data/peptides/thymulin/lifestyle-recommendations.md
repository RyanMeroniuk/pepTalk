# Thymulin

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Zinc Optimization Is Critical**: Thymulin is biologically inactive without zinc. Test serum zinc levels and supplement 30-50mg daily (zinc picolinate or zinc bisglycinate for best absorption). Take zinc with food to avoid nausea, and separate from iron/calcium supplements by 2 hours
- **Prioritize Sleep for Immune Recovery**: Aim for 7-9 hours — sleep is when the immune system performs critical maintenance. T-cell production and cytokine regulation peak during deep sleep. Poor sleep directly reduces thymulin's effectiveness by impairing the immune cells it supports
- **Moderate Exercise for Immune Enhancement**: Regular moderate exercise (walking, swimming, cycling 30-45 min, 4-5x/week) enhances T-cell circulation and immune surveillance. This amplifies thymulin's T-cell differentiation effects
- **Avoid Overtraining**: Excessive high-intensity training without recovery suppresses immune function for 24-72 hours post-session. Since thymulin supports T-cell maturation, overtraining undermines its mechanism of action
- **Manage Stress Actively**: Chronic stress elevates cortisol, which accelerates thymic involution (the shrinking of the thymus gland) — the exact process thymulin is counteracting. Practice daily stress management: meditation, breathwork, nature walks, or yoga
- **Support Gut Health**: 70% of immune tissue resides in the gut (GALT). A healthy gut microbiome supports the immune cells that thymulin helps mature. Eat fermented foods and prebiotic fiber daily
- **Limit Alcohol**: Alcohol depletes zinc stores and impairs T-cell function — directly counteracting thymulin's mechanism
- **Address Zinc Depletion Factors**: Certain medications (PPIs, diuretics, ACE inhibitors) and conditions (chronic stress, heavy sweating) deplete zinc. If you take any of these, increase zinc monitoring and supplementation
