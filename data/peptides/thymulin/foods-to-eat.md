# Thymulin

## Foods to Eat

- **Oysters** — the single richest food source of zinc (74mg per 6 oysters), directly supporting thymulin activation
- **Pumpkin Seeds** — excellent plant-based zinc source (7mg per ounce); also provide magnesium for immune function
- **Grass-Fed Beef and Lamb** — bioavailable zinc and iron for immune cell production
- **Shiitake and Maitake Mushrooms** — beta-glucans activate innate immune cells that work alongside thymulin-matured T-cells
- **Fermented Foods** (kimchi, sauerkraut, kefir, yogurt) — support gut microbiome diversity, enhancing immune tissue in the gut
- **Leafy Greens** (spinach, kale, Swiss chard) — provide folate and vitamins essential for immune cell division
- **Garlic and Onions** — allicin and quercetin support immune cell activity and have natural antimicrobial properties
- **Citrus Fruits** — vitamin C supports immune cell function and may enhance zinc absorption
- **Wild-Caught Fatty Fish** (salmon, sardines) — omega-3s reduce chronic inflammation that impairs immune function
- **Cashews and Almonds** — good zinc sources (cashews: 1.6mg per ounce) plus selenium and vitamin E for immune support
- **Eggs** — provide zinc, selenium, and vitamin D precursors for comprehensive immune nutrition
- **Dark Chocolate** (70%+ cacao) — contains zinc and immune-supporting flavonoids (in moderation)
