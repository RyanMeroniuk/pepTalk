# Thymulin

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Essential**: Zinc supplementation (30-50mg) — thymulin is inactive without zinc
- **Synergistic**: Thymosin Alpha-1 for comprehensive thymic/immune support
- **Enhanced by**: Vitamin D3, Vitamin C for immune support
- **Caution**: Immunosuppressive drugs
- **Contraindicated**: Active autoimmune flares (may modulate immune response unpredictably)
