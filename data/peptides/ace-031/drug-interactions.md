# ACE-031

## Drug Interactions

- **Anticoagulants/blood thinners (warfarin, heparin, DOACs)**: Contraindicated — ACE-031 itself causes bleeding (nosebleeds, gum bleeding); combining with blood thinners creates serious additive bleeding risk
- **Antiplatelet drugs (aspirin, clopidogrel) and NSAIDs (ibuprofen, naproxen)**: Caution to contraindicated — further impair clotting on top of ACE-031's documented bleeding tendency
- **Active cancer / chemotherapy**: Contraindicated — broad TGF-β/activin antagonism removes tumor-suppressive signaling and may interfere with treatment
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding**: Contraindicated — potent developmental pathway inhibitor with no safety data
- **Corticosteroids (prednisone, dexamethasone)**: Caution — catabolic to muscle and bone, opposing the intended effect and potentially worsening bone risk; monitor
- **Beta-blockers**: Monitor — relevant if vascular/cardiovascular effects emerge; do not assume cardiovascular safety
- **Other myostatin/IGF-axis agents**: Caution — additive off-target and bleeding risk; not a documented pharmacokinetic interaction but a serious practical one
- **General caveat**: Human dosing was discontinued, so robust drug-interaction data does not exist; the bleeding-related interactions are grounded in the documented trial adverse-event profile
