# ACE-031

## Foods to Avoid

- **Excess alcohol**: Thins the blood and irritates mucous membranes (including the nasal lining), potentially worsening the nosebleed and bleeding tendency associated with ACE-031
- **Natto and very high-dose fermented soy (nattokinase-containing)**: Nattokinase has fibrinolytic/blood-thinning activity that could compound bleeding risk
- **Large amounts of blood-thinning foods (excess garlic, ginger, turmeric)**: In high culinary/supplemental amounts these have mild antiplatelet effects that may add to bleeding risk
- **Added sugars and ultra-processed foods**: Pro-inflammatory and counterproductive to recovery and vascular health
- **Trans fats and fried foods**: Pro-inflammatory and harmful to blood-vessel health
- **Crash/very low-calorie diets**: Impair recovery and overall resilience
