# ACE-031

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Soluble ActRIIB decoy receptor**: A fusion of the ActRIIB extracellular domain with an antibody Fc region that circulates in the blood and binds ligands before they reach the real muscle receptor
- **Myostatin (GDF-8) trapping**: Sequesters myostatin, the primary negative regulator of muscle mass, removing its growth-limiting signal
- **Activin and GDF-11 inhibition**: Also binds activin A/B and GDF-11 — this broader ligand trapping accounts for both its potency and its off-target effects
- **Systemic vs local action**: Because it works as a circulating decoy, its action is body-wide rather than targeted to a single muscle
- **Massive preclinical hypertrophy**: Animal models showed ~30–40% increases in muscle mass, among the largest reported for any myostatin-pathway agent
- **Vascular pathway disruption**: ActRIIB ligands (including activin/GDF family members) participate in blood-vessel regulation; trapping them is the mechanistic basis for the bleeding and telangiectasia seen in humans
- **Fc-mediated long half-life**: The antibody Fc fusion extends circulating half-life, allowing infrequent dosing but also prolonging off-target exposure
