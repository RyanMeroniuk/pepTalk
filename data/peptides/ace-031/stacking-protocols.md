# ACE-031

## Stacking Protocols

- **Safety note first**: ACE-031 was discontinued in human trials for vascular bleeding events; the stacks below are documented as reference combinations seen in the research community, not endorsements
- **Mass Stack (reference only)**: ACE-031 + IGF-1 LR3 (40mcg/day) — pairs decoy-receptor myostatin trapping with IGF-1-driven satellite cell activation for maximal hypertrophy signaling
- **Myostatin Stack (reference only)**: ACE-031 + Follistatin-344 (100mcg/day) — combines two distinct myostatin/activin inhibition mechanisms, which also compounds off-target and bleeding risk
- **GH Axis Stack (reference only)**: ACE-031 + CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin (100mcg each before bed) — adds amplified endogenous GH/IGF-1 output to the anabolic environment
- **Recovery Stack (reference only)**: ACE-031 + BPC-157 (250mcg 2x/day) + TB-500 (2mg 2x/week) — connective-tissue and healing support, though BPC-157's angiogenic activity near a bleeding-prone agent warrants extra caution
- **Note**: Combining ACE-031 with anything that increases bleeding (including fish oil) is especially hazardous; none of these stacks is clinically validated
