# ACE-031

## Benefits

- **Large muscle mass increases (preclinical)**: Animal models showed roughly 30–40% gains in lean muscle mass via potent, broad myostatin/activin pathway inhibition
- **Strength gains in models**: Increased muscle mass was accompanied by greater strength in preclinical studies
- **Infrequent dosing (theoretical)**: The Fc-fusion design gives a long half-life, so far fewer injections would be needed than with short-acting peptides
- **Original therapeutic intent**: Was investigated for muscle-wasting conditions such as Duchenne muscular dystrophy, where increasing muscle mass could be clinically meaningful
- **Critical framing**: These benefits never translated into an approved therapy — human trials were stopped for safety, so the "benefits" are largely preclinical/theoretical and are outweighed in humans by the documented vascular harms
