# ACE-031

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT approved anywhere** — ACE-031 (ramatercept) never reached market
- **Human clinical development DISCONTINUED**: Trials, including a Duchenne muscular dystrophy program by Acceleron Pharma, were halted after participants developed vascular and bleeding-related adverse events (nosebleeds, gum bleeding, telangiectasias)
- **No approved indication**: Because development stopped on safety grounds, it has no legitimate medical use
- **Sold only as a gray-market "research chemical"**, explicitly not for human use, with no quality oversight
- **Quality is unregulated**: purity, sterility, dosing accuracy, and molecular identity are unverifiable, and a discontinued biologic is especially likely to be mislabeled
- **Banned in sport**: Myostatin/activin-pathway inhibitors are prohibited by WADA at all times
- **Legal status varies by jurisdiction**; importing/possessing an unapproved injectable biologic can carry legal risk
- **Bottom line**: A discontinued drug abandoned for documented safety failures — documented here for reference, not as a usable option
