# ACE-031

## Foods to Eat

- **Vitamin K-rich leafy greens (kale, spinach, collards)**: Vitamin K supports normal clotting, relevant given ACE-031's documented bleeding tendency
- **Citrus fruits and berries**: Vitamin C and flavonoids support blood-vessel integrity and collagen in vessel walls
- **Lean protein (chicken, turkey, lean beef)**: Supplies amino acids for muscle protein synthesis
- **Eggs (pasture-raised)**: Complete protein with vitamin K2 and choline
- **Fermented foods (natto aside, e.g. sauerkraut, kefir)**: Support gut health and overall nutrition
- **Iron-rich foods (lean red meat, lentils, spinach)**: Help maintain healthy blood given any bleeding losses
- **Sweet potatoes and oats**: Quality carbohydrates to fuel training
- **Calcium and vitamin D sources (dairy, sardines with bones)**: Support bone health given the broad activin/myostatin inhibition
