# ACE-031

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Understand the safety context first**: ACE-031 human trials were halted for vascular bleeding events — the most responsible "lifestyle" stance is awareness that this is a discontinued, unapproved compound
- **Watch for bleeding signs**: Nosebleeds, gum bleeding, easy bruising, or new dilated surface vessels are the documented warning signs and warrant immediate cessation and medical attention
- **Avoid activities with bleeding/impact risk**: Contact sports or anything that risks nosebleeds or bruising is inadvisable given the bleeding tendency
- **Do not combine with blood thinners or antiplatelet supplements**: The vascular profile makes added bleeding risk especially dangerous
- **Protect bone and connective tissue**: As with other broad myostatin/activin inhibitors, support bone health with weight-bearing exercise and adequate vitamin D
- **Monitor closely if used at all**: Frequent bloodwork and vigilant self-monitoring are essential, but cannot make an inherently risky discontinued agent safe
- **Recognize the long half-life**: Effects persist for weeks; there is no quick off-ramp once dosed
- **Honest framing**: There is no lifestyle protocol that makes ACE-031 a sound choice; it is documented here for reference, not recommendation
