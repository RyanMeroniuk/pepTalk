# ACE-031

## Things to Avoid

- **Ignoring why human trials were discontinued**: ACE-031 dosing was stopped because of vascular bleeding events (nosebleeds, gum bleeding, telangiectasias) — this is the single most important fact and should not be overlooked
- **Combining with blood thinners or antiplatelet agents**: Anticoagulants, aspirin, NSAIDs, fish oil, and similar supplements dangerously compound the documented bleeding risk
- **Continuing through bleeding signs**: New nosebleeds, gum bleeding, easy bruising, or dilated surface vessels are warning signs that warrant immediate cessation
- **Assuming the long half-life is reversible**: Because it persists for weeks, you cannot quickly undo an adverse effect by stopping — avoid treating it like a short-acting peptide
- **Use during pregnancy or breastfeeding**: A potent broad TGF-β pathway inhibitor with no safety data — absolutely avoid
- **Use with cancer history**: Broad TGF-β antagonism removes tumor-suppressive signaling — avoid
- **Trusting gray-market product identity**: Discontinued biologics sold as research chemicals are highly likely to be mislabeled, impure, or non-functional
- **Treating preclinical 30–40% gains as achievable safely**: Those results came at the cost of safety failures in humans — the trade-off is the point
