# ACE-031

## Dosing Protocols

- **No safe or approved dosing exists**: Human trials were discontinued for safety, so there is no validated dosing — any figures circulating are anecdotal gray-market practice and should not be treated as guidance
- **Trial-era administration**: In the discontinued clinical program it was given as a subcutaneous injection roughly every 2–4 weeks, reflecting the long Fc-fusion half-life
- **Long half-life caveat**: Because it persists in circulation for weeks, any adverse vascular effect also persists long after dosing — there is no quick way to clear it
- **Reconstitution**: Gray-market versions arrive as lyophilized powder reconstituted with bacteriostatic water; identity and potency are unverifiable
- **Storage**: Refrigerate after reconstitution
- **Strong cautionary note**: Unlike short-acting peptides, the prolonged half-life means a problem cannot be reversed by simply stopping — this is a key reason the safety profile is concerning
- **Bottom line**: This entry documents the compound for reference; it is not a recommendation, and no dosing can be considered safe given the trial outcome
