# ACE-031

## Side Effects

- **Nosebleeds (epistaxis)**: A hallmark adverse effect observed in human trials, reflecting disruption of normal blood-vessel regulation
- **Gum/gingival bleeding**: Bleeding from the gums was repeatedly reported in human participants
- **Telangiectasias (dilated surface blood vessels)**: Visible small dilated vessels on the skin — a vascular off-target effect that contributed to halting the program
- **Trial discontinuation for safety**: These vascular and bleeding events were serious enough that human dosing was stopped — this is the defining safety fact about ACE-031
- **Broad TGF-β/activin disruption effects**: Because it traps multiple ligands beyond myostatin, off-target effects extend well beyond muscle
- **Prolonged exposure risk**: The long half-life means adverse effects persist for weeks after the last dose and cannot be quickly reversed
- **Bone/reproductive theoretical risk**: Like other broad myostatin/activin inhibitors, effects on bone remodeling and reproductive hormones are a concern
- **Product-related risk**: Gray-market sourcing adds contamination, endotoxin, and mislabeling risk on top of the molecule's documented dangers
