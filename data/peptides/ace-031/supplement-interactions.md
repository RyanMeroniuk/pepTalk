# ACE-031

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Caution — fish oil / high-dose Omega-3**: Omega-3s have mild antiplatelet/blood-thinning effects; combined with ACE-031's documented bleeding tendency (nosebleeds, gum bleeding), this could worsen bleeding risk — avoid high doses
- **Caution — Vitamin E (high dose)**: High-dose vitamin E can impair clotting; combining with a compound that causes bleeding is inadvisable
- **Caution — Ginkgo, garlic extract, ginger, nattokinase**: Supplements with antiplatelet/blood-thinning properties should be avoided given the vascular bleeding profile
- **Critical — Vitamin D3**: Relevant for bone health given the broad activin/myostatin inhibition and theoretical bone-density concern (2,000–5,000 IU/day)
- **Enhanced by — Creatine Monohydrate**: Supports muscle protein synthesis, but does not offset the vascular safety problem (5g/day)
- **Enhanced by — Collagen Peptides**: May support connective tissue if muscle grows rapidly (10–15g/day)
- **Overriding note**: No supplement mitigates the core vascular/bleeding risk that ended human trials; the safety concern dominates any synergy
