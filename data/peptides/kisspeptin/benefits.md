# Kisspeptin

## Benefits

- **Fertility Support**: May improve fertility in both men and women with hypothalamic dysfunction
- **Testosterone Optimization**: Stimulates natural testosterone production via LH
- **IVF Support**: Being studied as trigger for oocyte maturation in IVF (lower OHSS risk than hCG)
- **Hormonal Assessment**: Used diagnostically to assess reproductive axis function
- **Sexual Behavior**: Some evidence of enhanced attraction and sexual arousal
- **Energy Balance**: May play a role in metabolism and body weight regulation
