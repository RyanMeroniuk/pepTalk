# Kisspeptin

## Mechanisms of Action

- **GnRH Stimulation**: Binds KISS1R (GPR54) receptors on GnRH neurons, triggering GnRH release
- **LH/FSH Release**: Via GnRH, stimulates pituitary release of luteinizing hormone and follicle-stimulating hormone
- **Testosterone/Estrogen**: Downstream effect of LH/FSH on gonads
- **Puberty Regulation**: Essential for normal puberty onset
- **Fertility**: Critical for normal reproductive function in both sexes
- **Sexual Behavior**: May enhance sexual arousal and attraction through limbic system effects
