# Kisspeptin

## Foods to Avoid

- **Excess Soy Protein Isolate** — concentrated phytoestrogens can disrupt the estrogen-testosterone balance that kisspeptin's cascade regulates. Moderate fermented soy (miso, tempeh, natto) is acceptable; avoid large amounts of soy protein powder, soy milk, and processed soy products
- **Alcohol** — directly toxic to reproductive cells. In men, alcohol reduces testosterone, impairs sperm quality, and suppresses GnRH. In women, even moderate alcohol can disrupt ovulation and reduce fertility. Counteracts kisspeptin's reproductive benefits
- **Excess Sugar and Refined Carbohydrates** — cause insulin resistance, which disrupts hormonal balance (insulin resistance is closely linked to PCOS and reduced fertility). Blood sugar instability impairs the HPG axis kisspeptin drives
- **Trans Fats** — associated with reduced fertility in both sexes. Found in partially hydrogenated oils, fried foods, and many processed snacks
- **Excess Caffeine** (>300mg/day) — high caffeine intake is associated with reduced fertility and may impair implantation. Keep to 1-2 cups of coffee daily if trying to conceive
- **Processed Meats** — nitrates and preservatives are associated with reduced sperm quality and hormonal disruption
- **Licorice Root** — glycyrrhizin reduces testosterone levels and can disrupt the hormonal cascade kisspeptin initiates
- **Very Low-Calorie Diets** — severe caloric restriction directly shuts down kisspeptin signaling, causing hypothalamic hypogonadism. This is one of the most direct ways to counteract kisspeptin therapy
