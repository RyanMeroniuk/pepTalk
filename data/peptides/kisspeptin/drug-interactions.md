# Kisspeptin

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Hormonal contraceptives (oral, patch, IUD)**: Contraceptives suppress the HPG axis through exogenous hormones; kisspeptin stimulates the same axis. Combining creates opposing signals and may reduce contraceptive efficacy or cause unpredictable hormonal effects
- **GnRH agonists (leuprolide, goserelin)**: Initially stimulate then suppress GnRH receptors; kisspeptin drives GnRH release. Combining may cause unpredictable hormonal surges or blunted response depending on timing
- **GnRH antagonists (cetrorelix, ganirelix)**: Directly block GnRH receptors, counteracting kisspeptin's entire mechanism of action. Concurrent use renders kisspeptin ineffective
- **Anabolic steroids and exogenous testosterone**: Suppress the HPG axis through negative feedback, shutting down LH/FSH production — directly opposing kisspeptin's stimulatory effect. These work in opposite directions
- **Aromatase inhibitors (anastrozole, letrozole)**: Alter estrogen-to-testosterone ratios, changing HPG axis feedback dynamics. May amplify kisspeptin's LH-stimulating effects unpredictably
- **SERMs (tamoxifen, clomiphene)**: Overlapping HPG axis modulation; dose adjustments may be needed under medical supervision
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy and breastfeeding — kisspeptin modulates reproductive hormones that must be tightly regulated during pregnancy; use only in fertility protocols under direct medical supervision
- **Contraindicated**: Hormone-sensitive cancers (breast, prostate, ovarian) — stimulating LH/FSH and downstream sex hormones may accelerate hormone-dependent tumor growth
