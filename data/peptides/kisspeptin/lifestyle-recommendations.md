# Kisspeptin

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Optimize Sleep for Hormonal Health**: Sleep is when the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal (HPG) axis is most active. Testosterone pulses occur during deep sleep, and kisspeptin drives this cascade. Aim for 7-9 hours of consistent, quality sleep. Poor sleep directly blunts the GnRH release that kisspeptin stimulates
- **Maintain Healthy Body Composition**: Body fat percentage directly affects kisspeptin signaling. Excess body fat increases aromatase activity (converting testosterone to estrogen) and leptin resistance, both of which impair kisspeptin neuron function. Conversely, extremely low body fat (as in female athletes or eating disorders) shuts down kisspeptin signaling, causing hypothalamic amenorrhea
- **Strength Training for Hormonal Support**: Compound resistance exercises (squats, deadlifts, bench press, rows) naturally stimulate the HPG axis and increase LH pulsatility — the same downstream pathway kisspeptin activates. Train 3-4x/week with progressive overload
- **Manage Stress for Fertility**: Cortisol directly suppresses kisspeptin neurons in the hypothalamus. Chronic stress is one of the primary causes of hypothalamic dysfunction and reduced fertility. Daily stress management (meditation, breathwork, yoga, nature exposure) protects the reproductive axis kisspeptin supports
- **Moderate Cardiovascular Exercise**: Regular moderate cardio (30-45 min, 4-5x/week) supports hormonal health and blood flow to reproductive organs. Avoid extreme endurance training, which can suppress the HPG axis
- **Avoid Energy Deficiency**: Kisspeptin neurons are highly sensitive to energy balance. Chronic caloric restriction or relative energy deficiency in sport (RED-S) suppresses kisspeptin signaling, leading to reduced LH, low testosterone, and infertility. Ensure adequate caloric intake, especially if training
- **Limit Alcohol for Reproductive Health**: Alcohol suppresses GnRH release, reduces testosterone, impairs sperm quality, and disrupts ovulation — directly counteracting kisspeptin's mechanism
- **For Fertility-Focused Users**: Track basal body temperature, maintain a consistent routine, manage stress actively, and ensure adequate nutrition. Kisspeptin works through the natural reproductive cascade, which requires a supportive hormonal environment
- **Circadian Rhythm Consistency**: The HPG axis follows circadian patterns. Irregular sleep schedules disrupt hormonal pulsatility. Maintain consistent wake/sleep times, get morning sunlight, and avoid blue light exposure at night
