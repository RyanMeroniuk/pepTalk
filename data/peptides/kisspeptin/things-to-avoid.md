# Kisspeptin

## Things to Avoid

- **Chronic Stress** — cortisol directly inhibits kisspeptin neurons in the hypothalamus, suppressing GnRH release. This is the primary lifestyle factor that impairs the reproductive axis. Stress-induced hypothalamic dysfunction is one of the leading causes of infertility
- **Endocrine Disruptors** — BPA (plastic bottles, canned food linings), phthalates (fragrances, soft plastics), parabens (cosmetics), and pesticides mimic or block reproductive hormones. Use glass/stainless steel containers, choose fragrance-free products, eat organic when possible, and never heat food in plastic
- **Extreme Exercise Without Adequate Nutrition** — the combination of high training volume and insufficient calories suppresses kisspeptin signaling, leading to hypothalamic amenorrhea in women and low testosterone in men. This is called RED-S (Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport)
- **Sleep Deprivation** — testosterone and LH pulses are sleep-dependent. Chronic poor sleep reduces the hormonal output that kisspeptin stimulates by 10-15%
- **Excessive Alcohol** — directly toxic to gonads, suppresses GnRH, and impairs fertility in both sexes. Even moderate drinking reduces conception rates
- **Anabolic Steroids and Exogenous Testosterone** — suppress the HPG axis through negative feedback, shutting down the very LH/FSH production that kisspeptin stimulates. Kisspeptin and exogenous androgens work in opposite directions
- **Exposure to Heat (for Male Fertility)** — hot tubs, saunas, laptop heat on the lap, and tight underwear elevate testicular temperature, impairing sperm production. The testes require temperatures below core body temperature for optimal spermatogenesis
- **Smoking and Nicotine** — reduce fertility in both sexes, impair blood flow to reproductive organs, damage egg and sperm DNA, and accelerate reproductive aging
- **Crash Diets and Extreme Fasting** — rapid weight loss and severe caloric restriction directly suppress kisspeptin neurons, shutting down reproductive function as the body prioritizes survival over reproduction
