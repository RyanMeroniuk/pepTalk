# Kisspeptin

## Dosing Protocols

- **Research Doses**: 1-10 mcg/kg intravenous or subcutaneous
- **Kisspeptin-10**: Most commonly used form
- **Kisspeptin-54**: Longer-acting form used in some clinical settings
- **Route**: IV (clinical/research) or subcutaneous
- **Timing**: Varies by application — single dose for diagnostics, repeated dosing for fertility
- **Very early stage**: Dosing protocols not well-established for therapeutic use
