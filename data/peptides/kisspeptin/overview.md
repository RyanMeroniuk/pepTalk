# Kisspeptin

## Overview

Kisspeptin (originally called metastin) is a naturally occurring neuropeptide encoded by the KISS1 gene. The most studied form is Kisspeptin-10 (the 10-amino acid C-terminal fragment). It is the master regulator of the reproductive hormone cascade — it triggers GnRH (gonadotropin-releasing hormone) release, which drives LH and FSH production, ultimately controlling testosterone, estrogen, and fertility. Discovered through its role in puberty onset. Emerging as a research tool for fertility treatment and hormonal optimization.
