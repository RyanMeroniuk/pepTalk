# Kisspeptin

## Stacking Protocols

- **Sexual Health Stack**: Kisspeptin (1-10 mcg/kg) + PT-141 (1.75 mg as needed) — kisspeptin drives upstream hormonal production (LH/FSH/testosterone) while PT-141 directly activates sexual arousal pathways; complementary mechanisms addressing both hormonal and neurological aspects of sexual function
- **Male Fertility Stack**: Kisspeptin (1-10 mcg/kg) + hCG (as prescribed) + HMG (as prescribed) — comprehensive fertility protocol using kisspeptin to stimulate natural GnRH pulsatility alongside gonadotropin support; use under specialist supervision
- **Female Fertility / IVF Support Stack**: Kisspeptin (as clinically dosed) + standard IVF protocol — kisspeptin used as oocyte maturation trigger with lower OHSS risk than hCG; strictly under reproductive endocrinologist supervision
- **Hormonal Optimization Stack**: Kisspeptin (1-10 mcg/kg) + Enclomiphene (12.5-25 mg daily) — dual approach to stimulating the HPG axis; kisspeptin drives GnRH while enclomiphene blocks estrogen negative feedback at the pituitary
- **Post-Cycle Recovery Stack**: Kisspeptin (1-10 mcg/kg) + hCG (as prescribed) — for HPG axis recovery after anabolic steroid use; kisspeptin restores hypothalamic signaling while hCG directly stimulates testicular function
