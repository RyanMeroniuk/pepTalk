# Kisspeptin

## Foods to Eat

- **Zinc-Rich Foods** (oysters, pumpkin seeds, grass-fed beef, lamb) — zinc is essential for testosterone synthesis, sperm production, and ovarian function. Directly supports the hormonal cascade kisspeptin initiates
- **Healthy Fats** (avocado, olive oil, coconut oil, nuts) — cholesterol from healthy fats is the precursor to all sex hormones (testosterone, estrogen, progesterone). Adequate dietary fat is essential for the hormones kisspeptin stimulates
- **Eggs** — provide cholesterol for hormone synthesis, choline for reproductive health, and vitamin D precursors
- **Fatty Fish** (wild-caught salmon, sardines, mackerel) — omega-3s support hormonal production, reduce inflammation in reproductive tissues, and improve sperm quality
- **Cruciferous Vegetables** (broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts, kale) — contain DIM (diindolylmethane) which supports healthy estrogen metabolism, balancing the hormonal environment
- **Pomegranate** — shown to increase testosterone levels and improve sperm quality; rich in antioxidants that protect reproductive cells
- **Brazil Nuts** (2-3 daily) — selenium supports thyroid function (thyroid health is critical for reproduction) and improves sperm motility
- **Maca Root** — adaptogenic root with evidence for improved fertility, libido, and hormonal balance in both sexes. Add to smoothies or take as supplement
- **Leafy Greens** (spinach, Swiss chard) — folate is critical for fertility and fetal development; also supports hormonal production
- **Berries** (blueberries, strawberries, raspberries) — antioxidants protect egg and sperm quality from oxidative damage
- **Whole Grains** (quinoa, oats, brown rice) — provide B vitamins and complex carbohydrates that support consistent energy balance for the HPG axis
