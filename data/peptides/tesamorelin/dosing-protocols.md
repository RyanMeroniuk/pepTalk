# Tesamorelin

## Dosing Protocols

- **FDA-Approved Dose**: 2mg subcutaneous injection daily
- **Timing**: Evening administration preferred
- **Format**: Lyophilized powder, reconstitute with sterile water
- **New Formulation**: Egrifta WR — weekly reconstitution instead of daily prep
- **Off-Label**: Some clinics use 1-2mg, 5-7 days per week
- **Cycle**: Continuous treatment recommended (visceral fat returns upon discontinuation). Some protocols use 2-3 months on, 1 month off
- **Storage**: Refrigerate at 36-46°F after reconstitution
- **Results Timeline**: 1-2 weeks for initial visceral fat reduction; 4-6 weeks significant loss; 8-12 weeks full therapeutic benefit
