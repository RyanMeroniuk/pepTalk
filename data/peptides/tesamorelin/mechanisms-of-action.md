# Tesamorelin

## Mechanisms of Action

- **GHRH Analog**: Stimulates pituitary gland to release GH in a pulsatile, physiologic pattern
- **IGF-1 Elevation**: Raises insulin-like growth factor 1 levels, driving cellular repair, metabolism, and body composition changes
- **Visceral Fat Targeting**: Specifically reduces visceral adipose tissue (VAT) via GH-mediated lipolysis — targets deep abdominal fat, not just subcutaneous
- **Does not suppress** endogenous GH production
- **Hepatic Effects**: Improves liver fat content, beneficial for NAFLD in HIV patients
- **Cognitive Effects**: Crosses blood-brain barrier; demonstrated effects on hippocampal function and cognition
