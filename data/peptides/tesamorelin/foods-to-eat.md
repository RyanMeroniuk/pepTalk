# Tesamorelin

## Foods to Eat

- **Wild-caught salmon and mackerel** — omega-3 fatty acids reduce visceral fat independently and improve the lipid profile that tesamorelin also targets
- **Chicken breast and turkey** — lean protein supports muscle preservation during fat loss
- **Leafy greens (spinach, kale, arugula)** — nutrient-dense, anti-inflammatory, and very low calorie — ideal for a slight caloric deficit
- **Cruciferous vegetables (broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts)** — fiber supports gut health and satiety; sulforaphane has anti-inflammatory properties
- **Avocado** — monounsaturated fats specifically associated with reduced visceral fat in research studies
- **Extra virgin olive oil** — anti-inflammatory monounsaturated fats; the Mediterranean diet pattern is strongly associated with reduced visceral adiposity
- **Berries (blueberries, strawberries, blackberries)** — polyphenols and antioxidants support metabolic health and reduce inflammation
- **Walnuts and almonds** — healthy fats, fiber, and protein. Walnut consumption specifically linked to reduced visceral fat in clinical studies
- **Green tea** — catechins (particularly EGCG) support fat oxidation and have been shown to preferentially reduce visceral fat
- **Lentils and chickpeas** — fiber-rich, protein-rich legumes that stabilize blood sugar and support gut microbiome
- **Turmeric** — curcumin reduces systemic inflammation and may support visceral fat reduction
- **Fermented foods (kimchi, sauerkraut, kefir)** — gut microbiome health influences visceral fat metabolism
- **Eggs (pasture-raised)** — complete protein with choline, which supports liver health (relevant given tesamorelin's hepatic benefits)
