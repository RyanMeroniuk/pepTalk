# Tesamorelin

## Regulatory Status

- **FDA-APPROVED** for HIV-associated lipodystrophy (visceral fat reduction)
- Multiple Phase III clinical trials completed with extensive safety data (52-week studies)
- Prescribed through standard pharmacy channels
- Egrifta WR (new weekly formulation) available since September 2025
- Covered by some insurance plans for HIV lipodystrophy indication
- Growing off-label use for general visceral fat reduction and anti-aging
- Available by prescription from pharmacies
