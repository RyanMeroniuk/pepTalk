# Tesamorelin

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Active cancer**: CONTRAINDICATED — IGF-1 elevation may promote tumor growth
- **Pituitary disorders**: May be ineffective or harmful
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding**: Safety not established
- **Diabetes medications**: May require monitoring and adjustment
- **Glucocorticoids**: May reduce effectiveness
- **Antiretrovirals**: Generally compatible (studied in HIV population)
