# Tesamorelin

## Overview

Tesamorelin (TH9507, brand names Egrifta, Egrifta SV, Egrifta WR) is a synthetic 44-amino acid analog of growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH). Manufactured by Theratechnologies. The only FDA-approved peptide in the GH secretagogue class — approved specifically for reducing excess abdominal fat in HIV-associated lipodystrophy. New weekly reconstitution formulation (Egrifta WR) approved March 2025, available since September 2025 — same molecule, same efficacy, just once-weekly prep instead of daily.
