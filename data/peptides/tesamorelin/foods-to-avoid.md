# Tesamorelin

## Foods to Avoid

- **Trans fats and hydrogenated oils** — directly promote visceral fat deposition, counteracting tesamorelin's primary mechanism
- **Added sugars and high-fructose corn syrup** — fructose is preferentially metabolized by the liver and drives visceral fat accumulation. Avoid soda, candy, baked goods, and sweetened beverages
- **Refined carbohydrates** — white bread, white rice, and pasta spike insulin, which promotes fat storage and blunts GH release
- **Alcohol** — directly increases visceral fat deposition, impairs liver function, suppresses GH release, and adds empty calories. Particularly counterproductive when the goal is visceral fat reduction
- **Processed meats (bacon, sausage, hot dogs)** — associated with increased visceral fat and systemic inflammation
- **Fried foods** — high in inflammatory omega-6 fatty acids and trans fats
- **Sugary beverages including fruit juice** — liquid sugar is rapidly absorbed and drives hepatic fat production and visceral fat storage
- **Excess sodium from processed foods** — promotes water retention and may exacerbate any edema from tesamorelin
