# Tesamorelin

## Benefits

- **Visceral Fat Reduction**: 18% decrease vs placebo in 12-month trial (404 HIV patients, 2mg daily). Consistently 15-18% reduction across multiple Phase III trials
- **Muscle Development**: Increased skeletal muscle area and density (confirmed in 2020 analysis of two clinical trials)
- **Cognitive Function**: Favorable effects on cognition in both adults with mild cognitive impairment and healthy older adults (20-week randomized trial)
- **Cardiovascular Health**: Significant reduction in cardiovascular disease risk in HIV patients (2025 study)
- **Lipid Profile**: Improved triglycerides and cholesterol levels
- **Liver Health**: Improved hepatic steatosis markers and liver fat content
- **Body Composition**: Better overall body composition without major metabolic disruption
