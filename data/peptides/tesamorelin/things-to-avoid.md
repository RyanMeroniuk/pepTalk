# Tesamorelin

## Things to Avoid

- **Discontinuing treatment without a plan** — visceral fat returns upon stopping tesamorelin. If you plan to stop, taper and have a strong exercise/nutrition program in place
- **Alcohol consumption** — directly promotes visceral fat accumulation, which is the exact opposite of tesamorelin's therapeutic goal. Even moderate drinking deposits visceral fat preferentially
- **Sedentary behavior** — sitting for prolonged periods promotes visceral fat deposition regardless of exercise habits. Stand, walk, or move every 30-60 minutes throughout the day
- **Chronic stress without management** — cortisol is the primary hormonal driver of visceral fat accumulation. Unmanaged stress directly opposes tesamorelin's mechanism
- **Skipping blood work** — tesamorelin elevates IGF-1, which requires monitoring. Additionally, glucose and lipid monitoring ensures metabolic safety
- **Late-night eating** — eating large meals late disrupts the nocturnal GH pulse and promotes fat storage, especially visceral fat
- **Relying solely on tesamorelin** — the peptide works best as part of a comprehensive approach. Without exercise and dietary changes, results are suboptimal and unsustainable
- **Ignoring liver health** — tesamorelin has hepatic benefits, but excessive alcohol, acetaminophen overuse, or a high-fructose diet can overwhelm these protective effects
- **Sleep deprivation** — insufficient sleep promotes visceral fat accumulation via cortisol elevation and disrupts the GH pulse that tesamorelin amplifies
