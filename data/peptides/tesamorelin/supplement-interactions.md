# Tesamorelin

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Enhanced by**: Regular exercise, especially resistance training, for body composition
- **Enhanced by**: Slight caloric deficit amplifies fat loss
- **Enhanced by**: Anti-inflammatory diet, adequate protein intake
- **Monitor**: IGF-1 levels periodically — adjust dose if above age-appropriate range
- **Caution**: Active malignancy — CONTRAINDICATED (theoretical tumor promotion via IGF-1)
- **Caution**: Pituitary gland disorders
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy/breastfeeding
- **May affect glucose metabolism**: Monitor in diabetic patients
- **Key limitation**: Results are treatment-dependent — visceral fat returns if therapy is discontinued
