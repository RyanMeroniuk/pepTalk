# Tesamorelin

## Side Effects

- **Common**: Injection site reactions (redness, swelling, discomfort)
- **Joint/Muscle Pain**: Arthralgia and myalgia
- **Flu-like Symptoms**: Fatigue, headache, nausea (mild)
- **Peripheral Edema**: Mild swelling
- **Elevated IGF-1**: Requires periodic monitoring and dose adjustment if above age-appropriate range
- **Rare/Severe**: High fever, injection site blistering, allergic reactions (itching, rash, hives)
- **Generally well-tolerated**: In clinical trials, 68% reported adverse events but the vast majority were mild
- **No significant glucose elevations** in clinical trials — safer than many alternatives
