# Tesamorelin

## Lifestyle Recommendations


### Injection Timing & Protocol
- **Evening injection is preferred** — aligns with the natural circadian GH pulse during sleep. Administer at least 2 hours after your last meal
- **Inject in a fasted or semi-fasted state** — while tesamorelin's clinical trials did not strictly require fasting, GH release is optimized when insulin levels are low
- **Consistency matters** — tesamorelin's visceral fat reduction is dose-dependent and cumulative. Daily adherence is critical; missing doses allows visceral fat to begin returning
- **Expect 1-2 weeks for initial results** — visceral fat reduction is measurable early, but visible body composition changes take 4-8 weeks

### Exercise
- **Resistance training 3-5x/week** — compound movements (squats, deadlifts, bench press, rows) are essential for maximizing tesamorelin's muscle-preserving and body recomposition effects
- **Include core-focused work** — tesamorelin specifically targets visceral abdominal fat. Strengthening the core musculature as visceral fat decreases reveals the underlying improvements
- **Moderate cardio 3-4x/week (30-45 minutes)** — brisk walking, cycling, or swimming supports cardiovascular health and amplifies the metabolic benefits. Tesamorelin's cardiovascular risk reduction is enhanced with regular aerobic exercise
- **HIIT 1-2x/week** — sprint intervals or metabolic circuits improve insulin sensitivity and amplify fat oxidation
- **Avoid excessive endurance training** — sessions beyond 60 minutes of moderate intensity elevate cortisol, which antagonizes GH-mediated benefits

### Sleep Optimization
- **7-9 hours of quality sleep** — GH release peaks during deep sleep. Tesamorelin amplifies this pulse, but sufficient sleep duration is required
- **Consistent sleep schedule** — go to bed and wake up at the same time daily to optimize circadian GH rhythm
- **Cool, dark bedroom (65-68°F)** — melatonin and GH release are both enhanced in optimal sleep conditions
- **Inject 20-30 minutes before lying down** — allows the peptide to begin signaling as you enter deep sleep

### Metabolic Health Monitoring
- **Regular blood work every 8-12 weeks**: IGF-1, fasting glucose, fasting insulin, HbA1c, lipid panel, liver enzymes
- **DEXA scan every 6-12 months** — the gold standard for tracking visceral fat reduction. Tesamorelin's benefits are visceral (deep abdominal), which is not always visible externally
- **Waist circumference measurements monthly** — a simple, practical way to track visceral fat changes between DEXA scans
- **If IGF-1 rises above age-appropriate range**, discuss dose adjustment with your prescribing physician

### Daily Habits
- **Maintain a slight caloric deficit (200-300 calories)** — amplifies tesamorelin's fat loss without sacrificing muscle. Tesamorelin is one of the few peptides where a mild deficit is synergistic
- **Manage stress** — cortisol directly promotes visceral fat deposition, counteracting tesamorelin's primary benefit. Daily meditation, walking, or breathwork helps
- **Morning sunlight (15-20 minutes)** — supports circadian rhythm, which optimizes the GH axis
- **Stay well hydrated** — supports metabolic function and helps manage any mild water retention
- **Commit to long-term use** — visceral fat returns upon discontinuation. Tesamorelin is most effective as an ongoing treatment paired with lifestyle optimization
