# Larazotide (AT-1001)

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT YET FDA-approved** — in advanced clinical development
- Phase III clinical trials completed for celiac disease
- Breakthrough therapy potential
- FDA Fast Track designation for celiac disease
- 9 Meters Biopharma developing
- If approved, would be the first pharmacological treatment for celiac disease
- Not yet available from compounding pharmacies
