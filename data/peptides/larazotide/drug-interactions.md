# Larazotide (AT-1001)

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Immunosuppressants (tacrolimus, cyclosporine, methotrexate)**: Larazotide reduces intestinal permeability, which may alter absorption of orally administered immunosuppressants. Monitor drug levels and therapeutic efficacy; dose adjustments may be needed
- **Celiac-specific medications and supplements**: If larazotide reduces gut inflammation and improves absorption, patients may need to reassess supplementation doses for iron, folate, B12, calcium, and vitamin D as malabsorption improves
- **NSAIDs (ibuprofen, naproxen, aspirin)**: NSAIDs directly damage tight junctions and increase intestinal permeability — the exact opposite of larazotide's mechanism. Avoid NSAIDs during larazotide therapy; use acetaminophen for pain relief instead
- **Proton pump inhibitors (omeprazole, pantoprazole)**: Long-term PPIs reduce stomach acid, potentially allowing bacterial overgrowth and impairing protein digestion. May indirectly undermine gut barrier restoration. Reassess PPI need with prescriber
- **Narrow therapeutic index oral medications**: By altering intestinal permeability, larazotide may affect absorption of drugs with narrow therapeutic windows (warfarin, levothyroxine, digoxin, lithium). Monitor drug levels closely when starting or stopping larazotide
- **Alcohol**: Damages intestinal epithelium and increases permeability through direct toxicity — counteracts larazotide's tight junction protection. Avoid during active gut healing protocols
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy and breastfeeding — insufficient safety data; effects on fetal intestinal development unknown
- **Contraindicated**: Known hypersensitivity to larazotide or its components
