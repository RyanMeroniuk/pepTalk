# Larazotide (AT-1001)

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Tight Junction Regulation**: Prevents pathological opening of intestinal tight junctions
- **Zonulin Antagonist**: Blocks zonulin-mediated intestinal permeability
- **Intestinal Barrier Restoration**: Restores proper barrier function of the gut lining
- **Local Action**: Acts locally in the GI tract — minimal systemic absorption (safety advantage)
- **Prevents Antigen Passage**: By maintaining tight junctions, prevents food proteins and bacteria from crossing into the bloodstream
