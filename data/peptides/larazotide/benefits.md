# Larazotide (AT-1001)

## Benefits

- **Celiac Disease**: Reduced symptoms even during gluten exposure in Phase II/III trials
- **Leaky Gut**: Directly addresses intestinal hyperpermeability
- **Autoimmune Support**: Leaky gut is implicated in many autoimmune conditions — restoring barrier function may reduce immune triggers
- **Gluten Sensitivity**: May allow celiac patients to tolerate incidental gluten exposure
- **Local Action**: Minimal systemic effects (acts in the gut lumen)
- **IBS**: Potential benefit for IBS with permeability component
- **Type 1 Diabetes**: Investigated for preventing disease progression (leaky gut connection)
