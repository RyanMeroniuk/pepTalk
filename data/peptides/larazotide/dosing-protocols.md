# Larazotide (AT-1001)

## Dosing Protocols

- **Clinical Trial Dose**: 0.5-1mg taken orally, 3x daily before meals
- **Route**: Oral (capsule)
- **Timing**: Before meals (to protect tight junctions during digestion)
- **Not yet commercially available** — still in clinical development
- **Designed for chronic/continuous use** in celiac disease
