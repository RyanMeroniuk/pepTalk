# Larazotide (AT-1001)

## Overview

Larazotide (AT-1001) is a synthetic octapeptide designed to regulate intestinal tight junctions. Developed by Alba Therapeutics (now Innovate Biopharmaceuticals / 9 Meters Biopharma). The most advanced tight junction regulator in clinical development. Specifically targets the zonulin pathway — zonulin is a protein that opens tight junctions between intestinal epithelial cells, increasing intestinal permeability ("leaky gut"). By blocking zonulin, larazotide prevents pathological intestinal permeability. Primarily developed for celiac disease but has implications for any condition involving leaky gut.
