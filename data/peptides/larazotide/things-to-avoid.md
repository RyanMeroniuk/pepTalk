# Larazotide (AT-1001)

## Things to Avoid

- **Intentional Gluten Consumption**: Larazotide reduces symptoms from accidental gluten exposure but is not a "gluten pill" that allows deliberate gluten intake. Ongoing gluten exposure causes villous atrophy and intestinal damage regardless of symptom reduction
- **Cross-Contamination Complacency**: Even with larazotide, maintain strict cross-contamination protocols. Shared toasters, cutting boards, pasta water, deep fryers, and buffets are common contamination sources. Larazotide is a safety net, not a license to relax vigilance
- **NSAIDs (Ibuprofen, Naproxen, Aspirin)**: NSAIDs directly increase intestinal permeability by damaging tight junctions — the opposite of larazotide's mechanism. Use acetaminophen for pain relief instead
- **Chronic Stress**: Psychological stress independently increases zonulin production and intestinal permeability through the HPA axis. Unmanaged stress creates the very problem larazotide is designed to prevent. Daily stress management is not optional
- **Antibiotics Without Probiotic Support**: Antibiotics disrupt the gut microbiome, which plays a critical role in maintaining barrier function. If antibiotics are necessary, use Saccharomyces boulardii during treatment and rebuild with broad-spectrum probiotics afterward
- **Proton Pump Inhibitors (Long-Term)**: PPIs reduce stomach acid, impairing protein digestion and allowing bacterial overgrowth. Many celiac patients are unnecessarily prescribed PPIs for reflux that resolves with a gluten-free diet
- **Ignoring Nutrient Deficiencies**: Celiac patients commonly have deficiencies in iron, folate, B12, vitamin D, calcium, zinc, and magnesium due to malabsorption. Get comprehensive nutrient testing and supplement deficiencies — healing requires adequate building materials
- **Eating Out Without Inquiry**: Restaurant food is a leading source of accidental gluten exposure. Always communicate celiac status clearly, ask about preparation methods, and avoid establishments that cannot guarantee gluten-free preparation
- **Environmental Gluten**: Gluten in lip balms, medications, supplements, communion wafers, Play-Doh (for children), and some cosmetics can trigger immune response through ingestion. Check all products that contact the mouth
