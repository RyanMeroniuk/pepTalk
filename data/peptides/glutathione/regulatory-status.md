# Glutathione

## Regulatory Status

- **A tripeptide, not a typical therapeutic peptide** — included here because it is widely used in IV, injectable, and liposomal biohacker protocols alongside peptides.
- **NOT FDA-approved** as a drug for anti-aging, skin lightening, or detoxification.
- Oral and liposomal glutathione are sold as dietary supplements in the United States.
- IV and injectable glutathione are administered through clinics and compounding pharmacies; not an FDA-approved injectable drug product, and the FDA has warned against unapproved injectable skin-lightening use.
- Endogenous glutathione is essential and present in all cells; supplementation aims to support or restore levels.
- Generally regarded as safe and well tolerated at typical doses.
- Supplementation efficacy is debated, particularly for plain oral forms with poor absorption.
- Active area of antioxidant, detoxification, and dermatology research (2025–2026).
