# Glutathione

## Side Effects

- Generally very well tolerated; serious effects are uncommon.
- Injection or infusion site reactions (stinging, redness).
- Mild GI symptoms (bloating, cramps, loose stools) with oral forms.
- Temporary sulfur-like smell or taste.
- Rare allergic/hypersensitivity reactions.
- Skin lightening from high-dose use reverses after discontinuation and may be uneven.
- Theoretical zinc depletion with prolonged high-dose use (monitor).
- Asthma-related caution: inhaled/nebulized glutathione has triggered bronchospasm in some asthmatics (not a concern for IV/oral).
