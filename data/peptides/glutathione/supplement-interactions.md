# Glutathione

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Critical Precursors**: NAC (600–1200mg) and glycine — supply cysteine and glycine to raise endogenous glutathione synthesis; the most reliable oral strategy.
- **Synergistic**: Vitamin-c (500–1000mg) — glutathione regenerates oxidized vitamin C and vice versa, amplifying antioxidant capacity.
- **Synergistic**: Alpha-lipoic-acid (300–600mg) — directly recycles glutathione back to its reduced form; a strong pairing.
- **Synergistic**: Vitamin E — regenerated by glutathione, extending the lipid-phase antioxidant network.
- **Enhanced by**: Selenium — cofactor for glutathione peroxidase, the enzyme that uses GSH.
- **Enhanced by**: Milk thistle (silymarin) — supports liver glutathione status and detox.
- **Synergistic**: B-complex (especially B6, folate, B12) — support the methylation and transsulfuration pathways feeding glutathione synthesis.
- **Enhanced by**: Omega-3 — complementary anti-inflammatory support.
- **Caution**: Avoid pairing high-dose oxidant therapies that would consume glutathione faster than it is replenished.
- **Contraindicated**: Active cancer (antioxidant effects on tumor cells and chemotherapy are uncertain — see Drug Interactions).
- **Caution**: Asthma with inhaled/nebulized forms (bronchospasm risk).
