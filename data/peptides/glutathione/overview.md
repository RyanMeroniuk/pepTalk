# Glutathione

## Overview

Glutathione (GSH) is a tripeptide composed of glutamate, cysteine, and glycine (gamma-Glu-Cys-Gly) and is the body's master endogenous antioxidant. Unusually, its glutamate is joined through a gamma-peptide bond, which makes it resistant to ordinary peptidases — so while it is technically a peptide, it behaves more like a small-molecule antioxidant than a typical therapeutic peptide. Found in high concentrations in every cell, it neutralizes free radicals, regenerates other antioxidants like vitamins C and E, and is central to liver detoxification. It is widely used in IV, injectable, and liposomal "biohacker" protocols for antioxidant support, skin brightening, and detox claims. The efficacy of supplementation is genuinely debated: oral glutathione is poorly absorbed and largely broken down in digestion, which is why injectable, liposomal, and precursor approaches (like NAC) are popular. It is generally regarded as safe and well tolerated.
