# Glutathione

## Foods to Eat

- **Sulfur-Rich Cruciferous Vegetables**: Broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts, cabbage, kale — sulforaphane upregulates the body's glutathione production.
- **Alliums**: Garlic, onions, leeks, shallots — sulfur compounds support glutathione synthesis.
- **Cysteine-Rich Protein**: Eggs, poultry, fish, whey protein — supply cysteine, the rate-limiting amino acid for glutathione.
- **Glycine Sources**: Bone broth, collagen, gelatin, poultry skin — provide glycine, another glutathione building block.
- **Vitamin C-Rich Produce**: Citrus, bell peppers, strawberries, kiwi — help recycle and spare glutathione.
- **Selenium Sources**: Brazil nuts, sardines, eggs — cofactor for glutathione peroxidase.
- **Glutathione-Containing Foods**: Fresh raw spinach, avocado, asparagus, okra — contain dietary glutathione (though absorption is modest).
- **Whey Protein**: A concentrated source of cysteine that reliably supports glutathione synthesis.
