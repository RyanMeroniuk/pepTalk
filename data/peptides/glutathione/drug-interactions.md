# Glutathione

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Chemotherapy**: Use with caution under oncology supervision — antioxidants may theoretically blunt the oxidative mechanism of some chemotherapy and radiation; some regimens actually use glutathione to reduce toxicity, so timing must be coordinated and it is contraindicated during active treatment without oversight.
- **Acetaminophen (high-dose/overdose)**: Glutathione and its precursor NAC are protective against acetaminophen-induced liver injury; relevant context rather than a harmful interaction.
- **Corticosteroids**: Long-term steroids can alter oxidative and immune status; monitor when combining, no major interaction established.
- **Anticoagulants / Blood Thinners**: No established interaction; standard injection-site caution applies, monitor for bruising.
- **Cisplatin and Nephrotoxic Agents**: Glutathione is studied to reduce their toxicity, but use only under medical supervision to avoid undermining efficacy.
- **Contraindicated**: Active cancer during oxidative chemotherapy/radiation without oncologist coordination.
- **Caution**: Pregnancy and breastfeeding — high-dose injectable use is not well studied; food-derived and modest intake is generally considered fine.
- **Caution**: Asthma with inhaled/nebulized formulations (bronchospasm risk); IV and oral routes are not implicated.
