# Glutathione

## Dosing Protocols

- **IV Infusion**: Common protocols use 600–2400mg per session, often weekly to several times weekly for antioxidant or skin-brightening goals.
- **Subcutaneous / Intramuscular**: Anecdotal protocols use ~200–600mg several times weekly to bypass poor oral absorption.
- **Liposomal Oral**: 250–500mg/day in a liposomal carrier, which improves absorption versus plain oral glutathione.
- **Oral Limitation**: Standard oral glutathione is largely broken down in digestion; precursors (NAC, glycine) or liposomal forms are preferred for oral routes.
- **Frequency**: IV courses often run as a weekly series then periodic maintenance; oral liposomal is typically daily.
- **Precursor Alternative**: NAC (600–1200mg/day) plus glycine reliably raises endogenous glutathione and is a practical oral strategy.
- **Reconstitution / Storage**: Injectable glutathione is light- and oxidation-sensitive; protect from light, refrigerate, and use promptly.
- **Note**: A gamma-bonded tripeptide rather than a typical therapeutic peptide — dosing follows antioxidant rather than peptide conventions.
