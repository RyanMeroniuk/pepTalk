# Glutathione

## Stacking Protocols

- **Antioxidant Recycling Stack**: Glutathione (IV 600–1200mg or liposomal 500mg/day) + vitamin-c (1000mg/day) + alpha-lipoic-acid (600mg/day) — the three regenerate each other for amplified antioxidant capacity.
- **Endogenous Synthesis Stack**: Glutathione (liposomal 500mg/day) + NAC (1200mg/day) + glycine (3g/day) — supplies the cysteine and glycine precursors to raise the body's own production.
- **Detox & Liver Stack**: Glutathione (IV 600–1200mg) + b-complex (1 serving/day) + omega-3 (3g/day EPA/DHA) — supports Phase II detox and methylation pathways.
- **Longevity Antioxidant Stack**: Glutathione (liposomal 500mg/day) + NMN (500mg/day) + resveratrol (250mg/day) — pairs antioxidant defense with NAD+ and sirtuin support.
- **Skin & Recovery Stack**: Glutathione (IV 600–1200mg) + vitamin-c (1000mg/day) + ghk-cu (1–2mg/day topical or subcutaneous) — combines antioxidant brightening with collagen and skin remodeling.
