# Glutathione

## Things to Avoid

- **Relying on Plain Oral Glutathione**: Standard oral GSH is largely degraded in digestion; use liposomal forms or precursors (NAC + glycine) for oral routes.
- **Excessive Alcohol**: Directly depletes glutathione and undermines the entire rationale for supplementation.
- **Nebulized/Inhaled Use in Asthma**: Inhaled glutathione has triggered bronchospasm in some asthmatics — avoid that route if asthmatic.
- **Use During Chemotherapy Without Oversight**: Antioxidants may theoretically interfere with oxidative chemotherapy mechanisms; coordinate with oncology (see Drug Interactions).
- **Unregulated IV Sourcing**: Injectable glutathione purity and sterility vary; non-sterile product risks infection and contamination.
- **Expecting Permanent Skin Lightening**: Skin-brightening effects are modest and reverse after discontinuation.
- **Smoking and Toxin Overload**: Continually depletes glutathione through detox demand, offsetting supplementation.
- **Overstating Detox Claims**: "Detox" marketing often outpaces evidence — glutathione supports normal liver detoxification but is not a cure-all.
