# Glutathione

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Direct Free Radical Scavenging**: Its cysteine thiol (-SH) group donates electrons to neutralize reactive oxygen and nitrogen species.
- **Glutathione Peroxidase Cofactor**: Serves as substrate for glutathione peroxidase to reduce hydrogen peroxide and lipid peroxides to harmless products.
- **Redox Cycling**: Cycles between reduced (GSH) and oxidized (GSSG) forms; the GSH/GSSG ratio is a key marker of cellular oxidative status.
- **Antioxidant Regeneration**: Recycles oxidized vitamin C and vitamin E back to their active forms, extending the antioxidant network.
- **Phase II Detoxification**: Conjugates toxins, heavy metals, and drug metabolites via glutathione-S-transferases for excretion, central to liver detox.
- **Protein Thiol Protection**: Maintains protein sulfhydryl groups in their functional reduced state.
- **Immune Modulation**: Supports lymphocyte function and balanced immune responses.
- **Mitochondrial Protection**: A dedicated mitochondrial GSH pool defends the electron transport chain from oxidative damage.
- **Melanin Pathway Influence**: Inhibits tyrosinase and shifts melanin synthesis, the basis for its skin-brightening use.
