# Glutathione

## Foods to Avoid

- **Excessive Alcohol**: The largest dietary drain on glutathione — alcohol metabolism in the liver consumes it heavily, undermining supplementation.
- **Ultra-Processed Foods**: Fast food, packaged snacks, deli meats — inflammatory additives raise oxidative load and deplete glutathione.
- **Refined Sugars**: Candy, soda, pastries — drive glycation and oxidative stress that consume antioxidant reserves.
- **Trans Fats and Oxidized Seed Oils**: Repeatedly fried oils and hydrogenated fats increase lipid peroxidation, taxing the glutathione system.
- **Charred and Processed Meats**: Form oxidized compounds and advanced glycation end-products that increase detox demand.
- **High-Fructose Corn Syrup**: Promotes liver fat and oxidative stress, competing for liver glutathione.
- **Heavily Pesticide-Laden Produce (unwashed)**: Adds to the Phase II detox burden that consumes glutathione.
