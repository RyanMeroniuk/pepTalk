# Glutathione

## Benefits

- **Antioxidant Defense**: The body's primary intracellular antioxidant; protects cells from oxidative stress — its core, well-established role.
- **Liver Detoxification**: Central to Phase II detox of toxins, drugs, and heavy metals; the rationale behind IV detox protocols.
- **Skin Brightening**: Popular for skin lightening and an even complexion via tyrosinase inhibition; evidence is modest and effects reverse on discontinuation.
- **Immune Support**: Supports lymphocyte function and is depleted in many chronic and inflammatory conditions.
- **Cellular and Mitochondrial Protection**: Defends mitochondria and proteins from oxidative damage, supporting overall cellular health.
- **Antioxidant Network Regeneration**: Recycles vitamins C and E, amplifying total antioxidant capacity.
- **Anti-Aging Interest**: Glutathione levels decline with age; repletion is pursued for general healthspan, though human longevity data is limited.
- **Efficacy Debate**: Benefits of supplementation are debated — oral absorption is poor, and many demonstrated effects come from IV, liposomal, or precursor (NAC) routes rather than plain oral GSH.
