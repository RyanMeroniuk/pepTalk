# Glutathione

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Exercise Raises Endogenous Glutathione**: Regular moderate aerobic and resistance training upregulates the body's own glutathione system. Avoid chronic overtraining, which can deplete it. Aim for 3–5 balanced sessions per week.
- **Prioritize Sleep**: Glutathione is replenished during sleep, and poor sleep lowers levels. Target 7–9 hours with consistent timing.
- **Limit Alcohol**: Alcohol metabolism heavily consumes glutathione in the liver, depleting the pool you are trying to support. Minimize intake.
- **Eat Sulfur-Rich Foods**: Cruciferous vegetables, alliums, and quality protein supply the cysteine and sulfur needed for glutathione synthesis (see Foods to Eat).
- **Reduce Toxin Exposure**: Smoking, pollution, and excess processed-food intake deplete glutathione through Phase II detox demand.
- **Manage Stress**: Chronic stress and inflammation raise oxidative load and consume glutathione faster.
- **Support the Liver**: Since glutathione is central to liver detox, a liver-supportive lifestyle (low alcohol, whole foods) preserves levels.
- **Stay Hydrated for IV Sessions**: Adequate hydration improves comfort and tolerance during IV infusions.
- **Track Skin and Energy**: For skin-brightening or antioxidant goals, monitor subjectively and note that skin effects reverse after stopping.
