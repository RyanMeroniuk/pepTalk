# Melanotan-I (Afamelanotide)

## Overview

Melanotan-I (afamelanotide) is a synthetic analog of alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone (alpha-MSH) that stimulates eumelanin production for skin pigmentation and photoprotection. Unlike Melanotan-II, it acts more selectively on the melanocortin-1 receptor (MC1R) and has far weaker activity at the MC3R/MC4R pathways, so it produces fewer libido, nausea, and erectile side effects. Its pharmaceutical form, afamelanotide (brand name Scenesse), is delivered as a subcutaneous controlled-release implant and is approved by the FDA and EMA to reduce phototoxic reactions in adults with erythropoietic protoporphyria (EPP). Because it increases melanocyte activity, mole and skin monitoring is an important safety consideration, and personal or family melanoma history is a serious concern. Gray-market "MT-I" sold as research powder raises significant purity, dosing, and sterility issues and is not the same as the regulated implant.
