# Melanotan-I (Afamelanotide)

## Foods to Eat

- **Antioxidant-Rich Produce**: Berries, leafy greens, and colorful vegetables provide polyphenols that help counter UV-induced oxidative stress in skin.
- **Carotenoid Foods**: Carrots, sweet potatoes, tomatoes (cooked for lycopene), and dark leafy greens support skin's natural photoprotection.
- **Wild-Caught Fatty Fish**: Salmon, sardines, and mackerel supply omega-3s and astaxanthin that reduce skin inflammation and UV-related damage.
- **Vitamin C-Rich Foods**: Bell peppers, citrus, and kiwi support collagen and antioxidant defense in skin undergoing pigment changes.
- **Vitamin D Sources**: Fatty fish, egg yolks, and fortified foods help offset lower vitamin D from reduced UV exposure.
- **Adequate Hydration**: Water and water-rich foods support overall skin health during pigmentation changes.
