# Melanotan-I (Afamelanotide)

## Dosing Protocols

- **Pharmaceutical Implant (Afamelanotide / Scenesse)**: Administered by a healthcare professional as a subcutaneous implant; for EPP, typically one implant every ~2 months during high-light seasons, per the approved label.
- **Gray-Market Research Powder**: Sold as lyophilized powder requiring reconstitution with bacteriostatic water; dosing is unstandardized and unverified — a significant safety and purity concern.
- **Reported Loading vs Maintenance (unofficial)**: Online protocols describe a short daily subcutaneous loading phase followed by reduced maintenance dosing, but these are not validated and carry quality risks.
- **Onset of Pigmentation**: Tanning develops gradually over days to weeks of sustained melanocortin signaling.
- **Sun Exposure**: Some users combine modest UV exposure to develop tan, but UV protection remains necessary; the peptide does not eliminate sunburn risk.
- **Note**: Only the regulated implant has validated dosing; research-powder use is unregulated and not recommended without clinical oversight.
