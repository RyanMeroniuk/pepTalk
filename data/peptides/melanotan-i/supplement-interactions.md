# Melanotan-I (Afamelanotide)

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Skin Monitoring (Critical)**: Regular dermatological exams for mole and pigmented-lesion changes are essential during melanocyte stimulation.
- **Sun Protection**: Continue using sunscreen — melanin from MT-I is not complete UV protection.
- **Synergistic**: GHK-Cu — copper-peptide support for skin quality and repair during melanocyte stimulation.
- **Enhanced by**: Vitamin-c and omega-3 for antioxidant and anti-inflammatory skin support.
- **Enhanced by**: Vitamin-d3 — relevant because reduced UV exposure can lower natural vitamin D synthesis.
- **Caution**: Other melanocortin agonists such as melanotan-ii or pt-141 — overlapping receptor stimulation compounds side effects; avoid combining.
- **Caution**: Personal or family melanoma history — strongly avoid.
