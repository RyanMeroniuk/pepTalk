# Melanotan-I (Afamelanotide)

## Things to Avoid

- **Skipping Skin Surveillance**: Never use MT-I without monitoring moles and pigmented lesions; report any change to a dermatologist promptly.
- **Assuming Full UV Protection**: The induced tan is partial protection only — continue sunscreen and avoid sunburn.
- **Intense Tanning-Bed Use**: Combining MT-I with strong UV compounds melanoma risk and is strongly discouraged.
- **Use With Melanoma History**: Avoid entirely with a personal or family history of melanoma or with suspicious pigmented lesions.
- **Combining With Other Melanocortin Agonists**: Do not stack with Melanotan-II or PT-141; overlapping receptor activation compounds side effects.
- **Gray-Market Research Powder**: Unregulated MT-I powder carries purity, dosing, and sterility risks; prefer the approved implant under medical supervision.
- **Ignoring New Side Effects**: Persistent nausea, headache, or rapidly darkening lesions warrant stopping and clinical evaluation.
