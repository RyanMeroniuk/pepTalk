# Melanotan-I (Afamelanotide)

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Other melanocortin agonists (melanotan-ii, pt-141, setmelanotide)**: Overlapping receptor activation increases melanocortin-mediated side effects including nausea, flushing, and hyperpigmentation. Do not combine.
- **Immunotherapy / checkpoint inhibitors for melanoma**: Stimulating melanocytes during melanoma immunotherapy is dangerous and contraindicated.
- **Photosensitizing drugs**: Caution and monitor, as combined effects on UV response and pigmentation are not well characterized.
- **Anticoagulants / blood thinners**: Caution; melanocortin effects on vasculature are incompletely defined — monitor if combined.
- **Contraindicated: Personal or family history of melanoma** — stimulating melanocyte activity may accelerate malignant transformation.
- **Contraindicated: Active skin cancer** of any type.
- **Contraindicated: Pregnancy and breastfeeding** — hormonal and melanocyte-stimulating effects pose unknown risks; safety data are insufficient.
- **Caution: Significant liver disease** — afamelanotide is used in porphyria but requires medical oversight; monitor in hepatic impairment.
