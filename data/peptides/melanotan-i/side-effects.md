# Melanotan-I (Afamelanotide)

## Side Effects

- **Generally Milder Than MT-II**: Greater MC1R selectivity means less nausea, flushing, and sexual side effects than Melanotan-II.
- **Nausea and Flushing**: Can still occur, particularly early in use, though typically less pronounced.
- **Darkening of Moles and Freckles**: Increased melanocyte activity can darken existing moles and freckles and may create new pigmented spots — warranting skin monitoring.
- **Injection/Implant Site Reactions**: Local irritation, redness, or discomfort at the implant or injection site.
- **Headache and Fatigue**: Reported in clinical use of the implant.
- **Melanoma Concern**: Stimulating melanocytes raises theoretical concern in those with melanoma risk; pigmented-lesion changes should be evaluated by a dermatologist.
- **Gray-Market Purity Risks**: Unregulated powders may contain impurities, incorrect dosing, or contamination, adding unpredictable risks.
