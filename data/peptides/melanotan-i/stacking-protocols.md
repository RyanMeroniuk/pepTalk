# Melanotan-I (Afamelanotide)

## Stacking Protocols

- **Limited Stacking Recommended**: As a melanocortin agonist, MT-I is best used cautiously; combining with other receptor-active agents increases unpredictable effects.
- **Skin Quality Support Stack**: Melanotan-I + ghk-cu (1-2 mg daily) — pigmentation support paired with copper-peptide collagen and skin-repair support during melanocyte stimulation.
- **Do NOT Stack with Melanotan-II**: Melanotan-I + melanotan-ii — overlapping melanocortin receptor activation compounds nausea, flushing, and pigmentation side effects.
- **Do NOT Stack with PT-141**: Melanotan-I + pt-141 — shared melanocortin targets produce additive adverse effects; avoid combining.
- **Note**: MT-I is more MC1R-selective than MT-II, but it should still be treated as a standalone melanocortin agent rather than layered with other melanocortin drugs.
