# Melanotan-I (Afamelanotide)

## Mechanisms of Action

- **MC1R Agonism**: Binds and activates the melanocortin-1 receptor on melanocytes, the primary trigger for pigment production.
- **Eumelanin Synthesis**: Drives a shift toward eumelanin (the darker, more photoprotective pigment), increasing skin tanning.
- **Greater Receptor Selectivity**: More selective for MC1R than Melanotan-II, with weaker MC3R/MC4R activity, which underlies its reduced libido, nausea, and flushing effects.
- **UV-Independent Pigmentation**: Stimulates melanogenesis without requiring UV exposure, enabling pigment increase with less sun.
- **Antioxidant / Photoprotective Signaling**: Increased melanin and melanocortin signaling provide some protection against UV-induced oxidative damage, the basis for its EPP indication.
- **Controlled-Release Delivery (pharmaceutical form)**: As an implant, afamelanotide releases steadily over days, maintaining melanocortin signaling without daily injection.
