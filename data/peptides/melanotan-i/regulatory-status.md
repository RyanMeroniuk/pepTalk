# Melanotan-I (Afamelanotide)

## Regulatory Status

- **Afamelanotide (Scenesse) is approved** by the FDA (2019) and EMA for reducing phototoxicity in adults with erythropoietic protoporphyria (EPP).
- The approved product is a prescription subcutaneous controlled-release implant administered by trained healthcare professionals.
- **Tanning/cosmetic use is NOT approved** — using MT-I for cosmetic tanning is off-label and unregulated.
- Gray-market "Melanotan-I" research powder is not approved, not quality-controlled, and sold with disclaimers as "not for human use."
- Several regulators have issued safety warnings about unlicensed melanotan products sold for tanning.
- All non-EPP use should be considered experimental and undertaken only with medical oversight.
