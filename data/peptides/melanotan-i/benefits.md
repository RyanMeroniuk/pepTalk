# Melanotan-I (Afamelanotide)

## Benefits

- **Photoprotection in EPP**: As afamelanotide (Scenesse), increases pain-free light exposure in adults with erythropoietic protoporphyria — its approved medical use.
- **Skin Tanning**: Stimulates eumelanin for darker pigmentation with less UV exposure than natural tanning requires.
- **More Selective, Fewer Side Effects**: Compared with Melanotan-II, it causes less nausea, flushing, and sexual/libido effects due to greater MC1R selectivity.
- **Reduced UV Damage Potential**: Increased melanin may lower UV-induced oxidative stress and sunburn risk, though it is not a substitute for sunscreen.
- **Established Pharmaceutical Track Record**: The regulated implant has clinical safety and efficacy data in its approved indication, unlike gray-market powders.
- **No Daily Injections (implant form)**: The controlled-release implant avoids frequent dosing.
