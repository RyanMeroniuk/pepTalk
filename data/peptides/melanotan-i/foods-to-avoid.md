# Melanotan-I (Afamelanotide)

## Foods to Avoid

- **Excess Alcohol**: Dehydrates skin, increases inflammation, and can worsen nausea or flushing, especially early in use.
- **Refined Sugar & High-Glycemic Foods**: Promote glycation and oxidative stress that undermine skin quality during pigmentation changes.
- **Processed Seed Oils**: Soybean, corn, and canola oils promote omega-6-driven inflammation that can aggravate skin oxidative stress.
- **Fried and Charred Foods**: Generate AGEs and oxidative byproducts that damage skin proteins.
- **Heavy, Greasy Meals When Nauseated**: May worsen MT-I-related nausea; favor lighter meals if nausea occurs.
- **Excess Caffeine and Energy Drinks**: Can compound flushing, jitteriness, or dehydration in some users.
