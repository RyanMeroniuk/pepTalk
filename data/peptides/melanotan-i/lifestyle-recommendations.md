# Melanotan-I (Afamelanotide)

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Regular Skin Checks (Non-Negotiable)**: Because MT-I stimulates melanocytes, perform monthly self-exams and see a dermatologist regularly to monitor moles for changes in size, shape, or color. Photograph pigmented lesions for comparison.
- **Continue Sun Protection**: The induced tan is not full UV protection. Use broad-spectrum SPF 30-50, wear protective clothing, and avoid sunburn.
- **Limit Tanning-Bed Use**: Combining MT-I with intense UV (sun beds) compounds melanoma risk and is strongly discouraged.
- **Monitor Vitamin D**: Reduced UV exposure can lower natural vitamin D production; consider intake from food, supplements, or moderate safe sun.
- **Track Side Effects**: Note nausea, flushing, headache, or new pigmented spots and adjust or discontinue with clinical guidance.
- **Use Regulated Sources**: Prefer the approved implant under medical supervision over gray-market research powder, which carries purity and sterility risks.
- **Hydration and General Skin Health**: Stay hydrated, eat antioxidant-rich foods, and maintain a healthy skin barrier to support skin during pigmentation changes.
- **Avoid in Melanoma Risk**: If you or close family have a melanoma history, avoid MT-I entirely.
