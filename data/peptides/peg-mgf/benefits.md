# PEG-MGF

## Benefits

- **Enhanced muscle repair (theoretical)**: By recruiting satellite cells after training damage, MGF may accelerate recovery and the addition of new myonuclei — demonstrated in models, anecdotal in humans
- **Support for hypertrophy**: New myonuclei increase a fiber's capacity to grow, theoretically supporting longer-term muscle gains when paired with training
- **Longer action than native MGF**: PEGylation extends the half-life from minutes to hours/days, making it more practical than unmodified MGF and requiring fewer injections
- **Synergy with IGF-1 pathway agents**: Conceptually complements IGF-1 LR3 by handling the early satellite-cell-priming step before IGF-1 drives maturation
- **Potential recovery from localized injury**: Some users employ it hoping for site-specific repair, though systemic distribution is expected with PEGylation
- **Important framing**: Human efficacy is unproven; reported benefits are extrapolated from preclinical work and anecdote, not controlled trials
