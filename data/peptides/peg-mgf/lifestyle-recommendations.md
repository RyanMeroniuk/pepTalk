# PEG-MGF

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Train hard enough to create the stimulus**: MGF acts on satellite cells recruited by mechanical load and microtrauma — without challenging resistance training there is little for it to amplify
- **Time around training**: Dosing on training days or post-workout aligns with the natural window of satellite cell activation
- **Prioritize protein**: Aim for 1.6–2.2g/kg bodyweight daily to supply substrate for the repair MGF initiates
- **Sleep 7–9 hours**: Muscle repair and satellite cell activity are sleep- and recovery-dependent
- **Manage training volume**: Allow adequate recovery between sessions so the repair process can complete rather than stacking damage
- **Hydrate well**: Supports cellular function and recovery
- **Conservative cycling**: The research community uses intermittent rather than continuous protocols, reflecting unknown long-term safety and the IGF-axis concern
- **Understand the evidence gap**: Treat any use as experimental; PEG-MGF has no human safety or efficacy validation
