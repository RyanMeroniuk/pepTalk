# PEG-MGF

## Things to Avoid

- **Use with active or prior cancer**: As an IGF-related growth factor, PEG-MGF could promote proliferation of malignant cells — avoid with any history of cancer
- **Chronic continuous dosing**: Long-term stimulation of the IGF axis has unknown safety; avoid open-ended use and prefer intermittent protocols
- **Assuming gray-market product is accurate**: Research-chemical PEG-MGF is frequently underdosed, mislabeled, or contaminated — purity and identity cannot be assumed
- **Use during pregnancy or breastfeeding**: A potent growth factor with no safety data — absolutely avoid
- **Stacking blind with multiple IGF-axis agents**: Combining with IGF-1 LR3, GH, or insulin compounds proliferative and hypoglycemic risks
- **Dosing without a training stimulus**: MGF acts on satellite cells recruited by exercise; without training the rationale collapses
- **Skipping bloodwork**: Without monitoring (including IGF-1 and glucose), metabolic or proliferative effects go undetected
- **Treating preclinical results as human-proven**: Efficacy data is from cell/animal studies; calibrate expectations accordingly
