# PEG-MGF

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Synergistic — Creatine Monohydrate**: Supports cell volumization and muscle protein synthesis alongside MGF-driven satellite cell activity (5g/day)
- **Synergistic — L-Glutamine**: Supports recovery and gut integrity during heavy training when repair demand is high (5g/day)
- **Synergistic — Omega-3 Fatty Acids**: Anti-inflammatory support that may improve the post-training repair environment MGF acts within (2–4g EPA/DHA daily)
- **Enhanced by — Vitamin D3**: IGF-family signaling is partially dependent on adequate vitamin D status; deficiency may blunt response (2,000–5,000 IU/day)
- **Enhanced by — Zinc**: Required for IGF-1 receptor function and protein synthesis (15–30mg/day with copper balance)
- **Enhanced by — Magnesium**: Supports recovery, sleep, and muscle function (300–400mg/day)
- **Critical — protein/EAA intake**: Adequate amino acids are the substrate for the repair MGF initiates; insufficient protein undercuts the entire mechanism
- **Caution — high-dose Curcumin/Berberine**: May modestly interfere with IGF/mTOR signaling at high doses; separate timing if mega-dosing
