# PEG-MGF

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT FDA-approved** for any indication — there is no approved MGF or PEG-MGF drug
- **No completed human efficacy trials**; the supporting data is from cell-culture and animal research
- **Sold only as a "research chemical"** through gray-market suppliers, explicitly not for human use
- **Not a dietary supplement** and not legally marketable for human consumption
- **Quality is unregulated**: purity, sterility, PEGylation integrity, dosing accuracy, and molecular identity are not verified by any authority
- **Banned in sport**: IGF-1 and its variants/releasing peptides are prohibited by WADA at all times
- **Legal status varies by jurisdiction**; possession/import of unapproved injectables can carry legal risk
- **Bottom line**: Experimental, unapproved, and unverified — any use is outside regulated medicine
