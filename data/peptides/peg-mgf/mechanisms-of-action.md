# PEG-MGF

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Satellite cell activation**: MGF's primary action is stimulating quiescent muscle satellite (stem) cells to proliferate, providing new myonuclei for fiber repair and growth
- **IGF-1Ec splice variant**: Derived from the mechano-sensitive C-terminal Ec peptide of IGF-1 that muscle naturally expresses after mechanical load and microtrauma
- **Local repair signaling**: Acts as an early-phase repair signal — distinct from systemic IGF-1, which handles later-phase growth and maturation of the activated cells
- **PEGylation for half-life**: Native MGF is cleared within minutes; attaching polyethylene glycol shields it from rapid degradation, extending its functional half-life to hours/days and allowing less frequent dosing
- **Complementary to IGF-1**: MGF primes the satellite cell pool while IGF-1/IGF-1 LR3 promotes differentiation and protein synthesis — the rationale for pairing them
- **Post-damage timing dependence**: Effects are thought to be greatest in the window following training-induced muscle damage, when satellite cell recruitment is naturally occurring
- **Evidence caveat**: Most mechanistic data is from cell-culture and rodent studies; human pharmacology of PEG-MGF injection is poorly characterized
