# PEG-MGF

## Side Effects

- **IGF-axis cancer concern**: As an IGF-1-related growth factor, chronic use carries a theoretical risk of promoting growth of existing or undetected malignancies — the most important safety consideration
- **Injection-site reactions**: Redness, swelling, irritation, or lumps from subcutaneous injection
- **Hypoglycemia (low risk)**: IGF-family signaling can have mild glucose-lowering effects, though PEG-MGF is far less insulin-like than IGF-1 LR3
- **Headache and lethargy**: Reported anecdotally by some users
- **PEG-related concerns**: Repeated exposure to PEGylated compounds raises theoretical questions about anti-PEG antibody formation and tissue accumulation, though not well characterized for this peptide
- **Tissue overgrowth (theoretical)**: Any potent growth signal carries unquantified risk of unwanted proliferative effects with chronic use
- **Product-related risk**: Contamination, endotoxin, and mislabeling from gray-market sourcing are independent of the molecule itself
- **Evidence caveat**: Human side-effect data is minimal; the above are mechanism-based and anecdotal rather than trial-documented
