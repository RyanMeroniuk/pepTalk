- **Repair & Growth Stack**: PEG-MGF + CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin (100mcg each before bed) — combines local repair signaling with amplified endogenous GH/IGF-1 output
- **Hyperplasia Stack**: PEG-MGF + Follistatin-344 (100mcg/day) — pairs satellite cell activation with myostatin inhibition for a maximally permissive growth environment
- **Recovery Stack**: PEG-MGF + BPC-157 (250mcg 2x/day) + TB-500 (2mg 2x/week) — adds tissue healing and anti-inflammatory support to the repair signal
- **GH Synergy Stack**: PEG-MGF + MK-677 (Ibutamoren, 25mg/day) — oral GH secretagogue raises baseline GH/IGF-1 alongside MGF's local action
- **Note**: All stacks are anecdotal research-community combinations; none are clinically validated, and gray-market quality undermines precision
