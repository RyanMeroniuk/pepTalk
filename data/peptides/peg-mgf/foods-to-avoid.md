# PEG-MGF

## Foods to Avoid

- **Excess alcohol**: Suppresses muscle protein synthesis and impairs recovery, directly undermining MGF's repair signal
- **Added sugars and high-fructose corn syrup**: Promote inflammation and fat gain that work against recomposition goals
- **Ultra-processed foods**: Nutrient-poor and pro-inflammatory, providing little of the protein and micronutrients repair requires
- **Trans fats and fried foods**: Pro-inflammatory and detrimental to recovery
- **Very low-protein diets**: Starve the repair process of amino-acid substrate, negating the mechanism
- **Crash/very low-calorie diets**: Insufficient energy impairs recovery and muscle building
- **Excessive processed-meat intake**: Associated with inflammation; given the theoretical IGF-axis cancer concern, minimizing processed/charred meats is reasonable
