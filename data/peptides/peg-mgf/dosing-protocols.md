# PEG-MGF

## Dosing Protocols

- **No approved or validated dosing**: All figures are anecdotal research-community practice, not clinically supported
- **Common anecdotal dose**: 200–400mcg subcutaneous per injection
- **Typical frequency**: 2–3x per week, exploiting PEGylation's extended half-life (vs daily/multiple-daily dosing required for non-PEGylated MGF)
- **Timing**: Often dosed post-workout on training days, based on the theory that satellite cell recruitment is greatest after muscle damage; some inject on rest days for recovery
- **Reconstitution**: Lyophilized powder reconstituted with bacteriostatic water; the PEGylated form is somewhat more stable than native MGF
- **Localized vs systemic**: Some users inject near a trained/injured muscle hoping for regional effect, but PEGylation favors systemic distribution
- **Storage**: Refrigerate after reconstitution
- **Major caveat**: Gray-market product purity, dosing accuracy, and identity are unverified; precise dosing is illusory with unverified source material
