# PEG-MGF

## Foods to Eat

- **Lean protein (chicken, turkey, lean beef)**: Supplies amino acids for the muscle repair MGF initiates
- **Eggs (pasture-raised)**: Complete protein rich in leucine to drive muscle protein synthesis
- **Wild-caught salmon and mackerel**: Omega-3 fatty acids reduce inflammation and support the post-training repair environment
- **Greek yogurt and cottage cheese**: Slow-digesting casein for sustained amino acid delivery, useful around rest and sleep
- **Sweet potatoes, rice, and oats**: Quality carbohydrates to replenish glycogen and fuel training that creates the MGF stimulus
- **Leafy greens and colorful vegetables**: Micronutrients and antioxidants that support recovery
- **Berries**: Polyphenols and vitamin C that aid recovery and connective tissue
- **Nuts and seeds (almonds, pumpkin seeds)**: Magnesium, zinc, and healthy fats supporting recovery
- **Lentils and beans**: Plant protein and fiber to support overall protein intake and gut health
