# PEG-MGF

## Drug Interactions

- **Active cancer / chemotherapy**: Contraindicated — as an IGF-related growth factor, PEG-MGF may promote cell proliferation and interfere with cytotoxic cancer treatment
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding**: Contraindicated — a potent growth factor with unknown effects on fetal development and lactation
- **Insulin and sulfonylureas**: Caution — IGF-family signaling can lower blood glucose; combining may additively increase hypoglycemia risk, so monitor glucose (risk is lower than with IGF-1 LR3)
- **Corticosteroids (prednisone, dexamethasone)**: Caution — catabolic and oppose the repair/anabolic intent; may blunt benefits, monitor
- **Beta-blockers**: Caution — may mask hypoglycemia symptoms (tremor, rapid heart rate) if any glucose-lowering occurs
- **Thyroid medications (levothyroxine, T3/T4)**: Monitor — thyroid status modulates IGF sensitivity and metabolic rate, which can alter response
- **Anticoagulants/blood thinners**: Caution — injectable use increases bruising/bleeding risk at injection sites; monitor
- **General caveat**: No formal human drug-interaction studies exist; all listed interactions are mechanism-based precautions, not documented clinical findings
