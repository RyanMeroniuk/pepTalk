# Dihexa

## Mechanisms of Action

- **HGF/c-Met Pathway**: Activates hepatocyte growth factor and its receptor c-Met in the brain
- **Synaptogenesis**: Promotes formation of entirely new synaptic connections between neurons
- **Neurogenesis**: May stimulate growth of new neurons
- **BDNF-Like Effects**: Mimics brain-derived neurotrophic factor but at dramatically higher potency
- **Blood-Brain Barrier Penetration**: Crosses BBB when taken orally — rare for peptides
- **Anti-Neurodegenerative**: Counteracts synaptic degradation in disease models
