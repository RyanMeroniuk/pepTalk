# Dihexa

## Foods to Eat

- **Wild-Caught Fatty Fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel)**: DHA omega-3 is the primary structural fat in neuronal membranes. New synapses formed by Dihexa require DHA for healthy membrane construction. Eat fatty fish 3-4x/week during active cycles
- **Eggs (whole, pastured)**: Rich in choline (precursor to acetylcholine) and DHA. New synaptic connections require neurotransmitter support, and acetylcholine is critical for memory and learning
- **Blueberries & Dark Berries**: Anthocyanins are among the few antioxidants that cross the blood-brain barrier, reducing neuroinflammation and supporting the survival of newly formed synapses
- **Walnuts**: Rich in ALA omega-3s, polyphenols, and vitamin E — support cerebrovascular health and provide antioxidant protection for new neural tissue
- **Cruciferous Vegetables (broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts)**: Contain sulforaphane, which activates Nrf2 antioxidant pathways and reduces oxidative stress in the brain — protecting newly formed synapses
- **Dark Chocolate (85%+ cacao)**: Flavonoids improve cerebral blood flow (delivering nutrients to new synapses) and independently support BDNF expression
- **Leafy Greens (spinach, kale)**: Folate, lutein, and nitrates support brain blood flow and the methylation pathways needed for neurogenesis
- **Turmeric/Curcumin (with black pepper for absorption)**: Potent anti-neuroinflammatory compound that crosses the BBB, supporting the health of newly formed neural connections
- **Liver & Organ Meats**: Highest bioavailable source of B12, folate, choline, and iron — all essential cofactors for neuronal growth and myelination
- **Avocado**: Monounsaturated fats support myelin sheath formation around new axonal connections
