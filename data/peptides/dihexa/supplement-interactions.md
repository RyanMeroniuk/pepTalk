# Dihexa

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Stack**: Semax for complementary nootropic mechanisms
- **Caution**: Other HGF/c-Met activating compounds
- **Caution**: Cancer history (c-Met pathway involvement in tumor growth)
- **Contraindicated**: Active cancer
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy/breastfeeding
- **Use with extreme caution**: Very limited human safety data
