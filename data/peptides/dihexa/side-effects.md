# Dihexa

## Side Effects

- **Very limited human safety data** — most evidence is preclinical
- Headache reported by some users
- Potential for overstimulation
- **Theoretical cancer concern**: HGF/c-Met pathway is involved in cell proliferation and is implicated in some cancers
- Long-term effects completely unknown
- One of the riskier peptides due to lack of human clinical data
