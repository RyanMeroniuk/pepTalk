# Dihexa

## Things to Avoid

- **Exceeding Recommended Doses**: Dihexa's extreme potency (10 million times more potent than BDNF) means that more is absolutely not better. Higher doses increase unknown risks without proportional cognitive benefit. Respect the dose ceiling
- **Extended Cycles Without Breaks**: Long-term sustained HGF/c-Met activation is unstudied in humans. The c-Met pathway is involved in cell proliferation and is implicated in some cancers. Short cycles with adequate off-periods are essential for safety
- **Ignoring the Cancer Risk**: The HGF/c-Met pathway plays a role in tumor growth and metastasis. Anyone with a personal or family history of cancer should either avoid Dihexa entirely or consult an oncologist before use
- **Combining with Other Potent Nootropics Without Caution**: Stacking Dihexa with other strong neurotropic compounds (Semax, Selank, racetams) simultaneously may produce unpredictable neurological effects. Introduce one compound at a time
- **Sleep Deprivation**: New synapses are consolidated during deep sleep. Depriving the brain of sleep during a Dihexa cycle wastes its most expensive benefit and may lead to dysfunctional neural pruning
- **Sedentary Behavior**: Physical inactivity reduces cerebral blood flow and BDNF levels. Dihexa's synaptogenic effects are activity-dependent — without cognitive and physical activity, new connections lack direction and purpose
- **Self-Experimenting Without Records**: Given the extremely limited human data, treating Dihexa use casually is unwise. Track doses, timing, cognitive changes, mood, sleep, and any physical symptoms meticulously
- **Sourcing from Unverified Suppliers**: Dihexa's potency means that purity matters enormously. Contaminants at even small percentages could have significant neurological effects. Source only from suppliers with third-party testing (COA) and verified purity
