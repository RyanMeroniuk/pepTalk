# Dihexa

## Dosing Protocols

- **Oral/Sublingual**: 10-20mg daily (common research dose)
- **Some protocols**: 5-40mg daily range
- **Timing**: Morning or early afternoon (cognitive effects)
- **Cycle**: Short cycles recommended (2-4 weeks on, 2-4 weeks off) due to limited safety data
- **No injection needed**: Oral bioavailability is a key advantage
