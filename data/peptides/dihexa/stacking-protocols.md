# Dihexa

## Stacking Protocols

- **Comprehensive Nootropic Stack**: Dihexa (10-20mg/day oral) + Semax (300-600mcg/day intranasal) — Dihexa drives synaptogenesis via HGF/c-Met pathway while Semax upregulates BDNF through melanocortin pathways. Complementary neurotropic mechanisms for cognitive enhancement. Introduce one at a time; do not start both simultaneously
- **Calm Focus & Cognition Stack**: Dihexa (10-20mg/day) + Selank (250-500mcg/day intranasal) — Dihexa for synapse formation and cognitive power, Selank for anxiolytic GABA modulation and calm focus. Selank's anti-anxiety effect balances any overstimulation from Dihexa's potent neurotropic activity
- **Neuroprotection & Repair Stack**: Dihexa (10-20mg/day) + BPC-157 (250-500mcg/day) — Dihexa promotes new synapse and neuron formation while BPC-157 provides neuroprotective and neuroregenerative support through distinct mechanisms (nitric oxide system, GH receptor interaction). Relevant for cognitive recovery from brain injury or neurodegeneration
- **Full Cognitive Enhancement Stack**: Dihexa (10mg/day, low end) + Semax (300mcg/day intranasal) + Lion's Mane Extract (1000mg/day) + DHA Omega-3 (2g/day) — multi-pathway cognitive support: Dihexa for synaptogenesis, Semax for BDNF, Lion's Mane for nerve growth factor (NGF), and DHA for neuronal membrane building blocks. Use lowest effective doses when combining
- **Neurorecovery Stack**: Dihexa (10-20mg/day, short cycle) + Pinealon (10mg/day sublingual) + Magnesium L-Threonate (2g/day) — Dihexa for synaptogenesis, Pinealon for neuroprotection and circadian support, magnesium L-threonate (the only form shown to cross BBB) for synaptic plasticity support
