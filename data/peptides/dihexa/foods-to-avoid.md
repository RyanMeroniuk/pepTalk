# Dihexa

## Foods to Avoid

- **Alcohol (strictly avoid during cycles)**: Directly neurotoxic and impairs synaptogenesis. Given Dihexa's potency and the lack of human safety data, combining with any neurotoxin is irresponsible
- **Processed Seed Oils (soybean, canola, corn, sunflower)**: High omega-6 content promotes neuroinflammation, which can damage newly formed synapses and impair the HGF/c-Met signaling pathway
- **Refined Sugar & High-Glycemic Foods**: Blood sugar instability causes neuroinflammation and oxidative stress, threatening the survival of newly formed neural connections
- **Ultra-Processed Foods**: Contain additives and inflammatory compounds that damage the gut-brain axis and promote systemic inflammation
- **Trans Fats & Fried Foods**: Directly impair synaptic plasticity and increase oxidative stress in neural tissue
- **Excess Caffeine**: While moderate caffeine is acceptable, excess stimulation may cause anxiety or overstimulation when combined with Dihexa's potent neurological effects
- **High-Mercury Fish (swordfish, king mackerel, tilefish)**: Mercury is a potent neurotoxin. When actively promoting synaptogenesis, protecting new neural tissue from heavy metal exposure is critical
