# Dihexa

## Benefits

- **Cognitive Enhancement**: Potent memory and learning improvement
- **Synapse Formation**: New neural connections — not just preservation of existing ones
- **Alzheimer's/Parkinson's**: May reverse cognitive deficits in animal models of neurodegeneration
- **Oral Availability**: Can be taken as a capsule/sublingual — no injection needed
- **Extreme Potency**: Active at very low doses
