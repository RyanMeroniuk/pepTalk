# Dihexa

## Overview

Dihexa (N-hexanoic-Tyr-Ile-(6) aminohexanoic amide) is a synthetic peptide derivative of angiotensin IV, developed at Washington State University. It is one of the most potent nootropic peptides ever discovered — reported to be approximately 10 million times more potent than BDNF at forming new neural connections. Orally active (crosses the blood-brain barrier). Primarily researched for neurodegenerative conditions including Alzheimer's and Parkinson's disease. Promotes new synapse formation (synaptogenesis), not just neuroprotection.
