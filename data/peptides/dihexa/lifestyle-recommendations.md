# Dihexa

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Prioritize Sleep (8+ hours)**: Dihexa promotes synaptogenesis — the formation of entirely new synaptic connections. These new synapses are consolidated and strengthened exclusively during deep sleep. Without adequate sleep, new connections are pruned rather than preserved, wasting Dihexa's primary benefit
- **Aerobic Exercise (30-45 min, 5x/week)**: Exercise independently activates the HGF/c-Met pathway (the same pathway Dihexa targets) and raises BDNF levels. This creates a powerful synergy — exercise primes the brain for synaptogenesis, and Dihexa amplifies the process
- **Deliberate Learning During Active Cycles**: Dihexa's synaptogenic effects are activity-dependent — new synapses form in response to cognitive demand. During active cycles, engage intensively in learning: new languages, instruments, complex problem-solving, or skill acquisition. This directs synapse formation toward useful neural circuits
- **Careful Dose Tracking**: Dihexa is approximately 10 million times more potent than BDNF at forming neural connections. This extreme potency demands rigorous dose tracking. Use a precise milligram scale, log every dose, and never estimate. Start at the lowest effective dose (5-10mg) and increase only with careful observation
- **Meditation & Mindfulness (15-20 min daily)**: Mindfulness increases hippocampal gray matter and supports the neurogenesis pathways Dihexa activates. It also helps develop awareness of subtle cognitive changes — important for monitoring Dihexa's powerful effects
- **Limit Alcohol Completely During Cycles**: Alcohol is directly neurotoxic and impairs synaptogenesis. Given Dihexa's potency and the limited safety data available, introducing any neurotoxin during active cycles is counterproductive and potentially risky
- **Regular Health Monitoring**: Due to Dihexa's activation of the HGF/c-Met pathway (which is involved in cell proliferation), consider regular health checkups and blood work. This is especially important given the limited human safety data
- **Stress Management**: Chronic stress elevates cortisol, which is neurotoxic to the hippocampus and impairs the formation of new neural connections. Manage stress actively through exercise, meditation, nature exposure, or therapy
- **Journaling Cognitive Changes**: Given Dihexa's extreme potency and limited human data, keep a daily log of cognitive function, mood, sleep quality, and any unusual symptoms. This helps optimize dosing and detect issues early
- **Limit Cycle Duration**: Stick strictly to short cycles (2-4 weeks) with equal off-periods. The long-term effects of sustained HGF/c-Met activation are unknown, and caution is warranted with this level of potency
