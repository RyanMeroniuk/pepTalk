# AOD-9604

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Minimal Known Drug Interactions**: AOD-9604 has a favorable interaction profile. It does not significantly affect IGF-1, insulin, blood glucose, or major hormonal axes, which limits most drug interaction concerns
- **Diabetes Medications (Insulin, Metformin, Sulfonylureas)**: While AOD-9604 does not directly affect blood glucose, its fat-metabolizing effects may modestly improve insulin sensitivity over time. Monitor blood glucose if on diabetes medications, as dose adjustments may eventually be needed
- **Thyroid Medications**: No established direct interaction, but significant fat loss and metabolic changes can alter thyroid hormone requirements. Monitor TSH during extended AOD-9604 cycles, especially if on levothyroxine
- **Growth Hormone Therapy**: Combining with exogenous GH or strong GH secretagogues may have overlapping effects on fat metabolism. AOD-9604 was designed to isolate GH's fat-burning fragment — combining with full GH therapy is redundant for fat loss and introduces the IGF-1 risks AOD-9604 was designed to avoid
- **Anticoagulants**: No known interaction, but as with any injectable peptide, use caution with injection site bleeding if on blood thinners
- **Pregnancy / Breastfeeding**: Contraindicated due to insufficient safety data. Fat-mobilizing agents are not appropriate during pregnancy or lactation
- **Active Cancer**: While AOD-9604 does not raise IGF-1 (unlike full GH), use caution in patients with active malignancies. Insufficient data to confirm safety in oncology patients
- **Children / Adolescents**: No pediatric safety data. Not recommended for use in individuals under 18
