# AOD-9604

## Foods to Avoid

- **Processed and Ultra-Processed Foods**: Chips, packaged snacks, frozen dinners, fast food, deli meats, hot dogs. These contain inflammatory seed oils and additives that impair fat metabolism and promote fat storage
- **Refined Sugars and Sweets**: Candy, pastries, ice cream, sugary cereals, fruit juice, regular soda. Sugar spikes insulin, which directly inhibits fat oxidation — counteracting AOD-9604's mechanism
- **Refined Carbohydrates**: White bread, white pasta, white rice, bagels, crackers. Cause rapid blood sugar spikes and insulin surges that block fat burning
- **Alcohol**: Slows fat metabolism, adds empty calories, disrupts sleep quality, and elevates cortisol. Even moderate intake can significantly reduce AOD-9604's effectiveness
- **Trans Fats and Seed Oils**: Margarine, vegetable shortening, deep-fried foods, many packaged baked goods. Promote inflammation and impair metabolic function
- **Excessive Dairy**: Full-fat cheese, cream, whole milk in large amounts. Can be inflammatory for some individuals and calorie-dense
- **Sugary Beverages**: Soda, fruit juice, sweetened coffee drinks, energy drinks. Liquid calories spike insulin and provide no satiety
