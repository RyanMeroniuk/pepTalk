# AOD-9604

## Foods to Eat

- **Lean Proteins**: Chicken breast, turkey breast, salmon, cod, tilapia, eggs, egg whites, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, lean ground turkey, tofu, tempeh, lentils
- **Metabolism-Supporting Foods**: Green tea (catechins support fat oxidation), cayenne pepper (capsaicin boosts thermogenesis), apple cider vinegar, ginger, turmeric
- **Healthy Fats**: Avocado, extra virgin olive oil, almonds, walnuts, chia seeds, flaxseed, fatty fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel) — supports hormone function and reduces inflammation
- **Complex Carbohydrates**: Sweet potatoes, quinoa, oats, brown rice, lentils, chickpeas, black beans — provide steady energy for workouts
- **Fibrous Vegetables**: Broccoli, spinach, kale, bell peppers, asparagus, Brussels sprouts, cauliflower, zucchini — nutrient-dense and support gut health
- **Low-Sugar Fruits**: Berries (blueberries, strawberries, raspberries), grapefruit, green apples, kiwi
- **Anti-Inflammatory Foods**: Fatty fish, turmeric, ginger, extra virgin olive oil, leafy greens, berries, walnuts
