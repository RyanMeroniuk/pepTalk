# AOD-9604

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Beta-3 Adrenergic Receptor Activation**: Activates fat cell receptors to enhance fat oxidation
- **Lipolysis Stimulation**: Breaks down stored fat into usable energy
- **GH Fragment**: Mimics the fat-metabolizing region of growth hormone only
- **No IGF-1 Elevation**: Does not raise insulin-like growth factor 1 (key safety advantage)
- **No GH Effects**: Does not produce muscle growth, bone effects, or other GH-mediated changes
- **Lipogenesis Inhibition**: May inhibit new fat cell formation
- **Dose-Dependent**: Results improve with calorie-controlled diet and exercise
