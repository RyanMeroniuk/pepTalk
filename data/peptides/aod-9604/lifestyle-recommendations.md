# AOD-9604

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Caloric Deficit is Required**: AOD-9604 enhances fat metabolism but will NOT produce meaningful results without a calorie-controlled diet. It is a fat-loss support tool, not a magic bullet. Aim for a moderate deficit of 300-500 calories below maintenance
- **Morning Fasted Injection Protocol**: Inject AOD-9604 first thing in the morning on an empty stomach. Wait 20-30 minutes before eating. Fasting state optimizes absorption and aligns with natural morning cortisol-driven fat mobilization
- **Exercise is Essential — Both Cardio and Resistance**: Combine moderate cardio (30-45 min, 4-5x/week — brisk walking, cycling, swimming) with resistance training (3-4x/week). AOD-9604 enhances fat oxidation during exercise, so physical activity amplifies results significantly
- **Daily Step Count**: Aim for 8,000-10,000 steps per day. Consistent low-level activity keeps fat metabolism elevated throughout the day
- **Sleep 7-9 Hours**: Sleep deprivation raises cortisol, which promotes fat storage (especially visceral fat) and directly opposes AOD-9604's fat-burning mechanism. Poor sleep also increases hunger hormones and impairs insulin sensitivity
- **Stress Management**: Chronic stress elevates cortisol, which promotes abdominal fat storage — the exact type of fat AOD-9604 targets. Meditation, deep breathing, yoga, and nature walks help keep cortisol in check
- **Track Body Composition, Not Just Weight**: AOD-9604 targets fat specifically without affecting muscle. Use waist measurements, body fat calipers, or bioimpedance scales to track progress. Scale weight may not change dramatically even as body composition improves
- **Stay Hydrated**: Drink 64-80oz of water daily. Adequate hydration supports fat metabolism and overall cellular function
- **Anti-Inflammatory Lifestyle**: Chronic inflammation impairs fat metabolism. Prioritize anti-inflammatory foods, adequate sleep, stress reduction, and regular movement
- **Cycling Protocol**: Follow 8-12 weeks on, 4 weeks off to prevent receptor desensitization and maintain efficacy
