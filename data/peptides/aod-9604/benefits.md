# AOD-9604

## Benefits

- **Targeted Fat Loss**: Specifically targets fat metabolism without broader GH effects
- **No IGF-1 Risk**: Safer metabolic profile than full GH therapy
- **Joint Health**: Some evidence of cartilage repair and joint support
- **Minimal Systemic Effects**: Does not affect blood sugar, muscle, or bone like GH
- **Well-Tolerated**: No significant adverse effects at doses up to 1mg/day in human trials
