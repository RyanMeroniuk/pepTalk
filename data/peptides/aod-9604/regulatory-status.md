# AOD-9604

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT FDA-approved** for any indication
- Phase III trials showed inconsistent efficacy — development terminated 2007
- No authority recognizes it as an active treatment for obesity
- Designated strictly for research use
- Available from compounding pharmacies and research suppliers
- TGA (Australian) granted it GRAS (generally recognized as safe) status for food use
- **Banned by WADA**
