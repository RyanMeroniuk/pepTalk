# AOD-9604

## Things to Avoid

- **Eating Before or Immediately After Injection**: Inject on an empty stomach in the morning and wait 20-30 minutes before eating. Food in the stomach impairs absorption and reduces efficacy
- **Sedentary Lifestyle**: AOD-9604 enhances fat metabolism during physical activity. Without exercise, results are minimal. It is not a substitute for movement
- **Chronic Stress Without Management**: Elevated cortisol promotes visceral fat accumulation and directly counteracts the fat-burning effects of AOD-9604. Active stress management is part of the protocol
- **Poor Sleep Habits**: Sleeping less than 6 hours consistently raises cortisol, increases hunger, and impairs fat oxidation. Prioritize sleep as part of your fat-loss strategy
- **Exceeding the Dose Ceiling**: More than 500mcg/day does not produce significantly better fat loss. Higher doses increase risk without benefit
- **Skipping the Off-Cycle**: Continuous use without cycling (8-12 weeks on, 4 weeks off) may lead to receptor desensitization and diminishing results
- **Expecting Results Without Diet Control**: AOD-9604 will not overcome a caloric surplus. You must be in a moderate caloric deficit for fat loss to occur
- **Relying on Scale Weight Alone**: AOD-9604 targets fat without affecting muscle mass. Body composition may improve even when scale weight stays stable — track measurements and body fat percentage
