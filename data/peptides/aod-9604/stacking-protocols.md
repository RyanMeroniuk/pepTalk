# AOD-9604

## Stacking Protocols

- **GH Fragment + GH Secretagogue Stack**: AOD-9604 (300-500mcg/day, morning fasted) + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin (100mcg/100mcg before bed) — AOD-9604 provides targeted fat metabolism without raising IGF-1, while CJC-1295/Ipamorelin stimulates natural GH release for muscle preservation and recovery. Complementary mechanisms: fat loss (AOD) + lean mass support (CJC/Ipa)
- **Triple Fat Loss Stack**: AOD-9604 (300-500mcg/day) + MOTS-c (5mg 3x/week) + 5-Amino-1MQ (50-100mg/day oral) — three distinct fat-loss mechanisms: AOD-9604 activates beta-3 adrenergic fat burning, MOTS-c acts as a mitochondrial exercise mimetic, and 5-Amino-1MQ inhibits NNMT to boost fat cell energy expenditure. No overlapping receptor pathways
- **Body Recomposition Stack**: AOD-9604 (300-500mcg/day, morning) + Tesamorelin (1-2mg/day) — dual approach to visceral fat: AOD-9604 targets general fat metabolism via GH fragment pathway while tesamorelin (FDA-approved GHRH analog) specifically reduces visceral adipose tissue. Tesamorelin does raise IGF-1, so monitor accordingly
- **Fat Loss + Joint Support Stack**: AOD-9604 (300-500mcg/day) + BPC-157 (250-500mcg/day) + TB-500 (2mg 2x/week) — AOD-9604 for fat metabolism with BPC-157 and TB-500 providing joint and tissue repair support, useful for individuals starting exercise programs alongside their fat loss protocol
- **Metabolic Optimization Stack**: AOD-9604 (300-500mcg/day) + Berberine (500mg 2x/day) + L-Carnitine (2g/day) — AOD-9604 enhances fat oxidation, berberine improves insulin sensitivity and activates AMPK, L-carnitine shuttles fatty acids into mitochondria for burning. Complementary fat metabolism support
