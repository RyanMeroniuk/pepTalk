# Humanin

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Chemotherapy**: Use is contraindicated — humanin's anti-apoptotic, cytoprotective signaling could theoretically shield cancer cells from treatment-induced death; do not combine.
- **Metformin and Antidiabetics**: Humanin influences insulin sensitivity and glucose handling; monitor blood glucose closely if combined, as additive effects are possible.
- **Insulin**: Monitor for hypoglycemia given humanin's metabolic signaling in preclinical models.
- **Corticosteroids**: Steroids alter immune and metabolic signaling that may overlap with humanin's pathways; monitor and use caution.
- **Anticoagulants / Blood Thinners**: No established interaction; standard injection-site caution applies, monitor for bruising.
- **Contraindicated**: Active cancer (anti-apoptotic mechanism may protect malignant cells).
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy and breastfeeding (no safety data).
- **Caution**: Liver disease or kidney disease — clearance and safety are uncharacterized.
