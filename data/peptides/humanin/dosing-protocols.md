# Humanin

## Dosing Protocols

- **No Established Human Protocol**: There are no validated human dosing guidelines — all dosing is extrapolated from animal studies.
- **Research Form**: The synthetic HNG analog is far more potent than native humanin and is the form typically studied.
- **Anecdotal Range**: Biohacker protocols cite roughly 1–5mg subcutaneous, but this is unvalidated and not evidence-based.
- **Route**: Subcutaneous injection in most anecdotal use; intranasal and intracerebroventricular routes used in some animal neuroprotection studies.
- **Frequency**: Anecdotally daily or several times weekly; no standard exists.
- **Half-Life**: Short in circulation; HNG is engineered for greater stability and potency.
- **Reconstitution**: Bacteriostatic water; refrigerate after reconstitution.
- **Storage**: Refrigerate and use reconstituted peptide within approximately 1 month.
- **Note**: Treat any dosing as experimental — human pharmacokinetics and safe dose ranges are unknown.
