# Humanin

## Side Effects

- Injection site reactions (redness, irritation) — expected with any subcutaneous peptide.
- Unknown human side-effect profile — no controlled human safety data exists.
- Theoretical risk from broad anti-apoptotic action (see Things to Avoid).
- Potential immune or allergic reaction to research-grade material of variable purity.
- **No long-term human safety data** — virtually all evidence is preclinical.
- **Highly experimental** — effects in humans are not characterized.
