# Humanin

## Foods to Eat

- **Omega-3 Rich Fish**: Wild salmon, sardines, mackerel — DHA/EPA support neural membranes and complement humanin's neuroprotective focus.
- **Polyphenol-Rich Foods**: Blueberries, dark chocolate (85%+), green tea, pomegranate, extra virgin olive oil — reduce oxidative stress on neurons and mitochondria.
- **Dark Leafy Greens**: Spinach, kale, Swiss chard — provide magnesium and folate for neural and mitochondrial function.
- **Cruciferous Vegetables**: Broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts — sulforaphane supports cellular antioxidant defenses.
- **Niacin (B3) Sources**: Chicken, tuna, turkey, mushrooms — support NAD+ for mitochondrial energetics.
- **Nuts and Seeds**: Walnuts, almonds, flaxseed, chia — healthy fats and magnesium for neural support.
- **Berries and Colorful Produce**: Antioxidant-dense foods that lower the oxidative load humanin counters.
- **Quality Protein**: Eggs, fish, poultry, legumes — amino acids for tissue maintenance and repair.
