# Humanin

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Anti-Apoptotic Signaling**: Binds and inhibits pro-apoptotic Bax and BimEL proteins, preventing them from triggering mitochondrial-mediated cell death.
- **Receptor Activation**: Signals through a trimeric receptor complex (CNTFR/WSX-1/gp130) and the FPRL1/FPR2 receptor to activate cell-survival pathways.
- **STAT3 Activation**: Triggers the JAK/STAT3 survival signaling cascade, promoting cytoprotective gene expression.
- **IGF-Binding Modulation**: Interacts with IGFBP-3, linking humanin to insulin-like growth factor and metabolic signaling.
- **Oxidative Stress Defense**: Reduces ROS-induced damage and protects neurons and other cells under oxidative or ischemic stress.
- **Mito-Nuclear Communication**: As a mitokine, conveys mitochondrial status to the rest of the cell and body, analogous to MOTS-c.
- **Metabolic Signaling**: Improves insulin sensitivity and influences glucose handling in preclinical models.
- **Neuroprotection**: Protects neurons from amyloid-beta toxicity in Alzheimer's models, the context of its original discovery.
- **Anti-Inflammatory Effect**: Dampens inflammatory signaling in vascular and neural tissue in animal studies.
