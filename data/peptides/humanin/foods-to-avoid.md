# Humanin

## Foods to Avoid

- **Excessive Alcohol**: Neurotoxic and damaging to mitochondria, directly opposing humanin's cytoprotective and neuroprotective mechanisms.
- **Refined Sugars**: Candy, soda, pastries — promote glycation, insulin resistance, and oxidative stress that strain neurons and mitochondria.
- **Ultra-Processed Foods**: Fast food, packaged snacks, deli meats — carry inflammatory additives that raise oxidative and neuroinflammatory burden.
- **Trans Fats and Oxidized Seed Oils**: Repeatedly fried oils and hydrogenated fats promote inflammation and membrane damage.
- **High-Fructose Corn Syrup**: Drives metabolic dysfunction and oxidative stress.
- **Charred Meats**: Form advanced glycation end-products that increase cellular oxidative stress.
- **Excess Caffeine Late in Day**: Disrupts the deep sleep needed for neural and mitochondrial repair.
