# Humanin

## Things to Avoid

- **Treating It as Proven**: Humanin is highly experimental with essentially no human efficacy or safety trials — do not assume benefits seen in cells or mice translate to people.
- **Use With Active or Suspected Cancer**: Humanin's anti-apoptotic action could theoretically help malignant cells evade programmed death; avoid entirely with active cancer.
- **Unregulated Sourcing**: Research peptides vary widely in purity; impure HNG or humanin risks contamination and unpredictable effects.
- **Smoking and Vaping**: Tobacco-driven oxidative and neural damage opposes humanin's protective mechanisms.
- **Chronic Sleep Deprivation**: Impairs neural and mitochondrial repair, undermining humanin's proposed roles.
- **Excessive Alcohol**: Neurotoxic and mitochondrially damaging — avoid around any use.
- **Blind Stacking**: Combining with other experimental peptides without monitoring carries unknown interaction risk.
- **Expecting Defined Outcomes**: Human pharmacokinetics and effective doses are unknown; any response is unpredictable.
