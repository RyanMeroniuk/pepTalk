# Humanin

## Stacking Protocols

- **Mitokine Longevity Stack**: Humanin (HNG, 1–5mg) + MOTS-c (5mg 2x/week) + NMN (500mg/day) — combines two mitochondrial-derived peptides with NAD+ support; experimental and unvalidated in humans.
- **Neuroprotection Stack**: Humanin (HNG, 1–5mg) + semax (300–600mcg intranasal) + omega-3 (3g/day EPA/DHA) — pairs cytoprotection with a nootropic peptide and neural lipid support.
- **Mitochondrial Support Stack**: Humanin (HNG, 1–5mg) + CoQ10 (200mg) + alpha-lipoic-acid (600mg/day) — adds electron-chain cofactor and antioxidant support.
- **Cellular Resilience Stack**: Humanin (HNG, 1–5mg) + epitalon (1mg/day, 10–20 day cycle) + resveratrol (250mg/day) — targets cytoprotection, telomeres, and sirtuin pathways.
- **Cognitive Stack**: Humanin (HNG, 1–5mg) + selank (250–500mcg intranasal) + magnesium (400mg/day) — combines neuroprotection with anxiolytic and cofactor support.
