# Humanin

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Synergistic**: MOTS-c — a fellow mitochondrial-derived peptide; pairing two mitokines is a common longevity-research concept, though combined human data is absent.
- **Synergistic**: CoQ10 (100–200mg) — supports the mitochondrial electron transport chain humanin signaling relates to.
- **Synergistic**: NMN (250–500mg) — replenishes NAD+ for mitochondrial energetics.
- **Enhanced by**: Omega-3 (2–3g EPA/DHA) — neuroprotective and anti-inflammatory support complementing humanin's proposed neural benefits.
- **Enhanced by**: Alpha-lipoic-acid (300–600mg) — mitochondrial antioxidant support.
- **Enhanced by**: Magnesium (300–400mg) — broad cellular and neural cofactor support.
- **Enhanced by**: Vitamin-d3 (2000–5000 IU) — supports neural and immune health.
- **Caution**: Avoid stacking with other unvalidated experimental peptides without monitoring — interactions are unknown.
- **Contraindicated**: Active cancer (anti-apoptotic signaling could theoretically protect malignant cells).
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy/breastfeeding.
