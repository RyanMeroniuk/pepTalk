# Humanin

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT FDA-approved** for any indication.
- No completed human therapeutic clinical trials for humanin or its HNG analog.
- Evidence base is overwhelmingly preclinical — cell culture and rodent studies.
- Human data is limited to observational/correlational studies linking circulating humanin to aging and longevity.
- First identified in 2001 from Alzheimer's-affected brain tissue; remains a research molecule.
- Available only from research peptide suppliers as a research-use compound.
- No established human pharmacokinetics, dosing, or long-term safety profile.
- Active area of mitochondrial-derived peptide and longevity research (2025–2026).
