# Humanin

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Exercise Raises Endogenous Mitokines**: Physical activity naturally increases mitochondrial-derived peptides like humanin and MOTS-c. Regular aerobic and resistance training supports the same pathways from within. Aim for 3–5 sessions per week.
- **Prioritize Sleep**: Quality sleep supports neural repair and mitochondrial maintenance, aligning with humanin's proposed neuroprotective role. Target 7–9 hours.
- **Anti-Inflammatory Diet**: Chronic inflammation drives the oxidative and apoptotic stress humanin counteracts. Emphasize whole foods, fatty fish, and polyphenols.
- **Cognitive Engagement**: Mental stimulation and learning support neural resilience, complementing humanin's neuroprotective interest.
- **Limit Alcohol**: Alcohol is neurotoxic and damages mitochondria, opposing humanin's cytoprotective mechanisms.
- **Manage Metabolic Health**: Maintaining insulin sensitivity through diet and movement aligns with humanin's metabolic signaling role.
- **Stress Management**: Chronic cortisol elevation harms neurons and mitochondria; incorporate meditation, breathing, or nature exposure.
- **Track Subjectively**: Without validated biomarkers for non-clinical use, monitor energy, cognition, and recovery to gauge any response.
- **Caloric and Fasting Cues**: Mild metabolic stress from time-restricted eating naturally engages mitochondrial stress-response pathways.
