# Humanin

## Benefits

- **Neuroprotection**: Protects neurons from amyloid-beta and oxidative toxicity in Alzheimer's and stroke models; the most studied benefit, but preclinical only.
- **Cytoprotection**: Broadly shields cells from apoptosis under stress, including cardiac and retinal tissue in animal studies.
- **Metabolic Regulation**: Improves insulin sensitivity and glucose handling in rodent models, paralleling its mitokine sibling MOTS-c.
- **Cardiovascular Protection**: Reduced atherosclerosis and protected heart tissue from ischemia-reperfusion injury in preclinical work.
- **Longevity Association**: Higher circulating humanin correlates with longevity in some human cohorts and centenarian offspring; levels decline with age.
- **Anti-Inflammatory**: Lowers inflammatory markers in vascular and neural tissue in animal studies.
- **Cellular Stress Resilience**: Enhances survival of cells exposed to toxins, hypoxia, and oxidative stress.
- **Highly Experimental**: All proposed human benefits are extrapolated from cell and animal data — no controlled human efficacy trials exist.
