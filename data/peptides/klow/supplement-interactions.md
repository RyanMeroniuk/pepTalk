# KLOW Stack

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Synergistic**: L-glutamine — critical partner for the gut-healing focus driven by the BPC-157 and KPV components
- **Synergistic**: Collagen peptides (10-15g/day) — substrate for the GHK-Cu-driven collagen synthesis
- **Synergistic**: Vitamin C (1000mg) — essential cofactor for collagen cross-linking, enhanced with GHK-Cu and BPC-157
- **Enhanced by**: Omega-3 fatty acids (2-3g EPA/DHA) — anti-inflammatory support that complements KPV
- **Enhanced by**: Adequate protein intake (1.6-2.2g/kg) to supply repair substrates
- **Enhanced by**: Probiotics and fermented foods for gut-focused protocols
- **Caution**: Zinc — high-dose zinc competes with copper absorption and may offset the GHK-Cu component; keep zinc moderate (under 30mg/day) and separate timing
- **Caution**: Blood thinners — theoretical bleeding risk from the angiogenesis-promoting components
- **Caution**: Immunosuppressants — theoretical interaction with TB-500's immune modulation and KPV's anti-inflammatory action
