# KLOW Stack

## Benefits

- **Enhanced Anti-Inflammatory Action**: The KPV component adds potent NF-kB-mediated anti-inflammatory effects beyond what GLOW provides — the blend's signature advantage
- **Gut Healing**: BPC-157 and KPV together make KLOW well-suited for gut inflammation, leaky gut, and IBD-type protocols alongside systemic repair
- **Comprehensive Tissue Repair**: Combines localized (BPC-157) and systemic (TB-500) healing for muscle, tendon, ligament, and skin
- **Skin Quality**: GHK-Cu's collagen and elastin stimulation plus KPV's skin anti-inflammatory action support firmer, calmer skin
- **Accelerated Recovery**: Community reports of faster recovery from training, injury, and surgery
- **Reduced Scarring**: TB-500 and GHK-Cu promote organized remodeling rather than fibrotic scar formation
- **Hair Support**: TB-500 and GHK-Cu support follicle health and regeneration
- **Calmer Inflammatory Skin Conditions**: KPV's alpha-MSH-derived action may benefit inflammatory skin issues (anecdotal)
- **Note on Evidence**: All benefits are extrapolated from studies of the individual peptides. No clinical trials have tested the KLOW blend itself — evidence is anecdotal and community-based
