# KLOW Stack

## Overview

KLOW is a pre-mixed peptide blend, not a single peptide — it is the GLOW blend with KPV added, combining four peptides in one vial: GHK-Cu/copper peptide (50mg), BPC-157 (10mg), TB-500/Thymosin Beta-4 (10mg), and KPV (10mg). It builds on GLOW's regenerative triad (BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu) by adding KPV — the anti-inflammatory tripeptide derived from alpha-MSH — which contributes a dedicated NF-kB-suppressing, gut and skin anti-inflammatory arm. The rationale is that KPV's potent anti-inflammatory action complements the repair, angiogenesis, and collagen-remodeling of the other three, making KLOW popular for protocols where gut or systemic inflammation is a limiting factor in recovery. As with GLOW, evidence is anecdotal and community-driven — no blend-specific clinical trials exist, and all support derives from studies of the individual components.
