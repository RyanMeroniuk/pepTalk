# KLOW Stack

## Side Effects

- Individual components are generally well-tolerated in research; combined four-peptide blend safety has not been formally studied
- Injection site reactions: redness, irritation, or stinging (GHK-Cu can sting more than other peptides)
- Mild fatigue or lethargy (commonly attributed to the TB-500 component)
- Headache (rare)
- Nausea (rare)
- Temporary flushing or lightheadedness after injection (rare)
- KPV is generally very well-tolerated with minimal reported side effects
- Theoretical concern: TB-500's pro-angiogenic and anti-apoptotic properties may theoretically support growth of existing tumors — not established
- Possible copper accumulation with prolonged high-dose GHK-Cu use — relevant for those with copper metabolism disorders (e.g., Wilson's disease)
- No established LD50 or organ toxicity for the blend
