# KLOW Stack

## Dosing Protocols

- **Standard Blend Composition**: GHK-Cu 50mg + BPC-157 10mg + TB-500 10mg + KPV 10mg per vial (~80mg total peptide). Ratio is approximately 5:1:1:1 (GHK:BPC:TB:KPV)
- **Reconstitution**: Add 2-3mL bacteriostatic water to the 80mg vial. At 3mL, each 0.1mL (10 units on an insulin syringe) delivers roughly GHK-Cu ~1.67mg + BPC-157 ~333mcg + TB-500 ~333mcg + KPV ~333mcg
- **Common Protocol**: 15-25 units (0.15-0.25mL) subcutaneously once daily — delivers roughly 2.5-4.2mg GHK-Cu, 500-833mcg BPC-157, 500-833mcg TB-500, and 500-833mcg KPV per dose
- **Daily Dosing Rationale**: BPC-157 and KPV favor daily dosing; TB-500 is normally 2-3x/week, so the blend compromises toward daily low-dose administration
- **Cycle**: Commonly 4-8 weeks on, then 4-6 weeks off — limited by TB-500's recommendation not to exceed ~3 months continuous use
- **Injection Site**: Subcutaneous; can inject near a target area for the localized BPC-157 effect, while TB-500, GHK-Cu, and KPV act more systemically
- **Storage**: Refrigerate after reconstitution; the GHK-Cu component gives the solution a normal blue tint
- **Gut Protocols**: For gut-focused use, some pair the injectable KLOW with separate oral BPC-157/KPV — but note this departs from the fixed-ratio blend
- **Caution**: Dosing is community-derived, not clinically validated. The fixed ratio means individual components cannot be adjusted independently
