# KLOW Stack

## Mechanisms of Action

- **NF-kB Suppression (KPV)**: KPV enters cells and inhibits NF-kB and the pro-inflammatory MAPK pathways, reducing cytokine production — the distinguishing mechanism KLOW adds over GLOW
- **Anti-Inflammatory (All Four)**: Collective reduction of TNF-alpha, IL-6, and IL-1beta, with KPV providing the strongest dedicated anti-inflammatory action
- **Angiogenesis (BPC-157 + TB-500)**: Both promote VEGF-driven new blood vessel formation for nutrient and oxygen delivery to healing tissue
- **Growth Factor Upregulation (BPC-157)**: Increases EGF, FGF, and HGF expression to drive tissue regeneration
- **Actin Regulation & Cell Migration (TB-500)**: Sequesters actin monomers to enable migration of endothelial and progenitor cells to injury sites
- **Anti-Apoptotic Signaling (TB-500)**: Activates the Akt survival pathway, reducing cell death in stressed tissue
- **Collagen Synthesis & Skin Remodeling (GHK-Cu)**: Stimulates fibroblast collagen and elastin production and delivers bioavailable copper for cross-linking
- **GI Mucosal Repair (BPC-157 + KPV)**: Both support gut barrier integrity — BPC-157 via mucosal repair, KPV via anti-inflammatory action on the gut lining, making KLOW especially gut-oriented
- **Antimicrobial/Skin Action (KPV)**: KPV retains alpha-MSH-derived antimicrobial and skin anti-inflammatory properties without pigmentation effects
- **Hair Follicle Stimulation (TB-500 + GHK-Cu)**: Both support follicle health and regeneration
