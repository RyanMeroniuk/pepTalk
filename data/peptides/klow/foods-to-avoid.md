# KLOW Stack

## Foods to Avoid

- **Alcohol**: Damages gut mucosa, increases intestinal permeability, impairs protein synthesis, and increases inflammation — counteracting all four components, especially the gut-focused ones. Eliminate during protocols
- **Refined Sugar & High-Fructose Corn Syrup**: Drive inflammatory cytokine production, feed pathogenic gut bacteria, and compete with vitamin C uptake — undermining gut repair and collagen synthesis
- **Processed Seed Oils**: Soybean, corn, canola, and sunflower oils promote omega-6-driven inflammation, directly opposing KPV's anti-inflammatory action
- **Gluten (if sensitive)**: Triggers zonulin release, opening tight junctions and increasing intestinal permeability — counteracting the gut-healing focus
- **Conventional Dairy (if sensitive)**: Casein A1 can trigger gut inflammation in sensitive individuals; choose A2, goat, or sheep dairy if tolerated
- **Processed & Ultra-Processed Foods**: Emulsifiers (polysorbate 80, carboxymethylcellulose) and artificial sweeteners damage the gut mucosal barrier the blend is repairing
- **Trans Fats & Fried Foods**: Advanced glycation end products and trans fats increase systemic inflammation and impair healing
- **High-Dose Zinc Supplements with Meals**: Excess zinc competes with the copper delivered by GHK-Cu
- **Excess Caffeine**: More than 1-2 cups of coffee can elevate cortisol and gastric acid, potentially irritating a healing gut lining
