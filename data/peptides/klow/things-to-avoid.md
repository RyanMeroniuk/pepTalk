# KLOW Stack

## Things to Avoid

- **Returning to Full Training Too Early**: The blend accelerates repair but new tissue needs time to mature. Avoid maximal loads, plyometrics, and sport-specific drills for the first 4-6 weeks
- **NSAIDs During Healing**: Ibuprofen, naproxen, and aspirin block the inflammatory phase needed for healing and irritate the gut. The blend modulates inflammation without fully suppressing it — avoid NSAIDs unless medically necessary
- **Corticosteroid Injections**: Cortisone weakens tendons and suppresses the tissue repair the blend enhances — avoid at the same site
- **Smoking & Nicotine**: Nicotine's vasoconstriction reduces blood flow to healing tissue, opposing the angiogenic action of the BPC-157 and TB-500 components
- **Excess Copper Load**: The GHK-Cu component delivers copper — avoid stacking additional high-dose copper; those with Wilson's disease or copper metabolism disorders should avoid the blend
- **High-Dose Zinc Stacking**: Large zinc doses antagonize copper and may blunt the GHK-Cu component — keep zinc moderate and separate timing
- **Eating While Stressed**: For the gut-focused KPV/BPC-157 action, avoid eating in a sympathetic ("fight or flight") state — it impairs digestion and gut repair
- **Chronic Stress & Sleep Deprivation**: Both elevate cortisol, increasing gut permeability and impairing healing
- **Treating the Blend as Clinically Proven**: No trials have tested KLOW itself; treat dosing and benefits as experimental and community-derived
- **Use During Pregnancy/Breastfeeding**: Insufficient safety data — avoid entirely
