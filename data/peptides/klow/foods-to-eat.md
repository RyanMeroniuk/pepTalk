# KLOW Stack

## Foods to Eat

- **Bone Broth**: Rich in collagen, glycine, proline, and glutamine — building blocks for the GHK-Cu, BPC-157, and KPV repair effects, and supportive of gut lining. Consume 1-2 cups daily
- **Fermented Foods**: Sauerkraut, kimchi, kefir, yogurt, and miso provide beneficial bacteria that complement the BPC-157/KPV gut-healing focus
- **Wild-Caught Fatty Fish**: Salmon, sardines, and mackerel supply omega-3s (EPA/DHA) that reinforce KPV's anti-inflammatory action
- **Collagen-Rich Foods**: Chicken skin, pork skin, oxtail, and fish with skin provide collagen precursors for the GHK-Cu component
- **Citrus & Bell Peppers**: High vitamin C for collagen cross-linking driven by GHK-Cu. Consume 2-3 servings daily
- **Organ Meats**: Liver and kidney provide copper, zinc, vitamin A, and B-vitamins for wound healing and collagen synthesis
- **Eggs (Whole)**: Complete protein with all essential amino acids plus choline for cell membrane repair
- **Dark Leafy Greens**: Spinach, kale, and Swiss chard provide vitamin C, vitamin K, folate, and magnesium for tissue repair
- **Berries & Pomegranate**: Antioxidants that reduce oxidative stress at injury and remodeling sites
- **Turmeric & Ginger**: Anti-inflammatories that complement KPV's NF-kB suppression; pair turmeric with black pepper for absorption
- **Aloe Vera Juice & Cabbage**: Support gut mucosal lining, complementing the gut-focused components
- **Sweet Potatoes & Squash**: Beta-carotene (vitamin A) for epithelial and connective tissue repair
