# KLOW Stack

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Anti-Inflammatory Diet**: With KPV adding a dedicated anti-inflammatory arm, maximize its effect through a whole-food, low-processed, omega-3-rich diet. Minimize chronic inflammatory inputs that compete with the blend's action
- **Gut-Specific Protocols**: KLOW is often used for gut healing — eat slowly, chew thoroughly, avoid eating when stressed, and space meals 3-4 hours apart to support the migrating motor complex and the BPC-157/KPV gut-repair effect
- **Protein Prioritization**: Consume 1.6-2.2g protein per kg body weight daily to supply amino acids for tissue and collagen synthesis. Space across 4-5 meals
- **Progressive Rehabilitation**: Support BPC-157 and TB-500 with gentle controlled movement of recovering areas — start at 30-50% intensity, increase ~10% per week, and avoid maximal loads for the first 4-6 weeks
- **Sleep Optimization**: Target 7-9 hours. Deep-sleep growth hormone release compounds with the blend's repair mechanisms. Keep a cool, dark room and a consistent schedule
- **Stress Management**: Chronic cortisol impairs healing and increases gut permeability — directly opposing the gut-focused components. Practice 10-15 minutes daily of breathwork or meditation; magnesium glycinate (300-400mg) at night
- **Avoid Alcohol & Nicotine**: Alcohol damages gut mucosa and impairs healing; nicotine's vasoconstriction opposes the angiogenic components
- **Hydration**: Drink at least half your body weight in ounces of water daily to support gut mucosa and connective tissue repair
- **Morning Sunlight**: 15-20 minutes of morning light supports circadian rhythm and vitamin D, both of which underpin healing
