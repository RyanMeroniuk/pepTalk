# SS-31 (Elamipretide)

## Foods to Eat

- **Omega-3 Rich Fish**: Wild salmon, sardines, mackerel, anchovies — provide DHA/EPA that supports mitochondrial membrane phospholipid quality alongside cardiolipin.
- **CoQ10-Containing Foods**: Organ meats (liver, heart), fatty fish, and grass-fed beef — supply the electron transport chain cofactor SS-31 helps stabilize.
- **Polyphenol-Rich Foods**: Blueberries, dark chocolate (85%+), green tea, pomegranate, extra virgin olive oil — reduce oxidative stress and support mitochondrial function.
- **Dark Leafy Greens**: Spinach, kale, Swiss chard — rich in magnesium needed for ATP-dependent reactions.
- **Nuts and Seeds**: Almonds, walnuts, flaxseed, chia — provide healthy fats and magnesium for mitochondrial support.
- **Niacin (B3) Sources**: Chicken, tuna, turkey, mushrooms — support the NAD+ pool the electron transport chain depends on.
- **Colorful Vegetables**: Bell peppers, broccoli, beets — antioxidants that complement SS-31's ROS reduction.
- **Quality Protein**: Eggs, fish, poultry, legumes — supply amino acids for tissue repair in high-energy organs.
