# SS-31 (Elamipretide)

## Side Effects

- Injection site reactions (redness, itching, swelling) — the most common finding in trials.
- Headache.
- Dizziness.
- Nausea or mild GI discomfort.
- Diarrhea (reported in some trials).
- Hypersensitivity/allergic reactions (uncommon).
- Generally well tolerated across human trials at studied doses.
- **Limited long-term safety data outside controlled trials** — chronic non-clinical use is unstudied.
