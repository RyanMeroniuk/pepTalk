# SS-31 (Elamipretide)

## Things to Avoid

- **Treating It as Approved Medicine**: SS-31 is not approved by any regulator and several Phase 3 trials missed primary endpoints — do not assume clinical efficacy for non-studied uses.
- **Smoking and Vaping**: Tobacco smoke is a potent source of mitochondrial oxidative damage that directly undermines SS-31's purpose.
- **Chronic Sleep Deprivation**: Impairs mitophagy and mitochondrial repair, working against the structural recovery SS-31 promotes.
- **Excessive Alcohol**: Mitochondrial toxin that depletes NAD+ and increases ROS — avoid around dosing.
- **Unregulated Sourcing**: Research-grade peptides vary widely in purity; impure product risks contamination and inconsistent dosing.
- **Stacking Blindly**: Combining many mitochondrial or AMPK agents without monitoring can cause unpredictable energy and fatigue swings.
- **Ignoring Underlying Disease**: SS-31 does not replace treatment for cardiac, metabolic, or ophthalmic conditions — work with appropriate clinicians.
- **Expecting Instant Results**: Mitochondrial structural improvements develop over weeks; consistency matters more than acute effect.
