# SS-31 (Elamipretide)

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Exercise for Mitochondrial Demand**: Zone 2 endurance and resistance training drive mitochondrial biogenesis, giving SS-31 healthier machinery to maintain. Aim for 3–5 sessions per week combining steady-state cardio and compound lifts.
- **Prioritize Sleep**: Mitochondrial repair and turnover (mitophagy) occur during deep sleep. Target 7–9 hours with consistent sleep/wake times to support the structural repair SS-31 promotes.
- **Anti-Inflammatory Diet**: Chronic inflammation drives ROS and cardiolipin oxidation — the very damage SS-31 counteracts. Emphasize whole foods, fatty fish, and polyphenols.
- **Limit Alcohol**: Alcohol is directly toxic to mitochondria and depletes NAD+, undermining SS-31's bioenergetic goals. Minimize or avoid, especially on dosing days.
- **Cold and Heat Exposure**: Sauna and cold exposure stimulate mitochondrial biogenesis and stress resilience, complementing SS-31's mechanism.
- **Manage Oxidative Load**: Avoid smoking and excess processed-food intake, which raise systemic oxidative stress that competes with SS-31's ROS-lowering action.
- **Track Energy and Recovery**: Monitor subjective energy, exercise recovery, and resting heart rate to gauge response, since validated biomarkers for non-clinical use do not exist.
- **Consistent Hydration**: Adequate hydration supports cellular metabolism and nutrient delivery to high-energy tissues.
- **Time-Restricted Eating**: Mild metabolic stress from a 10–12 hour eating window supports mitophagy and complements mitochondrial health goals.
