# SS-31 (Elamipretide)

## Dosing Protocols

- **Clinical Trial Dose**: Stealth BioTherapeutics trials used 40mg subcutaneous once daily for elamipretide; this is the best-supported reference point.
- **Biohacker Range**: Anecdotal protocols use 5–10mg subcutaneous daily — note these are not validated by clinical data.
- **Route**: Subcutaneous injection; an intravenous formulation was used in some acute cardiac/ischemia trials.
- **Frequency**: Once daily in clinical settings.
- **Half-Life**: Short plasma half-life (~2–4 hours), but mitochondrial accumulation extends functional effect.
- **Cycle**: Anecdotal protocols often run 4–8 weeks; clinical trials dosed continuously for weeks to months.
- **Timing**: Morning dosing common; some pair with exercise for mitochondrial demand.
- **Reconstitution**: Bacteriostatic water; refrigerate after reconstitution.
- **Storage**: Refrigerate reconstituted peptide and use within approximately 1 month.
- **Note**: No standardized non-clinical protocol exists — research-use compound only.
