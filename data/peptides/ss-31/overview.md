# SS-31 (Elamipretide)

## Overview

SS-31 is a synthetic, cell-permeable tetrapeptide (D-Arg-dimethylTyr-Lys-Phe-NH2) also known as elamipretide, MTP-131, and Bendavia. It selectively concentrates in the inner mitochondrial membrane, where it binds and stabilizes cardiolipin — a phospholipid essential for the structure and efficiency of the electron transport chain. By preserving cristae architecture and restoring electron flow, it improves ATP production while reducing reactive oxygen species (ROS) leak. Developed by Stealth BioTherapeutics, it is one of the few mitochondrial peptides to reach multiple human clinical trials, spanning primary mitochondrial myopathy, heart failure, and dry age-related macular degeneration, though Phase 3 results have been mixed and it is not approved by any regulator. It functions as a structural complement to MOTS-c: where MOTS-c signals metabolic adaptation, SS-31 physically repairs the energy-producing machinery itself.
