# SS-31 (Elamipretide)

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Chemotherapy**: Use with caution and only under oncology supervision — SS-31's effects on mitochondrial metabolism in malignant cells are not established and could theoretically interfere; contraindicated during active cancer treatment.
- **Statins**: These can cause mitochondrial CoQ10 depletion and myopathy; SS-31's mitochondrial effects warrant monitoring if combined, with no established interaction documented.
- **Metformin**: Both influence mitochondrial/metabolic function — monitor energy and glucose if combined, and separate any concerns with a clinician.
- **Insulin and Antidiabetics**: Monitor blood glucose, as improved cellular energetics could modestly shift metabolic response.
- **Anticoagulants / Blood Thinners**: No established interaction, but standard injection-site caution applies — monitor for bruising.
- **Contraindicated**: Active cancer (uncertain effects on tumor mitochondrial metabolism).
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy and breastfeeding (no safety data).
- **Caution**: Liver disease or kidney disease — clearance and safety in organ impairment are not characterized outside trials.
