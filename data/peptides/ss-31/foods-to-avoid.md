# SS-31 (Elamipretide)

## Foods to Avoid

- **Excessive Alcohol**: Directly toxic to mitochondria, depletes NAD+, and increases oxidative load — directly opposing SS-31's bioenergetic and ROS-lowering effects.
- **Trans Fats and Oxidized Seed Oils**: Repeatedly heated frying oils, margarine, and partially hydrogenated fats incorporate into membranes and promote oxidative damage.
- **Refined Sugars**: Candy, soda, pastries — promote glycation and oxidative stress that strain mitochondria.
- **Ultra-Processed Foods**: Packaged snacks, fast food, deli meats — carry inflammatory additives that raise the oxidative burden SS-31 works against.
- **High-Fructose Corn Syrup**: Drives liver fat accumulation and metabolic stress on energy-demanding tissues.
- **Charred and Heavily Grilled Meats**: Form advanced glycation end-products and oxidized compounds that increase cellular oxidative stress.
- **Excess Caffeine Late in Day**: Can disrupt the deep sleep needed for mitochondrial repair.
