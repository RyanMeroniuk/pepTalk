# SS-31 (Elamipretide)

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Cardiolipin Binding**: Selectively binds cardiolipin in the inner mitochondrial membrane, the lipid that scaffolds the electron transport chain complexes.
- **Cristae Stabilization**: Preserves the tightly folded cristae structure that maximizes surface area for ATP synthesis, preventing age- and disease-related cristae loss.
- **Electron Transport Restoration**: Improves coupling of complexes III and IV and cytochrome c retention, increasing efficient ATP output.
- **ROS Reduction**: Cuts electron leak and superoxide formation at the source rather than scavenging ROS after they form, lowering oxidative damage.
- **Membrane Selectivity**: Concentrates roughly 1,000–5,000-fold in mitochondria via its alternating aromatic/cationic structure, requiring no specific receptor.
- **Permeability Transition Pore Modulation**: Reduces pathological opening of the mitochondrial permeability transition pore, limiting cell death under ischemic stress.
- **ATP Synthase Support**: Stabilizes the lipid environment around ATP synthase to sustain phosphorylation efficiency.
- **Anti-Apoptotic Effect**: By preserving membrane integrity and cytochrome c localization, reduces the apoptotic cascade in stressed cells.
- **Tissue-Agnostic Action**: Works in any high-energy tissue (heart, skeletal muscle, retina, kidney, brain) because cardiolipin biology is universal.
