# SS-31 (Elamipretide)

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT FDA-approved** for any indication.
- Developed by Stealth BioTherapeutics as elamipretide (also MTP-131, Bendavia).
- Reached multiple human clinical trials — primary mitochondrial myopathy, heart failure (HFrEF and HFpEF), and dry age-related macular degeneration / geographic atrophy.
- Phase 3 results have been mixed; several trials did not meet primary endpoints, though some secondary and subgroup signals were positive.
- Has received orphan drug and rare-disease designations for certain mitochondrial indications.
- Not available as an approved prescription product; obtained only as a research-use compound.
- Long-term safety outside controlled trials is not established.
- Remains an active area of mitochondrial medicine and longevity research (2025–2026).
