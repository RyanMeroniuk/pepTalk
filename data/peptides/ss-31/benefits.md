# SS-31 (Elamipretide)

## Benefits

- **Mitochondrial Bioenergetics**: Restores ATP production in dysfunctional mitochondria across multiple preclinical models; the best-characterized benefit.
- **Skeletal Muscle Function**: Improved fatigue resistance and 6-minute walk distance in some primary mitochondrial myopathy trial subgroups, though primary endpoints were not consistently met.
- **Cardiac Support**: Studied in heart failure with reduced and preserved ejection fraction; preclinical data show improved cardiac efficiency, with mixed human results.
- **Retinal Health**: Investigated for dry age-related macular degeneration (geographic atrophy) and Leber hereditary optic neuropathy, targeting the highly energy-dependent retina.
- **Oxidative Stress Reduction**: Lowers ROS-driven damage at the membrane level, relevant to aging and ischemia-reperfusion injury.
- **Anti-Aging Interest**: Reverses some markers of mitochondrial aging in aged animal muscle, driving longevity-community interest despite limited human aging data.
- **Recovery Potential**: Anecdotally used by biohackers for energy and exercise recovery, but this use is unstudied in controlled trials.
- **Structural Repair**: Unlike signaling peptides, it physically reconditions the energy-producing apparatus, complementing metabolic peptides like MOTS-c.
