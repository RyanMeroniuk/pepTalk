# SS-31 (Elamipretide)

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Synergistic**: CoQ10 (100–200mg) — directly supports the electron transport chain that SS-31 stabilizes; a logical pairing for bioenergetics.
- **Synergistic**: PQQ (20mg) — promotes mitochondrial biogenesis, complementing SS-31's membrane repair.
- **Critical for ATP**: NMN (250–500mg) or NAD+ support — replenishes the NAD+ pool that the restored electron transport chain depends on.
- **Enhanced by**: Alpha-lipoic-acid (300–600mg) — mitochondrial antioxidant that complements ROS reduction.
- **Synergistic**: Omega-3 (2–3g EPA/DHA) — supports membrane phospholipid quality alongside cardiolipin stabilization.
- **Enhanced by**: Magnesium (300–400mg) — cofactor for ATP-dependent enzymatic reactions.
- **Synergistic**: MOTS-c — pairs metabolic mitochondrial signaling with SS-31's structural repair.
- **Enhanced by**: Resveratrol — supports mitochondrial function via complementary pathways.
- **Caution**: Stacking many AMPK or mitochondrial agents at once without monitoring may produce unpredictable energy/fatigue swings.
- **Contraindicated**: Active cancer (mitochondrial effects on tumor metabolism uncertain).
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy/breastfeeding.
