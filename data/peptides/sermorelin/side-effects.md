# Sermorelin

## Side Effects

- Generally very well-tolerated
- **Most Common**: Transient facial flushing (15%), injection site pain/redness (15%)
- **Rare**: Difficulty swallowing (dysphagia), dizziness, urticaria (hives)
- **Very Rare**: Headache, nausea
- No reports of GH excess due to somatostatin feedback regulation
- Favorable long-term safety profile from years of FDA-approved use
