# Sermorelin

## Benefits

- **Growth Hormone Optimization**: Restores youthful GH secretion patterns
- **Body Composition**: Improved muscle mass, reduced body fat
- **Cognitive Function**: 20-week study showed improved executive function and working memory, even in mild cognitive impairment
- **Sleep Quality**: Enhanced deep sleep via GH pulse optimization
- **Recovery**: Faster recovery from exercise and injury
- **Bone Health**: Improved bone density
- **Skin Health**: Better collagen production and skin elasticity
- **Anti-Aging**: Preserves pituitary function and slows neuroendocrine aging
- **Safer than GH**: Self-limiting mechanism prevents GH excess
