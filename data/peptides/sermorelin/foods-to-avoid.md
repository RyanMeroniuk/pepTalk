# Sermorelin

## Foods to Avoid

- **Sugar and refined carbs within 2 hours of injection** — insulin release directly suppresses the GH pulse from sermorelin. This includes candy, soda, white bread, and desserts
- **High-fat meals near injection** — free fatty acids independently blunt pituitary GH release
- **Alcohol** — suppresses GH secretion acutely (up to 75% reduction) and destroys deep sleep architecture. Since sermorelin works primarily through the sleep-time GH pulse, alcohol directly undermines its mechanism
- **Large meals before bed** — a full stomach near your bedtime injection blunts GH release and can impair sleep quality
- **Heavily processed foods** — promote inflammation and insulin resistance, impairing long-term GH signaling
- **Excessive caffeine** — more than 2-3 cups of coffee daily, especially after noon, disrupts sleep quality and reduces deep sleep where GH is released
- **Fruit juice and sweetened beverages** — concentrated sugar without fiber causes rapid insulin spikes
