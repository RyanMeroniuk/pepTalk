# Sermorelin

## Mechanisms of Action

- **GHRH Receptor Binding**: Binds GHRHR on pituitary somatotroph cells, mimicking natural GHRH signaling
- **Endogenous GH Production**: Stimulates the pituitary to increase hGH gene transcription and GH secretion
- **Pulsatile Release**: Triggers GH release in natural bursts regulated by somatostatin feedback (unlike constant levels from rhGH)
- **IGF-1 Elevation**: Indirectly raises IGF-1 through GH-mediated hepatic production
- **Pituitary Preservation**: Supports pituitary function and helps slow age-related decline of the GH neuroendocrine axis
- **Self-Limiting**: Somatostatin feedback prevents excessive GH elevation — built-in safety mechanism
