# Sermorelin

## Overview

Sermorelin acetate (formerly brand names Geref, Gerel) is a 29-amino acid synthetic peptide representing the 1-29 fragment of endogenous human growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH). It is the shortest fully functional fragment of GHRH. Unlike exogenous growth hormone (rhGH) which directly supplies GH, sermorelin stimulates the patient's own pituitary gland to produce and secrete endogenous human growth hormone. Its effects are naturally regulated by somatostatin negative feedback, making it very difficult to overdose — unlike synthetic GH. Originally FDA-approved in 1997 for pediatric GH deficiency, discontinued by manufacturer in 2008 for commercial reasons but still widely available through compounding pharmacies.
