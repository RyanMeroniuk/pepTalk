# Sermorelin

## Dosing Protocols

- **Standard Adult Dose**: 200-500 mcg/day subcutaneous injection
- **Timing**: Before bedtime — aligns with natural circadian GH release peak during sleep
- **Adjustment**: After 4-6 weeks, adjust based on IGF-1 lab results and clinical response
- **Cycle**: Continuous use common; some protocols cycle 3 months on, 1 month off
- **Reconstitution**: Bacteriostatic water; refrigerate
- **Results Timeline**: Sleep improvements 1-2 weeks; body composition changes 4-8 weeks
