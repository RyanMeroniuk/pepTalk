# Sermorelin

## Lifestyle Recommendations


### Injection Timing & Fasting
- **Inject before bed (20-30 minutes)** — this is the gold standard timing for sermorelin. It amplifies the natural nocturnal GH surge that peaks during Stage 3/4 deep sleep
- **Always inject in a fasted state** — minimum 2 hours after your last meal. Insulin and free fatty acids from food suppress GH release at the pituitary level
- **Wait 20-30 minutes after injection before eating** — though you should be heading to sleep, avoid any late-night snacking after injection
- **Sermorelin is self-limiting via somatostatin feedback** — this built-in safety mechanism means the risk of GH excess is very low, making it one of the safest GH secretagogues for long-term use
- **Dose adjustment after 4-6 weeks** — get IGF-1 labs and adjust with your prescriber. Sermorelin's efficacy depends on having functional pituitary somatotroph cells

### Exercise
- **Resistance training 3-5x/week** — compound movements (squats, deadlifts, bench press, overhead press, rows) provide the strongest stimulus to synergize with sermorelin's GH elevation
- **Moderate rest periods (60-90 seconds)** — lactic acid accumulation independently stimulates GH release, creating an additive effect with sermorelin
- **HIIT 2x/week** — 15-20 minute sessions of sprint intervals, cycling, or metabolic circuits boost GH pulses and improve insulin sensitivity
- **Evening training followed by bedtime injection** — the exercise-induced GH spike combines with sermorelin's pulse for maximum overnight recovery
- **Include flexibility and mobility work** — GH enhances collagen synthesis and joint repair. Yoga, stretching, or foam rolling 2-3x/week complements this
- **Avoid chronic long-duration cardio** — sessions over 60 minutes at moderate intensity raise cortisol, which suppresses GH signaling

### Sleep Optimization
- **Prioritize 7-9 hours of quality sleep** — sermorelin's primary mechanism is amplifying the nocturnal GH pulse. Without sufficient sleep, there is less deep sleep to amplify
- **Consistent sleep and wake times** — even on weekends. Circadian regularity is essential for predictable GH pulsatility
- **Cool, dark, quiet bedroom (65-68°F)** — optimal conditions for melatonin and GH secretion
- **Avoid screens for 30-60 minutes before injection and sleep** — blue light suppresses melatonin, which is synergistic with GH release
- **Magnesium glycinate (300-400mg before bed)** — supports both GABA-mediated relaxation and GH secretion

### Daily Habits
- **Morning sunlight exposure (15-20 minutes)** — anchors the circadian rhythm, optimizing the timing and amplitude of the nocturnal GH pulse
- **Adequate hydration** — aim for half your body weight in ounces of water daily. Dehydration impairs recovery and hormonal signaling
- **Stress management** — chronic cortisol directly suppresses GH release. Sermorelin stimulates the pituitary, but cortisol inhibits it. Daily meditation, walks, or breathwork practice
- **Blood work every 8-12 weeks**: IGF-1, fasting glucose, fasting insulin, and a metabolic panel
- **Patience** — sermorelin works gradually by restoring natural GH production. Sleep improvements come in 1-2 weeks, but body composition changes take 4-8 weeks of consistent use
