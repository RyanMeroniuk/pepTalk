# Sermorelin

## Regulatory Status

- **Was FDA-approved** (1997) for pediatric GH deficiency — Geref brand
- **Discontinued by manufacturer** in 2008 for commercial reasons (not safety)
- Still widely available through compounding pharmacies with prescription
- One of the most well-studied GH secretagogues with established safety data
- **Banned by WADA** for competitive athletes
