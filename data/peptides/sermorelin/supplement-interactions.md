# Sermorelin

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Synergistic**: GHRP (Ipamorelin or GHRP-2) for amplified GH release
- **Synergistic**: Resistance training for body composition
- **Enhanced by**: Zinc, Magnesium, Vitamin D3 for GH axis support
- **Enhanced by**: Adequate protein intake
- **Inject fasted**: Carbs and fats blunt GH release
- **Caution**: Diabetes medications — monitor glucose
- **Caution**: Other GH-elevating compounds
