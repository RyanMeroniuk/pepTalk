# Sermorelin

## Foods to Eat

- **Wild-caught salmon** — omega-3 fatty acids support GH receptor sensitivity and reduce inflammation; complete protein for muscle repair
- **Pasture-raised eggs** — amino acids for muscle synthesis, cholesterol for hormone production, and choline for brain health (sermorelin also has cognitive benefits)
- **Chicken breast and lean turkey** — high-quality protein without excess fat, supporting the anabolic environment sermorelin creates
- **Cottage cheese before bed** — casein protein provides slow-release amino acids throughout the night, feeding muscle repair during sermorelin's enhanced GH window
- **Greek yogurt (plain)** — glutamine content independently supports GH release; probiotics benefit gut health
- **Sweet potatoes** — complex carbohydrate with moderate glycemic index; excellent post-workout to replenish glycogen (eat well before injection time)
- **Steel-cut oats** — slow-digesting carbs with fiber for sustained energy. Ideal for morning meals when injection is at bedtime
- **Spinach, kale, and Swiss chard** — magnesium-rich greens that support sleep quality and GH axis function
- **Pumpkin seeds** — excellent source of zinc (critical for GH production) and magnesium
- **Tart cherry juice (small amount before bed)** — natural melatonin source that may enhance sleep quality synergistically with sermorelin
- **Bone broth** — glycine supports sleep and collagen precursors complement GH-mediated tissue repair
- **Brazil nuts (2-3 daily)** — selenium supports thyroid function, which interacts with the GH/IGF-1 axis
- **Watermelon** — L-citrulline converts to arginine, a GH-stimulating amino acid
