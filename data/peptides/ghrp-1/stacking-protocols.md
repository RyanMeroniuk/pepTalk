# GHRP-1

## Stacking Protocols

- **Classic GH Pulse Stack**: GHRP-1 (100mcg) + CJC-1295 (100mcg before bed) — the GHRP + GHRH pairing where the two different pathways combine for an amplified, synergistic GH pulse
- **Recovery Stack**: GHRP-1 (100mcg 2x/day) + Sermorelin (200mcg before bed) — GHRH analog plus GHRP for steady GH/IGF-1 support and recovery
- **Body Recomp Stack**: GHRP-1 + Ipamorelin (100mcg each) + CJC-1295 — some users layer a cleaner GHRP (ipamorelin) to balance GHRP-1's cortisol/prolactin bump
- **Oral GH Support Stack**: GHRP-1 (100mcg pre-bed) + MK-677 (Ibutamoren, 25mg/day) — combines injectable pulse with sustained oral secretagogue baseline
- **Healing Stack**: GHRP-1 + BPC-157 (250mcg 2x/day) + TB-500 (2mg 2x/week) — pairs GH-driven recovery with direct tissue-healing peptides
- **Note**: GHRP-1 is largely interchangeable with GHRP-2/ipamorelin in these stacks; all combinations are anecdotal and not clinically validated
