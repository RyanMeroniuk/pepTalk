# GHRP-1

## Dosing Protocols

- **No approved or validated dosing**: All figures are anecdotal research-community practice based on older pharmacological data, not clinically supported
- **Common anecdotal dose**: ~100mcg subcutaneous per administration, the standard "saturation dose" concept shared across the GHRP class
- **Frequency**: 1–3x per day, often pre-bed and/or post-workout, mirroring GHRP-2/GHRP-6 protocols
- **Timing relative to food**: Best injected fasted or at least ~30 minutes before/after eating, since high blood glucose and fatty meals blunt the GH pulse
- **GHRP + GHRH pairing**: Frequently combined with a GHRH analog (e.g., CJC-1295) at matched ~100mcg doses for an amplified, synergistic pulse
- **Reconstitution**: Lyophilized powder reconstituted with bacteriostatic water
- **Storage**: Refrigerate after reconstitution
- **Major caveat**: GHRP-1 is less characterized than GHRP-2 or ipamorelin, and gray-market purity/identity is unverified — newer GHRPs are generally preferred for more predictable dosing
