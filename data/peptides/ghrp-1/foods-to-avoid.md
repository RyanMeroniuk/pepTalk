# GHRP-1

## Foods to Avoid

- **Large or fatty meals right around injection**: Dietary fat and a full stomach blunt the GH pulse — avoid eating heavily within ~30–60 minutes of dosing
- **High-glycemic carbs and sugary foods near injection**: Elevated insulin/glucose suppresses GH release, undercutting the secretagogue effect
- **Alcohol**: Suppresses GH release, disrupts sleep (when the main GH pulse occurs), and impairs recovery
- **Added sugars and high-fructose corn syrup**: Promote insulin resistance, which is compounded by GH's mild glucose effects
- **Ultra-processed foods**: Nutrient-poor and pro-inflammatory, working against recovery
- **Trans fats and fried foods**: Pro-inflammatory and detrimental to metabolic health
- **Excess caffeine late in the day**: Disrupts the sleep that the pre-bed GH pulse depends on
