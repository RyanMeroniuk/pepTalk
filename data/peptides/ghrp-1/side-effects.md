# GHRP-1

## Side Effects

- **Cortisol elevation**: Like other early GHRPs, it can produce a modest rise in ACTH/cortisol — generally milder than hexarelin but more than ipamorelin
- **Prolactin elevation**: A mild prolactin bump is possible, which at higher/chronic doses could theoretically affect libido or cause other prolactin-related effects
- **Increased appetite**: Ghrelin-mimetic action can stimulate hunger, though usually less than GHRP-6
- **Water retention**: Mild fluid retention from GH elevation, sometimes with transient bloating
- **Injection-site reactions**: Redness, swelling, or irritation
- **Numbness/tingling**: Transient paresthesia or flushing reported across the GHRP class shortly after injection
- **Blood glucose effects**: GH elevation can mildly reduce insulin sensitivity over time; monitor in those with glucose concerns
- **Evidence caveat**: GHRP-1's human side-effect data is older and sparse; the profile is inferred from the closely related GHRP family and gray-market sourcing adds independent contamination risk
