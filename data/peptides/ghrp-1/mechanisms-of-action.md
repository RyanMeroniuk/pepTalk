# GHRP-1

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Ghrelin receptor agonist**: Binds the GH secretagogue receptor (GHS-R1a, the ghrelin receptor) on the pituitary and hypothalamus to trigger growth hormone release
- **Pulsatile GH release**: Stimulates a pulse of GH that mimics the body's natural secretion pattern rather than a flat elevation
- **Synergy with GHRH analogs**: Works through a different pathway than GHRH analogs (CJC-1295, sermorelin, tesamorelin), so combining the two classes produces a larger, amplified GH pulse
- **Downstream IGF-1 elevation**: The GH pulse drives hepatic IGF-1 production, mediating most of the anabolic/recovery effects
- **Cortisol and prolactin bump**: Like other early GHRPs (notably GHRP-6 and hexarelin), it stimulates some ACTH/cortisol and prolactin release — generally milder than hexarelin but present
- **Mild appetite stimulation**: As a ghrelin mimetic it can increase hunger, though typically less pronounced than GHRP-6
- **Evidence caveat**: Most data is older pharmacological research; GHRP-1 is less characterized in modern human use than GHRP-2 or ipamorelin
