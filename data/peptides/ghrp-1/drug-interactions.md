# GHRP-1

## Drug Interactions

- **Insulin and sulfonylureas**: Caution — GH elevation can reduce insulin sensitivity and raise glucose, potentially requiring dose adjustment of glucose-lowering medications; monitor
- **Corticosteroids (prednisone, dexamethasone)**: Caution — glucocorticoids blunt GH release and are catabolic, opposing GHRP-1's intended effect; monitor
- **Thyroid medications (levothyroxine, T3/T4)**: Monitor — thyroid status modulates GH/IGF-1 response and metabolic rate
- **Active cancer / chemotherapy**: Contraindicated — GH/IGF-1 elevation may promote tumor growth
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding**: Contraindicated — no safety data
- **Diabetes**: Caution — GH's glucose effects can complicate management; monitor closely
- **Dopamine agonists / antipsychotics (prolactin-active drugs)**: Monitor — GHRP-1's mild prolactin bump could interact with drugs that modulate prolactin
- **General caveat**: Robust human drug-interaction data for GHRP-1 specifically is lacking; these are class-based and mechanism-based precautions, not documented trial findings
