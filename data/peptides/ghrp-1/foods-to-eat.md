# GHRP-1

## Foods to Eat

- **Lean protein (chicken, turkey, fish, lean beef)**: Supplies amino acids for the recovery and anabolism that elevated GH/IGF-1 supports
- **Eggs (pasture-raised)**: Complete protein with choline and quality fats
- **Greek yogurt and cottage cheese**: Slow-digesting casein, useful at night around a pre-bed dose
- **Leafy greens and colorful vegetables**: Micronutrient-dense and anti-inflammatory
- **Berries**: Polyphenols and antioxidants supporting recovery
- **Nuts and seeds (almonds, pumpkin seeds)**: Magnesium and zinc that support the GH/IGF-1 axis and sleep
- **Sweet potatoes and oats**: Quality carbohydrates to fuel training — best kept away from the injection window
- **Fatty fish (salmon, sardines)**: Omega-3s for anti-inflammatory recovery support (away from injection timing, since fat blunts the pulse)
- **Tart cherries or glycine-rich bone broth**: May support the sleep quality that maximizes the nocturnal GH pulse
