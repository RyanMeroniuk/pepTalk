# GHRP-1

## Things to Avoid

- **Eating heavily around injections**: Fat and high glucose blunt the GH pulse — the most common practical mistake with any GHRP
- **Stacking multiple prolactin/cortisol-raising GHRPs**: Combining GHRP-1 with GHRP-6 or hexarelin can compound the cortisol and prolactin bump; ipamorelin is cleaner if minimizing this matters
- **Chronic high-dose use**: Exceeding the ~100mcg saturation dose mostly raises cortisol/prolactin without proportionally more GH, and chronic GH elevation can reduce insulin sensitivity
- **Ignoring glucose over time**: GH mildly impairs insulin sensitivity; skipping glucose monitoring on long-term use is unwise
- **Use with active cancer**: GH/IGF-1 elevation may promote tumor growth — avoid with any cancer history
- **Use during pregnancy or breastfeeding**: No safety data — avoid
- **Assuming gray-market product accuracy**: GHRP-1 is less common than GHRP-2, so mislabeling and purity issues are a real risk
- **Defaulting to GHRP-1 out of novelty**: It is largely redundant with better-characterized GHRPs; choosing it without reason adds uncertainty for no clear benefit
