# GHRP-1

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Inject fasted or away from meals**: GH release is blunted by elevated blood glucose and dietary fat — separate injections from large meals by ~30+ minutes for a stronger pulse
- **Pre-bed dosing aligns with natural GH rhythm**: The largest endogenous GH pulse occurs in early deep sleep; a bedtime dose can amplify it
- **Prioritize sleep (7–9 hours)**: GH benefits are sleep-dependent; poor sleep undercuts the entire point of a secretagogue
- **Resistance training**: Mechanical loading directs the anabolic/recovery benefits of elevated GH/IGF-1
- **Manage stress**: Given GHRP-1's mild cortisol bump, layering chronic stress on top is counterproductive — use breathwork, walking, or meditation
- **Consider newer GHRPs**: Practically, GHRP-2 or ipamorelin offer more predictable, better-characterized effects; GHRP-1 is largely redundant
- **Monitor glucose over time**: Chronic GH elevation can mildly reduce insulin sensitivity
- **Understand the evidence gap**: GHRP-1 use is experimental and based on older/sparse data; treat accordingly
