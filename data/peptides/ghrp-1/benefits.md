# GHRP-1

## Benefits

- **Increased GH secretion**: Triggers a pulse of endogenous growth hormone, with downstream IGF-1 elevation driving most benefits
- **Recovery and tissue repair**: Elevated GH/IGF-1 supports muscle recovery, connective tissue repair, and general anabolism — common to the GHRP class
- **Potential body composition support**: GH elevation can favor lean mass and fat metabolism over time, similar to other secretagogues
- **Sleep quality (possible)**: GHRPs taken before bed may support deeper sleep via the GH pulse, as reported with related peptides
- **Synergy in GHRP + GHRH stacks**: Its main practical value today is as a GHRP-class partner to a GHRH analog for an amplified GH pulse
- **Important framing**: GHRP-1 is largely redundant with better-characterized GHRPs (GHRP-2, ipamorelin); its benefits are class-typical and based on older research and anecdote, not robust modern trials
