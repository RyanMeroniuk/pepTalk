# GHRP-1

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT FDA-approved** for any indication — no GHRP-1 drug product exists
- **Characterized mainly in older pharmacological research** (1980s–1990s) as the GHRP class was being defined; largely superseded by GHRP-2 and ipamorelin
- **Sold only as a "research chemical"** through gray-market suppliers, explicitly not for human use
- **Not a dietary supplement** and not legally marketable for human consumption
- **Quality is unregulated**: purity, sterility, dosing accuracy, and identity are unverified, and GHRP-1 is less common than its siblings so mislabeling risk is higher
- **Banned in sport**: GH secretagogues/GHRPs are prohibited by WADA at all times
- **Legal status varies by jurisdiction**; importing/possessing unapproved injectables can carry legal risk
- **Bottom line**: Unapproved, niche, and largely redundant with better-studied GHRPs — documented here for completeness
