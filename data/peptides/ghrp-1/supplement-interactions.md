# GHRP-1

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Synergistic — Glycine**: Supports GH release and sleep quality, complementing the pre-bed GH pulse (3g before bed)
- **Synergistic — Magnesium**: Supports sleep, recovery, and insulin sensitivity, which helps offset GH's mild glucose effects (300–400mg/day)
- **Synergistic — L-Arginine/Ornithine context**: Amino acids that support the GH axis, though effects are modest
- **Enhanced by — Vitamin D3**: Supports the GH/IGF-1 axis and overall hormonal health (2,000–5,000 IU/day)
- **Enhanced by — Zinc**: Supports IGF-1 production and recovery (15–30mg/day with copper balance)
- **Synergistic — Ashwagandha**: May help blunt the mild cortisol bump GHRP-1 can cause and supports sleep/recovery (300–600mg/day)
- **Caution — large/fatty meals or high-glucose foods near injection**: Blunt the GH pulse; keep injections away from big meals
- **Caution — anything raising prolactin**: Be mindful when combining with other prolactin-raising agents given GHRP-1's mild prolactin effect
