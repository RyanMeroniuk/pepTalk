# KPV

## Overview

KPV is a naturally occurring tripeptide (Lys-Pro-Val) derived from the C-terminal end of alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone (α-MSH). It retains the potent anti-inflammatory properties of α-MSH without its pigmentation effects. Primarily used for gut inflammation, skin inflammation, and wound healing. Gaining popularity in gut healing protocols as a complement to BPC-157.
