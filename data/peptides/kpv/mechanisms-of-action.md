# KPV

## Mechanisms of Action

- **NF-kB Inhibition**: Suppresses nuclear factor kappa B — a master regulator of inflammatory gene expression
- **Anti-Inflammatory Cytokines**: Modulates inflammatory signaling cascades
- **Interleukin-10 Regulation**: Regulates IL-10 production by keratinocytes, preventing skin inflammation
- **Anti-Allergic**: Acts as a natural anti-allergic basophil-response modifier
- **Wound Healing**: Promotes tissue repair alongside anti-inflammatory action
- **Intestinal Barrier**: Supports gut epithelial barrier integrity
- **No Pigmentation**: Unlike parent molecule α-MSH, does not stimulate melanocytes
