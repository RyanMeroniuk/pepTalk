# KPV

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Immunosuppressants (tacrolimus, cyclosporine, mycophenolate, azathioprine)**: KPV modulates immune function via NF-kB inhibition. Combining with immunosuppressive drugs may cause additive or unpredictable immune suppression, increasing infection risk
- **Biologic therapies for IBD (infliximab, adalimumab, vedolizumab, ustekinumab)**: KPV targets overlapping inflammatory pathways (NF-kB, cytokine modulation) with these biologics. Concurrent use may cause excessive immune suppression or unpredictable immune modulation. Coordinate with your gastroenterologist before combining
- **TNF-alpha inhibitors (infliximab, adalimumab, etanercept)**: KPV suppresses pro-inflammatory cytokines including TNF-alpha pathway signaling. Additive suppression may increase risk of opportunistic infections
- **Systemic corticosteroids (prednisone, budesonide)**: Both KPV and corticosteroids suppress inflammation through partially overlapping mechanisms. Combined use may over-suppress the immune response. Monitor for signs of infection
- **JAK inhibitors (tofacitinib, upadacitinib)**: These drugs and KPV both modulate inflammatory signaling cascades. Potential for additive immunosuppression
- **Methotrexate**: Combined immune-modulating effects may increase susceptibility to infection. Monitor blood counts and liver function
- **Contraindicated: Active systemic infection** — KPV's immune-modulating effects could impair the body's ability to fight acute infections
- **Contraindicated: Pregnancy and breastfeeding** — insufficient safety data in humans; effects on fetal immune development are unknown
- **Caution: Individuals on multiple immunomodulatory therapies** — the risk of compounded immune suppression increases with each added agent
- **Caution: History of opportunistic infections** — KPV's immune modulation may lower defenses in already-vulnerable individuals
