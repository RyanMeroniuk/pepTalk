# KPV

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Eat in a Parasympathetic State**: The gut heals best when the nervous system is in "rest and digest" mode. Sit down for meals, take 3-5 deep breaths before eating, chew each bite 20-30 times, and avoid eating while stressed, driving, or working. KPV reduces gut inflammation, but chronic stress-eating undermines barrier repair
- **Meal Spacing for MMC Function**: Space meals 3-4 hours apart and avoid constant snacking. The migrating motor complex (MMC) — the gut's self-cleaning wave — only activates during fasting periods between meals. MMC dysfunction contributes to SIBO and bacterial overgrowth, worsening the inflammation KPV is trying to resolve
- **Sleep for Gut Repair**: Gut epithelial cells turn over every 3-5 days, and much of this repair occurs during sleep. Target 7-9 hours nightly. Poor sleep increases intestinal permeability and inflammatory cytokine production, directly opposing KPV's NF-kB inhibition
- **Stress-Gut Axis Management**: The gut-brain axis means psychological stress directly increases intestinal permeability and inflammatory markers. Practice daily vagal toning: deep diaphragmatic breathing (4-7-8 pattern), cold water face immersion, humming, or gargling. These activate the vagus nerve and promote parasympathetic gut function
- **Gentle Movement**: Walk for 10-15 minutes after meals to promote motility and blood flow to the GI tract. Avoid high-intensity exercise within 1 hour of eating — blood diverts away from the digestive system during intense activity
- **Elimination Diet Consideration**: If gut inflammation is the primary target, consider a 30-day elimination of common triggers (gluten, dairy, corn, soy, eggs) and reintroduce one at a time every 3-4 days while monitoring symptoms. KPV reduces inflammation, but continued exposure to trigger foods creates a cycle of damage and repair
- **Hydration Between Meals**: Drink water between meals rather than during meals to avoid diluting digestive enzymes. Adequate hydration supports mucosal lining integrity — target half your body weight in ounces daily
- **Skin Protocol (if using for dermatitis/eczema)**: For skin inflammation, combine topical KPV with gentle, fragrance-free skincare. Avoid hot showers (strip skin barrier), use lukewarm water, and apply moisturizer within 3 minutes of bathing to lock in hydration
- **Probiotics Timing**: If taking probiotics alongside KPV, take them on an empty stomach (first thing in the morning or before bed) for optimal colonization. Space at least 30 minutes from KPV dosing
