# KPV

## Foods to Eat

- **Bone Broth**: Rich in glutamine, glycine, and collagen — critical for gut epithelial repair. Glutamine is the primary fuel source for intestinal enterocytes. Consume 1-2 cups daily during gut healing protocols
- **Fermented Foods**: Sauerkraut (unpasteurized), kimchi, kefir, yogurt (if dairy-tolerant), miso, and kvass provide live beneficial bacteria that support microbiome diversity and reduce pathogenic overgrowth. Start with small amounts (1-2 tbsp) and increase gradually
- **Cooked Vegetables**: Steamed or roasted zucchini, squash, carrots, sweet potatoes, and beets are easier on an inflamed gut than raw vegetables. Cooking breaks down cellulose fibers that can irritate a compromised gut lining
- **Wild-Caught Fatty Fish**: Salmon, sardines, and mackerel provide EPA/DHA omega-3s that resolve inflammation through specialized pro-resolving mediators (SPMs), complementing KPV's anti-inflammatory action
- **Ginger & Turmeric**: Both are potent natural NF-kB inhibitors, synergizing with KPV's primary mechanism. Use fresh ginger in teas and cooking; take turmeric with black pepper and fat for absorption
- **Aloe Vera Juice (Inner Fillet)**: Soothes inflamed gut mucosa and supports epithelial repair. Start with 2 oz daily and increase to 4 oz
- **Slippery Elm & Marshmallow Root Tea**: Mucilaginous herbs that coat and protect inflamed gut lining, providing a physical barrier while KPV reduces underlying inflammation
- **Coconut Oil & MCT Oil**: Medium-chain triglycerides have antimicrobial properties (caprylic acid) and are easy to digest, reducing burden on an inflamed GI tract
- **Bananas (Ripe)**: Gentle on the gut, provide prebiotic fiber (fructooligosaccharides) that feed beneficial bacteria, and contain pectin that supports mucosal healing
- **Pumpkin & Winter Squash**: Anti-inflammatory, rich in beta-carotene (vitamin A precursor essential for gut epithelial integrity), and easy to digest when cooked
- **Manuka Honey (UMF 10+)**: Contains methylglyoxal with antimicrobial and gut-healing properties. Use 1 tsp daily — supports mucosal repair
