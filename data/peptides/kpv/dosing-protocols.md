# KPV

## Dosing Protocols

- **Oral (Gut Focus)**: 200-500 mcg daily, or as directed by provider
- **Subcutaneous**: 200-500 mcg daily
- **Topical**: Applied to skin for localized inflammatory conditions
- **Cycle**: 4-8 weeks on, 2-4 weeks off
- **Timing**: Can take with or without food
- **Often combined with BPC-157** for comprehensive gut healing
