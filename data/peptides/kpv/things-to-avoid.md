# KPV

## Things to Avoid

- **Stress Eating**: Eating when stressed, anxious, or emotionally activated diverts blood away from the digestive tract and increases gut permeability through cortisol-mediated mechanisms. This directly undermines KPV's gut-healing action
- **NSAIDs (Ibuprofen, Naproxen, Aspirin)**: NSAIDs damage the gut mucosa and increase intestinal permeability — the exact conditions KPV is working to repair. Use acetaminophen if pain relief is needed, or explore BPC-157 as an alternative
- **Antibiotics (Unless Medically Necessary)**: Antibiotics devastate the gut microbiome, killing beneficial bacteria and allowing pathogenic overgrowth. If antibiotics are unavoidable, use Saccharomyces boulardii probiotic during treatment and rebuild with broad-spectrum probiotics afterward
- **Chronic Stress Without Management**: The gut contains 70% of the immune system and is directly connected to the brain via the vagus nerve. Unmanaged chronic stress keeps the gut in a perpetual inflammatory state. Daily stress management is as important as the peptide itself
- **Snacking & Grazing**: Constant eating prevents MMC activation, leading to bacterial overgrowth and increased gut inflammation. Eat defined meals and allow 3-4 hours between them
- **Alcohol**: Even occasional drinking damages the gut lining and disrupts microbiome balance. Zero alcohol during KPV gut healing protocols
- **Rushing Through Meals**: Inadequate chewing increases the burden on the digestive system. Poorly broken down food particles can trigger immune responses in a permeable gut. Chew each bite thoroughly
- **Environmental Toxins**: Pesticides (glyphosate), BPA from plastics, and chlorine in tap water all damage gut barrier function. Choose organic produce, use glass food containers, and filter drinking water
- **Proton Pump Inhibitors (Long-Term)**: PPIs reduce stomach acid, which impairs protein digestion and allows bacterial overgrowth in the small intestine. Discuss tapering with your healthcare provider if using long-term
