# IGF-1 LR3 (Long R3 Insulin-like Growth Factor-1)

## Drug Interactions

- **Insulin and Sulfonylureas**: Severe interaction — both lower blood glucose through different mechanisms; combining with IGF-1 LR3 creates extreme hypoglycemia risk that can be life-threatening; avoid or use only under medical supervision with continuous glucose monitoring
- **Metformin**: Moderate interaction — metformin activates AMPK (which opposes mTOR) and lowers blood glucose; may blunt anabolic effects and increase hypoglycemia risk; monitor glucose closely if both are used
- **Corticosteroids (Prednisone, Dexamethasone)**: Corticosteroids are catabolic and raise blood glucose — they oppose IGF-1 LR3's anabolic and glucose-lowering effects, potentially negating benefits while masking hypoglycemia symptoms
- **Chemotherapy and Anti-Cancer Drugs**: IGF-1 LR3 promotes cell proliferation and may interfere with cytotoxic drug mechanisms; contraindicated during cancer treatment
- **Thyroid Hormones (Levothyroxine, T3/T4)**: Thyroid hormones modulate IGF-1 sensitivity and metabolic rate; dosing changes in thyroid medication may alter IGF-1 LR3 response; inform prescribing physician of peptide use
- **Beta-Blockers**: May mask hypoglycemia symptoms (tremor, rapid heart rate), making it harder to detect low blood sugar during IGF-1 LR3 use
- **Pregnancy/Breastfeeding**: Absolutely contraindicated — IGF-1 LR3 is a potent growth factor with unknown effects on fetal development and lactation
