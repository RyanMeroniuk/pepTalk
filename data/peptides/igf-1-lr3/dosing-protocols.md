# IGF-1 LR3 (Long R3 Insulin-like Growth Factor-1)

## Dosing Protocols

- **Subcutaneous Injection**: 20-50 mcg/day for general anabolic and body composition effects — most common protocol
- **Intramuscular Injection**: 20-40 mcg injected bilaterally into target muscle groups post-workout for localized growth effects
- **Beginner Protocol**: Start at 20 mcg/day for 1-2 weeks to assess tolerance before increasing
- **Advanced Protocol**: 50-100 mcg/day, split into AM/PM doses — significantly increases hypoglycemia risk
- **Cycle Length**: 4-6 weeks on, 4 weeks off — longer cycles risk IGF-1 receptor desensitization and potential organ growth
- **Timing**: Post-workout administration is preferred to capitalize on elevated blood flow to trained muscles; on rest days, inject in the morning
- **Reconstitution**: Use bacteriostatic water, inject slowly along the vial wall — IGF-1 LR3 is fragile and can denature with agitation
- **Storage**: Reconstituted solution stable for 2-4 weeks refrigerated at 2-8°C; do not freeze
- **No human clinical trials exist for performance dosing** — all protocols are derived from research contexts and community experience
