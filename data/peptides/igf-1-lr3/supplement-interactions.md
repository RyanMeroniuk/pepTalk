# IGF-1 LR3 (Long R3 Insulin-like Growth Factor-1)

## Supplement Interactions

- **Synergistic — Creatine Monohydrate**: Both enhance intracellular hydration and mTOR signaling; combined use may amplify muscle protein synthesis (5g/day)
- **Synergistic — L-Leucine/EAAs**: Essential amino acids provide the raw substrate for the elevated protein synthesis IGF-1 LR3 drives; take with post-workout meal (3-5g leucine)
- **Synergistic — Magnesium Glycinate**: Supports insulin sensitivity and glucose metabolism, helping buffer the hypoglycemic effects of IGF-1 LR3 (400-800mg/day)
- **Synergistic — Omega-3 Fatty Acids**: Anti-inflammatory support and improved insulin sensitivity complement IGF-1 LR3's anabolic effects (2-4g EPA/DHA daily)
- **Synergistic — Vitamin D3**: IGF-1 signaling is partially dependent on adequate vitamin D status; deficiency may blunt anabolic response (5,000 IU/day)
- **Caution — Berberine**: Activates AMPK which opposes mTOR signaling — may partially blunt the anabolic effects of IGF-1 LR3; separate timing by several hours if both are used
- **Caution — Alpha Lipoic Acid (ALA)**: Also lowers blood glucose independently; combining with IGF-1 LR3 significantly increases hypoglycemia risk — reduce dose or monitor closely
- **Enhanced by — Zinc**: Zinc is required for IGF-1 receptor function and IGF-1 production; deficiency impairs the peptide's effectiveness (30-50mg/day with copper balance)
- **Caution — High-dose Curcumin**: May inhibit IGF-1R signaling at high doses; standard supplemental doses (500mg) are likely fine, but mega-dosing could reduce efficacy
