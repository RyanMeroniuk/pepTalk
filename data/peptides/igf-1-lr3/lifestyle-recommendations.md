# IGF-1 LR3 (Long R3 Insulin-like Growth Factor-1)

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Resistance Training**: IGF-1 LR3 is most effective when combined with progressive resistance training. Train each muscle group 2x/week with compound movements to maximize satellite cell activation and nutrient partitioning. The peptide amplifies the training stimulus, so intensity matters more than volume.
- **Protein Intake**: Increase protein intake to 1.2-1.5g per pound of bodyweight during an IGF-1 LR3 cycle. The elevated protein synthesis rate demands significantly more substrate — insufficient protein will blunt results and waste the peptide's potential.
- **Carbohydrate Management**: Because IGF-1 LR3 lowers blood glucose, maintain steady carbohydrate intake throughout the day. Never inject on an empty stomach. Keep fast-acting carbs (glucose tabs, juice) available at all times, especially during the first week when you're assessing your glucose response.
- **Meal Timing**: Eat a meal containing protein and carbohydrates within 30 minutes of injection. Post-workout injection should coincide with your post-workout meal to capitalize on elevated blood flow and glucose uptake.
- **Sleep**: Prioritize 7-9 hours of quality sleep. Growth and repair processes driven by IGF-1 signaling are most active during deep sleep. Poor sleep blunts mTOR activation and growth hormone release, reducing the peptide's effectiveness.
- **Hydration**: IGF-1 LR3 increases intracellular water retention. Increase water intake to at least 1 gallon per day to support cell volumization and prevent dehydration-related side effects like headaches and cramping.
- **Blood Glucose Monitoring**: Consider using a continuous glucose monitor (CGM) or at minimum a fingerstick glucometer during the first week of use. This is especially important at higher doses or when stacking with other glucose-lowering compounds.
- **Avoid Alcohol**: Alcohol impairs protein synthesis, disrupts sleep architecture, and independently lowers blood glucose — all of which are counterproductive and potentially dangerous during IGF-1 LR3 use.
- **Bloodwork**: Get baseline bloodwork before starting (fasting glucose, fasting insulin, IGF-1, HbA1c, complete metabolic panel) and recheck at the end of each cycle. Monitor for any changes in tumor markers if you have risk factors.
