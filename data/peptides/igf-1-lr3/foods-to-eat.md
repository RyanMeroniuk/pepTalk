# IGF-1 LR3 (Long R3 Insulin-like Growth Factor-1)

## Foods to Eat

- **Lean Proteins (chicken, turkey, fish, eggs)**: Provides the essential amino acids needed to fuel the elevated protein synthesis rate; aim for a protein source at every meal, prioritizing leucine-rich options like chicken breast and eggs
- **Complex Carbohydrates (sweet potatoes, oats, rice)**: Sustained glucose release helps prevent the hypoglycemic episodes that IGF-1 LR3 can trigger; include at every meal and especially pre/post-injection
- **Fatty Fish (salmon, mackerel, sardines)**: Rich in omega-3 EPA/DHA which support insulin sensitivity and provide anti-inflammatory benefits that complement the peptide's recovery effects; aim for 3-4 servings per week
- **Cruciferous Vegetables (broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts)**: Contain indole-3-carbinol and sulforaphane which support healthy estrogen metabolism — important since IGF-1 can interact with estrogen signaling pathways
- **Berries (blueberries, strawberries, blackberries)**: High in antioxidants that protect cells from oxidative stress during the accelerated growth and repair processes; 1-2 cups daily
- **Eggs (whole)**: One of the most complete protein sources with a full amino acid profile plus choline, which supports cell membrane synthesis during hyperplastic growth; 3-4 whole eggs daily
- **Avocados**: Provide monounsaturated fats and potassium that support insulin sensitivity and electrolyte balance; the healthy fats also slow glucose absorption, helping stabilize blood sugar
- **Legumes (lentils, chickpeas, black beans)**: Excellent source of slow-digesting carbohydrates and plant protein; the high fiber content helps moderate blood glucose response
- **Greek Yogurt**: High in protein (particularly casein) and provides probiotics for gut health; the slow-digesting casein provides sustained amino acid delivery between meals
- **Bone Broth**: Rich in collagen, glycine, and proline which support the connective tissue repair that IGF-1 LR3 stimulates; 1-2 cups daily, especially during injury recovery protocols
- **Liver and Organ Meats**: Dense in zinc, B vitamins, and iron — all cofactors for IGF-1 receptor function and cellular energy production during accelerated growth
- **Bananas**: Quick-acting natural carbohydrate source useful for managing mild hypoglycemic episodes; also provides potassium for electrolyte balance during water retention
