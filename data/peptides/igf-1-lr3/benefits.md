# IGF-1 LR3 (Long R3 Insulin-like Growth Factor-1)

## Benefits

- **Muscle Growth**: Potent stimulator of both muscle hypertrophy (larger fibers) and hyperplasia (new fibers), making it one of the most powerful anabolic peptides available
- **Fat Loss**: Promotes nutrient partitioning away from fat storage and toward muscle tissue, enhancing body recomposition even at maintenance calories
- **Recovery Acceleration**: Dramatically reduces recovery time between training sessions by upregulating protein synthesis and reducing muscle protein breakdown simultaneously
- **Connective Tissue Repair**: Stimulates collagen production and fibroblast proliferation, supporting tendon, ligament, and joint recovery from injury or heavy training
- **Anti-Catabolic Protection**: Prevents muscle wasting during caloric deficit, illness, or periods of reduced training by blocking protein degradation pathways
- **Nutrient Partitioning**: Increases glucose and amino acid uptake into muscle cells while reducing fat storage, improving the ratio of muscle gained to fat stored
- **Satellite Cell Activation**: Recruits and activates muscle stem cells (satellite cells), enabling long-term structural changes in muscle tissue that persist after the peptide is discontinued
- **Bone Density Support**: Stimulates osteoblast activity and bone mineral deposition, potentially beneficial for injury recovery and long-term skeletal health
- **Wound Healing**: Accelerates healing of skin wounds, surgical incisions, and soft tissue injuries through growth factor signaling
- **Insulin Sensitivity**: Can improve peripheral insulin sensitivity through enhanced GLUT4 expression, though this effect must be balanced against potential hypoglycemia risk
