# IGF-1 LR3 (Long R3 Insulin-like Growth Factor-1)

## Mechanisms of Action

- **IGF-1 Receptor Activation**: Binds the IGF-1R with similar affinity to native IGF-1, activating the PI3K/Akt and MAPK/ERK signaling cascades that drive cell growth, proliferation, and survival
- **Reduced IGFBP Binding**: The LR3 modification reduces binding affinity to all six IGF-binding proteins by over 100-fold, keeping the peptide in its free (active) form far longer than endogenous IGF-1
- **Muscle Hyperplasia**: Stimulates satellite cell proliferation and differentiation, potentially increasing the number of muscle fibers (hyperplasia) rather than just the size of existing fibers (hypertrophy)
- **Protein Synthesis Upregulation**: Activates mTOR signaling via PI3K/Akt pathway, directly increasing muscle protein synthesis rates and nitrogen retention
- **Anti-Catabolic Effects**: Inhibits muscle protein breakdown through Akt-mediated suppression of the ubiquitin-proteasome pathway and FoxO transcription factors
- **Glucose Uptake Enhancement**: Increases glucose transport into muscle cells via GLUT4 translocation, independent of insulin, contributing to nutrient partitioning
- **Lipid Metabolism**: Promotes fatty acid oxidation and inhibits lipogenesis in adipose tissue, shifting nutrient partitioning toward muscle and away from fat storage
- **Cell Survival Signaling**: Activates anti-apoptotic pathways (Bcl-2 upregulation, Bad phosphorylation) that protect cells from programmed death during stress conditions
- **Connective Tissue Growth**: Stimulates collagen synthesis and proliferation of fibroblasts, chondrocytes, and osteoblasts, supporting tendon, cartilage, and bone repair
