# IGF-1 LR3 (Long R3 Insulin-like Growth Factor-1)

## Side Effects

- **Hypoglycemia**: The most immediate and potentially dangerous side effect — IGF-1 LR3 lowers blood glucose by increasing cellular uptake; symptoms include dizziness, shakiness, sweating, confusion, and in severe cases, loss of consciousness
- **Gut Distension**: Prolonged use at high doses may stimulate growth of smooth muscle in the intestinal walls, contributing to visible abdominal distension
- **Joint Pain**: Some users report transient joint pain or swelling, particularly in the hands and wrists, likely due to water retention and connective tissue growth
- **Jaw/Extremity Growth**: Extended use at high doses carries theoretical risk of acromegalic-like effects (growth of hands, feet, jaw) due to sustained growth factor stimulation
- **Tumor Risk**: IGF-1 signaling is mitogenic — it promotes cell proliferation, which may accelerate growth of existing pre-cancerous or cancerous cells; individuals with a history of cancer should avoid use entirely
- **Water Retention**: Mild to moderate water retention is common, particularly in the first 1-2 weeks; usually resolves or stabilizes
- **Injection Site Reactions**: Redness, swelling, or irritation at the injection site; rotate injection locations to minimize
- **Headaches**: Some users report mild headaches during the first week of use, typically resolving with continued administration
- **Lethargy**: Fatigue and drowsiness reported at higher doses, possibly related to blood glucose fluctuations
