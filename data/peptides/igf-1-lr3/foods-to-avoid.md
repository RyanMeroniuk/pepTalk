# IGF-1 LR3 (Long R3 Insulin-like Growth Factor-1)

## Foods to Avoid

- **Alcohol**: Directly impairs protein synthesis by up to 20%, disrupts sleep quality, and lowers blood glucose — tripling the hypoglycemia risk when combined with IGF-1 LR3
- **Highly Processed Sugars**: While IGF-1 LR3 lowers blood glucose, relying on candy and soda creates erratic glucose spikes and crashes; use complex carbs for steady energy instead
- **Trans Fats and Processed Seed Oils**: Promote systemic inflammation, impair insulin receptor sensitivity, and interfere with the nutrient partitioning benefits of IGF-1 LR3
- **Excessive Soy Products**: High soy intake may modulate estrogen and IGF-1 receptor signaling; moderate intake is fine, but avoid making soy the primary protein source during a cycle
- **Very Low-Carb or Ketogenic Diets**: Extremely low carbohydrate intake combined with IGF-1 LR3's glucose-lowering effect creates serious hypoglycemia risk; maintain at least moderate carbohydrate intake (150g+/day)
- **Excessive Caffeine**: High doses can further lower blood glucose and mask early hypoglycemia symptoms like shakiness; limit to 200-300mg/day and avoid taking caffeine near injection time
- **Raw Cruciferous in Excess**: While cooked cruciferous vegetables are beneficial, excessive raw consumption can impair thyroid function, which interacts with IGF-1 signaling
- **Ultra-Processed Foods**: Lack the micronutrient density needed to support accelerated growth and repair; empty calories waste the anabolic window IGF-1 LR3 creates
- **Large Amounts of Dairy (for some individuals)**: Dairy naturally raises IGF-1 levels, which when combined with exogenous IGF-1 LR3 may push total IGF-1 signaling higher than intended; monitor response individually
