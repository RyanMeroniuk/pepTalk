# GHRP-2 (Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide-2)

## Benefits

- **Strong GH Release**: More potent than Ipamorelin or Sermorelin
- **Fat Loss**: Markedly greater fat-burning effect compared to other GHRPs
- **Muscle Growth**: Enhanced protein synthesis via GH/IGF-1
- **Recovery**: Accelerated tissue repair and exercise recovery
- **Sleep**: Improved deep sleep quality
- **Cardioprotection**: GHRP class demonstrates cytoprotective effects on cardiac tissue
- **Bone Density**: Improved with sustained use
