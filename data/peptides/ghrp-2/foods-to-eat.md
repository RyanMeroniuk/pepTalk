# GHRP-2 (Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide-2)

## Foods to Eat

- **Chicken breast and turkey** — lean protein satisfies GHRP-2's appetite stimulation without excess calories. High arginine content also supports GH release
- **Wild-caught salmon** — omega-3s reduce inflammation and support GH receptor sensitivity. The protein and fat content provide lasting satiety against the appetite increase
- **Pasture-raised eggs** — complete amino acid profile, cholesterol for hormone synthesis. Satiating breakfast option when morning hunger is elevated
- **Lean beef (grass-fed)** — rich in arginine, ornithine, and complete protein. GHRP-2's fat-burning effect means dietary fat is utilized more efficiently
- **Greek yogurt (plain)** — glutamine independently supports GH release; protein keeps you full. Eat at meals away from injection times
- **Cottage cheese before bed** — slow-release casein protein feeds muscle repair overnight during GHRP-2's enhanced GH window
- **Lentils and black beans** — high fiber and protein provide long-lasting satiety, critical for managing GHRP-2's appetite increase
- **Steel-cut oats** — complex carbs with beta-glucan fiber stabilize blood sugar. Eat at meals well-separated from injection
- **Sweet potatoes** — moderate glycemic index carbs with fiber; replenish glycogen without sharp insulin spikes
- **Spinach and kale** — magnesium supports sleep and GH axis; virtually zero calories helps manage intake
- **Cruciferous vegetables (broccoli, cauliflower)** — fiber-rich, filling, and very low calorie. Essential for managing the appetite increase without gaining unwanted fat
- **Bone broth** — glycine supports sleep, collagen precursors complement GH-driven tissue repair, and warm liquid is satiating
- **Pumpkin seeds** — zinc and magnesium, both critical for GH production and sleep quality
