# GHRP-2 (Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide-2)

## Side Effects

- **Appetite increase**: Moderate (less than GHRP-6 but more than Ipamorelin)
- **Elevated cortisol**: Slight, dose-dependent increase
- **Elevated prolactin**: Slight, dose-dependent increase
- **Water retention**: Mild to moderate
- **Tingling/numbness**: Transient
- **Injection site reactions**: Redness, irritation
- Less clean hormonal profile than Ipamorelin but more potent GH release
