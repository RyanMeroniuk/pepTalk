# GHRP-2 (Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide-2)

## Things to Avoid

- **Eating within 2 hours before injection** — the most critical mistake. GHRP-2's strong GH pulse is significantly blunted by food-induced insulin. This is especially important because GHRP-2's potency advantage over Ipamorelin is wasted if the pulse is suppressed
- **Giving in to every appetite signal** — GHRP-2's ghrelin activation creates genuine hunger, but the caloric need is not proportional to the hunger signal. Pre-plan meals, portion food, and drink water when hunger strikes between meals
- **Ignoring cortisol and prolactin monitoring** — unlike Ipamorelin, GHRP-2 stimulates both cortisol and prolactin in a dose-dependent manner. Without monitoring, chronic elevation can cause fatigue (cortisol) or hormonal imbalances (prolactin)
- **High doses to chase more GH** — increasing beyond 200-300mcg per injection increases cortisol and prolactin disproportionately compared to additional GH release. More is not better
- **Alcohol on injection days** — suppresses GH, disrupts sleep, adds empty calories, and the appetite increase from GHRP-2 combined with alcohol-lowered inhibitions often leads to binge eating
- **Chronic cardio without adequate recovery** — prolonged moderate-intensity cardio raises cortisol. Since GHRP-2 already slightly elevates cortisol, stacking chronic cardio on top creates an unfavorable hormonal environment
- **Skipping resistance training** — GHRP-2 has a markedly stronger fat-burning effect than other GHRPs. Without the resistance training stimulus, you miss this key benefit
- **Combining with other compounds that raise cortisol or prolactin** — be cautious stacking GHRP-2 with GHRP-6 or hexarelin, which also elevate these hormones
- **Chronic unmanaged stress** — external stress plus GHRP-2's mild cortisol stimulation creates compounding cortisol elevation that opposes GH benefits
