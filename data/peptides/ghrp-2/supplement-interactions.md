# GHRP-2 (Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide-2)

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Best Stack**: CJC-1295 no DAC for synergistic GH release
- **Synergistic**: Resistance training, adequate protein
- **Monitor**: Cortisol and prolactin with prolonged use
- **Caution**: Diabetes medications — affects glucose metabolism
- **Contraindicated**: Active cancer, pregnancy/breastfeeding
