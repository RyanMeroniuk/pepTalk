# GHRP-2 (Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide-2)

## Dosing Protocols

- **Standard**: 100-300 mcg subcutaneous injection, 2-3x daily
- **Common Dose**: 100-200 mcg per injection
- **Timing**: Morning fasted, post-workout, and/or before bed
- **Must fast**: 2+ hours before injection
- **Cycle**: 8-12 weeks on, 4 weeks off
- **Best combined with**: CJC-1295 no DAC for synergistic GH amplification
- **Reconstitution**: Bacteriostatic water; refrigerate
