# GHRP-2 (Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide-2)

## Lifestyle Recommendations


### Injection Timing & Fasting
- **Inject in a fasted state** — minimum 2 hours after last meal. Insulin and free fatty acids from food suppress pituitary GH release, blunting GHRP-2's potent pulse
- **Three optimal injection windows**: (1) Morning fasted upon waking, (2) Post-workout if fasted or 2+ hours post-meal, (3) Before bed — the most important window
- **Wait 20-30 minutes after injection before eating** — allow the GH pulse to peak before introducing insulin
- **Bedtime injection is highest priority** — amplifies the natural nocturnal GH surge during deep sleep
- **GHRP-2 stimulates appetite moderately** — less than GHRP-6 but noticeably more than Ipamorelin. Plan for this, especially with morning and pre-bed injections

### Exercise
- **Resistance training 4-5x/week** — GHRP-2's strong GH release creates a powerful anabolic environment. Compound movements (squats, deadlifts, bench press, rows, overhead press) maximize this advantage
- **GHRP-2 has a pronounced fat-burning effect** — pair with resistance training and controlled nutrition to capitalize on this unique benefit among GHRPs
- **HIIT 2-3x/week** — sprint intervals, rowing, or kettlebell complexes for 15-20 minutes. Lactic acid from intense exercise independently stimulates additional GH release
- **Train in the evening before your bedtime injection** — the exercise-induced GH pulse stacks with GHRP-2's potent stimulation for maximum overnight recovery and growth
- **Keep rest periods at 60-90 seconds** — moderate rest elevates lactic acid, amplifying the GH response
- **Avoid excessive endurance training** — prolonged moderate cardio (60+ minutes) raises cortisol. GHRP-2 already produces slight cortisol elevation; adding more from overtraining compounds the issue

### Managing Appetite
- **Expect moderate appetite increase** — GHRP-2 activates ghrelin pathways. The hunger is real but manageable (roughly half the intensity of GHRP-6)
- **Use the appetite strategically** — if you are trying to build muscle, time your post-injection meal (after the 20-30 minute wait) to be a high-protein, nutrient-dense meal
- **If cutting or maintaining weight**: dose before bed to sleep through the worst appetite spike, and keep pre-portioned meals ready for daytime doses
- **High-protein, high-fiber meals** are key — they satisfy the ghrelin-driven hunger far more effectively than carb-heavy foods
- **Drink 16oz of water after injection** — helps manage hunger signals and supports hydration

### Sleep Optimization
- **7-9 hours of quality sleep** — GHRP-2 enhances deep sleep via GH pulse amplification. Sufficient sleep duration is essential
- **Consistent sleep schedule** — same bedtime and wake time daily to optimize circadian GH rhythm
- **Cool, dark, quiet bedroom (65-68°F)** — optimal conditions for melatonin and GH secretion
- **Bedtime injection 15-20 minutes before lying down** — times the GH pulse onset to coincide with deep sleep entry

### Monitoring & Daily Habits
- **Blood work every 8-12 weeks**: IGF-1, fasting glucose, cortisol, prolactin, and a metabolic panel. GHRP-2's cortisol and prolactin stimulation (unlike Ipamorelin) requires monitoring
- **If cortisol trends upward**, consider reducing dose or switching to Ipamorelin for a cleaner hormonal profile
- **If prolactin trends upward**, discuss with your prescriber. Vitamin B6 (P-5-P form, 50-100mg) may help modulate prolactin
- **Morning sunlight (15-20 minutes)** — sets circadian rhythm and optimizes nocturnal GH pulsatility
- **Stress management** — GHRP-2 already mildly elevates cortisol. Adding chronic psychological stress on top creates an unfavorable cortisol environment. Practice meditation, walks, or breathwork daily
