# GHRP-2 (Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide-2)

## Foods to Avoid

- **Sugar and refined carbs within 2 hours of injection** — insulin directly suppresses the GH pulse. GHRP-2 produces one of the strongest GH pulses of any GHRP; do not waste it with poorly timed sugar intake
- **High-fat meals near injection** — free fatty acids independently blunt pituitary GH secretion
- **Processed and fast food** — when GHRP-2 increases appetite, the temptation to eat convenience foods is high. These are inflammatory, high-sodium, and high-sugar — all counterproductive
- **Alcohol** — suppresses GH by up to 75%, disrupts sleep, and the empty calories combined with GHRP-2's appetite increase can cause rapid fat gain
- **Excessive sodium** — promotes water retention, which GHRP-2 can already cause. Avoid canned soups, chips, deli meats, and restaurant meals
- **Sugary cereals and granola** — marketed as healthy but spike blood sugar rapidly; particularly problematic near injection times
- **Fruit juice** — concentrated sugar without fiber; eat whole fruit instead
- **Large meals immediately before bed** — if dosing before sleep, a full stomach blunts GH release and impairs sleep quality
