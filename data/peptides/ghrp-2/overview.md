# GHRP-2 (Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide-2)

## Overview

GHRP-2 (pralmorelin, D-Ala-D-beta-Nal-Trp-D-Phe-Lys-NH2) is a synthetic hexapeptide growth hormone secretagogue that acts on the ghrelin receptor (GHS-R1a) to stimulate growth hormone release. It is a super-analog of GHRP-6 with enhanced potency and a markedly stronger fat-burning effect. GHRP-2 releases more GH than GHRH alone. Among the GHRPs, it offers a good balance between potency and side effects — more powerful than Ipamorelin but with fewer side effects than GHRP-6.
