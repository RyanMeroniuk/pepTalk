# GHRP-2 (Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide-2)

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Ghrelin Receptor Agonist**: Binds GHS-R1a in hypothalamus and pituitary
- **GH Release**: Stimulates potent GH pulses — releases more GH than GHRH alone
- **Somatostatin Suppression**: Reduces inhibitory somatostatin signaling
- **Hypothalamic Stimulation**: Stimulates hypothalamic neurons to release GHRH (amplification effect)
- **IGF-1 Elevation**: Raises IGF-1 via GH-mediated pathway
- **Appetite Stimulation**: Moderate ghrelin-mediated appetite increase (less than GHRP-6)
- **Cortisol/Prolactin**: Slight stimulatory effect on ACTH, cortisol, and prolactin (dose-dependent)
