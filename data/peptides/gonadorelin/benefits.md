# Gonadorelin

## Benefits

- **Maintains Testicular Function on TRT**: Used in TRT protocols to keep the testes active and reduce the shrinkage and shutdown caused by exogenous testosterone, by preserving LH/FSH signaling.
- **hCG Alternative**: Increasingly used via compounding pharmacies as a shorter-acting alternative to hCG for preserving fertility and testicular volume.
- **Fertility Support**: Pulsatile gonadorelin can induce ovulation or support spermatogenesis in certain hypothalamic forms of infertility (an approved use).
- **Endogenous (Not Exogenous) Stimulation**: Works by prompting the body's own hormone production rather than supplying outside hormones, which some prefer for HPG-axis preservation.
- **Diagnostic Use**: Used clinically to test pituitary gonadotropin reserve.
- **Post-Cycle / Axis Recovery**: Sometimes used to help restart the HPG axis, conceptually similar to kisspeptin's upstream role.
- **Note**: Benefits depend heavily on correct pulsatile dosing; improper continuous dosing produces the opposite (suppressive) effect.
