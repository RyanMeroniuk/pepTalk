# Gonadorelin

## Dosing Protocols

- **Pulsatile Dosing Is Essential**: Must be given in pulses to stimulate (not suppress) the pituitary; continuous exposure downregulates the axis. This is the single most important dosing principle.
- **TRT Clinic Use (common)**: Frequently dosed subcutaneously at low microgram amounts (often ~100-200 mcg) several times per week or more, per a prescribing clinician's protocol, to maintain testicular function.
- **Fertility (clinical pulsatile)**: Classic ovulation-induction protocols use a programmable pump delivering small microgram pulses every ~60-120 minutes — a medical, supervised setup.
- **Diagnostic Use**: A single bolus (e.g., ~100 mcg) is given to assess pituitary LH/FSH response.
- **Short Half-Life**: Cleared within minutes, which is exactly why frequent pulses or pump delivery are required for sustained effect.
- **Reconstitution**: Supplied as a powder reconstituted with sterile/bacteriostatic water; refrigerate after reconstitution and use within the labeled window.
- **Timing**: Frequency and timing should follow the prescribing protocol; spacing pulses appropriately is critical to avoid receptor downregulation.
- **Supervision**: Best used under an endocrinologist or TRT-clinic protocol given the hormonal stakes and the pulsatile-dosing nuance.
