# Gonadorelin

## Foods to Eat

- **Oysters & Shellfish**: Among the richest dietary zinc sources, supporting testosterone synthesis and LH signaling.
- **Eggs (whole, pastured)**: Provide cholesterol and vitamin D — the raw substrate and cofactor for steroidogenesis.
- **Fatty Fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel)**: Omega-3s and vitamin D support Leydig cell function and reduce inflammation.
- **Red Meat (lean, quality)**: Supplies zinc, iron, and saturated/cholesterol substrate that supports testosterone production.
- **Leafy Greens (spinach, Swiss chard)**: Magnesium content supports free testosterone and reduces sex-hormone-binding globulin effects.
- **Brazil Nuts**: Selenium supports testosterone and sperm quality (a few per day is sufficient).
- **Pomegranate & Berries**: Antioxidants support vascular function and may support testosterone.
- **Healthy Fats (olive oil, avocado)**: Dietary fat is necessary for adequate sex-hormone production; very low-fat diets can lower testosterone.
