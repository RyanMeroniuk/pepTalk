# Gonadorelin

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Supplement**: Zinc (15-30mg) — critical cofactor for testosterone synthesis and LH signaling; synergistic with gonadorelin's testosterone-supporting effect.
- **Supplement**: Vitamin D3 (2000-5000 IU) — functions as a pro-hormone supporting healthy testosterone levels; foundational for HPG-axis support.
- **Supplement**: Magnesium — supports free testosterone and sleep quality, enhancing the hormonal environment.
- **Supplement**: Omega-3 (EPA/DHA) — supports Leydig cell function and reduces inflammation, complementary to endogenous testosterone support.
- **Supplement**: Ashwagandha (300-600mg KSM-66) — may support testosterone and reduce cortisol, synergistic with axis stimulation.
- **Supplement**: B-Complex — supports energy metabolism and steroidogenesis cofactors.
- **Caution**: High-dose exogenous hormones or pro-hormone supplements — may interfere with the natural feedback gonadorelin relies on; avoid stacking suppressive agents.
- **Note**: Discuss any hormonal supplement with the prescribing clinician, since gonadorelin works through delicate HPG feedback.
