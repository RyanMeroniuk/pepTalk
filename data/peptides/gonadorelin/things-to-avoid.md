# Gonadorelin

## Things to Avoid

- **Continuous (Non-Pulsatile) Dosing**: The most important pitfall — steady exposure downregulates GnRH receptors and suppresses the very axis you are trying to stimulate.
- **Combining With Exogenous Testosterone Without a Plan**: High-dose exogenous androgens suppress LH/FSH via negative feedback, working against gonadorelin; coordinate timing with a clinician.
- **Skipping Blood Work**: Without monitoring LH, FSH, testosterone, and estradiol, you cannot tell if the protocol is helping or suppressing.
- **Self-Dosing Without Supervision**: HPG-axis manipulation is delicate; unsupervised use risks paradoxical suppression and hormonal imbalance.
- **Use in Hormone-Sensitive Cancers**: Stimulating LH/FSH and downstream sex hormones is contraindicated where it could feed hormone-dependent tumors.
- **Inconsistent Schedule**: Clustering or skipping pulses undermines the pulsatile signal the pituitary needs.
- **Chronic Stress and Poor Sleep**: Both suppress endogenous GnRH/LH pulsatility, blunting the benefit.
- **Use During Pregnancy/Breastfeeding**: Avoid outside specific supervised fertility protocols.
