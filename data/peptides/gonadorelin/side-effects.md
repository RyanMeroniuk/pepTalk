# Gonadorelin

## Side Effects

- **Common**: Injection-site reactions — redness, swelling, irritation, or mild pain at the subcutaneous site.
- **Common**: Headache, flushing, or mild nausea.
- **Hormonal Fluctuations**: Mood changes, libido shifts, or transient hormone swings as LH/FSH and downstream testosterone respond.
- **Axis Suppression From Improper Dosing**: Continuous (non-pulsatile) administration paradoxically suppresses the HPG axis — the opposite of the intended effect.
- **Abdominal/Pelvic Discomfort**: Reported in fertility-protocol use related to gonadal stimulation.
- **Rare Hypersensitivity**: Allergic or, rarely, anaphylactic-type reactions to the peptide.
- **Ovarian Hyperstimulation (fertility context)**: In ovulation-induction settings, monitoring is needed to avoid overstimulation.
- **Generally Well Tolerated**: At correct diagnostic/pulsatile doses, adverse effects are typically mild and transient.
