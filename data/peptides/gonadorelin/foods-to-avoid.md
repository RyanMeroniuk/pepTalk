# Gonadorelin

## Foods to Avoid

- **Alcohol**: Suppresses LH secretion and lowers testosterone, directly opposing gonadorelin's axis-stimulating goal.
- **Excess Refined Sugar**: Drives insulin resistance and inflammation that lower testosterone and disrupt HPG signaling.
- **Ultra-Processed Foods**: Often high in additives and seed oils that promote inflammation and hormonal disruption.
- **Excess Soy (very high intake)**: Large amounts of phytoestrogens may shift estrogen balance; moderate intake is generally fine.
- **Processed Seed Oils (soybean, canola, corn)**: High omega-6 load promotes inflammation that can impair gonadal function.
- **Very Low-Fat Diets**: Inadequate dietary fat reduces the substrate for testosterone synthesis.
- **Excess Trans Fats & Fried Foods**: Associated with impaired sperm quality and lower testosterone.
- **Licorice (large amounts)**: Glycyrrhizin can lower testosterone; avoid heavy intake.
