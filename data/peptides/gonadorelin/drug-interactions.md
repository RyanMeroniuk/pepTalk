# Gonadorelin

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Exogenous Testosterone / Anabolic Steroids**: Caution — suppress LH/FSH via negative feedback, directly opposing gonadorelin; combining requires careful clinician-managed timing.
- **GnRH Agonists (leuprolide, goserelin)**: Caution — continuous GnRH-agonist therapy downregulates the axis; concurrent gonadorelin produces conflicting signals.
- **GnRH Antagonists (cetrorelix, ganirelix)**: Contraindicated together — directly block GnRH receptors, rendering gonadorelin ineffective.
- **Corticosteroids**: Caution — suppress GnRH/LH pulsatility and may blunt the response; monitor.
- **Spironolactone / Anti-Androgens**: Caution — counteract downstream androgen effects.
- **Oral Contraceptives / Hormonal Agents**: Caution — exogenous sex hormones alter HPG feedback and may interfere with response.
- **Contraindicated in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding**: Use only within specific supervised fertility protocols.
- **Contraindicated in Hormone-Sensitive Cancers** (breast, prostate, ovarian) and **active pituitary tumors** — stimulating gonadotropins and sex hormones may worsen these conditions.
