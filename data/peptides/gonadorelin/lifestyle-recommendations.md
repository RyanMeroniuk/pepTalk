# Gonadorelin

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Prioritize Sleep (7-9 hours)**: The largest natural testosterone release occurs during sleep; poor sleep blunts LH pulsatility and undercuts gonadorelin's axis support.
- **Resistance Training (3-4x/week)**: Strength training acutely and chronically supports testosterone and androgen-receptor sensitivity, reinforcing the hormonal goal.
- **Maintain Healthy Body Fat**: Excess adipose tissue raises aromatase, converting testosterone to estrogen and shifting HPG feedback; lean body composition supports the axis.
- **Stress Management**: Chronic cortisol suppresses GnRH and LH pulsatility, directly opposing gonadorelin's mechanism. Use meditation, breathwork, or adequate recovery.
- **Morning Sunlight**: Supports circadian rhythm and the cortisol/testosterone diurnal pattern.
- **Limit Alcohol**: Alcohol lowers testosterone and disrupts LH secretion.
- **Consistent Dosing Schedule**: Because the effect depends on pulsatile timing, keep injections on the clinician-prescribed schedule rather than skipping or clustering doses.
- **Regular Blood Work**: Monitor LH, FSH, total/free testosterone, and estradiol to confirm the protocol is working as intended.
