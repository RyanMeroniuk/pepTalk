# Gonadorelin

## Overview

Gonadorelin is a synthetic decapeptide identical to natural gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH). It acts on the anterior pituitary to trigger the release of luteinizing hormone (LH) and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH), which in turn drive endogenous testosterone production and support fertility. Because natural GnRH is secreted in pulses, gonadorelin must be dosed in a pulsatile manner to stimulate the pituitary — continuous exposure paradoxically downregulates GnRH receptors and suppresses the axis. It has a very short half-life (minutes), which is why pulsatile delivery matters. Gonadorelin has approved diagnostic and fertility uses, and in TRT clinics it is increasingly used via compounding pharmacies as an alternative to hCG for maintaining testicular function. It sits downstream of kisspeptin, which stimulates the neurons that release GnRH.
