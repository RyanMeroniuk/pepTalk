# Gonadorelin

## Mechanisms of Action

- **GnRH Receptor Agonism**: Binds pituitary GnRH receptors, identical in action to native gonadotropin-releasing hormone.
- **LH and FSH Release**: Stimulates the anterior pituitary to secrete luteinizing hormone and follicle-stimulating hormone, the two gonadotropins that drive gonadal function.
- **Endogenous Testosterone Support**: Downstream LH stimulates testicular Leydig cells to produce testosterone, helping maintain natural production rather than replacing it.
- **Fertility / Spermatogenesis Support**: FSH supports sperm and follicular maturation, underpinning fertility applications.
- **Pulsatile Requirement**: Mimics natural pulsatile GnRH secretion; continuous (non-pulsatile) exposure downregulates receptors and suppresses the axis — the basis of GnRH-agonist "chemical castration" when given continuously.
- **Short Half-Life**: Cleared within minutes, requiring frequent or pump-delivered pulsatile dosing.
- **Downstream of Kisspeptin**: Kisspeptin stimulates the hypothalamic neurons that release GnRH, so gonadorelin acts one step lower in the HPG axis.
