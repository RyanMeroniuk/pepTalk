# Gonadorelin

## Regulatory Status

- **FDA-recognized active**: Gonadorelin has historically been an FDA-approved agent for diagnostic and fertility (ovulation-induction) uses; some original branded products have been discontinued.
- **Prescription compounded use**: Widely available today through compounding pharmacies, where TRT clinics prescribe it as an hCG alternative.
- **Approved fertility / diagnostic indications**: Pulsatile gonadorelin for hypothalamic infertility and as a pituitary-function diagnostic test.
- **Prescription-only**: A regulated hormone product requiring a prescription; not an OTC or pure "research chemical" like the experimental peptides.
- **hCG-shortage driver**: Increased compounded gonadorelin use partly reflects periodic hCG supply constraints.
- **WADA**: Releasing factors that affect gonadotropins/testosterone fall under anti-doping scrutiny; athletes should verify prohibited status (especially for males) before use.
- **Use under medical supervision**: Given its hormonal action, legitimate use is clinician-directed.
