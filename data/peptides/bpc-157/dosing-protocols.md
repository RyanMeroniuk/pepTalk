# BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157)

## Dosing Protocols

- **Oral**: 500-750 mcg/day (capsule or liquid) — particularly good for GI-focused healing since it originates from gastric juice
- **Subcutaneous Injection**: 250-500 mcg, 1-2x daily
- **Human Equivalent Dose** (extrapolated from rat studies): ~110 mcg (150 lb person), ~145 mcg (200 lb), ~180 mcg (250 lb)
- **Cycle**: 4-12 weeks on, some protocols suggest 2-4 weeks off between cycles
- **Timing**: Can take with or without food; inject near injury site when possible for localized effects
- **Split Dosing**: Often split into AM/PM doses for sustained therapeutic effect
- **No human pharmacokinetic studies exist** — all dosing is extrapolated from animal research
