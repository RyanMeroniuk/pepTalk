# BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157)

## Side Effects

- Generally very well-tolerated across all animal studies
- Mild nausea possible (oral route)
- Injection site irritation and redness
- Dizziness (rare)
- No significant hormonal disruption reported in any studies
- No known organ toxicity at therapeutic doses
- No carcinogenic effects observed
- LD50 has not been established (no lethal dose found in studies — extremely high safety margin)
