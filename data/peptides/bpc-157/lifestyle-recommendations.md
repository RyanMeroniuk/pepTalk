# BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157)

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Protein Prioritization**: Consume 1.6-2.2g protein per kg of body weight daily to supply amino acids for tissue synthesis. Space protein intake across 4-5 meals to maintain a positive nitrogen balance throughout the day
- **Gentle Movement & Rehabilitation**: Perform low-impact movement of injured areas daily (walking, swimming, range-of-motion exercises) to promote blood flow and nutrient delivery to healing tissues. Avoid immobilization beyond acute injury phase — controlled movement accelerates BPC-157's angiogenic effects
- **Sleep Optimization for Tissue Repair**: Target 7-9 hours of quality sleep per night. Growth hormone release peaks during deep sleep stages, synergizing with BPC-157's healing mechanisms. Keep a consistent sleep schedule, sleep in a cool dark room (65-68F), and avoid screens 1 hour before bed
- **Stress Management**: Chronic cortisol elevation impairs wound healing and increases gut permeability. Practice daily stress reduction: 10-15 minutes of breathwork, meditation, or gentle yoga. Consider magnesium glycinate (400mg) before bed for both sleep and stress
- **Hydration**: Drink at least half your body weight in ounces of water daily. Adequate hydration supports nutrient transport to healing tissues and maintains gut mucosal integrity
- **Gut-Specific Protocols**: If using BPC-157 orally for gut healing, eat slowly and chew thoroughly. Avoid eating when stressed or rushed — the parasympathetic "rest and digest" state is essential for gut repair. Space meals 3-4 hours apart to allow migrating motor complex (MMC) to sweep the small intestine
- **Alcohol Elimination**: Completely avoid alcohol during healing protocols. Even moderate alcohol disrupts gut barrier integrity, increases intestinal permeability, and directly counteracts BPC-157's mucosal healing effects
- **Anti-Inflammatory Lifestyle**: Minimize exposure to environmental toxins (pesticides, plastics, household chemicals). Use glass containers for food storage, choose organic produce when possible, and filter drinking water
- **Sun Exposure**: Get 15-20 minutes of morning sunlight to support circadian rhythm and vitamin D production, both of which enhance healing cascades
