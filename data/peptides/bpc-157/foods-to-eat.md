# BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157)

## Foods to Eat

- **Bone Broth**: Rich in collagen, glycine, proline, and glutamine — the same amino acid building blocks BPC-157 uses to repair tissue. Consume 1-2 cups daily during healing protocols
- **Fermented Foods**: Sauerkraut, kimchi, kefir, yogurt, miso, and kombucha provide beneficial bacteria that support gut barrier integrity and complement BPC-157's gut-healing action
- **Wild-Caught Fatty Fish**: Salmon, sardines, mackerel, and anchovies provide omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA) that reduce inflammation and support tissue repair
- **Organ Meats**: Liver, heart, and kidney are dense sources of zinc, copper, vitamin A, and B-vitamins critical for wound healing and collagen synthesis
- **Collagen-Rich Foods**: Chicken skin, pork skin, oxtail, and fish with skin provide direct collagen precursors
- **Dark Leafy Greens**: Spinach, kale, and Swiss chard provide vitamin C, vitamin K, folate, and magnesium essential for tissue repair pathways
- **Berries**: Blueberries, blackberries, and strawberries provide antioxidants that reduce oxidative stress at injury sites and support BPC-157's free radical scavenging action
- **Turmeric & Ginger**: Natural anti-inflammatories that complement BPC-157's cytokine modulation. Use fresh turmeric with black pepper (piperine) for absorption
- **Eggs**: Complete protein source with all essential amino acids, plus choline for cell membrane repair and vitamin D for immune regulation
- **Sweet Potatoes & Squash**: Provide vitamin A (beta-carotene) essential for epithelial tissue repair and immune function
- **Garlic & Onions**: Contain allicin and quercetin — natural antimicrobials and anti-inflammatories that support gut health
- **Aloe Vera Juice**: Supports gut mucosal lining repair, complements BPC-157's GI protective effects
