# BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157)

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- No known direct drug interactions established in literature
- Theoretical caution with anticoagulants (promotes angiogenesis)
- Theoretical caution with immunosuppressive drugs
- Pregnancy/breastfeeding: insufficient safety data, avoid
- Active malignancy: theoretical concern with growth factor upregulation (not established)
