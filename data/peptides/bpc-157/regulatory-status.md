# BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157)

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT FDA-approved** for any indication
- Was placed on FDA Category 2 bulk drug list (under evaluation for compounding)
- Extensive animal study data: primarily rats, over 100 published studies
- Very limited human clinical trials
- Phase II clinical trials underway for inflammatory bowel disease (IBD/ulcerative colitis)
- Available from compounding pharmacies (with prescription) and research chemical suppliers
- **Banned by WADA** for competitive athletes
- Legal to purchase for research purposes in most countries
