# BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157)

## Overview

BPC-157 is a synthetic pentadecapeptide (15 amino acids: Gly-Glu-Pro-Pro-Pro-Gly-Lys-Pro-Ala-Asp-Asp-Ala-Gly-Leu-Val) derived from a naturally occurring protein found in human gastric juice. First characterized in 1993 by Sikiric et al. Exhibits extraordinary stability in gastric juice — remains bioactive for over 24 hours, unlike most peptides which degrade rapidly in the GI tract. Available as oral capsules or injectable (subcutaneous/intramuscular).
