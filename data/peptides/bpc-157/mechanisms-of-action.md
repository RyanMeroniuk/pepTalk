# BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157)

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Angiogenesis**: Promotes VEGF expression and new blood vessel formation, accelerating tissue repair and oxygen delivery to damaged areas
- **Nitric Oxide System**: Modulates NO pathways, maintaining vascular tone and supporting healing processes
- **Growth Factor Upregulation**: Increases expression of EGF (epidermal growth factor), FGF (fibroblast growth factor), and HGF (hepatocyte growth factor) for tissue regeneration
- **Anti-inflammatory**: Reduces pro-inflammatory cytokines including TNF-alpha, IL-6, and IL-1beta
- **Cytoprotection**: Protects cells from oxidative stress, alcohol toxicity, and NSAID-induced damage
- **Neuroprotection**: Supports the dopaminergic system, promotes nerve repair, reduces excitotoxicity. Demonstrated effects on serotonergic and GABAergic systems
- **GI Mucosal Integrity**: Maintains and repairs gut barrier function, counteracts NSAID and alcohol damage to stomach lining
- **Collagen/Tendon Repair**: Stimulates fibroblast activity and promotes tendon-to-bone healing
- **Free Radical Scavenging**: Reduces oxidative damage at the cellular level
