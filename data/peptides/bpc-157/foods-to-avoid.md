# BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157)

## Foods to Avoid

- **Alcohol**: Directly damages gastric mucosa, increases intestinal permeability, and counteracts BPC-157's gut healing mechanisms. Eliminate completely during protocols
- **NSAIDs with Food Habits**: While BPC-157 counteracts NSAID damage, avoid taking NSAIDs on an empty stomach. If you must use NSAIDs, take them with food and use BPC-157 concurrently for GI protection
- **Processed Seed Oils**: Soybean oil, canola oil, corn oil, sunflower oil, and safflower oil are high in omega-6 fatty acids that promote inflammatory prostaglandin production, opposing BPC-157's anti-inflammatory action
- **Refined Sugar & High-Fructose Corn Syrup**: Drives inflammatory cytokine production (TNF-alpha, IL-6) and feeds pathogenic gut bacteria, undermining gut barrier repair
- **Gluten (if sensitive)**: Triggers zonulin release in sensitive individuals, opening tight junctions and increasing intestinal permeability — directly counteracting gut healing
- **Conventional Dairy (if sensitive)**: Casein A1 protein can trigger gut inflammation in sensitive individuals. If tolerated, choose A2 dairy, goat, or sheep milk products
- **Processed & Ultra-Processed Foods**: Emulsifiers (polysorbate 80, carboxymethylcellulose), artificial sweeteners, and preservatives damage the gut mucosal barrier
- **Fried Foods**: Advanced glycation end products (AGEs) from high-heat cooking promote oxidative stress and inflammation
- **Excess Caffeine**: More than 1-2 cups of coffee daily can increase cortisol and gastric acid, potentially irritating a healing gut lining
- **Artificial Sweeteners**: Sucralose, aspartame, and saccharin disrupt gut microbiome composition and may increase intestinal permeability
