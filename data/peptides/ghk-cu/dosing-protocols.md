# GHK-Cu (Copper Peptide)

## Dosing Protocols

- **Topical (Most Common)**:
  - Serums and creams: Apply 1-2x daily to clean skin
  - Results timeline: Texture improvements ~4 weeks; measurable thickness/wrinkle changes 8-12 weeks
  - No prescription needed for topical
- **Injectable (Subcutaneous)**:
  - 1-2mg daily or every other day
  - Cycle: 4-8 weeks on, 4 weeks off
  - Higher bioavailability than topical
  - Requires reconstitution with bacteriostatic water
- **Microneedling**: Applied topically during microneedling for enhanced penetration
