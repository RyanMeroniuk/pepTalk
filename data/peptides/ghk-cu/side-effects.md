# GHK-Cu (Copper Peptide)

## Side Effects

- Generally well-tolerated
- **Topical**: Mild skin irritation or redness at higher concentrations; possible interaction with other active skincare ingredients
- **Injectable**: Injection site irritation; mild redness
- Excessive copper signaling may theoretically increase inflammation
- Benefits occur gradually and subtly — "a supportive, restorative ingredient, not a quick fix"
