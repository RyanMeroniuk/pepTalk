# GHK-Cu (Copper Peptide)

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Collagen Synthesis**: Stimulates production of collagen types I and III, the primary structural proteins of skin
- **Elastin Production**: Increases elastin synthesis for skin elasticity and resilience
- **Glycosaminoglycan Synthesis**: Promotes production of skin hydration molecules
- **Fibroblast Stimulation**: Enhances dermal fibroblast proliferation and activity
- **Copper Delivery**: Acts as a matrixial carrier peptide delivering copper to enzymatic processes
- **MMP Regulation**: Regulates matrix metalloproteinases for controlled tissue remodeling
- **Antioxidant**: Reduces intracellular reactive oxygen species and oxidative damage
- **Anti-Inflammatory**: Modulates inflammatory gene expression
- **Angiogenesis**: Stimulates blood vessel and nerve outgrowth for tissue repair
- **Gene Expression**: Affects a large number of genes related to stress response, tissue remodeling, anti-oxidant defense, and wound healing
