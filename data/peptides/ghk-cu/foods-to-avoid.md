# GHK-Cu (Copper Peptide)

## Foods to Avoid

- **Refined Sugar & High-Glycemic Foods**: Sugar triggers glycation — a process where glucose molecules bind to collagen fibers, making them stiff and brittle (advanced glycation end products/AGEs). This directly counteracts GHK-Cu's collagen synthesis
- **Alcohol**: Dehydrates the skin, depletes zinc and vitamin A, disrupts sleep (impairing nighttime collagen synthesis), and increases MMP enzymes that break down collagen
- **Processed Seed Oils**: Soybean, corn, canola, and sunflower oils promote omega-6-driven inflammation that accelerates skin aging and counteracts GHK-Cu's anti-inflammatory action
- **Fried Foods**: Deep frying creates AGEs and acrylamide, both of which damage skin proteins and increase oxidative stress
- **Excess Dairy (for acne-prone individuals)**: Dairy can spike insulin and IGF-1, promoting sebum production and inflammatory acne. If skin issues are present, trial a 30-day dairy elimination
- **Charred & Blackened Meats**: Heterocyclic amines and AGEs from high-heat cooking promote oxidative damage to skin tissue
- **Excess Salt**: High sodium intake leads to facial puffiness and can dehydrate skin cells, reducing the hydrating effects of GHK-Cu's glycosaminoglycan promotion
- **Artificial Additives**: Food dyes, preservatives (BHA/BHT), and artificial sweeteners can trigger systemic inflammation that manifests in the skin
