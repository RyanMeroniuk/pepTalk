# GHK-Cu (Copper Peptide)

## Foods to Eat

- **Copper-Rich Foods**: Oysters, dark chocolate (70%+), shiitake mushrooms, cashews, sunflower seeds, and liver. GHK-Cu delivers copper to enzymatic processes, and adequate dietary copper ensures systemic support. Target 0.9mg copper daily (RDA)
- **Vitamin C-Rich Foods**: Bell peppers, citrus fruits, kiwi, strawberries, and broccoli. Vitamin C is an essential cofactor for collagen synthesis (prolyl and lysyl hydroxylase enzymes). Consume 2-3 servings daily — food-based vitamin C is preferred over supplements for skin benefits
- **Zinc-Rich Foods**: Oysters, pumpkin seeds, grass-fed beef, lentils, and chickpeas. Zinc is required for cell division and protein synthesis during skin repair. Zinc and copper must be balanced — excessive zinc depletes copper
- **Wild-Caught Fatty Fish**: Salmon, sardines, and mackerel provide omega-3s that reduce skin inflammation and support cell membrane integrity, plus astaxanthin (in salmon) which protects against UV-induced collagen breakdown
- **Bone Broth**: Provides collagen, glycine, proline, and hyaluronic acid — the raw materials for skin matrix repair. Consume 1-2 cups daily
- **Avocados**: Rich in vitamin E (protects cell membranes from oxidation), healthy monounsaturated fats, and biotin for skin health
- **Tomatoes (Cooked)**: Provide lycopene, a powerful antioxidant that protects skin from UV damage and supports collagen preservation. Cooking increases lycopene bioavailability
- **Berries (Blueberries, Acai, Goji)**: Dense in anthocyanins and polyphenols that neutralize free radicals and support skin elasticity
- **Green Tea**: Contains EGCG (epigallocatechin gallate), which inhibits collagen-degrading enzymes and provides UV protection from the inside
- **Eggs**: Provide biotin, lutein, and complete protein for skin cell renewal
- **Sweet Potatoes & Carrots**: Beta-carotene converts to retinol (vitamin A), essential for skin cell turnover and repair
