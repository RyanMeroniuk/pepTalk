# GHK-Cu (Copper Peptide)

## Benefits

- **Skin Anti-Aging**: Increased skin thickness, elasticity, hydration, and reduced wrinkles (clinical studies confirm). Healing time reductions of 30-50% documented
- **Wound Healing**: Accelerates all phases: inflammatory modulation, proliferative stimulation, remodeling optimization
- **Hair Growth**: Stimulates fibroblasts, promotes blood vessel formation to follicles, inhibits TGF-beta (prevents follicle shrinkage), supports dermal papilla cells
- **Scar Reduction**: Promotes organized tissue repair
- **Tissue Regeneration**: Demonstrated for skin, lung, bone, liver, and stomach lining
- **Anti-Inflammatory**: Reduces inflammatory markers in tissue
- **Bone Health**: Promotes osteoblast activity
