# GHK-Cu (Copper Peptide)

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Consistent Application**: Apply GHK-Cu topical products 1-2x daily at the same time each day. Results are cumulative — texture improvements appear around 4 weeks, measurable thickness and wrinkle changes at 8-12 weeks. Do not skip days or expect rapid results; this peptide works through gradual gene expression changes
- **Sun Protection (Non-Negotiable)**: UV radiation breaks down collagen 10x faster than aging alone. Apply broad-spectrum SPF 30-50 sunscreen every morning, reapply every 2 hours during sun exposure. Wear hats and protective clothing. UV damage directly counteracts every benefit GHK-Cu provides
- **Hydration for Skin**: Drink at least half your body weight in ounces of water daily. GHK-Cu promotes glycosaminoglycan production (skin hydration molecules), but these require adequate water intake to function. Consider adding electrolytes (sodium, potassium, magnesium) for cellular hydration
- **Sleep for Skin Repair**: Skin cell turnover and collagen synthesis peak during deep sleep (10pm-2am). Target 7-9 hours nightly. Sleep on silk or satin pillowcases to reduce mechanical collagen compression on the face
- **Stress Management**: Cortisol directly degrades collagen and elastin through MMP (matrix metalloproteinase) activation. Practice daily stress reduction — 10-15 minutes of meditation, breathwork, or gentle yoga. Chronically elevated cortisol undermines GHK-Cu's collagen-building effects
- **Exercise for Skin Health**: Regular moderate exercise (30-45 minutes, 4-5x/week) increases blood flow to the skin, delivering nutrients and oxygen. Exercise also reduces systemic inflammation. Avoid excessive endurance training, which can increase oxidative stress
- **Skincare Layering Protocol**: Apply GHK-Cu serum to clean, slightly damp skin. Wait 5-10 minutes before layering other products. Apply vitamin C serum at the opposite time of day (morning if GHK-Cu is used at night, or vice versa) to avoid copper-ascorbic acid interaction
- **Microneedling Enhancement**: If combining GHK-Cu with microneedling, apply the peptide immediately after the procedure for enhanced penetration through micro-channels. Space microneedling sessions 4-6 weeks apart to allow full collagen remodeling
- **Avoid Smoking & Secondhand Smoke**: Smoking reduces blood flow to the skin by up to 40%, depletes vitamin C (essential for collagen), and dramatically accelerates skin aging through oxidative damage
- **Limit Alcohol**: Alcohol dehydrates skin, depletes zinc and vitamin A, and increases inflammatory MMP activity — all of which counteract GHK-Cu's mechanisms
