# GHK-Cu (Copper Peptide)

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Blood thinners (warfarin, heparin, aspirin, NSAIDs)**: GHK-Cu promotes angiogenesis (new blood vessel formation), which may increase bleeding risk or complicate anticoagulant therapy
- **Copper supplements**: Concurrent use risks copper overload, leading to oxidative damage, liver toxicity, and GI distress. Dietary copper from food is sufficient alongside GHK-Cu
- **Chemotherapy / anti-angiogenic drugs (bevacizumab, etc.)**: GHK-Cu's pro-angiogenic and cell-proliferative effects may theoretically counteract anti-cancer therapies
- **Iron supplements**: High copper can interfere with iron absorption and metabolism; monitor if supplementing both
- **Zinc supplements (high dose)**: Zinc and copper compete for absorption. High-dose zinc depletes copper, while excessive copper can impair zinc status. Balance is critical
- **Contraindicated: Wilson's disease** — genetic disorder causing copper accumulation in tissues. GHK-Cu delivers additional copper and could accelerate organ damage
- **Contraindicated: Copper sensitivity or copper toxicity** — individuals with known copper metabolism disorders should avoid GHK-Cu entirely
- **Contraindicated: Active cancer or history of cancer** — GHK-Cu promotes cell proliferation, angiogenesis, and tissue growth, which may theoretically support tumor growth
- **Contraindicated: Pregnancy and breastfeeding** — insufficient safety data; effects on fetal development and copper transfer via breast milk are unknown
