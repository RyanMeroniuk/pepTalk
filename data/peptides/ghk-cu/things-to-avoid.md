# GHK-Cu (Copper Peptide)

## Things to Avoid

- **UV Exposure Without Protection**: This is the single biggest threat to GHK-Cu's benefits. Unprotected sun exposure destroys collagen faster than GHK-Cu can rebuild it. Never skip sunscreen — even on cloudy days (80% of UV penetrates clouds)
- **Layering Vitamin C Directly with GHK-Cu**: Copper ions can oxidize ascorbic acid, reducing efficacy of both products. Separate application by 8-12 hours (e.g., vitamin C in the morning, GHK-Cu at night)
- **Retinol Overuse**: While retinoids and GHK-Cu are synergistic for collagen, using both at full strength simultaneously can cause irritation, redness, and barrier damage. Alternate nights or use one in the morning and one at night
- **Harsh Chemical Exfoliants**: Strong AHA/BHA peels (above 15%) can strip the skin barrier and cause inflammation, counteracting GHK-Cu's healing and regenerative effects. Use gentle exfoliation (5-10%) 1-2x per week maximum
- **Smoking & Vaping**: Nicotine constricts blood vessels supplying the skin, reducing nutrient delivery by up to 40%. Smoking depletes vitamin C and accelerates collagen breakdown. Vaping exposes skin to oxidative chemicals
- **Hot Water on Face**: Hot water strips natural oils and damages the skin barrier. Wash face with lukewarm water only
- **Picking or Scrubbing Skin**: Mechanical damage triggers uncontrolled inflammation and scarring, opposing GHK-Cu's organized repair mechanisms
- **Sleep Deprivation**: Fewer than 6 hours of sleep increases cortisol (collagen-degrading) and reduces growth hormone (collagen-building). Chronic sleep loss is visible in skin quality within weeks
- **Excessive Copper Supplementation**: Do not take high-dose copper supplements alongside GHK-Cu without medical guidance. Copper excess can cause oxidative damage. Dietary copper from food is sufficient
