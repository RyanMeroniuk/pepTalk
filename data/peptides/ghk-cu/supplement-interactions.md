# GHK-Cu (Copper Peptide)

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Synergistic**: BPC-157 and TB-500 for comprehensive healing
- **Synergistic**: Vitamin C serum (use at different times of day to avoid copper-ascorbic acid interaction)
- **Synergistic**: Retinol/retinoids (at different times — both promote collagen)
- **Enhanced by**: Adequate zinc and copper dietary intake
- **Enhanced by**: Sun protection (sunscreen) to preserve collagen benefits
- **Caution**: Do not layer with high-concentration Vitamin C simultaneously (copper can oxidize ascorbic acid)
- **Caution**: Wilson's disease or copper sensitivity
