# Mazdutide

## Benefits

- **Weight Loss**: Substantial body weight reduction in the GLORY Phase 3 program in China, driven by combined appetite suppression and increased energy expenditure
- **Diabetes Management**: GLP-1 arm supports HbA1c reduction; studied for type 2 diabetes alongside obesity
- **Increased Energy Expenditure**: Glucagon agonism raises metabolic rate, a mechanism distinct from GLP-1-only agents
- **Liver Fat Reduction**: Glucagon-driven fat oxidation reduces hepatic fat
- **Additional Metabolic Effects**: Trial data have suggested improvements in lipids, blood pressure, and markers such as uric acid
- **Once-Weekly Dosing**: Long half-life allows weekly subcutaneous injection
