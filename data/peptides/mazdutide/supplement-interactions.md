# Mazdutide

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Critical**: High protein intake (1.6-2.2g/kg/day) to defend lean mass during rapid weight loss
- **Synergistic**: Creatine monohydrate (5g/day) for muscle preservation during a caloric deficit
- **Enhanced by**: Omega-3 (2-4g EPA/DHA) for cardiovascular and liver support
- **Synergistic**: Vitamin-d3 and magnesium for broad metabolic support
- **Supportive**: B-complex to support energy metabolism when intake is reduced
- **Caution**: Berberine or other glucose-lowering supplements may interact with glycemic effects — monitor blood glucose given the glucagon arm
- **Caution**: Oral supplements may have delayed absorption from slowed gastric emptying — separate timing where it matters
