# Mazdutide

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Oxyntomodulin Analog**: Engineered long-acting analog of the natural gut hormone oxyntomodulin, which intrinsically activates both GLP-1 and glucagon receptors
- **GLP-1 Receptor Agonist**: Suppresses appetite, slows gastric emptying, enhances glucose-dependent insulin secretion, and reduces glucagon-driven hyperglycemia
- **Glucagon Receptor Agonist**: Increases energy expenditure and stimulates hepatic lipolysis and fat oxidation, contributing extra weight loss and liver-fat reduction
- **Increased Energy Expenditure**: Glucagon agonism raises metabolic rate, a lever GLP-1-only drugs lack
- **Liver Fat Reduction**: Glucagon-driven fat oxidation reduces hepatic fat, of interest for fatty liver disease
- **Long Half-Life**: Acylated for albumin binding, enabling once-weekly subcutaneous dosing
