# Mazdutide

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Insulin**: Caution — combined use affects glucose control; insulin dose adjustment and frequent monitoring needed given the dual glucagon/GLP-1 glucose effects
- **Sulfonylureas**: Caution — additive hypoglycemia risk from the GLP-1 arm; dose reduction often required, monitor blood glucose
- **Metformin**: Generally compatible; monitor for additive GI effects
- **Levothyroxine**: Delayed gastric emptying may alter absorption — take on an empty stomach, separate from meals, and monitor TSH
- **Oral Contraceptives**: Slowed gastric emptying may reduce absorption — consider backup contraception, especially during escalation
- **Anticoagulants / Blood Thinners**: Altered absorption possible — monitor INR more frequently when starting or changing dose
- **Beta-Blockers**: Caution — may mask heart-rate changes; relevant given the class can raise resting heart rate
- **Pregnancy / Breastfeeding**: Contraindicated — investigational agent, weight loss is harmful during pregnancy
- **Pancreatitis**: Caution — discontinue if pancreatitis is suspected (severe persistent abdominal pain)
- **Diabetes**: Use with caution and close glucose monitoring, especially alongside other glucose-lowering drugs
- **Other Oral Medications**: Slowed gastric emptying may delay absorption — separate timing by several hours where clinically important
