# Mazdutide

## Overview

Mazdutide (development codes IBI362 and LY3305677) is an investigational oxyntomodulin-based dual agonist of the GLP-1 and glucagon receptors. Oxyntomodulin is a naturally occurring gut hormone that activates both receptors, and mazdutide is engineered as a long-acting analog of it. It was originally developed by Eli Lilly and is licensed to Innovent Biologics for development in China, where it has advanced through the GLORY Phase 3 program for obesity and is also studied for type 2 diabetes. The GLP-1 arm suppresses appetite and supports glucose control while the glucagon arm increases energy expenditure and reduces liver fat. It is administered as a once-weekly subcutaneous injection and is investigational; it has filed for or neared approval in China but is not approved in the US or EU.
