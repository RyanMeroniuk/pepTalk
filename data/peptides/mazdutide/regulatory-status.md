# Mazdutide

## Regulatory Status

- **INVESTIGATIONAL (most advanced in China)**: Not approved by the FDA or EMA
- Originally developed by Eli Lilly (LY3305677); licensed to Innovent Biologics (IBI362) for development in China
- Completed the GLORY Phase 3 program for obesity in China and studied for type 2 diabetes; has progressed toward regulatory filing/approval in China
- Not approved or marketed in the US or EU
- Not legally available by prescription as an approved product outside its approval jurisdictions; research-use-only sourcing carries quality and legal risk
- Long-term safety, including the glucagon-receptor profile, still being characterized in broader populations
