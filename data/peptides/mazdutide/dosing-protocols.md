# Mazdutide

## Dosing Protocols

- **Investigational — No Globally Approved Dosing**: The following reflects clinical trial regimens, not medical guidance
- **Trial Doses**: Once-weekly subcutaneous dosing studied at target maintenance doses of roughly 4mg to 6mg weekly, reached via gradual escalation
- **Route**: Subcutaneous injection, once weekly
- **Dose Escalation**: Stepwise monthly titration is essential to manage nausea and GI tolerability
- **Obesity vs Diabetes Programs**: Separate programs; target doses and titration may differ by indication
- **Continuous Use**: As with other incretin agents, weight regain is expected upon discontinuation
