# Mazdutide

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Protein-First Eating**: Eat protein first at every meal and target 1.6-2.2g/kg/day to defend against muscle loss during rapid weight reduction
- **Resistance Training 3-5x/Week**: Progressive-overload compound lifts preserve lean mass far better than cardio alone and improve insulin sensitivity
- **Daily Walking**: 8,000-10,000+ steps per day supports fat loss, insulin sensitivity, and GI motility without triggering nausea
- **Small Frequent Meals**: Slowed gastric emptying means large meals cause nausea and bloating — eat slowly, stop at ~80% fullness
- **Hydration Between Meals**: 80-100oz water daily between meals so liquid does not fill the slow-emptying stomach
- **Limit Alcohol**: Adds empty calories, worsens nausea, and burdens the liver — particularly relevant given the drug's liver-fat effects
- **Sleep 7-9 Hours**: Poor sleep raises ghrelin and cortisol and worsens insulin resistance, undermining metabolic benefits
- **GI Symptom Management**: Ginger for nausea, peppermint for bloating, psyllium or magnesium citrate for constipation, gentle walks after meals
- **Body Composition Tracking**: Use DEXA, bioimpedance, or tape measurements rather than scale weight alone to confirm fat loss
- **Plan for Discontinuation**: Build durable habits while on the drug — weight regain is expected after stopping
