# Mazdutide

## Things to Avoid

- **Skipping Meals**: Even with suppressed appetite, skipping meals accelerates muscle loss and nutrient deficiency. Protein shakes count when solid food is hard
- **Lying Down After Eating**: Wait 30-60 minutes — slowed emptying plus gravity worsens reflux and nausea. Walk instead
- **Relying Only on Cardio**: Cardio does not prevent muscle loss — resistance training is required
- **Drinking Large Volumes With Meals**: Fills the slow-emptying stomach and crowds out protein — hydrate between meals
- **Crash Dieting on Top of the Drug**: The agent already creates a strong deficit; extreme restriction risks muscle wasting and deficiencies
- **Rapid Dose Escalation**: Sharply worsens GI side effects without improving outcomes — follow gradual titration
- **Ignoring Cardiac Symptoms**: Report persistent palpitations or significant resting heart rate increases — the glucagon/incretin class can raise heart rate
- **Ignoring Persistent GI Symptoms**: Severe vomiting, inability to keep down food/water for 24+ hours, or severe abdominal pain require medical attention
- **Assuming Global Approval**: Mazdutide's most advanced regulatory progress is in China — it is not approved in the US or EU and long-term safety outside trials is not established
