## Foods to Avoid

- **High-Fat Greasy/Fried Foods**: Fries, fried chicken, fast food burgers, pizza, cream-based sauces. The leading trigger of nausea and vomiting on incretin agents — slowed gastric emptying means greasy food sits for hours
- **High-Sugar and High-Fructose Foods**: Candy, cookies, cake, ice cream, soda, fruit juice. Empty calories that also promote liver fat, working against the glucagon arm's benefit
- **Carbonated Beverages**: Soda, sparkling water, seltzer, beer. Carbonation creates trapped gas in a slow-emptying stomach, causing bloating
- **Alcohol**: Unpredictable absorption with slowed emptying, empty calories, worsens nausea, and burdens the liver
- **Large Portion Sizes**: Even healthy food in large amounts causes nausea and bloating with delayed emptying
- **Very Spicy Foods**: Worsen reflux and nausea
- **Raw Fibrous Vegetables in Large Amounts**: Hard to digest with slowed motility — cook vegetables instead
- **Ultra-Processed Foods**: Low nutritional density when intake is limited
