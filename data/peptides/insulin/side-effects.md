# Insulin

## Side Effects

- **Hypoglycemia (LIFE-THREATENING)**: The dominant, potentially lethal risk. Symptoms escalate from shakiness, sweating, hunger, confusion, and slurred speech to seizures, coma, and death. Severe hypoglycemia can occur within minutes to hours and is the #1 reason insulin misuse is so dangerous
- **Brain Damage**: Prolonged severe hypoglycemia starves the brain of glucose and can cause permanent neurological injury even if the person survives
- **Weight Gain / Fat Storage**: Insulin is lipogenic and promotes fat storage, counterproductive for body composition
- **Hypokalemia (Low Potassium)**: Insulin drives potassium into cells; large doses can dangerously lower blood potassium, risking cardiac arrhythmias
- **Injection Site Reactions / Lipohypertrophy**: Repeated injections in one site cause fatty lumps that alter absorption
- **Hypoglycemia Unawareness**: Repeated lows blunt warning symptoms over time, making future lows more dangerous
- **Death**: Insulin overdose is a recognized cause of accidental and intentional death — this is not a theoretical risk
