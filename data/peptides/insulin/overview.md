# Insulin

## Overview

Insulin is a peptide hormone produced by the beta cells of the pancreas that is essential for life — it allows cells to take up glucose from the blood and is the body's master anabolic (nutrient-storage) signal. As a medication it is FDA-approved and indispensable for type 1 diabetes and many cases of type 2 diabetes, available in rapid-acting (lispro, aspart, glulisine), short-acting (regular/Humulin R), intermediate (NPH), and long-acting basal (glargine, detemir, degludec) forms. Within bodybuilding it is misused off-label for its anabolic effects on muscle glycogen and protein storage. This is extremely dangerous: insulin has the narrowest safety margin of any commonly misused performance compound, and an accidental overdose can cause severe hypoglycemia, brain damage, coma, or death within minutes to hours. This entry covers insulin in an educational/research context only and is emphatically not medical advice or an endorsement of non-prescribed use.
