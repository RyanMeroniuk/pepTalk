# Insulin

## Foods to Eat

- **Fast-Acting Glucose (EMERGENCY — keep within reach always)**: Glucose tablets, fruit juice, regular soda, honey, candy. These treat acute hypoglycemia and can be life-saving — the single most important food category for anyone on insulin
- **Carbohydrates Matched to Each Dose**: Rice, oats, potatoes, fruit, whole grains — insulin must always be covered by adequate carbohydrate intake to prevent a dangerous low
- **Lean Proteins**: Chicken, fish, eggs, Greek yogurt, lean beef — support the anabolic effect and provide steady amino acids
- **Slow-Release Complex Carbs**: Oats, quinoa, sweet potatoes, legumes — provide sustained glucose to reduce sharp swings
- **Balanced Mixed Meals**: Combining protein, fat, and fiber with carbs slows glucose absorption and smooths the glycemic response
- **Bedtime Snack (if basal insulin causes overnight lows)**: A protein-plus-complex-carb snack can help prevent nocturnal hypoglycemia, under medical guidance
- **Adequate, Consistent Carbohydrate Intake**: Consistency in carbohydrate amount and timing makes glucose far more predictable and safer
