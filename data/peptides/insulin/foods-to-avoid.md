# Insulin

## Foods to Avoid

- **Alcohol (especially on an empty stomach)**: The most dangerous combination — alcohol blocks the liver's release of glucose and masks hypoglycemia symptoms, a leading cause of severe lows on insulin
- **Skipping Carbohydrates After Dosing**: Not a food but the deadliest dietary mistake — taking insulin and then under-eating or skipping a meal causes fatal hypoglycemia
- **Very Low-Carb / Fasting While Dosed for Carbs**: Mismatching a carb-based insulin dose with a low-carb meal drives blood sugar dangerously low
- **Unpredictable High-Sugar Binges**: Large erratic sugar loads cause sharp spikes followed by reactive lows, making glucose hard to control
- **Excessive Caffeine**: Can mask or mimic hypoglycemia symptoms, delaying treatment of a real low
- **Erratic Meal Skipping**: Irregular eating with fixed insulin doses produces dangerous glucose swings
- **Note**: Unlike most entries, the danger here is mismatch between food and dose — the core rule is that carbohydrate intake must always match insulin on board
