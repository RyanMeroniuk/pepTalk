# Insulin

## Stacking Protocols

- **SAFETY OVERRIDE**: Stacking insulin with other compounds multiplies the already-lethal hypoglycemia risk. The information below describes documented practices for education, NOT endorsement — non-diabetic insulin use is dangerous and discouraged
- **Insulin + GH Peptides (documented misuse, high risk)**: Insulin is sometimes misused alongside cjc-1295 / ipamorelin or mk-677 because growth hormone is counter-regulatory and raises blood glucose. This interaction makes glucose control unpredictable and is reported here only to flag the danger, not to recommend it
- **Insulin + Creatine (lower-risk adjunct)**: Insulin (prescribed) + creatine (5g/day) — creatine supports muscle glycogen and strength legitimately and does not lower blood glucose, making it a far safer way to pursue muscle fullness
- **Insulin + Protein (anabolic support)**: Insulin (prescribed) + whey protein — amino acids support the anabolic signal; still requires precise carbohydrate matching
- **AVOID Insulin + Glucose-Lowering Stacks**: Combining insulin with berberine or other glucose-lowering agents stacks hypoglycemia risk dangerously — do not combine without medical supervision
- **Honest Alternative**: For muscle goals, creatine, adequate protein, and training deliver anabolic benefit without insulin's risk of death
