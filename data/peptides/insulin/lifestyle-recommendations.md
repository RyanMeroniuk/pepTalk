# Insulin

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Continuous Glucose Monitoring Is Essential**: Anyone using insulin should monitor blood glucose with a meter or CGM — flying blind with insulin is how fatal lows happen
- **Always Carry Fast-Acting Carbs**: Glucose tablets, juice, or candy must be on your person at all times to treat the first signs of a low (shakiness, sweating, confusion)
- **Never Use Insulin Alone**: Severe hypoglycemia can cause unconsciousness before you can self-treat. Someone should know you are using it and how to use glucagon/call emergency services
- **Match Insulin to Food Precisely**: Every unit must be covered by adequate carbohydrate intake — skipping or under-eating after dosing is the classic lethal mistake
- **Avoid Alcohol Around Dosing**: Alcohol impairs the liver's ability to release glucose and masks hypoglycemia symptoms, a dangerous combination with insulin
- **Be Cautious With Exercise**: Physical activity increases insulin sensitivity and glucose uptake, deepening lows — adjust carbs and monitor closely around training
- **Consistent Meal Timing**: Erratic eating with fixed insulin doses drives unpredictable glucose swings
- **Educate Those Around You**: Family, training partners, or roommates should recognize hypoglycemia and know how to administer glucagon and call for help
- **The Honest Recommendation**: For non-diabetics seeking muscle, the safe and effective path is protein, creatine, and training — not a hormone that can kill you if mistimed by a single meal
