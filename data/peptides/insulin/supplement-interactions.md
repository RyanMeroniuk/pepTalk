# Insulin

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **CRITICAL**: Any supplement that lowers blood glucose stacks dangerously with insulin and raises hypoglycemia risk — this is the central interaction concern
- **Avoid / Caution**: Berberine independently lowers blood glucose and can compound insulin's effect, increasing hypoglycemia risk — monitor glucose very closely if combined
- **Caution**: Chromium, alpha-lipoic acid, and high-dose magnesium can enhance insulin sensitivity and lower glucose further — additive hypoglycemia potential
- **Caution**: Ashwagandha and other adaptogens may modestly affect glucose — monitor
- **Monitor Potassium**: Because insulin lowers blood potassium, potassium-affecting supplements should be used carefully
- **Generally Compatible**: Creatine, protein powders, omega-3, and vitamin-d3 do not directly cause hypoglycemia, but any change to carbohydrate timing around them still matters
- **Fast-Acting Glucose Is Non-Negotiable**: Glucose tablets or juice are the most important "supplement" — always within reach to treat a low
