# Insulin

## Benefits

- **Life-Saving Diabetes Treatment**: For type 1 diabetes, insulin is not optional — it is required for survival. This is its only universally legitimate benefit
- **Glycemic Control (Type 2)**: For many with type 2 diabetes, insulin achieves blood-glucose control when other therapies are insufficient
- **Anabolic / Muscle Glycogen (misuse context)**: Bodybuilders misuse it to drive glycogen and amino acids into muscle for fullness and recovery — a benefit that is real but does NOT justify the lethal risk
- **Anti-Catabolic Effect**: Suppresses muscle protein breakdown, which is part of why it is misused around training
- **CRITICAL CONTEXT**: Outside of prescribed diabetes management, the anabolic "benefits" of insulin are overwhelmingly outweighed by the risk of fatal hypoglycemia. Safer, legal anabolic strategies (protein, creatine, training) achieve muscle goals without risking death
