# Insulin

## Things to Avoid

- **Dosing Without a Blood Glucose Reading**: Never inject insulin without knowing your blood sugar — guessing is how fatal lows happen
- **Using Insulin Alone / Unsupervised**: Severe hypoglycemia can cause unconsciousness before you can treat it. Someone nearby must know you use it and how to respond
- **Taking Insulin and Not Eating**: The classic lethal mistake — every dose must be covered by adequate carbohydrates
- **Non-Diabetic Bodybuilding Misuse**: This practice has caused hospitalizations and deaths. The anabolic gain does not remotely justify the risk of dying from hypoglycemia
- **Alcohol Around Dosing**: Blocks hepatic glucose release and masks low-blood-sugar symptoms — extremely dangerous
- **Mixing Up Insulin Types**: Confusing rapid-acting with long-acting insulin can cause catastrophic dosing errors
- **Stacking Doses ("rage dosing")**: Taking more insulin because the first dose "isn't working yet" causes overlapping action and severe delayed lows
- **Exercising Hard After Dosing Without Extra Carbs**: Exercise amplifies glucose uptake and deepens lows
- **Ignoring Early Hypoglycemia Symptoms**: Shakiness, sweating, confusion, and irritability are warnings — treat immediately with fast glucose, do not wait
- **Reusing/Sharing Needles**: Infection and dosing-accuracy risks
- **No Glucagon Emergency Kit**: Anyone using insulin should have glucagon available and ensure others know how to use it
