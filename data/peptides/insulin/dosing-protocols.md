# Insulin

## Dosing Protocols

- **SAFETY FIRST — Prescribed Use Only**: Insulin dosing must be individualized by a physician based on blood glucose, carbohydrate intake, and insulin type. The information below is educational, NOT a protocol to follow. Misdosed insulin can kill within hours
- **Diabetes (Medical)**: Basal-bolus regimens combine a long-acting basal insulin (glargine, detemir, degludec) with rapid-acting boluses (lispro, aspart) dosed to carbohydrate counts and correction factors, all set by a care team
- **Carbohydrate Matching Is Everything**: Every unit of insulin must be matched to sufficient carbohydrate intake. Taking insulin without eating adequate carbs is the classic cause of fatal hypoglycemia
- **Bodybuilding Misuse (documented, NOT endorsed)**: Practitioners misuse small amounts of rapid-acting insulin post-workout with large carbohydrate doses — this practice has caused hospitalizations and deaths and is reported here only so the danger is understood
- **Never Dose Alone**: Hypoglycemia can render a person unconscious before they can treat it; misuse without supervision is especially lethal
- **Fast-Acting Carbs On Hand Always**: Anyone using insulin must have glucose tabs or juice immediately available and a glucose meter to verify blood sugar
