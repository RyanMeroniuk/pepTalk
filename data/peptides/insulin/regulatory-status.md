# Insulin

## Regulatory Status

- **FDA-APPROVED**: Long-established, essential medicine for diabetes; available in rapid-acting, short-acting, intermediate (NPH), and long-acting basal formulations
- **Prescription Required**: Modern analog insulins require a prescription; some older human insulins (e.g., regular, NPH) have been available without prescription in certain US states, though use without medical guidance is dangerous
- **On the WHO List of Essential Medicines**: Reflecting its life-saving role in diabetes care
- **Performance/Anabolic Use Is Off-Label and Dangerous**: Using insulin for bodybuilding is not an approved indication and has caused documented hospitalizations and deaths
- **Not a Controlled Substance**: But its lethal overdose potential makes unsupervised use extremely hazardous regardless of legal status
- **Bottom Line**: Legitimate, approved, and life-saving for diabetes under medical supervision; reckless and potentially fatal when misused for muscle gain
