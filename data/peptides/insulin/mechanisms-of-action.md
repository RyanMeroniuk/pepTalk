# Insulin

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Glucose Uptake**: Binds the insulin receptor on muscle and fat cells, triggering GLUT4 transporter translocation that pulls glucose out of the blood and into cells — the basis of both its therapeutic effect and its lethal overdose risk
- **Glycogen Storage**: Drives glucose into muscle and liver as glycogen, the anabolic effect bodybuilders chase for muscle fullness
- **Protein Synthesis / Anti-Catabolism**: Promotes amino acid uptake and protein synthesis while suppressing protein breakdown — a genuinely anabolic signal
- **Fat Storage (Lipogenesis)**: Promotes fat storage and inhibits fat breakdown, a double-edged effect for body composition
- **Suppresses Hepatic Glucose Output**: Tells the liver to stop releasing glucose, further lowering blood sugar
- **Narrow Therapeutic Window**: The same glucose-lowering action that treats diabetes causes life-threatening hypoglycemia when dosed in excess of carbohydrate intake — the central danger of insulin
