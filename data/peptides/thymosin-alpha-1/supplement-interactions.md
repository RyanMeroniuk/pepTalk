# Thymosin Alpha-1

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Synergistic**: Zinc (supports T-cell function)
- **Synergistic**: Vitamin D3 (immune modulation)
- **Synergistic**: Vitamin C (immune support)
- **Enhanced by**: Adequate sleep and stress management (cortisol suppresses immune function)
- **Caution**: Immunosuppressive drugs (may counteract or interact)
- **Caution**: Post-transplant patients (GVHD risk)
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy/breastfeeding
- **Contraindicated**: Active immunosuppression therapy (HSCT recipients)
