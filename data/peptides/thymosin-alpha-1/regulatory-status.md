# Thymosin Alpha-1

## Regulatory Status

- **FDA-approved** for chemotherapy adjuvant and chronic hepatitis B/C (as thymalfasin/Zadaxin)
- **Approved in 35+ countries** as an immune enhancer
- Available off-label via compounding pharmacies in the US
- One of the most extensively studied therapeutic peptides
- Reproductive toxicity data incomplete; long-term safety beyond 1 year understudied
