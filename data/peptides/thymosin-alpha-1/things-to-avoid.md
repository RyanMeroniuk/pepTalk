# Thymosin Alpha-1

## Things to Avoid

- **Chronic Psychological Stress** — sustained cortisol elevation directly shrinks the thymus and suppresses T-cell production, counteracting the core benefit of Thymosin Alpha-1
- **Sleep Deprivation** — even one night of poor sleep (<6 hours) measurably reduces T-cell function and increases inflammatory markers. Consistent poor sleep undermines immune peptide therapy
- **Overtraining Without Recovery** — excessive exercise volume or intensity without adequate rest creates an immunosuppressive window. Monitor for signs: frequent illness, prolonged soreness, fatigue
- **Smoking and Vaping** — tobacco smoke directly damages immune cells, impairs mucosal immunity, and increases infection risk. Vaping also disrupts lung immune defenses
- **Chronic Alcohol Use** — impairs both innate and adaptive immunity; reduces the very T-cell populations Thymosin Alpha-1 supports
- **Environmental Toxin Exposure** — mold, heavy metals, and persistent organic pollutants suppress immune function. Address any mold or environmental contamination in living/working spaces
- **Unnecessary Antibiotic Use** — broad-spectrum antibiotics devastate the gut microbiome, which is tightly linked to systemic immune function. Use only when medically necessary
- **Social Isolation** — chronic loneliness is associated with measurable immune suppression and increased inflammation. Maintain social connections for immune health
