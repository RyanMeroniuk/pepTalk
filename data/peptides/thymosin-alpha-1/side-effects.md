# Thymosin Alpha-1

## Side Effects

- **Common**: Injection site reactions (redness, swelling, discomfort), fatigue, headache, GI upset
- **Uncommon**: ALT elevation (hepatitis B patients), TSH abnormalities
- **Rare/Serious**: Potential graft-versus-host disease in transplant recipients, allergic reactions
- **Immunogenicity**: High doses may trigger excessive immune response
- No adverse effects in animal studies at doses up to 6mg/kg/day for 13 weeks
- Minimal systemic toxicity in clinical database
- No genotoxicity or carcinogenicity signals
