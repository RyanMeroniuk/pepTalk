# Thymosin Alpha-1

## Dosing Protocols

- **Standard**: 1-1.6mg subcutaneous injection
- **Frequency**: Typically 2x per week
- **Duration**: Up to 52 weeks in clinical studies
- **Safety Margins**: Established at 3-12x above typical human doses in animal models
- **Reconstitution**: Bacteriostatic water; refrigerate
- **Used continuously** in many clinical protocols without cycling
