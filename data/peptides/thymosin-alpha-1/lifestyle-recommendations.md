# Thymosin Alpha-1

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Prioritize Sleep Quality**: Aim for 7-9 hours of uninterrupted sleep — sleep deprivation dramatically reduces T-cell counts and cytokine production within a single night. Maintain a consistent sleep-wake schedule to support circadian-driven immune cycling
- **Stress Management Is Non-Negotiable**: Chronic stress elevates cortisol, which directly suppresses T-cell proliferation and thymic output — the exact pathways Thymosin Alpha-1 is trying to enhance. Practice daily stress reduction: meditation, deep breathing, nature exposure, or journaling
- **Exercise at Moderate Intensity**: Moderate exercise (brisk walking, cycling, swimming 30-45 minutes, 4-5x/week) enhances immune surveillance and T-cell circulation. Avoid chronic overtraining — excessive high-intensity exercise without recovery suppresses immune function for 24-72 hours post-session (the "open window" theory)
- **Avoid Overtraining**: If you are training hard, ensure adequate recovery days. Signs of immune suppression from overtraining include frequent colds, slow wound healing, and persistent fatigue
- **Optimize Vitamin D Levels**: Test serum 25(OH)D and target 40-60 ng/mL — vitamin D is a direct immune modulator that enhances both innate and adaptive immunity. Supplement 4000-5000 IU daily if levels are low
- **Cold Exposure (Optional)**: Brief cold showers or cold plunges (2-5 minutes) may increase circulating T-cells and NK cells. Avoid prolonged cold exposure which can suppress immunity
- **Limit Alcohol**: Even moderate alcohol consumption impairs immune cell function and gut barrier integrity, reducing the effectiveness of immune-supporting peptides
- **Stay Hydrated**: Adequate hydration supports lymphatic circulation, which is essential for immune cell trafficking
