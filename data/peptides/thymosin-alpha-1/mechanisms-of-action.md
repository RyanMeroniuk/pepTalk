# Thymosin Alpha-1

## Mechanisms of Action

- **T-Cell Modulation**: Influences production, maturation, and activity of T lymphocytes — the adaptive immune system's key players
- **Dendritic Cell Regulation**: Modulates immune-presenting cells to balance inflammatory responses
- **Th1/Treg Enhancement**: Promotes regulatory T cells while enhancing helper T cell function
- **Immunosurveillance**: Enhances tumor antigen expression and immune detection of cancer cells
- **Cytokine Modulation**: Balances pro- and anti-inflammatory cytokine production
- **NK Cell Enhancement**: Supports natural killer cell activity
- **Toll-Like Receptor Signaling**: Enhances innate immune recognition pathways
