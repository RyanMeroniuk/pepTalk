# Thymosin Alpha-1

## Overview

Thymosin alpha-1 (Ta-1) is a synthetic 28-amino acid peptide discovered in 1972 that mimics naturally occurring thymic hormones. The pharmaceutical form is called thymalfasin (brand name Zadaxin). One of the most clinically studied peptides in existence. Approved in 35+ countries as an immune enhancer. Used for immunocompromised states, malignancies, vaccine response enhancement, sepsis, and chronic infections. The thymus gland shrinks with age (thymic involution), reducing natural thymosin production — supplementation may help restore immune function in aging.
