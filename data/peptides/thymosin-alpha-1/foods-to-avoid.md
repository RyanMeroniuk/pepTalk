# Thymosin Alpha-1

## Foods to Avoid

- **Excess Sugar and Refined Carbohydrates** — high blood sugar impairs neutrophil phagocytosis (the ability to engulf pathogens) for up to 5 hours after consumption. Sugar also promotes chronic inflammation that suppresses adaptive immunity
- **Alcohol** — directly toxic to immune cells, impairs gut barrier function (increasing endotoxin translocation), and reduces T-cell and NK cell activity. Even moderate drinking blunts the immune benefits of Thymosin Alpha-1
- **Highly Processed Foods** — contain preservatives, emulsifiers, and seed oils that disrupt gut microbiome balance and promote systemic inflammation
- **Excess Omega-6 Seed Oils** (soybean, corn, canola, sunflower) — when consumed in excess relative to omega-3s, these promote pro-inflammatory prostaglandins that undermine immune balance
- **Trans Fats and Fried Foods** — directly impair immune cell function and promote oxidative stress
- **Artificial Sweeteners** — may disrupt gut microbiome composition, indirectly impairing immune function
- **Excess Caffeine** — while moderate coffee has antioxidant benefits, excessive caffeine (>400mg/day) elevates cortisol and can suppress immune function
