# Thymosin Alpha-1

## Benefits

- **Immune Restoration**: Restores immune function in immunocompromised patients
- **Cancer Adjunct**: Combined with dacarbazine chemotherapy, produced 3x response rate in stage IV melanoma. Enhances immunosurveillance
- **COVID-19**: Reduces proinflammatory cytokines and improves lymphocyte function during SARS-CoV-2 (2023 research)
- **Long COVID**: May restore appropriate immune response in chronically dysregulated patients
- **Hepatitis B/C**: Historical efficacy data (now superseded by direct-acting antivirals)
- **Vaccine Enhancement**: Improves vaccine response, especially in elderly and immunocompromised
- **Autoimmune Support**: Autoimmune patients have significantly lower serum Ta-1 levels; potential benefits for RA, lupus, MS
- **Sepsis**: Some evidence of mortality reduction (though a 2025 trial with 1000+ subjects was inconclusive)
