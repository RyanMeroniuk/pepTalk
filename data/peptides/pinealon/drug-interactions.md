# Pinealon

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Melatonin supplements**: Pinealon normalizes endogenous melatonin production; exogenous melatonin supplementation may cause excessive melatonin levels, leading to grogginess, vivid dreams, or disrupted sleep architecture. Use low-dose melatonin (0.3-0.5 mg) only if combining, and monitor effects
- **Sedatives and sleep medications (zolpidem, eszopiclone, benzodiazepines)**: Additive CNS depression possible; Pinealon's sleep-promoting effects combined with pharmaceutical sedatives may cause excessive drowsiness, impaired motor function, or respiratory depression
- **Circadian-affecting drugs (beta-blockers, corticosteroids)**: Beta-blockers suppress melatonin production; corticosteroids disrupt circadian cortisol rhythms. Both may counteract Pinealon's circadian normalization mechanism
- **Antihistamines (diphenhydramine, doxylamine)**: Sedating antihistamines combined with Pinealon may cause excessive drowsiness and next-day impairment
- **Stimulant medications (methylphenidate, amphetamines)**: May counteract Pinealon's circadian and sleep-supporting effects if taken late in the day
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy and breastfeeding — insufficient safety data; melatonin-modulating effects may affect fetal development
- **Contraindicated**: Narcolepsy or hypersomnia — additional sleep-promoting effects may worsen excessive daytime sleepiness
