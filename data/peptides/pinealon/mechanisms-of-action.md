# Pinealon

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Pineal Gland Support**: Regulates pineal gland function and melatonin production
- **Circadian Rhythm Regulation**: Normalizes sleep-wake cycles
- **Neuroprotection**: Protects CNS neurons, particularly in aging
- **Gene Expression**: Penetrates cell membranes and interacts with DNA to regulate gene expression (bioregulator mechanism)
- **Antioxidant**: Reduces oxidative stress in neural tissue
- **Memory Enhancement**: Improves learning and memory formation pathways
