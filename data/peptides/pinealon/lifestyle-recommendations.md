# Pinealon

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Strict Circadian Rhythm Discipline**: Pinealon is a pineal gland bioregulator designed to normalize circadian rhythms. Its effectiveness depends entirely on supporting the sleep-wake cycle it is trying to restore. Go to bed and wake at the same time every day — including weekends — with no more than 30 minutes of variation
- **Morning Sunlight Exposure (10-20 min within first hour of waking)**: Bright morning light suppresses melatonin and sets the circadian clock via the suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN). This is the single most powerful circadian signal and directly supports Pinealon's rhythm-normalizing mechanism
- **Evening Light Restriction (2-3 hours before bed)**: Dim all lights after sunset and use warm-toned (amber/red) lighting. Bright or blue-enriched light at night suppresses the pineal gland's melatonin production — the exact function Pinealon is trying to restore
- **Blue Light Blocking Glasses (after sunset)**: If screens must be used in the evening, wear blue-light-blocking glasses (amber or red lensed). Blue light wavelengths (450-490nm) are the most potent melatonin suppressors
- **Cool Sleeping Environment (65-68°F / 18-20°C)**: Core body temperature must drop 2-3°F to initiate sleep. A cool bedroom facilitates this thermoregulatory process, working in concert with Pinealon's melatonin-supporting effects
- **Complete Darkness for Sleep**: Use blackout curtains and cover all LED indicators. Even small amounts of light detected through closed eyelids can suppress melatonin secretion via the retinohypothalamic tract
- **Evening Dose Timing**: Take Pinealon in the evening (1-2 hours before bed) to align with the natural rise of melatonin. This synchronizes the bioregulator's effects with the pineal gland's endogenous rhythm
- **Aerobic Exercise (morning or early afternoon)**: Exercise supports circadian rhythm entrainment and improves sleep architecture. However, vigorous exercise within 3 hours of bedtime can delay sleep onset by raising core temperature and cortisol
- **Meditation or Relaxation Practice (10-15 min before bed)**: Activates the parasympathetic nervous system and lowers cortisol, preparing the brain for the transition to sleep. This complements Pinealon's neuroprotective and calming effects
- **Cognitive Engagement During Daytime**: Pinealon enhances memory and learning pathways. Use daytime hours for mentally demanding tasks to leverage the cognitive benefits while reserving evenings for wind-down activities
- **Limit Napping (max 20 min, before 2 PM)**: Long or late naps reduce sleep drive (adenosine pressure) and fragment the circadian rhythm Pinealon is working to normalize
- **Regular Meal Timing**: Eating at consistent times reinforces circadian rhythms via peripheral clocks in the liver and gut. Avoid eating within 3 hours of bedtime, as digestion raises core temperature and can impair sleep onset
