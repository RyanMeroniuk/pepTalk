# Pinealon

## Overview

Pinealon is a synthetic tripeptide (Glu-Asp-Arg) bioregulator derived from the pineal gland. Part of Professor Khavinson's bioregulator peptide research program in Russia (same researcher behind Epitalon). Designed to support pineal gland function, regulate circadian rhythms, and provide neuroprotective cognitive benefits. Often used alongside Epitalon for comprehensive pineal support. Available in both injectable and oral/sublingual forms.
