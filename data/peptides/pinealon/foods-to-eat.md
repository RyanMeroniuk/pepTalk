# Pinealon

## Foods to Eat

- **Tart Cherries / Tart Cherry Juice**: One of the only natural foods containing significant melatonin. Studies show tart cherry juice increases sleep duration and quality — directly supporting Pinealon's melatonin-normalizing goal
- **Kiwifruit (2 kiwis, 1 hour before bed)**: Clinical studies show eating 2 kiwis before bed significantly improves sleep onset, duration, and efficiency. Rich in serotonin, folate, and antioxidants
- **Fatty Fish (salmon, sardines)**: High in omega-3s and vitamin D, both of which regulate serotonin production — the precursor to melatonin. Also supports the neuroprotective benefits Pinealon provides
- **Turkey & Poultry**: Rich in tryptophan, the amino acid precursor to serotonin and ultimately melatonin. Adequate tryptophan intake supports the melatonin production Pinealon is designed to restore
- **Pumpkin Seeds**: Excellent source of magnesium (nature's relaxation mineral), zinc, and tryptophan — all supporting GABA function and melatonin synthesis
- **Almonds & Walnuts**: Walnuts contain melatonin directly; almonds are rich in magnesium. Both support sleep quality
- **Bananas**: Contain tryptophan, magnesium, and vitamin B6 (needed to convert tryptophan to serotonin to melatonin)
- **Warm Milk or Chamomile Tea**: Traditional sleep aids with genuine science — milk contains tryptophan and casein-derived peptides, chamomile binds GABA-A receptors
- **Dark Leafy Greens (spinach, Swiss chard)**: Rich in magnesium, which supports GABA receptor function and melatonin synthesis
- **Whole Grains (oats, brown rice)**: Complex carbohydrates facilitate tryptophan transport across the blood-brain barrier by triggering insulin release, which clears competing amino acids
