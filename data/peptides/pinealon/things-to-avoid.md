# Pinealon

## Things to Avoid

- **Irregular Sleep Schedule**: This is the single most damaging behavior for circadian rhythm health. Pinealon works to normalize the sleep-wake cycle, but varying bedtimes by hours on weekends versus weekdays creates "social jet lag" that overwhelms its bioregulatory effects
- **Blue Light Exposure at Night**: Screens (phones, tablets, TVs, computers) emit blue wavelengths that suppress pineal melatonin production for up to 90 minutes after exposure. This directly counteracts Pinealon's primary function of restoring melatonin secretion
- **Late-Night Exercise (within 3 hours of bed)**: Vigorous exercise raises cortisol and core body temperature, both of which must be low for sleep onset. Exercise in the morning or early afternoon instead
- **Late Caffeine Consumption**: Even "afternoon" coffee at 2-3 PM can have residual caffeine active at bedtime in slow metabolizers. Know your sensitivity and cut off accordingly
- **Napping After 2 PM or Longer Than 20 Minutes**: Late or long naps reduce adenosine sleep pressure and shift the circadian phase, making it harder to fall asleep at the target bedtime
- **Bright Overhead Lights in the Evening**: Even non-screen light sources (overhead LEDs, fluorescent tubes) suppress melatonin if they are bright and blue-enriched. Switch to dim, warm-toned lamps after sunset
- **Night Shift Work (if avoidable)**: Night shifts are fundamentally incompatible with circadian rhythm normalization. If unavoidable, use strategic light exposure and discuss timing adjustments with a healthcare provider
- **Chronic Stress Without Management**: Cortisol is the primary antagonist of melatonin. Chronic stress keeps cortisol elevated at night, directly suppressing the pineal gland function Pinealon is designed to support
