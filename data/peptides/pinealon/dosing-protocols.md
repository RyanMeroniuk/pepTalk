# Pinealon

## Dosing Protocols

- **Oral/Sublingual**: 5-10mg daily
- **Injectable**: 1-2mg subcutaneous daily (less common)
- **Capsule**: Available as oral bioregulator capsules
- **Cycle**: 10-30 days on, 2-3 months off
- **Timing**: Evening/before bed for sleep benefits
- **Can be combined with Epitalon** for comprehensive pineal support
