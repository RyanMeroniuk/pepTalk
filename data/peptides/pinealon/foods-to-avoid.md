# Pinealon

## Foods to Avoid

- **Caffeine After 12 PM (noon)**: Caffeine has a half-life of 5-7 hours and blocks adenosine receptors, directly suppressing sleep drive. Even afternoon caffeine can fragment sleep architecture and reduce the deep sleep phases Pinealon promotes
- **Alcohol**: While it may feel sedating, alcohol fragments sleep architecture, suppresses REM sleep, and disrupts melatonin secretion by the pineal gland — directly counteracting Pinealon's core mechanism
- **Heavy Meals Within 3 Hours of Bed**: Digestion raises core body temperature and can cause acid reflux, both of which impair sleep onset and quality
- **Spicy Foods in the Evening**: Capsaicin raises core body temperature and can cause gastric discomfort, delaying sleep onset
- **Excess Sugar Before Bed**: Blood sugar spikes followed by crashes trigger cortisol and adrenaline release, which disrupts the calm neurochemical state needed for melatonin-driven sleep onset
- **Chocolate After 4 PM**: Contains caffeine and theobromine, both stimulants that can interfere with sleep when consumed late
- **High-Tyramine Foods Late at Night (aged cheese, cured meats)**: Tyramine promotes norepinephrine release, which is alerting and can impair sleep onset
- **Excess Fluids Before Bed**: Nocturia (nighttime urination) fragments sleep. Limit fluid intake in the final 2 hours before bed
