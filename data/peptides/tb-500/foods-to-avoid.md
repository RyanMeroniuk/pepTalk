# TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4)

## Foods to Avoid

- **Alcohol**: Impairs protein synthesis, disrupts growth hormone release during sleep, increases inflammation, and delays wound healing. Eliminate during TB-500 protocols
- **Refined Sugar**: Drives inflammatory cytokine production and competes with vitamin C for cellular uptake — directly impairing collagen synthesis
- **Processed Seed Oils**: Soybean, corn, canola, and sunflower oils promote omega-6-driven inflammation, counteracting TB-500's anti-inflammatory benefits
- **Trans Fats & Hydrogenated Oils**: Found in fried foods, margarine, and packaged snacks. Increase systemic inflammation and impair cell membrane fluidity needed for tissue repair
- **Excess Sodium**: High sodium intake promotes water retention and swelling, potentially worsening inflammation around healing tissue
- **Processed Meats**: Hot dogs, deli meats, and bacon contain nitrates and preservatives that increase oxidative stress and inflammatory markers
- **Fried & Charred Foods**: Advanced glycation end products (AGEs) from high-heat cooking promote oxidative damage and impair healing
- **Excess Caffeine**: More than 200mg/day (about 2 cups coffee) can elevate cortisol and impair sleep quality, both of which slow tissue repair
