# TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4)

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Blood thinners (warfarin, heparin, aspirin, clopidogrel, rivaroxaban)**: TB-500 promotes angiogenesis (new blood vessel formation) and enhances cell migration, which may increase bleeding risk or complicate anticoagulant therapy. Monitor INR closely if on warfarin
- **Immunosuppressants (tacrolimus, cyclosporine, mycophenolate)**: TB-500 modulates immune function and promotes immune-mediated tissue repair. Combining with immunosuppressive drugs may cause unpredictable immune responses — either reduced healing efficacy or altered immunosuppression
- **Corticosteroid injections (cortisone shots)**: Corticosteroids weaken tendons and suppress tissue repair, directly opposing TB-500's healing mechanisms. Avoid concurrent use at the same injury site
- **Chemotherapy / anti-angiogenic drugs (bevacizumab, etc.)**: TB-500's pro-angiogenic and cell-proliferative properties may theoretically counteract anti-cancer therapies designed to inhibit blood vessel formation and cell growth
- **NSAIDs (ibuprofen, naproxen)**: While not strictly contraindicated, NSAIDs suppress the initial inflammatory cascade required for proper tissue healing. TB-500 modulates inflammation naturally; NSAIDs may reduce its effectiveness
- **Growth factors and other proliferative agents**: Combining TB-500 with other growth-promoting compounds may create excessive or disorganized cell proliferation at injury sites
- **Contraindicated: Active cancer or history of cancer** — TB-500 promotes cell migration, proliferation, angiogenesis, and anti-apoptotic signaling (Akt pathway activation). These mechanisms may theoretically support tumor growth, survival, and metastasis
- **Contraindicated: Pregnancy and breastfeeding** — insufficient safety data; TB-500's effects on cell proliferation and migration pose unknown risks to fetal development
- **Contraindicated: Active systemic infection** — TB-500's immune-modulating effects and promotion of cell migration could theoretically complicate an active infection
- **Caution: Autoimmune conditions** — TB-500's immune modulation may unpredictably affect autoimmune disease activity. Use under close medical supervision
