# TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4)

## Foods to Eat

- **Grass-Fed Beef & Organ Meats**: Rich in bioavailable iron, zinc, and B-vitamins critical for tissue repair. Liver provides retinol (vitamin A) for cell differentiation at injury sites
- **Wild-Caught Salmon & Sardines**: Omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA) reduce chronic inflammation and support the resolution phase of healing that TB-500 promotes
- **Eggs (Whole)**: Complete protein with all essential amino acids, plus choline for cell membrane synthesis during tissue regeneration
- **Bone Broth**: Provides collagen, glycine, proline, and hyaluronic acid — structural components that TB-500 needs to rebuild connective tissue. Consume 1-2 cups daily
- **Collagen Peptides**: 10-15g daily dissolved in liquid. Provides direct building blocks for tendon, ligament, and connective tissue repair
- **Dark Leafy Greens**: Spinach, kale, and collard greens deliver vitamin K (essential for bone healing), vitamin C (collagen synthesis cofactor), and magnesium (muscle relaxation)
- **Citrus Fruits & Bell Peppers**: High vitamin C content is critical for collagen cross-linking. Consume 2-3 servings daily during healing protocols
- **Pineapple**: Contains bromelain, a natural anti-inflammatory enzyme that reduces swelling and complements TB-500's anti-inflammatory action
- **Tart Cherry Juice**: Natural anti-inflammatory and sleep promoter. Contains melatonin precursors and anthocyanins that reduce muscle damage markers
- **Turmeric with Black Pepper**: Curcumin reduces NF-kB inflammatory signaling. Combine with piperine (black pepper) for 2000% increased absorption
- **Sweet Potatoes**: Provide beta-carotene (vitamin A precursor) for epithelial and connective tissue repair, plus complex carbohydrates for sustained energy during recovery
- **Walnuts & Pumpkin Seeds**: Plant-based omega-3s (ALA) and zinc for immune-supported healing
