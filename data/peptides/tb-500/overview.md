# TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4)

## Overview

TB-500 is the synthetic version of thymosin beta-4 (TB4), a naturally occurring 43-amino acid protein found abundantly in nearly all mammalian cells. Originally identified in the thymus gland, thymosin beta-4 is one of the most abundant intracellular proteins and serves as a critical regulator of actin polymerization and cellular motility. TB-500 is distinguished from endogenous TB4 by its N-terminal acetylation, which enhances stability and biological activity. Often paired with BPC-157 in the popular "Wolverine Stack" for maximum healing.
