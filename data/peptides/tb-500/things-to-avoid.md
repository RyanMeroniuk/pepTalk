# TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4)

## Things to Avoid

- **Returning to Full Training Too Early**: TB-500 accelerates healing but new tissue needs time to mature and strengthen. Avoid maximal loads, plyometrics, or sport-specific drills for at least 4-6 weeks into the protocol. Premature return to full activity risks re-injury and disorganized scar tissue formation
- **NSAIDs (Ibuprofen, Naproxen)**: NSAIDs block the initial inflammatory cascade required for proper tissue healing. TB-500 modulates inflammation naturally without suppressing the necessary healing response. Avoid NSAIDs unless medically necessary
- **Corticosteroid Injections**: Cortisone shots weaken tendons and connective tissue over time. They suppress the tissue repair processes that TB-500 is designed to enhance
- **Smoking & Nicotine**: Nicotine constricts blood vessels and reduces oxygen delivery to healing tissues, directly opposing TB-500's angiogenic (new blood vessel formation) effects
- **Chronic Stress & Sleep Deprivation**: Both elevate cortisol, which breaks down connective tissue and impairs the immune-mediated healing cascade. Prioritize sleep and stress management as seriously as the peptide itself
- **Prolonged Ice Application**: Extended icing (beyond 15 minutes) reduces blood flow and may impair TB-500's ability to promote cell migration to the injury site. Brief ice (10-15 minutes) for acute pain is acceptable
- **Heavy Stretching of Healing Tissue**: Aggressive stretching of a repairing tendon or ligament can disrupt newly forming collagen fibers. Use gentle range-of-motion work instead of deep stretching during the first 4 weeks
- **Dehydration**: Insufficient water intake reduces blood volume and impairs nutrient delivery to healing sites. Connective tissue is heavily dependent on hydration for proper function
- **Extended Fasting**: Prolonged fasts (16+ hours) during active healing protocols deprive the body of amino acids needed for tissue synthesis. Maintain regular protein-rich meals every 3-4 hours
