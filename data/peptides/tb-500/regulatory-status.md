# TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4)

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT FDA-approved** for any human indication
- Not an over-the-counter treatment — requires prescription from licensed provider
- Available from compounding pharmacies and research suppliers
- **Banned by WADA** for competitive athletes
- Extensive use in veterinary medicine (racehorses)
- Human clinical trials limited but promising (venous ulcers, cardiac)
