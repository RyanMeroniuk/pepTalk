# TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4)

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Progressive Rehabilitation**: TB-500 works systemically to promote cell migration to injury sites. Support this by performing gentle, controlled movement daily — range-of-motion exercises, walking, swimming, or light cycling. Begin at 30-50% of normal intensity and increase by 10% per week as healing progresses
- **Protein Loading for Repair**: Consume 1.8-2.2g protein per kg body weight daily during TB-500 protocols. The peptide promotes cell migration and tissue repair, but requires adequate amino acid substrates. Prioritize leucine-rich sources (eggs, chicken, whey) to stimulate muscle protein synthesis
- **Sleep for Growth Hormone Synergy**: Target 8-9 hours during active healing. TB-500's tissue repair effects compound with the growth hormone pulse that occurs during deep sleep (stages 3-4). Maintain a consistent sleep-wake schedule and avoid blue light 90 minutes before bed
- **Reduce Re-Injury Risk**: TB-500 promotes organized tissue repair rather than fibrotic scarring, but this new tissue is still vulnerable. Avoid high-impact, explosive, or maximal-effort activities during the first 4-6 weeks of a protocol. Use braces, wraps, or supports as needed
- **Stress Reduction**: Cortisol impairs wound healing and increases tissue breakdown. Implement daily stress management: 10 minutes of diaphragmatic breathing, meditation, nature walks, or gentle stretching. Magnesium glycinate (300-400mg) at night supports both relaxation and tissue repair
- **Hydration**: Maintain robust hydration (minimum half your body weight in ounces of water daily). Hydrated connective tissue heals more efficiently and maintains better flexibility — one of TB-500's key benefits
- **Contrast Therapy**: Alternate warm and cool water exposure (3 minutes warm, 1 minute cool, repeat 3-4 cycles) to promote blood flow and reduce inflammation. This supports TB-500's angiogenic mechanisms by enhancing circulation to healing tissues
- **Avoid Immobilization**: Unless medically required, avoid prolonged immobilization of injured areas. Controlled movement promotes aligned collagen deposition and enhances TB-500's cell migration effects
- **Morning Sunlight**: Get 10-20 minutes of morning sun exposure to optimize vitamin D levels and circadian rhythm, both of which support immune function and tissue repair
