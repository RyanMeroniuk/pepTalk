# TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4)

## Dosing Protocols

- **Loading Phase**: 2-5mg subcutaneous injection, 2-3x per week for 4-6 weeks
- **Maintenance Phase**: 2-5mg every 1-2 weeks
- **Cycle**: 3 months on, 6 weeks off — OR — 6 weeks on, 6 weeks off
- **Do not exceed 3 months** continuous use without cycling
- **Injection Site**: Can inject anywhere subcutaneously — TB-500 acts systemically
- **Reconstitution**: Bacteriostatic water; refrigerate after reconstitution
