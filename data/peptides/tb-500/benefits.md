# TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4)

## Benefits

- **Tissue Repair**: Repairs damaged muscles, tendons, ligaments, and skin
- **Injury Recovery**: Accelerates healing from sprains, strains, and surgical procedures
- **Wound Healing**: In human trials for venous ulcers, 0.03% synthetic TB4 increased healing rate — 25% of patients achieved full healing within 3 months
- **Cardiac Health**: Enhanced myocyte survival and improved cardiac function in ischemic damage models
- **Flexibility**: Improved range of motion and joint flexibility
- **Hair Regrowth**: Stimulates follicle stem cells for hair regeneration
- **Reduced Scarring**: Promotes organized tissue repair rather than fibrotic scarring
- **Systemic Action**: Unlike BPC-157 which works best near injection site, TB-500 has systemic effects regardless of injection location
