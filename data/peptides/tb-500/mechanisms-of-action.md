# TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4)

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Actin Regulation**: Binds and sequesters actin monomers, influencing cytoskeletal filament assembly and enabling cell migration
- **Cell Migration**: Enhances migration of endothelial cells, myocardial cells, and progenitor cells to injury sites
- **Anti-Apoptotic**: Inhibits cellular death by activating the Akt survival pathway via focal adhesion complex
- **Anti-Inflammatory**: Reduces inflammation systemically and locally at injury sites
- **Angiogenesis**: Promotes formation of new blood vessels to damaged tissue
- **Hair Growth**: Stimulates hair follicle stem cells and promotes follicle regeneration
- **Cardiac Protection**: Binds cardiac tissue and promotes myocyte survival after cardiac damage
