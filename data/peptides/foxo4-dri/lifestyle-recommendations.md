# FOXO4-DRI

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Understand the Experimental Nature**: Before any consideration, recognize FOXO4-DRI has no human data; lifestyle measures below are evidence-based ways to reduce senescent-cell burden naturally.
- **Exercise Reduces Senescence**: Regular aerobic and resistance training lowers senescent-cell accumulation and SASP inflammation in humans — a proven, safe alternative to experimental senolytics. Aim for 3–5 sessions per week.
- **Prioritize Sleep**: Quality sleep supports cellular repair and immune clearance of damaged cells. Target 7–9 hours.
- **Anti-Inflammatory Diet**: Chronic inflammation accelerates senescence; whole foods, fatty fish, and polyphenols help counter it.
- **Caloric Moderation / Fasting**: Time-restricted eating and avoiding chronic overnutrition reduce metabolic drivers of cellular senescence.
- **Avoid Senescence Accelerators**: Limit UV overexposure, smoking, and excess alcohol — all increase senescent-cell burden.
- **Regular Cancer Screening**: Anyone even considering p53-modulating compounds should maintain age-appropriate cancer screening.
- **Work With Clinicians**: Given the theoretical risks, no self-experimentation should occur without medical oversight.
- **Track Nothing Blindly**: There are no validated biomarkers of response in humans — do not interpret subjective changes as proof of benefit.
