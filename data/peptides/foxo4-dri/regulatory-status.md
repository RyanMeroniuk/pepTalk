# FOXO4-DRI

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT FDA-approved** for any indication.
- **ZERO human clinical trials** — has never been tested in people for safety or efficacy.
- Evidence is limited to cell-culture and animal work, anchored by the landmark 2017 Baar et al. mouse study published in Cell.
- No established human dosing, pharmacokinetics, or toxicology.
- Available only from research peptide suppliers as a research-use-only compound; not a medicine.
- Carries a central theoretical safety concern around p53 manipulation and cancer risk.
- Should be regarded as highly experimental and unsuitable for non-research human use.
- Active topic in senolytic and geroscience research (2025–2026), but far from clinical translation.
