# FOXO4-DRI

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **No Validated Interactions**: There are no established human interaction data — all pairings below are theoretical concepts from the senolytic/longevity community, not recommendations.
- **Conceptually Synergistic**: NMN (250–500mg) — supports NAD+ and DNA repair, a complementary anti-aging pathway after senescent-cell clearance.
- **Conceptually Synergistic**: Resveratrol — sirtuin support discussed alongside senolytics for healthspan, though combined data is absent.
- **Conceptually Synergistic**: Quercetin — itself studied as a senolytic; combining two senolytics is unstudied and could amplify off-target effects.
- **Enhanced by**: Omega-3 (2–3g EPA/DHA) — general anti-inflammatory support that may complement reduced SASP.
- **Enhanced by**: Vitamin-c — antioxidant support during periods of cell turnover.
- **Caution**: Avoid stacking with other senolytics or apoptosis-modulating agents — additive off-target cell death is a real theoretical risk.
- **Contraindicated**: Active cancer or cancer history (p53/apoptosis manipulation concern).
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy/breastfeeding.
