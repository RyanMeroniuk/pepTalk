# FOXO4-DRI

## Things to Avoid

- **Assuming It Is Safe or Effective in Humans**: FOXO4-DRI has ZERO human trials. Mouse results do not establish human safety, dosing, or benefit — treat it as purely experimental.
- **Use With Any Cancer Risk**: Manipulating the FOXO4-p53 axis has unknown effects on cancer biology. Avoid entirely with active cancer, a cancer history, or elevated cancer risk.
- **Self-Experimentation Without Oversight**: With no validated dose or safety data, unsupervised use is unsanctioned and risky.
- **Stacking With Other Senolytics**: Combining with quercetin/dasatinib-type senolytics or apoptosis modulators risks additive off-target cell death.
- **Unregulated Sourcing**: Research peptides vary widely in purity; impure FOXO4-DRI risks contamination and unpredictable toxicity.
- **Extrapolating Dose From Animals**: Mouse intraperitoneal dosing cannot be scaled to a human protocol — there is no valid conversion.
- **Skipping Cancer Screening**: Anyone considering p53-modulating agents should maintain rigorous, age-appropriate screening.
- **Ignoring the Evidence Gap**: The entire human risk-benefit picture is unknown — do not let mouse-study hype substitute for evidence.
