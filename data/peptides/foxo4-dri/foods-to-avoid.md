# FOXO4-DRI

## Foods to Avoid

- **Excessive Alcohol**: Accelerates cellular senescence and inflammation, working against any senolytic intent and stressing the liver.
- **Refined Sugars**: Candy, soda, pastries — drive glycation and metabolic stress that promote senescence.
- **Ultra-Processed Foods**: Fast food, packaged snacks, deli meats — inflammatory additives increase senescent-cell burden.
- **Trans Fats and Oxidized Seed Oils**: Repeatedly fried oils and hydrogenated fats promote inflammation and cellular damage.
- **High-Fructose Corn Syrup**: Drives metabolic dysfunction and oxidative stress that accelerate aging.
- **Charred and Processed Meats**: Form advanced glycation end-products and carry compounds linked to DNA damage.
- **Chronic Overnutrition**: Persistent caloric excess promotes metabolic stress and senescence regardless of food source.
