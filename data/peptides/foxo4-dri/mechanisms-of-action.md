# FOXO4-DRI

## Mechanisms of Action

- **FOXO4-p53 Disruption**: Competitively blocks the binding between FOXO4 and p53, the interaction that keeps p53 sequestered in senescent cells.
- **p53 Release and Apoptosis**: Freed p53 relocates to the mitochondria and nucleus, triggering programmed cell death selectively in senescent cells.
- **Senolytic Selectivity**: Healthy cells, which depend less on the FOXO4-p53 axis for survival, are largely spared at appropriate exposures.
- **D-Retro-Inverso Design**: Built from D-amino acids in reversed sequence to resist proteases, dramatically increasing stability and half-life versus a native L-peptide.
- **SASP Reduction (Indirect)**: By clearing senescent cells, it lowers the senescence-associated secretory phenotype — the inflammatory cytokine output that drives tissue aging.
- **Cell-Penetrating Action**: Engineered to cross cell membranes to reach intracellular FOXO4-p53 complexes.
- **Targeted Clearance Concept**: Aims to remove damaged cells that resist normal apoptosis, a core "geroscience" anti-aging strategy.
- **Note on Evidence**: All mechanistic claims derive from cell and mouse studies — human mechanism and selectivity are unconfirmed.
