# FOXO4-DRI

## Dosing Protocols

- **No Human Dosing Exists**: There is NO established, validated, or safe human dose — FOXO4-DRI has never been tested in human clinical trials.
- **Animal Reference Only**: The 2017 mouse study used intraperitoneal injections given as several pulses over a period of days; this does not translate to a human protocol.
- **Intermittent/Pulsed Concept**: Senolytics are generally conceived as "hit-and-run" intermittent dosing rather than daily continuous use, since the goal is periodic clearance.
- **Anecdotal Internet Protocols**: Unverified online sources cite milligram-range subcutaneous pulses, but these have NO scientific basis and carry unknown risk.
- **Route**: Animal studies used intraperitoneal injection; no validated human route exists.
- **Half-Life**: The DRI design extends stability, but human pharmacokinetics are entirely uncharacterized.
- **Reconstitution / Storage**: If handled as a research compound, bacteriostatic water and refrigeration are typical, but this is not a use recommendation.
- **Bottom Line**: Any human use is unsanctioned self-experimentation with unknown dosing and unknown safety.
