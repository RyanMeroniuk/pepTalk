# FOXO4-DRI

## Benefits

- **Senescent Cell Clearance**: Selectively eliminated senescent cells in the 2017 Baar et al. mouse study; the foundational and best-known finding.
- **Restored Physical Fitness**: Aged mice showed improved spontaneous activity and running endurance after treatment.
- **Fur Density Recovery**: Mice with chemotherapy- and age-induced fur loss regrew fur, a striking visible result.
- **Kidney Function**: Improved markers of renal function in aged and damaged mice.
- **Reduced Inflammation (Theoretical)**: Clearing senescent cells should lower SASP-driven chronic inflammation, though this is inferred for humans.
- **Anti-Aging Interest**: A prominent proof-of-concept for the senolytic approach to extending healthspan.
- **No Human Benefits Demonstrated**: There are ZERO human trials — none of these benefits have been shown in people, and all are extrapolated from animals.
- **Highly Experimental**: Should be regarded as a research molecule with unproven and unquantified human value.
