# FOXO4-DRI

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Chemotherapy**: Contraindicated — FOXO4-DRI manipulates the p53/apoptosis axis that chemotherapy also targets; combining could produce unpredictable and dangerous effects on both healthy and malignant cells.
- **Other Senolytics (Dasatinib, Quercetin)**: Avoid combining — additive senolytic and apoptotic activity risks excessive off-target cell death.
- **Corticosteroids**: Steroids modulate apoptosis and immune clearance pathways that may overlap with FOXO4-DRI; use caution and monitor.
- **Anticoagulants / Blood Thinners**: No established interaction; standard injection-site caution applies, monitor for bruising.
- **Immunosuppressants**: Theoretical interaction with immune clearance of dying cells; caution warranted.
- **Contraindicated**: Active cancer or cancer history (p53/apoptosis manipulation — theoretical cancer concern is central to this peptide).
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy and breastfeeding (no safety data; apoptosis induction risk to a fetus).
- **Caution**: Liver disease or kidney disease — clearance and safety entirely uncharacterized in humans.
