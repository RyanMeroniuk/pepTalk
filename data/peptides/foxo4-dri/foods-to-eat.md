# FOXO4-DRI

## Foods to Eat

- **Senolytic-Adjacent Polyphenols**: Foods rich in quercetin (capers, red onions, apples) and fisetin (strawberries) — natural compounds studied for mild senolytic and anti-senescence effects.
- **Omega-3 Rich Fish**: Wild salmon, sardines, mackerel — anti-inflammatory support that complements reduced SASP.
- **Colorful Berries**: Blueberries, strawberries, blackberries — antioxidants and fisetin that counter cellular aging.
- **Dark Leafy Greens**: Spinach, kale, Swiss chard — folate, magnesium, and antioxidants supporting cellular repair.
- **Cruciferous Vegetables**: Broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts — sulforaphane supports detoxification and antioxidant defenses.
- **Green Tea**: Provides EGCG polyphenols associated with reduced inflammation and cellular stress.
- **Extra Virgin Olive Oil**: Polyphenol-rich healthy fat that supports an anti-inflammatory diet pattern.
- **Quality Protein**: Eggs, fish, poultry, legumes — amino acids for healthy tissue maintenance.
