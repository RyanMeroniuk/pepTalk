# FOXO4-DRI

## Overview

FOXO4-DRI is a synthetic, D-retro-inverso (DRI) peptide engineered to be a senolytic — an agent that selectively kills senescent "zombie" cells that accumulate with age and secrete inflammatory signals. It works by disrupting the interaction between the FOXO4 protein and p53 inside senescent cells, releasing p53 to trigger their apoptosis while sparing healthy cells. The DRI modification uses D-amino acids in reverse sequence to resist enzymatic degradation, greatly extending the peptide's stability. It rose to prominence from a landmark 2017 mouse study (Baar et al., published in Cell) that showed restored fitness, fur density, and kidney function in aged mice. Critically, FOXO4-DRI has undergone ZERO human clinical trials, has no established human dosing, and carries a theoretical concern around p53 manipulation and cancer — it should be regarded as highly experimental.
