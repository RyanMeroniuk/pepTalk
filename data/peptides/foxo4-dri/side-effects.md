# FOXO4-DRI

## Side Effects

- Unknown human side-effect profile — no human safety data of any kind exists.
- Injection site reactions expected with any injectable peptide.
- Theoretical risk of off-target apoptosis in healthy cells if selectivity fails in humans.
- Theoretical p53-related risks, including unknown effects on cancer biology (see Things to Avoid).
- Possible acute toxicity at unvalidated doses — no safe range is established.
- Risk from research-grade material of variable, unverified purity.
- **ZERO human safety data** — all toxicology is from animals.
- **Highly experimental** — human risks are genuinely unknown.
