# Argireline (Acetyl Hexapeptide-8)

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT FDA-approved** as a drug; regulated as a cosmetic ingredient.
- Sold freely in over-the-counter skincare serums and creams without prescription.
- Widely used as a cosmetic active in the US, EU, and elsewhere under INCI name "Acetyl Hexapeptide-8" (formerly Acetyl Hexapeptide-3).
- Marketing claims are cosmetic ("appearance of wrinkles"), not therapeutic; it is not a substitute for botulinum toxin.
- Considered safe for topical cosmetic use; not authorized for injection or oral use.
