# Argireline (Acetyl Hexapeptide-8)

## Overview

Argireline (acetyl hexapeptide-8, also marketed as acetyl hexapeptide-3) is a synthetic six-amino-acid peptide developed as a topical cosmetic ingredient and often described as "topical Botox-lite." Its sequence mimics the N-terminal end of SNAP-25, a protein in the SNARE complex that mediates neurotransmitter release at the neuromuscular junction, allowing it to partially interfere with the muscle contractions that form expression lines. It is used almost exclusively in leave-on skincare serums and creams targeting forehead lines, crow's feet, and frown lines. Efficacy is modest compared to injectable botulinum toxin, and its large, water-soluble molecule penetrates skin poorly, which limits how much reaches the underlying muscle. It is well-tolerated topically and frequently formulated alongside GHK-Cu and other peptides; it is not used as an injectable.
