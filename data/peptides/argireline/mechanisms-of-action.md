# Argireline (Acetyl Hexapeptide-8)

## Mechanisms of Action

- **SNAP-25 Mimicry**: The peptide sequence mimics the N-terminal domain of SNAP-25, a core protein of the SNARE complex that drives vesicle fusion and neurotransmitter release.
- **SNARE Complex Inhibition**: By competing with native SNAP-25, it partially destabilizes SNARE complex formation, reducing acetylcholine release at the neuromuscular junction.
- **Reduced Muscle Contraction**: Lower acetylcholine signaling means weaker contraction of facial mimic muscles, softening the dynamic wrinkles those muscles create.
- **Topical, Reversible Action**: Unlike botulinum toxin, the effect is mild, surface-level, and reversible — it does not cleave SNAP-25, only competes with it.
- **Limited Skin Penetration**: As a relatively large, hydrophilic peptide, only a small fraction crosses the stratum corneum to reach muscle, capping real-world potency.
- **No Systemic Activity**: Designed for local cosmetic effect; negligible systemic absorption at cosmetic concentrations.
