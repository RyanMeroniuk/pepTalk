# Argireline (Acetyl Hexapeptide-8)

## Things to Avoid

- **UV Exposure Without Protection**: Unprotected sun deepens expression lines faster than any topical can soften them; daily sunscreen is essential.
- **Over-Layering Irritants**: Applying high-strength retinoids or strong AHA/BHA acids at the same time can compromise the barrier and increase irritation. Separate or alternate.
- **Expecting Injectable-Level Results**: Argireline is modest and surface-acting; treating it as a Botox replacement leads to disappointment and overuse.
- **Skipping Days**: Because the effect is reversible, inconsistent use prevents results from building.
- **Applying to Broken or Irritated Skin**: Apply only to intact skin to avoid stinging and increased absorption-related irritation.
- **Getting Product in the Eyes**: When treating crow's feet, keep the serum off the lash line and out of the eye.
- **Smoking and Heavy Sun-Bed Use**: Both dramatically accelerate the wrinkling the peptide is meant to address.
