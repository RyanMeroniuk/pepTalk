# Argireline (Acetyl Hexapeptide-8)

## Side Effects

- **Generally Well-Tolerated**: Topical cosmetic use is associated with a low side-effect profile.
- **Mild Irritation**: Occasional redness, stinging, or dryness, more likely at higher concentrations or on sensitive skin.
- **Contact Sensitivity**: Rare allergic or irritant contact reactions; patch testing is prudent for reactive skin.
- **Eye-Area Caution**: When applied near the eyes, avoid direct contact with the eye itself; can cause transient irritation.
- **Modest, Reversible Effect**: Not a side effect per se, but users should expect subtle results that reverse on discontinuation — managing expectations prevents overuse.
- **Limited Long-Term Data**: Long-term topical safety is considered favorable but not exhaustively characterized.
