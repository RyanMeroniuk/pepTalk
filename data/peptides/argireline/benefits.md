# Argireline (Acetyl Hexapeptide-8)

## Benefits

- **Expression Line Softening**: May reduce the appearance of dynamic wrinkles (forehead lines, crow's feet, glabellar frown lines) with consistent topical use over weeks.
- **Non-Invasive Alternative**: Offers a needle-free, lower-commitment option for those not pursuing injectable neuromodulators; effect is subtle and gradual.
- **Well-Tolerated**: Low irritation profile makes it suitable for daily leave-on use and for sensitive skin in most cases.
- **Formulation-Friendly**: Stable and compatible with many other cosmetic actives, so it pairs easily in multi-peptide serums.
- **Cumulative Smoothing**: Benefits build with continued use and reverse when discontinued; best viewed as maintenance, not a one-time fix.
- **Modest, Realistic Efficacy**: Cosmetic studies report measurable but modest wrinkle-depth improvements — clearly less than injectable botulinum toxin.
