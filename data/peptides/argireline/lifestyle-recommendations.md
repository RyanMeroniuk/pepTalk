# Argireline (Acetyl Hexapeptide-8)

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Consistent Daily Application**: Argireline works through cumulative, reversible effects. Apply 1-2x daily at the same times and expect visible smoothing only after 4-8 weeks of uninterrupted use.
- **Sun Protection**: UV exposure is the dominant driver of expression-line deepening and overall photoaging. Apply broad-spectrum SPF 30-50 every morning to preserve any smoothing benefit.
- **Hydration and Moisture Barrier**: Keep skin well-moisturized; a healthy barrier improves the look of fine lines and supports peptide tolerance. Drink adequate water daily.
- **Sleep Quality**: Skin repair and collagen synthesis peak during deep sleep. Target 7-9 hours nightly and consider sleeping on your back to reduce mechanical creasing of the face.
- **Stress Management**: Chronic facial tension and frowning reinforce dynamic lines; stress also elevates cortisol, which degrades collagen. Daily relaxation practice complements the peptide's action.
- **Avoid Smoking**: Smoking accelerates wrinkle formation, depletes vitamin C, and reduces skin blood flow — directly working against Argireline's goal.
- **Patch Test First**: Before full-face use, test on a small area for 24-48 hours to rule out irritation, especially with sensitive skin.
- **Realistic Expectations**: This is maintenance skincare, not a substitute for injectable neuromodulators; consistency matters more than concentration.
