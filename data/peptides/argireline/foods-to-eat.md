# Argireline (Acetyl Hexapeptide-8)

## Foods to Eat

- **Vitamin C-Rich Foods**: Bell peppers, citrus, kiwi, strawberries, and broccoli supply the cofactor needed for collagen synthesis, supporting overall skin firmness alongside topical line-softening.
- **Wild-Caught Fatty Fish**: Salmon, sardines, and mackerel provide omega-3s that reduce skin inflammation and support a healthy moisture barrier.
- **Collagen Sources**: Bone broth and lean protein supply glycine, proline, and amino acids that the skin matrix uses for repair.
- **Colorful Antioxidant Produce**: Berries, tomatoes, and leafy greens deliver polyphenols and carotenoids that protect skin from UV-driven free-radical damage.
- **Healthy Fats**: Avocados, olive oil, and nuts provide vitamin E and monounsaturated fats that support skin-barrier integrity and hydration.
- **Adequate Water and Electrolytes**: Well-hydrated skin shows fewer fine lines; pair water intake with mineral-rich whole foods.
