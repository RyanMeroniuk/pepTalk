# Argireline (Acetyl Hexapeptide-8)

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Synergistic**: GHK-Cu — pairing neuromodulating wrinkle-softening with copper-peptide collagen support is a common topical anti-aging combination.
- **Synergistic**: Collagen-peptides (oral) — support the dermal matrix while Argireline addresses dynamic expression lines from the surface.
- **Enhanced by**: Vitamin-C (topical, applied at a separate time of day) — supports collagen synthesis and brightening alongside line-softening.
- **Enhanced by**: Omega-3 and vitamin-d3 for general skin-barrier and anti-inflammatory support.
- **Caution**: Layering with strong exfoliating acids or high-strength retinoids in the same application can increase irritation; separate or alternate them.
- **Note**: Effects are cosmetic and topical; no meaningful systemic supplement interactions are expected.
