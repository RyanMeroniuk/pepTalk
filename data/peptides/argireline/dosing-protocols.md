# Argireline (Acetyl Hexapeptide-8)

## Dosing Protocols

- **Topical Only**: Used exclusively as a leave-on serum or cream; not injected and not taken orally.
- **Typical Concentration**: Cosmetic formulations commonly contain 5-10% Argireline solution (the raw material is supplied as a low-concentration aqueous solution).
- **Application Frequency**: Apply 1-2x daily to clean, dry skin over target expression-line areas (forehead, around eyes, between brows).
- **Results Timeline**: Visible smoothing typically emerges after 4-8 weeks of consistent daily use; effects are cumulative.
- **Layering**: Apply to clean skin and allow a few minutes to absorb before layering moisturizer or sunscreen.
- **Maintenance**: Benefits fade once discontinued, so ongoing daily application is needed to maintain results.
