# Melanotan II

## Overview

Melanotan II (MT-II) is a synthetic analog of alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone (α-MSH). Developed at the University of Arizona in the 1990s. Non-selectively activates melanocortin receptors (MC1R through MC5R), producing skin tanning, sexual arousal, and appetite suppression. PT-141 was derived from MT-II by isolating the sexual function component. MT-II is primarily known for producing a deep tan without UV exposure, though it carries more side effects than its more targeted derivative PT-141.
