# Melanotan II

## Foods to Eat

- **Antioxidant-Rich Foods** (berries, dark leafy greens, colorful vegetables) — protect skin cells from oxidative damage, which is critical when melanocytes are being actively stimulated by MT-II
- **Fatty Fish** (wild-caught salmon, sardines, mackerel) — omega-3 fatty acids support skin cell membrane health, reduce inflammation, and provide photoprotective effects
- **Tomatoes** (cooked) — lycopene provides natural photoprotection from the inside, supporting skin health during MT-II use
- **Ginger** — proven anti-nausea food; essential for managing MT-II's most common side effect. Consume as tea, chews, or fresh before injections
- **Sweet Potatoes and Carrots** — beta-carotene supports skin health and provides a natural complement to melanin for skin tone
- **Green Tea** — EGCG polyphenols provide photoprotective and anti-inflammatory skin benefits
- **Avocado** — healthy fats and vitamin E support skin cell integrity and repair
- **Walnuts** — omega-3s and vitamin E for skin membrane health
- **Dark Chocolate** (70%+ cacao) — flavonoids improve skin hydration and UV resistance
- **Citrus Fruits** — vitamin C is essential for collagen production and skin repair
- **Bone Broth** — collagen peptides and glycine support skin structure and repair
