# Melanotan II

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Skin Safety Is Paramount**: Melanotan II stimulates melanocyte activity, which produces a tan but also increases the importance of skin monitoring. Get a full-body dermatological skin exam BEFORE starting MT-II and every 6 months during use. Photograph all existing moles for comparison
- **Do NOT Use MT-II as a Substitute for Sunscreen**: The melanin produced by MT-II provides some UV protection but is NOT sufficient sun protection. Continue using broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen daily. MT-II-induced tanning can create a false sense of security about UV exposure
- **Avoid Excessive Sun Exposure**: Do NOT combine MT-II with deliberate tanning, tanning beds, or prolonged unprotected sun exposure. The goal of melanocyte stimulation plus excessive UV is a dangerous combination that significantly increases skin cancer risk. A minimal amount of sun (15-20 min) may enhance the tanning response, but extended sessions are counterproductive and dangerous
- **Manage Nausea**: Nausea is very common, especially early in use. Start with the lowest effective dose (0.1-0.25mg), dose in the evening so nausea occurs during sleep, and use ginger tea or ginger chews before injection
- **Monitor Moles Obsessively**: Track every mole using the ABCDE criteria (Asymmetry, Border irregularity, Color variation, Diameter >6mm, Evolution/change). Any changing mole should be evaluated by a dermatologist immediately. New moles appearing during MT-II use require professional evaluation
- **Prioritize Sleep**: Evening dosing is preferred for nausea management, but ensure it does not disrupt sleep quality. MT-II can cause fatigue/drowsiness — use this to your advantage by dosing at bedtime
- **Stay Hydrated**: Adequate hydration supports skin health and may reduce some side effects
- **Moderate Exercise**: Regular exercise supports skin health through improved circulation and hormonal balance
- **Address Sexual Side Effects Proactively**: Men may experience spontaneous erections, which is an expected MC4R-mediated effect. If priapism occurs (erection lasting >4 hours), seek immediate medical attention
