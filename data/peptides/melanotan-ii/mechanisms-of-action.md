# Melanotan II

## Mechanisms of Action

- **MC1R Activation**: Stimulates melanocytes to produce melanin — produces skin darkening/tanning
- **MC4R Activation**: Increases sexual arousal and libido (same pathway as PT-141)
- **MC3R/MC4R**: Appetite suppression and metabolic effects
- **Non-Selective**: Activates multiple melanocortin receptors (explains broad range of effects and side effects)
