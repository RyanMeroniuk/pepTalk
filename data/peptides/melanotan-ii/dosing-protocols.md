# Melanotan II

## Dosing Protocols

- **Loading Phase**: 0.25-0.5mg subcutaneous injection daily for 2-3 weeks
- **Maintenance**: 0.5mg 1-2x per week to maintain tan
- **Start Very Low**: Begin at 0.1-0.25mg to assess tolerance (nausea is common)
- **Timing**: Evening dosing preferred (nausea occurs during sleep)
- **Reconstitution**: Bacteriostatic water; refrigerate; light-sensitive
