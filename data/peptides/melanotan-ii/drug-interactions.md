# Melanotan II

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Blood pressure medications (ACE inhibitors, ARBs, beta-blockers, calcium channel blockers)**: Melanotan II can affect blood pressure through melanocortin receptor activation and vasodilation/flushing. Combining may cause unpredictable blood pressure fluctuations; monitor BP closely
- **Erectile dysfunction drugs (sildenafil, tadalafil, vardenafil)**: MT-II increases sexual arousal and erectile function via MC4R; combining with PDE5 inhibitors creates additive effects on erectile response, significantly increasing priapism risk. Do not combine without medical supervision
- **Other melanocortin agonists (PT-141, setmelanotide)**: Overlapping receptor activation dramatically increases all melanocortin-mediated side effects including nausea, blood pressure changes, and hyperpigmentation. Do not combine
- **MAO inhibitors**: Potential interaction through dopaminergic pathways activated by MC4R stimulation
- **Anticoagulants**: MT-II may affect platelet function through melanocortin pathways; use caution with blood thinners
- **Immunotherapy / checkpoint inhibitors**: Melanocyte stimulation during immunotherapy for melanoma is extremely dangerous and absolutely contraindicated
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy and breastfeeding — hormonal and melanocyte-stimulating effects pose unknown risks to fetal development
- **Contraindicated**: Personal or family history of melanoma — stimulating melanocyte activity in individuals with melanoma risk may accelerate malignant transformation
- **Contraindicated**: Active skin cancer of any type
- **Contraindicated**: Uncontrolled cardiovascular disease or hypertension
