# Melanotan II

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Sun Protection**: Still use sunscreen — MT-II melanin is not complete UV protection
- **Skin Monitoring**: Regular dermatological exams for mole changes — CRITICAL
- **Caution**: Melanoma history or high melanoma risk — strongly avoid
- **Caution**: PT-141 (overlapping melanocortin mechanisms — do not combine)
- **Contraindicated**: Active skin cancer, melanoma history, pregnancy/breastfeeding
