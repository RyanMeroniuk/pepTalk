# Melanotan II

## Side Effects

- **Nausea**: Very common, especially at higher doses and early in use
- **Facial Flushing**: Common
- **Fatigue/Drowsiness**: Common
- **Spontaneous Erections**: In men (can be prolonged — priapism risk)
- **Mole Darkening**: Existing moles become darker (concerning for melanoma monitoring)
- **New Nevi**: New moles may appear
- **Skin Hyperpigmentation**: Uneven darkening possible
- **Injection Site Reactions**: Standard
- **Appetite Suppression**: Can be excessive
- **Melanoma Concern**: Theoretical risk — stimulating melanocytes in patients with melanoma risk
