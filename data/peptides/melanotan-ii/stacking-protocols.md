# Melanotan II

## Stacking Protocols

- **Limited Stacking Recommended**: Melanotan II activates multiple melanocortin receptors non-selectively (MC1R-MC5R), producing broad systemic effects. This non-selectivity increases unpredictable interactions when combined with other peptides. Approach all stacks with caution
- **Skin Health Support Stack**: Melanotan II (0.25-0.5 mg maintenance) + GHK-Cu (1-2 mg daily) — melanin production paired with collagen and skin regeneration support; GHK-Cu may help maintain skin quality during melanocyte stimulation
- **Body Composition Stack**: Melanotan II (0.25-0.5 mg maintenance) + AOD-9604 (300 mcg daily) — leverages MT-II's appetite suppression alongside targeted fat metabolism; monitor blood pressure closely
- **Do NOT Stack with PT-141**: Overlapping melanocortin receptor activation causes additive side effects (severe nausea, blood pressure changes, priapism risk, hyperpigmentation). These peptides share the same receptor targets
- **Do NOT Stack with Other Melanocortin Agonists**: Any additional MC receptor stimulation compounds the risk of adverse effects. Use Melanotan II as a standalone melanocortin agent
