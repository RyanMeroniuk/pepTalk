# Melanotan II

## Foods to Avoid

- **Alcohol** — dehydrates skin, promotes inflammation, increases oxidative stress on skin cells, and worsens MT-II nausea. Alcohol also impairs the liver's ability to process peptides
- **Excess Sugar** — promotes glycation (sugar bonding to proteins), which damages collagen and elastin in the skin. When actively stimulating melanocytes, skin health should be prioritized
- **Processed Meats** (bacon, hot dogs, deli meats) — contain nitrates and advanced glycation end products (AGEs) that damage skin and may promote oxidative stress in actively dividing skin cells
- **Fried Foods and Trans Fats** — promote systemic inflammation and oxidative stress that damage skin cells
- **Excess Caffeine** — can dehydrate skin and may worsen some MT-II side effects when consumed in excess
- **Heavy Meals Before Dosing** — increase nausea risk substantially. Keep pre-injection meals light or dose on a relatively empty stomach at bedtime
- **Excessive Omega-6 Seed Oils** — promote inflammation that impairs skin health and healing
