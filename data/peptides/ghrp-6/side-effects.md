# GHRP-6 (Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide-6)

## Side Effects

- **Intense appetite increase**: The most significant — can be overwhelming for some
- **Elevated cortisol**: More pronounced than other GHRPs
- **Elevated prolactin**: More pronounced than other GHRPs
- **Water retention**: Moderate to significant
- **Blood sugar effects**: May cause transient blood sugar fluctuations
- **Tingling/numbness**: Transient
- **Injection site reactions**: Standard
- Less selective hormonal profile compared to Ipamorelin
