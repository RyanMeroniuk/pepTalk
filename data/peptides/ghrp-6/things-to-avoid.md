# GHRP-6 (Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide-6)

## Things to Avoid

- **Having junk food accessible** — this cannot be overstated. GHRP-6's appetite increase is pharmacological and intense. Willpower alone is insufficient. Remove temptation from your environment entirely
- **Eating within 2 hours before injection** — food-induced insulin blunts the GH pulse. GHRP-6's potency advantage is wasted if the fasting protocol is not followed
- **Using GHRP-6 for fat loss** — this is the wrong tool for cutting. The appetite increase overwhelmingly counteracts any GH-driven fat burning. Use Ipamorelin or GHRP-2 instead for fat loss protocols
- **Alcohol on injection days** — the combination of suppressed GH, disrupted sleep, lowered inhibitions, and GHRP-6's appetite creates a perfect storm for massive caloric overconsumption
- **Ignoring cortisol and prolactin monitoring** — GHRP-6 elevates both more than any other GHRP except hexarelin. Without monitoring, chronic elevation causes fatigue, mood changes, and potential hormonal imbalances
- **High doses** — increasing beyond 100-200mcg per injection amplifies cortisol, prolactin, and appetite disproportionately to additional GH release
- **Sedentary use** — GHRP-6 without exercise means the appetite increase goes directly to fat storage. The GH-driven muscle and recovery benefits require a training stimulus
- **Extended use without cortisol/prolactin labs** — get labs at 8 weeks minimum. If either is trending high, reduce dose, cycle off, or switch to a cleaner GHRP
- **Combining with hexarelin** — stacking the two most cortisol/prolactin-stimulating GHRPs creates an unfavorable hormonal environment
- **Chronic stress without management** — GHRP-6 already elevates cortisol. Adding unmanaged psychological or physical stress creates cumulative cortisol burden that opposes GH benefits and promotes visceral fat storage
