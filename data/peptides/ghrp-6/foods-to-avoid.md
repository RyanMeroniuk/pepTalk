# GHRP-6 (Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide-6)

## Foods to Avoid

- **Junk food and processed snacks** — this is the most critical rule for GHRP-6 users. The appetite surge is intense and you will eat whatever is accessible. If chips, cookies, ice cream, or candy are in your house, you will eat them. Remove them entirely
- **Sugar and refined carbs within 2 hours of injection** — insulin suppresses the GH pulse. GHRP-6's potent GH release is wasted if combined with sugar
- **Fast food** — high in sodium, inflammatory oils, and empty calories. The appetite increase makes drive-through stops extremely tempting and counterproductive
- **High-fat meals near injection** — free fatty acids blunt pituitary GH release
- **Alcohol** — suppresses GH, disrupts sleep, adds empty calories, and lowers inhibitions. Combined with GHRP-6's appetite, alcohol nights typically result in consuming 2000-3000+ excess calories
- **Sugary beverages (soda, fruit juice, energy drinks)** — liquid sugar spikes insulin and provides zero satiety. Particularly dangerous when appetite is elevated
- **Excessive sodium from processed foods** — worsens GHRP-6's water retention
- **Sugary cereal and pastries for breakfast** — if you inject in the morning and eat 30 minutes later, a sugar-laden breakfast negates the hormonal benefit and feeds the appetite cycle
