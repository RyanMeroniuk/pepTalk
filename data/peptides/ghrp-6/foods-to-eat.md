# GHRP-6 (Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide-6)

## Foods to Eat

- **Chicken breast and turkey** — lean, high-protein, and satiating. The go-to protein source for managing GHRP-6's appetite while building muscle
- **Lean ground beef (grass-fed, 90/10)** — calorie-dense protein source ideal for bulking. Rich in arginine and iron
- **Wild-caught salmon** — omega-3s reduce inflammation (counteracting GHRP-6's cortisol elevation), plus protein and healthy fats for sustained satiety
- **Whole eggs (pasture-raised)** — calorie-dense, highly satiating, complete protein. Essential for bulking; moderate for cutting
- **Rice (white or brown)** — the classic mass-building carb source. White rice post-workout for fast glycogen replenishment; brown rice for slower digestion at other meals (eat away from injection time)
- **Sweet potatoes** — complex carbs with fiber, potassium, and moderate glycemic index
- **Oats (steel-cut or rolled)** — slow-digesting carbs that sustain energy and help manage hunger between meals
- **Black beans and lentils** — fiber and protein powerhouse. Extremely filling and an excellent choice for managing GHRP-6's appetite without excess calories
- **Broccoli and green beans** — high volume, high fiber, very low calorie. Essential for filling the stomach when appetite is elevated
- **Greek yogurt (plain)** — protein-rich, contains glutamine for GH support, and probiotics for gut health
- **Cottage cheese** — casein protein for slow overnight release. Mix with berries for a satiating pre-bed snack (eat at least 2 hours before injection if injecting before bed)
- **Bone broth** — glycine for sleep, collagen for repair, and the warm liquid helps manage hunger
- **Bananas** — convenient carb source with potassium; useful for post-workout nutrition when appetite is high
