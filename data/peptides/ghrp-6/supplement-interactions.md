# GHRP-6 (Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide-6)

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Best Stack**: CJC-1295 no DAC for amplified GH release
- **Useful for**: Those who struggle to eat enough (hardgainers, recovery from illness)
- **Monitor**: Cortisol and prolactin levels with prolonged use
- **Caution**: Diabetes medications
- **Caution**: Those trying to lose weight (appetite increase counterproductive)
- **Contraindicated**: Active cancer, pregnancy/breastfeeding
