# GHRP-6 (Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide-6)

## Dosing Protocols

- **Standard**: 100 mcg subcutaneous injection, 2-3x daily
- **Timing**: 30 minutes before meals (appetite effect helps with eating), or before bed
- **Must fast**: 2+ hours before injection for optimal GH release
- **Cycle**: 8-12 weeks on, 4 weeks off
- **Combine with**: CJC-1295 no DAC for synergistic effect
- **Start low**: Appetite increase can be intense — begin at 100mcg once daily
