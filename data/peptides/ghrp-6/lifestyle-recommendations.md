# GHRP-6 (Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide-6)

## Lifestyle Recommendations


### Injection Timing & Fasting
- **Inject in a fasted state** — minimum 2 hours after your last meal. Despite GHRP-6's powerful GH stimulation, insulin from food still suppresses the pulse
- **Strategic timing around the appetite surge**: GHRP-6 causes intense hunger 15-30 minutes after injection. If bulking, inject 30 minutes before a planned meal. If maintaining/cutting, inject before bed to sleep through the worst of it
- **Three injection windows**: (1) 30 minutes before breakfast (use the appetite to eat a large, nutrient-dense meal), (2) Post-workout fasted, (3) Before bed
- **Wait at least 20 minutes after injection before eating** — allow the GH pulse to peak, then eat to satisfy the hunger
- **Start with 100mcg once daily** — assess the appetite increase before adding additional daily doses. The hunger can be overwhelming for first-time users

### Exercise
- **Resistance training 4-5x/week is essential** — GHRP-6 is best suited for those actively trying to gain muscle mass. Without heavy training, the appetite increase simply leads to fat gain
- **Compound movements** (squats, deadlifts, bench press, rows, overhead press) — maximize the anabolic environment from GHRP-6's potent GH release
- **Higher training volume is sustainable** — GHRP-6's GH elevation significantly enhances recovery capacity. Consider adding extra sets or an additional training day compared to natural training
- **HIIT 1-2x/week** — sprint intervals or metabolic circuits for 15-20 minutes. This helps manage body composition and improves insulin sensitivity
- **Train before your pre-meal injection** — exercise fasted or 2+ hours post-meal, inject GHRP-6 post-workout, then eat a large recovery meal 20-30 minutes later. This sequence maximizes the GH pulse, leverages the appetite increase, and optimizes nutrient partitioning
- **Limit long-duration cardio** — GHRP-6 already elevates cortisol more than other GHRPs. Adding 60+ minutes of steady-state cardio compounds cortisol elevation and can become catabolic

### Managing the Appetite (Critical for GHRP-6)
- **The appetite increase is intense and immediate** — this is GHRP-6's defining characteristic. Within 15-30 minutes of injection, hunger becomes overwhelming. This is not psychological — it is direct ghrelin receptor activation
- **For bulking, this is a feature, not a bug** — hardgainers who struggle to eat enough find GHRP-6 invaluable. Time injections before meals to eat 500-800 calorie nutrient-dense meals with ease
- **For non-bulking users, this is the primary challenge** — pre-portion all meals, remove junk food from your environment, and use bedtime dosing to sleep through the peak appetite
- **High-protein, high-fiber meals** are the only way to manage the hunger sustainably — 40-50g of protein per meal with vegetables and complex carbs
- **Drink a large glass of water (16-20oz) immediately after injection** — partially fills the stomach before the hunger wave hits
- **Have your post-injection meal prepped and ready** — when the hunger hits, you will eat whatever is available. Make sure the available option is chicken, rice, and vegetables rather than chips and cookies
- **The appetite does partially attenuate** after 2-3 weeks as ghrelin receptors partially downregulate, but it never fully disappears

### Sleep Optimization
- **7-9 hours of quality sleep** — GHRP-6 enhances deep sleep, but you need adequate sleep duration for this to matter
- **Bedtime injection is often the most practical** — the appetite spike occurs during sleep, and the GH pulse amplifies the natural nocturnal surge
- **Consistent schedule** — same sleep and wake times daily for optimal circadian GH rhythm
- **Cool, dark bedroom (65-68°F)** — supports melatonin and GH release
- **If you eat before bed due to appetite**: keep it to a small protein-focused snack (cottage cheese, casein shake) rather than a carb-heavy meal that spikes insulin

### Monitoring & Daily Habits
- **Blood work every 8-12 weeks**: IGF-1, fasting glucose, cortisol, prolactin, metabolic panel. GHRP-6 elevates cortisol and prolactin more than GHRP-2 or Ipamorelin — monitoring is not optional
- **Track caloric intake** — GHRP-6's appetite increase makes it extremely easy to overshoot calories unintentionally. Use a food tracking app during the first 2-4 weeks to understand your actual intake
- **If cortisol trends upward**, reduce dose or switch to a cleaner GHRP like Ipamorelin
- **If prolactin trends upward**, discuss with prescriber. Vitamin B6 (P-5-P form, 50-100mg) may help modulate prolactin
- **Morning sunlight (15-20 minutes)** — supports circadian rhythm and optimizes nocturnal GH pulse timing
- **Stress management** — GHRP-6 already elevates cortisol more than other GHRPs. Chronic external stress compounds this. Daily meditation, walking, or breathwork is important
