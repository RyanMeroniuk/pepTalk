# GHRP-6 (Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide-6)

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Diabetes medications (insulin, metformin, sulfonylureas)**: GHRP-6 elevates growth hormone, which raises blood glucose and reduces insulin sensitivity. May require significant dose adjustments; monitor blood glucose frequently
- **Exogenous insulin**: GH-driven glucose elevation opposes insulin's action. Unpredictable blood sugar fluctuations and increased hypo/hyperglycemia risk
- **Glucocorticoids (prednisone, dexamethasone, hydrocortisone)**: GHRP-6 elevates cortisol more than other GHRPs. Adding exogenous glucocorticoids creates excessive cortisol burden — muscle wasting, fat gain, bone loss, immune suppression, and metabolic dysfunction
- **Prolactin-elevating drugs (antipsychotics, metoclopramide, SSRIs)**: GHRP-6 raises prolactin more than GHRP-2 or Ipamorelin. Combining with other prolactin-elevating medications increases risk of clinically significant hyperprolactinemia
- **Appetite-suppressing medications (phentermine, topiramate, GLP-1 agonists)**: GHRP-6 produces the strongest appetite stimulation of any GHRP via direct ghrelin receptor activation. This directly opposes appetite-suppressing drugs, creating conflicting pharmacological signals and unpredictable eating behavior
- **Anti-obesity medications (semaglutide, tirzepatide, orlistat)**: GHRP-6's powerful appetite increase fundamentally conflicts with weight loss goals and the mechanisms of these medications
- **Exogenous growth hormone (rhGH)**: Concurrent use risks supraphysiological GH/IGF-1 levels with increased side effects (insulin resistance, edema, joint pain)
- **Thyroid medications**: GH elevation can alter T4-to-T3 conversion. Monitor thyroid function and adjust dosing as needed
- **Contraindicated: Active cancer or history of cancer** — GH and IGF-1 are growth factors that may promote tumor growth, angiogenesis, and cancer cell proliferation
- **Contraindicated: Pregnancy and breastfeeding** — insufficient safety data; GH axis manipulation poses unknown risks to fetal development
- **Contraindicated: Active pituitary tumors** — direct pituitary stimulation could accelerate tumor growth
- **Contraindicated: Individuals actively trying to lose weight** — GHRP-6's intense appetite stimulation makes it counterproductive for weight loss protocols. Use Ipamorelin or GHRP-2 instead
