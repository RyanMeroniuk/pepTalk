# GHRP-6 (Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide-6)

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Ghrelin Receptor Agonist**: Binds GHS-R1a receptors
- **Potent GH Release**: Strong GH pulses from pituitary
- **Somatostatin Suppression**: Reduces GH inhibition
- **Strong Appetite Stimulation**: Significant ghrelin-mediated hunger increase — the defining characteristic
- **Cortisol/Prolactin**: Elevates both in dose-dependent fashion (more than GHRP-2 or Ipamorelin)
- **Gastric Motility**: Increases stomach emptying and GI motility
