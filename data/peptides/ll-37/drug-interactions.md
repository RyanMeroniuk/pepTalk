# LL-37

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Immunosuppressants (tacrolimus, cyclosporine, mycophenolate)**: LL-37 activates innate immune pathways; immunosuppressants deliberately suppress these same pathways. Combining may reduce immunosuppressant efficacy or cause unpredictable immune responses. Use only under specialist supervision
- **Antibiotics**: Potential synergy — LL-37 disrupts biofilms and bacterial membranes, potentially improving antibiotic penetration and efficacy. Some protocols intentionally combine LL-37 with antibiotics for chronic/biofilm infections. Coordinate with prescribing physician
- **Corticosteroids (prednisone, dexamethasone)**: Systemic corticosteroids suppress antimicrobial peptide production and immune function; may counteract LL-37's immune-enhancing effects
- **Autoimmune medications (biologics, DMARDs)**: LL-37's immune activation may conflict with medications designed to suppress immune overactivity. Risk of flare in autoimmune conditions
- **TNF-alpha inhibitors (infliximab, adalimumab)**: LL-37 modulates cytokine production including TNF pathways; combining may create unpredictable immune modulation
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy and breastfeeding — insufficient safety data for therapeutic doses; immune modulation effects on fetal development unknown
- **Contraindicated**: Active autoimmune flares — immune activation may worsen autoimmune symptoms including lupus, rheumatoid arthritis, and inflammatory bowel disease
- **Contraindicated**: Organ transplant recipients on immunosuppression — risk of graft rejection from immune activation
