# LL-37

## Things to Avoid

- **Sugar Consumption During Antimicrobial Protocols** — sugar is the primary fuel for pathogenic bacteria and yeast, directly promoting the biofilm formation that LL-37 is designed to disrupt. This is the most counterproductive behavior during LL-37 therapy
- **Chronic Stress** — cortisol suppresses antimicrobial peptide production in mucosal surfaces, reducing both natural and supplemental LL-37 effectiveness
- **Sleep Deprivation** — impairs innate immune cell function, reducing the neutrophils and macrophages that work alongside LL-37
- **Smoking and Vaping** — destroys mucosal immune defenses in the airways, where LL-37 naturally provides first-line protection. Tobacco smoke also promotes bacterial biofilm formation in the respiratory tract
- **Unnecessary Broad-Spectrum Antibiotics** — while antibiotics may be part of a biofilm protocol, unnecessary use devastates beneficial gut bacteria and can promote resistant biofilm-forming organisms
- **NSAIDs Overuse** — chronic NSAID use damages the gut mucosal barrier, impairing the epithelial surface where LL-37 is naturally produced and active
- **Mold Exposure** — mold and mycotoxins suppress innate immune function and are often associated with the chronic inflammatory conditions (CIRS) where LL-37 is used. Address any water damage or mold in living spaces
- **Dental Infections and Poor Oral Hygiene** — the oral cavity is a major site of biofilm formation. Untreated dental infections can seed systemic biofilm issues. Maintain rigorous oral hygiene during LL-37 protocols
