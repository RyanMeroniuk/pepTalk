# LL-37

## Benefits

- **Infection Defense**: Broad-spectrum antimicrobial against bacteria, viruses, fungi
- **Biofilm Disruption**: Particularly useful for chronic infections involving biofilms (Lyme, sinus infections, prosthetic infections)
- **Wound Healing**: Accelerates healing through immune recruitment and angiogenesis
- **Immune Enhancement**: Strengthens innate immune response
- **Upper Respiratory**: May support defense against respiratory infections
- **Chronic Infection Support**: Adjunct for persistent/biofilm-related infections
- **Post-Surgical**: May reduce infection risk and support healing
