# LL-37

## Foods to Avoid

- **Sugar and Refined Carbohydrates** — sugar feeds pathogenic bacteria and yeast, directly promoting biofilm formation. This is the single most counterproductive food when using LL-37 for chronic infections. High blood sugar also impairs neutrophil function (the primary immune cell that produces LL-37)
- **Alcohol** — suppresses innate immune function, damages gut mucosal barriers where LL-37 is active, and promotes pathogenic bacterial overgrowth
- **Highly Processed Foods** — preservatives and emulsifiers disrupt gut microbiome balance and weaken mucosal immunity
- **Excess Dairy** (conventional, pasteurized) — can promote mucus production and may feed certain pathogenic bacteria in those with chronic infections
- **Gluten** (for those with sensitivity or chronic infections) — can increase intestinal permeability, compromising the gut mucosal barrier where LL-37 functions
- **Artificial Sweeteners** — disrupt gut microbiome composition, potentially promoting pathogenic species that form biofilms
- **Excess Iron Supplements** — pathogenic bacteria use iron for growth and biofilm formation. Only supplement iron if confirmed deficient, and use forms less available to pathogens (lactoferrin-bound iron)
