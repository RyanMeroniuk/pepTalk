# LL-37

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Optimize Vitamin D Levels**: Vitamin D directly upregulates LL-37 gene expression — this is one of the most important synergies in peptide therapy. Test serum 25(OH)D and target 50-70 ng/mL. Supplement 4000-5000 IU daily with K2 for absorption. Sun exposure (15-20 min midday) also helps
- **Prioritize Sleep**: Sleep deprivation suppresses innate immune function, including the antimicrobial peptide production that LL-37 enhances. Aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep to maintain immune vigilance
- **Moderate Exercise**: Regular moderate exercise enhances innate immune cell circulation (neutrophils, macrophages) — the same cells that produce and deploy LL-37. Walk, swim, or cycle 30-45 minutes, 4-5x/week
- **Avoid Overtraining**: Intense exercise without recovery temporarily suppresses innate immunity, reducing the body's antimicrobial defenses during the recovery window
- **Manage Chronic Stress**: Cortisol suppresses antimicrobial peptide production and impairs mucosal immunity. Daily stress management practices (meditation, breathwork, nature exposure) support LL-37's antimicrobial activity
- **Support Gut Barrier Integrity**: LL-37 is produced in the gut epithelium as a first-line defense. Support gut health with fermented foods, prebiotic fiber, and by avoiding gut-disrupting substances (NSAIDs, excess alcohol, artificial sweeteners)
- **Address Biofilm-Forming Infections**: If using LL-37 for chronic/biofilm infections (Lyme, CIRS, chronic sinusitis), work with a practitioner experienced in biofilm protocols. Biofilm disruption can cause Herxheimer-like reactions — go slowly
- **Nasal and Oral Hygiene**: LL-37 is naturally active in mucosal surfaces. Support these defenses with nasal saline rinses (xylitol-based), oil pulling, and good oral hygiene
