# LL-37

## Foods to Eat

- **Vitamin D-Rich Foods** (wild-caught salmon, sardines, mackerel, egg yolks, cod liver oil) — vitamin D directly upregulates LL-37 production, making these foods the most strategically important
- **Garlic** — contains allicin with direct antimicrobial and anti-biofilm properties that complement LL-37's biofilm disruption
- **Oregano** (fresh or as oil) — carvacrol and thymol have potent antimicrobial and anti-biofilm activity
- **Coconut Oil** — lauric acid and monolaurin have antimicrobial properties and can disrupt biofilms
- **Fermented Foods** (raw sauerkraut, kimchi, kefir, kvass) — support gut microbiome diversity and mucosal immune function where LL-37 is active
- **Bone Broth** — provides glutamine for gut barrier integrity, supporting the mucosal surfaces where LL-37 operates
- **Cruciferous Vegetables** (broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts) — sulforaphane supports immune cell function and has antimicrobial properties
- **Ginger** — anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial; supports innate immune function
- **Turmeric** — curcumin has anti-biofilm properties that synergize with LL-37's biofilm disruption
- **Raw Honey** (especially Manuka) — natural antimicrobial with anti-biofilm activity; supports mucosal immunity
- **Apple Cider Vinegar** (raw, with mother) — acetic acid has antimicrobial properties and supports gut pH balance
- **NAC-Rich Precursors** (whey protein, eggs, cruciferous vegetables) — N-acetylcysteine is a potent biofilm disruptor that complements LL-37
