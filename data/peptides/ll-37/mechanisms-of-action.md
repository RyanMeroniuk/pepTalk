# LL-37

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Direct Antimicrobial**: Disrupts bacterial cell membranes, killing bacteria directly
- **Anti-Biofilm**: Disrupts and prevents bacterial biofilm formation (critical for chronic infections)
- **Antiviral**: Activity against enveloped viruses
- **Antifungal**: Disrupts fungal cell membranes
- **Immune Modulation**: Recruits immune cells to infection sites via chemotactic signaling
- **Wound Healing**: Promotes angiogenesis and re-epithelialization at wound sites
- **Anti-Inflammatory**: Neutralizes endotoxin (LPS) to reduce inflammatory cascading
- **Vitamin D Connection**: Vitamin D receptor activation upregulates LL-37 gene expression
