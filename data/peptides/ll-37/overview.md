# LL-37

## Overview

LL-37 is the only human cathelicidin antimicrobial peptide — a 37-amino acid peptide that forms part of the innate immune system. Named for its two leading leucine (L) residues and 37-amino acid length. Naturally produced by immune cells (neutrophils, macrophages), epithelial cells, and other tissues. Functions as a first-line defense against bacteria, viruses, fungi, and biofilms. Also modulates the immune and inflammatory response. Vitamin D directly upregulates LL-37 production — this is one mechanism by which vitamin D supports immunity.
