# VIP (Vasoactive Intestinal Peptide)

## Benefits

- **CIRS/Mold Illness**: Primary treatment in Dr. Shoemaker's CIRS protocol — reduces inflammatory markers, improves symptoms
- **Gut Health**: Regulates GI secretion, motility, and blood flow
- **Pulmonary Hypertension**: Reduces pulmonary artery pressure
- **Neuroprotection**: Protects against neuroinflammation and oxidative damage
- **Blood Pressure**: Vasodilatory effects reduce hypertension
- **Immune Regulation**: Calms overactive inflammatory immune response
- **Respiratory**: May benefit asthma and COPD through bronchodilation
- **Metabolic**: Supports pancreatic function and glucose regulation
