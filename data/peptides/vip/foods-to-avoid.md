# VIP (Vasoactive Intestinal Peptide)

## Foods to Avoid

- **Mold-Prone Foods (Critical for CIRS Patients)**: Aged cheeses, dried fruits, peanuts/pistachios, corn, wine, beer, coffee (conventional), soy sauce, vinegar (except apple cider), mushrooms, and leftovers stored more than 24 hours. These contain mycotoxins that re-expose CIRS patients and counteract VIP therapy
- **Alcohol**: All forms — beer and wine contain mycotoxins; spirits deplete glutathione (needed for detox); alcohol increases intestinal permeability and inflammatory markers. Absolutely contraindicated during CIRS treatment
- **Sugar & Refined Carbohydrates**: Feed Candida and pathogenic organisms common in CIRS patients. Increase inflammatory cytokines that VIP is trying to reduce. Eliminate white sugar, white flour, and high-fructose corn syrup
- **Processed Foods**: Preservatives, emulsifiers, and artificial additives increase gut permeability and systemic inflammation. Eat whole, single-ingredient foods prepared at home
- **Gluten**: Triggers zonulin release and increases intestinal permeability. Many CIRS patients have concurrent gluten sensitivity. Eliminate during VIP protocols
- **Conventional Dairy**: Can be inflammatory and may contain mold metabolites from animal feed. If dairy is tolerated, choose organic, grass-fed, fresh options only
- **Histamine-Rich Foods (if histamine-intolerant)**: Many CIRS patients have mast cell activation and histamine intolerance. Avoid fermented foods, aged meats, smoked fish, spinach, avocado, and tomatoes if histamine symptoms are present. This is individual — test your tolerance
- **High-Mercury Fish**: Tuna, swordfish, king mackerel, and tilefish. CIRS patients often have impaired detoxification pathways, making them more vulnerable to heavy metal accumulation
- **Excess Caffeine**: Can worsen anxiety, disrupt sleep, and increase cortisol — all problematic for CIRS patients. Conventional coffee is also a high-mold-risk food. If using caffeine, choose organic, mold-tested coffee or switch to green tea
