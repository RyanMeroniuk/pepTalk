# VIP (Vasoactive Intestinal Peptide)

## Side Effects

- Generally well-tolerated intranasally
- Nasal congestion or irritation
- Diarrhea (from GI stimulatory effects)
- Low blood pressure/dizziness (vasodilation)
- Flushing
- Headache
- Should not use if MARCoNS (nasal staph biofilm) is present — must treat first in CIRS protocol
