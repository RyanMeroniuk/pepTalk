# VIP (Vasoactive Intestinal Peptide)

## Overview

VIP (Vasoactive Intestinal Peptide) is a naturally occurring 28-amino acid neuropeptide produced by tissues of the gut, pancreas, gallbladder, and hypothalamus. Has wide-ranging effects including vasodilation, GI tract regulation, immune modulation, and neuroprotection. Gained significant attention in the treatment of Chronic Inflammatory Response Syndrome (CIRS), particularly mold illness, through the work of Dr. Ritchie Shoemaker. Also studied for respiratory conditions, neuroprotection, and metabolic regulation.
