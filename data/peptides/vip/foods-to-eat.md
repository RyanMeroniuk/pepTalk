# VIP (Vasoactive Intestinal Peptide)

## Foods to Eat

- **Anti-Inflammatory Whole Foods**: Wild-caught salmon, sardines, and mackerel provide omega-3s that support VIP's immune-modulating effects. Aim for 3-4 servings of fatty fish per week
- **Low-Mold Foods (Critical for CIRS)**: Choose fresh, unprocessed foods. Fresh meats, wild-caught fish, fresh vegetables, rice, quinoa, and freshly prepared meals. Mold-colonized foods re-expose CIRS patients to mycotoxins, counteracting VIP therapy
- **Cruciferous Vegetables (Cooked)**: Broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts, and cabbage support Phase II liver detoxification (glucosinolates/sulforaphane), aiding biotoxin elimination. Cook to reduce goitrogenic effects
- **Binders-Friendly Foods**: High-fiber foods like ground flaxseed, chia seeds, and psyllium husk support toxin binding in the GI tract, complementing cholestyramine protocols
- **Garlic & Onions**: Contain allicin and sulfur compounds that support glutathione production (master antioxidant) and have antimicrobial properties
- **Turmeric & Ginger**: Natural anti-inflammatories that support VIP's cytokine modulation. Use fresh forms in cooking and teas
- **Bone Broth**: Provides glutamine for gut lining repair, glycine for liver detoxification support, and minerals for electrolyte balance (important given VIP's vasodilatory effects)
- **Leafy Greens**: Spinach, kale, arugula, and Swiss chard provide folate, magnesium, and chlorophyll which supports detoxification pathways
- **Beets**: Support liver detoxification through betalain pigments and nitric oxide production. Roast or juice fresh beets
- **Olive Oil (Extra Virgin)**: Anti-inflammatory monounsaturated fats and polyphenols (oleocanthal) that support VIP's immune regulation
- **Green Tea**: EGCG supports detoxification enzymes and provides anti-inflammatory antioxidants without the mold risk of coffee
