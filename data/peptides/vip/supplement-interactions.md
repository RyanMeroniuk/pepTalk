# VIP (Vasoactive Intestinal Peptide)

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **CIRS Protocol**: Must complete preceding Shoemaker steps before starting VIP
- **Enhanced by**: Anti-inflammatory diet, mold avoidance (for CIRS)
- **Synergistic**: BPC-157 for gut healing
- **Caution**: Blood pressure medications (additive hypotension)
- **Caution**: Must clear MARCoNS nasal infection before intranasal use
- **Contraindicated**: Active MARCoNS infection, pregnancy/breastfeeding
