# VIP (Vasoactive Intestinal Peptide)

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Vasodilation**: Relaxes smooth muscle in blood vessels, reducing blood pressure
- **GI Regulation**: Stimulates water and electrolyte secretion in the gut; regulates intestinal blood flow
- **Anti-Inflammatory**: Potent inhibitor of pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-alpha, IL-6, IL-12)
- **Immune Modulation**: Shifts immune response from Th1 (inflammatory) to Th2 (regulatory)
- **Neuroprotection**: Protects neurons from oxidative stress and inflammation
- **Aromatase Inhibition**: May inhibit aromatase enzyme (relevant to estrogen metabolism)
- **Pancreatic Function**: Regulates insulin and glucagon secretion
- **Pulmonary Vasodilation**: Reduces pulmonary artery pressure
