# VIP (Vasoactive Intestinal Peptide)

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Mold Avoidance (CIRS Patients — Critical)**: VIP is the final step in the Shoemaker CIRS protocol and will not be effective if mold exposure continues. Remediate or leave water-damaged buildings. Use HEPA air purifiers (IQAir, Austin Air) in bedroom and main living areas. Monitor indoor air quality with ERMI testing. Wash clothing in borax. VIP cannot overcome ongoing biotoxin exposure
- **Complete Shoemaker Protocol Steps**: Before starting VIP, ensure cholestyramine/Welchol binding is complete, MARCoNS nasal infection is cleared (confirmed by negative nasal culture), VCS (visual contrast sensitivity) test shows improvement, and inflammatory markers (C4a, TGF-beta-1, MMP-9) are trending down. Skipping steps makes VIP ineffective or counterproductive
- **Sleep Optimization**: VIP regulates circadian rhythm and neuroprotection. Support its effects by maintaining a strict sleep-wake schedule: same bedtime and wake time daily, including weekends. Sleep in complete darkness. Morning sunlight exposure within 30 minutes of waking helps reset circadian rhythm disrupted by CIRS
- **Stress Management for Immune Regulation**: VIP shifts the immune system from Th1 (inflammatory) to Th2 (regulatory). Chronic stress activates Th1 pathways, opposing VIP's mechanism. Daily practices: vagus nerve stimulation through cold water face immersion, deep breathing (4-7-8 pattern), meditation, or gentle yoga. 15-20 minutes minimum daily
- **Gentle Exercise Only**: CIRS patients and those with inflammatory conditions often experience exercise intolerance and post-exertional malaise. Start with 10-15 minute walks and increase gradually. Monitor for symptom flares 24-48 hours after activity. Swimming and gentle yoga are well-tolerated options
- **Environmental Control**: Beyond mold, minimize exposure to other inflammatory triggers: VOCs (volatile organic compounds) from new furniture, paint, and cleaning products. Use natural cleaning products. Avoid fragrances and scented candles. Filter shower water (chlorine is inhaled as steam)
- **Nasal Hygiene**: Since VIP is typically administered intranasally, maintain nasal passage health. Use saline nasal rinse (NeilMed or neti pot with distilled water) daily. Keep humidity at 40-50% to prevent nasal dryness. Avoid nasal decongestant sprays which can damage mucosa
- **Monitor Blood Pressure**: VIP is a vasodilator and can lower blood pressure. Rise slowly from sitting or lying positions. Stay well-hydrated. If on blood pressure medications, work with your provider to adjust doses as VIP takes effect
- **Detoxification Support**: Support biotoxin elimination with adequate fiber (25-35g daily), sweating through gentle sauna (infrared preferred, 20-30 minutes, 2-3x/week), and liver support. Ensure regular bowel movements (1-2 daily minimum) — constipation recirculates toxins
