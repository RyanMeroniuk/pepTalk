# VIP (Vasoactive Intestinal Peptide)

## Things to Avoid

- **Continued Mold Exposure**: The single most important factor. VIP will fail if the patient remains in a water-damaged building. Professional ERMI testing (not just visual inspection) is required. Even "remediated" buildings may have residual contamination. Some CIRS patients need to relocate entirely
- **MARCoNS Nasal Infection**: VIP must not be used intranasally while MARCoNS (Multiple Antibiotic Resistant Coagulase Negative Staphylococci) is present. The biofilm must be eradicated first with BEG spray (Bactroban/EDTA/Gentamicin) or similar protocol, confirmed by negative deep nasal culture
- **Skipping Shoemaker Protocol Steps**: VIP is Step 12 of the Shoemaker protocol. Using it before completing cholestyramine binding, correcting MARCoNS, normalizing ADH/osmolality, correcting androgens, etc. reduces effectiveness and may worsen symptoms
- **Blood Pressure-Lowering Substances Without Monitoring**: VIP is a vasodilator. Combining with blood pressure medications, high-dose magnesium, or other vasodilators without medical supervision risks symptomatic hypotension (dizziness, fainting)
- **Intense Exercise (CIRS Patients)**: Exercise intolerance is common in CIRS due to impaired oxygen delivery and mitochondrial dysfunction. Pushing through exercise can trigger multi-day symptom flares. Increase activity very gradually
- **Environmental Chemicals**: VOCs from new carpeting, paint, cleaning products, and personal care products add to the total inflammatory burden in CIRS patients. Use natural, fragrance-free products exclusively
- **NSAIDs**: Increase intestinal permeability and gut inflammation, undermining VIP's gut-regulatory effects
- **Chronic Stress**: Activates Th1 inflammatory pathways that VIP is working to modulate. Chronic stress also impairs detoxification and disrupts the HPA axis. Active stress management is essential
- **Smoking & Vaping**: Respiratory toxins increase pulmonary inflammation and counteract VIP's bronchodilatory and pulmonary protective effects
- **Nasal Decongestant Sprays**: Oxymetazoline and similar sprays damage nasal mucosa with chronic use, impairing intranasal VIP absorption and effectiveness
