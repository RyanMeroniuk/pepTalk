# VIP (Vasoactive Intestinal Peptide)

## Dosing Protocols

- **Intranasal (CIRS Protocol)**: 50 mcg per spray, typically 4 sprays/day (50mcg each)
- **Injectable**: 50-100 mcg subcutaneous (less common)
- **CIRS Protocol**: Used as final step in Shoemaker protocol after other markers normalized
- **Cycle**: Varies by condition — CIRS protocols may be months-long
- **Must address underlying triggers** (mold, biotoxins) before VIP is effective in CIRS
