# DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide)

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Delta Sleep Promotion**: Promotes slow-wave (delta) sleep — the deepest, most restorative sleep stage
- **GABA-A Receptor Modulation**: Affects the same inhibitory pathways as common sleep medications
- **HPA Axis Regulation**: Normalizes cortisol and ACTH levels, particularly when elevated from stress
- **Growth Hormone Release**: May enhance GH release during sleep
- **Circadian Rhythm Regulation**: Helps normalize disrupted sleep-wake cycles
- **Analgesic Properties**: Demonstrated pain-reducing effects in animal studies
- **Non-Sedating**: Does not produce sedation or dependency — promotes natural sleep
