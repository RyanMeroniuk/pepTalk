# DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide)

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Benzodiazepines (Xanax, Valium, Ativan, Klonopin)**: DSIP modulates GABA-A receptors — the same receptor system benzodiazepines target. Combining may cause excessive sedation, respiratory depression, or unpredictable CNS depression. Do not combine without medical supervision. If transitioning from benzodiazepines to DSIP, do so gradually under professional guidance
- **Z-Drugs (Zolpidem/Ambien, Eszopiclone/Lunesta, Zaleplon)**: Similar GABA-A mechanism overlap. Additive sedation risk. Do not combine
- **Opioids**: Opioids cause CNS and respiratory depression. Combining with DSIP's GABAergic effects increases risk of excessive sedation and respiratory compromise. Avoid combination
- **Other CNS Depressants (Barbiturates, Muscle Relaxants, Antihistamines)**: Additive CNS depression with any sedating medication. Exercise caution with gabapentin, pregabalin, cyclobenzaprine, hydroxyzine, and similar drugs
- **Alcohol**: Alcohol is a CNS depressant and GABA-A positive allosteric modulator. Combining with DSIP amplifies sedation and disrupts the natural sleep architecture DSIP is designed to restore. Avoid alcohol during DSIP cycles
- **Melatonin (High-Dose)**: Low-dose melatonin (0.5-1mg) is complementary, but high-dose melatonin (5-10mg+) combined with DSIP may cause excessive drowsiness, vivid dreams, or next-day grogginess. Start with low-dose melatonin if combining
- **Antidepressants with Sedating Properties (Trazodone, Mirtazapine, Doxepin)**: Additive sedation possible. If using sedating antidepressants for sleep, DSIP adds overlapping mechanism. Discuss with prescriber before combining
- **MAO Inhibitors**: DSIP's effects on neurotransmitter systems are not fully characterized. Combining with MAOIs (which broadly affect monoamine metabolism) carries unpredictable risk
- **Pregnancy / Breastfeeding**: Contraindicated. Insufficient safety data for DSIP during pregnancy or lactation. Effects on fetal neurodevelopment are unknown
- **Respiratory Conditions (Sleep Apnea, COPD)**: Use with caution — any compound affecting CNS depression and sleep depth could theoretically worsen respiratory conditions during sleep. Untreated sleep apnea patients should address apnea before adding DSIP
