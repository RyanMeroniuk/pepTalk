# DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide)

## Foods to Eat

- **Tart Cherries / Tart Cherry Juice (8 oz, 1-2 hours before bed)**: One of the richest natural sources of melatonin. Clinical studies demonstrate increased sleep time and efficiency. Supports DSIP's circadian normalization
- **Kiwifruit (2 kiwis, 1 hour before bed)**: Studies show significant improvements in sleep onset, duration, and efficiency. Contains serotonin precursors, folate, and antioxidants
- **Turkey**: High in tryptophan, the amino acid precursor to serotonin and melatonin. Consuming tryptophan-rich protein with complex carbs enhances brain tryptophan uptake
- **Fatty Fish (salmon, sardines, herring)**: Rich in omega-3s and vitamin D. Studies link regular fatty fish consumption to better sleep quality and faster sleep onset
- **Magnesium-Rich Foods (pumpkin seeds, spinach, dark chocolate, almonds)**: Magnesium activates the parasympathetic nervous system and regulates GABA receptors — supporting the same inhibitory pathways DSIP acts on
- **Warm Milk**: Contains tryptophan and casein-derived peptides (casomorphins) with mild sedative properties. The warmth also promotes vasodilation and core temperature regulation
- **Chamomile Tea**: Apigenin binds GABA-A receptors, producing mild sedation that complements DSIP's GABAergic mechanism
- **Passionflower Tea**: Contains chrysin, which enhances GABA activity. Studies show improved sleep quality scores
- **Bananas**: Tryptophan, magnesium, and vitamin B6 — all required for the tryptophan-to-serotonin-to-melatonin conversion pathway
- **Complex Carbohydrates (oats, sweet potato, brown rice)**: A moderate carbohydrate evening meal triggers insulin release, which clears competing amino acids and facilitates tryptophan transport across the blood-brain barrier
- **Bone Broth**: Rich in glycine, which lowers core body temperature and improves sleep quality in studies. Also supports gut health
