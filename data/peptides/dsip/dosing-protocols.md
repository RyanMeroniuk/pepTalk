# DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide)

## Dosing Protocols

- **Standard**: 100-500 mcg subcutaneous injection
- **Timing**: Evening, 30-60 minutes before bed
- **Cycle**: 2-4 weeks on to avoid potential tolerance
- **Frequency**: Daily during cycle, or as needed
- **Start Low**: Begin at 100mcg, titrate up based on response
- **Reconstitution**: Bacteriostatic water; refrigerate
