# DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide)

## Benefits

- **Deep Sleep Enhancement**: Promotes delta-wave sleep specifically — the most restorative phase
- **Sleep Quality**: Improves overall sleep architecture without sedation
- **Cortisol Reduction**: Lowers elevated cortisol from chronic stress
- **Stress Modulation**: Normalizes HPA axis function under stress
- **Pain Management**: Analgesic effects, studied for chronic pain and fibromyalgia
- **Recovery**: Enhanced physical and cognitive recovery through improved sleep
- **No Dependency**: Unlike benzodiazepines or Z-drugs, no tolerance or withdrawal
- **Circadian Reset**: Useful for jet lag and shift work sleep disruption
