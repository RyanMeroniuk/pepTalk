# DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide)

## Overview

DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide) is a naturally occurring neuropeptide first isolated in 1977 from rabbit brain during induced sleep. It is a nonapeptide (9 amino acids: Trp-Ala-Gly-Gly-Asp-Ala-Ser-Gly-Glu) that promotes deep, restorative delta-wave sleep. Unlike traditional sleep medications, DSIP does not sedate — it normalizes sleep architecture and promotes natural sleep-wake cycles. Also demonstrates cortisol reduction, analgesic, and stress-modulating properties.
