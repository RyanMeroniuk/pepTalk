# DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide)

## Things to Avoid

- **Irregular Sleep Schedule ("Social Jet Lag")**: This is the number one behavior that undermines DSIP. Sleeping in on weekends and staying up late creates a state equivalent to crossing time zones. DSIP normalizes circadian function, but inconsistent scheduling overwhelms this mechanism
- **Blue Light Exposure Before Bed**: Phones, tablets, computers, and TVs emit blue wavelengths (450-490nm) that suppress melatonin for 60-90 minutes after exposure. This directly opposes the natural sleep onset DSIP promotes. Implement a hard digital sunset 60-90 minutes before bed
- **Using Phone/Tablet in Bed**: The bed should be associated exclusively with sleep. Using devices in bed creates a conditioned association between the bed and wakefulness, fragmenting sleep onset even without DSIP
- **Late-Night Exercise**: Vigorous exercise within 3-4 hours of bedtime raises cortisol and core temperature — both must be declining for delta sleep. Schedule workouts for morning or early afternoon
- **Relying on DSIP While Ignoring Sleep Hygiene**: DSIP is a sleep normalizer, not a sedative. It works with the body's natural sleep mechanisms. If sleep hygiene is poor (bright room, noisy, hot, irregular schedule), DSIP's effectiveness is dramatically reduced
- **Late Caffeine (the most common sleep saboteur)**: Many people underestimate caffeine sensitivity. Some individuals have genetic variants (slow CYP1A2 metabolizers) where caffeine active effects persist 8-10 hours. Cut all caffeine by noon
- **Alcohol as a Sleep Aid**: This cannot be emphasized enough — alcohol is the most common sleep-destroying substance that people mistakenly use as a sleep aid. It suppresses exactly the delta-wave sleep DSIP promotes
- **Chronic Stress Without Intervention**: DSIP reduces cortisol, but a lifestyle of chronic high stress continuously replenishes it. Implement at least one structured stress management practice daily
- **Stimulating Content Before Bed**: Intense TV shows, arguments, work emails, or distressing news activate the sympathetic nervous system and raise cortisol, making it harder for DSIP to initiate the transition to delta sleep
- **Oversleeping on Off Days**: Sleeping 10-12 hours on weekends desynchronizes the circadian clock. DSIP works best with consistent 7-9 hour sleep windows
