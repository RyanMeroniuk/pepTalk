# Semaglutide

## Dosing Protocols

- **Wegovy (Weight Loss)**: 0.25mg → 0.5mg → 1mg → 1.7mg → 2.4mg weekly (escalated monthly)
- **Ozempic (Diabetes)**: 0.25mg → 0.5mg → 1mg → 2mg weekly
- **Rybelsus (Oral)**: 3mg → 7mg → 14mg daily, taken fasted with small sip of water
- **Route**: Subcutaneous injection (weekly) or oral tablet (daily)
- **Dose Escalation**: Gradual increase essential to manage GI side effects
- **Continuous Use**: Weight regain occurs upon discontinuation in most patients
