# Semaglutide

## Side Effects

- **GI Effects**: Nausea (44%), diarrhea (30%), vomiting (24%), constipation (24%) — usually improve over time
- **Decreased Appetite**: Therapeutic but can be excessive
- **Fatigue**: Common during dose escalation
- **Muscle Loss**: Significant lean mass loss (up to 40% of total weight lost may be lean mass)
- **Gallbladder**: Increased gallstone risk with rapid weight loss
- **Pancreatitis**: Rare but serious risk
- **Injection Site Reactions**: Mild
- **Thyroid**: Black box warning for medullary thyroid carcinoma risk (rodent studies)
- **"Ozempic Face"**: Facial volume loss from rapid fat loss
