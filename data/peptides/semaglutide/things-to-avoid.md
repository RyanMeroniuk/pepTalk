# Semaglutide

## Things to Avoid

- **Skipping Meals**: Even though you are not hungry, you MUST eat. Skipping meals accelerates muscle loss, causes nutrient deficiencies, hair loss, fatigue, and metabolic slowdown. Set meal reminders if needed. Protein shakes count if solid food feels impossible
- **Lying Down After Eating**: Wait at least 30-60 minutes after eating before lying down or reclining. Slowed gastric emptying plus gravity = severe acid reflux and nausea. Take a gentle walk after meals instead
- **Relying Only on Cardio for Exercise**: Running, cycling, and swimming burn calories but do NOT prevent muscle loss. You MUST include resistance training (weights, resistance bands, bodyweight exercises). Muscle preservation requires mechanical stimulus
- **Drinking Large Volumes of Fluid With Meals**: Sip only small amounts during meals. Drink water BETWEEN meals. Large amounts of liquid during meals fill the slow-emptying stomach, causing bloating and preventing you from eating enough protein
- **Crash Dieting on Top of Semaglutide**: The drug already creates a significant caloric deficit. Adding extreme restriction leads to dangerous muscle wasting, severe nutrient deficiency, hair loss, gallstones, "Ozempic face," and metabolic adaptation. Eat at minimum your basal metabolic rate
- **Ignoring Protein Targets**: This cannot be overstated. If you eat only 800 calories of carbs and fat while on semaglutide, you will lose substantial muscle. Track protein intake — at least 100-150g/day for most people
- **Sedentary Lifestyle**: Sitting all day accelerates muscle loss and slows GI motility (worsening constipation and bloating). Move regularly — walk after meals, take movement breaks, aim for 8,000+ steps
- **Rapid Dose Escalation**: Jumping up doses too quickly to speed weight loss dramatically worsens GI side effects and does not improve long-term outcomes. Follow the monthly escalation schedule
- **Ignoring Persistent GI Symptoms**: Severe vomiting, inability to keep down any food/water for 24+ hours, severe abdominal pain, or signs of dehydration require immediate medical attention. These can indicate pancreatitis or bowel obstruction
- **Drinking Alcohol During Dose Escalation**: GI tolerance is lowest when increasing doses. Alcohol amplifies nausea and unpredictable absorption during this period
- **Expecting the Drug to Do All the Work**: Semaglutide is a powerful tool, but without exercise and proper nutrition, you will lose muscle, regain weight upon stopping, and develop nutrient deficiencies. Build habits now
