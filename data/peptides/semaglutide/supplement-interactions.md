# Semaglutide

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Critical**: High protein intake (1.6-2.2g/kg) to minimize muscle loss
- **Critical**: Resistance training to preserve lean mass
- **Enhanced by**: Creatine monohydrate for muscle preservation
- **Caution**: Other diabetes medications (hypoglycemia risk, especially sulfonylureas and insulin)
- **Caution**: Oral medications (delayed absorption from slowed gastric emptying)
- **Rybelsus specific**: Must take fasted, 30 minutes before food, with no more than 4oz water
- **Contraindicated**: MTC/MEN2 history, pancreatitis history, pregnancy/breastfeeding
