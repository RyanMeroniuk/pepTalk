# Semaglutide

## Foods to Avoid

- **High-Fat Greasy/Fried Foods**: French fries, fried chicken, onion rings, fast food burgers, pizza, cream-based pasta sauces, heavy takeout food. These are the #1 trigger for severe nausea and vomiting on semaglutide. Slowed gastric emptying means greasy food sits in the stomach for hours, causing "dumping syndrome" — nausea, cramping, diarrhea, and sweating
- **Carbonated Beverages**: Regular and diet soda, sparkling water, seltzer, beer, champagne. Carbonation creates trapped gas in a stomach that cannot empty normally, leading to painful bloating, distension, and belching
- **High-Sugar Foods and Drinks**: Candy, cookies, cake, ice cream, donuts, sugary cereals, fruit juice, regular soda, sweetened coffee drinks. With dramatically reduced appetite, every calorie must count — sugar provides zero nutritional value and causes blood sugar crashes
- **Alcohol**: Wine, beer, cocktails, spirits. Alcohol absorption is unpredictable with slowed gastric emptying (can hit much harder and faster), provides empty calories, increases nausea, worsens dehydration, and impairs liver function needed for fat metabolism
- **Large Portion Sizes**: Even healthy food in large amounts causes distress. Semaglutide fundamentally changes how fast food moves through your stomach
- **Very Spicy Foods**: Hot sauces, spicy curries, jalapenos, habaneros. Increase acid reflux and nausea, which are already common side effects
- **Raw Fibrous Vegetables in Large Amounts**: Large salads, raw broccoli, raw cauliflower, raw cabbage, raw kale. Hard to digest with slowed motility — cook vegetables instead
- **Tough Red Meats**: Fatty steak cuts, ribs, brisket. Very slow to digest and often trigger nausea. Choose lean cuts and cook until tender
- **Processed and Ultra-Processed Foods**: Chips, packaged cookies, deli meats, hot dogs, frozen dinners, fast food. Low nutritional density when you can only eat small amounts
