# Semaglutide

## Regulatory Status

- **FDA-APPROVED**: Ozempic (T2D, 2017), Wegovy (weight management, 2021), Rybelsus (oral T2D, 2019)
- Blockbuster drug — billions in annual sales
- Global supply shortages (2023-2025)
- Compounded versions available (regulatory disputes ongoing)
- Prescription required
- Covered by some insurance plans
