# Semaglutide

## Foods to Eat

- **Lean Proteins (eat FIRST at every meal)**: Chicken breast, turkey breast, wild-caught salmon, cod, tilapia, shrimp, tuna (canned in water), eggs and egg whites, Greek yogurt (plain, 0% or 2% fat), cottage cheese (low-fat), lean ground turkey, bison, tofu, tempeh, edamame
- **Protein Supplements**: Whey protein isolate shakes (when solid food causes nausea), casein protein (slow-release, good before bed), collagen peptides (skin elasticity during weight loss), bone broth (soothing and protein-rich)
- **Complex Carbohydrates (small portions after protein)**: Sweet potatoes, quinoa, oats (not instant), brown rice, lentils, chickpeas, black beans, whole grain bread (small amounts)
- **Healthy Fats (essential for satiety and skin health)**: Avocado (half per serving), extra virgin olive oil, almonds, walnuts, chia seeds, flaxseed, pumpkin seeds, fatty fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel)
- **Cooked Vegetables (easier to digest than raw)**: Steamed broccoli, roasted zucchini, sauteed spinach, baked sweet potato, cooked carrots, steamed green beans, roasted bell peppers, mashed cauliflower
- **Fruits (low-sugar, easy to digest)**: Blueberries, strawberries, raspberries, cantaloupe, watermelon, kiwi, banana (ripe — easier to digest)
- **Gut-Soothing Foods**: Bone broth, ginger (tea or fresh), papaya, cooked oatmeal, plain rice, fermented foods in small amounts (kefir, yogurt, sauerkraut)
- **Hydrating Foods**: Cucumber, watermelon, zucchini, soup broths — these help with hydration when drinking fluids feels uncomfortable
