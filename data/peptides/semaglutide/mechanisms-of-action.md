# Semaglutide

## Mechanisms of Action

- **GLP-1 Receptor Agonist**: Mimics the incretin hormone GLP-1
- **Appetite Suppression**: Acts on hypothalamic appetite centers to reduce hunger and increase satiety
- **Gastric Emptying**: Slows stomach emptying, prolonging fullness after meals
- **Insulin Secretion**: Enhances glucose-dependent insulin release from pancreatic beta cells
- **Glucagon Suppression**: Reduces glucagon secretion when blood glucose is elevated
- **Beta Cell Protection**: May support pancreatic beta cell health
