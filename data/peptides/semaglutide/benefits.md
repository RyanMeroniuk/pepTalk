# Semaglutide

## Benefits

- **Weight Loss**: Average 15-17% body weight reduction in clinical trials (Wegovy 2.4mg)
- **Diabetes Management**: Significant HbA1c reduction (Ozempic)
- **Cardiovascular**: 20% reduction in major cardiovascular events (SELECT trial)
- **Kidney Protection**: Reduced kidney disease progression (FLOW trial)
- **Blood Pressure**: Modest improvements
- **Lipid Profile**: Improved triglycerides and cholesterol
- **NASH/Fatty Liver**: Significant liver fat reduction
