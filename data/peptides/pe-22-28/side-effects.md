# PE-22-28

## Side Effects

- **Unknown Human Safety Profile**: No human safety data exists; the side-effect profile in people is entirely uncharacterized.
- **Theoretical CNS Effects**: As a TREK-1 modulator affecting mood circuitry, unpredictable mood shifts, agitation, or affective changes are plausible but unstudied.
- **TREK-1 Off-Target Risk**: TREK-1 channels are expressed in the cardiovascular system and other tissues, so systemic ion-channel effects cannot be excluded.
- **Injection-Site Reactions**: As with any reconstituted research peptide, redness, irritation, or infection risk at injection sites.
- **Contamination / Purity Risk**: Research-chemical sourcing means impurities, endotoxins, or mislabeled content are realistic hazards.
- **Animal Data Reassurance Is Limited**: Rodent studies reported tolerability, but animal tolerability does not establish human safety.
- **No Long-Term Data**: Chronic-use effects, dependency potential, and withdrawal are completely unknown.
