# PE-22-28

## Foods to Eat

- **Wild-Caught Fatty Fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel)**: EPA/DHA omega-3s support BDNF signaling and neuronal membranes, reinforcing the neurotrophic pathway PE-22-28 targets.
- **Blueberries & Dark Berries**: Anthocyanins cross the blood-brain barrier, reduce neuroinflammation, and support memory consolidation.
- **Leafy Greens (spinach, kale)**: Folate supports methylation and neurotransmitter synthesis, including serotonin pathways relevant to mood.
- **Eggs (whole, pastured)**: Choline and B vitamins support cholinergic function and overall neuronal health.
- **Fermented Foods (kimchi, kefir, sauerkraut)**: Gut-brain axis health influences serotonin availability, complementing the serotonergic mechanism.
- **Turmeric/Curcumin**: Anti-neuroinflammatory and independently BDNF-supportive.
- **Dark Chocolate (85%+ cacao)**: Flavonoids improve cerebral blood flow and support BDNF expression.
- **Walnuts & Pumpkin Seeds**: Provide ALA omega-3s, magnesium, and tryptophan precursors for serotonin synthesis.
