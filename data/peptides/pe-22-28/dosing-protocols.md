## Dosing Protocols

- **No Established Human Protocol**: There is no validated human dose. Any figure circulating online is extrapolated from rodent studies and is not safety-tested in people.
- **Animal Study Reference**: Rodent antidepressant studies used microgram-per-kilogram intraperitoneal or intravenous dosing — routes and scaling that do not translate cleanly to human use.
- **Anecdotal Research Use**: Self-experimenters report subcutaneous microgram-range dosing, but this is unverified, unmonitored, and carries unknown risk.
- **Reconstitution**: Lyophilized powder is typically reconstituted with bacteriostatic water if used in a research setting.
- **Storage**: Lyophilized peptide stored frozen; reconstituted solution refrigerated and used within a short window — standard research-peptide handling.
- **Half-Life Unknown**: Human pharmacokinetics are uncharacterized, so dosing frequency cannot be responsibly defined.
- **Strong Caution**: Given zero human safety data, this peptide should be regarded as purely experimental; the only defensible "protocol" is non-use outside formal research.
