# PE-22-28

## Benefits

- **Antidepressant-Like Effects (animal-only)**: Produced rapid reductions in depressive behaviors in rodent forced-swim and chronic-stress models; no human antidepressant data exists.
- **Rapid Onset (preclinical)**: Behavioral changes appeared within days in animals, faster than the multi-week lag of SSRIs — a key research interest, not a proven human benefit.
- **BDNF / Neuroplasticity Support**: Upregulated BDNF and promoted hippocampal neurogenesis in rodents, mechanisms associated with learning and resilience.
- **Potential Neuroprotection (early)**: Some stroke-model studies suggest reduced neuronal damage, though this is very preliminary.
- **No Hormonal Effects**: Acts on ion channels rather than the endocrine system, so it does not directly affect cortisol or sex hormones (based on its mechanism).
- **Research Tool Value**: Useful as an experimental probe of TREK-1 biology in depression research.
- **IMPORTANT — No Human Data**: Every listed benefit is from animal studies. There are no clinical trials, no efficacy data, and no established human benefit.
