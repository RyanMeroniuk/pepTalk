# PE-22-28

## Things to Avoid

- **Treating It as Proven**: There is zero human data — avoid assuming animal antidepressant effects translate to people.
- **Combining with Serotonergic Drugs**: Because PE-22-28 enhances serotonergic tone preclinically, stacking with SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, or 5-HTP raises a theoretical serotonin-excess risk; avoid.
- **Unverified Sourcing**: Research-chemical suppliers vary widely in purity; contaminated or mislabeled product is a real hazard.
- **Guessing at Doses**: With no human pharmacokinetics, self-selected doses are pure guesswork and risk overstimulation or unknown harm.
- **Self-Treating Depression**: Using an untested peptide instead of evidence-based care for a serious mood disorder is dangerous; seek professional help.
- **Stacking Multiple Experimental Compounds**: Layering PE-22-28 with other unstudied peptides compounds unknown risks and makes adverse-event attribution impossible.
- **Chronic Use Without Monitoring**: Long-term effects are unknown; open-ended use is not defensible.
