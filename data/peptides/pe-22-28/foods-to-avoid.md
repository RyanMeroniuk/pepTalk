# PE-22-28

## Foods to Avoid

- **Excess Refined Sugar**: Blood glucose spikes and chronic high intake reduce BDNF expression, opposing the peptide's proposed neurotrophic mechanism.
- **Alcohol**: Directly neurotoxic and suppresses BDNF for 24-48 hours, counteracting any neurogenesis benefit.
- **Processed Seed Oils (soybean, canola, corn)**: High omega-6 load promotes neuroinflammation and disrupts the omega-3/omega-6 balance critical to brain health.
- **Ultra-Processed Foods**: Damage the gut lining and disrupt the gut-brain axis that supports serotonin production.
- **Trans Fats & Fried Foods**: Promote systemic and neural inflammation that impairs synaptic plasticity.
- **Excessive Caffeine (>300mg/day)**: May add overstimulation risk on top of a mood-active experimental peptide.
- **Artificial Sweeteners (aspartame, sucralose)**: May disrupt gut microbiome and neurotransmitter signaling.
