# PE-22-28

## Mechanisms of Action

- **TREK-1 Channel Blockade**: Selectively blocks the TREK-1 two-pore potassium channel, which is implicated in mood regulation; TREK-1 blockade mimics the antidepressant phenotype seen in TREK-1 knockout animals (preclinical).
- **~100x Spadin Potency**: Reported in rodent studies to inhibit TREK-1 at roughly 100-fold lower concentrations than the parent peptide spadin, the fragment it was optimized from.
- **BDNF Upregulation**: Increases brain-derived neurotrophic factor expression in the hippocampus in animal models, supporting synaptic plasticity and neuronal survival.
- **Rapid Neurogenesis**: Drives hippocampal neurogenesis on a days-to-week timescale in rodents, faster than the multi-week onset of classical SSRIs (animal data only).
- **Serotonergic Coupling**: TREK-1 modulation enhances serotonergic neurotransmission downstream, contributing to the antidepressant-like signature observed preclinically.
- **Neuroprotection (preclinical)**: Some rodent stroke models suggest reduced infarct size, though this is early and not replicated in humans.
- **Unknown Human Pharmacology**: Half-life, bioavailability, and brain penetration in humans are not characterized — all kinetic claims are extrapolated from animals.
