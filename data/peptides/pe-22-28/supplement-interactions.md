# PE-22-28

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Supplement**: Omega-3 (EPA/DHA, 2-3g) — supports BDNF signaling and neuronal membrane health, theoretically synergistic with PE-22-28's neurotrophic mechanism.
- **Supplement**: Lion's Mane (500-1000mg) — promotes NGF/neurotrophic activity, complementary to the neurogenesis-focused mechanism.
- **Supplement**: Magnesium (glycinate or L-threonate) — supports calm mood and neuronal function; enhanced when paired with neuroplasticity-targeting compounds.
- **Supplement**: Vitamin D3 — low vitamin D is linked to depressive symptoms; foundational support for any mood-targeting protocol.
- **Supplement**: L-Theanine — promotes calm focus and may smooth potential overstimulation.
- **Caution**: Other serotonergic supplements (5-HTP, St. John's Wort) — because PE-22-28 enhances serotonergic tone preclinically, combining adds theoretical serotonin-excess risk; avoid stacking.
- **Note**: No supplement interaction has been studied in humans; all pairings above are theoretical and based on mechanism.
