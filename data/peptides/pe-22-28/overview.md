# PE-22-28

## Overview

PE-22-28 is a synthetic seven-amino-acid peptide derived from spadin, itself a fragment of the sortilin propeptide. It acts as a blocker of the TREK-1 (TWIK-related potassium) channel and has been reported in animal studies to be roughly 100x more potent than parent spadin at this target. Blocking TREK-1 in the brain produces rapid antidepressant-like effects and upregulates BDNF, driving neurogenesis in the hippocampus on a timescale of days rather than weeks. All current evidence is preclinical — rodent models of depression and stroke — and there is zero published human cognitive, antidepressant, or safety data. It is sold only as a research chemical, is not approved by any regulatory agency, and any human use is unstudied and experimental.
