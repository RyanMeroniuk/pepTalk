# PE-22-28

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **SSRIs / SNRIs**: Caution — PE-22-28 enhances serotonergic neurotransmission preclinically; combining with serotonin reuptake inhibitors raises a theoretical risk of excess serotonergic activity. Monitor closely or avoid.
- **MAOIs**: Contraindicated — MAOIs sharply raise synaptic serotonin; layering a serotonergic-acting peptide could compound serotonin toxicity risk.
- **Beta-Blockers**: Caution — TREK-1 channels are expressed in cardiovascular tissue, so cardiovascular-acting drugs warrant monitoring given unknown systemic ion-channel effects.
- **Other Antidepressants**: Caution — overlapping mood-pathway activity with unpredictable additive effects.
- **Contraindicated in Pregnancy**: No reproductive safety data; avoid entirely.
- **Contraindicated in Breastfeeding**: Unknown transfer and infant effects; avoid.
- **Contraindicated with Active Cancer**: No oncology safety data for an experimental neurotrophic agent; avoid without specialist guidance.
- **Timing Note**: If any research use occurs alongside other CNS agents, separate dosing by several hours is a minimal precaution, though no interaction kinetics are established.
