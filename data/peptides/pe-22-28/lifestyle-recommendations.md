# PE-22-28

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Prioritize Sleep (7-9 hours)**: BDNF-driven neuroplasticity, the proposed mechanism of PE-22-28, depends on sleep to consolidate new neural connections. Keep a consistent sleep-wake schedule.
- **Aerobic Exercise (30-45 min, 4-5x/week)**: Cardio independently raises BDNF by 200-300%, directly reinforcing the neurotrophic pathway PE-22-28 targets in animal studies.
- **Sunlight & Circadian Anchoring**: Morning light supports serotonergic tone and mood regulation, complementary to PE-22-28's serotonergic mechanism.
- **Stress Management**: Chronic cortisol elevation suppresses BDNF and hippocampal neurogenesis — the exact processes PE-22-28 aims to enhance. Use meditation, breathwork, or therapy.
- **Mental Engagement**: Active learning and novel cognitive challenges leverage any neuroplasticity benefit during use.
- **Limit Alcohol**: Alcohol is neurotoxic and suppresses BDNF, directly opposing the peptide's proposed mechanism.
- **Treat as Experimental**: Because there is no human data, the most evidence-based "lifestyle" recommendation is to rely on proven mood and neuroplasticity drivers (sleep, exercise, light, stress control) rather than an untested peptide.
