# SLU-PP-332

## Dosing Protocols

- **No Established Human Dose**: There are no human clinical trials, so no validated or safe human dosing exists.
- **Animal-Study Dosing Only**: Published doses are from rodent research (weight-based mg/kg given by injection) and do not translate to safe human protocols.
- **Gray-Market Research Powder**: Sold as research chemical powder with unverified purity, concentration, and identity; self-dosing is experimental and unstandardized.
- **Unknown Pharmacokinetics in Humans**: Half-life, bioavailability, and accumulation in people are not characterized.
- **Route Uncertainty**: Animal work commonly uses injection; oral human bioavailability is not established.
- **Note**: Because no human safety data exist, any human dosing should be regarded as unproven and high-uncertainty; this entry is educational, not a dosing recommendation.
