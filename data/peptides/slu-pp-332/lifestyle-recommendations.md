# SLU-PP-332

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Treat as Experimental**: There are no human trials. Approach SLU-PP-332 as an unproven research compound, not a validated supplement or exercise replacement.
- **Real Exercise Still Wins**: Even if marketed as an "exercise mimetic," actual endurance and resistance training deliver proven, multi-system benefits that no animal-only molecule can claim to replicate in humans.
- **Prioritize Foundations**: Sleep, whole-food nutrition, and consistent training drive the mitochondrial and metabolic adaptations SLU-PP-332 only mimics in mice.
- **Be Wary of Sourcing**: Gray-market powders carry purity and identity risks; verify nothing can be assumed about contents.
- **Monitor If Used**: Anyone experimenting should track cardiovascular markers and overall health closely, given theoretical cardiac concerns and absent human data.
- **Avoid in Vulnerable States**: Do not use during pregnancy, breastfeeding, or with significant cardiac or metabolic disease given the unknown risk profile.
- **Frame as Research**: Use is educational/experimental only; this is not medical advice.
- **It Is Not a Peptide**: Included for longevity-stack completeness; it is a small-molecule nuclear-receptor agonist with a distinct profile.
