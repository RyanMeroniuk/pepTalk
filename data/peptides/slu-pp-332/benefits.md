# SLU-PP-332

## Benefits

- **Reported Fat Loss (Animal Data)**: Rodent studies report reduced fat mass and body weight without reduced food intake.
- **Improved Endurance Markers (Animal Data)**: Mice showed increased running capacity and oxidative muscle adaptations resembling endurance training.
- **Increased Energy Expenditure (Animal Data)**: Raised metabolic rate in animal models, the basis for the "exercise mimetic" framing.
- **Mitochondrial Biogenesis (Mechanistic)**: Promotes mitochondrial density and oxidative capacity via ERR activation in preclinical models.
- **Metabolic Health Interest**: Investigated as a potential approach for obesity and metabolic dysfunction — strictly preclinical at this stage.
- **Important Caveat**: All listed benefits derive from animal or in-vitro studies. There are no human trials, so none of these benefits are established in people.
