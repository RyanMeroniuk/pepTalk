# SLU-PP-332

## Foods to Avoid

- **Refined Sugar & High-Glycemic Foods**: Promote fat storage and metabolic dysfunction, working against the fat-oxidation and metabolic-health rationale.
- **Ultra-Processed Foods**: Drive inflammation and poor mitochondrial health, undermining oxidative-metabolism goals.
- **Processed Seed Oils**: Soybean, corn, and canola oils promote omega-6-driven inflammation.
- **Excess Alcohol**: Impairs mitochondrial function and adds metabolic and cardiovascular stress — relevant given theoretical cardiac concerns.
- **Trans Fats and Fried Foods**: Harm cardiovascular and metabolic health.
- **Excess Caffeine/Stimulants With an Unstudied Compound**: Stacking strong stimulants with an uncharacterized metabolic agent adds cardiovascular uncertainty.
