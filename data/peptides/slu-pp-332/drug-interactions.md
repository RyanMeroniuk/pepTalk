# SLU-PP-332

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **No Human Interaction Data**: With no clinical trials, no drug interactions are formally characterized; all considerations below are precautionary.
- **Caution: Cardiac and blood-pressure medications**: ERR receptors are highly expressed in the heart; monitor closely given theoretical cardiometabolic effects and combine only with medical oversight.
- **Caution: Diabetes / metabolic medications (insulin, metformin, GLP-1 agonists)**: Potential additive effects on energy metabolism and fat oxidation; monitor for unexpected metabolic changes.
- **Caution: Stimulants and other metabolic enhancers**: Combining with an uncharacterized metabolic agent may compound cardiovascular load; use caution.
- **Caution: Anticoagulants / blood thinners**: Monitor, as combined effects are unknown.
- **Contraindicated: Pregnancy and breastfeeding** — no safety data; avoid.
- **Contraindicated: Significant cardiovascular disease** — given strong cardiac ERR expression and absent human safety data.
- **Note**: Because human pharmacology is unknown, any concurrent medication use should be treated as high-uncertainty and discussed with a clinician.
