# SLU-PP-332

## Things to Avoid

- **Assuming Human Safety or Efficacy**: There are no human trials; do not assume animal results translate to people.
- **Using It as an Exercise Replacement**: It mimics some exercise adaptations only in mice; real training has proven, broad human benefits it cannot substitute for.
- **Stacking With Other Unproven Metabolic Agents**: Combining experimental compounds compounds unknown cardiovascular and metabolic risk.
- **Ignoring Cardiac Concerns**: ERR receptors are highly expressed in the heart; theoretical cardiac effects are unstudied long-term — do not dismiss them.
- **Trusting Gray-Market Purity**: Research powders may be impure or mislabeled; identity and dose cannot be assumed.
- **Use in Pregnancy/Breastfeeding or With Heart/Metabolic Disease**: Avoid given the unknown risk profile.
- **Treating Media Hype as Evidence**: 2026 longevity press attention is not clinical validation; remain skeptical and research-oriented.
