# SLU-PP-332

## Overview

SLU-PP-332 is a synthetic small molecule, not a peptide, included here because it trends in 2026 longevity and "exercise in a pill" media. It is a pan-agonist of the estrogen-related receptors (ERRalpha, ERRbeta, ERRgamma) — orphan nuclear receptors that regulate mitochondrial biogenesis, oxidative metabolism, and fat oxidation. In rodent studies it has been reported to increase energy expenditure, improve endurance-type metabolic markers, and reduce fat mass without changes in food intake, leading to its description as an "exercise mimetic." Critically, the evidence base is animal-only: there are no published human clinical trials establishing efficacy or safety, and long-term effects in people are unknown. It should be treated as an experimental research compound, and any claims about human benefits are unproven.
