# SLU-PP-332

## Side Effects

- **Human Side-Effect Profile Unknown**: With no human trials, the side-effect profile in people is entirely uncharacterized.
- **Theoretical Cardiac Concerns**: ERR receptors are highly expressed in heart and high-energy tissues; potent agonism raises theoretical concern for cardiac and metabolic effects that have not been studied long-term in humans.
- **Broad Receptor Activity**: As a pan-ERR agonist, it acts on multiple tissues, increasing the chance of off-target or systemic effects.
- **Unknown Long-Term Risk**: Chronic effects on the heart, metabolism, and hormonal systems are unstudied.
- **Gray-Market Quality Risks**: Research-grade powder may contain impurities or be mislabeled, adding unpredictable risks independent of the molecule itself.
- **Note**: Absence of reported side effects reflects absence of human study, not demonstrated safety.
