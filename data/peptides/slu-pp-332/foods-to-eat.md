# SLU-PP-332

## Foods to Eat

- **Mitochondria-Supporting Whole Foods**: Leafy greens, colorful vegetables, and quality protein supply cofactors for the oxidative metabolism SLU-PP-332 targets in animal models.
- **Healthy Fats**: Olive oil, avocados, nuts, and fatty fish support fat-oxidation pathways and provide omega-3s for metabolic and cardiovascular health.
- **Complex Carbohydrates**: Whole grains, legumes, and vegetables fuel endurance-type activity and stable energy.
- **CoQ10 and B-Vitamin Sources**: Organ meats, fatty fish, eggs, and whole grains supply nutrients central to mitochondrial function.
- **Antioxidant-Rich Produce**: Berries and vegetables provide polyphenols that support redox balance during increased oxidative metabolism.
- **Adequate Protein**: Supports muscle and the oxidative-muscle adaptations associated with endurance training.
