# SLU-PP-332

## Mechanisms of Action

- **ERR Pan-Agonism**: Activates the estrogen-related receptors ERRalpha, ERRbeta, and ERRgamma, orphan nuclear receptors central to oxidative metabolism.
- **Mitochondrial Biogenesis**: ERR activation upregulates genes (in concert with PGC-1alpha pathways) that increase mitochondrial number and oxidative capacity, mimicking adaptations to endurance exercise.
- **Increased Fat Oxidation**: Shifts metabolism toward burning fatty acids, reported in rodents to reduce fat mass without reduced food intake.
- **Enhanced Energy Expenditure**: Raises resting and activity energy expenditure in animal models, underpinning the "exercise mimetic" label.
- **Endurance-Type Adaptations (Animal Data)**: Reported to improve running capacity and oxidative muscle phenotype in mice.
- **Note**: These mechanisms are characterized in cell and rodent studies; whether they translate to humans is unverified.
