# 5-Amino-1MQ

## Things to Avoid

- **Sedentary Lifestyle**: 5-Amino-1MQ enhances fat cell metabolism, but without physical activity, the overall impact on body composition is limited. Exercise is the essential co-factor for results
- **Excessive Alcohol Consumption**: Alcohol directly depletes NAD+, which 5-Amino-1MQ works to preserve. Regular drinking undermines the core mechanism of action. Minimize or eliminate alcohol during your cycle
- **Chronic Sleep Deprivation**: Poor sleep impairs NAD+ metabolism, raises cortisol, increases hunger hormones, and promotes fat storage — all of which work against the metabolic benefits of 5-Amino-1MQ
- **Chronic Unmanaged Stress**: Prolonged cortisol elevation promotes visceral fat accumulation and impairs overall fat metabolism. Active stress management is part of the protocol
- **Skipping Meals or Extreme Caloric Restriction**: Very low calorie intake can slow metabolism and is unnecessary with 5-Amino-1MQ. A moderate, consistent deficit is more effective than extreme restriction
- **Expecting Dramatic Overnight Results**: 5-Amino-1MQ works by gradually shifting fat cell metabolism over weeks. It is not a dramatic weight loss drug — it is a metabolic optimizer. Pair with consistent diet and exercise for best results
- **Ignoring the SAM Cycle**: 5-Amino-1MQ affects SAM (S-adenosylmethionine) metabolism. Long-term use without cycling (8-12 weeks on, 4 weeks off) is not well-studied. Follow cycling protocols
- **Taking Without Lifestyle Changes**: 5-Amino-1MQ is a support tool, not a standalone solution. Without dietary management and regular exercise, meaningful body composition changes are unlikely
