# 5-Amino-1MQ

## Overview

5-Amino-1MQ (5-amino-1-methylquinolinium) is a small molecule (not technically a peptide, but commonly grouped with peptides in the metabolic/weight loss category) that inhibits the enzyme NNMT (nicotinamide N-methyltransferase). NNMT is overexpressed in fat tissue and is linked to obesity and metabolic dysfunction. By inhibiting NNMT, 5-Amino-1MQ may increase NAD+ levels, enhance fat cell metabolism, and reduce fat accumulation. Orally bioavailable — no injection needed.
