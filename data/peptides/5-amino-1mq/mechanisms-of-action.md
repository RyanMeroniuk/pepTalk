# 5-Amino-1MQ

## Mechanisms of Action

- **NNMT Inhibition**: Blocks nicotinamide N-methyltransferase enzyme in fat tissue
- **NAD+ Increase**: NNMT normally consumes NAD+ precursors; inhibiting it preserves NAD+ levels
- **Fat Cell Metabolism**: Increases energy expenditure in adipocytes
- **Lipogenesis Reduction**: Reduces new fat cell formation and fat accumulation
- **SAM Cycle**: Affects S-adenosylmethionine (SAM) metabolism, influencing epigenetic processes
- **Oral Bioavailability**: Taken as a capsule — no injection required
