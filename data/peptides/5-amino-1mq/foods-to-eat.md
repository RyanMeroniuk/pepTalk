# 5-Amino-1MQ

## Foods to Eat

- **Lean Proteins**: Chicken breast, turkey, salmon, cod, tilapia, eggs, egg whites, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, tofu, tempeh, lentils — protein supports satiety and lean mass while fat metabolism is enhanced
- **NAD+-Supporting Foods**: Foods rich in niacin (vitamin B3) — chicken breast, tuna, turkey, mushrooms, green peas, avocado, peanuts. Complements 5-Amino-1MQ's NAD+ preservation mechanism
- **Metabolism-Boosting Foods**: Green tea (EGCG supports fat oxidation), cayenne pepper (capsaicin boosts thermogenesis), ginger, turmeric, apple cider vinegar, cinnamon
- **Healthy Fats**: Avocado, extra virgin olive oil, almonds, walnuts, chia seeds, flaxseed, fatty fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel) — support cellular membrane function and reduce inflammation
- **Complex Carbohydrates**: Sweet potatoes, quinoa, oats, brown rice, lentils, chickpeas, black beans — provide steady energy for workouts without insulin spikes
- **Fibrous Vegetables**: Broccoli, spinach, kale, Brussels sprouts, cauliflower, bell peppers, asparagus, zucchini — nutrient-dense, filling, and low-calorie
- **Low-Sugar Fruits**: Berries (blueberries, strawberries, raspberries), grapefruit, green apples, kiwi — antioxidants without excessive sugar
- **Anti-Inflammatory Foods**: Fatty fish, turmeric, ginger, berries, leafy greens, extra virgin olive oil, walnuts
