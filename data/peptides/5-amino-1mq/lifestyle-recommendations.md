# 5-Amino-1MQ

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Caloric Management is Essential**: 5-Amino-1MQ enhances fat cell metabolism but will not override a caloric surplus. Maintain a moderate caloric deficit (300-500 calories below maintenance) for meaningful fat loss results
- **Exercise Amplifies Results**: Combine resistance training (3-4x/week) with cardio (3-5x/week). 5-Amino-1MQ increases energy expenditure in fat cells — exercise increases whole-body energy expenditure. Together, they create a powerful fat-loss environment
- **Resistance Training for Body Recomposition**: Compound lifts (squats, deadlifts, presses, rows) build muscle while fat metabolism is enhanced. Since 5-Amino-1MQ does not affect hormones or muscle, resistance training is the primary driver of lean mass
- **Daily Step Count**: Aim for 8,000-10,000 steps per day. Consistent low-level activity supports sustained fat metabolism throughout the day
- **Sleep 7-9 Hours**: Sleep deprivation impairs NAD+ metabolism (the same pathway 5-Amino-1MQ supports), raises cortisol, promotes fat storage, and increases hunger. Sleep is foundational to metabolic health
- **Stress Management**: Chronic stress elevates cortisol, which promotes visceral fat accumulation and impairs fat metabolism — counteracting the fat-burning support 5-Amino-1MQ provides. Practice meditation, deep breathing, yoga, or regular walks in nature
- **Morning Dosing**: Take 5-Amino-1MQ in the morning to align with the body's natural circadian metabolic rhythm when fat oxidation is most active
- **NAD+ Lifestyle Support**: Since 5-Amino-1MQ works by preserving NAD+ levels, support this pathway with regular exercise, adequate sleep, time-restricted eating, and avoiding excessive alcohol — all of which naturally support NAD+ metabolism
- **Track Body Composition**: Use waist measurements, body fat calipers, bioimpedance scales, or progress photos — not just scale weight. Fat-specific changes may not show dramatically on the scale
- **Consistency Over Intensity**: 5-Amino-1MQ works gradually by shifting fat cell metabolism. Expect changes over weeks, not days. Consistent daily habits (diet, exercise, sleep) matter more than extreme short-term efforts
