# 5-Amino-1MQ

## Foods to Avoid

- **Refined Sugars**: Candy, pastries, ice cream, sugary cereals, soda, fruit juice. Insulin spikes from sugar promote fat storage and directly counteract the fat-burning mechanism 5-Amino-1MQ supports
- **Refined Carbohydrates**: White bread, white pasta, white rice, bagels, crackers. Cause rapid glucose and insulin spikes that inhibit fat oxidation
- **Processed and Ultra-Processed Foods**: Fast food, chips, packaged snacks, frozen dinners, deli meats, hot dogs. Contain inflammatory additives and provide low nutritional value
- **Excessive Alcohol**: Alcohol depletes NAD+ — the exact molecule 5-Amino-1MQ works to preserve. Even moderate regular drinking significantly undermines the metabolic benefits. Alcohol also adds empty calories and impairs fat metabolism
- **Trans Fats and Deep-Fried Foods**: Margarine, vegetable shortening, fried foods. Promote inflammation and impair cellular energy metabolism
- **Sugary Beverages**: Soda, fruit juice, sweetened coffee drinks, energy drinks. Liquid calories spike insulin with no satiety benefit
- **High-Fructose Corn Syrup**: Found in many processed foods. Promotes liver fat accumulation and metabolic dysfunction
