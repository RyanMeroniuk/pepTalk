# Selank

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Consistent Sleep Schedule (7-9 hours)**: Selank's anxiolytic effects depend on balanced GABA signaling, which is restored during deep sleep. Poor sleep increases baseline anxiety and blunts Selank's calming effects. Maintain the same sleep and wake times daily
- **Aerobic Exercise (30-45 min, 4-5x/week)**: Moderate cardio increases serotonin and GABA naturally, amplifying Selank's anxiolytic mechanism. Running, swimming, or cycling also boost BDNF, supporting the cognitive enhancement side of Selank
- **Yoga & Breathwork**: Practices like box breathing (4-4-4-4), alternate nostril breathing, or yin yoga directly activate the parasympathetic nervous system and enhance GABAergic tone — directly synergistic with Selank's GABA-A modulation
- **Meditation & Mindfulness (10-20 min daily)**: Regular meditation reduces amygdala reactivity (the brain's fear center) and increases prefrontal cortex activity, working in concert with Selank's anxiety-reducing mechanism
- **Reduce Stimulant Intake**: While Selank is often paired with Semax for calm focus, adding excessive caffeine or other stimulants on top can override the anxiolytic benefits. Keep caffeine moderate (1-2 cups coffee max)
- **Active Learning & Cognitive Challenge**: Selank enhances learning ability, particularly in those with initially poor learning performance. Leverage this by studying, reading, or practicing skills during active cycles
- **Nature Exposure (30+ min daily)**: Time in natural environments reduces cortisol and activates the parasympathetic nervous system, complementing Selank's stress resilience effects
- **Social Connection**: Chronic isolation elevates stress hormones and anxiety. Meaningful social interaction supports the serotonergic pathways Selank modulates
- **Limit Alcohol**: While Selank protects against ethanol-induced memory impairment in studies, alcohol disrupts GABAergic balance and worsens baseline anxiety during withdrawal — counteracting Selank's therapeutic goals
- **Morning Sunlight (10-15 min)**: Light exposure regulates serotonin production, which Selank modulates. Optimizing natural serotonin rhythms amplifies Selank's mood-stabilizing effects
- **Journaling or Cognitive Behavioral Practices**: Pairing Selank's neurochemical anxiety reduction with cognitive restructuring techniques creates a dual approach — addressing anxiety at both the biological and psychological levels
