# Selank

## Things to Avoid

- **Chronic Unmanaged Stress**: Selank modulates the stress response, but it cannot overcome a lifestyle of constant high stress. Without stress management practices, cortisol will continue to suppress GABAergic function and promote anxiety
- **Sleep Deprivation**: GABA receptor sensitivity is restored during sleep. Chronic sleep loss downregulates GABA-A receptors — the exact receptors Selank acts on — reducing its effectiveness
- **Excessive Stimulant Use**: Combining Selank with high-dose caffeine, pre-workouts, or stimulant medications overwhelms its calming mechanism. The anxiolytic benefits require a reasonably calm neurochemical baseline to work from
- **Social Isolation**: Loneliness is a chronic stressor that elevates cortisol and reduces serotonin. Selank supports stress resilience, but isolation creates a biochemical headwind it must work against
- **Doom-Scrolling & Negative Media Overconsumption**: Constant exposure to alarming content activates the amygdala and keeps cortisol elevated. Selank calms this pathway, but persistent triggering reduces its net impact
- **Combining with Other GABAergic Substances Without Supervision**: Alcohol, benzodiazepines, phenibut, or GHB combined with Selank can cause excessive GABA-A activation, leading to oversedation. Always consult a healthcare provider before combining
- **Ignoring Gut Health**: The gut produces the majority of the body's serotonin. Gut dysbiosis from poor diet, antibiotics, or chronic stress directly limits Selank's serotonergic and immune-modulatory effects
- **Irregular Dosing Schedules**: Selank's nootropic effects build over 4-6 weeks of consistent use. Sporadic dosing prevents the neuroadaptive changes needed for robust cognitive and anxiolytic benefits
