# Selank

## Foods to Eat

- **Wild-Caught Fatty Fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel)**: EPA/DHA omega-3s reduce neuroinflammation and support serotonin receptor sensitivity — amplifying Selank's serotonergic modulation
- **Fermented Foods (kimchi, sauerkraut, yogurt, kefir)**: 90% of serotonin is produced in the gut. A healthy microbiome directly supports the serotonergic pathways Selank modulates. The gut-brain axis is critical for anxiety management
- **Turkey & Poultry**: Rich in tryptophan, the amino acid precursor to serotonin. Adequate tryptophan ensures Selank has sufficient substrate to work with
- **Eggs (whole, pastured)**: Provide choline for acetylcholine production and complete amino acid profile for neurotransmitter synthesis
- **Bananas**: Contain tryptophan and vitamin B6, both required for serotonin synthesis
- **Dark Chocolate (85%+ cacao)**: Contains tryptophan, magnesium, and flavonoids that support calm mood and cerebral blood flow
- **Leafy Greens (spinach, kale, Swiss chard)**: Rich in magnesium (nature's relaxation mineral) and folate needed for neurotransmitter methylation
- **Nuts & Seeds (pumpkin seeds, almonds, cashews)**: Excellent sources of magnesium, zinc, and tryptophan — all essential for GABA and serotonin production
- **Avocado**: B vitamins and healthy fats support neurotransmitter synthesis and brain cell membrane integrity
- **Chamomile & Passionflower Tea**: Mild GABAergic herbs that complement Selank's GABA-A modulation without overpowering it
- **Bone Broth**: Rich in glycine, which has calming properties and supports gut lining integrity for optimal gut-brain axis function
