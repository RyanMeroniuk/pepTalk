# Selank

## Mechanisms of Action

- **GABA-A Modulation**: Increases neuronal inhibition similar to benzodiazepines, producing anxiolytic effects
- **Neurotransmitter Modulation**: Influences brain levels of noradrenaline, serotonin, and dopamine — supporting vigilance, attention, learning, memory, and cognitive motivation
- **Immunomodulation**: Affects Bcl6 gene expression in immune development; increases anti-inflammatory cytokines
- **Enkephalinase Inhibition**: May stabilize endogenous enkephalins for mood and pain modulation
- **No Sedation**: Avoids the hypnosedation and muscle relaxation of benzodiazepines
- **No Dependence**: Does not produce tolerance, withdrawal, or addiction
