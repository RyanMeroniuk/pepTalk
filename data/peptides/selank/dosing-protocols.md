# Selank

## Dosing Protocols

- **Injectable**:
  - Standard: 0.5mg (adjustable by age/weight)
  - Frequency: 5 days weekly
  - Cycle: 1 month on, 1 month off (up to 6 cycles per year)
  - Timing: Any time, morning preferred for nootropic benefits
- **Intranasal Spray**:
  - Starting dose: 300 mcg
  - Frequency: 1-2x daily
  - No reconstitution required (pre-mixed sprays available)
- **Results Timeline**: Mental clarity 1-2 weeks; robust nootropic effects 4-6 weeks; long-term stabilization 8-12 weeks
