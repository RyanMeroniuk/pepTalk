# Selank

## Benefits

- **Anxiety Reduction**: Effective long-term anxiolytic properties demonstrated; reduced anxiety-phobic states sustained over weeks. Anti-anxiety without sedation or cognitive impairment
- **Cognitive Enhancement**: Enhanced learning (especially in those with initially poor learning ability), memory protection, improved attention and motivation
- **Memory Protection**: Shields against ethanol-induced memory impairment; positive implications for age-related memory disturbances
- **Mood Stabilization**: Emotional regulation through serotonergic and GABAergic pathways
- **Immune Support**: Positive immunomodulatory effects demonstrated in human trials with anxiety-asthenic disorder patients
- **Stress Resilience**: Can enhance benzodiazepine effects under chronic stress conditions
- **No Cognitive Side Effects**: Unlike benzodiazepines, does not cause amnesia or cognitive dulling
