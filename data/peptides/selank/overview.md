# Selank

## Overview

Selank is a synthetic heptapeptide developed in Russia at the Institute of Molecular Genetics. It is an analog of the immunomodulatory peptide tuftsin with an added Pro-Gly-Pro sequence for stability. Primarily used for its anxiolytic (anti-anxiety) and nootropic (cognitive-enhancing) properties. Functions through GABAergic mechanisms similar to benzodiazepines but without their characteristic side effects (no sedation, amnesia, withdrawal, or dependence). Often paired with Semax for a complementary focus + calm stack.
