# Selank

## Foods to Avoid

- **Excess Caffeine (>200mg/day)**: Caffeine blocks adenosine receptors and stimulates cortisol release, directly opposing Selank's anxiolytic mechanism. One cup of coffee is fine; four cups will undermine the calming effects
- **Refined Sugar & High-Glycemic Foods**: Blood sugar spikes trigger cortisol and adrenaline release during the subsequent crash, creating biochemical anxiety that Selank must work harder to counteract
- **Alcohol**: Despite short-term anxiolytic effects, alcohol disrupts GABA receptor sensitivity and causes rebound anxiety 12-48 hours later. This directly undermines Selank's long-term GABAergic rebalancing
- **Processed Seed Oils (soybean, canola, corn oil)**: Promote systemic inflammation including neuroinflammation, which worsens anxiety and impairs the immune modulation Selank provides
- **Ultra-Processed Foods**: Damage gut microbiome diversity, reducing gut serotonin production and weakening the gut-brain axis
- **Excessive Gluten (in sensitive individuals)**: Can trigger gut inflammation and zonulin release in sensitive people, disrupting the gut-brain axis and increasing anxiety
- **Artificial Sweeteners**: May disrupt gut bacteria and neurotransmitter signaling, potentially worsening anxiety symptoms
- **High-Histamine Foods (in excess)**: Aged cheeses, cured meats, and fermented alcohol can increase histamine, which promotes anxiety and agitation in some individuals
