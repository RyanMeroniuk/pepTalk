# Selank

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **GABAergic drugs (phenibut, gabapentin, pregabalin)**: Selank modulates GABA-A receptors; combining with other GABAergic agents may cause excessive CNS depression, oversedation, or respiratory depression
- **Benzodiazepines (diazepam, alprazolam, clonazepam)**: Overlapping GABA-A mechanism — Selank may potentiate benzodiazepine effects. Use only under medical supervision; dose adjustments may be required
- **SSRIs/SNRIs (sertraline, fluoxetine, venlafaxine)**: Selank influences serotonin levels; combining with serotonergic medications may increase serotonin activity. Monitor for serotonin syndrome symptoms (agitation, tremor, hyperthermia)
- **Alcohol**: Both Selank and alcohol act on GABA-A receptors. Combining may cause excessive sedation, impaired coordination, and unpredictable CNS depression
- **MAO inhibitors**: Selank modulates monoamine neurotransmitters; combining with MAOIs could lead to dangerous neurotransmitter accumulation
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy and breastfeeding — insufficient safety data for fetal/neonatal exposure
- **Contraindicated**: Active cancer — immunomodulatory effects may interfere with cancer treatment protocols
