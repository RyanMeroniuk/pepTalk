# Selank

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Best Stack**: Semax — complementary focus (Semax) + calm (Selank)
- **Stack**: DSIP — anxiety reduction + sleep optimization
- **Enhanced by**: Ashwagandha for cortisol management
- **Enhanced by**: Magnesium for GABA support
- **Enhanced by**: L-theanine for additional calm focus
- **Can enhance**: Benzodiazepine effects (use with medical supervision)
- **Caution**: Other GABAergic drugs — may amplify effects
- **Contraindicated**: Pregnancy, breastfeeding, active cancer
