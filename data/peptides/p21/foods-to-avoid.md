# P21 (P021)

## Foods to Avoid

- **Excess Refined Sugar**: Chronic high intake suppresses BDNF and impairs hippocampal neurogenesis, opposing P21's proposed mechanism.
- **Alcohol**: Directly suppresses hippocampal neurogenesis and BDNF — among the most counterproductive substances for a neurogenesis-focused protocol.
- **Processed Seed Oils (soybean, canola, corn)**: High omega-6 load promotes neuroinflammation and disrupts the omega-3/omega-6 balance.
- **Ultra-Processed Foods**: Disrupt the gut-brain axis and impair nutrient availability for neurotransmitter synthesis.
- **Trans Fats & Fried Foods**: Promote neuroinflammation that impairs synaptic plasticity.
- **Artificial Sweeteners (aspartame, sucralose)**: May disrupt gut microbiome and neural signaling.
- **High-Sodium Processed Foods**: Can impair cerebrovascular function and blood-brain barrier integrity.
