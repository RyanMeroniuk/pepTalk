# P21 (P021)

## Side Effects

- **Unknown Human Safety Profile**: No human safety data exists; the side-effect profile in people is entirely uncharacterized.
- **Theoretical Over-Proliferation Risk**: Potent stimulation of cell growth and neurogenesis raises a theoretical concern about uncontrolled proliferation, unstudied in humans.
- **Possible CNS Effects**: As a neuroactive compound, headache, mood changes, or altered cognition are plausible but unverified.
- **Injection-Site Reactions**: Redness, irritation, or infection risk at injection sites for the injectable route.
- **Contamination / Purity Risk**: Research-chemical sourcing means impurities, endotoxins, or mislabeled content are realistic hazards.
- **Animal Tolerability Is Not Human Safety**: Rodent studies reported good tolerability, but this does not establish human safety.
- **No Long-Term Data**: Chronic-use effects and any cancer-relevant signals are completely unknown.
