# P21 (P021)

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Prioritize Sleep (7-9 hours)**: New neurons and synapses generated via neurogenesis require sleep to integrate and consolidate into functional memory circuits. Keep a consistent schedule.
- **Aerobic Exercise (30-45 min, 4-5x/week)**: Cardio is itself one of the strongest natural drivers of hippocampal neurogenesis and BDNF, directly reinforcing P21's proposed mechanism.
- **Active Learning & Cognitive Challenge**: Newly generated neurons survive preferentially when engaged by learning; deliberately study or practice skills during use.
- **Stress Management**: Chronic cortisol elevation suppresses hippocampal neurogenesis — the exact process P21 aims to enhance. Use meditation, breathwork, or therapy.
- **Morning Sunlight**: Supports circadian rhythm and mood systems that underpin cognition.
- **Limit Alcohol**: Alcohol suppresses hippocampal neurogenesis and BDNF, directly opposing the mechanism.
- **Treat as Experimental**: With no human data, the strongest evidence-based path to neurogenesis is exercise, sleep, and learning rather than an untested peptide.
