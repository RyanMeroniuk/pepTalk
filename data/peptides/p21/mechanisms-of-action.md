# P21 (P021)

## Mechanisms of Action

- **CNTF Mimetic**: Derived from a peptidergic active region of ciliary neurotrophic factor, mimicking its neurotrophic signaling without the full protein's drawbacks (preclinical).
- **Hippocampal Neurogenesis**: Potently stimulates the birth of new neurons in the hippocampal dentate gyrus in rodent models, a key driver of memory formation.
- **BDNF Upregulation**: Increases brain-derived neurotrophic factor expression, supporting synaptic plasticity and neuronal survival in animal studies.
- **BBB Penetrant**: Adamantylation and small size allow it to cross the blood-brain barrier after oral or peripheral dosing in animals, a notable advantage over many neurotrophic proteins.
- **Synaptic Protein Restoration**: Restores synaptophysin and other synaptic markers in rodent models of neurodegeneration.
- **Tau / Amyloid Pathology Reduction (preclinical)**: Reduced abnormal tau phosphorylation and improved cognition in transgenic Alzheimer's mouse models.
- **Reported Edge Over Cerebrolysin**: In some animal comparisons, produced greater memory and neurogenesis improvements than cerebrolysin — an animal finding, not a human one.
- **Unknown Human Pharmacology**: Half-life, oral bioavailability, and dosing in humans are uncharacterized.
