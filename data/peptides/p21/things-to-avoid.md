# P21 (P021)

## Things to Avoid

- **Treating It as Proven**: All efficacy is from animal studies — avoid assuming the memory and neurogenesis benefits translate to humans.
- **Ignoring Proliferation Concerns**: Potent growth/neurogenesis stimulation carries a theoretical proliferation risk; avoid use with any active or suspected cancer.
- **Unverified Sourcing**: Research-chemical purity varies widely; contaminated or mislabeled product is a real hazard.
- **Guessing at Doses**: With no human pharmacokinetics, self-selected doses are guesswork with unknown risk.
- **Self-Treating Cognitive Decline**: Using an untested peptide in place of evidence-based medical evaluation for memory problems is dangerous; seek professional care.
- **Stacking Multiple Experimental Peptides**: Layering P21 with other unstudied neurotrophic compounds compounds unknown risk and obscures adverse-event causes.
- **Chronic Use Without Monitoring**: Long-term effects are unknown; open-ended use is not defensible.
