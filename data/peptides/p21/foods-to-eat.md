## Foods to Eat

- **Wild-Caught Fatty Fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel)**: EPA/DHA omega-3s support BDNF and neuronal membrane health, reinforcing the neurotrophic pathway.
- **Blueberries & Dark Berries**: Anthocyanins cross the blood-brain barrier and have been shown to support hippocampal neurogenesis and memory.
- **Eggs (whole, pastured)**: Choline supports acetylcholine synthesis and overall neuronal health.
- **Leafy Greens (spinach, kale)**: Folate and nitrates support methylation and cerebral blood flow.
- **Turmeric/Curcumin**: Anti-neuroinflammatory and shown to independently support neurogenesis and BDNF in animal models.
- **Green Tea**: EGCG provides neuroprotective antioxidant effects; L-theanine supports calm focus.
- **Walnuts**: ALA omega-3s and polyphenols supporting cerebrovascular and neuronal health.
- **Dark Chocolate (85%+ cacao)**: Flavonoids improve cerebral blood flow and support BDNF expression.
