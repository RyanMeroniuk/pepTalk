# P21 (P021)

## Overview

P21 (also written P021) is a synthetic tetrapeptide derived from an active region of ciliary neurotrophic factor (CNTF), adamantylated and modified to be small, stable, and blood-brain-barrier penetrant after oral or peripheral administration. Its defining property in animal research is potent stimulation of hippocampal neurogenesis alongside increased BDNF expression, leading to improvements in memory and synaptic markers in rodent models of Alzheimer's disease and cognitive decline. In some head-to-head animal studies it has been reported to outperform cerebrolysin on memory and neurogenesis endpoints. All evidence is preclinical — there are no human trials and no human safety or efficacy data. P21 is available only as a research chemical and is not approved for human use anywhere.
