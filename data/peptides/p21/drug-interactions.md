# P21 (P021)

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Contraindicated with Active Cancer**: A potent stimulator of cell proliferation and neurogenesis carries a theoretical risk of promoting tumor growth; avoid in anyone with active or suspected malignancy.
- **Corticosteroids**: Caution — corticosteroids suppress neurogenesis and BDNF, working against P21's mechanism; combining may blunt any effect.
- **CNS Medications (antidepressants, antipsychotics)**: Caution — overlapping neuroactive effects with unpredictable additive outcomes; monitor.
- **Anticoagulants / Blood Thinners**: Caution — for injectable use, separate injection timing and watch injection sites for bruising or bleeding.
- **Contraindicated in Pregnancy**: No reproductive safety data; avoid entirely.
- **Contraindicated in Breastfeeding**: Unknown transfer and infant effects; avoid.
- **Timing Note**: If used alongside other CNS-active agents, separating doses by several hours is a minimal precaution, though no interaction kinetics are established.
