# P21 (P021)

## Benefits

- **Memory Enhancement (animal-only)**: Improved learning and memory in rodent models of aging and Alzheimer's disease; no human cognitive data exists.
- **Neurogenesis Promotion**: Among the more potent neurogenesis-stimulating peptides in preclinical research, increasing new hippocampal neurons.
- **Neuroprotection (preclinical)**: Reduced tau pathology and synaptic loss in transgenic mouse models.
- **Oral Bioavailability (animal)**: Demonstrated activity after oral dosing in animals, unusual for a neurotrophic peptide and a key research interest.
- **Reported Superiority to Cerebrolysin (animal)**: Outperformed cerebrolysin on memory/neurogenesis endpoints in some rodent comparisons — strictly an animal finding.
- **No Hormonal Effects**: Acts via neurotrophic signaling, not the endocrine axis.
- **IMPORTANT — No Human Data**: All benefits derive from animal studies. There are no clinical trials and no proven human benefit.
