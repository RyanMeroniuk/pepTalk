# Methylene Blue

## Benefits

- **Methemoglobinemia Antidote**: Established clinical use to treat acquired methemoglobinemia by restoring functional hemoglobin.
- **Mitochondrial / Cognitive Support**: Low-dose use is studied and anecdotally reported to support energy, focus, and mental clarity via enhanced mitochondrial respiration; human nootropic evidence remains limited.
- **Antioxidant Effects (Low Dose)**: May reduce oxidative stress at low concentrations, a rationale behind its longevity-stack popularity.
- **Neuroprotection (Research)**: Investigated in preclinical and early clinical work for neurodegenerative and mood conditions, but not established for these uses.
- **Hormetic, Dose-Dependent**: Benefits are tied to low doses; higher doses reverse to pro-oxidant and toxic effects, so the therapeutic window matters.
- **Note**: Many touted longevity benefits rest on preclinical or small studies; framing should remain cautious and research-oriented.
