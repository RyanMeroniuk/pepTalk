# Methylene Blue

## Overview

Methylene blue is a synthetic phenothiazine dye, not a peptide, included here for completeness because it appears in longevity and nootropic stacks. At low doses it acts as a mitochondrial electron cycler, serving as an alternative electron carrier in the electron transport chain to support cellular ATP production and reduce oxidative stress. It has a long medical history as the standard antidote for acquired methemoglobinemia and is also studied for cognitive and metabolic effects. Critically, methylene blue is a potent monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI), so combining it with serotonergic drugs such as SSRIs, SNRIs, or other MAOIs can cause life-threatening serotonin syndrome. Other important cautions include G6PD deficiency (risk of hemolysis) and harmless but noticeable blue-green discoloration of urine. Low-dose nootropic use is popular but should account for these serious interaction risks.
