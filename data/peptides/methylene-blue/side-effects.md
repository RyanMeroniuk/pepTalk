# Methylene Blue

## Side Effects

- **Blue-Green Urine and Stool**: Harmless but universal discoloration; may also tint the mouth and skin.
- **Serotonin Syndrome (Serious)**: When combined with serotonergic drugs, can cause agitation, hyperthermia, rapid heart rate, tremor, and potentially fatal serotonin syndrome — its most dangerous risk.
- **GI Upset**: Nausea, abdominal discomfort, and diarrhea, especially at higher doses.
- **Headache and Dizziness**: Reported with use.
- **Hemolysis in G6PD Deficiency**: Can trigger dangerous breakdown of red blood cells in people with glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase (G6PD) deficiency — contraindicated in that population.
- **Pro-Oxidant Toxicity at High Dose**: High doses reverse the antioxidant effect, causing oxidative damage and potential methemoglobinemia (paradoxically, the condition it treats at low dose).
- **Photosensitivity**: May increase sensitivity to bright light.
