# Methylene Blue

## Things to Avoid

- **Combining With Serotonergic Drugs (Critical)**: Never combine with SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, triptans, or other serotonergic medications — methylene blue is an MAOI and this can cause fatal serotonin syndrome.
- **Use With G6PD Deficiency**: Avoid entirely; risk of dangerous hemolysis.
- **High Doses**: Higher doses become pro-oxidant and toxic and can paradoxically cause methemoglobinemia; respect the low-dose hormetic window.
- **Non-Pharmaceutical Grade Dye**: Never ingest aquarium or industrial methylene blue; it contains contaminants and heavy metals.
- **Use During Pregnancy/Breastfeeding**: Avoid; associated with fetal harm and insufficient safety data.
- **Self-Treating Serious Conditions**: Do not use as a substitute for medical care; framing is educational and research-oriented, not medical advice.
- **Ignoring Light Sensitivity**: Be cautious with intense light/sun exposure due to potential photosensitivity.
