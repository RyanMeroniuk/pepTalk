# Methylene Blue

## Foods to Avoid

- **Tyramine-Rich Aged Foods**: Because methylene blue is an MAOI, large amounts of aged cheeses, cured/fermented meats, and aged/fermented products could theoretically contribute to blood-pressure spikes; moderation is prudent, especially at higher doses.
- **Serotonergic Supplement-Foods**: Avoid concentrated 5-HTP or tryptophan supplements; combined with its MAOI activity, these raise serotonin-syndrome risk.
- **Excess Alcohol**: Adds oxidative and metabolic stress and can compound dizziness or GI upset.
- **Refined Sugar & Ultra-Processed Foods**: Increase oxidative stress and work against the mitochondrial-support rationale.
- **Heavily Fermented Beverages in Large Amounts**: Such as large quantities of tap/draft beer or some wines, which carry tyramine — relevant given MAOI activity.
- **Note**: Dietary tyramine concern is greatest at higher methylene blue exposure; low-dose nootropic users should still exercise sensible moderation.
