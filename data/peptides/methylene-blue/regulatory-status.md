# Methylene Blue

## Regulatory Status

- **FDA-approved as a drug** for the treatment of acquired methemoglobinemia (injectable, prescription).
- **Not approved** for nootropic, cognitive-enhancement, or longevity uses — these are off-label and unstudied for approval.
- Sold widely as a non-prescription "research chemical" or supplement, with quality varying enormously by source.
- Only USP/pharmaceutical-grade material is appropriate for human use; aquarium and industrial dye are not.
- It is a phenothiazine dye and MAOI, not a peptide; its serious drug interactions are well documented in clinical literature.
- Self-directed use should be considered experimental and undertaken cautiously with awareness of MAOI and G6PD risks.
