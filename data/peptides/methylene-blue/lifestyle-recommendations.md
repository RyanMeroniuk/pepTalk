# Methylene Blue

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Review All Medications First (Critical)**: Before any use, confirm you are not taking SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, or other serotonergic drugs — methylene blue is an MAOI and the combination can cause life-threatening serotonin syndrome. Consult a clinician.
- **Screen for G6PD Deficiency**: People with G6PD deficiency can suffer dangerous hemolysis; know your status before use.
- **Use Pharmaceutical-Grade Only**: Never use aquarium or industrial dye; only USP-grade material is appropriate for human use.
- **Start Low**: Effects are hormetic and dose-dependent; low doses support mitochondria while higher doses become toxic. Begin at the low end.
- **Expect Discoloration**: Urine and possibly the mouth will turn blue-green; this is harmless and expected.
- **Avoid Excess Light Exposure**: Methylene blue can increase photosensitivity; use sun protection.
- **Pair With Mitochondrial Health Habits**: Regular exercise, quality sleep, and a nutrient-dense diet support the same energy pathways methylene blue targets.
- **It Is Not a Peptide**: Included for stack completeness; treat it as a distinct pharmacologic compound with its own interaction profile.
