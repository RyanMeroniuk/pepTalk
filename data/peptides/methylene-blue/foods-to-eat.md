# Methylene Blue

## Foods to Eat

- **Mitochondria-Supporting Whole Foods**: Leafy greens, colorful vegetables, and quality protein supply cofactors for cellular energy metabolism that complement methylene blue's electron-transport support.
- **Antioxidant-Rich Produce**: Berries and vegetables provide polyphenols that support redox balance alongside low-dose methylene blue.
- **CoQ10 and B-Vitamin Sources**: Organ meats, fatty fish, eggs, and whole grains supply nutrients central to mitochondrial function.
- **Adequate Hydration**: Drink water throughout the day (urine will appear blue-green, which is normal).
- **Iron- and Folate-Rich Foods**: Support healthy red blood cells, relevant given methylene blue's effects on hemoglobin.
- **Note**: Avoid tyramine-heavy aged foods only matters at higher MAOI exposure; still, moderation with aged cheeses and cured meats is prudent given its MAOI activity.
