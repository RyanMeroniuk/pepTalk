# Methylene Blue

## Stacking Protocols

- **Screen for Serotonergic Drugs First**: No stack is safe if the user takes SSRIs, SNRIs, or MAOIs — methylene blue's MAOI activity makes that combination potentially fatal. Clear this before any stack.
- **Mitochondrial Longevity Stack**: Methylene-blue (low dose) + coq10 (100-200 mg) — pairs an electron-transport cycler with a core mitochondrial cofactor for cellular energy support.
- **Cellular Energy Stack**: Methylene-blue (low dose) + pqq (10-20 mg) — combines mitochondrial respiration support with mitochondrial-biogenesis signaling.
- **NAD+ Support Stack**: Methylene-blue (low dose) + nmn (250-500 mg) — overlapping mitochondrial energy-pathway rationale for longevity protocols.
- **Antioxidant Pairing**: Methylene-blue (low dose) + alpha-lipoic-acid — complementary redox support; keep antioxidant doses moderate to preserve hormetic signaling.
- **Note**: Methylene blue is not a peptide and should not be combined with serotonergic peptides or supplements.
