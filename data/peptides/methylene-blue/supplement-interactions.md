# Methylene Blue

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Critical**: Avoid serotonergic supplements such as 5-HTP, L-tryptophan, and St. John's Wort — methylene blue is an MAOI and combining them risks serotonin syndrome.
- **Synergistic**: CoQ10 and pqq — mitochondrial cofactors that may complement methylene blue's electron-transport support.
- **Synergistic**: Nmn — NAD+ precursor that supports the same mitochondrial energy pathways (general longevity-stack rationale).
- **Enhanced by**: Alpha-lipoic-acid and vitamin-c for complementary antioxidant/redox support at low methylene blue doses.
- **Caution**: High-dose antioxidants taken simultaneously could blunt any hormetic redox signaling; space them or keep doses moderate.
- **Caution**: Any supplement marketed to "boost serotonin" or "mood" should be avoided due to MAOI interaction risk.
