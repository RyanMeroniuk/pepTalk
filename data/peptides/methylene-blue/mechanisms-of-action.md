# Methylene Blue

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Alternative Electron Carrier**: At low doses, cycles between oxidized and reduced forms to shuttle electrons in the mitochondrial electron transport chain, bypassing impaired complexes and supporting ATP synthesis.
- **Mitochondrial Support**: Enhances cellular respiration and oxygen consumption, the basis for proposed nootropic and metabolic effects.
- **Antioxidant at Low Dose**: Reduces reactive oxygen species and supports cellular redox balance (a hormetic, dose-dependent effect — high doses become pro-oxidant).
- **Methemoglobin Reduction**: Accelerates conversion of methemoglobin back to functional hemoglobin via the NADPH-dependent pathway, the basis for its antidote use.
- **Monoamine Oxidase Inhibition (MAOI)**: Potently inhibits MAO-A, raising synaptic serotonin and other monoamines — the mechanism behind its serious serotonergic drug interactions.
- **Nitric Oxide / Guanylate Cyclase Inhibition**: Inhibits nitric oxide synthase and guanylate cyclase, contributing to its vascular and clinical effects.
