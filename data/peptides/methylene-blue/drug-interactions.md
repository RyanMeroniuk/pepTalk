# Methylene Blue

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **Contraindicated: SSRIs (fluoxetine, sertraline, escitalopram, etc.)**: Methylene blue is a potent MAOI; combining with SSRIs can cause life-threatening serotonin syndrome. Do not combine.
- **Contraindicated: SNRIs (venlafaxine, duloxetine)**: Same serotonin-syndrome risk via additive serotonergic activity. Do not combine.
- **Contraindicated: MAOIs and other serotonergic agents (triptans, tramadol, lithium, St. John's Wort)**: Additive MAO inhibition and serotonergic load create severe serotonin-syndrome risk.
- **Contraindicated: G6PD deficiency** — risk of severe hemolysis.
- **Caution: Serotonergic clearance time** — serotonergic drugs and methylene blue should be separated by an extended washout (days to weeks depending on the drug), not merely hours apart; clinician guidance is essential.
- **Caution: Vasoactive and blood-pressure medications** — methylene blue affects nitric oxide/guanylate cyclase and can influence blood pressure; monitor.
- **Caution: Anticoagulants / blood thinners** — monitor, as combined effects are not well characterized.
- **Contraindicated: Pregnancy and breastfeeding** — associated with fetal harm and insufficient safety data.
