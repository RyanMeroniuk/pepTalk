# Methylene Blue

## Dosing Protocols

- **Pharmaceutical-Grade Only**: Use only USP/pharmaceutical-grade methylene blue; industrial or aquarium-grade dye contains contaminants and heavy metals and is unsafe.
- **Low-Dose Nootropic Range (reported)**: Anecdotal nootropic use cites roughly 0.5-4 mg/kg, but lower microdose ranges (a few mg total) are commonly described; evidence for cognitive dosing is limited.
- **Oral Administration**: Usually taken orally, often diluted in water; the solution and urine turn blue-green.
- **Clinical Antidote Dosing**: Methemoglobinemia treatment uses specific IV dosing administered by clinicians — distinct from self-directed nootropic use.
- **Hormetic Window**: Effects are dose-dependent — low doses support mitochondria while higher doses become pro-oxidant and toxic. More is not better.
- **Timing With Medications**: Because it is an MAOI, it must not be combined with serotonergic drugs regardless of timing; separation by hours is not sufficient and clearance can take days.
- **Note**: Self-directed dosing is unstandardized; medical oversight is advised, especially given drug-interaction risks.
