# Cerebrolysin

## Drug Interactions & Contraindications

- **MAOIs**: Contraindicated by labeling — concurrent use with monoamine oxidase inhibitors may cause additive effects; avoid or separate per prescribing guidance.
- **Antidepressants (SSRIs/SNRIs)**: Caution — overlapping neuroactive effects; monitor for agitation or overstimulation.
- **Corticosteroids**: Caution — corticosteroids suppress neurotrophic signaling and may blunt Cerebrolysin's intended effect.
- **Other IV Solutions**: Should not be mixed in the same infusion with certain solutions (e.g., balanced amino-acid solutions) per labeling; administer separately.
- **Antiepileptics**: Caution — use carefully in seizure-prone patients; contraindicated in status epilepticus.
- **Contraindicated in Pregnancy**: Insufficient safety data; avoid unless a clinician judges benefit to outweigh risk.
- **Contraindicated in Breastfeeding**: Insufficient data on transfer; avoid.
- **Contraindicated in Severe Renal Impairment** and **known hypersensitivity** per approved labeling.
