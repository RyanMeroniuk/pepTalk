# Cerebrolysin

## Things to Avoid

- **Unsupervised IV/IM Self-Administration**: The IV route carries infusion-reaction and anaphylaxis risk; using it without medical oversight is dangerous.
- **Gray-Market Sourcing**: Because it is animal-brain-derived, counterfeit or improperly processed product raises real contamination concerns; only use pharmaceutical-grade supply in approved markets.
- **Overstating the Evidence**: Human trial results are mixed and often modest — avoid treating it as a guaranteed cognitive enhancer.
- **Late-Day Dosing**: Its stimulating effects can disrupt sleep; avoid evening administration.
- **Use With Active Seizure Disorders**: Avoid in status epilepticus and use caution in epilepsy per labeling.
- **Use in Severe Renal Impairment**: Contraindicated per approved labels.
- **Combining With Other Unstudied Experimental Peptides**: Layering with other research neurotrophics adds unknown risk and obscures adverse-event causes.
- **Ignoring Allergy History**: Avoid with known hypersensitivity to the product or its components.
