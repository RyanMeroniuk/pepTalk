# Cerebrolysin

## Supplement Interactions & Recommendations

- **Supplement**: Omega-3 (EPA/DHA, 2-3g) — supports BDNF and neuronal membranes; synergistic with Cerebrolysin's neurotrophic activity.
- **Supplement**: Lion's Mane (500-1000mg) — promotes NGF; complementary to the NGF-like fragments in the mixture.
- **Supplement**: Alpha-GPC (300mg) — acetylcholine precursor supporting memory, often paired in nootropic contexts.
- **Supplement**: Magnesium L-Threonate (2g) — brain-targeted magnesium supporting synaptic plasticity.
- **Supplement**: B-Complex — supports methylation and neurotransmitter synthesis, enhancing the amino-acid component's utility.
- **Supplement**: Vitamin D3 — foundational neuronal support.
- **Caution**: Other strongly serotonergic or stimulant supplements (5-HTP, high-dose caffeine) — may add to agitation/overstimulation; use conservatively.
- **Note**: Formal human supplement-interaction studies are lacking; pairings are mechanism-based and should be discussed with a clinician.
