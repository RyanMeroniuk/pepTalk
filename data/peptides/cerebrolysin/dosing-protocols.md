# Cerebrolysin

## Dosing Protocols

- **Clinical Route**: Administered intravenously (often as a slow infusion) or intramuscularly in approved medical settings — not designed for subcutaneous self-use.
- **Typical Clinical Range**: Studies and approved labels use roughly 5-30 mL/day depending on indication and severity, given in courses.
- **Course-Based Dosing**: Commonly delivered in cycles (e.g., daily for 10-20 days), repeated periodically, rather than continuous daily use.
- **Stroke/TBI Protocols**: Higher daily volumes over a defined acute course under physician supervision.
- **Administration Setting**: IV dosing requires medical oversight due to infusion-reaction risk; this is a clinic-administered drug in its approved markets.
- **Storage**: Supplied as a sterile ampoule solution; store per label, protect from light.
- **Caution**: Self-administration outside medical supervision is strongly discouraged given the IV route, anaphylaxis risk, and animal-derived sourcing. Dosing should follow a prescribing clinician in a market where it is approved.
