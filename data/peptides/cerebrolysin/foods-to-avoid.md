# Cerebrolysin

## Foods to Avoid

- **Excess Refined Sugar**: Blood glucose swings and chronic intake reduce BDNF and impair the cognitive recovery Cerebrolysin supports.
- **Alcohol**: Neurotoxic and BDNF-suppressing; especially counterproductive during neurorehabilitation.
- **Processed Seed Oils (soybean, canola, corn)**: High omega-6 load promotes neuroinflammation.
- **Ultra-Processed Foods**: Disrupt the gut-brain axis and impair nutrient availability for recovery.
- **Trans Fats & Fried Foods**: Promote systemic and neural inflammation impairing plasticity.
- **Excessive Caffeine (>300mg/day)**: May compound Cerebrolysin-related agitation or insomnia.
- **High-Sodium Processed Foods**: Impair cerebrovascular function, relevant in stroke recovery contexts.
