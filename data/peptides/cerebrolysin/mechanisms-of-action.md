# Cerebrolysin

## Mechanisms of Action

- **Peptide Mixture, Not a Single Molecule**: Acts through a blend of low-MW peptides and amino acids derived from porcine brain — its effects are the sum of many fragments, not one defined compound.
- **Neurotrophic Mimicry**: Contains fragments with BDNF-, NGF-, and GDNF-like activity, supporting neuronal survival, growth, and differentiation.
- **Neuroplasticity Support**: Promotes synaptic plasticity and dendritic growth in preclinical models.
- **Neuroprotection**: Reduces excitotoxicity, oxidative stress, and apoptosis in models of ischemia and brain injury.
- **Amyloid Modulation (preclinical)**: Shown in animal/lab models to reduce amyloid burden and abnormal tau processing relevant to Alzheimer's.
- **Metabolic Support**: Proposed to improve neuronal glucose transport and mitochondrial function.
- **Anti-Inflammatory**: Modulates neuroinflammatory signaling after injury.
- **Mechanism Uncertainty**: Because the active mixture is incompletely characterized, the precise contributions of each component remain unclear even after decades of use.
