# Cerebrolysin

## Foods to Eat

- **Wild-Caught Fatty Fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel)**: EPA/DHA omega-3s support BDNF signaling and neuronal membranes, complementing the neurotrophic mechanism.
- **Blueberries & Dark Berries**: Anthocyanins reduce neuroinflammation and support memory consolidation during recovery.
- **Eggs (whole, pastured)**: Choline supports acetylcholine synthesis for memory and cognition.
- **Leafy Greens (spinach, kale)**: Folate and nitrates support methylation and cerebral blood flow.
- **Quality Protein (poultry, fish, legumes)**: Provides amino acids that complement the amino-acid component of the mixture and support neuronal repair.
- **Turmeric/Curcumin**: Anti-neuroinflammatory and BDNF-supportive.
- **Walnuts & Pumpkin Seeds**: ALA omega-3s, magnesium, and zinc for neuronal function.
- **Green Tea**: Neuroprotective antioxidants plus calm-focus L-theanine.
