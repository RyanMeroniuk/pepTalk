# Cerebrolysin

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- **Pair With Cognitive Rehabilitation**: In its strongest evidence (stroke/TBI), Cerebrolysin is used alongside structured rehab; neurotrophic support works best when paired with active cognitive and physical retraining.
- **Prioritize Sleep (7-9 hours)**: Neuroplasticity and recovery driven by neurotrophic signaling consolidate during sleep. Keep a consistent schedule.
- **Aerobic Exercise (as tolerated)**: Cardio independently raises BDNF and supports cerebrovascular health, reinforcing the neurotrophic mechanism.
- **Stress Management**: Chronic cortisol suppresses BDNF and neurogenesis; meditation and breathwork protect the pathways Cerebrolysin supports.
- **Dose Earlier in the Day**: Its stimulating effects can disrupt sleep if administered late; schedule courses for morning/midday.
- **Hydration**: Supports cerebrovascular function and reduces headache risk during courses.
- **Medical Supervision**: Because it is an IV/IM drug derived from animal tissue, use it within a supervised, approved-market clinical setting rather than as an at-home nootropic.
