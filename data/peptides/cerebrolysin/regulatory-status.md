# Cerebrolysin

## Regulatory Status

- **NOT FDA-approved** in the United States; cannot be legally marketed as a drug there.
- **Approved in many other countries**: Registered and prescribed in parts of Europe, Asia, Russia/CIS, and Latin America for stroke, dementia, and TBI.
- **Manufactured by Ever Pharma (Austria)** as the long-standing branded product.
- **Animal-derived biologic**: Purified from porcine brain protein, which underlies both its mechanism and its contamination-caution profile.
- **Prescription drug** in its approved markets — intended for clinician-supervised IV/IM use, not OTC.
- **Imported gray-market in the US**: Often obtained informally for off-label cognitive use, which is unregulated and carries quality/safety risk.
- **WADA**: Not a specifically prohibited substance, but athletes should verify current status before use.
