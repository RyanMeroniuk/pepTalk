# Cerebrolysin

## Benefits

- **Stroke Recovery (mixed human evidence)**: Studied for acute ischemic stroke recovery; some trials show modest functional improvement while others (including large pooled analyses) show limited or uncertain benefit.
- **Traumatic Brain Injury**: Used in some countries for TBI recovery with reported cognitive and functional gains, though evidence quality varies.
- **Vascular & Alzheimer's Dementia**: Some trials report modest cognitive improvement in dementia; results are inconsistent and effect sizes small.
- **Cognitive Support**: Anecdotally used off-label as a nootropic for memory and mental clarity, with limited controlled human data for healthy users.
- **Neuroprotection**: Preclinical and some clinical signals of reduced neuronal damage after injury.
- **Neurorehabilitation Adjunct**: Sometimes combined with physical/cognitive rehab in approved markets.
- **IMPORTANT — Evidence Is Mixed**: Human trial results are inconsistent and often modest; it is not FDA-approved, and benefits for healthy cognitive enhancement are largely anecdotal.
