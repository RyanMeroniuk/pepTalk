# Cerebrolysin

## Side Effects

- **Common**: Sensation of heat, sweating, or flushing, particularly with rapid IV infusion (slowing the infusion reduces this).
- **Common**: Headache, dizziness, or mild agitation.
- **Injection/Infusion Reactions**: Local irritation, and rarely infusion-related reactions requiring slower administration.
- **Allergic Reactions**: Hypersensitivity is possible given the protein-derived nature; anaphylaxis is rare but serious and is why IV dosing needs supervision.
- **Overstimulation**: Agitation, restlessness, or insomnia if dosed late in the day.
- **Animal-Derived Contamination Concern**: Because it is purified from porcine brain tissue, there is a theoretical (manufacturer-mitigated) concern about animal-sourced contaminants; this is a recognized caution for brain-derived biologics.
- **Contraindicated Populations**: Avoid in severe renal impairment, status epilepticus, and known hypersensitivity per approved labeling.
- **Mixed-Mixture Variability**: Being a complex mixture, batch-to-batch consistency and individual response can vary.
