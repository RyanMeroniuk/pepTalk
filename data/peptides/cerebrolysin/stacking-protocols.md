# Cerebrolysin

## Stacking Protocols

- **Cognitive Recovery Stack**: Cerebrolysin (clinical IV/IM dose) + Semax (300-600mcg intranasal) — pairs broad neurotrophic mixture support with semax's BDNF and focus effects; anecdotal, use under supervision.
- **Neurogenesis Stack**: Cerebrolysin (clinical dose) + P21 (research dose) — overlapping neurotrophic mechanisms; redundant and unstudied together, listed for completeness.
- **Neuroprotection Stack**: Cerebrolysin (clinical dose) + BPC-157 (250-500mcg) — adds tissue repair and general neuroprotection during recovery.
- **Memory Support Stack**: Cerebrolysin (clinical dose) + Lion's Mane (500-1000mg) + Alpha-GPC (300mg) — layers NGF and cholinergic support onto the neurotrophic base.
- **IMPORTANT**: Cerebrolysin is an IV/IM clinical drug; stacks should only be considered with a supervising clinician, and combination evidence in humans is minimal.
